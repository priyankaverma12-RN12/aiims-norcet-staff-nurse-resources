## Initial study explanations

### Anemia

Iron hemoglobin banane ke liye chahiye. Iron stores kam ho toh ferritin gir sakta hai aur RBC chhoti ho jaati hain. Is pattern mein iron deficiency suspect hoti hai.

### Transfusion reaction

Blood reaction mein patient ki condition jaldi kharab ho sakti hai. Nurse transfusion turant rok kar emergency assessment aur blood bank notification ka protocol follow karti hai.

### Thrombocytopenia

Platelets bleeding rokne mein help karti hain. Count bahut kam ho toh chhoti injury se bhi bleeding ho sakti hai. Nurse gentle care aur bleeding surveillance karti hai.

### Sickle cell disease

Sickle cells blood vessels ko block kar sakti hain. Chest pain aur fever acute chest syndrome ya infection ka clue ho sakte hain. Nurse oxygenation aur breathing jaldi assess karti hai.
