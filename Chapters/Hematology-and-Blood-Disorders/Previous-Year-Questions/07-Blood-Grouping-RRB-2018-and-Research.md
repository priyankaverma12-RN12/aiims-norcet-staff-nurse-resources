### Q1. In explaining traditional ABO red cell recipient compatibility, which plasma antibody pattern is expected in an ordinary group AB adult?

**Difficulty:** 🟢 Easy · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD · Source-linked adaptation

- **A.** Anti-A only
- **B.** Neither expected anti-A nor expected anti-B
- **C.** Both anti-A and anti-B
- **D.** Anti-B only

<details><summary>✅ Correct answer and detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: B. Neither expected anti-A nor expected anti-B**

- **A (Incorrect):** Anti-A normally group B aur group O plasma mein expected hoti hai. Group AB cells par A antigen bhi hota hai, isliye normal antibody pattern anti-A only wala nahi hai.
- **B (Correct):** Group AB red cells par A aur B dono antigens hote hain. Plasma mein normally corresponding ABO antibodies absent hoti hain, isliye ABO-compatible red cell groups ka range wider hota hai. RhD aur unexpected antibodies separately check karna zaroori hai.
- **C (Incorrect):** Dono anti-A aur anti-B ordinarily group O plasma ka pattern hai. Group AB patient ke liye yeh pattern assume karna galat hoga.
- **D (Incorrect):** Anti-B ordinarily group A aur group O plasma mein expected hoti hai. Group AB patient mein normal ABO pattern dono expected antibodies ka absent hona hai.

**Exam clue:** Universal recipient ka shortcut ABO red cell compatibility tak limited hai.

**Clinical reference:** [Pretransfusion testing](https://professionaleducation.blood.ca/en/transfusion/clinical-guide/pre-transfusion-testing).
