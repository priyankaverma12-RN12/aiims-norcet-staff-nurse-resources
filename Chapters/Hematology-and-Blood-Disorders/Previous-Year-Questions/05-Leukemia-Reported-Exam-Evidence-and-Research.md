### Q1. In an acute leukemia teaching session, which cell population should a student identify as the abnormal immature white-cell precursor population rather than a young red-cell population?

**Difficulty:** 🟢 Easy · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD · Source-linked adaptation

- **A.** Reticulocytes
- **B.** Leukemic blasts
- **C.** Mature erythrocytes
- **D.** Platelet fragments

<details><summary>✅ Correct answer and detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: B. Leukemic blasts**

- **A (Incorrect):** Reticulocytes immature erythroid cells hain, leukemic white-cell precursors nahi. Reticulocyte count erythropoietic response assess karta hai.
- **B (Correct):** Acute leukemias mein abnormal immature hematopoietic blast populations accumulate karti hain. Exact lymphoid ya myeloid lineage morphology, immunophenotype aur genetics se establish hoti hai.
- **C (Incorrect):** Mature erythrocytes oxygen transport karte hain aur normally nucleus absent hota hai. Yeh immature leukemic white-cell population nahi.
- **D (Incorrect):** Platelets megakaryocytes ke cytoplasmic fragments hain aur primary hemostasis mein participate karte hain. Yeh leukemic blasts nahi.

**Reported source:** [Testbook, AIIMS NORCET 2020 memory-based paper, reported 1 September 2020](https://testbook.com/question-answer/acute-leukemia-the-blood-cells--6177b7f98d130f22861961b8). Retrieved question and third-party answer (immature leukocytes). Official paper question number and official key not authenticated. Adaptation assesses lineage distinction rather than duplicating the original wording.
