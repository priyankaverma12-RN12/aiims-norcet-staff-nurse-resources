### Q1. Which circulating formed element normally lacks a nucleus but retains mitochondria that support its metabolic activity?

**Difficulty:** 🟡 Medium · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD · **Provenance:** Related-concept adaptation, HPRCA reported Q35

- **A.** Mature erythrocyte
- **B.** Platelet
- **C.** Mature circulating neutrophil
- **D.** Mature circulating lymphocyte

<details><summary>✅ Correct answer and detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: B. Platelet**

- **A (Incorrect):** Mature human erythrocytes mein nucleus aur mitochondria dono absent hote hain. Yeh energy ke liye mainly anaerobic glycolysis par depend karte hain, isliye nucleus absent hone ke bawajood mitochondria retain karne wala option nahi hai.
- **B (Correct):** Platelets megakaryocytes se nikle anucleate cytoplasmic fragments hote hain. Inmein mitochondria present hote hain, jo metabolic activity aur platelet function mein contribute karte hain.
- **C (Incorrect):** Mature neutrophil mein segmented nucleus present hota hai. Isliye yeh nucleus absent hone wali condition fulfill nahi karta.
- **D (Incorrect):** Mature lymphocytes nucleated immune cells hain. Inmein nucleus aur mitochondria dono hote hain, isliye yeh answer nahi hai.

**Exam clue:** Mature RBC aur platelet dono anucleate hain, par mitochondria presence inko distinguish karne mein useful hai.
