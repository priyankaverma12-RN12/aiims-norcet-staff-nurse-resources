### Q1. A father with HbAS believes he can pass the HbS allele to sons but not daughters. Which counseling response is correct?

**Difficulty:** 🟡 Medium · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD · Reported-exam adaptation

- **A.** HbS can be inherited only by sons from an affected father.
- **B.** HbS transmission depends on the father's ABO blood group.
- **C.** HbS transmission is autosomal and can occur in children of either sex.
- **D.** A daughter can inherit HbS only if her mother has HbSS.

<details><summary>✅ Correct answer and detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: C. HbS transmission is autosomal and can occur in children of either sex.**

- **A (Incorrect):** Father ka HbS allele Y chromosome par located nahi hota. HBB gene autosome par hai, isliye sons-only transmission wala conclusion sahi nahi hai.
- **B (Incorrect):** ABO blood group aur HBB genotype separate genetic characteristics hain. ABO compatibility ya blood group se HbS transmission determine nahi hota.
- **C (Correct):** HbAS father apne A ya S allele mein se ek transmit kar sakta hai, chahe child son ho ya daughter. Disease ka actual risk mother's hemoglobin genotype par bhi depend karta hai. Partner testing aur voluntary genetic counseling se family ko informed decision lene mein help milti hai.
- **D (Incorrect):** Daughter father se S allele inherit kar sakti hai even if mother has HbAA. Maternal HbSS hona daughter mein S allele transmission ki necessary condition nahi hai.

**Reported source:** [HPSSC Staff Nurse 2020 reported inheritance question](https://testbook.com/question-answer/sickle-cell-anemia-is--697afe8f2bca8a3dfc2ed7df). Third-party source directly retrieved and reports autosomal recessive inheritance as the answer. [Alternate report of October 2020 paper](https://testbook.com/question-answer/sickle-cell-anemia-is--61b9c39c69372dd28e4f84e7) indexed. Neither is proof of official key authentication. Adaptation tests sex-independent transmission, rather than copying the original options or the new bank's AS × AS and SS × AA genotype calculations.

**Clinical reference:** [GeneReviews sickle cell disease](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/sites/books/NBK1377/).
