### Q1. A patient with established hemophilia asks whether an old report of prolonged whole-blood clotting time should be used alone to assess the severity of the condition today. Which explanation is most appropriate?

**Difficulty:** 🟡 Medium · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD · **Provenance:** Official-source related-concept adaptation, not a verbatim PYQ

- **A.** A whole-blood clotting time precisely determines the severity in every patient.
- **B.** A normal platelet count is enough to grade hemophilia severity.
- **C.** Specific clotting factor activity and the clinical bleeding history are more informative than a historical nonspecific clotting time alone.
- **D.** The hemoglobin concentration alone identifies the missing clotting factor.

<details><summary>✅ Correct answer and detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: C. Specific clotting factor activity and the clinical bleeding history are more informative than a historical nonspecific clotting time alone.**

- **A (Incorrect):** Whole-blood clotting time ek purana nonspecific test hai. Iski value collection aur testing conditions se influence ho sakti hai. Isse har patient mein severity accurately grade nahi hoti.
- **B (Incorrect):** Hemophilia mein platelet number normal ho sakta hai, kyunki main problem plasma clotting factor ki activity mein hai. Normal platelet count se factor activity ka level pata nahi chalta.
- **C (Correct):** Hemophilia A aur B mein relevant clotting factor ki activity measure karna important hai. Patient ka actual bleeding history aur recent factor replacement bhi interpretation mein matter karte hain. Isliye old clotting time ko akela use nahi karna chahiye.
- **D (Incorrect):** Hemoglobin bleeding ke baad anemia assess karne mein useful ho sakta hai. Yeh deficient factor ko identify ya grade nahi karta.

**Clinical reference:** [ARUP hemostasis and thrombosis testing](https://arupconsult.com/content/hemostasis-and-thrombosis-testing).
