## Anemia

### Q1. A patient has fatigue, low hemoglobin, low MCV and low ferritin. Which condition is most likely?

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Topic:** Anemia

- **A.** Iron deficiency anemia
- **B.** Vitamin B12 deficiency as the typical cause
- **C.** Polycythemia
- **D.** Isolated thrombocytopenia

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: A. Iron deficiency anemia**

Iron hemoglobin banane ke liye chahiye. Iron stores kam ho toh ferritin gir sakta hai aur RBC chhoti ho jaati hain. Is pattern mein iron deficiency suspect hoti hai.

**Baaki options kyun galat hain?**

- **B.** B12 deficiency mein RBC often badi hoti hain.
- **C.** Polycythemia mein RBC mass badhta hai.
- **D.** Platelet kam hona anemia ka ye pattern nahi batata.

</details>

## Transfusion reaction

### Q2. Ten minutes after a blood transfusion begins, a patient develops fever, back pain and hypotension. Which action is first?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Topic:** Transfusion reaction

- **A.** Increase the transfusion rate
- **B.** Discard all identifying information
- **C.** Give the remaining blood through another vein
- **D.** Stop the transfusion and initiate the reaction protocol

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: D. Stop the transfusion and initiate the reaction protocol**

Blood reaction mein patient ki condition jaldi kharab ho sakti hai. Nurse transfusion turant rok kar emergency assessment aur blood bank notification ka protocol follow karti hai.

**Baaki options kyun galat hain?**

- **A.** Rate badhane se reaction aur severe ho sakta hai.
- **B.** Identification reaction investigation ke liye chahiye.
- **C.** Doosri vein se same blood dena unsafe hai.

</details>

## Thrombocytopenia

### Q3. A patient has a platelet count of 12,000/mm³ and new petechiae. Which nursing precaution is appropriate?

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Topic:** Thrombocytopenia

- **A.** Give routine intramuscular injections without review
- **B.** Use a hard-bristled toothbrush
- **C.** Minimize trauma and unnecessary invasive procedures
- **D.** Encourage vigorous flossing until gums bleed

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: C. Minimize trauma and unnecessary invasive procedures**

Platelets bleeding rokne mein help karti hain. Count bahut kam ho toh chhoti injury se bhi bleeding ho sakti hai. Nurse gentle care aur bleeding surveillance karti hai.

**Baaki options kyun galat hain?**

- **A.** IM injection hematoma bana sakta hai.
- **B.** Hard brush gums ko injure kar sakta hai.
- **D.** Gum bleeding ka risk badhega.

</details>

## Sickle cell disease

### Q4. A patient with sickle cell disease reports severe pain, fever and new chest discomfort. What should the nurse prioritize?

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Topic:** Sickle cell disease

- **A.** Assume that every chest symptom is benign
- **B.** Urgent assessment for acute chest syndrome and infection
- **C.** Routine discharge after pain alone improves
- **D.** Restrict all oxygen assessment

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: B. Urgent assessment for acute chest syndrome and infection**

Sickle cells blood vessels ko block kar sakti hain. Chest pain aur fever acute chest syndrome ya infection ka clue ho sakte hain. Nurse oxygenation aur breathing jaldi assess karti hai.

**Baaki options kyun galat hain?**

- **A.** Chest symptoms ko harmless assume nahi karna chahiye.
- **C.** Sirf pain relief se emergency exclude nahi hoti.
- **D.** Oxygen assessment important hai.

</details>
