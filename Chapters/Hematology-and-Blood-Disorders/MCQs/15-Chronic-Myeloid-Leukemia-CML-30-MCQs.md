### Q1. Persistent leukocytosis with myelocytes, metamyelocytes, mature neutrophils and basophilia suggests investigation for which disorder?

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Priority:** High

B. Immune thrombocytopenia

<details>
<summary>Answer & explanation</summary>
