### Q1. A blood centre has separated a whole-blood donation into red cells, plasma and platelets. What is the main patient-care advantage of this approach?

**Difficulty:** 🟡 Medium · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD · Original practice

- **A.** Each component becomes compatible with every recipient
- **B.** One donation can provide targeted treatment for different recipients
- **C.** All infectious agents are removed during centrifugation
- **D.** All three products must be transfused to the same recipient

<details><summary>✅ Correct answer and detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: B. One donation can provide targeted treatment for different recipients**

- **A (Incorrect):** Component separation ke baad bhi blood group aur doosre compatibility rules apply hote hain. Separation se antigen ya antibody ka risk apne aap khatam nahi hota.
- **B (Correct):** Jab blood ko alag components mein baanta jaata hai, toh har patient ko uski actual kami ke hisaab se product mil sakta hai. Isse unnecessary components dene ki zaroorat kam hoti hai.
- **C (Incorrect):** Centrifugation cells ko unki physical properties ke basis par alag karta hai. Infection screening aur validated safety processes ki zaroorat ab bhi hoti hai.
- **D (Incorrect):** Patient ko sirf indicated component diya jaata hai. Stable anemia mein baaki products dene se benefit ke bina extra exposure ho sakta hai.

**Exam clue:** Component therapy mein deficiency ko target karna hai, poore donation ko har patient ko dena zaroori nahi hai.

### Q3. A stable patient with iron deficiency anemia is improving on iron treatment and has no urgent oxygen-delivery problem. Why should a request for routine red cell transfusion be reviewed?

- **A (Correct):** Jab patient stable ho aur iron se recovery ka time mil sakta ho, toh body apne red cells bana sakti hai. Unnecessary transfusion se patient ko avoidable reaction aur sensitization ka risk milta hai.

- **C (Incorrect):** Blood test iron therapy ko contraindicate nahi karta. Iron deficiency ki treatment laboratory findings aur clinical assessment se plan hoti hai.
- **D (Incorrect):** Plasma mein iron deficiency treat karne ke liye useful dose mein hemoglobin nahi hota. Isliye unnecessary plasma exposure bhi appropriate alternative nahi hai.
