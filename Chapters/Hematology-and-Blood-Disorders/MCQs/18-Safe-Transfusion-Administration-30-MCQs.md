### Q1. A stable patient has a scheduled red cell transfusion but cannot explain why it was prescribed and says no clinician discussed the alternatives. What should the nurse do before obtaining the unit?

**Difficulty:** 🟡 Medium · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD · Original practice

- **A.** Collect the blood immediately and discuss consent after infusion
- **B.** Treat the prescription itself as proof of consent
- **C.** Arrange clarification of the indication and informed consent before routine transfusion
- **D.** Ask an unrelated visitor to sign instead of the competent patient

<details><summary>✅ Correct answer and detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: C. Arrange clarification of the indication and informed consent before routine transfusion**

- **A (Incorrect):** Elective transfusion mein pehle consent clarify karna chahiye. Baad mein discussion karne se patient ka informed choice ka opportunity chala jaata hai.
- **B (Incorrect):** Prescription clinician ka treatment order hai. Isse patient ki informed agreement automatically establish nahi hoti.
- **C (Correct):** Consent ka matlab sirf signature nahi hai. Patient ko treatment ka reason, important risks aur alternatives samajhne ka chance milna chahiye.
- **D (Incorrect):** Decision-making capacity wale patient ki apni informed agreement important hai. Unrelated visitor ko patient ka substitute nahi maan sakte.
