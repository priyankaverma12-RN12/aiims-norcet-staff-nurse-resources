### Q1. An adult has hemoglobin 7.8 g/dL, leukocytes 2,000/µL and platelets 48,000/µL. Which term describes this combined laboratory pattern?

**Difficulty:** 🟡 Medium · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD · Original practice

- **A.** Isolated anemia
- **B.** Pancytopenia
- **C.** Isolated thrombocytopenia
- **D.** Leukocytosis

<details><summary>✅ Correct answer and detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: B. Pancytopenia**

- **A (Incorrect):** Anemia mein hemoglobin kam hota hai, lekin yahan white cells aur platelets bhi kam hain, isliye isolated anemia poori report ko explain nahi karta.
- **B (Correct):** Pancytopenia ka matlab teen major blood cell lines ka kam hona hai. Yahan anemia, leukopenia aur thrombocytopenia saath hain. Is pattern ki wajah jaanne ke liye further evaluation chahiye.
- **C (Incorrect):** Isolated thrombocytopenia mein platelet count kam hota hai aur doosri cell lines ka kam hona zaroori nahi hai.
- **D (Incorrect):** Leukocytosis mein white cell count badhta hai, jabki is report mein count kam hai.

**Exam clue:** Teen cell lines kam hon toh ek hi diagnosis assume mat karo.

**Clinical reference:** [PANCY reference](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/sites/books/NBK563146/)

### Q2. A patient has MCV 72 fL but hemoglobin is within the laboratory's age-appropriate reference interval. What is the most accurate interpretation?

**Difficulty:** 🟡 Medium · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD · Original practice

- **A.** Anemia is established regardless of hemoglobin

- **D.** The blood count excludes an iron disorder

- **A (Incorrect):** Anemia ka assessment hemoglobin aur appropriate reference interval se hota hai. Sirf MCV kam hone se anemia establish nahi hota.

- **C (Correct):** MCV average red cell volume hai. Kam MCV microcytosis dikhata hai, lekin hemoglobin reference interval mein hai, isliye anemia abhi demonstrate nahi hua.
- **D (Incorrect):** Iron deficiency early stage mein hemoglobin normal reh sakta hai. Isliye clinical assessment aur iron studies ki zaroorat ho sakti hai.

**Exam clue:** Cell size aur anemia alag findings hain.

### Q7. An anemic patient has an RBC count of 2.0 million/µL and a reticulocyte percentage of 2%. What is the absolute reticulocyte count?

<details><summary>✅ Correct answer and detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: D. 40,000/µL**

### Q21. A patient without bleeding has an unexpectedly low automated platelet count. The laboratory reports platelet clumps in the sample. What is the best next step?

**Difficulty:** 🔴 Difficult · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD · Original practice

- **A.** Accept the count as definitely accurate
- **B.** Discuss possible spurious thrombocytopenia with the laboratory and arrange confirmation as directed
- **C.** Administer a platelet transfusion without an order
- **D.** Ignore all subsequent platelet measurements

<details><summary>✅ Correct answer and detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: B. Discuss possible spurious thrombocytopenia with the laboratory and arrange confirmation as directed**
