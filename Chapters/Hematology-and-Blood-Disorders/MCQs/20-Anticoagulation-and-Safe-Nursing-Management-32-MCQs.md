### Q1. A patient with proximal DVT asks why anticoagulation is useful even though the thrombus already exists. Which explanation is most accurate?

**Difficulty:** 🟡 Medium · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD · Original practice

- **A.** It directly activates plasminogen to dissolve fibrin
- **B.** It physically removes the thrombus from the vein
- **C.** It reduces clot extension and new clot formation while endogenous fibrinolysis can remodel the thrombus
- **D.** It acts solely on platelet adhesion

<details><summary>✅ Correct answer and detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: C. It reduces clot extension and new clot formation while endogenous fibrinolysis can remodel the thrombus**

- **A (Incorrect):** Plasminogen activation thrombolytic drugs ka action hai. Routine anticoagulants existing fibrin clot ko directly dissolve nahi karte.
- **B (Incorrect):** Physical clot removal thrombectomy procedure se ho sakti hai. Anticoagulant medicine us mechanical intervention ka equivalent nahi hai.
- **C (Correct):** Anticoagulant coagulation reactions ko slow karta hai, isliye existing clot ke badhne aur naye clots banne ka risk kam hota hai. Body ka natural fibrinolytic system gradually clot remodel kar sakta hai.
- **D (Incorrect):** Platelet adhesion primary hemostasis ka part hai. DVT anticoagulation mainly coagulation factor activity reduce karti hai, sirf platelet adhesion nahi.

**Exam clue:** Anticoagulant aur thrombolytic mechanism ko alag rakho.
