### Q1. A patient reports repeated bleeding into knee joints after minor trauma but rarely develops petechiae. Which category of hemostatic disorder is most consistent with this history?

**Difficulty:** 🟡 Medium · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD · Original practice

- **A.** Isolated mild platelet dysfunction
- **B.** Coagulation factor deficiency
- **C.** Isolated iron deficiency without hemostatic disease
- **D.** Uncomplicated leukopenia

<details><summary>✅ Correct answer and detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: B. Coagulation factor deficiency**

- **A (Incorrect):** Platelet dysfunction mein aam taur par gums, nose aur skin se bleeding zyada prominent hoti hai. Deep joint bleeding is pattern ka typical feature nahi hai.
- **B (Correct):** Clotting factors fibrin clot ko stable banane mein madad karte hain. Inki kami mein deep tissue aur joint bleeding zyada typical hoti hai, isliye factor disorder evaluate karna chahiye.
- **C (Incorrect):** Iron deficiency anemia ka cause ho sakti hai, lekin baar-baar joint bleeding ko independently explain nahi karti.
- **D (Incorrect):** Leukopenia mein white cells kam hote hain aur infection ka risk badh sakta hai. Yeh bleeding pattern coagulation evaluation maangta hai.
