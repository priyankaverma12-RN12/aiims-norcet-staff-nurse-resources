### Q1. A blast population expresses strong myeloperoxidase activity. Which lineage is supported?

**Difficulty:** 🟡 Medium · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD · Original practice

- **A.** Mature B-cell origin alone
- **B.** Normal platelet maturation
- **C.** Myeloid differentiation
- **D.** Mature erythrocyte differentiation

<details><summary>✅ Correct answer and detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: C. Myeloid differentiation**

- **A (Incorrect):** B-cell lineage ke liye B-cell markers assess karte hain. MPO activity us lineage ka defining marker nahi hai.
- **B (Incorrect):** Platelets megakaryocytes se arise karti hain. Abnormal MPO-positive blasts normal platelet maturation prove nahi karte.
- **C (Correct):** MPO myeloid granules ka enzyme hai. Strong expression myeloid origin support karti hai, lekin final classification immunophenotype aur genetics ke saath hoti hai.
- **D (Incorrect):** Mature RBCs oxygen transport karti hain. MPO-positive blasts erythrocyte maturation ka typical result nahi hain.

- **C.** Heinz bodies
