### Q1. A patient has confirmed ABO-incompatible red cell exposure. Which immune mechanism best explains the rapid intravascular destruction of donor cells?

**Difficulty:** 🟡 Medium · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD · Original practice

- **A.** Donor platelets suppress erythropoietin production
- **B.** Recipient anti-A or anti-B activates complement against donor red cells
- **C.** Recipient iron stores immediately become depleted
- **D.** Donor T cells engraft and attack the marrow immediately

<details><summary>✅ Correct answer and detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: B. Recipient anti-A or anti-B activates complement against donor red cells**

- **A (Incorrect):** Platelets ka primary role hemostasis hai. Erythropoietin suppression se acute donor-cell destruction ka mechanism explain nahi hota.
- **B (Correct):** Recipient ki naturally occurring ABO antibodies incompatible donor cells se bind kar sakti hain. Complement activation se cells circulation ke andar rapidly break ho sakti hain aur severe systemic reaction develop ho sakti hai.
- **C (Incorrect):** Iron deficiency red cell production ko gradually affect karti hai. Yeh minutes mein incompatible donor cells destroy hone ka mechanism nahi hai.
- **D (Incorrect):** Donor T-cell engraftment TA-GVHD ka mechanism hai aur usually delayed presentation hoti hai. Acute ABO hemolysis ka main mechanism antibody aur complement hai.

**Exam clue:** ABO mismatch mein antibody-mediated complement injury yaad rakho.

<details><summary>✅ Correct answer and detailed explanation</summary>

- **D (Incorrect):** B12 aur folate chronic hematopoiesis ke assessment mein useful hain. Acute reaction-associated DIC ka immediate assessment in par depend nahi karta.
