### Q1. Which mechanism best explains primary immune thrombocytopenia?

**Difficulty:** 🟡 Medium · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD · Original practice

- **A.** Isolated deficiency of factor VIII
- **B.** Mechanical fragmentation of red cells as the sole mechanism
- **C.** Immune-mediated platelet destruction with impaired platelet production
- **D.** Excessive absorption of dietary iron

<details><summary>✅ Correct answer and detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: C. Immune-mediated platelet destruction with impaired platelet production**

- **A (Incorrect):** Factor VIII deficiency secondary coagulation pathway affect karti hai. ITP mein main problem platelet count aur immune-mediated production or clearance hai.
- **B (Incorrect):** Red cell fragmentation microangiopathic hemolysis suggest karti hai. ITP typically isolated thrombocytopenia hoti hai, not primary red cell injury.
- **C (Correct):** ITP mein immune system platelet surface targets ke against react kar sakta hai. Platelet clearance badhta hai aur megakaryocyte function bhi impair ho sakta hai, isliye low platelet count sirf destruction ka result nahi hota.
- **D (Incorrect):** Iron absorption erythropoiesis aur iron burden se related hai. ITP ka immune platelet mechanism isse explain nahi hota.
