### Q1. Which age group commonly develops acute lymphoblastic leukemia, although the disease can occur at any age?

**Difficulty:** 🟢 Easy · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD · Original practice

- **A.** Only adults over 80 years
- **B.** Children
- **C.** Only newborns in the first week
- **D.** Only pregnant adults

<details><summary>✅ Correct answer and detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: B. Children**

- **A (Incorrect):** Older adults mein ALL possible hai, lekin disease isi age tak restricted nahi hai.
- **B (Correct):** ALL childhood cancer ka common type hai. Age distribution diagnostic suspicion guide karti hai, lekin adults mein bhi ALL possible hai.
- **C (Incorrect):** Congenital leukemia rare ho sakti hai. ALL ka common presentation exclusively neonatal period mein nahi hota.
- **D (Incorrect):** Pregnancy ALL diagnosis ki necessary condition nahi hai. Disease children aur nonpregnant adults mein bhi occur karti hai.
