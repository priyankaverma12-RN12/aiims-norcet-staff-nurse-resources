### Q1. An adult has a falling hemoglobin concentration with no obvious external blood loss. Which combination of laboratory trends most strongly supports ongoing hemolysis with an appropriate marrow response?

**Difficulty:** 🔴 Difficult · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD · Original practice

- **A.** Falling reticulocytes, low ferritin and increased total iron-binding capacity
- **B.** Low reticulocytes with isolated low vitamin B12 and no biochemical evidence of cell destruction
- **C.** Rising reticulocytes, rising LDH and indirect bilirubin, falling haptoglobin
- **D.** Rising haptoglobin with isolated elevation of C-reactive protein and unchanged hemoglobin

<details><summary>✅ Correct answer and detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: C. Rising reticulocytes, rising LDH and indirect bilirubin, falling haptoglobin**

- **A (Incorrect):** Yeh pattern iron deficiency aur limited red cell production ke saath fit ho sakta hai. Is combination se ongoing hemolysis aur appropriate marrow response demonstrate nahi hote.
- **B (Incorrect):** Yeh deficient red cell production ka clue ho sakta hai. Hemolysis ka assessment additional biochemical markers aur clinical context se karna hoga.
- **C (Correct):** Jab red cells jaldi break hoti hain toh LDH aur indirect bilirubin badh sakte hain. Haptoglobin consume hota hai aur functioning marrow replacement cells release karta hai, isliye yeh combined pattern hemolysis ko strongly support karta hai.
- **D (Incorrect):** Inflammation haptoglobin aur CRP badha sakti hai. Is pattern mein falling hemoglobin aur RBC breakdown ka direct evidence nahi diya gaya hai.

### Q2. A patient with predominantly extravascular hemolysis develops mild jaundice and an increased unconjugated bilirubin concentration. Urine bilirubin is negative. Which explanation is consistent with these findings?

**Difficulty:** 🔴 Difficult · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD · Original practice

- **A.** Unconjugated bilirubin is albumin-bound and is not normally filtered into urine
- **B.** All circulating bilirubin is freely filtered by the kidney
- **C.** Negative urine bilirubin excludes all forms of jaundice
- **D.** Jaundice must result exclusively from biliary obstruction

<details><summary>✅ Correct answer and detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: A. Unconjugated bilirubin is albumin-bound and is not normally filtered into urine**

- **A (Correct):** Red cell heme breakdown se unconjugated bilirubin banta hai. Yeh albumin ke saath circulate karta hai aur urine mein normally filter nahi hota, isliye jaundice ke bawajood urine bilirubin negative ho sakta hai.
- **B (Incorrect):** Bilirubin ke different forms ka solubility aur transport alag hota hai. Unconjugated bilirubin freely filtered nahi hota.
- **C (Incorrect):** Urine bilirubin test primarily conjugated bilirubin detect karta hai. Unconjugated hyperbilirubinemia mein negative result possible hai.
- **D (Incorrect):** Hemolysis bilirubin production badha sakti hai. Biliary obstruction jaundice ka ek cause hai, har case ka exclusive cause nahi.
