### Q1. A bone marrow report describes erythroid precursors with relatively mature cytoplasm but delayed nuclear maturation. What mechanism best explains this finding in megaloblastic anemia?

**Difficulty:** 🔴 Difficult · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD · Original practice

- **A.** Accelerated DNA synthesis produces premature nuclear maturation
- **B.** Impaired DNA synthesis slows nuclear maturation
- **C.** Isolated impairment of heme synthesis with otherwise normal DNA replication
- **D.** Selective peripheral destruction of normal mature erythrocytes

<details><summary>✅ Correct answer and detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: B. Impaired DNA synthesis slows nuclear maturation**

- **A (Incorrect):** Yahan nucleus ki maturation delayed hai. Accelerated DNA synthesis is observed mismatch ke opposite mechanism hoga.
- **B (Correct):** B12 ya folate ki kami mein DNA synthesis disturb hoti hai. Cell ka cytoplasm grow karta rehta hai, lekin nucleus time par mature nahi hota. Isi mismatch ko nuclear-cytoplasmic asynchrony kehte hain.
- **C (Incorrect):** Heme synthesis ki problem microcytic anemia de sakti hai. Yahan nuclear maturation ka delay DNA synthesis defect ko support karta hai.
- **D (Incorrect):** Peripheral destruction hemolysis ka mechanism hai. Marrow precursor ke abnormal nuclear maturation ko akela explain nahi karta.

### Q2. Which peripheral blood smear combination most strongly supports a megaloblastic process in an adult with unexplained macrocytosis?

**Difficulty:** 🟡 Medium · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD · Original practice

- **B.** Schistocytes and marked thrombocytopenia without macro-ovalocytes
- **C.** Predominant microcytic hypochromic cells with pencil forms
- **D.** Macro-ovalocytes and hypersegmented neutrophils

<details><summary>✅ Correct answer and detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: D. Macro-ovalocytes and hypersegmented neutrophils**

- **A (Incorrect):** Target cells liver disease ya hemoglobin disorders mein mil sakte hain. Yeh combination megaloblastic process ka characteristic pattern nahi hai.

- **C (Incorrect):** Chhoti pale cells aur pencil forms iron deficiency mein mil sakte hain. Yeh megaloblastic pattern se different hain.
- **D (Correct):** Macro-ovalocytes bade oval red cells hain aur hypersegmented neutrophils mein nucleus ke lobes zyada hote hain. Yeh combination impaired DNA maturation ka important clue hai.
