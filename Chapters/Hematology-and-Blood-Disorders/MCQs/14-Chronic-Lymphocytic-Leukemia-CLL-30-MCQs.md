### Q1. Which cell population typically accumulates in classic chronic lymphocytic leukemia?

**Difficulty:** 🟡 Medium · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD · Original practice

- **A.** Clonal erythroblasts
- **B.** Reactive polyclonal neutrophils
- **C.** Clonal mature B lymphocytes
- **D.** Isolated megakaryocytes

<details><summary>✅ Correct answer and detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: C. Clonal mature B lymphocytes**

- **A (Incorrect):** Erythroblasts red cell precursors hain. CLL mature B lymphoid malignancy hai.
- **B (Incorrect):** Reactive neutrophilia infection ya inflammation mein ho sakti hai. CLL mein clonal B-cell expansion hoti hai.
- **C (Correct):** CLL mein mature-looking clonal B cells accumulate hoti hain. Yeh acute lymphoblastic leukemia ke immature precursor population se distinct hai.
- **D (Incorrect):** Megakaryocytes platelet-producing marrow cells hain. CLL ka typical malignant population B lymphocytes hain.
