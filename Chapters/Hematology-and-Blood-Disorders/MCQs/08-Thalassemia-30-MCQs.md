### Q1. Which defect distinguishes beta-thalassemia from a structural variant such as HbS?

**Difficulty:** 🟡 Medium · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD · Original practice

- **A.** Replacement of glutamic acid by valine alone
- **B.** Reduced or absent production of beta-globin chains
- **C.** Complete absence of heme iron in every erythrocyte
- **D.** Selective destruction of platelets

<details><summary>✅ Correct answer and detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: B. Reduced or absent production of beta-globin chains**

- **A (Incorrect):** Yeh HbS ki specific structural substitution hai. Thalassemia mein primary problem globin synthesis ki quantity hai.
- **B (Correct):** Beta-thalassemia mein beta chains kam banti hain ya absent hoti hain. HbS mein beta chain ka structure change hota hai, isliye dono mechanisms different hain.
- **C (Incorrect):** Heme iron oxygen binding ke liye essential hai. Globin synthesis defect ko iron absence ke equal nahi kehna chahiye.
- **D (Incorrect):** Platelet destruction hemostasis affect karti hai. Thalassemia ka primary defect globin synthesis mein hai.

<details><summary>✅ Correct answer and detailed explanation</summary>
