### Q1. Which molecular change produces hemoglobin S?

**Difficulty:** 🟡 Medium · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD · Original practice

- **A.** Deletion of all alpha-globin genes
- **B.** Loss of iron from every heme group
- **C.** Substitution of valine for glutamic acid in beta-globin
- **D.** Replacement of gamma-globin with albumin

<details><summary>✅ Correct answer and detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: C. Substitution of valine for glutamic acid in beta-globin**

- **A (Incorrect):** Alpha-globin gene deletion alpha thalassemia se related hai. HbS ek beta-globin structural variant hai.
- **B (Incorrect):** Heme iron oxygen binding ke liye important hai, lekin HbS ki underlying mutation globin chain mein hai.
- **C (Correct):** Beta-globin ki specific amino acid substitution se deoxygenated HbS polymer bana sakta hai. Isse RBC rigid aur sickle-shaped ho sakti hai.
- **D (Incorrect):** Albumin plasma protein hai aur gamma-globin fetal hemoglobin ki chain hai. Dono interchangeable nahi hain.
