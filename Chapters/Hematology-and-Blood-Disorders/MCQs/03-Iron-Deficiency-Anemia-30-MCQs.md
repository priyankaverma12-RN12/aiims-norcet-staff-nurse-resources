### Q1. A patient with documented anemia repeatedly chews ice despite having no thirst. Which interpretation should guide the nurse's assessment?

**Difficulty:** 🟡 Medium · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD · Original practice

- **A.** The behavior establishes diabetes insipidus
- **B.** The behavior confirms vitamin B12 deficiency
- **C.** Pagophagia is a clue to possible iron deficiency
- **D.** The behavior excludes nutritional deficiency

<details><summary>✅ Correct answer and detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: C. Pagophagia is a clue to possible iron deficiency**

- **A (Incorrect):** Diabetes insipidus mein excessive dilute urine aur thirst mil sakti hai. Ice ki specific craving se yeh diagnosis establish nahi hota.
- **B (Incorrect):** B12 deficiency neurological changes ya megaloblastic anemia de sakti hai. Ice craving is deficiency ki confirmation nahi hai.
- **C (Correct):** Pagophagia ka matlab baar-baar ice khane ki craving hai. Yeh iron deficiency ke saath mil sakti hai. Nurse patient ki dietary aur bleeding history leti hai aur iron studies ki zaroorat treating team se discuss karti hai.
- **D (Incorrect):** Nonfood ya ice craving kabhi nutritional deficiency ka clue hoti hai. Isliye behavior ko dismiss karne ki jagah assess karna chahiye.
