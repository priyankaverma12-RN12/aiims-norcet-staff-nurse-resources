### Q1. A patient asks why leukemia can cause anemia even when no external bleeding is visible. Which explanation is most appropriate?

**Difficulty:** 🟡 Medium · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD · Original practice

- **A.** Leukemia always causes complete dietary iron malabsorption
- **B.** Leukemic cells convert all mature red cells into platelets
- **C.** Leukemic proliferation can suppress normal erythropoiesis
- **D.** All anemia in leukemia is caused by external hemorrhage

<details><summary>✅ Correct answer and detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: C. Leukemic proliferation can suppress normal erythropoiesis**

- **A (Incorrect):** Iron absorption intestinal function par depend karti hai. Leukemia-associated anemia mein marrow suppression important cause hai, universal malabsorption nahi.
- **B (Incorrect):** RBCs aur platelets different lineages se develop hote hain. Mature erythrocytes platelets mein convert nahi hote.
- **C (Correct):** Marrow mein abnormal leukemic cells expand hoti hain aur normal blood cell production disturb ho sakti hai. Isliye bleeding ke bina bhi RBC output kam ho sakta hai.
- **D (Incorrect):** Bleeding contribute kar sakti hai, lekin marrow failure aur treatment effects bhi anemia cause karte hain.
