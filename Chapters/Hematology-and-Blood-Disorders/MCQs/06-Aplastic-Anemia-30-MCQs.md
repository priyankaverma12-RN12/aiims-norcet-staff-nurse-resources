### Q1. Which mechanism most directly explains the simultaneous reduction of erythrocytes, leukocytes and platelets in acquired aplastic anemia?

**Difficulty:** 🟡 Medium · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD · Original practice

- **A.** Selective destruction of mature erythrocytes in the spleen
- **B.** Failure of hematopoietic stem cell production, often mediated by immune injury
- **C.** Isolated deficiency of intrinsic factor
- **D.** Increased erythropoietin production with normal marrow reserve

<details><summary>✅ Correct answer and detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: B. Failure of hematopoietic stem cell production, often mediated by immune injury**

- **A (Incorrect):** Splenic red cell destruction hemolytic anemia ka mechanism ho sakta hai. Isse isolated red cell loss explain hota hai, lekin stem cell failure aur multilineage reduction directly explain nahi hote.
- **B (Correct):** Hematopoietic stem cells marrow mein sabhi major blood cell lineages banati hain. Acquired aplastic anemia mein immune injury stem cells ko reduce kar sakti hai, jisse red cells, white cells aur platelets sab kam ho sakte hain.
- **C (Incorrect):** Intrinsic factor deficiency vitamin B12 absorption ko reduce karti hai aur megaloblastic changes de sakti hai. Aplastic anemia ka characteristic hypocellular marrow mechanism isse different hai.
- **D (Incorrect):** Erythropoietin functioning marrow ko RBC production ka signal deta hai. Normal stem cell reserve ke saath increased signal generalized marrow failure ka primary mechanism nahi hai.

- **A (Incorrect):** Anaphylaxis typically rapid onset emergency hoti hai. Several days baad rash aur arthralgia serum sickness ki possibility raise karte hain.
