### Q1. An adult's red cells agglutinate with anti-B but not anti-A. Plasma reacts with reagent A cells but not B cells. Which interpretation agrees with both results?

**Difficulty:** 🟡 Medium · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD · Original practice

- **A.** Group A
- **B.** Group AB
- **C.** Group B
- **D.** Group O

<details><summary>✅ Correct answer and detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: C. Group B**

- **A (Incorrect):** Group A red cells anti-A se react karte hain aur plasma mein anti-B expected hoti hai. Yahan dono test results is pattern ke opposite hain.
- **B (Incorrect):** AB red cells par dono A aur B antigens hote hain. Forward test mein sirf anti-B reaction milna AB ke normal pattern se match nahi karta.
- **C (Correct):** Forward test mein anti-B se clumping B antigen ko show karti hai. Reverse test mein A cells se reaction expected anti-A ko show karti hai. Dono results group B ko support karte hain.
- **D (Incorrect):** Ordinary O cells par A aur B antigen nahi hote. Yahan anti-B se clear reaction hai, isliye ordinary O interpretation fit nahi hoti.

**Exam clue:** Forward aur reverse tests ko ek saath interpret karo.

### Q2. An adult's forward ABO result suggests AB, but reverse grouping unexpectedly reacts with reagent B cells. The patient is stable and transfusion is elective. What is the next step?

**Difficulty:** 🟡 Medium · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD · Original practice

- **A.** Resolve the ABO discrepancy before routine group-specific issue
- **B.** Ignore reverse grouping
- **C.** Issue group B red cells based solely on the reverse reaction
- **D.** Report group O without investigation

<details><summary>✅ Correct answer and detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: A. Resolve the ABO discrepancy before routine group-specific issue**

- **A (Correct):** Forward aur reverse grouping normally ek doosre ko confirm karte hain. Dono disagree karein toh cause investigate karna zaroori hai, kyunki guess karke unit release karna unsafe ho sakta hai.
- **B (Incorrect):** Forward testing technical ya sample-related problem se affected ho sakti hai. Reverse result ko ignore karne se important discrepancy miss ho sakti hai.
- **C (Incorrect):** Reverse test patient plasma ki antibody reaction dikhata hai. Is ek result se unresolved patient group aur donor compatibility determine nahi kar sakte.
- **D (Incorrect):** Group O report karne ke liye consistent test evidence chahiye. Discrepancy ko naya label dekar solve nahi kiya ja sakta.

**Exam clue:** Elective issue se pehle ABO discrepancy resolve karo.

**Correct answer: D. Early infant plasma antibodies may be maternal rather than reliably infant-produced**

- **A (Incorrect):** ABO antigens birth ke time red cells par present ho sakte hain. Limitation mainly reverse grouping mein antibody interpretation ki hoti hai.

- **D (Correct):** Newborn ka immune system abhi develop ho raha hota hai. Maternal antibodies infant plasma mein ho sakti hain, isliye adult-style reverse grouping reliable confirmation nahi hoti.
