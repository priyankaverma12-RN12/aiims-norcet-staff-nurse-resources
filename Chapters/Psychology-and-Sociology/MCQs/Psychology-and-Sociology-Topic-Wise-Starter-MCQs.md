## Learning

### Q1. A nurse demonstrates insulin administration and asks the patient to repeat the procedure. Which learning approach is being used?

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Topic:** Learning

- **A.** Return demonstration to assess psychomotor learning
- **B.** Passive reading alone
- **C.** Punishment-based learning
- **D.** Assessment of social class only

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: A. Return demonstration to assess psychomotor learning**

Jab patient procedure khud karke dikhata hai, toh nurse dekh sakti hai ki skill sahi seekhi ya nahi. Isko return demonstration kehte hain.

**Baaki options kyun galat hain?**

- **B.** Reading alone skill perform karna confirm nahi karti.
- **C.** Punishment is method ka purpose nahi hai.
- **D.** Social class assessment skill demonstration nahi hai.

</details>

## Coping

### Q2. A patient awaiting surgery says they feel frightened and cannot sleep. Which initial response supports coping?

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Topic:** Coping

- **A.** Dismiss fear as irrational
- **B.** Give false guarantees about the outcome
- **C.** Avoid discussing the procedure entirely
- **D.** Explore the concern and provide understandable information

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: D. Explore the concern and provide understandable information**

Jab patient ko surgery ka darr hota hai, toh pehle uski baat sunna helpful hai. Nurse simple information dekar uncertainty kam kar sakti hai aur support arrange kar sakti hai.

**Baaki options kyun galat hain?**

- **A.** Fear dismiss karne se trust kam ho sakta hai.
- **B.** False guarantee honest communication nahi hai.
- **C.** Discussion avoid karne se confusion reh sakti hai.

</details>

## Social determinants

### Q3. A patient repeatedly misses clinic visits because transport is unaffordable. Which action best addresses the barrier?

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Topic:** Social determinants

- **A.** Stop all clinical follow-up
- **B.** Assume the patient does not value health
- **C.** Explore accessible follow-up arrangements and available support
- **D.** Label the patient noncompliant without discussion

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: C. Explore accessible follow-up arrangements and available support**

Health care tak pahunch paisa aur transport se affect ho sakti hai. Nurse reason samajhkar practical follow-up option dhoondhti hai. Isse patient ko blame kiye bina care improve hoti hai.

**Baaki options kyun galat hain?**

- **A.** Follow-up stop karne se care gap badhta hai.
- **B.** Assumption patient ki situation ko ignore karti hai.
- **D.** Labeling actual barrier solve nahi karti.

</details>

## Culture and communication

### Q4. A patient has limited understanding of the language used for consent discussions. Which approach is appropriate?

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Topic:** Culture and communication

- **A.** Use an unrelated patient's consent form
- **B.** Arrange qualified interpretation and check understanding
- **C.** Ask the patient to sign without explanation
- **D.** Speak louder in the same unfamiliar language

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: B. Arrange qualified interpretation and check understanding**

Consent tab meaningful hota hai jab patient information samjhe. Qualified interpreter aur teach-back understanding confirm karne mein madad karte hain.

**Baaki options kyun galat hain?**

- **A.** Doosre patient ka form identity aur privacy ko violate kar sakta hai.
- **C.** Bina explanation consent informed nahi hoga.
- **D.** Louder bolna language barrier solve nahi karta.

</details>
