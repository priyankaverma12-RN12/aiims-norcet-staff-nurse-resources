- **C (Galat):** Blind force se tissue injury, false passage ya tube displacement worsen ho sakta hai.
- **D (Sahi):** Urgent help aur established tracheostomy algorithm systematic airway evaluation aur oxygenation support karte hain.

- **C (Galat):** Running se muscle movement badhegi aur safe transport delay ho sakta hai.
- **D (Sahi):** Calm reassurance, immobilization aur rapid transport WHO first-aid principle ke consistent hain.

**Exam clue:** Snakebite: immobilize and transport, no cutting or tight tourniquet.

### Q63. A patient has BP 84/48 mmHg. What is the approximate mean arterial pressure at a regular resting heart rate?

<!-- job4-metadata -->

- **B.** 132 mmHg
- **C.** 36 mmHg
- **D.** 60 mmHg

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: D. 60 mmHg**

<!-- job5-rationale -->
Mean arterial pressure (MAP) ek cardiac cycle ke average arterial pressure ka estimate hai. Ordinary resting rate par diastole zyada duration leta hai, isliye simple arithmetic average use nahi karte. Formula MAP ≈ DBP + (SBP − DBP)/3, equivalent to (SBP + 2 × DBP)/3. First PP = 84 − 48 = 36 mmHg. One third = 12. MAP ≈ 48 + 12 = 60 mmHg. Low BP aur MAP ko patient perfusion, mental status, urine output aur clinical context ke saath urgently assess karo. - **B:** 132 mmHg SBP+DBP sum hai.
- **C:** 36 mmHg pulse pressure hai, MAP nahi.
- **D:** 60 mmHg correct weighted estimate hai.

**Exam clue:** MAP ≈ DBP + PP/3. Do not confuse with pulse pressure.

### Q120. A patient receiving a blood product through an IV develops chills, dyspnea and back pain. Which immediate nursing response is appropriate?

**Difficulty:** 🔴 Difficult · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD · **[🟣 AIIMS NORCET 9 Mains 27 September 2025 — related tested concept only](https://testbook.com/question-answer/during-a-blood-transfusion-a-patient-suddenly-dev--692ee9e35f2b488969718e97)** · Legacy topic-bank reuse

- **A.** Give the next blood unit without investigation
- **B.** Discard the bag and tubing before notifying anyone
- **C.** Stop the transfusion, assess the patient and initiate the transfusion reaction protocol
- **D.** Increase the transfusion speed to finish the unit

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: C. Stop the transfusion, assess the patient and initiate the transfusion reaction protocol**

<!-- job5-rationale -->
Chills, dyspnea aur back pain serious transfusion reaction suggest karte hain. Transfusion immediately stop karo, ABC/vitals assess karo, urgent clinical help aur blood bank inform karo. IV access approved separate/new setup se maintain karo if required, implicated blood tubing flush mat karo. Bag/tubing investigation ke liye preserve karo as directed. Serious reaction mein restart mat karo.

**Option-by-option reasoning**

- **A:** Next unit without review further harm cause kar sakti hai.
- **B:** Discard karne se investigation evidence lose ho sakta hai.
- **C:** Stop, assess and activate reaction protocol correct priority hai.
- **D:** Faster transfusion suspected trigger exposure badhati hai.

**Exam clue:** Stop first, assess, notify, preserve evidence.
