### Q4. A patient has a deeply embedded object in a wound. Which first aid approach is safest?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Priority:** High

- **A.** Push it deeper to stop movement
- **B.** Apply direct pressure on its protruding end
- **C.** Pull the object out immediately
- **D.** Stabilize the object, control bleeding around it and obtain urgent help

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: D. Stabilize the object, control bleeding around it and obtain urgent help**

Deeply embedded object wound ke andar injured tissue ya blood vessel ke against pressure bana raha ho sakta hai. Field mein object nikalne se bleeding ya injury suddenly worsen ho sakti hai. Object ko movement se protect karo, bleeding ke liye suitable pressure uske aas-paas do, protruding object par nahi. Urgent clinical care arrange karo.

**Option-wise explanation:**
- **A (Galat):** Object deeper push karne se blood vessels, nerves aur tissues ko further injury ho sakti hai.
- **B (Galat):** Protruding end par pressure object ko andar move kar sakta hai aur injury worsen kar sakta hai.
- **C (Galat):** Object pull karne se clot ya injured vessel disturb ho sakti hai.
- **D (Sahi):** Object stabilize karke around-wound bleeding control aur urgent help safest listed action hai.

**Exam clue:** Embedded object: do not remove or press on it. Stabilize and escalate.

### Q18. A patient has BP 128/78 mmHg supine and 100/62 mmHg within three minutes of standing, with dizziness. Which finding is supported?

<!-- job4-metadata -->
**Difficulty:** 🔴 Difficult · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD
<!-- /job4-metadata -->

- **A.** Orthostatic hypotension
- **B.** Hypertension due to increased standing pressure
- **C.** Normal rise in blood pressure after standing
- **D.** Isolated tachypnea proven by BP readings

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: A. Orthostatic hypotension**

<!-- job5-rationale -->
Standing par gravity se blood lower body mein pool hota hai. Normally autonomic compensation vascular tone aur heart response adjust karti hai. Orthostatic hypotension mein supine se standing ke first 3 minutes mein systolic BP kam se kam 20 mmHg ya diastolic kam se kam 10 mmHg girta hai. Yahan systolic fall = 128 − 100 = 28 mmHg, diastolic fall = 78 − 62 = 16 mmHg. Dono thresholds cross hain aur dizziness present hai. Patient ko safely sit ya lie karvao, fall prevention aur clinical assessment karo. Volume depletion ya medicine effects possible hain, but cause further assessment se establish hoga.

**Option-by-option reasoning**

- **A:** calculated fall aur symptoms orthostatic hypotension support karte hain.
- **B:** standing pressure increase nahi hua.
- **C:** pressure rise nahi hua, dono values fall hui hain.
- **D:** BP readings respiration rate establish nahi karti.

**Exam clue:** 20 systolic OR 10 diastolic within 3 minutes. Dizziness = fall risk.

### Q120. Which crutch instruction helps avoid pressure-related nerve injury?

**Difficulty:** 🟡 Medium · **Priority:** 🟡 IMPORTANT · **🟣 NORCET 20 November 2021 Shift II — related reported concept** · Legacy topic-bank reuse

- **C (Galat):** Entire body weight axillary pad par dene se nerve compression aur pain ya weakness ho sakti hai.
