### Q2. Which mechanism contributes to increased venous thromboembolism risk during prolonged immobility?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Priority:** High

- **C.** Complete elimination of coagulation factors
- **D.** Increased venous return from constant walking

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>
