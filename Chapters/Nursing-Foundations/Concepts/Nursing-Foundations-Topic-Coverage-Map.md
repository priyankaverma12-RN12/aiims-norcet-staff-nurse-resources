## Initial study explanations

### Nursing process

Sabse pehle nurse patient se pain ki jagah aur intensity poochti hai. Saath mein vital signs aur doosre symptoms dekhti hai. Is information ke baad hi diagnosis aur care plan banta hai.

### Medication safety

Jab identity match nahi karti, toh galat patient ko dawai mil sakti hai. Nurse administration rok kar do approved identifiers aur order ko verify karti hai.

### Pressure injury

Pressure se skin ko blood kam milta hai. Jab skin abhi phati nahi hai lekin redness pressure hatane par bhi gayab nahi hoti, toh stage 1 pressure injury suspect hoti hai. Pressure hatana aur skin assess karna zaroori hai.

### Urinary catheter care

Urine gravity se bag mein jaata hai. Bag bladder ke upar hoga toh urine wapas ja sakta hai. Isliye bag neeche rakho aur closed system ko intact rakho.
