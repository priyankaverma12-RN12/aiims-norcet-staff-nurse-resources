### Q1. A catheter package is marked 15 Fr. What is its approximate external diameter?

**Difficulty:** 🟡 Medium · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD · **Exam focus:** NORCET / all-India Staff Nurse · **Topic:** Size units

- **A.** 15 mm
- **B.** 5 mm
- **C.** 45 mm
- **D.** 0.5 mm

<details><summary>✅ Correct answer and detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: B. 5 mm**

**A. Kyun galat hai:** 15 Fr ka number millimetres ke barabar nahi hota. Ye circumference-based size scale hai.

**B. Kyun sahi hai:** French scale mein diameter Fr ko 3 se divide karke millimetres mein nikalte hain. Isliye 15/3 = 5 mm hota hai.

**C. Kyun galat hai:** 45 mm nikalne ke liye galti se 3 se multiply kiya gaya hai. External diameter ke liye divide karna hota hai.

**D. Kyun galat hai:** 0.5 mm correct conversion se das guna chhota hai. Fr aur mm ki units alag hain.

### Q3. A short female-length catheter is accidentally included in the equipment for an adult male. What is the safest preparation?

**Difficulty:** 🔴 Difficult · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD · **Exam focus:** NORCET / all-India Staff Nurse · **Topic:** Length selection

- **A.** Replace it with an appropriate male-length or standard-length catheter
- **B.** Use the short catheter and inflate as soon as the tip enters the urethra
- **C.** Increase balloon volume to compensate for length
- **D.** Cut the drainage end to extend insertion depth

<details><summary>✅ Correct answer and detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: A. Replace it with an appropriate male-length or standard-length catheter**

**A. Kyun sahi hai:** Male urethra comparatively lambi hoti hai. Suitable catheter length choose karne se balloon urethra ke andar inflate hone ka risk kam hota hai.

**B. Kyun galat hai:** Short catheter bladder tak adequate depth se nahi pahunch sakta. Urethra mein balloon inflation serious trauma cause kar sakti hai.

**C. Kyun galat hai:** Balloon ka volume catheter ki physical length ko change nahi karta. Extra inflation se injury ho sakti hai.

**D. Kyun galat hai:** Catheter ko cut karne se manufacturer-designed structure aur sterility compromise hoti hai. Correct product lena chahiye.

### Q17. Before an authorized routine catheter removal, why should the nurse review the documented balloon volume?

<details><summary>✅ Correct answer and detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: B. It helps assess whether the expected balloon fluid has returned during deflation**

**A. Kyun galat hai:** Balloon volume glomerular filtration ka measure nahi hai. Kidney function ke liye separate clinical data chahiye.
