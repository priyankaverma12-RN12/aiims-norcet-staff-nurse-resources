### Q1. Which insertion angle is conventional for a correctly selected intramuscular injection?

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Priority:** Medium

- **A.** 15 degrees
- **B.** 25 degrees
- **C.** 5 degrees
- **D.** 90 degrees

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: D. 90 degrees**

IM injection ko muscle tak pahunchna hai. Perpendicular angle correct needle aur site ke saath ismein help karta hai.

**Baaki options kyun sahi nahi hain?** 15, 25 aur 5 degrees shallow approaches hain jo routine IM technique nahi hain.

</details>

### Q2. Which angle is commonly used for an intradermal injection with the bevel facing upward?

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Priority:** Medium

- **A.** 90 degrees
- **B.** 70–80 degrees
- **C.** 5–15 degrees
- **D.** 45–60 degrees

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: C. 5–15 degrees**

ID injection dermis ki shallow layer mein di jaati hai. Low angle se needle superficial plane mein rehti hai.

**Baaki options kyun sahi nahi hain?** Higher angles deeper tissue tak ja sakte hain, jo ID route ka target nahi hai.

</details>

### Q3. What determines whether a subcutaneous injection is given at 45 or 90 degrees?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Priority:** High

- **A.** A universal 45-degree rule
- **B.** Needle length, tissue thickness and product-specific technique
- **C.** The dose in milligrams alone
- **D.** Patient age alone

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: B. Needle length, tissue thickness and product-specific technique**

SC injection ka target fat layer hai. Needle length aur tissue thickness ke hisaab se angle choose karna muscle penetration avoid karta hai.

**Baaki options kyun sahi nahi hain?** Dose aur age alone depth nahi batate. Universal angle har device ke liye suitable nahi hai.

</details>

### Q5. Why is ventrogluteal placement often preferred to dorsogluteal placement for a suitable non-vaccine IM drug?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Priority:** High

- **A.** It is relatively distant from the sciatic nerve and major vessels
- **B.** It absorbs all drugs faster than IV delivery
- **C.** It contains no muscle
- **D.** It eliminates the need to assess anatomy

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: A. It is relatively distant from the sciatic nerve and major vessels**

Ventrogluteal region mein sciatic nerve injury ka risk relatively lower ho sakta hai. Nurse drug, volume aur patient anatomy assess karti hai.

**Baaki options kyun sahi nahi hain?** IV direct circulation mein jaata hai. Ventrogluteal region muscle contain karta hai aur landmarking required hai.

</details>

### Q6. Which statement about aspiration before routine IM vaccination reflects current guidance?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Priority:** High

- **A.** It is mandatory for every vaccine
- **B.** It is performed only after vaccine delivery
- **C.** Blood return is required before injection
- **D.** It is not recommended at correctly selected vaccine sites

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: D. It is not recommended at correctly selected vaccine sites**

Recommended vaccination sites par large vessels generally nahi hoti. Routine aspiration recommended nahi hai. Non-vaccine drugs ke liye product aur policy check karo.

**Baaki options kyun sahi nahi hain?** Mandatory aspiration, after-injection aspiration aur deliberate blood return vaccination guidance se match nahi karte.

</details>

### Q7. What is the purpose of the Z-track technique for selected IM medications?

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Priority:** Medium

- **A.** Increase venous access
- **B.** Prevent every drug allergy
- **C.** Reduce leakage back into subcutaneous tissue
- **D.** Create a dermal wheal

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: C. Reduce leakage back into subcutaneous tissue**

Skin aur subcutaneous tissue displace karke needle track offset kiya jaata hai. Isse selected irritating IM drugs ka leakage kam ho sakta hai.

**Baaki options kyun sahi nahi hain?** Wheal ID injection mein banti hai. Venous access IV route hai. Allergy Z-track se prevent nahi hoti.

</details>

### Q8. A patient injecting insulin repeatedly into one spot develops firm fatty lumps. Which teaching is appropriate?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Priority:** High

- **A.** Use the identical puncture point daily
- **B.** Rotate sites systematically and avoid abnormal tissue according to guidance
- **C.** Continue injecting into the lumps
- **D.** Massage the lumps vigorously after every dose

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: B. Rotate sites systematically and avoid abnormal tissue according to guidance**

Repeated injections lipohypertrophy cause kar sakti hain aur insulin absorption unpredictable ho sakti hai. Rotation aur site assessment important hain.

**Baaki options kyun sahi nahi hain?** Lump mein repeated dosing, forceful massage aur same puncture point tissue problems worsen kar sakte hain.

</details>

### Q9. Which practice is required for an insulin pen used in a hospital?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Priority:** High

- **A.** Use another patient's pen if the insulin type matches
- **B.** Dedicate it to one patient even when the needle is changed
- **C.** Share it after each needle change
- **D.** Use a pen with no patient label

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: B. Dedicate it to one patient even when the needle is changed**

Pen cartridge mein blood contamination ho sakti hai. Needle change ke baad bhi pen patient-specific rehni chahiye.

**Baaki options kyun sahi nahi hain?** Sharing aur missing identification bloodborne transmission aur medication error risk badhate hain.

</details>

### Q10. Which injection equipment practice is safe?

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Priority:** Medium

- **A.** Use a new sterile syringe and needle for each injection as indicated
- **B.** Reuse the syringe between patients after changing needles
- **C.** Reuse a needle wiped with alcohol
- **D.** Keep opened needles for later reuse

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: A. Use a new sterile syringe and needle for each injection as indicated**

Single-use sterile equipment microorganisms aur blood transfer ka risk kam karti hai.

**Baaki options kyun sahi nahi hain?** Needle change syringe ko sterile nahi banata. Alcohol wiping aur opened storage sterility guarantee nahi karte.

</details>

### Q11. Which practice prevents contamination during multidose vial access?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Priority:** High

- **A.** Reenter with a used patient syringe
- **B.** Leave a needle in the stopper permanently
- **C.** Use beyond the applicable discard date
- **D.** Use a new sterile needle and syringe each time and follow dating and storage policy

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: D. Use a new sterile needle and syringe each time and follow dating and storage policy**

New sterile equipment vial mein germs introduce hone ka risk reduce karti hai. Storage aur beyond-use date product guidance se verify hoti hai.

**Baaki options kyun sahi nahi hain?** Used syringe, exposed needle aur expired vial contamination risk badhate hain.

</details>

### Q14. An eye drop and ointment are prescribed together for the same eye. Which sequence is generally appropriate?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Priority:** High

- **A.** Apply both to the closed eyelid
- **B.** Drops before ointment with the recommended interval
- **C.** Ointment before drops
- **D.** Mix them in the same dropper

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

Ointment surface par coating bana sakti hai jo drops ki delivery affect kare. Drops pehle dena aur recommended interval maintain karna useful hai.

**Baaki options kyun sahi nahi hain?** Ointment-first, mixing aur eyelid-only administration intended drug delivery interfere kar sakte hain.

</details>

### Q16. A patient reports dizziness when cold ear drops are administered. What can help prevent this?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Priority:** High

- **A.** Boil the bottle
- **B.** Insert the dropper against the eardrum
- **C.** Instill the drops as cold as possible
- **D.** Allow drops to reach an appropriate temperature according to instructions

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: D. Allow drops to reach an appropriate temperature according to instructions**

Cold liquid vestibular stimulation se dizziness cause kar sakti hai. Product ki safe temperature guidance follow karni chahiye.

**Baaki options kyun sahi nahi hain?** Boiling burns aur product damage cause kar sakti hai. Deep contact injury risk hai. Cold instillation symptoms repeat kar sakti hai.

</details>

### Q19. A patient taking anticoagulants develops an enlarging hematoma after an injection. What is appropriate?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Priority:** High

- **C.** Massage the swelling vigorously
- **D.** Inject the next dose into the swelling

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Baaki options kyun sahi nahi hain?** Massage aur repeat injection tissue injury badha sakte hain. Enlarging swelling ignore nahi karni chahiye.

</details>
