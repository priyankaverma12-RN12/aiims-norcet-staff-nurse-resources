### Q1. Which insertion angle is conventional for a correctly selected intramuscular injection?

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Priority:** Medium

- **A.** 15 degrees
- **B.** 25 degrees
- **C.** 5 degrees
- **D.** 90 degrees

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: D. 90 degrees**

Intramuscular ya IM injection ka target skeletal muscle hota hai, jo skin aur subcutaneous fat ke neeche hoti hai. Conventional perpendicular approach mein needle skin ke plane se 90 degrees par enter karti hai. Sirf angle correct hona enough nahi hai. Nurse prescribed route, medication, patient anatomy, suitable site aur needle length verify karti hai taaki medicine intended tissue mein deliver ho.

**Option-wise explanation:**

- **A (Galat):** 15 degrees shallow approach hai aur intradermal route ke liye commonly relevant hai, routine IM ke liye nahi.
- **B (Galat):** 25 degrees ka angle muscle tak reliable delivery establish nahi karta. IV access ke angles situation ke according different ho sakte hain.
- **C (Galat):** 5 degrees almost parallel approach hai, jo dermis jaise superficial tissue ke liye suitable ho sakti hai.
- **D (Sahi):** 90 degrees conventional IM insertion angle hai. Appropriate landmark aur needle length bhi essential hain.

**Exam clue:** IM = 90 degrees, but site and needle selection matter.

### Q10. Which injection equipment practice is safe?

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Priority:** Medium

Needle aur syringe skin barrier cross karte hain, isliye asepsis important hai. Used needle aur syringe blood ya microorganisms se contaminated ho sakte hain. - **C (Galat):** Alcohol wiping used needle ko validated sterile equipment mein convert nahi karti.
- **D (Galat):** Opened needles ko later reuse ke liye store karna sterility aur safety assure nahi karta.

**Exam clue:** Never reuse a used syringe between patients, even if the needle changes.

- **A (Galat):** Actuation ke waqt spacer mein exhale karne se intended aerosol inhalation disrupt ho sakti hai.
- **B (Galat):** Technique errors time ke saath develop ho sakte hain. Reassessment aur demonstration useful hain.
- **C (Sahi):** Single actuation aur taught compatible-device technique lung delivery support karte hain.
- **D (Galat):** Multiple puffs chamber mein ek saath spray karne se aerosol deposition aur delivered dose variability badh sakti hai.

**Exam clue:** Compatible MDI spacer: one puff at a time, assess technique.

### Q19. A patient taking anticoagulants develops an enlarging hematoma after an injection. What is appropriate?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Priority:** High

- **A.** Ignore it until the next routine visit
- **B.** Assess bleeding and tissue status, apply suitable pressure and seek review
- **C.** Massage the swelling vigorously
- **D.** Inject the next dose into the swelling

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: B. Assess bleeding and tissue status, apply suitable pressure and seek review**

Hematoma ka matlab tissue mein blood collect hona hai. Anticoagulant therapy clotting ability affect kar sakti hai, isliye injection ke baad enlarging swelling ongoing bleeding ka clue ho sakti hai. Nurse size, pain, skin, distal sensation aur circulation assess karti hai. Suitable local pressure aur prompt clinical review arrange karo. Rapid expansion, severe pain, numbness, perfusion change ya systemic instability urgent escalation maangte hain. Anticoagulant medicine khud se stop ya change mat karo.

**Option-wise explanation:**

- **A (Galat):** Enlarging hematoma ko ignore karne se ongoing bleeding ya tissue compression miss ho sakti hai.
- **B (Sahi):** Assessment, appropriate pressure aur prompt review possible bleeding complication address karte hain.
- **C (Galat):** Vigorous massage local tissue trauma aur bleeding worsen kar sakti hai.
- **D (Galat):** Swollen abnormal site mein repeat injection further injury aur medication delivery problems cause kar sakti hai.

**Exam clue:** Expanding hematoma: assess perfusion, control bleeding, escalate.

### Q20. What should an injection administration record include?

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Priority:** Medium

- **A.** Drug, dose, route, site, actual time and relevant response
- **B.** Only the prescribed time
- **C.** Only the syringe size
- **D.** Only the name of the ward

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: A. Drug, dose, route, site, actual time and relevant response**

Injection documentation se team ko actual administered treatment aur patient response ka reliable record milta hai. Drug name, dose, route, site, actual administration time aur relevant response record karo. Vaccine ya special products mein required batch/lot information bhi policy ke hisaab se record ho sakti hai. Omitted, refused ya adverse reaction events accurately document aur communicate karo. Pre-charting actual administration ka substitute nahi hai.

**Option-wise explanation:**

- **A (Sahi):** Drug, dose, route, site, time aur response traceability, site rotation aur follow-up support karte hain.
- **B (Galat):** Prescribed time actual administration ka time prove nahi karta.
- **C (Galat):** Syringe size administered medicine, dose aur route establish nahi karti.
- **D (Galat):** Ward name location batata hai, patient-specific treatment details nahi.

**Exam clue:** Record actual administration and response, not only planned details.
