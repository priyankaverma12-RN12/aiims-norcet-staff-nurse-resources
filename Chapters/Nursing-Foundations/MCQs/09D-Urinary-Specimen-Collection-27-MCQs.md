### Q1. A routine urine microscopy order specifies an early-morning specimen. Why may this timing improve detection of urinary particles?

**Difficulty:** 🟡 Medium · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD · **Exam focus:** NORCET / all-India Staff Nurse · **Topic:** Specimen timing

- **A.** The overnight specimen is automatically sterile
- **B.** It excludes all renal disease if the appearance is clear
- **C.** The longer overnight concentration period may increase the concentration of detectable constituents
- **D.** It removes the need for prompt laboratory handling

<details><summary>✅ Correct answer and detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: C. The longer overnight concentration period may increase the concentration of detectable constituents**

**A. Kyun galat hai:** Urine morning mein collect karne se bacteria absent hona guarantee nahi hota. Collection contamination aur actual infection dono possible hain.

**B. Kyun galat hai:** Clear appearance se microscopic abnormalities exclude nahi hoti. Appropriate urinalysis aur clinical assessment still important hain.

**C. Kyun sahi hai:** Overnight bladder storage aur relatively concentrated urine se kuch cells aur dissolved constituents detect karna easier ho sakta hai. Lekin test-specific instructions follow karni chahiye.

**D. Kyun galat hai:** Morning specimen bhi delay se change ho sakta hai. Isliye proper transport aur handling zaroori hain.
