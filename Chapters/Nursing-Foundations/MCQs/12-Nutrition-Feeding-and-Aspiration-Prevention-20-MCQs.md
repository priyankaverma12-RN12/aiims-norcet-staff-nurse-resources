# Nutrition, feeding and aspiration prevention — 20 original MCQs

**NORCET-style and Indian staff nurse practice. Unattempted.** Difficulty and priority are editorial estimates. These are not official PYQs. [Chapter](../README.md)

### Q1. A patient with impaired swallowing is about to receive a meal. Which position usually reduces aspiration risk when tolerated?

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Priority:** Medium

- **A.** Upright sitting with individualized swallowing recommendations
- **B.** Flat supine
- **C.** Head-down tilt
- **D.** Prone without assessment

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: A. Upright sitting with individualized swallowing recommendations**

Upright position mein food ko safely swallow karne mein help mil sakti hai. Dysphagia specialist ki recommended posture aur texture follow karna important hai.

**Baaki options kyun sahi nahi hain?** Flat aur head-down positions aspiration risk badha sakti hain. Unassessed prone feeding unsafe hai.

</details>

### Q2. A patient coughs repeatedly and develops a wet voice while drinking water after stroke. What should the nurse do?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Priority:** High

- **A.** Encourage larger sips to improve swallowing
- **B.** Continue the meal because coughing is normal in all patients
- **C.** Give tablets with water before assessment
- **D.** Stop oral intake temporarily, assess airway and arrange swallowing evaluation

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: D. Stop oral intake temporarily, assess airway and arrange swallowing evaluation**

Cough aur wet voice aspiration risk ke clues hain. Nurse feeding stop karke breathing assess karti hai aur safe swallowing plan confirm karti hai.

**Baaki options kyun sahi nahi hain?** Larger sips aur continued oral intake aspiration worsen kar sakte hain. Tablets bhi unsafe swallow ho sakti hain.

</details>

### Q3. What is the purpose of a prescribed texture-modified diet in dysphagia?

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Priority:** Medium

- **A.** Replace swallowing assessment permanently
- **B.** Increase choking risk deliberately
- **C.** Provide a consistency suited to the patient's assessed swallowing ability
- **D.** Ensure all patients receive exactly the same food

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: C. Provide a consistency suited to the patient's assessed swallowing ability**

Dysphagia mein different textures swallow karne ki difficulty vary karti hai. Assessment-based consistency safer intake support kar sakti hai.

**Baaki options kyun sahi nahi hain?** Universal diet individualized need ignore karti hai. Texture plan assessment replace nahi karta. Aim choking risk reduce karna hai.

</details>

### Q4. A patient who is drowsy after sedation is scheduled for oral feeding. Which action is appropriate?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Priority:** High

- **A.** Use a straw regardless of swallowing plan
- **B.** Reassess alertness and swallowing safety before offering food
- **C.** Feed rapidly before the patient falls asleep
- **D.** Place food in the mouth while the patient is unresponsive

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: B. Reassess alertness and swallowing safety before offering food**

Safe swallow ke liye alertness aur coordination important hain. Sedated patient mein aspiration risk increase ho sakta hai. Nurse feeding safety reassess karti hai.

**Baaki options kyun sahi nahi hain?** Rapid feeding, unresponsive feeding aur unapproved straw use unsafe ho sakte hain.

</details>

### Q5. Which method best supports independence in a patient with limited hand strength during feeding?

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Priority:** Medium

- **A.** Require use of standard utensils despite difficulty
- **B.** Provide suitable adaptive utensils and assistance only as needed
- **C.** Feed the patient completely without checking ability
- **D.** Remove the meal if the patient eats slowly

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: B. Provide suitable adaptive utensils and assistance only as needed**

Adaptive utensils grip aur control improve kar sakte hain. Nurse patient ko jitna safely possible ho khud eat karne deti hai.

**Baaki options kyun sahi nahi hain?** Unnecessary full assistance independence reduce kar sakti hai. Meal remove karna intake reduce karta hai. Standard utensils barrier ho sakte hain.

</details>

### Q6. A newly inserted nasogastric tube is intended for feeding. Which action is essential before first use?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Priority:** High

- **A.** Confirm placement using the approved evidence-based method and document authorization for use
- **B.** Inject air and rely on an abdominal whoosh alone
- **C.** Assume correct placement because the patient can speak
- **D.** Use the tube immediately if its external length seems correct

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: A. Confirm placement using the approved evidence-based method and document authorization for use**

NG tube accidentally respiratory tract mein ja sakti hai. Initial placement approved method, such as radiography when indicated, se confirm karna essential hai. Local protocol follow karna chahiye.

**Baaki options kyun sahi nahi hain?** Air auscultation reliable confirmation nahi hai. Speech aur external length alone correct location prove nahi karte.

</details>

### Q7. During enteral feeding, the tube's external marking changes substantially. What should the nurse do?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Priority:** High

- **A.** Advance the tube blindly to the old marking and resume
- **B.** Increase feeding rate to compensate for interruption
- **C.** Ignore the change if the patient does not cough
- **D.** Stop feeding and re-establish placement according to protocol before further use

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: D. Stop feeding and re-establish placement according to protocol before further use**

External length change tube migration indicate kar sakti hai. Nurse feed stop karke position verify karwati hai.

**Baaki options kyun sahi nahi hain?** Blind advancement airway injury ya malposition cause kar sakti hai. Faster rate aur ignoring change unsafe hain.

</details>

### Q8. What is the usual purpose of flushing an enteral feeding tube with prescribed water?

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Priority:** Medium

- **A.** Sterilize the stomach
- **B.** Replace all nutritional requirements
- **C.** Maintain tube patency and provide ordered water when appropriate
- **D.** Confirm gastric placement without other checks

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: C. Maintain tube patency and provide ordered water when appropriate**

Water flush formula aur medicines ke residues clear karke blockage risk reduce kar sakti hai. Amount fluid restriction aur protocol ke hisaab se hoti hai.

**Baaki options kyun sahi nahi hain?** Flushing position proof nahi hai. Stomach sterilize nahi hota. Water calories aur nutrients replace nahi karta.

</details>

### Q9. Which practice is appropriate when administering several compatible prescribed medicines through an enteral tube?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Priority:** High

- **A.** Use an IV syringe connected to an enteral line
- **B.** Skip all flushes to save time
- **C.** Verify each formulation and give separately with appropriate flushes according to policy
- **D.** Crush every medication together into the feeding formula

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: C. Verify each formulation and give separately with appropriate flushes according to policy**

Separate administration interactions aur tube blockage risk reduce kar sakti hai. Pharmacy formulation compatibility verify karti hai aur enteral-specific connectors wrong-route error prevent karte hain.

**Baaki options kyun sahi nahi hain?** Crushing all tablets unsafe ho sakti hai. IV connection wrong-route risk hai. Flushes patency support karti hain.

</details>

### Q10. An enterally fed patient develops sudden coughing, breathlessness and oxygen desaturation. Which action is appropriate?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Priority:** High

- **A.** Wait until the current bag empties
- **B.** Stop feeding, assess and support breathing and seek urgent help
- **C.** Increase feeding rate
- **D.** Lay the patient flat and continue formula

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: B. Stop feeding, assess and support breathing and seek urgent help**

Sudden respiratory findings aspiration ya other acute complication indicate kar sakte hain. Nurse feed stop karke airway-breathing support arrange karti hai.

**Baaki options kyun sahi nahi hain?** Faster feeding aur flat position risk worsen kar sakte hain. Delay unsafe hai.

</details>

### Q11. Which information should be included when monitoring nutritional intake?

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Priority:** Medium

- **A.** Actual amount consumed and relevant tolerance findings
- **B.** Only the amount delivered on the tray
- **C.** The food preferences of the neighboring patient
- **D.** Assume the full meal was eaten if the tray is removed

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: A. Actual amount consumed and relevant tolerance findings**

Delivered food aur consumed food different ho sakte hain. Actual intake aur nausea, swallowing difficulty jaise findings nutritional plan evaluate karne mein help karti hain.

**Baaki options kyun sahi nahi hain?** Tray amount consumption prove nahi karta. Neighbor preference irrelevant hai. Assumed intake inaccurate hai.

</details>

### Q12. A patient has severe malnutrition and is about to restart nutrition. Which electrolyte disturbance is particularly associated with refeeding syndrome?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Priority:** High

- **A.** Hyperphosphatemia as the typical defining early shift
- **B.** Isolated high sodium in every patient
- **C.** Guaranteed normal electrolyte levels
- **D.** Hypophosphatemia

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: D. Hypophosphatemia**

Nutrition restart hone par insulin rise se phosphate, potassium aur magnesium cells ke andar shift ho sakte hain. Severe phosphate fall dangerous ho sakti hai. Risk-based slow advancement aur monitoring needed hai.

**Baaki options kyun sahi nahi hain?** High phosphate typical early shift nahi hai. Sodium alone defining feature nahi hai. Electrolytes normal guaranteed nahi hain.

</details>

### Q14. A patient on continuous enteral nutrition has an interruption because of a procedure and receives glucose-lowering medication. Which risk should the nurse consider?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Priority:** High

- **A.** Immediate protein overload
- **B.** No need for monitoring because feeding has stopped
- **C.** Hypoglycemia if medication effect continues without the expected carbohydrate delivery
- **D.** Guaranteed hyperglycemia in every case

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: C. Hypoglycemia if medication effect continues without the expected carbohydrate delivery**

Feeding stop hone par carbohydrate supply kam ho sakti hai lekin insulin effect continue reh sakta hai. Nurse glucose monitor karti hai aur interruption protocol follow karti hai.

**Baaki options kyun sahi nahi hain?** Glucose response variable hai. Protein overload defining concern nahi hai. Monitoring important hai.

</details>

### Q15. Which is a key advantage of enteral nutrition when the gastrointestinal tract is functional and accessible?

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Priority:** Medium

- **B.** It uses the gastrointestinal tract and supports gut function
- **C.** It always eliminates aspiration risk
- **D.** It never requires placement verification

</details>

### Q16. A patient receiving parenteral nutrition through a central line develops fever and rigors. Which response is appropriate?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Priority:** High

- **A.** Assess urgently for possible bloodstream infection and follow line and sepsis protocols
- **B.** Assume fever is a normal nutrient response
- **C.** Mix an unprescribed antibiotic into the nutrition bag
- **D.** Ignore the findings until the infusion finishes

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

Central access infection ka risk rakhti hai. Fever-rigors serious infection indicate kar sakte hain. Nurse vitals assess karke urgent review aur prescribed investigations arrange karti hai.

**Baaki options kyun sahi nahi hain?** Fever normal assume nahi karna chahiye. Unapproved mixing unsafe hai. Delay infection deterioration kar sakti hai.

</details>

### Q17. Which intervention supports oral health in a patient who is not eating by mouth?

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Priority:** Medium

- **A.** Provide regular individualized oral care despite the NPO order
- **B.** Avoid oral hygiene until oral meals resume
- **C.** Give unapproved water to drink despite aspiration restrictions
- **D.** Use oral care as a replacement for prescribed nutrition

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: A. Provide regular individualized oral care despite the NPO order**

NPO ka matlab oral intake restriction hai, oral hygiene restriction nahi. Mouth care comfort aur bacterial burden reduce karne mein help karti hai, with aspiration precautions.

**Baaki options kyun sahi nahi hain?** Hygiene avoid karna dryness aur plaque badha sakta hai. Unapproved drinking unsafe ho sakti hai. Oral care nutrition nahi deti.

</details>

### Q18. Which action is appropriate when a patient has a prescribed fluid restriction and receives enteral tube flushes?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Priority:** High

- **A.** Exclude all tube flushes from intake
- **B.** Increase flush volume without reviewing the order
- **C.** Stop essential flushes without obtaining a plan
- **D.** Count flush water and other measurable fluids toward the ordered allowance

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

Enteral flush water body mein absorb ho sakti hai aur fluid intake ka part hai. Nurse total allowance track karke necessary patency aur hydration plan coordinate karti hai.

### Q20. A patient has a new prescription for nutritional supplementation but reports a severe allergy to an ingredient. What should the nurse do?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Priority:** High

- **A.** Dilute the supplement and assume the allergen is harmless
- **B.** Withhold the product until the ingredient and safe alternatives are verified
- **C.** Give a small trial dose without authorization
- **D.** Remove the allergy alert because it is food-related

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: B. Withhold the product until the ingredient and safe alternatives are verified**

Food allergy severe reaction cause kar sakti hai. Nurse label aur allergy history verify karke dietitian-prescriber se suitable alternative arrange karti hai.

**Baaki options kyun sahi nahi hain?** Small trial aur dilution risk eliminate nahi karte. Allergy alert remove karna unsafe hai.

</details>
