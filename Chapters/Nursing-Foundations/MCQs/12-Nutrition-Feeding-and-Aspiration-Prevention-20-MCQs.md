### Q1. A patient with impaired swallowing is about to receive a meal. Which position usually reduces aspiration risk when tolerated?

**Difficulty:** 🟡 Medium · **Priority:** 🟡 IMPORTANT · **[🟣 NORCET 9 Mains 27 September 2025 — related aspiration-prevention concept](https://testbook.com/question-answer/a-68-year-old-patient-has-just-received-enteral-nu--692eebf93e5062de751da5a1)**

- **A.** Upright sitting with individualized swallowing recommendations
- **B.** Flat supine
- **C.** Head-down tilt
- **D.** Prone without assessment

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: A. Upright sitting with individualized swallowing recommendations**

Dysphagia ka matlab nigalne mein dikkat hai. Aspiration tab hoti hai jab food, saliva ya liquid airway mein chala jaata hai. Agar patient oral intake ke liye assessed safe hai, toh upright position aur prescribed head posture swallowing control mein help karte hain. Patient ko small manageable bites aur individualized feeding assistance do. Sirf upright bithane se unsafe swallow safe nahi ho jaata.

**Option-wise explanation:**
- **A (Sahi):** Upright sitting aur assessed swallow plan food ka control aur airway protection support karte hain. Yeh best listed choice hai.
- **B (Galat):** Flat supine mein food aur liquid ko safely control karna mushkil ho sakta hai.
- **C (Galat):** Head-down tilt routine oral feeding posture nahi hai aur swallowing safety compromise kar sakta hai.
- **D (Galat):** Bina assessment prone feeding mein airway aur swallowing monitor karna difficult ho sakta hai.

**Exam clue:** Position plus individualized swallow plan, not position alone.

### Q2. A patient coughs repeatedly and develops a wet voice while drinking water after stroke. What should the nurse do?

**Difficulty:** 🔴 Difficult · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD

- **C.** Give tablets with water before assessment
- **D.** Stop oral intake temporarily, assess airway and arrange swallowing evaluation

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: D. Stop oral intake temporarily, assess airway and arrange swallowing evaluation**

Stroke se swallowing muscles ka control affect ho sakta hai. Repeated cough aur wet ya gurgly voice liquid ke airway ke paas pahunchne ka warning sign ho sakte hain. Oral intake rok kar airway aur breathing assess karo. Agar severe distress hai toh emergency help activate karo. Trained swallowing assessment aur safe alternative medication/nutrition plan arrange karo. Cough absent hona bhi aspiration ko rule out nahi karta.

**Option-wise explanation:**
- **A (Galat):** Large sips patient ki swallowing capacity exceed kar sakte hain.
- **B (Galat):** Repeated coughing ko routine maan kar feeding continue karna unsafe hai.
- **C (Galat):** Tablets aur water bhi aspirate ho sakte hain. Oral medication route reassess karna chahiye.
- **D (Sahi):** Feeding stop karna, airway assess karna aur swallowing evaluation arrange karna patient safety prioritize karta hai.

**Exam clue:** Cough or wet voice during intake = stop, assess, refer.

- **C.** Provide a consistency suited to the patient's assessed swallowing ability

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

- **C (Sahi):** Assessed ability ke hisaab se consistency select karna individualized dysphagia care ka part hai.
- **D (Galat):** Har patient ka swallowing impairment same nahi hota. Universal diet unsuitable ho sakti hai.

**Exam clue:** Texture is prescribed after assessment, not universally thickened.

- **C (Galat):** Patient speak kar paaye toh bhi tube respiratory tract mein ho sakti hai.
- **D (Galat):** External length insertion estimate deta hai, tip ki actual location prove nahi karta.

**Exam clue:** No first use before approved placement confirmation. Never rely on whoosh.

### Q16. A patient receiving parenteral nutrition through a central line develops fever and rigors. Which response is appropriate?

- **C.** Mix an unprescribed antibiotic into the nutrition bag
- **D.** Ignore the findings until the infusion finishes

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: A. Assess urgently for possible bloodstream infection and follow line and sepsis protocols**

Parenteral nutrition nutrients ko venous circulation ke through deliver karti hai. Central line ke through germs bloodstream tak pahunch sakte hain. Rigors yani intense shaking chills aur fever possible bloodstream infection ke signs hain. Vitals aur perfusion urgently assess karo, sepsis pathway activate karo if indicated aur line management/cultures prescribed protocol se arrange karo. Antibiotic khud se nutrition bag mein mix mat karo.

**Option-wise explanation:**
- **A (Sahi):** Urgent assessment aur line/sepsis protocol infection aur deterioration ko timely address karte hain.
- **B (Galat):** Fever aur rigors ko routine nutrient response assume karna dangerous ho sakta hai.
- **C (Galat):** Unprescribed antibiotic mixing sterility, compatibility aur dose safety compromise karti hai.

### Q17. Which intervention supports oral health in a patient who is not eating by mouth?

### Q20. A patient has a new prescription for nutritional supplementation but reports a severe allergy to an ingredient. What should the nurse do?

- **C (Galat):** Unauthorized trial dose even small amount mein severe reaction trigger kar sakti hai.
- **D (Galat):** Food allergy bhi important safety alert hai. Alert remove karna future exposure ka risk badhata hai.

**Exam clue:** Known severe allergy = hold and verify before administration.
