### Q1. A stable adult needs a peripheral-compatible antibiotic. Two suitable veins are available, one over the wrist joint and one in the straight forearm. Both can accommodate the required catheter. Which choice best reduces mechanical disruption during daily activity?

**Difficulty:** 🟡 Medium · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD · Clinical application · Original practice

A. The largest catheter in either site regardless of vein size
B. The straight forearm vein
C. The wrist joint vein because movement keeps it patent
D. A vein in the foot despite suitable upper-limb veins

<details>
<summary>✅ Sahi answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct answer: B. The straight forearm vein**

- **A (Incorrect):** Gauge ko therapy aur vein ke size se match karna hota hai. Sirf bada catheter choose karna vessel trauma badha sakta hai.
- **B (Correct):** Forearm ka straight segment joint movement se kam disturb hota hai. Isse catheter bend hone aur traction ka chance kam ho sakta hai.
- **C (Incorrect):** Wrist movement catheter ko bend ya pull kar sakta hai. Movement patency ka reliable method nahi hai.
- **D (Incorrect):** Adult mein suitable upper limb available ho toh lower limb ko routine first choice nahi banate.

**Exam clue:** Vein ko future use aur patient comfort ke liye preserve karo.

### Q7. Two peripheral catheters have the same internal diameter and are tested under identical pressure and fluid conditions, but one catheter is substantially longer. Which general physical relationship is expected?

**Difficulty:** 🔴 Difficult · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD · Clinical application · Original practice

A. The longer catheter always offers lower flow resistance
B. Length has no effect on resistance
C. The longer catheter doubles flow regardless of other factors
D. The shorter catheter generally offers lower flow resistance

<details>
<summary>✅ Sahi answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct answer: D. The shorter catheter generally offers lower flow resistance**

- **A (Incorrect):** Longer path fluid ke flow ko zyada resist karta hai, kam nahi.
- **B (Incorrect):** Length flow resistance ka ek factor hai. Diameter, pressure aur fluid properties bhi important hain.
- **C (Incorrect):** Length badhane se flow automatically double nahi hota. Ye relationship ulta samajh liya gaya hai.
- **D (Correct):** Same radius aur fluid conditions mein tube lambi hogi toh resistance badhega. Shorter tube generally kam resistance deti hai. Actual manufacturer flow ratings bhi check karo.

**Exam clue:** Therapy, vein, catheter diameter aur length ko saath mein assess karo.
