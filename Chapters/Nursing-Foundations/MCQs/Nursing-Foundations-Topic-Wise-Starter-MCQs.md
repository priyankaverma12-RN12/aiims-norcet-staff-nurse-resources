## Nursing process

### Q1. A patient reports new severe pain after abdominal surgery. Which action represents the assessment phase of the nursing process?

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Topic:** Nursing process

- **A.** Determine the location, severity and associated signs of pain
- **B.** Document that the pain goal has been achieved
- **C.** Select a final nursing diagnosis without collecting data
- **D.** Administer an additional analgesic without checking the order

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: A. Determine the location, severity and associated signs of pain**

Sabse pehle nurse patient se pain ki jagah aur intensity poochti hai. Saath mein vital signs aur doosre symptoms dekhti hai. Is information ke baad hi diagnosis aur care plan banta hai.

**Baaki options kyun galat hain?**

- **B.** Goal achieve hua ya nahi dekhna evaluation hai.
- **C.** Bina information ke diagnosis sahi nahi banega.
- **D.** Dawai dena implementation hai aur order check karna zaroori hai.

</details>

## Medication safety

### Q2. Before administering a high-alert medication, the barcode system displays a different patient identifier from the wristband. What is the best response?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Topic:** Medication safety

- **A.** Override the warning if the patient's bed number matches
- **B.** Ask the neighboring patient to confirm the identity
- **C.** Give the medication and correct the record later
- **D.** Pause administration and resolve the identification mismatch

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: D. Pause administration and resolve the identification mismatch**

Jab identity match nahi karti, toh galat patient ko dawai mil sakti hai. Nurse administration rok kar do approved identifiers aur order ko verify karti hai.

**Baaki options kyun galat hain?**

- **A.** Bed number patient ka reliable identifier nahi hai.
- **B.** Doosre patient ki baat se identity verify nahi hoti.
- **C.** Dawai dene ke baad correction karna harm ko nahi rokta.

</details>

## Pressure injury

### Q3. An immobile patient has intact sacral skin with nonblanchable redness. Which finding best supports a stage 1 pressure injury?

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Topic:** Pressure injury

- **A.** Visible subcutaneous fat
- **B.** Exposed tendon or bone
- **C.** Persistent nonblanchable erythema of intact skin
- **D.** A shallow open ulcer with exposed dermis

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: C. Persistent nonblanchable erythema of intact skin**

Pressure se skin ko blood kam milta hai. Jab skin abhi phati nahi hai lekin redness pressure hatane par bhi gayab nahi hoti, toh stage 1 pressure injury suspect hoti hai. Pressure hatana aur skin assess karna zaroori hai.

**Baaki options kyun galat hain?**

- **A.** Visible fat deeper tissue loss ka sign hai.
- **B.** Exposed tendon ya bone stage 4 ko support karta hai.
- **D.** Open shallow wound stage 2 ho sakta hai.

</details>

## Urinary catheter care

### Q4. A catheterized patient has a drainage bag resting on the bed above bladder level. What should the nurse do?

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Topic:** Urinary catheter care

- **A.** Clamp the catheter routinely for several hours
- **B.** Place the bag below bladder level without letting it touch the floor
- **C.** Disconnect the tubing and drain it into a basin
- **D.** Raise the bag further to improve flow

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: B. Place the bag below bladder level without letting it touch the floor**

Urine gravity se bag mein jaata hai. Bag bladder ke upar hoga toh urine wapas ja sakta hai. Isliye bag neeche rakho aur closed system ko intact rakho.

**Baaki options kyun galat hain?**

- **A.** Routine clamping urine drainage rok sakti hai.
- **C.** Connection kholne se infection ka risk badhta hai.
- **D.** Bag aur upar karne se backflow ka risk badhega.

</details>
