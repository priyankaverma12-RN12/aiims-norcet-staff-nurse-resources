### Q1. Before a planned lumbar puncture, a patient develops new focal neurological deficits and declining consciousness. Which action is appropriate?

**Difficulty:** 🔴 Difficult · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD · Original practice

- **A.** Alert the clinician for urgent reassessment of procedure safety before proceeding
- **B.** Proceed because the consent is signed
- **C.** Ignore neurological changes
- **D.** Ask the patient to walk unassisted to the procedure room

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: A. Alert the clinician for urgent reassessment of procedure safety before proceeding**

**A kyun sahi hai:** Naye focal neurological deficits ka matlab body ke kisi specific neurological function mein nayi dikkat hai. Consciousness girne ke saath yeh intracranial pathology ya pressure-related danger ka signal ho sakta hai. Nurse LP ki routine preparation rok kar ABC aur neurological status assess karti hai aur urgent clinician review arrange karti hai.

**B kyun galat hai:** Signed consent se nayi contraindication khatam nahi hoti. Procedure ka risk patient ki present condition ke according dobara assess hona chahiye.

**C kyun galat hai:** Neurological changes ignore karne se serious intracranial emergency miss ho sakti hai. LP se pehle safety reassessment zaroori hai.

**D kyun galat hai:** Declining consciousness mein patient ko akela chalane se fall aur injury ka risk hai. Safe supervised assessment aur transport plan chahiye.

**Exam clue:** LP se pehle new focal deficit ya consciousness change ho toh urgent reassessment karo.

### Q2. Which position is commonly used to facilitate lumbar puncture when appropriate?

**Difficulty:** 🟡 Medium · **Priority:** 🟡 IMPORTANT · Original practice · **🟣 AIIMS NORCET 6 Prelims 2024 — related reported concept**

- **A.** Standing unsupported
- **B.** Flat prone with the neck forcefully extended
- **C.** Trendelenburg in every patient
- **D.** Lateral decubitus with suitable spinal flexion, or supported sitting as instructed

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: D. Lateral decubitus with suitable spinal flexion, or supported sitting as instructed**

**A kyun galat hai:** Standing unsupported position mein patient stable nahi rahega aur fall ka risk ho sakta hai. Lumbar puncture ke liye controlled position chahiye.

**B kyun galat hai:** Neck ko forcefully extend karne se discomfort aur injury ho sakti hai. Yeh lumbar interspaces ko safely access karne ki standard position nahi hai.

**C kyun galat hai:** Trendelenburg mein bed ka head neeche hota hai. Yeh har LP ke liye required position nahi hai aur clinical condition ke hisaab se unsuitable ho sakta hai.

**D kyun sahi hai:** Lateral decubitus mein patient side par leta hai aur suitable lumbar flexion se vertebrae ke beech access aasaan hota hai. Supported sitting bhi CSF collection ke liye use ho sakti hai. Nurse patient ko safely support karti hai aur clinician ke instructions follow karti hai.

**Exam clue:** CSF collection aur opening pressure measurement ki positioning requirements ko alag yaad rakho.

### Q3. When cerebrospinal fluid opening pressure is being measured, which patient position is generally needed for standard interpretation?

**Difficulty:** 🔴 Difficult · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD · Original practice

- **A.** High Fowler sitting with legs dangling as the standard pressure position
- **B.** Prone with sustained abdominal compression
- **C.** Relaxed lateral decubitus position according to procedure guidance
- **D.** Standing

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: C. Relaxed lateral decubitus position according to procedure guidance**

**A kyun galat hai:** Sitting se hydrostatic pressure change hota hai. Isliye sitting reading ko standard lateral opening pressure ke reference se directly interpret nahi karna chahiye.

**B kyun galat hai:** Abdominal compression venous aur CSF pressure affect kar sakti hai. Yeh standard relaxed measurement condition nahi hai.

**C kyun sahi hai:** CSF opening pressure ka matlab sample nikalne se pehle fluid ka pressure hai. Standard interpretation ke liye patient generally side par relaxed rehta hai aur unnecessary straining avoid karta hai. Clinician manometer aur positioning protocol follow karta hai.

**D kyun galat hai:** Standing mein pressure posture se affect hota hai aur patient safety bhi compromise ho sakti hai. Yeh routine opening pressure position nahi hai.

**Exam clue:** Opening pressure ke liye relaxed lateral decubitus yaad rakho.

**C kyun galat hai:** Unassisted walking se unstable patient ki oxygen demand aur fall risk badh sakta hai. Pehle respiratory status assess karna chahiye.

**D kyun galat hai:** Next meal tak wait karne se potentially serious complication worsen ho sakti hai. New respiratory distress immediate action maangti hai.

### Q7. After removal of a large volume of ascitic fluid, a patient becomes dizzy and hypotensive. What should the nurse do?

**C kyun galat hai:** Bina measure kiye normal vital signs record karna inaccurate documentation hai. Isse deterioration miss ho sakti hai.

**D kyun sahi hai:** Nurse blood pressure, pulse, consciousness, peripheral perfusion aur puncture site promptly check karti hai aur urgent help leti hai. Large-volume paracentesis ke baad effective circulation affect ho sakti hai. Clinician cause aur prescribed fluids ya albumin ki need review karta hai, especially cirrhosis mein.

**Exam clue:** Drainage ke baad symptomatic hypotension ko assess aur escalate karo, expected kehkar ignore mat karo.

### Q20. A patient becomes acutely unwell during a diagnostic test. What is the appropriate nursing priority?

**Difficulty:** 🔴 Difficult · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD · Original practice

- **C.** Complete the test regardless of deterioration
- **D.** Record the symptoms only after the patient leaves

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: B. Stop or pause the test when safe, assess immediate threats and activate appropriate help**

**B kyun sahi hai:** Agar patient test ke dauran acutely unwell ho, toh nurse test ko safe tareeke se pause ya stop karwati hai. Airway, breathing aur circulation jaise immediate threats assess karti hai aur emergency help activate karti hai. Patient stabilize hone ke baad team further testing ka decision leti hai.

**C kyun galat hai:** Patient deteriorate kar raha ho toh test complete karna safety se zyada important nahi hai. Continued procedure harm worsen kar sakta hai.

**D kyun galat hai:** Sirf baad mein symptoms document karne se immediate threat treat nahi hota. Assessment, help aur timely intervention pehle aate hain, phir accurate documentation hoti hai.
