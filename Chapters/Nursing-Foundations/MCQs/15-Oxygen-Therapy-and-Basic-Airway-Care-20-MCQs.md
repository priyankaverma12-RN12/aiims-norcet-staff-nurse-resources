### Q1. Which device commonly provides low-flow supplemental oxygen while allowing a stable patient to speak and eat?

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Priority:** Medium

- **A.** Non-rebreather mask continuously during meals
- **B.** Endotracheal tube as routine low-flow delivery
- **C.** Bag-mask ventilation as a routine eating device
- **D.** Nasal cannula

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: D. Nasal cannula**

Nasal cannula nares mein oxygen deliver karti hai aur mouth relatively free rehta hai. Actual flow aur target clinical order se decide hote hain.

**Baaki options kyun sahi nahi hain?** Masks meal access limit kar sakti hain. Endotracheal tube aur bag-mask ventilation advanced support ke liye hain.

</details>

### Q2. A patient requires a controlled and relatively precise oxygen concentration. Which device is commonly selected?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Priority:** High

- **A.** Nasal cannula with no attention to breathing pattern
- **B.** A disconnected reservoir mask
- **C.** Venturi mask with the correct adapter and flow
- **D.** Simple mask at an arbitrarily low flow

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: C. Venturi mask with the correct adapter and flow**

Venturi system fixed air-oxygen entrainment se specified concentration deliver karne mein help karti hai. Correct adapter aur flow manufacturer instructions se match karna chahiye.

**Baaki options kyun sahi nahi hain?** Simple mask aur cannula delivered concentration vary kar sakti hain. Disconnected mask oxygen deliver nahi karti.

</details>

### Q3. Why must a simple oxygen face mask be used at an appropriate minimum flow specified by the device?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Priority:** High

- **A.** Carbon dioxide cannot accumulate inside any mask
- **B.** Adequate flow helps clear exhaled carbon dioxide from the mask
- **C.** Low flow always increases oxygen concentration safely
- **D.** Flow rate has no effect on mask gas clearance

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: B. Adequate flow helps clear exhaled carbon dioxide from the mask**

Mask ke andar exhaled gas collect ho sakti hai. Appropriate flow CO2 washout support karta hai. Device-specific minimum follow karna important hai.

**Baaki options kyun sahi nahi hain?** Arbitrarily low flow rebreathing cause kar sakta hai. Flow gas clearance affect karta hai.

</details>

### Q4. A reservoir mask bag collapses substantially during inspiration. Which nursing response is appropriate?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Priority:** High

- **A.** Check oxygen supply, flow, fit and valves and reassess the patient urgently if needed
- **B.** Turn off the oxygen source
- **C.** Ignore the bag because collapse proves adequate delivery
- **D.** Cover the reservoir bag with a heavy object

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: A. Check oxygen supply, flow, fit and valves and reassess the patient urgently if needed**

Reservoir bag oxygen store karti hai. Excess collapse inadequate supply ya high patient demand indicate kar sakti hai. Nurse device aur oxygenation check karti hai.

**Baaki options kyun sahi nahi hain?** Source off karna delivery rokta hai. Collapse reassuring sign nahi hai. External weight reservoir function impair karta hai.

</details>

### Q5. What is the role of pulse oximetry in oxygen therapy?

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Priority:** Medium

- **A.** Estimate peripheral arterial oxygen saturation and track trends with clinical assessment
- **B.** Directly measure arterial carbon dioxide
- **C.** Measure hemoglobin concentration accurately
- **D.** Replace assessment of breathing effort

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: A. Estimate peripheral arterial oxygen saturation and track trends with clinical assessment**

Pulse oximeter hemoglobin oxygen saturation estimate karta hai. Nurse waveform, perfusion aur patient symptoms ke saath reading interpret karti hai.

**Baaki options kyun sahi nahi hain?** PaCO2 ABG se assess hoti hai. Hb separate blood test hai. Respiratory effort still assess karna chahiye.

</details>

### Q6. A patient at risk of hypercapnic respiratory failure has an oxygen prescription specifying a target saturation range. Which action is appropriate?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Priority:** High

- **A.** Aim for 100% saturation in every patient regardless of the plan
- **B.** Withhold oxygen during severe hypoxemia solely because COPD is present
- **C.** Set oxygen once and never reassess
- **D.** Titrate oxygen to the prescribed target and arrange indicated blood gas assessment

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: D. Titrate oxygen to the prescribed target and arrange indicated blood gas assessment**

Some patients mein uncontrolled oxygen hypercapnia worsen kar sakti hai. Target-based oxygen aur clinical monitoring safety improve karte hain. Severe hypoxemia urgent treatment maangti hai.

**Baaki options kyun sahi nahi hain?** Universal 100% target inappropriate ho sakta hai. Oxygen completely withhold karna harmful ho sakta hai. Reassessment essential hai.

</details>

### Q7. Which nursing action reduces skin injury from a nasal cannula?

### Q8. A patient on oxygen suddenly becomes confused, tachypneic and cyanotic. Which response is appropriate?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Priority:** High

- **A.** Wait until the prescribed oxygen bag is empty
- **B.** Assess airway and breathing, verify delivery and activate urgent clinical help
- **C.** Assume the oxygen device prevents all deterioration
- **D.** Remove monitoring because readings may be abnormal

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: B. Assess airway and breathing, verify delivery and activate urgent clinical help**

Oxygen therapy ke bawajood patient deteriorate ho sakta hai. Nurse airway, ventilation aur oxygen supply assess karke emergency escalation karti hai.

**Baaki options kyun sahi nahi hain?** Device presence adequate oxygenation guarantee nahi karti. Monitoring remove aur delay unsafe hain.

</details>

### Q10. A postoperative patient cannot cough effectively because of incisional pain. Which approach may support airway clearance?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Priority:** High

- **A.** Assess pain and use prescribed analgesia and incision support with coached coughing
- **B.** Ask the patient to suppress every cough
- **C.** Apply intense pressure directly into the wound
- **D.** Withhold all pain treatment to improve alertness

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: A. Assess pain and use prescribed analgesia and incision support with coached coughing**

Pain deep breathing aur cough limit kar sakta hai. Safe analgesia aur incision splinting patient ko effective cough karne mein help kar sakti hain.

**Baaki options kyun sahi nahi hain?** Cough suppression secretions retain kar sakti hai. Intense wound pressure injure kar sakta hai. Pain treatment assessment-based honi chahiye.

</details>

### Q11. Which finding is a clinical indication to assess whether airway suction is required?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Priority:** High

- **A.** A fixed schedule regardless of secretion findings
- **B.** Normal quiet breathing with no secretion concern
- **C.** Patient request for a routine meal
- **D.** Retained secretions with ineffective cough and signs of airway obstruction

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

Suction secretions remove karne ke liye use hoti hai jab patient independently clear nahi kar paata. Nurse actual need aur safety assess karti hai.

**Baaki options kyun sahi nahi hain?** Routine unnecessary suction mucosal injury aur hypoxemia cause kar sakti hai. Normal breathing ya meal request indication nahi hain.

</details>

### Q12. A patient develops marked desaturation and bradycardia during airway suction. What should the nurse do?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Priority:** High

- **A.** Increase suction duration without reassessment
- **B.** Disconnect oxygen for the next attempt
- **C.** Stop suction, support oxygenation and ventilation, and reassess urgently
- **D.** Continue suction until all secretions are removed

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: C. Stop suction, support oxygenation and ventilation, and reassess urgently**

Suction temporarily oxygenation affect aur vagal response trigger kar sakti hai. Nurse stop karke breathing-circulation support aur urgent assessment karti hai.

**Baaki options kyun sahi nahi hain?** Continued suction, prolonged duration aur oxygen removal deterioration worsen kar sakte hain.

</details>

### Q19. A patient has a tracheostomy and suddenly develops severe respiratory distress. Which approach is appropriate?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Priority:** High

- **A.** Seal all airway openings without assessment
- **B.** Activate emergency help and follow the tracheostomy airway emergency algorithm
- **C.** Blindly push equipment deep into the tube with force
- **D.** Assume distress is anxiety and wait

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: B. Activate emergency help and follow the tracheostomy airway emergency algorithm**

Tracheostomy obstruction ya displacement life-threatening ho sakti hai. Nurse trained emergency protocol se oxygenation aur airway patency urgently assess karti hai.

**Baaki options kyun sahi nahi hain?** Blind force injury cause kar sakti hai. Delay unsafe hai. All openings seal karna ventilation rok sakta hai.

</details>

### Q20. Which finding should be documented after an oxygen therapy adjustment?

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>
