# First aid and basic life support — 20 original MCQs

**NORCET-style and Indian staff nurse practice. Unattempted.** Difficulty and priority are editorial estimates. These are not official PYQs. [Chapter](../README.md)

### Q1. A healthcare worker finds an adult unresponsive with absent normal breathing and no definite pulse after a check lasting no more than 10 seconds. What should happen next?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Priority:** High

- **A.** Activate emergency response, start CPR and obtain an AED
- **B.** Observe for five minutes before initiating CPR
- **C.** Give oral water while waiting
- **D.** Start rescue breathing alone indefinitely

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: A. Activate emergency response, start CPR and obtain an AED**

Definite pulse na mile aur normal breathing absent ho toh cardiac arrest suspect hota hai. Emergency help aur CPR organs tak blood flow maintain karne ke liye jaldi start karni chahiye.

**Baaki options kyun sahi nahi hain?** Waiting perfusion delay karti hai. Oral water unsafe hai. Pulse absent hone par compressions zaroori hain.

</details>

### Q2. Which chest compression rate is recommended for adult CPR under the AHA 2025 guidance?

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Priority:** Medium

- **A.** 60–80/min
- **B.** 140–160/min
- **C.** 40–60/min
- **D.** 100–120/min

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: D. 100–120/min**

Adult CPR mein 100–120 compressions per minute recommended hain. Rate ke saath depth, recoil aur minimum interruptions bhi important hain.

**Baaki options kyun sahi nahi hain?** Bahut slow rate inadequate perfusion aur excessive rate poor depth-recoil contribute kar sakti hai.

</details>

### Q3. What is the recommended manual chest compression depth for an average adult during CPR?

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Priority:** Medium

- **A.** About 7–9 cm
- **B.** Only 1 cm
- **C.** About 5–6 cm
- **D.** About 2–3 cm

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: C. About 5–6 cm**

Adult chest ko at least 5 cm compress karna hai aur excessive depth greater than 6 cm avoid karni hai. Isse blood flow aur safety balance karne ki koshish hoti hai.

**Baaki options kyun sahi nahi hain?** Shallow compression inadequate ho sakti hai. Excess depth injury risk badha sakti hai.

</details>

### Q4. During adult CPR before placement of an advanced airway, what compression-to-ventilation ratio is generally used by trained healthcare providers?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Priority:** High

- **A.** 3:1
- **B.** 30:2
- **C.** 15:2
- **D.** 5:1

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: B. 30:2**

Adult conventional CPR mein 30 compressions ke baad two breaths diye jaate hain. Advanced airway ke baad ventilation pattern different ho sakta hai.

**Baaki options kyun sahi nahi hain?** 15:2 certain two-rescuer pediatric situations mein hai. 3:1 neonatal resuscitation mein use ho sakta hai. 5:1 standard adult ratio nahi hai.

</details>

### Q5. Why should rescuers allow complete chest recoil between compressions?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Priority:** High

- **A.** Recoil is needed only after defibrillation
- **B.** Recoil supports venous return and cardiac filling
- **C.** Continuous leaning increases ventricular filling
- **D.** Recoil prevents the heart from receiving blood

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: B. Recoil supports venous return and cardiac filling**

Compression release hone par chest pressure decrease hota hai aur blood heart ki taraf return kar sakta hai. Leaning is filling ko impair kar sakti hai.

**Baaki options kyun sahi nahi hain?** Continuous pressure normal recoil rokta hai. Recoil har compression cycle mein important hai.

</details>

### Q6. When two trained rescuers are available, how often should the chest compressor generally change during adult CPR?

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Priority:** Medium

- **A.** Approximately every two minutes or sooner if fatigued
- **B.** Only every ten minutes
- **C.** After every single compression
- **D.** Only when the patient regains consciousness

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: A. Approximately every two minutes or sooner if fatigued**

CPR physically tiring hai aur fatigue se compression quality gir sakti hai. Around two-minute switch short interruption ke saath quality maintain karne mein help karta hai.

**Baaki options kyun sahi nahi hain?** Long delay fatigue badhati hai. Every compression switch unnecessary interruption karega. ROSC tak wait nahi karna chahiye.

</details>

### Q7. An AED announces that a shock is advised. Which action is correct?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Priority:** High

- **A.** Keep a hand on the patient during shock
- **B.** Delay shock until a complete clinical history is obtained
- **C.** Wait several minutes after shock before resuming compressions
- **D.** Ensure no one is touching the patient, deliver the shock as directed and resume CPR promptly

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: D. Ensure no one is touching the patient, deliver the shock as directed and resume CPR promptly**

AED shock ke waqt contact avoid karna rescuer safety ke liye important hai. Shock ke baad CPR promptly resume hoti hai according to algorithm.

**Baaki options kyun sahi nahi hain?** Touching unsafe hai. History aur long pause perfusion delay karte hain.

</details>

### Q8. An adult is unresponsive and makes occasional gasping sounds. How should a trained responder interpret gasping?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Priority:** High

- **A.** It excludes the need for emergency assessment
- **B.** It proves spontaneous circulation has returned
- **C.** It is abnormal breathing that can occur during cardiac arrest
- **D.** It establishes effective normal breathing

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: C. It is abnormal breathing that can occur during cardiac arrest**

Agonal gasps ineffective breathing ho sakte hain. Nurse rapid pulse check aur arrest assessment karke appropriate CPR start karti hai.

**Baaki options kyun sahi nahi hain?** Gasping normal ventilation ya definite pulse prove nahi karti.

</details>

### Q9. A conscious adult cannot speak or cough effectively because of severe foreign-body airway obstruction. Which sequence is recommended in AHA 2025 adult guidance?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Priority:** High

- **A.** Give water to wash the obstruction down
- **B.** Begin routine chest compressions while the patient remains responsive
- **C.** Cycles of five back blows followed by five abdominal thrusts
- **D.** Blind finger sweeps repeated until relief

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: C. Cycles of five back blows followed by five abdominal thrusts**

Severe choking mein effective cough absent ho sakti hai. AHA 2025 back blows aur abdominal thrusts alternate karne ko kehti hai. Pregnancy ya inability to encircle abdomen mein chest thrusts use ho sakti hain.

**Baaki options kyun sahi nahi hain?** Blind sweep object deeper push kar sakta hai. Water unsafe hai. Unresponsive hone par CPR algorithm follow hota hai.

</details>

### Q10. An adult with severe choking becomes unresponsive. What is the appropriate transition?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Priority:** High

- **A.** Wait for spontaneous coughing before taking action
- **B.** Activate emergency help and begin CPR, checking for a visible object before breaths
- **C.** Continue standing abdominal thrusts indefinitely
- **D.** Perform blind finger sweeps after every compression

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: B. Activate emergency help and begin CPR, checking for a visible object before breaths**

Unresponsive choking patient ko safe surface par CPR start karna hota hai. Mouth open karte waqt visible object remove kiya ja sakta hai, blind sweep nahi.

**Baaki options kyun sahi nahi hain?** Standing thrusts unresponsive patient ke liye suitable nahi hain. Delay oxygenation compromise kar sakti hai.

</details>

### Q11. Which first aid action is appropriate for significant external bleeding from a limb?

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Priority:** Medium

- **A.** Apply firm direct pressure using suitable dressing and activate help
- **B.** Remove the developing clot repeatedly
- **C.** Wash the wound continuously instead of controlling bleeding
- **D.** Raise the limb only and ignore ongoing bleeding

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: A. Apply firm direct pressure using suitable dressing and activate help**

Direct pressure bleeding vessel par compression provide karke blood loss reduce karti hai. Severe life-threatening limb bleeding mein trained tourniquet use may be indicated.

**Baaki options kyun sahi nahi hain?** Clot removal bleeding restart kar sakta hai. Washing aur elevation alone major bleeding control replace nahi karte.

</details>

### Q12. A patient has a deeply embedded object in a wound. Which first aid approach is safest?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Priority:** High

- **A.** Pull the object out immediately
- **B.** Push it deeper to stop movement
- **C.** Apply direct pressure on its protruding end
- **D.** Stabilize the object, control bleeding around it and obtain urgent help

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: D. Stabilize the object, control bleeding around it and obtain urgent help**

Embedded object remove karne se bleeding worsen ho sakti hai. Nurse object stabilize karke around-wound pressure aur urgent care arrange karti hai.

**Baaki options kyun sahi nahi hain?** Removal, pushing aur direct pressure on object deeper tissue damage kar sakte hain.

</details>

### Q13. What is appropriate immediate first aid for a small recent thermal burn when safe water is available?

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Priority:** Medium

- **A.** Apply ice directly for a prolonged period
- **B.** Apply toothpaste to the injured skin
- **C.** Break all blisters immediately
- **D.** Cool the burn with cool running water and avoid ice

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: D. Cool the burn with cool running water and avoid ice**

Cool running water residual heat remove karne mein help karti hai. Large burns mein hypothermia avoid karna aur medical assessment important hai.

**Baaki options kyun sahi nahi hain?** Ice cold injury kar sakti hai. Toothpaste contamination aur blister breaking infection risk badha sakte hain.

</details>

### Q14. A person collapses following electrical contact. What must a responder ensure before touching the person?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Priority:** High

- **A.** Water is poured over the electrical device
- **B.** The person is lifted while still contacting the live source
- **C.** The electrical source has been safely isolated
- **D.** The person is dragged away with bare wet hands

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: C. The electrical source has been safely isolated**

Electrical source active ho toh rescuer bhi shock receive kar sakta hai. Scene safety aur power isolation ke baad emergency assessment start hoti hai.

**Baaki options kyun sahi nahi hain?** Wet contact, water aur live-source contact dangerous hain.

</details>

### Q15. A patient has a convulsive seizure in bed. Which action is appropriate during the event?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Priority:** High

- **A.** Give oral tablets during active convulsions
- **B.** Protect from injury, time the seizure and assess airway and breathing
- **C.** Force a spoon between the teeth
- **D.** Restrain the limbs tightly

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: B. Protect from injury, time the seizure and assess airway and breathing**

Seizure mein involuntary movement injury cause kar sakti hai. Nurse nearby hazards remove karti hai aur airway-breathing assess karti hai. Prolonged seizure urgent help maangta hai.

**Baaki options kyun sahi nahi hain?** Objects mouth mein daalne, restraint aur oral medicines aspiration ya injury cause kar sakte hain.

</details>

### Q16. An alert patient with a suspected limb fracture awaits emergency assessment. Which first aid principle is appropriate?

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Priority:** Medium

- **A.** Support the injured limb in its found position and avoid unnecessary movement
- **B.** Force the limb straight regardless of pain
- **C.** Massage the fracture site vigorously
- **D.** Ask the patient to bear weight to test the injury

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: A. Support the injured limb in its found position and avoid unnecessary movement**

Support aur immobilization movement-related pain aur further injury reduce kar sakte hain. Distal circulation-sensation check important hai.

**Baaki options kyun sahi nahi hain?** Forceful realignment, massage aur weight testing tissue damage worsen kar sakte hain.

</details>

### Q17. An adult with diabetes is alert, has low measured glucose and can swallow safely. Which initial response is commonly appropriate under a hypoglycemia protocol?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Priority:** High

- **A.** Give a measured fast-acting carbohydrate and recheck glucose at the specified interval
- **B.** Give additional insulin immediately
- **C.** Give a large high-fat meal as the only rapid intervention
- **D.** Withhold carbohydrate until loss of consciousness

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: A. Give a measured fast-acting carbohydrate and recheck glucose at the specified interval**

Fast-acting carbohydrate blood glucose quickly raise kar sakti hai. Nurse protocol ke according dose aur repeat glucose monitoring karti hai.

**Baaki options kyun sahi nahi hain?** Insulin glucose aur reduce karegi. High-fat food slower absorption de sakta hai. Loss of consciousness ka wait unsafe hai.

</details>

### Q18. A hypoglycemic patient is unconscious and cannot swallow safely. What should the nurse do?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Priority:** High

- **A.** Pour sweet liquid into the patient's mouth
- **B.** Force the patient to swallow tablets
- **C.** Wait until the patient becomes alert without monitoring
- **D.** Activate urgent help and administer prescribed emergency treatment by a safe nonoral route

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: D. Activate urgent help and administer prescribed emergency treatment by a safe nonoral route**

Unconscious patient oral intake aspirate kar sakta hai. Nurse airway support aur protocol-based glucagon ya IV glucose as indicated arrange karti hai.

**Baaki options kyun sahi nahi hain?** Oral liquid-tablets unsafe hain. Delay neurological injury risk badha sakti hai.

</details>

### Q19. A person with a suspected venomous snakebite is waiting for transport. Which first aid principle is appropriate?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Priority:** High

- **A.** Apply ice and a tightly occlusive arterial tourniquet
- **B.** Encourage running to the hospital
- **C.** Keep the person calm, minimize limb movement and arrange urgent hospital transport
- **D.** Cut the wound and suck out venom

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: C. Keep the person calm, minimize limb movement and arrange urgent hospital transport**

Movement venom spread accelerate kar sakti hai. Limb ko still support karke urgent medical care arrange karni chahiye. Region-specific guidelines follow hoti hain.

**Baaki options kyun sahi nahi hain?** Cutting, suction, ice aur tight tourniquet injury cause kar sakte hain. Running movement badhati hai.

</details>

### Q20. A patient develops severe wheezing and hypotension after an allergen exposure. What is the priority under anaphylaxis protocol?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Priority:** High

- **A.** Allow the patient to stand and walk alone
- **B.** Activate emergency help and give indicated intramuscular epinephrine promptly under protocol
- **C.** Wait for oral antihistamine to work before escalation
- **D.** Give water as the sole treatment

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: B. Activate emergency help and give indicated intramuscular epinephrine promptly under protocol**

Anaphylaxis airway aur circulation rapidly compromise kar sakti hai. IM epinephrine first-line emergency treatment hai aur trained team supportive care arrange karti hai.

**Baaki options kyun sahi nahi hain?** Antihistamine epinephrine replace nahi karti. Water shock treat nahi karta. Walking collapse risk badhati hai.

</details>
