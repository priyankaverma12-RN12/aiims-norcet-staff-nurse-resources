### Q1. A healthcare worker finds an adult unresponsive with absent normal breathing and no definite pulse after a check lasting no more than 10 seconds. What should happen next?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Priority:** High

- **A.** Activate emergency response, start CPR and obtain an AED
- **B.** Observe for five minutes before initiating CPR
- **C.** Give oral water while waiting
- **D.** Start rescue breathing alone indefinitely

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: A. Activate emergency response, start CPR and obtain an AED**

Cardiac arrest ka matlab hai heart body ko effective blood circulation nahi de pa raha hai. Agar adult unresponsive hai, normal breathing nahi hai aur trained healthcare worker ko 10 seconds ke andar definite pulse nahi milti, toh cardiac arrest assume karo. Emergency system activate karo, chest compressions shuru karo aur AED mangwao. AED ek device hai jo rhythm analyse karke zaroorat hone par shock advise karta hai. Trained team ventilation bhi provide karti hai.

**Option-wise explanation:**
- **A (Sahi):** CPR aur emergency response se brain aur heart ko blood supply milne ka chance improve hota hai. AED jaldi available hona bhi important hai.
- **B (Galat):** Paanch minute observe karne se circulation restore karne ka precious time waste hota hai.
- **C (Galat):** Unresponsive patient safely swallow nahi kar sakta. Water airway mein ja sakta hai.
- **D (Galat):** Rescue breathing alone definite pulse hone par respiratory arrest ke liye useful ho sakti hai. Pulseless arrest mein compressions zaroori hain.

**Exam clue:** No definite pulse within 10 seconds with absent normal breathing = start CPR.

### Q2. Which chest compression rate is recommended for adult CPR under the AHA 2025 guidance?

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Priority:** Medium

- **A.** 60–80/min
- **B.** 140–160/min
- **C.** 40–60/min
- **D.** 100–120/min

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: D. 100–120/min**

Chest compression mein sternum ko rhythmically press karke heart se blood flow support kiya jaata hai. AHA 2025 adult CPR ke liye 100–120 compressions per minute ka target rakhti hai. Rate ke saath proper depth, full chest recoil aur chhoti interruptions bhi zaroori hain. Sirf fast compressions karne se quality automatically achhi nahi hoti.

**Option-wise explanation:**
- **A (Galat):** 60–80/min recommended adult CPR rate se kam hai. Isse per minute compressions kam ho sakti hain.
- **B (Galat):** 140–160/min recommended upper range se zyada hai. Itni speed par adequate depth aur recoil maintain karna mushkil ho sakta hai.
- **C (Galat):** 40–60/min adult CPR ke liye bahut slow hai aur adequate circulation support nahi kar sakta.
- **D (Sahi):** 100–120/min adult CPR ka recommended range hai. Depth aur recoil bhi maintain karo.

**Exam clue:** Adult CPR: 100–120/min, full recoil.

### Q4. During adult CPR before placement of an advanced airway, what compression-to-ventilation ratio is generally used by trained healthcare providers?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Priority:** High

- **A.** 3:1
- **B.** 30:2
- **C.** 15:2
- **D.** 5:1

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: B. 30:2**

Adult cardiac arrest mein trained rescuer advanced airway placement se pehle 30 chest compressions ke baad 2 breaths deta hai. Breaths se visible chest rise hona chahiye, excessive ventilation nahi. Is ratio se circulation aur oxygenation dono support hote hain. Advanced airway lagne ke baad trained team continuous compressions aur asynchronous ventilation ka protocol follow kar sakti hai.

**Option-wise explanation:**
- **A (Galat):** 3:1 birth ke samay selected neonatal resuscitation ka ratio hai. Yeh routine adult CPR ka ratio nahi hai.
- **B (Sahi):** 30:2 conventional adult CPR before advanced airway ka standard ratio hai.
- **C (Galat):** 15:2 two-rescuer pediatric CPR ke selected situations mein use hota hai, routine adult CPR mein nahi.
- **D (Galat):** 5:1 current conventional adult CPR ka standard ratio nahi hai.

**Exam clue:** Adult pre-advanced airway: 30:2. Two-rescuer pediatric: 15:2.

### Q5. Why should rescuers allow complete chest recoil between compressions?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Priority:** High

- **A.** Recoil is needed only after defibrillation
- **B.** Recoil supports venous return and cardiac filling
- **C.** Continuous leaning increases ventricular filling
- **D.** Recoil prevents the heart from receiving blood

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: B. Recoil supports venous return and cardiac filling**

Recoil ka matlab har compression ke baad chest ko apni normal position mein poora wapas aane dena hai. Jab chest release hoti hai toh venous blood ko heart ki taraf return karne ka mauka milta hai. Agar rescuer chest par leaned rehta hai toh filling aur circulation compromise ho sakti hai. Hands ko position par rakho lekin residual pressure release karo.

**Option-wise explanation:**
- **A (Galat):** Recoil har compression cycle ka part hai. Defibrillation ke baad hi zaroori nahi hota.
- **B (Sahi):** Complete recoil heart filling aur agle compression ke liye blood flow support karta hai.
- **C (Galat):** Leaning intrathoracic pressure ko elevated rakh sakti hai aur venous return impair kar sakti hai.
- **D (Galat):** Recoil heart ko blood receive karne se nahi rokta. Yeh filling support karta hai.

**Exam clue:** Press, release fully, avoid leaning.

### Q6. When two trained rescuers are available, how often should the chest compressor generally change during adult CPR?

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Priority:** Medium

- **A.** Approximately every two minutes or sooner if fatigued
- **B.** Only every ten minutes
- **C.** After every single compression
- **D.** Only when the patient regains consciousness

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: A. Approximately every two minutes or sooner if fatigued**

Repeated chest compressions physically tiring hoti hain. Rescuer fatigue hone par compressions shallow ya irregular ho sakti hain, kabhi fatigue notice hone se pehle bhi. Do trained rescuers available hon toh approximately har 2 minutes mein compressor change karo, ya fatigue hone par pehle. Switch ko rhythm analysis ya planned brief pause ke saath coordinate karo taaki interruption minimum ho.

**Option-wise explanation:**
- **A (Sahi):** Around two-minute rotation compression quality maintain karne mein help karti hai. Fatigue pehle ho toh sooner switch appropriate hai.
- **B (Galat):** Ten minutes tak same compressor rakhne se fatigue ke karan quality decline ho sakti hai.
- **C (Galat):** Har single compression ke baad switch karne se unnecessary interruptions aur coordination problem hongi.
- **D (Galat):** Consciousness return ka wait karna rotation ka criterion nahi hai. CPR quality abhi maintain karni hai.

**Exam clue:** Switch compressor about every 2 minutes, with minimal interruption.

### Q8. An adult is unresponsive and makes occasional gasping sounds. How should a trained responder interpret gasping?

- **C (Sahi):** Gasping abnormal breathing hai aur cardiac arrest mein ho sakti hai.
- **D (Galat):** Occasional gasps effective normal breathing establish nahi karte.

### Q17. An adult with diabetes is alert, has low measured glucose and can swallow safely. Which initial response is commonly appropriate under a hypoglycemia protocol?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Priority:** High

### Q19. A person with a suspected venomous snakebite is waiting for transport. Which first aid principle is appropriate?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Priority:** High

- **A.** Apply ice and a tightly occlusive arterial tourniquet
- **B.** Encourage running to the hospital
- **C.** Keep the person calm, minimize limb movement and arrange urgent hospital transport
- **D.** Cut the wound and suck out venom

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: C. Keep the person calm, minimize limb movement and arrange urgent hospital transport**

Suspected venomous snakebite mein venom body mein spread ho sakta hai. Patient ko reassure karo, unnecessary movement avoid karo aur bitten limb ko gently immobilize karke urgent hospital transport arrange karo. Rings ya tight items safely remove karo agar swelling ka risk ho. Airway aur breathing monitor karo. Tight arterial tourniquet, cutting, suction aur ice se harm ho sakta hai. Pressure-immobilization technique ko har snakebite par apply nahi karna chahiye. Local Indian guideline aur trained advice follow karo.

- **D (Galat):** Wound cutting aur suction venom ka reliable removal nahi karte aur tissue injury/infection risk badha sakte hain.

**Exam clue:** Snakebite: immobilize and transport, no cutting or tight tourniquet.

### Q20. A patient develops severe wheezing and hypotension after an allergen exposure. What is the priority under anaphylaxis protocol?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Priority:** High

- **A.** Allow the patient to stand and walk alone
- **B.** Activate emergency help and give indicated intramuscular epinephrine promptly under protocol
- **C.** Wait for oral antihistamine to work before escalation
- **D.** Give water as the sole treatment

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: B. Activate emergency help and give indicated intramuscular epinephrine promptly under protocol**

Anaphylaxis severe allergic reaction hai jisme airway swelling, bronchospasm aur blood pressure girna rapidly develop ho sakta hai. IM epinephrine, jise adrenaline bhi kehte hain, first-line treatment hai. Emergency team activate karo aur indicated protocol ke mutabik IM dose anterolateral thigh mein do. Breathing aur circulation support aur reassessment zaroori hai. Patient ko suddenly stand ya walk mat karwao. Position breathing aur circulation ki condition ke hisaab se decide karo.

**Option-wise explanation:**
- **A (Galat):** Hypotension mein standing ya walking circulation collapse ka risk badha sakti hai.
- **B (Sahi):** Prompt indicated IM epinephrine aur emergency escalation airway aur circulatory compromise address karne ki priority hai.
- **C (Galat):** Antihistamines skin symptoms mein help kar sakti hain, lekin shock ya airway obstruction ke liye epinephrine replace nahi karti.
- **D (Galat):** Water anaphylactic shock ya airway swelling ka effective treatment nahi hai.

**Exam clue:** Anaphylaxis: IM epinephrine first, not antihistamine first.
