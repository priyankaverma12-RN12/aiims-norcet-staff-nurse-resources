### Q01. A 138-kg patient is scheduled for a sling transfer. The compatible lift is rated for 180 kg and its available sling is rated for 125 kg. Which action is appropriate?

**Difficulty:** 🔴 Difficult · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD · **Evidence:** Original practice · **Exam focus:** NORCET / Staff Nurse · **Topic:** Component capacity

- **A.** Obtain a compatible sling rated for the patient before the transfer
- **B.** Proceed because the lift capacity exceeds the patient's weight
- **C.** Use two 125-kg slings together to increase capacity
- **D.** Raise the patient only a short distance with the available sling

<details><summary>✅ Correct answer and detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: A. Obtain a compatible sling rated for the patient before the transfer**

**A. Kyun sahi hai:** Lift aur sling dono patient ka weight safely handle karne chahiye. Yahan sling ki limit 125 kg hai aur patient 138 kg ka hai. Isliye suitable compatible sling milne tak lift start nahi karni chahiye.

**B. Kyun galat hai:** Lift ka frame patient ka weight handle kar sakta hai, lekin sling ki limit alag hai. Sling fail ho jaaye toh patient gir sakta hai.

**C. Kyun galat hai:** Do slings jodne se certified capacity add nahi ho jaati. Unapproved arrangement attachments aur balance ko unsafe bana sakta hai.

**D. Kyun galat hai:** Kam height par bhi sling par patient ka weight aata hai. Chhota lift overload ki problem solve nahi karta.

**Exam clue:** Capacity ke liye system ke har load-bearing component ki limit check karo.

**Clinical reference:** [Safe handling guidance](https://www.canada.ca/en/health-canada/services/drugs-medical-devices/patient-lifts.html).

### Q03. During preparation for a powered lift transfer, a nurse finds fraying at the stitching of one load-bearing sling strap. The sling has not exceeded its scheduled replacement date. What is the safest response?

**Difficulty:** 🟡 Medium · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD · **Evidence:** Original practice · **Exam focus:** NORCET / Staff Nurse · **Topic:** Pre-use inspection

- **A.** Use the sling because its replacement date has not arrived
- **B.** Place the damaged strap on the lighter side of the patient
- **C.** Remove the damaged sling from use and arrange an intact compatible replacement
- **D.** Reinforce the stitching with a temporary dressing before lifting

<details><summary>✅ Correct answer and detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: C. Remove the damaged sling from use and arrange an intact compatible replacement**

**A. Kyun galat hai:** Replacement date ek routine maintenance limit hai. Actual damage milne par date future mein ho tab bhi sling unsafe ho sakti hai.

**B. Kyun galat hai:** Patient ke weight ka distribution movement ke saath badal sakta hai. Damaged strap ko lighter side par rakhna reliable protection nahi hai.

**C. Kyun sahi hai:** Frayed stitching load ke dauran tear ho sakti hai. Sling ko use se hata kar facility ke reporting process ke according replacement arrange karo.

**D. Kyun galat hai:** Dressing wound cover karne ke liye hoti hai, load-bearing seam repair ke liye nahi. Temporary patch sling ki certified strength restore nahi karta.

**Exam clue:** Visible structural damage ko routine expiry date se zyada importance do.
