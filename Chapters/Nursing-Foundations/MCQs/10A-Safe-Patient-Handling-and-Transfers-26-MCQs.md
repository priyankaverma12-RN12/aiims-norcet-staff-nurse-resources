### Q01. A 138-kg patient is scheduled for a sling transfer. The compatible lift is rated for 180 kg and its available sling is rated for 125 kg. Which action is appropriate?

**Difficulty:** 🟡 Medium · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD · **Evidence:** Original practice · **Exam focus:** NORCET / Staff Nurse · **Topic:** Component capacity

- **A.** Use a 160-kg sling with an attachment system not approved for this lift
- **B.** Select the available sling because it is correctly sized, but keep the lift height low
- **C.** Obtain a compatible sling rated for the patient before the transfer
- **D.** Proceed because the lift capacity exceeds the patient's weight

<details><summary>✅ Correct answer and detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: C. Obtain a compatible sling rated for the patient before the transfer**

**A. Kyun galat hai:** 160 kg ki rating patient ke weight se zyada hai, lekin sling ka attachment system lift ke liye approved nahi hai. Capacity satisfy hone ke bawajood compatibility confirm honi chahiye, warna sling detach ho sakti hai.

**B. Kyun galat hai:** Correct size aur low lifting height dono useful considerations hain, lekin 125 kg ki load limit change nahi hoti. Patient ka poora supported weight sling par aayega. Isliye overload ka risk remain karega.

**C. Kyun sahi hai:** Lift aur sling dono patient ka weight safely handle karne chahiye. Yahan sling ki limit 125 kg hai aur patient 138 kg ka hai. Isliye suitable compatible sling milne tak lift start nahi karni chahiye.

**D. Kyun galat hai:** Lift ka frame patient ka weight handle kar sakta hai, lekin sling ki limit alag hai. Sling fail ho jaaye toh patient gir sakta hai.

**Exam clue:** Capacity ke liye system ke har load-bearing component ki limit check karo.

**Clinical reference:** [Safe handling guidance](https://www.canada.ca/en/health-canada/services/drugs-medical-devices/patient-lifts.html).

### Q02. A replacement sling fits the patient's body size and has an adequate weight rating, but its label lists a different hanger-bar attachment system. What should the nurse verify before use?

**Difficulty:** 🟡 Medium · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD · **Evidence:** Original practice · **Exam focus:** NORCET / Staff Nurse · **Topic:** Compatibility

- **A.** Whether a second nurse can hold the unmatched attachment in place
- **B.** Manufacturer-approved compatibility of sling, attachment and lift
- **C.** Whether both devices are stored on the same ward
- **D.** Whether the sling fabric has the same colour as the usual sling

<details><summary>✅ Correct answer and detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: B. Manufacturer-approved compatibility of sling, attachment and lift**

**A. Kyun galat hai:** Nurse ka haath approved locking attachment ko replace nahi kar sakta. Load move hone par connection slip kar sakta hai.

**C. Kyun galat hai:** Same ward mein equipment hona compatibility ka proof nahi hai. Alag lift systems mein hooks ya clips ka design different ho sakta hai.

**D. Kyun galat hai:** Colour se fabric ya sling size ka clue mil sakta hai, lekin attachment type confirm nahi hota. Manufacturer ki instructions check karni chahiye.

**Exam clue:** Fit, capacity aur compatibility teen separate checks hain.

### Q03. During preparation for a powered lift transfer, a nurse finds fraying at the stitching of one load-bearing sling strap. The sling has not exceeded its scheduled replacement date. What is the safest response?

**Difficulty:** 🟡 Medium · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD · **Evidence:** Original practice · **Exam focus:** NORCET / Staff Nurse · **Topic:** Pre-use inspection

- **B.** Use the sling because its replacement date has not arrived
- **C.** Place the damaged strap on the lighter side of the patient
- **D.** Reinforce the stitching with a temporary dressing before lifting

<details><summary>✅ Correct answer and detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: A. Remove the damaged sling from use and arrange an intact compatible replacement**

**A. Kyun sahi hai:** Frayed stitching load ke dauran tear ho sakti hai. Sling ko use se hata kar facility ke reporting process ke according replacement arrange karo.

**C. Kyun galat hai:** Patient ke weight ka distribution movement ke saath badal sakta hai. Damaged strap ko lighter side par rakhna reliable protection nahi hai.

**D. Kyun galat hai:** Dressing wound cover karne ke liye hoti hai, load-bearing seam repair ke liye nahi. Temporary patch sling ki certified strength restore nahi karta.

**Exam clue:** Visible structural damage ko routine expiry date se zyada importance do.
