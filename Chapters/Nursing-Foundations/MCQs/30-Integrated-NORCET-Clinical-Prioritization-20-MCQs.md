### Q1. Four patients call simultaneously. Which needs assessment first?

**Difficulty:** 🔴 Difficult · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD · Original practice

- **A.** A stable patient whose discharge teaching is due
- **B.** A patient asking for help choosing lunch
- **C.** A patient with sudden chest tightness, wheeze and facial swelling after an injection
- **D.** A patient requesting routine oral care

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: C. A patient with sudden chest tightness, wheeze and facial swelling after an injection**

**A kyun galat hai:** Discharge teaching patient ko ghar par safe care karne mein madad karti hai. Lekin stable patient ki teaching kuch samay ke liye postpone ya doosri qualified nurse ko handover ki ja sakti hai, kyunki doosre patient ki breathing ko immediate threat hai.

**B kyun galat hai:** Lunch choose karne mein help dena bhi nursing care ka part hai. Lekin is request mein acute deterioration ka koi clue nahi hai, isliye emergency ko pehle handle karna chahiye.

**C kyun sahi hai:** Injection ke baad chest tightness, wheeze aur facial swelling ek severe allergic reaction, yaani anaphylaxis, ke signs ho sakte hain. Airway swell ho sakti hai aur circulation fail ho sakta hai. Nurse turant emergency help bulati hai, airway, breathing aur circulation assess karti hai aur protocol ke according IM adrenaline aur resuscitation support mein delay nahi karti.

**D kyun galat hai:** Routine mouth care comfort aur infection prevention mein help karti hai. Stable patient ke liye yeh care airway emergency ke baad ya suitable assistance se ki ja sakti hai.

**Exam clue:** Sudden airway ya breathing problem ko stable routine tasks se pehle address karo.

### Q2. Which patient should the nurse assess first after receiving handover?

**Difficulty:** 🔴 Difficult · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD · Original practice

- **A.** A patient with longstanding unchanged mild constipation
- **B.** A patient with a new drop in consciousness and unequal pupils after a fall
- **C.** A stable patient waiting for a routine dressing change
- **D.** A patient requesting a medication information leaflet

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: B. A patient with a new drop in consciousness and unequal pupils after a fall**

**A kyun galat hai:** Longstanding unchanged constipation ko assess aur manage karna chahiye, lekin is patient mein nayi acute deterioration nahi batayi gayi hai. Head injury ke warning signs zyada urgent hain.

**B kyun sahi hai:** Fall ke baad consciousness girna aur pupils unequal hona brain injury, bleeding ya increased intracranial pressure ka warning sign ho sakta hai. Nurse patient ka airway, breathing aur circulation check karti hai, neurological observations leti hai aur emergency clinical review activate karti hai. Trauma precautions bhi condition ke according follow hoti hain.

**C kyun galat hai:** Stable dressing change infection prevention aur wound healing ke liye important hai. Lekin jab acute neurological deterioration present ho, toh routine dressing ko safely postpone ya handover kar sakte hain.

**D kyun galat hai:** Medication leaflet patient education ke liye useful hai. Is request mein immediate physiological threat nahi hai, isliye yeh emergency neurological assessment se pehle nahi aati.

**Exam clue:** New consciousness change aur unequal pupils after head injury urgent assessment maangte hain.

### Q3. A patient is receiving an IV infusion and reports burning at the cannula site. The site is red and warm along the vein, without cool swelling. What should the nurse suspect?

**Difficulty:** 🔴 Difficult · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD · Original practice

- **A.** Phlebitis requiring assessment and appropriate line management
- **B.** Infiltration as the only possible complication
- **C.** A normal finding that always needs no intervention
- **D.** A pulmonary embolism proven by local redness

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: A. Phlebitis requiring assessment and appropriate line management**

**A kyun sahi hai:** Phlebitis ka matlab vein ki inflammation hai. Cannula wali vein ke along burning, warmth aur redness iski taraf ishara karte hain. Nurse infusion pause ya stop karti hai, site assess karti hai aur local protocol ke according line remove ya manage karti hai. Agar vesicant leakage ka doubt ho toh specific extravasation protocol follow karti hai, kyunki management alag ho sakti hai.

**B kyun galat hai:** Infiltration mein nonvesicant fluid vein se surrounding tissue mein leak hota hai. Cool swelling, tightness ya slow flow common clues hain. Yahan warm redness vein ke along hai, isliye phlebitis better fit hai, lekin nurse complete assessment se doosri complications bhi consider karti hai.

**C kyun galat hai:** Cannula site par new burning, redness ya warmth normal maan kar ignore karna unsafe hai. Inflammation ya infection worsen ho sakti hai aur line reassessment chahiye.

**D kyun galat hai:** Local redness se pulmonary embolism diagnose nahi hota. PE ke liye breathlessness, chest pain ya circulatory findings aur appropriate clinical evaluation chahiye.

**Exam clue:** Vein ke along warm redness phlebitis suggest karti hai, jabki cool swelling infiltration ka common clue hai.

### Q5. A patient with a feeding tube is due for medicines, but initial placement has not been confirmed. What is the priority?

- **C.** Use the tube because the external marking is visible
- **D.** Withhold tube use and obtain approved placement confirmation

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: D. Withhold tube use and obtain approved placement confirmation**

**A kyun galat hai:** Tube placement confirm nahi hui toh thoda sa water bhi respiratory tract mein ja sakta hai. Isliye test flush ya test dose ko placement check ke substitute ke roop mein use nahi karte.

**B kyun galat hai:** Whoosh test mein air inject karke sound suna jaata hai, lekin sound se tube ka stomach mein hona reliably establish nahi hota. Current safety guidance initial placement ke liye is method ko accept nahi karti.

**C kyun galat hai:** External marking tube ki visible length track karne mein useful ho sakti hai. Lekin initial insertion ke baad tip ka correct location sirf marking se prove nahi hota.

**D kyun sahi hai:** Agar tube stomach ki jagah airway mein ho toh feed ya medicine lungs ko serious harm kar sakti hai. Nurse tube use withhold karti hai aur tube type ke approved confirmation protocol ko follow karti hai. Gastric NG tube ke liye accepted methods mein suitable aspirate pH ya correctly interpreted X-ray aate hain. Confirmation document hone se pehle kuch instill nahi karna chahiye.

**Exam clue:** Unconfirmed feeding tube ko water, feed ya medicine ke liye use mat karo.

**D kyun galat hai:** Balloon volume badhane se drainage lumen ki obstruction solve nahi hoti. Extra inflation bladder neck ya urethra ko injure kar sakti hai.

**D kyun galat hai:** Next unit dene se investigation delay aur further exposure ho sakta hai. Further transfusion ka decision authorized transfusion team clinical assessment ke baad leti hai.

### Q14. After a limb bandage is applied, the patient develops new pallor and tingling distal to it. Which assessment should be performed immediately?

### Q15. A bedbound patient's sacral skin is intact but nonblanchable discoloration has appeared. Which plan is best?

**Difficulty:** 🔴 Difficult · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD · Original practice

Patient safety ke saath factual documentation, notification aur institutional error reporting bhi complete karti hai.
