### Q1. Four patients call simultaneously. Which needs assessment first?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Priority:** High

- **A.** A stable patient whose discharge teaching is due
- **B.** A patient asking for help choosing lunch
- **C.** A patient with sudden chest tightness, wheeze and facial swelling after an injection
- **D.** A patient requesting routine oral care

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: C. A patient with sudden chest tightness, wheeze and facial swelling after an injection**

Injection ke baad wheeze aur facial swelling anaphylaxis suggest kar sakte hain. Airway aur circulation compromise ka risk immediate priority banata hai.

**Baaki options kyun sahi nahi hain?** Routine hygiene, teaching aur meal choice important hain lekin airway emergency ke baad address kiye ja sakte hain.

</details>

### Q2. Which patient should the nurse assess first after receiving handover?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Priority:** High

- **A.** A patient with longstanding unchanged mild constipation
- **B.** A patient with a new drop in consciousness and unequal pupils after a fall
- **C.** A stable patient waiting for a routine dressing change
- **D.** A patient requesting a medication information leaflet

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: B. A patient with a new drop in consciousness and unequal pupils after a fall**

New consciousness change aur unequal pupils intracranial injury ka warning sign ho sakte hain. Nurse urgent neurological aur ABC assessment karti hai.

**Baaki options kyun sahi nahi hain?** Routine dressing, information aur unchanged constipation immediate neurological threat se lower priority hain.

</details>

### Q3. A patient is receiving an IV infusion and reports burning at the cannula site. The site is red and warm along the vein, without cool swelling. What should the nurse suspect?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Priority:** High

- **A.** Phlebitis requiring assessment and appropriate line management
- **B.** Infiltration as the only possible complication
- **C.** A normal finding that always needs no intervention
- **D.** A pulmonary embolism proven by local redness

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: A. Phlebitis requiring assessment and appropriate line management**

Vein ke along redness, warmth aur tenderness inflammation suggest karte hain. Nurse infusion-site assessment aur management protocol follow karti hai.

**Baaki options kyun sahi nahi hain?** Infiltration often cool swelling deti hai. New redness normal assume nahi karni chahiye. Local finding PE prove nahi karti.

</details>

### Q4. During a medication round, a patient has a newly low blood pressure and is due for an antihypertensive. What is the safest first step?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Priority:** High

- **A.** Give the medication before checking the reading
- **B.** Skip every future dose permanently
- **C.** Give an unprescribed stimulant to raise BP
- **D.** Assess symptoms and current BP, review hold parameters and clarify the dose as needed

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: D. Assess symptoms and current BP, review hold parameters and clarify the dose as needed**

Low BP mein antihypertensive further hypotension cause kar sakti hai. Nurse current assessment aur authorized hold instructions verify karti hai.

**Baaki options kyun sahi nahi hain?** Blind administration unsafe hai. Permanent cessation aur unapproved stimulant inappropriate hain.

</details>

### Q5. A patient with a feeding tube is due for medicines, but initial placement has not been confirmed. What is the priority?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Priority:** High

- **A.** Give a small test amount of water by tube
- **B.** Auscultate injected air and use the tube regardless of the result
- **C.** Use the tube because the external marking is visible
- **D.** Withhold tube use and obtain approved placement confirmation

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: D. Withhold tube use and obtain approved placement confirmation**

Unconfirmed tube respiratory tract mein ho sakti hai. Feed, water ya medicines introduce karne se severe injury ho sakti hai, isliye validated confirmation required hai.

**Baaki options kyun sahi nahi hain?** Test water bhi unsafe ho sakta hai. Whoosh aur external marking alone position prove nahi karte.

</details>

### Q6. A patient with a urinary catheter has new suprapubic pain and low drainage. Which is the best initial nursing assessment?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Priority:** High

- **A.** Administer a diuretic without review
- **B.** Assume all low drainage is caused by renal failure
- **C.** Check tubing, bladder distension and patient vital status before escalating appropriately
- **D.** Increase balloon volume immediately

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: C. Check tubing, bladder distension and patient vital status before escalating appropriately**

Obstruction, kink ya low urine production possible causes hain. Nurse simple device checks aur patient assessment se cause identify karne mein help karti hai.

**Baaki options kyun sahi nahi hain?** Balloon increase trauma cause kar sakta hai. Blind diuretic aur renal failure assumption inappropriate hain.

</details>

### Q7. A patient receiving blood develops fever and shaking chills, but the nurse is unsure whether the symptoms are transfusion-related. What is the safest action?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Priority:** High

- **D.** Give the next unit before investigating

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

Transfusion reaction confirm hone se pehle further exposure stop karna important hai. Nurse assessment aur blood bank-clinician notification karti hai.

**Baaki options kyun sahi nahi hain?** Continuing ya next unit harm badha sakte hain. Nonspecific fever bhi context mein important hai.

</details>

### Q9. A patient is confused, pulling at an IV and attempting to climb out of bed. Which intervention should be considered before routine restraint use?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Priority:** High

- **A.** Assess reversible causes and use supervision, orientation and other least restrictive alternatives
- **B.** Apply restraints solely for staff convenience
- **C.** Give an unprescribed sedative
- **D.** Leave the patient alone behind raised rails

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: A. Assess reversible causes and use supervision, orientation and other least restrictive alternatives**

Confusion pain, infection, medication ya toileting need se related ho sakti hai. Nurse cause aur alternatives assess karke safety support karti hai.

**Baaki options kyun sahi nahi hain?** Routine restraint aur unapproved sedation harm kar sakte hain. Rails alone supervision replace nahi karte.

</details>

### Q10. A nurse is preparing a sterile dressing and a patient suddenly develops severe respiratory distress. What takes priority?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Priority:** High

- **A.** Complete the sterile dressing before assessing breathing
- **B.** Ask the patient to wait quietly
- **C.** Continue care without notifying anyone
- **D.** Attend to breathing and summon help, abandoning or replacing the sterile setup as needed

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: D. Attend to breathing and summon help, abandoning or replacing the sterile setup as needed**

Airway-breathing emergency planned procedure se higher priority hai. Contaminated sterile setup later replace ki ja sakti hai.

**Baaki options kyun sahi nahi hain?** Dressing completion aur delay respiratory deterioration worsen kar sakte hain.

</details>

### Q12. A patient with severe pain is prescribed opioid analgesia but is currently difficult to arouse with a respiratory rate of 8/min. Which response is best?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Priority:** High

- **A.** Wait until the next scheduled pain check
- **B.** Prioritize airway and breathing assessment and urgent opioid safety management
- **C.** Give the opioid because pain is severe
- **D.** Ask the patient to drink water before assessment

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: B. Prioritize airway and breathing assessment and urgent opioid safety management**

Slow breathing aur reduced alertness respiratory depression suggest kar sakte hain. Nurse additional opioid hold karke urgent support aur review arrange karti hai.

**Baaki options kyun sahi nahi hain?** More opioid depression worsen kar sakti hai. Oral intake aspiration risk hai. Delay unsafe hai.

</details>

### Q13. A patient's glucose is low and the patient is confused and unable to swallow safely. Which action is correct?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Priority:** High

- **A.** Wait until a family member arrives to decide
- **B.** Initiate urgent hypoglycemia protocol using an appropriate nonoral treatment route
- **C.** Offer sweet juice orally regardless of swallowing ability
- **D.** Give the scheduled rapid-acting insulin

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: B. Initiate urgent hypoglycemia protocol using an appropriate nonoral treatment route**

Brain ko glucose continuously chahiye. Unsafe swallow mein oral treatment aspiration cause kar sakti hai. Nurse emergency protocol aur nonoral therapy as indicated use karti hai.

**Baaki options kyun sahi nahi hain?** Juice unsafe ho sakta hai. Insulin glucose aur reduce karegi. Family ka wait treatment delay karta hai.

</details>

### Q15. A bedbound patient's sacral skin is intact but nonblanchable discoloration has appeared. Which plan is best?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Priority:** High

- **A.** Massage the affected skin vigorously
- **B.** Wait for an open ulcer before changing care
- **C.** Keep the patient on the affected area to test tolerance
- **D.** Relieve pressure, document the assessment and revise the individualized prevention plan

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: D. Relieve pressure, document the assessment and revise the individualized prevention plan**

Intact nonblanchable change early pressure injury indicate kar sakti hai. Offloading aur prevention review further damage reduce karne mein help karte hain.

**Baaki options kyun sahi nahi hain?** Massage tissue damage kar sakti hai. Open wound ka wait aur continued pressure unsafe hain.

</details>

### Q16. A patient returns from a procedure with new oxygen requirements and a pending urgent laboratory test. What is the safest nursing action?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Priority:** High

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

### Q19. Two urgent tasks compete: a stable patient needs scheduled oral medication and another has new stridor. What is the best prioritization?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Priority:** High

- **A.** Assess the patient with stridor immediately and arrange coverage for the medication task
- **B.** Give the scheduled medication first because it is due
- **C.** Ask the stridor patient to wait for routine rounds
- **D.** Cancel every medication dose for the entire ward

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: A. Assess the patient with stridor immediately and arrange coverage for the medication task**

Stridor possible airway obstruction ka clue hai. Immediate assessment aur help priority hai, while delegation se scheduled medication timely manage ki ja sakti hai.

**Baaki options kyun sahi nahi hain?** Due-time task airway threat se higher priority nahi hai. Waiting aur indiscriminate cancellation inappropriate hain.

</details>

### Q20. A nurse reassesses an intervention and finds the patient has deteriorated rather than met the goal. Which approach demonstrates clinical judgment?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Priority:** High

- **A.** Continue the original plan regardless of findings
- **B.** Record that the goal was achieved
- **C.** Wait for the next shift to notice the deterioration
- **D.** Recognize the change, reassess priorities, escalate as indicated and revise the plan

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: D. Recognize the change, reassess priorities, escalate as indicated and revise the plan**

Nursing process dynamic hai aur patient status change hone par priorities adjust karni hoti hain. Deterioration prompt reassessment aur appropriate escalation maangti hai.

**Baaki options kyun sahi nahi hain?** Ineffective plan continue karna, false documentation aur delay patient safety compromise karte hain.

</details>
