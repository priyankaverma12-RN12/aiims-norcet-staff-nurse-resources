### Q1. An authorized prescription requires 375 mg. Suitable whole tablets of 250 mg and 125 mg are available. Which combination delivers the dose?

**Difficulty:** 🟡 Medium · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD · Calculation · Original practice

A. One 250-mg tablet and one 125-mg tablet
B. Two 250-mg tablets
C. Three 250-mg tablets
D. Two 125-mg tablets

<details>
<summary>✅ Sahi answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct answer: A. One 250-mg tablet and one 125-mg tablet**

**Formula, steps aur reverse check:** 250 + 125 = 375 mg. Reverse: 375 − 250 = 125 mg.

- **A (Correct):** Dono tablets exact 375 mg deti hain.
- **B (Incorrect):** 2 × 250 = 500 mg, 125 mg zyada.
- **C (Incorrect):** 3 × 250 = 750 mg, double.
- **D (Incorrect):** 2 × 125 = 250 mg, 125 mg kam.

**Exam clue:** Har tablet ki strength individually check karo.
