D. Two 125-mg tablets

<details>

- **A (Correct):** 250 + 125 = 375 mg. Dono strengths verify karo, kyunki similar labels selection error cause kar sakte hain.
- **B (Incorrect):** 500 mg hoga, jo 125 mg zyada hai.
- **C (Incorrect):** 750 mg hoga, jo double dose hai.
