### Q1. Which ethical principle supports a competent patient's right to accept or refuse treatment?

**Difficulty:** 🟡 Medium · **Priority:** 🟡 IMPORTANT · Original practice · **🟣 AIIMS Raipur Nursing Officer, 9 September 2017 — related reported concept**

- **A.** Nonmaleficence alone
- **B.** Fidelity alone
- **C.** Autonomy
- **D.** Justice alone

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: C. Autonomy**

**A kyun galat hai:** Nonmaleficence mein nurse patient ko avoidable harm se bachati hai. Yeh safety ka principle hai, lekin treatment choose karne ka haq define nahi karta.

**B kyun galat hai:** Fidelity ka matlab hai ki nurse apni professional commitments nibhaye. Yeh trust banata hai, lekin consent ya refusal ka principle nahi hai.

**C kyun sahi hai:** Autonomy ka matlab hai ki patient apne treatment ke baare mein khud faisla kar sake. Jab patient relevant information samajhkar bina pressure ke decision leta hai, toh nurse uski choice ka respect karti hai, zaroori clarification deti hai aur decision document karti hai.

**D kyun galat hai:** Justice care aur resources ke fair allocation se related hai. Individual ki informed choice ko specifically autonomy protect karti hai.

**Exam clue:** Consent ya refusal ki baat ho, toh autonomy yaad rakho.

### Q2. Which ethical principle refers to acting for the patient's benefit?

**Difficulty:** 🟡 Medium · **Priority:** 🟡 IMPORTANT · Original practice

- **A.** Confidentiality
- **B.** Beneficence
- **C.** Autonomy
- **D.** Veracity

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: B. Beneficence**

**A kyun galat hai:** Confidentiality mein nurse patient ki private information ko unauthorized disclosure se bachati hai. Yeh information protection hai, welfare ke liye action lene wale principle ka naam nahi hai.

**B kyun sahi hai:** Beneficence ka matlab hai ki nurse patient ki bhalai ke liye meaningful action le. Jaise pressure injury ka risk ho, toh nurse patient ke saath safe repositioning plan banati hai. Benefit ke saath risk aur informed preference bhi consider ki jaati hai.

**C kyun galat hai:** Autonomy patient ke khud decision lene ke haq se related hai. Nurse ko beneficence ke naam par capable patient par apna decision thopna nahi chahiye.

**D kyun galat hai:** Veracity ka matlab hai ki nurse sach aur accurate information de. Yeh honesty ka principle hai, jabki beneficence patient ki bhalai par focus karti hai.

**Exam clue:** Beneficence bhalai karne se aur nonmaleficence avoidable harm rokne se related hai.

### Q3. Which principle is illustrated by avoiding an unnecessary invasive procedure that may injure a patient?

- **A.** Nonmaleficence
- **B.** Justice alone
- **C.** Fidelity alone
- **D.** Veracity alone

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: A. Nonmaleficence**

**A kyun sahi hai:** Nonmaleficence ka matlab hai ki nurse avoidable harm ko roke. Bina clear indication ke invasive procedure se pain, bleeding ya infection ka risk badh sakta hai. Nurse indication clarify karti hai aur concern appropriate clinician ko batati hai.

**B kyun galat hai:** Justice mein nurse patients ke saath fair treatment aur fair resource allocation support karti hai. Unnecessary injury ko avoid karna specifically nonmaleficence hai.

**C kyun galat hai:** Fidelity mein nurse apni commitments nibhati hai. Sirf commitment nibhane se procedure ka clinical benefit ya safety establish nahi hoti.

**D kyun galat hai:** Veracity mein nurse procedure ke risks honestly samjhati hai. Lekin procedure se hone wale unnecessary harm ko rokna nonmaleficence hai.

**Exam clue:** Do no harm ka clue mile, toh nonmaleficence yaad rakho.

### Q4. Two patients need the same limited clinical resource. Which ethical principle guides fair allocation based on relevant criteria?

**Difficulty:** 🔴 Difficult · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD · Original practice · **🟣 RRB Nursing Superintendent, 29 April 2025 Shift III — related reported concept**

- **A.** Favoritism
- **B.** Confidentiality alone
- **C.** Personal convenience
- **D.** Justice

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: D. Justice**

**A kyun galat hai:** Favoritism mein kisi patient ko personal pasand ya relationship ki wajah se preference milti hai. Yeh clinical need ko ignore kar sakta hai, isliye fair allocation ka basis nahi hai.

**B kyun galat hai:** Confidentiality patient ki sensitive information ko protect karti hai. Limited resource kisko diya jaye, iske liye fair clinical criteria chahiye.

**C kyun galat hai:** Personal convenience staff ka kaam aasaan bana sakti hai, lekin sirf convenience ke basis par resource dene se patient ki actual need miss ho sakti hai.

**D kyun sahi hai:** Justice ka matlab hai ki resources fair aur relevant criteria ke basis par allocate hon. Team established clinical priorities, urgency aur applicable policy ko follow karti hai, na ki personal favoritism ko.

**Exam clue:** Fair allocation hamesha identical treatment nahi hota, kyunki patients ki clinical needs alag ho sakti hain.

### Q5. Which action demonstrates veracity in nursing?

**Difficulty:** 🟡 Medium · **Priority:** 🟡 IMPORTANT · Original practice

- **A.** Promise guaranteed recovery without evidence
- **B.** Hide every adverse effect from the patient
- **C.** Invent an explanation to avoid questions
- **D.** Provide truthful information within the nurse's role and clarify uncertainty

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: D. Provide truthful information within the nurse's role and clarify uncertainty**

**A kyun galat hai:** Jab outcome uncertain ho, toh guaranteed recovery ka vaada misleading hota hai. Nurse realistic support deti hai, lekin unsupported assurance nahi deti.

**B kyun galat hai:** Relevant adverse effects ko chhupane se patient informed decision nahi le payega. Nurse risks apne role ke andar explain karti hai aur detailed questions appropriate clinician ko refer karti hai.

**C kyun galat hai:** Invented explanation se patient galat decision le sakta hai. Agar nurse ko answer nahi pata, toh woh honestly batakar correct information verify karti hai.

**D kyun sahi hai:** Veracity ka matlab hai sach aur accurate information dena. Nurse apne scope ke andar clear jawab deti hai, uncertainty ko accept karti hai aur zaroorat par treating team se clarification leti hai.

**Exam clue:** Veracity sach bolne se aur fidelity vaada nibhane se related hai.

**Correct: B. Arrange assessment of decision-making capacity and follow applicable consent and emergency policies**

**A kyun galat hai:** Patient ki wishes ko poori tarah ignore karna sahi nahi hai. Capacity impaired ho tab bhi nurse patient ko jitna possible ho discussion mein involve karti hai aur known preferences team ko batati hai.

Acute delirium mein yeh ability temporarily affect ho sakti hai. **C kyun galat hai:** Adult hone par capacity initially presume ki jaati hai, lekin significant confusion ho toh assessment ki zaroorat pad sakti hai. Capacity har decision aur har time par ek jaisi nahi hoti.

**D kyun galat hai:** Har visitor authorized decision-maker nahi hota. Nurse applicable law aur policy ke mutabik representative identify karne mein team ki madad karti hai, aur urgent needs ko emergency safeguards ke saath handle karti hai.

**D kyun galat hai:** Documentation assessment ko record karne mein help karti hai. Yeh learning domain nahi hai aur actual injection technique assess karne ka substitute nahi hai.

### Q15. A patient reports using a traditional herbal product along with prescribed anticoagulants. What should the nurse do?

**A kyun galat hai:** Herbal product ki information team se chhupane se medication history incomplete ho jaati hai. Isse possible interaction ya bleeding risk miss ho sakta hai.

**B kyun galat hai:** Natural product ka label harmless hone ki guarantee nahi hai. Kuch supplements drug effect badal sakte hain, lekin actual risk particular product aur medicine par depend karta hai.

**C kyun galat hai:** Prescribed anticoagulant independently band karne se clot banne ka risk badh sakta hai. Nurse urgent concerns prescriber ko batati hai aur authorized orders ya safety protocol ke mutabik action leti hai.

**D kyun sahi hai:** Nurse bina judgment ke herbal product ka naam, ingredients, dose aur timing poochti hai aur exact anticoagulant verify karti hai. Kuch combinations bleeding risk badha sakte hain ya drug effect badal sakte hain. Nurse pharmacist aur prescriber se interaction review karwati hai aur bleeding symptoms ko promptly escalate karti hai.

**Exam clue:** Herbal products bhi medication history ka hissa hain, aur interaction product-specific assess karna chahiye.

### Q16. What is health literacy in the context of nursing education?

**Difficulty:** 🟡 Medium · **Priority:** 🟡 IMPORTANT · Original practice

- **B.** Ability to read the hospital name

**D kyun galat hai:** Medical terminology recite karna practical understanding ka proof nahi hai. Patient ko ghar par kya karna hai aur kab help leni hai, yeh samajhna zyada important hai.

### Q17. A nurse has a conflict of interest in recommending a paid product to a patient. Which action is appropriate?

**Difficulty:** 🔴 Difficult · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD · Original practice

- **B.** Pressure the patient to buy immediately

**B kyun galat hai:** Immediately purchase karne ka pressure patient ki voluntary informed choice ko undermine karta hai. Nurse relevant options ko fairly explain karti hai.

### Q18. Which nursing action reflects accountability?

- **B.** Accept responsibility for assigned care and accurately report actions and errors

- **D.** Record procedures that were not performed

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: B. Accept responsibility for assigned care and accurately report actions and errors**

**B kyun sahi hai:** Accountability ka matlab hai ki nurse apne assigned care, decisions aur actions ki responsibility leti hai. Agar error ho, toh nurse patient ki immediate safety assess karti hai, appropriate team ko inform karti hai aur facts policy ke mutabik accurately document aur report karti hai.

**D kyun galat hai:** Jo procedure perform nahi hua usko completed record karna false documentation hai. Isse future clinical decisions galat ho sakte hain aur professional accountability violate hoti hai.

**Exam clue:** Accountability mein apne actions ki responsibility lena, competence maintain karna aur errors appropriately report karna shamil hai.

### Q20. Which teaching strategy is most appropriate when the patient is exhausted and unable to concentrate?

**Difficulty:** 🟡 Medium · **Priority:** 🟡 IMPORTANT · Original practice

- **A.** Deliver a long lecture regardless of readiness
- **B.** Label the patient noncompliant
- **C.** Require memorization immediately
- **D.** Address immediate needs and reschedule nonurgent education for a more suitable time

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: D. Address immediate needs and reschedule nonurgent education for a more suitable time**

**A kyun galat hai:** Exhausted patient ko long lecture dene se important information miss ho sakti hai. Nurse teaching ko readiness aur concentration ke hisaab se adapt karti hai.

**B kyun galat hai:** Patient ko noncompliant label karne se fatigue ka actual barrier address nahi hota. Nurse bina judgment ke pain, sleep, symptoms aur understanding assess karti hai.

**D kyun sahi hai:** Nurse pehle immediate needs aur exhaustion ka reason assess karti hai. Essential safety instructions short aur clear rakhti hai aur understanding check karti hai. Nonurgent education ko aise time reschedule karti hai jab patient alert aur ready ho.

**Exam clue:** Teaching ka timing readiness par depend karta hai, lekin urgent safety information unnecessarily postpone nahi karni chahiye.
