### Q01. A nurse finds that the raised backrest of an adjustable hospital bed is at 20 degrees above horizontal. The care plan requires a 35-degree backrest elevation. Which adjustment is needed?

**Difficulty:** 🟡 Medium · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD · **Evidence:** Original practice · **Exam focus:** NORCET / Staff Nurse · **Topic:** Measured elevation

- **A.** Lower the backrest by 15 degrees
- **B.** Increase the backrest angle by 15 degrees
- **C.** Tilt the entire bed by 35 degrees without changing the backrest
- **D.** Increase the backrest angle by 35 degrees

<details><summary>✅ Correct answer and detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: B. Increase the backrest angle by 15 degrees**

**A. Kyun galat hai:** 15 degree lower karne se angle 5 degree reh jaayega. Ye target se door hai.

**B. Kyun sahi hai:** Abhi backrest horizontal se 20 degree upar hai aur target 35 degree hai. Difference 35 minus 20 yani 15 degree hai. Angle adjust karke dobara verify karo.

**C. Kyun galat hai:** Whole-bed tilt aur backrest elevation alag adjustments hain. Bed ko tilt karna prescribed backrest angle ka automatic substitute nahi hai.

**D. Kyun galat hai:** Agar 35 degree aur add karoge toh total 55 degree ho jaayega. Prescribed 35 degree achieve nahi hoga.

**Exam clue:** Target angle aur change in angle ko alag samjho.

### Q02. A patient has been placed in a 30-degree lateral tilt for pressure redistribution. On inspection, the sacrum still bears weight because of the patient's body shape. What is the best next action?

**Difficulty:** 🔴 Difficult · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD · **Evidence:** Original practice · **Exam focus:** NORCET / Staff Nurse · **Topic:** Individualized lateral tilt

- **A.** Replace the tilt with direct 90-degree loading over the greater trochanter
- **B.** Increase the frequency of the same ineffective turn without changing support
- **C.** Maintain the exact angle regardless of the observed contact
- **D.** Individualize the tilt and support so that the sacrum and trochanter are offloaded

<details><summary>✅ Correct answer and detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: D. Individualize the tilt and support so that the sacrum and trochanter are offloaded**

**A. Kyun galat hai:** Direct 90-degree side-lying se greater trochanter par concentrated load aa sakta hai. Pressure ek bony area se doosre par shift karna desirable result nahi hai.

**B. Kyun galat hai:** Frequency important hai, lekin agar har turn mein sacrum par load reh raha hai toh positioning technique ko correct karna bhi zaroori hai.

**C. Kyun galat hai:** Angle ek guide hai, success ka sole measure nahi. Actual tissue offloading verify karna zaroori hai.

**D. Kyun sahi hai:** International guideline mein 30-degree lateral position conditional suggestion hai. Body shape aur tissue contact ke hisaab se angle aur support adjust karke sacrum aur hip ke bony prominence ka pressure kam karna chahiye.

**Exam clue:** Prescribed position ka actual mechanical effect check karo.

**C. Kyun galat hai:** Fixed schedule ko mechanically follow karna individualized goals ko ignore karta hai. Full turn tolerate nahi ho raha toh plan review karo.

**D. Kyun galat hai:** Palliative care mein comfort aur skin assessment dono relevant rehte hain. Assessment se painful pressure points identify karke suitable relief diya ja sakta hai.
