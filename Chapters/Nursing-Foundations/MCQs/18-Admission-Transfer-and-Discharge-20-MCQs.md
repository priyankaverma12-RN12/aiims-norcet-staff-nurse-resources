# Admission, transfer and discharge — 20 original MCQs

**NORCET-style and Indian staff nurse practice. Unattempted.** Difficulty and priority are editorial estimates. These are not official PYQs. [Chapter](../README.md)

### Q1. When should discharge planning generally begin for an admitted patient?

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Priority:** Medium

- **A.** Only when transportation arrives
- **B.** After all medical records are archived
- **C.** At admission and continue throughout the stay
- **D.** Only after the patient leaves

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: C. At admission and continue throughout the stay**

Discharge needs early identify karne se teaching, equipment, follow-up aur family support arrange karne ka time milta hai. Plan condition ke saath update hota hai.

**Baaki options kyun sahi nahi hain?** Late planning avoidable delays aur unsafe transitions cause kar sakti hai.

</details>

### Q2. A new admission arrives with severe breathlessness and hypotension. Which action takes priority over routine admission paperwork?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Priority:** High

- **A.** Provide the ward orientation booklet first
- **B.** Immediate clinical assessment and stabilization with escalation
- **C.** Complete the entire demographic form first
- **D.** Inventory belongings before checking vital signs

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: B. Immediate clinical assessment and stabilization with escalation**

Breathing aur circulation compromise time-sensitive ho sakta hai. Nurse ABC assessment aur urgent help arrange karti hai. Administrative tasks baad mein complete ho sakte hain.

**Baaki options kyun sahi nahi hain?** Paperwork, inventory aur orientation emergency stabilization se lower priority hain.

</details>

### Q3. Which information is essential during medication reconciliation at admission?

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Priority:** Medium

- **A.** Actual current prescription, OTC and relevant supplement use including doses and timing
- **B.** Only medications available on the ward shelf
- **C.** Only the patient's favorite medicine color
- **D.** Only the name of the most recent hospital

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: A. Actual current prescription, OTC and relevant supplement use including doses and timing**

Complete medication history interactions, omissions aur duplicates identify karne mein help karti hai. Nurse patient, caregiver aur records se verify kar sakti hai.

**Baaki options kyun sahi nahi hain?** Shelf list aur colors actual use establish nahi karte. Hospital name alone medication list nahi hai.

</details>

### Q4. A patient is transferred from ICU to a general ward with a newly changed oxygen target. What is a key handover requirement?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Priority:** High

- **A.** Report only that the patient was in ICU
- **B.** Leave the old oxygen prescription as the only reference
- **C.** Assume the receiving ward uses identical targets for everyone
- **D.** Communicate the current target, device, recent trends and escalation instructions

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: D. Communicate the current target, device, recent trends and escalation instructions**

Transfer ke waqt current respiratory plan clearly communicate karna important hai. Receiving nurse target aur deterioration signs samajhkar monitoring continue kar sakti hai.

**Baaki options kyun sahi nahi hain?** ICU location clinical plan nahi batati. Old order outdated ho sakta hai. Universal targets inappropriate ho sakte hain.

</details>

### Q5. Which practice improves patient orientation after admission?

</details>

### Q6. A patient has a new ostomy and is being discharged tomorrow but cannot demonstrate appliance care. What should the nurse do?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Priority:** High

- **A.** Cancel follow-up because the patient can read
- **B.** Tell the patient to improvise without equipment
- **C.** Assess barriers and arrange further teaching and necessary discharge support
- **D.** Mark teaching complete because a leaflet was provided

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: C. Assess barriers and arrange further teaching and necessary discharge support**

Safe home care ke liye practical skill important hai. Nurse teach-back, demonstration aur caregiver or specialist support coordinate kar sakti hai.

**Baaki options kyun sahi nahi hain?** Leaflet alone competence prove nahi karta. Follow-up useful hai. Improvised care leakage aur skin injury cause kar sakti hai.

</details>

### Q7. Which document element is essential for safe discharge medication instructions?

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Priority:** Medium

- **A.** Only the date of admission
- **B.** Clear drug name, dose, route, frequency and relevant changes
- **C.** Only the color of each tablet
- **D.** Unexplained abbreviations alone

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: B. Clear drug name, dose, route, frequency and relevant changes**

Patient ko ghar par medicine ka correct schedule pata hona chahiye. Clear instructions aur changes explain karne se dosing errors kam ho sakte hain.

**Baaki options kyun sahi nahi hain?** Color unreliable identification hai. Abbreviations confuse kar sakti hain. Admission date medicine plan nahi batati.

</details>

### Q8. A patient is medically ready for discharge but has no safe means of obtaining essential medication. What is the appropriate nursing response?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Priority:** High

- **A.** Escalate the barrier to the discharge team and coordinate a feasible plan
- **B.** Discharge without mentioning the issue
- **C.** Advise the patient to stop all prescribed medication
- **D.** Provide another patient's medication supply

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: A. Escalate the barrier to the discharge team and coordinate a feasible plan**

Access barrier treatment interruption cause kar sakta hai. Nurse social work, pharmacy aur team ke saath safe continuity plan arrange karti hai.

**Baaki options kyun sahi nahi hain?** Omission aur medication stopping unsafe hain. Another patient's medicine transfer inappropriate hai.

</details>

### Q9. Which transfer action protects the continuity of care for a patient with an active IV infusion?

Transfer mein line interruption ya rate error ho sakta hai. Nurse infusion details aur monitoring needs receiving team ko communicate karti hai.

**Baaki options kyun sahi nahi hain?** Automatic disconnection treatment interrupt kar sakti hai. Label removal verification reduce karti hai. Pump management trained staff ka role hai.

</details>

### Q10. A patient chooses to leave before recommended treatment is complete. What should the nurse do?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Priority:** High

- **A.** Physically detain every competent patient by default
- **B.** Erase the treatment recommendation from the record
- **C.** Refuse to provide safety instructions
- **D.** Assess capacity and immediate risk, inform the team, explain risks and document according to policy

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

Competent patient informed decision le sakta hai. Nurse capacity concerns, risks, alternatives aur follow-up discuss karne ke liye team involve karti hai.

**Baaki options kyun sahi nahi hain?** Routine detention autonomy violate kar sakti hai. Record deletion aur safety teaching refusal harmful hain.

</details>

### Q12. A patient with an indwelling catheter will be discharged with the device temporarily. Which skill should be confirmed?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Priority:** High

- **C.** Routine disconnection of the system several times daily
- **D.** Keeping the bag above the bladder during sleep

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

Patient ko closed drainage aur bag below bladder maintain karna sikhana important hai. Nurse infection, blockage aur removal follow-up signs explain karti hai.

**Baaki options kyun sahi nahi hain?** Routine disconnection, elevated bag aur unclean container infection risk badhate hain.

</details>

### Q13. Why should patients receive explicit warning signs and follow-up contact information at discharge?

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Priority:** Medium

- **C.** All complications can be prevented by a leaflet alone
- **D.** Follow-up is unnecessary after hospital treatment

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

Discharge ke baad patient ko new symptoms identify aur help access karni hoti hai. Clear return precautions delayed treatment risk reduce kar sakte hain.

**Baaki options kyun sahi nahi hain?** Leaflet complications guarantee se prevent nahi karti. Follow-up condition-specific important hai. Aim safety hai, anxiety nahi.

</details>

</details>

### Q20. A patient returns from another department with a new procedure-related restriction not reflected in the ward care plan. What should the nurse do?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Priority:** High

- **A.** Continue the outdated plan without clarification
- **B.** Assume the restriction applies only to other departments
- **C.** Ask the patient to disregard it if comfortable
- **D.** Verify the updated restriction, modify nursing care and communicate it to the team

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: D. Verify the updated restriction, modify nursing care and communicate it to the team**

Procedures ke baad activity, diet ya device care instructions change ho sakti hain. Nurse current authorized instructions reconcile karke care update karti hai.

**Baaki options kyun sahi nahi hain?** Outdated plan aur assumptions complication risk badha sakte hain. Comfort restriction override nahi karta.

</details>
