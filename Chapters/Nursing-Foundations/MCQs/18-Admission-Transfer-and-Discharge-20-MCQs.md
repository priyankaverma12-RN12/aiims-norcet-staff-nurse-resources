### Q1. When should discharge planning generally begin for an admitted patient?

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Priority:** Medium

- **A.** Only when transportation arrives
- **B.** After all medical records are archived
- **C.** At admission and continue throughout the stay
- **D.** Only after the patient leaves

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: C. At admission and continue throughout the stay**

Discharge planning ka matlab hospital se ghar ya next care setting mein safe transition ki preparation hai. Admission ke time se patient ki mobility, home support, medicines, equipment aur teaching needs assess karo. Condition aur goals change hone par plan update karo. Actual discharge ka decision authorized team clinical readiness aur practical safety ke hisaab se leti hai.

**Option-wise explanation:**
- **A (Galat):** Transport aane tak wait karne se teaching aur equipment arrange karne ka time nahi mil sakta.
- **B (Galat):** Medical records archive karna discharge planning start karne ka clinical criterion nahi hai.
- **C (Sahi):** Admission se planning start karne par barriers early identify hote hain aur poore stay mein address kiye ja sakte hain.
- **D (Galat):** Patient ke leave karne ke baad planning start karna preventable transition problems ko timely address nahi karta.

**Exam clue:** Discharge planning starts at admission, not discharge day.

### Q2. A new admission arrives with severe breathlessness and hypotension. Which action takes priority over routine admission paperwork?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Priority:** High

- **A.** Provide the ward orientation booklet first
- **B.** Immediate clinical assessment and stabilization with escalation
- **C.** Complete the entire demographic form first
- **D.** Inventory belongings before checking vital signs

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: B. Immediate clinical assessment and stabilization with escalation**

Severe breathlessness ka matlab ventilation ya oxygenation compromise ho sakti hai. Hypotension low blood pressure hai jo inadequate circulation ka clue ho sakta hai. Nurse immediate ABCDE assessment karti hai, urgent help bulati hai aur scope/protocol ke according stabilization support karti hai. Life-threatening problems ko milte hi address karo. Paperwork clinical care ke baad complete ho sakta hai.

**Option-wise explanation:**
- **A (Galat):** Orientation useful hai, lekin acute respiratory aur circulatory compromise ke waqt first priority nahi hai.
- **B (Sahi):** Immediate assessment, stabilization aur escalation patient ke time-sensitive threats ko address karte hain.
- **C (Galat):** Demographic form complete karte hue treatment delay ho sakti hai.
- **D (Galat):** Belongings inventory important hai, lekin unstable patient ke airway, breathing aur circulation se pehle nahi.

**Exam clue:** Unstable admission: ABCDE and urgent help before administrative work.

### Q3. Which information is essential during medication reconciliation at admission?

- **D (Galat):** Previous hospital ka naam complete medication history replace nahi karta.

### Q19. Which nursing action respects patient participation during discharge planning?

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Priority:** Medium

- **A.** Include the patient and chosen support persons with consent in care discussions
- **B.** Make all decisions without asking the patient
- **C.** Inform relatives regardless of confidentiality
- **D.** Ignore cultural or practical preferences

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: A. Include the patient and chosen support persons with consent in care discussions**

Patient-centered discharge mein patient ki goals, preferences, home situation aur practical barriers consider kiye jaate hain. Patient ki permission se chosen support person ko teaching aur planning mein include karo. Confidentiality maintain karo aur decisions mein patient participation support karo. Capacity aur applicable consent rules ke according appropriate surrogate involvement arrange karo if needed.

**Option-wise explanation:**
- **A (Sahi):** Patient aur consented support persons ko include karne se feasible aur understandable care plan develop ho sakta hai.
- **B (Galat):** Patient ko exclude karne se autonomy aur practical needs miss ho sakti hain.
- **C (Galat):** Confidential information relatives ko bina relevant consent ya legal basis disclose nahi karni chahiye.
- **D (Galat):** Cultural aur practical preferences treatment feasibility aur acceptance affect kar sakti hain.

**Exam clue:** Shared planning with consent and confidentiality.

### Q20. A patient returns from another department with a new procedure-related restriction not reflected in the ward care plan. What should the nurse do?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Priority:** High

- **A.** Continue the outdated plan without clarification
- **B.** Assume the restriction applies only to other departments
- **C.** Ask the patient to disregard it if comfortable
- **D.** Verify the updated restriction, modify nursing care and communicate it to the team

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: D. Verify the updated restriction, modify nursing care and communicate it to the team**

Procedure ke baad diet, activity, monitoring ya device-related restrictions change ho sakti hain. Nurse current authorized instruction verify karti hai, discrepancy clarify karti hai aur relevant nursing care plan update karti hai. Team aur patient ko change communicate karo. Restriction ka reason aur duration clear hona chahiye. Unclear order par assumption laga kar old plan continue mat karo.

**Option-wise explanation:**
- **A (Galat):** Outdated plan follow karne se procedure-specific complication ka risk ho sakta hai.
- **B (Galat):** Department change hone se restriction automatically expire nahi hoti.
- **C (Galat):** Patient comfortable feel kare toh bhi safety restriction ka clinical reason present ho sakta hai.
- **D (Sahi):** New instruction verify, care update aur team communication continuity of care maintain karte hain.

**Exam clue:** Reconcile post-procedure instructions before continuing care.
