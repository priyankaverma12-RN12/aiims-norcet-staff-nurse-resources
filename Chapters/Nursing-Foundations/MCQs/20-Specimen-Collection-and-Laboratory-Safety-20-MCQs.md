### Q1. Two unlabeled blood specimens are found beside different patients. What should the nurse do?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Priority:** High

- **A.** Follow rejection or recollection policy without guessing identity
- **B.** Label by nearest bed
- **C.** Combine them
- **D.** Ask the laboratory to guess

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: A. Follow rejection or recollection policy without guessing identity**

Wrong specimen identity wrong diagnosis aur treatment cause kar sakti hai. Nurse safe recollection arrange karti hai.

**Baaki options kyun sahi nahi hain?** Bed location identity proof nahi hai. Combining ya guessing result unreliable bana sakti hai.

</details>

### Q2. When should a specimen be labeled according to bedside identification policy?

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Priority:** Medium

- **A.** At the nursing station hours later
- **B.** Before identifying the patient
- **C.** After the result returns
- **D.** At the patient's side after collection with verified identifiers

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: D. At the patient's side after collection with verified identifiers**

Bedside labeling actual sample aur patient ka link preserve karti hai. Collection details bhi verify karne chahiye.

**Baaki options kyun sahi nahi hain?** Late labeling aur pre-identification labeling mix-up risk badhate hain. Result ke baad labeling too late hai.

</details>

### Q3. What reduces blood culture contamination?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Priority:** High

- **A.** Collect from any line without reviewing indication
- **B.** Leave bottle tops exposed
- **C.** Approved skin antisepsis and aseptic collection
- **D.** Palpate the prepared site with unclean fingers

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: C. Approved skin antisepsis and aseptic collection**

Skin organisms culture contaminate kar sakte hain. Correct antisepsis aur aseptic handling true infection detect karne mein help karti hai.

**Baaki options kyun sahi nahi hain?** Unclean contact, unreviewed line collection aur exposure contamination risk badhate hain.

</details>

### Q4. In suspected sepsis, when should ordered blood cultures be collected relative to urgent antibiotics?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Priority:** High

- **A.** Never when antibiotics are ordered
- **B.** Before antibiotics when feasible without meaningful treatment delay
- **C.** Several hours before antibiotics even if unstable
- **D.** Only after completing the antibiotic course

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: B. Before antibiotics when feasible without meaningful treatment delay**

Antibiotics culture yield reduce kar sakti hain. Pre-treatment culture useful hai, lekin urgent sepsis therapy delay nahi honi chahiye.

**Baaki options kyun sahi nahi hain?** Long delay harmful hai. Late-only collection yield affect karti hai. Culture clinically important ho sakti hai.

</details>

### Q5. Which additive is commonly used for a CBC sample?

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Priority:** Medium

- **A.** No additive with clot formation
- **B.** EDTA
- **C.** Sodium citrate as the usual CBC additive
- **D.** Sodium fluoride alone

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: B. EDTA**

EDTA calcium bind karke clotting prevent karti hai aur blood cell count ke liye sample suitable rakhti hai.

**Baaki options kyun sahi nahi hain?** Citrate coagulation tests aur fluoride glucose tests mein use ho sakti hain. Clotted CBC sample unsuitable ho sakta hai.

</details>

- **B.** EDTA as the standard

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

### Q7. Why can a markedly underfilled citrate tube affect coagulation tests?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Priority:** High

- **A.** It improves accuracy
- **B.** It prevents all calcium binding
- **C.** The ratio never affects the result
- **D.** Excess citrate relative to blood may prolong measured clotting time

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: D. Excess citrate relative to blood may prolong measured clotting time**

Blood volume kam ho toh citrate proportion zyada ho jaata hai. Extra calcium binding test ko distort kar sakti hai.

**Baaki options kyun sahi nahi hain?** Underfill accuracy improve nahi karti. Calcium binding citrate ka mechanism hai aur ratio relevant hai.

</details>

### Q8. How should a filled anticoagulant tube be mixed?

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Priority:** Medium

- **A.** Open and stir with a nonsterile stick
- **B.** Allow it to clot completely
- **C.** Gently invert it according to manufacturer directions
- **D.** Shake vigorously until foamy

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: C. Gently invert it according to manufacturer directions**

Gentle inversion additive ko blood ke saath distribute karti hai. Excess shaking cell damage cause kar sakti hai.

**Baaki options kyun sahi nahi hain?** Vigorous shaking hemolysis risk hai. Open stirring contamination aur clotting test error cause kar sakte hain.

</details>

### Q9. An unexpectedly high potassium result comes from a hemolyzed sample. What should be considered?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Priority:** High

- **A.** Hemolysis always causes low potassium
- **B.** No patient assessment is needed
- **C.** Pseudohyperkalemia from release of intracellular potassium
- **D.** Hemolysis proves true hyperkalemia

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: C. Pseudohyperkalemia from release of intracellular potassium**

RBC rupture se intracellular potassium sample mein release hota hai. Result falsely high ho sakta hai, lekin clinical status aur ECG assess karna zaroori hai.

**Baaki options kyun sahi nahi hain?** Hemolysis true disease prove nahi karti. Potassium falsely low hona typical nahi hai. Serious clinical concern ignore nahi karna chahiye.

</details>

### Q10. Why is a specimen collected near an active IV infusion potentially unreliable?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Priority:** High

- **A.** The blood becomes arterial
- **B.** Infused fluid may dilute or contaminate the sample
- **C.** IV fluid cannot alter any lab result
- **D.** Infused fluid always improves test accuracy

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: B. Infused fluid may dilute or contaminate the sample**

Saline, glucose ya medicines sample mein mix hokar measured values distort kar sakti hain. Approved sampling method use karna chahiye.

**Baaki options kyun sahi nahi hain?** IV contamination possible hai. Accuracy automatically improve nahi hoti. Venous blood arterial nahi banta.

</details>

### Q14. A wound culture is ordered for suspected infection. Which sampling approach is appropriate?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Priority:** High

- **A.** Sample unrelated intact skin
- **B.** Omit the site information
- **C.** Use the approved representative collection method after appropriate cleansing
- **D.** Swab the dried outer dressing only

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: C. Use the approved representative collection method after appropriate cleansing**

Surface contaminants actual wound organisms se different ho sakte hain. Representative sample aur site documentation result interpretation improve karte hain.

**Baaki options kyun sahi nahi hain?** Old dressing aur unrelated skin misleading organisms collect kar sakti hain. Site omission context lose karti hai.

</details>

### Q15. Which specimen requires prompt handling under laboratory transport instructions?

- **C.** Every specimen may wait indefinitely
- **D.** Only specimens with red labels

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

### Q16. Which handling method is appropriate for arterial blood gas collection?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Priority:** High

- **B.** Leave the syringe open to air
- **C.** Use a clotted serum tube

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

### Q17. Why is direct pressure applied after arterial sampling?

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Priority:** Medium

- **A.** Control puncture-site bleeding
- **B.** Increase bleeding
- **C.** Stop all limb circulation permanently
- **D.** Change arterial blood into venous blood

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: A. Control puncture-site bleeding**

Arterial pressure high hota hai, isliye adequate compression puncture-site bleeding reduce karti hai. Anticoagulation mein longer observation needed ho sakti hai.

**Baaki options kyun sahi nahi hain?** Bleeding increase desired nahi hai. Entire limb perfusion stop nahi karni chahiye. Blood type change nahi hota.

</details>

### Q20. A laboratory rejects a specimen because identifiers conflict with the order. What should happen?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Priority:** High

- **A.** Ask the patient to guess
- **B.** Investigate and recollect or correct only through verified policy
- **C.** Relabel by assumption
- **D.** Use the rejected result as definitive

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: B. Investigate and recollect or correct only through verified policy**

Mismatch wrong-patient specimen ka risk hai. Nurse approved verification aur recollection process follow karti hai.

**Baaki options kyun sahi nahi hain?** Assumed relabeling unsafe hai. Rejected result definitive nahi hai. Guessing identity verification replace nahi karti.

</details>
