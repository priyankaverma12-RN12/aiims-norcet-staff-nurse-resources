### Q1. Two unlabeled blood specimens are found beside different patients. What should the nurse do?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Priority:** High

- **A.** Follow rejection or recollection policy without guessing identity
- **B.** Label by nearest bed
- **C.** Combine them
- **D.** Ask the laboratory to guess

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: A. Follow rejection or recollection policy without guessing identity**

Specimen ka matlab patient se liya gaya sample hai. Agar sample ka patient se link prove nahi ho sakta toh result kisi galat patient ke record mein ja sakta hai. Dono specimens ko alag rakho, lab aur responsible team ko inform karo aur rejection ya safe recollection policy follow karo. Irreplaceable sample ke exceptional handling mein bhi authorized verified process chahiye.

**Option-wise explanation:**

- **A (Sahi):** Rejection ya recollection patient identity ka risk address karti hai. Nurse bina evidence ke label nahi lagati.
- **B (Galat):** Nearest bed sirf location batata hai. Patient kabhi bed change kar sakta hai.
- **C (Galat):** Dono samples milane se individual patient ka result identify nahi kiya ja sakta aur contamination ka risk hai.
- **D (Galat):** Laboratory bhi bina verified identity ke reliable patient assignment nahi kar sakti.

**Exam clue:** Unlabeled sample: guess mat karo, policy follow karo.

### Q3. What reduces blood culture contamination?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Priority:** High

- **A.** Collect from any line without reviewing indication
- **B.** Leave bottle tops exposed
- **C.** Approved skin antisepsis and aseptic collection
- **D.** Palpate the prepared site with unclean fingers

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

- **C (Sahi):** Skin antisepsis aur aseptic handling skin organisms ka sample mein transfer kam karte hain.
- **D (Galat):** Prepared site ko unclean fingers se touch karne se antisepsis ka benefit kam ho sakta hai.

**Exam clue:** Culture ka sample sterile handling se lo, skin flora ko sample mein mat jaane do.

### Q4. In suspected sepsis, when should ordered blood cultures be collected relative to urgent antibiotics?

- **C (Galat):** Fluoride glucose metabolism ko slow karne wale sample additives mein use ho sakta hai. PT/aPTT ke liye standard nahi.
- **D (Galat):** Clot activator serum formation promote karta hai. Coagulation test ke liye properly anticoagulated plasma chahiye.

**Exam clue:** PT/aPTT = sodium citrate, correct fill line.

### Q20. A laboratory rejects a specimen because identifiers conflict with the order. What should happen?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Priority:** High

- **A.** Ask the patient to guess
- **B.** Investigate and recollect or correct only through verified policy
- **C.** Relabel by assumption
- **D.** Use the rejected result as definitive

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: B. Investigate and recollect or correct only through verified policy**

Order aur specimen identifiers match na karein toh sample kisi doosre patient ka ho sakta hai. Laboratory rejection safety barrier hai, punishment nahi. Responsible team ko inform karo, mismatch investigate karo aur safe recollection arrange karo. Correction sirf approved verified exceptional policy mein ho sakti hai. Assumption se kabhi relabel mat karo.

**Option-wise explanation:**

- **A (Galat):** Patient se guess karwana actual specimen identity establish nahi karta.
- **B (Sahi):** Investigation aur policy-based recollection ya verified correction wrong-patient result prevent karte hain.
- **C (Galat):** Assumption se relabel karna mismatch ko hide karta hai aur wrong diagnosis ka risk badhata hai.
- **D (Galat):** Rejected sample ko definitive result maan kar treatment karna unreliable aur unsafe ho sakta hai.

**Exam clue:** Identifier mismatch: stop, investigate, verify or recollect.
