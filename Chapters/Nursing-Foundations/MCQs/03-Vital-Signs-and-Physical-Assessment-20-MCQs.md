### Q5. The apical pulse is 112/min and simultaneous radial pulse is 98/min. What is the pulse deficit?

<!-- job4-metadata -->
**Difficulty:** 🔴 Difficult · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD · **🟣 RRB Staff Nurse 2019 · Shift II — related concept** · **🟣 AIIMS Bhatinda Nursing Officer 2019 — related concept**
<!-- /job4-metadata -->

- **A.** 14 beats/min
- **B.** 210 beats/min
- **C.** 98 beats/min
- **D.** 112 beats/min

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: A. 14 beats/min**

Pulse deficit apical aur peripheral pulse ka difference hai. 112 minus 98 = 14 beats/min. Kuch heartbeats peripheral pulse generate nahi kar pa rahi ho sakti hain.

**Baaki options kyun sahi nahi hain?** 210 sum hai. 98 radial rate aur 112 apical rate hai.

### Q18. A patient has BP 84/48 mmHg. What is the approximate mean arterial pressure at a regular resting heart rate?

<!-- job4-metadata -->
**Difficulty:** 🔴 Difficult · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD · **🟣 UP NHM Staff Nurse 2021 · Shift I — related concept**
<!-- /job4-metadata -->

- **A.** 66 mmHg
- **B.** 132 mmHg
- **C.** 60 mmHg
- **D.** 36 mmHg

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: C. 60 mmHg**

MAP ka common estimate diastolic BP plus one-third pulse pressure hai. 48 + (84 minus 48)/3 = 60 mmHg. Low value ko perfusion findings ke saath assess karna chahiye.

**Baaki options kyun sahi nahi hain?** 36 pulse pressure hai. 66 simple mean hai. 132 sum hai.
