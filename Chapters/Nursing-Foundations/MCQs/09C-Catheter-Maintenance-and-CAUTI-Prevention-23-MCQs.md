### Q1. Why may bacteria persist on the inner surface of a long-term urinary catheter despite free urine drainage?

**Difficulty:** 🟡 Medium · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD · **Exam focus:** NORCET / all-India Staff Nurse · **Topic:** Biofilm

- **A.** Gravity sterilizes only the outside of the catheter
- **B.** Urine production permanently destroys all bacteria
- **C.** They can form a matrix-associated biofilm attached to the catheter surface
- **D.** The catheter balloon actively secretes an antibiotic

<details><summary>✅ Correct answer and detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: C. They can form a matrix-associated biofilm attached to the catheter surface**

**A. Kyun galat hai:** Gravity fluid ko move karti hai. Ye surface microorganisms ko sterilize nahi karti.

**B. Kyun galat hai:** Urine flow kuch organisms ko wash out kar sakta hai, lekin adherent biofilm remain kar sakti hai.

**C. Kyun sahi hai:** Biofilm mein microorganisms surface par chipak kar protective matrix bana sakte hain. Isliye normal drainage hona catheter ke sterile hone ka proof nahi hai.

**D. Kyun galat hai:** Retention balloon catheter ko bladder mein hold karta hai. Standard balloon antibiotic secrete nahi karta.

**Clinical reference:** [Guidance](https://nurses.uroweb.org/guidelines/indwelling-catheterisation-in-adults-urethral-and-suprapubic/chapter/catheter-complications).

### Q2. A removed long-term catheter has hard crystalline deposits and the urine has become alkaline. Which bacterial mechanism best explains the finding?

**Difficulty:** 🔴 Difficult · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD · **Exam focus:** NORCET / all-India Staff Nurse · **Topic:** Crystalline obstruction

- **A.** Urease splits urea and raises urinary pH, encouraging crystal deposition
- **B.** Bacterial insulin production lowers urinary pH
- **C.** Bacteria dissolve all mineral salts by producing oxygen
- **D.** Glomerular loss of albumin alone creates a catheter biofilm

<details><summary>✅ Correct answer and detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: A. Urease splits urea and raises urinary pH, encouraging crystal deposition**

**A. Kyun sahi hai:** Proteus jaise urease-producing bacteria urea ko break karte hain aur ammonia formation se urine alkaline ho sakta hai. Is environment mein struvite aur calcium phosphate crystals catheter par deposit ho sakte hain.

**B. Kyun galat hai:** Insulin glucose regulation ka hormone hai. Is finding ka relevant mechanism bacterial urease hai, na ki insulin.

**C. Kyun galat hai:** Alkaline urine mein kuch mineral salts precipitate kar sakte hain. Oxygen production se crystals dissolve hone ka ye mechanism nahi hai.

**D. Kyun galat hai:** Albuminuria kidney disease mein ho sakti hai. Lekin alkaline urine aur crystalline catheter deposits ko urease mechanism better explain karta hai.

<details><summary>✅ Correct answer and detailed explanation</summary>

**D. Kyun galat hai:** Pyuria inflammation ka marker ho sakti hai. Ye sterility ka proof nahi hai.

### Q20. A patient has clinician-diagnosed symptomatic CAUTI and still requires an indwelling catheter that has been in place for ten days. Under NICE NG113, which care coordination is appropriate?

<details><summary>✅ Correct answer and detailed explanation</summary>

**D. Kyun sahi hai:** NICE NG113 seven days se zyada purane catheter mein symptomatic CAUTI par remove ya, agar needed ho, change consider karta hai. Antibiotic treatment ko is procedure ke liye delay nahi karna chahiye.
