### Q1. Smoke is detected in an unoccupied hospital utility room and the nurse is safely beside a manual call point. What is the most appropriate immediate action?

<!-- job4-metadata -->
**Difficulty:** 🔴 Difficult · **Priority:** 🔴 MOST IMPORTANT · ⚪ Original practice · **🟣 JIPMER Staff Nurse 2020 — related concept** · **🟣 RRB Nursing Superintendent 2026 · Shift 3 — related concept**
<!-- /job4-metadata -->

A. Wait for visible flames before notifying anyone
B. Enter the room to locate the source
C. Collect cleaning supplies from the room
D. Activate the fire alarm and initiate the facility response from a safe location

<details>
<summary>✅ Sahi answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct answer: D. Activate the fire alarm and initiate the facility response from a safe location**

- **A (Incorrect):** Visible flame ka wait emergency response delay karega.
- **B (Incorrect):** Unsafe room enter karne se smoke inhalation aur injury ho sakti hai.
- **C (Incorrect):** Supplies retrieve karna unnecessary exposure hai.
- **D (Correct):** Koi patient immediate danger mein nahi hai aur alarm accessible hai. Alarm activate karke facility response initiate karo.
