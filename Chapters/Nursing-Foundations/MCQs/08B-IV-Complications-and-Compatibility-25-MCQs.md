### Q1. A patient receiving a peripheral infusion reports tightness beneath a transparent dressing. The infusion pump has not alarmed. Which interpretation is safest?

**Difficulty:** 🟡 Medium · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD · Clinical application · Original practice

A. The pump alarm threshold should be increased before examining the site
B. The dressing alone proves that the catheter is within the vein
C. Local tissue leakage remains possible and requires immediate assessment
D. A silent pump excludes infiltration

<details>
<summary>✅ Sahi answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct answer: C. Local tissue leakage remains possible and requires immediate assessment**

- **A (Incorrect):** Alarm threshold badhane se underlying problem hide ho sakti hai. Pehle patient ko assess karna zaroori hai.
- **B (Incorrect):** Transparent dressing site dekhne mein madad karti hai, lekin tip position prove nahi karti.
- **C (Correct):** Pump pressure monitor karta hai, catheter tip ki exact location nahi. Tissue mein fluid leak ho sakta hai bina alarm ke, isliye infusion rok kar site assess karo.
- **D (Incorrect):** Pump silent hone se infiltration exclude nahi hoti.

**Exam clue:** Pump alarm aur local assessment alag cheezein hain.
