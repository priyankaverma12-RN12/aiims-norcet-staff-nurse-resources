<!-- job4-metadata -->

<!-- /job4-metadata -->

- **A.** Apply four-point restraints immediately without assessment
- **B.** Keep all four side rails raised as the only intervention
- **C.** Encourage the patient to walk alone to improve confidence
- **D.** Assess the cause, arrange supervision and implement individualized fall precautions

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Difficulty:** 🟡 Medium · **Priority:** 🟡 IMPORTANT
<!-- /job4-metadata -->

- **A.** Ask the patient to hold a loose IV pole for support
- **B.** Remove all footwear even if the floor is slippery
- **C.** Lock wheelchair brakes and assess transfer assistance needs
- **D.** Keep the wheelchair moving throughout transfer

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: C. Lock wheelchair brakes and assess transfer assistance needs**

Locked brakes chair movement prevent karte hain. Transfer se pehle strength, balance aur assistive equipment assess karna important hai.

**Baaki options kyun sahi nahi hain?** Moving chair, loose pole aur slippery surface falls ka risk badhate hain.

### Q20. A medication error has reached a patient, but no symptoms are currently evident. What should the nurse do first?

<!-- job4-metadata -->
**Difficulty:** 🔴 Difficult · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD
<!-- /job4-metadata -->

- **A.** Discard the medication package to prevent investigation
- **B.** Assess the patient and arrange prompt clinical review and required reporting
- **C.** Wait for symptoms before informing anyone
- **D.** Change the record to match the dose given

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: B. Assess the patient and arrange prompt clinical review and required reporting**

Some medicine effects delayed ho sakte hain. Nurse patient assess karti hai, clinician ko notify karti hai aur policy ke according monitoring aur report arrange karti hai.

**Baaki options kyun sahi nahi hain?** Symptoms ka wait unsafe hai. Record change aur evidence destroy karna inappropriate hai.
