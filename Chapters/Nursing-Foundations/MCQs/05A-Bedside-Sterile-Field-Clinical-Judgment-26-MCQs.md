### Q1. A disinfected bedside table is still wet before the nurse opens a sterile kit. Which preparation is appropriate?

**Difficulty:** 🟡 Medium · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD · Original practice

A. Cover the wet surface with gauze
B. Allow the surface to dry after the required disinfection contact time
C. Open the kit before drying the table
D. Use the patient's bedding instead

<details>
<summary>✅ Sahi answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct answer: B. Allow the surface to dry after the required disinfection contact time**

- **A (Incorrect):** Gauze liquid absorb kar sakti hai, lekin wet table aur sterile wrapper ke beech reliable moisture barrier nahi banati.
- **B (Correct):** Disinfectant ko pehle required wet contact time do, phir product instructions ke according table dry hone do. Wet surface se porous sterile barrier ke through moisture aur microbes transfer ho sakte hain. Dry, stable work surface field setup ko safe banati hai.
- **C (Incorrect):** Wet table par kit kholne se wrapper ki integrity compromise ho sakti hai. Field kholne ke baad cleaning karna bhi contamination ka risk hai.
- **D (Incorrect):** Patient ki bedding par skin organisms aur secretions ho sakte hain. Yeh controlled clean dry work surface ka replacement nahi hai.

**Exam clue:** Disinfection ka contact time complete karo, phir dry surface par sterile field banao.

### Q2. In the conventional four-flap sterile kit opening method, which flap is opened first?

**Difficulty:** 🟡 Medium · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD · Original practice

A. The flap farthest from the nurse
B. The nearest flap
C. Both side flaps simultaneously
D. The flap nearest the patient's head

<details>
<summary>✅ Sahi answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct answer: A. The flap farthest from the nurse**

- **A (Correct):** Door wala flap pehle kholne se central sterile area abhi exposed nahi hota jab nurse us direction mein move karti hai. Phir side flaps aur last mein nearest flap kholne se nonsterile arm ko sterile center ke upar le jaane ka risk kam hota hai.
- **B (Incorrect):** Nearest flap pehle kholne par far flap tak pahunchte waqt arm exposed center ke upar aa sakti hai.
- **C (Incorrect):** Dono side flaps ek saath kholne se control kam ho sakta hai aur wrapper wapas field par gir sakta hai.
- **D (Incorrect):** Patient ke head ki direction kit opening order decide nahi karti. Nurse ke relative flap position important hai.

**Exam clue:** Conventional kit opening: far flap, sides, then near flap.
