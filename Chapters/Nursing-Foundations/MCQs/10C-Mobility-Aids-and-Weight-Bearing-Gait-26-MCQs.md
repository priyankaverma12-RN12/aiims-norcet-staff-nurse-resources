### Q01. During cane-assisted walking, a patient advances the stronger leg before the cane and weaker leg have established support. Which sequence should the nurse reinforce for the prescribed conventional pattern?

**Difficulty:** 🟡 Medium · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD · **Evidence:** Original practice · **Exam focus:** NORCET / Staff Nurse · **Topic:** Cane sequence

- **A.** Stronger leg, cane, weaker leg
- **B.** Weaker leg, stronger leg, then cane
- **C.** Cane behind the body, then both legs
- **D.** Cane and weaker leg forward together, then stronger leg

<details><summary>✅ Correct answer and detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: D. Cane and weaker leg forward together, then stronger leg**

**A. Kyun galat hai:** Strong leg pehle aage jaane se weak leg ko support milne se pehle load transfer ho sakta hai.

**B. Kyun galat hai:** Dono legs move karne ke baad cane advance karne se support ki timing galat ho jaati hai.

**C. Kyun galat hai:** Cane ko peeche rakhne se forward movement mein useful support nahi milega.

**D. Kyun sahi hai:** Cane aur weaker leg ko prescribed conventional pattern mein saath aage le jaane se weaker limb ko support milta hai. Cane ka tip stable surface par aane ke baad stronger leg aage badhti hai. Kuch training programmes cane ko weak foot se just pehle advance karna sikhate hain. Dono mein key principle hai ki weaker limb load lene se pehle aid support ready ho.

**Exam clue:** Cane ki side aur cane advance karne ki timing alag concepts hain.

**Clinical reference:** [Mobility guidance](https://medlineplus.gov/ency/patientinstructions/000343.htm).

### Q18. In a hypothetical scale-based training exercise, a clinician specifies an affected-leg load equivalent to 25% of body weight. The patient weighs 80 kg. Which approximate mass-equivalent load is the training target?

**Difficulty:** 🟡 Medium · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD · **Evidence:** Original practice · **Exam focus:** NORCET / Staff Nurse · **Topic:** Weight-bearing calculation

- **B.** 60 kg
- **C.** 20 kg
- **D.** 80 kg

<details><summary>✅ Correct answer and detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: C. 20 kg**

**A. Kyun galat hai:** 40 kg body weight ka 50 percent hai, 25 percent nahi.
