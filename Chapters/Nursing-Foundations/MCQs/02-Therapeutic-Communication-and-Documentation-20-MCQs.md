### Q1. A patient says, I am afraid of my surgery tomorrow. Which response best encourages therapeutic communication?

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Priority:** Medium
<!-- job4-metadata -->
**🟡 Medium** | **🟡 IMPORTANT** | Clinical scenario · Editorial tags
<!-- /job4-metadata -->


- **A.** Everyone has surgery, so stop thinking about it
- **B.** You should not feel afraid

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: C. Can you tell me what worries you most about the surgery?**

**Baaki options kyun sahi nahi hain?** False reassurance, comparison aur feelings dismiss karna communication ko block kar sakta hai.
