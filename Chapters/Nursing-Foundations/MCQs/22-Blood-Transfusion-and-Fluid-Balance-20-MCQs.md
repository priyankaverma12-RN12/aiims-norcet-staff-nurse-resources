### Q1. Before starting a blood transfusion, which verification is essential?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Priority:** High

- **A.** Use the room number as identification
- **B.** Assume all units from the blood bank are for this patient
- **C.** Match patient identifiers, compatibility details, component and prescription using approved checks
- **D.** Check only the blood bag color

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: C. Match patient identifiers, compatibility details, component and prescription using approved checks**

Wrong blood transfusion severe hemolysis cause kar sakti hai. Bedside verification patient aur unit ko correctly match karti hai.

**Baaki options kyun sahi nahi hain?** Color, room number aur delivery assumption compatibility prove nahi karte.

</details>

### Q2. Which observation provides a baseline for monitoring transfusion reactions?

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Priority:** Medium

- **A.** Only the patient's age
- **B.** Vital signs and relevant clinical status before starting
- **C.** Only a weight from last year
- **D.** Only the bag volume

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: B. Vital signs and relevant clinical status before starting**

Baseline vitals se transfusion ke dauran new fever, BP changes aur breathing symptoms compare karne mein help milti hai.

**Baaki options kyun sahi nahi hain?** Old weight, volume aur age alone reaction detection ke liye insufficient hain.

</details>

### Q5. During transfusion an older patient develops dyspnea, hypertension and signs of volume overload. Which complication is particularly relevant?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Priority:** High

- **A.** Isolated iron deficiency
- **B.** Expected therapeutic response
- **C.** An uncomplicated vasovagal reaction
- **D.** Transfusion-associated circulatory overload

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: D. Transfusion-associated circulatory overload**

TACO mein transfused volume circulation overload kar sakta hai. Breathlessness aur hypertension possible clues hain. Urgent assessment aur reaction protocol needed hai.

**Baaki options kyun sahi nahi hain?** Iron deficiency acute overload explain nahi karti. New respiratory distress normal response nahi hai. Vasovagal reaction often hypotension ke saath hoti hai.

</details>

### Q6. A patient develops acute hypoxemia and bilateral lung infiltrates during or within hours of transfusion without clear circulatory overload. Which complication should be considered?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Priority:** High

- **A.** Simple dehydration
- **B.** A normal post-transfusion change
- **C.** Transfusion-related acute lung injury
- **D.** Chronic iron overload alone

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: C. Transfusion-related acute lung injury**

TRALI transfusion-related acute lung injury hai jisme pulmonary edema aur oxygenation failure ho sakti hai. TACO aur other causes bhi urgently assess karne hote hain.

**Baaki options kyun sahi nahi hain?** Chronic iron overload immediate pattern explain nahi karta. Dehydration aur normal response serious hypoxemia explain nahi karte.

</details>

### Q9. Which blood component primarily increases oxygen-carrying capacity in an anemic patient when transfusion is indicated?

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Priority:** Medium

- **A.** Packed red blood cells
- **B.** Platelet concentrate
- **C.** Fresh frozen plasma
- **D.** Cryoprecipitate

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: A. Packed red blood cells**

Red cells hemoglobin carry karti hain jo oxygen bind karta hai. Transfusion indication symptoms, Hb aur clinical context se decide hoti hai.

**Baaki options kyun sahi nahi hain?** Platelets hemostasis, plasma clotting factors aur cryoprecipitate fibrinogen replacement mein use ho sakte hain.

</details>

### Q13. A patient weighs 62 kg yesterday and 64 kg today using the same scale and comparable conditions. What should the nurse consider?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Priority:** High

- **A.** The change should never be assessed
- **B.** A 2-kg rapid gain may reflect approximately 2 L of fluid accumulation
- **C.** It proves 2 kg of new muscle was formed overnight
- **D.** It proves dehydration

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: B. A 2-kg rapid gain may reflect approximately 2 L of fluid accumulation**

Rapid weight gain often fluid accumulation se related ho sakta hai. Roughly one kg water one liter ke equal hota hai. Nurse edema, intake-output aur breathing assess karti hai.

**Baaki options kyun sahi nahi hain?** Muscle growth overnight unlikely hai. Gain dehydration prove nahi karta. Change clinical context mein important hai.

</details>

### Q14. A patient's output includes 900 mL urine and 300 mL drain fluid while measured intake is 1500 mL. What is recorded net balance?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Priority:** High

- **A.** +300 mL
- **B.** −300 mL
- **C.** +600 mL
- **D.** 2700 mL

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: A. +300 mL**

Total output 1200 mL hai. Intake minus output = 1500 minus 1200 = +300 mL. Insensible losses recorded balance mein usually measured nahi hoti.

**Baaki options kyun sahi nahi hain?** −300 reverse subtraction hai. +600 drain omit karta hai. 2700 sum hai.

</details>

### Q15. Which finding is consistent with possible extracellular fluid volume depletion?

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Priority:** Medium

- **A.** New generalized edema as the only sign

- **D.** Orthostatic dizziness with dry mucosa and reduced urine output

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

### Q18. Why must hypertonic saline administration be closely monitored?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Priority:** High

- **A.** It is safe at any rate for every patient
- **B.** It can alter sodium and water distribution rapidly, with serious complications if mismanaged
- **C.** It is physiologically identical to free water
- **D.** It has no effect on serum sodium

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: B. It can alter sodium and water distribution rapidly, with serious complications if mismanaged**

Hypertonic saline extracellular tonicity aur sodium change kar sakti hai. Rate, indication aur repeat labs specific protocol se manage hote hain.

**Baaki options kyun sahi nahi hain?** Free water opposite tonicity effect rakhta hai. Sodium response aur rate clinically important hain.

</details>

### Q20. Why should measurable liquid medication and tube flushes be included in intake records?

- **D.** They contribute to administered fluid volume

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

</details>
