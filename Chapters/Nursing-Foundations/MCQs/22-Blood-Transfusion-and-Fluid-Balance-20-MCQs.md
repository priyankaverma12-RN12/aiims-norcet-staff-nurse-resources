### Q1. Before starting a blood transfusion, which verification is essential?

**Difficulty:** 🔴 Difficult · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD · Original practice

- **A.** Use the room number as identification
- **B.** Assume all units from the blood bank are for this patient
- **C.** Match patient identifiers, compatibility details, component and prescription using approved checks
- **D.** Check only the blood bag color

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: C. Match patient identifiers, compatibility details, component and prescription using approved checks**

Blood transfusion mein kisi doosre patient ke liye compatible unit galti se lag jaaye toh severe hemolysis, yani red cells ka tootna, ho sakta hai. Bedside par approved independent check ke through patient ke verified identifiers, prescription, component, unit number, compatibility label, expiry aur bag integrity check karo. Mismatch ho toh transfusion start mat karo aur blood bank se clarify karo.

**Option-wise explanation:**

- **A (Galat):** Room number patient ka unique identifier nahi hota.
- **B (Galat):** Blood bank delivery alone bedside verification replace nahi karti.
- **C (Sahi):** Patient aur component ka complete match wrong-blood transfusion ka risk kam karta hai.
- **D (Galat):** Bag color blood group ya compatibility confirm nahi karta.

**Exam clue:** Patient-unit match before transfusion.

### Q2. Which observation provides a baseline for monitoring transfusion reactions?

**Difficulty:** 🟡 Medium · **Priority:** 🟡 IMPORTANT · Original practice

- **A.** Only the patient's age
- **B.** Vital signs and relevant clinical status before starting
- **C.** Only a weight from last year
- **D.** Only the bag volume

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: B. Vital signs and relevant clinical status before starting**

Baseline vital signs transfusion se pehle patient ki condition ka starting record hain. Temperature, pulse, BP, respiratory rate, oxygen saturation as indicated aur existing symptoms document karo. Initial period mein closely observe karo aur local policy ke according monitoring repeat karo. New symptoms baseline se compare karne par reaction jaldi identify ho sakti hai.

**Option-wise explanation:**

- **A (Galat):** Age risk assessment mein useful ho sakti hai, lekin reaction ke liye baseline vitals nahi hai.
- **B (Sahi):** Baseline vital signs aur clinical status new deterioration identify karne mein help karte hain.
- **C (Galat):** Purana weight current physiological condition nahi batata.
- **D (Galat):** Bag volume patient ki baseline status nahi batata.

**Exam clue:** Baseline assessment before blood administration.

### Q3. A patient develops fever, chills and flank pain soon after a red cell transfusion starts. What is the immediate action?

**Difficulty:** 🔴 Difficult · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD · Original practice · **🟣 AIIMS NORCET, 11 September 2022 — related reported concept** · **🟣 AIIMS NORCET 9 Mains, 27 September 2025 — related reported concept**

- **A.** Stop the transfusion and initiate the reaction protocol with patient assessment
- **B.** Increase the rate
- **C.** Administer the next unit
- **D.** Finish the current unit before reporting

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: A. Stop the transfusion and initiate the reaction protocol with patient assessment**

Fever, chills aur flank pain acute hemolytic reaction ya bacterial contamination jaisi serious reaction ka warning ho sakte hain. Transfusion immediately stop karo. ABC, vitals aur patient identity assess karo, urgent medical help aur blood bank ko notify karo. Existing blood tubing se flush mat karo. Local protocol ke hisaab se new tubing aur compatible saline se access maintain karo, aur implicated unit preserve karo. Is serious reaction mein transfusion restart mat karo.

**Option-wise explanation:**

- **A (Sahi):** Stop karna further exposure rokta hai aur reaction protocol timely activate karta hai.
- **B (Galat):** Rate badhane se suspected harmful exposure aur badh sakta hai.
- **C (Galat):** Agli unit lagana cause clarify hone se pehle unsafe hai.
- **D (Galat):** Current unit complete hone tak wait karna reaction worsen kar sakta hai.

**Exam clue:** Suspected serious transfusion reaction: stop, assess, escalate.

**Clinical reference:** [Clinical guidance](https://www.lifeblood.com.au/health-professionals/clinical-practice/adverse-events/management-of-suspected-reactions)

### Q5. During transfusion an older patient develops dyspnea, hypertension and signs of volume overload. Which complication is particularly relevant?

**Difficulty:** 🔴 Difficult · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD · Original practice · **🟣 AIIMS NORCET 6 Prelims, 2024 — related reported concept**

- **A.** Isolated iron deficiency
- **B.** Expected therapeutic response
- **C.** An uncomplicated vasovagal reaction
- **D.** Transfusion-associated circulatory overload

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: D. Transfusion-associated circulatory overload**

TACO ka full form transfusion-associated circulatory overload hai. Transfused volume patient ke heart aur circulation ki capacity se zyada ho jaaye toh fluid lungs mein jama ho sakta hai. Dyspnea, rising BP, crackles, raised JVP aur positive fluid balance clues ho sakte hain. Transfusion stop karo, ABC assess karo aur urgent help lo. Oxygen, positioning aur prescribed diuretics clinical assessment ke according use hote hain. TACO aur TRALI overlap bhi kar sakte hain.

**Option-wise explanation:**

- **A (Galat):** Iron deficiency acute pulmonary fluid overload explain nahi karti.
- **B (Galat):** New dyspnea aur hypertension ko expected response nahi maan sakte.
- **C (Galat):** Vasovagal reaction mein aksar BP fall aur relative bradycardia hoti hai, volume overload typical nahi.
- **D (Sahi):** TACO mein excess circulatory volume respiratory distress cause kar sakta hai.

**Exam clue:** TACO: respiratory distress with evidence of overload.

**Clinical reference:** [Clinical guidance](https://www.lifeblood.com.au/health-professionals/clinical-practice/adverse-events/TACO)

### Q6. A patient develops acute hypoxemia and bilateral lung infiltrates during or within hours of transfusion without clear circulatory overload. Which complication should be considered?

**Difficulty:** 🔴 Difficult · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD · Original practice

- **A.** Simple dehydration
- **B.** A normal post-transfusion change
- **C.** Transfusion-related acute lung injury
- **D.** Chronic iron overload alone

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: C. Transfusion-related acute lung injury**

TRALI transfusion-related acute lung injury hai. Lung capillaries leak karne par fluid air sacs mein aa sakta hai, bina primarily cardiac volume overload ke. Acute hypoxemia aur bilateral pulmonary edema during ya within six hours transfusion ke compatible clues hain. Transfusion stop karo, ABC aur oxygenation assess karo aur urgent medical help aur blood bank ko notify karo. TACO, sepsis aur other causes exclude karne ke liye clinical review zaroori hai.

**Option-wise explanation:**

- **A (Galat):** Simple dehydration acute bilateral lung edema pattern explain nahi karti.
- **B (Galat):** New hypoxemia normal transfusion response nahi hai.
- **C (Sahi):** TRALI is pattern ke liye relevant serious reaction hai. Diagnosis further assessment se hota hai.
- **D (Galat):** Chronic iron overload usually repeated transfusions ke baad develop hota hai, immediate lung injury pattern nahi.

**Exam clue:** TRALI: acute hypoxemia and bilateral edema, usually within six hours.

- **C.** +600 mL
- **D.** 2700 mL

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: A. +300 mL**

Recorded fluid balance mein same observation period ka measured intake minus measured output karte hain. Output = 900 + 300 = 1200 mL. Net = 1500 − 1200 = +300 mL. Positive sign ka matlab recorded intake measured output se zyada hai. Insensible losses aur unrecorded fluids ki wajah se actual body balance different ho sakta hai.

**Option-wise explanation:**

- **A (Sahi):** 1500 − 1200 = +300 mL correct hai.
- **B (Galat):** −300 reverse subtraction se aata hai.
- **C (Galat):** +600 drain output ko omit karta hai.
- **D (Galat):** 2700 intake aur output ka sum hai, net balance nahi.

**Exam clue:** Intake minus all measured output.

### Q15. Which finding is consistent with possible extracellular fluid volume depletion?

**Difficulty:** 🟡 Medium · **Priority:** 🟡 IMPORTANT · Original practice

- **C (Galat):** Free water ka tonicity effect different hota hai.
- **D (Galat):** Hypertonic saline serum sodium change kar sakti hai.
