### Q1. A child weighs 44 lb, but an electronic calculation incorrectly treats 44 as kilograms. For a fictional 3 mg/kg order, using 2.2 lb/kg, what correct dose and erroneous dose would result?

**Difficulty:** 🔴 Difficult · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD · Calculation · Original practice · **[🟣 AIIMS Nursing Officer Nagpur 2020 — related calculation concept only](https://testbook.com/question-answer/an-order-for-prednisolone-reads-10-mg-four-times-a--6171007417dfb802631324f2)**

A. 60 mg and 132 mg
B. 132 mg and 60 mg
C. 20 mg and 44 mg
D. 60 mg and 60 mg

<details>
<summary>✅ Sahi answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct answer: A. 60 mg and 132 mg**

**Formula, steps aur reverse check:** 44 lb / 2.2 lb/kg = 20 kg. Correct dose 20 × 3 = 60 mg, erroneous dose 44 × 3 = 132 mg. Reverse: 60/3 = 20 kg, 132/3 = 44 kg. Error factor 132/60 = 2.2.

- **A (Correct):** Correct aur erroneous values respectively 60 aur 132 mg hain.
- **B (Incorrect):** Values reverse hain.
- **C (Incorrect):** 20 aur 44 weights hain, doses nahi.
- **D (Incorrect):** Wrong weight se 132 mg aayega, 60 nahi.

**Exam clue:** Weight ki unit verify karo.
