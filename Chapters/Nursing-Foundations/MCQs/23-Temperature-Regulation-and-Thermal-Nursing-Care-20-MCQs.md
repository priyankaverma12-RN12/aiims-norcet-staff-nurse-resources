### Q1. Which temperature site is generally avoided in an unconscious patient who cannot safely hold an oral thermometer?

**Difficulty:** 🟡 Medium · **Priority:** 🟡 IMPORTANT · Original practice

- **A.** An approved nonoral route
- **B.** A correctly used tympanic route if suitable
- **C.** An appropriate core monitoring device if indicated
- **D.** Oral route

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: D. Oral route**

Unconscious patient oral thermometer ko safely hold nahi kar sakta. Swallowing aur protective reflexes impaired ho sakte hain. Oral device se injury ya aspiration ka risk ho sakta hai. Nurse patient ki condition aur monitoring requirement ke according validated nonoral route choose karti hai.

**Option-wise explanation:**

- **A (Galat):** Approved nonoral route patient aur device ke hisaab se suitable ho sakti hai.
- **B (Galat):** Tympanic measurement ear injury ya other contraindication na ho toh suitable alternative ho sakti hai.
- **C (Galat):** Appropriate core temperature device critical monitoring mein indicated ho sakta hai.
- **D (Sahi):** Oral route mein cooperation aur safe positioning chahiye, jo unconscious patient mein possible nahi ho sakta.

**Exam clue:** Unconscious patient: avoid oral thermometer.

### Q2. Why should a recent hot drink be considered before measuring oral temperature?

**Difficulty:** 🔴 Difficult · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD · Original practice · **🟣 JIPMER Staff Nurse, 4 June 2013 — related reported concept**

- **A.** It always lowers oral temperature
- **B.** It has no influence on oral measurement
- **C.** It may temporarily elevate the local measurement
- **D.** It permanently raises core temperature by several degrees

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: C. It may temporarily elevate the local measurement**

Oral temperature mouth ke andar ki local temperature measure karti hai. Hot drink se oral tissue temporarily warm ho sakta hai, isliye reading falsely high aa sakti hai. Cold drink opposite effect de sakti hai. Device instructions aur local policy ke according wait karo ya suitable alternate route use karo. Urgent clinical assessment ko wait ke liye delay mat karo.

**Option-wise explanation:**

- **A (Galat):** Hot drink local oral reading ko generally increase kar sakti hai, lower karna universal effect nahi hai.
- **B (Galat):** Recent food ya drink oral measurement ko affect kar sakti hai.
- **C (Sahi):** Local warming se temporarily higher reading mil sakti hai, even when core temperature unchanged ho.
- **D (Galat):** Hot drink se core temperature permanently several degrees increase hona expected nahi hai.

**Exam clue:** Recent food or drink: check timing before oral measurement.

### Q3. Which term describes elevated body temperature due to a raised hypothalamic set point?

**Difficulty:** 🟡 Medium · **Priority:** 🟡 IMPORTANT · Original practice

- **A.** Normothermia
- **B.** Fever
- **C.** Hyperthermia without set-point change
- **D.** Hypothermia

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: B. Fever**

Hypothalamus brain ka temperature-control center hai. Fever mein inflammatory signals uska target temperature, yani set point, higher kar sakte hain. Body heat conserve aur produce karke higher target tak pahunchti hai. Isliye fever mein chills aur vasoconstriction ho sakte hain. Hyperthermia mein heat production ya exposure heat loss se exceed hota hai without raised set point.

**Option-wise explanation:**

- **A (Galat):** Normothermia expected normal temperature regulation ko describe karti hai.
- **B (Sahi):** Raised hypothalamic set point fever ka mechanism hai.
- **C (Galat):** Hyperthermia without raised set point fever se physiologically different hai.
- **D (Galat):** Hypothermia core temperature abnormally low hone ki condition hai.

**Exam clue:** Fever = raised set point.

**Clinical reference:** [Clinical guidance](https://www.merckmanuals.com/professional/infectious-diseases/biology-of-infectious-disease/fever)

### Q4. A patient exposed to extreme heat has very high temperature and altered mental status. Which action is a priority?

**Difficulty:** 🔴 Difficult · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD · Original practice

- **A.** Activate emergency help and begin appropriate rapid cooling and supportive care
- **B.** Give a routine antipyretic as the sole treatment
- **C.** Wrap the patient in warm blankets
- **D.** Wait for spontaneous sweating before seeking help

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: A. Activate emergency help and begin appropriate rapid cooling and supportive care**

Extreme heat exposure ke baad very high temperature aur altered mental status heatstroke ka serious warning hai. Body heat control fail ho sakta hai aur brain, kidney, liver aur circulation damage ho sakte hain. Emergency help activate karo, ABC assess karo aur appropriate rapid active cooling turant shuru karo. Cooling method trained team aur setting ke hisaab se choose karo. Altered consciousness mein oral fluids force mat karo.

**Option-wise explanation:**

- **A (Sahi):** Rapid cooling aur emergency supportive care organ damage kam karne ke liye time-sensitive hain.
- **B (Galat):** Routine antipyretics heatstroke ka mechanism correct nahi karte aur sole treatment unsafe hai.
- **C (Galat):** Warm blankets heat retain kar sakti hain.
- **D (Galat):** Sweating present ya absent ho sakti hai. Emergency help ke liye sweating ka wait nahi karte.

**Exam clue:** Heatstroke: urgent active cooling plus emergency support.

**Clinical reference:** [Clinical guidance](https://sccm.org/clinical-resources/guidelines/guidelines/guideline-for-the-treatment-of-heat-stroke)

### Q5. A patient with suspected fever has shaking chills while temperature is rising. Which explanation is most accurate?

Fever ki onset mein hypothalamus higher set point establish karta hai. Body ka actual temperature is target se abhi kam hota hai, isliye patient ko thand lag sakti hai even while temperature rising ho. Shivering muscle contraction ke through heat generate karti hai aur peripheral vasoconstriction heat loss kam karti hai.

**Option-wise explanation:**

- **A (Sahi):** Current temperature raised set point se kam ho sakta hai, jo chills explain karta hai.
- **B (Galat):** Shivering normal core temperature prove nahi karti.
- **C (Galat):** Hypothalamus ka set point change hona permanent failure nahi hai.
- **D (Galat):** Shivering heat production badhati hai, band nahi karti.

**Exam clue:** Chills while fever rises = body trying to reach higher set point.

**Correct: D. Increasing pallor, numbness or pain**

Poor perfusion ka matlab limb tak adequate blood flow na pahunchna hai. Cold vasoconstriction, yani blood vessels ka narrow hona, cause kar sakti hai. Poorly perfused area par routine cold therapy generally avoid karte hain. Increasing pallor, pain ya numbness par application stop karo, skin aur distal circulation assess karo aur urgent review as indicated arrange karo.

**Option-wise explanation:**

- **A (Galat):** Stable comfort aur intact skin reassuring ho sakti hai, lekin monitoring still needed hai.
- **B (Galat):** Planned reassessment safe care ka part hai, deterioration ka sign nahi.
- **C (Galat):** Normal distal circulation alone future safety guarantee nahi karti, lekin listed urgent warning nahi hai.
- **D (Sahi):** Increasing pallor, numbness ya pain compromised circulation ya cold injury ka warning hai.

**Exam clue:** New pallor, pain or numbness: stop cold application and assess.

### Q17. What is the purpose of a protective layer between an approved heat or cold device and the skin?

**Difficulty:** 🟡 Medium · **Priority:** 🟡 IMPORTANT · Original practice

- **A.** Increase skin damage
- **B.** Make temperature monitoring unnecessary
- **C.** Prevent all heat transfer
- **D.** Reduce direct thermal injury risk

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: D. Reduce direct thermal injury risk**

Protective layer device aur skin ke direct contact ko moderate karti hai. Isse excessive heat ya cold se burns aur tissue injury ka risk kam ho sakta hai. Barrier use karna monitoring ka replacement nahi hai. Indication, sensation, circulation, temperature aur duration check karo.

**Option-wise explanation:**

- **A (Galat):** Barrier ka purpose skin damage reduce karna hai, increase nahi.
- **B (Galat):** Skin checks aur temperature monitoring still required hain.
- **C (Galat):** Barrier heat transfer ko slow kar sakti hai, completely prevent nahi karti.
- **D (Sahi):** Direct thermal injury risk reduce karna barrier ka intended purpose hai.

**Exam clue:** Barrier plus monitoring, not barrier alone.

### Q18. A patient with fever has new neck stiffness, confusion and a rapidly worsening condition. Which action is appropriate?

**Difficulty:** 🔴 Difficult · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD · Original practice

- **A.** Give a hot bath before assessing consciousness
- **B.** Assume every fever is uncomplicated
- **C.** Arrange urgent assessment and infection precautions as indicated
- **D.** Treat only the temperature and wait until tomorrow

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: C. Arrange urgent assessment and infection precautions as indicated**

Fever ke saath neck stiffness aur confusion serious CNS infection, jaise meningitis, ya other emergency ka warning pattern hai. ABC, consciousness aur vital signs assess karo aur urgent medical help activate karo. Suspected meningococcal disease mein standard plus droplet precautions as indicated initiate karo. Temperature-only care se emergency assessment delay mat karo.

- **C (Sahi):** Urgent assessment aur appropriate infection precautions serious cause aur transmission risk address karte hain.
- **D (Galat):** Temperature treat karna underlying emergency ka substitute nahi hai.

### Q19. Which term describes a core body temperature below 35°C?

**Difficulty:** 🟡 Medium · **Priority:** 🟡 IMPORTANT · Original practice

- **A.** Pyrexia
- **B.** Hypothermia
- **C.** Hyperpyrexia
- **D.** Normothermia

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: B. Hypothermia**

Hypothermia core body temperature 35°C se below hone ko kehte hain. Body heat loss production se exceed kar sakta hai. Severity ke hisaab se shivering, confusion, slow pulse ya breathing changes develop ho sakte hain. Clinical symptoms aur reliable core measurement se assessment karo.

**Option-wise explanation:**

- **A (Galat):** Pyrexia fever ka term hai.
- **B (Sahi):** Core temperature below 35°C hypothermia ki standard definition hai.
- **C (Galat):** Hyperpyrexia extremely high fever ke liye use hota hai.
- **D (Galat):** Normothermia normal temperature state hai.

**Exam clue:** Hypothermia: core temperature below 35°C.

### Q20. A patient has persistent fever despite antipyretic treatment. Which nursing response is appropriate?

**Difficulty:** 🔴 Difficult · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD · Original practice

- **A.** Reassess clinical condition, hydration and possible cause and communicate relevant changes
- **B.** Repeat medication sooner than prescribed automatically
- **C.** Ignore all other symptoms if the temperature falls later
- **D.** Use the highest available heat exposure

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: A. Reassess clinical condition, hydration and possible cause and communicate relevant changes**

Antipyretic fever ko kam karne mein help kar sakti hai, lekin underlying infection ya other cause ko automatically treat nahi karti. Persistent fever mein nurse vital signs, hydration, consciousness, other symptoms aur medication timing assess karti hai. Relevant changes responsible clinician ko communicate karo. Prescribed interval aur maximum dose follow karo, aur deterioration mein urgent escalation karo.

**Option-wise explanation:**

- **A (Sahi):** Whole-patient reassessment aur clinical communication persistent fever ka safe response hai.
- **B (Galat):** Automatic early repeat dose toxicity ya overdose risk create kar sakti hai.
- **C (Galat):** Temperature fall hone par bhi other serious symptoms clinically important rehte hain.
- **D (Galat):** Highest heat exposure body temperature aur discomfort worsen kar sakta hai.

**Exam clue:** Treat the patient, not only the temperature number.
