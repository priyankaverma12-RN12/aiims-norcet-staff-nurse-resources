### Q1. Which temperature site is generally avoided in an unconscious patient who cannot safely hold an oral thermometer?

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Priority:** Medium

- **A.** An approved nonoral route
- **B.** A correctly used tympanic route if suitable
- **C.** An appropriate core monitoring device if indicated
- **D.** Oral route

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: D. Oral route**

Unconscious patient oral thermometer ko safely hold nahi kar paata. Nurse aspiration aur injury risk dekhkar suitable alternative choose karti hai.

**Baaki options kyun sahi nahi hain?** Nonoral, tympanic ya core monitoring routes patient-specific assessment aur device guidance ke according suitable ho sakte hain.

</details>

### Q2. Why should a recent hot drink be considered before measuring oral temperature?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Priority:** High

- **B.** It has no influence on oral measurement
- **C.** It may temporarily elevate the local measurement
- **D.** It permanently raises core temperature by several degrees

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

Hot drink mouth ki local temperature increase kar sakti hai. Nurse device policy ke according wait ya suitable alternate route select karti hai.

**Baaki options kyun sahi nahi hain?** Permanent core rise expected nahi hai. Cold drink lower reading de sakti hai. Oral measurement local conditions se affect hoti hai.

### Q3. Which term describes elevated body temperature due to a raised hypothalamic set point?

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Priority:** Medium

- **A.** Normothermia
- **B.** Fever
- **C.** Hyperthermia without set-point change
- **D.** Hypothermia

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: B. Fever**

Fever mein inflammatory signals hypothalamus ka target temperature increase kar sakte hain. Body shivering aur heat retention se new target tak pahunchne ki koshish karti hai.

**Baaki options kyun sahi nahi hain?** Hyperthermia mein heat accumulation set-point rise ke bina ho sakti hai. Hypothermia low temperature aur normothermia normal range hai.

</details>

### Q4. A patient exposed to extreme heat has very high temperature and altered mental status. Which action is a priority?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Priority:** High

- **A.** Activate emergency help and begin appropriate rapid cooling and supportive care
- **B.** Give a routine antipyretic as the sole treatment
- **C.** Wrap the patient in warm blankets
- **D.** Wait for spontaneous sweating before seeking help

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: A. Activate emergency help and begin appropriate rapid cooling and supportive care**

Heatstroke mein body heat dangerously accumulate kar sakti hai aur brain function affect hota hai. Rapid cooling aur emergency supportive care time-sensitive hain.

**Baaki options kyun sahi nahi hain?** Antipyretic heatstroke ka primary treatment nahi hai. Blankets heat retain kar sakti hain. Sweating ka wait unsafe hai.

</details>

### Q5. A patient with suspected fever has shaking chills while temperature is rising. Which explanation is most accurate?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Priority:** High

- **A.** The body's current temperature may be below the newly raised set point
- **B.** Shivering always means the core temperature is normal
- **C.** The hypothalamus has permanently stopped functioning
- **D.** Shivering indicates that body heat production has ceased

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: A. The body's current temperature may be below the newly raised set point**

Fever onset mein set point raise hota hai. Body current temperature ko relative cold samajhkar muscles shiver karwati hai aur heat generate karti hai.

**Baaki options kyun sahi nahi hain?** Normal temperature necessarily nahi hai. Hypothalamic regulation active hai. Shivering heat produce karti hai.

</details>

### Q6. Which temperature pattern returns to normal at least once in a 24-hour period despite fever spikes?

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Priority:** Medium

- **B.** Remittent fever that never reaches normal
- **C.** Sustained hyperthermia by definition
- **D.** Intermittent fever

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

Intermittent fever mein temperature periodically normal range tak aa sakta hai. Pattern ko serial observations se identify kiya jaata hai.

**Baaki options kyun sahi nahi hain?** Continuous fever relatively sustained hoti hai. Remittent fever fluctuate karti hai lekin normal tak nahi aati. Hyperthermia separate mechanism hai.

</details>

### Q8. A patient has a temperature of 35°C after prolonged cold exposure and is becoming confused. Which approach is appropriate?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Priority:** High

- **C.** Apply direct intense heat to numb limbs

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Baaki options kyun sahi nahi hain?** Direct intense heat burns cause kar sakti hai. Exercise aur alcohol condition worsen kar sakte hain.

</details>

### Q9. Which mechanism transfers heat from a warm body to a colder solid surface in direct contact?

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Priority:** Medium

- **A.** Evaporation
- **B.** Conduction
- **C.** Convection
- **D.** Radiation only

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: B. Conduction**

Direct contact se heat warmer surface se colder surface ki taraf transfer hoti hai. Cold examination table iska example hai.

**Baaki options kyun sahi nahi hain?** Convection moving air-fluid, radiation distance par heat transfer aur evaporation liquid-to-vapor process hai.

</details>

### Q10. Which heat transfer mechanism is involved when moving cool air carries heat away from the skin?

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Priority:** Medium

- **A.** Convection
- **B.** Conduction alone
- **C.** Osmosis
- **D.** Thermogenesis

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: A. Convection**

Moving air skin ke paas warm air replace karti hai. Cooler air hone par heat transfer convection se badh sakta hai.

**Baaki options kyun sahi nahi hain?** Conduction direct contact hai. Osmosis water movement hai. Thermogenesis heat production hai.

</details>

### Q12. A febrile patient is sweating heavily as the temperature begins falling. Which response is appropriate?

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

</details>

### Q13. A patient has low platelet count and mucosal bleeding. Why may rectal temperature measurement be inappropriate?

- **D.** Rectal temperature can never be measured in any patient

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

Rectal instrumentation mucosa injure kar sakti hai. Thrombocytopenia mein bleeding risk high ho sakta hai, isliye safer route choose karna chahiye.

**Baaki options kyun sahi nahi hain?** Rectal route selected cases mein use ho sakti hai. Hyperthermia inevitable nahi hai. Platelets hemostasis mein important hain.

### Q16. A patient has a cold application over a poorly perfused limb. Which finding requires immediate reassessment?

### Q19. Which term describes a core body temperature below 35°C?

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Priority:** Medium

- **A.** Pyrexia
- **B.** Hypothermia
- **C.** Hyperpyrexia
- **D.** Normothermia

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

### Q20. A patient has persistent fever despite antipyretic treatment. Which nursing response is appropriate?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Priority:** High

- **A.** Reassess clinical condition, hydration and possible cause and communicate relevant changes
- **B.** Repeat medication sooner than prescribed automatically
- **C.** Ignore all other symptoms if the temperature falls later
- **D.** Use the highest available heat exposure

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: A. Reassess clinical condition, hydration and possible cause and communicate relevant changes**

Antipyretic fever symptoms reduce kar sakti hai lekin cause treat nahi karti. Persistent fever mein patient ki overall condition aur treatment response assess karna chahiye.

**Baaki options kyun sahi nahi hain?** Unapproved redosing overdose risk badhati hai. Other symptoms important hain. Heat exposure worsen kar sakta hai.

</details>
