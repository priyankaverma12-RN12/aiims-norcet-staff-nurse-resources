### Q1. Which assessment is the most reliable starting point for pain intensity in a patient who can communicate?

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Priority:** Medium

- **A.** The location of the hospital bed
- **B.** The patient's self-report using a suitable scale
- **C.** The nurse's estimate based on facial expression alone
- **D.** Blood pressure alone

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: B. The patient's self-report using a suitable scale**

Pain personal experience hai. Patient ka self-report intensity assess karne ka primary source hai. Nurse associated signs aur cause bhi assess karti hai.

**Baaki options kyun sahi nahi hain?** Facial expression aur BP pain severity independently prove nahi karte. Bed location irrelevant hai.

</details>

### Q2. A patient reports burning, electric-shock pain after nerve injury. Which pain mechanism is most likely?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Priority:** High

- **A.** Neuropathic pain
- **B.** Purely visceral nociceptive pain in every case
- **C.** Normal absence of pain sensation
- **D.** Referred pain proven by the description alone

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: A. Neuropathic pain**

Nerve damage abnormal electrical signaling cause kar sakti hai. Burning ya electric-shock descriptors neuropathic pain suggest karte hain.

**Baaki options kyun sahi nahi hain?** Visceral pain organs se arise hoti hai. Referred pain location relationship describe karti hai. Pain absence nahi hai.

</details>

### Q3. In PQRST pain assessment, what does R commonly represent?

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Priority:** Medium

- **A.** Respiratory rate only
- **B.** Renal function
- **C.** Recovery score
- **D.** Region and radiation

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: D. Region and radiation**

Region pain ki location aur radiation pain kis direction mein spread hota hai batata hai. Ye information possible cause identify karne mein help karti hai.

**Baaki options kyun sahi nahi hain?** Respiratory rate separate vital sign hai. Renal function aur recovery score PQRST ke R ka usual meaning nahi hain.

</details>

### Q4. A patient has severe new abdominal pain despite normal heart rate and blood pressure. Which response is appropriate?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Priority:** High

- **C.** Accept the pain report and assess possible serious causes

**Baaki options kyun sahi nahi hain?** Normal BP pain exclude nahi karta. Judgment aur delay assessment ko compromise karte hain.

### Q5. Which pain scale commonly uses numbers from 0 to 10 for a patient who can understand numerical ratings?

- **C.** Numeric rating scale
- **D.** Glasgow Coma Scale

### Q6. A patient has a patient-controlled analgesia pump. Which teaching is most appropriate?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Priority:** High

- **A.** Disable the prescribed lockout interval
- **B.** Only the patient should activate the dose button unless a specifically authorized protocol applies
- **C.** Family should press the button whenever the patient sleeps
- **D.** Press repeatedly on behalf of an unresponsive patient

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: B. Only the patient should activate the dose button unless a specifically authorized protocol applies**

PCA patient ko pain ke according prescribed bolus request karne deti hai. Patient asleep ya oversedated ho toh self-activation naturally kam hoti hai. Proxy dosing overdose risk create kar sakti hai.

**Baaki options kyun sahi nahi hain?** Family or staff unauthorized activation safety barrier bypass karti hai. Lockout disable karna unsafe hai.

</details>

### Q7. Following opioid administration, a patient has worsening sedation before respiratory rate falls markedly. Which action is most appropriate?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Priority:** High

- **A.** Assess respiratory status and sedation urgently and follow the opioid safety protocol
- **B.** Treat sedation as proof of ideal pain control
- **C.** Give another opioid because the patient is quiet
- **D.** Stop all monitoring once pain improves

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: A. Assess respiratory status and sedation urgently and follow the opioid safety protocol**

Increasing sedation opioid respiratory depression ka early warning ho sakti hai. Nurse breathing aur responsiveness check karke protocol se intervention arrange karti hai.

**Baaki options kyun sahi nahi hain?** Quietness safety prove nahi karti. Additional opioid deterioration worsen kar sakti hai. Monitoring required hai.

</details>

### Q8. Which nonpharmacological measure can supplement prescribed postoperative pain treatment?

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Priority:** Medium

- **A.** Withhold every prescribed analgesic
- **B.** Force painful exercise without assessment
- **C.** Apply uncontrolled heat to an insensate area
- **D.** Positioning, relaxation and suitable incision support

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: D. Positioning, relaxation and suitable incision support**

Relaxation aur positioning muscle tension aur discomfort reduce kar sakte hain. Incision support movement ke waqt useful ho sakti hai.

**Baaki options kyun sahi nahi hain?** Analgesic withholding aur forced movement unnecessary pain badha sakte hain. Uncontrolled heat burns cause kar sakti hai.

</details>

### Q9. A patient is scheduled for a painful dressing change and has an appropriate prescribed analgesic. What is the best planning approach?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Priority:** High

- **A.** Administer it only after the procedure in every case
- **B.** Double the dose without authorization
- **C.** Skip reassessment because pain is anticipated
- **D.** Coordinate analgesic timing with expected onset before the procedure

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: D. Coordinate analgesic timing with expected onset before the procedure**

Medicine ka onset time consider karne se procedure ke waqt pain relief improve ho sakti hai. Nurse sedation aur response bhi monitor karti hai.

**Baaki options kyun sahi nahi hain?** After-procedure-only dosing avoidable pain cause kar sakti hai. Double dose unauthorized hai. Reassessment essential hai.

</details>

### Q10. Which finding is most typical of REM sleep?

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Priority:** Medium

- **A.** No cerebral activity
- **B.** Complete cessation of respiration
- **C.** Rapid eye movements and markedly reduced skeletal muscle tone
- **D.** Continuous high skeletal muscle tone in every person

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: C. Rapid eye movements and markedly reduced skeletal muscle tone**

REM mein brain activity aur dreaming prominent ho sakti hai aur most skeletal muscle tone suppress hoti hai. Breathing continue hoti hai.

**Baaki options kyun sahi nahi hain?** High tone defining feature nahi hai. Brain aur respiratory activity present rehti hain.

</details>

### Q11. A patient reports difficulty falling asleep due to repeated noise and nighttime interruptions. Which intervention addresses the likely modifiable cause?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Priority:** High

- **A.** Automatically prescribe a sedative without assessment
- **B.** Reduce nonessential noise and coordinate routine care around rest periods
- **C.** Provide stimulating activities at midnight
- **D.** Increase overhead lighting throughout the night

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: B. Reduce nonessential noise and coordinate routine care around rest periods**

Hospital environment sleep disrupt kar sakta hai. Quiet periods aur coordinated care rest improve karte hain, while essential observations continue hoti hain.

**Baaki options kyun sahi nahi hain?** Stimulation aur bright light sleep delay kar sakti hain. Sedative independent prescription nursing scope mein nahi hai.

</details>

</details>

### Q13. An older postoperative patient snores loudly, has witnessed pauses in breathing and receives sedatives. Which concern deserves assessment?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Priority:** High

- **A.** Possible sleep-disordered breathing with increased respiratory depression risk
- **B.** Normal sleep that requires no observation
- **C.** Proof of complete airway patency
- **D.** An indication to increase sedatives without review

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: A. Possible sleep-disordered breathing with increased respiratory depression risk**

Witnessed apneas obstructive sleep apnea ya other breathing problem suggest kar sakti hain. Sedatives respiratory risk worsen kar sakti hain, isliye monitoring aur review needed hai.

**Baaki options kyun sahi nahi hain?** Snoring alone airway safety prove nahi karta. Additional sedation unsafe ho sakti hai.

</details>

### Q15. A patient with reduced peripheral circulation is prescribed cold therapy. Which safety approach is appropriate?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Priority:** High

- **A.** Assume reduced sensation protects against cold injury
- **B.** Cover the site tightly and leave it unobserved
- **C.** Verify suitability, use protective measures and reassess skin and circulation
- **D.** Apply ice directly for an unlimited time

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: C. Verify suitability, use protective measures and reassess skin and circulation**

Poor circulation aur reduced sensation thermal injury risk badha sakte hain. Cold therapy patient-specific assessment aur time limits ke saath use hoti hai.

**Baaki options kyun sahi nahi hain?** Direct prolonged ice aur unattended tight wrapping tissue damage kar sakte hain.

</details>

### Q16. Which physiological effect is commonly associated with local cold application?

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Priority:** Medium

- **A.** Permanent loss of all sensation
- **B.** Vasoconstriction and reduced local metabolic activity
- **C.** Immediate sustained vasodilation in every case
- **D.** Increased local metabolism without limit

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: B. Vasoconstriction and reduced local metabolic activity**

Cold generally small vessels constrict aur nerve conduction slow kar sakta hai. Isse swelling aur pain temporarily reduce ho sakte hain.

**Baaki options kyun sahi nahi hain?** Vasodilation context-specific later response ho sakti hai. Unlimited metabolic rise aur permanent numbness desired effects nahi hain.

</details>

### Q17. Which response commonly occurs with suitable local superficial heat application?

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Priority:** Medium

- **D.** Permanent destruction of sensory nerves

### Q18. A patient with acute unexplained unilateral calf swelling requests massage and heat. What should the nurse do?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Priority:** High

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

### Q20. Which approach supports a patient experiencing persistent anxiety-related tension before sleep?

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Priority:** Medium

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>
