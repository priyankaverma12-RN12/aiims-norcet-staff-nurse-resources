### Q1. Which assessment is the most reliable starting point for pain intensity in a patient who can communicate?

**Difficulty:** 🟡 Medium · **Priority:** 🟡 IMPORTANT · Original practice

- **A.** The location of the hospital bed
- **B.** The patient's self-report using a suitable scale
- **C.** The nurse's estimate based on facial expression alone
- **D.** Blood pressure alone

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: B. The patient's self-report using a suitable scale**

Pain ek personal experience hai. Agar patient communicate kar sakta hai toh intensity ka sabse useful starting point uska apna report hai. Suitable scale patient ki language aur understanding ke hisaab se choose karo. Nurse location, onset, quality, function, associated symptoms aur possible cause bhi assess karti hai. Patient ka report respect karne ka matlab serious causes ki assessment skip karna nahi hai.

**Option-wise explanation:**
- **A (Galat):** Bed ki location pain intensity ke baare mein reliable information nahi deti.
- **B (Sahi):** Patient apne experience ko suitable scale se bata sakta hai, isliye yeh best starting point hai.
- **C (Galat):** Facial expression useful additional clue hai, lekin kuch patients severe pain mein bhi grimace nahi karte.
- **D (Galat):** BP pain, medicines aur bahut saare doosre factors se change ho sakta hai. Isse intensity alone establish nahi hoti.

**Exam clue:** Self-report first when possible, clinical assessment alongside.

### Q2. A patient reports burning, electric-shock pain after nerve injury. Which pain mechanism is most likely?

**Difficulty:** 🔴 Difficult · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD · Original practice

- **A.** Neuropathic pain
- **B.** Purely visceral nociceptive pain in every case
- **C.** Normal absence of pain sensation
- **D.** Referred pain proven by the description alone

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: A. Neuropathic pain**

Neuropathic pain tab ho sakti hai jab somatosensory nervous system, yani sensation carry karne wala system, disease ya injury se affected ho. Damaged nerves abnormal signals bhej sakti hain. Burning, shooting aur electric-shock jaisi sensations is mechanism ka clue hain. Nurse sensory changes aur injury history assess karti hai. Sirf description se diagnosis final nahi hota.

**Option-wise explanation:**
- **A (Sahi):** Nerve injury ke saath burning aur electric-shock pain neuropathic mechanism suggest karti hai.
- **B (Galat):** Visceral nociceptive pain internal organs ke pain receptors se arise hoti hai. Nerve injury ke context ko purely visceral kehna appropriate nahi hai.
- **C (Galat):** Patient painful sensation report kar raha hai, isliye absence of pain iska meaning nahi hai.
- **D (Galat):** Referred pain ka matlab source se different location par pain feel hona hai. Diye gaye descriptors alone referred pain prove nahi karte.

**Exam clue:** Burning and electric shocks after nerve injury suggest neuropathic pain.

### Q3. In PQRST pain assessment, what does R commonly represent?

**Difficulty:** 🟡 Medium · **Priority:** 🟡 IMPORTANT · Original practice

- **A.** Respiratory rate only
- **B.** Renal function
- **C.** Recovery score
- **D.** Region and radiation

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: D. Region and radiation**

PQRST ek structured symptom assessment mnemonic hai. P provocation/palliation, Q quality, R region/radiation, S severity aur T timing ko refer karta hai. Region se pata chalta hai pain kahan hai. Radiation batata hai kya pain kisi aur area tak ja raha hai. In findings ko associated symptoms aur examination ke saath interpret karo.

**Option-wise explanation:**
- **A (Galat):** Respiratory rate vital sign hai, lekin PQRST ka R usually respiratory rate nahi hai.
- **B (Galat):** Renal function relevant investigation ho sakti hai, par mnemonic ka R yeh nahi batata.
- **C (Galat):** Recovery score recovery assessment ka tool ho sakta hai, lekin PQRST ka R nahi hai.
- **D (Sahi):** Region aur radiation pain ki location aur spread ko describe karte hain. Yeh correct mnemonic expansion hai.

**Exam clue:** R = Region and Radiation.

### Q4. A patient has severe new abdominal pain despite normal heart rate and blood pressure. Which response is appropriate?

**Difficulty:** 🔴 Difficult · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD · Original practice

- **D.** Dismiss pain because vital signs are normal

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: C. Accept the pain report and assess possible serious causes**

New severe abdominal pain serious illness ka symptom ho sakta hai, even jab heart rate aur BP normal hon. Physiological response patient, timing aur medicines se vary karta hai. Nurse pain report accept karke onset, site, character, abdominal findings, vomiting, bleeding aur other red flags assess karti hai. Urgent clinical review arrange karo aur deterioration monitor karo.

**Option-wise explanation:**
- **A (Galat):** Attention-seeking assume karne se real illness miss ho sakti hai. Patient report ko objectively assess karo.
- **B (Galat):** Tachycardia aane tak wait karne se important diagnosis delay ho sakti hai.
- **C (Sahi):** Pain ko accept karke serious causes evaluate karna patient-centered aur safe response hai.
- **D (Galat):** Normal vital signs severe pain ya dangerous abdominal condition ko rule out nahi karte.

**Exam clue:** Normal vital signs do not rule out severe pain or serious disease.

### Q6. A patient has a patient-controlled analgesia pump. Which teaching is most appropriate?

**Difficulty:** 🔴 Difficult · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD · Original practice

- **A.** Disable the prescribed lockout interval
- **B.** Only the patient should activate the dose button unless a specifically authorized protocol applies
- **C.** Family should press the button whenever the patient sleeps
- **D.** Press repeatedly on behalf of an unresponsive patient

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: B. Only the patient should activate the dose button unless a specifically authorized protocol applies**

Patient-controlled analgesia ya PCA ek programmed pump hai jisme patient prescribed bolus request karta hai. Lockout ka matlab dose ke baad set interval tak next bolus automatically nahi milti. Patient ka alert enough hona ek safety protection hai. Unauthorized proxy dosing is protection ko bypass kar sakti hai. Nurse pump settings, pain, sedation aur breathing monitor karti hai. Special clinician-controlled analgesia sirf explicitly authorized separate protocol ke under hoti hai.

**Option-wise explanation:**
- **A (Galat):** Prescribed lockout disable karne se repeated doses aur overdose ka risk badhta hai.
- **B (Sahi):** Routine PCA button sirf patient press kare. Special authorized protocol ko routine family dosing se confuse mat karo.
- **C (Galat):** Sleeping patient ke behalf par family ka button press karna sedation aur respiratory depression ka risk badha sakta hai.
- **D (Galat):** Unresponsive patient ko proxy bolus dene ki jagah urgent airway, breathing aur sedation assessment chahiye.

**Exam clue:** PCA: patient presses, lockout stays enabled.

### Q7. Following opioid administration, a patient has worsening sedation before respiratory rate falls markedly. Which action is most appropriate?

**Difficulty:** 🔴 Difficult · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD · Original practice · **🟣 AIIMS Bathinda Nursing Officer, 29 September 2019 — related reported concept only**

- **A.** Assess respiratory status and sedation urgently and follow the opioid safety protocol
- **B.** Treat sedation as proof of ideal pain control
- **C.** Give another opioid because the patient is quiet
- **D.** Stop all monitoring once pain improves

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: A. Assess respiratory status and sedation urgently and follow the opioid safety protocol**

Opioids brain ke breathing drive ko suppress kar sakte hain. Increasing sedation, yani patient ka progressively zyada drowsy ya difficult to arouse hona, respiratory depression se pehle warning ho sakti hai. Nurse responsiveness, breathing rate, depth, oxygenation aur appropriate ventilation monitoring urgently assess karti hai. Further opioid safety protocol ke hisaab se hold/review karo aur urgent help ya naloxone as indicated arrange karo.

**Option-wise explanation:**
- **A (Sahi):** Urgent assessment aur opioid safety protocol preventable deterioration ko identify aur treat karne mein help karte hain.
- **B (Galat):** Sedation adequate analgesia ka reliable proof nahi hai. Excessive sedation unsafe ho sakti hai.
- **C (Galat):** Quiet patient oversedated ho sakta hai. Additional opioid breathing ko aur depress kar sakti hai.
- **D (Galat):** Pain improve hone par bhi adverse effects develop ho sakte hain. Monitoring continue honi chahiye.

**Exam clue:** Increasing sedation after opioid = respiratory safety check, not extra opioid.

### Q8. Which nonpharmacological measure can supplement prescribed postoperative pain treatment?

**Difficulty:** 🟡 Medium · **Priority:** 🟡 IMPORTANT · Original practice

- **A.** Withhold every prescribed analgesic
- **B.** Force painful exercise without assessment
- **C.** Apply uncontrolled heat to an insensate area
- **D.** Positioning, relaxation and suitable incision support

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: D. Positioning, relaxation and suitable incision support**

Postoperative pain incision aur tissue inflammation se ho sakti hai. Positioning uncomfortable tension kam kar sakti hai. Gentle relaxation anxiety aur muscle tension kam karne mein help karti hai. Coached incision support movement ya coughing ko easier bana sakti hai. Ye measures individualized analgesic plan ko supplement karte hain, replace nahi.

**Option-wise explanation:**
- **A (Galat):** Prescribed analgesia automatically withhold karna unnecessary pain aur poor function ka risk badha sakta hai.
- **B (Galat):** Painful exercise force karne se patient injure ho sakta hai. Activity plan assessment-based hona chahiye.
- **C (Galat):** Insensate skin temperature injury notice nahi kar paati. Uncontrolled heat burn kar sakti hai.
- **D (Sahi):** Safe positioning, relaxation aur suitable incision support multimodal comfort plan ke useful components hain.

**Exam clue:** Combine non-drug measures with indicated analgesia.

### Q10. Which finding is most typical of REM sleep?

**Difficulty:** 🟡 Medium · **Priority:** 🟡 IMPORTANT · Original practice · **🟣 UPSC ESIC Nursing Officer, 7 July 2024 — related reported concept only**

- **A.** No cerebral activity
- **B.** Complete cessation of respiration
- **C.** Rapid eye movements and markedly reduced skeletal muscle tone
- **D.** Continuous high skeletal muscle tone in every person

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: C. Rapid eye movements and markedly reduced skeletal muscle tone**

REM ka full form rapid eye movement hai. Is stage mein eyes rapidly move kar sakti hain aur brain activity relatively active hoti hai. Most skeletal muscles ka tone markedly reduced hota hai, jise atonia kehte hain. Breathing aur essential muscle activity continue rehti hai. Dreaming REM mein common hai, lekin dreams sirf REM tak limited nahi hain.

**Option-wise explanation:**
- **A (Galat):** REM mein cerebral activity absent nahi hoti. Brain active reh sakta hai.
- **B (Galat):** Respiration continue rehti hai, although pattern variable ho sakta hai. Complete cessation normal REM feature nahi hai.
- **C (Sahi):** Rapid eye movements aur reduced skeletal muscle tone REM ki characteristic findings hain.
- **D (Galat):** Most skeletal muscles mein tone reduce hota hai. Universal sustained high tone typical REM nahi hai.

**Exam clue:** REM = active brain, rapid eye movements, reduced skeletal tone.

### Q12. Which sleep hygiene instruction is generally appropriate?

- **D (Galat):** Roz several hours timing change karne se regular body-clock cues disturb ho sakte hain.

**Exam clue:** Consistent timing, quiet environment, limit late caffeine.

### Q14. Which intervention commonly supports comfort in a patient with dry lips and an NPO order?

**Difficulty:** 🟡 Medium · **Priority:** 🟡 IMPORTANT · Original practice

- **A.** Give unrestricted water despite the order
- **B.** Avoid all oral care
- **C.** Apply an unapproved irritating chemical
- **D.** Provide approved lip and oral care while maintaining aspiration precautions

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: D. Provide approved lip and oral care while maintaining aspiration precautions**

NPO ka matlab nil per os, yani oral intake restricted hai. Patient ko dry lips aur mouth discomfort ho sakta hai. Approved oral hygiene aur lip care comfort maintain kar sakti hai bina NPO order violate kiye. Patient ki swallowing ability, consciousness, aspiration risk aur specific preprocedure instructions check karo. Suitable suction-assisted care trained staff kar sakta hai if indicated.

**Option-wise explanation:**
- **A (Galat):** Unrestricted water NPO restriction violate kar sakta hai aur aspiration ya procedure safety risk la sakta hai.
- **B (Galat):** Oral care avoid karne se dryness, discomfort aur hygiene worsen ho sakte hain.
- **C (Galat):** Unapproved irritating chemical mucosa damage kar sakta hai.
- **D (Sahi):** Approved oral and lip care with aspiration precautions comfort aur safety ko balance karti hai.

### Q15. A patient with reduced peripheral circulation is prescribed cold therapy. Which safety approach is appropriate?

**Difficulty:** 🔴 Difficult · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD · Original practice

### Q16. Which physiological effect is commonly associated with local cold application?

**Difficulty:** 🟡 Medium · **Priority:** 🟡 IMPORTANT · Original practice

- **A.** Permanent loss of all sensation
- **B.** Vasoconstriction and reduced local metabolic activity
- **C.** Immediate sustained vasodilation in every case
- **D.** Increased local metabolism without limit

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: B. Vasoconstriction and reduced local metabolic activity**

Local controlled cold se blood vessels commonly narrow hoti hain. Is process ko vasoconstriction kehte hain. Tissue metabolic activity aur nerve conduction temporarily slow ho sakti hain, jisse pain aur swelling selected conditions mein kam ho sakte hain. Response exposure, tissue condition aur duration par depend karta hai. Excessive cold harmful hai.

**Option-wise explanation:**

- **C (Galat):** Immediate sustained vasodilation universal cold response nahi hai. Prolonged exposure mein vascular response change ho sakta hai.
- **D (Galat):** Cold usually metabolism slow karti hai, unlimited increase expected nahi hai.

**Exam clue:** Cold = vasoconstriction and slower local activity.

### Q17. Which response commonly occurs with suitable local superficial heat application?

### Q18. A patient with acute unexplained unilateral calf swelling requests massage and heat. What should the nurse do?

**Difficulty:** 🔴 Difficult · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD · Original practice

- **A.** Avoid the requested treatment until possible thrombosis and other causes are assessed
- **B.** Massage deeply immediately
- **C.** Apply intense heat without assessment
- **D.** Assume swelling is harmless if skin is intact

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: A. Avoid the requested treatment until possible thrombosis and other causes are assessed**

DVT ya deep vein thrombosis mein deep vein ke andar clot ban sakta hai. New unilateral calf swelling aur pain is condition ke possible clues hain, although doosre causes bhi ho sakte hain. Nurse massage aur heat defer karke urgent clinical assessment arrange karti hai. Sudden dyspnea, chest pain ya collapse emergency escalation maangta hai kyunki pulmonary embolism possible hai. Diagnosis clinical evaluation aur indicated tests se hota hai.

**Option-wise explanation:**
- **A (Sahi):** Possible thrombosis aur other causes assess hone tak treatment defer karna safest listed approach hai.
- **B (Galat):** Deep massage unexplained painful swelling mein inappropriate hai aur assessment ko delay kar sakti hai.
- **C (Galat):** Intense heat cause ko treat nahi karti aur tissue harm ka risk la sakti hai.
- **D (Galat):** Intact skin se deep vein clot exclude nahi hota.

**Exam clue:** New unilateral calf swelling: urgent assessment before massage or heat.

### Q20. Which approach supports a patient experiencing persistent anxiety-related tension before sleep?

**Difficulty:** 🟡 Medium · **Priority:** 🟡 IMPORTANT · Original practice

- **A.** Encourage rapid hyperventilation
- **B.** Guarantee immediate sleep without assessment
- **C.** Offer relaxation breathing and discuss concerns while assessing underlying causes
- **D.** Dismiss the patient's concerns

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: C. Offer relaxation breathing and discuss concerns while assessing underlying causes**

Anxiety-related tension sleep onset difficult bana sakti hai. Gentle slow breathing aur supportive conversation patient ko relax karne mein help kar sakti hain. Nurse patient ki concerns sunti hai aur pain, breathing symptoms, medicines, environment aur persistent anxiety jaise possible causes assess karti hai. Severe or persistent symptoms par suitable clinical support arrange karo. Sleep immediately aane ka guarantee nahi diya ja sakta.

**Option-wise explanation:**
- **A (Galat):** Rapid hyperventilation se dizziness aur discomfort badh sakta hai. Gentle controlled breathing preferable hai.
- **B (Galat):** Immediate sleep guarantee unrealistic hai aur underlying cause miss kar sakti hai.
- **C (Sahi):** Relaxation, supportive listening aur cause assessment patient-centered comfort approach hai.
- **D (Galat):** Concerns dismiss karne se trust aur assessment quality reduce ho sakti hai.

**Exam clue:** Support relaxation and assess causes instead of dismissing anxiety.
