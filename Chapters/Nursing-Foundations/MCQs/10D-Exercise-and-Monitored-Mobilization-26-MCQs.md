### Q01. A patient can begin shoulder flexion voluntarily but needs a therapist to support the arm through the final portion of the permitted movement. How is this exercise classified?

**Difficulty:** 🟡 Medium · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD · **Evidence:** Original practice · **Exam focus:** NORCET / Staff Nurse · **Topic:** Active-assisted ROM

- **A.** Passive range of motion
- **B.** Isometric exercise
- **C.** Active-assisted range of motion
- **D.** Unassisted active range of motion

<details><summary>✅ Correct answer and detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: C. Active-assisted range of motion**

**A. Kyun galat hai:** Passive movement mein external person poora movement karata hai. Yahan patient khud movement initiate kar raha hai.

**B. Kyun galat hai:** Isometric exercise mein muscle tension develop hoti hai lekin joint angle appreciably change nahi hota. Yahan shoulder move ho raha hai.

**C. Kyun sahi hai:** Active-assisted movement mein patient khud muscle activate karta hai aur jahan strength kam padti hai wahan external help milti hai. Is case mein dono components present hain.

**D. Kyun galat hai:** Unassisted active exercise mein external movement assistance nahi hoti. Is patient ko final portion mein support chahiye.

**Exam clue:** Patient ka voluntary effort aur external assistance dono identify karo.

**Clinical reference:** [Exercise and mobility guidance](https://openstax.org/books/clinical-nursing-skills/pages/9-2-assessing-mobility).
