### Q1. Which nursing theorist emphasized environmental conditions such as ventilation, cleanliness and light in promoting healing?

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Priority:** Medium

- **A.** Florence Nightingale
- **B.** Dorothea Orem
- **C.** Hildegard Peplau
- **D.** Betty Neuman

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: A. Florence Nightingale**

Nightingale ne environment ko patient recovery ka important part maana. Cleanliness, fresh air aur suitable surroundings nursing care mein relevant hain.

**Baaki options kyun sahi nahi hain?** Orem self-care, Peplau interpersonal relations aur Neuman systems theory se associated hain.

</details>

### Q2. Who developed the Self-Care Deficit Nursing Theory?

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Priority:** Medium

- **A.** Virginia Henderson
- **B.** Sister Callista Roy
- **C.** Jean Watson
- **D.** Dorothea Orem

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: D. Dorothea Orem**

Orem theory ke according nursing tab needed hoti hai jab person apni self-care needs independently meet nahi kar paata. Nurse assistance aur teaching provide karti hai.

**Baaki options kyun sahi nahi hain?** Henderson basic needs, Roy adaptation aur Watson caring theory se associated hain.

</details>

### Q3. A patient can bathe the upper body but needs assistance with lower-body hygiene. Which Orem nursing system best fits?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Priority:** High

- **A.** Supportive-educative system alone with no direct help
- **B.** A system unrelated to self-care
- **C.** Partly compensatory system
- **D.** Wholly compensatory system

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: C. Partly compensatory system**

Partly compensatory mein patient kuch self-care khud karta hai aur nurse remaining tasks mein help karti hai. Is case mein dono care participate karte hain.

**Baaki options kyun sahi nahi hain?** Wholly compensatory mein patient largely unable hota hai. Supportive-educative mainly teaching-guidance situation hai.

</details>

### Q4. An independent patient needs teaching to manage a new home injection safely. Which Orem system is most appropriate when the patient can perform self-care after instruction?

- **C.** Wholly compensatory

### Q9. Which theory describes the person as an open system interacting with stressors and lines of defense?

### Q19. A nurse is assigned a procedure outside their competence. What is the appropriate professional action?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Priority:** High

- **A.** Ask an untrained attendant to perform it
- **B.** Document completion without performing it
- **C.** Communicate the limitation and arrange competent supervision or assistance
- **D.** Perform it unsupervised to avoid appearing inexperienced

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: C. Communicate the limitation and arrange competent supervision or assistance**

Professional accountability mein competence limits recognize karna important hai. Nurse patient safety maintain karke training ya suitable support arrange karti hai.

**Baaki options kyun sahi nahi hain?** Untrained practice aur delegation unsafe hain. False documentation professional violation hai.

</details>

### Q20. Which activity is an example of continuing professional development?
