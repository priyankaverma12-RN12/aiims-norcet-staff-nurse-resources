### Q1. When giving a bed bath, which general sequence minimizes transfer of organisms from heavily soiled areas?

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Priority:** Medium

- **A.** Start with any area without changing contaminated supplies
- **B.** Clean relatively clean areas before more contaminated areas
- **C.** Clean the perineum before the face with the same cloth
- **D.** Use one visibly soiled cloth throughout

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: B. Clean relatively clean areas before more contaminated areas**

Relatively clean areas pehle clean karne se organisms heavily soiled areas se transfer hone ka risk kam hota hai. Nurse supplies change karti hai aur hand hygiene follow karti hai.

**Baaki options kyun sahi nahi hain?** Same contaminated cloth aur random sequence cross-contamination badha sakte hain.

</details>

### Q2. Which direction is generally used for female perineal cleansing?

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Priority:** Medium

- **A.** From front to back
- **B.** From anus toward urethra
- **C.** Back and forth using the same soiled surface
- **D.** Only around the anus

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: A. From front to back**

Front-to-back cleaning stool organisms ko urethral opening ki taraf le jaane ka risk kam karti hai. Har pass ke liye clean surface use karna chahiye.

**Baaki options kyun sahi nahi hain?** Reverse ya repeated contaminated wiping organisms transfer kar sakti hai. Complete hygiene mein relevant areas gently clean hote hain.

</details>

### Q3. After cleaning an uncircumcised patient's glans, which action is essential?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Priority:** High

- **A.** Leave the foreskin retracted indefinitely
- **B.** Apply a tight band around the glans
- **C.** Avoid reassessing swelling or discomfort
- **D.** Return the foreskin to its normal position

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: D. Return the foreskin to its normal position**

Retracted foreskin wapas na laayein toh glans ke peeche tight ring ban sakti hai. Isse swelling aur compromised blood flow, yani paraphimosis, ho sakti hai.

**Baaki options kyun sahi nahi hain?** Prolonged retraction aur tight band unsafe hain. Swelling urgent assessment maang sakti hai.

</details>

### Q4. An unconscious patient requires oral care. Which approach minimizes aspiration risk?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Priority:** High

- **A.** Keep the patient flat with fluid pooling in the pharynx
- **B.** Use a loose cotton swab that can detach in the mouth
- **C.** Use safe positioning and suction-assisted oral care according to assessment and policy
- **D.** Pour a large volume of water into the mouth

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: C. Use safe positioning and suction-assisted oral care according to assessment and policy**

Unconscious patient secretions safely swallow nahi kar paata. Suitable side or head position aur suction se fluid accumulation kam karne mein help milti hai.

**Baaki options kyun sahi nahi hain?** Large fluid, flat pooling aur loose swab aspiration risk badha sakte hain.

</details>

### Q5. A patient wears removable dentures. Which action protects the dentures during cleaning?

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Priority:** Medium

- **A.** Boil every denture regardless of material
- **B.** Wrap them in a disposable tissue without labeling
- **C.** Handle them over a lined basin and use appropriate denture care
- **D.** Clean them over an empty hard floor

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

Denture drop hone par break ho sakti hai. Lined basin aur labeled storage accidental loss aur damage reduce karte hain.

**Baaki options kyun sahi nahi hain?** Hard surface damage risk badhati hai. Boiling material distort kar sakti hai. Tissue mein denture accidentally discard ho sakti hai.

</details>

### Q6. A patient has diabetes, reduced foot sensation and thick toenails. Which approach is safest?

### Q7. Which bed-making action reduces airborne dispersal of microorganisms from used linen?

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Priority:** Medium

- **A.** Roll soiled linen inward without shaking and place it in the designated container
- **B.** Shake sheets vigorously in the room
- **C.** Place soiled linen on another patient's bed
- **D.** Carry wet linen against the uniform

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: A. Roll soiled linen inward without shaking and place it in the designated container**

Soiled linen ko gently contain karne se microorganisms aur contaminants spread kam ho sakte hain. Facility laundry handling process follow karna hai.

**Baaki options kyun sahi nahi hain?** Shaking aur contact with uniform or other bed contamination spread kar sakte hain.

</details>

### Q8. What is the primary purpose of an occupied bed-making technique?

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Priority:** Medium

- **A.** Transfer every patient to a stretcher regardless of stability
- **B.** Avoid cleaning the bed when occupied
- **C.** Prepare only a bed for a future admission
- **D.** Change linen safely while a patient remains in bed

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: D. Change linen safely while a patient remains in bed**

Occupied bed technique dependent patient ko bed mein rakhkar linen change karne mein help karti hai. Nurse safety, privacy aur alignment maintain karti hai.

**Baaki options kyun sahi nahi hain?** Unnecessary transfer risk add kar sakta hai. Bed hygiene still required hai. Empty admission bed unoccupied technique hai.

</details>

### Q9. A patient has an IV line and a urinary catheter during an occupied bed change. What should the nurse prioritize?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Priority:** High

- **B.** Pull the tubing to guide body movement
- **C.** Place all drainage bags above the patient
- **D.** Maintain device security and avoid traction while repositioning

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

Linen change mein lines snag ya pull ho sakti hain. Nurse tubing trace karti hai aur repositioning ke dauran device alignment aur drainage maintain karti hai.

**Baaki options kyun sahi nahi hain?** Routine disconnection infection risk badhati hai. Pulling aur inappropriate bag height unsafe hain.

### Q10. Why should wrinkles be minimized in the linen beneath an immobile patient?

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Priority:** Medium

- **A.** Wrinkles prevent all moisture accumulation
- **B.** Wrinkles replace the need for skin assessment
- **C.** Wrinkles can create localized pressure and friction
- **D.** Wrinkles improve pressure distribution in every case

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: C. Wrinkles can create localized pressure and friction**

Uneven linen se skin par focal pressure aur friction ho sakti hai. Smooth dry surface comfort aur prevention mein help karti hai.

**Baaki options kyun sahi nahi hain?** Wrinkles pressure relief device nahi hain. Moisture aur skin assessment separately manage karni hain.

</details>

### Q11. A patient has fragile skin and adhesive residue after dressing removal. Which action is safest?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Priority:** High

- **A.** Peel remaining adhesive rapidly against resistance
- **B.** Use a suitable gentle removal method according to skin and product guidance
- **C.** Scrub vigorously until the skin is red
- **D.** Use an unapproved solvent

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

Fragile skin mein aggressive removal skin tear cause kar sakti hai. Gentle technique skin barrier preserve karne mein help karti hai.

**Baaki options kyun sahi nahi hain?** Scrubbing, unapproved solvent aur forceful peeling injury risk badhate hain.

- **D.** Cover damp skin with a tight nonbreathable wrap indefinitely

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

### Q15. A patient with limited sensation requests a hot-water bottle for comfort. Which response is safest?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Priority:** High

### Q20. Which action best maintains dignity during a complete bed bath?

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Priority:** Medium

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>
