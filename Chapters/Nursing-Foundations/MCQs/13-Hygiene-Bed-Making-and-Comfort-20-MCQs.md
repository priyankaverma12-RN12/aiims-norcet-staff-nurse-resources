### Q1. When giving a bed bath, which general sequence minimizes transfer of organisms from heavily soiled areas?

**Difficulty:** 🟡 Medium · **Priority:** 🟡 IMPORTANT · Original practice

- **A.** Start with any area without changing contaminated supplies
- **B.** Clean relatively clean areas before more contaminated areas
- **C.** Clean the perineum before the face with the same cloth
- **D.** Use one visibly soiled cloth throughout

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: B. Clean relatively clean areas before more contaminated areas**

Bed bath mein patient ko bed par hi clean kiya jaata hai. Relatively clean areas pehle aur heavily soiled areas baad mein clean karne se organisms ek area se doosre area tak transfer hone ka risk kam hota hai. Cloth ka clean portion ya fresh supplies use karo aur soiling ke baad gloves aur hand hygiene facility policy ke hisaab se manage karo.

**Option-wise explanation:**
- **A (Galat):** Random sequence aur contaminated supplies se germs clean skin tak transfer ho sakte hain.
- **B (Sahi):** Relatively clean se more contaminated area ki taraf jaana cross-contamination kam karne ka useful principle hai.
- **C (Galat):** Perineum ke organisms same cloth se face tak pahunch sakte hain.
- **D (Galat):** Visibly dirty cloth ko repeatedly use karna contamination badhata hai.

**Exam clue:** Clean to less clean, change soiled supplies.

### Q2. Which direction is generally used for female perineal cleansing?

**Difficulty:** 🟡 Medium · **Priority:** 🟡 IMPORTANT · Original practice

- **A.** From front to back
- **B.** From anus toward urethra
- **C.** Back and forth using the same soiled surface
- **D.** Only around the anus

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: A. From front to back**

Female perineum genital aur anal area ke beech ki region hai. Urethral opening anus ke aage hoti hai. Stool ke bacteria urethra tak transfer ho sakte hain, isliye gentle front-to-back cleansing aur har pass ke liye fresh clean surface use karo. Routine care aur sterile catheter insertion alag procedures hain.

**Option-wise explanation:**
- **A (Sahi):** Front-to-back wiping fecal organisms ko urethra ki taraf le jaane ka risk kam karti hai.
- **B (Galat):** Anus se urethra ki taraf wiping contamination transfer kar sakti hai.
- **C (Galat):** Same soiled surface se back-and-forth wiping organisms redistribute kar sakti hai.
- **D (Galat):** Sirf anus clean karne se baaki indicated perineal hygiene incomplete reh sakti hai.

**Exam clue:** Front to back, fresh surface each pass.

### Q3. After cleaning an uncircumcised patient's glans, which action is essential?

**Difficulty:** 🔴 Difficult · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD · Original practice

- **A.** Leave the foreskin retracted indefinitely
- **B.** Apply a tight band around the glans
- **C.** Avoid reassessing swelling or discomfort
- **D.** Return the foreskin to its normal position

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: D. Return the foreskin to its normal position**

Foreskin penis ke glans yani head ko cover karne wali skin hai. Adult mein agar woh easily retract hoti hai toh gentle cleaning ke baad usse normal position mein wapas lao. Retracted foreskin glans ke peeche tight ho jaaye toh swelling aur blood flow compromise ho sakta hai, jise paraphimosis kehte hain. Foreskin ko forcefully retract mat karo. Agar wapas na aaye ya painful swelling ho toh urgent medical assessment chahiye.

**Option-wise explanation:**
- **A (Galat):** Retracted foreskin ko indefinitely chhodne se paraphimosis ho sakti hai.
- **B (Galat):** Tight band venous drainage aur blood supply ko compromise kar sakta hai.
- **C (Galat):** Swelling aur pain vascular compromise ke warning signs ho sakte hain, inhe ignore mat karo.
- **D (Sahi):** Foreskin ko normal position mein return karna constricting ring formation ka risk kam karta hai.

**Exam clue:** Retract gently only if possible, replace after cleaning.

### Q6. A patient has diabetes, reduced foot sensation and thick toenails. Which approach is safest?

**Option-wise explanation:**

- **C (Galat):** Deep corner cutting ingrown nail, bleeding ya tissue injury cause kar sakti hai.
- **D (Galat):** Very hot water reduced sensation wale foot ko burn kar sakta hai.

**Exam clue:** Neuropathy = assess first, avoid heat and sharp self-care.

### Q7. Which bed-making action reduces airborne dispersal of microorganisms from used linen?

**Difficulty:** 🟡 Medium · **Priority:** 🟡 IMPORTANT · Original practice

- **A.** Roll soiled linen inward without shaking and place it in the designated container
- **B.** Shake sheets vigorously in the room
- **C.** Place soiled linen on another patient's bed
- **D.** Carry wet linen against the uniform

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: A. Roll soiled linen inward without shaking and place it in the designated container**

Used linen par microorganisms aur body fluids ho sakte hain. Shaking se contaminants hawa aur nearby surfaces tak spread ho sakte hain. Linen ko gently inward contain karke designated bag mein patient care area mein rakho. PPE aur hand hygiene facility policy ke mutabik use karo.

**Option-wise explanation:**
- **A (Sahi):** Gently rolling aur designated container contamination spread kam karte hain.
- **B (Galat):** Vigorous shaking contaminated particles disperse kar sakti hai.
- **C (Galat):** Doosre patient ke bed par dirty linen rakhne se cross-contamination hoti hai.
- **D (Galat):** Uniform se wet linen contact clothing contamination ka risk badhata hai.

**Exam clue:** Contain soiled linen at point of use, never shake.

**Clinical reference:** [CDC linen management](https://www.cdc.gov/healthcare-associated-infections/hcp/cleaning-global/appendix-d.html)

### Q16. Which action is appropriate when a patient asks for assistance with grooming but can perform part of the task?

**Difficulty:** 🟡 Medium · **Priority:** 🟡 IMPORTANT · Original practice

- **A.** Choose grooming preferences without asking
- **B.** Encourage the patient to do safe portions and assist with the remainder
- **C.** Perform every task without consulting the patient
- **D.** Refuse all assistance until independence returns

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: B. Encourage the patient to do safe portions and assist with the remainder**

Grooming patient ki personal appearance aur hygiene care ka part hai. Agar patient kuch steps independently kar sakta hai, toh nurse uski retained abilities use karne mein help karti hai. Preferred routine, fatigue, range of movement aur safety assess karke sirf required assistance do.

- **C (Galat):** Unnecessary full assistance patient ki remaining skills use karne ka chance kam karti hai.
- **D (Galat):** All assistance refuse karne se hygiene aur comfort needs unmet reh sakti hain.

### Q17. A patient becomes breathless and exhausted during a bed bath. What should the nurse do?

**Difficulty:** 🔴 Difficult · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD · Original practice

- **A.** Assume breathlessness is expected and ignore it
- **B.** Pause care, assess breathing and vital signs, and modify the plan
- **C.** Complete the bath rapidly regardless of symptoms
- **D.** Lay the patient flat without assessment

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: B. Pause care, assess breathing and vital signs, and modify the plan**

Bed bath bhi physical activity hai. Breathlessness aur exhaustion limited cardiopulmonary reserve ya acute deterioration ka signal ho sakte hain. Activity pause karo, patient ko safely support karo, airway, breathing, oxygen saturation aur vital signs assess karo. Severe ya persistent symptoms mein timely urgent escalation karo. Stable hone par rest breaks aur modified hygiene plan consider karo.

**Option-wise explanation:**
- **A (Galat):** New breathlessness ko routine maan kar ignore karna deterioration miss kar sakta hai.
- **B (Sahi):** Care pause aur assessment immediate safety prioritize karte hain.
- **C (Galat):** Bath jaldi finish karna oxygen demand aur distress badha sakta hai.
- **D (Galat):** Bina assessment flat karne se kuch patients mein breathing worsen ho sakti hai.

**Exam clue:** New dyspnea during hygiene: stop, assess, modify/escalate.

### Q20. Which action best maintains dignity during a complete bed bath?

**Difficulty:** 🟡 Medium · **Priority:** 🟡 IMPORTANT · Original practice

- **A.** Invite unnecessary observers
- **B.** Complete care without explaining to an alert patient
- **C.** Explain the care, provide privacy and expose only the area being cleaned
- **D.** Keep the entire body uncovered throughout

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: C. Explain the care, provide privacy and expose only the area being cleaned**

Complete bed bath mein patient vulnerable feel kar sakta hai. Nurse care explain karti hai, consent aur preferences respect karti hai, curtain ya door se privacy maintain karti hai aur sirf jis area ko clean kar rahi hai usse expose karti hai. Isse dignity, warmth aur comfort protect hote hain.

**Option-wise explanation:**
- **A (Galat):** Unnecessary observers patient ki privacy compromise kar sakte hain.
- **B (Galat):** Alert patient ko explanation na dena informed participation reduce karta hai.
- **C (Sahi):** Explanation, privacy aur limited exposure patient-centered care ke key steps hain.
- **D (Galat):** Whole body continuously expose karna unnecessary heat loss aur embarrassment cause kar sakta hai.

**Exam clue:** Explain, obtain cooperation, cover all but the care area.
