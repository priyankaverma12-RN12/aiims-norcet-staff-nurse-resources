### Q1. A patient has orthopnea and is hemodynamically stable. Which position usually facilitates breathing?

**Difficulty:** 🔴 Difficult · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD

- **A.** Trendelenburg
- **B.** Prone with the face unsupported
- **C.** High Fowler position
- **D.** Flat supine

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: C. High Fowler position**

Orthopnea ka matlab letne par saans lene mein dikkat hai. Stable patient ko High Fowler mein upright karne se diaphragm ki movement aur chest expansion aasaan ho sakti hai. Saans, oxygen saturation aur BP reassess karein. Severe distress mein emergency assessment ko delay na karein.

**Option-wise explanation:**
- **A (Galat):** Trendelenburg mein sir neeche hota hai. Isse abdomen diaphragm ki taraf shift ho sakta hai aur orthopnea badh sakti hai.
- **B (Galat):** Unsupported face-down position mein airway aur observation difficult ho sakte hain. Supervised therapeutic proning alag intervention hai.
- **C (Sahi):** High Fowler mein chest upright hoti hai. Gravity abdominal pressure ko diaphragm se door karne mein madad karti hai, isliye stable orthopnea mein yeh best listed choice hai.
- **D (Galat):** Flat supine mein orthopnea badh sakti hai kyunki diaphragm excursion aur pulmonary congestion ki symptoms worsen ho sakti hain.

**Exam clue:** Orthopnea + stable BP = upright position, phir ABC reassessment.

### Q2. Which position is commonly used for a rectal examination or a routine cleansing enema when appropriate?

**Difficulty:** 🟡 Medium · **Priority:** 🟡 IMPORTANT · **🟣 RRB Staff Nurse 21 July 2019 Shift I — related reported concept** · **[🟣 RRB Staff Nurse 20 July 2019 Shift III — related reported concept](https://testbook.com/question-answer/the-most-suitable-position-for-giving-an-enema-is--61ae0cbd6056094d7be1fa44)** · **[🟣 HPRCA Assistant Staff Nurse 20 March 2026 morning Q103 — related reported concept](https://www.scribd.com/document/1045794040/20-March-HPRCA-Nursing-Staff-paper)**

- **A.** Reverse Trendelenburg
- **B.** Left lateral Sims position
- **C.** High Fowler position
- **D.** Lithotomy in every case

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: B. Left lateral Sims position**

Rectal examination ya routine cleansing enema ke liye left lateral Sims mein patient left side par semi-prone rehta hai aur upper leg flexed hoti hai. Rectum tak access milta hai aur fluid rectosigmoid direction mein pass ho sakta hai. Privacy, contraindications aur authorized procedure check karein.

**Option-wise explanation:**
- **A (Galat):** Reverse Trendelenburg poore bed ko head-up tilt karta hai. Yeh rectal access ka usual choice nahi hai.
- **B (Sahi):** Left lateral Sims rectum tak access deta hai aur descending-sigmoid colon ki direction ke saath fluid passage support karta hai.
- **C (Galat):** High Fowler mein patient upright hota hai. Is position se rectal access aur enema retention generally difficult hote hain.
- **D (Galat):** Lithotomy pelvic procedures mein useful hai, lekin har rectal examination ya routine enema ke liye zaroori nahi hai.

**Exam clue:** Enema = left lateral Sims, jab clinically suitable ho.

### Q3. Which position is commonly used for a pelvic examination with the legs supported in stirrups?

- **B.** Prone
- **C.** Sims
- **D.** Orthopneic position

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

Lithotomy mein patient supine hota hai, hips aur knees flexed hote hain aur legs supported stirrups mein hoti hain. Pelvic access milta hai. Dono legs coordinated tareeke se support karein aur pressure, nerve stretch aur dignity ka dhyan rakhein.

**Option-wise explanation:**
- **A (Sahi):** Lithotomy pelvic examination ke liye suitable hai kyunki perineum accessible hota hai aur legs supported rehti hain.
- **B (Galat):** Prone mein patient abdomen par hota hai. Routine pelvic examination ke described access ke liye yeh suitable nahi hai.
- **C (Galat):** Sims side/semi-prone position hai. Ismein dono legs stirrups mein nahi hoti hain.
- **D (Galat):** Orthopneic mein patient upright lean karta hai. Yeh breathing support ke liye hai, pelvic stirrup examination ke liye nahi.

### Q4. A patient with a suspected cervical spinal injury must be repositioned. Which approach is appropriate?

**Difficulty:** 🔴 Difficult · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD

- **A.** Turn the head independently while the trunk remains fixed
- **B.** Pull the patient upright by both arms
- **C.** Allow unsupported neck flexion during transfer
- **D.** Maintain spinal alignment and use coordinated trained assistance according to trauma protocol

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: D. Maintain spinal alignment and use coordinated trained assistance according to trauma protocol**

Suspected cervical injury mein unstable vertebral injury ho sakti hai. Unnecessary rotation ya flexion secondary neurological injury ka risk badha sakti hai. Trained team local trauma protocol se spine alignment maintain kare. Life-saving airway ya resuscitation ko delay nahi karna chahiye.

**Option-wise explanation:**
- **A (Galat):** Head ko trunk se alag turn karne se cervical rotation ho sakti hai. Suspected injury mein yeh unsafe hai.
- **B (Galat):** Arms se kheenchkar upright karna spine aur shoulder par uncontrolled force laga sakta hai.
- **C (Galat):** Unsupported neck flexion possible unstable segment ko move kar sakti hai.
- **D (Sahi):** Coordinated trained assistance aur alignment protection uncontrolled movement ko minimize karte hain, jab reposition clinically required ho.

**Exam clue:** Suspected spinal injury = alignment + trained team + trauma protocol.

**Clinical reference:** [NICE NG41 spinal injury](https://www.nice.org.uk/guidance/ng41/chapter/Recommendations)

### Q5. Which body mechanics technique reduces spinal strain when moving an object within safe lifting limits?

**Difficulty:** 🟡 Medium · **Priority:** 🟡 IMPORTANT · **[🟣 AIIMS Bhatinda Nursing Officer, 29 September 2019 — related memory-based concept](https://testbook.com/question-answer/a-principle-of-good-body-mechanics-includes-which--617656cab2cc971558e83de3)**

- **A.** Hold the load far away and twist the trunk
- **B.** Keep knees rigid while bending from the waist
- **C.** Lift with one hand while reaching across the body
- **D.** Keep the load close and use a stable base with hip and knee movement

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: D. Keep the load close and use a stable base with hip and knee movement**

Object lifting mein load ka weight aur body se distance dono spinal torque par asar karte hain. Load paas rakhne se moment arm kam hota hai. Stable base aur hip-knee movement help karte hain. Yeh patient ke manual lifting ko safe banane ki guarantee nahi hai. Patient handling mein assessed mechanical aids use karein.

**Option-wise explanation:**
- **A (Galat):** Door load aur trunk twist dono lumbar strain badhate hain. Load jitna door, moment arm utna bada.
- **B (Galat):** Rigid knees aur waist se excessive bending back muscles par strain badha sakti hai.
- **C (Galat):** One-hand cross-body reach asymmetrical load aur rotation badhata hai.
- **D (Sahi):** Close load, stable stance aur hip-knee movement object lifting mein control aur leverage improve karte hain.

**Exam clue:** Object lifting ≠ dependent patient lifting. Mechanical aids ko priority dein.

### Q6. A patient requires maximal assistance and cannot bear weight. Which transfer method is safest?

**Difficulty:** 🔴 Difficult · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD

- **A.** Attempt an unsupported stand-pivot transfer
- **B.** Have the patient hold the nurse's neck for support
- **C.** Use an appropriate mechanical lift with trained staff after assessment
- **D.** Ask one nurse to lift the patient manually

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: C. Use an appropriate mechanical lift with trained staff after assessment**

Non-weight-bearing dependent patient apne legs se transfer support nahi kar sakta. Assessment ke baad compatible full-body mechanical lift, suitable sling aur trained staff use karein. Equipment capacity, lines, skin aur patient comfort check karein.

**Option-wise explanation:**
- **A (Galat):** Unsupported stand-pivot ke liye adequate weight bearing aur control chahiye. Is patient mein fall ka risk hai.
- **B (Galat):** Nurse ki neck pakadne se nurse aur patient dono injured ho sakte hain. Yeh approved transfer support nahi hai.
- **C (Sahi):** Suitable mechanical lift body weight support karta hai aur manual lifting strain kam karta hai. Trained staff aur assessed sling zaroori hain.
- **D (Galat):** Ek nurse ka manual dependent lift patient fall aur musculoskeletal injury ka high-risk method hai.

**Exam clue:** Cannot bear weight + maximal assistance = assessed mechanical lift.

**Clinical reference:** [OSHA safe patient handling](https://www.osha.gov/healthcare/safe-patient-handling) · [OpenStax transfer and aids](https://openstax.org/books/clinical-nursing-skills/pages/9-3-transferring-patients)

### Q7. When adjusting a walker for a patient, which finding generally indicates a suitable handgrip height?

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

Walker grips generally standing mein wrist crease ke aas-paas set hote hain, jisse shoulders relaxed aur elbows slightly flexed reh sakein. Therapist patient ki footwear, balance aur specific device ke hisaab se fitting karta hai.

- **C (Galat):** Locked elbows aur elevated shoulders poor fit ya excessive height suggest karte hain.
- **D (Galat):** Marked trunk flexion walker too low ya poor setup indicate kar sakta hai. Isse balance aur back strain affect ho sakte hain.

**Exam clue:** Walker grip fit = upright, relaxed shoulders, slight elbow bend.

- **C.** Both hands holding the cane behind the body
- **D.** Either hand without evaluating gait

Usual unilateral weakness mein cane stronger ya contralateral hand mein hold ki jaati hai. Cane aur weaker leg ka coordinated movement base of support badhata hai aur affected hip ki demand reduce kar sakta hai. Gait pattern therapist se individualize karein.

**Option-wise explanation:**

- **C (Galat):** Cane body ke peeche dono hands se pakadna safe forward support aur normal gait pattern ko compromise karta hai.
- **D (Galat):** Bina gait assessment kisi bhi side use karna unsafe ho sakta hai, especially poor balance ya upper-limb weakness mein.

### Q9. Which crutch instruction helps avoid pressure-related nerve injury?

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

Axillary crutch par body weight handgrips se transfer hona chahiye. Axilla mein brachial plexus aur vessels compression se hurt ho sakte hain. Proper clearance, elbow bend aur crutch fitting assessment zaroori hai.

**Option-wise explanation:**
- **A (Sahi):** Handgrips load ko arms ke through distribute karte hain aur axillary compression se bachate hain.
- **B (Galat):** Entire body weight axillary pad par dene se nerve compression aur pain ya weakness ho sakti hai.
- **C (Galat):** Armpit ke bilkul tight crutches fit rakhna axillary tissues par pressure badhata hai.
- **D (Galat):** Fit check na karne se unsuitable height, unstable gait aur pressure injury ka risk badh sakta hai.

**Exam clue:** Crutch weight = hands, not armpits.

### Q11. Which exercise involves a caregiver moving the patient's joint without active muscle effort from the patient?

**Difficulty:** 🟡 Medium · **Priority:** 🟡 IMPORTANT

- **A.** Resisted isometric exercise
- **B.** Independent ambulation
- **C.** Passive range-of-motion exercise
- **D.** Active range of motion

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: C. Passive range-of-motion exercise**

Passive ROM mein patient joint ko actively contract karke move nahi karta. Caregiver ya prescribed device gentle movement deta hai. Joint ko support karein aur pain, resistance, injury aur restrictions ka dhyan rakhein. Forceful stretch nahi karein.

**Option-wise explanation:**
- **A (Galat):** Resisted isometric exercise mein muscle tension generate hoti hai, joint angle usually change nahi hota. Yeh passive movement nahi hai.
- **B (Galat):** Independent ambulation active walking hai, caregiver-driven joint movement nahi.
- **C (Sahi):** Passive ROM mein external assistance joint move karti hai jab patient active effort nahi karta.
- **D (Galat):** Active ROM mein patient apni muscle power se joint move karta hai. Isliye given description se match nahi karta.

**Exam clue:** Passive = caregiver moves. Active = patient moves.

### Q12. An immobile patient has a painful swollen calf before scheduled leg exercises. What should the nurse do?

**Difficulty:** 🔴 Difficult · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD

- **A.** Ignore the finding because immobility is expected

- **C.** Massage the calf vigorously before exercise
- **D.** Apply forceful stretching to reduce swelling

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: B. Withhold manipulation of the affected calf and arrange prompt assessment for possible thrombosis**

Immobility venous stasis badha sakti hai. New painful swollen calf DVT ya doosri acute condition ka warning sign hai. Routine manipulation rok kar prompt clinical assessment arrange karein. Sudden dyspnea, chest pain ya collapse ho to emergency escalation karein. Sirf calf symptoms se DVT confirm nahi hota.

**Option-wise explanation:**
- **A (Galat):** New unilateral swelling expected immobility symptom maan kar ignore nahi karni chahiye.
- **B (Sahi):** Manipulation rokna aur possible thrombosis ki timely assessment patient safety priority hai. Diagnosis aur activity plan clinician determine karega.
- **C (Galat):** Vigorous massage suspected thrombosis ya tissue injury mein inappropriate hai aur assessment delay karta hai.
- **D (Galat):** Forceful stretching swelling ka cause treat nahi karti aur pain ya tissue injury badha sakti hai.

**Exam clue:** New painful swollen calf = stop manipulation, assess urgently.

### Q13. Which positioning principle helps prevent foot drop during prolonged bed rest?

**Difficulty:** 🟡 Medium · **Priority:** 🟡 IMPORTANT · **[🟣 AIIMS Raipur Nursing Officer, 9 September 2017 — related reported concept only](https://testbook.com/question-answer/which-comfort-device-is-used-to-prevent-foot-drop--62d788109982d749dafefa33)**

- **A.** Avoid inspecting the heels
- **B.** Maintain neutral ankle alignment using appropriate support and reassess the skin
- **C.** Keep the ankles plantarflexed continuously
- **D.** Place heavy bedding directly against the toes

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: B. Maintain neutral ankle alignment using appropriate support and reassess the skin**

Prolonged bed rest mein ankle plantarflexed rehne se soft tissue shortening aur contracture develop ho sakti hai. Neutral alignment ko suitable support se maintain karein. Foot drop neurological weakness se bhi ho sakta hai, isliye support alone treatment nahi hai. Heel offloading aur skin checks zaroori hain.

**Option-wise explanation:**
- **A (Galat):** Heel inspection avoid karne se pressure damage early identify nahi hoga.
- **B (Sahi):** Neutral ankle support prolonged plantarflexion ko limit karta hai. Skin, circulation aur comfort reassessment se device-related pressure harm avoid karte hain.
- **C (Galat):** Continuous plantarflexion Achilles-calf shortening aur ankle contracture ka risk badhata hai.
- **D (Galat):** Heavy bedding toes par force daal kar plantarflexion aur pressure injury badha sakti hai.

**Exam clue:** Neutral ankle alignment + skin checks, no prolonged plantarflexion.
