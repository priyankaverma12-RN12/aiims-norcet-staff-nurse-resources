### Q1. A patient has orthopnea and is hemodynamically stable. Which position usually facilitates breathing?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Priority:** High

- **A.** Trendelenburg
- **B.** Prone with the face unsupported
- **C.** High Fowler position
- **D.** Flat supine

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: C. High Fowler position**

Upright position mein abdominal organs diaphragm par kam pressure daal sakte hain aur lung expansion easier ho sakti hai. Nurse comfort aur vital signs assess karti hai.

**Baaki options kyun sahi nahi hain?** Flat aur head-down positions orthopnea worsen kar sakti hain. Unsupported prone positioning airway safety compromise kar sakti hai.

</details>

### Q2. Which position is commonly used for a rectal examination or a routine cleansing enema when appropriate?

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Priority:** Medium

- **A.** Reverse Trendelenburg
- **B.** Left lateral Sims position
- **C.** High Fowler position
- **D.** Lithotomy in every case

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: B. Left lateral Sims position**

Left lateral Sims position rectal access aur descending colon ke anatomical direction mein solution passage facilitate kar sakti hai.

**Baaki options kyun sahi nahi hain?** Fowler upright hai. Lithotomy pelvic procedures mein useful ho sakti hai. Reverse Trendelenburg rectal access ka usual position nahi hai.

</details>

### Q3. Which position is commonly used for a pelvic examination with the legs supported in stirrups?

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Priority:** Medium

- **A.** Lithotomy
- **B.** Prone
- **C.** Sims
- **D.** Orthopneic position

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: A. Lithotomy**

Lithotomy mein patient back par hoti hai aur hips-knees flexed aur legs supported hoti hain. Privacy aur safe leg support important hain.

**Baaki options kyun sahi nahi hain?** Prone face-down hai. Sims side-lying hai. Orthopneic sitting-forward position hai.

</details>

### Q4. A patient with a suspected cervical spinal injury must be repositioned. Which approach is appropriate?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Priority:** High

- **A.** Turn the head independently while the trunk remains fixed
- **B.** Pull the patient upright by both arms
- **C.** Allow unsupported neck flexion during transfer
- **D.** Maintain spinal alignment and use coordinated trained assistance according to trauma protocol

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: D. Maintain spinal alignment and use coordinated trained assistance according to trauma protocol**

Suspected spine injury mein unnecessary movement cord damage worsen kar sakti hai. Trained team alignment maintain karke coordinated repositioning karti hai.

**Baaki options kyun sahi nahi hain?** Independent twisting, arm traction aur neck flexion alignment disturb kar sakte hain.

</details>

### Q5. Which body mechanics technique reduces spinal strain when moving an object within safe lifting limits?

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Priority:** Medium

- **A.** Hold the load far away and twist the trunk
- **B.** Keep knees rigid while bending from the waist
- **C.** Lift with one hand while reaching across the body
- **D.** Keep the load close and use a stable base with hip and knee movement

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: D. Keep the load close and use a stable base with hip and knee movement**

Load body ke paas ho toh spine par leverage kam padta hai. Stable stance aur legs ka use strain reduce kar sakta hai. Patient transfers ke liye mechanical aids bhi use karne chahiye.

**Baaki options kyun sahi nahi hain?** Far load, twisting aur awkward reaching back strain badhate hain.

</details>

### Q6. A patient requires maximal assistance and cannot bear weight. Which transfer method is safest?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Priority:** High

- **A.** Attempt an unsupported stand-pivot transfer
- **B.** Have the patient hold the nurse's neck for support
- **C.** Use an appropriate mechanical lift with trained staff after assessment
- **D.** Ask one nurse to lift the patient manually

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: C. Use an appropriate mechanical lift with trained staff after assessment**

Non-weight-bearing dependent patient manual transfer mein fall aur caregiver injury ka risk high hai. Suitable lift aur trained team safer transfer provide karte hain.

**Baaki options kyun sahi nahi hain?** Single manual lift, unsupported standing aur neck holding unsafe ho sakte hain.

</details>

### Q7. When adjusting a walker for a patient, which finding generally indicates a suitable handgrip height?

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Priority:** Medium

- **A.** Grips at eye level
- **B.** Slight elbow flexion while standing upright with hands on the grips
- **C.** Fully elevated shoulders and locked elbows
- **D.** Marked forward trunk flexion to reach the grips

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: B. Slight elbow flexion while standing upright with hands on the grips**

Walker height aisi honi chahiye ki patient upright rahe aur elbows slightly bend ho. Isse weight transfer aur control easier hota hai.

**Baaki options kyun sahi nahi hain?** Raised shoulders, rigid elbows aur excessive bending incorrect adjustment suggest karte hain.

</details>

### Q8. A patient has unilateral leg weakness and is learning to walk with a cane. In which hand is the cane usually held?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Priority:** High

- **A.** The hand opposite the weaker leg
- **B.** The hand on the weaker side in all cases
- **C.** Both hands holding the cane behind the body
- **D.** Either hand without evaluating gait

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: A. The hand opposite the weaker leg**

Opposite hand mein cane weak leg ke stance phase ko support karti hai. Physiotherapist patient-specific gait instruction deta hai.

**Baaki options kyun sahi nahi hain?** Same-side use kuch situations mein modified ho sakta hai lekin usual pattern opposite hand hai. Behind-body support unsafe hai. Gait assessment needed hai.

</details>

### Q9. Which crutch instruction helps avoid pressure-related nerve injury?

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Priority:** Medium

- **A.** Support body weight through the handgrips rather than the axillae
- **B.** Rest the entire body weight on the axillary pads
- **C.** Set crutches tightly against the armpits
- **D.** Use crutches without checking fit

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: A. Support body weight through the handgrips rather than the axillae**

Axilla mein nerves aur vessels superficial hoti hain. Weight hands par transfer karne se axillary compression risk kam hota hai.

**Baaki options kyun sahi nahi hain?** Armpit weight bearing aur tight fit nerve injury cause kar sakte hain. Fit assessment essential hai.

</details>

### Q10. A patient using crutches is permitted partial weight bearing. What determines the appropriate gait pattern?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Priority:** High

- **A.** A single standard gait for every patient
- **B.** The patient's age alone
- **C.** The crutch material alone
- **D.** The prescribed weight-bearing restriction, balance and therapist's assessment

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: D. The prescribed weight-bearing restriction, balance and therapist's assessment**

Gait pattern patient ke affected limb par allowed load aur balance par depend karta hai. Nurse prescribed restriction reinforce karti hai.

**Baaki options kyun sahi nahi hain?** Universal gait unsuitable ho sakti hai. Age aur material alone weight-bearing capacity determine nahi karte.

</details>

### Q11. Which exercise involves a caregiver moving the patient's joint without active muscle effort from the patient?

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Priority:** Medium

- **A.** Resisted isometric exercise
- **B.** Independent ambulation
- **C.** Passive range-of-motion exercise
- **D.** Active range of motion

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: C. Passive range-of-motion exercise**

Passive ROM mein caregiver joint ko safe range mein move karta hai jab patient actively movement nahi kar paata. Isse mobility maintain karne mein help mil sakti hai.

**Baaki options kyun sahi nahi hain?** Active ROM patient khud karta hai. Isometric mein tension produce hoti hai. Ambulation walking hai.

</details>

### Q12. An immobile patient has a painful swollen calf before scheduled leg exercises. What should the nurse do?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Priority:** High

- **A.** Ignore the finding because immobility is expected
- **B.** Withhold manipulation of the affected calf and arrange prompt assessment for possible thrombosis
- **C.** Massage the calf vigorously before exercise
- **D.** Apply forceful stretching to reduce swelling

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: B. Withhold manipulation of the affected calf and arrange prompt assessment for possible thrombosis**

Unilateral painful swelling DVT ka warning sign ho sakta hai. Nurse manipulation avoid karke urgent clinical assessment arrange karti hai.

**Baaki options kyun sahi nahi hain?** Massage aur forceful stretching possible clot ya tissue injury mein unsafe hain. New swelling ignore nahi karni chahiye.

</details>

### Q13. Which positioning principle helps prevent foot drop during prolonged bed rest?

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Priority:** Medium

- **A.** Avoid inspecting the heels
- **B.** Maintain neutral ankle alignment using appropriate support and reassess the skin
- **C.** Keep the ankles plantarflexed continuously
- **D.** Place heavy bedding directly against the toes

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: B. Maintain neutral ankle alignment using appropriate support and reassess the skin**

Foot drop risk mein ankle ko neutral alignment mein support karna useful ho sakta hai. Nurse skin pressure aur comfort regularly assess karti hai.

**Baaki options kyun sahi nahi hain?** Prolonged plantarflexion contracture favor karti hai. Heavy bedding pressure add kar sakti hai. Heel inspection important hai.

</details>

### Q14. A patient with a weak hemiplegic arm is being transferred. Which action protects the shoulder?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Priority:** High

- **A.** Support the affected arm and avoid pulling on it
- **B.** Lift the patient by the affected wrist
- **C.** Allow the arm to hang without support during transfer
- **D.** Force the shoulder through resistance

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: A. Support the affected arm and avoid pulling on it**

Hemiplegia mein shoulder muscles joint stabilize nahi kar paati hain. Arm support traction aur subluxation risk reduce karta hai.

**Baaki options kyun sahi nahi hain?** Wrist lifting, unsupported hanging aur forced movement injury cause kar sakte hain.

</details>

### Q15. Which intervention helps reduce deconditioning during a prolonged hospital stay?

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Priority:** Medium

- **A.** Routine strict bed rest for every patient
- **B.** Avoiding all active movement after admission
- **C.** Keeping the patient in one position throughout the day
- **D.** Early individualized mobilization when clinically safe

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: D. Early individualized mobilization when clinically safe**

Safe mobilization muscle strength, circulation aur functional independence support karti hai. Activity patient ki stability ke according progress hoti hai.

**Baaki options kyun sahi nahi hain?** Unnecessary bed rest aur fixed positioning weakness aur complications badha sakti hain.

</details>

### Q16. During first ambulation after surgery a patient becomes pale and dizzy. Which action is safest?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Priority:** High

- **A.** Leave the patient standing while obtaining equipment
- **B.** Encourage rapid walking to improve circulation
- **C.** Assist the patient to a safe sitting or lying position and assess vital signs
- **D.** Insist on completing the walking distance

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: C. Assist the patient to a safe sitting or lying position and assess vital signs**

Dizziness aur pallor orthostatic hypotension ya other problem indicate kar sakte hain. Nurse fall prevent karke vitals aur symptoms assess karti hai.

**Baaki options kyun sahi nahi hain?** Walking insist karna aur unsupported standing injury risk badhate hain.

</details>

### Q17. Which bed position raises the head while keeping the bed surface angled with the head higher than the feet?

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Priority:** Medium

- **A.** Lithotomy
- **B.** Sims
- **C.** Reverse Trendelenburg
- **D.** Trendelenburg

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: C. Reverse Trendelenburg**

Reverse Trendelenburg mein whole bed tilt hoti hai aur head feet se higher hota hai. Ye Fowler se different hai jahan bed ka head section bend hota hai.

**Baaki options kyun sahi nahi hain?** Trendelenburg head-down tilt hai. Lithotomy aur Sims body positions hain.

</details>

### Q18. A patient slides repeatedly down a raised bed. Which tissue-damaging force is especially relevant?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Priority:** High

- **A.** Active transport
- **B.** Shear
- **C.** Osmosis
- **D.** Radiation

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: B. Shear**

Shear mein skin surface relatively fixed reh sakti hai jab deeper tissue slide karti hai. Isse vessels stretch aur tissue injury ho sakti hai.

**Baaki options kyun sahi nahi hain?** Osmosis water movement hai. Radiation heat transfer aur active transport cellular mechanism hai.

</details>

### Q19. Which equipment is appropriate for reducing friction during repositioning of a dependent patient?

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Priority:** Medium

- **A.** A suitable slide sheet or transfer aid
- **B.** Dragging directly across rough linen
- **C.** Pulling the patient by the elbows
- **D.** Using a loose pillowcase as an unassessed lifting sling

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: A. A suitable slide sheet or transfer aid**

Slide sheet surface friction kam karne mein help karti hai. Nurse trained technique aur equipment capacity follow karti hai.

**Baaki options kyun sahi nahi hain?** Dragging aur elbow traction skin-joint injury kar sakte hain. Improvised sling unsafe ho sakti hai.

</details>

### Q20. A patient has a new restriction against hip flexion beyond the prescribed limit. Which nursing approach is appropriate during toileting?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Priority:** High

- **A.** Use a very low toilet seat regardless of the restriction
- **B.** Flex the hip forcefully to improve range
- **C.** Ignore the restriction if the patient reports no pain
- **D.** Use suitable equipment and transfer technique that respects the specific restriction

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: D. Use suitable equipment and transfer technique that respects the specific restriction**

Postoperative restrictions procedure aur patient ke hisaab se hoti hain. Suitable raised seat aur assistance prescribed angle maintain karne mein help kar sakte hain.

**Baaki options kyun sahi nahi hain?** Low seat aur forceful flexion restriction violate kar sakti hain. Pain absent hona safety prove nahi karta.

</details>
