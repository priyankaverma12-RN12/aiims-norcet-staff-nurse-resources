### Q1. What is the main goal of palliative care?

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Priority:** Medium

- **A.** Stop every disease-directed treatment at diagnosis
- **B.** Provide only care during the final hours of life
- **C.** Avoid discussions about patient preferences
- **D.** Improve quality of life by addressing symptoms and psychosocial needs alongside appropriate treatment

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: D. Improve quality of life by addressing symptoms and psychosocial needs alongside appropriate treatment**

Palliative care serious illness mein pain, breathlessness aur emotional concerns address karti hai. Ye disease-directed treatment ke saath bhi start ho sakti hai.

**Baaki options kyun sahi nahi hain?** Treatment stop karna mandatory nahi hai. Care sirf final hours tak limited nahi hai. Patient preferences central hain.

</details>

### Q2. A patient with advanced illness says that maintaining alertness matters more than complete pain relief. What should the nurse do?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Priority:** High

- **A.** Withhold all comfort measures permanently
- **B.** Tell the patient there is only one acceptable goal
- **C.** Explore this preference and communicate it for an individualized symptom plan
- **D.** Choose the highest sedating dose regardless of the preference

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: C. Explore this preference and communicate it for an individualized symptom plan**

Patient ke goals symptom treatment guide karte hain. Nurse pain relief aur alertness ke balance par patient-team discussion facilitate karti hai.

**Baaki options kyun sahi nahi hain?** Unwanted sedation aur complete withholding both inappropriate ho sakte hain. Goals individualized hote hain.

</details>

### Q3. Which response is most therapeutic when a newly bereaved person begins crying?

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Priority:** Medium

- **A.** Insist that grief must end within a week
- **B.** Remain present, acknowledge the loss and allow expression of grief
- **C.** Tell the person to stop crying
- **D.** Immediately change the subject

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: B. Remain present, acknowledge the loss and allow expression of grief**

Grief natural response hai aur har person ka pattern different ho sakta hai. Nurse support aur listening provide karti hai.

**Baaki options kyun sahi nahi hain?** Crying suppress karna, topic change aur rigid deadline feelings dismiss karte hain.

</details>

### Q4. A family member feels angry and sad at different times after a death. What explanation is accurate?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Priority:** High

- **A.** Grief responses may fluctuate and need not follow a fixed sequence
- **B.** All people must progress through grief stages in one exact order
- **C.** Anger always proves a psychiatric disorder
- **D.** Sadness after death is abnormal in every case

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: A. Grief responses may fluctuate and need not follow a fixed sequence**

Grief mein emotions change ho sakte hain aur fixed stage order necessary nahi hai. Nurse coping aur support needs assess karti hai.

**Baaki options kyun sahi nahi hain?** Rigid stages universal nahi hain. Anger aur sadness normal grief mein possible hain, though severe persistent distress assessment maang sakta hai.

</details>

### Q5. Which term describes grief occurring before an expected loss, such as anticipated death from advanced illness?

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Priority:** Medium

- **A.** Anticipatory grief
- **B.** Disenfranchised grief by definition
- **C.** Complicated grief in every case
- **D.** Absence of grief

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: A. Anticipatory grief**

Anticipatory grief loss hone se pehle develop ho sakta hai. Patient aur family future changes ko process karne ki koshish karte hain.

**Baaki options kyun sahi nahi hain?** Disenfranchised grief socially unrecognized loss se related hai. Anticipatory grief automatically disorder nahi hai.

</details>

### Q6. A dying patient has dry mouth, limited oral intake and an aspiration risk. Which comfort measure is appropriate?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Priority:** High

- **A.** Force large volumes of water orally
- **B.** Avoid all mouth care
- **C.** Give food despite inability to swallow
- **D.** Provide frequent safe oral and lip care and discuss individualized hydration goals

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: D. Provide frequent safe oral and lip care and discuss individualized hydration goals**

End-of-life mein dry mouth distressing ho sakta hai. Gentle oral care comfort provide kar sakti hai without unsafe swallowing.

**Baaki options kyun sahi nahi hain?** Forced fluids aur food aspiration risk badhate hain. Oral care stop karna discomfort worsen kar sakta hai.

</details>

### Q7. A patient near end of life has distressing breathlessness despite an initial plan. What should the nurse do?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Priority:** High

- **A.** Give an unprescribed sedative dose without assessment
- **B.** Insist on vigorous exercise
- **C.** Assess symptoms and reversible contributors, provide comfort measures and request symptom-treatment review
- **D.** Assume breathlessness must remain untreated

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: C. Assess symptoms and reversible contributors, provide comfort measures and request symptom-treatment review**

Breathlessness ko palliative care mein actively address kiya jaata hai. Positioning, calm support aur prescribed medicines patient goals ke according use ho sakti hain.

**Baaki options kyun sahi nahi hain?** Untreated symptoms distress badhate hain. Unprescribed drugs aur forced activity unsafe hain.

</details>

### Q9. A patient has a documented do-not-attempt-resuscitation order. Which interpretation is correct?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Priority:** High

- **A.** It authorizes staff to ignore every acute symptom
- **B.** It limits CPR according to the order but does not automatically stop other appropriate care
- **C.** It means all medicines must stop immediately
- **D.** It prohibits pain relief and nursing care

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: B. It limits CPR according to the order but does not automatically stop other appropriate care**

DNAR order cardiac arrest ke waqt CPR decision address karta hai. Other treatments aur comfort care separately agreed plan ke according continue ho sakte hain.

**Baaki options kyun sahi nahi hain?** All medicines stop, no pain relief aur symptoms ignore karna DNAR ka meaning nahi hai.

</details>

End-of-life conflict mein applicable law, valid orders aur patient wishes important hain. Nurse team ko involve karke clear compassionate communication karti hai.

**Baaki options kyun sahi nahi hain?** Independent order change aur conflict escalation inappropriate hain. Documented plan ignore nahi karna chahiye.

</details>

### Q14. A family asks why the dying patient is eating less. Which response is most appropriate?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Priority:** High

- **A.** Stop every oral comfort intervention automatically
- **B.** Explain that appetite and swallowing may decline and discuss comfort-focused feeding and safety
- **C.** Force feeding to reach the previous calorie target regardless of distress
- **D.** Promise appetite will return within hours

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: B. Explain that appetite and swallowing may decline and discuss comfort-focused feeding and safety**

Advanced illness mein appetite aur swallowing ability change ho sakti hain. Nurse patient goals aur aspiration risk ke hisaab se food-fluid options discuss karti hai.

**Baaki options kyun sahi nahi hain?** Forced feeding discomfort aur aspiration badha sakti hai. Recovery guarantee nahi ki ja sakti. Comfort care continue honi chahiye.

</details>

### Q20. A nurse feels emotionally overwhelmed after repeated patient deaths. Which professional response is appropriate?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Priority:** High

- **A.** Seek supervision or peer support and use appropriate well-being resources while maintaining confidentiality
- **B.** Share identifiable patient details publicly
- **C.** Ignore distress until care quality deteriorates
- **D.** Assume asking for support is always unprofessional

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: A. Seek supervision or peer support and use appropriate well-being resources while maintaining confidentiality**

Repeated loss emotional stress create kar sakti hai. Confidential debriefing, supervision aur support nurse well-being aur safe care mein help karte hain.

**Baaki options kyun sahi nahi hain?** Public identifiable sharing confidentiality breach hai. Distress ignore karna care affect kar sakta hai. Support lena appropriate hai.

</details>
