### Q1. What is the main goal of palliative care?

**Difficulty:** 🟡 Medium · **Priority:** 🟡 IMPORTANT · Original practice

- **A.** Stop every disease-directed treatment at diagnosis
- **B.** Provide only care during the final hours of life
- **C.** Avoid discussions about patient preferences
- **D.** Improve quality of life by addressing symptoms and psychosocial needs alongside appropriate treatment

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: D. Improve quality of life by addressing symptoms and psychosocial needs alongside appropriate treatment**

**A kyun galat hai:** Palliative care shuru hone par har disease-directed treatment ko band karna zaroori nahi hai. Jo treatment patient ke goals aur clinical needs se match karta hai, woh jaari reh sakta hai.

**B kyun galat hai:** Palliative care illness ke earlier phase mein bhi shuru ho sakti hai. Sirf final hours tak wait karne se patient ko pehle mil sakne wala symptom relief nahi milta.

**C kyun galat hai:** Patient ki wishes samajhna zaroori hai, kyunki unhi se care ke goals decide hote hain. Nurse discussion ko avoid nahi karti.

**D kyun sahi hai:** Palliative care mein serious illness ke saath jee rahe patient ki takleef kam karne aur quality of life behtar banane par focus hota hai. Nurse pain, breathlessness, emotional aur spiritual needs assess karti hai aur team ke saath appropriate care arrange karti hai.

**Exam clue:** Palliative care aur disease-directed treatment ek saath chal sakte hain.

### Q2. A patient with advanced illness says that maintaining alertness matters more than complete pain relief. What should the nurse do?

**Difficulty:** 🔴 Difficult · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD · Original practice

- **A.** Withhold all comfort measures permanently
- **B.** Tell the patient there is only one acceptable goal
- **C.** Explore this preference and communicate it for an individualized symptom plan
- **D.** Choose the highest sedating dose regardless of the preference

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: C. Explore this preference and communicate it for an individualized symptom plan**

**A kyun galat hai:** Saare comfort measures band karne se avoidable suffering ho sakti hai. Pain relief ke doosre options aur dose review abhi bhi possible hain.

**B kyun galat hai:** Har patient ki priorities alag ho sakti hain. Nurse ko apna goal impose karne ki jagah patient ka informed preference samajhna chahiye.

**C kyun sahi hai:** Patient alert rehkar family se baat karna chahta ho sakta hai. Nurse pain aur sedation assess karti hai, preference document karti hai aur prescriber ke saath comfort aur alertness ka individualized balance discuss karti hai.

**D kyun galat hai:** Bina assessment ya authorized prescription ke sedating dose badhana unsafe hai. Isse patient ki consciousness aur breathing affect ho sakti hai.

**Exam clue:** Symptom relief ka plan patient ki priorities ke according individualize karo.

### Q3. Which response is most therapeutic when a newly bereaved person begins crying?

**Difficulty:** 🟡 Medium · **Priority:** 🟡 IMPORTANT · Original practice

- **A.** Insist that grief must end within a week
- **B.** Remain present, acknowledge the loss and allow expression of grief
- **C.** Tell the person to stop crying
- **D.** Immediately change the subject

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: B. Remain present, acknowledge the loss and allow expression of grief**

**A kyun galat hai:** Grief khatam hone ki koi universal deadline nahi hoti. Ek hafte ki limit lagane se person apni feelings chhupane lag sakta hai.

**B kyun sahi hai:** Kisi apne ki death ke baad rona grief express karne ka natural tareeka ho sakta hai. Nurse shaant rehkar saath baithti hai, loss acknowledge karti hai aur person ko apni pace par bolne ya chup rehne deti hai.

**C kyun galat hai:** Rona rokne ko kehne se person ko lag sakta hai ki uski feelings galat hain. Feelings ko respect karna zyada supportive hai.

**D kyun galat hai:** Topic turant badalne se person ki baat adhuri reh jaati hai. Nurse ko sensitive listening ka mauka dena chahiye.

**Exam clue:** Therapeutic response mein loss acknowledge karo aur bina judgment ke suno.

### Q4. A family member feels angry and sad at different times after a death. What explanation is accurate?

**Difficulty:** 🔴 Difficult · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD · Original practice · **🟣 NVS Staff Nurse, September 2019 — related reported concept**

- **A.** Grief responses may fluctuate and need not follow a fixed sequence
- **B.** All people must progress through grief stages in one exact order
- **C.** Anger always proves a psychiatric disorder
- **D.** Sadness after death is abnormal in every case

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: A. Grief responses may fluctuate and need not follow a fixed sequence**

**A kyun sahi hai:** Grief ke dauran sadness, anger aur doosre emotions badal sakte hain ya dobara aa sakte hain. Har person ka experience alag hota hai. Nurse coping, functioning aur safety needs assess karti hai.

**B kyun galat hai:** Kübler-Ross model exam mein poochha ja sakta hai, lekin real life mein har person usi sequence se nahi guzarta. Model ko fixed clinical timetable nahi samajhna chahiye.

**C kyun galat hai:** Anger akela psychiatric disorder ka proof nahi hai. Severe persistent distress, functioning ki dikkat ya safety concern ho toh further assessment zaroori hai.

**D kyun galat hai:** Death ke baad sadness normal grief ka part ho sakti hai. Sirf sadness dekhkar disorder diagnose nahi karte.

**Exam clue:** Grief stages ek possible model hain, compulsory sequence nahi.

**D kyun galat hai:** Loss hone se pehle bhi sadness, fear ya worry ho sakti hai. Isliye death abhi nahi hui hai, iska matlab grief absent nahi hai.

### Q6. A dying patient has dry mouth, limited oral intake and an aspiration risk. Which comfort measure is appropriate?

**Difficulty:** 🔴 Difficult · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD · Original practice

- **A.** Force large volumes of water orally
- **B.** Avoid all mouth care
- **C.** Give food despite inability to swallow
- **D.** Provide frequent safe oral and lip care and discuss individualized hydration goals

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: D. Provide frequent safe oral and lip care and discuss individualized hydration goals**

**A kyun galat hai:** Swallowing weak ho toh force karke paani pilane se liquid airway mein ja sakta hai. Isko aspiration kehte hain aur isse breathing ki problem ho sakti hai.

**B kyun galat hai:** Mouth care band karne se dryness aur discomfort badh sakte hain. Safe mouth care abhi bhi useful hai.

**C kyun galat hai:** Patient swallow nahi kar paa raha hai toh food dene se choking aur aspiration ka risk badhta hai. Feeding assessment aur care goals ke according honi chahiye.

**D kyun sahi hai:** Nurse alertness aur swallowing assess karke lips aur mouth ko gently moist rakhti hai. Agar patient safely swallow kar sake toh suitable sips offer kar sakti hai. Hydration ke benefits aur risks patient aur team ke saath discuss kiye jaate hain.

**Exam clue:** Dry mouth mein safe oral comfort care do aur hydration individualize karo.

### Q7. A patient near end of life has distressing breathlessness despite an initial plan. What should the nurse do?

**Difficulty:** 🔴 Difficult · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD · Original practice

- **A.** Give an unprescribed sedative dose without assessment
- **B.** Insist on vigorous exercise
- **C.** Assess symptoms and reversible contributors, provide comfort measures and request symptom-treatment review
- **D.** Assume breathlessness must remain untreated

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: C. Assess symptoms and reversible contributors, provide comfort measures and request symptom-treatment review**

**A kyun galat hai:** Unprescribed sedative se consciousness aur breathing affect ho sakti hai. Nurse assessment ke bina medicine administer nahi karti.

**B kyun galat hai:** Vigorous exercise se oxygen demand badh sakti hai aur already breathless patient ko aur takleef ho sakti hai. Activity tolerance aur goals ke according honi chahiye.

**C kyun sahi hai:** Nurse breathlessness ki severity, breathing effort aur possible reversible causes assess karti hai. Comfortable positioning aur calm support se relief mil sakta hai. Treating team se prescribed symptom plan review karwati hai aur acute deterioration mein agreed emergency plan ke according urgent help leti hai.

**D kyun galat hai:** Palliative care ka matlab distress ko untreated chhodna nahi hai. Breathlessness ke liye comfort aur appropriate treatment dono consider karte hain.

**Exam clue:** Breathlessness mein assessment, comfort aur cause-directed review saath chalte hain.

### Q9. A patient has a documented do-not-attempt-resuscitation order. Which interpretation is correct?

- **D.** It prohibits pain relief and nursing care

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**B kyun sahi hai:** DNAR ka matlab Do Not Attempt Resuscitation hai. Valid DNAR order cardiac ya respiratory arrest ke samay CPR ke decision se related hai. Pain relief, nursing care aur doosra suitable treatment alag clinical plan ke according jaari reh sakta hai.

**C kyun galat hai:** Saari medicines automatically band karne se untreated symptoms ya reversible problems worsen ho sakti hain. Medicine review ke liye alag authorized decision chahiye.

**D kyun galat hai:** Pain relief aur comfort care DNAR ke saath bhi important hain. Patient ki dignity aur takleef kam karne ki responsibility bani rehti hai.

**Exam clue:** DNAR CPR ko address karta hai, do not treat ka order nahi hai.

### Q15. A patient with advanced disease develops new severe pain that is different from the usual symptoms. What should the nurse do?

**Difficulty:** 🔴 Difficult · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD · Original practice

- **A.** Assess promptly for new causes and seek appropriate review consistent with care goals
- **B.** Assume all pain is inevitable and ignore it
- **C.** Repeat all previous doses without checking timing
- **D.** Avoid documenting because the patient is receiving palliation

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: A. Assess promptly for new causes and seek appropriate review consistent with care goals**

**A kyun sahi hai:** New severe pain ko purane pain ka hissa maan lena unsafe hai. Nurse onset, site, severity, associated symptoms aur prior treatment response assess karti hai. Agreed goals ke hisaab se prompt clinical review arrange karke symptom relief aur new cause dono consider karti hai.

**B kyun galat hai:** Pain ko inevitable kehkar ignore karne se avoidable suffering aur new complication miss ho sakti hai. Palliation mein bhi assessment zaroori hai.
