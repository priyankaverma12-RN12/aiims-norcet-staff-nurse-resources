### Q1. Which mechanism contributes to increased venous thromboembolism risk during prolonged immobility?

**Difficulty:** 🔴 Difficult · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD · Original practice

- **A.** Complete elimination of coagulation factors
- **B.** Reduced skeletal muscle pumping and venous stasis
- **C.** Increased venous return from constant walking
- **D.** Reduced blood viscosity in every case

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: B. Reduced skeletal muscle pumping and venous stasis**

**A kyun galat hai:** Bed rest se clotting factors khatam nahi hote. Clot banne ki tendency aur venous flow dono patient ke risk factors par depend karte hain.

**B kyun sahi hai:** Jab hum chalte hain, toh leg muscles contract hokar veins ka blood heart ki taraf bhejti hain. Bed rest mein ye muscle pump kam active hota hai. Blood dheere flow karta hai, jise venous stasis kehte hain. Isse deep vein thrombosis ka risk badhta hai.

**C kyun galat hai:** Walking muscle pump ko active karti hai aur venous return support karti hai. Yeh prolonged immobility ka mechanism nahi hai.

**D kyun galat hai:** Blood viscosity blood ke flow ki resistance se related hai. Immobility har patient mein viscosity kam nahi karti aur clot ka risk khatam nahi hota.

**Exam clue:** Virchow triad mein immobility ko venous stasis se jodo.

**D kyun galat hai:** Routine mobility assessment baad mein ho sakti hai. Naye chest pain aur dyspnea mein emergency care priority hai.

### Q3. What is a common musculoskeletal consequence of prolonged disuse?

**Difficulty:** 🟡 Medium · **Priority:** 🟡 IMPORTANT · Original practice

- **A.** Increased muscle mass without activity
- **B.** Permanent improvement in all joint movements
- **C.** Increased bone density in every case
- **D.** Muscle atrophy and reduced strength

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: D. Muscle atrophy and reduced strength**

**A kyun galat hai:** Muscle ko strength maintain karne ke liye suitable activity aur nutrition chahiye. Disuse se muscle mass apne aap nahi badhta.

**B kyun galat hai:** Joint movement kam hone se stiffness aur contracture ho sakte hain. Har joint ki movement permanently improve nahi hoti.

**C kyun galat hai:** Bones ko suitable loading se strength maintain karne mein madad milti hai. Prolonged inactivity mein bone mineral loss bhi ho sakta hai.

**D kyun sahi hai:** Jab muscle ka workload kam hota hai, toh protein breakdown aur synthesis ka balance badal sakta hai. Muscle size kam hone ko atrophy kehte hain. Isse strength aur daily activities karne ki ability kam ho sakti hai.

**Exam clue:** Disuse ka common result muscle atrophy aur weakness hai.

### Q4. Why can prolonged immobilization increase the risk of a joint contracture?

**Difficulty:** 🔴 Difficult · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD · Original practice

- **A.** All ligaments disappear immediately
- **B.** Joint mobility increases without limit
- **C.** Muscles and connective tissues can shorten when joints remain in one position
- **D.** Cartilage becomes an actively contracting muscle

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: C. Muscles and connective tissues can shorten when joints remain in one position**

**A kyun galat hai:** Ligaments joints ko support karte hain. Immobilization mein flexibility badal sakti hai, lekin ligaments turant disappear nahi hote.

**B kyun galat hai:** Regular movement na milne se stiffness badh sakti hai. Unlimited joint mobility expected nahi hai.

**C kyun sahi hai:** Jab joint bahut samay tak ek position mein rehta hai, toh muscle aur connective tissue shorten aur stiff ho sakte hain. Persistent movement limitation ko contracture kehte hain. Suitable positioning aur prescribed range-of-motion exercises risk kam karne mein madad karti hain.

**D kyun galat hai:** Cartilage joint surfaces ko cushion karta hai. Yeh actively contracting muscle mein convert nahi hota.

**Exam clue:** Fixed posture aur soft tissue shortening ko contracture se jodo.

### Q5. Which intervention helps preserve muscle function in a stable bedbound patient who can contract muscles but cannot yet walk?

**Difficulty:** 🟡 Medium · **Priority:** 🟡 IMPORTANT · Original practice

- **A.** Forceful exercise beyond the prescribed limit
- **B.** Keep joints fixed without reassessment
- **C.** Prescribed active or assisted exercises according to restrictions
- **D.** Complete avoidance of all muscle contraction

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: C. Prescribed active or assisted exercises according to restrictions**

**A kyun galat hai:** Prescribed limit se zyada force lagane par pain, injury ya healing mein problem ho sakti hai. Exercise restrictions ke according honi chahiye.

**B kyun galat hai:** Joint ko bina reassessment fixed rakhne se stiffness aur pressure-related problems badh sakti hain. Kuch injuries mein immobilization zaroori hai, lekin plan clinician decide karta hai.

**C kyun sahi hai:** Active exercise mein patient khud muscle contract karta hai. Active-assisted exercise mein zaroorat ke hisaab se help milti hai. Stable patient ki remaining ability use karne se strength aur function maintain karne mein madad milti hai.

**D kyun galat hai:** Har contraction avoid karne se disuse aur weakness badh sakti hai. Patient ko allowed activity ke liye encourage karna chahiye.

**Exam clue:** Available strength use karo aur prescribed restrictions respect karo.

### Q6. A patient has been on bed rest for weeks and is beginning rehabilitation. Why should upright activity be progressed gradually?

**Difficulty:** 🔴 Difficult · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD · Original practice

- **A.** Gradual progression eliminates all future fall risk
- **B.** Cardiovascular deconditioning can impair tolerance of sudden standing
- **C.** Bed rest guarantees improved orthostatic responses
- **D.** Standing always causes permanent paralysis

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: B. Cardiovascular deconditioning can impair tolerance of sudden standing**

**A kyun galat hai:** Gradual activity risk kam kar sakti hai, lekin balance, medicines aur environment ki wajah se falls ka risk reh sakta hai.

**B kyun sahi hai:** Bed rest ke baad cardiovascular system standing ki demand ke liye kam prepared ho sakta hai. Patient uthta hai toh blood legs mein pool ho sakta hai aur BP gir sakta hai. Nurse gradually sitting aur standing progress karti hai, symptoms aur BP check karti hai aur zaroorat par assistance deti hai.

**C kyun galat hai:** Bed rest se orthostatic tolerance guaranteed improve nahi hoti. Prolonged inactivity standing tolerate karne mein difficulty badha sakti hai.

**D kyun galat hai:** Standing apne aap permanent paralysis ka cause nahi hai. Actual neurological condition aur restrictions assess karni chahiye.

**Exam clue:** Bed rest ke baad standing par dizziness ko orthostatic intolerance ka possible sign samjho.

**C kyun galat hai:** Secretions retain hone aur ineffective cough se pneumonia ka risk reh sakta hai. Bed rest pneumonia se protection nahi deta.

### Q16. A patient becomes exhausted midway through a rehabilitation session. Which action is appropriate?

**Difficulty:** 🔴 Difficult · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD · Original practice

- **A.** Document the session as completed if the patient stopped
- **B.** Assess symptoms and vital status, provide rest and adapt the session
- **C.** Insist that the full session must be completed without rest
- **D.** Increase exercise intensity immediately

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: B. Assess symptoms and vital status, provide rest and adapt the session**

**A kyun galat hai:** Agar patient session complete nahi kar paaya toh completed record karna inaccurate hai. Actual activity aur symptoms document karne chahiye.

**B kyun sahi hai:** Exhaustion expected exertion ya new deterioration ka sign ho sakti hai. Nurse session pause karke symptoms, breathing, pulse aur BP jaise findings assess karti hai. Rest deti hai aur red flags par urgent help leti hai. Further activity tolerance aur team plan ke according adjust hoti hai.

**C kyun galat hai:** Without rest session force karne se fatigue aur deterioration badh sakti hai. Safety target duration se zyada important hai.

**D kyun galat hai:** Exhausted patient mein intensity immediately badhana bina assessment unsafe hai. Pehle cause aur tolerance samajhna zaroori hai.

### Q17. Which intervention helps maintain joint alignment in a patient unable to reposition independently?

**Difficulty:** 🟡 Medium · **Priority:** 🟡 IMPORTANT · Original practice

- **A.** Avoid all reassessment after applying pillows
- **B.** Use appropriate supports and regular individualized repositioning
- **C.** Place limbs in extreme flexion continuously
- **D.** Use hard objects directly beneath all bony prominences

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: B. Use appropriate supports and regular individualized repositioning**

**A kyun galat hai:** Pillow lagane ke baad bhi alignment aur skin response badal sakta hai. Nurse reassess karti hai ki support harmful pressure toh nahi bana raha.

**B kyun sahi hai:** Suitable pillows ya supports limbs ko proper alignment mein rakhne mein help karte hain. Individualized repositioning sustained pressure aur stiffness ka risk kam kar sakti hai. Nurse condition, skin aur restrictions dekhkar plan adjust karti hai.

**C kyun galat hai:** Continuous extreme flexion se soft tissue shortening aur pressure injury ka risk badh sakta hai. Positioning clinically appropriate honi chahiye.

**D kyun galat hai:** Bony prominences ke neeche hard objects concentrated pressure create kar sakte hain. Suitable pressure redistribution aur support use karo.

**Exam clue:** Alignment ke saath pressure relief aur regular reassessment zaroori hain.

### Q19. Which finding is an appropriate measurable rehabilitation outcome?

**Difficulty:** 🟡 Medium · **Priority:** 🟡 IMPORTANT · Original practice

- **A.** The patient will become better eventually
- **B.** The nurse will provide all care forever
- **C.** The patient's diagnosis will disappear immediately
- **D.** The patient transfers from bed to chair with one-person assistance by the planned date

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: D. The patient transfers from bed to chair with one-person assistance by the planned date**

**A kyun galat hai:** Eventually better hone mein specific task, assistance ya time limit clear nahi hai. Progress objectively measure karna mushkil hai.

**B kyun galat hai:** Nurse ka indefinitely saari care dena patient ke functional improvement ko measure nahi karta. Goal patient ki ability par focus hona chahiye.

**C kyun galat hai:** Diagnosis turant disappear hone ki guarantee nahi hoti. Rehabilitation disease cure ke bina bhi daily function improve kar sakti hai.

**D kyun sahi hai:** Bed se chair transfer, one-person assistance aur planned date specify karne se goal observable aur measurable banta hai. Nurse actual performance compare karke rehabilitation plan review kar sakti hai.

**Exam clue:** Measurable goal mein task, assistance level aur timeframe clear rakho.

### Q20. An older patient with prolonged immobility is preparing for discharge home. Which plan addresses multiple safety risks?

**Difficulty:** 🔴 Difficult · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD · Original practice

- **A.** Advise indefinite bed rest without review
- **B.** Provide only a list of exercises without discussing safety
- **C.** Assess home access, caregiver ability, equipment, skin care and mobility follow-up
- **D.** Send the patient home without assessing transfer needs

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: C. Assess home access, caregiver ability, equipment, skin care and mobility follow-up**

**A kyun galat hai:** Indefinite bed rest se deconditioning, pressure injury aur dependence badh sakte hain. Activity individual restrictions aur follow-up ke according plan honi chahiye.

**B kyun galat hai:** Exercise list useful ho sakti hai, lekin transfers, equipment aur caregiver support ki needs address nahi karti. Practical teaching aur assessment chahiye.

**C kyun sahi hai:** Discharge se pehle nurse home access, transfer ability, caregiver capacity aur required equipment assess karti hai. Skin protection, safe mobility aur follow-up ka plan patient aur team ke saath banati hai. Isse avoidable injury aur dependence ka risk kam karne mein madad milti hai.

**D kyun galat hai:** Transfer needs assess kiye bina discharge se patient aur caregiver dono ko injury ka risk ho sakta hai. Safe support arrangement pehle verify karo.

**Exam clue:** Discharge mein function, environment, caregiver aur follow-up saath dekho.
