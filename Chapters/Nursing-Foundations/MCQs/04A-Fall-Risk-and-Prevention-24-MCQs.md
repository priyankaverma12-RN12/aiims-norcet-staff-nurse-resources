### Q1. A patient has a recent fall, secondary diagnosis, walker, IV, weak gait and recognizes limitations. Using respective Morse item scores of 25, 15, 15, 20, 10 and 0, what is the total?

**Difficulty:** 🔴 Difficult | **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD | **Type:** Calculation | **Evidence:** Original practice, historical match pending

A. 70
B. 85
C. 100
D. 110

<details>
<summary>✅ Sahi answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct answer: B. 85**

- **A (Incorrect):** 70 mein 15 points miss hain.
- **B (Correct):** 25+15+15+20+10=85. Local cutoff aur individual plan follow karo.
- **C (Incorrect):** 100 mein extra 15 points hain.
- **D (Incorrect):** 110 mein unsupported 25 extra points hain.

</details>
