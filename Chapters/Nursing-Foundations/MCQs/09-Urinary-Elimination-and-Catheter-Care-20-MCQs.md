# Urinary elimination and catheter care — 20 original MCQs

### Q1. Which is an appropriate indication for an indwelling urinary catheter?

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Priority:** Medium

- **A.** Preventing every episode of nocturia
- **B.** Acute urinary retention when catheterization is clinically indicated
- **C.** Routine convenience for every ambulatory patient
- **D.** Replacing all toilet assistance in independent patients

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: B. Acute urinary retention when catheterization is clinically indicated**

Urinary retention mein bladder empty nahi ho paata aur catheter indicated ho sakti hai. Nurse indication, alternatives aur order assess karti hai.

**Baaki options kyun sahi nahi hain?** Convenience, independent toileting aur nocturia alone routine indwelling catheter indications nahi hain.

</details>

### Q2. An indwelling catheter drainage bag is found above the level of the bladder. What should the nurse do?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Priority:** High

- **A.** Reposition the bag below bladder level and assess drainage
- **B.** Keep it elevated to increase urine collection
- **C.** Disconnect the catheter to drain the bag
- **D.** Place the bag directly on the floor

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: A. Reposition the bag below bladder level and assess drainage**

Urine gravity se drainage bag tak flow karti hai. Bag bladder ke upar ho toh backflow ka risk ho sakta hai. Nurse bag neeche aur floor se off rakhti hai.

**Baaki options kyun sahi nahi hain?** Elevation backflow favor kar sakti hai. Disconnection closed system break karti hai. Floor contamination risk hai.

</details>

### Q3. Why should a urinary drainage system normally remain closed?

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Priority:** Medium

- **A.** A closed system stops all urine flow
- **B.** The bag must never be emptied under any circumstances
- **C.** The system is used to measure arterial pressure
- **D.** Opening connections can introduce microorganisms into the urinary tract

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: D. Opening connections can introduce microorganisms into the urinary tract**

Closed drainage bacteria entry ke opportunities reduce karti hai. Bag designated outlet se aseptically empty ki ja sakti hai.

**Baaki options kyun sahi nahi hain?** Closed system urine flow allow karti hai. Emptying needed hoti hai. Arterial pressure different device se measure hoti hai.

</details>

### Q4. A catheterized patient has no urine in the bag for two hours and reports suprapubic discomfort. What should the nurse do first?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Priority:** High

- **A.** Increase IV fluids regardless of fluid status
- **B.** Assume the kidneys have permanently failed
- **C.** Assess the patient and check for kinks, obstruction and bladder distension
- **D.** Forcefully irrigate the catheter without an order

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: C. Assess the patient and check for kinks, obstruction and bladder distension**

Urine absent ho toh kink, obstruction, retention ya low production possible hai. Nurse patient, tubing aur bladder assess karke timely review arrange karti hai.

**Baaki options kyun sahi nahi hain?** Blind irrigation injury cause kar sakti hai. Extra fluid har cause treat nahi karta. No drainage renal failure prove nahi karti.

</details>

### Q5. Which practice helps prevent urethral traction from an indwelling catheter?

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Priority:** Medium

- **A.** Leave the heavy drainage bag hanging from the catheter
- **B.** Tie the tubing to a moving bed rail with no slack
- **C.** Use an appropriate securement device and allow adequate slack
- **D.** Attach the tubing tightly so the catheter pulls during movement

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: C. Use an appropriate securement device and allow adequate slack**

Securement catheter movement limit karti hai aur slack patient movement ke dauran urethral pulling kam karta hai. Location anatomy aur policy se choose hoti hai.

**Baaki options kyun sahi nahi hain?** Tight tubing, hanging bag aur rail movement traction badha sakte hain.

</details>

### Q6. A urine culture is ordered for a patient with an existing indwelling catheter. Where should a fresh specimen usually be obtained?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Priority:** High

- **A.** From fluid used to wash the patient's skin
- **B.** From the designated sampling port using aseptic technique
- **C.** From urine that has been in the drainage bag all day
- **D.** By disconnecting the tubing at any point

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: B. From the designated sampling port using aseptic technique**

Sampling port fresh urine collect karne mein help karta hai without unnecessarily breaking system. Nurse port disinfect karke protocol follow karti hai.

**Baaki options kyun sahi nahi hain?** Old bag urine bacterial growth se misleading ho sakti hai. Disconnection infection risk badhati hai. Skin wash urine specimen nahi hai.

</details>

### Q7. Which approach is appropriate for routine meatal hygiene in a catheterized patient?

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Priority:** Medium

- **A.** Provide routine hygiene and clean after soiling according to policy
- **B.** Apply strong antiseptic repeatedly as a universal CAUTI prevention method
- **C.** Avoid all perineal cleaning
- **D.** Use abrasive brushes on the urethral opening

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: A. Provide routine hygiene and clean after soiling according to policy**

Routine gentle hygiene secretions aur soil remove karti hai. Har patient mein repeated antiseptic cleaning CAUTI prevent karne ka standard substitute nahi hai.

**Baaki options kyun sahi nahi hain?** Strong chemicals irritation cause kar sakte hain. No cleaning aur abrasive brushes skin damage risk badhate hain.

</details>

### Q8. A patient has an indwelling catheter solely because of a now-resolved short-term indication. What is the best next step?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Priority:** High

- **A.** Keep the catheter for the rest of admission automatically
- **B.** Clamp it indefinitely to test bladder strength
- **C.** Replace it with a larger catheter regardless of need
- **D.** Review removal eligibility and remove promptly according to authorized protocol

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: D. Review removal eligibility and remove promptly according to authorized protocol**

Catheter jitni der unnecessary rahegi utna infection aur trauma exposure badh sakta hai. Nurse daily indication review aur removal pathway follow karti hai.

**Baaki options kyun sahi nahi hain?** Automatic retention avoid karna chahiye. Indefinite clamping aur larger catheter unnecessary harm kar sakte hain.

</details>

### Q10. A patient voids 100 mL and has a large postvoid residual on repeated bladder assessment. Which problem is suggested?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Priority:** High

- **A.** Anuria proven by the result
- **B.** Excessive renal filtration proven by residual volume
- **C.** Incomplete bladder emptying
- **D.** Normal complete emptying established by the voided volume alone

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: C. Incomplete bladder emptying**

Postvoid residual bladder mein urination ke baad bacha urine hai. Large residual incomplete emptying suggest kar sakta hai aur clinical review chahiye.

**Baaki options kyun sahi nahi hain?** Voided amount alone complete emptying prove nahi karta. Anuria urine absence hai. Residual kidney filtration rate nahi batata.

</details>

### Q11. Which instruction is appropriate for a midstream clean-catch urine specimen?

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Priority:** Medium

- **A.** Collect urine from the toilet bowl

- **C.** Collect only the first drops in an unclean cup
- **D.** Touch the container interior to stabilize it

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: B. Begin voiding, then collect the middle portion without touching the inside of the container**

Midstream technique urethral aur skin contamination reduce karne mein help karti hai. Sterile container interior clean rakhna important hai.

**Baaki options kyun sahi nahi hain?** First drops contaminants carry kar sakti hain. Interior touch aur toilet collection specimen contaminate kar sakte hain.

</details>

### Q12. A timed 24-hour urine collection begins at 7 AM. What is the usual collection procedure?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Priority:** High

- **A.** Discard the initial 7 AM void, then collect all urine including the final 7 AM void next day
- **B.** Collect the first void and stop before the final scheduled void
- **C.** Collect only nighttime urine
- **D.** Discard any specimen with a large volume

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: A. Discard the initial 7 AM void, then collect all urine including the final 7 AM void next day**

Initial bladder emptying collection start define karti hai. Uske baad next 24 hours ka saara urine include karna hota hai, including end-time void. Lab storage directions follow karni hain.

**Baaki options kyun sahi nahi hain?** First preperiod urine include karna aur final omit karna timing distort karta hai. Missing voids result inaccurate kar sakti hain.

</details>

### Q13. A urinary drainage bag needs emptying. Which technique reduces cross-contamination?

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Priority:** Medium

- **A.** Use a separate clean collection container and avoid outlet contact with the container
- **B.** Touch the drainage outlet to the shared measuring jug
- **C.** Use the same unclean jug for every patient
- **D.** Place the outlet on the floor while measuring

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

Outlet contact se organisms drainage system ya shared equipment contaminate kar sakte hain. Nurse appropriate container aur hand hygiene use karti hai.

**Baaki options kyun sahi nahi hain?** Shared unclean jug aur floor contact infection spread kar sakte hain.

</details>

### Q14. A catheterized patient develops fever and new flank pain. What is the appropriate nursing response?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Priority:** High

- **A.** Diagnose infection solely from cloudy urine
- **B.** Automatically start an unprescribed antibiotic
- **C.** Ignore symptoms because a catheter is present
- **D.** Assess for infection and other causes, escalate and collect specimens only as indicated

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: D. Assess for infection and other causes, escalate and collect specimens only as indicated**

Fever aur flank pain possible urinary infection ya other serious condition ke signs hain. Nurse vitals aur clinical findings assess karke clinician ko inform karti hai.

**Baaki options kyun sahi nahi hain?** Cloudiness alone infection diagnose nahi karti. Antibiotic authorization chahiye. Catheter infection risk badha sakti hai.

</details>

### Q15. What does intermittent catheterization mean?

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Priority:** Medium

- **A.** A catheter is inserted into the ureter through the skin in every case
- **B.** The patient never empties the bladder
- **C.** A catheter is inserted to drain the bladder and then removed at scheduled or indicated times
- **D.** A catheter remains continuously in the bladder for weeks

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: C. A catheter is inserted to drain the bladder and then removed at scheduled or indicated times**

Intermittent catheterization bladder empty karne ke baad device remove kar deti hai. Frequency patient needs aur prescription se decide hoti hai.

**Baaki options kyun sahi nahi hain?** Continuous retention indwelling catheter hai. Ureter access separate procedure hai. Intermittent method bladder emptying ke liye hoti hai.

</details>

### Q16. During catheter insertion a patient experiences severe pain and marked resistance. Which action is safest?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Priority:** High

- **A.** Use a larger catheter automatically
- **B.** Stop advancing and seek appropriate clinical assistance
- **C.** Push the catheter forcefully past resistance
- **D.** Inflate the balloon immediately despite uncertain placement

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: B. Stop advancing and seek appropriate clinical assistance**

Resistance obstruction ya incorrect path indicate kar sakti hai. Forceful advancement urethral trauma cause kar sakti hai. Nurse stop karke trained review leti hai.

**Baaki options kyun sahi nahi hain?** Force, uncertain balloon inflation aur arbitrary larger catheter injury badha sakte hain.

</details>

### Q17. Before inflating an indwelling urinary catheter balloon, what must be confirmed?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Priority:** High

- **A.** That the drainage bag has already been disconnected
- **B.** Correct bladder placement according to the insertion protocol
- **C.** That the patient feels severe urethral pain
- **D.** That the balloon is positioned in the urethra

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: B. Correct bladder placement according to the insertion protocol**

Balloon bladder ke andar hona chahiye. Urethra mein inflation severe injury kar sakti hai, isliye urine return aur procedure-specific placement confirmation important hain.

**Baaki options kyun sahi nahi hain?** Pain correct position prove nahi karta. Urethral inflation unsafe hai. Drainage disconnection confirmation nahi hai.

</details>

### Q19. A patient with functional incontinence can sense bladder fullness but cannot reach the toilet in time due to limited mobility. Which plan addresses the main problem?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Priority:** High

- **A.** Assume permanent loss of bladder sensation
- **B.** Insert an indwelling catheter routinely without reviewing alternatives
- **C.** Restrict all fluid intake throughout the day
- **D.** Provide accessible toileting, scheduled assistance and suitable mobility support

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

Functional incontinence mein bladder control se zyada access ya mobility problem ho sakti hai. Timely toilet support actual barrier address karta hai.

**Baaki options kyun sahi nahi hain?** Sensation present hai. Routine catheter infection risk add karti hai. Complete fluid restriction dehydration cause kar sakti hai.

</details>

### Q20. A postoperative patient has low urine output despite a patent catheter. Which assessment is most appropriate?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Priority:** High

- **A.** Give a large unprescribed fluid bolus to every patient
- **B.** Document normal output without measuring
- **C.** Assess fluid balance, perfusion, vital signs and possible renal causes and escalate as indicated
- **D.** Assume catheter replacement alone will restore urine production

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: C. Assess fluid balance, perfusion, vital signs and possible renal causes and escalate as indicated**

Patent catheter ke saath low urine output renal perfusion, dehydration ya kidney injury se related ho sakta hai. Nurse whole patient assess karti hai.

**Baaki options kyun sahi nahi hain?** Catheter replacement production problem solve nahi karti. Blind fluids overload kar sakte hain. False output documentation unsafe hai.

</details>
