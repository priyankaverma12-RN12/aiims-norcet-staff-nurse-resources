### Q1. Which is an appropriate indication for an indwelling urinary catheter?

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Priority:** Medium

- **A.** Preventing every episode of nocturia
- **B.** Acute urinary retention when catheterization is clinically indicated
- **C.** Routine convenience for every ambulatory patient
- **D.** Replacing all toilet assistance in independent patients

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: B. Acute urinary retention when catheterization is clinically indicated**

Urinary retention ka matlab hai ki bladder mein urine jama hai, lekin patient usse theek se bahar nahi nikaal pa raha. Jab acute retention clinically confirm ho aur catheter ki zarurat ho, drainage se bladder ka pressure aur discomfort kam hota hai. Nurse indication aur alternatives assess karti hai aur authorized protocol follow karti hai.

**Har option ka reason**

- **A (Galat):** Raat ko baar-baar urine aana nocturia hai. Is symptom ki wajah assess karni chahiye, routine catheter lagana zaroori nahi hai.
- **B (Sahi):** Acute retention mein indicated catheter bladder ko drain kar sakti hai. Iski zarurat roz review karni chahiye.
- **C (Galat):** Chal sakne wale patient ki convenience catheter-related infection aur trauma ka extra risk justify nahi karti.
- **D (Galat):** Independent patient ko toilet access dena behtar hai. Catheter toileting assistance ka routine substitute nahi hai.

**Exam clue:** Retention aur incontinence alag problems hain. Catheter sirf appropriate indication ke liye lagti hai.

### Q2. An indwelling catheter drainage bag is found above the level of the bladder. What should the nurse do?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Priority:** High

- **A.** Reposition the bag below bladder level and assess drainage
- **B.** Keep it elevated to increase urine collection
- **C.** Disconnect the catheter to drain the bag
- **D.** Place the bag directly on the floor

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: A. Reposition the bag below bladder level and assess drainage**

Bladder urine ka storage organ hai aur drainage bag tak urine gravity se jaata hai. Jab bag bladder se upar hota hai, drainage ruk sakti hai aur contaminated urine ke wapas jaane ka risk badh sakta hai. Bag ko bladder ke neeche, floor se upar, aur tubing ko kink-free rakho.

**Har option ka reason**

- **A (Sahi):** Bag neeche rakhne se gravity drainage restore ho sakti hai. Nurse urine flow aur patient ke symptoms bhi check karti hai.
- **B (Galat):** Bag upar karne se gravity drainage behtar nahi hoti aur reverse flow ka risk ho sakta hai.
- **C (Galat):** Connection kholna unnecessary hai aur germs ko andar aane ka rasta de sakta hai.
- **D (Galat):** Floor se bag contaminate ho sakta hai. Use suitable support par rakhna chahiye.

**Exam clue:** Bag below bladder, off floor, no kinks.

### Q3. Why should a urinary drainage system normally remain closed?

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Priority:** Medium

- **A.** A closed system stops all urine flow
- **B.** The bag must never be emptied under any circumstances
- **C.** The system is used to measure arterial pressure
- **D.** Opening connections can introduce microorganisms into the urinary tract

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: D. Opening connections can introduce microorganisms into the urinary tract**

Closed system ka matlab hai ki catheter aur collecting tubing ka connection unnecessary khola nahi jaata. Microorganisms connection se enter karke urinary tract tak pahunch sakte hain. Bag ka approved outlet emptying ke liye use hota hai aur usko clean rakha jaata hai.

**Har option ka reason**

- **A (Galat):** Closed system mein urine freely drain ho sakta hai. Closed ka matlab blocked nahi hota.
- **B (Galat):** Bag bharne se pehle usse designated outlet se safely empty karna zaroori hai.
- **C (Galat):** Arterial pressure blood pressure hai aur uske liye alag monitoring equipment use hota hai.
- **D (Sahi):** Unnecessary disconnection se contamination ka rasta banta hai. Isliye aseptic closed drainage maintain karte hain.

**Exam clue:** Drainage outlet use karo, catheter-tubing junction ko routine mein mat kholo.

### Q4. A catheterized patient has no urine in the bag for two hours and reports suprapubic discomfort. What should the nurse do first?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Priority:** High

- **A.** Increase IV fluids regardless of fluid status
- **B.** Assume the kidneys have permanently failed
- **C.** Assess the patient and check for kinks, obstruction and bladder distension
- **D.** Forcefully irrigate the catheter without an order

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: C. Assess the patient and check for kinks, obstruction and bladder distension**

Suprapubic discomfort ka matlab lower abdomen mein bladder ke upar dard ya pressure hai. Urine bag khaali ho aur discomfort ho toh pehle patient ki condition, tubing ki position aur bladder distension check karo. Kink hatane par bhi flow na aaye toh timely escalation aur protocol ke hisaab se bladder assessment chahiye.

**Har option ka reason**

- **A (Galat):** Bina fluid status jaane fluids badhane se retention solve nahi hoti aur overload ho sakta hai.
- **B (Galat):** Do ghante ki absent drainage se permanent renal failure prove nahi hota. Mechanical blockage bhi ho sakta hai.
- **C (Sahi):** Patient aur drainage pathway dono check karne se blockage aur low production ke beech farq samajhne mein madad milti hai.
- **D (Galat):** Forceful irrigation mucosa ko damage kar sakti hai. Irrigation sirf specific indication aur authorized protocol ke mutabik honi chahiye.

**Exam clue:** No drainage plus bladder discomfort suggests possible obstruction. Assess first, escalate promptly.

### Q5. Which practice helps prevent urethral traction from an indwelling catheter?

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Priority:** Medium

- **A.** Leave the heavy drainage bag hanging from the catheter
- **B.** Tie the tubing to a moving bed rail with no slack
- **C.** Use an appropriate securement device and allow adequate slack
- **D.** Attach the tubing tightly so the catheter pulls during movement

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: C. Use an appropriate securement device and allow adequate slack**

Traction ka matlab catheter se urethra par khinchav padna hai. Jab patient hilta hai ya bag heavy hota hai, tube kheench sakti hai aur dard, bleeding ya tissue damage ho sakta hai. Appropriate securement aur enough slack movement ke dauran is khinchav ko kam karte hain.

**Har option ka reason**

- **A (Galat):** Bag ka weight catheter par padne se urethra par pull badhta hai.
- **B (Galat):** Moving rail se tube bandhne par rail upar-neeche hone se catheter khinch sakta hai.
- **C (Sahi):** Suitable securement catheter ko stable rakhta hai aur slack patient movement allow karta hai. Site anatomy aur policy par depend karti hai.
- **D (Galat):** Tube tight rakhna movement ko catheter par transfer karta hai aur trauma ka risk badhata hai.

**Exam clue:** Secure catheter and support bag separately.

### Q6. A urine culture is ordered for a patient with an existing indwelling catheter. Where should a fresh specimen usually be obtained?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Priority:** High

- **A.** From fluid used to wash the patient's skin
- **B.** From the designated sampling port using aseptic technique
- **C.** From urine that has been in the drainage bag all day
- **D.** By disconnecting the tubing at any point

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: B. From the designated sampling port using aseptic technique**

Urine culture lab mein microorganisms ki growth check karta hai. Purana bag urine patient ke fresh bladder urine ko accurately represent nahi kar sakta. Fresh sample ke liye designated needleless sampling port ko disinfect karke sterile syringe ya compatible adaptor se aseptic collection karo. Arbitrary tubing puncture mat karo.

**Har option ka reason**

- **A (Galat):** Skin wash fluid mein urine sample nahi hota aur contamination misleading result de sakti hai.
- **B (Sahi):** Designated port fresh sample lene deta hai aur closed connection preserve karta hai. Sample ko label karke lab instructions follow karo.
- **C (Galat):** Din bhar bag mein pada urine contaminated ya colonized ho sakta hai, isliye culture ke liye appropriate nahi hai.
- **D (Galat):** Tubing disconnect karna unnecessary contamination aur CAUTI risk badhata hai.

**Exam clue:** Culture = fresh aseptic sampling port. Old bag urine ya arbitrary puncture nahi.

**Clinical reference:** [Topic reference](https://www.cdc.gov/infection-control/hcp/cauti/summary-of-recommendations.html).

### Q10. A patient voids 100 mL and has a large postvoid residual on repeated bladder assessment. Which problem is suggested?

- **B.** Excessive renal filtration proven by residual volume

**Har option ka reason**

- **C (Sahi):** Repeated large residual incomplete bladder emptying suggest karta hai aur further assessment chahiye.
- **D (Galat):** Sirf 100 mL voided volume se complete emptying establish nahi hoti, kyunki residual bhi important hai.

### Q16. During catheter insertion a patient experiences severe pain and marked resistance. Which action is safest?

- **C (Galat):** Forceful advancement urethral wall ko damage kar sakti hai.
- **D (Galat):** Uncertain placement mein balloon inflate karne se urethra stretch ya tear ho sakti hai.

**Exam clue:** Resistance or severe pain means stop, never force.

### Q17. Before inflating an indwelling urinary catheter balloon, what must be confirmed?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Priority:** High

- **A.** That the drainage bag has already been disconnected
- **B.** Correct bladder placement according to the insertion protocol
- **C.** That the patient feels severe urethral pain
- **D.** That the balloon is positioned in the urethra

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: B. Correct bladder placement according to the insertion protocol**

Foley balloon ek chhota retaining balloon hai jo bladder ke andar inflated rehkar catheter ko place par rakhta hai. Urethra mein balloon inflate karne se serious trauma ho sakta hai. Urine return aur protocol ke mutabik sufficient advancement se bladder placement confirm karo. Agar placement uncertain ho toh balloon inflate na karo aur assistance lo.

**Har option ka reason**

- **A (Galat):** Bag disconnect karna bladder placement prove nahi karta aur infection risk badhata hai.
- **B (Sahi):** Correct bladder placement confirm karna balloon inflation ki necessary safety condition hai.
- **C (Galat):** Severe pain warning sign hai, correct placement ka proof nahi.
- **D (Galat):** Urethra mein balloon inflate karna tissue injury aur bleeding cause kar sakta hai.

**Exam clue:** Urine return alone may not establish balloon position. Follow device-specific insertion protocol.

### Q19. A patient with functional incontinence can sense bladder fullness but cannot reach the toilet in time due to limited mobility. Which plan addresses the main problem?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Priority:** High

- **A.** Assume permanent loss of bladder sensation
- **B.** Insert an indwelling catheter routinely without reviewing alternatives
- **C.** Restrict all fluid intake throughout the day
- **D.** Provide accessible toileting, scheduled assistance and suitable mobility support

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: D. Provide accessible toileting, scheduled assistance and suitable mobility support**

Functional incontinence mein patient ko bladder fullness ka pata ho sakta hai, lekin mobility, cognition ya environment ki wajah se toilet tak time par nahi pahunch pata. - **C (Galat):** Complete fluid restriction se dehydration ho sakti hai. Fluids individualized clinical plan ke mutabik dene chahiye.
- **D (Sahi):** Accessible toilet aur scheduled safe help patient ko time par bladder empty karne mein support karte hain.

**Exam clue:** Functional incontinence = access barrier. Improve access rather than routine catheter.

### Q20. A postoperative patient has low urine output despite a patent catheter. Which assessment is most appropriate?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Priority:** High

- **A.** Give a large unprescribed fluid bolus to every patient
- **B.** Document normal output without measuring
- **C.** Assess fluid balance, perfusion, vital signs and possible renal causes and escalate as indicated
- **D.** Assume catheter replacement alone will restore urine production

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: C. Assess fluid balance, perfusion, vital signs and possible renal causes and escalate as indicated**

Patent catheter ka matlab hai drainage pathway open hai. Phir bhi low urine output ho toh low circulating volume, bleeding, poor perfusion, acute kidney injury ya postoperative factors assess karo. Vitals, intake-output trend, fluid status aur relevant labs review karo. At-risk adults mein 0.5 mL/kg/hour se kam output ek important warning threshold hai, lekin urgent clinical response ke liye diagnostic duration ka wait mat karo.

**Har option ka reason**

- **A (Galat):** Har patient ko large unprescribed bolus dene se heart ya kidney problem mein overload ho sakta hai.
- **B (Galat):** Measurement ke bina normal output document karna inaccurate aur unsafe hai.
- **C (Sahi):** Whole-patient assessment low urine production ki possible cause identify karta hai aur timely treatment guide karta hai.
- **D (Galat):** Patent catheter replace karna kidney perfusion ya urine production problem ka direct treatment nahi hai.

**Exam clue:** Patent catheter plus oliguria = assess perfusion and renal causes, not automatic fluids.
