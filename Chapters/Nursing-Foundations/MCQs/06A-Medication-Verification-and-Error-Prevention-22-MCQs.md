### Q1. A prescription for a patient with rheumatoid arthritis lists oral methotrexate daily, but the previous verified regimen was once weekly. What should the nurse do before the next dose?

**Difficulty:** 🔴 Difficult · **Priority:** 🔴 MOST IMPORTANT · Original practice

A. Use the daily order because it is newer
B. Administer half the daily dose
C. Hold the questionable dose and urgently clarify the intended frequency with the prescriber and pharmacist
D. Change the order to weekly independently

<details>
<summary>✅ Sahi answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct answer: C. Hold the questionable dose and urgently clarify the intended frequency with the prescriber and pharmacist**

- **A (Incorrect):** New prescription bhi transcription ya prescribing error contain kar sakti hai. Prior verified weekly regimen se discrepancy ko resolve karna zaroori hai.
- **B (Incorrect):** Half daily dose karne se frequency error resolve nahi hota. Repeated exposure se toxicity accumulate ho sakti hai.
- **C (Correct):** Rheumatoid arthritis ke liye methotrexate commonly once weekly diya jaata hai. Accidental daily dosing se bone marrow suppression, mouth ulcers, infection aur potentially fatal toxicity ho sakti hai. Questionable dose hold karo aur urgently authorized clarification lo. Agar extra doses already di gayi hain toh urgent clinical assessment arrange karo.
- **D (Incorrect):** Nurse independently order ko weekly rewrite nahi karti. Prescriber se current authorized order chahiye.

**Exam clue:** Non-oncology methotrexate frequency mismatch is a high-risk warning.

- **B (Incorrect):** Swallow karane se potential wrong drug exposure verification se pehle ho jaayega.
- **C (Correct):** Patient concern ko safety signal samjho. Label, active ingredient, strength, formulation aur order compare karo, pharmacy se confirm karo aur verified difference explain karo.
- **D (Incorrect):** Color alone incorrect medicine prove nahi karta, blanket discard unnecessary hai.

**Exam clue:** Patient questions are an additional medication safety barrier.

### Q20. At shift change, the medication record indicates a time-critical dose was given, but the outgoing nurse reports it may not have been administered. What is safest?

**Difficulty:** 🔴 Difficult · **Priority:** 🔴 MOST IMPORTANT · Original practice

<details>
<summary>✅ Sahi answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct answer: D. Promptly verify actual administration, assess the patient and obtain an authorized decision before any repeat dose**
