### Q1. A nurse realizes at the end of a shift that a care event was not documented when it occurred. Which action is appropriate?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Priority:** High

- **A.** Backdate the entry to make it appear contemporaneous
- **B.** Ask the next nurse to sign the event as their own
- **C.** Omit the event because the shift is ending
- **D.** Enter a clearly identified late entry with actual event time and current entry time according to policy

<details><summary>✅ Sahi answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct answer: D. Enter a clearly identified late entry with actual event time and current entry time according to policy**

**Kyun sahi hai:** Late entry honest and traceable honi chahiye. Actual event time and entry time distinguish karo, observed facts document karo.

**Baaki options kyun galat hain?**

- **A.** Record chronology misleading ho jayegi.
- **B.** Accountability wrong person par transfer hogi.
- **C.** Relevant clinical information missing rahegi.

</details>

### Q2. In a handwritten record a nurse makes a minor factual error. What is the usual safe correction principle?

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Priority:** High

<details><summary>✅ Sahi answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

- **D.** Audit trail lose ho sakta hai.

</details>
