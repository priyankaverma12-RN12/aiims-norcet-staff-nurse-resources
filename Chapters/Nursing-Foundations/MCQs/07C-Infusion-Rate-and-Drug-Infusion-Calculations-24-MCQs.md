### Q1. A pump must deliver 270 mL over 2 hours and 15 minutes. The nurse can program whole mL/hr values. What rate delivers the ordered volume in the specified time?

**Difficulty:** 🟡 Medium · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD · Original practice · Calculation

A. 120 mL/hr
B. 135 mL/hr
C. 108 mL/hr
D. 125 mL/hr

<details>
<summary>✅ Sahi answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct answer: A. 120 mL/hr**

**Formula aur reasoning:** 2 hours 15 minutes ka matlab 2.25 hours hai. Rate = 270/2.25 = 120 mL/hr. Reverse check mein 120 × 2.25 = 270 mL.

- **A (Correct):** 120 × 2.25 se poora 270 mL milta hai.
- **B (Incorrect):** 135 × 2.25 = 303.75 mL hota hai, jo zyada hai.
- **C (Incorrect):** 108 × 2.25 = 243 mL hota hai, jo kam hai.
- **D (Incorrect):** 125 × 2.25 = 281.25 mL hota hai, jo zyada hai.

**Exam clue:** Drop factor ka unit gtt/mL aur gravity time minutes mein rakho.

D. The nurse should give 150 mL as an IV push

<details>
<summary>✅ Sahi answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

- **C (Incorrect):** Sirf deadline meet karne ke liye rate badhana fluid ya medicine overdose ka risk de sakta hai.
- **D (Incorrect):** IV push ek alag administration route aur rate hai, jo is order se authorized nahi hai.
