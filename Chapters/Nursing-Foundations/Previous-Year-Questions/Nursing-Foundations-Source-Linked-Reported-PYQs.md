### PYQ1. Before preparing a surgical wound dressing at the bedside, which basic infection-prevention action is essential?

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Topic:** Dressing procedure

- **A.** Perform hand hygiene
- **B.** Open all sterile supplies before cleaning hands
- **C.** Reuse soiled gloves
- **D.** Touch the wound to test its temperature

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: A. Perform hand hygiene**

Hands par microorganisms ho sakte hain jo wound ya dressing supplies tak transfer ho sakte hain. Hand hygiene clean ya aseptic task se pehle karna infection prevention ka basic step hai. Wound dressing change mein patient, order, pain aur supplies assess karo aur aseptic non-touch technique follow karo. Old dressing remove karne ke baad gloves change aur hand hygiene as indicated karo, taaki contaminated material se clean wound tak organisms transfer na hon. Gloves hand hygiene ka replacement nahi hain.

**Option-wise explanation:**

- **A (Sahi):** Hand hygiene procedure se pehle hand contamination kam karti hai. Listed options mein yahi essential infection-prevention action hai.
- **B (Galat):** Unclean hands se sterile supplies open karne par sterile field contaminate ho sakta hai. Supplies ko aseptic technique se handle karo.
- **C (Galat):** Soiled gloves reuse karne se microorganisms wound aur clean equipment tak transfer ho sakte hain. Gloves ko appropriate time par remove ya replace karo aur hand hygiene karo.
- **D (Galat):** Wound ko unnecessary direct touch karna contamination aur tissue injury ka risk badha sakta hai. Temperature ya infection assess karne ke liye approved clinical assessment method use karo.

**Exam clue:** Before aseptic wound care, perform hand hygiene. Gloves do not replace it.
