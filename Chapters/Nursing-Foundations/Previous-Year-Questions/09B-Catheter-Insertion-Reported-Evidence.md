### Related independently worded exercise — uncounted

An adult woman without mobility restrictions requires routine urethral catheterization at the bedside. Which commonly used position permits visualization of the urethral meatus while maintaining appropriate draping?

**Difficulty:** 🟡 Medium · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD

- **A.** Prone with legs straight
- **B.** Dorsal recumbent with knees flexed
- **C.** Trendelenburg with legs together
- **D.** High Fowler's with legs hanging over the bed

<details><summary>✅ Clinically supported answer and explanations</summary>

**Correct answer: B. Dorsal recumbent with knees flexed.**

**A kyun galat hai:** Prone position mein patient pet ke bal hoti hai. Routine female urethral catheterization ke liye meatus ka suitable access mushkil hota hai.

**B kyun sahi hai:** Dorsal recumbent mein patient back par leti hai aur knees flexed hoti hain. Suitable leg positioning aur draping se meatus visualize karna aasaan hota hai. Mobility restrictions ho toh trained team safe alternative choose karti hai.

**C kyun galat hai:** Trendelenburg mein head body se neeche hota hai. Ye routine female catheterization ke liye required position nahi hai.

**D kyun galat hai:** High Fowler's mein trunk upright hota hai aur bed ke edge par legs latkana routine sterile field aur genital access ke liye suitable nahi hai.
