### 🔵 NORCET 7 Prelims 2024 (memory-based report)

During conventional upper-arm manual BP measurement, which artery is the routine listening site for cuff-related sounds?

- A. Femoral
- B. Brachial
- C. Carotid
- D. Dorsalis pedis

<details><summary>Answer and explanation</summary>

**B.** Brachial artery auscultation Korotkoff sounds identify karti hai. Other sites routine upper-arm auscultatory method ke listening sites nahi hain.
