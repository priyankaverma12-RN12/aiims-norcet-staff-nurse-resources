### GMCH Nursing Officer, 29 June 2025 — reported

Which information is the main evidence base for identifying an individual patient's nursing care problems?

- A. Only the physician's disease label
- B. Relevant observed and patient-reported assessment findings
- C. Ward bed allocation
- D. The standard discharge date

<details><summary>Answer and explanation</summary>

**B.** Relevant subjective and objective findings patient response identify karne mein help karte hain. A disease label context provide karta hai but alone individualized response establish nahi karta. C and D clinical evidence nahi hain. ### AIIMS Jodhpur Nursing Officer, 10 December 2019 — memory-based report

Which phase involves gathering and verifying information about a patient's actual and potential care needs?

- A. Implementation
- B. Planning
- C. Assessment
- D. Evaluation

<details><summary>Answer and explanation</summary>

**C.** Assessment information collection and validation hai. Planning goals/interventions choose karti hai, implementation actual actions, evaluation outcomes compare karti hai.
