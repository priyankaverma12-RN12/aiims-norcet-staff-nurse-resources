Which information is the main evidence base for identifying an individual patient's nursing care problems?

- A. Only the physician's disease label
- B. Relevant observed and patient-reported assessment findings
- C. Ward bed allocation
- D. The standard discharge date

<details><summary>Answer and explanation</summary>

**B.** Relevant subjective and objective findings patient response identify karne mein help karte hain. A disease label context provide karta hai but alone individualized response establish nahi karta. C and D clinical evidence nahi hain.
