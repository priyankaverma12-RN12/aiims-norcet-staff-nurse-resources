# Pulse assessment — reported exam evidence

### SGPGI Staff Nurse, 28 February 2024 shift I — independently adapted reported item

Which anatomical landmark usually corresponds to the adult cardiac apical impulse?

- A. Left second intercostal space near sternum
- B. Right second intercostal space near sternum
- C. Left fifth intercostal space near the midclavicular line
- D. Epigastric midline

<details><summary>Answer and explanation</summary>

**C.** Usual adult apex left fifth intercostal space near MCL hai. A and B are more superior cardiac auscultation areas, D not usual apex. Individual anatomy can vary. Palpation of PMI and auscultation of apical rate are related but different. </details>
