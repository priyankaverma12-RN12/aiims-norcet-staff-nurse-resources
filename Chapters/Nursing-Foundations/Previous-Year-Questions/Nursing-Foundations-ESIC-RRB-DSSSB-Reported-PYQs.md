### PYQ1. What distinguishes medical asepsis from surgical asepsis?

**Reported exam:** ESIC Nursing Officer, 2019 Shift 2

[Question source](https://nprep.in/question-answer/medical-asepsis-differs-from-surgical-asepsis-in-that-it-is-aimed-at-OjzwE-PUUdH6Zdbva2pZO)

- **A.** Medical asepsis reduces microorganisms and their spread, whereas surgical asepsis maintains a sterile field
- **B.** Medical asepsis always removes every bacterial spore
- **C.** Surgical asepsis permits routine contamination of sterile tissue
- **D.** Both eliminate the need for hand hygiene

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: A. Medical asepsis reduces microorganisms and their spread, whereas surgical asepsis maintains a sterile field**

Medical asepsis clean technique se organisms aur spread reduce karti hai. Surgical asepsis sterile field maintain karne par focus karti hai. Hand hygiene dono mein important hai.

</details>

### PYQ2. Which bedside device measures a capillary blood glucose value?

**Reported exam:** RRB Staff Nurse Delhi, 2015

[Question source](https://nprep.in/question-answer/which-instrument-is-used-to-check-rbs-HXZRIE3fpS_BajgWTKzDm)

- **A.** Sphygmomanometer
- **B.** Pulse oximeter
- **C.** Peak flow meter
- **D.** Glucometer

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: D. Glucometer**

Glucometer capillary blood sample ka glucose estimate karta hai. Sphygmomanometer BP, pulse oximeter oxygen saturation aur peak flow meter expiratory airflow assess karta hai.

</details>

### PYQ3. Which gauge is conventionally represented by a pink peripheral IV cannula in standard color coding?

**Reported exam:** DSSSB October 2024

[Question source](https://nprep.in/question-answer/what-is-the-size-of-the-pink-colour-coded-iv-cannula-_vj4BTnmn8OKmPqADiHJ0)

- **A.** 22 G
- **B.** 24 G
- **C.** 20 G
- **D.** 18 G

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: C. 20 G**

<!-- job5-rationale -->
Common peripheral IV cannula color convention mein pink 20 G hai. Gauge number increase hone par usual outer diameter decrease hota hai. Same gauge ke devices ki length aur internal diameter different ho sakti hai, isliye actual flow rate manufacturer label se verify karo.

**Option-by-option reasoning**

- **A:** 22 G commonly blue hai, pink nahi.
- **B:** 24 G commonly yellow hai aur comparatively smaller access hai.
- **C:** 20 G commonly pink hai, isliye stated convention mein correct hai. Printed size confirm karo.
- **D:** 18 G commonly green hai aur larger access ho sakta hai.

**Exam clue:** Pink = 20 G, verify printed device size rather than color alone.

### PYQ4. Which conceptual framework places immediate physiological needs before higher-level psychosocial needs when planning routine care?

**Reported exam:** DSSSB Public Health Nurse, 2015

[Question source](https://nprep.in/question-answer/a-common-framework-that-helps-to-guide-the-prioritization-of-nursing-tasks-JN8eJd3ZqhiCY5lZ19qPN)

- **A.** Glasgow Coma Scale
- **B.** Maslow's hierarchy of needs
- **C.** Apgar score
- **D.** Braden Scale

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: B. Maslow's hierarchy of needs**

Maslow framework physiological aur safety needs ko foundational level par rakhti hai. Acute clinical emergencies mein ABC aur actual patient stability bhi priority guide karte hain. Other scales different clinical assessments ke liye hain.

</details>

### PYQ5. Which finding is secondary subjective information in a nursing assessment?

**Reported exam:** DSSSB, 28 August 2019 Shift 1

[Question source](https://nprep.in/question-answer/which-of-the-following-elements-comes-under-secondary-subjective-data-4HfSpPZDAaVJHvpa9xzix)

- **A.** The patient directly describes current pain
- **B.** A family member reports that the patient complained of nausea overnight
- **C.** The nurse measures BP as 110/70 mmHg
- **D.** A laboratory reports serum sodium

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: B. A family member reports that the patient complained of nausea overnight**

Secondary subjective information doosre informant se reported experience hoti hai. BP aur lab values objective hain. Patient ka direct pain report primary subjective data hai.

</details>
