## 🔵 HPSSC Staff Nurse 2021 — retrieved third-party report

### Q1. In a gravity-flow exercise, 500 mL is to be infused over 5 hours using a 20-gtt/mL set. If the observed flow is 30 gtt/min, which comparison with the calculated target is correct?

**Difficulty:** 🟡 Medium · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD · Source-based adaptation

A. The calculated target is about 33 gtt/min, so the observed rate is lower.
B. The calculated target is 25 gtt/min, so the observed rate is higher.
C. The calculated target is 100 gtt/min, so the observed rate is lower.
D. The calculated target and observed rate are identical.

<details>
<summary>✅ Sahi answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct answer: A. The calculated target is about 33 gtt/min, so the observed rate is lower.**

Pehle 5 hours ko 300 minutes mein convert karo. Target = 500 × 20/300 = 33.33 gtt/min hai. Nearest whole drop 33 gtt/min hai. Observed 30 gtt/min is target se kam hai. Actual clinical care mein patient, tubing aur order assess karke authorized plan follow karo.

- **A (Correct):** 33 gtt/min target aur 30 observed ka comparison sahi hai.
- **B (Incorrect):** 25 gtt/min tab milta agar 15-gtt/mL factor hota. Yahan set 20 gtt/mL hai.
- **C (Incorrect):** 100 mL/hr hourly volume rate hai, 100 gtt/min nahi.
- **D (Incorrect):** 30 aur approximately 33 equal nahi hain.

**Exam clue:** Pehle drop factor aur time ki unit confirm karo.

## 🔵 AIIMS NORCET 6 Prelims 2024 — retrieved memory-based report

### Q2. In a fictional intermittent infusion exercise, 50 mL is prescribed over 30 minutes. Two compatible training sets have verified drop factors of 15 and 20 gtt/mL. Which pair of nearest whole gravity rates corresponds to the two sets respectively?

**Difficulty:** 🟡 Medium · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD · Source-based adaptation

A. 25 and 25 gtt/min
B. 25 and 33 gtt/min
C. 33 and 25 gtt/min
D. 100 and 100 gtt/min

<details>
<summary>✅ Sahi answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct answer: B. 25 and 33 gtt/min**

First set mein 50 × 15/30 = 25 gtt/min hota hai. Doosre set mein 50 × 20/30 = 33.33, nearest whole drop 33 gtt/min hota hai. Fluid ka prescribed volume aur time same rehne par bhi drop size ke hisaab se count change hota hai.

- **A (Incorrect):** Doosre set ka factor alag hai, isliye count same nahi rahega.
- **B (Correct):** Dono calculated rates respective factors ke hisaab se correct hain.
- **C (Incorrect):** Rates reverse hain.
- **D (Incorrect):** 50 mL over half hour se 100 mL/hr aata hai. Ye gravity drop count nahi hai.

**Exam clue:** Macrodrip set ka factor kabhi guess mat karo.

## 🔵 AIIMS NORCET 9 Prelims 2025 — retrieved memory-based report

### Q3. A fictional continuous infusion contains 50 mg of medication in a final volume of 500 mL. The authorized dose is 15 mcg/min. A pump log instead records 6 mL/hr. Which evaluation is correct?

**Difficulty:** 🔴 Difficult · **Priority:** 🔴 MOST IMPORTANT · Source-based adaptation

A. The logged setting represents 15 mcg/min.
B. The logged setting represents 60 mcg/min.
C. The logged setting represents 10 mcg/min, below the authorized dose.
D. The logged setting represents 600 mcg/min.

<details>
<summary>✅ Sahi answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct answer: C. The logged setting represents 10 mcg/min, below the authorized dose.**

Bag mein 50 mg = 50,000 mcg hai. Concentration 50,000/500 = 100 mcg/mL hai. Logged 6 mL/hr se 6 × 100 = 600 mcg/hr deliver hota hai. Per minute 600/60 = 10 mcg/min hota hai. Ye 15 mcg/min se kam hai. Authorized order ka numerical volume rate 15 × 60/100 = 9 mL/hr hai. Actual discrepancy mein patient assessment, order confirmation aur authorized medication safety process follow karo.

- **A (Incorrect):** 15 mcg/min original order hai, logged rate ka result nahi.
- **B (Incorrect):** 60 mcg/min ke liye 36 mL/hr chahiye hota.
- **C (Correct):** Unit conversion aur logged rate dono consider kiye gaye hain.
- **D (Incorrect):** 600 mcg/hr hourly mass hai, minute wali dose nahi.

**Exam clue:** Pump log se drug dose nikalte waqt concentration se multiply aur 60 se divide karo.
