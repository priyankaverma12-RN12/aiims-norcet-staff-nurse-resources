### Related practice exercise — uncounted

What can happen to bacterial counts when an unpreserved culture specimen remains at room temperature for an excessive time after collection?

- **A.** Bacterial counts may increase because organisms multiply after collection
- **B.** The collection time becomes irrelevant to interpretation
- **C.** All bacteria are immediately destroyed by room-temperature storage
- **D.** Delayed storage always improves correlation with the patient's original urinary bacterial count

<details><summary>✅ Correct answer and individual explanations</summary>

**Correct answer: A. Bacterial counts may increase because organisms multiply after collection**

**A kyun sahi hai:** Bacteria collection ke baad bhi suitable conditions mein divide kar sakte hain. Delayed transport se culture count artificially increase ho sakta hai, isliye laboratory-specific time aur preservation instructions follow karo.

**B kyun galat hai:** Collection time se lab ko transport delay aur sample acceptability assess karne mein help milti hai. Is information ko ignore nahi karna chahiye.

**C kyun galat hai:** Room temperature sterilization method nahi hai. Kuch organisms grow kar sakte hain aur kuch ki recovery decrease ho sakti hai.

**D kyun galat hai:** Delay bacterial population change kar sakta hai. Isliye original urinary count aur reported count ka correlation worsen ho sakta hai.
