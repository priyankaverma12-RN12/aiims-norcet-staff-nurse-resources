### Related independently phrased practice adaptation — uncounted

An adult experiences involuntary urinary leakage with a sudden compelling desire to void. Which bladder mechanism is commonly associated with this symptom pattern?

A. Urethral outlet obstruction causing incomplete emptying

B. Involuntary detrusor contractions during bladder filling

C. Loss of pelvic support causing exertional leakage

D. An abnormal communication allowing continuous leakage

<details><summary>✅ Clinically supported answer and rationales</summary>

**Correct: B. Involuntary detrusor contractions during bladder filling.**

- **A — Incorrect:** Outlet obstruction bladder emptying ko impair kar sakta hai. Yeh retention aur overflow symptoms ka contributor ho sakta hai, lekin sudden urgency-associated leakage ka classic mechanism nahi hai.
- **B — Correct:** Storage phase mein involuntary detrusor contractions urgency aur leakage contribute kar sakti hain. Clinical urgency incontinence aur urodynamically proven detrusor overactivity ko identical diagnosis nahi samajhna chahiye.
- **C — Incorrect:** Pelvic support loss pressure-related urethral closure ko impair kar sakta hai. Yeh coughing ya lifting ke saath stress leakage se zyada closely associated hai.
- **D — Incorrect:** Fistula se urine abnormal pathway se leak ho sakta hai. Iska pattern persistent leakage ho sakta hai aur sudden urgency ke saath classic association nahi hai.

Clinical reference: [NIDDK bladder control symptoms and causes](https://www.niddk.nih.gov/health-information/urologic-diseases/bladder-control-problems/symptoms-causes).
