## 🔵 AIIMS Delhi Nursing Officer, 15 September 2019 — memory-based report

### Q1. An ICU nurse is asked to use an ordinary central venous catheter to obtain a direct measurement of left ventricular ejection fraction. Which explanation is correct?

**Difficulty:** 🟡 Medium · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD · Source-based adaptation

A. An ordinary central venous catheter cannot directly measure left ventricular ejection fraction.
B. Any central venous pressure value is numerically equal to the ejection fraction.
C. A central catheter measures left ventricular volume directly whenever fluid is infused.
D. A central catheter in a vein measures systemic arterial pressure without further equipment.

<details>
<summary>✅ Sahi answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct answer: A. An ordinary central venous catheter cannot directly measure left ventricular ejection fraction.**

- **A (Correct):** Ejection fraction ka matlab ventricle mein filling ke baad present blood mein se kitna percentage ek contraction mein bahar nikalta hai. Ordinary CVC se suitable setup ke saath central venous pressure measure kiya ja sakta hai. Lekin left ventricle ka ejection fraction measure karne ke liye echocardiography jaise appropriate method ki zaroorat hoti hai.
- **B (Incorrect):** CVP pressure ki unit mein measure hota hai, jabki ejection fraction percentage hoti hai. Dono alag physiological measurements hain.
- **C (Incorrect):** Central vein se fluid dene ka matlab ye nahi hai ki catheter left ventricular volume ko directly measure kar raha hai. Pressure aur volume ka relationship bhi patient ki condition ke hisaab se change ho sakta hai.
- **D (Incorrect):** Venous catheter ka tip vein mein hota hai. Systemic arterial pressure ki direct measurement ke liye appropriate arterial catheter aur monitoring setup chahiye.

**Exam clue:** CVP right-sided filling pressure ko reflect karta hai, lekin ye left ventricular ejection fraction ka direct measurement nahi hai.
