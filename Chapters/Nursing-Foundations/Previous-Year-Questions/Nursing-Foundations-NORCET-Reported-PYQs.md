### PYQ1. What measurement error can result from using an excessively small BP cuff on an adult arm?

**Reported exam:** NORCET 9 Prelims, 2025

[Question source](https://nprep.in/question-answer/when-measuring-blood-pressure-if-a-cuff-that-is-too-small-is-hd6dALQy2PFx3kJ8Relnh)

- **A.** Falsely elevated BP
- **B.** Falsely low BP in every case
- **C.** No effect on BP
- **D.** A falsely low body temperature

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: A. Falsely elevated BP**

Small cuff artery ko compress karne ke liye extra pressure require kar sakta hai. Isliye BP falsely high aa sakti hai. Correct cuff size arm circumference se choose karna hai.

### PYQ2. If the systolic pressure is 160 and the diastolic pressure is 80 mmHg, what is the pulse pressure?

**Reported exam:** NORCET 2, 2021 Shift 2

[Question source](https://nprep.in/question-answer/a-patient-s-blood-pressure-is-160-80-mm-hg-then-what-T2fPlkIup9vvWFPa-9VQb)

- **A.** 120 mmHg
- **B.** 107 mmHg
- **C.** 240 mmHg
- **D.** 80 mmHg

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: D. 80 mmHg**

Pulse pressure systolic minus diastolic BP hai. 160 minus 80 = 80 mmHg. 120 simple mean, 107 approximate MAP aur 240 sum hai.

</details>

### PYQ4. A drug is prescribed as 200 mg twice daily. For this independently constructed example, the available tablet strength is 100 mg. How many tablets are needed per administration?

**Reported exam:** NORCET 6 Prelims, 2024, dose-calculation concept

[Question source](https://nprep.in/question-answer/a-physician-orders-a-medication-to-be-administered-to-a-patient-in-Xnb2u_iUTYFaDu4gGQSH-)

- **A.** 0.5 tablet
- **B.** 2 tablets
- **C.** 4 tablets
- **D.** 1 tablet

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: B. 2 tablets**

Per-dose amount 200 mg hai. 200/100 = 2 tablets per administration. Daily total four tablets hoga. Source snippet mein full stock strength available nahi thi, isliye 100 mg strength nayi practice value hai, original paper ka claim nahi.

</details>

### PYQ5. Which routine practice reduces transmission of microorganisms between patient contacts?

**Reported exam:** NORCET 2, reported Shift 2

[Question source](https://nprep.in/question-answer/what-are-the-best-things-to-break-the-chain-of-infection-k3QjA7VxC2wrmE7X0xqxp)

- **A.** Skip hand cleaning when skin appears clean
- **B.** Perform appropriate hand hygiene at the indicated moments
- **C.** Wear the same gloves for multiple patients
- **D.** Use antibiotics in every healthy patient

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: B. Perform appropriate hand hygiene at the indicated moments**

Hands microorganisms transfer kar sakte hain. Indicated moments par hand hygiene chain of infection break karne mein help karti hai. Same gloves reuse nahi karni chahiye. Routine unnecessary antibiotics appropriate nahi hain aur visible cleanliness sterility prove nahi karti.

</details>

### PYQ6. During female urinary catheter preparation, which cleaning direction helps avoid transfer of organisms from the anal area toward the urethra?

**Reported exam:** NORCET 4, 2023, reported paper transcription

[Question source](https://nextnurse.in/pyq/norcet/norcet-norcet-4)

- **A.** Front to back with the approved aseptic technique
- **B.** Back to front with one reused swab
- **C.** Repeated wiping from the anus toward the urethra
- **D.** No cleaning before insertion

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: A. Front to back with the approved aseptic technique**

Female urinary meatus anus ke paas hota hai. Anus ke area mein gut bacteria ho sakte hain, isliye cleansing front se back ki taraf ki jaati hai. Acute-care catheter insertion mein approved aseptic preparation aur fresh sterile supplies follow karna zaroori hai. Routine catheter maintenance hygiene ko insertion preparation se confuse mat karo.

**Har option ka reason**

- **A (Sahi):** Front-to-back cleaning anus se urethra ki taraf contamination transfer ka risk kam karti hai. Approved insertion protocol follow karna chahiye.
- **B (Galat):** Back-to-front wiping anal organisms urethral opening tak la sakti hai. Used swab reuse karna contamination aur badha sakta hai.
- **C (Galat):** Anus se urethra ki taraf repeatedly wiping karne se contamination ka risk badhta hai.
- **D (Galat):** Insertion se pehle indicated aseptic preparation skip karna infection prevention ke against hai.

**Exam clue:** Front to back during preparation, each swab as protocol directs.

**Clinical reference:** [CDC CAUTI recommendations](https://www.cdc.gov/infection-control/hcp/cauti/summary-of-recommendations.html). Historical source attribution pending direct verification. **YouTube MCQ practice:** Specific verification pending.

**Supplementary topic practice:** [Nurses Pro — catheterization exam MCQs, published chapter at 29:34](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=jQhLFhssrNE&t=1774s). Publisher description and chapter marker indexed. Specific question coverage and playback remain unverified.

</details>

### PYQ7. What is the key purpose of the Z-track technique for an indicated intramuscular medication?

**Reported exam:** NORCET 4, 2023, reported paper transcription

[Question source](https://nextnurse.in/pyq/norcet/norcet-norcet-4)

- **A.** Create an intradermal wheal
- **B.** Deliver medication directly into a vein
- **C.** Eliminate the risk of allergy
- **D.** Reduce leakage of medication into subcutaneous tissue

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: D. Reduce leakage of medication into subcutaneous tissue**

Z-track skin aur subcutaneous tissue displace karke needle pathway offset karti hai. Isse selected irritating IM drugs ka leakage kam ho sakta hai. Intradermal wheal aur IV route different hain. Allergy prevention guaranteed nahi hai.

</details>

### PYQ8. Which pressure injury stage is consistent with partial-thickness skin loss and exposed dermis without visible adipose or deeper structures?

**Reported exam:** NORCET 4, 2023, reported paper transcription

[Question source](https://nextnurse.in/pyq/norcet/norcet-norcet-4)

- **A.** Stage 3
- **B.** Stage 4
- **C.** Stage 2
- **D.** Stage 1

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: C. Stage 2**

Stage 2 partial-thickness loss hai. Stage 1 skin intact hoti hai. Stage 3 full-thickness skin loss aur stage 4 exposed or palpable deeper structures se related hai. Current staging definitions use ki gayi hain rather than older wording.

</details>
