### Q1. A hypothetical drug is ordered as 28 micrograms. The supplied solution contains 42 micrograms in 21 mL. What volume corresponds to the prescribed amount?

**Difficulty:** 🟡 Medium · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD · **[🔵 AIIMS NORCET 11 September 2022 — reported recall](https://testbook.com/question-answer/the-drug-prescription-is-20mcg-available-dose-for--6320757f33461993ecaf6e27)**

A. 7 mL
B. 14 mL
C. 21 mL
D. 28 mL

<details>
<summary>✅ Sahi answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct answer: B. 14 mL**

- **A (Incorrect):** 7 mL mein 14 micrograms milengi, jo ordered amount ka half hai.
- **B (Correct):** 42/21 = 2 micrograms/mL. Ab 28/2 = 14 mL. Reverse check 14 × 2 = 28 micrograms hai. Original reported numbers differ, tested formula same hai.
- **C (Incorrect):** 21 mL poori 42 micrograms provide karti hai.
- **D (Incorrect):** 28 mL × 2 = 56 micrograms hai, jo double dose hai.

### Q2. In a pharmacy-supervised arithmetic exercise, a high-alert electrolyte stock is labeled 36 mEq per 18 mL. What stock volume contains 24 mEq before any authorized dilution or preparation?

**Difficulty:** 🟡 Medium · **Priority:** 🔴 MOST IMPORTANT · **[🔵 AIIMS NORCET 1 September 2020 — reported recall](https://testbook.com/question-answer/you-are-preparing-to-give-potassium-chloride-30-me--6177bc4d69d7518029004f60)**

A. 6 mL
B. 18 mL
C. 24 mL
D. 12 mL

<details>
<summary>✅ Sahi answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct answer: D. 12 mL**

- **A (Incorrect):** 6 mL mein 12 mEq milengi, required dose ka half.
- **B (Incorrect):** 18 mL mein full 36 mEq hain.
- **C (Incorrect):** 24 mL se 48 mEq milengi.
- **D (Correct):** 36/18 = 2 mEq/mL. 24/2 = 12 mL. Yeh stock-volume arithmetic hai, administration volume ya infusion instruction nahi. Concentrated potassium chloride must never be given IV push. Pharmacy-approved dilution, rate, monitoring and high-alert policy required hain.
