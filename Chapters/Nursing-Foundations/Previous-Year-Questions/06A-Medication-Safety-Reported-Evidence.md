### Q1. Which check completes the conventional six-right framework in addition to correct patient, medicine, dose, route and time?

**Difficulty:** 🟡 Medium · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD · **🔵 HSSC Staff Nurse 2019, held 25 July 2021 — directly retrieved third-party report**

A. Right ward location
B. Right documentation
C. Right package color
D. Right brand familiarity

<details>
<summary>✅ Sahi answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct answer: B. Right documentation**

- **A (Incorrect):** Ward location patient ki identity establish nahi karti. Bed transfer ho sakta hai, isliye do approved patient-specific identifiers use karo.
- **B (Correct):** Conventional six-right framework mein right documentation include hoti hai. Nurse actual administration ke baad correct dose, route aur time record karti hai. Refusal, omission ya delay ko given record nahi karna chahiye. Accurate record next shift ko duplicate ya missed dose prevent karne mein help karta hai. Extended frameworks right reason, response aur refusal jaise additional rights bhi include kar sakte hain.
- **C (Incorrect):** Package color strength ya active ingredient ka reliable proof nahi hai. Full label aur current order match karo.
- **D (Incorrect):** Brand familiarity ke bawajood manufacturer, formulation aur strength change ho sakte hain. Familiar appearance verification replace nahi karti.

**Exam clue:** Traditional six rights include documentation. Different expanded frameworks may list additional rights.

### Q2. During handover, the nurse discovers a medication administration record discrepancy that could affect the next scheduled dose. Which response is appropriate?

**Difficulty:** 🟡 Medium · **Priority:** 🔴 MOST IMPORTANT · **🔵 RRB Nursing Superintendent 12 March 2026 Shift 3 — directly retrieved third-party report**

A. Remove the conflicting entry without an audit trail
B. Wait for the following shift regardless of clinical urgency
C. Promptly escalate and reconcile the discrepancy through the responsible team before a potentially unsafe repeat dose
D. Ignore the discrepancy if the patient currently feels well

<details>
<summary>✅ Sahi answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct answer: C. Promptly escalate and reconcile the discrepancy through the responsible team before a potentially unsafe repeat dose**

- **A (Incorrect):** Conflicting entry bina traceable correction delete karne se actual administration history lose ho sakti hai. Approved amendment process use karo.
- **B (Incorrect):** Following shift tak wait karne se time-critical dose miss ho sakti hai ya duplicate administration ka risk badh sakta hai. Urgency assess karo.
- **C (Correct):** Patient ki current condition assess karo aur outgoing nurse, medication record aur available evidence se actual dose status promptly verify karo. Uncertainty remain ho toh clinician ya pharmacist se authorized decision lo before repeat dose. Accurate correction aur handover se next nurse ko safe plan milta hai.
- **D (Incorrect):** Patient abhi well feel karta hai, lekin some dosing errors ka effect delayed ho sakta hai. Discrepancy investigate karna zaroori hai.

**Exam clue:** Uncertain dose status: verify and escalate before repeat.
