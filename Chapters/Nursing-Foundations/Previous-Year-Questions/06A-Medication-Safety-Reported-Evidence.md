### Q1. Which check completes the conventional six-right framework in addition to correct patient, medicine, dose, route and time?

**Difficulty:** 🟡 Medium · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD · **🔵 HSSC Staff Nurse 2019, held 25 July 2021 — retrieved indexed report**

A. Right ward location
B. Right documentation
C. Right package color
D. Right brand familiarity

<details>
<summary>✅ Sahi answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct answer: B. Right documentation**

- **A (Incorrect):** Location patient-specific medication check ka substitute nahi hai.
- **B (Correct):** Traditional six rights mein actual administration ka accurate documentation included hai. Record promptly after giving the medicine to prevent omissions and duplicate doses. Extended frameworks include other rights too.
- **C (Incorrect):** Packaging color medication identity reliably establish nahi karti.
- **D (Incorrect):** Familiarity full label and order verification replace nahi karti.

### Q2. During handover, the nurse discovers a medication administration record discrepancy that could affect the next scheduled dose. Which response is appropriate?

**Difficulty:** 🟡 Medium · **Priority:** 🔴 MOST IMPORTANT · **🔵 RRB Nursing Superintendent 12 March 2026 Shift 3 — retrieved indexed report**

A. Remove the conflicting entry without an audit trail
B. Wait for the following shift regardless of clinical urgency
C. Promptly escalate and reconcile the discrepancy through the responsible team before a potentially unsafe repeat dose
D. Ignore the discrepancy if the patient currently feels well

<details>
<summary>✅ Sahi answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct answer: C. Promptly escalate and reconcile the discrepancy through the responsible team before a potentially unsafe repeat dose**

- **A (Incorrect):** Untraceable alteration record integrity compromise karti hai.
- **B (Incorrect):** Delay missed or duplicated medicine ka risk badha sakta hai.
- **C (Correct):** Discrepancy report and verify actual administration karo. Patient assessment aur authorized clinical decision important hain, especially time-critical medicine.
- **D (Incorrect):** Symptoms absent hone se medication error exclude nahi hoti.
