### PYQ1. Which electrolyte may require replacement when an ileostomy has substantial ongoing output?

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Topic:** Ileostomy

- **A.** Sodium
- **B.** Lead
- **C.** Mercury
- **D.** Excess iron as the main lost electrolyte

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: A. Sodium**

Ileostomy se fluid ke saath sodium bhi nikal sakta hai. High output mein dehydration aur electrolyte loss ka assessment zaroori hai.

**Baaki options kyun galat hain?**

- **B.** Lead toxic metal hai, replacement nutrient nahi hai.
- **C.** Mercury toxic hai.
- **D.** Iron high-output salt loss ka main replacement nahi hai.

</details>
