### PYQ1. In gastric digestion, which nutrient is the main substrate for pepsin?

**Difficulty:** Easy/Medium | **Topic:** Pepsin

- **A.** Protein
- **B.** Starch
- **C.** Triglycerides
- **D.** Vitamins

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation</summary>

**Correct: A. Protein**

Pepsin stomach ke acidic environment mein protein ko smaller peptides mein todta hai. Starch ke liye amylase aur triglycerides ke liye lipase ka role hota hai. Vitamins ko pepsin digest nahi karta.

</details>
