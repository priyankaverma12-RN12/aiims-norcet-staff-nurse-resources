- **C.** Pain reflects progressive gastric acid reflux
- **D.** Pain reflects a fall in circulating calcium

<details><summary>✅ Correct answer and detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: A. Initial visceral pain followed by localized parietal peritoneal irritation**

**A kyun sahi hai:** Early visceral afferents poorly localized pain transmit karte hain. Adjacent parietal peritoneum involve hone par pain more localized ho sakta hai.

**B kyun galat hai:** Food transit se appendiceal inflammatory pain migration explain nahi hoti.

**C kyun galat hai:** Reflux upper digestive tract ka process hai aur right lower quadrant localization explain nahi karta.

**D kyun galat hai:** Calcium change is characteristic temporal pain pattern ka explanation nahi hai.

**Exam clue:** Visceral pain vague, parietal irritation localized ho sakti hai.

### Q20. A recovering appendectomy patient requests guidance on resuming activity. Which instruction is appropriate?

**Difficulty:** 🟡 Medium · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD · **Exam focus:** Original NORCET/AIIMS/PGIMER-style practice

- **A.** Perform heavy lifting immediately despite pain
- **B.** Follow the surgeon's individualized activity and lifting guidance and progress as tolerated
- **C.** Remain immobile indefinitely after uncomplicated recovery
- **D.** Use another patient's discharge plan without checking instructions

<details><summary>✅ Correct answer and detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: B. Follow the surgeon's individualized activity and lifting guidance and progress as tolerated**

**A kyun galat hai:** Unreviewed strain healing tissue aur symptoms worsen kar sakta hai.

**B kyun sahi hai:** Recovery approach operative findings, wound status aur patient condition ke hisaab se differ karta hai. Graded activity aur prescribed restrictions follow karo.

**C kyun galat hai:** Prolonged unnecessary immobility deconditioning aur thrombotic risk badha sakti hai.

**D kyun galat hai:** Different procedures aur complications different restrictions demand karte hain.

**Exam clue:** Postoperative activity individualized instructions se guide karo.
