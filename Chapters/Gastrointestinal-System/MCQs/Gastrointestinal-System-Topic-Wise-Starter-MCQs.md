## GI bleeding

### Q1. A patient with black tarry stool becomes dizzy and tachycardic. Which assessment is the priority?

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Topic:** GI bleeding

- **A.** Hemodynamic status and possible gastrointestinal blood loss
- **B.** Routine dietary fiber intake alone
- **C.** Visual acuity testing
- **D.** A nonurgent dental examination

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: A. Hemodynamic status and possible gastrointestinal blood loss**

Black tarry stool ko melena kehte hain. Ye digested blood ka sign ho sakta hai. Chakkar aur fast pulse blood loss ke clues hain, isliye BP aur circulation jaldi assess karo.

**Baaki options kyun galat hain?**

- **B.** Fiber intake acute bleeding ki priority nahi hai.
- **C.** Vision test blood loss assess nahi karta.
- **D.** Dental examination is emergency ko delay nahi karna chahiye.

</details>

## Bowel obstruction

### Q2. A patient has abdominal distension, repeated vomiting and inability to pass stool or flatus. Which action is appropriate while urgent evaluation is arranged?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Topic:** Bowel obstruction

- **A.** Administer a large oral meal
- **B.** Give a stimulant laxative without review
- **C.** Encourage unrestricted oral fluids despite vomiting
- **D.** Keep the patient NPO and monitor fluid status according to orders

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: D. Keep the patient NPO and monitor fluid status according to orders**

Bowel obstruction mein intestine ke andar food aur fluid aage nahi ja paate. Vomiting se paani aur salts kam ho sakte hain. Nurse patient ko NPO rakhti hai aur prescribed fluids aur decompression plan follow karti hai.

**Baaki options kyun galat hain?**

- **A.** Bada meal obstruction aur vomiting badha sakta hai.
- **B.** Laxative suspected obstruction mein unsafe ho sakta hai.
- **C.** Unrestricted oral fluids aspiration aur vomiting ka risk badha sakte hain.

</details>

## Ostomy

### Q3. A newly formed stoma becomes dark purple and cold. What should the nurse do?

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Topic:** Ostomy

- **A.** Apply a tight dressing around the stoma
- **B.** Wait for the next outpatient appointment
- **C.** Report possible impaired stoma perfusion urgently
- **D.** Reassure that a cold dark stoma is always normal

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: C. Report possible impaired stoma perfusion urgently**

Stoma intestine ka hissa hai jo skin par laya gaya hai. Healthy stoma mein blood supply hoti hai. Dark aur cold stoma ko blood kam milne ka doubt hai, isliye jaldi report karo.

**Baaki options kyun galat hain?**

- **A.** Tight dressing blood flow aur kam kar sakti hai.
- **B.** Delay tissue damage badha sakta hai.
- **D.** Dark cold appearance normal healing assume nahi karna chahiye.

</details>

## Enteral feeding

### Q4. A patient receiving gastric tube feeding starts coughing and develops new respiratory distress. What is the priority response?

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Topic:** Enteral feeding

- **A.** Document the event only after feeding ends
- **B.** Stop the feeding, assess airway and breathing, and escalate promptly
- **C.** Increase the feeding rate
- **D.** Lay the patient flat and continue the feed

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: B. Stop the feeding, assess airway and breathing, and escalate promptly**

Feed lungs mein ja sakta hai, jise aspiration kehte hain. Jab cough aur breathing problem aaye, toh feed rok kar saans assess karni chahiye.

**Baaki options kyun galat hain?**

- **A.** Documentation baad mein hoti hai, safety pehle hai.
- **C.** Rate badhane se aspiration badh sakta hai.
- **D.** Flat position risk badha sakti hai.

</details>
