### Q1. An adult with endoscopically confirmed duodenal ulcer denies NSAID exposure. Which treatable cause should be investigated?

**Difficulty:** 🟡 Medium · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD · **Exam focus:** Original NORCET/AIIMS/PGIMER-style practice

- **A.** Helicobacter pylori infection
- **B.** Giardia infection
- **C.** Lactase deficiency
- **D.** Hepatitis A infection

<details><summary>✅ Correct answer and detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: A. Helicobacter pylori infection**

**A kyun sahi hai:** H. pylori gastric mucosa ki protection disturb kar sakta hai aur ulcer ka major treatable cause hai. Testing se eradication therapy ka decision guide hota hai.

**B kyun galat hai:** Giardia small bowel mein malabsorption aur diarrhea kar sakta hai, lekin typical peptic ulcer ka major cause nahi hai.

**C kyun galat hai:** Lactase deficiency lactose intolerance aur bloating se related hai, mucosal ulceration se nahi.

**D kyun galat hai:** Hepatitis A liver infection hai, duodenal ulcer ka usual cause nahi hai.

**Exam clue:** Ulcer ke do major causes H. pylori aur NSAIDs hain.

### Q2. A patient takes ibuprofen daily and develops a gastric ulcer. Which mechanism best explains the drug-related mucosal injury?

**Difficulty:** 🔴 Difficult · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD · **Exam focus:** Original NORCET/AIIMS/PGIMER-style practice

- **B.** Excess pancreatic bicarbonate release
- **C.** Reduced protective prostaglandin production
- **D.** Increased intestinal lactase activity

<details><summary>✅ Correct answer and detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: C. Reduced protective prostaglandin production**

**A kyun galat hai:** Intrinsic factor vitamin B12 absorption se related hai. Iski increased secretion NSAID injury ka mechanism nahi hai.

**D kyun galat hai:** Lactase lactose digest karta hai, gastric mucosal protection ka regulator nahi hai.

**Exam clue:** NSAID injury mein mucosal defense loss samjho.

**B kyun galat hai:** Respiratory acidosis carbon dioxide retention se related hai, isolated gastric acid loss ka primary effect nahi.

**C kyun galat hai:** Ye bicarbonate loss mein common pattern ho sakta hai, gastric HCl loss mein expected opposite pattern hai.

**D kyun galat hai:** Hyperventilation respiratory alkalosis de sakti hai, lekin question gastric hydrogen loss ka mechanism poochta hai.

**Exam clue:** Vomiting mein acid loss, diarrhea mein bicarbonate loss yaad rakho.
