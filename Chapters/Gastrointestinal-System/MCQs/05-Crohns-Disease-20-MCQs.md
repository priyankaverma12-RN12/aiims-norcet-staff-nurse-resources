### Q1. A Crohn disease specimen shows inflammation extending through the bowel wall. Which complication is most directly explained by this depth of injury?

**Difficulty:** 🔴 Difficult · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD · **Exam focus:** Original NORCET/AIIMS/PGIMER-style practice

- **A.** Fistula formation
- **B.** Rectal tenesmus without deep injury
- **C.** Isolated superficial gastric erythema
- **D.** Uncomplicated functional diarrhea

<details><summary>✅ Correct answer and detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: A. Fistula formation**

**A kyun sahi hai:** Transmural inflammation deeper tissue planes tak extend kar sakti hai aur abnormal connections create kar sakti hai. Superficial mucosal injury alone is mechanism ko adequately explain nahi karti.

**B kyun galat hai:** Tenesmus distal irritation se occur kar sakta hai, full-thickness inflammation require nahi karta.

**C kyun galat hai:** Gastric erythema deep intestinal tissue tract formation ka direct result nahi hai.

**D kyun galat hai:** Functional diarrhea tissue-destructive inflammation ka defining outcome nahi hai.

**Exam clue:** Transmural injury penetrating complications ka basis hai.
