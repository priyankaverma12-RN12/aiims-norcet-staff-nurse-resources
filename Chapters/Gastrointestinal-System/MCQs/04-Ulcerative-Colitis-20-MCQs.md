### Q1. Which distribution on initial colonoscopy most strongly supports typical untreated ulcerative colitis?

**Difficulty:** 🟡 Medium · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD · **Exam focus:** Original NORCET/AIIMS/PGIMER-style practice

- **A.** Continuous inflammation extending proximally from the rectum
- **B.** Separated jejunal ulcers with normal intervening bowel
- **C.** Isolated gastric erosions
- **D.** Isolated appendiceal inflammation

<details><summary>✅ Correct answer and detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: A. Continuous inflammation extending proximally from the rectum**

**A kyun sahi hai:** Rectum se contiguous colonic involvement typical UC pattern hai, although treatment ke baad atypical distribution possible hai.

**B kyun galat hai:** Skip-pattern small bowel disease UC ka classic distribution nahi hai.

**C kyun galat hai:** Gastric lesions alone colonic UC pattern establish nahi karte.

**D kyun galat hai:** Appendicitis localized process hai, typical diffuse mucosal colitis nahi.

**Exam clue:** Distribution ko treatment history ke saath interpret karo.
