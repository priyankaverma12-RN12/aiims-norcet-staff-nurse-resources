### Q1. A patient describes sour liquid rising into the mouth after bending forward without retching. Which symptom is being described?

**Difficulty:** 🟡 Medium · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD · **Exam focus:** Original NORCET/AIIMS/PGIMER-style practice

- **A.** Regurgitation
- **B.** Hematemesis
- **C.** Odynophagia
- **D.** Early satiety

<details><summary>✅ Correct answer and detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: A. Regurgitation**

**A kyun sahi hai:** Gastric content bina forceful vomiting ke mouth tak wapas aa raha hai. Isi symptom ko regurgitation kehte hain.

**B kyun galat hai:** Hematemesis mein patient blood vomit karta hai. Khatta fluid apne aap mein bleeding ka evidence nahi hai.

**C kyun galat hai:** Odynophagia mein nigalte waqt dard hota hai. Is patient ka symptom swallowing pain nahi, gastric content ka wapas aana hai.

**D kyun galat hai:** Early satiety mein thoda khana khaane par pet bhara feel hota hai. Is symptom se mouth mein sour liquid aane ka mechanism explain nahi hota.

**Exam clue:** Regurgitation aur forceful vomiting ko alag samjho.

### Q2. An adult with occasional reflux asks why repeated troublesome reflux warrants follow-up. Which explanation is most accurate?

**Difficulty:** 🟡 Medium · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD · **Exam focus:** Original NORCET/AIIMS/PGIMER-style practice

- **A.** Every brief reflux event establishes erosive disease
- **B.** Reflux primarily damages the large-intestinal mucosa
- **C.** Repeated reflux may injure the esophageal lining
- **D.** Absence of vomiting excludes reflux-related injury

<details><summary>✅ Correct answer and detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: C. Repeated reflux may injure the esophageal lining**

**A kyun galat hai:** Occasional reflux aur erosive esophagitis ek hi diagnosis nahi hain. Tissue injury ko symptoms aur clinically indicated investigations se assess karte hain.

**B kyun galat hai:** Reflux mein content stomach se esophagus ki taraf aata hai. Colon ki lining is condition ka primary target nahi hai.

**C kyun sahi hai:** Jab gastric content baar-baar esophagus ki lining ke contact mein aata hai, toh irritation aur injury ho sakti hai. Troublesome recurrent symptoms ko assess karna isliye important hai.

**D kyun galat hai:** Gastric content reflux hone ke liye forceful vomiting zaroori nahi hai. Kuch patients mein typical symptoms bhi kam prominent hote hain.

**Exam clue:** Occasional reflux ko automatically chronic disease mat label karo.

<details><summary>✅ Correct answer and detailed explanation</summary>

**D kyun sahi hai:** Is symptom combination mein cardiac ischemia ka concern hai. Vital signs, ECG aur urgent escalation local protocol ke mutabik karna chahiye, kyunki reflux history cardiac emergency ko exclude nahi karti.

### Q18. The clinician recommends fundoplication for objectively established reflux disease. Which explanation describes its intended mechanism?
