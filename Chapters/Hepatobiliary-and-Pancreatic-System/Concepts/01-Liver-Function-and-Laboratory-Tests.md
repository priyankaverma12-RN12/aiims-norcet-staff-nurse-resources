# Liver function and laboratory tests

## Liver ka kaam samjho

Liver ko body ki processing factory samjho. Intestine se absorbed nutrients portal vein ke through liver tak aate hain. Liver glucose ko store aur zaroorat par release karta hai. Ye albumin aur kai clotting factors bhi banata hai. Albumin blood vessels ke andar paani retain karne mein help karta hai.

### Injury aur function alag hain

ALT aur AST cell injury ke markers hain. AST muscles aur RBCs mein bhi hota hai, isliye muscle injury ya hemolyzed sample mein AST badh sakta hai. ALP aur GGT bile flow se related patterns ko samajhne mein help karte hain. Albumin aur PT/INR synthesis assess karne mein useful hain, lekin nutrition, inflammation, anticoagulants aur vitamin K jaise factors ko bhi consider karna hai.

### Bilirubin

RBC breakdown se bilirubin banta hai. Unconjugated bilirubin albumin ke saath liver tak pahunchta hai. Liver ise water soluble conjugated form mein process karta hai. Agar conjugated bilirubin blood mein badhe toh urine mein aa sakta hai. Hemolysis mein unconjugated bilirubin badhne par urine bilirubin negative reh sakta hai.

### Exam calculation

R ratio = (ALT / ALT ULN) / (ALP / ALP ULN). Dono enzyme values ko unki apni upper reference limit se divide karo. R ratio injury pattern ko classify karne mein help karta hai, disease ka final diagnosis nahi karta.

### Nursing priorities

Unexpected abnormal result ko sample quality aur patient history ke saath interpret karo. Acute liver injury mein mental status, glucose aur coagulation changes important hain. Naya confusion, hypotension ya rising INR ho toh promptly assess aur escalate karo.
