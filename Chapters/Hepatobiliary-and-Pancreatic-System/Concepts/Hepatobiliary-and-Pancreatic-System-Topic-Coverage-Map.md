- pancreatitis and pancreatic surgery

## Initial study explanations

### Cirrhosis

Liver waste substances ko process karta hai. Jab liver ka kaam bahut kam ho jaata hai, toh brain affect ho sakta hai. Confusion aur flapping tremor hepatic encephalopathy ke clues hain.

### Portal hypertension

Portal pressure badhne se food pipe ki veins badi ho sakti hain. Agar ye veins phat jaayein toh severe bleeding ho sakti hai. Nurse saans aur circulation pehle assess karti hai.

### Pancreatitis

Pancreas mein swelling hone par upper abdomen ka severe pain back tak ja sakta hai. Lipase pancreas ka enzyme hai jo pancreatitis mein badh sakta hai. Clinical assessment aur tests se diagnosis confirm hota hai.

### Biliary obstruction

Bile intestine mein jaakar stool ko color dene mein help karti hai. Bile duct block ho toh stool pale ho sakta hai. Conjugated bilirubin urine mein jaakar urine dark kar sakta hai.
