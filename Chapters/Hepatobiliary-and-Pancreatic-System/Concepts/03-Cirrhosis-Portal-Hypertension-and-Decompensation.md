## Cirrhosis aur portal hypertension

Cirrhosis mein long-term injury ke baad liver mein scar tissue banta hai. Normal architecture distort hone se portal blood ko liver ke through flow karne mein resistance badh sakta hai. Portal hypertension ke saath spleen enlarge ho sakta hai, platelets kam ho sakte hain aur varices develop ho sakti hain.

### Ascites

Ascites abdomen mein fluid jama hone ko kehte hain. Cirrhosis mein circulation ke changes ki wajah se kidneys sodium aur water retain kar sakti hain. Roz comparable conditions mein weight aur prescribed abdominal girth measure karo. New ascites ya hospitalized decompensation mein diagnostic paracentesis ki need assess hoti hai. Ascitic PMN 250 cells/mm³ ya usse zyada SBP support karta hai. Serum albumin minus ascitic albumin se SAAG milta hai. High gradient portal hypertension related ascites ko support karta hai.

### Encephalopathy

Hepatic encephalopathy mein liver dysfunction aur shunting ki wajah se brain function affect ho sakta hai. Constipation, infection, bleeding, sedatives aur kidney dysfunction jaise triggers assess karo. Altered consciousness mein airway aur glucose jaise urgent competing causes bhi assess karne hain. Lactulose prescribed target ke according diya jaata hai. Excess diarrhea dehydration aur electrolyte disturbance kar sakti hai. Rifaximin selected patients mein recurrence reduce karne ke liye add ho sakti hai.

### Nursing priorities

Vitals, intake-output, renal function, electrolytes, mental status aur fall risk monitor karo. Diuretic aur beta-blocker administration se pehle prescribed hold parameters check karo. Severe abdominal pain, fever, bleeding ya sudden confusion ko promptly escalate karo. Adequate nutrition aur individualized sodium advice important hain. Eligible cirrhosis patients ko HCC surveillance ki zaroorat ho sakti hai.
