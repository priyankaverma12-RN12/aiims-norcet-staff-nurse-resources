# Gallstones, cholecystitis and biliary nursing

## Bile aur gallbladder

Liver bile banata hai aur gallbladder ise store karta hai. Meal ke baad bile intestine mein pahunchkar fat digestion aur absorption mein help karti hai. Stone cystic duct mein temporarily atke toh biliary colic ho sakta hai. Persistent inflammation aur fever cholecystitis ka concern badhate hain.

### Biliary infection

Common bile duct mein stone ko choledocholithiasis kehte hain. Obstructed bile duct mein infection ho toh cholangitis develop ho sakti hai. Fever, jaundice aur right upper abdominal pain important clues hain. Hypotension aur confusion severe infection ke red flags hain. Sepsis care aur urgent drainage assessment ki zaroorat ho sakti hai.

### Imaging aur procedures

Ultrasound suspected gallstones mein common first test hai. MRCP ducts ko noninvasively visualize karti hai. ERCP endoscopic procedure hai jo selected obstruction mein drainage ya stone removal allow kar sakta hai. Sedation, fasting, allergies, consent aur relevant medicines ki review local protocol ke according karo. Procedure ke baad severe pain ya vomiting ko promptly report karo.

### Surgery ke baad

Cholecystectomy gallbladder remove karti hai, liver bile banana continue karta hai. Early recovery mein smaller meals aur tolerance ke according gradual dietary adjustment helpful ho sakta hai. New fever, jaundice, severe pain, hypotension ya unexpected bilious drainage ko normal recovery assume mat karo. Drain ko prescribed orders ke bina clamp ya remove nahi karna chahiye.
