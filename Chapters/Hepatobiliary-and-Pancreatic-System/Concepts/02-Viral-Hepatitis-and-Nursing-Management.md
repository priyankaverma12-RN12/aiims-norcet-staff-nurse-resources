## Hepatitis ka meaning

Hepatitis ka matlab liver mein inflammation hai. Viral hepatitis ke types alag routes se spread karte hain. A aur E aksar contaminated water ya food ke through spread karte hain. B aur C blood exposure se spread kar sakte hain. HBV sexual aur perinatal transmission se bhi spread kar sakta hai. HDV ko HBV surface antigen ki zaroorat hoti hai.

### Hepatitis B test markers

HBsAg current infection ka clue hai. Anti-HBs immunity se associated hai. Total anti-HBc natural virus exposure suggest karta hai, vaccine-only immunity mein ye expected nahi hota. IgM anti-HBc recent acute infection mein useful hai. HBsAg six months ya zyada persist kare toh chronic infection consider hota hai. Patient ka complete clinical context aur confirmatory assessment important hai.

### Hepatitis C

Reactive HCV antibody past ya current exposure batati hai. RNA test current infection detect karta hai. Virus clear hone ke baad bhi antibody positive reh sakti hai aur reinfection possible hai.

### Nursing care

Standard precautions follow karo. Blood exposure report karke source status aur documented HBV vaccine response ke according occupational health guidance lo. Needle injury chhoti ho tab bhi report karna important hai. Patient ko nonstigmatizing counseling do. HAV/HEV ke relevant settings mein safe water, sanitation aur handwashing samjhao. Pregnancy mein suspected HEV severe ho sakta hai, isliye prompt assessment zaroori hai. Acute hepatitis mein worsening consciousness ya bleeding ko urgent red flag samjho.
