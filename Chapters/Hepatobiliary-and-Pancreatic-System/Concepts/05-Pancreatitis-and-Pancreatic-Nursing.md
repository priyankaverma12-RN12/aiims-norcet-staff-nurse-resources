## Pancreas ka function

Pancreas ki acinar cells digestive enzymes banati hain. Islet cells hormones banati hain, jaise insulin aur glucagon. Pancreatitis mein digestive enzymes ki premature activation aur inflammation pancreatic tissue ko damage kar sakti hai.

### Diagnosis aur severity

Acute pancreatitis diagnosis ke liye usually three mein se two criteria chahiye: typical pain, lipase/amylase at least three times upper limit, ya compatible imaging. Har clear diagnosis mein immediate CT zaroori nahi hota. Persistent organ failure severe disease define karne mein important hai.

### Acute nursing care

Patient ka BP, pulse, oxygen saturation, urine output, pain aur lab trends assess karo. Prescribed fluids ke response ko repeatedly check karo, kyunki inadequate perfusion aur fluid overload dono possible hain. Mild improving disease mein early oral feeding as tolerated supported hai. Oral intake possible nahi ho aur gut usable ho toh enteral route prefer kiya ja sakta hai. Sterile necrosis mein routine prophylactic antibiotics recommended nahi hote. Clinical infection aur associated cholangitis ka management separate hai.

### Chronic pancreatitis

Long-term pancreatic damage se digestive enzyme secretion kam ho sakti hai. Is wajah se greasy stools, weight loss aur malnutrition develop ho sakte hain. Pancreatic enzyme replacement ko prescribed timing ke according meals aur snacks ke saath lena useful hai. Endocrine tissue damage glucose regulation affect kar sakta hai. Alcohol avoidance, smoking cessation aur follow-up counseling support karo.

### Surgical monitoring

Postoperative drain output, abdominal pain, temperature aur hemodynamics mein sudden changes ko promptly assess aur report karo. Drain ko independently manipulate mat karo.
