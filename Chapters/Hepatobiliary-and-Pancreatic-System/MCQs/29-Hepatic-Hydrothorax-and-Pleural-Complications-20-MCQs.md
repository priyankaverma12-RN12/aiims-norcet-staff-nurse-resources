### Q1. Which anatomical compartment contains the excess fluid in hepatic hydrothorax?

- **A.** Pericardial sac
- **B.** Pulmonary alveoli exclusively
- **C.** Pleural space

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: C. Pleural space**

**Option-wise explanation:**
- **A (Galat):** Pericardial effusion heart ke surrounding sac mein hota hai.

**Exam clue:** Pleural effusion aur pulmonary edema mein location alag hai.

### Q2. Which mechanism most commonly accounts for hepatic hydrothorax in cirrhosis?

**Difficulty:** 🟡 Medium · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD · Original practice

- **A.** Movement of ascitic fluid through small diaphragmatic defects
- **B.** Primary destruction of alveolar surfactant
- **C.** Obstruction of the main pulmonary artery by a thrombus
- **D.** Leakage of pancreatic juice directly into the pericardium

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: A. Movement of ascitic fluid through small diaphragmatic defects**

**Option-wise explanation:**
- **A (Sahi):** Abdomen se thorax ki taraf pressure gradient fluid ko diaphragm ke defects se pleural space mein kheench sakta hai.
- **B (Galat):** Surfactant loss alveolar collapse contribute kar sakta hai, lekin hepatic fluid transfer ka main mechanism nahi hai.
- **C (Galat):** Pulmonary embolism separate vascular disease hai jo kabhi effusion cause kar sakti hai.
- **D (Galat):** Pancreatic leak alag pathology hai, hepatic hydrothorax ka typical mechanism nahi.

**Exam clue:** Diaphragmatic transfer pleural fluid ka source samjhata hai.
