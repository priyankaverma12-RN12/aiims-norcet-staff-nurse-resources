### Q1. Which organ stores and concentrates bile between meals?

**Difficulty:** Easy | **Priority:** High | **Topic:** Gallstones, Cholecystitis and Biliary Nursing | **Evidence:** Original practice

- **A.** Gallbladder
- **B.** Pancreas
- **C.** Spleen
- **D.** Duodenum

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: A. Gallbladder**

- **A.** Gallbladder liver ki banayi hui bile ko store aur concentrate karta hai.
- **B.** Pancreas digestive enzymes aur hormones banata hai, bile store nahi karta.
- **C.** Spleen blood filtration aur immune function se juda hai, bile reservoir nahi hai.
- **D.** Duodenum mein bile enter karti hai, lekin storage gallbladder mein hota hai.

**Exam clue:** Liver makes bile, gallbladder stores it.

</details>

### Q2. A patient reports episodic right upper quadrant pain after meals, which settles within two hours without fever. Which condition is most consistent?

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Priority:** High | **Topic:** Gallstones, Cholecystitis and Biliary Nursing | **Evidence:** Original practice

- **A.** Persistent acute cholangitis with sepsis
- **B.** Uncomplicated biliary colic
- **C.** Perforated gallbladder with generalized peritonitis
- **D.** Established severe acute pancreatitis

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: B. Uncomplicated biliary colic**

- **A.** Cholangitis mein infection ke signs, fever ya jaundice jaise findings ho sakte hain. Ye short afebrile episode less typical hai.
- **B.** Temporary cystic duct obstruction se episodic biliary pain aa sakta hai aur obstruction hatne par settle ho sakta hai.
- **C.** Perforation mein persistent severe pain aur peritoneal signs expected ho sakte hain, brief resolved episode nahi.
- **D.** Severe pancreatitis usually sustained pain aur systemic illness ke saath hota hai, is pattern se confirm nahi hota.

**Exam clue:** Transient obstruction produces biliary colic.

</details>

### Q3. Which finding in a patient with known gallstones makes acute cholecystitis more likely than uncomplicated biliary colic?

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Priority:** High | **Topic:** Gallstones, Cholecystitis and Biliary Nursing | **Evidence:** Original practice

- **A.** Brief discomfort resolving fully without fever
- **B.** Isolated chronic knee pain
- **C.** Persistent right upper quadrant tenderness with fever
- **D.** Normal abdominal examination between short episodes

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: C. Persistent right upper quadrant tenderness with fever**

- **A.** Brief self-resolving pain without fever uncomplicated biliary colic se zyada fit hota hai.
- **B.** Knee pain gallbladder inflammation ka characteristic sign nahi hai.
- **C.** Persistent inflammation se localized tenderness aur fever aa sakte hain. Ye acute cholecystitis ka concern badhate hain.
- **D.** Episodes ke beech normal exam uncomplicated colic mein mil sakta hai, cholecystitis ka strong clue nahi.

**Exam clue:** Persistent symptoms and inflammation distinguish cholecystitis.

</details>

### Q4. A patient stops inspiration because palpation of the right subcostal area causes pain. Which clinical sign is described?

- **B.** Kernig sign
- **C.** Tinel sign
- **D.** Murphy sign

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: D. Murphy sign**

- **A.** Rovsing sign appendiceal irritation assess karne se related hai.
- **B.** Kernig sign meningeal irritation ke examination se juda hai.

**Exam clue:** Murphy sign is associated with gallbladder inflammation.

</details>

### Q5. Which imaging test is generally preferred first for suspected gallbladder stones?

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Priority:** High | **Topic:** Gallstones, Cholecystitis and Biliary Nursing | **Evidence:** Original practice

- **A.** Right upper quadrant ultrasound
- **B.** Routine abdominal plain radiograph alone
- **C.** MRI brain
- **D.** Barium swallow

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: A. Right upper quadrant ultrasound**

- **A.** Ultrasound gallstones, gallbladder wall aur surrounding findings assess karne mein useful first test hai.
- **B.** Bahut se gallstones plain radiograph par visible nahi hote. X-ray alone adequate first evaluation nahi hai.
- **C.** Brain MRI gallbladder anatomy nahi dikhata.
- **D.** Barium swallow upper gastrointestinal lumen assess karta hai, gallbladder stones nahi.

**Exam clue:** Ultrasonography is a first-line test.

</details>

### Q6. An adult with suspected acute cholecystitis has an equivocal ultrasound. Which functional imaging investigation may be requested to assess cystic duct patency?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Priority:** High | **Topic:** Gallstones, Cholecystitis and Biliary Nursing | **Evidence:** Original practice

- **A.** Electrocardiography
- **B.** Hepatobiliary iminodiacetic acid scan
- **C.** Spirometry
- **D.** DEXA scan

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: B. Hepatobiliary iminodiacetic acid scan**

- **A.** ECG heart electrical activity assess karta hai, cystic duct patency nahi.
- **B.** HIDA scan tracer ke bile pathway mein movement aur gallbladder filling assess karta hai. Equivocal cases mein useful ho sakta hai.
- **C.** Spirometry lung airflow measure karti hai, bile flow nahi.
- **D.** DEXA bone density measure karta hai, gallbladder function nahi.

**Exam clue:** HIDA assesses hepatobiliary tracer transit.

</details>

- **B.** Chronic appendicitis
- **C.** Choledocholithiasis
- **D.** Splenic infarction

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: C. Choledocholithiasis**

- **A.** Gallbladder-confined cholelithiasis common bile duct stone se anatomical location mein alag hai.

- **C.** Choledocholithiasis common bile duct mein stone hone ko kehte hain.
- **D.** Splenic infarction spleen blood supply loss se hota hai, biliary obstruction nahi.

**Exam clue:** Choledocho refers to the common bile duct.

</details>

- **C.** Flexible sigmoidoscopy
- **D.** Diagnostic bronchoscopy

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: A. Endoscopic retrograde cholangiopancreatography**

- **A.** ERCP se selected patients mein obstructed bile duct ko drain kar sakte hain aur stone removal ya stent possible hai.

### Q10. Which nursing action is important before a planned sedated ERCP?

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Priority:** High | **Topic:** Gallstones, Cholecystitis and Biliary Nursing | **Evidence:** Original practice

- **A.** Give an unrestricted meal immediately before sedation
- **B.** Verify fasting instructions, consent status, allergies and relevant medication review
- **C.** Omit anticoagulant review because bleeding cannot occur
- **D.** Cancel all monitoring because ERCP is noninvasive

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: B. Verify fasting instructions, consent status, allergies and relevant medication review**

- **A.** Unrestricted meal sedation ke time aspiration risk badha sakta hai. Prescribed fasting follow karna hai.
- **B.** Sedation aur endoscopic intervention ke liye fasting, consent, allergies aur medicines check karna patient safety support karta hai.
- **C.** Sphincterotomy jaise intervention bleeding risk rakhte hain. Anticoagulant review important hai.
- **D.** ERCP invasive procedure hai. Monitoring aur recovery assessment required hote hain.

**Exam clue:** Prepare for both sedation and endoscopic intervention.

</details>

**Exam clue:** Escalate severe pain after ERCP.

### Q14. A patient asks where bile goes after uncomplicated cholecystectomy. What is the best explanation?

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Priority:** High | **Topic:** Gallstones, Cholecystitis and Biliary Nursing | **Evidence:** Original practice

- **A.** Bile production permanently stops
- **B.** Bile continues to flow from the liver through ducts into the intestine
- **C.** The pancreas becomes the main organ producing bile
- **D.** The spleen takes over bile storage and concentration

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: B. Bile continues to flow from the liver through ducts into the intestine**

- **A.** Gallbladder remove hone se liver ki bile production automatically stop nahi hoti.
- **B.** Liver bile banana continue karta hai. Gallbladder absent hone par bile ducts se intestine mein jaati hai.
- **C.** Pancreas enzymes banata hai, bile synthesis liver ka function hai.
- **D.** Spleen gallbladder ka bile reservoir function take over nahi karta.

**Exam clue:** Gallbladder removal does not remove the liver.

</details>

- **C.** Transient shoulder-tip discomfort
- **D.** Unexplained shock with a falling hemoglobin

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: C. Transient shoulder-tip discomfort**

- **A.** Jaundice aur fever biliary complication suggest karte hain, routine gas pain nahi.
- **B.** Rigidity peritoneal irritation ka red flag hai aur urgent assessment chahiye.
- **C.** Residual gas diaphragm irritate kar sakti hai aur referred shoulder pain de sakti hai. Stable patient mein ye temporary ho sakta hai.

### Q20. Which complication can result when a gallstone obstructs the pancreatic drainage pathway near the ampulla?

**Difficulty:** Easy | **Priority:** High | **Topic:** Gallstones, Cholecystitis and Biliary Nursing | **Evidence:** Original practice

- **A.** Primary diabetic ketoacidosis in every case
- **B.** Isolated otitis externa
- **C.** Pulmonary fibrosis from bile storage
- **D.** Acute biliary pancreatitis

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: D. Acute biliary pancreatitis**

- **A.** DKA insulin deficiency se hota hai. Har biliary stone DKA cause nahi karta.
- **B.** Otitis externa ear canal infection hai, pancreatic duct obstruction se nahi.
- **C.** Pulmonary fibrosis lung scarring hai, biliary drainage ka direct typical result nahi.
- **D.** Stone ampulla ke paas pancreatic drainage disturb karke inflammation trigger kar sakta hai.

**Exam clue:** Biliary and pancreatic ducts meet near the duodenum.

</details>
