### Q1. Which route is recommended for indomethacin prophylaxis against post-ERCP pancreatitis in suitable adults?

**Difficulty:** Easy · **Priority:** HIGH YIELD · Original practice

- **A.** Intradermal
- **B.** Rectal
- **C.** Intrathecal
- **D.** Inhaled

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: B. Rectal**

**Option-wise explanation:**
- **A (Galat):** Intradermal injection recommended prophylactic route nahi hai.
- **B (Sahi):** Rectal NSAID peri-procedure use se post-ERCP pancreatitis ka risk reduce ho sakta hai. Contraindications aur prescription verify karte hain.
- **C (Galat):** Intrathecal administration inappropriate aur dangerous hai.
- **D (Galat):** Inhaled indomethacin is indication ka recommended route nahi hai.

**Exam clue:** Rectal NSAID is a preventive option.

</details>
