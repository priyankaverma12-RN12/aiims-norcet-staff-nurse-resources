### Q1. Which term describes acquisition of HDV by a person already chronically infected with HBV?

**Difficulty:** Easy · **Priority:** HIGH YIELD · **Exam focus:** NORCET / HPRCA HP Staff Nurse / PGIMER · Original practice

- **A.** Simultaneous coinfection
- **B.** Superinfection
- **C.** Reinfection after HCV cure
- **D.** Vaccine breakthrough by definition

<details><summary>✅ Correct answer and detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: B. Superinfection**

**A. Kyun galat hai:** Coinfection mein HBV aur HDV saath acquire hote hain.

**B. Kyun sahi hai:** Pehle chronic HBV ho aur baad mein HDV acquire ho, isko superinfection kehte hain.

**C. Kyun galat hai:** HCV unrelated virus hai.

**D. Kyun galat hai:** HBV infection already present hone se vaccine breakthrough automatically prove nahi hota.

**Exam clue:** Timing distinguishes superinfection from coinfection.

</details>

### Q2. Compared with simultaneous acute HBV-HDV infection, what is a major concern with HDV superinfection of chronic HBV?

**Difficulty:** Medium · **Priority:** HIGH YIELD · **Exam focus:** NORCET / HPRCA HP Staff Nurse / PGIMER · Original practice

- **A.** Guaranteed spontaneous resolution
- **B.** No possibility of hepatic fibrosis
- **C.** Permanent suppression of every hepatitis virus
- **D.** Greater likelihood of persistent HDV infection and progressive liver disease

<details><summary>✅ Correct answer and detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: D. Greater likelihood of persistent HDV infection and progressive liver disease**

**A. Kyun galat hai:** Superinfection often chronic ho sakti hai.

**B. Kyun galat hai:** Fibrosis progression major concern hai.

**C. Kyun galat hai:** Ek virus acquire karna doosre viruses ko permanently eradicate nahi karta.

**D. Kyun sahi hai:** Chronic HBV already HBsAg supply karta hai, isliye HDV persistence aur severe progression ka risk zyada hai.

**Exam clue:** Superinfection has higher chronicity risk.

</details>
