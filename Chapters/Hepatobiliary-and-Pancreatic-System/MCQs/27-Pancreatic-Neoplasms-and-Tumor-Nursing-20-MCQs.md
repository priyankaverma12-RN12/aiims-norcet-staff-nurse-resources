### Q1. Which cellular origin characterizes the most common pancreatic malignancy?

**Difficulty:** 🟢 Easy · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD · Original practice

- **A.** Endocrine beta cells
- **B.** Endocrine alpha cells
- **C.** Exocrine ductal epithelium
- **D.** Pancreatic stromal fibroblasts

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: C. Exocrine ductal epithelium**

**Option-wise explanation:**
- **A (Galat):** Beta-cell tumor insulinoma ho sakta hai. Yeh most common pancreatic malignancy nahi hai.
- **B (Galat):** Alpha-cell tumor glucagonoma ho sakta hai. Yeh comparatively rare neuroendocrine tumor hai.
- **C (Sahi):** Pancreatic ductal adenocarcinoma exocrine ducts ki lining cells se related hai aur pancreatic cancers ka most common type hai.

**Exam clue:** Ductal adenocarcinoma ko hormone-secreting NET se distinguish karo.
