### Q1. Which alcohol-associated liver change describes the early accumulation of fat within hepatocytes?

**Difficulty:** Easy · **Priority:** High · **Exam focus:** NORCET / HPRCA HP Staff Nurse / PGIMER · Original practice

- **A.** Alcohol-associated hepatic encephalopathy
- **B.** Alcohol-associated portal vein thrombosis
- **C.** Alcohol-associated biliary stricture
- **D.** Alcohol-associated hepatic steatosis

<details><summary>✅ Correct answer and detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: D. Alcohol-associated hepatic steatosis**

**A. Kyun galat hai:** Hepatic encephalopathy brain function disturbance hai. Hepatocyte fat accumulation ka term nahi.

**B. Kyun galat hai:** Portal vein thrombosis blood clot hai. Fatty change se distinct hai.

**C. Kyun galat hai:** Biliary stricture duct narrowing hai. Liver cell fat deposition nahi.

**D. Kyun sahi hai:** Alcohol exposure liver lipid metabolism disturb karke hepatocytes mein fat accumulate kar sakta hai. Early steatosis abstinence ke saath improve ho sakti hai.

**Exam clue:** Alcohol-related fatty change is steatosis.

</details>

### Q2. Which organ is the principal site of ethanol metabolism after absorption?

**Difficulty:** Easy · **Priority:** High · **Exam focus:** NORCET / HPRCA HP Staff Nurse / PGIMER · Original practice

- **A.** Liver
- **B.** Spleen
- **C.** Gallbladder
- **D.** Urinary bladder

<details><summary>✅ Correct answer and detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: A. Liver**

**A. Kyun sahi hai:** Most ethanol liver mein enzymes ke through metabolize hota hai. Isi exposure aur metabolites se liver injury develop ho sakti hai.

**B. Kyun galat hai:** Spleen blood cells aur immune function se related hai. Main ethanol metabolism site nahi.

**C. Kyun galat hai:** Gallbladder bile store karti hai. Ethanol ka principal metabolism liver mein hota hai.

**D. Kyun galat hai:** Urinary bladder urine store karta hai. Ethanol breakdown ka main organ nahi.

**Exam clue:** Most ethanol metabolism occurs in the liver.

</details>

### Q3. Which toxic intermediate is formed when alcohol dehydrogenase oxidizes ethanol?

**Difficulty:** Easy · **Priority:** High · **Exam focus:** NORCET / HPRCA HP Staff Nurse / PGIMER · Original practice

- **A.** Acetoacetate
- **B.** Creatinine
- **C.** Acetaldehyde
- **D.** Conjugated bilirubin

<details><summary>✅ Correct answer and detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: C. Acetaldehyde**

**A. Kyun galat hai:** Acetoacetate ketone body hai. Alcohol dehydrogenase reaction ka direct product nahi.

**B. Kyun galat hai:** Creatinine muscle creatine metabolism se related waste product hai. Ethanol oxidation ka direct intermediate nahi.

**C. Kyun sahi hai:** Alcohol dehydrogenase ethanol ko acetaldehyde mein convert karta hai. Acetaldehyde reactive metabolite hai jo tissue injury mein contribute kar sakta hai.

**D. Kyun galat hai:** Conjugated bilirubin heme breakdown product ki liver processing se banta hai. Ethanol ka oxidation product nahi.

**Exam clue:** Ethanol is oxidized to acetaldehyde.

</details>

### Q4. Which term refers to a problematic pattern of alcohol use causing clinically significant impairment or distress?

**Difficulty:** Easy · **Priority:** High · **Exam focus:** NORCET / HPRCA HP Staff Nurse / PGIMER · Original practice

- **A.** Alcohol use disorder
- **B.** Hepatic steatosis alone
- **C.** Alcohol intoxication as an identical diagnosis
- **D.** Alcohol withdrawal as an identical diagnosis

<details><summary>✅ Correct answer and detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: A. Alcohol use disorder**

**A. Kyun sahi hai:** Alcohol use disorder harmful use pattern aur related impairment ko describe karta hai. Diagnosis structured clinical criteria se hota hai.

**B. Kyun galat hai:** Steatosis liver fat accumulation hai. AUD ke behavioral criteria ka substitute nahi.

**C. Kyun galat hai:** Intoxication acute alcohol effects hai. AUD longer-term use pattern ko describe karta hai.

**D. Kyun galat hai:** Withdrawal reduction ya cessation ke baad symptoms describe karta hai. AUD se related ho sakta hai par identical diagnosis nahi.

**Exam clue:** Alcohol use disorder is distinct from intoxication or withdrawal.

</details>

### Q5. Which reversible nutritional problem should be assessed when an alcohol-associated liver disease patient reports inadequate food intake?

**Difficulty:** Easy · **Priority:** High · **Exam focus:** NORCET / HPRCA HP Staff Nurse / PGIMER · Original practice

- **A.** Hypervitaminosis as an inevitable finding
- **B.** Undernutrition
- **C.** Obesity in every patient
- **D.** Excess protein intake in every patient

<details><summary>✅ Correct answer and detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: B. Undernutrition**

**A. Kyun galat hai:** Vitamin excess har patient mein expected nahi hai. Deficiencies more relevant ho sakti hain.

**B. Kyun sahi hai:** Poor intake se energy, protein aur micronutrient deficiency develop ho sakti hai. ALD patients mein nutrition assessment important hai.

**C. Kyun galat hai:** Obesity coexist kar sakti hai, par poor intake se undernutrition bhi possible hai.

**D. Kyun galat hai:** Protein intake adequate hai ya nahi actual history se assess karte hain. Har patient mein excessive intake assume nahi karte.

**Exam clue:** Alcohol-associated liver disease may coexist with undernutrition.

</details>

### Q6. Which clinical term describes acute neurological symptoms after a person physiologically dependent on alcohol abruptly reduces or stops drinking?

**Difficulty:** Easy · **Priority:** High · **Exam focus:** NORCET / HPRCA HP Staff Nurse / PGIMER · Original practice

- **A.** Acute alcohol intoxication
- **B.** Alcohol withdrawal syndrome
- **C.** Alcohol-associated hepatic steatosis
- **D.** Alcohol-associated gastritis alone

<details><summary>✅ Correct answer and detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: B. Alcohol withdrawal syndrome**

**A. Kyun galat hai:** Intoxication alcohol ke acute pharmacological effects se hoti hai. Withdrawal reduced exposure ke baad develop hota hai.

**B. Kyun sahi hai:** Dependent person mein alcohol exposure suddenly kam hone par nervous system hyperexcitable ho sakta hai. Tremor aur severe complications possible hain, isliye medical assessment important hai.

**C. Kyun galat hai:** Steatosis hepatic fat deposition hai. Acute neurological withdrawal syndrome nahi.

**D. Kyun galat hai:** Gastritis gastric mucosal inflammation hai. Withdrawal neurological aur autonomic features rakh sakta hai.

**Exam clue:** Withdrawal follows reduction or cessation after physiological dependence.

</details>
