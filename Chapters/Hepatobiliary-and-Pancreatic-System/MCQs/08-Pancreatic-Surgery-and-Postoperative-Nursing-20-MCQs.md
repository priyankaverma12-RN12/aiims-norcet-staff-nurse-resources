### Q1. Which pancreatic region is removed during a standard pancreaticoduodenectomy?

**Difficulty:** Easy · **Priority:** HIGH YIELD · **Exam focus:** NORCET / HPRCA HP Staff Nurse / PGIMER · Original practice

- **A.** Pancreatic tail alone
- **B.** Entire pancreas in every case
- **C.** Pancreatic head
- **D.** Pancreatic islets alone

<details><summary>✅ Correct answer and detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: C. Pancreatic head**

**A. Kyun galat hai:** Tail ki isolated resection distal pancreatectomy mein hoti hai.

**B. Kyun galat hai:** Total pancreatectomy alag operation hai. Standard Whipple mein pancreatic remnant rehta hai.

**C. Kyun sahi hai:** Whipple procedure mein pancreatic head aur duodenum remove hote hain. Baaki resection anatomy aur surgical technique par depend karti hai.

**D. Kyun galat hai:** Islets endocrine cells hain. Whipple sirf islet removal nahi hai.

**Exam clue:** Whipple = pancreatic head plus duodenum.

</details>

### Q2. After a Whipple procedure, a student asks why the surgeon connects the remaining pancreatic duct or pancreatic tissue to the digestive tract. What is the purpose?

**Difficulty:** Medium · **Priority:** HIGH YIELD · **Exam focus:** NORCET / HPRCA HP Staff Nurse / PGIMER · Original practice

- **A.** Allow pancreatic secretions to enter the digestive tract
- **B.** Permanently prevent all pancreatic juice production
- **C.** Redirect pancreatic secretions into the urinary bladder
- **D.** Eliminate the need to reconstruct biliary drainage

<details><summary>✅ Correct answer and detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: A. Allow pancreatic secretions to enter the digestive tract**

**A. Kyun sahi hai:** Pancreatic anastomosis ek surgical connection hai jo remaining gland ki digestive secretions ko gut tak pahunchne deta hai.

**B. Kyun galat hai:** Remaining pancreatic cells enzymes bana sakte hain. Connection unki production ko stop karne ke liye nahi hai.

**C. Kyun galat hai:** Pancreatic juice digestion mein kaam aata hai, urinary bladder mein nahi.

**D. Kyun galat hai:** Bile ka route alag reconstruct karna pad sakta hai. Pancreatic connection uska substitute nahi hai.

**Exam clue:** Pancreatic aur biliary reconstruction ke functions alag hain.

</details>

### Q3. Which operation removes the body and tail of the pancreas while retaining the pancreatic head?

**Difficulty:** Easy · **Priority:** HIGH YIELD · **Exam focus:** NORCET / HPRCA HP Staff Nurse / PGIMER · Original practice

- **A.** Pancreaticoduodenectomy
- **B.** Total pancreatectomy
- **C.** Cholecystectomy
- **D.** Distal pancreatectomy

<details><summary>✅ Correct answer and detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: D. Distal pancreatectomy**

**A. Kyun galat hai:** Whipple mainly head aur duodenum remove karta hai.

**B. Kyun galat hai:** Total pancreatectomy mein entire pancreas remove hota hai.

**C. Kyun galat hai:** Cholecystectomy gallbladder ka operation hai.

**D. Kyun sahi hai:** Distal pancreatectomy mein pancreas ka left-sided body aur tail part remove hota hai. Exact extent lesion aur surgical plan par depend karti hai.

**Exam clue:** Distal pancreas ka matlab body aur tail side.

</details>

### Q4. A patient's drain amylase on postoperative day 3 after pancreatic resection is 420 U/L. The laboratory upper normal serum amylase limit is 100 U/L. Which conclusion is appropriate?

**Difficulty:** Difficult · **Priority:** HIGH YIELD · **Exam focus:** NORCET / HPRCA HP Staff Nurse / PGIMER · Original practice

- **A.** The result excludes pancreatic leakage
- **B.** The biochemical threshold is exceeded, but clinical relevance must also be assessed
- **C.** Grade C pancreatic fistula is proven by amylase alone
- **D.** The result is normal because the patient has undergone surgery

<details><summary>✅ Correct answer and detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: B. The biochemical threshold is exceeded, but clinical relevance must also be assessed**

**A. Kyun galat hai:** Elevated drain amylase pancreatic secretion leak ka clue ho sakta hai, exclusion nahi.

**B. Kyun sahi hai:** 420 divided by 100 equals 4.2. Ye threefold threshold se zyada hai. Clinically relevant postoperative pancreatic fistula classify karne ke liye associated clinical effect bhi assess karte hain.

**C. Kyun galat hai:** Grade C severe clinical consequences se defined hota hai. Amylase value alone grade prove nahi karti.

**D. Kyun galat hai:** Operation hone se har elevated drain amylase automatically normal nahi ho jaata.

**Exam clue:** POD3 onward drain amylase >3 times serum ULN plus clinical impact.

</details>

### Q5. A patient has elevated drain amylase on day 3 following pancreatic surgery but remains well and has no fistula-related change in management. Which ISGPS term is most appropriate?

**Difficulty:** Difficult · **Priority:** HIGH YIELD · **Exam focus:** NORCET / HPRCA HP Staff Nurse / PGIMER · Original practice

- **A.** Grade C postoperative pancreatic fistula
- **B.** Biochemical leak
- **C.** Confirmed septic shock
- **D.** Delayed postpancreatectomy hemorrhage

<details><summary>✅ Correct answer and detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: B. Biochemical leak**

**A. Kyun galat hai:** Grade C mein fistula-related organ failure, reoperation ya death jaise severe consequences hote hain.

**B. Kyun sahi hai:** Biochemical leak mein amylase-rich drainage ho sakti hai, lekin clinically important fistula-related consequence nahi hota. Isko old grade A ki jagah biochemical leak kehte hain.

**C. Kyun galat hai:** Septic shock establish karne ke liye infection aur circulatory dysfunction assess karte hain. Amylase alone enough nahi.

**D. Kyun galat hai:** Hemorrhage blood loss hai. Enzyme-rich drainage alone bleeding prove nahi karti.

**Exam clue:** Biochemical leak clinically relevant fistula nahi hai.

</details>

### Q6. A clinically relevant pancreatic fistula requires image-guided drain placement but has not caused organ failure or required reoperation. Which classification is most consistent?

**Difficulty:** Difficult · **Priority:** HIGH YIELD · **Exam focus:** NORCET / HPRCA HP Staff Nurse / PGIMER · Original practice

- **A.** Grade B postoperative pancreatic fistula
- **B.** Grade C postoperative pancreatic fistula by definition
- **C.** Biochemical leak without clinical consequence
- **D.** Normal pancreatic healing without complication

<details><summary>✅ Correct answer and detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: A. Grade B postoperative pancreatic fistula**

**A. Kyun sahi hai:** Grade B mein fistula management change karna padta hai, jaise additional percutaneous drainage. Organ failure ya reoperation absent hone par grade C automatically apply nahi hoti.

**B. Kyun galat hai:** Grade C severe fistula-related organ failure, reoperation ya death se associated hoti hai.

**C. Kyun galat hai:** Biochemical leak mein clinically meaningful intervention ki requirement nahi hoti.

**D. Kyun galat hai:** Extra drainage ki need ek real postoperative complication indicate karti hai.

**Exam clue:** Fistula-related management change can indicate grade B.

</details>

### Q8. A stable postoperative patient develops a pancreatic fistula-associated fluid collection. Which intervention may be planned to obtain source control without immediate open surgery?

- **B.** Removal of all existing drains without review
- **C.** Oral antacid therapy as definitive source control
- **D.** Image-guided percutaneous drainage when anatomically feasible

<details><summary>✅ Correct answer and detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: D. Image-guided percutaneous drainage when anatomically feasible**

**A. Kyun galat hai:** Skin dressing internal collection ko drain nahi karti.

**B. Kyun galat hai:** Existing drains bina review remove karne se collection worsen ho sakti hai.

**C. Kyun galat hai:** Antacid stomach acid reduce karta hai. Ye abdominal collection remove nahi karta.

**D. Kyun sahi hai:** Image-guided drainage infected ya symptomatic collection se fluid nikalne mein madad kar sakti hai. Team location aur patient stability assess karke route choose karti hai.

**Exam clue:** Suitable postoperative collections may need minimally invasive source control.

</details>

### Q9. On day 8 after pancreatic resection, a stable patient with a known fistula has a small episode of blood appearing in a drain that then stops. What should the nurse recognize?

**Difficulty:** Difficult · **Priority:** HIGH YIELD · **Exam focus:** NORCET / HPRCA HP Staff Nurse / PGIMER · Original practice

- **A.** Proof that hemorrhage risk has resolved
- **B.** A routine sign of enzyme secretion
- **C.** A reason to remove the drain independently
- **D.** A possible sentinel bleed requiring urgent surgical evaluation

<details><summary>✅ Correct answer and detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: D. A possible sentinel bleed requiring urgent surgical evaluation**

**A. Kyun galat hai:** Bleeding temporarily stop hone se underlying vascular risk exclude nahi hota.

**B. Kyun galat hai:** Enzyme secretion bloody drainage ko normal nahi banati.

**C. Kyun galat hai:** Independent drain removal warning sign ko treat nahi karta aur risk badha sakta hai.

**D. Kyun sahi hai:** Small transient bleeding kabhi delayed major hemorrhage ka warning sign ho sakti hai. Fistula ke enzymes nearby vessels ko damage kar sakte hain. Turant team assessment chahiye.

**Exam clue:** Sentinel bleeding ko ignore mat karo.

</details>

### Q10. After pancreaticoduodenectomy a patient repeatedly vomits undigested food and cannot advance oral intake despite initial recovery of bowel function. Which complication needs assessment?

- **B.** Isolated pancreatic endocrine failure as the only explanation
- **C.** Expected permanent inability to eat
- **D.** Uncomplicated wound scar formation

<details><summary>✅ Correct answer and detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: A. Delayed gastric emptying**

**A. Kyun sahi hai:** Delayed gastric emptying mein stomach se food aage jaane mein time lagta hai. Persistent vomiting aur intake failure par surgical team obstruction aur other complications bhi evaluate karti hai.

**B. Kyun galat hai:** Endocrine failure glucose regulation affect karti hai. Ye pattern akela explain nahi karti.

**C. Kyun galat hai:** Most patients oral intake resume kar sakte hain. Persistent vomiting ko permanent expected finding nahi maan sakte.

**D. Kyun galat hai:** External scar gastric emptying ka explanation nahi hai.

**Exam clue:** Persistent food intolerance after Whipple suggests DGE assessment.

</details>

**Correct answer: B. Small frequent energy- and protein-dense meals**

### Q14. A patient is discharged after total pancreatectomy. Which endocrine teaching is essential?

**Difficulty:** Difficult · **Priority:** HIGH YIELD · **Exam focus:** NORCET / HPRCA HP Staff Nurse / PGIMER · Original practice

- **A.** Lifelong insulin replacement and individualized hypoglycemia planning are generally required
- **B.** Insulin will never be needed after removal of the pancreas
- **C.** Only oral pancreatic enzymes will regulate blood glucose
- **D.** Glucose monitoring can stop after the wound heals

<details><summary>✅ Correct answer and detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: A. Lifelong insulin replacement and individualized hypoglycemia planning are generally required**

**A. Kyun sahi hai:** Entire pancreas remove hone par endogenous insulin aur pancreatic glucagon production lost hoti hai. Insulin replacement aur careful hypoglycemia education essential hai.

**B. Kyun galat hai:** Pancreas insulin ka major source hai. Removal se insulin dependence expected hoti hai.

**C. Kyun galat hai:** Digestive enzymes food digestion mein help karti hain, insulin ka role replace nahi karti.

**D. Kyun galat hai:** Glucose monitoring surgical wound recovery ke baad bhi zaroori rehti hai.

**Exam clue:** Total pancreatectomy affects both digestive and endocrine functions.

</details>

### Q15. Why can hypoglycemia be particularly challenging after total pancreatectomy in a patient receiving insulin?

**Difficulty:** Difficult · **Priority:** HIGH YIELD · **Exam focus:** NORCET / HPRCA HP Staff Nurse / PGIMER · Original practice

- **A.** The pancreas continues to release excess glucagon
- **B.** Pancreatic enzymes directly replace glucagon
- **C.** The liver can no longer store any glycogen by definition
- **D.** Loss of pancreatic glucagon weakens an important counterregulatory response

<details><summary>✅ Correct answer and detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: D. Loss of pancreatic glucagon weakens an important counterregulatory response**

**A. Kyun galat hai:** Entire pancreas remove ho chuka hai, isliye excessive pancreatic glucagon explanation nahi hai.

**B. Kyun galat hai:** Enzyme replacement digestion ke liye hai, hormonal counterregulation ke liye nahi.

**C. Kyun galat hai:** Total pancreatectomy ka matlab liver glycogen storage completely absent hona nahi hai.

**D. Kyun sahi hai:** Glucagon low glucose ke response mein liver ko glucose release karne ka signal deta hai. Total pancreatectomy ke baad pancreatic glucagon absent hone se recovery response impaired ho sakta hai.

**Exam clue:** Insulin plus reduced glucagon counterregulation increases hypoglycemia vulnerability.

</details>
