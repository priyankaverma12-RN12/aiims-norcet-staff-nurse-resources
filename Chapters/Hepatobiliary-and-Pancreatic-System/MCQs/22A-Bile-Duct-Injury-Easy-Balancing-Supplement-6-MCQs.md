### Q1. What does the term iatrogenic bile duct injury describe?

**Difficulty:** Easy · **Priority:** High · **Exam focus:** NORCET / HPRCA HP Staff Nurse / PGIMER · Original practice

- **A.** A congenital absence of all bile ducts
- **B.** Biliary damage related to a medical or surgical intervention
- **C.** A bacterial infection without structural injury
- **D.** A bile duct tumor arising spontaneously

<details><summary>✅ Correct answer and detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: B. Biliary damage related to a medical or surgical intervention**

**A. Kyun galat hai:** Congenital condition birth se present hoti hai. Iatrogenic injury treatment-related hoti hai.

**B. Kyun sahi hai:** Iatrogenic injury medical ya surgical intervention ke context mein hoti hai. Cholecystectomy ke dauran bile duct injury iska example hai.

**C. Kyun galat hai:** Infection injury ki complication ho sakti hai, par term iatrogenic ka meaning infection nahi hai.

**D. Kyun galat hai:** Spontaneous tumor neoplastic disease hai. Intervention-related duct damage alag entity hai.

**Exam clue:** Iatrogenic describes harm related to healthcare intervention.

</details>

### Q2. Which operation removes the gallbladder and carries a recognized risk of bile duct injury?

**Difficulty:** Easy · **Priority:** High · **Exam focus:** NORCET / HPRCA HP Staff Nurse / PGIMER · Original practice

- **A.** Choledochotomy
- **B.** Cholecystostomy
- **C.** Hepaticojejunostomy
- **D.** Cholecystectomy

<details><summary>✅ Correct answer and detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: D. Cholecystectomy**

**A. Kyun galat hai:** Choledochotomy common bile duct ko surgically open karna hai. Yeh gallbladder removal ka term nahi.

**B. Kyun galat hai:** Cholecystostomy gallbladder drainage ke liye opening ya catheter procedure hai. Organ removal nahi hai.

**C. Kyun galat hai:** Hepaticojejunostomy bile drainage restore karne ke liye bile duct aur jejunum ka connection banati hai.

**D. Kyun sahi hai:** Cholecystectomy gallbladder removal operation hai. Dissection ke dauran adjacent bile ducts injury ka risk hota hai.

**Exam clue:** Distinguish removal, incision, drainage and reconstruction procedures.

</details>

### Q3. Which postoperative finding directly suggests that bile may be leaking into a surgically placed abdominal drain?

**Difficulty:** Easy · **Priority:** High · **Exam focus:** NORCET / HPRCA HP Staff Nurse / PGIMER · Original practice

- **A.** New bilious yellow-green drainage
- **B.** A small amount of clear serous fluid alone
- **C.** A dry intact dressing
- **D.** Normal bowel sounds

<details><summary>✅ Correct answer and detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: A. New bilious yellow-green drainage**

**A. Kyun sahi hai:** Yellow-green bilious output bile leak ka clue ho sakta hai. Drain ka intended purpose aur operative details verify karke team ko unexpected change report karo.

**B. Kyun galat hai:** Clear serous drainage nonspecific ho sakti hai. Yeh bilious output ka direct appearance nahi.

**C. Kyun galat hai:** Dry dressing external leakage absent dikhati hai, internal leak exclude nahi karti.

**D. Kyun galat hai:** Bowel sounds intestinal activity describe karte hain. Bile leak ka direct drain finding nahi hain.

**Exam clue:** Unexpected bilious drainage after surgery warrants assessment.

</details>

### Q4. Which measurement is used to document the volume of liquid collected in a postoperative biliary drainage bag?

**Difficulty:** Easy · **Priority:** High · **Exam focus:** NORCET / HPRCA HP Staff Nurse / PGIMER · Original practice

- **A.** Millimeters of mercury
- **B.** International units per milliliter
- **C.** Milliliters
- **D.** Grams per deciliter

<details><summary>✅ Correct answer and detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: C. Milliliters**

**A. Kyun galat hai:** mmHg pressure measure karta hai, drain volume nahi.

**B. Kyun galat hai:** IU/mL standardized concentration ka unit hai. Total liquid volume nahi.

**C. Kyun sahi hai:** Drain output liquid volume hai aur mL mein record karte hain. Time interval aur appearance bhi document karna useful hai.

**D. Kyun galat hai:** g/dL mass concentration ka unit hai. Drainage amount mL mein record hota hai.

**Exam clue:** Record fluid output as volume over a defined time.

</details>

### Q5. What does the term biliary fistula refer to?

**Difficulty:** Easy · **Priority:** High · **Exam focus:** NORCET / HPRCA HP Staff Nurse / PGIMER · Original practice

- **A.** An abnormal communication through which bile can drain
- **B.** Normal bile passage into the duodenum
- **C.** A normal healing skin incision without fluid leakage
- **D.** A blood clot in the hepatic artery

<details><summary>✅ Correct answer and detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: A. An abnormal communication through which bile can drain**

**A. Kyun sahi hai:** Fistula abnormal communication hoti hai jiske through bile unintended site par drain ho sakti hai. Postoperative leak iska cause ho sakta hai.

**B. Kyun galat hai:** Normal duct se duodenum tak bile flow physiological pathway hai, fistula nahi.

**C. Kyun galat hai:** Healed incision abnormal communication establish nahi karti. Fistula mein abnormal tract ya connection hota hai.

**D. Kyun galat hai:** Arterial thrombus blood vessel blockage hai. Biliary fistula bile passage se related hai.

**Exam clue:** A fistula is an abnormal connection, not normal drainage.

</details>

### Q6. Which term describes the surgical joining of two hollow structures to create a continuous passage after bile duct reconstruction?

**Difficulty:** Easy · **Priority:** High · **Exam focus:** NORCET / HPRCA HP Staff Nurse / PGIMER · Original practice

- **A.** Aspiration
- **B.** Anastomosis
- **C.** Ablation
- **D.** Embolization

<details><summary>✅ Correct answer and detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: B. Anastomosis**

**A. Kyun galat hai:** Aspiration needle ya suction se fluid remove karna ho sakta hai. Surgical joining nahi.

**B. Kyun sahi hai:** Anastomosis do structures ke lumen connect karne ka surgical term hai. Biliary reconstruction mein bile duct ko bowel se connect kiya ja sakta hai.

**C. Kyun galat hai:** Ablation tissue destroy karne ki technique hai. Continuous passage banana uska meaning nahi.

**D. Kyun galat hai:** Embolization blood vessel flow block karne ki intervention hai. Anastomosis joining procedure hai.

**Exam clue:** Anastomosis means surgically joining structures.

</details>
