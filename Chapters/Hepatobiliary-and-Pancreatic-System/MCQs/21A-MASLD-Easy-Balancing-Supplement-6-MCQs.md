### Q1. What does hepatic steatosis mean?

**Difficulty:** Easy · **Priority:** High · **Exam focus:** NORCET / HPRCA HP Staff Nurse / PGIMER · Original practice

- **A.** Accumulation of air in the portal vein
- **B.** Complete absence of bile production in every patient
- **C.** A malignant tumor in every liver
- **D.** Excess fat accumulation in liver cells

<details><summary>✅ Correct answer and detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: D. Excess fat accumulation in liver cells**

**A. Kyun galat hai:** Portal venous gas alag imaging finding hai. Steatosis liver cells ke fat se related hai.

**B. Kyun galat hai:** Fatty change hone ka matlab bile production completely absent hona nahi hai.

**C. Kyun galat hai:** Steatosis benign metabolic disease mein bhi ho sakti hai. Har case cancer nahi hota.

**D. Kyun sahi hai:** Steatosis hepatocytes mein excess lipid accumulation ko describe karti hai. Yeh malignancy ka synonym nahi hai.

**Exam clue:** Steatosis refers to fat within hepatocytes.

</details>

### Q2. What does the abbreviation MASH stand for?

**Difficulty:** Easy · **Priority:** High · **Exam focus:** NORCET / HPRCA HP Staff Nurse / PGIMER · Original practice

- **A.** Metabolic arterial systemic hypertension
- **B.** Malignant autoimmune sclerosing hepatitis
- **C.** Metabolic dysfunction-associated steatohepatitis
- **D.** Microbial acute splenic hemorrhage

<details><summary>✅ Correct answer and detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: C. Metabolic dysfunction-associated steatohepatitis**

**A. Kyun galat hai:** Systemic hypertension cardiometabolic risk factor hai, MASH ka expansion nahi.

**B. Kyun galat hai:** Autoimmune hepatitis separate diagnosis hai. MASH metabolic liver disease category hai.

**C. Kyun sahi hai:** MASH metabolic dysfunction-associated steatohepatitis ka abbreviation hai. Ismein steatosis ke saath inflammatory injury hoti hai.

**D. Kyun galat hai:** Splenic hemorrhage spleen bleeding ko describe karta hai. MASH se unrelated hai.

**Exam clue:** MASH is the inflammatory form of metabolic steatotic liver disease.

</details>

### Q3. Which simple bedside measurement helps assess central adiposity in MASLD risk evaluation?

**Difficulty:** Easy · **Priority:** High · **Exam focus:** NORCET / HPRCA HP Staff Nurse / PGIMER · Original practice

- **A.** Head circumference in every adult
- **B.** Pupil diameter
- **C.** Waist circumference
- **D.** Upper eyelid length

<details><summary>✅ Correct answer and detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: C. Waist circumference**

**A. Kyun galat hai:** Head circumference pediatric growth mein relevant hai. Adult abdominal adiposity estimate nahi karta.

**B. Kyun galat hai:** Pupil diameter neurological assessment ka part hai. Central obesity measure nahi karta.

**C. Kyun sahi hai:** Waist circumference abdominal fat distribution ka practical estimate hai. Cardiometabolic risk assessment mein BMI ke saath useful ho sakta hai.

**D. Kyun galat hai:** Eyelid length central fat distribution ka validated routine measure nahi hai.

**Exam clue:** Waist circumference helps assess abdominal obesity.

</details>

### Q4. Which lipid abnormality is commonly included among cardiometabolic risk features relevant to MASLD?

**Difficulty:** Easy · **Priority:** High · **Exam focus:** NORCET / HPRCA HP Staff Nurse / PGIMER · Original practice

- **A.** Elevated triglycerides
- **B.** Raised hemoglobin alone
- **C.** Increased platelet count alone
- **D.** Elevated serum chloride alone

<details><summary>✅ Correct answer and detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: A. Elevated triglycerides**

**A. Kyun sahi hai:** High triglycerides dyslipidemia ka component hai aur MASLD ke cardiometabolic risk assessment mein relevant hai.

**B. Kyun galat hai:** Hemoglobin anemia ya erythrocytosis evaluate karta hai. Triglyceride abnormality ka substitute nahi.

**C. Kyun galat hai:** Platelets clotting aur fibrosis scores mein relevant ho sakte hain. Dyslipidemia define nahi karte.

**D. Kyun galat hai:** Chloride electrolyte hai. Metabolic lipid risk ka direct marker nahi hai.

**Exam clue:** Triglycerides are part of cardiometabolic risk assessment.

</details>

### Q5. Which imaging method uses sound waves and may identify hepatic fat in routine assessment?

**Difficulty:** Easy · **Priority:** High · **Exam focus:** NORCET / HPRCA HP Staff Nurse / PGIMER · Original practice

- **A.** Electroencephalography
- **B.** Spirometry
- **C.** Electrocardiography
- **D.** Abdominal ultrasonography

<details><summary>✅ Correct answer and detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: D. Abdominal ultrasonography**

**A. Kyun galat hai:** EEG brain electrical activity record karta hai. Liver fat image nahi deta.

**B. Kyun galat hai:** Spirometry lung airflow assess karti hai. Hepatic steatosis ka imaging method nahi hai.

**C. Kyun galat hai:** ECG cardiac electrical activity record karta hai. Liver tissue visualize nahi karta.

**D. Kyun sahi hai:** Ultrasound sound waves se liver image banata hai aur steatosis ke clues de sakta hai. Mild disease miss bhi ho sakti hai.

**Exam clue:** Ultrasound can detect fatty liver but has sensitivity limitations.

</details>

### Q6. Which health professional can assist with an individualized sustainable nutrition plan for a patient with MASLD?

**Difficulty:** Easy · **Priority:** High · **Exam focus:** NORCET / HPRCA HP Staff Nurse / PGIMER · Original practice

- **A.** Radiographer acting as sole dietary prescriber
- **B.** Registered dietitian
- **C.** Optometrist acting as sole dietary prescriber
- **D.** Audiologist acting as sole dietary prescriber

<details><summary>✅ Correct answer and detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: B. Registered dietitian**

**A. Kyun galat hai:** Radiographer imaging mein trained hota hai. Individual clinical nutrition plan dietitian ke scope mein aata hai.

**B. Kyun sahi hai:** Dietitian patient ki preferences, nutritional status aur metabolic goals ke hisaab se sustainable eating plan develop kar sakta hai.

**C. Kyun galat hai:** Optometrist eye care provide karta hai. MASLD diet planning uska primary role nahi.

**D. Kyun galat hai:** Audiologist hearing assessment karta hai. Individual nutrition support ke liye dietitian appropriate hai.

**Exam clue:** Use coordinated, individualized dietary support.

</details>
