### Q1. In primary sclerosing cholangitis, what does the word sclerosing describe?

**Difficulty:** Easy · **Priority:** High · **Exam focus:** NORCET / HPRCA HP Staff Nurse / PGIMER · Original practice

- **A.** Rapid formation of pancreatic insulin
- **B.** Progressive scarring and hardening of affected ducts
- **C.** Destruction of red cells in the spleen
- **D.** Formation of urinary calculi

<details><summary>✅ Correct answer and detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: B. Progressive scarring and hardening of affected ducts**

**A. Kyun galat hai:** Insulin pancreatic endocrine cells banati hain. Ye duct scarring nahi hai.

**B. Kyun sahi hai:** Sclerosing fibrosis aur scarring ko describe karta hai. Bile ducts narrow ho sakti hain aur bile flow obstruct ho sakta hai.

**C. Kyun galat hai:** Ye hemolysis se related process hai, bile duct fibrosis nahi.

**D. Kyun galat hai:** Urinary calculi kidney ya urinary tract mein stones hote hain.

**Exam clue:** Sclerosis refers to scarring.

</details>

### Q2. Which term describes inflammation of the bile ducts?

**Difficulty:** Easy · **Priority:** High · **Exam focus:** NORCET / HPRCA HP Staff Nurse / PGIMER · Original practice

- **A.** Cholecystitis
- **B.** Pancreatitis
- **C.** Gastritis
- **D.** Cholangitis

<details><summary>✅ Correct answer and detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: D. Cholangitis**

**A. Kyun galat hai:** Cholecystitis gallbladder ki inflammation hai, duct inflammation ka specific term nahi.

**B. Kyun galat hai:** Pancreatitis pancreas ki inflammation hai.

**C. Kyun galat hai:** Gastritis stomach lining ki inflammation hai.

**D. Kyun sahi hai:** Cholangitis bile ducts ki inflammation hai. PSC mein chronic inflammation aur fibrosis important hain.

**Exam clue:** Cholangitis involves the bile ducts.

</details>

### Q3. Which symptom pattern can suggest a superimposed bile duct infection in a patient with PSC?

**Difficulty:** Easy · **Priority:** High · **Exam focus:** NORCET / HPRCA HP Staff Nurse / PGIMER · Original practice

- **A.** Stable visual acuity
- **B.** Longstanding unchanged toenail discoloration
- **C.** New fever and shaking chills
- **D.** Normal appetite with stable temperature

<details><summary>✅ Correct answer and detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: C. New fever and shaking chills**

**A. Kyun galat hai:** Stable vision bile duct infection ka clue nahi hai.

**B. Kyun galat hai:** Toenail change new systemic infection ka typical clue nahi hai.

**C. Kyun sahi hai:** New fever aur chills bacterial cholangitis ka clue ho sakte hain. Patient ko promptly assess karna chahiye.

**D. Kyun galat hai:** Ye findings by themselves infection suggest nahi karti hain.

**Exam clue:** New fever in PSC warrants assessment for cholangitis.

</details>

### Q4. What is the name for a narrowed segment of a bile duct in PSC?

**Difficulty:** Easy · **Priority:** High · **Exam focus:** NORCET / HPRCA HP Staff Nurse / PGIMER · Original practice

- **A.** Biliary stricture
- **B.** Esophageal varix
- **C.** Renal calculus
- **D.** Gastric polyp

<details><summary>✅ Correct answer and detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: A. Biliary stricture**

**A. Kyun sahi hai:** Stricture duct ke lumen ki narrowing hai. PSC mein scarring ki wajah se bile passage restricted ho sakta hai.

**B. Kyun galat hai:** Varix dilated vein hai, narrowed bile duct nahi.

**C. Kyun galat hai:** Renal calculus kidney stone hai.

**D. Kyun galat hai:** Polyp stomach lining se projecting growth hai.

**Exam clue:** Biliary stricture means narrowing of a bile duct.

</details>

### Q5. Which body fluid normally passes through the ducts affected by PSC?

**Difficulty:** Easy · **Priority:** High · **Exam focus:** NORCET / HPRCA HP Staff Nurse / PGIMER · Original practice

- **A.** Urine
- **B.** Cerebrospinal fluid
- **C.** Synovial fluid
- **D.** Bile

<details><summary>✅ Correct answer and detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: D. Bile**

**A. Kyun galat hai:** Urine kidneys se urinary tract mein travel karti hai.

**B. Kyun galat hai:** CSF brain aur spinal cord ke aas paas circulate hota hai.

**C. Kyun galat hai:** Synovial fluid joints mein lubrication provide karta hai.

**D. Kyun sahi hai:** Bile liver se ducts ke through intestine tak pahunchti hai aur digestion mein help karti hai.

**Exam clue:** PSC affects bile transport.

</details>

### Q6. A patient with PSC has chronic diarrhea. Which history is particularly relevant to share with the hepatology team?

**Difficulty:** Easy · **Priority:** High · **Exam focus:** NORCET / HPRCA HP Staff Nurse / PGIMER · Original practice

- **A.** Previous simple spectacle prescription
- **B.** Known ulcerative colitis
- **C.** Remote healed ankle sprain
- **D.** History of uncomplicated earwax impaction

<details><summary>✅ Correct answer and detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: B. Known ulcerative colitis**

**A. Kyun galat hai:** Spectacle prescription bowel inflammation explain nahi karti.

**B. Kyun sahi hai:** Ulcerative colitis PSC ke saath associated inflammatory bowel disease hai. Diarrhea bowel disease activity ka sign ho sakta hai.

**C. Kyun galat hai:** Ankle sprain chronic diarrhea ka typical cause nahi hai.

**D. Kyun galat hai:** Earwax issue PSC-associated intestinal disease se unrelated hai.

**Exam clue:** Ulcerative colitis is an important PSC association.

</details>
