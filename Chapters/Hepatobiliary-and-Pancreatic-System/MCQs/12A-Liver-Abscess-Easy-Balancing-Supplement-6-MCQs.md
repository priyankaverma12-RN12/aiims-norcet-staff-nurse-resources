### Q1. What does pyogenic mean in the diagnosis of a hepatic abscess?

**Difficulty:** Easy · **Priority:** High · **Exam focus:** NORCET / HPRCA HP Staff Nurse / PGIMER · Original practice

- **A.** Stone-forming only
- **B.** Hormone-secreting
- **C.** Blood-cell producing
- **D.** Pus-forming

<details><summary>✅ Correct answer and detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: D. Pus-forming**

**A. Kyun galat hai:** Stone formation calculi se related hai, pyogenic ka meaning nahi.

**B. Kyun galat hai:** Hormone secretion endocrine function hai, abscess terminology nahi.

**C. Kyun galat hai:** Blood cell formation hematopoiesis hai.

**D. Kyun sahi hai:** Pyogenic pus formation se related hai. Bacterial infection liver mein pus-containing collection bana sakti hai.

**Exam clue:** Pyogenic means pus-forming.

</details>

- **A.** Uncomplicated presbyopia
- **B.** Diabetes mellitus
- **C.** Seasonal rhinitis alone
- **D.** Healed uncomplicated finger fracture

<details><summary>✅ Correct answer and detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: B. Diabetes mellitus**

**A. Kyun galat hai:** Age-related near vision difficulty liver abscess ka metabolic risk factor nahi hai.

**B. Kyun sahi hai:** Diabetes mein infection risk badh sakta hai. Poor glycemic control host defense affect kar sakta hai.

**C. Kyun galat hai:** Rhinitis liver abscess ka recognized metabolic condition nahi hai.

**D. Kyun galat hai:** Healed fracture diabetes jaisa systemic infection risk factor nahi hai.

**Exam clue:** Diabetes is a relevant risk factor.

</details>

### Q3. Which hepatic lobe is a frequent site of a solitary liver abscess?

**Difficulty:** Easy · **Priority:** High · **Exam focus:** NORCET / HPRCA HP Staff Nurse / PGIMER · Original practice

- **A.** Right lobe
- **B.** Right lung lobe
- **C.** Left renal cortex
- **D.** Splenic hilum

<details><summary>✅ Correct answer and detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: A. Right lobe**

**A. Kyun sahi hai:** Right hepatic lobe large hai aur substantial blood flow receive karta hai. Solitary abscess frequently yahan mil sakta hai.

**B. Kyun galat hai:** Lung lobe hepatic tissue nahi hai.

**C. Kyun galat hai:** Ye kidney ka part hai.

**D. Kyun galat hai:** Ye spleen ka vascular region hai, hepatic lobe nahi.

**Exam clue:** The right hepatic lobe is a common abscess location.

</details>

### Q4. Which blood count finding may support a bacterial liver abscess when interpreted with clinical findings?

**Difficulty:** Easy · **Priority:** High · **Exam focus:** NORCET / HPRCA HP Staff Nurse / PGIMER · Original practice

- **A.** Isolated increased red cell size as proof of abscess
- **B.** Normal platelet count as proof of abscess
- **C.** Leukocytosis
- **D.** Normal hemoglobin as proof of abscess

<details><summary>✅ Correct answer and detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: C. Leukocytosis**

**A. Kyun galat hai:** MCV red cell size batata hai. Ye abscess confirm nahi karta.

**B. Kyun galat hai:** Normal platelets liver infection ka diagnostic proof nahi hai.

**C. Kyun sahi hai:** Bacterial infection ke response mein white cells rise ho sakte hain. Normal count infection ko completely exclude nahi karta.

**D. Kyun galat hai:** Hemoglobin oxygen-carrying protein ka measure hai, abscess confirm nahi karta.

**Exam clue:** White cell rise supports infection assessment.

</details>

### Q5. Which term describes spread of organisms through the bloodstream to form a liver abscess?

**Difficulty:** Easy · **Priority:** High · **Exam focus:** NORCET / HPRCA HP Staff Nurse / PGIMER · Original practice

- **A.** Mechanical abrasion
- **B.** Hematogenous spread
- **C.** Osmotic diffusion
- **D.** Neural conduction

<details><summary>✅ Correct answer and detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: B. Hematogenous spread**

**A. Kyun galat hai:** Abrasion superficial tissue injury hai, bloodstream spread nahi.

**B. Kyun sahi hai:** Hematogenous spread mein organisms blood circulation ke through liver tak pahunch sakte hain. Infection ka source evaluate karna important hai.

**C. Kyun galat hai:** Osmosis water movement describe karti hai, infectious bloodstream spread nahi.

**D. Kyun galat hai:** Neural conduction nerve signals ka movement hai.

**Exam clue:** Hemato refers to blood.

</details>

### Q6. Which imaging modality may characterize a deep or multiloculated hepatic abscess when further cross-sectional detail is needed?

- **A.** Electroencephalogram
- **B.** Audiometry
- **C.** Electrocardiogram
- **D.** Contrast-enhanced CT when clinically suitable

<details><summary>✅ Correct answer and detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: D. Contrast-enhanced CT when clinically suitable**

**A. Kyun galat hai:** EEG brain electrical activity record karta hai.

**B. Kyun galat hai:** Audiometry hearing measure karti hai.

**C. Kyun galat hai:** ECG cardiac electrical signals assess karta hai, hepatic collection image nahi karta.

**D. Kyun sahi hai:** CT liver collection ke location, extent aur internal compartments ko assess karne mein help karta hai. Contrast safety clinician assess karta hai.

**Exam clue:** CT can define abscess extent and complexity.

</details>
