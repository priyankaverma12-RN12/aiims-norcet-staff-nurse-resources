### Q1. Which feature distinguishes acute-on-chronic liver failure under the EASL-CLIF framework from uncomplicated stable cirrhosis?

**Difficulty:** 🟢 Easy · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD · Original practice

- **A.** Stable compensated disease without organ dysfunction
- **B.** An isolated old scar on liver imaging
- **C.** Acute deterioration associated with organ failure and high short-term mortality
- **D.** Persistent normal liver function without symptoms

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: C. Acute deterioration associated with organ failure and high short-term mortality**

**Option-wise explanation:**
- **A (Galat):** Stable compensated cirrhosis mein acute organ failure ka syndrome present nahi hota.
- **B (Galat):** Purana scar chronic structural change dikhata hai, current organ failure nahi.
- **C (Sahi):** ACLF mein chronic liver disease ke context mein acute deterioration aur organ failure hota hai. Isliye short-term mortality ka risk badhta hai.
- **D (Galat):** Normal functioning aur stable clinical state ACLF ka description nahi hai.

**Exam clue:** ACLF is an acute high-risk syndrome.
