### Q1. What is palmar erythema?

**Difficulty:** Easy · **Priority:** High · **Exam focus:** NORCET / HPRCA HP Staff Nurse / PGIMER · Original practice

- **A.** Yellow discoloration of the sclera
- **B.** Swelling of the ankle
- **C.** Bleeding from the nose
- **D.** Redness of the palms

<details><summary>✅ Correct answer and detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: D. Redness of the palms**

**A. Kyun galat hai:** Sclera ka yellow hona jaundice ka sign hai.

**B. Kyun galat hai:** Ankle swelling peripheral edema hai.

**C. Kyun galat hai:** Nose bleeding ko epistaxis kehte hain.

**D. Kyun sahi hai:** Palmar erythema palms ki redness hai. Ye chronic liver disease mein ho sakti hai, lekin sirf cirrhosis ka diagnostic proof nahi hai.

**Exam clue:** Palmar means palms and erythema means redness.

</details>

### Q2. Which term describes the small radiating superficial blood vessels sometimes seen in chronic liver disease?

**Difficulty:** Easy · **Priority:** High · **Exam focus:** NORCET / HPRCA HP Staff Nurse / PGIMER · Original practice

- **A.** Petechiae
- **B.** Spider angiomas
- **C.** Keloids
- **D.** Urticaria

<details><summary>✅ Correct answer and detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: B. Spider angiomas**

**A. Kyun galat hai:** Petechiae tiny nonblanching blood spots hain, radiating vessel pattern nahi.

**B. Kyun sahi hai:** Spider angioma mein central vascular spot aur radiating vessels hote hain. Ye chronic liver disease mein mil sakte hain.

**C. Kyun galat hai:** Keloids excessive scar growth hain.

**D. Kyun galat hai:** Urticaria itchy raised wheals hain.

**Exam clue:** Recognize the spider-like vascular pattern.

</details>

### Q3. Which term means an enlarged spleen in a patient with portal hypertension?

**Difficulty:** Easy · **Priority:** High · **Exam focus:** NORCET / HPRCA HP Staff Nurse / PGIMER · Original practice

- **A.** Splenomegaly
- **B.** Hepatomegaly
- **C.** Cardiomegaly
- **D.** Nephromegaly

<details><summary>✅ Correct answer and detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: A. Splenomegaly**

**A. Kyun sahi hai:** Splenomegaly spleen enlargement hai. Portal pressure se spleen congestion ho sakti hai.

**B. Kyun galat hai:** Hepatomegaly liver enlargement hai, spleen ka nahi.

**C. Kyun galat hai:** Cardiomegaly heart enlargement hai.

**D. Kyun galat hai:** Nephromegaly kidney enlargement describe karta hai.

**Exam clue:** Spleno means spleen.

</details>

### Q4. Which clinical term means loss of skeletal muscle mass that may develop in cirrhosis?

**Difficulty:** Easy · **Priority:** High · **Exam focus:** NORCET / HPRCA HP Staff Nurse / PGIMER · Original practice

- **A.** Thrombocytopenia
- **B.** Leukocytosis
- **C.** Sarcopenia
- **D.** Hyperbilirubinemia

<details><summary>✅ Correct answer and detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: C. Sarcopenia**

**A. Kyun galat hai:** Thrombocytopenia low platelet count hai.

**B. Kyun galat hai:** Leukocytosis white cells ka increased count hai.

**C. Kyun sahi hai:** Sarcopenia muscle mass aur function ke loss se related hai. Chronic liver disease mein nutrition aur activity affect ho sakti hai.

**D. Kyun galat hai:** Hyperbilirubinemia raised bilirubin hai, muscle loss nahi.

**Exam clue:** Sarcopenia concerns muscle.

</details>

### Q5. Which finding describes gynecomastia in a male patient with chronic liver disease?

**Difficulty:** Easy · **Priority:** High · **Exam focus:** NORCET / HPRCA HP Staff Nurse / PGIMER · Original practice

- **A.** An enlarged thyroid gland
- **B.** Enlargement of glandular breast tissue
- **C.** A swollen salivary gland
- **D.** A painful enlarged testis

<details><summary>✅ Correct answer and detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: B. Enlargement of glandular breast tissue**

**A. Kyun galat hai:** Thyroid enlargement goiter hai.

**B. Kyun sahi hai:** Gynecomastia male breast gland tissue ki enlargement hai. Hormonal changes aur medicines isme role play kar sakti hain.

**C. Kyun galat hai:** Salivary gland swelling breast gland enlargement nahi hai.

**D. Kyun galat hai:** Testicular enlargement ek different clinical finding hai.

**Exam clue:** Gynecomastia refers to male breast gland tissue.

</details>

### Q6. Which term describes yellowing of the skin and sclera caused by excess bilirubin?

**Difficulty:** Easy · **Priority:** High · **Exam focus:** NORCET / HPRCA HP Staff Nurse / PGIMER · Original practice

- **A.** Cyanosis
- **B.** Pallor
- **C.** Erythema
- **D.** Jaundice

<details><summary>✅ Correct answer and detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: D. Jaundice**

**A. Kyun galat hai:** Cyanosis bluish discoloration hai jo oxygenation ya perfusion se related ho sakti hai.

**B. Kyun galat hai:** Pallor unusual paleness hai.

**C. Kyun galat hai:** Erythema redness describe karta hai.

**D. Kyun sahi hai:** Jab bilirubin tissues mein accumulate hota hai toh skin aur sclera yellow dikh sakte hain.

**Exam clue:** Jaundice is bilirubin-related yellow discoloration.

</details>
