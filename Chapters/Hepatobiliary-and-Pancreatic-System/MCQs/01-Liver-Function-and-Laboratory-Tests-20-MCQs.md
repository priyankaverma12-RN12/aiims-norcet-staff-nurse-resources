### Q1. Which liver enzyme is most directly associated with hepatocyte injury rather than liver synthetic capacity?

**Difficulty:** Easy | **Priority:** High | **Topic:** Liver Function and Laboratory Tests | **Evidence:** Original practice

- **A.** ALT
- **B.** Albumin
- **C.** INR
- **D.** Serum sodium

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: A. ALT**

- **A.** ALT liver cells ke andar hota hai. Jab ye cells damage hote hain toh enzyme blood mein aa sakta hai.
- **B.** Albumin liver mein banne wala protein hai, isliye iska level synthesis aur doosre factors se juda hai.
- **C.** INR clotting factors ke kaam ko reflect karta hai, na ki seedha liver cell injury ko.
- **D.** Sodium body ke water balance ka marker hai, liver cell injury ka specific test nahi hai.

**Exam clue:** Injury aur function ke markers ko alag yaad rakho.

</details>

### Q2. A patient has ALT 640 U/L (ULN 40) and ALP 150 U/L (ULN 120). Which biochemical pattern predominates?

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Priority:** High | **Topic:** Liver Function and Laboratory Tests | **Evidence:** Original practice

- **A.** Cholestatic
- **B.** Hepatocellular
- **C.** Isolated synthetic failure
- **D.** Normal liver biochemistry

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: B. Hepatocellular**

- **A.** Cholestasis mein ALP ka proportional rise ALT se zyada hota hai.
- **B.** ALT normal upper limit se 16 guna hai aur ALP sirf 1.25 guna hai. Isliye liver cell injury ka pattern dominate karta hai.
- **C.** Synthetic failure ko samajhne ke liye albumin aur INR jaise tests bhi chahiye.
- **D.** Dono results reference limit se upar hain, isliye normal nahi hain.

**Exam clue:** Compare multiples of ULN, not raw enzyme values.

</details>

### Q3. ALT is 180 U/L (ULN 45) and ALP is 300 U/L (ULN 100). What is the R ratio?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Priority:** High | **Topic:** Liver Function and Laboratory Tests | **Evidence:** Original practice

- **A.** 0.60
- **B.** 4.00
- **C.** 1.33
- **D.** 7.00

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: C. 1.33**

- **A.** 0.60 raw values 180/300 se milta hai. Dono enzymes ki normal limits alag hain.
- **B.** 4 sirf ALT ka normalized multiple hai. Isko ALP multiple se divide karna hai.
- **C.** ALT ka multiple 180/45 = 4 hai. ALP ka multiple 300/100 = 3 hai. R = 4/3, lagbhag 1.33.
- **D.** 7 dono multiples ko jodne se milta hai. R ratio mein division hota hai.

**Exam clue:** R = (ALT/ULN)/(ALP/ULN).

</details>

### Q4. A patient has a raised ALP but normal GGT. Which additional explanation deserves consideration?

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Priority:** High | **Topic:** Liver Function and Laboratory Tests | **Evidence:** Original practice

- **A.** Definite complete bile duct obstruction
- **B.** Confirmed acute liver failure
- **C.** Established acute hepatitis B
- **D.** A nonhepatic source such as bone

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: D. A nonhepatic source such as bone**

- **A.** Complete obstruction ko sirf ALP se confirm nahi kar sakte. Imaging aur doosre findings chahiye.
- **B.** Acute liver failure ke liye clotting disturbance aur encephalopathy ka assessment chahiye.
- **C.** Hepatitis B ke diagnosis ke liye viral markers chahiye, ALP alone kaafi nahi hai.
- **D.** ALP bone se bhi release ho sakta hai. Normal GGT ke saath bone source ko clinical context mein check karna useful hai.

**Exam clue:** GGT helps localize an elevated ALP.

</details>

### Q5. Which blood protein is synthesized by the liver and contributes substantially to plasma oncotic pressure?

**Difficulty:** Easy | **Priority:** High | **Topic:** Liver Function and Laboratory Tests | **Evidence:** Original practice

- **A.** Albumin
- **B.** Hemoglobin
- **C.** Troponin I
- **D.** Myoglobin

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: A. Albumin**

- **A.** Albumin paani ko blood vessels ke andar rakhne mein help karta hai. Liver iska major source hai.
- **B.** Hemoglobin red blood cells mein oxygen carry karta hai, plasma oncotic pressure ka major protein nahi hai.
- **C.** Troponin I muscle contraction se juda hai aur cardiac injury mein measure hota hai.
- **D.** Myoglobin muscle cells mein oxygen store karta hai, plasma oncotic pressure maintain nahi karta.

**Exam clue:** Albumin helps maintain intravascular fluid.

</details>

### Q6. A patient with severe acute hepatitis develops a rising INR despite vitamin K administration. Which concern is most important?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Priority:** High | **Topic:** Liver Function and Laboratory Tests | **Evidence:** Original practice

- **A.** Increased renal potassium excretion
- **B.** Impaired hepatic clotting factor synthesis
- **C.** Increased albumin production
- **D.** Isolated erythrocyte destruction

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: B. Impaired hepatic clotting factor synthesis**

- **A.** Kidney se potassium loss electrolytes badalta hai, lekin is pattern ko seedha explain nahi karta.
- **B.** Liver clotting factors banata hai. Vitamin K dene ke baad bhi INR badhe toh synthesis kharab hone ka concern hai aur urgent assessment chahiye.
- **C.** Albumin production badhna synthetic deterioration ke opposite hai.
- **D.** RBC tootne se bilirubin badh sakta hai, lekin isolated hemolysis rising INR ka usual cause nahi hai.

**Exam clue:** A rising INR in acute liver injury deserves prompt escalation.

</details>

### Q7. Which finding helps explain why a normal serum albumin does not rule out very recent acute liver injury?

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Priority:** High | **Topic:** Liver Function and Laboratory Tests | **Evidence:** Original practice

- **A.** Albumin is synthesized only by the kidneys
- **B.** Albumin rises immediately after hepatocyte death
- **C.** Albumin has a relatively long circulating half-life
- **D.** Albumin is an enzyme released only during cholestasis

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: C. Albumin has a relatively long circulating half-life**

- **A.** Albumin mainly liver banata hai, kidneys normally ise urine mein lose hone se bachati hain.
- **B.** Liver cell damage albumin ko immediate injury marker nahi banata.
- **C.** Albumin blood mein kaafi samay rehta hai. Isliye recent injury ke turant baad iska level normal reh sakta hai.
- **D.** Albumin enzyme nahi, ek plasma protein hai. Cholestasis ka specific marker bhi nahi hai.

**Exam clue:** Albumin changes more slowly than many injury markers.

</details>

### Q8. Which bilirubin fraction is water soluble and can appear in urine?

**Difficulty:** Easy | **Priority:** High | **Topic:** Liver Function and Laboratory Tests | **Evidence:** Original practice

- **A.** Albumin-bound unconjugated bilirubin
- **B.** Hemoglobin inside intact erythrocytes
- **C.** Urobilinogen-free albumin
- **D.** Conjugated bilirubin

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: D. Conjugated bilirubin**

- **A.** Unconjugated bilirubin albumin ke saath bound hota hai, isliye normally urine mein filter nahi hota.
- **B.** Intact RBC ka hemoglobin bilirubin ka water-soluble fraction nahi hai.
- **C.** Albumin ek protein hai. Ye bilirubin fraction nahi hai.
- **D.** Liver bilirubin ko conjugate karke water soluble banata hai. Ye fraction blood mein badhne par urine mein aa sakta hai.

**Exam clue:** Urine bilirubin points toward conjugated bilirubin.

</details>

### Q9. A patient with brisk hemolysis has isolated indirect hyperbilirubinemia. Which additional finding is most consistent?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Priority:** High | **Topic:** Liver Function and Laboratory Tests | **Evidence:** Original practice

- **A.** Negative urine bilirubin
- **B.** Markedly positive urine bilirubin from the indirect fraction
- **C.** Isolated high ALP due to hemolysis
- **D.** A prolonged INR caused by indirect bilirubin alone

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: A. Negative urine bilirubin**

- **A.** Hemolysis se unconjugated bilirubin badh sakta hai. Albumin-bound fraction urine mein filter nahi hota.
- **B.** Indirect bilirubin khud urine mein nahi aata. Positive urine bilirubin conjugated fraction suggest karta hai.
- **C.** Hemolysis RBC breakdown hai. ALP rise isse directly expected nahi hai.
- **D.** INR clotting process se juda hai. Indirect bilirubin ka rise alone INR prolongation establish nahi karta.

**Exam clue:** Separate hemolysis-related bilirubin from biliary excretion disorders.

</details>

### Q10. Which specimen result may be spuriously elevated when a blood sample is significantly hemolyzed?

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Priority:** High | **Topic:** Liver Function and Laboratory Tests | **Evidence:** Original practice

- **A.** Albumin exclusively
- **B.** AST
- **C.** Direct bilirubin exclusively
- **D.** INR exclusively

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: B. AST**

- **A.** Albumin mainly plasma protein hai. Hemolysis ka effect test method par depend karta hai, exclusive rise nahi hota.
- **B.** Red blood cells mein AST bhi hota hai. Sample mein RBC tootne se AST artificially high ho sakta hai.
- **C.** Direct bilirubin measurement mein interference ho sakti hai, lekin isolated rise predict nahi kar sakte.
- **D.** INR clotting assay hai. Hemolysis se exclusive predictable INR rise establish nahi hota.

**Exam clue:** Look at sample quality before interpreting unexpected results.

</details>

### Q11. A patient has muscle pain after a seizure, elevated AST, mildly elevated ALT and markedly elevated creatine kinase. What should the nurse recognize?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Priority:** High | **Topic:** Liver Function and Laboratory Tests | **Evidence:** Original practice

- **A.** AST proves viral hepatitis
- **B.** Creatine kinase proves biliary obstruction
- **C.** Skeletal muscle injury may contribute to the AST rise
- **D.** The patient has definite decompensated cirrhosis

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: C. Skeletal muscle injury may contribute to the AST rise**

- **A.** Viral hepatitis ka proof viral testing se milta hai. AST specific nahi hai.
- **B.** CK muscle injury ka marker hai, bile duct blockage ka diagnostic test nahi hai.
- **C.** AST liver ke alawa muscle mein bhi hota hai. Seizure ke baad muscle injury aur high CK is source ko support karte hain.
- **D.** Cirrhosis ki decompensation ko ascites, bleeding ya encephalopathy jaise findings se assess karte hain.

**Exam clue:** AST is not liver-specific.

</details>

### Q12. A patient with poor nutrition has low albumin but normal INR and near-normal liver enzymes. Which interpretation is appropriate?

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Priority:** High | **Topic:** Liver Function and Laboratory Tests | **Evidence:** Original practice

- **A.** Low albumin proves cirrhosis
- **B.** Normal INR proves all liver function is normal
- **C.** Low albumin confirms extrahepatic obstruction
- **D.** Hypoalbuminemia is nonspecific and requires broader evaluation

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: D. Hypoalbuminemia is nonspecific and requires broader evaluation**

- **A.** Cirrhosis confirm karne ke liye history, examination aur suitable tests chahiye.
- **B.** Normal INR reassuring ho sakta hai, lekin har liver disorder ko exclude nahi karta.
- **C.** Extrahepatic obstruction ke assessment mein bile markers aur imaging useful hain, albumin alone nahi.
- **D.** Nutrition, inflammation, protein loss aur liver synthesis sab albumin ko affect karte hain. Ek result se cause decide nahi hota.

**Exam clue:** Albumin is influenced by nonhepatic factors.

</details>

### Q13. What is a major physiological role of bile salts?

**Difficulty:** Easy | **Priority:** High | **Topic:** Liver Function and Laboratory Tests | **Evidence:** Original practice

- **A.** Facilitating intestinal absorption of fats
- **B.** Transporting oxygen inside red blood cells
- **C.** Producing insulin in hepatocytes
- **D.** Digesting dietary protein as a pancreatic protease

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: A. Facilitating intestinal absorption of fats**

- **A.** Bile salts fat ko chhote droplets aur micelles ke form mein process karne mein help karte hain. Isse fat absorption aasaan hota hai.
- **B.** Oxygen transport hemoglobin ka kaam hai, bile salts ka nahi.
- **C.** Insulin pancreatic beta cells banate hain, liver cells nahi.
- **D.** Protein digestion mein proteases ka role hai. Bile salts enzymes nahi hain.

**Exam clue:** Bile supports fat absorption.

</details>

### Q14. A patient with prolonged cholestasis is at increased risk of impaired absorption of which vitamin group?

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Priority:** High | **Topic:** Liver Function and Laboratory Tests | **Evidence:** Original practice

- **A.** B1, B2, B6 and B12 exclusively
- **B.** A, D, E and K
- **C.** C and folate only
- **D.** Niacin and pantothenic acid exclusively

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: B. A, D, E and K**

- **A.** Ye B vitamins water-soluble hain. Cholestasis se fat absorption ka characteristic issue inka nahi hai.
- **B.** A, D, E aur K fat-soluble vitamins hain. Intestine mein bile kam pahunchne par inka absorption ghat sakta hai.
- **C.** Vitamin C aur folate water-soluble hain. Inhe fat digestion ki wahi requirement nahi hoti.
- **D.** Niacin aur pantothenic acid bhi water-soluble hain, isliye ye characteristic group nahi hai.

**Exam clue:** ADEK are the fat-soluble vitamins.

</details>

### Q15. In a patient with chronic cholestasis and prolonged PT, improvement after prescribed parenteral vitamin K most strongly suggests what contributed to the abnormal result?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Priority:** High | **Topic:** Liver Function and Laboratory Tests | **Evidence:** Original practice

- **A.** Definite irreversible loss of all hepatic synthesis
- **B.** Excess platelet production
- **C.** Vitamin K deficiency from impaired fat absorption
- **D.** Isolated hemolysis

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: C. Vitamin K deficiency from impaired fat absorption**

- **A.** Irreversible severe synthesis failure mein vitamin K se correction limited ho sakta hai. Improvement us conclusion ko prove nahi karta.
- **B.** Platelet count aur vitamin K dependent coagulation factors alag components hain.
- **C.** Bile ki kami se vitamin K absorb kam ho sakta hai. Injection ke baad PT improve hona deficiency ke contribution ko support karta hai.
- **D.** RBC destruction bilirubin ko affect karta hai, vitamin K responsive PT ko seedha explain nahi karta.

**Exam clue:** Response to vitamin K can help distinguish causes of prolonged PT.

</details>

### Q17. After a prolonged hypotensive episode, a patient has a sudden marked aminotransferase rise. Which mechanism is particularly concerning?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Priority:** High | **Topic:** Liver Function and Laboratory Tests | **Evidence:** Original practice

- **A.** Hypoxic injury to hepatocytes
- **B.** Increased bile salt absorption
- **C.** Isolated dietary vitamin C deficiency
- **D.** Uncomplicated chronic gallbladder stones without obstruction

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: A. Hypoxic injury to hepatocytes**

- **A.** Low blood pressure se liver tak oxygen aur perfusion kam pahunch sakti hai. Isse acute hypoxic injury ho sakti hai.
- **B.** Bile salt absorption ka rise shock ke baad massive enzyme rise ka usual mechanism nahi hai.
- **C.** Vitamin C deficiency collagen aur healing ko affect karti hai. Sudden major enzyme rise explain nahi karti.
- **D.** Uncomplicated gallstones without obstruction is pattern ko adequately explain nahi karte.

**Exam clue:** Correlate severe enzyme changes with recent hemodynamics.

</details>

### Q19. A patient with acute hepatitis has new disorientation and INR 1.8 without known pre-existing cirrhosis. What is the appropriate nursing response?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Priority:** High | **Topic:** Liver Function and Laboratory Tests | **Evidence:** Original practice

- **A.** Wait for the albumin level to fall before acting
- **B.** Reassure because bilirubin is not provided
- **C.** Urgently escalate for possible acute liver failure
- **D.** Schedule a routine outpatient enzyme repeat only

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: C. Urgently escalate for possible acute liver failure**

- **A.** Albumin slow change karta hai. Iske fall ka wait karna dangerous delay ho sakta hai.
- **B.** Bilirubin ka result absent hona clotting aur mental status ke red flags ko remove nahi karta.
- **C.** Naya confusion aur elevated INR acute severe hepatic dysfunction ke red flags hain. Immediate assessment aur escalation zaroori hai.
- **D.** Routine follow-up unstable clinical situation ke liye appropriate nahi hai.

**Exam clue:** Acute liver injury plus coagulopathy and altered mentation is an emergency.

</details>

### Q20. Which anatomical vessel supplies the liver with most of its blood flow from the gastrointestinal tract?

**Difficulty:** Easy | **Priority:** High | **Topic:** Liver Function and Laboratory Tests | **Evidence:** Original practice

- **A.** Hepatic vein
- **B.** Inferior vena cava directly
- **C.** Common bile duct
- **D.** Portal vein

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: D. Portal vein**

- **A.** Hepatic veins liver se blood bahar vena cava mein le jaati hain.
- **B.** Vena cava systemic venous return carry karti hai, gut se direct major hepatic inflow nahi.
- **C.** Common bile duct bile transport karta hai, blood nahi.
- **D.** Portal vein intestine se nutrient-rich blood liver tak laati hai. Liver ke total blood flow ka major hissa yahin se aata hai.

**Exam clue:** Portal vein brings blood to the liver, hepatic veins drain it.

</details>
