### Q1. Which blood vessel carries venous blood from much of the small intestine into the portal venous system?

**Difficulty:** Easy · **Priority:** High · **Exam focus:** NORCET / HPRCA HP Staff Nurse / PGIMER · Original practice

- **A.** Superior mesenteric artery
- **B.** Renal vein
- **C.** Pulmonary artery
- **D.** Superior mesenteric vein

<details><summary>✅ Correct answer and detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: D. Superior mesenteric vein**

**A. Kyun galat hai:** Ye artery intestine tak arterial blood le jaati hai. Venous drainage vein ka kaam hai.

**B. Kyun galat hai:** Renal vein kidney se blood drain karti hai.

**C. Kyun galat hai:** Pulmonary artery right heart se blood lungs tak le jaati hai.

**D. Kyun sahi hai:** Superior mesenteric vein intestine se venous blood collect karti hai aur splenic vein ke saath portal circulation mein contribute karti hai.

**Exam clue:** Mesenteric venous blood enters portal circulation.

</details>

### Q2. What does a nonocclusive portal vein thrombus mean?

**Difficulty:** Easy · **Priority:** High · **Exam focus:** NORCET / HPRCA HP Staff Nurse / PGIMER · Original practice

- **A.** A clot that always occupies the entire lumen
- **B.** A clot that does not completely block the vessel lumen
- **C.** A clot located only inside a heart chamber
- **D.** A bile duct stone without vascular disease

<details><summary>✅ Correct answer and detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: B. A clot that does not completely block the vessel lumen**

**A. Kyun galat hai:** Complete blockage occlusive thrombus hai, nonocclusive nahi.

**B. Kyun sahi hai:** Nonocclusive clot mein lumen ka kuch part open reh sakta hai. Actual obstruction extent imaging se assess hota hai.

**C. Kyun galat hai:** Portal vein clot cardiac chamber mein located nahi hai.

**D. Kyun galat hai:** Bile duct stone duct lumen mein hota hai, thrombus blood vessel mein hota hai.

**Exam clue:** Nonocclusive means some lumen remains patent.

</details>

### Q3. Which chronic liver disorder is a common risk factor for portal vein thrombosis in adults?

**Difficulty:** Easy · **Priority:** High · **Exam focus:** NORCET / HPRCA HP Staff Nurse / PGIMER · Original practice

- **A.** Isolated myopia
- **B.** Simple external ear infection
- **C.** Cirrhosis
- **D.** Healed uncomplicated skin abrasion

<details><summary>✅ Correct answer and detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: C. Cirrhosis**

**A. Kyun galat hai:** Myopia vision refractive issue hai, portal thrombosis ka common risk factor nahi.

**B. Kyun galat hai:** External ear infection portal blood flow ka typical chronic risk factor nahi hai.

**C. Kyun sahi hai:** Cirrhosis mein altered portal blood flow aur hemostatic changes thrombosis risk badha sakte hain.

**D. Kyun galat hai:** Superficial healed abrasion portal thrombosis ka usual risk factor nahi hai.

**Exam clue:** Cirrhosis is a major clinical context for PVT.

</details>

### Q4. Which term describes an abnormal tendency of blood to form clots that can increase portal vein thrombosis risk?

**Difficulty:** Easy · **Priority:** High · **Exam focus:** NORCET / HPRCA HP Staff Nurse / PGIMER · Original practice

- **A.** Hypercoagulability
- **B.** Hyperventilation
- **C.** Hyperacusis
- **D.** Hyperopia

<details><summary>✅ Correct answer and detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: A. Hypercoagulability**

**A. Kyun sahi hai:** Hypercoagulability mein clot formation ki tendency badh sakti hai. Acquired aur inherited causes evaluate kiye ja sakte hain.

**B. Kyun galat hai:** Hyperventilation excessive ventilation hai, clotting tendency ka term nahi.

**C. Kyun galat hai:** Hyperacusis sound sensitivity hai.

**D. Kyun galat hai:** Hyperopia refractive vision issue hai.

**Exam clue:** Hypercoagulability means increased clotting tendency.

</details>

### Q5. Which imaging method can demonstrate the direction of blood flow within the portal vein?

**Difficulty:** Easy · **Priority:** High · **Exam focus:** NORCET / HPRCA HP Staff Nurse / PGIMER · Original practice

- **A.** Routine chest radiography
- **B.** Doppler ultrasonography
- **C.** Electrocardiography
- **D.** Bone densitometry

<details><summary>✅ Correct answer and detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: B. Doppler ultrasonography**

**A. Kyun galat hai:** Chest X-ray portal flow direction measure nahi karti.

**B. Kyun sahi hai:** Doppler ultrasound blood flow direction aur velocity ke baare mein information provide kar sakta hai. PVT mein flow assessment useful hai.

**C. Kyun galat hai:** ECG heart electrical activity record karta hai.

**D. Kyun galat hai:** Bone densitometry bone mineral density assess karti hai, portal flow nahi.

**Exam clue:** Doppler assesses flow as well as vessel anatomy.

</details>

### Q6. What does vessel recanalization mean during follow-up of a portal vein clot?

**Difficulty:** Easy · **Priority:** High · **Exam focus:** NORCET / HPRCA HP Staff Nurse / PGIMER · Original practice

- **A.** Removal of the gallbladder
- **B.** Loss of all circulating platelets
- **C.** Permanent closure of an initially open vessel
- **D.** Restoration of a channel for blood flow through a previously obstructed segment

<details><summary>✅ Correct answer and detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: D. Restoration of a channel for blood flow through a previously obstructed segment**

**A. Kyun galat hai:** Gallbladder removal cholecystectomy hai.

**B. Kyun galat hai:** Platelet loss recanalization ka meaning nahi hai.

**C. Kyun galat hai:** Ye occlusion describe karta hai, recanalization iska opposite process hai.

**D. Kyun sahi hai:** Recanalization mein obstructed segment mein blood flow ka path partly ya fully restore ho sakta hai. Imaging response document karne mein help karti hai.

**Exam clue:** Recanalization refers to reopening a vessel channel.

</details>
