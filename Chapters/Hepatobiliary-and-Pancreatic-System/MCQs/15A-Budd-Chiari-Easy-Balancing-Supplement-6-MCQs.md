### Q1. Which combination is classically associated with symptomatic Budd-Chiari syndrome?

**Difficulty:** Easy · **Priority:** High · **Exam focus:** NORCET / HPRCA HP Staff Nurse / PGIMER · Original practice

- **A.** Cough, wheeze and sore throat
- **B.** Abdominal pain, hepatomegaly and ascites
- **C.** Polyuria, polydipsia and polyphagia
- **D.** Ear pain, tinnitus and vertigo

<details><summary>✅ Correct answer and detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: B. Abdominal pain, hepatomegaly and ascites**

**A. Kyun galat hai:** Ye respiratory symptoms hain. Budd-Chiari ki classic abdominal triad nahi.

**B. Kyun sahi hai:** Venous outflow obstruction liver congestion aur portal pressure changes cause kar sakti hai. Pain, liver enlargement aur abdominal fluid classic combination hai, though not every patient has all three.

**C. Kyun galat hai:** Ye hyperglycemia se associated symptoms ho sakte hain, hepatic venous congestion ka classic triad nahi.

**D. Kyun galat hai:** Ye ear-related manifestations hain, Budd-Chiari ka characteristic triad nahi.

**Exam clue:** Remember the classic abdominal triad.

</details>

### Q2. Which large vein normally receives the hepatic veins before blood returns to the heart?

**Difficulty:** Easy · **Priority:** High · **Exam focus:** NORCET / HPRCA HP Staff Nurse / PGIMER · Original practice

- **A.** Superior mesenteric artery
- **B.** Pulmonary vein
- **C.** Inferior vena cava
- **D.** Carotid artery

<details><summary>✅ Correct answer and detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: C. Inferior vena cava**

**A. Kyun galat hai:** Ye artery intestine ko oxygenated blood supply karti hai.

**B. Kyun galat hai:** Pulmonary veins lungs se blood left atrium tak laati hain.

**C. Kyun sahi hai:** Hepatic veins blood inferior vena cava mein drain karti hain, jo right atrium ki taraf blood le jaati hai.

**D. Kyun galat hai:** Carotid arteries head aur brain ko blood supply karti hain.

**Exam clue:** Hepatic venous drainage enters the IVC.

</details>

### Q3. What does the term venous congestion mean in Budd-Chiari syndrome?

**Difficulty:** Easy · **Priority:** High · **Exam focus:** NORCET / HPRCA HP Staff Nurse / PGIMER · Original practice

- **A.** Increased bile production without blood-flow changes
- **B.** Loss of all arterial blood from the body
- **C.** Excess insulin release from the pancreas
- **D.** Accumulation of blood due to impaired venous drainage

<details><summary>✅ Correct answer and detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: D. Accumulation of blood due to impaired venous drainage**

**A. Kyun galat hai:** Bile production aur venous congestion alag processes hain.

**B. Kyun galat hai:** Congestion impaired drainage hai, complete arterial blood loss nahi.

**C. Kyun galat hai:** Insulin secretion blood pooling ka definition nahi hai.

**D. Kyun sahi hai:** Jab venous blood freely exit nahi kar paata, liver mein congestion develop ho sakti hai. Isse tissue pressure aur function affect ho sakte hain.

**Exam clue:** Congestion reflects impaired outflow.

</details>

### Q4. Which clinical time-course term describes Budd-Chiari syndrome developing gradually over several months?

**Difficulty:** Easy · **Priority:** High · **Exam focus:** NORCET / HPRCA HP Staff Nurse / PGIMER · Original practice

- **A.** Chronic Budd-Chiari syndrome
- **B.** Hyperacute allergic reaction
- **C.** Transient biliary colic
- **D.** Acute appendicitis

<details><summary>✅ Correct answer and detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: A. Chronic Budd-Chiari syndrome**

**A. Kyun sahi hai:** Chronic presentation gradually develop ho sakti hai aur initially mild symptoms ho sakte hain. Ongoing outflow obstruction fibrosis aur portal complications cause kar sakti hai.

**B. Kyun galat hai:** Hyperacute allergy rapid immune reaction hai, months-long hepatic obstruction nahi.

**C. Kyun galat hai:** Biliary colic gallstone-related pain episode hai, hepatic venous obstruction ka time course nahi.

**D. Kyun galat hai:** Appendicitis appendix inflammation hai aur typically short-duration presentation hoti hai.

**Exam clue:** Chronic disease develops gradually over time.

</details>

### Q5. Which investigation uses contrast to depict the hepatic venous lumen directly during selected invasive evaluation of Budd-Chiari syndrome?

**Difficulty:** Easy · **Priority:** High · **Exam focus:** NORCET / HPRCA HP Staff Nurse / PGIMER · Original practice

- **A.** Electroencephalography
- **B.** Spirometry
- **C.** Hepatic venography
- **D.** Electromyography

<details><summary>✅ Correct answer and detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: C. Hepatic venography**

**A. Kyun galat hai:** EEG brain electrical signals record karta hai, hepatic venous lumen nahi.

**B. Kyun galat hai:** Spirometry pulmonary function test hai.

**C. Kyun sahi hai:** Venography contrast imaging se hepatic veins ke lumen aur obstruction ki anatomy define karne mein help kar sakti hai. Invasive test specialist plan ke under hota hai.

**D. Kyun galat hai:** EMG muscles aur nerves ki electrical function assess karta hai, venous anatomy nahi.

**Exam clue:** Venography depicts veins.

</details>

### Q6. Which type of specialist commonly coordinates evaluation of a suspected hepatic venous outflow disorder?

**Difficulty:** Easy · **Priority:** High · **Exam focus:** NORCET / HPRCA HP Staff Nurse / PGIMER · Original practice

- **A.** Dermatologist alone
- **B.** Hepatologist
- **C.** Audiologist alone
- **D.** Ophthalmologist alone

<details><summary>✅ Correct answer and detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: B. Hepatologist**

**A. Kyun galat hai:** Dermatology skin disorders ka specialty hai. Hepatic vascular evaluation ka primary coordinator nahi.

**B. Kyun sahi hai:** Hepatologist liver diseases aur vascular liver complications evaluate karta hai. Hematology aur interventional radiology ki help bhi needed ho sakti hai.

**C. Kyun galat hai:** Audiologist hearing assess karta hai, hepatic venous disease nahi.

**D. Kyun galat hai:** Ophthalmologist eye diseases manage karta hai, liver vascular disorder nahi.

**Exam clue:** Hepatic vascular disease requires specialist liver care.

</details>
