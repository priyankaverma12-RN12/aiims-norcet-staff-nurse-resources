### Q1. In the revised Atlanta classification, what distinguishes a pancreatic pseudocyst from a true epithelial-lined cyst?

**Difficulty:** Easy · **Priority:** HIGH YIELD · Original practice

- **A.** It always has a malignant epithelial lining
- **B.** It consists exclusively of normal pancreatic acini
- **C.** It contains a complete gallbladder wall
- **D.** It has a mature inflammatory wall without an epithelial lining

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: D. It has a mature inflammatory wall without an epithelial lining**

**Option-wise explanation:**
- **A (Galat):** Malignant epithelial lining pseudocyst ki definition nahi hai.
- **B (Galat):** Normal acini fluid collection nahi banate.
- **C (Galat):** Gallbladder wall pancreatic pseudocyst ka defining tissue nahi hai.
- **D (Sahi):** Pseudocyst ke around inflammatory fibrous wall hoti hai, true epithelial lining nahi. Ye pancreatic injury ke baad fluid collection se develop ho sakti hai.

**Exam clue:** Pseudocyst lacks an epithelial lining.

</details>

### Q2. Ten days after interstitial edematous pancreatitis, CT shows homogeneous fluid near the pancreas without a defined wall or necrotic debris. What is the appropriate Atlanta term?

**Difficulty:** Medium · **Priority:** HIGH YIELD · Original practice

- **A.** Walled-off necrosis
- **B.** Acute peripancreatic fluid collection
- **C.** Mature pancreatic pseudocyst
- **D.** Pancreatic ductal adenocarcinoma

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: B. Acute peripancreatic fluid collection**

**Option-wise explanation:**
- **A (Galat):** WON mein encapsulated necrotic material hota hai.
- **B (Sahi):** Interstitial pancreatitis ke early phase mein wall absent aur fluid-only collection APFC kehlati hai. Timing aur contents classification guide karte hain.
- **C (Galat):** Mature pseudocyst usually about four weeks ke baad organized wall develop karti hai.
- **D (Galat):** Fluid-only collection cancer ka automatic diagnosis nahi hai.

**Exam clue:** Early fluid-only unencapsulated collection is APFC.

</details>

### Q4. Six weeks after interstitial pancreatitis, imaging shows an encapsulated homogeneous fluid collection without solid necrotic components. Which classification fits best?

</details>

### Q6. A stable patient with an asymptomatic mature pancreatic pseudocyst measures 7 cm on imaging, without infection or obstruction. Which management principle is appropriate?

**Option-wise explanation:**

**Exam clue:** Drain for clinical indications, not size alone.

### Q10. A clinically stable patient with symptomatic pancreatic necrosis is being considered for debridement during the first week. Why might the team delay debridement if feasible?

**Difficulty:** Difficult · **Priority:** HIGH YIELD · Original practice

- **B.** Make the collection malignant before surgery
- **C.** Allow necrosis to organize and mature toward a walled-off collection
- **D.** Wait for every laboratory value to become normal

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: C. Allow necrosis to organize and mature toward a walled-off collection**

**Option-wise explanation:**
- **A (Galat):** Enzyme absence delay ka goal nahi hai.
- **B (Galat):** Malignancy develop karna treatment goal nahi hai.

**Exam clue:** Avoid early debridement when clinical circumstances permit delay.
