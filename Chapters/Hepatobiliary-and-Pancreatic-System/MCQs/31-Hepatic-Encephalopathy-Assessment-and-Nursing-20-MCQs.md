### Q1. Which term describes the spectrum of neuropsychiatric dysfunction associated with liver insufficiency or portosystemic shunting?

**Difficulty:** 🟢 Easy · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD · Original practice

- **A.** Hepatic hydrothorax
- **B.** Hepatic encephalopathy
- **C.** Hepatorenal syndrome
- **D.** Cholangitis

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: B. Hepatic encephalopathy**

**Option-wise explanation:**
- **A (Galat):** Hydrothorax pleural fluid accumulation hai aur primarily breathing affect karti hai.
- **B (Sahi):** Hepatic encephalopathy mein liver clearance aur portosystemic shunting ke changes brain function affect karte hain. Severity subtle changes se coma tak ho sakti hai.
- **C (Galat):** Hepatorenal syndrome cirrhosis-associated kidney dysfunction ka syndrome hai.
- **D (Galat):** Cholangitis biliary tract infection hai, brain dysfunction ki definition nahi hai.

**Exam clue:** HE is a neuropsychiatric syndrome.
