### Q1. Which drug class directly inhibits hepatitis C viral replication during current oral treatment?

**Difficulty:** Easy · **Priority:** HIGH YIELD · **Exam focus:** NORCET / HPRCA HP Staff Nurse / PGIMER · Original practice

- **A.** Loop diuretics
- **B.** Beta blockers
- **C.** Direct-acting antivirals
- **D.** Bile acid sequestrants

<details><summary>✅ Correct answer and detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: C. Direct-acting antivirals**

**A. Kyun galat hai:** Diuretics fluid overload control karti hain, virus nahi.

**B. Kyun galat hai:** Beta blockers portal pressure ke liye useful ho sakte hain, antiviral nahi.

**C. Kyun sahi hai:** DAA viral proteins ko target karke HCV replication rokne mein help karte hain.

**D. Kyun galat hai:** Sequestrants bile acids bind karte hain, HCV proteins target nahi karte.

**Exam clue:** DAAs target HCV replication.

</details>

### Q3. A 60-year-old HCV patient has AST 80 U/L, ALT 64 U/L and platelets 150 times 10^9/L. Calculate FIB-4.

**Difficulty:** Difficult · **Priority:** HIGH YIELD · **Exam focus:** NORCET / HPRCA HP Staff Nurse / PGIMER · Original practice

- **A.** 0.4
- **B.** 1.5
- **C.** 8.0
- **D.** 4.0

<details><summary>✅ Correct answer and detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: D. 4.0**

**A. Kyun galat hai:** 0.4 tenfold lower hai.

**B. Kyun galat hai:** 1.5 formula se nahi milta.

**C. Kyun galat hai:** 8.0 denominator miscalculation hai.

**D. Kyun sahi hai:** Formula age times AST divided by platelets times square root ALT hai. 4800 divided by 1200 equals 4.0.

**Exam clue:** FIB-4 helps triage fibrosis, not confirm histology.

</details>
