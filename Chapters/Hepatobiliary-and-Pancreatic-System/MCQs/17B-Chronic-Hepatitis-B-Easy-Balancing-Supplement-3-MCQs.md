### Q1. What viral enzyme is targeted by nucleos(t)ide analogue medicines used in chronic hepatitis B?

**Difficulty:** Easy · **Priority:** High · **Exam focus:** NORCET / HPRCA HP Staff Nurse / PGIMER · Original practice

- **A.** Human gastric proton pump
- **B.** Bacterial cell wall transpeptidase
- **C.** HBV DNA polymerase/reverse transcriptase
- **D.** Human pancreatic lipase

<details><summary>✅ Correct answer and detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: C. HBV DNA polymerase/reverse transcriptase**

**A. Kyun galat hai:** Proton pump stomach acid secretion control karta hai, viral DNA replication nahi.

**B. Kyun galat hai:** Transpeptidase bacterial cell wall synthesis ka target hai, HBV ka nahi.

**C. Kyun sahi hai:** HBV DNA polymerase viral genetic material ki replication mein kaam karta hai. Nucleos(t)ide analogues is process ko inhibit karte hain.

**D. Kyun galat hai:** Pancreatic lipase fats digest karta hai. HBV antiviral therapy ka target nahi hai.

**Exam clue:** HBV uses reverse transcription during replication.

</details>

### Q2. Which blood test is a routine marker of hepatocyte injury during chronic HBV follow-up?

**Difficulty:** Easy · **Priority:** High · **Exam focus:** NORCET / HPRCA HP Staff Nurse / PGIMER · Original practice

- **A.** ALT
- **B.** Serum sodium alone
- **C.** Blood urea alone
- **D.** Serum calcium alone

<details><summary>✅ Correct answer and detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: A. ALT**

**A. Kyun sahi hai:** ALT hepatocytes se injury ke dauran blood mein release ho sakta hai. Serial ALT HBV activity ke assessment ka ek component hai.

**B. Kyun galat hai:** Sodium fluid balance ka marker hai, hepatocyte injury ka direct routine marker nahi.

**C. Kyun galat hai:** Urea renal clearance aur metabolism se related hai. ALT ki jagah liver inflammation assess nahi karta.

**D. Kyun galat hai:** Calcium bone aur mineral metabolism se related hai. HBV-related hepatocyte injury ke liye ALT relevant hai.

**Exam clue:** ALT reflects liver-cell injury, not viral quantity.

</details>

### Q3. Which ultrasound-based technique estimates liver stiffness during chronic HBV fibrosis assessment?

**Difficulty:** Easy · **Priority:** High · **Exam focus:** NORCET / HPRCA HP Staff Nurse / PGIMER · Original practice

- **A.** Echocardiographic ejection fraction
- **B.** Renal Doppler resistive index
- **C.** Carotid intima-media thickness
- **D.** Transient elastography

<details><summary>✅ Correct answer and detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: D. Transient elastography**

**A. Kyun galat hai:** Ejection fraction cardiac pumping function measure karta hai. Liver stiffness nahi.

**B. Kyun galat hai:** Renal resistive index kidney blood flow se related hai. Hepatic fibrosis assess nahi karta.

**C. Kyun galat hai:** Carotid thickness vascular risk assessment mein useful hai. Liver stiffness ka measure nahi hai.

**D. Kyun sahi hai:** Transient elastography liver tissue ki stiffness estimate karti hai. Fibrosis assessment mein clinical context ke saath interpret hoti hai.

**Exam clue:** Liver stiffness provides a noninvasive fibrosis estimate.

</details>
