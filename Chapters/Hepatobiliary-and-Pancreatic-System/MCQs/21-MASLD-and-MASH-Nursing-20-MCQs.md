### Q1. What does the term MASLD designate?

**Difficulty:** Easy · **Priority:** HIGH YIELD · **Exam focus:** NORCET / HPRCA HP Staff Nurse / PGIMER · Original practice

- **A.** Hepatic steatosis associated with cardiometabolic dysfunction under defined diagnostic criteria
- **B.** All acute viral hepatitis regardless of metabolic status
- **C.** An isolated gallstone without liver fat
- **D.** A congenital absence of the pancreas

<details><summary>✅ Correct answer and detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: A. Hepatic steatosis associated with cardiometabolic dysfunction under defined diagnostic criteria**

**A. Kyun sahi hai:** MASLD mein hepatic fat aur at least one cardiometabolic risk factor present hota hai, with alcohol and alternate causes assessed.

**B. Kyun galat hai:** Viral hepatitis alag etiology hai.

**C. Kyun galat hai:** Gallstone alone steatosis establish nahi karta.

**D. Kyun galat hai:** Pancreas absent hona MASLD definition nahi hai.

**Exam clue:** MASLD is the updated metabolic steatotic liver disease term.

</details>

**B. Kyun galat hai:** Cystic duct obstruction different biliary disorder hai.

### Q3. An adult with type 2 diabetes has normal aminotransferases. Which statement about MASLD-related fibrosis assessment is correct?

- **B.** Fibrosis assessment is contraindicated when enzymes are normal
- **C.** Diabetes protects against steatohepatitis
- **D.** Normal enzymes do not reliably exclude advanced fibrosis

<details><summary>✅ Correct answer and detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: D. Normal enzymes do not reliably exclude advanced fibrosis**

**A. Kyun galat hai:** Normal ALT steatosis rule out nahi karta.

**B. Kyun galat hai:** Risk assessment normal enzymes mein bhi useful hai.

**C. Kyun galat hai:** Diabetes metabolic liver disease ka risk factor hai.

### Q4. A stable 52-year-old with diabetes has FIB-4 of 1.1 and no other signs of advanced liver disease. What is the EASL 2024 risk-stratification principle?

- **C.** Immediate biopsy for every such result
- **D.** Automatic diagnosis of decompensated cirrhosis

<details><summary>✅ Correct answer and detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: B. Low-risk classification with periodic reassessment rather than permanent exclusion**

**A. Kyun galat hai:** Low risk zero lifetime risk nahi hai.

**B. Kyun sahi hai:** FIB-4 below 1.3 suitable adults mein low advanced-fibrosis risk indicate karta hai. Reassessment metabolic risk ke according plan hoti hai.

**C. Kyun galat hai:** Har low-risk result par biopsy necessary nahi.

**D. Kyun galat hai:** Decompensation clinical complications se assess hoti hai.

**Exam clue:** Low-risk FIB-4 still needs periodic review.

</details>
