### Q1. What structural change characterizes cirrhosis?

**Difficulty:** Easy | **Priority:** High | **Topic:** Cirrhosis, Portal Hypertension and Decompensation | **Evidence:** Original practice

- **A.** Diffuse fibrosis with regenerative nodules
- **B.** Transient hepatocyte swelling without scarring in every case
- **C.** Isolated pancreatic duct dilatation
- **D.** A single uncomplicated gallbladder polyp

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: A. Diffuse fibrosis with regenerative nodules**

- **A.** Cirrhosis mein normal liver architecture scar tissue aur regenerative nodules ki wajah se distort ho jaata hai.
- **B.** Temporary swelling acute injury mein ho sakti hai. Ye cirrhosis ka defining structural change nahi hai.
- **C.** Pancreatic duct pancreas ka part hai, liver fibrosis ko define nahi karta.
- **D.** Gallbladder polyp localized finding hai. Diffuse hepatic scarring alag condition hai.

**Exam clue:** Cirrhosis remodels the liver architecture.

</details>

### Q2. Why can chronic portal hypertension lead to a low platelet count?

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Priority:** High | **Topic:** Cirrhosis, Portal Hypertension and Decompensation | **Evidence:** Original practice

- **A.** Increased intestinal absorption of platelets
- **B.** Splenic sequestration from congestive splenomegaly
- **C.** Complete absence of platelet destruction in the spleen
- **D.** Increased hepatic bile acid synthesis alone

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: B. Splenic sequestration from congestive splenomegaly**

- **A.** Platelets intestine se absorb nahi hote, bone marrow mein bante hain.
- **B.** Portal pressure se spleen enlarge ho sakta hai. Wahan zyada platelets retain hone se circulating count kam ho sakta hai.
- **C.** Splenic sequestration aur turnover low count mein contribute karte hain. Complete absence wala statement fit nahi hota.
- **D.** Bile acid synthesis platelet sequestration ka direct mechanism nahi hai.

**Exam clue:** Low platelets can be a clue to portal hypertension.

</details>

### Q3. Which finding marks hepatic decompensation rather than compensated cirrhosis without complications?

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Priority:** High | **Topic:** Cirrhosis, Portal Hypertension and Decompensation | **Evidence:** Original practice

- **A.** Stable liver enzymes alone
- **B.** Normal appetite without symptoms
- **C.** New clinically apparent ascites
- **D.** An incidental uncomplicated liver cyst

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: C. New clinically apparent ascites**

- **A.** Stable enzymes alone se decompensation define nahi hota.
- **B.** Normal appetite se patient symptomatic decompensation establish nahi hota.
- **C.** Ascites yani abdomen mein abnormal fluid jama hona cirrhosis decompensation ka important event hai.
- **D.** Simple liver cyst cirrhosis decompensation ka defining event nahi hai.

**Exam clue:** Ascites, variceal bleeding and overt encephalopathy are major decompensation events.

</details>

### Q4. A patient with portal hypertension develops ascites despite reduced effective arterial circulation. Which renal response contributes to fluid retention?

- **A.** Complete suppression of aldosterone in every patient
- **B.** Obligatory massive sodium loss in urine
- **C.** Permanent absence of vasopressin release
- **D.** Activation of sodium-retaining neurohormonal pathways

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: D. Activation of sodium-retaining neurohormonal pathways**

- **A.** Aldosterone activity ascites mein badh sakti hai, universally suppress nahi hoti.
- **B.** Kidneys sodium retain kar sakti hain. Massive urinary loss usual mechanism nahi hai.
- **C.** Vasopressin water retention mein contribute kar sakta hai. Iski release permanently absent nahi hoti.
- **D.** Effective circulating volume kam lagne par body sodium retain karne wale pathways activate karti hai. Isse ascites aur badh sakta hai.

**Exam clue:** Ascites can coexist with effective arterial underfilling.

</details>

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Exam clue:** Consistency matters more than a single girth value.

</details>

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Exam clue:** New ascites in cirrhosis generally warrants diagnostic sampling.

### Q8. Ascitic fluid has 480 nucleated cells/mm³, of which 60% are neutrophils. What is the absolute neutrophil count?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Priority:** High | **Topic:** Cirrhosis, Portal Hypertension and Decompensation | **Evidence:** Original practice

- **A.** 80 cells/mm³
- **B.** 192 cells/mm³
- **C.** 480 cells/mm³
- **D.** 288 cells/mm³

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: D. 288 cells/mm³**

- **A.** 80 total ko galat divide karne se aa sakta hai. Percentage multiply karna hai.
- **B.** 192 non-neutrophil 40 percent hai, requested neutrophils nahi.
- **C.** 480 total nucleated cell count hai. Sab neutrophils nahi hain.
- **D.** 480 ka 60 percent 480 x 0.60 = 288 hota hai. Ye PMN count SBP threshold se upar hai, urgent clinical review chahiye.

**Exam clue:** Ascitic PMN count of at least 250/mm³ supports SBP.

</details>

### Q10. Serum albumin is 3.0 g/dL and ascitic albumin is 1.5 g/dL from contemporaneous samples. What is the serum-ascites albumin gradient?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Priority:** High | **Topic:** Cirrhosis, Portal Hypertension and Decompensation | **Evidence:** Original practice

- **A.** 4.5 g/dL
- **B.** 1.5 g/dL
- **C.** 2.0 g/dL
- **D.** 0.5 g/dL

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: B. 1.5 g/dL**

- **A.** 4.5 dono values add karne se milta hai. Gradient subtraction se calculate hota hai.
- **B.** SAAG serum albumin minus ascitic albumin hota hai. Yahan 3.0 minus 1.5 = 1.5 hai, jo portal hypertension related ascites ko support karta hai.
- **C.** 2.0 ratio hai, albumin gradient nahi.
- **D.** 0.5 correct difference nahi hai. Dono values ko same unit mein subtract karo.

**Exam clue:** SAAG at least 1.1 g/dL supports portal hypertension.

</details>

### Q11. During prescribed large-volume paracentesis, a patient becomes dizzy and hypotensive. What is the appropriate immediate response?

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Priority:** High | **Topic:** Cirrhosis, Portal Hypertension and Decompensation | **Evidence:** Original practice

- **A.** Encourage the patient to stand and walk immediately
- **B.** Ignore dizziness because fluid removal always lowers pressure safely
- **C.** Assess vital signs and perfusion, alert the treating team and follow procedure safety protocol
- **D.** Administer an extra unprescribed diuretic

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: C. Assess vital signs and perfusion, alert the treating team and follow procedure safety protocol**

- **A.** Standing se fall aur further hypotension ka risk badh sakta hai.
- **B.** Low BP ko normal assume karna unsafe hai. Assessment aur intervention ki zaroorat ho sakti hai.
- **C.** Dizziness aur low BP circulating volume ya perfusion problem ka sign ho sakta hai. Procedure team ko promptly alert karna chahiye.
- **D.** Extra diuretic volume depletion aur BP problem worsen kar sakta hai. Prescription ke bina nahi dena chahiye.

**Exam clue:** Monitor hemodynamics around fluid removal.

</details>

- **B.** Limit high-sodium packaged foods and use individualized dietitian advice

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: B. Limit high-sodium packaged foods and use individualized dietitian advice**

- **B.** Packaged salty foods sodium intake badhate hain. Individualized advice fluid control aur adequate nutrition dono maintain karne mein help karti hai.
- **C.** Salt drinks sodium intake badhate hain aur ascites management ke opposite ho sakte hain.

</details>

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

- **B.** Lactulose ka aim controlled soft stools hai, severe diarrhea nahi. Automatic escalation unsafe hai.

- **B.** Reduce intestinal bacterial contribution to ammonia and recurrent episodes

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>
