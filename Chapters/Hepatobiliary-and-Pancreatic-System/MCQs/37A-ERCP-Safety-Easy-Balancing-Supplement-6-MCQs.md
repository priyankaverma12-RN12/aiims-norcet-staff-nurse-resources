### Q1. What is the full form of ERCP?

**Difficulty:** 🟢 Easy · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD · Original practice

- **A.** Endoscopic renal contrast perfusion
- **B.** External respiratory cardiac pacing
- **C.** Endoscopic retrograde cholangiopancreatography
- **D.** Endoscopic retrograde colon polypectomy

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: C. Endoscopic retrograde cholangiopancreatography**

**Option-wise explanation:**
- **A (Galat):** Renal contrast perfusion iska accepted full form nahi hai.
- **B (Galat):** Cardiac pacing heart rhythm management se related hai.
- **C (Sahi):** ERCP endoscopy aur X-ray contrast imaging combine karke biliary aur pancreatic ducts ka assessment aur treatment kar sakti hai.
- **D (Galat):** Colon polypectomy different endoscopic procedure hai.

**Exam clue:** ERCP stands for endoscopic retrograde cholangiopancreatography.

</details>

- **C.** Bone densitometry

### Q3. To which part of the gastrointestinal tract is the conventional ERCP endoscope usually advanced to reach the major papilla?

**Difficulty:** 🟢 Easy · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD · Original practice

- **A.** Duodenum
- **B.** Terminal ileum
- **C.** Sigmoid colon
- **D.** Rectum

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: A. Duodenum**

**Option-wise explanation:**
- **A (Sahi):** Major duodenal papilla usually second part of duodenum mein hoti hai. Conventional scope mouth se stomach cross karke duodenum tak jaata hai.
- **B (Galat):** Terminal ileum small intestine ka distal end hai.
- **C (Galat):** Sigmoid colon large intestine ka segment hai.
- **D (Galat):** Rectum distal bowel hai, routine ERCP access nahi.

**Exam clue:** The major papilla is usually in the duodenum.

</details>

### Q4. Why should possible pregnancy be disclosed to the ERCP team before a procedure?

**Difficulty:** 🟢 Easy · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD · Original practice

- **A.** Pregnancy makes every endoscopic procedure automatically impossible
- **B.** Fluoroscopy involves ionizing radiation and the team must assess risk and necessity
- **C.** Pregnancy eliminates the need for sedation assessment
- **D.** Pregnancy guarantees no biliary obstruction

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: B. Fluoroscopy involves ionizing radiation and the team must assess risk and necessity**

**Option-wise explanation:**
- **A (Galat):** Necessary procedures pregnancy mein individualized precautions ke saath possible ho sakti hain.
- **B (Sahi):** Pregnancy status radiation exposure aur procedure planning ke liye relevant hai. Specialist urgent indication aur fetal risk balance karta hai.
- **C (Galat):** Sedation safety review still needed hai.
- **D (Galat):** Pregnancy biliary disease se protection guarantee nahi karti.

**Exam clue:** Communicate possible pregnancy before fluoroscopy.

</details>

### Q5. Which endoscopic accessory can be used to capture and extract a common bile duct stone during therapeutic ERCP?

**Difficulty:** 🟢 Easy · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD · Original practice

- **A.** Biopsy forceps intended only for mucosal sampling
- **B.** Injection needle for submucosal therapy
- **C.** Esophageal dilator
- **D.** Stone retrieval basket

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: D. Stone retrieval basket**

**Option-wise explanation:**
- **A (Galat):** Mucosal biopsy forceps tissue samples collect karne ke liye design hoti hain, routine CBD stone extraction accessory nahi hain.
- **B (Galat):** Injection needle fluid ya medicine deliver karti hai, duct stone ko capture nahi karti.
- **C (Galat):** Esophageal dilator narrowing expand karne ke liye use ho sakta hai, CBD stone capture ke liye nahi.
- **D (Sahi):** Retrieval basket selected duct stones ko capture karke extraction mein help karta hai. Endoscopist stone size aur anatomy ke hisaab se basket, extraction balloon ya other suitable technique choose karta hai.

**Exam clue:** A stone retrieval basket is a therapeutic ERCP accessory.

</details>

### Q6. Which device may be placed between the teeth during oral ERCP to protect the endoscope and mouth?

**Difficulty:** 🟢 Easy · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD · Original practice

- **A.** Nasal cannula inserted between the teeth
- **B.** Urinary catheter
- **C.** Bite block
- **D.** Abdominal drain

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: C. Bite block**

**Option-wise explanation:**
- **A (Galat):** Nasal cannula oxygen delivery ke liye nostrils par use hoti hai.
- **B (Galat):** Urinary catheter bladder drainage device hai.
- **C (Sahi):** Bite block mouth mein scope passage facilitate aur teeth or instrument damage risk reduce karne mein help karta hai. Team appropriate placement verify karti hai.
- **D (Galat):** Abdominal drain fluid collection drain karta hai, oral protection nahi.

**Exam clue:** A bite block protects teeth and the scope.

</details>
