### Q1. What is the primary mechanism of autoimmune hepatitis (AIH)?

**Difficulty:** Easy · **Priority:** HIGH YIELD · **Exam focus:** NORCET / HPRCA HP Staff Nurse / PGIMER · Original practice

- **A.** Immune-mediated inflammation of hepatocytes
- **B.** Mechanical obstruction by a cystic duct stone
- **C.** Exclusive bacterial infection of the bile ducts
- **D.** Failure of pancreatic enzyme secretion

<details><summary>✅ Correct answer and detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: A. Immune-mediated inflammation of hepatocytes**

**A. Kyun sahi hai:** AIH mein immune system liver cells par inappropriate attack karta hai. Is inflammation se hepatocytes damage hote hain aur untreated disease fibrosis tak progress kar sakti hai.

**B. Kyun galat hai:** Cystic duct stone gallbladder drainage block karta hai. AIH ka primary mechanism mechanical obstruction nahi hai.

**C. Kyun galat hai:** Bacterial cholangitis infection hai. AIH immune-mediated hepatitis hai.

**D. Kyun galat hai:** Pancreatic exocrine insufficiency digestion affect karti hai. Ye AIH ka mechanism nahi hai.

**Exam clue:** Autoimmune hepatitis primarily targets hepatocytes.

</details>

**D. Kyun galat hai:** Fecal elastase pancreatic exocrine function assess karta hai.

**Exam clue:** Hepatocellular injury plus IgG elevation supports AIH suspicion.
