### Q1. Which autoantibody is characteristically associated with type 2 autoimmune hepatitis?

**Difficulty:** Easy · **Priority:** High · **Exam focus:** NORCET / HPRCA HP Staff Nurse / PGIMER · Original practice

- **A.** Anti-LKM1
- **B.** Anti-HBs alone
- **C.** Anti-HAV IgM
- **D.** Anti-HCV alone

<details><summary>✅ Correct answer and detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: A. Anti-LKM1**

**A. Kyun sahi hai:** Anti-LKM1 liver-kidney microsomal antigen ke against antibody hai aur type 2 AIH ko support kar sakti hai.

**B. Kyun galat hai:** Anti-HBs HBV immunity se related marker hai.

**C. Kyun galat hai:** Anti-HAV IgM recent hepatitis A infection ko support karta hai.

**D. Kyun galat hai:** Anti-HCV hepatitis C exposure ka marker hai, type 2 AIH ka characteristic antibody nahi.

**Exam clue:** Anti-LKM1 is associated with type 2 AIH.

</details>

### Q2. What does the abbreviation ANA mean in an autoimmune hepatitis workup?

**Difficulty:** Easy · **Priority:** High · **Exam focus:** NORCET / HPRCA HP Staff Nurse / PGIMER · Original practice

- **A.** Antiviral nucleoside analogue
- **B.** Acute neutrophil activation
- **C.** Antinuclear antibody
- **D.** Arterial nitrogen assessment

<details><summary>✅ Correct answer and detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: C. Antinuclear antibody**

**A. Kyun galat hai:** Ye antiviral medicines ka class hai, ANA ka expansion nahi.

**B. Kyun galat hai:** Ye ANA laboratory abbreviation ka accepted meaning nahi hai.

**C. Kyun sahi hai:** ANA cell nucleus ke components ke against antibody ko refer karta hai. Ye AIH ke assessment mein supportive ho sakti hai, specific proof nahi.

**D. Kyun galat hai:** Ye autoimmune antibody test nahi hai.

**Exam clue:** ANA means antinuclear antibody.

</details>

### Q3. Which term describes an antibody directed against the body's own tissue antigens?

**Difficulty:** Easy · **Priority:** High · **Exam focus:** NORCET / HPRCA HP Staff Nurse / PGIMER · Original practice

- **A.** Allergen
- **B.** Antibiotic
- **C.** Enzyme inhibitor
- **D.** Autoantibody

<details><summary>✅ Correct answer and detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: D. Autoantibody**

**A. Kyun galat hai:** Allergen allergy trigger karne wala antigen hai, antibody nahi.

**B. Kyun galat hai:** Antibiotic bacteria ke treatment mein use hone wali medicine hai.

**C. Kyun galat hai:** Enzyme inhibitor enzyme activity reduce karta hai. Autoantibody ki definition ye nahi hai.

**D. Kyun sahi hai:** Autoantibody body ke apne antigen ko target karti hai. AIH mein aise markers immune-mediated disease assess karne mein help karte hain.

**Exam clue:** Auto means self.

</details>

### Q4. What is the general meaning of remission during autoimmune hepatitis treatment?

**Difficulty:** Easy · **Priority:** High · **Exam focus:** NORCET / HPRCA HP Staff Nurse / PGIMER · Original practice

- **A.** Every antibody has necessarily disappeared
- **B.** Disease activity has become controlled
- **C.** A new infection is confirmed
- **D.** The liver has been surgically removed

<details><summary>✅ Correct answer and detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: B. Disease activity has become controlled**

**A. Kyun galat hai:** Remission ka matlab sab autoantibodies ka disappear hona zaroori nahi hai.

**B. Kyun sahi hai:** Remission mein disease activity reduce ya control ho sakti hai. Ye treatment permanently unnecessary hone ka proof nahi hai.

**C. Kyun galat hai:** Infection remission ka meaning nahi hai.

**D. Kyun galat hai:** Liver removal operation hai, remission nahi.

**Exam clue:** Remission means disease control, not necessarily cure.

</details>

### Q5. Which term describes renewed autoimmune hepatitis activity after a period of control?

**Difficulty:** Easy · **Priority:** High · **Exam focus:** NORCET / HPRCA HP Staff Nurse / PGIMER · Original practice

- **A.** Relapse
- **B.** Incubation
- **C.** Metastasis
- **D.** Hemolysis

<details><summary>✅ Correct answer and detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: A. Relapse**

**A. Kyun sahi hai:** Relapse mein controlled disease dobara active ho jaati hai. Follow-up tests aur symptoms se recurrence evaluate karte hain.

**B. Kyun galat hai:** Incubation infection exposure aur symptom onset ke beech ka interval hai.

**C. Kyun galat hai:** Metastasis malignant cells ke spread ko describe karta hai.

**D. Kyun galat hai:** Hemolysis red cells ka breakdown hai.

**Exam clue:** Relapse is return of disease activity.

</details>

### Q6. Which characteristic microscopic liver injury pattern is commonly described in autoimmune hepatitis?

**Difficulty:** Easy · **Priority:** High · **Exam focus:** NORCET / HPRCA HP Staff Nurse / PGIMER · Original practice

- **A.** Glomerular crescents
- **B.** Villous atrophy of small bowel alone
- **C.** Interface hepatitis
- **D.** Alveolar consolidation

<details><summary>✅ Correct answer and detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: C. Interface hepatitis**

**A. Kyun galat hai:** Ye kidney glomerular disease ki histology ho sakti hai.

**B. Kyun galat hai:** Villous atrophy intestinal mucosal finding hai, liver histology nahi.

**C. Kyun sahi hai:** Interface hepatitis mein portal area aur surrounding hepatic tissue ke border par inflammatory injury hoti hai. Histology ko other findings ke saath interpret karte hain.

**D. Kyun galat hai:** Ye lung pathology hai, AIH tissue pattern nahi.

**Exam clue:** Interface hepatitis is a characteristic but not exclusive AIH finding.

</details>
