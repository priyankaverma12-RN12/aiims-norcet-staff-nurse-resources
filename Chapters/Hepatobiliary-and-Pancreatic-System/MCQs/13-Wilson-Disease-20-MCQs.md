### Q1. Which inheritance pattern is typical of Wilson disease?

**Difficulty:** Easy · **Priority:** HIGH YIELD · **Exam focus:** NORCET / HPRCA HP Staff Nurse / PGIMER · Original practice

- **A.** Autosomal dominant
- **B.** X-linked dominant
- **C.** Autosomal recessive
- **D.** Mitochondrial

<details><summary>✅ Correct answer and detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: C. Autosomal recessive**

**A. Kyun galat hai:** Single changed copy wala dominant pattern typical nahi hai.

**B. Kyun galat hai:** ATP7B autosomal gene hai, X chromosome par nahi.

**C. Kyun sahi hai:** Dono ATP7B copies mein pathogenic changes hone par disease develop ho sakti hai. Parents carrier ho sakte hain.

**D. Kyun galat hai:** Mitochondrial maternal transmission iska usual pattern nahi hai.

**Exam clue:** Wilson disease is autosomal recessive.

</details>

### Q2. Which primary hepatic transport defect causes copper retention in Wilson disease?

**Difficulty:** Medium · **Priority:** HIGH YIELD · **Exam focus:** NORCET / HPRCA HP Staff Nurse / PGIMER · Original practice

- **A.** Impaired ATP7B-mediated biliary copper excretion
- **B.** Increased renal sodium filtration
- **C.** Deficient intestinal iron absorption
- **D.** Excess pancreatic bicarbonate secretion

<details><summary>✅ Correct answer and detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: A. Impaired ATP7B-mediated biliary copper excretion**

**A. Kyun sahi hai:** ATP7B copper ko bile mein excrete karne mein help karta hai. Defect se copper hepatocytes mein accumulate hota hai.

**B. Kyun galat hai:** Sodium filtration copper transporter defect explain nahi karti.

**C. Kyun galat hai:** Iron absorption aur copper excretion alag pathways hain.

**D. Kyun galat hai:** Pancreatic bicarbonate acid neutralize karta hai, copper elimination ka primary mechanism nahi.

**Exam clue:** Copper excretion into bile is impaired.

</details>

- **C.** 50%

<details><summary>✅ Correct answer and detailed explanation</summary>
