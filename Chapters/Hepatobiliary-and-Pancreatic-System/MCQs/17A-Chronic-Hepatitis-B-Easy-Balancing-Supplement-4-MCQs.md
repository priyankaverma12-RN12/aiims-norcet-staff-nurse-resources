### Q1. What does HBeAg stand for in chronic hepatitis B monitoring?

**Difficulty:** Easy · **Priority:** High · **Exam focus:** NORCET / HPRCA HP Staff Nurse / PGIMER · Original practice

- **A.** Hepatitis B e antigen
- **B.** Hepatitis B envelope antibody
- **C.** Hepatitis E blood antigen
- **D.** Hepatic enzyme bilirubin antigen

<details><summary>✅ Correct answer and detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: A. Hepatitis B e antigen**

**A. Kyun sahi hai:** HBeAg hepatitis B viral protein marker hai. Isse HBV replication ke pattern ki information mil sakti hai, lekin HBV DNA bhi important hai.

**B. Kyun galat hai:** HBeAg antigen hai, antibody nahi.

**C. Kyun galat hai:** HBeAg HBV marker hai, hepatitis E ka marker nahi.

**D. Kyun galat hai:** Ye accepted viral marker ka expansion nahi hai.

**Exam clue:** HBeAg is an antigen, not an antibody.

</details>

### Q2. What does the term HBeAg seroconversion usually mean in a previously HBeAg-positive person with hepatitis B?

**Difficulty:** Easy · **Priority:** High · **Exam focus:** NORCET / HPRCA HP Staff Nurse / PGIMER · Original practice

- **A.** Loss of serum creatinine with appearance of albumin
- **B.** Appearance of hepatitis A IgM alone
- **C.** Loss of HBeAg with development of anti-HBe
- **D.** Conversion of bilirubin into HBV DNA

<details><summary>✅ Correct answer and detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: C. Loss of HBeAg with development of anti-HBe**

**A. Kyun galat hai:** Creatinine aur albumin viral e antigen-antibody transition ke markers nahi hain.

**B. Kyun galat hai:** HAV IgM different viral infection se related hai.

**C. Kyun sahi hai:** Seroconversion mein e antigen disappear hota hai aur e antibody appear ho sakti hai. Ye virological course ka marker hai, complete cure ka automatic proof nahi.

**D. Kyun galat hai:** Bilirubin pigment hai aur viral DNA genetic material hai. Ye conversion nahi hoti.

**Exam clue:** Distinguish e antigen seroconversion from cure.

</details>

### Q3. Which kind of medication supply record helps a nurse identify potential interruptions in long-term hepatitis B treatment?

**Difficulty:** Easy · **Priority:** High · **Exam focus:** NORCET / HPRCA HP Staff Nurse / PGIMER · Original practice

- **A.** The patient's shoe size chart
- **B.** A record of visual field testing
- **C.** A record of blood group alone
- **D.** Dispensing and refill dates

<details><summary>✅ Correct answer and detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: D. Dispensing and refill dates**

**A. Kyun galat hai:** Shoe size treatment supply track nahi karta.

**B. Kyun galat hai:** Visual field assessment HBV medication supply ka evidence nahi hai.

**C. Kyun galat hai:** Blood group treatment adherence ka record nahi hai.

**D. Kyun sahi hai:** Refill dates missed supply periods identify karne mein help karti hain. Patient se actual use respectfully confirm karna bhi zaroori hai.

**Exam clue:** Refill gaps can reveal medication access problems.

</details>

### Q4. Which unit is commonly reported for quantitative HBV DNA viral load in standardized laboratory reports?

**Difficulty:** Easy · **Priority:** High · **Exam focus:** NORCET / HPRCA HP Staff Nurse / PGIMER · Original practice

- **A.** Beats/minute
- **B.** IU/mL
- **C.** mmHg
- **D.** kg/m²

<details><summary>✅ Correct answer and detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: B. IU/mL**

**A. Kyun galat hai:** Ye pulse rate unit hai.

**B. Kyun sahi hai:** International units per milliliter viral load quantify karne ka common standardized unit hai. Trend same method ke context mein interpret karte hain.

**C. Kyun galat hai:** Ye pressure unit hai.

**D. Kyun galat hai:** Ye BMI unit hai, viral load measurement nahi.

**Exam clue:** Read the viral-load unit correctly.

</details>
