### Q1. What is the principal disease process in autoimmune pancreatitis (AIP)?

**Difficulty:** 🟢 Easy · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD · Original practice

- **A.** Bacterial invasion of pancreatic ducts in every case
- **B.** Immune-mediated inflammation of pancreatic tissue
- **C.** Mechanical pancreatic laceration
- **D.** Inherited absence of the pancreas

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: B. Immune-mediated inflammation of pancreatic tissue**

**Option-wise explanation:**
- **A (Galat):** Bacterial infection pancreatitis ki complication ho sakti hai, lekin AIP mein primary problem immune inflammation hai.
- **B (Sahi):** Immune system pancreas ki tissue ko target kar sakta hai aur inflammation produce karta hai. Isi mechanism ki wajah se AIP ko immune-mediated disease kehte hain.
- **C (Galat):** Laceration trauma se hoti hai. AIP ka diagnosis injury history par based nahi hota.
- **D (Galat):** Pancreas absent hona developmental condition hai, jabki AIP mein existing tissue inflamed hoti hai.

**Exam clue:** Autoimmune ka matlab immune-mediated tissue inflammation hai.

### Q2. Type 1 autoimmune pancreatitis belongs to which systemic disease spectrum?

- **B.** Systemic sclerosis exclusively
- **C.** IgG4-related disease
- **D.** Antiphospholipid syndrome

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: C. IgG4-related disease**

**Option-wise explanation:**
- **A (Galat):** IgA vasculitis small vessels mein IgA-associated inflammation hai. Yeh type 1 AIP ki classification nahi hai.

**Exam clue:** Type 1 AIP ko IgG4-related disease se jodo.
