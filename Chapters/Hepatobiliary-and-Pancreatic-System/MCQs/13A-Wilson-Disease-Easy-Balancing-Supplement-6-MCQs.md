### Q1. Which trace element accumulates to harmful levels in Wilson disease?

**Difficulty:** Easy · **Priority:** High · **Exam focus:** NORCET / HPRCA HP Staff Nurse / PGIMER · Original practice

- **A.** Iodine
- **B.** Fluoride
- **C.** Copper
- **D.** Selenium

<details><summary>✅ Correct answer and detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: C. Copper**

**A. Kyun galat hai:** Iodine thyroid hormone synthesis ke liye zaroori hai, Wilson disease ka accumulated metal nahi.

**B. Kyun galat hai:** Fluoride teeth aur bone mineralization se related hai.

**C. Kyun sahi hai:** Wilson disease mein copper excretion impaired hoti hai. Excess copper liver, brain aur other organs mein deposit ho sakta hai.

**D. Kyun galat hai:** Selenium antioxidant enzymes mein role rakhta hai. Wilson disease copper metabolism ka disorder hai.

**Exam clue:** Wilson disease is a copper storage disorder.

</details>

### Q2. Which plasma protein carries most circulating copper under normal physiological conditions?

**Difficulty:** Easy · **Priority:** High · **Exam focus:** NORCET / HPRCA HP Staff Nurse / PGIMER · Original practice

- **A.** Ceruloplasmin
- **B.** Transferrin
- **C.** Fibrinogen
- **D.** Hemoglobin

<details><summary>✅ Correct answer and detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: A. Ceruloplasmin**

**A. Kyun sahi hai:** Ceruloplasmin blood mein copper transport karta hai. Wilson disease mein iska level often low ho sakta hai, lekin isolated level diagnosis confirm nahi karta.

**B. Kyun galat hai:** Transferrin mainly iron transport karti hai.

**C. Kyun galat hai:** Fibrinogen clot formation mein role rakhta hai.

**D. Kyun galat hai:** Hemoglobin red cells mein oxygen carry karta hai.

**Exam clue:** Ceruloplasmin is the principal circulating copper carrier.

</details>

### Q3. Which term means sustained or intermittent involuntary muscle contractions that can cause abnormal postures in neurological Wilson disease?

**Difficulty:** Easy · **Priority:** High · **Exam focus:** NORCET / HPRCA HP Staff Nurse / PGIMER · Original practice

- **A.** Dysuria
- **B.** Dystonia
- **C.** Diplopia
- **D.** Dyspepsia

<details><summary>✅ Correct answer and detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: B. Dystonia**

**A. Kyun galat hai:** Dysuria painful urination hai, movement disorder nahi.

**B. Kyun sahi hai:** Dystonia mein involuntary muscle contractions se twisting ya abnormal posture develop ho sakta hai. Wilson disease brain ko affect karne par ye manifestation de sakti hai.

**C. Kyun galat hai:** Diplopia double vision ko kehte hain.

**D. Kyun galat hai:** Dyspepsia indigestion ke symptoms describe karti hai.

**Exam clue:** Dystonia is a movement disorder.

</details>

### Q4. Which term describes difficulty articulating speech due to impaired motor control in neurological Wilson disease?

**Difficulty:** Easy · **Priority:** High · **Exam focus:** NORCET / HPRCA HP Staff Nurse / PGIMER · Original practice

- **A.** Dysphonia alone
- **B.** Dysuria
- **C.** Dysmenorrhea
- **D.** Dysarthria

<details><summary>✅ Correct answer and detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: D. Dysarthria**

**A. Kyun galat hai:** Dysphonia voice quality ka disorder hai. Speech articulation problem ko dysarthria kehte hain.

**B. Kyun galat hai:** Dysuria urinary discomfort hai.

**C. Kyun galat hai:** Dysmenorrhea painful menstruation hai.

**D. Kyun sahi hai:** Dysarthria mein speech muscles ka motor control affected hota hai. Patient ki speech unclear ya slurred ho sakti hai.

**Exam clue:** Dysarthria concerns speech motor control.

</details>

### Q5. Which body system may develop psychiatric and behavioral manifestations from copper deposition in Wilson disease?

**Difficulty:** Easy · **Priority:** High · **Exam focus:** NORCET / HPRCA HP Staff Nurse / PGIMER · Original practice

- **A.** Central nervous system
- **B.** Hair follicle system alone
- **C.** External ear canal alone
- **D.** Tooth enamel alone

<details><summary>✅ Correct answer and detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: A. Central nervous system**

**A. Kyun sahi hai:** Copper brain tissue mein accumulate ho sakta hai aur mood, behavior ya movement affect kar sakta hai. Psychiatric symptoms ko medical assessment mein include karna important hai.

**B. Kyun galat hai:** Hair follicles behavior control nahi karte hain.

**C. Kyun galat hai:** Ear canal psychiatric functioning ka control center nahi hai.

**D. Kyun galat hai:** Tooth enamel psychiatric manifestations explain nahi karta.

**Exam clue:** Wilson disease can involve the brain as well as the liver.

</details>

### Q6. Which type of specialist can help assess new involuntary movements in a patient with Wilson disease?

**Difficulty:** Easy · **Priority:** High · **Exam focus:** NORCET / HPRCA HP Staff Nurse / PGIMER · Original practice

- **A.** Dentist alone
- **B.** Optician alone
- **C.** Neurologist
- **D.** Podiatrist alone

<details><summary>✅ Correct answer and detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: C. Neurologist**

**A. Kyun galat hai:** Dentist oral health assess karta hai, systemic movement disorder ka primary specialist nahi.

**B. Kyun galat hai:** Optician visual correction support karta hai, neurological motor symptoms assess nahi karta.

**C. Kyun sahi hai:** Neurologist tremor, dystonia aur other movement problems assess kar sakta hai. Hepatology team ke saath coordinated care useful hai.

**D. Kyun galat hai:** Podiatry foot health se related hai. Whole-body movement disorder neurological assessment require kar sakta hai.

**Exam clue:** Neurohepatic disease needs coordinated neurological care.

</details>
