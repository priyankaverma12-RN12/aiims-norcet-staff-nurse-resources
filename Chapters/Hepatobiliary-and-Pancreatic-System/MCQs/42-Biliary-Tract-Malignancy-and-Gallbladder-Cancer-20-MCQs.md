### Q1. What is the alternative name for a perihilar cholangiocarcinoma?

- **D.** Germ cell tumor

**Correct: C. Klatskin tumor**

**Option-wise explanation:**

**Exam clue:** Klatskin means perihilar cholangiocarcinoma.
