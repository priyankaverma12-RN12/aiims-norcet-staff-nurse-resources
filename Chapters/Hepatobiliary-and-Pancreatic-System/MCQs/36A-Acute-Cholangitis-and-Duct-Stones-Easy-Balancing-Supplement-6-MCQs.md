### Q1. Which two ducts normally unite to form the common bile duct?

**Difficulty:** 🟢 Easy · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD · Original practice

- **A.** Right and left hepatic ducts directly
- **B.** Common hepatic duct and cystic duct
- **C.** Main pancreatic duct and cystic duct
- **D.** Right hepatic duct and main pancreatic duct

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: B. Common hepatic duct and cystic duct**

**Option-wise explanation:**
- **A (Galat):** Right aur left hepatic ducts normally common hepatic duct banati hain.
- **B (Sahi):** Common hepatic duct cystic duct se join karke CBD banati hai. Biliary anatomy mein variants possible hain.
- **C (Galat):** Pancreatic duct usually distal biliary drainage region mein join karti hai, cystic duct ke saath CBD nahi banati.
- **D (Galat):** Right hepatic duct directly main pancreatic duct se CBD nahi banati.

**Exam clue:** Common hepatic duct plus cystic duct forms CBD.

</details>

### Q2. Which anatomical structure is primarily inflamed in acute cholangitis rather than cholecystitis?

**Difficulty:** 🟢 Easy · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD · Original practice

- **A.** Bile ducts
- **B.** Gallbladder wall
- **C.** Pancreatic acinar tissue
- **D.** Appendix

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: A. Bile ducts**

**Option-wise explanation:**
- **A (Sahi):** Cholangitis bile duct inflammation or infection hai. Obstruction commonly underlying problem hoti hai.
- **B (Galat):** Gallbladder wall inflammation cholecystitis kehlati hai.
- **C (Galat):** Pancreatic inflammation pancreatitis hai.
- **D (Galat):** Appendix inflammation appendicitis hai.

**Exam clue:** Cholangitis affects bile ducts.

</details>

### Q3. Which term refers to stones located within intrahepatic bile ducts?

**Difficulty:** 🟢 Easy · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD · Original practice

- **A.** Nephrolithiasis
- **B.** Ureterolithiasis
- **C.** Hepatolithiasis
- **D.** Sialolithiasis

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: C. Hepatolithiasis**

**Option-wise explanation:**
- **A (Galat):** Nephrolithiasis kidney stones ko refer karti hai.
- **B (Galat):** Ureterolithiasis ureteric stones hai.
- **C (Sahi):** Hepatolithiasis liver ke andar bile ducts mein stones ko refer karti hai. Recurrent biliary infection possible complication hai.
- **D (Galat):** Sialolithiasis salivary gland or duct stones hai.

**Exam clue:** Hepatolithiasis means intrahepatic duct stones.

</details>

### Q4. Which term describes reduced or obstructed flow of bile?

**Difficulty:** 🟢 Easy · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD · Original practice

- **A.** Hemostasis
- **B.** Diuresis
- **C.** Peristalsis
- **D.** Cholestasis

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: D. Cholestasis**

**Option-wise explanation:**
- **A (Galat):** Hemostasis bleeding control ka process hai.
- **B (Galat):** Diuresis increased urine production se related hai.
- **C (Galat):** Peristalsis coordinated muscular propulsion hai.
- **D (Sahi):** Cholestasis bile formation ya flow impair hone ko describe karta hai. Duct stone extrahepatic obstruction cause kar sakta hai.

**Exam clue:** Cholestasis is impaired bile flow.

</details>

### Q5. What is the clinical meaning of rigors reported by a patient with suspected cholangitis?

**Difficulty:** 🟢 Easy · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD · Original practice

- **A.** Episodes of pronounced involuntary shivering
- **B.** Persistent painless jaundice alone
- **C.** Complete loss of urine output
- **D.** Slow painless abdominal swelling

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: A. Episodes of pronounced involuntary shivering**

**Option-wise explanation:**
- **A (Sahi):** Rigors severe shivering episodes hain jo systemic infection ke saath occur kar sakte hain. Vitals aur clinical assessment important hain.
- **B (Galat):** Jaundice bilirubin accumulation se yellow discoloration hai.
- **C (Galat):** Urine absence anuria kehlati hai.
- **D (Galat):** Abdominal swelling rigors ka meaning nahi hai.

**Exam clue:** Rigors are pronounced shivering episodes.

</details>

### Q6. Which term denotes surgical incision into the common bile duct during selected duct stone operations?

**Difficulty:** 🟢 Easy · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD · Original practice

- **A.** Cholecystectomy
- **B.** Choledochotomy
- **C.** Hepatectomy
- **D.** Pancreatectomy

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: B. Choledochotomy**

**Option-wise explanation:**
- **A (Galat):** Cholecystectomy gallbladder remove karti hai.
- **B (Sahi):** Choledochotomy CBD mein surgical incision ko describe karti hai. Selected duct exploration mein surgeon iska use kar sakta hai.
- **C (Galat):** Hepatectomy liver tissue resection hai.
- **D (Galat):** Pancreatectomy pancreas resection hai.

**Exam clue:** Choledochotomy is an incision into the common bile duct.

</details>
