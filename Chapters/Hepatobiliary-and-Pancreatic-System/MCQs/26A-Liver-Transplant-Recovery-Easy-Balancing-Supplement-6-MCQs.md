### Q1. Which activity is generally encouraged during early uncomplicated liver transplant recovery when the team permits mobilization?

**Difficulty:** 🟢 Easy · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD · Original practice

- **A.** Heavy weightlifting
- **B.** Prolonged strict bed rest
- **C.** Strenuous abdominal exercise
- **D.** Short regular walks

<details><summary>✅ Correct answer and detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: D. Short regular walks**

**A. Kyun galat hai:** Heavy weightlifting healing abdominal wound par strain daal sakti hai, isliye early recovery mein suitable nahi hai.

**B. Kyun galat hai:** Strict prolonged bed rest clot aur deconditioning risk badha sakta hai, jab mobilization allowed ho toh walks helpful hain.

**C. Kyun galat hai:** Strenuous abdominal exercise healing tissues ko strain kar sakti hai, isliye clearance ka wait karna chahiye.

**D. Kyun sahi hai:** Short walks recovery aur circulation ko support karti hain, distance team ki advice ke according gradually badhaya jata hai.

**Exam clue:** Early recovery mein permitted short walks aur rest dono important hain.

</details>

### Q2. Which instruction is appropriate for the healing liver transplant incision at home?

**Difficulty:** 🟢 Easy · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD · Original practice

- **A.** Apply unprescribed herbal paste
- **B.** Keep it clean and dry as instructed
- **C.** Soak the incision daily in a public pool
- **D.** Scrub the incision vigorously

<details><summary>✅ Correct answer and detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: B. Keep it clean and dry as instructed**

**A. Kyun galat hai:** Herbal paste infection ya irritation ka risk create kar sakta hai, isliye bina team ki permission use nahi karna chahiye.

**B. Kyun sahi hai:** Team ki wound instructions follow karte hue incision clean aur dry rakhna healing mein help karta hai.

**C. Kyun galat hai:** Public pool mein healing wound expose karna infection risk badha sakta hai.

**D. Kyun galat hai:** Vigorous scrubbing healing skin ko damage kar sakti hai, isliye gentle prescribed wound care follow karo.

**Exam clue:** Healing transplant incision ko clean aur protected rakho.

</details>

### Q3. A liver transplant recipient realizes that a prescribed immunosuppressant dose was missed. What should the nurse advise first?

**Difficulty:** 🟢 Easy · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD · Original practice

- **A.** Stop all future doses
- **B.** Double every dose for the next week
- **C.** Contact the transplant team for missed-dose instructions
- **D.** Replace the medicine with a vitamin

<details><summary>✅ Correct answer and detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: C. Contact the transplant team for missed-dose instructions**

**A. Kyun galat hai:** Saare doses stop karne se graft rejection ka risk badh sakta hai, isliye aisa khud se nahi karna chahiye.

**B. Kyun galat hai:** Repeated double doses toxicity ka risk badha sakti hain, missed-dose plan drug-specific hota hai.

**C. Kyun sahi hai:** Transplant team ko batane se patient ko medicine aur timing ke hisab se safe instructions mil sakti hain.

**D. Kyun galat hai:** Vitamin immunosuppression replace nahi kar sakta, graft protection ke liye prescribed medicine important hai.

**Exam clue:** Missed dose par khud se schedule change mat karo.

</details>

### Q4. When should a recovering liver transplant patient return to swimming after surgery?

**Difficulty:** 🟢 Easy · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD · Original practice

- **A.** After the wound is fully healed and the team permits it
- **B.** As soon as the hospital discharge letter is received
- **C.** While the wound is still draining
- **D.** Whenever the dressing becomes wet

<details><summary>✅ Correct answer and detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: A. After the wound is fully healed and the team permits it**

**A. Kyun sahi hai:** Wound fully heal hone aur team ki permission milne ke baad pool exposure safer hota hai.

**B. Kyun galat hai:** Discharge ka matlab wound fully heal hona nahi hai, isliye swimming ke liye clearance chahiye.

**C. Kyun galat hai:** Draining wound ko pool water ke contact mein lana infection risk badha sakta hai.

**D. Kyun galat hai:** Wet dressing healing ka sign nahi hai aur wound care instructions follow karni chahiye.

**Exam clue:** Swimming ke liye wound healing aur team clearance check karo.

</details>

### Q5. Which health habit should a liver transplant recipient who previously had alcohol-associated liver disease maintain?

**Difficulty:** 🟢 Easy · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD · Original practice

- **A.** Resume daily alcohol use once the scar heals
- **B.** Use alcohol to manage postoperative pain
- **C.** Avoid alcohol
- **D.** Replace meals with alcoholic drinks

<details><summary>✅ Correct answer and detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: C. Avoid alcohol**

**A. Kyun galat hai:** Scar heal hone se alcohol-related liver harm ka risk khatam nahi hota.

**B. Kyun galat hai:** Alcohol pain treatment ka safe substitute nahi hai aur medicines ke saath risk badha sakta hai.

**C. Kyun sahi hai:** Alcohol avoid karna graft aur overall health protect karne ke liye important hai, especially previous alcohol-associated disease mein.

**D. Kyun galat hai:** Alcohol meals ki nutrition replace nahi karta aur recovery ko adversely affect kar sakta hai.

**Exam clue:** Alcohol-associated disease ke baad transplant ka matlab alcohol risk khatam hona nahi hai.

</details>

### Q6. Which action supports safe pain management at home after liver transplantation?

**Difficulty:** 🟢 Easy · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD · Original practice

- **A.** Discuss uncontrolled pain with the transplant team
- **B.** Use any available pain medicine without checking
- **C.** Borrow another recipient's opioid prescription
- **D.** Stop immunosuppression whenever pain develops

<details><summary>✅ Correct answer and detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: A. Discuss uncontrolled pain with the transplant team**

**A. Kyun sahi hai:** Uncontrolled pain team ko report karne se safe pain regimen aur possible complication ka assessment ho sakta hai.

**B. Kyun galat hai:** Available pain medicines graft ya kidney function aur drug interactions ko affect kar sakti hain, isliye review zaroori hai.

**C. Kyun galat hai:** Kisi aur ki opioid prescription use karna unsafe hai kyunki dose aur clinical condition different ho sakti hai.

**D. Kyun galat hai:** Immunosuppression pain ke hisab se khud se stop nahi karni chahiye, kyunki graft rejection ka risk hota hai.

**Exam clue:** Transplant ke baad pain medicine ki choice team se verify karo.

</details>
