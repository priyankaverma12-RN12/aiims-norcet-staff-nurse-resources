### Q1. What does the term incidental liver lesion mean?

**Difficulty:** 🟢 Easy · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD · Original practice

- **A.** A lesion discovered during an investigation performed for another reason
- **B.** A lesion proven malignant by definition
- **C.** A lesion that always causes acute liver failure
- **D.** A lesion detected only during liver transplantation

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: A. A lesion discovered during an investigation performed for another reason**

**Option-wise explanation:**
- **A (Sahi):** Incidental finding ka matlab imaging ya investigation kisi aur reason se hui aur liver lesion unexpectedly mila. Characterization still needed ho sakti hai.
- **B (Galat):** Incidental term malignancy confirm nahi karta.
- **C (Galat):** Most incidental lesions acute failure cause nahi karti.
- **D (Galat):** Incidental lesions routine imaging par bhi detect ho sakti hain.

**Exam clue:** Incidental describes discovery, not pathology.

</details>

### Q2. What does a simple hepatic cyst usually contain?

**Difficulty:** 🟢 Easy · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD · Original practice

- **A.** Solid bone
- **B.** Fluid
- **C.** A network of patent blood vessels only
- **D.** Pancreatic islet tissue

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: B. Fluid**

**Option-wise explanation:**
- **A (Galat):** Bone tissue simple cyst ka usual content nahi hai.
- **B (Sahi):** Simple cyst generally thin-walled fluid-filled structure hoti hai. Complex features ho toh separate evaluation chahiye.
- **C (Galat):** Vascular channels hemangioma ke characteristic ho sakte hain.
- **D (Galat):** Pancreatic islet tissue hepatic simple cyst ka usual content nahi hai.

**Exam clue:** A simple cyst is fluid-filled.

</details>

### Q3. Which ultrasound appearance supports a typical simple hepatic cyst?

**Difficulty:** 🟢 Easy · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD · Original practice

- **A.** A solid irregular vascular mass
- **B.** A thick enhancing mural nodule
- **C.** Anechoic content with a thin smooth wall
- **D.** A densely calcified solid lesion

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: C. Anechoic content with a thin smooth wall**

**Option-wise explanation:**
- **A (Galat):** Solid vascular lesion simple cyst ke expected appearance se different hai.
- **B (Galat):** Mural nodule complex lesion ka concerning feature ho sakta hai.
- **C (Sahi):** Simple fluid ultrasound par typically anechoic dikhta hai aur thin smooth wall hoti hai. Other features bhi radiologist assess karta hai.
- **D (Galat):** Calcified solid lesion typical simple fluid cyst nahi hai.

**Exam clue:** Simple cysts are typically anechoic and thin-walled.

</details>

### Q4. Which term describes a disorder characterized by numerous hepatic cysts rather than a solitary simple cyst?

**Difficulty:** 🟢 Easy · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD · Original practice

- **A.** Hepatic steatosis
- **B.** Acute cholangitis
- **C.** Hepatic encephalopathy
- **D.** Polycystic liver disease

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: D. Polycystic liver disease**

**Option-wise explanation:**
- **A (Galat):** Steatosis hepatocytes mein fat accumulation hai.
- **B (Galat):** Cholangitis biliary inflammation or infection se related hai.
- **C (Galat):** Encephalopathy neuropsychiatric dysfunction hai, cyst disorder nahi.
- **D (Sahi):** Polycystic liver disease mein multiple hepatic cysts develop hoti hain. Kidney association aur symptoms specialist assess kar sakta hai.

**Exam clue:** Multiple cysts can suggest polycystic liver disease.

</details>

### Q5. Which internal imaging feature can be seen in focal nodular hyperplasia, although it is not always present?

**Difficulty:** 🟢 Easy · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD · Original practice

- **A.** Central scar
- **B.** Air-filled bronchus
- **C.** A gallbladder stone inside every lesion
- **D.** A pancreatic duct traversing the lesion

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: A. Central scar**

**Option-wise explanation:**
- **A (Sahi):** FNH mein central fibrous scar ho sakta hai. Scar alone definitive nahi hai aur full enhancement pattern interpret karna chahiye.
- **B (Galat):** Air-filled bronchus pulmonary structure hai.
- **C (Galat):** Gallstones FNH ka defining central feature nahi hain.
- **D (Galat):** Pancreatic duct liver lesion ke andar expected structure nahi hai.

**Exam clue:** FNH may show a central scar.

</details>

### Q6. Which cell type gives rise to hepatocellular adenoma?

**Difficulty:** 🟢 Easy · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD · Original practice

- **A.** Pleural mesothelial cells
- **B.** Hepatocytes
- **C.** Renal podocytes
- **D.** Pancreatic beta cells

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: B. Hepatocytes**

**Option-wise explanation:**
- **A (Galat):** Pleural mesothelial cells chest lining se related hain.
- **B (Sahi):** Hepatocellular adenoma benign hepatocyte-derived neoplasm hai. Hemorrhage aur malignant transformation risk lesion context par depend karta hai.
- **C (Galat):** Podocytes renal glomerular filtration barrier ka part hain.
- **D (Galat):** Beta cells pancreatic insulin-producing cells hain.

**Exam clue:** Hepatocellular means derived from liver cells.

</details>
