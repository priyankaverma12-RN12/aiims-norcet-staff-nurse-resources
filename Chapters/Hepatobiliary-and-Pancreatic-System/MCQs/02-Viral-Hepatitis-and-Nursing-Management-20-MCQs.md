### Q1. Which hepatitis virus generally causes a self-limited acute infection without chronic hepatitis?

**Difficulty:** Easy | **Priority:** High | **Topic:** Viral Hepatitis and Nursing Management | **Evidence:** Original practice

- **A.** Hepatitis A virus
- **B.** Hepatitis B virus
- **C.** Hepatitis C virus
- **D.** Hepatitis D virus

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: A. Hepatitis A virus**

- **A.** HAV infection ke baad aam taur par patient recover karta hai aur chronic HAV nahi hota.
- **B.** HBV chronic infection bana sakta hai, khaaskar early age mein infection hone par.
- **C.** HCV untreated rehne par chronic infection aur progressive liver damage kar sakta hai.
- **D.** HDV ko HBV ki presence chahiye aur ye chronic disease ko severe bana sakta hai.

**Exam clue:** HAV does not cause chronic hepatitis.

</details>

### Q2. Which hepatitis B marker is most directly associated with immunity after vaccination alone?

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Priority:** High | **Topic:** Viral Hepatitis and Nursing Management | **Evidence:** Original practice

- **A.** HBsAg
- **B.** Anti-HBs
- **C.** IgM anti-HBc
- **D.** HBV DNA

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: B. Anti-HBs**

- **A.** HBsAg virus ka surface antigen hai aur current infection suggest kar sakta hai.
- **B.** Vaccine surface antigen ke against antibody banwati hai, jise anti-HBs kehte hain.
- **C.** IgM anti-HBc core antigen ke against recent infection ka marker hai, vaccine alone ka nahi.
- **D.** HBV DNA viral genetic material hai, protective antibody nahi.

**Exam clue:** Vaccination alone produces anti-HBs, not anti-HBc.

</details>

### Q3. HBsAg is negative, total anti-HBc is positive, and anti-HBs is positive. What is the usual interpretation?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Priority:** High | **Topic:** Viral Hepatitis and Nursing Management | **Evidence:** Original practice

- **A.** Immunity from vaccination alone
- **B.** Typical acute hepatitis B with active antigenemia
- **C.** Resolved natural HBV infection
- **D.** Never infected and susceptible

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: C. Resolved natural HBV infection**

- **A.** Vaccine alone se core antibody nahi banti, isliye ye pattern vaccine-only immunity nahi hai.
- **B.** Active antigenemia mein HBsAg positive expected hota hai. Yahan wo negative hai.
- **C.** Core antibody batata hai ki body pehle natural virus se mili thi. Surface antibody recovery aur immunity ko support karta hai.
- **D.** Anti-HBs aur anti-HBc dono positive hain, isliye completely susceptible pattern nahi hai.

**Exam clue:** Anti-HBc differentiates natural exposure from vaccination alone.

</details>

### Q4. Which HBV marker supports a recent acute infection in a symptomatic HBsAg-positive patient?

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Priority:** High | **Topic:** Viral Hepatitis and Nursing Management | **Evidence:** Original practice

- **A.** Isolated anti-HBs
- **B.** Anti-HAV IgG
- **C.** HCV antibody
- **D.** IgM anti-HBc

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: D. IgM anti-HBc**

- **A.** Anti-HBs immunity ka marker hai. Acute HBV ko alone identify nahi karta.
- **B.** HAV IgG past hepatitis A exposure ya immunity se juda hai, HBV se nahi.
- **C.** HCV antibody hepatitis C se exposure batata hai, hepatitis B ki timing nahi.
- **D.** IgM core antibody recent HBV infection ko support karti hai. Kabhi severe chronic flare mein bhi positive ho sakti hai.

**Exam clue:** Interpret IgM anti-HBc with the clinical context.

</details>

### Q5. A patient's HBsAg has remained positive for seven months. Which condition is supported?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Priority:** High | **Topic:** Viral Hepatitis and Nursing Management | **Evidence:** Original practice

- **A.** Chronic hepatitis B infection
- **B.** Resolved hepatitis A
- **C.** Vaccine-induced HBV immunity alone
- **D.** Transient antibody positivity only

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: A. Chronic hepatitis B infection**

- **A.** HBsAg chhe mahine ya usse zyada persistent ho toh chronic HBV infection consider kiya jaata hai.
- **B.** HAV chronic infection establish nahi karta aur HBV surface antigen produce nahi karta.
- **C.** Vaccination ke baad anti-HBs immunity ka marker hai, prolonged HBsAg nahi.
- **D.** HBsAg antigen hai, antibody nahi. Persistence ko ignore nahi karna chahiye.

**Exam clue:** Persistent HBsAg for at least six months supports chronic infection.

</details>

### Q6. A patient with suspected acute hepatitis B is in the serologic window period. Which result may remain detectable?

- **A.** Anti-HBs as the only possible marker
- **B.** IgM anti-HBc
- **C.** Anti-HAV IgM
- **D.** HCV RNA

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: B. IgM anti-HBc**

- **A.** Anti-HBs abhi absent ho sakta hai, isliye isko mandatory sole marker nahi keh sakte.
- **B.** Window period mein HBsAg disappear ho sakta hai aur anti-HBs abhi detectable nahi hota. IgM anti-HBc important clue hai.
- **C.** HAV IgM ek alag virus ki acute infection ka marker hai.
- **D.** HCV RNA hepatitis C ka viral material hai, HBV window ka marker nahi.

**Exam clue:** Core IgM bridges the classic HBV window.

</details>

### Q7. Which hepatitis virus requires hepatitis B surface antigen to complete its life cycle?

**Difficulty:** Easy | **Priority:** High | **Topic:** Viral Hepatitis and Nursing Management | **Evidence:** Original practice

- **A.** Hepatitis A virus
- **B.** Hepatitis C virus
- **C.** Hepatitis D virus
- **D.** Hepatitis E virus

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: C. Hepatitis D virus**

- **A.** HAV independently replicate karta hai. Use HBV ki zaroorat nahi hai.
- **B.** HCV ek independent RNA virus hai, HBV surface antigen par depend nahi karta.
- **C.** HDV apni infectious particles banane ke liye HBV surface antigen par depend karta hai.
- **D.** HEV bhi independently infection karta hai aur HBV ko helper nahi banata.

**Exam clue:** HBV vaccination also prevents acquisition of HDV in susceptible people.

</details>

### Q8. A patient has a reactive HCV antibody screening result. Which test establishes whether current viremia is present?

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Priority:** High | **Topic:** Viral Hepatitis and Nursing Management | **Evidence:** Original practice

- **A.** Repeat ALT alone
- **B.** HBsAg alone
- **C.** Anti-HAV IgG
- **D.** HCV RNA nucleic acid testing

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: D. HCV RNA nucleic acid testing**

- **A.** ALT liver injury batata hai, lekin HCV active hai ya cleared hai ye decide nahi karta.
- **B.** HBsAg hepatitis B infection ka marker hai, C ka nahi.
- **C.** HAV IgG hepatitis A immunity ya exposure se related hai.
- **D.** RNA test directly virus ka genetic material detect karta hai. Positive RNA current infection ko support karta hai.

**Exam clue:** Reactive HCV antibody requires RNA confirmation of current infection.

</details>

### Q9. HCV antibody is reactive but HCV RNA is not detected in a patient without recent exposure. Which explanation is appropriate?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Priority:** High | **Topic:** Viral Hepatitis and Nursing Management | **Evidence:** Original practice

- **A.** No current detectable HCV infection
- **B.** Confirmed active HCV requiring treatment solely on antibody result
- **C.** Established chronic HBV infection
- **D.** Complete immunity against future HCV infection

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: A. No current detectable HCV infection**

- **A.** Antibody past exposure ke baad reh sakti hai. RNA absent hone par current detectable infection ka evidence nahi milta.
- **B.** Active HCV treatment decide karne ke liye RNA result important hai. Antibody alone enough nahi hai.
- **C.** HBV ko identify karne ke liye specific HBV tests chahiye.
- **D.** HCV antibody future infection ke against reliable protective immunity prove nahi karti.

**Exam clue:** HCV antibody may persist after clearance.

</details>

### Q10. A nurse assesses a patient after successful hepatitis C treatment. Which counseling is accurate?

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Priority:** High | **Topic:** Viral Hepatitis and Nursing Management | **Evidence:** Original practice

- **A.** The antibody must immediately become negative
- **B.** Reinfection remains possible after cure
- **C.** The patient is permanently protected from hepatitis C
- **D.** Routine hepatitis C vaccination is now required

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: B. Reinfection remains possible after cure**

- **A.** Antibody cure ke baad bhi detectable reh sakti hai.
- **B.** Virus clear hone ke baad bhi new exposure se infection dobara ho sakta hai. Prevention counseling important hai.
- **C.** Past infection future HCV infection ke against guaranteed protection nahi deti.
- **D.** Abhi routine effective hepatitis C vaccine available nahi hai.

**Exam clue:** Cure does not provide guaranteed immunity.

</details>

### Q11. Which intervention is available to prevent hepatitis B before exposure in a susceptible adult?

**Difficulty:** Easy | **Priority:** High | **Topic:** Viral Hepatitis and Nursing Management | **Evidence:** Original practice

- **A.** Long-term hepatitis C antiviral treatment
- **B.** Routine hepatitis A immunoglobulin instead of vaccination
- **C.** Hepatitis B vaccination
- **D.** Prophylactic broad-spectrum antibiotics

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: C. Hepatitis B vaccination**

- **A.** HCV antiviral medicines established HCV infection ke treatment ke liye hain, HBV vaccine ka replacement nahi.
- **B.** HAV immunoglobulin hepatitis A ke selected exposures mein use ho sakta hai, HBV immunity nahi deta.
- **C.** HBV vaccine protective anti-HBs develop karne mein help karti hai.
- **D.** Antibiotics bacteria ke liye hote hain, HBV infection prevent nahi karte.

**Exam clue:** Vaccination is a key HBV prevention measure.

</details>

### Q12. A completely unvaccinated nurse has a needlestick from a confirmed HBsAg-positive source. Which hepatitis B-specific management should occupational health assess promptly?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Priority:** High | **Topic:** Viral Hepatitis and Nursing Management | **Evidence:** Original practice

- **A.** Hepatitis B vaccination only after six months
- **B.** HCV direct-acting antiviral prophylaxis
- **C.** No prophylaxis because the puncture is small
- **D.** HBIG plus initiation of hepatitis B vaccination

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: D. HBIG plus initiation of hepatitis B vaccination**

- **A.** Chhe mahine wait karne se timely postexposure prevention ka mauka miss ho sakta hai.
- **B.** HCV antiviral prophylaxis HBV exposure ke liye indicated treatment nahi hai.
- **C.** Needlestick chhota ho tab bhi infected blood exposure significant ho sakta hai.
- **D.** HBIG immediate passive protection deta hai aur vaccine future active protection banwati hai. Exposure protocol ke hisaab se dono timely assess hote hain.

**Exam clue:** Use source status and documented vaccine response to guide PEP.

</details>

### Q13. A fully vaccinated immunocompetent nurse has documented postvaccination anti-HBs of 45 mIU/mL and sustains HBV exposure. Which HBV-specific action is generally indicated under CDC guidance?

- **B.** HBIG for every exposure regardless of immunity
- **C.** Repeat an entire vaccine series automatically
- **D.** Start oral HBV antiviral therapy immediately in all cases

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: A. No additional HBV prophylaxis after reporting and assessment**

- **A.** Documented anti-HBs 10 ya usse zyada vaccine response ko support karta hai. Exposure report karna hai, lekin additional HBV PEP generally nahi chahiye.
- **B.** HBIG har protected responder ko automatically nahi diya jaata.

</details>

### Q14. A patient with acute hepatitis A asks how to reduce spread to family members while recovering. Which advice is most appropriate?

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Priority:** High | **Topic:** Viral Hepatitis and Nursing Management | **Evidence:** Original practice

- **A.** Use shared razors to reduce disposal waste
- **B.** Careful handwashing after toileting and before food handling
- **C.** Rely on a face mask as the only measure
- **D.** Avoid all protein permanently

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: B. Careful handwashing after toileting and before food handling**

- **A.** Razors share karna bloodborne infections ke liye risk hai aur useful HAV prevention nahi hai.
- **B.** HAV stool se mouth tak contaminated hands ya food ke through spread kar sakta hai. Handwashing is route ko interrupt karti hai.
- **C.** HAV ka main route respiratory droplets nahi hai, isliye mask alone kaafi nahi hai.
- **D.** Protein permanently avoid karna transmission prevent nahi karta aur nutrition harm kar sakta hai.

**Exam clue:** Match precautions to fecal-oral transmission.

</details>

### Q15. During a waterborne hepatitis outbreak, a pregnant patient in the third trimester develops jaundice and vomiting. Which viral cause deserves special concern due to severe maternal disease risk?

- **A.** Hepatitis C virus acquired by mosquito bite
- **B.** Hepatitis D virus in every waterborne case
- **C.** Hepatitis E virus
- **D.** Hepatitis B vaccine antigen

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: C. Hepatitis E virus**

- **A.** HCV mainly blood exposure se spread karta hai. Mosquito bite usual route nahi hai.
- **B.** HDV HBV par dependent hai aur har waterborne outbreak ka expected cause nahi hai.
- **C.** Waterborne HEV kuch settings mein pregnancy ke late stages mein severe hepatitis aur liver failure ka risk badhata hai. Urgent evaluation chahiye.
- **D.** HBV vaccine antigen infectious hepatitis E outbreak ka cause nahi hai.

**Exam clue:** Pregnancy and waterborne hepatitis E is a high-risk combination.

### Q20. Which discharge instruction is appropriate for a patient receiving prescribed hepatitis B antiviral treatment?

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Priority:** High | **Topic:** Viral Hepatitis and Nursing Management | **Evidence:** Original practice

- **A.** Stop treatment independently once symptoms improve
- **B.** Share tablets with an untested family member
- **C.** Double all future doses after any missed tablet
- **D.** Discuss missed doses or stopping treatment with the treating team

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: D. Discuss missed doses or stopping treatment with the treating team**

- **A.** Symptoms improve hona virus control ya safe stopping ka proof nahi hai.
- **B.** Family member ko testing aur clinician assessment chahiye. Prescription share karna unsafe hai.
- **C.** Double dosing har medication ke liye safe rule nahi hai. Specific instructions follow karni chahiye.
- **D.** Antiviral treatment ko suddenly stop karne par selected patients mein hepatitis flare ho sakta hai. Missed dose ke liye prescribed guidance follow karni chahiye.

**Exam clue:** Long-term HBV treatment decisions require supervised follow-up.

</details>
