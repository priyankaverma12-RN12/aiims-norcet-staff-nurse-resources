### Q1. Which skin color change may occur with advanced systemic iron overload?

**Difficulty:** Easy · **Priority:** High · **Exam focus:** NORCET / HPRCA HP Staff Nurse / PGIMER · Original practice

- **A.** Bronze or grayish hyperpigmentation
- **B.** Blue discoloration due to acute hypoxia
- **C.** White patches due to loss of melanocytes
- **D.** Localized redness only after a bee sting

<details><summary>✅ Correct answer and detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: A. Bronze or grayish hyperpigmentation**

**A. Kyun sahi hai:** Excess iron aur pigmentation changes se skin bronze ya grayish dark dikh sakti hai. Ye finding alone diagnostic nahi hai.

**B. Kyun galat hai:** Cyanosis oxygenation problem ka sign ho sakti hai, classical iron-overload pigmentation nahi.

**C. Kyun galat hai:** Vitiligo pigment loss se related hai.

**D. Kyun galat hai:** Local inflammatory reaction systemic iron overload ka typical pigment pattern nahi hai.

**Exam clue:** Bronze skin is a possible iron-overload clue.

</details>

### Q2. Which serum protein transports iron in the bloodstream?

**Difficulty:** Easy · **Priority:** High · **Exam focus:** NORCET / HPRCA HP Staff Nurse / PGIMER · Original practice

- **A.** Ceruloplasmin
- **B.** Amylase
- **C.** Insulin
- **D.** Transferrin

<details><summary>✅ Correct answer and detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: D. Transferrin**

**A. Kyun galat hai:** Ceruloplasmin mainly copper transport se related hai.

**B. Kyun galat hai:** Amylase carbohydrate digestion enzyme hai.

**C. Kyun galat hai:** Insulin glucose regulation hormone hai.

**D. Kyun sahi hai:** Transferrin iron bind karke blood mein transport karti hai. Saturation iron loading assess karne mein help karti hai.

**Exam clue:** Transferrin transports iron.

</details>

### Q3. Which protein stores iron within cells and is commonly reflected by a serum laboratory marker?

**Difficulty:** Easy · **Priority:** High · **Exam focus:** NORCET / HPRCA HP Staff Nurse / PGIMER · Original practice

- **A.** Lipase
- **B.** Thrombin
- **C.** Ferritin
- **D.** Myosin

<details><summary>✅ Correct answer and detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: C. Ferritin**

**A. Kyun galat hai:** Lipase fat digestion enzyme hai.

**B. Kyun galat hai:** Thrombin clot formation mein function karta hai.

**C. Kyun sahi hai:** Ferritin iron storage protein hai. Serum ferritin iron stores assess karne mein useful ho sakti hai, par inflammation mein bhi rise ho sakti hai.

**D. Kyun galat hai:** Myosin muscle contraction protein hai, principal iron storage marker nahi.

**Exam clue:** Ferritin is associated with iron storage.

</details>

### Q4. What is the common clinical meaning of arthralgia in hereditary hemochromatosis?

**Difficulty:** Easy · **Priority:** High · **Exam focus:** NORCET / HPRCA HP Staff Nurse / PGIMER · Original practice

- **A.** Muscle paralysis
- **B.** Joint pain
- **C.** Urinary retention
- **D.** Ear discharge

<details><summary>✅ Correct answer and detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: B. Joint pain**

**A. Kyun galat hai:** Paralysis movement loss hai, joint pain ka term nahi.

**B. Kyun sahi hai:** Arthralgia joints mein pain ko kehte hain. Iron overload mein arthropathy aur discomfort develop ho sakte hain.

**C. Kyun galat hai:** Urinary retention bladder emptying problem hai.

**D. Kyun galat hai:** Ear discharge otologic symptom hai, arthralgia nahi.

**Exam clue:** Arthralgia means joint pain.

</details>

### Q5. Which term describes a disease of the heart muscle that can develop with substantial cardiac iron deposition?

**Difficulty:** Easy · **Priority:** High · **Exam focus:** NORCET / HPRCA HP Staff Nurse / PGIMER · Original practice

- **A.** Cholecystopathy
- **B.** Nephrolithiasis
- **C.** Retinopathy
- **D.** Cardiomyopathy

<details><summary>✅ Correct answer and detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: D. Cardiomyopathy**

**A. Kyun galat hai:** Ye gallbladder disease ko refer karta hai.

**B. Kyun galat hai:** Nephrolithiasis kidney stone formation hai.

**C. Kyun galat hai:** Retinopathy retina ki disease hai.

**D. Kyun sahi hai:** Cardiomyopathy heart muscle ki disease hai. Iron deposition contraction ya relaxation ko impair kar sakta hai.

**Exam clue:** Cardiac iron overload can affect myocardium.

</details>

### Q6. Which procedure name is synonymous with therapeutic venesection for iron overload?

**Difficulty:** Easy · **Priority:** High · **Exam focus:** NORCET / HPRCA HP Staff Nurse / PGIMER · Original practice

- **A.** Phlebotomy
- **B.** Plasmapheresis only
- **C.** Thoracentesis
- **D.** Paracentesis

<details><summary>✅ Correct answer and detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: A. Phlebotomy**

**A. Kyun sahi hai:** Phlebotomy mein prescribed amount blood remove kiya jaata hai. Isse body ko new red cells banane mein stored iron use karna padta hai.

**B. Kyun galat hai:** Plasmapheresis plasma ko separate karti hai. Standard therapeutic blood removal ko phlebotomy kehte hain.

**C. Kyun galat hai:** Thoracentesis pleural fluid removal hai.

**D. Kyun galat hai:** Paracentesis abdominal fluid drainage hai.

**Exam clue:** Venesection and therapeutic phlebotomy are related terms.

</details>
