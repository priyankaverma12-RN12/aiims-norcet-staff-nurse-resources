### Q1. Under Tokyo Guidelines 2018, which three categories of findings establish definite acute cholangitis when at least one criterion from each is present?

**Difficulty:** Medium · **Priority:** HIGH YIELD · Original practice

- **A.** Systemic inflammation, cholestasis and supportive biliary imaging
- **B.** Anemia, hypoglycemia and hypercalcemia
- **C.** Ascites, edema and low albumin alone
- **D.** Cough, wheezing and reduced spirometry

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: A. Systemic inflammation, cholestasis and supportive biliary imaging**

**Option-wise explanation:**
- **A (Sahi):** Tokyo criteria infection or inflammation, impaired bile drainage aur imaging evidence ko combine karti hain. Complete classic triad absent hone par bhi diagnosis possible hai.
- **B (Galat):** Anemia aur metabolic abnormalities cholangitis ke three diagnostic categories nahi hain.
- **C (Galat):** Ascites aur low albumin chronic liver dysfunction mein ho sakte hain, acute biliary infection establish nahi karte.
- **D (Galat):** Respiratory findings biliary diagnostic criteria replace nahi karte.

**Exam clue:** TG18 uses inflammation, cholestasis and imaging.

</details>
