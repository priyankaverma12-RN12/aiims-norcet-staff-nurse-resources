### Q1. Which pancreatic enzyme helps digest starch?

**Difficulty:** 🟢 Easy · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD · Original practice

- **A.** Trypsin
- **B.** Lipase
- **C.** Amylase
- **D.** Lactase

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: C. Amylase**

**Option-wise explanation:**
- **A (Galat):** Trypsin protein digestion mein involved hai.
- **B (Galat):** Lipase mainly triglyceride digestion karti hai.
- **C (Sahi):** Amylase starch ki chains break karke carbohydrate digestion mein help karti hai.
- **D (Galat):** Lactase small intestinal brush border par lactose digest karti hai.

**Exam clue:** Amylase digests starch.

</details>

### Q2. Which pancreatic enzyme is involved in protein digestion?

**Difficulty:** 🟢 Easy · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD · Original practice

- **A.** Trypsin
- **B.** Amylase
- **C.** Lipase
- **D.** Sucrase

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: A. Trypsin**

**Option-wise explanation:**
- **A (Sahi):** Trypsin activated pancreatic protease hai jo protein ke peptide bonds break karne mein help karta hai.
- **B (Galat):** Amylase starch digestion mein involved hai.
- **C (Galat):** Lipase fats digest karti hai.
- **D (Galat):** Sucrase intestinal brush border enzyme hai, sucrose digest karti hai.

**Exam clue:** Trypsin is a protease.

</details>

### Q3. A patient with pancreatic maldigestion reports difficulty seeing in dim light. Which vitamin deficiency is a recognized possible cause?

**Difficulty:** 🟢 Easy · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD · Original practice

- **A.** Vitamin B1
- **B.** Vitamin B3
- **C.** Vitamin C
- **D.** Vitamin A

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: D. Vitamin A**

**Option-wise explanation:**
- **A (Galat):** B1 deficiency mainly neurological aur cardiac complications se related hai.
- **B (Galat):** B3 deficiency pellagra se associated hai.
- **C (Galat):** Vitamin C deficiency scurvy cause kar sakti hai.
- **D (Sahi):** Vitamin A retinal visual pigment ke liye important hai. Deficiency mein night blindness possible hai, other eye causes bhi assess karte hain.

**Exam clue:** Vitamin A deficiency may cause night blindness.

</details>

### Q4. Which vitamin deficiency may contribute to impaired clotting-factor activation in pancreatic fat maldigestion?

**Difficulty:** 🟢 Easy · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD · Original practice

- **A.** Vitamin B2
- **B.** Vitamin K
- **C.** Vitamin B6
- **D.** Vitamin C

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: B. Vitamin K**

**Option-wise explanation:**
- **A (Galat):** B2 cellular energy metabolism mein role rakhti hai.
- **B (Sahi):** Vitamin K clotting factors II, VII, IX aur X ke functional activation mein needed hai. Fat malabsorption se deficiency risk ho sakta hai.
- **C (Galat):** B6 amino acid metabolism aur other reactions mein involved hai.
- **D (Galat):** Vitamin C collagen formation mein important hai, vitamin K dependent clotting activation ka substitute nahi.

**Exam clue:** Vitamin K supports clotting-factor function.

</details>

### Q5. How should prescription pancrelipase capsules generally be stored at home?

**Difficulty:** 🟢 Easy · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD · Original practice

- **A.** Loose in an open container near the stove
- **B.** In their original tightly closed container according to product temperature and moisture instructions
- **C.** Mixed with water for the entire week
- **D.** In a humid bathroom without the cap

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: B. In their original tightly closed container according to product temperature and moisture instructions**

**Option-wise explanation:**
- **A (Galat):** Stove ke paas excess heat aur open exposure undesirable hai.
- **B (Sahi):** Heat aur moisture enzyme activity affect kar sakte hain. Specific product label ke storage instructions follow karne chahiye.
- **C (Galat):** Advance aqueous mixing stability compromise kar sakti hai.
- **D (Galat):** Bathroom humidity aur open container medicine ko moisture expose karte hain.

**Exam clue:** Protect enzyme capsules from heat and moisture.

</details>

### Q6. Which inherited disorder involving CFTR dysfunction is a recognized cause of pancreatic exocrine insufficiency?

**Difficulty:** 🟢 Easy · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD · Original practice

- **A.** Cystic fibrosis
- **B.** Hemophilia A
- **C.** Duchenne muscular dystrophy
- **D.** Sickle cell disease

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: A. Cystic fibrosis**

**Option-wise explanation:**
- **A (Sahi):** Cystic fibrosis mein CFTR dysfunction se thick pancreatic secretions aur duct obstruction ho sakti hai. Isse digestive enzymes ki intestinal delivery impair ho sakti hai.
- **B (Galat):** Hemophilia A factor VIII deficiency ka disorder hai, CFTR dysfunction nahi.
- **C (Galat):** Duchenne muscular dystrophy dystrophin-related muscle disorder hai, CFTR-related pancreatic disease nahi.
- **D (Galat):** Sickle cell disease hemoglobin disorder hai, CFTR dysfunction se cause nahi hoti.

**Exam clue:** Cystic fibrosis is a recognized high-risk condition for exocrine pancreatic insufficiency.

</details>
