### Q1. Which membrane lines the abdominal cavity and covers many abdominal organs?

**Difficulty:** 🟢 Easy · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD · Original practice

- **A.** Pleura
- **B.** Pericardium
- **C.** Meninges
- **D.** Peritoneum

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: D. Peritoneum**

**Option-wise explanation:**
- **A (Galat):** Pleura lungs aur chest cavity se related hai.
- **B (Galat):** Pericardium heart ke around sac hai.
- **C (Galat):** Meninges brain aur spinal cord cover karti hain.
- **D (Sahi):** Peritoneum abdominal cavity aur kai organs ki serous lining hai. Peritonitis isi compartment ki inflammation hoti hai.

**Exam clue:** Peritoneum is the abdominal serous lining.

</details>

### Q2. Which enteric bacterium is a recognized common cause of community-acquired SBP?

**Difficulty:** 🟢 Easy · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD · Original practice

- **A.** Mycobacterium leprae
- **B.** Bordetella pertussis
- **C.** Escherichia coli
- **D.** Clostridium tetani

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: C. Escherichia coli**

**Option-wise explanation:**
- **A (Galat):** M. leprae leprosy cause karta hai, usual SBP pathogen nahi.
- **B (Galat):** B. pertussis respiratory infection se related hai.
- **C (Sahi):** E. coli intestinal gram-negative organism hai aur SBP ke recognized pathogens mein include hota hai. Local resistance aur culture matter karte hain.
- **D (Galat):** C. tetani tetanus ka organism hai.

**Exam clue:** Enteric bacteria including E. coli may cause SBP.

</details>

### Q3. What is the term for movement of intestinal bacteria or their products across the gut barrier to normally sterile sites?

**Difficulty:** 🟢 Easy · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD · Original practice

- **A.** Bacterial translocation
- **B.** Phagocytosis
- **C.** Osmotic diuresis
- **D.** Hemolysis

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: A. Bacterial translocation**

**Option-wise explanation:**
- **A (Sahi):** Bacterial translocation intestinal barrier cross karne ka process hai. Cirrhosis mein altered gut barrier aur host defense infection risk badha sakte hain.
- **B (Galat):** Phagocytosis immune cell uptake ka process hai.
- **C (Galat):** Osmotic diuresis urine output ka mechanism hai.
- **D (Galat):** Hemolysis red cell breakdown hai.

**Exam clue:** Gut bacterial translocation contributes to SBP risk.

</details>

### Q4. Which laboratory cell type is counted in ascitic fluid to assess the standard SBP diagnostic criterion?

**Difficulty:** 🟢 Easy · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD · Original practice

- **A.** Erythrocytes only
- **B.** Polymorphonuclear neutrophils
- **C.** Platelets only
- **D.** Eosinophils only

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: B. Polymorphonuclear neutrophils**

**Option-wise explanation:**
- **A (Galat):** RBC count bloody sample interpretation mein useful ho sakta hai but main SBP criterion nahi.
- **B (Sahi):** Ascitic PMN count infection assessment ka central component hai. Bacterial culture complementary information deti hai.
- **C (Galat):** Platelets ascitic infection criterion establish nahi karte.
- **D (Galat):** Eosinophils standard SBP neutrophil criterion nahi hain.

**Exam clue:** Ascitic neutrophils are the key cell count.

</details>

### Q5. Which route is used for prescribed human albumin as adjunct treatment in appropriate SBP patients?

**Difficulty:** 🟢 Easy · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD · Original practice

- **A.** Oral capsule
- **B.** Intramuscular injection
- **C.** Intravenous infusion
- **D.** Subcutaneous injection

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: C. Intravenous infusion**

**Option-wise explanation:**
- **A (Galat):** Oral protein IV albumin ka equivalent nahi hai.
- **B (Galat):** IM injection albumin infusion ka indicated route nahi hai.
- **C (Sahi):** Human albumin solution IV infusion se intravascular compartment mein diya jaata hai. Prescription aur infusion monitoring follow karo.
- **D (Galat):** Subcutaneous injection standard SBP albumin administration nahi hai.

**Exam clue:** Albumin is given intravenously when indicated.

</details>

### Q6. Which assessment should be checked during intravenous albumin administration to a patient with SBP?

**Difficulty:** 🟢 Easy · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD · Original practice

- **A.** Visual acuity as the only assessment
- **B.** Hearing threshold alone
- **C.** Skin pigmentation alone
- **D.** Respiratory status and signs of circulatory overload

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: D. Respiratory status and signs of circulatory overload**

**Option-wise explanation:**
- **A (Galat):** Vision infusion-related fluid overload assess nahi karti.
- **B (Galat):** Hearing test circulatory response measure nahi karta.
- **C (Galat):** Pigmentation alone acute fluid overload detect nahi karti.
- **D (Sahi):** Albumin intravascular volume expand kar sakti hai. Breathlessness, oxygenation aur volume overload ke signs monitor karna important hai.

**Exam clue:** Monitor volume and respiratory response during albumin.

</details>
