### Q1. Which cell type gives rise to hepatocellular carcinoma (HCC)?

**Difficulty:** 🟢 Easy · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD · Original practice

- **A.** Biliary duct epithelial cell
- **B.** Hepatocyte
- **C.** Pancreatic acinar cell
- **D.** Hepatic stellate cell

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: B. Hepatocyte**

**Option-wise explanation:**
- **A (Galat):** Biliary epithelial cells se cholangiocarcinoma arise ho sakta hai.
- **B (Sahi):** Hepatocyte liver ki main functional cell hai aur HCC isi lineage se arise hota hai.
- **C (Galat):** Acinar cells pancreas mein digestive enzymes banati hain.
- **D (Galat):** Stellate cells fibrosis mein role rakhti hain, HCC ka usual cell origin nahi hain.

**Exam clue:** HCC aur biliary cancer ka cell origin alag hai.
