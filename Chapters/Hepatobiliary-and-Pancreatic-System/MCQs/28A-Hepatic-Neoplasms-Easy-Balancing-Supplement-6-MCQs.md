### Q1. Which fungal toxin in improperly stored food is a recognized risk factor for hepatocellular carcinoma?

**Difficulty:** 🟢 Easy · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD · Original practice

- **A.** Botulinum toxin
- **B.** Tetanospasmin
- **C.** Aflatoxin B1
- **D.** Shiga toxin

<details><summary>✅ Correct answer and detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: C. Aflatoxin B1**

**A. Kyun galat hai:** Botulinum toxin botulism mein neuromuscular transmission affect karta hai, yeh HCC ka established dietary fungal toxin nahi hai.

**B. Kyun galat hai:** Tetanospasmin tetanus mein muscle spasms se associated bacterial toxin hai, HCC ka dietary risk factor nahi hai.

**C. Kyun sahi hai:** Aflatoxin B1 kuch fungi se ban sakta hai aur contaminated food ke through exposure liver cancer ka risk badha sakta hai.

**D. Kyun galat hai:** Shiga toxin intestinal disease aur hemolytic uremic syndrome se associated ho sakta hai, aflatoxin-related HCC risk se nahi.

**Exam clue:** Aflatoxin aur liver cancer ka association yaad rakho.

</details>

### Q2. Which vaccination can reduce childhood liver cancer risk by preventing a major chronic viral infection?

**Difficulty:** 🟢 Easy · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD · Original practice

- **A.** Measles vaccine
- **B.** Hepatitis B vaccine
- **C.** Influenza vaccine
- **D.** Tetanus vaccine

<details><summary>✅ Correct answer and detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: B. Hepatitis B vaccine**

**A. Kyun galat hai:** Measles vaccine measles infection prevent karti hai, chronic HBV infection ko prevent nahi karti.

**B. Kyun sahi hai:** HBV vaccination infection prevent karne mein help karti hai aur newborn vaccination se childhood liver cancer risk kam hone ka evidence hai.

**C. Kyun galat hai:** Influenza vaccine influenza ke risk ko reduce karti hai, HBV infection ko target nahi karti.

**D. Kyun galat hai:** Tetanus vaccine tetanus prevent karti hai, chronic viral hepatitis B ko nahi.

**Exam clue:** HBV prevention liver cancer prevention ka bhi important part hai.

</details>

### Q3. Which symptom should be documented as early satiety in a person being evaluated for liver cancer?

**Difficulty:** 🟢 Easy · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD · Original practice

- **A.** Feeling full after eating a small amount
- **B.** Difficulty swallowing solids
- **C.** Burning sensation behind the sternum after meals
- **D.** Persistent hunger immediately after meals

<details><summary>✅ Correct answer and detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: A. Feeling full after eating a small amount**

**A. Kyun sahi hai:** Early satiety mein patient thoda khana khakar hi full feel karta hai, jo liver cancer mein possible symptom hai.

**B. Kyun galat hai:** Solid food swallow karne mein difficulty dysphagia hai, early satiety nahi hai.

**C. Kyun galat hai:** Meal ke baad sternum ke peeche burning heartburn ko suggest kar sakti hai, early satiety ka meaning yeh nahi hai.

**D. Kyun galat hai:** Meal ke baad persistent hunger early satiety ka opposite experience hai.

**Exam clue:** Early satiety ka matlab chhota meal lene par fullness hai.

</details>

### Q4. Which pre-existing metabolic liver condition can progress to cirrhosis and increase hepatocellular carcinoma risk?

**Difficulty:** 🟢 Easy · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD · Original practice

- **A.** Uncomplicated gallbladder polyp
- **B.** Acute self-limited gastritis
- **C.** Functional dyspepsia
- **D.** Metabolic dysfunction-associated steatohepatitis

<details><summary>✅ Correct answer and detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: D. Metabolic dysfunction-associated steatohepatitis**

**A. Kyun galat hai:** Gallbladder polyp gallbladder ki condition hai, MASH ki tarah progressive metabolic liver inflammation nahi hai.

**B. Kyun galat hai:** Gastritis stomach lining ko affect karti hai, isse MASH-related cirrhosis ka mechanism explain nahi hota.

**C. Kyun galat hai:** Functional dyspepsia upper gastrointestinal symptoms ka disorder hai, progressive metabolic liver injury nahi hai.

**D. Kyun sahi hai:** MASH mein metabolic factors ke saath fatty liver inflammation aur injury hoti hai, jo fibrosis aur cirrhosis ke through HCC risk badha sakti hai.

**Exam clue:** MASH ko purane NASH terminology se bhi pehchano.

</details>

### Q5. Which food storage practice helps reduce exposure to aflatoxin associated with liver cancer?

**Difficulty:** 🟢 Easy · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD · Original practice

- **A.** Keeping grains and nuts dry and discarding visibly mouldy stock
- **B.** Storing damp grain in warm sealed containers
- **C.** Mixing visibly mouldy grain with uncontaminated grain
- **D.** Relying on normal cooking alone to eliminate all aflatoxin

<details><summary>✅ Correct answer and detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: A. Keeping grains and nuts dry and discarding visibly mouldy stock**

**A. Kyun sahi hai:** Dry storage fungal growth ka risk kam karta hai aur visibly mouldy stock ko discard karna exposure prevention ka important part hai.

**B. Kyun galat hai:** Warm aur damp storage fungi ki growth ko support kar sakti hai.

**C. Kyun galat hai:** Mouldy grain ko clean grain ke saath mix karne se contamination ka risk khatam nahi hota.

**D. Kyun galat hai:** Normal cooking se aflatoxin ka complete elimination guarantee nahi hota, isliye safe storage important hai.

**Exam clue:** Aflatoxin prevention mein safe drying aur storage important hain.

</details>

### Q6. A patient with newly diagnosed liver cancer has lost weight without trying. Which nursing assessment best documents this concern at baseline?

**Difficulty:** 🟢 Easy · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD · Original practice

- **A.** Only the patient's current height
- **B.** Recent measured weight trend and dietary intake
- **C.** Only the latest blood group result
- **D.** Only the patient's usual sleep duration

<details><summary>✅ Correct answer and detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: B. Recent measured weight trend and dietary intake**

**A. Kyun galat hai:** Height body size assess karne mein useful hai, lekin recent weight loss quantify nahi karti.

**B. Kyun sahi hai:** Recent weight trend aur food intake note karne se nutritional decline identify hota hai aur individualized nutrition assessment plan ban sakta hai.

**C. Kyun galat hai:** Blood group transfusion compatibility mein useful hai, lekin weight loss ki magnitude nahi batata.

**D. Kyun galat hai:** Sleep history overall assessment ka part hai, lekin nutritional decline ka direct record nahi hai.

**Exam clue:** Unintentional weight loss ko baseline weight aur intake history se assess karo.

</details>
