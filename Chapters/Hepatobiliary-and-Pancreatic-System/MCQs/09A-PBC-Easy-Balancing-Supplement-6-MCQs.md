### Q1. Which term describes yellowish lipid deposits around the eyelids that may occur in primary biliary cholangitis?

**Difficulty:** Easy · **Priority:** High · **Exam focus:** NORCET / HPRCA HP Staff Nurse / PGIMER · Original practice

- **A.** Conjunctivitis
- **B.** Ptosis
- **C.** Cataract
- **D.** Xanthelasma

<details><summary>✅ Correct answer and detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: D. Xanthelasma**

**A. Kyun galat hai:** Conjunctivitis conjunctiva inflammation hai aur redness ya discharge cause kar sakti hai.

**B. Kyun galat hai:** Ptosis upper eyelid drooping hai, lipid deposit nahi.

**C. Kyun galat hai:** Cataract eye lens opacity hai.

**D. Kyun sahi hai:** Xanthelasma eyelids ke aas paas yellow lipid deposits hain. Cholestatic disease mein lipid metabolism changes se ye ho sakte hain.

**Exam clue:** Xanthelasma refers to yellow eyelid plaques.

</details>

### Q2. Which sex is more frequently affected by primary biliary cholangitis?

**Difficulty:** Easy · **Priority:** High · **Exam focus:** NORCET / HPRCA HP Staff Nurse / PGIMER · Original practice

- **A.** Male exclusively
- **B.** Both sexes equally in every population
- **C.** Female
- **D.** Neither sex because it affects only infants

<details><summary>✅ Correct answer and detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: C. Female**

**A. Kyun galat hai:** Men PBC develop kar sakte hain, par disease male-exclusive nahi hai.

**B. Kyun galat hai:** Frequency generally women mein higher hai, though exact ratios population ke saath vary karte hain.

**C. Kyun sahi hai:** PBC women mein more common hai, lekin men bhi affected ho sakte hain. Sex alone diagnosis confirm nahi karta.

**D. Kyun galat hai:** PBC adult liver disease hai aur infants tak limited nahi hai.

**Exam clue:** PBC has a female predominance, not exclusivity.

</details>

### Q3. Which blood lipid abnormality may accompany chronic cholestasis in primary biliary cholangitis?

**Difficulty:** Easy · **Priority:** High · **Exam focus:** NORCET / HPRCA HP Staff Nurse / PGIMER · Original practice

- **A.** Low blood glucose by definition
- **B.** Hypercholesterolemia
- **C.** Low serum sodium by definition
- **D.** High serum creatinine by definition

<details><summary>✅ Correct answer and detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: B. Hypercholesterolemia**

**A. Kyun galat hai:** Hypoglycemia lipid abnormality nahi hai aur PBC ki defining finding nahi.

**B. Kyun sahi hai:** Bile flow disturbance lipid handling ko affect kar sakti hai, isliye cholesterol high ho sakta hai. Cardiovascular risk individual assessment se decide hota hai.

**C. Kyun galat hai:** Sodium electrolyte hai, cholesterol lipid hai.

**D. Kyun galat hai:** Creatinine renal marker hai, lipid abnormality nahi.

**Exam clue:** Cholestasis can alter serum lipid patterns.

</details>

### Q4. Which term refers to impaired bile formation or flow in primary biliary cholangitis?

**Difficulty:** Easy · **Priority:** High · **Exam focus:** NORCET / HPRCA HP Staff Nurse / PGIMER · Original practice

- **A.** Cholestasis
- **B.** Hemostasis
- **C.** Homeostasis
- **D.** Leukostasis

<details><summary>✅ Correct answer and detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: A. Cholestasis**

**A. Kyun sahi hai:** Bile ka normal flow impaired ho toh cholestasis kehte hain. Bile constituents retain ho sakte hain aur liver injury develop ho sakti hai.

**B. Kyun galat hai:** Hemostasis bleeding ko stop karne ki physiological process hai.

**C. Kyun galat hai:** Homeostasis body ke internal environment ko stable rakhne ki process hai.

**D. Kyun galat hai:** Leukostasis excessive abnormal white cells se microvascular problems ko describe karti hai.

**Exam clue:** Chole refers to bile.

</details>

### Q5. Which term describes excessive darkening of skin that can occur during chronic primary biliary cholangitis?

**Difficulty:** Easy · **Priority:** High · **Exam focus:** NORCET / HPRCA HP Staff Nurse / PGIMER · Original practice

- **A.** Hypopigmentation
- **B.** Cyanosis
- **C.** Hyperpigmentation
- **D.** Petechiae

<details><summary>✅ Correct answer and detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: C. Hyperpigmentation**

**A. Kyun galat hai:** Hypopigmentation pigment loss hai, darkening nahi.

**B. Kyun galat hai:** Cyanosis blue discoloration hai jo oxygenation ya perfusion se related ho sakti hai.

**C. Kyun sahi hai:** Hyperpigmentation skin pigment increase hone se dark appearance ko describe karta hai. PBC mein ye possible finding hai.

**D. Kyun galat hai:** Petechiae tiny bleeding spots hain, generalized pigment darkening nahi.

**Exam clue:** Hyperpigmentation means increased skin pigmentation.

</details>

### Q6. Which term describes loss of bile duct structures in progressive primary biliary cholangitis?

**Difficulty:** Easy · **Priority:** High · **Exam focus:** NORCET / HPRCA HP Staff Nurse / PGIMER · Original practice

- **A.** Neutropenia
- **B.** Thrombocytosis
- **C.** Polycythemia
- **D.** Ductopenia

<details><summary>✅ Correct answer and detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: D. Ductopenia**

**A. Kyun galat hai:** Neutropenia low neutrophil count hai, bile duct loss nahi.

**B. Kyun galat hai:** Thrombocytosis platelet count increase hai.

**C. Kyun galat hai:** Polycythemia red cell mass increase se related hai.

**D. Kyun sahi hai:** Ductopenia ka matlab bile ducts ki reduced number hai. Progressive small duct injury se bile flow impair ho sakta hai.

**Exam clue:** Ductopenia means loss or scarcity of ducts.

</details>
