### Q1. Which laboratory measurement directly quantifies circulating hepatitis B viral DNA?

**Difficulty:** Easy · **Priority:** HIGH YIELD · **Exam focus:** NORCET / HPRCA HP Staff Nurse / PGIMER · Original practice

- **A.** Serum albumin alone
- **B.** Quantitative HBV DNA assay
- **C.** Peripheral platelet count alone
- **D.** Anti-HBs antibody concentration

<details><summary>✅ Correct answer and detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: B. Quantitative HBV DNA assay**

**A. Kyun galat hai:** Albumin liver synthetic function reflect karta hai, viral DNA directly measure nahi karta.

**B. Kyun sahi hai:** Quantitative HBV DNA assay blood mein viral genetic material measure karti hai. Isse replication aur antiviral response assess karne mein help milti hai.

**C. Kyun galat hai:** Platelets fibrosis assessment mein useful ho sakte hain, viral load test nahi hain.

**D. Kyun galat hai:** Anti-HBs immunity-related marker hai, viral DNA quantity nahi batata.

**Exam clue:** HBV DNA is the viral-load measurement.

</details>

### Q2. A 42-year-old adult with chronic HBV has clinically established cirrhosis, normal ALT and low HBV DNA. Which treatment eligibility principle applies under WHO 2024 guidance?

**Difficulty:** Difficult · **Priority:** HIGH YIELD · **Exam focus:** NORCET / HPRCA HP Staff Nurse / PGIMER · Original practice

- **A.** Treatment is excluded whenever ALT is normal
- **B.** Wait until viral load exceeds one million IU/mL in every case
- **C.** Antiviral treatment is recommended regardless of ALT or HBV DNA when cirrhosis is established
- **D.** Treatment is indicated only after a variceal hemorrhage

<details><summary>✅ Correct answer and detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: C. Antiviral treatment is recommended regardless of ALT or HBV DNA when cirrhosis is established**

**A. Kyun galat hai:** Normal ALT established cirrhosis ka risk eliminate nahi karta.

**B. Kyun galat hai:** Universal million IU/mL threshold WHO criterion nahi hai.

**C. Kyun sahi hai:** Cirrhosis advanced liver disease hai. WHO 2024 mein confirmed cirrhosis treatment indication hai even when current ALT aur viral load low hon.

**D. Kyun galat hai:** Bleeding develop hone ka wait avoidable progression risk create karta hai.

**Exam clue:** Cirrhosis alone can establish WHO treatment eligibility.

</details>

### Q3. An adult with chronic HBV has AST 60 U/L, laboratory AST upper limit 40 U/L and platelets 150 times 10^9/L. What is the calculated APRI score?

**Difficulty:** Difficult · **Priority:** HIGH YIELD · **Exam focus:** NORCET / HPRCA HP Staff Nurse / PGIMER · Original practice

- **A.** 1.0
- **B.** 0.1
- **C.** 2.5
- **D.** 10.0

<details><summary>✅ Correct answer and detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: A. 1.0**

**A. Kyun sahi hai:** APRI formula mein AST divided by ULN, divided by platelet count in 10^9/L, times 100 hota hai. 60/40 = 1.5, then 1.5/150 times 100 = 1.0.

**B. Kyun galat hai:** 0.1 calculation mein decimal tenfold low ho gaya hai.

**C. Kyun galat hai:** 2.5 given values se nahi aata.

**D. Kyun galat hai:** 10.0 decimal tenfold high hai.

**Exam clue:** APRI = AST ratio divided by platelets times 100.

</details>

### Q4. Under WHO 2024 adult treatment criteria, what does an APRI score above 0.5 support when assessing chronic HBV?

**Difficulty:** Medium · **Priority:** HIGH YIELD · **Exam focus:** NORCET / HPRCA HP Staff Nurse / PGIMER · Original practice

- **A.** Proof of complete viral eradication
- **B.** A mandatory diagnosis of decompensated cirrhosis in every patient
- **C.** A reason to exclude all antiviral treatment
- **D.** Evidence of significant fibrosis for treatment eligibility, interpreted clinically

<details><summary>✅ Correct answer and detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: D. Evidence of significant fibrosis for treatment eligibility, interpreted clinically**

**A. Kyun galat hai:** APRI viral DNA ya eradication directly measure nahi karta.

**B. Kyun galat hai:** Significant fibrosis aur decompensated cirrhosis same stage nahi hain.

**C. Kyun galat hai:** Elevated APRI treatment consideration support kar sakta hai, exclusion nahi.

**D. Kyun sahi hai:** WHO 2024 adult eligibility mein APRI above 0.5 significant fibrosis ka noninvasive evidence hai. Test imperfect hai, isliye clinical context bhi important hai.

**Exam clue:** WHO 2024 uses a lower APRI threshold for significant fibrosis.

</details>

### Q5. A man with confirmed chronic HBV has HBV DNA 5,000 IU/mL and ALT 45 U/L, with no significant fibrosis. Which statement follows the WHO 2024 treatment criteria?

- **B.** His ALT alone establishes acute liver failure
- **C.** He meets the combined HBV DNA and ALT treatment criterion
- **D.** Treatment is prohibited until cirrhosis develops

<details><summary>✅ Correct answer and detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: C. He meets the combined HBV DNA and ALT treatment criterion**

**A. Kyun galat hai:** 20,000 ka older threshold universal current WHO criterion nahi hai.

**B. Kyun galat hai:** ALT 45 alone acute liver failure diagnose nahi karta.

**C. Kyun sahi hai:** WHO 2024 adults mein DNA above 2,000 IU/mL plus ALT above WHO ULN criterion hai. Male ULN 30 U/L use hoti hai, toh given results criterion meet karte hain.

**D. Kyun galat hai:** Cirrhosis ka wait necessary nahi when another criterion meets.

**Exam clue:** WHO 2024: DNA above 2,000 with elevated ALT.

</details>

### Q8. Which medicines are preferred first-line nucleos(t)ide analogues in WHO 2024 chronic HBV guidance for suitable patients?

- **B.** Metronidazole or paromomycin
- **C.** Amoxicillin or cefixime

<details><summary>✅ Correct answer and detailed explanation</summary>

**A. Kyun galat hai:** Ye drugs other viral infections mein use hoti hain, routine HBV suppression ke liye nahi.

**B. Kyun galat hai:** Ye antimicrobial agents amoebiasis jaise indications mein use ho sakte hain, HBV antivirals nahi.

**C. Kyun galat hai:** Antibacterial drugs HBV replication directly suppress nahi karti.

**D. Kyun sahi hai:** TDF aur entecavir HBV replication suppress karte hain aur resistance ka high genetic barrier rakhte hain. Patient-specific contraindications assess karte hain.

**Exam clue:** TDF or entecavir are WHO preferred regimens.

</details>

### Q9. Why is lamivudine monotherapy generally not preferred when high-barrier first-line HBV agents are suitable and available?

- **A.** Lamivudine always causes complete copper depletion
- **B.** It cannot have any antiviral activity against HBV
- **C.** It is a permanent vaccine rather than a medicine
- **D.** A lower genetic barrier can permit antiviral resistance during long-term treatment

<details><summary>✅ Correct answer and detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: D. A lower genetic barrier can permit antiviral resistance during long-term treatment**

**A. Kyun galat hai:** Copper depletion lamivudine choice ka defining issue nahi hai.

**B. Kyun galat hai:** Lamivudine HBV replication inhibit kar sakti hai, but resistance limitation hai.

**C. Kyun galat hai:** Ye antiviral drug hai, vaccine nahi.

**D. Kyun sahi hai:** Lamivudine HBV activity rakhti hai, lekin long-term monotherapy mein resistance develop hone ka risk higher hai. High-barrier agents preferred hain.

**Exam clue:** Prefer high resistance-barrier regimens.

</details>

<details><summary>✅ Correct answer and detailed explanation</summary>

**D. Kyun galat hai:** Calcium bone nutrition support kar sakta hai, antiviral regimen independently select nahi karte.

**Exam clue:** Choose an appropriate renal and bone-sparing option.

### Q12. A patient receiving TDF develops new normoglycemic glycosuria, hypophosphatemia and muscle weakness. Which concern should be escalated?

**Correct answer: C. Possible proximal renal tubular dysfunction**

**Exam clue:** Glycosuria with normal glucose plus low phosphate suggests tubular injury.
