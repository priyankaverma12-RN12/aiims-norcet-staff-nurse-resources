### Q1. What distinguishes spontaneous bacterial peritonitis (SBP) from secondary bacterial peritonitis?

**Difficulty:** 🟢 Easy · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD · Original practice

- **A.** Infection caused only by a perforated bowel
- **B.** Sterile ascites without inflammation
- **C.** Infection limited to the abdominal skin
- **D.** Ascitic fluid infection without a surgically treatable intra-abdominal source

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: D. Ascitic fluid infection without a surgically treatable intra-abdominal source**

**Option-wise explanation:**
- **A (Galat):** Bowel perforation se secondary peritonitis ho sakti hai jisme source control chahiye.
- **B (Galat):** Sterile uncomplicated ascites infection nahi hoti.
- **C (Galat):** Skin infection superficial tissue mein hoti hai, ascitic fluid infection se alag hai.
- **D (Sahi):** SBP mein ascitic fluid infect hoti hai bina perforation ya abscess jaise surgically treatable source ke. Source ka assessment important hai.

**Exam clue:** Spontaneous means no surgically treatable intra-abdominal source.
