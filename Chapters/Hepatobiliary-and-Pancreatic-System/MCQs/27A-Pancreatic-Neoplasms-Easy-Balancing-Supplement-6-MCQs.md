### Q1. Which modifiable exposure is an established risk factor for pancreatic cancer?

**Difficulty:** 🟢 Easy · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD · Original practice

- **A.** Cigarette smoking
- **B.** Routine seasonal influenza vaccination
- **C.** Regular physical activity
- **D.** Use of corrective eyeglasses

<details><summary>✅ Correct answer and detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: A. Cigarette smoking**

**A. Kyun sahi hai:** Tobacco smoke mein harmful chemicals pancreatic cells ke DNA ko damage kar sakte hain, isliye smoking risk badhati hai.

**B. Kyun galat hai:** Seasonal influenza vaccination infection prevention ke liye hoti hai aur pancreatic cancer ka established risk factor nahi hai.

**C. Kyun galat hai:** Regular physical activity healthy lifestyle ka part hai, ise pancreatic cancer ka established cause nahi maana jata.

**D. Kyun galat hai:** Corrective eyeglasses vision improve karte hain, pancreatic cancer ka risk factor nahi hain.

**Exam clue:** Smoking history pancreatic cancer assessment mein relevant hai.

</details>

### Q2. Which chronic inflammatory disease of the pancreas increases the risk of pancreatic cancer?

**Difficulty:** 🟢 Easy · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD · Original practice

- **A.** Chronic gastritis
- **B.** Chronic pancreatitis
- **C.** Chronic rhinitis
- **D.** Chronic cystitis

<details><summary>✅ Correct answer and detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: B. Chronic pancreatitis**

**A. Kyun galat hai:** Gastritis stomach lining ka inflammation hai aur yeh pancreatic cancer ka specific established inflammatory risk factor nahi hai.

**B. Kyun sahi hai:** Chronic pancreatitis mein pancreatic tissue ko long-term damage hota hai, jisse pancreatic malignancy ka risk badh sakta hai.

**C. Kyun galat hai:** Rhinitis nasal mucosa ko affect karta hai, pancreatic tissue ko chronically inflame nahi karta.

**D. Kyun galat hai:** Cystitis bladder ka inflammation hai aur pancreatic cancer se specific association nahi hai.

**Exam clue:** Long-term pancreatic inflammation cancer risk se associated hai.

</details>

### Q3. Which pain location is a recognized possible presenting symptom of pancreatic cancer?

**Difficulty:** 🟢 Easy · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD · Original practice

- **A.** Pain limited to the great toe
- **B.** Pain limited to the wrist joint
- **C.** Upper abdominal pain extending to the back
- **D.** Pain limited to the external ear

<details><summary>✅ Correct answer and detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: C. Upper abdominal pain extending to the back**

**A. Kyun galat hai:** Great toe pain ka cause gout ya local injury ho sakta hai, yeh pancreatic malignancy ka typical described pain pattern nahi hai.

**B. Kyun galat hai:** Wrist pain usually local musculoskeletal ya nerve causes ke assessment ki taraf le jata hai, pancreatic tumour ka characteristic pain nahi hai.

**C. Kyun sahi hai:** Pancreatic tumour upper abdominal pain produce kar sakta hai jo back tak feel ho, lekin yeh symptom alone diagnostic nahi hai.

**D. Kyun galat hai:** External ear pain ear-related disorders ka symptom ho sakta hai, pancreatic cancer ka typical pattern nahi hai.

**Exam clue:** Upper abdominal aur back pain ko clinical context mein assess karo.

</details>

### Q4. A patient receiving care for pancreatic cancer reports new unilateral painful leg swelling. Which complication should the nurse promptly assess and escalate?

**Difficulty:** 🟢 Easy · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD · Original practice

- **A.** Chronic stable peripheral neuropathy
- **B.** Uncomplicated osteoarthritis
- **C.** Iron deficiency without bleeding
- **D.** Possible deep vein thrombosis

<details><summary>✅ Correct answer and detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: D. Possible deep vein thrombosis**

**A. Kyun galat hai:** Neuropathy burning ya altered sensation produce kar sakti hai, lekin nayi painful unilateral swelling mein clot exclude karna important hai.

**B. Kyun galat hai:** Osteoarthritis joint pain de sakti hai, lekin cancer patient mein acute unilateral swelling ko sirf arthritis nahi samajhna chahiye.

**C. Kyun galat hai:** Iron deficiency fatigue de sakti hai, lekin acute unilateral leg swelling explain nahi karti.

**D. Kyun sahi hai:** Pancreatic cancer thrombosis ka risk badha sakta hai, isliye nayi painful unilateral leg swelling mein DVT ki prompt assessment zaroori hai.

**Exam clue:** Pancreatic malignancy ke saath new unilateral leg swelling clot ka warning sign ho sakti hai.

</details>

### Q5. Which nonmodifiable demographic characteristic is associated with increasing pancreatic cancer risk?

**Difficulty:** 🟢 Easy · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD · Original practice

- **A.** Advancing age
- **B.** Current tobacco use
- **C.** Excess body weight
- **D.** Physical inactivity

<details><summary>✅ Correct answer and detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: A. Advancing age**

**A. Kyun sahi hai:** Age badhne ke saath pancreatic cancer ka risk badh sakta hai aur age ko change nahi kiya ja sakta.

**B. Kyun galat hai:** Tobacco use risk factor hai lekin smoking cessation ke through is exposure ko modify kiya ja sakta hai.

**C. Kyun galat hai:** Excess body weight risk se associated ho sakta hai lekin lifestyle aur treatment se isko address kiya ja sakta hai.

**D. Kyun galat hai:** Physical inactivity ek behaviour hai jo patient ki capacity aur care plan ke hisab se modify ho sakta hai.

**Exam clue:** Age nonmodifiable hai, tobacco exposure modifiable hai.

</details>

### Q6. What should a nurse explain when a person with one pancreatic cancer risk factor asks whether cancer is inevitable?

**Difficulty:** 🟢 Easy · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD · Original practice

- **A.** One risk factor confirms the diagnosis
- **B.** Risk is increased but cancer is not inevitable
- **C.** Cancer is impossible without several risk factors
- **D.** Risk factors replace diagnostic investigations

<details><summary>✅ Correct answer and detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: B. Risk is increased but cancer is not inevitable**

**A. Kyun galat hai:** Risk factor diagnosis confirm nahi karta, diagnosis ke liye appropriate clinical evaluation chahiye.

**B. Kyun sahi hai:** Risk factor disease develop hone ki probability badha sakta hai, lekin har exposed person ko cancer nahi hota.

**C. Kyun galat hai:** Pancreatic cancer known risk factors ke bina bhi develop ho sakta hai, isliye multiple factors hona compulsory nahi hai.

**D. Kyun galat hai:** Risk factor history assessment guide karti hai lekin required investigations ko replace nahi karti.

**Exam clue:** Risk aur confirmed diagnosis ko kabhi ek na samjho.

</details>
