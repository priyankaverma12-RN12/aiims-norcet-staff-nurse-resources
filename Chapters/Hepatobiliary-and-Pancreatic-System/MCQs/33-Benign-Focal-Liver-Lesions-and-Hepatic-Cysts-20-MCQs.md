### Q1. Which benign hepatic lesion consists predominantly of vascular channels?

**Difficulty:** Easy · **Priority:** HIGH YIELD · Original practice

- **A.** Hepatocellular adenoma
- **B.** Focal nodular hyperplasia
- **C.** Hepatic hemangioma
- **D.** Simple biliary cyst

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: C. Hepatic hemangioma**

**Option-wise explanation:**
- **A (Galat):** Adenoma hepatocyte-derived lesion hai, vascular channels iska defining tissue nahi hai.
- **B (Galat):** FNH hyperplastic hepatocytes ka lesion hai.
- **C (Sahi):** Hemangioma mein benign vascular spaces hote hain, isliye imaging par characteristic blood-pool enhancement mil sakti hai.
- **D (Galat):** Simple cyst fluid-filled hoti hai, vascular channels ka mass nahi.

**Exam clue:** Hemangioma is a benign vascular lesion.

### Q3. Which contrast enhancement pattern is typical of a hepatic hemangioma?

- **C.** Enhancement limited to the extrahepatic bile duct
- **D.** Peripheral discontinuous nodular enhancement with progressive centripetal fill-in

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: D. Peripheral discontinuous nodular enhancement with progressive centripetal fill-in**

**Option-wise explanation:**
- **A (Galat):** Water attenuation without enhancement simple cyst ke favor mein ho sakti hai.
- **B (Galat):** Washout pattern typical hemangioma fill-in se different hai.
- **C (Galat):** Bile duct enhancement vascular liver lesion ka characteristic pattern nahi hai.
- **D (Sahi):** Hemangioma vascular spaces contrast ko periphery se gradually center ki taraf fill kar sakte hain. Radiologist complete pattern interpret karta hai.

**Exam clue:** Peripheral nodules with centripetal fill-in suggest hemangioma.
