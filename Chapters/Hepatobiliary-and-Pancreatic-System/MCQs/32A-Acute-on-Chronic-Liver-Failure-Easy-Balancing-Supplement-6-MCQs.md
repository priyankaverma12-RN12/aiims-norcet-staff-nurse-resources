### Q1. Which infectious complication can precipitate acute-on-chronic liver failure in a patient with cirrhosis?

**Difficulty:** 🟢 Easy · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD · Original practice

- **A.** Uncomplicated seasonal pollen allergy
- **B.** Stable refractive error
- **C.** Bacterial sepsis
- **D.** An old healed fracture

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: C. Bacterial sepsis**

**Option-wise explanation:**
- **A (Galat):** Pollen allergy usual ACLF infectious precipitant nahi hai.
- **B (Galat):** Refractive error infection nahi hai.
- **C (Sahi):** Sepsis systemic inflammation aur organ dysfunction trigger kar sakti hai. Cirrhosis patient mein infection ka prompt assessment important hai.
- **D (Galat):** Healed fracture active infection ka evidence nahi hai.

**Exam clue:** Sepsis is a recognized ACLF trigger.

</details>

### Q2. Which routine bedside measurement helps monitor renal perfusion and kidney function trends in a critically ill ACLF patient?

**Difficulty:** 🟢 Easy · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD · Original practice

- **A.** Pupil color
- **B.** Hearing acuity
- **C.** Chest circumference alone
- **D.** Urine output

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: D. Urine output**

**Option-wise explanation:**
- **A (Galat):** Pupil color kidney function measure nahi karta.
- **B (Galat):** Hearing acuity renal output assess nahi karti.
- **C (Galat):** Chest circumference urinary function ka substitute nahi hai.
- **D (Sahi):** Urine output trend renal perfusion aur AKI assessment mein useful hai. Creatinine aur clinical status ke saath interpret karo.

**Exam clue:** Track urine output during organ support.

</details>

### Q3. Which blood test is routinely used to assess the clotting-related component of liver synthetic dysfunction in ACLF?

**Difficulty:** 🟢 Easy · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD · Original practice

- **A.** Prothrombin time expressed as INR
- **B.** Serum amylase
- **C.** Serum troponin alone
- **D.** Serum uric acid alone

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: A. Prothrombin time expressed as INR**

**Option-wise explanation:**
- **A (Sahi):** INR clotting time standardize karta hai aur liver synthetic dysfunction assessment mein useful hai. Cirrhosis mein INR alone bleeding risk predict nahi karta.
- **B (Galat):** Amylase pancreatic injury assessment mein use hoti hai.
- **C (Galat):** Troponin cardiac injury marker hai.
- **D (Galat):** Uric acid clotting factor synthesis ka routine measure nahi hai.

**Exam clue:** INR is a synthetic-function marker, not a standalone bleeding predictor.

</details>

### Q4. Which routine infection-prevention action is essential before accessing an intravenous line in a patient with ACLF?

**Difficulty:** 🟢 Easy · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD · Original practice

- **A.** Touching the disinfected hub with bare fingers
- **B.** Hand hygiene and aseptic access technique
- **C.** Reusing a visibly contaminated connector
- **D.** Leaving the connector open to room air

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: B. Hand hygiene and aseptic access technique**

**Option-wise explanation:**
- **A (Galat):** Bare fingers disinfected surface contaminate kar sakti hain.
- **B (Sahi):** Critically ill cirrhosis patients infection-prone hote hain. Hand hygiene aur aseptic line access catheter-related infection risk reduce karte hain.
- **C (Galat):** Contaminated equipment infection risk badhata hai.
- **D (Galat):** Open hub contamination aur other complications ka risk create karta hai.

**Exam clue:** Use aseptic technique for line access.

</details>

### Q5. Which routine observation measures the frequency of breathing in a patient with ACLF?

**Difficulty:** 🟢 Easy · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD · Original practice

- **A.** Pulse pressure
- **B.** Body mass index
- **C.** Capillary refill time
- **D.** Respiratory rate

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: D. Respiratory rate**

**Option-wise explanation:**
- **A (Galat):** Pulse pressure systolic aur diastolic BP ka difference hai.
- **B (Galat):** BMI body size estimate hai.
- **C (Galat):** Capillary refill peripheral perfusion assess karta hai.
- **D (Sahi):** Respiratory rate breaths per minute record karti hai. Trend respiratory deterioration ka early clue ho sakta hai.

**Exam clue:** Count breaths and trend respiratory rate.

</details>

### Q6. Which healthcare setting is appropriate for an ACLF patient needing ongoing invasive ventilation and vasopressor support?

**Difficulty:** 🟢 Easy · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD · Original practice

- **A.** Routine unmonitored waiting area
- **B.** Outpatient physiotherapy room
- **C.** Intensive care unit
- **D.** Independent home care without support

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: C. Intensive care unit**

**Option-wise explanation:**
- **A (Galat):** Unmonitored area life-support care ke liye unsuitable hai.
- **B (Galat):** Outpatient physiotherapy acute organ support provide nahi karti.
- **C (Sahi):** Invasive ventilation aur vasopressors continuous monitoring aur trained critical care require karte hain. ICU multidisciplinary organ support provide karta hai.
- **D (Galat):** Independent home care unstable organ failure manage nahi kar sakti.

**Exam clue:** Organ support requires critical care.

</details>
