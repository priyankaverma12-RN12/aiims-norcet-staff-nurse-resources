### Q1. Which structural process is characteristic of established chronic pancreatitis?

**Difficulty:** 🟢 Easy · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD · Original practice

- **C.** An isolated infection of the gallbladder
- **D.** Reversible dilation of the renal pelvis

**Correct: B. Progressive fibrosis and loss of pancreatic tissue**

**Option-wise explanation:**

**Exam clue:** Chronic pancreatic injury leads to persistent structural damage.
