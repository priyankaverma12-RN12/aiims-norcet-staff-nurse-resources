### Q1. Which specimen is obtained during a core liver biopsy?

**Difficulty:** Easy · **Priority:** HIGH YIELD · **Exam focus:** NORCET / HPRCA HP Staff Nurse / PGIMER · Original practice

- **A.** A sample of bile only
- **B.** A sample of peripheral venous blood only
- **C.** A sample of gastric aspirate
- **D.** A small cylinder of liver tissue

<details><summary>✅ Correct answer and detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: D. A small cylinder of liver tissue**

**A. Kyun galat hai:** Bile sample fluid hota hai. Isse core tissue architecture assess nahi hoti.

**B. Kyun galat hai:** Blood tests liver ke markers batate hain, par core biopsy tissue leti hai.

**C. Kyun galat hai:** Gastric aspirate stomach contents hai. Liver histology isse nahi milti.

**D. Kyun sahi hai:** Core biopsy mein liver ka tissue sample milta hai. Pathologist cells aur tissue structure examine karta hai.

**Exam clue:** Core biopsy provides tissue architecture.

</details>

### Q4. What does non-lesional liver biopsy mean?

- **B.** Aspiration of an identified pancreatic cyst

<details><summary>✅ Correct answer and detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: A. Sampling liver parenchyma rather than a specific focal mass**

**A. Kyun sahi hai:** Non-lesional biopsy diffuse liver disease evaluate karne ke liye representative tissue leti hai.

**B. Kyun galat hai:** Pancreatic cyst sample alag organ se liya jaata hai.

**Exam clue:** Distinguish diffuse disease from a focal target.

- **B.** Bleeding
- **C.** Hypothyroidism
- **D.** Chronic hearing loss

<details><summary>✅ Correct answer and detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: B. Bleeding**

**A. Kyun galat hai:** Retention kuch procedures ya drugs ke baad ho sakti hai, par needle liver injury ka main vascular risk bleeding hai.

**B. Kyun sahi hai:** Liver mein blood supply bahut hoti hai. Needle se vessel injury hone par bleeding ho sakti hai.

**C. Kyun galat hai:** Thyroid hormone deficiency liver needle injury ka expected complication nahi hai.

**D. Kyun galat hai:** Hearing loss biopsy ke vascular puncture se directly expected nahi hoti.

**Exam clue:** Bleeding surveillance is central to aftercare.

<details><summary>✅ Correct answer and detailed explanation</summary>

</details>
