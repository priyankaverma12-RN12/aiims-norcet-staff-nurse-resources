### Q1. Which enzyme is inhibited by the hepatitis C medicine sofosbuvir?

**Difficulty:** Easy · **Priority:** High · **Exam focus:** NORCET / HPRCA HP Staff Nurse / PGIMER · Original practice

- **A.** NS5B RNA-dependent RNA polymerase
- **B.** HBV surface antigen
- **C.** Human ACE enzyme
- **D.** Bacterial DNA gyrase

<details><summary>✅ Correct answer and detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: A. NS5B RNA-dependent RNA polymerase**

**A. Kyun sahi hai:** Sofosbuvir HCV NS5B polymerase ko inhibit karta hai. Viral RNA replication isse rukti hai.

**B. Kyun galat hai:** HBsAg hepatitis B ka antigen hai. Sofosbuvir ka HCV-specific target alag hai.

**C. Kyun galat hai:** ACE blood pressure regulation se related enzyme hai. HCV replication ka drug target nahi.

**D. Kyun galat hai:** DNA gyrase bacterial enzyme hai. HCV RNA replication mein iska role nahi hai.

**Exam clue:** Sofosbuvir is an HCV NS5B inhibitor.

</details>

### Q2. Which HCV protein is targeted by velpatasvir?

**Difficulty:** Easy · **Priority:** High · **Exam focus:** NORCET / HPRCA HP Staff Nurse / PGIMER · Original practice

- **A.** NS5B exclusively
- **B.** HIV integrase
- **C.** Bacterial ribosomal 30S subunit
- **D.** NS5A

<details><summary>✅ Correct answer and detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: D. NS5A**

**A. Kyun galat hai:** NS5B sofosbuvir ka target hai. Combination ke dono drugs ka action alag hai.

**B. Kyun galat hai:** Integrase HIV replication ka enzyme hai. HCV velpatasvir ka target nahi.

**C. Kyun galat hai:** 30S bacterial protein synthesis se related hai. Velpatasvir antibacterial drug nahi hai.

**D. Kyun sahi hai:** Velpatasvir NS5A protein inhibitor hai. HCV replication complex aur viral assembly affect hoti hai.

**Exam clue:** Velpatasvir is an NS5A inhibitor.

</details>

### Q3. Which type of specimen is normally collected for quantitative HCV RNA testing?

**Difficulty:** Easy · **Priority:** High · **Exam focus:** NORCET / HPRCA HP Staff Nurse / PGIMER · Original practice

- **A.** A routine hair clipping
- **B.** Blood
- **C.** A nail scraping
- **D.** A throat swab

<details><summary>✅ Correct answer and detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: B. Blood**

**A. Kyun galat hai:** Hair clipping standard HCV viral-load specimen nahi hai.

**B. Kyun sahi hai:** HCV RNA blood-derived serum ya plasma mein laboratory method ke according measure hota hai. Correct tube aur handling verify karo.

**C. Kyun galat hai:** Nail scraping fungal examination mein useful ho sakti hai. HCV RNA monitoring ka standard specimen nahi.

**D. Kyun galat hai:** Throat swab respiratory infection assessment mein use ho sakta hai. HCV viral-load testing blood se hoti hai.

**Exam clue:** HCV RNA monitoring uses a blood specimen.

</details>

### Q4. What does the abbreviation SVR stand for in hepatitis C follow-up?

**Difficulty:** Easy · **Priority:** High · **Exam focus:** NORCET / HPRCA HP Staff Nurse / PGIMER · Original practice

- **A.** Serum vascular resistance
- **B.** Severe ventricular regurgitation
- **C.** Standard vaccine reaction
- **D.** Sustained virologic response

<details><summary>✅ Correct answer and detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: D. Sustained virologic response**

**A. Kyun galat hai:** Vascular resistance hemodynamics se related term hai. HCV treatment outcome nahi.

**B. Kyun galat hai:** Ventricular regurgitation cardiac terminology hai. SVR ka hepatitis-specific expansion nahi.

**C. Kyun galat hai:** Vaccine reaction immunization adverse event ho sakta hai. SVR is context mein treatment response hai.

**D. Kyun sahi hai:** SVR ka matlab antiviral treatment ke baad sustained virologic response hai. HCV RNA follow-up se assess hota hai.

**Exam clue:** SVR means sustained virologic response.

</details>

### Q5. Which additional viral infection should be assessed before hepatitis C DAA therapy because treatment may be associated with reactivation?

**Difficulty:** Easy · **Priority:** High · **Exam focus:** NORCET / HPRCA HP Staff Nurse / PGIMER · Original practice

- **A.** Common rhinovirus infection exclusively
- **B.** Rotavirus infection exclusively
- **C.** Hepatitis B
- **D.** Seasonal influenza exclusively

<details><summary>✅ Correct answer and detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: C. Hepatitis B**

**A. Kyun galat hai:** Rhinovirus respiratory infection hai. DAA-associated HBV reactivation screening ka substitute nahi.

**B. Kyun galat hai:** Rotavirus gastroenteritis cause karta hai. HCV DAA initiation ke specific reactivation concern se alag hai.

**C. Kyun sahi hai:** DAA therapy ke dauran kuch HBV-infected patients mein HBV reactivation ho sakti hai. Baseline HBV serology aur management plan important hai.

**D. Kyun galat hai:** Influenza prevention useful hai, lekin HBV reactivation risk ko assess nahi karti.

**Exam clue:** Screen for HBV before DAA treatment.

</details>

### Q6. Which route is used for the standard tablet combination of glecaprevir/pibrentasvir?

**Difficulty:** Easy · **Priority:** High · **Exam focus:** NORCET / HPRCA HP Staff Nurse / PGIMER · Original practice

- **A.** Intrathecal
- **B.** Intra-articular
- **C.** Oral
- **D.** Nebulized inhalation

<details><summary>✅ Correct answer and detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: C. Oral**

**A. Kyun galat hai:** Intrathecal route spinal fluid access karta hai. Yeh HCV combination us route se nahi di jaati.

**B. Kyun galat hai:** Intra-articular injections joint mein di jaati hain. HCV treatment tablet oral hoti hai.

**C. Kyun sahi hai:** Glecaprevir/pibrentasvir oral tablets ke form mein diya jaata hai. Food aur dosing instructions product-specific follow karo.

**D. Kyun galat hai:** Nebulization respiratory drugs deliver karti hai. Yeh systemic antiviral tablet ki route nahi hai.

**Exam clue:** Modern DAA tablet combinations are oral medicines.

</details>
