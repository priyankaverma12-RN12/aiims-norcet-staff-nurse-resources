### Q1. Which interval best represents the usual incubation range reported by WHO for hepatitis E?

**Difficulty:** Easy · **Priority:** High · **Exam focus:** NORCET / HPRCA HP Staff Nurse / PGIMER · Original practice

- **A.** 2–10 minutes
- **B.** 2–10 weeks
- **C.** 2–10 years
- **D.** 2–10 hours

<details><summary>✅ Correct answer and detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: B. 2–10 weeks**

**A. Kyun galat hai:** Minutes mein symptoms develop hona HEV incubation se match nahi karta.

**B. Kyun sahi hai:** WHO ke mutabik HEV exposure ke baad incubation generally 2 se 10 weeks hota hai. Exposure history recent weeks cover karti hai.

**C. Kyun galat hai:** Years-long interval acute hepatitis E ka typical incubation nahi hai.

**D. Kyun galat hai:** Hours mein toxin-related illness ho sakti hai, HEV incubation usually weeks hota hai.

**Exam clue:** HEV incubation is measured in weeks.

</details>

### Q2. Which early symptom of acute hepatitis E refers to loss of appetite?

**Difficulty:** Easy · **Priority:** High · **Exam focus:** NORCET / HPRCA HP Staff Nurse / PGIMER · Original practice

- **A.** Dysphagia
- **B.** Anorexia
- **C.** Hematemesis
- **D.** Orthopnea

<details><summary>✅ Correct answer and detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: B. Anorexia**

**A. Kyun galat hai:** Dysphagia ka matlab swallowing mein difficulty hai, appetite loss nahi.

**B. Kyun sahi hai:** Anorexia ka matlab appetite kam hona hai. HEV ke early symptomatic phase mein reduced appetite ho sakti hai.

**C. Kyun galat hai:** Hematemesis vomiting of blood hai. Yeh appetite loss ka term nahi.

**D. Kyun galat hai:** Orthopnea letne par breathlessness hai. Yeh eating desire se related symptom nahi.

**Exam clue:** Anorexia is reduced appetite, not difficulty swallowing.

</details>

### Q3. Which HEV genotypes are predominantly associated with human waterborne outbreaks?

**Difficulty:** Easy · **Priority:** High · **Exam focus:** NORCET / HPRCA HP Staff Nurse / PGIMER · Original practice

- **A.** Genotypes 3 and 4 exclusively
- **B.** HCV genotypes 1 and 2
- **C.** Genotypes 1 and 2
- **D.** HBV genotypes A and D

<details><summary>✅ Correct answer and detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: C. Genotypes 1 and 2**

**A. Kyun galat hai:** HEV genotypes 3 aur 4 frequently zoonotic transmission se associated hote hain. Waterborne epidemic pattern 1 aur 2 se linked hai.

**B. Kyun galat hai:** HCV genotypes hepatitis C virus ke hain, HEV ke nahi.

**C. Kyun sahi hai:** HEV genotypes 1 aur 2 predominantly humans infect karte hain aur contaminated water outbreaks se associated hain.

**D. Kyun galat hai:** HBV genotypes hepatitis B virus ki classification hain. HEV classification alag hai.

**Exam clue:** HEV 1 and 2 are associated with waterborne outbreaks.

</details>

### Q4. Which visible urine change can accompany jaundice in symptomatic acute hepatitis E?

**Difficulty:** Easy · **Priority:** High · **Exam focus:** NORCET / HPRCA HP Staff Nurse / PGIMER · Original practice

- **A.** Milky urine in every patient
- **B.** Bright blue urine as the defining sign
- **C.** Dark urine
- **D.** Absence of urine in every uncomplicated case

<details><summary>✅ Correct answer and detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: C. Dark urine**

**A. Kyun galat hai:** Milky urine ke other causes ho sakte hain. HEV mein universally expected nahi hai.

**B. Kyun galat hai:** Bright blue urine HEV diagnosis ka characteristic sign nahi hai.

**C. Kyun sahi hai:** Conjugated bilirubin urine mein excrete ho sakta hai aur urine dark ho sakta hai. Yeh symptomatic hepatitis ka supportive finding hai.

**D. Kyun galat hai:** Anuria serious renal or circulatory problem ho sakti hai. Uncomplicated HEV mein har patient anuric nahi hota.

**Exam clue:** Dark urine can accompany hepatitis-associated jaundice.

</details>

### Q5. Which prevention category includes access to sanitary toilets and safe disposal of human feces during hepatitis E control?

**Difficulty:** Easy · **Priority:** High · **Exam focus:** NORCET / HPRCA HP Staff Nurse / PGIMER · Original practice

- **A.** Sanitation
- **B.** Vector fogging alone
- **C.** Air filtration alone
- **D.** Radiation protection

<details><summary>✅ Correct answer and detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: A. Sanitation**

**A. Kyun sahi hai:** Safe fecal disposal water contamination reduce karti hai. HEV fecal-oral spread prevention ke liye sanitation important hai.

**B. Kyun galat hai:** Vector fogging mosquito control ke liye hai. Waterborne HEV prevention mein sanitation key hai.

**C. Kyun galat hai:** Air filtration respiratory particles reduce karti hai. Fecal contamination control nahi karti.

**D. Kyun galat hai:** Radiation protection unrelated occupational hazard ke liye hai. HEV transmission water sanitation se address hoti hai.

**Exam clue:** Sanitation interrupts fecal contamination of water.

</details>

### Q6. Which general infection prevention action should be performed after toilet use to help interrupt fecal-oral HEV spread?

**Difficulty:** Easy · **Priority:** High · **Exam focus:** NORCET / HPRCA HP Staff Nurse / PGIMER · Original practice

- **A.** Wear a surgical mask instead of washing hands
- **B.** Use only perfume on the hands
- **C.** Rinse gloves and reuse them as a substitute for hand hygiene
- **D.** Wash hands thoroughly with soap and safe water

<details><summary>✅ Correct answer and detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: D. Wash hands thoroughly with soap and safe water**

**A. Kyun galat hai:** Mask respiratory droplets ke liye useful ho sakta hai. Contaminated hands clean nahi karta.

**B. Kyun galat hai:** Perfume cleaning agent nahi hai aur fecal contamination reliably remove nahi karta.

**C. Kyun galat hai:** Gloves hand hygiene replace nahi karte. Single-use gloves ka safe disposal aur handwashing important hai.

**D. Kyun sahi hai:** Soap aur safe water se handwashing fecal contamination remove karne mein help karta hai. Food handling se pehle bhi hygiene important hai.

**Exam clue:** Handwashing after toilet use reduces fecal-oral transmission.

</details>
