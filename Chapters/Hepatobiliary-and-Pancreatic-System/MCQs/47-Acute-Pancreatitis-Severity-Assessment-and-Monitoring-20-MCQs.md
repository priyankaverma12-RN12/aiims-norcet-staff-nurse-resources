### Q1. What does SIRS stand for in acute pancreatitis severity assessment?

**Difficulty:** Easy · **Priority:** HIGH YIELD · **Exam focus:** NORCET / HPRCA HP Staff Nurse / PGIMER · Original practice

- **A.** Serum insulin resistance score
- **B.** Systemic inflammatory response syndrome
- **C.** Surgical incision recovery scale
- **D.** Standard intestinal rehydration schedule

<details><summary>✅ Correct answer and detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: B. Systemic inflammatory response syndrome**

**A. Kyun galat hai:** Insulin resistance glucose regulation se related hai. SIRS iska abbreviation nahi hai.

**B. Kyun sahi hai:** SIRS ka matlab body-wide inflammatory response hai. Pancreatitis mein ye local pancreatic swelling se aage systemic reaction indicate kar sakta hai.

**C. Kyun galat hai:** Incision recovery wound assessment hai. SIRS systemic inflammation describe karta hai.

**D. Kyun galat hai:** Rehydration fluid treatment hai. SIRS ek clinical inflammatory response hai.

**Exam clue:** SIRS refers to systemic inflammation.

</details>

### Q2. What is the primary purpose of the BISAP score in acute pancreatitis?

**Difficulty:** Easy · **Priority:** HIGH YIELD · **Exam focus:** NORCET / HPRCA HP Staff Nurse / PGIMER · Original practice

- **A.** Early estimation of risk of severe outcomes
- **B.** Confirmation of every pancreatic infection
- **C.** Measurement of pancreatic enzyme replacement dose
- **D.** Selection of the exact type of gallstone

<details><summary>✅ Correct answer and detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: A. Early estimation of risk of severe outcomes**

**A. Kyun sahi hai:** BISAP bedside clinical information se risk estimate karta hai. Isse observation level aur further review plan karne mein help mil sakti hai.

**B. Kyun galat hai:** BISAP infection culture ya definitive diagnostic test nahi hai.

**C. Kyun galat hai:** Enzyme dose exocrine insufficiency ke hisaab se prescribe hoti hai. BISAP dose calculate nahi karta.

**D. Kyun galat hai:** Gallstone type imaging ya analysis se assess hota hai. BISAP prognosis ke liye hai.

**Exam clue:** A risk score is not an etiologic diagnosis.

</details>

</details>

### Q9. An acute pancreatitis patient has organ failure that resolves within 24 hours and no persistent organ failure. Which revised Atlanta severity category is appropriate?

- **B.** Severe acute pancreatitis solely because any failure occurred
- **C.** Moderately severe acute pancreatitis
- **D.** Chronic pancreatitis

<details><summary>✅ Correct answer and detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: C. Moderately severe acute pancreatitis**

**A. Kyun galat hai:** Mild category mein organ failure nahi hoti. Temporary failure bhi category change karti hai.

**B. Kyun galat hai:** Severe category mein persistent organ failure chahiye. Transient episode alag category mein hai.

**C. Kyun sahi hai:** Short-duration organ failure transient hai. Persistent organ failure ke bina moderately severe category fit hoti hai.

**D. Kyun galat hai:** Chronic disease structural long-term changes describe karti hai. Acute severity category se alag hai.

**Exam clue:** Transient organ failure is not mild disease.

</details>

### Q10. A patient has typical acute pancreatitis, a clear diagnosis and improving symptoms 12 hours after admission. What is the ACG 2024 approach to routine CT solely for severity prediction at this point?

**Difficulty:** Medium · **Priority:** HIGH YIELD · **Exam focus:** NORCET / HPRCA HP Staff Nurse / PGIMER · Original practice

- **B.** Use CT instead of clinical observation
- **C.** Delay any clinically indicated CT until the patient is discharged
- **D.** Avoid routine early CT unless an additional clinical indication exists

<details><summary>✅ Correct answer and detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: D. Avoid routine early CT unless an additional clinical indication exists**

**A. Kyun galat hai:** Frequent unnecessary CT radiation aur contrast risk badha sakti hai.

**B. Kyun galat hai:** Imaging vitals, organ function aur clinical reassessment ka substitute nahi hai.

**C. Kyun galat hai:** Uncertain diagnosis ya deterioration mein imaging urgent ho sakti hai. Routine avoidance ka matlab prohibition nahi hai.

**D. Kyun sahi hai:** Early scan necrosis extent reliably show nahi kar sakta aur routine severity prediction ke liye recommended nahi hai. New diagnostic concern ho toh imaging plan change ho sakta hai.

**Exam clue:** Early CT is selective, not automatic.

</details>

<details><summary>✅ Correct answer and detailed explanation</summary>
