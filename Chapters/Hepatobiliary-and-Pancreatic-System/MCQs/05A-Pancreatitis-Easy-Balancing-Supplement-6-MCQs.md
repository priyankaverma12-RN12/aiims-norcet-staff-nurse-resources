### Q1. Which region of the abdomen typically contains the pancreatic body?

**Difficulty:** Easy · **Priority:** High · **Exam focus:** NORCET / HPRCA HP Staff Nurse / PGIMER · Original practice

- **A.** Pelvis behind the urinary bladder
- **B.** Right lower abdominal wall
- **C.** Left thigh
- **D.** Upper abdomen behind the stomach

<details><summary>✅ Correct answer and detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: D. Upper abdomen behind the stomach**

**A. Kyun galat hai:** Pelvic location pancreas ki normal anatomy nahi hai.

**B. Kyun galat hai:** Appendix right lower abdomen ke paas hota hai, pancreatic body nahi.

**C. Kyun galat hai:** Thigh lower limb ka part hai aur pancreas abdomen mein hota hai.

**D. Kyun sahi hai:** Pancreas upper abdomen mein stomach ke peeche hota hai. Is position ki wajah se pancreatic symptoms upper abdominal region mein feel ho sakte hain.

**Exam clue:** Pancreas lies behind the stomach.

</details>

### Q2. What does the term recurrent acute pancreatitis refer to?

**Difficulty:** Easy · **Priority:** High · **Exam focus:** NORCET / HPRCA HP Staff Nurse / PGIMER · Original practice

- **A.** A single uninterrupted lifelong episode of pain
- **B.** Separate episodes of acute pancreatic inflammation
- **C.** Inflammation limited to the gallbladder
- **D.** A pancreatic tumor by definition

<details><summary>✅ Correct answer and detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: B. Separate episodes of acute pancreatic inflammation**

**A. Kyun galat hai:** Persistent pain chronic disease mein ho sakta hai, lekin separate recurrent attacks se different hai.

**B. Kyun sahi hai:** Recurrent acute episodes mein patient ko distinct attacks hote hain. Underlying cause evaluate karna important hai.

**C. Kyun galat hai:** Gallbladder inflammation cholecystitis hai.

**D. Kyun galat hai:** Tumor abnormal tissue growth hai. Recurrent inflammation automatically tumor nahi hoti.

**Exam clue:** Recurrent means more than one distinct attack.

</details>

### Q3. Which symptom commonly accompanies upper abdominal pain during acute pancreatitis?

**Difficulty:** Easy · **Priority:** High · **Exam focus:** NORCET / HPRCA HP Staff Nurse / PGIMER · Original practice

- **A.** Nausea and vomiting
- **B.** Painless hearing loss
- **C.** Isolated itchy scalp
- **D.** Sudden loss of hair color

<details><summary>✅ Correct answer and detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: A. Nausea and vomiting**

**A. Kyun sahi hai:** Pancreatic inflammation ke saath gastrointestinal symptoms jaise nausea aur vomiting common ho sakte hain.

**B. Kyun galat hai:** Hearing loss acute pancreatic inflammation ka typical accompanying symptom nahi hai.

**C. Kyun galat hai:** Scalp itch common pancreatic symptom nahi hai.

**D. Kyun galat hai:** Hair color change acute pancreatitis ka typical symptom nahi hai.

**Exam clue:** Pain with nausea and vomiting is a common pattern.

</details>

### Q4. Which substance produced by the exocrine pancreas helps neutralize acidic gastric contents in the duodenum?

**Difficulty:** Easy · **Priority:** High · **Exam focus:** NORCET / HPRCA HP Staff Nurse / PGIMER · Original practice

- **A.** Hydrochloric acid
- **B.** Bilirubin
- **C.** Bicarbonate
- **D.** Hemoglobin

<details><summary>✅ Correct answer and detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: C. Bicarbonate**

**A. Kyun galat hai:** Hydrochloric acid stomach se aata hai aur neutralize hone wala acid hai.

**B. Kyun galat hai:** Bilirubin bile pigment hai, pancreatic alkaline secretion nahi.

**C. Kyun sahi hai:** Pancreatic duct cells bicarbonate-rich fluid se acidic chyme neutralize karne mein help karti hain.

**D. Kyun galat hai:** Hemoglobin oxygen carrier hai, duodenal neutralizing secretion nahi.

**Exam clue:** Pancreatic bicarbonate buffers gastric acid.

</details>

### Q5. Which blood electrolyte elevation is a recognized metabolic cause of acute pancreatitis?

**Difficulty:** Easy · **Priority:** High · **Exam focus:** NORCET / HPRCA HP Staff Nurse / PGIMER · Original practice

- **A.** Chloride within reference range
- **B.** Calcium
- **C.** Normal sodium
- **D.** Normal potassium

<details><summary>✅ Correct answer and detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: B. Calcium**

**A. Kyun galat hai:** Normal chloride metabolic pancreatitis ka recognized cause nahi hai.

**B. Kyun sahi hai:** Hypercalcemia selected cases mein pancreatitis ka cause ho sakti hai. Clinical evaluation se actual cause establish hota hai.

**C. Kyun galat hai:** Normal sodium pancreatitis etiology suggest nahi karta.

**D. Kyun galat hai:** Normal potassium pancreatic inflammation ka metabolic trigger nahi hai.

**Exam clue:** Hypercalcemia is a possible metabolic cause.

</details>

### Q6. Which professional can support an individualized meal plan when pancreatic disease affects nutrition?

**Difficulty:** Easy · **Priority:** High · **Exam focus:** NORCET / HPRCA HP Staff Nurse / PGIMER · Original practice

- **A.** Audiologist
- **B.** Optometrist
- **C.** Radiographer alone
- **D.** Registered dietitian

<details><summary>✅ Correct answer and detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: D. Registered dietitian**

**A. Kyun galat hai:** Audiologist hearing assess karta hai, nutrition plan nahi.

**B. Kyun galat hai:** Optometrist vision assessment karta hai.

**C. Kyun galat hai:** Radiographer imaging perform karta hai. Diet planning trained nutrition professional ka role hai.

**D. Kyun sahi hai:** Dietitian symptoms, nutritional requirements aur treatment plan ke hisaab se intake plan karne mein help karta hai.

**Exam clue:** Nutrition support can be multidisciplinary.

</details>
