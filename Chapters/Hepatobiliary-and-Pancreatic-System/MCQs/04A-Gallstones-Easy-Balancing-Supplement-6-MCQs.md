### Q1. Which substance forms the major component of cholesterol gallstones?

**Difficulty:** Easy · **Priority:** High · **Exam focus:** NORCET / HPRCA HP Staff Nurse / PGIMER · Original practice

- **A.** Cholesterol
- **B.** Calcium oxalate
- **C.** Uric acid
- **D.** Hemoglobin

<details><summary>✅ Correct answer and detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: A. Cholesterol**

**A. Kyun sahi hai:** Cholesterol bile mein excess ho kar crystals bana sakta hai. Ye crystals cholesterol stone develop kar sakte hain.

**B. Kyun galat hai:** Calcium oxalate urinary stones mein common hota hai, cholesterol stone ka main component nahi.

**C. Kyun galat hai:** Uric acid kidney stones aur gout se related ho sakta hai.

**D. Kyun galat hai:** Hemoglobin red cells ka oxygen-carrying protein hai. Cholesterol gallstone primarily isse nahi banta.

**Exam clue:** Cholesterol stones are named for their main component.

</details>

### Q2. Which bile pigment contributes to the formation of pigment gallstones?

**Difficulty:** Easy · **Priority:** High · **Exam focus:** NORCET / HPRCA HP Staff Nurse / PGIMER · Original practice

- **A.** Insulin
- **B.** Creatinine
- **C.** Bilirubin
- **D.** Pepsin

<details><summary>✅ Correct answer and detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: C. Bilirubin**

**A. Kyun galat hai:** Insulin glucose control hormone hai, bile pigment nahi.

**B. Kyun galat hai:** Creatinine muscle metabolism waste hai aur kidney test mein measure hota hai.

**C. Kyun sahi hai:** Bilirubin bile pigment hai. Iske calcium salts pigment stones ka part ho sakte hain.

**D. Kyun galat hai:** Pepsin stomach mein protein digestion karta hai.

**Exam clue:** Pigment stones contain bilirubin derivatives.

</details>

### Q3. Which condition increases the risk of pigment gallstones through chronic red cell destruction?

**Difficulty:** Easy · **Priority:** High · **Exam focus:** NORCET / HPRCA HP Staff Nurse / PGIMER · Original practice

- **A.** Iron deficiency from low dietary intake
- **B.** Hemolytic anemia
- **C.** Uncomplicated seasonal allergy
- **D.** Isolated refractive error

<details><summary>✅ Correct answer and detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: B. Hemolytic anemia**

**A. Kyun galat hai:** Iron deficiency alone increased red cell breakdown ka typical mechanism nahi hai.

**B. Kyun sahi hai:** Hemolysis se red cells break hote hain aur bilirubin production badhti hai. Isse pigment stones ka risk increase ho sakta hai.

**C. Kyun galat hai:** Allergic rhinitis red cell destruction ka usual cause nahi hai.

**D. Kyun galat hai:** Refractive error eye focusing issue hai, hemolysis nahi.

**Exam clue:** Chronic hemolysis increases bilirubin load.

</details>

### Q4. What is the term for gallstones that cause no symptoms?

**Difficulty:** Easy · **Priority:** High · **Exam focus:** NORCET / HPRCA HP Staff Nurse / PGIMER · Original practice

- **A.** Acute cholangitis
- **B.** Gallbladder empyema
- **C.** Biliary colic
- **D.** Silent gallstones

<details><summary>✅ Correct answer and detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: D. Silent gallstones**

**A. Kyun galat hai:** Cholangitis bile duct infection hai aur clinically serious ho sakti hai.

**B. Kyun galat hai:** Empyema gallbladder mein pus collection hai.

**C. Kyun galat hai:** Colic symptomatic pain episode hai, silent stone nahi.

**D. Kyun sahi hai:** Silent ka matlab patient ko stone-related symptoms nahi hain. Most such stones routine treatment require nahi karte.

**Exam clue:** Incidental stones can be asymptomatic.

</details>

### Q5. Which weight-loss pattern is associated with an increased risk of gallstone formation?

**Difficulty:** Easy · **Priority:** High · **Exam focus:** NORCET / HPRCA HP Staff Nurse / PGIMER · Original practice

- **A.** Gradual weight reduction under a health plan
- **B.** Stable healthy body weight
- **C.** Very rapid weight loss
- **D.** Routine daily walking without rapid weight loss

<details><summary>✅ Correct answer and detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: C. Very rapid weight loss**

**A. Kyun galat hai:** Gradual planned loss rapid crash dieting se safer approach ho sakti hai.

**B. Kyun galat hai:** Healthy stable weight gallstone prevention mein supportive ho sakta hai.

**C. Kyun sahi hai:** Rapid weight loss bile composition aur gallbladder emptying affect kar sakta hai. Isse stone formation ka risk badh sakta hai.

**D. Kyun galat hai:** Walking alone rapid weight loss ka equivalent nahi hai.

**Exam clue:** Avoid crash dieting.

</details>

### Q6. Which duct connects the gallbladder to the extrahepatic biliary system?

**Difficulty:** Easy · **Priority:** High · **Exam focus:** NORCET / HPRCA HP Staff Nurse / PGIMER · Original practice

- **A.** Cystic duct
- **B.** Ureter
- **C.** Thoracic duct
- **D.** Parotid duct

<details><summary>✅ Correct answer and detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: A. Cystic duct**

**A. Kyun sahi hai:** Cystic duct gallbladder ko main bile pathway se connect karti hai. Isse bile gallbladder ke andar ya bahar ja sakti hai.

**B. Kyun galat hai:** Ureter kidney se bladder tak urine le jaata hai.

**C. Kyun galat hai:** Thoracic duct lymphatic drainage ka part hai.

**D. Kyun galat hai:** Parotid duct saliva mouth mein le jaati hai.

**Exam clue:** The cystic duct is the gallbladder connection.

</details>
