### Q1. A patient with cirrhosis has suspected acute esophageal variceal hemorrhage. Which prescribed drug class should be started promptly while endoscopy is arranged?

**Difficulty:** Medium · **Priority:** HIGH YIELD · **Exam focus:** NORCET / HPRCA HP Staff Nurse / PGIMER · Original practice

- **A.** Proton pump inhibitor as the only treatment
- **B.** Oral bulk-forming laxative
- **C.** Vasoactive therapy such as octreotide
- **D.** Long-term oral iron alone

<details><summary>✅ Correct answer and detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: C. Vasoactive therapy such as octreotide**

**A. Kyun galat hai:** PPI acid secretion kam karta hai, lekin variceal pressure ko target nahi karta. Isko akela adequate therapy nahi maan sakte.

**B. Kyun galat hai:** Bulk laxative constipation ke liye hai aur active hemorrhage control nahi karta.

**C. Kyun sahi hai:** Vasoactive therapy splanchnic blood flow aur portal pressure ko kam karne mein help karti hai. Suspected variceal bleed mein endoscopy ka wait kiye bina team isko start kar sakti hai.

**D. Kyun galat hai:** Iron deficiency ko baad mein correct kar sakte hain, lekin oral iron acute bleeding ko control nahi karta.

**Exam clue:** Suspected variceal bleeding mein vasoactive therapy early start hoti hai.

</details>

<details><summary>✅ Correct answer and detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: A. Bacterial infection is a recognized complication and prophylaxis improves important outcomes**

**Exam clue:** Infection absent dikhne par bhi cirrhotic GI bleed mein prophylaxis indicated ho sakti hai.

### Q3. A hemodynamically stable adult with controlled acute variceal bleeding, no cardiac ischemia and hemoglobin 7.6 g/dL is prescribed red cells solely to raise hemoglobin to 12 g/dL. What should the nurse do?

**Difficulty:** Difficult · **Priority:** HIGH YIELD · **Exam focus:** NORCET / HPRCA HP Staff Nurse / PGIMER · Original practice

- **A.** Administer additional units independently until 14 g/dL
- **B.** Withhold all blood products permanently
- **C.** Replace red cells with albumin to correct anemia
- **D.** Clarify the liberal target with the treating team

<details><summary>✅ Correct answer and detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: D. Clarify the liberal target with the treating team**

**A. Kyun galat hai:** Independent transfusion target change unsafe hai aur volume overload ka risk badha sakta hai.

**B. Kyun galat hai:** Kabhi transfusion life-saving hoti hai. Isliye permanent prohibition correct nahi hai.

**C. Kyun galat hai:** Albumin plasma volume support kar sakta hai, lekin oxygen-carrying red cells replace nahi karta.

**D. Kyun sahi hai:** Stable selected patients mein conservative transfusion strategy recommend hoti hai. Unnecessarily high target portal pressure aur volume burden badha sakta hai. Active hemorrhage, ischemia aur shock mein targets individualize hote hain.

**Exam clue:** Stable bleed mein restrictive strategy, shock ya ischemia mein individualized decision.

</details>

**D. Kyun galat hai:** Bleeding stop hui hai ya nahi, isko ek hemoglobin result se prove nahi kar sakte.

**Exam clue:** Acute hemorrhage mein clinical shock ko isolated hemoglobin se upar priority do.
