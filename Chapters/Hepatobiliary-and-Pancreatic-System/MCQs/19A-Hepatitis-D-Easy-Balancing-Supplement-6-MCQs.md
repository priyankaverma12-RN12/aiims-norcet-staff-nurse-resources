### Q1. Which descriptive name is used for hepatitis D virus?

**Difficulty:** Easy · **Priority:** High · **Exam focus:** NORCET / HPRCA HP Staff Nurse / PGIMER · Original practice

- **A.** Dengue virus
- **B.** Diphtheria bacillus
- **C.** Deltacoronavirus
- **D.** Delta virus

<details><summary>✅ Correct answer and detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: D. Delta virus**

**A. Kyun galat hai:** Dengue mosquito-borne virus hai. Hepatitis delta virus se different hai.

**B. Kyun galat hai:** Diphtheria bacterium se hoti hai. HDV viral hepatitis agent hai.

**C. Kyun galat hai:** Deltacoronavirus coronavirus group hai. Hepatitis D ka alternate naam delta virus hai.

**D. Kyun sahi hai:** Hepatitis D virus ko delta virus bhi kehte hain. HDV hepatitis B ke context mein infection cause karta hai.

**Exam clue:** Hepatitis D is also called delta hepatitis.

</details>

### Q2. What is the nature of the hepatitis D viral genome?

**Difficulty:** Easy · **Priority:** High · **Exam focus:** NORCET / HPRCA HP Staff Nurse / PGIMER · Original practice

- **A.** Linear double-stranded DNA
- **B.** Circular single-stranded RNA
- **C.** Segmented double-stranded RNA
- **D.** Circular bacterial chromosome

<details><summary>✅ Correct answer and detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: B. Circular single-stranded RNA**

**A. Kyun galat hai:** Double-stranded DNA HDV ka genome nahi hai. Hepatitis viruses ke genome types different hote hain.

**B. Kyun sahi hai:** HDV ka genome small circular single-stranded RNA hota hai. Yeh defective hepatitis virus ki distinctive feature hai.

**C. Kyun galat hai:** Segmented double-stranded RNA rotavirus jaise agents mein hota hai. HDV circular RNA rakhta hai.

**D. Kyun galat hai:** Bacterial chromosome DNA hota hai. HDV bacteria nahi hai.

**Exam clue:** HDV has a small circular RNA genome.

</details>

### Q3. Which route is a recognized means of hepatitis D transmission?

**Difficulty:** Easy · **Priority:** High · **Exam focus:** NORCET / HPRCA HP Staff Nurse / PGIMER · Original practice

- **A.** Routine airborne droplet spread
- **B.** Mosquito bites as the principal route
- **C.** Ordinary contact with intact skin
- **D.** Exposure to infected blood through shared injection equipment

<details><summary>✅ Correct answer and detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: D. Exposure to infected blood through shared injection equipment**

**A. Kyun galat hai:** Routine respiratory droplet spread HDV ka principal route nahi hai.

**B. Kyun galat hai:** Mosquito bite hepatitis D ka recognized major transmission route nahi hai.

**C. Kyun galat hai:** Intact skin ordinary contact mein effective barrier hai. Blood exposure ka risk alag hai.

**D. Kyun sahi hai:** Contaminated injection equipment infected blood transfer kar sakta hai. HDV bloodborne transmission risk se associated hai.

**Exam clue:** HDV prevention includes safe injection and blood exposure precautions.

</details>

### Q4. Which term describes simultaneous acquisition of hepatitis B and hepatitis D in a susceptible person?

**Difficulty:** Easy · **Priority:** High · **Exam focus:** NORCET / HPRCA HP Staff Nurse / PGIMER · Original practice

- **A.** Reinfection with hepatitis A only
- **B.** Bacterial superinfection
- **C.** Coinfection
- **D.** Vaccine seroconversion

<details><summary>✅ Correct answer and detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: C. Coinfection**

**A. Kyun galat hai:** HAV reinfection different virus ki condition hoti hai. Simultaneous HBV-HDV ko coinfection bolte hain.

**B. Kyun galat hai:** Bacterial superinfection bacterial pathogen se related hai. Dono agents yahan viruses hain.

**C. Kyun sahi hai:** Jab HBV aur HDV ek hi infection episode mein acquire hote hain, ise coinfection kehte hain. Existing chronic HBV par new HDV superinfection hai.

**D. Kyun galat hai:** Seroconversion antibody ya antigen pattern change describe karta hai. Simultaneous infection ka term nahi.

**Exam clue:** Coinfection is simultaneous HBV and HDV acquisition.

</details>

### Q5. Which group should receive hepatitis D prevention counseling when hepatitis B vaccination is being planned?

**Difficulty:** Easy · **Priority:** High · **Exam focus:** NORCET / HPRCA HP Staff Nurse / PGIMER · Original practice

- **A.** Only people with documented immunity to HBV
- **B.** Only patients with bacterial hepatitis
- **C.** People susceptible to hepatitis B, especially those at blood exposure risk
- **D.** Only people with no blood exposure risk

<details><summary>✅ Correct answer and detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: C. People susceptible to hepatitis B, especially those at blood exposure risk**

**A. Kyun galat hai:** Documented immune individuals ko routine susceptibility-based vaccination ki need alag assess hoti hai. Susceptible contacts important group hain.

**B. Kyun galat hai:** Bacterial infection hepatitis D susceptibility determine nahi karti. HDV prevention HBV risk se related hai.

**C. Kyun sahi hai:** HBV-susceptible people mein vaccination HBV acquisition prevent karke HDV prevention bhi support karti hai. Blood exposure risk counseling additional useful hai.

**D. Kyun galat hai:** Blood exposure risk wale susceptible people ko ignore nahi karte. Vaccination local schedule ke according hoti hai.

**Exam clue:** Prevent HBV acquisition to reduce HDV risk.

</details>

### Q6. Which organ is the primary site of clinical injury from chronic hepatitis D?

**Difficulty:** Easy · **Priority:** High · **Exam focus:** NORCET / HPRCA HP Staff Nurse / PGIMER · Original practice

- **A.** Liver
- **B.** Urinary bladder
- **C.** Middle ear
- **D.** Thyroid gland

<details><summary>✅ Correct answer and detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: A. Liver**

**A. Kyun sahi hai:** Chronic HDV infection liver inflammation aur fibrosis cause kar sakti hai. Liver disease progression monitor karna important hai.

**B. Kyun galat hai:** Bladder urinary tract ka organ hai. HDV ka primary clinical target liver hai.

**C. Kyun galat hai:** Middle ear infection HDV ka typical main organ manifestation nahi.

**D. Kyun galat hai:** Thyroid monitoring kuch treatments mein relevant ho sakti hai, par primary viral injury liver mein hoti hai.

**Exam clue:** Chronic delta hepatitis primarily damages the liver.

</details>
