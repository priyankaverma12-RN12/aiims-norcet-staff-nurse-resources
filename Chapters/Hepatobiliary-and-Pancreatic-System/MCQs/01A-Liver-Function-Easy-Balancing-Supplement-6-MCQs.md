### Q1. What is the principal storage form of glucose in the liver?

**Difficulty:** Easy · **Priority:** High · **Exam focus:** NORCET / HPRCA HP Staff Nurse / PGIMER · Original practice

- **A.** Triglyceride
- **B.** Creatinine
- **C.** Glycogen
- **D.** Hemoglobin

<details><summary>✅ Correct answer and detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: C. Glycogen**

**A. Kyun galat hai:** Triglyceride fat storage ka form hai, glucose polymer nahi.

**B. Kyun galat hai:** Creatinine muscle metabolism ka waste product hai.

**C. Kyun sahi hai:** Liver glucose ko glycogen mein store karta hai aur zaroorat par glucose availability maintain karne mein help karta hai.

**D. Kyun galat hai:** Hemoglobin red cells mein oxygen transport karta hai.

**Exam clue:** Glycogen is stored carbohydrate.

</details>

### Q2. Which cells perform most of the metabolic functions of the liver?

**Difficulty:** Easy · **Priority:** High · **Exam focus:** NORCET / HPRCA HP Staff Nurse / PGIMER · Original practice

- **A.** Hepatocytes
- **B.** Cholangiocytes
- **C.** Enterocytes
- **D.** Podocytes

<details><summary>✅ Correct answer and detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: A. Hepatocytes**

**A. Kyun sahi hai:** Hepatocytes liver ke main functional cells hain jo nutrients process aur proteins synthesize karte hain.

**B. Kyun galat hai:** Ye bile duct lining ke cells hain aur bile modify karte hain.

**C. Kyun galat hai:** Enterocytes intestine mein nutrient absorption karte hain.

**D. Kyun galat hai:** Podocytes kidney filtration barrier ka part hain.

**Exam clue:** Hepatocytes are the main metabolic cells.

</details>

### Q3. Which liver-resident macrophages help clear material from sinusoidal blood?

**Difficulty:** Easy · **Priority:** High · **Exam focus:** NORCET / HPRCA HP Staff Nurse / PGIMER · Original practice

- **A.** Beta cells
- **B.** Osteoclasts
- **C.** Goblet cells
- **D.** Kupffer cells

<details><summary>✅ Correct answer and detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: D. Kupffer cells**

**A. Kyun galat hai:** Beta cells pancreas mein insulin banati hain.

**B. Kyun galat hai:** Osteoclasts bone resorption karte hain.

**C. Kyun galat hai:** Goblet cells mucus secrete karte hain.

**D. Kyun sahi hai:** Kupffer cells liver ke resident immune cells hain jo blood se debris aur microbes ko remove karne mein help karte hain.

**Exam clue:** Kupffer cells are hepatic macrophages.

</details>

### Q4. Which blood vessel provides oxygenated arterial blood directly to the liver?

**Difficulty:** Easy · **Priority:** High · **Exam focus:** NORCET / HPRCA HP Staff Nurse / PGIMER · Original practice

- **A.** Hepatic vein
- **B.** Hepatic artery
- **C.** Splenic vein
- **D.** Inferior vena cava

<details><summary>✅ Correct answer and detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: B. Hepatic artery**

**A. Kyun galat hai:** Hepatic vein blood liver se bahar le jaati hai.

**B. Kyun sahi hai:** Hepatic artery arterial blood liver tak laati hai aur oxygen supply mein important hai.

**C. Kyun galat hai:** Splenic vein spleen ka venous blood portal system tak laati hai.

**D. Kyun galat hai:** Inferior vena cava systemic venous blood heart tak le jaati hai.

**Exam clue:** Arteries supply, hepatic veins drain.

</details>

### Q5. Which liver function helps detoxify nitrogen waste from amino acid metabolism?

**Difficulty:** Easy · **Priority:** High · **Exam focus:** NORCET / HPRCA HP Staff Nurse / PGIMER · Original practice

- **A.** Conversion of ammonia to urea
- **B.** Conversion of urea to hemoglobin
- **C.** Production of gastric hydrochloric acid
- **D.** Secretion of pancreatic insulin

<details><summary>✅ Correct answer and detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: A. Conversion of ammonia to urea**

**A. Kyun sahi hai:** Liver toxic ammonia ko comparatively less toxic urea mein convert karta hai, jo kidneys excrete karti hain.

**B. Kyun galat hai:** Hemoglobin red cell precursors mein banta hai, urea se nahi.

**C. Kyun galat hai:** Acid stomach ke parietal cells banate hain.

**D. Kyun galat hai:** Insulin pancreatic beta cells banati hain.

**Exam clue:** The urea cycle handles nitrogen waste.

</details>

### Q6. Which abbreviation on a liver chemistry report stands for gamma-glutamyl transferase?

**Difficulty:** Easy · **Priority:** High · **Exam focus:** NORCET / HPRCA HP Staff Nurse / PGIMER · Original practice

- **A.** GFR
- **B.** GCS
- **C.** GGT
- **D.** GDM

<details><summary>✅ Correct answer and detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: C. GGT**

**A. Kyun galat hai:** GFR kidney filtration rate hai.

**B. Kyun galat hai:** GCS consciousness assessment scale hai.

**C. Kyun sahi hai:** GGT ek enzyme hai jo hepatobiliary evaluation mein useful ho sakta hai. Iska result other tests ke saath interpret hota hai.

**D. Kyun galat hai:** GDM gestational diabetes ka abbreviation hai.

**Exam clue:** Recognize the actual laboratory abbreviation.

</details>
