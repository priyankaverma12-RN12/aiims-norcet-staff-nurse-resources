### Q1. What does idiosyncratic drug-induced liver injury describe?

**Difficulty:** Easy · **Priority:** HIGH YIELD · **Exam focus:** NORCET / HPRCA HP Staff Nurse / PGIMER · Original practice

- **A.** An inevitable reaction to every prescribed medication
- **B.** A bacterial infection of the liver
- **C.** An uncommon liver reaction that is not reliably predicted by usual therapeutic dose
- **D.** A congenital absence of the gallbladder

<details><summary>✅ Correct answer and detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: C. An uncommon liver reaction that is not reliably predicted by usual therapeutic dose**

**A. Kyun galat hai:** Har patient ko liver injury nahi hoti. Drug exposure ke saath individual susceptibility matter karti hai.

**B. Kyun galat hai:** Bacterial infection aur medicine se liver injury alag causes hain.

**C. Kyun sahi hai:** Idiosyncratic reaction har patient mein dose ke hisaab se predictable nahi hoti. Normal dose par bhi susceptible patient mein injury ho sakti hai.

**D. Kyun galat hai:** Ye developmental condition hai. Idiosyncratic injury acquired adverse reaction hai.

**Exam clue:** Idiosyncratic reactions can occur at prescribed doses.

</details>

### Q3. In suspected medication-associated hepatitis, what does the term latency describe?

**Difficulty:** Easy · **Priority:** HIGH YIELD · **Exam focus:** NORCET / HPRCA HP Staff Nurse / PGIMER · Original practice

- **A.** Time required for a blood sample to clot
- **B.** The half-life of serum albumin
- **C.** Duration of a liver ultrasound
- **D.** The interval between starting the exposure and onset of injury

<details><summary>✅ Correct answer and detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: D. The interval between starting the exposure and onset of injury**

**A. Kyun galat hai:** Sample clotting laboratory process hai, exposure timeline nahi.

**B. Kyun galat hai:** Albumin half-life protein turnover batati hai. Drug reaction ki onset history alag hai.

**C. Kyun galat hai:** Ultrasound ka duration injury develop hone ka interval nahi hai.

**D. Kyun sahi hai:** Latency se medicine shuru hone aur injury develop hone ka time relationship samajh aata hai.

**Exam clue:** Document drug start and symptom dates.

</details>

### Q5. What is dechallenge in the evaluation of suspected drug-induced liver injury?

**Difficulty:** Easy · **Priority:** HIGH YIELD · **Exam focus:** NORCET / HPRCA HP Staff Nurse / PGIMER · Original practice

- **A.** Deliberately doubling the suspected dose
- **B.** Replacing a medication label without clinical assessment
- **C.** Giving the same drug again to test recurrence
- **D.** Observing the clinical course after withdrawal of the suspected agent

<details><summary>✅ Correct answer and detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: D. Observing the clinical course after withdrawal of the suspected agent**

**A. Kyun galat hai:** Dose double karna injury badha sakta hai. Ye dechallenge nahi hai.

**B. Kyun galat hai:** Label change exposure stop nahi karta. Clinical course track karna zaroori hai.

**C. Kyun galat hai:** Dobara exposure ko rechallenge kehte hain. Ye potentially dangerous ho sakta hai.

**D. Kyun sahi hai:** Suspected agent ko clinician stop karta hai aur recovery trend observe kiya jaata hai. Isse causality assess karne mein help milti hai.

**Exam clue:** Dechallenge means assessment after withdrawal.

</details>

### Q6. Which term describes giving a suspected causative medicine again after recovery from an adverse liver reaction?

- **B.** Decompensation
- **C.** Rechallenge

<details><summary>✅ Correct answer and detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: C. Rechallenge**

**A. Kyun galat hai:** Dehydration body fluid deficit hai. Medicine dobara dene ka term nahi.

**B. Kyun galat hai:** Decompensation organ function worsen hone ko describe karta hai, repeat exposure ko nahi.

**D. Kyun galat hai:** Revascularization blood supply restore karne ka procedure hai.

**Exam clue:** Rechallenge may provoke a more severe reaction.

<details><summary>✅ Correct answer and detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: B. A high-risk jaundiced hepatocellular injury pattern requiring prompt specialist assessment**

**A. Kyun galat hai:** ALP normal hai aur ALT markedly high hai. Isko isolated ALP elevation nahi bol sakte.

### Q20. A patient with suspected idiosyncratic DILI has high ALT and jaundice. A normal initial INR is documented. Which monitoring approach is best?

<details><summary>✅ Correct answer and detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: D. Track symptoms, mental status, bilirubin and INR trends rather than relying on ALT alone**

**A. Kyun galat hai:** ALT synthetic capacity ya encephalopathy directly measure nahi karta.

**B. Kyun galat hai:** Early mental status changes serious ho sakti hain. Escalation delay unsafe hai.

**Exam clue:** Monitor function and symptoms as well as injury markers.
