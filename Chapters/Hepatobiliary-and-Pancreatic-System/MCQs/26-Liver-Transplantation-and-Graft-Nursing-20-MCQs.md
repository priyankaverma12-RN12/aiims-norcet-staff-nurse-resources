### Q1. Which medication class includes tacrolimus, a commonly used maintenance immunosuppressant after liver transplantation?

**Difficulty:** 🟢 Easy · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD · Original practice

- **A.** Proton pump inhibitor
- **B.** Calcineurin inhibitor
- **C.** Direct thrombin inhibitor
- **D.** HMG-CoA reductase inhibitor

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: B. Calcineurin inhibitor**

**Option-wise explanation:**
- **A (Galat):** Proton pump inhibitors stomach acid kam karte hain. Yeh graft immunity suppress nahi karte.
- **B (Sahi):** Tacrolimus calcineurin pathway ko inhibit karke T-cell activation kam karta hai, jisse graft rejection ka risk kam hota hai.
- **C (Galat):** Direct thrombin inhibitors clotting ko reduce karte hain. Tacrolimus anticoagulant nahi hai.
- **D (Galat):** Statins cholesterol synthesis reduce karte hain. Tacrolimus ka primary action immune signaling par hai.

**Exam clue:** Tacrolimus is a calcineurin inhibitor.

**Clinical reference:** [AASLD/AST graft guidance](https://doi.org/10.1097/lvt.0000000000000715) · [AASLD teaching guide](https://www.aasld.org/liver-fellow-network/core-series/clinical-pearls/beginners-guide-liver-transplant-immunosuppression). **YouTube MCQ practice:** specific coverage pending.

**Exam clue:** Trough sampling ka relation next dose aur formulation se hai.

### Q20. A liver transplant recipient was originally transplanted for chronic hepatitis B. Which long-term teaching principle is appropriate?

**Difficulty:** 🟡 Medium · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD · Original practice
