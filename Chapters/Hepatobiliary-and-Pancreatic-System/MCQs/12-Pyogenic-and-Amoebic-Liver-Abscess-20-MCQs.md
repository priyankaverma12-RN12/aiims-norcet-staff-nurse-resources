### Q1. Which organism causes amoebic liver abscess?

**Difficulty:** Easy · **Priority:** HIGH YIELD · **Exam focus:** NORCET / HPRCA HP Staff Nurse / PGIMER · Original practice

- **A.** Plasmodium vivax
- **B.** Entamoeba histolytica
- **C.** Giardia duodenalis
- **D.** Ascaris lumbricoides

<details><summary>✅ Correct answer and detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: B. Entamoeba histolytica**

**A. Kyun galat hai:** Plasmodium vivax malaria cause karta hai. Ye amoebic abscess ka organism nahi hai.

**B. Kyun sahi hai:** Entamoeba histolytica intestine se tissue invade karke portal circulation ke through liver tak pahunch sakti hai. Isse amoebic liver abscess develop ho sakta hai.

**C. Kyun galat hai:** Giardia intestinal malabsorption aur diarrhea cause kar sakti hai, lekin typical amoebic liver abscess nahi.

**D. Kyun galat hai:** Ascaris roundworm hai aur kabhi biliary tract mein migrate kar sakta hai. Ye amoebiasis ka protozoan nahi hai.

**Exam clue:** Amoebic abscess is an extraintestinal manifestation of E. histolytica.

</details>

### Q2. By which route does invasive intestinal amoebiasis commonly reach the liver?

**Difficulty:** Medium · **Priority:** HIGH YIELD · **Exam focus:** NORCET / HPRCA HP Staff Nurse / PGIMER · Original practice

- **A.** Ureteric lumen
- **B.** Pancreatic endocrine ducts
- **C.** Portal venous circulation
- **D.** Coronary arterial circulation as the usual primary route

<details><summary>✅ Correct answer and detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: C. Portal venous circulation**

**A. Kyun galat hai:** Ureter kidney se urine bladder tak transport karta hai, intestinal organisms liver tak nahi.

**B. Kyun galat hai:** Pancreatic endocrine cells hormones blood mein release karte hain. Is route se intestinal amoebae liver nahi pahunchti.

**C. Kyun sahi hai:** Intestine se venous blood portal vein ke through liver tak jaata hai. Invasive trophozoites is route se hepatic tissue tak pahunch sakte hain.

**D. Kyun galat hai:** Coronary arteries heart ko supply karti hain. Intestinal drainage ka usual hepatic route portal circulation hai.

**Exam clue:** Gut-to-liver spread usually follows the portal circulation.

</details>

### Q3. A patient with fever and a hepatic abscess reports a recent episode of untreated obstructive biliary infection. Which mechanism particularly supports a pyogenic origin?

**Difficulty:** Medium · **Priority:** HIGH YIELD · **Exam focus:** NORCET / HPRCA HP Staff Nurse / PGIMER · Original practice

- **A.** Ascending bacterial infection from the biliary tract
- **B.** Exclusive loss of pancreatic insulin production
- **C.** Autoimmune destruction of all hepatocytes
- **D.** Dietary vitamin A deficiency

<details><summary>✅ Correct answer and detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: A. Ascending bacterial infection from the biliary tract**

**A. Kyun sahi hai:** Infected biliary tract se bacteria hepatic tissue mein spread karke pus collection bana sakte hain. Biliary disease pyogenic liver abscess ke important sources mein se hai.

**B. Kyun galat hai:** Insulin deficiency infection risk influence kar sakti hai, lekin ascending biliary bacterial source ka mechanism nahi hai.

**C. Kyun galat hai:** Autoimmune hepatitis immune-mediated inflammation hai, bacterial pus collection ka direct mechanism nahi.

**D. Kyun galat hai:** Vitamin A deficiency abscess ka usual bacterial spread route nahi hai.

**Exam clue:** Biliary infection can seed a pyogenic hepatic abscess.

</details>

### Q7. A patient with suspected pyogenic liver abscess has blood cultures ordered before empirical antibiotics. Which principle is safest?

<details><summary>✅ Correct answer and detailed explanation</summary>

**C. Kyun galat hai:** Completed antibiotics culture yield reduce kar sakti hain. Early sample zyada informative ho sakta hai.

**Exam clue:** Early cultures and prompt treatment must be coordinated.
