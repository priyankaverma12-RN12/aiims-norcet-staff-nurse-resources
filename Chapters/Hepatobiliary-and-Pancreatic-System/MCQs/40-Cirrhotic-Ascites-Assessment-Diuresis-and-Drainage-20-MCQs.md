### Q1. What is the anatomical location of ascitic fluid?

**Difficulty:** 🟢 Easy · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD · Original practice

- **A.** Peritoneal cavity
- **B.** Pleural cavity
- **C.** Pericardial sac
- **D.** Renal collecting system

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: A. Peritoneal cavity**

**Option-wise explanation:**
- **A (Sahi):** Ascites mein free fluid peritoneal cavity ke andar accumulate hota hai. Is wajah se abdomen distended ho sakta hai.
- **B (Galat):** Pleural cavity mein fluid ko pleural effusion kehte hain. Yeh chest mein hota hai.
- **C (Galat):** Pericardial sac mein fluid pericardial effusion hai. Ascites abdominal compartment mein hota hai.
- **D (Galat):** Renal collecting system urine carry karta hai. Iska dilation hydronephrosis ho sakta hai.

**Exam clue:** Ascites is free fluid within the peritoneal cavity.

### Q4. Which drug class describes spironolactone in a cirrhotic ascites regimen?

**Difficulty:** 🟢 Easy · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD · Original practice

- **A.** Thiazide diuretic
- **B.** Mineralocorticoid receptor antagonist
- **C.** Osmotic diuretic
- **D.** Carbonic anhydrase inhibitor

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: B. Mineralocorticoid receptor antagonist**

**Option-wise explanation:**
- **A (Galat):** Thiazide drugs distal tubular sodium chloride transport affect karti hain. Spironolactone us class mein nahi hai.

- **D (Galat):** Carbonic anhydrase inhibitors bicarbonate reabsorption reduce karte hain. Yeh spironolactone ka mechanism nahi hai.

**Exam clue:** Spironolactone is an aldosterone antagonist.

- **D (Galat):** Long-term drains selected circumstances mein consider ho sakte hain, par infection aur fluid loss risks evaluate hote hain. Routine use universally appropriate nahi hai.
