### Q1. Which structure is primarily injured by the autoimmune process in primary biliary cholangitis (PBC)?

**Difficulty:** Easy · **Priority:** HIGH YIELD · **Exam focus:** NORCET / HPRCA HP Staff Nurse / PGIMER · Original practice

- **A.** Pancreatic acinar ducts
- **B.** Small intrahepatic bile ducts
- **C.** Extrahepatic portal vein wall
- **D.** Gallbladder muscular wall

<details><summary>✅ Correct answer and detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: B. Small intrahepatic bile ducts**

**A. Kyun galat hai:** Pancreatic ducts digestive enzymes transport karti hain. Ye PBC ki primary target nahi hain.

**B. Kyun sahi hai:** PBC mein immune system liver ke andar chhoti bile ducts ko damage karta hai. Bile drainage impair hone se chronic cholestasis develop ho sakti hai.

**C. Kyun galat hai:** Portal vein liver ko blood supply deti hai. PBC ka primary injury bile ducts mein hota hai.

**D. Kyun galat hai:** Gallbladder wall ki inflammation cholecystitis mein hoti hai, PBC mein nahi.

**Exam clue:** PBC = small intrahepatic bile duct injury.

</details>

### Q2. A woman has persistent cholestatic liver tests and no obstruction on imaging. Which antibody result strongly supports primary biliary cholangitis?

**Difficulty:** Medium · **Priority:** HIGH YIELD · **Exam focus:** NORCET / HPRCA HP Staff Nurse / PGIMER · Original practice

- **A.** Anti-HBs alone
- **B.** Anti-acetylcholine receptor antibody
- **C.** Anti-glomerular basement membrane antibody
- **D.** Antimitochondrial antibody

<details><summary>✅ Correct answer and detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: D. Antimitochondrial antibody**

**A. Kyun galat hai:** Anti-HBs hepatitis B immunity ka marker ho sakta hai. Ye PBC-specific marker nahi hai.

**B. Kyun galat hai:** Acetylcholine receptor antibody myasthenia gravis se associated hai.

**C. Kyun galat hai:** Anti-GBM antibody kidney aur lung disease se associated ho sakti hai, PBC diagnosis se nahi.

**D. Kyun sahi hai:** Antimitochondrial antibody ya AMA PBC ke diagnosis mein useful marker hai. Cholestatic liver tests ke saath positive result diagnosis support karta hai.

**Exam clue:** Cholestasis plus AMA suggests PBC.

</details>

### Q3. A patient has suspected PBC with persistent cholestatic biochemistry but negative AMA. Which additional antibody tests may help clarify the diagnosis?

**Difficulty:** Difficult · **Priority:** HIGH YIELD · **Exam focus:** NORCET / HPRCA HP Staff Nurse / PGIMER · Original practice

- **A.** Anti-sp100 and anti-gp210
- **B.** Anti-HAV IgM and anti-HBs
- **C.** Anti-dsDNA and anti-GBM only
- **D.** Anti-tetanus and anti-diphtheria antibodies

<details><summary>✅ Correct answer and detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: A. Anti-sp100 and anti-gp210**

**A. Kyun sahi hai:** Kuch PBC patients AMA-negative hote hain. Anti-sp100 aur anti-gp210 PBC-specific antinuclear antibodies ho sakti hain aur specialist diagnosis mein help karti hain.

**B. Kyun galat hai:** Ye hepatitis A aur B ke markers hain. AMA-negative PBC ko specifically clarify nahi karte.

**C. Kyun galat hai:** Ye doosre autoimmune disorders ke markers ho sakte hain. PBC-specific alternatives nahi hain.

**D. Kyun galat hai:** Ye vaccine-related antibody tests hain, PBC diagnostic markers nahi.

**Exam clue:** AMA negative does not exclude PBC.

</details>

### Q4. A patient with persistent cholestasis is AMA-negative and PBC-specific antibodies are also absent. Why might the hepatologist consider liver biopsy?

- **B.** To mechanically remove obstructing gallstones
- **C.** To clarify an uncertain diagnosis and assess alternative or overlapping disease
- **D.** To measure gallbladder contraction after a meal

<details><summary>✅ Correct answer and detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: C. To clarify an uncertain diagnosis and assess alternative or overlapping disease**

**A. Kyun galat hai:** Typical cholestasis aur characteristic antibodies mein biopsy har patient ko mandatory nahi hoti.

**B. Kyun galat hai:** Biopsy tissue sample leti hai, gallstones remove nahi karti.

**C. Kyun sahi hai:** Jab antibody aur imaging results diagnosis clear nahi karte, biopsy tissue pattern dikha sakti hai. Specialist alternative liver disorders ya overlap assess kar sakta hai.

**D. Kyun galat hai:** Gallbladder contraction assess karna functional imaging ka role hai, liver biopsy ka nahi.

**Exam clue:** Uncertain serology may require specialist histologic clarification.

</details>

### Q5. An asymptomatic adult has an incidentally positive low-titer AMA but normal liver biochemistry. What is the most appropriate interpretation?

**Difficulty:** Difficult · **Priority:** HIGH YIELD · **Exam focus:** NORCET / HPRCA HP Staff Nurse / PGIMER · Original practice

- **A.** It proves decompensated biliary cirrhosis
- **B.** It confirms mechanical obstruction requiring ERCP
- **C.** The finding requires clinical context and follow-up rather than automatic diagnosis of active PBC
- **D.** It eliminates the need for future liver assessment

<details><summary>✅ Correct answer and detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: C. The finding requires clinical context and follow-up rather than automatic diagnosis of active PBC**

**A. Kyun galat hai:** Decompensation ascites, bleeding ya encephalopathy jaise findings se assess hoti hai. AMA alone stage confirm nahi karti.

**B. Kyun galat hai:** Mechanical obstruction imaging se assess hoti hai. AMA positive hone se ERCP automatic nahi hoti.

**C. Kyun sahi hai:** AMA positive hona risk marker ho sakta hai, lekin normal biochemistry ke saath active PBC automatically establish nahi hoti. Clinical context aur planned follow-up important hain.

**D. Kyun galat hai:** Follow-up se evolving cholestasis identify ho sakti hai. Isliye monitoring ka plan useful hai.

**Exam clue:** An isolated antibody result is not a complete diagnosis.

</details>

### Q6. What is the usual first-line disease-modifying medicine for primary biliary cholangitis?

**Difficulty:** Easy · **Priority:** HIGH YIELD · **Exam focus:** NORCET / HPRCA HP Staff Nurse / PGIMER · Original practice

- **A.** Ursodeoxycholic acid
- **B.** Lactulose
- **C.** Octreotide
- **D.** Pancrelipase

<details><summary>✅ Correct answer and detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: A. Ursodeoxycholic acid**

**A. Kyun sahi hai:** Ursodeoxycholic acid ya UDCA bile acid composition aur cholestatic liver injury ko improve karne mein help karti hai. PBC mein ye standard first-line treatment hai.

**B. Kyun galat hai:** Lactulose hepatic encephalopathy mein use ho sakti hai. Ye PBC ki first-line disease-modifying medicine nahi hai.

**C. Kyun galat hai:** Octreotide acute variceal bleeding mein use ho sakti hai. Ye chronic PBC treatment nahi hai.

**D. Kyun galat hai:** Pancrelipase pancreatic digestive enzyme replacement hai. PBC progression ko directly target nahi karti.

**Exam clue:** UDCA is first-line PBC treatment.

</details>

### Q7. A 60-kg adult with PBC has prescribed ursodeoxycholic acid 900 mg daily. What is the calculated weight-based daily dose?

**Difficulty:** Medium · **Priority:** HIGH YIELD · **Exam focus:** NORCET / HPRCA HP Staff Nurse / PGIMER · Original practice

- **A.** 10 mg/kg/day
- **B.** 25 mg/kg/day
- **C.** 60 mg/kg/day
- **D.** 15 mg/kg/day

<details><summary>✅ Correct answer and detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: D. 15 mg/kg/day**

**A. Kyun galat hai:** 10 mg/kg/day hota toh 60 kg patient ko 600 mg daily milte.

**B. Kyun galat hai:** 25 mg/kg/day par total dose 1500 mg hoti.

**C. Kyun galat hai:** 60 mg/kg/day par dose 3600 mg hoti. Calculation prescription se match nahi karti.

**D. Kyun sahi hai:** 900 mg ko 60 kg se divide karne par 15 mg/kg/day milta hai. Ye usual 13–15 mg/kg/day range ke upper end par hai.

**Exam clue:** Weight-based dose = total daily milligrams divided by kilograms.

</details>

### Q8. A patient with PBC receiving weight-appropriate UDCA has persistently elevated alkaline phosphatase after adequate treatment. Which response is appropriate?

- **D.** Use AMA titer alone to decide that treatment succeeded

<details><summary>✅ Correct answer and detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: B. Review adherence and biochemical response with the specialist for risk assessment and possible additional therapy**

**A. Kyun galat hai:** High-dose escalation se benefit guaranteed nahi hota. Prescription change specialist decide karta hai.

**B. Kyun sahi hai:** Persistent ALP elevation inadequate biochemical response ka clue ho sakti hai. Team adherence, bilirubin, risk markers aur treatment options evaluate karti hai. Dose ko independently recommended range se upar badhana appropriate nahi hai.

**C. Kyun galat hai:** Itching aur disease progression alag endpoints hain. Persistent itch treatment stop karne ka automatic reason nahi.

**D. Kyun galat hai:** AMA titer alone biochemical response ka reliable substitute nahi hai.

**Exam clue:** Persistent ALP needs structured response assessment.

</details>

### Q9. A PBC patient asks why bilirubin is monitored along with alkaline phosphatase during follow-up. Which answer is most appropriate?

**Difficulty:** Medium · **Priority:** HIGH YIELD · **Exam focus:** NORCET / HPRCA HP Staff Nurse / PGIMER · Original practice

- **A.** Bilirubin alone identifies the exact autoimmune antibody
- **B.** Both help assess disease course and biochemical risk
- **C.** ALP measures blood glucose control
- **D.** Normal bilirubin guarantees no future fibrosis

<details><summary>✅ Correct answer and detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: B. Both help assess disease course and biochemical risk**

**A. Kyun galat hai:** Bilirubin antibody test nahi hai. AMA aur PBC-specific antibodies separate tests hain.

**B. Kyun sahi hai:** ALP cholestatic injury ka marker hai aur bilirubin bile processing aur excretion ki information deta hai. Dono trends risk assessment mein help karte hain.

**C. Kyun galat hai:** Blood glucose control ke liye glucose ya HbA1c jaise tests use hote hain. ALP liver ya bone sources se aa sakta hai.

**D. Kyun galat hai:** Normal bilirubin early disease mein mil sakta hai. Future fibrosis ka risk completely exclude nahi hota.

**Exam clue:** Follow biochemical response using trends, not one isolated test.

</details>

### Q10. A patient with PBC is prescribed cholestyramine for itching and UDCA for disease management. Which administration schedule follows the AASLD separation principle?

**Difficulty:** Medium · **Priority:** HIGH YIELD · **Exam focus:** NORCET / HPRCA HP Staff Nurse / PGIMER · Original practice

- **A.** Both medicines together in the same drink
- **B.** UDCA ten minutes after cholestyramine
- **C.** UDCA at least one hour before or four hours after cholestyramine
- **D.** Stop UDCA whenever cholestyramine is prescribed

<details><summary>✅ Correct answer and detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: C. UDCA at least one hour before or four hours after cholestyramine**

**A. Kyun galat hai:** Same drink mein dene se binding interaction ka risk badhta hai.

**B. Kyun galat hai:** Ten-minute separation recommended interval se kam hai.

**C. Kyun sahi hai:** Cholestyramine gut mein bile acids bind karti hai aur UDCA absorption interfere kar sakti hai. Isliye adequate time separation chahiye. Local prescription aur product instructions confirm karo.

**D. Kyun galat hai:** Cholestyramine itching ke liye hai. UDCA disease treatment hai aur automatically stop nahi hoti.

**Exam clue:** Separate bile acid sequestrant from UDCA.

</details>

### Q12. A patient with PBC reports disabling pruritus despite improving liver tests on treatment. Which interpretation is appropriate?

- **B.** Severe itching establishes acute liver failure by itself

<details><summary>✅ Correct answer and detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: D. Symptom severity may not parallel biochemical response and merits separate management**

**A. Kyun galat hai:** Lab improvement patient ke real symptoms ko invalidate nahi karta.

**B. Kyun galat hai:** ALF diagnosis mein acute injury, coagulopathy aur encephalopathy jaise criteria relevant hain. Itching alone ALF prove nahi karti.

**C. Kyun galat hai:** UDCA ko independently stop karna disease management harm kar sakta hai.

**D. Kyun sahi hai:** PBC mein itching burden biochemical markers ke saath perfectly correlate nahi karta. Patient ke symptoms ko seriously lekar separate treatment options review karte hain.

**Exam clue:** Biochemical improvement and itch control are different goals.

</details>

### Q16. A PBC patient with advanced cholestasis develops night vision difficulty. Which deficiency should the clinician consider among other causes?

- **B.** Vitamin A deficiency
- **C.** Vitamin C excess
- **D.** Sodium excess alone

<details><summary>✅ Correct answer and detailed explanation</summary>

**A. Kyun galat hai:** B12 water-soluble vitamin hai. Excess B12 PBC-related night blindness ka typical explanation nahi hai.

**B. Kyun sahi hai:** Bile fat absorption mein help karti hai. Advanced cholestasis mein fat-soluble vitamin A absorption impair ho sakti hai. Vitamin A vision mein important hai, isliye night vision symptoms evaluation maangte hain.

**C. Kyun galat hai:** Vitamin C excess night vision loss ka expected cholestatic mechanism nahi hai.

**D. Kyun galat hai:** Sodium excess fluid balance affect kar sakta hai, vitamin A-dependent vision mechanism ko explain nahi karta.

**Exam clue:** Advanced cholestasis plus night blindness raises vitamin A concern.

</details>
