### Q1. Which nutrient digestion is particularly affected when pancreatic lipase delivery is inadequate?

**Difficulty:** Medium · **Priority:** HIGH YIELD · Original practice

- **A.** Water alone
- **B.** Dietary triglycerides
- **C.** Sodium chloride alone
- **D.** Inhaled oxygen

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: B. Dietary triglycerides**

**Option-wise explanation:**
- **A (Galat):** Water ko pancreatic enzyme digestion ki zaroorat nahi hoti.
- **B (Sahi):** Lipase triglycerides ko smaller absorbable products mein break karti hai. Lipase delivery kam hone se fat maldigestion prominent ho sakti hai.
- **C (Galat):** Salt ko triglyceride digestion jaisi enzymatic breakdown nahi chahiye.
- **D (Galat):** Oxygen lung se absorb hoti hai, pancreatic digestive substrate nahi.

**Exam clue:** Lipase deficiency impairs fat digestion.

</details>

### Q2. Which fecal elastase specimen is preferred for evaluating pancreatic exocrine insufficiency under AGA guidance?

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: D. A formed or semisolid stool specimen**

**Option-wise explanation:**
- **A (Galat):** Deliberate dilution concentration unreliable bana sakti hai.
- **B (Galat):** Urine fecal elastase assay ka intended specimen nahi hai.

**Exam clue:** Collect formed or semisolid stool.

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: A. Crushing can destroy protective coating and expose oral mucosa to enzymes**

**Option-wise explanation:**
- **A (Sahi):** Enteric coating gastric acid se enzymes protect karti hai. Crushing se premature release aur oral mucosal irritation possible hai. Product-specific instructions follow karo.
- **B (Galat):** Crushing protective mechanism compromise kar sakti hai.
- **C (Galat):** PERT digestive enzymes contain karti hai, insulin nahi.

**Exam clue:** Do not crush enteric-coated enzyme granules.
