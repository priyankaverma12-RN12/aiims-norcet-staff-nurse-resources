### Q1. Where are esophageal varices located?

**Difficulty:** Easy · **Priority:** High · **Exam focus:** NORCET / HPRCA HP Staff Nurse / PGIMER · Original practice

- **A.** Within the renal pelvis
- **B.** Within the tracheal lumen
- **C.** Within the wall of the esophagus
- **D.** Within the gallbladder lumen

<details><summary>✅ Correct answer and detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: C. Within the wall of the esophagus**

**A. Kyun galat hai:** Renal pelvis urine collect karti hai, esophageal veins ka site nahi hai.

**B. Kyun galat hai:** Trachea airway hai, esophageal varices food pipe mein hoti hain.

**C. Kyun sahi hai:** Portal hypertension se esophageal wall ki submucosal veins dilate ho sakti hain. Ye rupture hone par bleed kar sakti hain.

**D. Kyun galat hai:** Gallbladder bile store karta hai. Varices dilated blood vessels hain.

**Exam clue:** Esophageal varices are dilated veins of the food pipe.

</details>

### Q2. What is the purpose of the gastric balloon in a Sengstaken-Blakemore tube used by a specialist team?

- **A.** To measure blood glucose
- **B.** To deliver intravenous antibiotics
- **C.** To provide routine long-term nutrition
- **D.** To anchor the device at the gastroesophageal junction with controlled traction

<details><summary>✅ Correct answer and detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: D. To anchor the device at the gastroesophageal junction with controlled traction**

**A. Kyun galat hai:** Glucose meter blood glucose measure karta hai. Balloon is kaam ke liye nahi hai.

**B. Kyun galat hai:** Antibiotics IV line se diye jaate hain, gastric balloon se nahi.

**C. Kyun galat hai:** Tamponade emergency temporary intervention hai, feeding device nahi.

**D. Kyun sahi hai:** Gastric balloon stomach mein inflate karke specialist-directed traction se device position maintain karne mein help karta hai. Placement aur monitoring trained team karti hai.

**Exam clue:** Distinguish the gastric and esophageal balloons.

</details>

### Q3. Which medication is a vasoactive agent used in the management of acute variceal hemorrhage?

**Difficulty:** Easy · **Priority:** High · **Exam focus:** NORCET / HPRCA HP Staff Nurse / PGIMER · Original practice

- **A.** Terlipressin
- **B.** Levothyroxine
- **C.** Metformin
- **D.** Cetirizine

<details><summary>✅ Correct answer and detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: A. Terlipressin**

**A. Kyun sahi hai:** Terlipressin splanchnic circulation par effect se portal inflow kam karne mein help karti hai. Ye clinician ke prescription aur monitoring ke under di jaati hai.

**B. Kyun galat hai:** Levothyroxine thyroid hormone replacement hai, variceal bleeding ke liye vasoactive therapy nahi.

**C. Kyun galat hai:** Metformin diabetes treatment hai. Ye acute variceal hemostasis ke liye nahi hai.

**D. Kyun galat hai:** Cetirizine antihistamine hai, portal pressure therapy nahi.

**Exam clue:** Recognize the variceal-specific vasoactive medication.

</details>

### Q4. What is the meaning of the term gastroesophageal varices?

**Difficulty:** Easy · **Priority:** High · **Exam focus:** NORCET / HPRCA HP Staff Nurse / PGIMER · Original practice

- **A.** Ulcers limited to the duodenum
- **B.** Abnormally dilated veins involving the stomach and esophagus region
- **C.** Tumors of the pancreas
- **D.** Gallstones within the common bile duct

<details><summary>✅ Correct answer and detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: B. Abnormally dilated veins involving the stomach and esophagus region**

**A. Kyun galat hai:** Duodenal ulcers mucosal defects hain, dilated veins nahi.

**B. Kyun sahi hai:** Portal hypertension se stomach aur esophagus ke aas paas veins enlarge ho sakti hain. Inka exact location treatment planning affect karta hai.

**C. Kyun galat hai:** Pancreatic tumor alag pathology hai.

**D. Kyun galat hai:** Duct stones biliary obstruction kar sakte hain, varices nahi hain.

**Exam clue:** Gastroesophageal identifies the upper gastrointestinal region.

</details>

### Q5. Which procedure permits direct inspection of red markings on esophageal varices?

**Difficulty:** Easy · **Priority:** High · **Exam focus:** NORCET / HPRCA HP Staff Nurse / PGIMER · Original practice

- **A.** Upper gastrointestinal endoscopy
- **B.** Electrocardiography
- **C.** Abdominal plain radiography
- **D.** Spirometry

<details><summary>✅ Correct answer and detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: A. Upper gastrointestinal endoscopy**

**A. Kyun sahi hai:** Endoscopy mucosal surface aur varices directly dikhati hai. Red markings bleeding risk assessment mein useful ho sakti hain.

**B. Kyun galat hai:** ECG heart rhythm assess karta hai, esophagus inspect nahi karta.

**C. Kyun galat hai:** Plain X-ray esophageal mucosal red markings directly nahi dikhata.

**D. Kyun galat hai:** Spirometry lung function assess karti hai.

**Exam clue:** Endoscopy visualizes variceal surface features.

</details>

### Q6. What does secondary prevention mean after an episode of variceal hemorrhage?

**Difficulty:** Easy · **Priority:** High · **Exam focus:** NORCET / HPRCA HP Staff Nurse / PGIMER · Original practice

- **A.** Preventing only a first-ever episode before any bleeding
- **B.** Stopping all follow-up once bleeding ceases
- **C.** Preventing another bleeding episode after the initial event
- **D.** Treating an unrelated respiratory infection

<details><summary>✅ Correct answer and detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: C. Preventing another bleeding episode after the initial event**

**A. Kyun galat hai:** Ye primary prevention hai.

**B. Kyun galat hai:** Bleeding control ke baad recurrence risk remain kar sakta hai.

**C. Kyun sahi hai:** Secondary prevention ka focus pehle bleed ke baad recurrence risk kam karna hai. Follow-up treatment patient ki tolerance aur specialist plan par depend karta hai.

**D. Kyun galat hai:** Respiratory infection treatment secondary variceal prevention nahi hai.

**Exam clue:** Secondary prevention follows an established first event.

</details>
