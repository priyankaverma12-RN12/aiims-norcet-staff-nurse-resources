### Q1. Which type of viral genome is present in hepatitis A virus?

**Difficulty:** 🟢 Easy · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD · Original practice

- **A.** Double-stranded DNA
- **B.** Negative-sense segmented RNA
- **C.** Single-stranded positive-sense RNA
- **D.** Double-stranded RNA

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: C. Single-stranded positive-sense RNA**

**Option-wise explanation:**
- **A (Galat):** HBV ka genome partially double-stranded DNA hai, HAV ka nahi.
- **B (Galat):** Segmented negative-sense RNA influenza jaise viruses mein milta hai. HAV ka genome different hai.
- **C (Sahi):** HAV positive-sense single-stranded RNA virus hai. Iski genetic information RNA form mein hoti hai.
- **D (Galat):** Double-stranded RNA rotavirus jaise agents mein milta hai. HAV single-stranded RNA virus hai.

**Exam clue:** HAV belongs to the positive-sense RNA viruses.
