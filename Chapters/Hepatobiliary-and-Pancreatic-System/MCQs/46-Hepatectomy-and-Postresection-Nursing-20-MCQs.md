### Q1. What does the term partial hepatectomy mean?

**Difficulty:** Easy · **Priority:** HIGH YIELD · **Exam focus:** NORCET / HPRCA HP Staff Nurse / PGIMER · Original practice

- **A.** Surgical removal of a portion of the liver
- **B.** Removal of the gallbladder alone
- **C.** Removal of the entire pancreas
- **D.** Replacement of the entire liver with a graft

<details><summary>✅ Correct answer and detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: A. Surgical removal of a portion of the liver**

**A. Kyun sahi hai:** Partial hepatectomy mein liver ka selected hissa remove kiya jaata hai. Baaki liver body mein rehta hai.

**B. Kyun galat hai:** Gallbladder removal cholecystectomy hai. Hepatectomy liver ka operation hai.

**C. Kyun galat hai:** Pancreatectomy pancreas ko remove karti hai. Ye hepatectomy nahi hai.

**D. Kyun galat hai:** Liver transplant mein graft use hota hai. Partial hepatectomy mein selected liver tissue remove hota hai.

**Exam clue:** Hepatectomy is hepatic tissue resection.

</details>

### Q2. What is meant by the future liver remnant in liver resection planning?

**Difficulty:** Easy · **Priority:** HIGH YIELD · **Exam focus:** NORCET / HPRCA HP Staff Nurse / PGIMER · Original practice

- **A.** The tissue specimen already sent to pathology
- **B.** The volume of bile drained during surgery
- **C.** The liver tissue expected to remain after the planned operation
- **D.** The donor organ used in transplantation

<details><summary>✅ Correct answer and detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: C. The liver tissue expected to remain after the planned operation**

**A. Kyun galat hai:** Resected specimen body se bahar hota hai. Remnant body mein bacha tissue hai.

**B. Kyun galat hai:** Bile output fluid quantity hai, remaining liver tissue nahi.

**C. Kyun sahi hai:** Remnant woh tissue hai jo operation ke baad body mein rahega aur liver ka kaam karega.

**D. Kyun galat hai:** Graft transplant ka part hai. Remnant original liver ka remaining portion hai.

**Exam clue:** The remaining functional liver matters.

</details>

### Q3. Which term describes failure of liver function following surgical removal of hepatic tissue?

**Difficulty:** Easy · **Priority:** HIGH YIELD · **Exam focus:** NORCET / HPRCA HP Staff Nurse / PGIMER · Original practice

- **A.** Postoperative pancreatic fistula
- **B.** Posthepatectomy liver failure
- **C.** Postcholecystectomy syndrome
- **D.** Primary adrenal insufficiency

<details><summary>✅ Correct answer and detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: B. Posthepatectomy liver failure**

**A. Kyun galat hai:** Pancreatic fistula pancreas juice leakage hai. Ye hepatic functional failure nahi hai.

**B. Kyun sahi hai:** Resection ke baad bacha liver agar required functions maintain nahi kar pata, toh posthepatectomy liver failure develop ho sakta hai.

**C. Kyun galat hai:** Ye gallbladder removal ke baad symptoms describe karta hai, hepatic resection failure nahi.

**D. Kyun galat hai:** Adrenal hormone deficiency alag endocrine disease hai.

**Exam clue:** Recognize the operation-specific complication.

</details>

### Q4. Which functional ability of the remaining liver supports recovery after partial hepatectomy?

**Difficulty:** Easy · **Priority:** HIGH YIELD · **Exam focus:** NORCET / HPRCA HP Staff Nurse / PGIMER · Original practice

- **A.** Production of pancreatic insulin
- **B.** Formation of new nephrons
- **C.** Production of pulmonary surfactant
- **D.** Regeneration of liver tissue

<details><summary>✅ Correct answer and detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: D. Regeneration of liver tissue**

**A. Kyun galat hai:** Insulin pancreatic beta cells banati hain. Liver regeneration isse replace nahi hoti.

**B. Kyun galat hai:** Nephrons kidney units hain. Liver mein ye structure nahi bante.

**C. Kyun galat hai:** Surfactant lungs ke cells produce karte hain. Ye hepatic recovery ka mechanism nahi.

**D. Kyun sahi hai:** Liver mein regeneration ki capacity hoti hai. Recovery remnant ki quality aur size par depend karti hai.

**Exam clue:** Liver can regenerate, but capacity is not unlimited.

</details>

**B. Kyun galat hai:** Sodium zero hona physiological nahi hai. Electrolyte changes patient-specific hote hain.

**C. Kyun galat hai:** Potassium fluid, renal function aur medicines ke saath change ho sakta hai.

**D. Kyun sahi hai:** Hepatectomy ke baad phosphate kam ho sakta hai. Regeneration aur other metabolic changes contribute kar sakte hain. Nurse ordered monitoring follow karti hai.

**Exam clue:** Check phosphate along with other postoperative electrolytes.

</details>

### Q9. A stable patient without obstruction or persistent vomiting is recovering from elective hepatectomy. What does the ERAS approach say about routine prophylactic nasogastric intubation?

- **B.** Insert a tube because the liver cannot regenerate without suction

<details><summary>✅ Correct answer and detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: D. Avoid routine use unless a specific clinical indication arises**

**A. Kyun galat hai:** Universal prolonged use patient discomfort aur delayed feeding cause kar sakta hai.

**B. Kyun galat hai:** Liver regeneration gastric suction par dependent nahi hai.

**C. Kyun galat hai:** New vomiting ka cause assess karna chahiye. Suction selected cases mein support ho sakti hai.

**D. Kyun sahi hai:** Routine NG tube recovery improve karna proven nahi hai aur oral intake delay kar sakti hai. Indication develop ho toh team reassess karti hai.

**Exam clue:** Prophylactic tubes differ from indicated therapeutic tubes.

</details>

**B. Kyun sahi hai:** Liver medicine metabolism karta hai. Major resection ke baad dosing review ki zaroorat ho sakti hai. Nurse prescribed safe limit confirm karti hai.

**C. Kyun galat hai:** Pain control recovery aur mobility ke liye important hai. Appropriate regimen select kiya jaata hai.

**D. Kyun galat hai:** Combination products mein acetaminophen repeat ho sakta hai. Total dose verify karna zaroori hai.

**Exam clue:** Analgesic dosing is individualized after major resection.

- **B.** Postoperative pancreatic fistula
- **C.** Isolated early surgical transaminase release
- **D.** Uncomplicated wound seroma

<details><summary>✅ Correct answer and detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: A. The drain-to-serum ratio is four, meeting the ISGLS biochemical threshold for bile leakage**

**C. Kyun galat hai:** Drain bilirubin leakage assess karta hai. Hepatic failure ke criteria different hain.

**Exam clue:** Compare simultaneous drain and serum bilirubin.

<details><summary>✅ Correct answer and detailed explanation</summary>
