### Q1. Which sleep pattern may be an early clue to hepatic encephalopathy?

**Difficulty:** 🟢 Easy · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD · Original practice

- **A.** Regular uninterrupted nighttime sleep with usual daytime alertness
- **B.** Sleeping in daytime and remaining awake at night
- **C.** Normal sleep after a routine night shift alone
- **D.** Brief sleep after exercise alone

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: B. Sleeping in daytime and remaining awake at night**

**Option-wise explanation:**
- **A (Galat):** Normal sleep pattern HE-specific clue nahi hai.
- **B (Sahi):** Sleep-wake reversal HE ka possible early clue hai. Other causes aur baseline schedule bhi assess karte hain.
- **C (Galat):** Shift work se sleep timing change ho sakti hai without HE.
- **D (Galat):** Exercise ke baad short sleep nonspecific hai.

**Exam clue:** New sleep-wake reversal deserves assessment.

</details>

### Q2. Which West Haven grade describes coma in hepatic encephalopathy?

**Difficulty:** 🟢 Easy · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD · Original practice

- **A.** Grade IV
- **B.** Grade I
- **C.** Grade II
- **D.** Grade III

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: A. Grade IV**

**Option-wise explanation:**
- **A (Sahi):** Grade IV coma ko describe karta hai. Unresponsive patient ko urgent airway aur medical evaluation chahiye.
- **B (Galat):** Grade I subtle behavioral or cognitive changes se associated hai.
- **C (Galat):** Grade II lethargy aur disorientation jaise findings ho sakti hain.
- **D (Galat):** Grade III severe somnolence with response to stimuli ho sakta hai, coma grade IV hai.

**Exam clue:** Grade IV is coma.

</details>

### Q3. Which nitrogen-containing substance is a major contributor to the pathophysiology of hepatic encephalopathy?

**Difficulty:** 🟢 Easy · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD · Original practice

- **A.** Sodium chloride
- **B.** Glucose
- **C.** Ammonia
- **D.** Calcium carbonate

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: C. Ammonia**

**Option-wise explanation:**
- **A (Galat):** Sodium balance important hai, but sodium chloride is question ka nitrogenous toxin nahi.
- **B (Galat):** Glucose altered mental status ka separate cause ho sakta hai, nitrogenous compound nahi.
- **C (Sahi):** Ammonia gut metabolism aur protein breakdown se generate ho sakti hai. Impaired hepatic clearance aur shunting brain exposure increase kar sakte hain.
- **D (Galat):** Calcium carbonate is mechanism ka major nitrogenous substance nahi.

**Exam clue:** Ammonia contributes to HE but diagnosis is clinical.

</details>

### Q4. Which routine safety measure is appropriate for a confused but mobile patient with hepatic encephalopathy?

**Difficulty:** 🟢 Easy · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD · Original practice

- **A.** Encourage unassisted stair climbing
- **B.** Remove the call bell
- **C.** Keep walking aids out of reach
- **D.** Assist ambulation and apply individualized fall precautions

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: D. Assist ambulation and apply individualized fall precautions**

**Option-wise explanation:**
- **A (Galat):** Unassisted stairs injury risk badha sakti hain.
- **B (Galat):** Call bell help request karne mein useful hai.
- **C (Galat):** Suitable walking aid accessible hona chahiye with supervision as indicated.
- **D (Sahi):** Confusion aur impaired coordination falls ka risk badha sakte hain. Assisted mobility aur environmental safety helpful hain.

**Exam clue:** Cognitive impairment increases fall risk.

</details>

### Q5. Which orientation aid may help a patient recovering from hepatic encephalopathy?

**Difficulty:** 🟢 Easy · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD · Original practice

- **A.** A visible clock and calendar with calm repeated orientation
- **B.** Frequent unnecessary room changes
- **C.** Avoiding all communication
- **D.** Concealing the date and time

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: A. A visible clock and calendar with calm repeated orientation**

**Option-wise explanation:**
- **A (Sahi):** Clock aur calendar patient ko time aur day identify karne mein help kar sakte hain. Calm communication recovery support karti hai.
- **B (Galat):** Frequent room changes confusion worsen kar sakte hain.
- **C (Galat):** Communication avoidance patient ko disorient kar sakti hai.
- **D (Galat):** Time cues conceal karne se orientation support nahi milta.

**Exam clue:** Use simple orientation cues.

</details>

### Q6. Which simple observation helps document a patient's level of responsiveness during neurological nursing assessment?

**Difficulty:** 🟢 Easy · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD · Original practice

- **A.** The patient's preferred food alone
- **B.** Whether the patient opens eyes spontaneously, to voice or to a stronger stimulus
- **C.** The length of the patient's hair
- **D.** The number of meals ordered

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: B. Whether the patient opens eyes spontaneously, to voice or to a stronger stimulus**

**Option-wise explanation:**
- **A (Galat):** Food preference consciousness measure nahi karti.
- **B (Sahi):** Eye opening responsiveness assessment ka recognized component hai. Standard neurological chart aur other findings ke saath record karo.
- **C (Galat):** Hair length neurological responsiveness se related nahi.
- **D (Galat):** Meals ordered level of consciousness establish nahi karte.

**Exam clue:** Record responsiveness objectively.

</details>
