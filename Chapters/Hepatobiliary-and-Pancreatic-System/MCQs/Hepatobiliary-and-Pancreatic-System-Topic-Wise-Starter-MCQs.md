## Cirrhosis

### Q1. A cirrhotic patient develops confusion and a flapping hand tremor. Which complication is most likely?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Topic:** Cirrhosis

- **A.** Hepatic encephalopathy
- **B.** Uncomplicated gallstones
- **C.** Isolated appendicitis
- **D.** Stable gastroesophageal reflux

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: A. Hepatic encephalopathy**

Liver waste substances ko process karta hai. Jab liver ka kaam bahut kam ho jaata hai, toh brain affect ho sakta hai. Confusion aur flapping tremor hepatic encephalopathy ke clues hain.

**Baaki options kyun galat hain?**

- **B.** Gallstones generally biliary pain dete hain.
- **C.** Appendicitis abdominal pain ka cause hai.
- **D.** Reflux se ye neurological pattern expected nahi hai.

</details>

## Portal hypertension

### Q2. A patient with known esophageal varices vomits a large amount of blood. Which priority is appropriate?

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Topic:** Portal hypertension

- **A.** Routine oral antacid administration only
- **B.** Encourage the patient to walk
- **C.** Schedule review for the following week
- **D.** Emergency airway and circulation assessment and escalation

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: D. Emergency airway and circulation assessment and escalation**

Portal pressure badhne se food pipe ki veins badi ho sakti hain. Agar ye veins phat jaayein toh severe bleeding ho sakti hai. Nurse saans aur circulation pehle assess karti hai.

**Baaki options kyun galat hain?**

- **A.** Antacid major bleeding ka treatment nahi hai.
- **B.** Walking unstable patient ke liye unsafe hai.
- **C.** Delay life-threatening ho sakta hai.

</details>

## Pancreatitis

### Q3. A patient has severe epigastric pain radiating to the back and elevated lipase. Which diagnosis is supported?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Topic:** Pancreatitis

- **A.** Primary hypothyroidism
- **B.** Isolated otitis media
- **C.** Acute pancreatitis
- **D.** Uncomplicated cystitis

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: C. Acute pancreatitis**

Pancreas mein swelling hone par upper abdomen ka severe pain back tak ja sakta hai. Lipase pancreas ka enzyme hai jo pancreatitis mein badh sakta hai. Clinical assessment aur tests se diagnosis confirm hota hai.

**Baaki options kyun galat hain?**

- **A.** Hypothyroidism metabolism slow karta hai.
- **B.** Otitis media ear infection hai.
- **D.** Cystitis urine symptoms deti hai.

</details>

## Biliary obstruction

### Q4. A patient has jaundice, dark urine and pale stools. Which mechanism fits biliary obstruction?

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Topic:** Biliary obstruction

- **A.** Loss of hemoglobin only from urine
- **B.** Reduced bile pigment reaching the intestine
- **C.** Excess bile pigment reaching stool in every case
- **D.** Increased insulin release into the intestine

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: B. Reduced bile pigment reaching the intestine**

Bile intestine mein jaakar stool ko color dene mein help karti hai. Bile duct block ho toh stool pale ho sakta hai. Conjugated bilirubin urine mein jaakar urine dark kar sakta hai.

**Baaki options kyun galat hain?**

- **A.** Hemoglobin loss alone ye pattern explain nahi karta.
- **C.** Bile intestine mein kam pahunch rahi hai.
- **D.** Insulin bile pigment nahi hai.

</details>
