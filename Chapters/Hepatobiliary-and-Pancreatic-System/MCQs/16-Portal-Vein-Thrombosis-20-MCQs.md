### Q1. Which vessel is obstructed in portal vein thrombosis (PVT)?

**Difficulty:** Easy · **Priority:** HIGH YIELD · **Exam focus:** NORCET / HPRCA HP Staff Nurse / PGIMER · Original practice

- **A.** The portal venous channel carrying splanchnic blood toward the liver
- **B.** The common hepatic bile duct
- **C.** The cystic artery exclusively
- **D.** The main pancreatic duct

<details><summary>✅ Correct answer and detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: A. The portal venous channel carrying splanchnic blood toward the liver**

**A. Kyun sahi hai:** Portal vein gut aur spleen se blood liver tak laati hai. PVT mein isi venous pathway mein clot hota hai.

**B. Kyun galat hai:** Bile duct bile transport karti hai, venous blood nahi.

**C. Kyun galat hai:** Cystic artery gallbladder supply karti hai.

**D. Kyun galat hai:** Pancreatic duct enzymes transport karta hai, portal blood nahi.

**Exam clue:** PVT affects portal inflow.

</details>

**Correct answer: C. Contrast-enhanced CT or MRI when appropriate**

**A. Kyun galat hai:** Sodium se thrombus anatomy nahi pata chalti.

**Exam clue:** Confirm extent and nature of the clot.

</details>
