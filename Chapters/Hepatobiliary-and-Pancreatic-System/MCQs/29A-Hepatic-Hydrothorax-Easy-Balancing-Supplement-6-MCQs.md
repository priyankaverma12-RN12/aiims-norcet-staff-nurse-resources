### Q1. Which percussion note is expected directly over a sizable hepatic pleural effusion?

**Difficulty:** 🟢 Easy · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD · Original practice

- **A.** Hyperresonant
- **B.** Tympanic
- **C.** Dull
- **D.** Normal resonant throughout

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: C. Dull**

**Option-wise explanation:**
- **A (Galat):** Hyperresonance excess air jaise pneumothorax mein mil sakti hai, fluid mein typical nahi.
- **B (Galat):** Tympany gas-filled structure ke upar expected hoti hai.
- **C (Sahi):** Pleural fluid hawa ki jagah sound transmission ko change karti hai, isliye percussion dull ho sakta hai.
- **D (Galat):** Large effusion ke affected area par normal resonance kam ho sakti hai.

**Exam clue:** Fluid produces dullness.

</details>

### Q2. Which auscultatory finding is commonly expected over a sizable hepatic hydrothorax?

**Difficulty:** 🟢 Easy · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD · Original practice

- **A.** Reduced breath sounds
- **B.** Diffuse loud wheezing as the defining sign
- **C.** Stridor over the neck
- **D.** A pleural friction rub in every case

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: A. Reduced breath sounds**

**Option-wise explanation:**
- **A (Sahi):** Pleural fluid lung sounds ko chest wall tak pahunchne se dampen kar sakti hai.
- **B (Galat):** Wheezing airway narrowing ka sign hai, pleural fluid ka defining sign nahi.
- **C (Galat):** Stridor upper airway narrowing indicate karta hai.
- **D (Galat):** Friction rub inflammation mein ho sakta hai, har effusion mein nahi.

**Exam clue:** Pleural fluid can reduce breath sounds.

</details>

### Q3. Which bedside measurement estimates oxygen saturation in a breathless patient with hepatic hydrothorax?

**Difficulty:** 🟢 Easy · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD · Original practice

- **A.** Peak expiratory flow alone
- **B.** Abdominal girth alone
- **C.** Capillary blood glucose alone
- **D.** Pulse oximetry

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: D. Pulse oximetry**

**Option-wise explanation:**
- **A (Galat):** Peak flow airflow assess karta hai, oxygen saturation nahi.
- **B (Galat):** Girth ascites trend batata hai, oxygenation nahi.
- **C (Galat):** Glucose metabolic measurement hai, oxygen saturation nahi.
- **D (Sahi):** Pulse oximeter peripheral arterial oxygen saturation estimate karta hai. Respiratory symptoms ke saath reading interpret karte hain.

**Exam clue:** Measure SpO2 during respiratory assessment.

</details>

### Q4. Which position may help a conscious breathless patient with hydrothorax while awaiting assessment, if tolerated?

**Difficulty:** 🟢 Easy · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD · Original practice

- **A.** Head-down Trendelenburg
- **B.** Supported upright sitting
- **C.** Flat supine regardless of symptoms
- **D.** Prone with the face covered

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: B. Supported upright sitting**

**Option-wise explanation:**
- **A (Galat):** Head-down posture abdominal pressure aur breathlessness worsen kar sakti hai.
- **B (Sahi):** Upright posture diaphragm movement aur comfortable breathing mein help kar sakti hai, patient tolerance assess karo.
- **C (Galat):** Flat position kuch patients mein dyspnea badha sakti hai.
- **D (Galat):** Face cover karna airway monitoring compromise karta hai.

**Exam clue:** Support breathing with a tolerated upright position.

</details>

### Q5. Which dietary component is commonly restricted as part of prescribed conservative management of cirrhosis-related hydrothorax?

**Difficulty:** 🟢 Easy · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD · Original practice

- **A.** All dietary protein
- **B.** Sodium
- **C.** All dietary fat
- **D.** All water-soluble vitamins

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: B. Sodium**

**Option-wise explanation:**
- **A (Galat):** Protein restriction routine nahi hai aur malnutrition worsen kar sakti hai.
- **B (Sahi):** Sodium retention fluid accumulation mein contribute karti hai. Prescribed sodium restriction fluid management support karti hai.
- **C (Galat):** All fat restriction hydrothorax ka specific treatment nahi hai.
- **D (Galat):** Vitamin elimination nutritional deficiency ka risk badhati hai.

**Exam clue:** Sodium control supports fluid management.

</details>

### Q6. Which imaging method is commonly used at the bedside to locate pleural fluid before thoracentesis?

**Difficulty:** 🟢 Easy · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD · Original practice

- **A.** Thoracic ultrasound
- **B.** Electrocardiogram
- **C.** Spirometry
- **D.** Abdominal plain radiograph alone

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: A. Thoracic ultrasound**

**Option-wise explanation:**
- **A (Sahi):** Thoracic ultrasound fluid pocket aur nearby structures identify karne mein help karta hai. Trained clinician safe procedure planning karta hai.
- **B (Galat):** ECG cardiac electrical signals record karta hai.
- **C (Galat):** Spirometry airflow measure karti hai, puncture site locate nahi karti.
- **D (Galat):** Abdominal X-ray pleural access localization ka preferred bedside tool nahi hai.

**Exam clue:** Ultrasound helps locate pleural fluid.

</details>
