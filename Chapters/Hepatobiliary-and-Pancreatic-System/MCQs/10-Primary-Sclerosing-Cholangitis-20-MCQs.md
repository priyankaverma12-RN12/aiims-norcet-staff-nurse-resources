### Q1. Which duct distribution is characteristically affected in large-duct primary sclerosing cholangitis (PSC)?

**Difficulty:** Easy · **Priority:** HIGH YIELD · **Exam focus:** NORCET / HPRCA HP Staff Nurse / PGIMER · Original practice

- **A.** Only microscopic pancreatic acini
- **B.** Only the gallbladder mucosa
- **C.** Only the hepatic veins
- **D.** Intrahepatic and/or extrahepatic bile ducts

<details><summary>✅ Correct answer and detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: D. Intrahepatic and/or extrahepatic bile ducts**

**A. Kyun galat hai:** Pancreatic acini digestive enzymes banate hain. PSC ka main target biliary tree hai.

**B. Kyun galat hai:** Gallbladder mucosa alone ki disease se PSC define nahi hoti.

**C. Kyun galat hai:** Hepatic veins blood drain karti hain. Unki obstruction Budd-Chiari syndrome se related ho sakti hai.

**D. Kyun sahi hai:** PSC mein bile ducts ke andar inflammation aur fibrosis se narrowing hoti hai. Large-duct disease liver ke andar ya bahar ke ducts ko affect kar sakti hai.

**Exam clue:** PSC mein biliary tree ki multifocal injury hoti hai.

</details>

### Q2. Which cholangiographic pattern most strongly supports large-duct PSC after secondary causes are excluded?

**Difficulty:** Medium · **Priority:** HIGH YIELD · **Exam focus:** NORCET / HPRCA HP Staff Nurse / PGIMER · Original practice

- **A.** A single stone in an otherwise normal cystic duct
- **B.** Multiple strictures alternating with normal or dilated duct segments
- **C.** Uniform dilation of every duct without narrowing
- **D.** An isolated pancreatic tail cyst

<details><summary>✅ Correct answer and detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: B. Multiple strictures alternating with normal or dilated duct segments**

**A. Kyun galat hai:** Isolated cystic duct stone gallstone disease suggest karta hai, PSC ka typical multifocal pattern nahi.

**B. Kyun sahi hai:** Multiple narrowed aur relatively dilated segments se beaded appearance banti hai. Appropriate clinical context mein ye large-duct PSC support karti hai.

**C. Kyun galat hai:** Uniform dilation mein alternating strictures absent hain.

**D. Kyun galat hai:** Pancreatic tail cyst biliary beading ka diagnostic pattern nahi hai.

**Exam clue:** Multifocal beading is the characteristic imaging clue.

</details>

### Q3. A stable adult has suspected PSC after initial ultrasound and liver tests. Which study is preferred to characterize the biliary tree noninvasively?

**Difficulty:** Medium · **Priority:** HIGH YIELD · **Exam focus:** NORCET / HPRCA HP Staff Nurse / PGIMER · Original practice

- **A.** High-quality MRI with MRCP
- **B.** Routine diagnostic ERCP as the first choice
- **C.** Plain abdominal radiography
- **D.** Barium swallow

<details><summary>✅ Correct answer and detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: A. High-quality MRI with MRCP**

**A. Kyun sahi hai:** MRCP magnetic resonance imaging se bile ducts visualize karti hai without routine duct instrumentation. Typical PSC diagnosis mein ye preferred approach hai.

**B. Kyun galat hai:** ERCP invasive hai aur pancreatitis ya infection ka risk hai. Isko routine diagnostic first choice nahi banate.

**C. Kyun galat hai:** Plain X-ray duct strictures ki detailed map nahi deta.

**D. Kyun galat hai:** Barium swallow esophagus assess karta hai, biliary tree nahi.

**Exam clue:** MRCP diagnoses, ERCP is reserved for appropriate intervention or sampling.

</details>

### Q4. A patient has persistent unexplained cholestatic liver tests, but a high-quality MRCP is normal. Which next step may establish small-duct PSC?

- **A.** Diagnose large-duct PSC solely from the normal scan
- **B.** Immediate routine biliary stent insertion
- **C.** Specialist-directed liver biopsy
- **D.** Exclude all chronic liver disease permanently

<details><summary>✅ Correct answer and detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: C. Specialist-directed liver biopsy**

**A. Kyun galat hai:** Normal MRCP characteristic large-duct findings demonstrate nahi karta.

**B. Kyun galat hai:** Visible treatable stricture absent hone par routine stenting justified nahi hai.

**C. Kyun sahi hai:** Small-duct PSC mein microscopic ducts affected ho sakte hain jo MRCP par visible nahi hote. Specialist biopsy se small-duct disease aur alternative diagnoses assess kar sakta hai.

**D. Kyun galat hai:** Normal cholangiography microscopic liver disease exclude nahi karti.

**Exam clue:** Normal MRCP with persistent suspicion may require biopsy.

</details>

### Q5. A patient with multifocal biliary strictures has a history of major bile duct injury during previous surgery. Why is this history important before labeling the condition PSC?

**Difficulty:** Medium · **Priority:** HIGH YIELD · **Exam focus:** NORCET / HPRCA HP Staff Nurse / PGIMER · Original practice

- **A.** It may indicate secondary sclerosing cholangitis
- **B.** It proves idiopathic PSC without further evaluation
- **C.** It rules out biliary disease altogether
- **D.** It establishes primary biliary cholangitis based on surgery alone

<details><summary>✅ Correct answer and detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: A. It may indicate secondary sclerosing cholangitis**

**A. Kyun sahi hai:** Surgical injury se bile ducts scar ho sakti hain aur PSC jaisi imaging aa sakti hai. Primary disease diagnose karne se pehle identifiable secondary causes assess karte hain.

**B. Kyun galat hai:** Known duct injury alternate explanation hai. Isko ignore karke idiopathic PSC label nahi karna chahiye.

**C. Kyun galat hai:** Duct injury biliary disease ka important risk hai.

**D. Kyun galat hai:** PBC autoimmune small-duct disease hai. Surgery history alone PBC establish nahi karti.

**Exam clue:** Exclude secondary causes before diagnosing primary sclerosing cholangitis.

</details>

**Exam clue:** PSC mimics include IgG4-related cholangitis.

<details><summary>✅ Correct answer and detailed explanation</summary>

**B. Kyun sahi hai:** PSC aur colitis ka combination colorectal malignancy risk badhata hai. Specialist shortened surveillance interval, usually every one to two years under AASLD guidance, plan karta hai.

**C. Kyun galat hai:** Abdominal ultrasound colonic mucosa ka adequate surveillance test nahi hai.

**D. Kyun galat hai:** Symptoms controlled hone se cancer risk eliminate nahi hota.

**Exam clue:** PSC-IBD needs intensified colorectal surveillance.

- **B.** Only pancreatic insulinoma risk increases
- **C.** PSC increases the risk of cholangiocarcinoma and gallbladder malignancy

<details><summary>✅ Correct answer and detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: C. PSC increases the risk of cholangiocarcinoma and gallbladder malignancy**

**D. Kyun galat hai:** Biliary malignancy risk established cirrhosis ke bina bhi relevant ho sakta hai.

**Exam clue:** PSC cancer follow-up extends beyond HCC screening.

</details>
