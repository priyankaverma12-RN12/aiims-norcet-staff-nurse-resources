### Q1. What is a biloma after biliary surgery?

**Difficulty:** Easy · **Priority:** HIGH YIELD · **Exam focus:** NORCET / HPRCA HP Staff Nurse / PGIMER · Original practice

- **A.** A clot within a coronary artery
- **B.** A localized collection of bile outside the normal biliary tract
- **C.** A collection of cerebrospinal fluid
- **D.** A benign tumor of pancreatic islet cells

<details><summary>✅ Correct answer and detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: B. A localized collection of bile outside the normal biliary tract**

**A. Kyun galat hai:** Coronary clot blood vessel pathology hai, bile collection nahi.

**B. Kyun sahi hai:** Bile leak hone par bile surrounding tissue mein collect ho sakti hai. Is localized collection ko biloma kehte hain.

**C. Kyun galat hai:** CSF brain aur spinal structures se related hai.

**D. Kyun galat hai:** Islet tumor solid endocrine lesion ho sakta hai, biloma nahi.

**Exam clue:** Biloma means an abnormal localized bile collection.

</details>

### Q2. A patient has an imaging-confirmed postoperative biloma but no jaundice. Which interpretation is appropriate?

**Difficulty:** Medium · **Priority:** HIGH YIELD · **Exam focus:** NORCET / HPRCA HP Staff Nurse / PGIMER · Original practice

- **A.** Normal bilirubin excludes every bile duct injury
- **B.** A biloma is impossible unless the common duct is completely obstructed
- **C.** A bile leak can occur without obstructive jaundice
- **D.** The collection must be pancreatic in origin

<details><summary>✅ Correct answer and detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: C. A bile leak can occur without obstructive jaundice**

**A. Kyun galat hai:** Bilirubin normal hona leak ko rule out nahi karta.

**B. Kyun galat hai:** Minor duct leak without complete obstruction possible hai.

**C. Kyun sahi hai:** Leak mein bile normal duct se bahar escape karti hai. Obstruction absent ho toh jaundice prominent na bhi ho sakti hai.

**D. Kyun galat hai:** Collection ki origin clinical imaging aur fluid assessment se determine hoti hai.

**Exam clue:** Bile leakage does not necessarily cause jaundice.

</details>
