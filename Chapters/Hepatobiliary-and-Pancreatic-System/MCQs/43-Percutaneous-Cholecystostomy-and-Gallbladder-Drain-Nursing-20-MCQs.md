### Q1. Which structure is directly decompressed by a percutaneous cholecystostomy catheter?

**Difficulty:** Easy · **Priority:** HIGH YIELD · **Exam focus:** NORCET / HPRCA HP Staff Nurse / PGIMER · Original practice

- **A.** Common bile duct
- **B.** Gallbladder
- **C.** Pancreatic duct
- **D.** Hepatic vein

<details><summary>✅ Correct answer and detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: B. Gallbladder**

**A. Kyun galat hai:** Common bile duct ki drainage ek alag biliary intervention hai.

**B. Kyun sahi hai:** Is tube ka tip gallbladder mein hota hai. Isse inflamed gallbladder ke andar ka fluid nikal sakta hai.

**C. Kyun galat hai:** Pancreatic duct digestive enzymes le jaata hai aur is procedure ka target nahi hai.

**D. Kyun galat hai:** Hepatic vein liver se blood bahar le jaati hai. Isme gallbladder drainage nahi hoti.

**Exam clue:** Remember the organ-specific target of the catheter.

</details>

### Q2. What does the term acalculous cholecystitis mean?

**Difficulty:** Easy · **Priority:** HIGH YIELD · **Exam focus:** NORCET / HPRCA HP Staff Nurse / PGIMER · Original practice

- **A.** Inflammation caused only by a common duct stone
- **B.** Gallbladder cancer with metastases
- **C.** Pancreatic inflammation without alcohol use
- **D.** Gallbladder inflammation without gallstones

<details><summary>✅ Correct answer and detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: D. Gallbladder inflammation without gallstones**

**A. Kyun galat hai:** Common duct stone alag location par hota hai aur is definition ko fit nahi karta.

**B. Kyun galat hai:** Cancer malignant cells ki growth hai. Acalculous inflammation ka matlab cancer nahi hota.

**C. Kyun galat hai:** Pancreatitis pancreas mein hoti hai. Is term mein gallbladder affected hai.

**D. Kyun sahi hai:** Acalculous ka matlab stone ke bina hai. Gallbladder mein inflammation phir bhi serious ho sakti hai.

**Exam clue:** Acalculous does not mean absence of inflammation.

</details>

**B. Kyun galat hai:** Urinary bladder urine store karta hai aur biliary approach se unrelated hai.

**Exam clue:** Transhepatic means through the liver.

### Q5. Which prescribed solution is commonly used to maintain cholecystostomy catheter patency?

- **A.** Sterile normal saline
- **B.** Tap water
- **C.** Concentrated antiseptic
- **D.** Hypertonic glucose

<details><summary>✅ Correct answer and detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: A. Sterile normal saline**

**A. Kyun sahi hai:** Sterile saline prescribed technique se tube mein use hota hai taaki blockage ka risk kam ho.

**B. Kyun galat hai:** Tap water sterile nahi hota. Isse infection ka risk ho sakta hai.

**C. Kyun galat hai:** Antiseptic tissue ko harm kar sakta hai aur routine catheter flush nahi hai.

**Exam clue:** Use the prescribed sterile solution and volume.

### Q9. A surgically fit adult with acute calculous cholecystitis asks whether every patient should receive a cholecystostomy tube instead of surgery. What is the best response?

- **D.** Tube insertion eliminates the need for clinical assessment

<details><summary>✅ Correct answer and detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: C. Early laparoscopic cholecystectomy is generally preferred when appropriate**

**A. Kyun galat hai:** Drain fluid nikalta hai. Ye har stone ko remove nahi karta.

**B. Kyun galat hai:** Acute inflammation surgery ka common indication hai. Fitness aur surgical expertise matter karte hain.

**C. Kyun sahi hai:** Fit patient mein surgery diseased gallbladder ko remove karti hai. Drainage selected unfit patients ke liye alternative hai.

**D. Kyun galat hai:** Drain bhi procedure hai jiske indications aur risks assess karne padte hain.

**Exam clue:** A drain is not first choice for every fit patient.

</details>

### Q10. A patient has a cholecystostomy drain and a documented prescribed flush. The flush meets unusual resistance. What should the nurse do?

**Difficulty:** Medium · **Priority:** HIGH YIELD · **Exam focus:** NORCET / HPRCA HP Staff Nurse / PGIMER · Original practice

- **A.** Stop the attempt and seek instructions from the responsible team
- **B.** Inject the same volume with greater pressure
- **C.** Insert a rigid instrument through the tube
- **D.** Remove the catheter independently

<details><summary>✅ Correct answer and detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: A. Stop the attempt and seek instructions from the responsible team**

**A. Kyun sahi hai:** Resistance obstruction ya catheter problem ka sign ho sakti hai. Force karne se tube ya tissue ko harm ho sakta hai.

**B. Kyun galat hai:** High pressure injured tissue ya catheter ko damage kar sakta hai.

**C. Kyun galat hai:** Instrument se tube perforate ho sakti hai aur infection ka risk hai.

**D. Kyun galat hai:** Removal ke liye tract maturity aur specialist plan important hai. Bedside par independent removal unsafe hai.

**Exam clue:** Never force a resistant drain flush.

</details>

### Q11. A patient arrives for review with the gallbladder drain partly pulled out and the fixation marking displaced. Which action is appropriate?

- **B.** Cut off the protruding section
- **C.** Ignore the displacement if the dressing is dry
- **D.** Secure it in its current position and promptly contact the drain team

<details><summary>✅ Correct answer and detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: D. Secure it in its current position and promptly contact the drain team**

**A. Kyun galat hai:** Blind reinsertion wrong position aur bile leakage cause kar sakta hai.

**B. Kyun galat hai:** Cutting se drainage system damage ho sakta hai aur tube retrieve karna mushkil hoga.

**C. Kyun galat hai:** Dry dressing correct internal position confirm nahi karti. Displacement assess karna zaroori hai.

**D. Kyun sahi hai:** Catheter tip gallbladder se bahar aa sakta hai. Specialist position assess karega aur nurse further movement rokti hai.

**Exam clue:** A displaced catheter requires specialist assessment.

</details>
