### Q1. Which vascular abnormality is central to hepatopulmonary syndrome (HPS)?

**Difficulty:** 🟢 Easy · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD · Original practice

- **A.** Pulmonary arterial thrombus in every case
- **B.** Fixed narrowing of the main bronchus
- **C.** Isolated left ventricular outflow obstruction
- **D.** Intrapulmonary vascular dilatation

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: D. Intrapulmonary vascular dilatation**

**Option-wise explanation:**
- **A (Galat):** Pulmonary embolism hypoxia ka separate cause hai. HPS mein thrombus hona required nahi hai.
- **B (Galat):** Bronchial narrowing airflow problem hai, jabki HPS primarily pulmonary vascular abnormality hai.
- **C (Galat):** Left ventricular outflow obstruction cardiac problem hai. HPS ke dilated lung vessels ko define nahi karta.
- **D (Sahi):** HPS mein lungs ki chhoti vessels dilate ho jaati hain, jisse oxygen transfer inefficient ho sakta hai.

**Exam clue:** HPS mein dilated pulmonary vessels gas exchange ko affect karti hain.
