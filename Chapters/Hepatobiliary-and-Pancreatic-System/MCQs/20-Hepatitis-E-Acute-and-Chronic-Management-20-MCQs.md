### Q1. Which viral genome type is characteristic of hepatitis E virus?

**Difficulty:** Easy · **Priority:** HIGH YIELD · **Exam focus:** NORCET / HPRCA HP Staff Nurse / PGIMER · Original practice

- **A.** Double-stranded human chromosomal DNA
- **B.** Negative-sense segmented RNA
- **C.** A bacterial plasmid without viral RNA
- **D.** Single-stranded positive-sense RNA

<details><summary>✅ Correct answer and detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: D. Single-stranded positive-sense RNA**

**A. Kyun galat hai:** Human chromosomal DNA HEV ka genome nahi hai.

**B. Kyun galat hai:** Segmented negative-sense RNA HEV ka genome type nahi hai.

**C. Kyun galat hai:** HEV virus hai, bacterial plasmid nahi.

**D. Kyun sahi hai:** HEV positive-sense single-stranded RNA virus hai. Iska genome viral protein translation ke liye template provide karta hai.

**Exam clue:** HEV is a positive-sense RNA virus.

</details>

### Q2. An outbreak of jaundice occurs after flooding damages a municipal drinking-water system. Which HEV-specific public health action should accompany case investigation?

- **A.** Provide antibiotics to every resident without evaluation
- **B.** Restore safe drinking water and investigate fecal contamination of the water supply
- **C.** Close all antenatal clinics indefinitely
- **D.** Distribute hepatitis B vaccine as the only HEV intervention

<details><summary>✅ Correct answer and detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: B. Restore safe drinking water and investigate fecal contamination of the water supply**

**A. Kyun galat hai:** Mass antibiotics viral waterborne source ko correct nahi karti.

**B. Kyun sahi hai:** Genotypes 1 aur 2 waterborne fecal contamination se spread kar sakte hain. Safe water restoration outbreak source ko address karti hai.

**C. Kyun galat hai:** Antenatal services pregnant patients ke liye particularly important hain.

**D. Kyun galat hai:** HBV vaccine HEV-specific protection nahi deti.

**Exam clue:** Waterborne HEV control requires safe water and sanitation.

</details>
