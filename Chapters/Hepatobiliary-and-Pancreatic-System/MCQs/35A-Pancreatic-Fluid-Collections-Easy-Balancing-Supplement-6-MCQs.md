### Q1. What does necrosectomy mean in the treatment of pancreatic necrosis?

**Difficulty:** 🟢 Easy · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD · Original practice

- **A.** Removal of a normal gallbladder
- **B.** Repair of a hernia
- **C.** Removal of a kidney stone
- **D.** Removal of dead tissue

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: D. Removal of dead tissue**

**Option-wise explanation:**
- **A (Galat):** Gallbladder removal cholecystectomy hai.
- **B (Galat):** Hernia repair ka aim abdominal wall defect correct karna hai.
- **C (Galat):** Kidney stone removal urological procedure hai.
- **D (Sahi):** Necrosectomy mein nonviable tissue remove kiya jaata hai. Pancreatic necrosis mein selected patients ko specialist procedure ki zaroorat ho sakti hai.

**Exam clue:** Necrosectomy removes necrotic tissue.

</details>

### Q2. What does percutaneous drainage of a pancreatic collection mean?

**Difficulty:** 🟢 Easy · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD · Original practice

- **A.** Drainage exclusively through the mouth using an endoscope
- **B.** Removal of the whole pancreas
- **C.** Placement of a drainage catheter through the skin
- **D.** Oral administration of digestive enzymes

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: C. Placement of a drainage catheter through the skin**

**Option-wise explanation:**
- **A (Galat):** Mouth se endoscopic drainage different access route hai.
- **B (Galat):** Total pancreatectomy organ removal hai, drainage nahi.
- **C (Sahi):** Percutaneous ka meaning skin ke through hai. Image guidance se catheter selected collection tak place karte hain.
- **D (Galat):** Enzymes nutrient digestion mein help karti hain, collection drain nahi karti.

**Exam clue:** Percutaneous means through the skin.

</details>

### Q3. What is cystogastrostomy in the treatment of a suitable pancreatic collection?

**Difficulty:** 🟢 Easy · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD · Original practice

- **A.** Creation of a drainage connection between the collection and stomach
- **B.** Connection of the urinary bladder to the colon
- **C.** Removal of the gastric antrum alone
- **D.** Closure of the main bile duct

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: A. Creation of a drainage connection between the collection and stomach**

**Option-wise explanation:**
- **A (Sahi):** Cystogastrostomy selected collection ko stomach lumen mein drain karne ke liye connection create karti hai. Anatomy aur indication expert assess karta hai.
- **B (Galat):** Urinary bladder colon se connect karna is pancreatic procedure ka meaning nahi.
- **C (Galat):** Gastric resection alone drainage connection establish nahi karti.
- **D (Galat):** Bile duct closure collection drainage ka aim nahi hai.

**Exam clue:** Cystogastrostomy drains into the stomach.

</details>

### Q4. Which term describes an abnormal persistent communication between the pancreas and the skin after external drainage?

**Difficulty:** 🟢 Easy · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD · Original practice

- **A.** Pleuropericardial fistula
- **B.** Pancreaticocutaneous fistula
- **C.** Vesicovaginal fistula
- **D.** Arteriovenous fistula

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: B. Pancreaticocutaneous fistula**

**Option-wise explanation:**
- **A (Galat):** Pleuropericardial term thoracic structures se related hai.
- **B (Sahi):** Pancreatic secretions skin tak abnormal tract se aa sakti hain. External drainage ke baad persistent output specialist assessment maang sakta hai.
- **C (Galat):** Vesicovaginal fistula bladder aur vagina ke beech hoti hai.
- **D (Galat):** Arteriovenous fistula artery aur vein ke beech communication hai.

**Exam clue:** Pancreaticocutaneous fistula reaches the skin.

</details>

### Q5. Which description of drainage fluid is particularly useful when assessing possible purulent output from a pancreatic collection?

**Difficulty:** 🟢 Easy · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD · Original practice

- **A.** Clear pale-yellow fluid alone
- **B.** Transparent fluid without particles
- **C.** Thick cloudy pus-like material
- **D.** Clear serous fluid alone

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: C. Thick cloudy pus-like material**

**Option-wise explanation:**
- **A (Galat):** Clear pale fluid pus ki characteristic appearance nahi hai.
- **B (Galat):** Transparent fluid infection exclude nahi karti, lekin pus-like appearance different hai.
- **C (Sahi):** Thick cloudy pus-like output infection ka clue ho sakta hai. Appearance alone definitive culture result nahi hai, clinical review zaroori hai.
- **D (Galat):** Serous fluid usually clear hoti hai, purulent material thick ya cloudy ho sakta hai.

**Exam clue:** Describe drain appearance objectively and report concerning changes.

</details>

### Q6. Which basic precaution helps protect an external pancreatic drainage catheter from accidental traction during transfers?

**Difficulty:** 🟢 Easy · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD · Original practice

- **A.** Lift the patient by the drainage tubing
- **B.** Allow tubing to catch under the bed rail
- **C.** Place the full collection bag on the unsupported insertion site
- **D.** Support the tubing and secure the catheter according to the prescribed device-care plan

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: D. Support the tubing and secure the catheter according to the prescribed device-care plan**

**Option-wise explanation:**
- **A (Galat):** Tubing patient ko lift karne ke liye nahi hai.
- **B (Galat):** Bed rail mein tubing trap hone se traction ho sakti hai.
- **C (Galat):** Full bag ka weight insertion site par tension create kar sakta hai.
- **D (Sahi):** Supported tubing aur appropriate securement se accidental pulling aur displacement ka risk kam hota hai.

**Exam clue:** Protect catheter and tubing during movement.

</details>
