### Q2. A patient reports drinking 180 mL of a beverage containing 40% alcohol by volume. Using ethanol density 0.789 g/mL, approximately how many grams of ethanol were consumed?

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: C. 57 g**

**Option-wise explanation:**
- **A (Galat):** 72 ethanol ka volume mL mein hai, mass grams mein nahi. Density ka conversion abhi baaki hai.
- **B (Galat):** 142 g tab aata hai jab total beverage volume ko pure ethanol maan kar density lagate hain. Beverage sirf 40% alcohol hai.

- **D (Galat):** 180 mL total drink ka volume hai. Yeh ethanol ka gram weight nahi hai.

**Exam clue:** Volume × alcohol fraction × ethanol density se grams milte hain.
