### Q1. Which term best describes severe acute hepatic injury with coagulopathy but without encephalopathy in an adult without known chronic liver disease?

**Difficulty:** Easy · **Priority:** HIGH YIELD · **Exam focus:** NORCET / HPRCA HP Staff Nurse / PGIMER · Original practice

- **A.** Established acute liver failure by definition
- **B.** Compensated cirrhosis
- **C.** Acute liver injury without established acute liver failure
- **D.** Isolated extrahepatic biliary obstruction by definition

<details><summary>✅ Correct answer and detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: C. Acute liver injury without established acute liver failure**

**A. Kyun galat hai:** Established adult ALF mein coagulopathy ke saath hepatic encephalopathy bhi hoti hai. Encephalopathy absent hone par definition complete nahi hai.

**B. Kyun galat hai:** Compensated cirrhosis chronic scarring ki stage hai. Scenario recent severe injury batata hai aur known chronic disease absent hai.

**C. Kyun sahi hai:** Acute liver injury mein liver cells recently damage hue hain. Coagulopathy ka matlab clotting abnormal hai. Jab tak hepatic encephalopathy develop nahi hui hai, standard adult ALF definition fully meet nahi hoti. Phir bhi severe acute injury urgent assessment aur monitoring maangti hai.

**D. Kyun galat hai:** Extrahepatic biliary obstruction bile duct blockage hai. Coagulopathy aur acute hepatic injury alone obstruction confirm nahi karte.

**Exam clue:** Encephalopathy absent ho toh ALF definition complete nahi hai, lekin severe injury ko urgent samjho.

</details>

### Q3. Which medication should the nurse anticipate promptly when acetaminophen-associated acute liver failure is suspected?

**Difficulty:** Medium · **Priority:** HIGH YIELD · **Exam focus:** NORCET / HPRCA HP Staff Nurse / PGIMER · Original practice

- **A.** Flumazenil
- **B.** Atropine
- **C.** Deferoxamine
- **D.** N-acetylcysteine

<details><summary>✅ Correct answer and detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: D. N-acetylcysteine**

**A. Kyun galat hai:** Flumazenil benzodiazepine receptor antagonist hai. Ye acetaminophen toxicity ka antidote nahi hai.

**B. Kyun galat hai:** Atropine muscarinic receptors ko block karti hai. Iska acetaminophen metabolite detoxification mein role nahi hai.

**C. Kyun galat hai:** Deferoxamine iron chelator hai. Ye iron poisoning mein use ho sakti hai, acetaminophen injury mein nahi.

**D. Kyun sahi hai:** N-acetylcysteine glutathione ko replenish karne mein madad karti hai. Glutathione acetaminophen ke toxic metabolite ko neutralize karta hai. Suspected toxicity mein clinician-directed treatment jaldi shuru karna important hai.

**Exam clue:** Acetaminophen toxicity ka specific antidote NAC hai.

</details>

### Q6. Why can acute liver failure cause cerebral edema more abruptly than established chronic liver disease?

**Difficulty:** Medium · **Priority:** HIGH YIELD · **Exam focus:** NORCET / HPRCA HP Staff Nurse / PGIMER · Original practice

- **A.** The brain has less time to adapt to rapid changes in ammonia and osmotic balance
- **B.** Portal pressure always falls to zero during acute injury
- **C.** Bilirubin directly obstructs the cerebral arteries
- **D.** Chronic liver disease prevents ammonia from reaching the brain

<details><summary>✅ Correct answer and detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: A. The brain has less time to adapt to rapid changes in ammonia and osmotic balance**

**A. Kyun sahi hai:** ALF mein ammonia jaldi badh sakta hai aur astrocytes naam ke brain support cells swell kar sakte hain. Brain ko gradual adaptation ka time kam milta hai.

**B. Kyun galat hai:** Portal pressure zero hona ALF ki defining mechanism nahi hai.

**C. Kyun galat hai:** Bilirubin cerebral arteries ko mechanically block karke typical ALF edema nahi banata.

**D. Kyun galat hai:** Chronic liver disease mein bhi ammonia brain ko affect kar sakta hai. Adaptation complete protection nahi deti.

**Exam clue:** Acute ammonia rise aur limited brain adaptation se edema risk badhta hai.

</details>

### Q12. A patient with acute liver failure has a falling ALT, but INR and lactate continue to rise despite resuscitation. Which interpretation is most appropriate?

- **B.** Falling ALT alone does not establish hepatic recovery
- **C.** Rising INR is expected during complete liver regeneration
- **D.** Lactate can be disregarded in liver failure

<details><summary>✅ Correct answer and detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: B. Falling ALT alone does not establish hepatic recovery**

**A. Kyun galat hai:** ALT akela prognosis decide nahi karta. INR, lactate aur clinical state bhi important hain.

**B. Kyun sahi hai:** ALT liver cell injury ka marker hai. Bahut severe injury mein viable liver cells kam hone par ALT gir sakta hai, jabki synthetic function aur perfusion kharab ho rahe hon. Overall trends ko team review karti hai.

**C. Kyun galat hai:** Rising INR ko complete recovery ka proof nahi maan sakte, kyunki clotting factor synthesis impaired ho sakti hai.

**D. Kyun galat hai:** Lactate impaired clearance aur tissue perfusion ki information de sakta hai. Isko ignore nahi karna chahiye.

**Exam clue:** Enzyme kam hone ko recovery mat samjho jab INR aur perfusion markers worsen ho rahe hain.
