### Q1. Which pancreatic cells produce digestive enzymes released into the pancreatic ducts?

**Difficulty:** Easy | **Priority:** High | **Topic:** Pancreatitis and Pancreatic Nursing | **Evidence:** Original practice

- **A.** Acinar cells
- **B.** Islet beta cells
- **C.** Islet alpha cells
- **D.** Hepatic Kupffer cells

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: A. Acinar cells**

- **A.** Acinar cells exocrine pancreas ka part hain. Ye digestion ke liye enzymes banate hain jo ducts mein release hote hain.
- **B.** Beta cells insulin hormone banate hain, digestive enzymes nahi.
- **C.** Alpha cells glucagon hormone banate hain, pancreatic digestive secretions nahi.
- **D.** Kupffer cells liver ke macrophages hain. Pancreatic acinar function nahi karte.

**Exam clue:** Acinar cells are exocrine and islets are endocrine.

</details>

### Q2. Which process contributes directly to acute pancreatic inflammation?

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Priority:** High | **Topic:** Pancreatitis and Pancreatic Nursing | **Evidence:** Original practice

- **A.** Failure of hemoglobin synthesis in pancreatic ducts
- **B.** Premature activation of digestive enzymes within the pancreas
- **C.** Obligatory absence of all inflammatory responses
- **D.** Excess intestinal absorption of intact pancreatic cells

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: B. Premature activation of digestive enzymes within the pancreas**

- **A.** Hemoglobin red blood cell protein hai. Pancreatic duct mein iska synthesis typical mechanism nahi hai.
- **B.** Digestive enzymes normally intestine mein kaam karte hain. Pancreas ke andar premature activation injury aur inflammation contribute kar sakti hai.
- **C.** Pancreatitis mein inflammation hoti hai. Inflammatory responses absent hona defining mechanism nahi hai.
- **D.** Intestine intact pancreatic cells absorb karke pancreatitis cause nahi karta.

**Exam clue:** Premature enzyme activation contributes to pancreatic injury.

</details>

### Q3. A patient has typical acute pancreatic pain and lipase four times the upper reference limit. According to standard diagnostic criteria, what is generally true?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Priority:** High | **Topic:** Pancreatitis and Pancreatic Nursing | **Evidence:** Original practice

- **A.** All patients require immediate contrast CT regardless of the clinical findings
- **B.** A lipase elevation must reach ten times the upper limit in every case
- **C.** Two diagnostic criteria are already met and immediate CT is not always needed to establish the diagnosis
- **D.** A normal abdominal radiograph excludes pancreatitis

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: C. Two diagnostic criteria are already met and immediate CT is not always needed to establish the diagnosis**

- **A.** Routine early CT har clearly diagnosed patient ke liye required nahi hai. Clinical indication matter karti hai.
- **B.** Standard biochemical criterion at least three times ULN hai, ten times mandatory nahi.
- **C.** Typical pain aur enzyme level three times ULN se zyada hone par do criteria meet hote hain. Imaging diagnosis uncertain ho ya complications assess karni ho toh useful hai.
- **D.** Plain radiograph normal ho tab bhi pancreatitis ho sakta hai.

**Exam clue:** Two of three criteria usually establish the diagnosis.

</details>

### Q5. A patient with acute pancreatitis receiving prescribed IV fluids has falling urine output, increasing respiratory rate and new basal crackles. What is the best nursing response?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Priority:** High | **Topic:** Pancreatitis and Pancreatic Nursing | **Evidence:** Original practice

- **A.** Promptly reassess perfusion and respiratory status, notify the team and review the fluid plan
- **B.** Automatically double the infusion rate without assessment
- **C.** Stop all monitoring until the next morning
- **D.** Encourage rapid unrestricted oral water intake despite vomiting

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: A. Promptly reassess perfusion and respiratory status, notify the team and review the fluid plan**

- **A.** Low urine output perfusion issue suggest kar sakta hai, lekin crackles aur tachypnea fluid overload ya lung complication bhi ho sakte hain. Immediate reassessment chahiye.
- **B.** Blindly fluid rate double karne se overload worsen ho sakta hai. Goal-directed review important hai.
- **C.** Respiratory aur urine changes time-sensitive hain. Monitoring delay unsafe hai.
- **D.** Vomiting aur respiratory concern mein unrestricted oral fluids aspiration aur intolerance risk rakhte hain.

**Exam clue:** Balance resuscitation with repeated assessment for overload.

</details>

### Q6. Which IV crystalloid is generally preferred in current acute pancreatitis guidance when appropriate for the patient and prescribed?

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Priority:** High | **Topic:** Pancreatitis and Pancreatic Nursing | **Evidence:** Original practice

- **A.** Sterile water for intravenous infusion alone
- **B.** Lactated Ringer solution
- **C.** Hypertonic saline for every case
- **D.** Dextrose 50% as routine volume resuscitation

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: B. Lactated Ringer solution**

- **A.** Sterile water alone intravenously dena unsafe ho sakta hai kyunki ye hypotonic hai aur hemolysis cause kar sakta hai.
- **B.** Lactated Ringer ek balanced isotonic crystalloid hai. Current guidance mein suitable patients ke liye preference milti hai, with repeated reassessment.
- **C.** Hypertonic saline specific indications mein use hoti hai, routine pancreatitis resuscitation ke liye nahi.
- **D.** Concentrated dextrose specific glucose emergencies mein use hoti hai, routine volume replacement nahi.

**Exam clue:** Use prescribed isotonic crystalloid and reassess response.

</details>

### Q7. Which trend in acute pancreatitis raises concern about inadequate perfusion or worsening disease and merits prompt review?

- **B.** Stable oxygenation and resolving pain

- **D.** Improving urine output with stable renal markers

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: C. Rising blood urea nitrogen with tachycardia and declining urine output**

- **A.** Improving intake aur stable observations recovery support karte hain.
- **B.** Stable oxygenation aur resolving pain generally reassuring trends hain.

</details>

### Q8. A patient with mild acute pancreatitis is clinically improving and tolerates intake. Which nutritional strategy is supported by current guidance?

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Priority:** High | **Topic:** Pancreatitis and Pancreatic Nursing | **Evidence:** Original practice

- **A.** Mandatory fasting until every enzyme result normalizes
- **B.** Immediate total parenteral nutrition for all patients
- **C.** High-fat forced feeding despite nausea
- **D.** Early oral feeding as tolerated, often starting with an appropriate low-fat solid diet

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: D. Early oral feeding as tolerated, often starting with an appropriate low-fat solid diet**

- **A.** Enzyme normalization ka wait karke unnecessary fasting current approach nahi hai.
- **B.** Parenteral nutrition har patient ko required nahi hai. Gut use ho sake toh enteral route prefer hota hai.
- **C.** High-fat forced meal symptoms worsen kar sakta hai. Tolerance important hai.
- **D.** Improving mild pancreatitis mein early oral nutrition as tolerated gut function aur recovery support karti hai. Prescribed diet aur tolerance follow karte hain.

**Exam clue:** Feeding depends on clinical tolerance, not enzyme normalization alone.

</details>

### Q9. A patient with severe acute pancreatitis cannot maintain oral intake but has a functioning gastrointestinal tract. Which feeding route is generally preferred when clinically feasible?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Priority:** High | **Topic:** Pancreatitis and Pancreatic Nursing | **Evidence:** Original practice

- **A.** Enteral tube feeding
- **B.** Routine total parenteral nutrition as first choice in every case
- **C.** Prolonged starvation regardless of nutritional risk
- **D.** Intramuscular glucose injections as complete nutrition

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: A. Enteral tube feeding**

- **A.** Enteral feeding gut ko use karti hai aur intestinal barrier support karti hai. Oral intake impossible ho toh suitable tube feeding consider hoti hai.
- **B.** Parenteral nutrition selected patients ke liye hai jab enteral route adequate ya possible na ho.
- **C.** Long fasting malnutrition risk badhata hai. Nutritional support evaluate karna chahiye.
- **D.** Glucose injections complete nutrition nahi dete. Protein, fat aur micronutrients bhi required hain.

**Exam clue:** Use the gastrointestinal tract when safe and feasible.

</details>

### Q10. Which antibiotic approach is appropriate for a patient with sterile pancreatic necrosis and no evidence of infection?

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Priority:** High | **Topic:** Pancreatitis and Pancreatic Nursing | **Evidence:** Original practice

- **A.** Give broad-spectrum antibiotics indefinitely to all patients
- **B.** Routine prophylactic antibiotics are not generally recommended
- **C.** Antibiotics replace all supportive care
- **D.** Administer antifungals routinely regardless of clinical status

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: B. Routine prophylactic antibiotics are not generally recommended**

- **A.** Indefinite broad-spectrum use resistance aur adverse effects badha sakta hai. Clear indication chahiye.
- **B.** Sterile necrosis mein routine antibiotics benefit establish nahi karte. Infection suspicion ya confirmation par targeted clinical treatment decide hota hai.
- **C.** Fluids, pain control, monitoring aur nutrition supportive management ka important part hain.
- **D.** Antifungal drugs bhi routine prophylaxis ke liye indicated nahi hote without specific reason.

**Exam clue:** Necrosis does not automatically equal infection.

</details>

**Exam clue:** ERCP decisions depend on associated cholangitis or obstruction.

### Q15. Which stool feature is associated with pancreatic exocrine insufficiency?

**Difficulty:** Easy | **Priority:** High | **Topic:** Pancreatitis and Pancreatic Nursing | **Evidence:** Original practice

- **A.** Uniformly normal stool in every affected patient
- **B.** Black tarry stool specifically from pancreatic enzyme deficiency
- **C.** Bulky greasy stool that may be difficult to flush
- **D.** Bright red blood as the defining sign of fat malabsorption

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: C. Bulky greasy stool that may be difficult to flush**

- **A.** Exocrine insufficiency mein stool symptoms develop ho sakte hain, hamesha normal nahi.
- **B.** Black tarry stool GI bleeding ka clue ho sakta hai. Fat malabsorption ka characteristic sign nahi.
- **C.** Lipase aur doosre enzymes kam hone par fat properly digest nahi hota. Isse greasy bulky stool yani steatorrhea ho sakta hai.
- **D.** Bright red blood bleeding suggest karta hai. Steatorrhea blood ka defining feature nahi hai.

**Exam clue:** Steatorrhea suggests maldigestion of fat.

</details>

### Q17. A patient with chronic pancreatitis has weight loss despite adequate reported food intake, greasy stools and low fecal elastase. Which complication is most consistent?

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

### Q18. Which long-term metabolic complication should be monitored in chronic pancreatitis with progressive pancreatic tissue damage?

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Priority:** High | **Topic:** Pancreatitis and Pancreatic Nursing | **Evidence:** Original practice

- **A.** Permanent protection against all diabetes
- **B.** Pancreatogenic diabetes mellitus
- **C.** Primary adrenal failure in every patient
- **D.** Isolated vitamin C overdose

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: B. Pancreatogenic diabetes mellitus**

- **A.** Pancreatic injury diabetes risk badha sakti hai, protection nahi.
- **B.** Pancreatic damage insulin-producing cells ko affect kar sakta hai. Isliye glucose disturbance aur diabetes monitoring important hai.
- **C.** Adrenal gland alag endocrine organ hai. Chronic pancreatitis har patient mein adrenal failure cause nahi karti.
- **D.** Vitamin C overdose pancreatic endocrine damage ka characteristic result nahi hai.

**Exam clue:** Chronic pancreatitis affects both exocrine and endocrine functions.

</details>

- **A.** Independently remove the drain immediately
- **B.** Ignore output changes if a drain is present
- **C.** Clamp the drain without reviewing procedure-specific orders

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: D. Assess the patient, document drainage characteristics and promptly notify the surgical team**
