### Q1. What is the alternative name for acetaminophen?

**Difficulty:** Easy · **Priority:** High · **Exam focus:** NORCET / HPRCA HP Staff Nurse / PGIMER · Original practice

- **A.** Ibuprofen
- **B.** Paracetamol
- **C.** Amoxicillin
- **D.** Metformin

<details><summary>✅ Correct answer and detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: B. Paracetamol**

**A. Kyun galat hai:** Ibuprofen NSAID hai aur paracetamol se different drug hai.

**B. Kyun sahi hai:** Paracetamol aur acetaminophen same active medicine ke names hain. Medication history mein dono names recognize karna important hai.

**C. Kyun galat hai:** Amoxicillin antibiotic hai.

**D. Kyun galat hai:** Metformin diabetes management medicine hai.

**Exam clue:** Acetaminophen and paracetamol are the same drug.

</details>

### Q2. What does cerebral edema mean in a patient with acute liver failure?

**Difficulty:** Easy · **Priority:** High · **Exam focus:** NORCET / HPRCA HP Staff Nurse / PGIMER · Original practice

- **A.** Fluid collection in the pleural space
- **B.** Swelling of the ankle
- **C.** Fluid inside the peritoneal cavity
- **D.** Swelling of brain tissue

<details><summary>✅ Correct answer and detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: D. Swelling of brain tissue**

**A. Kyun galat hai:** Ye pleural effusion hai, brain swelling nahi.

**B. Kyun galat hai:** Ankle edema peripheral tissue fluid hai.

**C. Kyun galat hai:** Ye ascites hai, cerebral edema nahi.

**D. Kyun sahi hai:** Cerebral edema brain tissue mein excess fluid aur swelling ko describe karta hai. Skull ke andar pressure badh sakta hai.

**Exam clue:** Cerebral refers to the brain.

</details>

### Q3. Which abbreviation refers to the standardized measure of prothrombin time used in liver failure assessment?

**Difficulty:** Easy · **Priority:** High · **Exam focus:** NORCET / HPRCA HP Staff Nurse / PGIMER · Original practice

- **A.** BMI
- **B.** SpO2
- **C.** INR
- **D.** HbA1c

<details><summary>✅ Correct answer and detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: C. INR**

**A. Kyun galat hai:** BMI weight aur height se calculate hota hai.

**B. Kyun galat hai:** SpO2 oxygen saturation ka estimate hai.

**C. Kyun sahi hai:** International normalized ratio clotting time ko standardize karta hai. Liver synthetic dysfunction ke assessment mein iska trend useful hai.

**D. Kyun galat hai:** HbA1c longer-term blood glucose exposure ka marker hai.

**Exam clue:** INR standardizes prothrombin time.

</details>

### Q4. Which term describes an abnormally low blood glucose level in acute liver failure?

**Difficulty:** Easy · **Priority:** High · **Exam focus:** NORCET / HPRCA HP Staff Nurse / PGIMER · Original practice

- **A.** Hypoglycemia
- **B.** Hypernatremia
- **C.** Hyperglycemia
- **D.** Hypoxemia

<details><summary>✅ Correct answer and detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: A. Hypoglycemia**

**A. Kyun sahi hai:** Hypoglycemia blood glucose low hone ko kehte hain. Liver dysfunction glucose regulation ko impair kar sakti hai.

**B. Kyun galat hai:** Hypernatremia sodium high hone ko kehte hain.

**C. Kyun galat hai:** Hyperglycemia glucose high hone ko kehte hain, low nahi.

**D. Kyun galat hai:** Hypoxemia arterial blood oxygen low hone ko kehte hain.

**Exam clue:** Hypo means low.

</details>

### Q5. Which organ function is commonly assessed using serum creatinine during acute liver failure monitoring?

**Difficulty:** Easy · **Priority:** High · **Exam focus:** NORCET / HPRCA HP Staff Nurse / PGIMER · Original practice

- **A.** Visual accommodation
- **B.** Hearing sensitivity
- **C.** Thyroid hormone production
- **D.** Kidney function

<details><summary>✅ Correct answer and detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: D. Kidney function**

**A. Kyun galat hai:** Eye focusing function creatinine se measure nahi hoti.

**B. Kyun galat hai:** Hearing audiometry se assess hoti hai.

**C. Kyun galat hai:** Thyroid function TSH aur thyroid hormones se evaluate hoti hai.

**D. Kyun sahi hai:** Creatinine renal filtration assess karne mein help karta hai. Acute liver failure mein kidney dysfunction bhi develop ho sakti hai.

**Exam clue:** Creatinine is a renal function marker.

</details>

### Q6. Which clinical term describes abnormal blood clotting function in acute liver failure?

**Difficulty:** Easy · **Priority:** High · **Exam focus:** NORCET / HPRCA HP Staff Nurse / PGIMER · Original practice

- **A.** Neuropathy
- **B.** Coagulopathy
- **C.** Retinopathy
- **D.** Myopathy

<details><summary>✅ Correct answer and detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: B. Coagulopathy**

**A. Kyun galat hai:** Neuropathy nerves ki dysfunction hai.

**B. Kyun sahi hai:** Coagulopathy clotting system ki dysfunction hai. Liver clotting factors synthesize karta hai, isliye severe injury mein coagulation tests abnormal ho sakte hain.

**C. Kyun galat hai:** Retinopathy retina ki disease hai.

**D. Kyun galat hai:** Myopathy muscles ki disease hai.

**Exam clue:** Coagulopathy concerns hemostasis.

</details>
