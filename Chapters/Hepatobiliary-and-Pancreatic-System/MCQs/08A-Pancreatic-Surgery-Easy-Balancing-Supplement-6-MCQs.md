### Q1. Which part of the small intestine is resected during a standard pancreaticoduodenectomy?

**Difficulty:** Easy · **Priority:** High · **Exam focus:** NORCET / HPRCA HP Staff Nurse / PGIMER · Original practice

- **A.** Duodenum
- **B.** Terminal ileum
- **C.** Entire jejunum
- **D.** Cecum

<details><summary>✅ Correct answer and detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: A. Duodenum**

**A. Kyun sahi hai:** Duodenum pancreas ke head ke paas hota hai aur Whipple operation mein iska major portion remove hota hai.

**B. Kyun galat hai:** Terminal ileum small intestine ka distal part hai aur standard Whipple ka routine target nahi.

**C. Kyun galat hai:** Jejunum reconstruction mein use ho sakta hai, lekin entire jejunum routinely remove nahi hota.

**D. Kyun galat hai:** Cecum large intestine ka initial part hai, standard Whipple resection mein nahi.

**Exam clue:** The name pancreaticoduodenectomy includes the duodenum.

</details>

### Q2. Which bile-storing organ is generally removed during a standard Whipple operation?

**Difficulty:** Easy · **Priority:** High · **Exam focus:** NORCET / HPRCA HP Staff Nurse / PGIMER · Original practice

- **A.** Urinary bladder
- **B.** Gallbladder
- **C.** Spleen
- **D.** Left kidney

<details><summary>✅ Correct answer and detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: B. Gallbladder**

**A. Kyun galat hai:** Urinary bladder pelvic urine reservoir hai aur routine Whipple ka target nahi.

**B. Kyun sahi hai:** Whipple mein gallbladder aur distal bile duct ka portion remove ho sakta hai taaki involved region resect aur reconstruct ho.

**C. Kyun galat hai:** Spleen distal pancreatic surgery mein kabhi remove hoti hai, standard Whipple mein routine nahi.

**D. Kyun galat hai:** Kidney standard pancreaticoduodenectomy ka routine organ removal nahi hai.

**Exam clue:** Gallbladder removal is part of the usual Whipple procedure.

</details>

### Q3. What is meant by a pylorus-preserving pancreaticoduodenectomy?

**Difficulty:** Easy · **Priority:** High · **Exam focus:** NORCET / HPRCA HP Staff Nurse / PGIMER · Original practice

- **A.** The pancreatic head is always preserved
- **B.** The entire pancreas is removed
- **C.** The spleen is the only organ removed
- **D.** The pylorus at the stomach outlet is preserved

<details><summary>✅ Correct answer and detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: D. The pylorus at the stomach outlet is preserved**

**A. Kyun galat hai:** Pancreaticoduodenectomy mein head resection hoti hai, pylorus preservation head ko preserve nahi karti.

**B. Kyun galat hai:** Entire pancreas removal total pancreatectomy hai.

**C. Kyun galat hai:** Splenectomy alag operation hai.

**D. Kyun sahi hai:** Pylorus stomach ke outlet ka part hai. Pylorus-preserving approach mein surgeon is structure ko preserve karne ki koshish karta hai.

**Exam clue:** Pylorus preservation refers to the gastric outlet.

</details>

### Q4. Which surgical connection restores the passage of bile into the intestine following selected pancreaticoduodenectomy procedures?

**Difficulty:** Easy · **Priority:** High · **Exam focus:** NORCET / HPRCA HP Staff Nurse / PGIMER · Original practice

- **A.** Nephrostomy
- **B.** Tracheostomy
- **C.** Hepaticojejunostomy
- **D.** Colostomy

<details><summary>✅ Correct answer and detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: C. Hepaticojejunostomy**

**A. Kyun galat hai:** Nephrostomy kidney se urine drainage ka route hai.

**B. Kyun galat hai:** Tracheostomy airway opening hai.

**C. Kyun sahi hai:** Hepatic bile duct ko jejunum se connect karke bile flow ka naya route banaya ja sakta hai.

**D. Kyun galat hai:** Colostomy colon se stool drainage opening hai.

**Exam clue:** Hepatico-jejunostomy connects bile duct and jejunum.

</details>

### Q5. Which endocrine hormone may need replacement if pancreatic tissue removal leads to insufficient glucose regulation?

**Difficulty:** Easy · **Priority:** High · **Exam focus:** NORCET / HPRCA HP Staff Nurse / PGIMER · Original practice

- **A.** Melatonin
- **B.** Thyroxine
- **C.** Oxytocin
- **D.** Insulin

<details><summary>✅ Correct answer and detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: D. Insulin**

**A. Kyun galat hai:** Melatonin pineal gland se related hormone hai. Ye insulin deficiency replace nahi karta.

**B. Kyun galat hai:** Thyroxine thyroid hormone hai, glucose-lowering insulin replacement nahi.

**C. Kyun galat hai:** Oxytocin uterine contraction aur milk let-down se related hai.

**D. Kyun sahi hai:** Pancreatic beta cells insulin banati hain. Tissue loss ke baad insulin production inadequate ho sakti hai aur prescribed replacement ki need ho sakti hai.

**Exam clue:** Pancreatic endocrine loss may require insulin.

</details>

### Q6. Which term describes a surgical connection between the pancreas and the jejunum?

**Difficulty:** Easy · **Priority:** High · **Exam focus:** NORCET / HPRCA HP Staff Nurse / PGIMER · Original practice

- **A.** Pancreaticojejunostomy
- **B.** Gastrostomy
- **C.** Ureterostomy
- **D.** Ileostomy

<details><summary>✅ Correct answer and detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: A. Pancreaticojejunostomy**

**A. Kyun sahi hai:** Pancreatic remnant ya duct ko jejunum se connect kiya ja sakta hai taaki digestive secretions intestine mein pahunch sakein.

**B. Kyun galat hai:** Gastrostomy stomach mein access opening hai.

**C. Kyun galat hai:** Ureterostomy urinary drainage se related hai.

**D. Kyun galat hai:** Ileostomy ileum se external stool diversion hai.

**Exam clue:** Recognize the two structures named in an anastomosis.

</details>
