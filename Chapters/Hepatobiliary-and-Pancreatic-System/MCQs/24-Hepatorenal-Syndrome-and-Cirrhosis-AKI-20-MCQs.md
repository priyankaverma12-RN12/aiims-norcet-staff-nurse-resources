### Q2. In a patient with advanced cirrhosis, which hemodynamic change most directly promotes the development of hepatorenal syndrome?

**Difficulty:** 🟡 Medium · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD · Original practice

- **A.** Splanchnic vasodilation with compensatory renal vasoconstriction
- **B.** Isolated narrowing of the urethral lumen
- **C.** Increased glomerular filtration from isolated renal hyperperfusion
- **D.** Direct compression of both ureters by a bladder calculus

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: A. Splanchnic vasodilation with compensatory renal vasoconstriction**

**Option-wise explanation:**
- **A (Sahi):** Portal hypertension ke saath abdominal organs ki vessels dilate ho sakti hain. Effective arterial circulation kam hone par sympathetic aur hormonal responses kidney vessels ko constrict kar sakte hain.
- **B (Galat):** Urethral narrowing urine outflow block kar sakti hai. Yeh postrenal AKI hai, HRS ka primary circulatory mechanism nahi.
- **C (Galat):** HRS mein kidney perfusion aur filtration compromise hote hain. Isolated hyperperfusion iska typical mechanism nahi hai.
- **D (Galat):** Mechanical obstruction ko alag cause ke roop mein assess karte hain. HRS primarily cirrhosis-related circulatory dysfunction hai.

**Exam clue:** Splanchnic dilation aur renal constriction ek saath ho sakte hain.

- **C (Galat):** Fluid restriction ka decision sodium aur clinical status se guide hota hai. Har patient ke liye fixed restriction post-drainage protection nahi deti.
- **D (Galat):** Terlipressin har outpatient drainage ke liye routine prophylaxis nahi hai. Iske specific indications aur adverse risks hain.

**Exam clue:** Large-volume paracentesis ke baad circulatory protection ka plan check karo.

### Q20. A patient with cirrhosis and suspected HRS-AKI has a rising creatinine and new evidence of urinary infection. Which principle should guide reassessment?

**Difficulty:** 🔴 Difficult · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD · Original practice

- **A.** Ignore the infection because HRS-AKI cannot be associated with infection
- **B.** Treat and assess the infection promptly while evaluating whether it precipitated or primarily caused the AKI
- **C.** Use a positive urine culture alone as proof of HRS-AKI
- **D.** Delay infection assessment until a vasoconstrictor course is finished

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: B. Treat and assess the infection promptly while evaluating whether it precipitated or primarily caused the AKI**

**Option-wise explanation:**
- **A (Galat):** Infection aur HRS physiology coexist kar sakte hain. Infection ignore karna unsafe hai.
- **B (Sahi):** Infection cirrhosis mein circulatory dysfunction trigger kar sakti hai aur sepsis-related kidney injury bhi cause kar sakti hai. Clinical signs, culture aur severity ke basis par prompt care aur AKI phenotype review chahiye.
- **C (Galat):** Culture ka interpretation symptoms aur systemic findings ke saath hota hai. Positive culture HRS-AKI ka diagnostic marker nahi hai.
- **D (Galat):** Delayed infection management se sepsis aur organ injury worsen ho sakte hain. Evaluation treatment ke saath timely honi chahiye.

**Exam clue:** Infection trigger aur competing primary cause dono ho sakti hai.
