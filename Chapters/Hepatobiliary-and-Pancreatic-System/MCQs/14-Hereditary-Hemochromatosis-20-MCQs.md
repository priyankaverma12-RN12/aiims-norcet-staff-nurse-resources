### Q1. Which mineral accumulates excessively in hereditary hemochromatosis?

**Difficulty:** Easy · **Priority:** HIGH YIELD · **Exam focus:** NORCET / HPRCA HP Staff Nurse / PGIMER · Original practice

- **A.** Copper
- **B.** Iron
- **C.** Iodine
- **D.** Fluoride

<details><summary>✅ Correct answer and detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: B. Iron**

**A. Kyun galat hai:** Copper accumulation Wilson disease ki hallmark hai.

**B. Kyun sahi hai:** Hereditary hemochromatosis mein iron absorption regulation impaired hoti hai aur iron tissues mein accumulate hota hai.

**C. Kyun galat hai:** Iodine thyroid hormone synthesis mein important hai, iron overload ki hallmark nahi.

**D. Kyun galat hai:** Fluoride excess fluorosis se associated hai.

**Exam clue:** Hemochromatosis means iron overload.

</details>

### Q2. In classical HFE hemochromatosis, how does inadequate hepcidin activity contribute to iron loading?

**Difficulty:** Medium · **Priority:** HIGH YIELD · **Exam focus:** NORCET / HPRCA HP Staff Nurse / PGIMER · Original practice

- **A.** Intestinal iron absorption stops completely
- **B.** Renal iron filtration becomes the sole source of tissue iron
- **C.** Hepcidin causes direct destruction of all hepatocytes
- **D.** Ferroportin-mediated intestinal iron transfer remains relatively increased

<details><summary>✅ Correct answer and detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: D. Ferroportin-mediated intestinal iron transfer remains relatively increased**

**A. Kyun galat hai:** Absorption stop hone se iron overload ka mechanism explain nahi hota.

**B. Kyun galat hai:** Kidney filtration iron loading ka primary mechanism nahi hai.

**C. Kyun galat hai:** Hepcidin iron regulation hormone hai, direct hepatocyte cytotoxin nahi.

**D. Kyun sahi hai:** Hepcidin normally ferroportin ko downregulate karta hai. Inadequate signaling se enterocytes blood mein zyada iron release kar sakte hain.

**Exam clue:** Low hepcidin signaling permits excess iron entry.

</details>

### Q3. A patient has serum iron 180 micrograms/dL and total iron-binding capacity 300 micrograms/dL. What is the calculated transferrin saturation?

**Difficulty:** Medium · **Priority:** HIGH YIELD · **Exam focus:** NORCET / HPRCA HP Staff Nurse / PGIMER · Original practice

- **A.** 60%
- **B.** 40%
- **C.** 30%
- **D.** 167%

<details><summary>✅ Correct answer and detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: A. 60%**

**A. Kyun sahi hai:** 180 ko 300 se divide karke 100 se multiply karte hain, result 60% hai. Elevated saturation further iron-overload assessment guide kar sakti hai.

**B. Kyun galat hai:** 40% is calculation ka result nahi hai.

**C. Kyun galat hai:** 30% ke liye iron 90 micrograms/dL hota.

**D. Kyun galat hai:** 167% numerator aur denominator reverse karne se aata hai.

**Exam clue:** TSAT = iron divided by TIBC times 100.

</details>

### Q4. A patient with acute inflammatory illness has ferritin 850 micrograms/L but no persistent elevation in transferrin saturation. Which interpretation is appropriate?

**Difficulty:** Difficult · **Priority:** HIGH YIELD · **Exam focus:** NORCET / HPRCA HP Staff Nurse / PGIMER · Original practice

- **A.** Ferritin elevation alone proves HFE hemochromatosis
- **B.** Inflammation cannot affect ferritin
- **C.** Ferritin may be elevated as an acute-phase reactant and does not establish hereditary iron overload alone
- **D.** Immediate venesection is mandatory on the basis of ferritin alone

<details><summary>✅ Correct answer and detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: C. Ferritin may be elevated as an acute-phase reactant and does not establish hereditary iron overload alone**

**A. Kyun galat hai:** Ferritin nonspecific ho sakti hai, isliye isolated elevation definitive diagnosis nahi.

**B. Kyun galat hai:** Inflammation ferritin ko increase kar sakti hai.

**C. Kyun sahi hai:** Ferritin iron storage marker bhi hai aur inflammation mein badh sakta hai. Full iron profile, clinical context aur follow-up se cause clarify karte hain.

**D. Kyun galat hai:** Venesection indication established iron overload aur patient suitability par depend karti hai.

**Exam clue:** Ferritin alone is not diagnostic.

</details>

### Q7. Which treatment removes excess iron in a suitable patient with confirmed hereditary hemochromatosis without anemia?

**Difficulty:** Medium · **Priority:** HIGH YIELD · **Exam focus:** NORCET / HPRCA HP Staff Nurse / PGIMER · Original practice

- **B.** Oral iron supplementation
- **C.** Copper chelation with penicillamine
- **D.** Therapeutic phlebotomy

<details><summary>✅ Correct answer and detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: D. Therapeutic phlebotomy**

**A. Kyun galat hai:** Transfusion additional iron introduce karti hai.

**B. Kyun galat hai:** Iron supplementation overload worsen kar sakti hai.

**C. Kyun galat hai:** Penicillamine copper overload treatment hai, routine hereditary iron overload therapy nahi.

**D. Kyun sahi hai:** Venesection se red blood cells remove hote hain. New erythrocytes banane ke liye stored iron use hota hai, jisse iron burden gradually reduce hota hai.

**Exam clue:** Phlebotomy removes iron through red cells.

</details>

### Q9. A patient undergoing induction phlebotomy has reached ferritin 48 micrograms/L. Which follow-up principle is consistent with EASL guidance?

- **B.** Review transition to individualized maintenance rather than continuing intensive depletion automatically
- **C.** Stop all follow-up permanently
- **D.** Administer high-dose iron to restore overload

<details><summary>✅ Correct answer and detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: B. Review transition to individualized maintenance rather than continuing intensive depletion automatically**

**A. Kyun galat hai:** Zero ferritin target iron deficiency risk create karta hai.

**B. Kyun sahi hai:** Induction target around 50 micrograms/L hai. Maintenance mein iron reaccumulation monitor karke interval adjust karte hain. Excess depletion avoid karna important hai.

**Exam clue:** Induction and maintenance have different treatment goals.

</details>

### Q20. A patient with transfusion-dependent anemia develops iron overload. Why is the standard venesection plan for HFE hemochromatosis not automatically suitable?

</details>
