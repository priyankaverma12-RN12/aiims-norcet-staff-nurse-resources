### PYQ1. Which motor finding supports hepatic encephalopathy in a patient with cirrhosis?

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Topic:** Hepatic encephalopathy

- **A.** Asterixis
- **B.** Positive Babinski sign as the defining finding
- **C.** Isolated foot drop
- **D.** Normal coordinated finger movements

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: A. Asterixis**

Asterixis mein haath ko extend karne par flapping jaisi movement aa sakti hai. Liver dysfunction se brain affected ho toh ye sign mil sakta hai.

**Baaki options kyun galat hain?**

- **B.** Babinski specific hepatic encephalopathy marker nahi hai.
- **C.** Foot drop local nerve ya muscle problem ka sign ho sakta hai.
- **D.** Normal movement asterixis nahi hai.

</details>
