### Q1. Two formulations contain the same drug dose. Formulation X delivers 60% of the dose unchanged into systemic circulation and formulation Y delivers 30%. Which statement is correct?

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD | **Exam focus:** Original NORCET-style / all-India Staff Nurse practice

- **A.** X has twice the systemic bioavailability of Y
- **B.** Y has twice the systemic bioavailability of X
- **C.** Both have identical bioavailability
- **D.** X necessarily has twice the receptor affinity

<details><summary>Answer aur detailed explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: A. X has twice the systemic bioavailability of Y**

- **A. Kyun sahi hai:** Bioavailability batati hai ki di hui dose ka kitna fraction unchanged form mein systemic blood tak pahunchta hai. Yahan 60 ko 30 se divide karne par 2 milta hai, isliye X ki bioavailability double hai.

- **B. Kyun galat hai:** Y se sirf 30 percent dose blood tak pahunchti hai. Isliye Y ko double kehna comparison ko ulta karna hoga.

- **C. Kyun galat hai:** Dono mein drug ki dose same hai, lekin blood tak pahunchne wala fraction alag hai. Isliye same dose ka matlab same bioavailability nahi hai.

- **D. Kyun galat hai:** Receptor affinity batati hai ki drug receptor ke saath kitni aasani se bind karti hai. Is calculation se receptor binding ke baare mein kuch pata nahi chalta.

- **A.** A constant fraction of the remaining drug is removed per hour
- **B.** A constant amount of drug is removed per hour
- **C.** Its elimination rate necessarily doubles whenever concentration doubles
- **D.** No drug is eliminated

<details><summary>Answer aur detailed explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: B. A constant amount of drug is removed per hour**

- **A. Kyun galat hai:** Constant fraction per hour first-order elimination ko describe karta hai. Zero-order interval mein removed amount constant maana jaata hai.

- **B. Kyun sahi hai:** Zero-order situation mein elimination pathway capacity limit par ho sakta hai. Is range mein roughly same amount per unit time nikalta hai, isliye first-order half-life rule blindly apply nahi karte.

- **C. Kyun galat hai:** Concentration double hone par elimination rate double hona linear first-order behavior ke closer hai. Zero-order interval mein rate constant maana jaata hai.

- **D. Kyun galat hai:** Zero-order ka matlab zero elimination nahi hai. Iska matlab concentration change ke saath elimination rate is interval mein proportional nahi badalta.

### Q20. A patient vomits shortly after swallowing a prescribed tablet. The patient asks the nurse to give the same dose again immediately. What is the safest action?

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Priority:** 🔴 MOST IMPORTANT | **Exam focus:** Original NORCET-style / all-India Staff Nurse practice

- **A.** Repeat every dose automatically
- **B.** Assume the complete dose was absorbed
- **C.** Double the next scheduled dose without assessment
- **D.** Assess timing and visible medication, then follow drug-specific guidance and clarify the order

<details><summary>Answer aur detailed explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: D. Assess timing and visible medication, then follow drug-specific guidance and clarify the order**

- **A. Kyun galat hai:** Vomiting ke baad kitni medicine absorb hui hai yeh uncertain ho sakta hai. Automatic repeat se already absorbed drug ke upar extra dose aa sakti hai.

- **B. Kyun galat hai:** Tablet jaldi vomit hone par absorption incomplete bhi ho sakti hai. Bina timing aur formulation check kiye full absorption assume karna sahi nahi hai.

- **C. Kyun galat hai:** Next dose double karne se concentration toxic range tak ja sakti hai. Nurse independent dose adjustment nahi karti.

- **D. Kyun sahi hai:** Nurse vomiting ka time, formulation aur visible tablet ke baare mein assess karti hai. Drug-specific guidance aur clinical team ki clarification se decide hota hai ki dose repeat karna appropriate hai ya nahi.
