- **B.** Brain with intact drug permeability
- **C.** Poorly perfused skeletal muscle
- **D.** A scar with minimal vascular supply

<details><summary>Answer aur detailed explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: B. Brain with intact drug permeability**

- **A. Kyun galat hai:** Fat tissue mein blood supply comparatively slow ho sakti hai. Drug lipid soluble ho tab bhi early delivery ke liye perfusion matter karti hai.

- **B. Kyun sahi hai:** Agar drug brain barrier cross kar sakti hai, toh brain ki rich blood supply ki wajah se early delivery relatively fast ho sakti hai. Question ne permeability ki condition pehle se clear ki hai.

- **C. Kyun galat hai:** Resting muscle mein blood supply brain ke comparison mein kam ho sakti hai. Isliye similar permeability par early tissue delivery slower ho sakti hai.

- **D. Kyun galat hai:** Scar mein blood supply kam ho toh circulating drug ko wahan pahunchne mein time lag sakta hai. Yeh richly perfused tissue jaisi early delivery nahi deta.

### Q20. A patient with obesity receives a lipophilic drug. A colleague proposes using total body weight for every medication simply because fat mass is increased. Which response is most appropriate?

**Difficulty:** 🟡 Medium · **Priority:** 🔴 MOST IMPORTANT · Original NORCET-style / all-India nursing practice

- **A.** All drugs distribute into fat in proportion to total body weight
- **B.** Lean body weight is the correct dosing scalar for every drug
- **C.** Body weight should never be considered in medication dosing
- **D.** Use the medication-specific dosing weight and monitoring plan rather than a universal rule

<details><summary>Answer aur detailed explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: D. Use the medication-specific dosing weight and monitoring plan rather than a universal rule**

- **A. Kyun galat hai:** Har medicine ki tissue affinity aur solubility different hoti hai. Increased fat mass ka effect sab drugs par same proportional nahi hota.

- **B. Kyun galat hai:** Lean body weight kuch medications ke liye relevant ho sakta hai, lekin universal dosing rule nahi hai. Medicine-specific evidence follow karna zaroori hai.

- **C. Kyun galat hai:** Weight medication dosing mein important ho sakta hai, especially kuch weight-based orders mein. Weight ko completely ignore karna bhi unsafe ho sakta hai.

- **D. Kyun sahi hai:** Nurse actual weight aur relevant prescribed dosing scalar verify karti hai aur pharmacy ya prescriber se doubt clarify karti hai. Isse inappropriate extrapolation aur dose-related harm ka risk kam karne mein help milti hai.
