## Dosage calculation

### Q1. A medicine is supplied as 250 mg in 5 mL. The prescribed dose is 375 mg. What volume is required?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Topic:** Dosage calculation

- **A.** 7.5 mL
- **B.** 5 mL
- **C.** 3 mL
- **D.** 15 mL

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: A. 7.5 mL**

5 mL mein 250 mg hai, toh 1 mL mein 50 mg hai. 375 mg ko 50 se divide karne par 7.5 mL milta hai. Nurse concentration aur dose dobara check karti hai.

**Baaki options kyun galat hain?**

- **B.** 5 mL mein sirf 250 mg hai.
- **C.** 3 mL mein 150 mg hai.
- **D.** 15 mL mein 750 mg hai.

</details>

## Adverse drug reaction

### Q2. A patient receiving an aminoglycoside develops new tinnitus and rising creatinine. Which toxicities should be considered?

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Topic:** Adverse drug reaction

- **A.** Only expected improvement in hearing
- **B.** Isolated hypoglycemia in every case
- **C.** Normal harmless discoloration of urine
- **D.** Ototoxicity and nephrotoxicity

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: D. Ototoxicity and nephrotoxicity**

Aminoglycoside antibiotics kuch patients mein ear aur kidney ko damage kar sakti hain. Tinnitus aur creatinine badhna warning signs hain. Nurse drug level aur renal review ke liye team ko batati hai.

**Baaki options kyun galat hain?**

- **A.** Tinnitus hearing toxicity ka clue hai, improvement nahi.
- **B.** Hypoglycemia typical paired toxicity nahi hai.
- **C.** Creatinine rise ko harmless assume nahi karna chahiye.

</details>

## Antidotes

### Q3. A patient with suspected opioid overdose has markedly depressed respirations. Which specific antagonist may be used alongside ventilation support?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Topic:** Antidotes

- **A.** Vitamin K
- **B.** Atropine as the specific opioid antagonist
- **C.** Naloxone
- **D.** Flumazenil

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: C. Naloxone**

Opioid brain mein breathing ko slow kar sakta hai. Naloxone opioid receptors par drug ka effect reverse karne mein madad karta hai. Saans ka support aur repeat assessment bhi zaroori hai.

**Baaki options kyun galat hain?**

- **A.** Vitamin K clotting factor synthesis mein help karta hai.
- **B.** Atropine opioid receptor antagonist nahi hai.
- **D.** Flumazenil benzodiazepine antagonist hai aur selected use hota hai.

</details>

## Drug interaction

### Q4. A patient taking warfarin starts an interacting antibiotic and the INR rises substantially. What is the safest nursing action?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Topic:** Drug interaction

- **A.** Advise stopping all medications permanently
- **B.** Assess bleeding and arrange urgent anticoagulation review
- **C.** Double the warfarin dose
- **D.** Ignore the result because antibiotics never interact

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: B. Assess bleeding and arrange urgent anticoagulation review**

Warfarin clot banne ki ability kam karta hai. Kuch antibiotics iska effect badha sakti hain. INR high ho toh bleeding risk badh sakta hai. Nurse bleeding signs check karke prescribed plan review karwati hai.

**Baaki options kyun galat hain?**

- **A.** All medicines permanently stop karna appropriate nahi hai.
- **C.** Double dose bleeding risk aur badha sakti hai.
- **D.** Drug interactions possible hain.

</details>
