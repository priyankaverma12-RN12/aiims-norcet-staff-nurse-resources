### Q20. A sedative and an opioid are prescribed together. Their concentrations are unchanged by the combination, but the patient becomes excessively sedated with depressed breathing. Which interaction category best explains the observed increased effect?

<details><summary>Answer aur detailed explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: B. Pharmacodynamic interaction with overlapping CNS and respiratory depressant effects**

- **D. Kyun galat hai:** Clinical effects enhance hue hain, cancel nahi. Complete receptor blockade ka evidence nahi hai aur combined respiratory depression ko urgently assess karna chahiye.
