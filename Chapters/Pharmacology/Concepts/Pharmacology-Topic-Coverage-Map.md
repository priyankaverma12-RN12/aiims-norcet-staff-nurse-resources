## Initial study explanations

### Dosage calculation

5 mL mein 250 mg hai, toh 1 mL mein 50 mg hai. 375 mg ko 50 se divide karne par 7.5 mL milta hai. Nurse concentration aur dose dobara check karti hai.

### Adverse drug reaction

Aminoglycoside antibiotics kuch patients mein ear aur kidney ko damage kar sakti hain. Tinnitus aur creatinine badhna warning signs hain. Nurse drug level aur renal review ke liye team ko batati hai.

### Antidotes

Opioid brain mein breathing ko slow kar sakta hai. Naloxone opioid receptors par drug ka effect reverse karne mein madad karta hai. Saans ka support aur repeat assessment bhi zaroori hai.

### Drug interaction

Warfarin clot banne ki ability kam karta hai. Kuch antibiotics iska effect badha sakti hain. INR high ho toh bleeding risk badh sakta hai. Nurse bleeding signs check karke prescribed plan review karwati hai.
