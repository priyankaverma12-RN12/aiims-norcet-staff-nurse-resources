### PYQ1. Which tuberculosis medicine can cause harmless orange-red discoloration of urine and tears?

**Difficulty:** Easy/Medium | **Topic:** Antitubercular drugs

- **A.** Rifampicin
- **B.** Ethambutol
- **C.** Isoniazid
- **D.** Pyrazinamide

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation</summary>

**Correct: A. Rifampicin**

Rifampicin body fluids ko orange-red kar sakti hai. Patient ko pehle se samjhana helpful hai. Ethambutol optic toxicity se, isoniazid neuropathy se aur pyrazinamide hyperuricemia se associated ho sakti hai.

</details>

### PYQ2. Which process belongs primarily to pharmacokinetics rather than pharmacodynamics?

**Difficulty:** Easy/Medium | **Topic:** Pharmacology basics

- **A.** Receptor-mediated therapeutic action
- **B.** Renal elimination of a drug
- **C.** Drug-receptor binding
- **D.** Dose-response relationship

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation</summary>

**Correct: B. Renal elimination of a drug**

Pharmacokinetics batati hai body drug ko kaise absorb, distribute, metabolize aur eliminate karti hai. Pharmacodynamics batati hai drug body par kya effect karti hai. Receptor binding, dose-response aur therapeutic action pharmacodynamics se related hain.

</details>
