### PYQ1. A suitable formulation is designed for absorption through the mucosa under the tongue to reduce initial hepatic first-pass exposure. Which route does this describe?

**Difficulty:** Easy | **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD | **Evidence:** 🔵 Reported adaptation

- **A.** Intradermal
- **B.** Sublingual
- **C.** Swallowed oral
- **D.** Subcutaneous

<details><summary>Answer aur detailed explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: B. Sublingual**

- **A. Kyun galat hai:** Intradermal route mein medicine skin ki dermis layer mein di jaati hai. Yeh under-tongue mucosa ka route nahi hai.
- **B. Kyun sahi hai:** Sublingual mein suitable medicine tongue ke neeche rakhi jaati hai aur oral mucosa se absorb ho sakti hai. Absorbed portion largely initial hepatic first pass ko bypass karta hai, jabki swallowed portion liver ke through ja sakta hai.
- **C. Kyun galat hai:** Swallowed medicine GI tract se absorb hoti hai aur portal circulation se liver tak ja sakti hai. Isliye yeh under-tongue mucosal delivery nahi hai.
- **D. Kyun galat hai:** Subcutaneous injection skin ke neeche tissue mein diya jaata hai. Yeh bhi initial hepatic first pass ko bypass kar sakta hai, lekin question mein specific oral mucosa location sublingual route ko identify karti hai.
