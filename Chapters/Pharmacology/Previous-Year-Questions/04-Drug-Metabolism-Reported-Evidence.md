### PYQ1. Which group of reactions belongs to Phase I drug biotransformation rather than conjugation or distribution?

**Difficulty:** 🟢 Easy · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD · 🔵 Reported adaptation

- **A.** Glucuronidation and sulfation
- **B.** Reversible albumin binding
- **C.** Oxidation, reduction and hydrolysis
- **D.** Acetylation and methylation

<details><summary>Answer aur detailed explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: C. Oxidation, reduction and hydrolysis**

- **A. Kyun galat hai:** Glucuronidation aur sulfation mein drug ke saath specific chemical group attach hota hai. Dono Phase II conjugation reactions ke examples hain.
- **B. Kyun galat hai:** Albumin binding reversible distribution process hai. Ismein drug ka chemical structure enzyme se transform hona zaroori nahi hai.
- **C. Kyun sahi hai:** Phase I reactions mein drug ka functional group ya chemical structure change ho sakta hai. Oxidation, reduction aur hydrolysis is functional classification ke main examples hain. Yeh classification MSD reference se independently supported hai.
- **D. Kyun galat hai:** Acetylation aur methylation Phase II reactions mein classify hoti hain. Dono Phase I ki classical oxidation, reduction aur hydrolysis list se alag hain.
