### PYQ1. A patient has dermatitis, diarrhea and cognitive changes. Which vitamin deficiency classically causes this combination?

**Difficulty:** Easy/Medium | **Topic:** Vitamin deficiency

- **A.** Niacin
- **B.** Thiamine
- **C.** Riboflavin
- **D.** Vitamin C

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation</summary>

**Correct: A. Niacin**

Niacin deficiency se pellagra hota hai. Iske classical three D dermatitis, diarrhea aur dementia hain. Thiamine deficiency beriberi aur Wernicke syndrome se related hai. Riboflavin deficiency oral changes kar sakti hai. Vitamin C deficiency scurvy se related hai.

</details>
