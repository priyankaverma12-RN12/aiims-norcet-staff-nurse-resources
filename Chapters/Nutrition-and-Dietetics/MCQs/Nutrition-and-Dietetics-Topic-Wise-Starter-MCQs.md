## Refeeding syndrome

### Q1. A severely malnourished patient starts nutrition support and develops a rapid fall in phosphate. Which complication should be considered?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Topic:** Refeeding syndrome

- **A.** Refeeding syndrome
- **B.** Uncomplicated hyperthyroidism alone
- **C.** Normal response requiring no monitoring
- **D.** Isolated dehydration from lack of oral water

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: A. Refeeding syndrome**

Lambe starvation ke baad feeding shuru hoti hai toh insulin badhta hai. Phosphate cells ke andar shift ho sakta hai. Severe fall heart aur breathing muscles ko affect kar sakta hai, isliye cautious nutrition aur labs chahiye.

**Baaki options kyun galat hain?**

- **B.** Thyroid disease is timing aur phosphate fall ko best explain nahi karti.
- **C.** Electrolyte monitoring essential hai.
- **D.** Dehydration alone refeeding pattern explain nahi karti.

</details>

## Deficiency

### Q2. A patient has bleeding gums, poor wound healing and a very limited fruit and vegetable intake. Which deficiency is most suggestive?

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Topic:** Deficiency

- **A.** Vitamin D excess
- **B.** Vitamin B12 excess
- **C.** Iodine excess
- **D.** Vitamin C deficiency

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: D. Vitamin C deficiency**

Vitamin C collagen banane mein help karta hai. Collagen tissues aur blood vessels ko strength deta hai. Kami se gums bleed aur wound healing slow ho sakti hai.

**Baaki options kyun galat hain?**

- **A.** Vitamin D excess calcium balance affect kar sakta hai.
- **B.** B12 excess typical scurvy cause nahi hai.
- **C.** Iodine thyroid se related hai.

</details>

## Therapeutic diet

### Q3. A patient with dysphagia following stroke is being considered for oral feeding. What should be done first?

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Topic:** Therapeutic diet

- **A.** Allow feeding while lying flat
- **B.** Force solid food to test swallowing
- **C.** Assess swallowing safety and follow the recommended texture plan
- **D.** Give thin fluids to all patients regardless of assessment

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: C. Assess swallowing safety and follow the recommended texture plan**

Dysphagia mein khana ya paani food pipe ki jagah lungs mein ja sakta hai. Nurse swallow assessment ke baad safe texture aur position follow karti hai.

**Baaki options kyun galat hain?**

- **A.** Flat position unsafe ho sakti hai.
- **B.** Force feeding harm kar sakti hai.
- **D.** Thin fluid kuch patients mein aspiration risk badha sakti hai.

</details>

## Nutrition assessment

### Q4. A patient loses 6 kg unintentionally over a short period and reports poor intake. Which approach is appropriate?

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Topic:** Nutrition assessment

- **A.** Recommend fasting until weight stabilizes
- **B.** Perform nutritional risk assessment and arrange individualized support
- **C.** Use body weight alone to exclude malnutrition
- **D.** Ignore the change if BMI is still within range

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: B. Perform nutritional risk assessment and arrange individualized support**

Nutrition risk sirf current BMI se nahi pata chalta. Jaldi weight loss aur poor intake important warning hain. Nurse intake, weight trend aur clinical condition assess karti hai.

**Baaki options kyun galat hain?**

- **A.** Fasting nutrition deficit badha sakti hai.
- **C.** Weight alone complete assessment nahi hai.
- **D.** Normal BMI ke bawajood malnutrition risk ho sakta hai.

</details>
