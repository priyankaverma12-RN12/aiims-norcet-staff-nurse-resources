### Q1. A cook prepares vegetables by cutting them into small pieces before washing them repeatedly. Which change best reduces avoidable nutrient leaching?

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Priority:** High | **Topic:** Nutrient preservation and food standards | **Type:** Original practice

- **A.** Wash intact produce with potable water before cutting
- **B.** Avoid washing all vegetables
- **C.** Soak chopped vegetables for several hours
- **D.** Peel every vegetable deeply regardless of edible skin

<details><summary>Answer aur detailed explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: A. Wash intact produce with potable water before cutting**

- **A. Correct:** Cut surfaces expose soluble nutrients to water. Washing before cutting helps retain vitamins and minerals while maintaining hygiene.
- **B. Incorrect:** Unwashed produce may carry contaminants. Nutrient retention does not justify unsafe preparation.
- **C. Incorrect:** Long soaking can increase leaching of water-soluble nutrients.
- **D. Incorrect:** Deep peeling can remove edible nutrient-rich tissue unnecessarily.

</details>

### Q2. A kitchen compares prolonged boiling of greens in excess water with short steaming. Which mechanism generally favours nutrient retention during steaming?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Priority:** High | **Topic:** Nutrient preservation and food standards | **Type:** Original practice

- **A.** Steam creates new vitamins in every vegetable
- **B.** Steaming eliminates all heat-related nutrient changes
- **C.** Reduced direct contact with water limits leaching of water-soluble nutrients
- **D.** Boiling always destroys all minerals completely

<details><summary>Answer aur detailed explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: C. Reduced direct contact with water limits leaching of water-soluble nutrients**

- **A. Incorrect:** Cooking nutrient availability change kar sakti hai but every vitamin synthesize nahi karti.
- **B. Incorrect:** Steam heat transfer karti hai, so heat-sensitive nutrients still change ho sakte hain.
- **C. Correct:** Steam cooking mein vegetables directly water mein submerged nahi hote. Vitamin C aur B vitamins ki leaching kam ho sakti hai, though heat losses still possible hain.
- **D. Incorrect:** Minerals heat se completely disappear nahi hote. Some cooking water mein leach ho sakte hain.

</details>

<details><summary>Answer aur detailed explanation dekho</summary>

</details>
