### Q1. A prescribed meal contains 20 g of digestible fat. Approximately how much energy comes from the fat?

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Priority:** Medium | **Topic:** Fats and lipids | **Type:** Original practice

- **A.** 180 kcal
- **B.** 80 kcal
- **C.** 20 kcal
- **D.** 140 kcal

<details><summary>Answer aur detailed explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: A. 180 kcal**

- **A. Correct:** Fat ka standard energy factor 9 kcal per gram hai. Isliye 20 ko 9 se multiply karne par 180 kcal milti hain.
- **B. Incorrect:** Yeh 4 kcal per gram ka conversion hai jo protein aur digestible carbohydrate ke liye hota hai.
- **C. Incorrect:** Yeh gram ko direct kcal maan raha hai, jabki energy factor use karna chahiye.
- **D. Incorrect:** Yeh 7 kcal per gram ka calculation hai jo fat ka standard factor nahi hai.

</details>

### Q2. Which structure is formed by esterification of glycerol with three fatty acids?

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Priority:** Medium | **Topic:** Fats and lipids | **Type:** Original practice

- **A.** Glycogen
- **B.** Phospholipid with two acyl chains
- **C.** Triglyceride
- **D.** Cholesterol

<details><summary>Answer aur detailed explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: C. Triglyceride**

- **A. Incorrect:** Glycogen glucose units ka branched polymer hai, glycerol aur fatty acids se nahi banta.
- **B. Incorrect:** Common membrane phospholipid mein do fatty acid chains aur phosphate-containing head hota hai, teen fatty acids wala storage molecule nahi.
- **C. Correct:** Triglyceride mein glycerol backbone ke saath teen fatty acids judte hain. Yeh body aur food mein common storage lipid hai.
- **D. Incorrect:** Cholesterol sterol hai jiska ring structure hota hai. Triglyceride se iski molecular structure alag hai.

</details>

### Q3. What is the primary digestive role of bile salts in the small intestine?

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Priority:** High | **Topic:** Fats and lipids | **Type:** Original practice

- **A.** Hydrolyse peptide bonds in proteins
- **B.** Convert starch directly into glucose
- **C.** Activate salivary amylase in the mouth
- **D.** Emulsify fat and facilitate micelle formation

<details><summary>Answer aur detailed explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: D. Emulsify fat and facilitate micelle formation**

- **A. Incorrect:** Protein ke peptide bonds proteases todte hain. Bile salts ka main role lipid handling hai.
- **B. Incorrect:** Starch digestion amylase aur brush border enzymes se hota hai, bile salts se nahi.
- **C. Incorrect:** Bile small intestine mein release hoti hai. Salivary amylase mouth mein apna separate kaam karta hai.
- **D. Correct:** Bile salts bade fat droplets ko disperse karte hain aur digestion products ko absorption ke liye micelles mein carry karne mein help karte hain. Yeh enzyme ki tarah bonds hydrolyze nahi karte.

</details>

### Q4. Which enzyme is chiefly responsible for hydrolysis of dietary triglycerides in the small intestine?

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Priority:** High | **Topic:** Fats and lipids | **Type:** Original practice

- **A.** Pepsin
- **B.** Pancreatic lipase
- **C.** Lactase
- **D.** Maltase

<details><summary>Answer aur detailed explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: B. Pancreatic lipase**

- **A. Incorrect:** Pepsin stomach mein protein fragments banata hai, triglyceride hydrolysis ka chief enzyme nahi hai.
- **B. Correct:** Pancreatic lipase fat droplets ke surface par triglycerides ke ester bonds todta hai. Isse fatty acids aur monoglycerides jaise absorption products milte hain.
- **C. Incorrect:** Lactase milk sugar ko glucose aur galactose mein todta hai, fat ko nahi.
- **D. Incorrect:** Maltase maltose ko glucose units mein todta hai, triglyceride ko nahi.

</details>

### Q5. After absorption, most long-chain dietary lipids are initially transported from intestinal cells in which particles?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Priority:** Medium | **Topic:** Fats and lipids | **Type:** Original practice

- **A.** Haemoglobin entering intestinal lacteals
- **B.** Chylomicrons entering intestinal lymphatic vessels
- **C.** Free glycogen particles entering the portal vein
- **D.** Platelets carrying all fatty acids directly to the liver

<details><summary>Answer aur detailed explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: B. Chylomicrons entering intestinal lymphatic vessels**

- **A. Incorrect:** Haemoglobin red cells mein oxygen carry karta hai, absorbed triglyceride ka intestinal carrier nahi hai.
- **B. Correct:** Enterocytes long-chain lipid products ko reassemble karke chylomicrons mein pack karte hain. Yeh lacteals ke through lymph aur phir blood tak pahunchte hain.
- **C. Incorrect:** Glycogen intact polymer ke form mein absorb nahi hota. Carbohydrates ke digestion products alag route se blood mein jaate hain.
- **D. Incorrect:** Platelets clotting mein role rakhte hain, intestinal long-chain fat transport ka primary particle nahi hain.

</details>

### Q6. Which pair consists of essential fatty acids that must be obtained from the diet?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Priority:** High | **Topic:** Fats and lipids | **Type:** Original practice

- **A.** Palmitic acid and stearic acid
- **B.** Oleic acid and palmitic acid
- **C.** Linoleic acid and alpha-linolenic acid
- **D.** Cholesterol and glycerol

<details><summary>Answer aur detailed explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: C. Linoleic acid and alpha-linolenic acid**

- **A. Incorrect:** Yeh saturated fatty acids hain jinhe body synthesize kar sakti hai. Inhe essential pair nahi kehte.
- **B. Incorrect:** Oleic aur palmitic acids body mein synthesis se bhi ban sakte hain, isliye dono essential pair nahi hain.
- **C. Correct:** Body linoleic aur alpha-linolenic acid ko required form mein synthesize nahi kar sakti. Yeh omega-6 aur omega-3 families ke essential precursors hain.
- **D. Incorrect:** Cholesterol sterol aur glycerol backbone molecule hai. Yeh essential fatty acids ka pair nahi hai.

</details>

### Q7. Which characteristic defines a saturated fatty acid?

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Priority:** Medium | **Topic:** Fats and lipids | **Type:** Original practice

- **A.** No carbon-carbon double bonds in its hydrocarbon chain
- **B.** Exactly one carbon-carbon double bond
- **C.** Two or more carbon-carbon double bonds
- **D.** A phosphate group attached to every carbon

<details><summary>Answer aur detailed explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: A. No carbon-carbon double bonds in its hydrocarbon chain**

- **A. Correct:** Saturated chain mein carbon-carbon single bonds hote hain aur hydrogen saturation hoti hai. Double bonds ki presence se unsaturated category banti hai.
- **B. Incorrect:** Ek double bond monounsaturated fatty acid ki characteristic hai.
- **C. Incorrect:** Multiple double bonds polyunsaturated fatty acids ki characteristic hain.
- **D. Incorrect:** Fatty acid chain ki saturation phosphate groups se define nahi hoti. Double bonds dekhne chahiye.

</details>

### Q8. A patient asks how to make a heart-healthy change to cooking fat without increasing total calories. Which choice is most appropriate?

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Priority:** High | **Topic:** Fats and lipids | **Type:** Original practice

- **A.** Add extra vegetable oil while keeping all current ghee and butter
- **B.** Replace vegetable oil with partially hydrogenated fat
- **C.** Use unlimited coconut oil because it is plant-derived
- **D.** Replace some ghee or butter with a measured amount of unsaturated vegetable oil

<details><summary>Answer aur detailed explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: D. Replace some ghee or butter with a measured amount of unsaturated vegetable oil**

- **A. Incorrect:** Oil unsaturated ho sakta hai, lekin extra amount total calories badha dega. Replacement aur addition mein difference hai.
- **B. Incorrect:** Partially hydrogenated oils industrial trans fat ka source ho sakte hain. Yeh heart-healthy substitution nahi hai.
- **C. Incorrect:** Plant origin se saturated fat automatically kam nahi hota. Coconut oil saturated fat rich hota hai aur portion matter karta hai.
- **D. Correct:** Saturated fat ka kuch hissa unsaturated fat se replace karna lipid profile ke liye useful ho sakta hai. Portion control se total calories ko bhi manage kiya ja sakta hai.

</details>

### Q10. A patient with fat malabsorption has greasy, bulky stools. Which term best describes this finding?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Priority:** High | **Topic:** Fats and lipids | **Type:** Original practice

- **A.** Melena
- **B.** Haematuria
- **C.** Steatorrhoea
- **D.** Glycosuria

<details><summary>Answer aur detailed explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: C. Steatorrhoea**

- **A. Incorrect:** Melena usually digested blood ki wajah se black tarry stool hota hai. Yeh greasy stool ka equivalent nahi hai.
- **B. Incorrect:** Haematuria urine mein blood ko kehte hain, stool fat ko nahi.
- **C. Correct:** Jab fat adequately digest ya absorb nahi hota toh stool mein excess fat aa sakta hai. Greasy aur bulky stool ko steatorrhoea kehte hain.
- **D. Incorrect:** Glycosuria urine mein glucose presence hai. Fat malabsorption se related stool finding ka naam alag hai.

</details>

### Q11. A patient has chronic cholestasis and impaired absorption of dietary fat. Which group of micronutrients requires particular assessment?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Priority:** High | **Topic:** Fats and lipids | **Type:** Original practice

- **A.** Vitamins A, D, E and K
- **B.** Vitamins B1, B2, B6 and C only
- **C.** Sodium, chloride, potassium and bicarbonate only
- **D.** Iron, iodine, selenium and copper only

<details><summary>Answer aur detailed explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: A. Vitamins A, D, E and K**

- **A. Correct:** In vitamins ka absorption intestinal lipid handling par depend karta hai. Bile flow kam hone se absorption impair ho sakta hai, isliye targeted monitoring useful hai.
- **B. Incorrect:** Yeh water-soluble vitamins hain aur inka absorption fat micelles par waise depend nahi karta. Other deficiencies possible hain, lekin listed risk ADEK ka hai.
- **C. Incorrect:** Yeh electrolytes hain, fat-soluble vitamin group nahi. Clinical electrolyte monitoring alag reason se ho sakti hai.
- **D. Incorrect:** Yeh trace minerals hain. Cholestasis se related classic fat-dependent vitamin risk ko yeh list capture nahi karti.

</details>

### Q12. Which lipid-rich structure is essential for the selective barrier of a typical human cell membrane?

- **A.** Glycogen coat
- **B.** Phospholipid bilayer
- **C.** Urea layer
- **D.** Calcium carbonate shell

<details><summary>Answer aur detailed explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: B. Phospholipid bilayer**

- **A. Incorrect:** Glycogen intracellular energy storage hai. Cell membrane ka basic lipid bilayer glycogen se nahi banta.
- **B. Correct:** Phospholipids ke hydrophilic heads aur hydrophobic tails bilayer form karte hain. Yeh membrane barrier ka basic arrangement hai.
- **C. Incorrect:** Urea nitrogen waste product hai aur membrane barrier ki structural layer nahi hai.

</details>

### Q13. What is the main role of adipose triglyceride during a sustained energy deficit?

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Priority:** Medium | **Topic:** Fats and lipids | **Type:** Original practice

- **A.** Produce intestinal lactase
- **B.** Provide mobilizable fatty acids for energy metabolism
- **C.** Store oxygen in the same manner as haemoglobin
- **D.** Provide all essential amino acids for protein synthesis

<details><summary>Answer aur detailed explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: B. Provide mobilizable fatty acids for energy metabolism**

- **A. Incorrect:** Lactase intestinal brush border enzyme hai. Adipose fat mobilization se lactase synthesis direct main role nahi hai.
- **B. Correct:** Adipose triglyceride lipolysis se fatty acids release karta hai. Tissues fatty acids oxidize karke energy bana sakte hain.
- **C. Incorrect:** Oxygen transport haemoglobin karta hai. Adipose triglyceride energy reserve hai.
- **D. Incorrect:** Triglyceride nitrogen-containing amino acids supply nahi karta. Protein ke liye amino acid sources chahiye.

</details>

### Q14. A patient reads that LDL and HDL are types of cholesterol. What clarification is most accurate?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Priority:** High | **Topic:** Fats and lipids | **Type:** Original practice

- **A.** LDL and HDL are digestive enzymes that split cholesterol
- **B.** HDL is dietary fibre and LDL is dietary starch
- **C.** LDL and HDL are forms of haemoglobin
- **D.** LDL and HDL are lipoprotein particles that transport cholesterol and other lipids

<details><summary>Answer aur detailed explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: D. LDL and HDL are lipoprotein particles that transport cholesterol and other lipids**

- **A. Incorrect:** Yeh circulating lipoproteins hain, digestive enzymes nahi hain.
- **B. Incorrect:** Fibre aur starch carbohydrates hain. HDL aur LDL lipid transport particles hain.
- **C. Incorrect:** Haemoglobin oxygen-carrying protein hai. LDL aur HDL ka role lipid transport mein hai.
- **D. Correct:** Cholesterol water mein freely dissolve nahi hota, isliye lipoprotein particles transport mein help karte hain. LDL aur HDL particle classes hain, cholesterol ke different chemical molecules nahi.

</details>

### Q16. Which explanation best describes why repeated deep-frying in the same oil is discouraged?

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Priority:** Medium | **Topic:** Fats and lipids | **Type:** Original practice

- **A.** Repeated heating can promote oxidation and degradation of oil
- **B.** Heating converts every fatty acid into dietary fibre
- **C.** Repeated heating sterilizes the oil permanently and eliminates chemical changes
- **D.** The oil becomes a complete protein after several heating cycles

<details><summary>Answer aur detailed explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: A. Repeated heating can promote oxidation and degradation of oil**

- **A. Correct:** High temperature par baar-baar heating se oil breakdown aur oxidation products ban sakte hain. Isliye safe cooking practices aur food-quality guidance follow karni chahiye.
- **B. Incorrect:** Fatty acids heating se fibre nahi bante. Fibre structurally different carbohydrate-related material hai.
- **C. Incorrect:** Heat microorganisms ko reduce kar sakti hai, lekin chemical degradation prevent nahi karti. Permanent safety assume nahi karni chahiye.
- **D. Incorrect:** Heating fatty acids ko essential amino acids wala complete protein nahi banati.

</details>

### Q17. A patient on a long-term highly restrictive fat-free diet asks why some dietary fat is necessary. Which response is best?

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Priority:** Medium | **Topic:** Fats and lipids | **Type:** Original practice

- **A.** Fat is the only nutrient that can provide any energy
- **B.** All dietary fat is converted directly to blood cholesterol
- **C.** It supplies essential fatty acids and supports absorption of fat-soluble vitamins
- **D.** Dietary fat is required only during infancy and never afterward

<details><summary>Answer aur detailed explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: C. It supplies essential fatty acids and supports absorption of fat-soluble vitamins**

- **A. Incorrect:** Carbohydrate aur protein bhi energy provide karte hain. Fat only energy nutrient nahi hai.
- **B. Incorrect:** Fat metabolism multiple pathways se hota hai, including energy oxidation aur triglyceride storage. Direct conversion ka blanket claim galat hai.
- **C. Correct:** Some fatty acids diet se lena zaroori hai, aur lipid handling vitamins ADEK ke absorption mein help karti hai. Fat ki quantity aur type individual needs ke hisaab se decide honi chahiye.
- **D. Incorrect:** Essential fatty acids aur membrane-related roles adulthood mein bhi important hain. Needs life stage se vary karti hain.

</details>

### Q19. Which feature of medium-chain triglycerides can be relevant in selected patients with impaired long-chain fat absorption?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Priority:** High | **Topic:** Fats and lipids | **Type:** Original practice

- **A.** They provide all essential fatty acids by themselves
- **B.** Their digestion products can be absorbed more directly into portal blood
- **C.** They are absorbed only after packaging into the largest chylomicrons
- **D.** They contain no energy and cannot affect calorie intake

<details><summary>Answer aur detailed explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: B. Their digestion products can be absorbed more directly into portal blood**

- **A. Incorrect:** MCT oils essential long-chain fatty acids ka complete replacement nahi hote. Diet ko essential fat needs bhi cover karni chahiye.
- **B. Correct:** Medium-chain fatty acids relatively water soluble hote hain aur long-chain fats ke comparison mein portal transport zyada direct ho sakta hai. Specialized use dietitian aur clinical plan se decide hota hai.
- **C. Incorrect:** Medium-chain products ka transport long-chain products se differ kar sakta hai. Large chylomicron packaging unka obligatory route nahi hai.
- **D. Incorrect:** MCT fats energy provide karte hain. Nutrition plan mein unka energy contribution count karna chahiye.

</details>

### Q20. A patient with a cardiovascular risk reduction plan asks whether all fats should be eliminated. Which teaching best reflects balanced dietary advice?

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Priority:** High | **Topic:** Fats and lipids | **Type:** Original practice

- **A.** Consider total energy and replace excessive saturated and trans fats with appropriate unsaturated sources
- **B.** Eliminate all fat regardless of nutritional status
- **C.** Choose foods only by whether they are labelled low-fat
- **D.** Increase all fried foods because fat is energy dense

<details><summary>Answer aur detailed explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: A. Consider total energy and replace excessive saturated and trans fats with appropriate unsaturated sources**

- **A. Correct:** Body ko essential fats chahiye aur fat quality bhi important hai. Isliye blanket elimination ki jagah balanced substitution aur prescribed goals useful hain.
- **B. Incorrect:** Complete avoidance se essential fatty acid aur fat-soluble vitamin needs meet karna difficult ho sakta hai.
- **C. Incorrect:** Low-fat label food ke sodium, sugar, fibre aur overall quality ko automatically describe nahi karta.
- **D. Incorrect:** Energy density alone heart-healthy diet define nahi karti. Cooking method, fat type aur portion size matter karte hain.

</details>
