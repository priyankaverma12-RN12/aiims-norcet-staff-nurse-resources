### Q1. An older adult completes a Mini Nutritional Assessment Short Form (MNA-SF) and scores 10 out of 14. What should the nurse document?

**Difficulty:** 🟡 Medium | **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD | **Topic:** MNA-SF interpretation | **Type:** Original practice

- **A.** Normal nutritional status
- **B.** At risk of malnutrition and needs further assessment
- **C.** Definite severe acute malnutrition requiring immediate tube feeding
- **D.** Uninterpretable because age exceeds 65

<details><summary>Answer aur detailed explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: B. At risk of malnutrition and needs further assessment**

- **A. Incorrect:** Normal category 12–14 hoti hai. Score 10 ko normal bolne se early risk miss ho sakta hai.

- **B. Correct:** Score 8–11 MNA-SF mein risk category hai. Isliye detailed nutrition assessment aur care planning karna sahi hai.

- **C. Incorrect:** Screening score se severe acute malnutrition ya tube feeding ka decision seedha nahi hota. Clinical assessment aur goals bhi chahiye.

- **D. Incorrect:** MNA-SF older adults ke liye hi design kiya gaya hai. Age 65 se zyada hona tool ko invalid nahi banata.

**Exam clue:** MNA-SF 8–11 means at risk, not a final etiological diagnosis.

### Q3. A clinically stable older adult weighs 60 kg, has no prescribed protein restriction and currently eats 42 g protein daily. Using the ESPEN geriatric guiding minimum of 1 g/kg/day, what is the initial daily protein shortfall to discuss with the dietitian?

- **B.** 6 g

<details><summary>Answer aur detailed explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: A. 18 g**

- **A. Correct:** 60 kg ke liye guiding minimum 60 g/day banta hai. Actual intake 42 g hai, to gap 18 g/day hai. Final target individual assessment se tay hoga.

### Q9. Direct osmolality testing is unavailable. Sodium is 143, potassium 4, glucose 5 and urea 7, all in mmol/L. Using the ESPEN equation 1.86 × (Na + K) + 1.15 × glucose + urea + 14, what is the estimated osmolarity and appropriate interpretation?

- **A.** Approximately 273 mmol/L, below the action threshold
- **B.** Approximately 300 mmol/L, above the 295 screening action threshold
- **C.** Approximately 300 mOsm/kg, a direct laboratory measurement

<details><summary>Answer aur detailed explanation dekho</summary>

- **D. Incorrect:** Equation ke terms sahi apply karne par 160 nahi aata. Symptoms aur screening result ke hisab se assessment continue karna hai.

### Q10. An older adult has repeated vomiting, hypotension and a directly measured serum osmolality of 289 mOsm/kg. Which interpretation is safest?

**Difficulty:** 🟡 Medium | **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD | **Topic:** Volume depletion distinction | **Type:** Original practice

- **A.** Normal osmolality rules out every clinically important fluid deficit
- **B.** Give unrestricted oral water without assessing vomiting or circulation
- **C.** Normal osmolality does not exclude extracellular volume depletion, so prompt clinical assessment is needed
- **D.** Diagnose low-intake dehydration solely from the normal result

<details><summary>Answer aur detailed explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: C. Normal osmolality does not exclude extracellular volume depletion, so prompt clinical assessment is needed**

- **A. Incorrect:** Osmolality concentration batati hai, circulating volume directly nahi. Normal value se hypovolaemia exclude nahi hota.

- **B. Incorrect:** Hypotension aur ongoing vomiting mein oral safety, perfusion aur replacement route assess karna zaroori hai. Unrestricted water automatic treatment nahi hai.

- **C. Correct:** Vomiting mein salt aur water dono lose ho sakte hain. Normal osmolality ke bawajood volume depletion aur hypotension serious ho sakte hain, isliye prompt assessment chahiye.

- **D. Incorrect:** Normal result low-intake dehydration ko akela establish nahi karta. Fluid-loss history aur clinical findings separate evaluate karni hain.

**Exam clue:** Isotonic volume loss may occur without high serum osmolality.

### Q20. A stable older adult's individualized target is 1800 kcal and 65 g protein daily. Meals provide 1350 kcal and 36 g protein, and the prescribed ONS is actually consumed, providing 400 kcal and 30 g protein. Which evaluation is correct?

**Difficulty:** 🔴 Difficult | **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD | **Topic:** Energy and protein contribution | **Type:** Original practice

- **A.** Both targets are reached exactly
- **B.** Energy remains 50 kcal below target, while protein reaches 66 g
- **C.** Protein remains 29 g below target despite the consumed supplement
- **D.** Energy exceeds target by 400 kcal and protein is unchanged

<details><summary>Answer aur detailed explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: B. Energy remains 50 kcal below target, while protein reaches 66 g**

- **A. Incorrect:** Protein 66 g hai aur energy 1750 kcal hai. Dono numbers exactly respective targets ke equal nahi hain.

- **D. Incorrect:** Supplement 400 kcal contribute karta hai, lekin total target exceed nahi hota. Protein bhi supplement se badhta hai.
