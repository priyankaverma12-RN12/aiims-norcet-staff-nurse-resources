### Q1. In an otherwise clinically stable adult whose assessed oral nutrition plan aims for gradual weight gain, which is the fundamental energy principle?

**Difficulty:** 🟢 Easy | **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD | **Topic:** Energy balance | **Type:** Original practice

- **A.** Energy expenditure should consistently exceed intake
- **B.** Energy intake should generally exceed energy expenditure
- **C.** Protein should supply all energy and other macronutrients should be eliminated
- **D.** Water intake alone should replace food intake

<details><summary>Answer aur detailed explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: B. Energy intake should generally exceed energy expenditure**

- **A. Incorrect:** Negative energy balance generally body stores use karwata hai. Weight regain ka objective is approach se match nahi karta.
- **B. Correct:** Body ko maintenance aur activity ke liye energy chahiye. Sustained additional energy available ho toh weight regain possible hota hai. Clinical condition ke hisaab se suitable plan banate hain.
- **C. Incorrect:** Protein tissue repair mein important hai, lekin balanced diet mein carbohydrate aur fat bhi energy provide karte hain. Unnecessary elimination nutrient adequacy compromise kar sakti hai.
- **D. Incorrect:** Water hydration ke liye essential hai lekin calories aur protein nahi deta. Isliye food ko replace karke nutritional rehabilitation nahi kar sakte.

**Exam clue:** Sustained weight gain ke liye energy intake expenditure se zyada hona chahiye.

**Evidence:** [North Tees NHS, Dietary Advice about Gaining Weight in a Healthy Way in Adults](https://www.nth.nhs.uk/resources/dietary-advice-about-gaining-weight-in-a-healthy-way/). [Concept guide](../Concepts/29-Adult-Underweight-and-Oral-Nutrition-Rehabilitation.md).

### Q20. A clinician proposes stopping oral nutritional supplements in a recovering adult whose body weight has increased, but the patient still obtains a substantial part of daily intake from the supplements. Which interpretation best supports a safe decision?

**Difficulty:** 🔴 Difficult | **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD | **Topic:** Dependence versus readiness to stop | **Type:** Original practice

- **A.** Assess whether normal food alone can maintain adequate intake before discontinuation
- **B.** Stop the supplement solely because any weight gain has occurred
- **C.** Continue the same supplement indefinitely without assessing food intake
- **D.** Use body weight alone to calculate every micronutrient deficit

<details><summary>Answer aur detailed explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: A. Assess whether normal food alone can maintain adequate intake before discontinuation**

- **A. Correct:** Weight gain support effective hone ka sign ho sakta hai. But current adequacy supplement-dependent ho sakti hai. Normal food alone ka sustainable intake review karke individualized stopping plan decide karna chahiye.

- **C. Incorrect:** Support duration needs aur outcomes par depend karti hai. Indefinite unchanged prescription without review appropriate nahi hai.
- **D. Incorrect:** Weight overall trend batata hai, individual nutrient deficiencies measure nahi karta. Dietary and clinical assessment bhi required hai.

**Exam clue:** Weight improvement alone normal diet se sufficient intake prove nahi karta.
