### Q1. A nutrition-awareness session has increased participants' ability to name healthy foods, but families report difficulty applying the advice while shopping and preparing meals. Which change best addresses the missing educational component?

**Difficulty:** 🟡 Medium | **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD | **Topic:** Action-oriented nutrition education | **Type:** Original practice

- **A.** Increase the number of nutrient names in the next lecture
- **B.** Add supervised practice in selecting and preparing suitable foods using familiar resources
- **C.** Repeat the same knowledge quiz immediately after every class
- **D.** Replace the practical content with a longer explanation of food-group definitions

<details><summary>Answer aur detailed explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: B. Add supervised practice in selecting and preparing suitable foods using familiar resources**

- **A. Incorrect:** More terminology se factual knowledge expand ho sakti hai. Lekin jo barrier shopping aur preparation mein aa raha hai, uske liye actual skills practise karna zyada relevant hai.
- **B. Correct:** Knowledge se patient ko pata chal sakta hai ki kya beneficial hai, lekin real shopping aur cooking mein skill ki zaroorat hoti hai. Familiar ingredients ke saath practice se knowledge ko daily action mein convert karne ka mauka milta hai.
- **C. Incorrect:** Quiz recall assess kar sakta hai, lekin family actual food selection ya preparation kar paati hai ya nahi, yeh directly establish nahi karta.
- **D. Incorrect:** Food-group definitions foundation deti hain. Is case mein information already samajh aa rahi hai, isliye sirf theory ko lamba karna identified skill gap solve nahi karta.

**Exam clue:** Nutrition education mein information ke saath practical skill aur action support chahiye.
