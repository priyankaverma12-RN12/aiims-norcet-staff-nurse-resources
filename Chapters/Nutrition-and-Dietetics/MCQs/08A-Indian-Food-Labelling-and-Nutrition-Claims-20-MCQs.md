### Q1. An ordinary packaged cereal lists refined wheat flour, sugar, oats and oil in that order. What can a nurse infer from the order alone?

**Difficulty:** 🟡 Medium | **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD | **Topic:** Ingredient ordering | **Type:** Original practice

- **A.** Oats constitute exactly half of the product
- **B.** Sugar supplies more calories than the flour
- **C.** The ingoing weight of refined wheat flour is at least as great as that of oats
- **D.** Oil is absent from the final product

<details><summary>Answer aur detailed explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: C. The ingoing weight of refined wheat flour is at least as great as that of oats**

- **A. Incorrect:** List se exact percentage nahi milta. Oats ka half hona prove karne ke liye quantitative declaration chahiye.

- **B. Incorrect:** Calories aur ingredient weight alag baatein hain. Order se sugar ki total energy contribution decide nahi kar sakte.

- **C. Correct:** Ingredients ko manufacturing ke samay weight ya volume ke descending order mein list karte hain. Is list mein flour oats se pehle hai, isliye uska ingoing weight kam nahi maana ja sakta.

- **D. Incorrect:** Oil list mein diya gaya hai. Last position oil ke absent hone ka evidence nahi hai.

**Exam clue:** Ingredient order composition ki relative quantity batata hai, exact nutrient profile nahi.

### Q5. Two ordinary snack labels use different serving sizes. Product X lists 150 kcal per 30 g and product Y lists 180 kcal per 45 g. Which statement is correct for equal 100 g quantities?

### Q9. A trainee says the optional best-before date and the use-by date have identical regulatory meanings. Which correction is accurate?

**Difficulty:** 🟡 Medium | **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD | **Topic:** Expiry versus quality date | **Type:** Original practice

- **A.** Best-before always overrides an expired use-by date
- **B.** Use-by describes only the package colour
- **C.** Food is automatically safe indefinitely after both dates
- **D.** Best-before primarily concerns retained quality under stated storage conditions, while use-by marks the end of estimated safe use

<details><summary>Answer aur detailed explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: D. Best-before primarily concerns retained quality under stated storage conditions, while use-by marks the end of estimated safe use**

- **A. Incorrect:** Best-before expired use-by ko override nahi karta. Use-by cross hone par food ko human consumption ke liye sell ya distribute nahi karna chahiye.

- **C. Incorrect:** Dates aur correct storage safety assess karne mein help karte hain. Dono dates ke baad indefinite safety assume karna wrong hai.

- **D. Correct:** Best-before quality retention se related hai, jabki use-by estimated safety period se related hai. Stated storage conditions bhi important hain aur unsafe food kisi bhi stage par use nahi karna chahiye.

**Exam clue:** Quality date aur safety-related date ka meaning alag hai.

### Q20. A teaching slide describes the newly notified milk logo as a mark of fortified milk only. Which correction accurately describes the November 2026 Second Amendment, whose stated commencement is 1 July 2028?
