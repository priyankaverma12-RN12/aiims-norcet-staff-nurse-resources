### Q1. A woman begins pregnancy with a BMI of 22 kg/m² and a singleton fetus. Which factor should guide the selection of a gestational weight-gain reference?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Priority:** Medium | **Topic:** Pregnancy nutrition | **Type:** Original practice

- **A.** Current fetal sex alone
- **B.** Maternal height alone
- **C.** Prepregnancy BMI and pregnancy characteristics
- **D.** A fixed target identical for all women

<details><summary>Answer aur detailed explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: C. Prepregnancy BMI and pregnancy characteristics**

- **A. Incorrect:** Fetal sex alone maternal weight-gain reference decide nahi karta.
- **B. Incorrect:** Height BMI calculation mein useful hai, lekin starting weight aur pregnancy characteristics bhi chahiye.
- **C. Correct:** Starting BMI aur singleton ya multiple pregnancy se suitable reference change hota hai. Serial progress clinical context mein assess karein.
- **D. Incorrect:** Universal target individual risk aur starting BMI ignore karta hai.

</details>

### Q3. A woman believes eating for two means doubling every meal from conception. Which teaching best addresses her misconception?

- **B.** Keep intake below prepregnancy needs in every trimester
- **C.** Add only oil to every meal
- **D.** Additional food requirements vary by trimester and nutritional status

<details><summary>Answer aur detailed explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: D. Additional food requirements vary by trimester and nutritional status**

- **A. Incorrect:** Energy need universally double nahi hoti. Excess intake bhi problematic ho sakta hai.

- **C. Incorrect:** Energy alone sufficient nahi. Protein aur micronutrient adequacy bhi important hai.
- **D. Correct:** Fetal growth aur maternal adaptation ke saath needs change hoti hain. Balanced additional portions individual assessment se decide karein.

</details>

- **B.** Prolonged fasting regardless of tolerance
- **C.** Exclude all carbohydrates
- **D.** Small frequent tolerated meals rather than large meals

<details><summary>Answer aur detailed explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: D. Small frequent tolerated meals rather than large meals**

- **A. Incorrect:** Large heavy meal symptoms worsen kar sakti hai.

- **D. Correct:** Small meals nausea mein easier tolerate ho sakte hain. Persistent vomiting ya dehydration ho toh assessment required hai.

### Q13. A patient has two drinks containing 80 mg and 110 mg caffeine. Under a maternity plan limiting caffeine to 200 mg daily, how much remains?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Priority:** Medium | **Topic:** Pregnancy nutrition | **Type:** Original practice

- **A.** 90 mg
- **B.** 10 mg
- **C.** 120 mg
- **D.** 190 mg

<details><summary>Answer aur detailed explanation dekho</summary>

- **B. Correct:** 80 plus 110 equals 190 mg. 200 minus 190 se 10 mg remaining hai. Other caffeine sources bhi count karein.

- **D. Incorrect:** Yeh consumed caffeine hai, remaining allowance nahi.

</details>
