### Q1. A hospital kitchen stores raw poultry above uncovered ready-to-eat salad in the refrigerator. Which correction best prevents cross-contamination?

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Priority:** High | **Topic:** Food safety and hygiene | **Type:** Original practice

- **A.** Place the salad directly beneath the poultry to keep it colder
- **B.** Store sealed raw poultry below and separately from ready-to-eat foods
- **C.** Rinse the salad only after raw poultry liquid contacts it
- **D.** Use the same open tray for both foods to save refrigerator space

<details><summary>Answer aur detailed explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: B. Store sealed raw poultry below and separately from ready-to-eat foods**

- **A. Incorrect:** Lower position alone salad safe nahi banati. Raw juices drip hone ka risk remain karta hai.
- **B. Correct:** Raw poultry se contaminated liquid drip ho sakta hai. Sealed lower storage aur separation salad ko contamination se protect karte hain.
- **C. Incorrect:** Rinsing contamination ko reliably eliminate nahi karti. Exposed ready-to-eat food ko safety protocol ke according handle karein.
- **D. Incorrect:** Shared tray direct cross-contamination allow karta hai. Raw aur ready-to-eat food separate rakhein.

</details>

### Q5. According to the WHO Five Keys to Safer Food educational guidance, which temperature range is described as the danger zone for microbial growth in food?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Priority:** High | **Topic:** Food safety and hygiene | **Type:** Original practice

- **A.** -20°C to -10°C
- **B.** 70°C to 100°C
- **C.** 5°C to 60°C
- **D.** -5°C to 0°C only

<details><summary>Answer aur detailed explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: C. 5°C to 60°C**

- **A. Incorrect:** Frozen temperature range WHO danger zone nahi hai. Freezing growth slow ya stop kar sakti hai, though organisms necessarily die nahi hote.
- **B. Incorrect:** Yeh high cooking temperature range hai, specified danger zone nahi. Time aur food-specific cooking guidance still important hai.
- **C. Correct:** WHO educational guidance 5 se 60 degrees Celsius range mein prolonged holding avoid karne ko kehti hai. Applicable local standards aur food type ke according controls follow karein.
- **D. Incorrect:** Near-freezing range WHO defined danger zone ka full range nahi hai. 5–60°C benchmark yaad rakhein.

</details>

### Q8. Cooked rice has been left at room temperature overnight. A caregiver proposes reheating it thoroughly for a child. Which response is safest?

- **C.** Add spices to neutralize all bacterial toxins
- **D.** Mix it with freshly cooked rice to dilute the risk

<details><summary>Answer aur detailed explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: B. Discard the rice because unsafe storage may allow toxin formation that reheating cannot reliably reverse**

- **B. Correct:** Bacillus cereus spores cooking survive kar sakte hain aur prolonged warm holding mein grow kar sakte hain. Kuch preformed toxins heat-stable ho sakte hain, isliye reheating unsafe storage ko undo nahi karti.
- **C. Incorrect:** Spices toxin elimination ka validated method nahi hain. Contaminated rice discard karna safer hai.
- **D. Incorrect:** Dilution food safety restore nahi karti aur fresh batch contaminate ho sakta hai.

</details>
