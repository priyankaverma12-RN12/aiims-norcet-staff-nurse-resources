### Q1. A hypothetical PN admixture includes 180 g of intravenous dextrose. Using 3.4 kcal per gram, how much energy does this component provide?

**Difficulty:** 🟢 Easy | **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD | **Topic:** Parenteral formulations | **Type:** Original practice

- **A.** 720 kcal
- **B.** 180 kcal
- **C.** 529 kcal
- **D.** 612 kcal

<details><summary>Answer aur detailed explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: D. 612 kcal**

- **A. Incorrect:** 720 kcal tab milta jab 180 ko 4 se multiply karte. Oral carbohydrate ka factor yahan IV dextrose par apply nahi hota.
- **B. Incorrect:** 180 g se humein dextrose ka weight pata chalta hai. Energy nikalne ke liye har gram ki calories se multiply karna padega.
- **C. Incorrect:** 529 kcal diye hue factor se nahi aata. Grams ko kcal per gram se multiply karne par units bhi kcal mein cancel hoti hain.
- **D. Correct:** IV dextrose se har gram par 3.4 kcal milti hain. Isliye 180 × 3.4 = 612 kcal hota hai.

**Exam clue:** IV dextrose ka factor question mein diya ho toh oral carbohydrate ka factor use mat karo.

**Evidence:** [Saudi MOH adult PN 2024, 5.6.2](https://www.moh.gov.sa/en/Ministry/MediaCenter/Publications/Documents/CPG_Adult_PARENTERAL_NUTRITION_with_Appendix_Sept2024.pdf) (extracted text).

### Q5. A PN order specifies 96 g amino acids per day. Using the conventional approximation of 6.25 g protein-equivalent per gram nitrogen, what nitrogen amount is estimated?

**Difficulty:** 🟢 Easy | **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD | **Topic:** Parenteral formulations | **Type:** Original practice

- **A.** 15.36 g
- **B.** 600 g
- **C.** 6.25 g
- **D.** 96 g

<details><summary>Answer aur detailed explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: A. 15.36 g**

- **A. Correct:** Conventional estimate mein 6.25 g protein-equivalent mein 1 g nitrogen maante hain. 96 ÷ 6.25 = 15.36 g nitrogen hota hai. Clinical calculation mein actual amino acid product ka nitrogen content check karo.
- **B. Incorrect:** 600 g nikalne ke liye multiplication ki gayi hai. Is direction mein conversion galat hai kyunki nitrogen protein-equivalent ka sirf ek hissa hai.
- **C. Incorrect:** 6.25 ek conversion ratio hai. Isse 96 g ka nitrogen amount tabhi milega jab 96 ko is ratio se divide karoge.
- **D. Incorrect:** 96 g poori amino acid mass hai. Nitrogen us mass ka ek hissa hai, isliye poora weight nitrogen nahi hota.

**Exam clue:** Protein-equivalent se nitrogen ke liye 6.25 se divide karo, actual product label alag ho sakta hai.

### Q6. A hypothetical adult weighs 60 kg and receives 180 g IV dextrose uniformly over 24 hours. What is the calculated glucose infusion rate, rounded to two decimal places?

**Difficulty:** 🔴 Difficult | **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD | **Topic:** Parenteral formulations | **Type:** Original practice

- **A.** 3.00 mg/kg/min
- **B.** 125 mg/kg/min
- **C.** 2.08 mg/kg/min
- **D.** 0.00208 mg/kg/min

<details><summary>Answer aur detailed explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: C. 2.08 mg/kg/min**

- **A. Incorrect:** 180 ÷ 60 = 3 g/kg/day milta hai. Abhi grams aur din ko mg aur minute mein convert nahi kiya gaya hai.
- **B. Incorrect:** 180000 ÷ 1440 = 125 mg/min poore patient ka rate hai. Per kg result ke liye 60 kg se bhi divide karna padega.
- **C. Correct:** 180 g = 180000 mg aur 24 hours = 1440 minutes hote hain. 180000 ÷ (60 × 1440) = 2.08 mg/kg/min hota hai. Yeh sirf hypothetical arithmetic hai, safe infusion prescription nahi.
- **D. Incorrect:** 0.00208 tab milta jab gram ko mg mein convert nahi karte. Unit conversion se result 1000 guna badalta hai.

**Exam clue:** GIR mein grams ko milligrams aur hours ko minutes mein convert karo.

### Q17. A patient receives long-term PN and serial serum triglycerides rise after lipid provision changes. Which review is most directly relevant to the formulation?

**Difficulty:** 🟡 Medium | **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD | **Topic:** Parenteral formulations | **Type:** Original practice

- **A.** Assess lipid dose, product, infusion pattern and clinical contributors with the nutrition team
- **B.** Increase lipid provision solely to correct the high result
- **C.** Remove all amino acids permanently
- **D.** Ignore triglycerides because intravenous lipid cannot affect circulating triglycerides

<details><summary>Answer aur detailed explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: A. Assess lipid dose, product, infusion pattern and clinical contributors with the nutrition team**

- **A. Correct:** Serum triglycerides badhne ka matlab lipid clearance ya overall nutrient delivery mein problem ho sakti hai. Nutrition team lipid dose, rate, dextrose, medicines aur clinical condition review karti hai. Nurse timely escalation kare aur prescribed hold ya stop protocol follow kare. Intralipid 20% ke adult label mein 400 mg/dL se upar infusion rokne ka instruction hai. Doosre products aur children ke liye same cutoff assume mat karo.
- **B. Incorrect:** High triglycerides ke saath extra lipid dene se level aur badh sakta hai. Pehle patient ki tolerance aur product-specific instructions review karni hoti hain.
- **C. Incorrect:** Amino acids protein support ke liye hote hain. Unhe permanently remove karna lipid clearance ki problem ka direct solution nahi hai.
