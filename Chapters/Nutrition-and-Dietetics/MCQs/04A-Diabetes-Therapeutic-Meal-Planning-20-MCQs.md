### Q1. A nurse uses the CDC diabetes plate method to teach a patient without special dietary restrictions. What occupies approximately half of the reference plate?

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Priority:** High | **Topic:** Diabetes therapeutic meal planning | **Type:** Original practice

- **A.** Rice and other starches
- **B.** Lean protein
- **C.** Nonstarchy vegetables
- **D.** Fruit juice

<details><summary>Answer aur detailed explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: C. Nonstarchy vegetables**

- **A. Incorrect:** Starchy foods reference plate ka roughly one-quarter part hote hain. Half starch portion intended proportion change kar dega.
- **B. Incorrect:** Protein foods one-quarter occupy karte hain. Half protein se reference plate ka vegetable share displace hoga.
- **C. Correct:** Half plate nonstarchy vegetables se fibre aur micronutrients milte hain with comparatively little carbohydrate. Remaining quarters protein aur carbohydrate foods ke liye hain.
- **D. Incorrect:** Juice beverage hai aur carbohydrate contribute karta hai. Half plate juice reference plate pattern nahi hai.

</details>

### Q2. A patient counts carbohydrate servings using the CDC convention of approximately 15 g per serving. A meal contains 45 g carbohydrate. How many carbohydrate servings does it provide?

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Priority:** High | **Topic:** Diabetes therapeutic meal planning | **Type:** Original practice

- **A.** 3
- **B.** 1.5
- **C.** 4.5
- **D.** 15

<details><summary>Answer aur detailed explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: A. 3**

- **A. Correct:** 45 ko 15 se divide karein toh 3 servings milti hain. Carbohydrate serving aur ordinary food serving hamesha identical nahi hote.
- **B. Incorrect:** 1.5 carbohydrate servings approximately 22.5 g hongi. Yeh given carbohydrate amount se kam hai.
- **C. Incorrect:** 4.5 servings 67.5 g hongi. Calculation mein 15 g conversion use karna chahiye.
- **D. Incorrect:** 15 grams ek serving ka reference hai, servings ki total number nahi.

</details>

### Q3. An adult trained in carbohydrate counting has a prescribed insulin-to-carbohydrate ratio of 1 unit per 12 g. For a planned 60-g meal, what is the calculated carbohydrate-coverage component alone?

- **B.** 12 units
- **C.** 5 units
- **D.** 60 units

<details><summary>Answer aur detailed explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: C. 5 units**

- **A. Incorrect:** 3 units prescribed ratio par 36 g carbohydrate cover karengi. Meal 60 g ka hai.
- **B. Incorrect:** 12 units ratio par 144 g cover karengi. Ratio denominator ko dose samajhna incorrect hai.
- **C. Correct:** 60/12 se 5 units milti hain. Yeh hypothetical carbohydrate component hai, correction dose, current glucose, active insulin aur actual administration protocol separately assess hote hain.
- **D. Incorrect:** Carbohydrate grams insulin units nahi hain. Prescribed ratio apply karna essential hai.

</details>

### Q4. A person using a prescribed mealtime insulin ratio orders a meal containing 36 g carbohydrate instead of the originally planned 60 g. What should guide the trained calculation before administration?

- **B.** Recalculate the carbohydrate component from the actual intended meal and prescribed ratio
- **C.** Omit all basal insulin for the day
- **D.** Use the same insulin units as carbohydrate grams

<details><summary>Answer aur detailed explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: B. Recalculate the carbohydrate component from the actual intended meal and prescribed ratio**

- **A. Incorrect:** Planned carbohydrate kam ho gayi hai. Original dose automatically dene se insulin-meal mismatch ho sakta hai.
- **B. Correct:** Meal change se nutritional insulin need change ho sakti hai. Individualized plan, glucose aur active insulin review karke actual order follow karein.
- **C. Incorrect:** Basal insulin background metabolic need cover karta hai. Meal adjustment se basal insulin automatically stop nahi hota.
- **D. Incorrect:** Grams aur units different quantities hain. Dose prescribed ratio se calculate hoti hai.

</details>

</details>

### Q6. A patient on mealtime insulin asks why high-fat mixed meals may produce later glucose elevations despite similar carbohydrate content. Which explanation is most appropriate?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Priority:** Medium | **Topic:** Diabetes therapeutic meal planning | **Type:** Original practice

- **A.** Fat always prevents all carbohydrate absorption
- **B.** Fat and protein can alter the timing and magnitude of postprandial glucose response
- **C.** Only sodium determines postprandial glucose
- **D.** Identical carbohydrate totals guarantee identical glucose curves

<details><summary>Answer aur detailed explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: B. Fat and protein can alter the timing and magnitude of postprandial glucose response**

- **A. Incorrect:** Fat absorption timing modify kar sakta hai, lekin carbohydrate uptake permanently prevent nahi karta.
- **B. Correct:** Mixed meals digestion aur absorption timing affect kar sakte hain. High-fat/protein meal ke late glucose pattern ko individualized monitoring aur insulin education mein consider karte hain.
- **C. Incorrect:** Sodium blood pressure mein important hai. Meal glucose response multiple macronutrients aur insulin se influenced hota hai.
- **D. Incorrect:** Carbohydrate amount important hai, lekin meal composition, activity aur insulin response bhi curve change karte hain.

</details>

### Q7. A patient taking an SGLT2 inhibitor proposes a ketogenic diet without consulting the diabetes team. What is the specific safety concern?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Priority:** High | **Topic:** Diabetes therapeutic meal planning | **Type:** Original practice

- **A.** Increased ketoacidosis risk, including possible relatively normal glucose
- **B.** Guaranteed severe hyperglycaemia after every low-carbohydrate meal
- **C.** Loss of all dietary sodium absorption
- **D.** Permanent vitamin C toxicity

<details><summary>Answer aur detailed explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: A. Increased ketoacidosis risk, including possible relatively normal glucose**

- **A. Correct:** SGLT2 inhibition aur very-low-carbohydrate intake ketone risk increase kar sakte hain. ADA ketogenic pattern discourage karti hai in at-risk patients, so clinician review essential hai.
- **B. Incorrect:** DKA SGLT2 users mein markedly high glucose ke bina bhi ho sakti hai. Glucose alone risk exclude nahi karta.
- **C. Incorrect:** SGLT2 drug renal glucose handling affect karti hai. Intestinal sodium absorption completely stop hona specific concern nahi.
- **D. Incorrect:** Ketogenic diet aur SGLT2 combination ka specific risk ketoacidosis hai, vitamin C toxicity nahi.

</details>

- **D. Incorrect:** Large unplanned meals glucose excursions create kar sakte hain. Planned portions aur prescribed insulin coordination useful hai.

</details>
