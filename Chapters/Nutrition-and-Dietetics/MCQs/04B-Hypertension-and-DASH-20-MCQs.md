- **B.** Daily Allowance for Sodium and Hydration
- **C.** Dietary Avoidance of Starch for Health
- **D.** Dietary Approaches to Stop Hypertension

<details><summary>Answer aur detailed explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: D. Dietary Approaches to Stop Hypertension**

- **A. Incorrect:** Haemoglobin assessment anaemia evaluation ka part hai. DASH hypertension-focused eating pattern describe karta hai.
- **B. Incorrect:** DASH ek balanced food pattern hai, sirf sodium aur fluid allowance calculate karne ka formula nahi.
- **C. Incorrect:** DASH whole grains include karta hai. Starch ki complete avoidance iska objective nahi.
- **D. Correct:** DASH blood pressure control support karne wala overall dietary pattern hai. Yeh sirf salt restriction ka doosra naam nahi hai.

</details>
