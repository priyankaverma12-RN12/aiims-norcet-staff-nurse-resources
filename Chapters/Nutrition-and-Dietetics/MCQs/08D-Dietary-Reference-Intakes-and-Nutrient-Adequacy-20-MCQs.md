### Q1. In the ICMR-NIN 2020 nutrient requirements framework, which interpretation of the Estimated Average Requirement is appropriate for a specified healthy reference group?

**Difficulty:** 🟡 Medium | **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD | **Topic:** Meaning of EAR | **Type:** Original practice

- **A.** The highest intake without any possible adverse effect
- **B.** The amount required by every individual in the group
- **C.** An intake corresponding to the median requirement of the group
- **D.** The measured average intake of all surveyed households

<details><summary>Answer aur detailed explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: C. An intake corresponding to the median requirement of the group**

- **A. Incorrect:** Highest tolerable intake ka concept TUL se related hai. EAR adequacy assess karne ke liye central requirement estimate hai, safety ceiling nahi.

- **B. Incorrect:** Healthy individuals ki requirements bhi vary karti hain. Ek central estimate ko sabki exact need nahi maan sakte.

- **C. Correct:** EAR requirement distribution ke median ko represent karta hai. Is value par lagbhag aadhe reference group ki requirement meet ho sakti hai, lekin har individual ki exact need pata nahi chalti.

- **D. Incorrect:** Survey mein jo log actually khate hain woh observed intake hai. EAR physiological requirement distribution se derive hota hai, survey mean se automatically nahi.

**Exam clue:** EAR ko requirement distribution ke centre se jodo, actual food consumption se nahi.

### Q2. A teacher describes the RDA for a nutrient as the physiological requirement of an average person. Which correction best distinguishes it from EAR?

**Difficulty:** 🟡 Medium | **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD | **Topic:** Meaning of RDA | **Type:** Original practice

- **A.** RDA is set to cover the requirements of nearly all healthy individuals in the specified reference group
- **B.** RDA is the median of observed nutrient intakes
- **C.** RDA identifies a dose that treats established nutrient deficiency
- **D.** RDA is the upper safety threshold for chronic consumption

<details><summary>Answer aur detailed explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: A. RDA is set to cover the requirements of nearly all healthy individuals in the specified reference group**

- **A. Correct:** RDA ko requirement distribution ke upper end par set kiya jata hai, generally lagbhag 97.5% healthy group ki needs cover karne ke liye. Isliye yeh median requirement se different hai.

- **B. Incorrect:** Observed intake ka median batata hai ki log aam taur par kitna consume karte hain. RDA biological requirement estimate se derive hota hai.

- **C. Incorrect:** Established deficiency ke treatment ki dose clinical diagnosis aur protocol par depend karti hai. RDA ko therapeutic prescription banana galat hai.

- **D. Incorrect:** Upper safety reference TUL ya UL hai. RDA ka purpose nutrient adequacy se related hai, maximum safe intake se nahi.

**Exam clue:** RDA nearly all healthy reference individuals ko cover karta hai, EAR median requirement hai.

### Q3. A district report labels every person consuming less than the RDA as having inadequate intake. Why is this method inappropriate for estimating group prevalence?

**Difficulty:** 🟡 Medium | **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD | **Topic:** Group RDA misuse | **Type:** Original practice

- **A.** The RDA is always lower than the EAR
- **B.** Anyone below the RDA necessarily has normal biochemical status
- **C.** The RDA measures only food expenditure
- **D.** Many individuals below the RDA can still meet their own requirements

<details><summary>Answer aur detailed explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: D. Many individuals below the RDA can still meet their own requirements**

- **A. Incorrect:** RDA generally EAR se upar set hota hai. Dono ko reverse karna group interpretation ko aur galat bana dega.

- **B. Incorrect:** Below-RDA intake se biochemical status normal ya abnormal prove nahi hota. Clinical aur laboratory evidence separately assess karna padta hai.

- **C. Incorrect:** RDA nutrient intake reference hai, rupees mein expenditure indicator nahi. Group assessment ke liye appropriate EAR-based method chahiye.

- **D. Correct:** RDA nearly sab healthy people ki requirement cover karne ke liye set hai. Bahut logon ki actual requirement isse kam hogi, isliye below-RDA count inadequacy ko overestimate kar sakta hai.

**Exam clue:** Group inadequacy ke liye below-RDA percentage ko disease prevalence mat bolo.

### Q4. A survey has reliable usual intake estimates for 240 comparable adults. For a nutrient meeting the assumptions of the EAR cut-point method, 54 adults have usual intakes below the EAR. What is the estimated prevalence of inadequate intake?

- **D.** 4.4%

<details><summary>Answer aur detailed explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: B. 22.5%**

- **A. Incorrect:** 54 participants ka count hai, percentage nahi. Correct denominator 240 hai.

- **B. Correct:** 54 ko 240 se divide karke 100 multiply karo. 22.5% estimated inadequate intake aata hai, given assumptions ke under. Yeh laboratory-confirmed deficiency prevalence nahi hai.

### Q20. A canteen intervention reduces the proportion of usual nutrient intakes below the EAR but creates a substantial new proportion above the applicable UL. Which evaluation best reflects sound group nutrition planning?

- **D.** Evaluate only the average cost per meal

<details><summary>Answer aur detailed explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: A. Review both the inadequacy and excess-risk tails and revise the intervention**

- **D. Incorrect:** Meal cost feasibility indicator hai, nutrient adequacy aur safety dono ko directly assess nahi karta. Intake distribution ki review zaroori hai.

**Exam clue:** Group planning should reduce both inadequate and potentially excessive intakes.
