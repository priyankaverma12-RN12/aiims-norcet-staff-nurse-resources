### Q1. A patient with severe hypoalbuminaemia has a low total calcium result but no symptoms. Which test most directly assesses physiologically active circulating calcium?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Priority:** High | **Topic:** Calcium and phosphorus | **Type:** Original practice

- **A.** Repeat total calcium alone
- **B.** Ionized calcium
- **C.** Serum phosphate alone
- **D.** Serum alkaline phosphatase

<details><summary>Answer aur detailed explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: B. Ionized calcium**

- **A. Incorrect:** Repeat se measurement confirm ho sakta hai, lekin protein binding ka effect separate nahi hota.
- **B. Correct:** Free calcium hi nerves aur muscles par direct effect karta hai. Albumin kam ho toh total calcium low dikh sakta hai, isliye ionized calcium clinical picture clear karta hai.
- **C. Incorrect:** Phosphate calcium regulation se related hai, lekin free calcium ka direct measurement nahi hai.
- **D. Incorrect:** ALP bone ya liver activity suggest kar sakta hai, lekin circulating free calcium nahi measure karta.

</details>

### Q2. During a large-volume transfusion, a patient develops perioral tingling and prolonged QT. Which mechanism should the nurse consider?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Priority:** High | **Topic:** Calcium and phosphorus | **Type:** Original practice

- **A.** Citrate increasing free calcium release
- **B.** Increased dietary phosphate absorption during transfusion
- **C.** Immediate increased bone mineralization
- **D.** Citrate binding of ionized calcium

<details><summary>Answer aur detailed explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: D. Citrate binding of ionized calcium**

- **A. Incorrect:** Citrate free calcium ko bind karta hai, release nahi karta. Symptoms opposite mechanism suggest karte hain.
- **B. Incorrect:** Transfusion ke acute timing ko gut absorption explain nahi karta. Citrate exposure more relevant hai.
- **C. Incorrect:** Bone mineral deposition itne acute symptoms ka usual mechanism nahi hai.
- **D. Correct:** Blood products mein citrate calcium bind kar sakta hai. Rapid transfusion mein free calcium gir sakta hai, isliye urgent ECG aur electrolyte review zaroori hai.

</details>

### Q3. Which hormone normally responds to a fall in ionized calcium by increasing renal calcium conservation?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Priority:** High | **Topic:** Calcium and phosphorus | **Type:** Original practice

- **A.** Parathyroid hormone
- **B.** Calcitonin
- **C.** Aldosterone
- **D.** Insulin

<details><summary>Answer aur detailed explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: A. Parathyroid hormone**

- **A. Correct:** PTH low calcium ko sense karke kidney mein calcium conservation aur phosphate excretion badhata hai. Isse serum calcium restore karne mein help milti hai.
- **B. Incorrect:** Calcitonin osteoclast activity reduce kar sakta hai. Low calcium ka main compensatory hormone PTH hai.
- **C. Incorrect:** Aldosterone sodium aur potassium balance regulate karta hai, calcium sensing ka primary hormone nahi hai.
- **D. Incorrect:** Insulin glucose uptake aur metabolism regulate karta hai. Renal calcium conservation ka main low-calcium response nahi hai.

</details>

### Q4. A patient has tingling, carpopedal spasm and a low ionized calcium result. What explains the neuromuscular symptoms?

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Priority:** High | **Topic:** Calcium and phosphorus | **Type:** Original practice

- **A.** Reduced neuromuscular excitability due to excess calcium
- **B.** Isolated impaired haemoglobin synthesis
- **C.** Increased excitability of excitable cell membranes
- **D.** Reduced sodium absorption from food only

<details><summary>Answer aur detailed explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: C. Increased excitability of excitable cell membranes**

- **A. Incorrect:** Excess calcium weakness se associated ho sakta hai, lekin yahan free calcium low hai.
- **B. Incorrect:** Iron-related haemoglobin synthesis problem tetany ka direct mechanism nahi hai.
- **C. Correct:** Free calcium kam hone se nerve aur muscle membrane excitability badh sakti hai. Spasm aur tetany isi wajah se aa sakte hain.
- **D. Incorrect:** Dietary sodium alone documented ionized calcium fall aur tetany ko explain nahi karta.

</details>

### Q5. A patient has persistent hypocalcaemia despite prescribed calcium replacement. Which additional mineral deficiency can impair PTH secretion or action?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Priority:** High | **Topic:** Calcium and phosphorus | **Type:** Original practice

- **A.** Zinc
- **B.** Iron
- **C.** Magnesium
- **D.** Iodine

<details><summary>Answer aur detailed explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: C. Magnesium**

- **A. Incorrect:** Zinc enzyme aur wound healing roles mein important hai, lekin refractory hypocalcaemia ka classic PTH-related mineral magnesium hai.
- **B. Incorrect:** Iron haemoglobin aur oxygen transport mein important hai. PTH release failure ka typical cause iron deficiency nahi hai.
- **C. Correct:** Severe magnesium deficiency PTH release aur response ko disturb kar sakti hai. Isliye refractory hypocalcaemia mein magnesium check karna useful hai.
- **D. Incorrect:** Iodine thyroid hormone synthesis mein use hota hai, PTH function ke liye specific mineral nahi hai.

</details>

### Q6. A patient who takes a prescribed calcium carbonate supplement asks when it is best absorbed. Which instruction is generally appropriate?

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Priority:** Medium | **Topic:** Calcium and phosphorus | **Type:** Original practice

- **A.** Take it with food unless the prescribed instructions differ
- **B.** Take it only with a strong antacid
- **C.** Always crush every extended-release formulation
- **D.** Take it at the same time as all medicines

<details><summary>Answer aur detailed explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: A. Take it with food unless the prescribed instructions differ**

- **A. Correct:** Calcium carbonate ko dissolve hone ke liye acid helpful hota hai. Meal ke saath absorption generally better ho sakta hai, lekin individual instructions follow karni chahiye.
- **B. Incorrect:** Acid neutralization carbonate dissolution ko impair kar sakti hai. Extra antacid absorption improve karne ka standard plan nahi hai.
- **C. Incorrect:** Formulation ko bina check crush karna unsafe ho sakta hai. Product-specific instructions zaroori hain.
- **D. Incorrect:** Calcium kuch medicines ke absorption mein interfere kar sakta hai. Timing pharmacist ya prescription se check karni chahiye.

</details>

### Q7. Which calcium salt is generally less dependent on gastric acidity for absorption than calcium carbonate?

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Priority:** Medium | **Topic:** Calcium and phosphorus | **Type:** Original practice

- **A.** Calcium carbonate
- **B.** Calcium phosphate as a universal preferred replacement
- **C.** Calcium oxalate
- **D.** Calcium citrate

<details><summary>Answer aur detailed explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: D. Calcium citrate**

- **A. Incorrect:** Carbonate dissolution acid se zyada influenced hoti hai, isliye food ke saath lena often helpful hota hai.
- **B. Incorrect:** Calcium phosphate har patient ke liye preferred nahi hai, especially phosphate balance concern ho toh.
- **C. Incorrect:** Oxalate calcium ke saath poorly absorbed compound bana sakta hai, isliye yeh useful standard supplement choice nahi hai.
- **D. Correct:** Citrate salt comparatively less acid-dependent hota hai. Low gastric acid wale patients mein formulation selection clinician guide kar sakta hai.

</details>

</details>

### Q13. A patient with advanced kidney disease has persistent hyperphosphataemia. Which explanation best connects it with renal dysfunction?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Priority:** High | **Topic:** Calcium and phosphorus | **Type:** Original practice

- **A.** Reduced renal phosphate elimination
- **B.** Increased phosphate excretion causing retention
- **C.** Loss of intestinal calcium transport alone
- **D.** Excessive conversion of phosphate into glucose

<details><summary>Answer aur detailed explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: A. Reduced renal phosphate elimination**

- **A. Correct:** Kidney phosphate excretion ka major route hai. Advanced dysfunction mein phosphate retention ho sakti hai aur mineral-bone management chahiye.
- **B. Incorrect:** Zyada urinary elimination retention ka opposite mechanism hai. Problem inadequate elimination ho sakti hai.
- **C. Incorrect:** Calcium absorption changes associated ho sakti hain, lekin phosphate retention ka direct renal mechanism reduced excretion hai.
- **D. Incorrect:** Phosphate glucose molecule mein convert nahi hota. Renal handling relevant hai.

</details>

### Q18. A patient with recurrent calcium oxalate stones asks whether all food calcium should be eliminated. Which general response is most appropriate?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Priority:** High | **Topic:** Calcium and phosphorus | **Type:** Original practice

- **A.** Maintain appropriate dietary calcium with meals unless the specialist advises otherwise
- **B.** Eliminate all calcium-rich foods indefinitely
- **C.** Take unlimited calcium supplements between meals
- **D.** Increase sodium intake to reduce urinary calcium

<details><summary>Answer aur detailed explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: A. Maintain appropriate dietary calcium with meals unless the specialist advises otherwise**

- **A. Correct:** Meal calcium intestinal oxalate bind kar sakta hai aur normal intake bone health support karta hai. Stone prevention fluid, sodium aur individual urine findings se bhi guide hoti hai.
- **B. Incorrect:** Very low calcium intake oxalate absorption badha sakti hai aur bone health affect kar sakti hai. Blanket restriction appropriate nahi hai.
- **C. Incorrect:** Supplement dose aur timing stone risk affect kar sakti hai. Unlimited supplementation safe assumption nahi hai.
- **D. Incorrect:** High sodium urinary calcium excretion badha sakta hai. Stone prevention mein excess sodium avoid karna often useful hai.

</details>

### Q20. A patient develops constipation after starting a calcium supplement. What is the best initial nursing response?

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Priority:** Medium | **Topic:** Calcium and phosphorus | **Type:** Original practice

- **A.** Immediately stop all dietary calcium
- **B.** Give an unrestricted magnesium laxative despite kidney disease
- **C.** Review formulation, prescribed dose, hydration and bowel symptoms with the care team
- **D.** Ignore constipation until severe abdominal pain develops

<details><summary>Answer aur detailed explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: C. Review formulation, prescribed dose, hydration and bowel symptoms with the care team**

- **A. Incorrect:** Dietary calcium essential nutrient hai. Supplement adverse effect ki wajah se all food calcium stop karna unnecessary ho sakta hai.
- **B. Incorrect:** Kidney function impaired ho toh magnesium accumulation ka risk ho sakta hai. Laxative choice clinical review se honi chahiye.
- **C. Correct:** Calcium supplements GI symptoms de sakte hain. Nurse hydration restrictions, bowel pattern aur medicine plan assess karke safe adjustment coordinate karti hai.
- **D. Incorrect:** Early assessment complications identify karne mein help karta hai. Severe pain ya obstruction signs mein urgent review chahiye.

</details>
