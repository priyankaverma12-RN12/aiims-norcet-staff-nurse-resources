### Q1. A woman with heavy menstrual bleeding has a low ferritin concentration but a haemoglobin value within the reference interval. Which interpretation is most appropriate?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Priority:** High | **Topic:** Iron nutrition | **Type:** Original practice

- **A.** Iron deficiency is excluded by normal haemoglobin
- **B.** The findings establish iron overload
- **C.** Iron stores may be depleted before anaemia develops
- **D.** The findings establish isolated vitamin B12 deficiency

<details><summary>Answer aur detailed explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: C. Iron stores may be depleted before anaemia develops**

- **A. Incorrect:** Haemoglobin late stage mein gir sakta hai. Normal result depleted stores ko exclude nahi karta.
- **B. Incorrect:** Iron overload mein stores excessive ho sakte hain. Low ferritin overload ke typical pattern ke opposite hai.
- **C. Correct:** Ferritin storage iron ka marker hai. Stores kam hone ke baad bhi haemoglobin kuch time normal reh sakta hai, isliye early deficiency ko ignore nahi karna chahiye.
- **D. Incorrect:** B12 deficiency ka diagnosis specific assessment se hota hai. Low ferritin depleted iron stores ko more directly suggest karta hai.

</details>

### Q2. An adult with an active infection has anaemia and a ferritin concentration that is not low. What is the best interpretation?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Priority:** High | **Topic:** Iron nutrition | **Type:** Original practice

- **A.** Inflammation can elevate ferritin and obscure iron deficiency
- **B.** Iron deficiency is impossible when ferritin is normal
- **C.** The patient necessarily has hereditary iron overload
- **D.** Only platelet count can assess iron stores

<details><summary>Answer aur detailed explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: A. Inflammation can elevate ferritin and obscure iron deficiency**

- **A. Correct:** Ferritin acute-phase reactant bhi hai. Infection mein ferritin normal ya high ho sakta hai, isliye inflammation markers aur other iron studies ko context mein interpret karna chahiye.
- **B. Incorrect:** Inflammation ferritin ko falsely reassuring bana sakti hai. Normal ferritin alone deficiency exclude nahi karta.
- **C. Incorrect:** Ferritin inflammation ke reason se bhi high ho sakta hai. Genetic overload diagnosis ke liye further evidence chahiye.
- **D. Incorrect:** Platelet count iron status ka direct marker nahi hai. Ferritin aur other iron studies clinical context ke saath useful hain.

</details>

### Q3. Which circulating protein principally transports iron to developing erythrocytes?

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Priority:** Medium | **Topic:** Iron nutrition | **Type:** Original practice

- **A.** Ferritin
- **B.** Haptoglobin
- **C.** Albumin
- **D.** Transferrin

<details><summary>Answer aur detailed explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: D. Transferrin**

- **A. Incorrect:** Ferritin primarily intracellular iron storage karta hai. Serum ferritin stores assess karne mein help karta hai.
- **B. Incorrect:** Haptoglobin free haemoglobin bind karta hai, normal circulating iron delivery ka principal protein nahi hai.
- **C. Incorrect:** Albumin kai substances transport karta hai, lekin iron delivery ka specialized protein transferrin hai.
- **D. Correct:** Transferrin blood mein iron bind karke marrow jaise tissues tak deliver karta hai. Yeh iron transport protein hai.

</details>

### Q4. In uncomplicated iron deficiency, which iron-study pattern is most consistent with depleted stores?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Priority:** High | **Topic:** Iron nutrition | **Type:** Original practice

- **A.** High ferritin and high transferrin saturation
- **B.** Low ferritin and transferrin saturation with raised total iron-binding capacity
- **C.** Normal ferritin and isolated raised bilirubin
- **D.** High serum iron with low total iron-binding capacity

<details><summary>Answer aur detailed explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: B. Low ferritin and transferrin saturation with raised total iron-binding capacity**

- **A. Incorrect:** Yeh pattern excessive available iron ya other contexts mein ho sakta hai. Uncomplicated deficiency ka expected pattern nahi hai.
- **B. Correct:** Stores kam hone par ferritin low hota hai. Available iron kam aur binding capacity often high hoti hai, isliye saturation girti hai.
- **C. Incorrect:** Bilirubin haemolysis ya liver disorders mein badh sakta hai. Yeh depleted iron stores ka characteristic combined pattern nahi hai.
- **D. Incorrect:** Iron availability low hone ki jagah high dikh rahi hai. Binding capacity bhi usual isolated deficiency se opposite hai.

</details>

### Q5. Which intestinal regulatory protein exports absorbed iron from enterocytes into the circulation?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Priority:** Medium | **Topic:** Iron nutrition | **Type:** Original practice

- **A.** Intrinsic factor
- **B.** Lactase
- **C.** Ferritin
- **D.** Ferroportin

<details><summary>Answer aur detailed explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: D. Ferroportin**

- **A. Incorrect:** Intrinsic factor B12 absorption mein help karta hai, iron export ka channel nahi hai.
- **B. Incorrect:** Lactase lactose digest karta hai. Iron transport protein se iska role alag hai.
- **C. Incorrect:** Ferritin cell ke andar iron store karta hai. Export ke liye ferroportin important hai.
- **D. Correct:** Ferroportin cell membrane se iron export karta hai. Enterocyte se blood tak iron pahunchane mein yeh important step hai.

</details>

### Q6. During chronic inflammation, increased hepcidin can contribute to restricted iron availability by which mechanism?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Priority:** High | **Topic:** Iron nutrition | **Type:** Original practice

- **A.** Increasing iron export from all storage cells
- **B.** Reducing ferroportin-mediated iron export
- **C.** Increasing intrinsic factor-mediated iron absorption
- **D.** Blocking all haemoglobin breakdown

<details><summary>Answer aur detailed explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: B. Reducing ferroportin-mediated iron export**

- **A. Incorrect:** High hepcidin iron export suppress karta hai. Increased export iske opposite hoga.
- **B. Correct:** Hepcidin ferroportin ko downregulate karta hai. Isse macrophages aur enterocytes se iron blood mein kam release ho sakta hai.
- **C. Incorrect:** Intrinsic factor vitamin B12 pathway se related hai. Hepcidin ka relevant target ferroportin hai.
- **D. Incorrect:** Haemoglobin recycling continue ho sakti hai, lekin iron release restricted hota hai. Hepcidin ka main effect breakdown complete stop karna nahi hai.

</details>

### Q7. Which meal contains haem iron as well as non-haem iron?

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Priority:** Medium | **Topic:** Iron nutrition | **Type:** Original practice

- **A.** Chicken with lentils
- **B.** Lentils with spinach only
- **C.** Iron-fortified rice with chickpeas
- **D.** Tea with an iron-fortified biscuit

<details><summary>Answer aur detailed explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: A. Chicken with lentils**

- **A. Correct:** Animal muscle mein haem iron hota hai aur lentils non-haem iron provide karti hain. Chicken meal mein dono forms mil sakte hain.
- **B. Incorrect:** Dono plant foods non-haem iron sources hain. Haem iron naturally animal haem proteins mein hota hai.
- **C. Incorrect:** Fortified iron aur plant iron generally non-haem forms hote hain. Yeh meal haem iron provide nahi karta.
- **D. Incorrect:** Fortification non-haem iron de sakti hai, lekin tea polyphenols absorption affect kar sakte hain. Haem source present nahi hai.

</details>

### Q8. A patient takes prescribed oral iron with strong tea every morning. Which change is most useful to discuss?

- **B.** Take iron only with calcium tablets

- **D.** Stop the iron course and drink tea alone

<details><summary>Answer aur detailed explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: C. Take the iron away from tea according to the prescribed instructions**

- **A. Incorrect:** Strong tea inhibitory polyphenols aur provide kar sakti hai. Yeh iron absorption enhance karne ka recommended approach nahi hai.
- **B. Incorrect:** Calcium iron absorption interfere kar sakta hai. Supplement timing pharmacist ya clinician se check karni chahiye.
- **C. Correct:** Tea polyphenols non-haem iron absorption reduce kar sakte hain. Tolerability aur prescription ko consider karke tea se separation useful hai.
- **D. Incorrect:** Tea deficiency treatment ka replacement nahi hai. Prescribed course aur underlying cause review important hain.

</details>

### Q9. Which preparation of plant foods may reduce phytate and thereby improve mineral bioavailability?

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Priority:** Medium | **Topic:** Iron nutrition | **Type:** Original practice

- **A.** Appropriate soaking, germination or fermentation of pulses and grains
- **B.** Adding extra unprocessed bran to every iron supplement
- **C.** Replacing pulses with refined sugar
- **D.** Keeping cooked food at room temperature overnight

<details><summary>Answer aur detailed explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: A. Appropriate soaking, germination or fermentation of pulses and grains**

- **A. Correct:** In processes se kuch phytate breakdown ho sakta hai, jisse minerals ka available fraction improve ho sakta hai. Safe food hygiene bhi follow karni chahiye.
- **B. Incorrect:** Bran phytate contribute kar sakta hai. Isko universal absorption enhancer nahi maanna chahiye.
- **C. Incorrect:** Sugar meaningful iron supply nahi karti. Phytate reduction ke naam par nutrient-rich food remove karna useful nahi hai.
- **D. Incorrect:** Unsafe storage infection risk badhati hai. Controlled fermentation aur accidental food spoilage same cheez nahi hain.

</details>

### Q10. A patient notices dark stools after starting oral iron but is otherwise well. Which teaching is appropriate?

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Priority:** High | **Topic:** Iron nutrition | **Type:** Original practice

- **A.** Every dark stool means an active gastrointestinal haemorrhage
- **B.** Darkening may occur with iron, but tarry stools with symptoms or suspected bleeding require assessment
- **C.** All dark stools can safely be ignored on iron
- **D.** Increase iron whenever stools become dark

<details><summary>Answer aur detailed explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: B. Darkening may occur with iron, but tarry stools with symptoms or suspected bleeding require assessment**

- **A. Incorrect:** Iron khud stool colour change kar sakta hai. Symptoms aur clinical evaluation se bleeding differentiate karte hain.
- **B. Correct:** Oral iron stool dark kar sakta hai. Lekin sticky tarry stool, dizziness ya bleeding concern ho toh ise routine side effect assume nahi karna chahiye.
- **C. Incorrect:** Bleeding bhi dark stools de sakti hai. Red flags ko ignore karna unsafe hai.
- **D. Incorrect:** Colour se deficiency severity ya dose requirement determine nahi hoti. Prescription aur monitoring follow karni chahiye.

</details>

### Q11. A patient receiving oral iron develops nausea and considers abandoning treatment. What should the nurse do?

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Priority:** High | **Topic:** Iron nutrition | **Type:** Original practice

- **A.** Tell the patient that nausea proves iron overload
- **B.** Advise taking several missed doses together
- **C.** Replace the prescribed course with dietary counselling alone without review
- **D.** Review tolerability and discuss a suitable regimen with the prescriber

<details><summary>Answer aur detailed explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: D. Review tolerability and discuss a suitable regimen with the prescriber**

- **A. Incorrect:** Nausea common GI adverse effect bhi ho sakta hai. Overload diagnosis ke liye other evidence chahiye.

- **C. Incorrect:** Established deficiency mein food alone sufficient na ho sakta hai. Treatment change aur cause assessment important hai.
- **D. Correct:** Iron GI upset kar sakta hai. Formulation, dose schedule ya food timing adjust karne se adherence improve ho sakti hai, lekin change clinical plan se hona chahiye.

</details>

### Q12. Why should iron tablets be kept secured away from young children?

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Priority:** High | **Topic:** Iron nutrition | **Type:** Original practice

- **A.** Iron tablets become harmless when swallowed with food
- **B.** Iron toxicity occurs only after many years of use
- **C.** Accidental ingestion can cause severe systemic poisoning
- **D.** Only injected iron can produce poisoning

<details><summary>Answer aur detailed explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: C. Accidental ingestion can cause severe systemic poisoning**

- **A. Incorrect:** Food ingestion overdose ko reliably neutralize nahi karta. Suspected poisoning urgent concern hai.
- **B. Incorrect:** Acute overdose bhi life-threatening ho sakta hai. Chronic accumulation alag problem hai.
- **C. Correct:** Iron overdose especially children mein serious GI aur systemic toxicity de sakti hai. Secure storage aur suspected ingestion par urgent poison/emergency advice zaroori hai.
- **D. Incorrect:** Oral iron tablets se bhi severe poisoning possible hai. Storage safety oral products ke liye equally important hai.

</details>

<details><summary>Answer aur detailed explanation dekho</summary>

- **C. Incorrect:** Dono numbers add karna incorrect hai kyunki elemental iron salt ke weight ka part hai.

</details>

### Q14. An adult taking levothyroxine is prescribed oral iron. Which counselling point is most important?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Priority:** High | **Topic:** Iron nutrition | **Type:** Original practice

- **A.** Take both at the same instant to improve absorption
- **B.** Stop thyroid replacement throughout the iron course
- **C.** Double both doses on alternate days
- **D.** Follow the recommended separation interval between the medicines

<details><summary>Answer aur detailed explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: D. Follow the recommended separation interval between the medicines**

- **A. Incorrect:** Iron binding thyroid medicine absorption impair kar sakti hai. Simultaneous use generally avoid karne ki advice hoti hai.
- **B. Incorrect:** Unsupervised interruption hypothyroidism worsen kar sakta hai. Timing adjustment safer clinical approach hai.
- **C. Incorrect:** Self-directed dose doubling se toxicity ya under-treatment ho sakta hai. Prescribed schedule follow karna chahiye.
- **D. Correct:** Iron levothyroxine absorption reduce kar sakta hai. Dosing intervals clinician ya pharmacist ke instructions ke according maintain karne chahiye.

</details>

<details><summary>Answer aur detailed explanation dekho</summary>

### Q18. Which monitoring finding can indicate an early marrow response after effective treatment of iron deficiency anaemia?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Priority:** Medium | **Topic:** Iron nutrition | **Type:** Original practice

- **B.** A permanent fall in all reticulocytes to zero
- **C.** An isolated increase in serum sodium
- **D.** An appropriate rise in reticulocyte production

<details><summary>Answer aur detailed explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: D. An appropriate rise in reticulocyte production**

- **A. Incorrect:** New red cells banne mein time lagta hai. Oral iron minutes mein haemoglobin normalize nahi karta.
- **B. Incorrect:** Effective marrow response mein new red cell production expected hai. Zero reticulocytes response nahi dikhate.
- **C. Incorrect:** Sodium fluid balance se related hai. Marrow response ka indicator reticulocyte count hai.

</details>

- **B. Incorrect:** Haemoglobin aur stores different measures hain. Normal haemoglobin overload establish nahi karta.
- **C. Incorrect:** Long-term iron bina indication excess aur adverse effects create kar sakta hai. Duration individualized honi chahiye.

### Q20. In an infant beginning complementary feeding at the appropriate age, which food-planning principle best supports iron nutrition?

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Priority:** High | **Topic:** Iron nutrition | **Type:** Original practice

- **A.** Use only diluted fruit juice as the main complementary food
- **B.** Include developmentally suitable iron-rich complementary foods while continuing appropriate milk feeding
- **C.** Delay all iron-containing foods until school age
- **D.** Replace all meals with unprescribed adult iron tablets

<details><summary>Answer aur detailed explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: B. Include developmentally suitable iron-rich complementary foods while continuing appropriate milk feeding**

- **A. Incorrect:** Juice iron aur other nutrients ka adequate source nahi hai. Dilution nutrient density further reduce kar sakti hai.
- **B. Correct:** Growth ke saath infant ki iron needs badhti hain. Safe texture wale iron-rich foods aur age-appropriate milk feeding together nutrient needs support karte hain.
- **C. Incorrect:** Iron growth aur neurodevelopment ke liye important hai. Appropriate complementary foods ko unnecessary delay nahi karna chahiye.
- **D. Incorrect:** Adult tablets infants ke liye dose aur choking/toxicity risk create karte hain. Supplements sirf age-specific clinical guidance se dene chahiye.

</details>
