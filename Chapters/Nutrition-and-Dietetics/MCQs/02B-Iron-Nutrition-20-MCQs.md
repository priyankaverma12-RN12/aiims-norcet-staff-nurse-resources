### Q1. A woman with heavy menstrual bleeding has a low ferritin concentration but a haemoglobin value within the reference interval. Which interpretation is most appropriate?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Priority:** High | **Topic:** Iron nutrition | **Type:** Original practice

- **A.** Iron deficiency is excluded by normal haemoglobin
- **B.** The findings establish iron overload
- **C.** The findings establish isolated vitamin B12 deficiency
- **D.** Iron stores may be depleted before anaemia develops

<details><summary>Answer aur detailed explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: D. Iron stores may be depleted before anaemia develops**

- **A. Incorrect:** Haemoglobin late stage mein gir sakta hai. Normal result depleted stores ko exclude nahi karta.
- **B. Incorrect:** Iron overload mein stores excessive ho sakte hain. Low ferritin overload ke typical pattern ke opposite hai.
- **C. Incorrect:** B12 deficiency ka diagnosis specific assessment se hota hai. Low ferritin depleted iron stores ko more directly suggest karta hai.
- **D. Correct:** Ferritin storage iron ka marker hai. Stores kam hone ke baad bhi haemoglobin kuch time normal reh sakta hai, isliye early deficiency ko ignore nahi karna chahiye.

</details>

### Q2. A district nutrition programme distributes iron-fortified staple foods and wants to assess whether the intervention is reaching households. Which measure best evaluates intervention coverage?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Priority:** High | **Topic:** Iron nutrition | **Type:** Original practice

- **A.** The proportion of sampled target households actually consuming the fortified staple
- **B.** The haemoglobin of one randomly selected patient
- **C.** The number of all food shops in the district
- **D.** The total kilograms of unfortified rice harvested

<details><summary>Answer aur detailed explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: A. The proportion of sampled target households actually consuming the fortified staple**

- **A. Correct:** Coverage ke liye dekhna hai ki intended households fortified food use kar rahe hain ya nahi. Anaemia prevalence outcome hai aur multiple causes se affect hoti hai.
- **B. Incorrect:** Ek individual ka Hb population coverage reliably represent nahi karta. Representative household information chahiye.
- **C. Incorrect:** Shop count se fortified staple ka use establish nahi hota. Product availability aur actual consumption separately assess karte hain.
- **D. Incorrect:** Harvest quantity fortification reach nahi batati. Intervention-specific consumption data relevant hai.

</details>

### Q3. Which circulating protein principally transports iron to developing erythrocytes?

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Priority:** Medium | **Topic:** Iron nutrition | **Type:** Original practice

- **A.** Ferritin
- **B.** Haptoglobin
- **C.** Transferrin
- **D.** Albumin

<details><summary>Answer aur detailed explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: C. Transferrin**

- **A. Incorrect:** Ferritin primarily intracellular iron storage karta hai. Serum ferritin stores assess karne mein help karta hai.
- **B. Incorrect:** Haptoglobin free haemoglobin bind karta hai, normal circulating iron delivery ka principal protein nahi hai.
- **C. Correct:** Transferrin blood mein iron bind karke marrow jaise tissues tak deliver karta hai. Yeh iron transport protein hai.
- **D. Incorrect:** Albumin kai substances transport karta hai, lekin iron delivery ka specialized protein transferrin hai.

</details>

### Q4. Which physiological change explains a substantial increase in iron requirements during pregnancy compared with the nonpregnant state?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Priority:** High | **Topic:** Iron nutrition | **Type:** Original practice

- **A.** Complete cessation of maternal red-cell production
- **B.** Conversion of maternal calcium stores directly into fetal iron
- **C.** Expansion of maternal red-cell mass and transfer of iron to the developing fetus and placenta
- **D.** Permanent elimination of placental nutrient transfer

<details><summary>Answer aur detailed explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: C. Expansion of maternal red-cell mass and transfer of iron to the developing fetus and placenta**

- **A. Incorrect:** Maternal erythropoiesis stop nahi hoti, red-cell mass expand hota hai. Yeh iron requirement increase ka opposite explanation hai.
- **B. Incorrect:** Calcium aur iron separate elements hain. Body calcium ko iron mein convert nahi kar sakti.
- **C. Correct:** Pregnancy mein maternal blood cell production badhti hai aur fetus aur placenta ko bhi iron chahiye. Isliye diet aur recommended supplementation ka review important hai.
- **D. Incorrect:** Placenta fetal nutrient supply mein important hai. Iron transfer pregnancy-related requirement ka part hai.

</details>

### Q5. Which intestinal regulatory protein exports absorbed iron from enterocytes into the circulation?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Priority:** Medium | **Topic:** Iron nutrition | **Type:** Original practice

- **A.** Intrinsic factor
- **B.** Lactase
- **C.** Ferroportin
- **D.** Ferritin

<details><summary>Answer aur detailed explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: C. Ferroportin**

- **A. Incorrect:** Intrinsic factor B12 absorption mein help karta hai, iron export ka channel nahi hai.
- **B. Incorrect:** Lactase lactose digest karta hai. Iron transport protein se iska role alag hai.
- **C. Correct:** Ferroportin cell membrane se iron export karta hai. Enterocyte se blood tak iron pahunchane mein yeh important step hai.
- **D. Incorrect:** Ferritin cell ke andar iron store karta hai. Export ke liye ferroportin important hai.

</details>

### Q6. Which transporter participates in uptake of non-haem ferrous iron from the intestinal lumen into absorptive enterocytes?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Priority:** High | **Topic:** Iron nutrition | **Type:** Original practice

- **A.** Ferroportin as the principal luminal uptake transporter
- **B.** Sodium-iodide symporter
- **C.** Divalent metal transporter 1
- **D.** Intrinsic factor receptor

<details><summary>Answer aur detailed explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: C. Divalent metal transporter 1**

- **A. Incorrect:** Ferroportin ka main relevant role iron export hai. Luminal ferrous uptake DMT1 se associated hai.
- **B. Incorrect:** Yeh iodide transport se related hai. Dietary ferrous iron ka specific intestinal transporter DMT1 hai.
- **C. Correct:** DMT1 ferrous iron ko intestinal brush border ke across cell mein la sakta hai. Ferroportin later enterocyte se iron export karta hai, isliye uptake aur export distinguish karein.
- **D. Incorrect:** Intrinsic factor complex receptor B12 absorption mein involved hai. Iron absorption ka mechanism alag hai.

</details>

### Q7. Which meal contains haem iron as well as non-haem iron?

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Priority:** Medium | **Topic:** Iron nutrition | **Type:** Original practice

- **A.** Chicken with lentils
- **B.** Lentils with spinach only
- **C.** Iron-fortified rice with chickpeas
- **D.** Tea with an iron-fortified biscuit

<details><summary>Answer aur detailed explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: A. Chicken with lentils**

- **A. Correct:** Animal muscle mein haem iron hota hai aur lentils non-haem iron provide karti hain. Chicken meal mein dono forms mil sakte hain.
- **B. Incorrect:** Dono plant foods non-haem iron sources hain. Haem iron naturally animal haem proteins mein hota hai.
- **C. Incorrect:** Fortified iron aur plant iron generally non-haem forms hote hain. Yeh meal haem iron provide nahi karta.
- **D. Incorrect:** Fortification non-haem iron de sakti hai, lekin tea polyphenols absorption affect kar sakte hain. Haem source present nahi hai.

</details>

### Q8. A nurse compares two hypothetical iron-containing menus: one includes a small serving of meat and the other has the same plant ingredients without meat. What effect may the meat have on the absorption of the plant iron?

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Priority:** High | **Topic:** Iron nutrition | **Type:** Original practice

- **A.** It converts all plant iron into haem iron before digestion
- **B.** It necessarily blocks all plant iron absorption
- **C.** It changes the iron atom into calcium
- **D.** It can enhance absorption of non-haem iron from the mixed meal

<details><summary>Answer aur detailed explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: D. It can enhance absorption of non-haem iron from the mixed meal**

- **A. Incorrect:** Plant iron chemically haem protein mein automatically convert nahi hota. Meal interaction absorption affect kar sakti hai.
- **B. Incorrect:** Animal protein ka effect universal inhibition nahi hai. Mixed meal mein enhancement possible hai.
- **C. Incorrect:** Food interactions mineral ki bioavailability affect karte hain, element ko doosre element mein convert nahi karte.
- **D. Correct:** Meat, poultry aur seafood meal ke non-haem iron absorption ko enhance kar sakte hain. Individual dietary preferences respect karte hue alternatives plan kiye ja sakte hain.

</details>

### Q9. Which preparation of plant foods may reduce phytate and thereby improve mineral bioavailability?

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Priority:** Medium | **Topic:** Iron nutrition | **Type:** Original practice

- **A.** Adding extra unprocessed bran to every iron supplement
- **B.** Replacing pulses with refined sugar
- **C.** Keeping cooked food at room temperature overnight
- **D.** Appropriate soaking, germination or fermentation of pulses and grains

<details><summary>Answer aur detailed explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: D. Appropriate soaking, germination or fermentation of pulses and grains**

- **A. Incorrect:** Bran phytate contribute kar sakta hai. Isko universal absorption enhancer nahi maanna chahiye.
- **B. Incorrect:** Sugar meaningful iron supply nahi karti. Phytate reduction ke naam par nutrient-rich food remove karna useful nahi hai.
- **C. Incorrect:** Unsafe storage infection risk badhati hai. Controlled fermentation aur accidental food spoilage same cheez nahi hain.
- **D. Correct:** In processes se kuch phytate breakdown ho sakta hai, jisse minerals ka available fraction improve ho sakta hai. Safe food hygiene bhi follow karni chahiye.

</details>

### Q13. A hypothetical fortified cereal supplies 8 mg of iron per 100 g of dry product. A patient uses a 35 g dry portion. How much iron does this portion contain before preparation losses?

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Priority:** High | **Topic:** Iron nutrition | **Type:** Original practice

- **A.** 8 mg
- **B.** 2.8 mg
- **C.** 28 mg
- **D.** 0.28 mg

<details><summary>Answer aur detailed explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: B. 2.8 mg**

- **A. Incorrect:** Yeh full 100 g portion ka amount hai. Actual dry portion 35 g hai.
- **B. Correct:** 8 mg per 100 g ko 35 g ke portion se multiply karte hain. 8 times 35 divided by 100 se 2.8 mg milta hai, absorbed amount alag ho sakta hai.
- **C. Incorrect:** Decimal placement se tenfold overestimate hua hai. 35 g less than 100 g hai, toh iron amount 8 mg se kam hona chahiye.
- **D. Incorrect:** Yeh tenfold underestimate hai. Correct proportion 35/100 times 8 mg hai.

</details>

### Q14. Why is iron required by skeletal muscle independently of its role in circulating haemoglobin?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Priority:** High | **Topic:** Iron nutrition | **Type:** Original practice

- **A.** Iron is the main carbohydrate polymer stored in muscle
- **B.** Myoglobin contains iron and supports oxygen handling within muscle
- **C.** Iron directly substitutes for ATP in muscle contraction
- **D.** Iron makes muscle contraction independent of oxygen

<details><summary>Answer aur detailed explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: B. Myoglobin contains iron and supports oxygen handling within muscle**

- **A. Incorrect:** Muscle glycogen glucose polymer hai. Iron glucose reserve ka structural unit nahi hai.
- **B. Correct:** Myoglobin muscle cells mein oxygen bind karta hai aur muscle metabolism support karta hai. Iron ka role red blood cells tak limited nahi hai.
- **C. Incorrect:** ATP energy transfer karta hai. Iron enzymes aur oxygen-binding proteins mein important hai, ATP ka direct substitute nahi hai.
- **D. Incorrect:** Muscle energy pathways different conditions mein work karte hain. Iron-dependent oxygen handling ka matlab oxygen requirement eliminate hona nahi hai.

</details>

### Q15. In a healthy adult, why is there no major regulated pathway that rapidly removes large amounts of excess body iron?

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Priority:** Medium | **Topic:** Iron nutrition | **Type:** Original practice

- **A.** Iron balance is controlled mainly through absorption and recycling, with limited physiological excretion
- **B.** The kidneys excrete all absorbed iron freely each day
- **C.** All stored iron is converted into dietary fibre
- **D.** The lungs remove all excess iron during ordinary breathing

<details><summary>Answer aur detailed explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: A. Iron balance is controlled mainly through absorption and recycling, with limited physiological excretion**

- **A. Correct:** Body iron ko extensively recycle karti hai aur intestinal absorption regulate karti hai. Active large-scale excretion limited hai, isliye unnecessary supplementation se accumulation risk ho sakta hai.
- **B. Incorrect:** Normal iron largely proteins se bound hota hai aur freely filtered large daily loss expected nahi hai. Iron regulation primarily absorption par depend karti hai.
- **C. Incorrect:** Iron chemical element hai aur fibre carbohydrate-related material hai. Body iron ko fibre mein convert nahi karti.
- **D. Incorrect:** Respiration gases exchange karti hai. Iron balance ke liye exhalation major regulated removal route nahi hai.

</details>

<details><summary>Answer aur detailed explanation dekho</summary>

- **C. Incorrect:** Outcome improvement household programme coverage directly measure nahi karti. Coverage survey separate chahiye.
- **D. Incorrect:** Iron stores aur Hb different measures hain. Population ya individual context ke according additional evaluation useful ho sakti hai.

</details>

### Q18. Which monitoring finding can indicate an early marrow response after effective treatment of iron deficiency anaemia?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Priority:** Medium | **Topic:** Iron nutrition | **Type:** Original practice

- **A.** An immediate normal haemoglobin within minutes
- **B.** An appropriate rise in reticulocyte production
- **C.** A permanent fall in all reticulocytes to zero
- **D.** An isolated increase in serum sodium

<details><summary>Answer aur detailed explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: B. An appropriate rise in reticulocyte production**

- **A. Incorrect:** New red cells banne mein time lagta hai. Oral iron minutes mein haemoglobin normalize nahi karta.
- **B. Correct:** Reticulocytes newly released red cells hain. Effective iron therapy ke baad marrow response haemoglobin recovery se pehle appear ho sakta hai.
- **C. Incorrect:** Effective marrow response mein new red cell production expected hai. Zero reticulocytes response nahi dikhate.
- **D. Incorrect:** Sodium fluid balance se related hai. Marrow response ka indicator reticulocyte count hai.

</details>

### Q19. A patient with diagnosed hereditary haemochromatosis plans to buy a multivitamin containing iron for general wellbeing. What should the nurse advise?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Priority:** High | **Topic:** Iron nutrition | **Type:** Original practice

- **A.** Review the product with the specialist and avoid unnecessary iron-containing supplements
- **B.** Start the iron supplement because everyone requires the same additional dose
- **C.** Remove all foods containing any iron permanently
- **D.** Use high-dose vitamin C supplements to ensure more iron absorption

<details><summary>Answer aur detailed explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: A. Review the product with the specialist and avoid unnecessary iron-containing supplements**

- **A. Correct:** Haemochromatosis mein excessive iron accumulation ka concern hota hai. Bina indication extra iron harmful ho sakta hai, isliye supplement list treatment team se review karein.
- **B. Incorrect:** Dietary iron essential hai, lekin extra supplement sabke liye necessary nahi hai. Iron overload mein individualized advice particularly important hai.
- **C. Incorrect:** Extreme dietary restriction generally required nahi hoti aur nutritional adequacy harm ho sakti hai. Specialist treatment aur balanced diet important hain.
- **D. Incorrect:** Vitamin C iron absorption enhance kar sakta hai. Iron overload context mein unsupervised high-dose supplementation appropriate nahi hai.

</details>

### Q20. In an infant beginning complementary feeding at the appropriate age, which food-planning principle best supports iron nutrition?

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Priority:** High | **Topic:** Iron nutrition | **Type:** Original practice

- **A.** Use only diluted fruit juice as the main complementary food
- **B.** Delay all iron-containing foods until school age
- **C.** Include developmentally suitable iron-rich complementary foods while continuing appropriate milk feeding
- **D.** Replace all meals with unprescribed adult iron tablets

<details><summary>Answer aur detailed explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: C. Include developmentally suitable iron-rich complementary foods while continuing appropriate milk feeding**

- **A. Incorrect:** Juice iron aur other nutrients ka adequate source nahi hai. Dilution nutrient density further reduce kar sakti hai.
- **B. Incorrect:** Iron growth aur neurodevelopment ke liye important hai. Appropriate complementary foods ko unnecessary delay nahi karna chahiye.
- **C. Correct:** Growth ke saath infant ki iron needs badhti hain. Safe texture wale iron-rich foods aur age-appropriate milk feeding together nutrient needs support karte hain.
- **D. Incorrect:** Adult tablets infants ke liye dose aur choking/toxicity risk create karte hain. Supplements sirf age-specific clinical guidance se dene chahiye.

</details>
