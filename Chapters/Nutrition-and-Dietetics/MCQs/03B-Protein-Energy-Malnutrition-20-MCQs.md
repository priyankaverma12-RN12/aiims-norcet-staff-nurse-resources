### Q1. A child with prolonged inadequate dietary energy has marked wasting of muscles and subcutaneous fat but no nutritional oedema. Which classical clinical description is most consistent?

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Priority:** High | **Topic:** Protein-energy malnutrition | **Type:** Original practice

- **A.** Kwashiorkor with nutritional oedema
- **B.** Isolated iron deficiency anaemia
- **C.** Uncomplicated overweight
- **D.** Marasmus

<details><summary>Answer aur detailed explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: D. Marasmus**

- **A. Incorrect:** Kwashiorkor ka classical defining feature bilateral nutritional oedema hai. Given child mein oedema absent hai.
- **B. Incorrect:** Iron deficiency pallor aur anaemia cause kar sakti hai, lekin severe generalized tissue wasting ko akela explain nahi karti.
- **C. Incorrect:** Marked muscle aur fat depletion overweight phenotype ko support nahi karti.
- **D. Correct:** Marasmus classical severe energy deficiency phenotype hai jisme body fat aur muscle waste ho sakte hain. Current classification anthropometry aur clinical assessment se karein.

</details>

- **B. Incorrect:** Classical marasmus mein severe wasting hoti hai without nutritional oedema. Bilateral oedema is distinction ko change karti hai.
- **C. Incorrect:** Constitutional thinness alone oedema aur dermatosis ka explanation nahi hai. Clinical assessment required hai.
- **D. Incorrect:** Fluorosis enamel ya skeleton affect karti hai. Nutritional oedema aur dermatosis iska typical pattern nahi.

</details>

### Q3. Why can the weight of a child with nutritional oedema underestimate the severity of tissue depletion?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Priority:** Medium | **Topic:** Protein-energy malnutrition | **Type:** Original practice

- **A.** Oedema directly increases skeletal muscle protein
- **B.** Retained extracellular fluid increases measured body weight
- **C.** Water retention always reduces measured body weight
- **D.** Weight automatically corrects itself for oedema

<details><summary>Answer aur detailed explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: B. Retained extracellular fluid increases measured body weight**

- **A. Incorrect:** Fluid accumulation new tissue protein synthesis ka proof nahi hai. Muscle depletion simultaneously possible hai.
- **B. Correct:** Oedema extra fluid add karti hai, jabki muscle aur fat stores depleted ho sakte hain. Weight alone se apparent nutritional status falsely reassuring ho sakta hai.
- **C. Incorrect:** Retained fluid normally scale weight badhata hai. Isliye true tissue deficit mask ho sakta hai.
- **D. Incorrect:** Scale total mass measure karta hai, tissue aur fluid separate nahi karta. Clinical oedema assessment essential hai.

</details>

### Q4. Which explanation best reflects current understanding of nutritional oedema in severe malnutrition?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Priority:** Medium | **Topic:** Protein-energy malnutrition | **Type:** Original practice

- **A.** It is caused exclusively by excessive water drinking in every child
- **B.** It always proves primary kidney failure
- **C.** It is multifactorial and should not be attributed solely to isolated dietary protein deficiency
- **D.** It is unrelated to systemic metabolic changes

<details><summary>Answer aur detailed explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: C. It is multifactorial and should not be attributed solely to isolated dietary protein deficiency**

- **A. Incorrect:** Water intake alone nutritional oedema ka universal mechanism nahi hai. Clinical and nutritional factors assess karne chahiye.
- **B. Incorrect:** Renal disease differential mein ho sakti hai, lekin oedema alone primary renal diagnosis establish nahi karti.
- **C. Correct:** Nutritional oedema mein altered metabolism, inflammation aur fluid regulation contribute kar sakte hain. Sirf protein deficiency ka simplistic explanation adequate nahi hai.
- **D. Incorrect:** Severe malnutrition systemic adaptation produce karti hai. Oedema ko isolated skin finding nahi samajhna chahiye.

</details>

<details><summary>Answer aur detailed explanation dekho</summary>

- **C. Incorrect:** Severe malnutrition mein infection signs muted ho sakte hain. Hypothermia se infection exclude nahi hoti.
- **D. Incorrect:** Cold fluid administration temperature problem worsen kar sakti hai. Fluid management specialist protocol guide karta hai.

</details>

### Q13. A child with severe wasting and diarrhoea has suspected dehydration. Which management principle is safest?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Priority:** High | **Topic:** Protein-energy malnutrition | **Type:** Original practice

- **A.** Automatically apply large rapid fluid boluses to every child with diarrhoea
- **B.** Avoid all fluids until nutritional recovery is complete
- **C.** Assess dehydration only from skin turgor and ignore circulation
- **D.** Use the severe-malnutrition-specific assessment and rehydration protocol with close monitoring

<details><summary>Answer aur detailed explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: D. Use the severe-malnutrition-specific assessment and rehydration protocol with close monitoring**

- **A. Incorrect:** Severe malnutrition mein fluid management carefully individualized hoti hai. Shock assessment aur protocol ke bina blanket boluses unsafe hain.
- **B. Incorrect:** Dehydration urgent complication ho sakti hai. Appropriate rehydration delay nahi karni chahiye.
- **C. Incorrect:** Skin turgor wasting se affected ho sakta hai. Circulation, history aur clinical findings together interpret karein.
- **D. Correct:** Wasting aur oedema traditional dehydration signs distort kar sakte hain. Fluid overload aur electrolyte risk ke karan specialized clinical plan important hai.

</details>

### Q17. A young child repeatedly develops wasting following diarrhoeal illnesses. Which explanation best describes the malnutrition-infection cycle?

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Priority:** Medium | **Topic:** Protein-energy malnutrition | **Type:** Original practice

- **C.** Illness can reduce intake and increase losses, while malnutrition increases vulnerability to infection

<details><summary>Answer aur detailed explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: C. Illness can reduce intake and increase losses, while malnutrition increases vulnerability to infection**

- **A. Incorrect:** Intestinal illness absorption impair kar sakti hai. Increased absorption universal response nahi.
- **B. Incorrect:** Immune defenses compromise ho sakti hain. Protection assume karna unsafe hai.
- **C. Correct:** Diarrhoea nutrient loss aur reduced appetite create kar sakti hai. Malnutrition immune function impair karke further infection risk badha sakti hai, isliye both factors address karein.
- **D. Incorrect:** Safe water, sanitation aur infection prevention repeated illness risk reduce kar sakte hain. Nutrition programme integrated hona chahiye.

</details>
