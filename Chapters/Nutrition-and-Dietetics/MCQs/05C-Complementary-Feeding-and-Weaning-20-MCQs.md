### Q1. A healthy term breastfed infant reaches 180 days of age. What feeding transition does WHO 2023 recommend?

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Priority:** High | **Topic:** Complementary feeding and weaning | **Type:** Original practice

- **A.** Replace every breastfeed immediately with solid meals
- **B.** Continue only breast milk until the second birthday
- **C.** Begin a diet consisting only of fruit juice
- **D.** Begin suitable complementary foods while continuing breastfeeding

<details><summary>Answer aur detailed explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: D. Begin suitable complementary foods while continuing breastfeeding**

- **A. Incorrect:** Complementary feeding breast milk ke saath hoti hai. Sudden replacement recommended nahi.
- **B. Incorrect:** Six months ke baad additional food ki need hoti hai. Exclusive feeding unnecessarily extend nahi karni chahiye.
- **C. Incorrect:** Juice adequate energy, protein aur micronutrients provide nahi karta.
- **D. Correct:** Six months par milk ke saath additional food nutrient needs support karta hai. Breastfeeding stop karna transition ka goal nahi.

</details>

**Correct: C. Recognize fullness cues and pause while continuing supportive feeding opportunities**
