### Q17. A clinically rehydrated child on a supervised persistent-diarrhoea diet weighs 8.0 kg at the start of a three-day assessment and 8.15 kg at the end. Fluid status is comparable. Stool frequency has fallen and appetite and activity have improved. Using the starting weight, how does average weight gain compare with the F-IMNCI 2023 Box 6.6 threshold?

**Difficulty:** 🔴 Difficult | **Priority:** Exam relevance provisional | **Topic:** Weight response criterion | **Type:** Original practice

- **A.** About 2.0 g/kg/day, below the marker
- **B.** 50 g/kg/day, exceeding the marker
- **C.** 18.75 g/kg/day, so no further clinical review is required
- **D.** About 6.25 g/kg/day, exceeding the stated greater-than-5 g/kg/day response marker

<details><summary>Answer aur detailed explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: D. About 6.25 g/kg/day, exceeding the stated greater-than-5 g/kg/day response marker**

- **A. Incorrect:** 150 g ko 24 kg-days se divide karna hai, not an arbitrary denominator. Correct result 6.25 hai.
- **B. Incorrect:** 50 g average gain per day hai. Per-kg value ke liye body weight se bhi divide karna zaroori hai.
- **C. Incorrect:** 18.75 g/kg poore 3-day interval ka value hai, daily rate nahi. Marker meet hone se ongoing assessment automatically end nahi hoti.
