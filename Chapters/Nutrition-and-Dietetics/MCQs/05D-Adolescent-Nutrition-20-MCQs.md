### Q1. According to WHO, which age range defines adolescence for nutrition programme planning?

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Priority:** High | **Topic:** Adolescent nutrition | **Type:** Original practice

- **A.** 5–9 years
- **B.** 15–24 years
- **C.** 20–29 years
- **D.** 10–19 years

<details><summary>Answer aur detailed explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: D. 10–19 years**

- **A. Incorrect:** Yeh school-age childhood ka earlier period hai, WHO adolescent range nahi.
- **B. Incorrect:** Yeh commonly used youth age range hai, adolescent definition nahi.
- **C. Incorrect:** Yeh adult age range hai. Adolescent programme definition different hai.
- **D. Correct:** WHO adolescence ko 10 se 19 years define karta hai. Is stage mein puberty, growth aur social changes nutrition needs affect karte hain.

</details>

### Q2. A 13-year-old and a 17-year-old have different pubertal stages despite similar body weights. Why should a nurse avoid prescribing identical nutrient portions solely by weight?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Priority:** Medium | **Topic:** Adolescent nutrition | **Type:** Original practice

- **A.** Growth velocity, maturation, activity and sex-specific requirements can differ
- **B.** Body weight alone determines all developmental nutrient needs
- **C.** Pubertal growth stops in every child at 13 years
- **D.** Food requirements depend only on school class

<details><summary>Answer aur detailed explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: A. Growth velocity, maturation, activity and sex-specific requirements can differ**

- **A. Correct:** Adolescence mein pubertal timing aur growth demand variable hoti hai. Same weight ka matlab identical nutrient requirements nahi.
- **B. Incorrect:** Weight useful hai but growth stage aur activity bhi matter karte hain.
- **C. Incorrect:** Puberty timing individual differ karti hai. Fixed age assumption incorrect hai.
- **D. Incorrect:** School grade physiological maturity establish nahi karta.

</details>

- **D.** Calcium prevents all sports injuries regardless of intake

<details><summary>Answer aur detailed explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: C. Adolescence is an important period of bone mineral accrual**

</details>

### Q4. A menstruating adolescent reports prolonged heavy bleeding and declining exercise tolerance. Which nutrition-related evaluation is most relevant?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Priority:** High | **Topic:** Adolescent nutrition | **Type:** Original practice

- **A.** Assume the symptoms reflect calcium deficiency alone
- **B.** Assess iron status and evaluate the bleeding with the clinical team
- **C.** Recommend only additional dietary salt
- **D.** Wait until adolescence is over before investigating

<details><summary>Answer aur detailed explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: B. Assess iron status and evaluate the bleeding with the clinical team**

- **A. Incorrect:** Calcium deficiency heavy menstrual blood loss-related iron risk explain nahi karti.
- **B. Correct:** Heavy menstrual loss iron depletion ka risk badhata hai. Fatigue aur reduced exercise tolerance anaemia se associated ho sakte hain, so cause and labs assess karein.
- **C. Incorrect:** Salt iron stores restore nahi karta. Underlying bleeding assessment needed hai.
- **D. Incorrect:** Ongoing blood loss timely evaluation demand karti hai.

</details>

### Q5. A school nurse uses the WHO 5–19 year BMI-for-age reference. An adolescent has a z score of −2.4. Which classification is appropriate?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Priority:** High | **Topic:** Adolescent nutrition | **Type:** Original practice

- **A.** Thinness
- **B.** Severe thinness because every value below −2 is severe
- **C.** Overweight
- **D.** Normal status based on a fixed adult BMI cut-off

<details><summary>Answer aur detailed explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: A. Thinness**

- **A. Correct:** WHO 5–19 reference mein BMI-for-age below −2 SD thinness hai. −2.4 is threshold se neeche hai. Clinical cause independently assess karein.
- **B. Incorrect:** Severe thinness below −3 SD define hoti hai, −2.4 us category mein nahi.
- **C. Incorrect:** Overweight positive z score threshold se related hai, negative −2.4 se nahi.
- **D. Incorrect:** Age and sex-specific reference adolescence mein appropriate hai. Adult cut-off automatically use nahi karna chahiye.

</details>

- **D.** Severe thinness

</details>

### Q20. A district reports anaemia prevalence falling among adolescents attending school but has no data on adolescents outside school. What is the key limitation?

- **D.** Out-of-school adolescents are excluded from all nutrition policy concerns

<details><summary>Answer aur detailed explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: A. The result cannot establish equivalent improvement in out-of-school adolescents**

- **A. Correct:** School sample coverage specific population tak limited hai. Out-of-school adolescents ki access and outcomes separately assess karni chahiye.
- **B. Incorrect:** Unmeasured populations automatically represented nahi hoti. Sampling and coverage matter karte hain.
- **C. Incorrect:** Anaemia multiple causes se affected hai. Outcome improvement alone cause establish nahi karta.
- **D. Incorrect:** Out-of-school groups may have greater access barriers. Appropriate outreach important hai.
