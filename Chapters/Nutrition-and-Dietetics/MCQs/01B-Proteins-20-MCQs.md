### Q1. A prescribed diet provides 75 g of protein daily. Approximately how much energy does the protein contribute?

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Priority:** Medium | **Topic:** Proteins | **Type:** Original practice

- **A.** 675 kcal
- **B.** 75 kcal
- **C.** 300 kcal
- **D.** 150 kcal

<details><summary>Answer aur detailed explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: C. 300 kcal**

- **A. Incorrect:** Yeh 9 kcal per gram ka fat wala conversion hai, protein ka nahi.
- **B. Incorrect:** Gram ko seedha kcal maan lena galat conversion hai.
- **C. Correct:** Protein ka standard energy factor 4 kcal per gram hai. Isliye 75 ko 4 se multiply karne par 300 kcal milti hain.
- **D. Incorrect:** Yeh 2 kcal per gram ka calculation hai, jo protein ke standard factor se match nahi karta.

</details>

### Q2. Why must essential amino acids be supplied by food?

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Priority:** High | **Topic:** Proteins | **Type:** Original practice

- **A.** The body cannot synthesize them in adequate amounts to meet requirements
- **B.** They cannot be absorbed from the intestine
- **C.** They are used only to make glucose
- **D.** They are stored in a separate permanent amino acid depot

<details><summary>Answer aur detailed explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: A. The body cannot synthesize them in adequate amounts to meet requirements**

- **A. Correct:** Body kuch amino acids apne aap bana leti hai, lekin essential amino acids ki sufficient synthesis nahi ho pati. Isliye diet se supply zaroori hai.
- **B. Incorrect:** Amino acids intestinal absorption ke baad blood mein pahunch sakte hain. Essential hone ka reason absorption failure nahi hai.
- **C. Incorrect:** Amino acids tissue proteins, enzymes aur doosre molecules banate hain. Glucose formation unka only role nahi hai.
- **D. Incorrect:** Body mein dedicated permanent amino acid store nahi hota. Tissue proteins functional roles perform karte hain aur turnover hota rehta hai.

</details>

- **A. Incorrect:** Sugar amino acids provide nahi karti, isliye cereal protein ki limiting amino acid ko complement nahi karti.

- **C. Incorrect:** Tea aur salt se adequate amino acids nahi milte, isliye protein quality improve nahi hoti.

</details>

### Q4. Which process begins substantial digestion of dietary protein in the stomach?

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Priority:** High | **Topic:** Proteins | **Type:** Original practice

- **A.** Lactase activity
- **B.** Acid-assisted denaturation and pepsin activity
- **C.** Bile emulsification
- **D.** Salivary amylase activity

<details><summary>Answer aur detailed explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: B. Acid-assisted denaturation and pepsin activity**

- **A. Incorrect:** Lactase lactose sugar todta hai aur small intestine mein kaam karta hai.
- **B. Correct:** Stomach acid protein ki folded structure ko open karta hai aur pepsin ko active environment deta hai. Pepsin peptide bonds todkar chhote fragments banata hai.
- **C. Incorrect:** Bile fat droplets disperse karti hai. Protein denaturation aur digestion mein pepsin ka role alag hai.
- **D. Incorrect:** Amylase starch par kaam karta hai, peptide bonds par nahi.

</details>

### Q5. A pancreatic enzyme deficiency reduces the breakdown of proteins in the small intestine. Which enzyme class is most directly involved?

- **A.** Amylases
- **B.** Lipases
- **C.** Disaccharidases
- **D.** Proteases

<details><summary>Answer aur detailed explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: D. Proteases**

- **A. Incorrect:** Amylase starch digestion mein help karta hai. Protein digestion mein yeh main enzyme class nahi hai.
- **B. Incorrect:** Lipase triglycerides ko todta hai, peptide bonds ko nahi.
- **C. Incorrect:** Disaccharidases double sugars ko smaller units mein convert karte hain, protein fragments ko nahi.
- **D. Correct:** Trypsin jaise pancreatic proteases protein fragments ke peptide bonds todte hain. Isliye deficiency mein protein digestion impair ho sakta hai.

</details>

### Q6. A dietitian describes a patient as being in positive nitrogen balance. What does this mean?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Priority:** High | **Topic:** Proteins | **Type:** Original practice

- **A.** Nitrogen loss exceeds nitrogen intake
- **B.** Nitrogen intake exceeds nitrogen loss
- **C.** Nitrogen intake and loss are equal
- **D.** All ingested nitrogen is excreted immediately

<details><summary>Answer aur detailed explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: B. Nitrogen intake exceeds nitrogen loss**

- **A. Incorrect:** Yeh negative nitrogen balance hai, jo net protein loss suggest karta hai.
- **B. Correct:** Jab nitrogen intake loss se zyada hota hai, toh net protein retention ka indication mil sakta hai. Growth aur recovery mein aisi state expected ho sakti hai.
- **C. Incorrect:** Equal intake aur loss approximate neutral balance dikhate hain, positive nahi.
- **D. Incorrect:** Agar retention bilkul nahi ho toh growth ya net protein gain explain nahi hoga.

</details>

### Q7. A critically ill patient has increased protein breakdown despite receiving calories. Which finding best represents a catabolic nitrogen balance?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Priority:** High | **Topic:** Proteins | **Type:** Original practice

- **A.** Nitrogen losses greater than intake
- **B.** A rise in dietary fibre alone
- **C.** Equal nitrogen intake and loss
- **D.** Urine containing no nitrogen compounds

<details><summary>Answer aur detailed explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: A. Nitrogen losses greater than intake**

- **A. Correct:** Severe stress mein muscle protein breakdown badh sakta hai. Agar nitrogen loss intake se zyada ho, toh negative balance net protein loss suggest karta hai.
- **B. Incorrect:** Fibre intake nitrogen balance define nahi karta. Nitrogen intake aur excretion assess karni hoti hai.
- **C. Incorrect:** Yeh neutral balance ka estimate hai, net nitrogen loss nahi.
- **D. Incorrect:** Urine normally urea jaise nitrogen compounds contain karta hai. Catabolism mein losses badh sakte hain, zero hona definition nahi hai.

</details>

### Q8. Why is adequate nonprotein energy important when planning nutrition for a recovering surgical patient?

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Priority:** Medium | **Topic:** Proteins | **Type:** Original practice

- **A.** It eliminates all protein requirements
- **B.** It blocks intestinal amino acid absorption
- **C.** It reduces the need to oxidize amino acids for energy
- **D.** It permanently stops nitrogen excretion

<details><summary>Answer aur detailed explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: C. It reduces the need to oxidize amino acids for energy**

- **A. Incorrect:** Tissue repair ke liye amino acids phir bhi chahiye. Energy alone protein ki structural role replace nahi karti.
- **B. Incorrect:** Nonprotein energy ka goal absorption block karna nahi, body ko energy provide karna hai.
- **C. Correct:** Jab carbohydrates aur fats se sufficient energy milti hai, toh amino acids ko tissue repair aur protein synthesis ke liye use karne ka mauka badhta hai. Yeh protein-sparing principle hai.
- **D. Incorrect:** Normal protein turnover aur nitrogen excretion continue hoti hai. Adequate energy se excessive breakdown kam ho sakta hai.

</details>

### Q9. A patient with severe burns requires specialized nutritional assessment. Which rationale best explains the need to assess increased protein requirements?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Priority:** High | **Topic:** Proteins | **Type:** Original practice

- **A.** Tissue loss and the stress response increase protein turnover and repair demands
- **B.** Burns abolish the need for amino acids
- **C.** Only water replacement can rebuild damaged tissue
- **D.** Protein should always be withheld until complete wound closure

<details><summary>Answer aur detailed explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: A. Tissue loss and the stress response increase protein turnover and repair demands**

- **A. Correct:** Burn injury se tissue damage aur metabolic stress hota hai. Protein repair aur immune function ke liye chahiye, isliye needs clinician aur dietitian individualize karte hain.
- **B. Incorrect:** Wound healing aur tissue rebuilding mein amino acids important hain. Burns mein requirement kam hone ke bajay badh sakti hai.
- **C. Incorrect:** Fluids circulation ke liye essential hain, lekin water alone structural proteins nahi bana sakta.
- **D. Incorrect:** Aisa universal delay repair ko compromise kar sakta hai. Nutrition support timing clinical stability aur plan par depend karti hai.

</details>

### Q10. Which statement about dietary protein and serum albumin is most appropriate during acute inflammation?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Priority:** High | **Topic:** Proteins | **Type:** Original practice

- **A.** A normal albumin excludes all malnutrition
- **B.** Low albumin alone does not diagnose inadequate protein intake
- **C.** Albumin measures yesterday's protein intake precisely
- **D.** Every low albumin result mandates identical protein supplementation

<details><summary>Answer aur detailed explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: B. Low albumin alone does not diagnose inadequate protein intake**

- **A. Incorrect:** Malnutrition normal albumin ke saath bhi possible hai. Single laboratory value complete assessment nahi hai.
- **B. Correct:** Inflammation, fluid shifts aur liver synthesis albumin ko affect kar sakte hain. Isliye diet history, physical findings aur clinical context ke bina nutrition diagnosis nahi karna chahiye.
- **C. Incorrect:** Albumin immediate meal intake ka direct marker nahi hai. Disease aur fluid status bhi usse affect karte hain.
- **D. Incorrect:** Cause aur organ function alag ho sakte hain. Supplementation patient-specific assessment se decide karni chahiye.

</details>

### Q13. An adult with chronic kidney disease asks whether a high-protein diet is always beneficial. Which response is best?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Priority:** High | **Topic:** Proteins | **Type:** Original practice

- **A.** Everyone with CKD must eat unrestricted high-protein meals
- **B.** Protein intake should be individualized according to disease stage, dialysis status and nutritional condition
- **C.** All patients with CKD should permanently avoid protein
- **D.** Only serum sodium determines protein needs

<details><summary>Answer aur detailed explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: B. Protein intake should be individualized according to disease stage, dialysis status and nutritional condition**

- **A. Incorrect:** Unrestricted protein har CKD patient ke liye appropriate nahi hota. Nondialysis aur dialysis situations alag hoti hain.
- **B. Correct:** CKD mein protein needs stage aur treatment se change ho sakti hain. Dialysis losses aur malnutrition risk bhi consider karne chahiye, isliye renal dietitian ka plan follow karna useful hai.
- **C. Incorrect:** Protein essential hai aur complete avoidance deficiency aur muscle loss badha sakti hai.
- **D. Incorrect:** Sodium fluid aur electrolyte context mein useful hai, lekin protein prescription decide karne ka single marker nahi hai.

</details>

### Q16. Which statement best distinguishes a complete dietary protein from an incomplete protein?

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Priority:** Medium | **Topic:** Proteins | **Type:** Original practice

- **A.** A complete protein contains no nitrogen
- **B.** An incomplete protein cannot be digested at all
- **C.** A complete protein supplies all essential amino acids in adequate proportions
- **D.** A complete protein contains only one type of amino acid

<details><summary>Answer aur detailed explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: C. A complete protein supplies all essential amino acids in adequate proportions**

- **A. Incorrect:** Proteins amino acids se bante hain jinmein nitrogen hota hai. Nitrogen-free hona completeness ki definition nahi hai.
- **B. Incorrect:** Incomplete protein digest ho sakta hai. Term amino acid pattern ke baare mein hai, absolute digestibility ke baare mein nahi.
- **C. Correct:** Protein quality mein essential amino acids ka pattern important hai. Complete protein un sabko adequate proportions mein supply karta hai, though total intake bhi matter karta hai.
- **D. Incorrect:** Body ko multiple essential amino acids chahiye. Ek amino acid wala source complete protein nahi hoga.

</details>

### Q17. Which organ converts much of the toxic ammonia generated by amino acid metabolism into urea?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Priority:** Medium | **Topic:** Proteins | **Type:** Original practice

- **A.** Gallbladder
- **B.** Spleen
- **C.** Liver
- **D.** Thyroid gland

<details><summary>Answer aur detailed explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: C. Liver**

- **A. Incorrect:** Gallbladder bile store aur concentrate karta hai, urea cycle ka main organ nahi hai.
- **B. Incorrect:** Spleen blood filtration aur immune roles perform karta hai. Ammonia detoxification ka main site liver hai.
- **C. Correct:** Amino acid nitrogen remove hone par ammonia ban sakta hai. Liver urea cycle se ise relatively less toxic urea mein convert karta hai, jise kidneys excrete kar sakti hain.
- **D. Incorrect:** Thyroid hormones metabolism regulate karte hain, lekin urea synthesis ka main organ nahi hai.

</details>

### Q18. A patient receiving adequate protein has persistent poor wound healing. Which nursing response is most appropriate?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Priority:** High | **Topic:** Proteins | **Type:** Original practice

- **A.** Double the protein dose without reviewing the clinical condition
- **B.** Conclude that nutritional status is normal solely from the protein intake
- **C.** Stop assessing the wound because nutrition has been addressed
- **D.** Assess total energy intake, wound factors, micronutrients and underlying disease rather than assuming protein alone is sufficient

<details><summary>Answer aur detailed explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: D. Assess total energy intake, wound factors, micronutrients and underlying disease rather than assuming protein alone is sufficient**

- **A. Incorrect:** Bina organ function aur nutrition plan dekhe dose badhana unsafe ho sakta hai. Protein alone har barrier resolve nahi karta.
- **B. Incorrect:** Adequate reported protein intake ke bawajood energy deficit ya absorption problem ho sakti hai.
- **C. Incorrect:** Wound assessment se infection aur perfusion jaise factors identify hote hain. Nutrition intervention monitoring ko replace nahi karta.
- **D. Correct:** Healing mein protein ke saath calories, perfusion, infection control aur micronutrients bhi chahiye. Isliye broader assessment se actual barriers samajh aate hain.

</details>

### Q20. A patient reports replacing ordinary meals with several protein supplements. Which teaching is most appropriate?

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Priority:** Medium | **Topic:** Proteins | **Type:** Original practice

- **A.** All protein supplements automatically provide a complete balanced diet
- **B.** Review the full diet and prescribed needs because protein supplements cannot replace all nutrients in a balanced diet
- **C.** Protein supplements never contribute calories
- **D.** More protein is always safer regardless of kidney or liver function

<details><summary>Answer aur detailed explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: B. Review the full diet and prescribed needs because protein supplements cannot replace all nutrients in a balanced diet**

- **A. Incorrect:** Product composition vary karti hai. Protein powder har essential nutrient provide kare, yeh assume nahi karna chahiye.
- **B. Correct:** Supplement protein gap fill kar sakta hai, lekin fibre, essential fats, micronutrients aur adequate energy bhi chahiye. Nurse actual intake aur clinical plan review karti hai.
- **C. Incorrect:** Protein khud energy provide karta hai aur product mein other macronutrients bhi ho sakte hain.
- **D. Incorrect:** Organ function aur medical condition protein planning ko affect karte hain. Isliye excess ko automatically safe nahi keh sakte.

</details>
