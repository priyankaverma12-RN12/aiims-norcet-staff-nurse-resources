### Q1. A patient with prolonged inadequate nutrition has impaired taste, slow wound healing and recurrent infections. Which trace mineral deserves specific assessment?

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Priority:** High | **Topic:** Zinc and trace minerals | **Type:** Original practice

- **A.** Iodine
- **B.** Fluoride
- **C.** Zinc
- **D.** Molybdenum

<details><summary>Answer aur detailed explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: C. Zinc**

- **A. Incorrect:** Iodine thyroid hormones ka structural part hai. Is symptom combination mein zinc-related functions more directly relevant hain.
- **B. Incorrect:** Fluoride dental mineralization aur caries prevention se related hai. Taste aur wound healing ka combined pattern fluoride deficiency ka typical clue nahi hai.
- **C. Correct:** Zinc protein aur DNA synthesis, taste aur immune function mein important hai. In findings se zinc deficiency possible hai, lekin clinical assessment se other causes bhi evaluate karne chahiye.
- **D. Incorrect:** Molybdenum specific enzymes ka cofactor hai. Is common nutritional presentation mein zinc deficiency zyada relevant consideration hai.

</details>

### Q2. Which effect of zinc most directly supports tissue repair after injury?

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Priority:** High | **Topic:** Zinc and trace minerals | **Type:** Original practice

- **A.** Participation in enzymes involved in DNA and protein synthesis and cellular proliferation
- **B.** Direct replacement of every essential amino acid
- **C.** Exclusive stimulation of red-cell haemoglobin synthesis
- **D.** Permanent elimination of all wound bacteria

<details><summary>Answer aur detailed explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: A. Participation in enzymes involved in DNA and protein synthesis and cellular proliferation**

- **A. Correct:** Wound repair ke liye naye cells aur proteins banne chahiye. Zinc multiple enzymes aur cellular processes support karta hai, lekin healing ke liye energy aur other nutrients bhi important hain.
- **B. Incorrect:** Zinc mineral hai, amino acid source nahi hai. Tissue repair mein adequate protein independently chahiye.
- **C. Incorrect:** Iron haemoglobin structure ka essential component hai. Zinc ki wound-healing role broader cellular synthesis aur repair se related hai.
- **D. Incorrect:** Zinc immune function support karta hai, lekin wound infection automatically eradicate nahi karta. Infection prevention aur indicated treatment zaroori hain.

</details>

### Q3. A patient self-prescribes high-dose zinc for several months and develops anaemia and neutropenia. Which secondary mineral deficiency is an important concern?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Priority:** High | **Topic:** Zinc and trace minerals | **Type:** Original practice

- **A.** Copper
- **B.** Iodine
- **C.** Fluoride
- **D.** Selenium

<details><summary>Answer aur detailed explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: A. Copper**

- **A. Correct:** Excess zinc intestinal metallothionein increase kar sakta hai, jo copper ko bind karke absorption reduce karta hai. Copper deficiency anaemia aur neutropenia cause kar sakti hai.
- **B. Incorrect:** Excess zinc ka classic mineral interaction copper ke saath hai. Iodine deficiency thyroid function affect karti hai.
- **C. Incorrect:** Fluoride dental exposure se related hai. Zinc-induced anaemia aur neutropenia ka typical mechanism fluoride depletion nahi hai.
- **D. Incorrect:** Selenium antioxidant enzymes mein role rakhta hai. Chronic excess zinc ka established secondary deficiency risk copper hai.

</details>

### Q4. A patient taking a tetracycline antibiotic also uses a zinc supplement. Which instruction is most appropriate?

- **B.** Check the prescribed separation interval because zinc can reduce antibiotic absorption
- **C.** Stop the antibiotic and continue zinc as an antimicrobial substitute
- **D.** Double the antibiotic dose whenever zinc is taken

<details><summary>Answer aur detailed explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: B. Check the prescribed separation interval because zinc can reduce antibiotic absorption**

- **A. Incorrect:** Zinc interaction antibiotic absorption reduce kar sakta hai. Simultaneous administration always beneficial nahi hai.
- **B. Correct:** Mineral aur antibiotic gut mein complex bana sakte hain. Isliye product-specific timing pharmacist ya prescriber se confirm karna useful hai.
- **C. Incorrect:** Zinc nutritional support de sakta hai, lekin prescribed antibiotic ka substitute nahi hai. Infection treatment clinician guide karta hai.
- **D. Incorrect:** Self-directed dose changes adverse effects aur treatment failure ka risk create karte hain. Timing adjustment appropriate review se honi chahiye.

</details>

### Q5. Which dietary pattern is most likely to reduce zinc bioavailability despite appreciable total zinc intake?

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Priority:** Medium | **Topic:** Zinc and trace minerals | **Type:** Original practice

- **A.** A varied diet containing animal protein and low-phytate foods
- **B.** A diet dominated by high-phytate unprocessed cereals with little dietary variety
- **C.** A meal containing an appropriate zinc-fortified food
- **D.** A mixed meal with appropriately processed pulses and other zinc sources

<details><summary>Answer aur detailed explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: B. A diet dominated by high-phytate unprocessed cereals with little dietary variety**

- **A. Incorrect:** Animal sources aur lower phytate pattern zinc absorption support kar sakte hain. Yeh reduced bioavailability ka most likely pattern nahi hai.
- **B. Correct:** Phytate zinc bind karke absorbable fraction reduce kar sakta hai. Food processing, variety aur total intake ka combined effect assess karna chahiye.
- **C. Incorrect:** Fortification zinc supply badha sakti hai. Actual absorption food matrix par depend karti hai, lekin fortification itself high phytate pattern nahi hai.
- **D. Incorrect:** Soaking ya fermentation kuch phytate reduce kar sakti hai. Varied food sources adequacy support karte hain.

</details>

### Q6. A child with severe zinc malabsorption develops periorificial and acral dermatitis, alopecia and diarrhoea. Which inherited disorder should be considered?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Priority:** High | **Topic:** Zinc and trace minerals | **Type:** Original practice

- **A.** Phenylketonuria
- **B.** Galactosaemia
- **C.** Hereditary haemochromatosis
- **D.** Acrodermatitis enteropathica

<details><summary>Answer aur detailed explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: D. Acrodermatitis enteropathica**

- **A. Incorrect:** Phenylketonuria amino acid metabolism disorder hai. Is triad ka classic inherited zinc-related diagnosis nahi hai.
- **B. Incorrect:** Galactosaemia galactose metabolism disturb karti hai aur infants mein liver ya infection complications de sakti hai. Is dermatitis pattern ka best explanation zinc malabsorption hai.
- **C. Incorrect:** Haemochromatosis iron accumulation se related hai. Zinc malabsorption aur periorificial dermatitis is disorder ki classic triad nahi hai.
- **D. Correct:** Inherited intestinal zinc transport defect se characteristic skin lesions, diarrhoea aur alopecia ho sakte hain. Prompt clinical confirmation aur specialist treatment chahiye.

</details>

### Q7. A child with acute diarrhoea is prescribed oral zinc. Which statement best explains its role?

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Priority:** High | **Topic:** Zinc and trace minerals | **Type:** Original practice

- **A.** Zinc alone replaces all oral rehydration solution
- **B.** Zinc makes dehydration assessment unnecessary
- **C.** Zinc requires stopping breastfeeding throughout the illness
- **D.** Zinc is an adjunct to appropriate rehydration and continued feeding under the current local protocol

<details><summary>Answer aur detailed explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: D. Zinc is an adjunct to appropriate rehydration and continued feeding under the current local protocol**

- **A. Incorrect:** Zinc water aur electrolyte losses replace nahi karta. ORS aur dehydration assessment ka role separate hai.
- **B. Incorrect:** Diarrhoea mein dehydration rapidly develop ho sakti hai. Zinc dene ke baad bhi assessment aur follow-up chahiye.
- **C. Incorrect:** Continued breastfeeding hydration aur nutrients support karti hai. Routine zinc therapy breastfeeding band karne ka reason nahi hai.
- **D. Correct:** Zinc diarrhoea management mein helpful adjunct ho sakta hai. Dehydration ko ORS ya indicated fluids se treat karna aur feeding continue karna essential hai. Dose current protocol ke according verify karein.

</details>

### Q8. A patient receives prolonged parenteral nutrition without appropriate trace-element review. Which action is most appropriate?

<details><summary>Answer aur detailed explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: B. Review the formulation and monitor trace-element provision according to clinical status**

- **C. Incorrect:** Unapproved additives se contamination, incompatibility aur overdose ho sakti hai. Sterile pharmacy preparation aur prescription zaroori hai.
- **D. Incorrect:** Height short-term trace-element status identify nahi karti. Clinical findings, formulation aur relevant labs more informative hain.

</details>

### Q9. Which copper-containing protein contributes to iron mobilization through its ferroxidase activity?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Priority:** Medium | **Topic:** Zinc and trace minerals | **Type:** Original practice

- **A.** Haemoglobin
- **B.** Ferritin
- **C.** Ceruloplasmin
- **D.** Albumin

<details><summary>Answer aur detailed explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: C. Ceruloplasmin**

- **A. Incorrect:** Haemoglobin oxygen carry karta hai aur iron contain karta hai, lekin copper-dependent circulating ferroxidase ceruloplasmin hai.
- **B. Incorrect:** Ferritin intracellular iron storage protein hai. Ceruloplasmin ka plasma ferroxidase role isse different hai.
- **C. Correct:** Ceruloplasmin ferrous iron ko ferric form mein oxidize karne mein help karta hai, jisse transferrin binding facilitate ho sakti hai. Copper deficiency iron handling disturb kar sakti hai.
- **D. Incorrect:** Albumin transport aur oncotic pressure mein important hai, lekin specified copper-dependent ferroxidase activity ka principal protein nahi hai.

</details>

### Q11. A patient with Wilson disease is prescribed a specialist treatment plan. What is the underlying trace-mineral problem?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Priority:** High | **Topic:** Zinc and trace minerals | **Type:** Original practice

- **A.** Pathological copper accumulation due to impaired copper handling
- **B.** A primary inability to absorb iron
- **C.** Excess fluoride deposition as the main cause of liver injury
- **D.** Failure to synthesize iodine from dietary protein

<details><summary>Answer aur detailed explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: A. Pathological copper accumulation due to impaired copper handling**

- **A. Correct:** Wilson disease mein ATP7B-related copper excretion defect se copper liver aur other tissues mein accumulate kar sakta hai. Dietary aur drug management specialist guide karta hai.
- **B. Incorrect:** Iron malabsorption alag nutritional problem hai. Wilson disease copper handling ka inherited disorder hai.
- **C. Incorrect:** Fluoride excess dental aur skeletal changes cause kar sakta hai, lekin Wilson disease copper accumulation se related hai.
- **D. Incorrect:** Iodine element hai aur body protein se synthesize nahi karti. Wilson disease ka mechanism isse unrelated hai.

</details>

### Q20. A patient with severe cholestatic liver disease is receiving prolonged parenteral nutrition. Which concern warrants specialist review of trace-element dosing?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Priority:** High | **Topic:** Zinc and trace minerals | **Type:** Original practice

- **A.** Automatic loss of all manganese through urine requiring unrestricted replacement
- **B.** Possible accumulation of trace elements excreted through bile, such as manganese
- **C.** Guaranteed deficiency of every trace element despite normal provision
- **D.** Permanent discontinuation of all parenteral nutrients without assessment

<details><summary>Answer aur detailed explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: B. Possible accumulation of trace elements excreted through bile, such as manganese**

- **A. Incorrect:** Manganese ka major excretion route bile hai. Cholestasis mein routine unrestricted replacement accumulation risk increase kar sakti hai.
- **B. Correct:** Manganese biliary excretion se eliminate hota hai. Cholestasis mein accumulation aur neurological toxicity risk ho sakta hai, isliye individualized review zaroori hai.
- **C. Incorrect:** Different elements ke absorption, excretion aur losses different hote hain. Clinical status aur formulation dekhkar specific decisions lene chahiye.
- **D. Incorrect:** Parenteral nutrition essential ho sakti hai. Trace-element formulation ko specialist adjust karte hain, complete nutrition abruptly stop nahi karna chahiye.

</details>
