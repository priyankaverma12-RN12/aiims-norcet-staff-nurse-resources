### Q1. Two proposed fortification vehicles are consumed by similar numbers of adults, but only one is commonly consumed in the target low-income child population and has an accessible industrial supply chain. Which evidence most directly supports the vehicle choice?

**Difficulty:** 🟡 Medium | **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD | **Topic:** Vehicle selection | **Type:** Original practice

- **A.** Consumption distribution in the target group and the fraction that can be reliably fortified
- **B.** The highest retail price among the two foods
- **C.** The total number of food advertisements
- **D.** The nutrient concentration of the premix alone

<details><summary>Answer aur detailed explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: A. Consumption distribution in the target group and the fraction that can be reliably fortified**

- **A. Correct:** Sirf total adult consumers count karna enough nahi hai. Target children ka intake pattern aur reliable fortified supply assess karna chahiye, taaki chosen vehicle deficiency-control objective tak pahunch sake.
- **B. Incorrect:** Price affordability ko affect karta hai, par high price se target group tak delivery prove nahi hoti. Actual access aur consumption dekhna zaroori hai.
- **C. Incorrect:** Advertisements awareness ka clue de sakte hain. Actual quantity consumed aur industrial fortification feasibility alag evidence maangti hain.
- **D. Incorrect:** Premix concentration formulation ka part hai. Vehicle kitna khaya jaata hai aur kitna reliably fortified hai, iske bina population exposure estimate nahi hota.

**Exam clue:** Target group ka actual consumption aur fortifiable supply fraction check karo.
