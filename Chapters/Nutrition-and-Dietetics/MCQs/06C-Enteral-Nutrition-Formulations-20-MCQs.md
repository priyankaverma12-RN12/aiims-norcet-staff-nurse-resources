### Q1. Which nitrogen source characterizes a standard polymeric enteral formula?

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Priority:** High | **Topic:** Enteral formulations | **Type:** Original practice

- **A.** Only free amino acids
- **B.** Only short peptides
- **C.** Intact dietary proteins
- **D.** Only non-protein nitrogen with no essential amino acid contribution

<details><summary>Answer aur detailed explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: C. Intact dietary proteins**

- **A. Incorrect:** Free amino acid nitrogen elemental formula ki characteristic hai, polymeric ki nahi.
- **B. Incorrect:** Peptides semi-elemental formula mein common hote hain, intact protein se different.
- **C. Correct:** Polymeric formula mein protein mostly intact form mein hota hai, jise functional digestive system digest karta hai. Ye standard choice ho sakti hai if no special indication.
- **D. Incorrect:** Adequate protein provision essential amino acids supply karti hai. Standard formula ko aise define nahi karte.
