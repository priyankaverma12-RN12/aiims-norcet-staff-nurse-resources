### Q1. Which nitrogen source characterizes a standard polymeric enteral formula?

**Difficulty:** 🟢 Easy | **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD | **Topic:** Enteral formulations | **Type:** Original practice

- **A.** Only free amino acids
- **B.** Only short peptides
- **C.** Intact dietary proteins
- **D.** Only non-protein nitrogen with no essential amino acid contribution

<details><summary>Answer aur detailed explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: C. Intact dietary proteins**

- **A. Incorrect:** Free amino acids protein ke individual building blocks hote hain. Jab formula mein nitrogen isi form mein milta hai, toh woh elemental category hoti hai, standard polymeric nahi.
- **B. Incorrect:** Short peptides amino acids ki chhoti chains hoti hain. Semi-elemental formula mein protein pehle se kuch had tak toot chuka hota hai.
- **C. Correct:** Polymeric formula mein protein intact form mein milta hai, jise patient ka digestive system todkar absorb kar sakta hai. Agar digestion suitable hai aur koi special indication nahi hai toh standard product consider kiya ja sakta hai.
- **D. Incorrect:** Patient ko essential amino acids ki supply chahiye hoti hai. Standard complete formula ko essential amino acids se rahit nonprotein nitrogen ke basis par define nahi karte.

**Exam clue:** Polymeric = intact protein.

- **A. Incorrect:** Sealed ready-to-hang product ke longer handling limits intact package aur approved connection par depend karte hain. Product ko modify karne ke baad wahi limits assume karna safe nahi hai.

### Q20. A home enteral nutrition team is evaluating whether a formulation change improved care rather than only increasing prescribed calories. Which outcome set is most relevant?

**Difficulty:** 🟡 Medium | **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD | **Topic:** Enteral formulations | **Type:** Original practice

- **A.** Actual nutrient delivery, hydration, gastrointestinal tolerance and agreed clinical outcomes
- **B.** Only the number of containers purchased
- **C.** Only the manufacturer's advertised energy content
- **D.** Only the frequency of formula-name changes

<details><summary>Answer aur detailed explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: A. Actual nutrient delivery, hydration, gastrointestinal tolerance and agreed clinical outcomes**

- **A. Correct:** Actual feed volume aur nutrient delivery ke saath hydration, GI tolerance aur agreed clinical outcomes assess karo. Isse pata chalta hai ki prescribed formula patient ke liye kaam kar raha hai ya nahi.
- **B. Incorrect:** Containers purchase hone se yeh prove nahi hota ki patient ne poora feed receive kiya. Interrupted feeding ya unused containers actual intake ko kam kar sakte hain.
- **C. Incorrect:** Advertised energy density calculation mein useful hai. Lekin label alone patient ki hydration, tolerance aur nutritional response establish nahi karta.
- **D. Incorrect:** Product change kitni baar hua yeh activity ka record hai, treatment benefit ka measure nahi. Agreed outcomes aur actual delivery compare karne honge.

**Exam clue:** Prescription aur patient ko actually mila nutrition alag ho sakte hain.
