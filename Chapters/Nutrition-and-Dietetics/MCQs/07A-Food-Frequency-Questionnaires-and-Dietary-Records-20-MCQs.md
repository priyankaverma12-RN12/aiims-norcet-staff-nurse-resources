### Q1. A community nurse wants to identify habitual consumption of iron-rich foods during the preceding six months, without requiring participants to weigh each meal. Which dietary instrument best matches this purpose?

**Difficulty:** 🟡 Medium | **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD | **Topic:** FFQ reference period and food pattern | **Type:** Original practice

- **A.** A weighed record of the food served at today's lunch
- **B.** A menu listing all foods available in the village shops
- **C.** A locally appropriate food-frequency questionnaire covering the six-month period
- **D.** A single fasting blood glucose measurement

<details><summary>Answer aur detailed explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: C. A locally appropriate food-frequency questionnaire covering the six-month period**

- **A. Incorrect:** Weighed record aaj ke lunch ki quantity ko accurately document karne mein help karega. Lekin ek lunch se pichhle chhe mahine ka pattern establish nahi hoga.
- **B. Incorrect:** Shop menu availability batata hai, actual consumption nahi. Participant ka usual food pattern jaanne ke liye individual intake information chahiye.
- **C. Correct:** FFQ mein specific foods ki usual consumption frequency ek defined period ke liye poochhi jaati hai. Isliye nurse habitual iron-rich food pattern screen kar sakti hai. Isse individual iron deficiency confirm nahi hoti.
- **D. Incorrect:** Blood glucose ek biochemical measurement hai. Isse pichhle chhe mahine mein iron-rich foods kitni baar khaye gaye, yeh pata nahi chalta.

**Exam clue:** FFQ ko long-term food pattern ke liye select karo, exact current-day intake ke liye nahi.

### Q2. An FFQ records that two adults eat lentils four times each week but collects no portion-size information. Which interpretation is defensible?

**Difficulty:** 🟡 Medium | **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD | **Topic:** Frequency-only FFQ interpretation | **Type:** Original practice

- **B.** Their weekly lentil weights must be equal
- **C.** Their total daily protein intakes must be equal
- **D.** Their lentil intakes can be compared only after a blood test

<details><summary>Answer aur detailed explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: A. Their reported lentil-eating frequencies are equal, but their usual quantities may differ**

- **A. Correct:** Dono ne same frequency report ki hai. Lekin ek person har baar chhoti katori aur doosra badi katori kha sakta hai. Portion information ke bina quantity ya iron intake equal assume nahi karna chahiye.
- **B. Incorrect:** Weekly weight ke liye eating frequency ke saath har occasion ki quantity bhi chahiye. Sirf chaar baar likha hone se gram weight equal prove nahi hota.
- **C. Incorrect:** Protein doosre foods se bhi milta hai. Lentils ki portion size aur baaki diet ki information ke bina total protein compare nahi kar sakte.
- **D. Incorrect:** Reported frequency ko directly compare kiya ja sakta hai. Blood test se exact lentil frequency ya portion size establish nahi hoti.

**Exam clue:** Same frequency ka matlab same grams ya same nutrient intake nahi hota.

### Q3. A semi-quantitative FFQ estimates that a participant eats a food three times per week, 140 g each time. The supplied composition value is 80 mg of a nutrient per 100 g. What is the estimated average daily contribution of this nutrient from that food?

**Difficulty:** 🔴 Difficult | **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD | **Topic:** Frequency, portion and composition conversion | **Type:** Original practice

- **A.** 112 mg/day
- **B.** 336 mg/day
- **C.** 60 mg/day
- **D.** 48 mg/day

<details><summary>Answer aur detailed explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: D. 48 mg/day**

- **A. Incorrect:** 140 g ki ek serving se 112 mg milta hai. Lekin participant roz ek serving nahi kha raha hai, isliye serving contribution ko daily contribution nahi maan sakte.
- **B. Incorrect:** Teen servings milakar 336 mg per week milta hai. Daily average ke liye ise saat din se divide karna zaroori hai.
- **C. Incorrect:** 60 g food ka average daily weight hai. Food weight aur nutrient mass alag units hain. 80 mg per 100 g composition apply karne par 48 mg/day milta hai.
- **D. Correct:** Teen portions ka weekly weight 3 × 140 = 420 g hai. Daily average 420 ÷ 7 = 60 g hai. Nutrient density 80 mg per 100 g hone par 60 × 80 ÷ 100 = 48 mg/day milta hai. Yeh estimated contribution hai, measured biological intake nahi.

**Exam clue:** Weekly frequency ko daily average mein convert karke portion aur composition apply karo.

### Q17. For a simplified teaching exercise, a patient's typical weekday intake is 1,800 kcal and typical weekend-day intake is 2,500 kcal. Assuming these estimates apply to all five weekdays and both weekend days, what is the weighted average daily intake for a seven-day week?

- **A.** 2,000 kcal/day
- **B.** 2,150 kcal/day
- **C.** 1,800 kcal/day
- **D.** 2,500 kcal/day

<details><summary>Answer aur detailed explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: A. 2,000 kcal/day**

- **A. Correct:** Five weekdays se 5 × 1800 = 9000 kcal aur two weekend days se 2 × 2500 = 5000 kcal milti hain. Weekly total 14000 kcal ko saat se divide karne par 2000 kcal/day aata hai. Yeh stated assumptions par based estimate hai, individual requirement nahi.

- **D. Incorrect:** Yeh sirf weekend-day estimate hai. Weekdays ki lower intake include karne se average 2500 se kam hona chahiye.

**Exam clue:** Different day types ko unki weekly frequency ke mutabik weight do.

<details><summary>Answer aur detailed explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: D. Retain and annotate the illness-day record, and obtain additional representative days after recovery**

- **A. Incorrect:** Planned menu actual consumed intake nahi hai. Record alter karne se illness ke dauran nutritional risk ki information kho sakti hai.

**Exam clue:** Acute illness ka actual record preserve karo, habitual intake estimate ko context ke saath interpret karo.

### Q20. A patient with limited reading and writing ability can measure portions using familiar utensils but cannot reliably complete a written food diary. Which practical adaptation best maintains the dietary-record objective?

**Difficulty:** 🟡 Medium | **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD | **Topic:** Accessible food-record collection | **Type:** Original practice

- **A.** Ask the patient to estimate all intake from memory at the next monthly visit and label it a real-time record
- **B.** Use another household member's food diary as a substitute without verifying what this patient ate
- **C.** Arrange supported real-time recording with agreed household measures or audio entries, followed by trained review
- **D.** Ask the patient to report only food names and assume every portion equals 100 g

<details><summary>Answer aur detailed explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: C. Arrange supported real-time recording with agreed household measures or audio entries, followed by trained review**

- **A. Incorrect:** Month ke end par memory se intake batana retrospective method hai. Isko prospective food record label karna inaccurate hoga.
- **B. Incorrect:** Family members ki portions aur choices different ho sakti hain. Individual assessment ke liye isi patient ki consumed quantities record karni hongi.
- **C. Correct:** Patient familiar katori, cup ya spoon se portions estimate kar sakta hai. Trained support ya suitable audio method se intake real time note ki ja sakti hai. Review mein amount, preparation aur unclear entries clarify karo, bina patient ki independence compromise kiye.
- **D. Incorrect:** Food names useful hain lekin quantity missing hone par nutrient estimate weak ho sakta hai. Familiar household measures ko suitable gram estimates se map karna better hai.

**Exam clue:** Record format patient ki ability ke hisaab se adapt karo, amount aur time ki information preserve karo.
