### Q1. A two-person team measures the length of a cooperative 15-month-old on an infantometer. Which technique gives the most reliable result?

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Priority:** High | **Topic:** Child growth and anthropometry | **Type:** Original practice

- **A.** Align the head against the fixed board and gently extend the legs with feet against the movable board
- **B.** Measure the child while sitting on the board
- **C.** Measure from the shoulder to the heel
- **D.** Record the distance while both knees remain markedly flexed

<details><summary>Answer aur detailed explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: A. Align the head against the fixed board and gently extend the legs with feet against the movable board**

- **A. Correct:** Infant length mein head position, straight body aur gentle leg extension se measurement consistent hoti hai. Force apply nahi karna chahiye.
- **B. Incorrect:** Sitting position full recumbent length capture nahi karti. Infantometer flat lying measurement ke liye hai.
- **C. Incorrect:** Shoulder se heel tak measure karne se head length exclude hoti hai. Standard length crown to heel hoti hai.
- **D. Incorrect:** Knees bent hone se length underestimate ho sakti hai. Comfortable gentle positioning zaroori hai.

</details>

### Q2. An 18-month-old cannot be measured lying down but a reliable standing height of 80.0 cm is obtained. Under WHO measurement guidance, what length should be entered for the recumbent-length standard?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Priority:** High | **Topic:** Child growth and anthropometry | **Type:** Original practice

- **A.** 79.3 cm
- **B.** 80.0 cm without noting the method
- **C.** 80.7 cm
- **D.** 87.0 cm

<details><summary>Answer aur detailed explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: C. 80.7 cm**

- **A. Incorrect:** Subtract karna older child ki recumbent length ko standing height mein convert karne ke liye hota hai. Yahan add karna hai.
- **B. Incorrect:** Standing and recumbent methods systematically differ karte hain. Adjustment aur method document karna chahiye.
- **C. Correct:** Under two years usually recumbent length use hoti hai. Standing height ko length equivalent banane ke liye 0.7 cm add karte hain.
- **D. Incorrect:** Correction 0.7 cm hai, 7 cm nahi. Decimal error growth classification affect kar sakta hai.

</details>

### Q3. A 3-year-old cannot stand safely and reliable recumbent length is 96.4 cm. Which height equivalent is appropriate under the WHO method?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Priority:** High | **Topic:** Child growth and anthropometry | **Type:** Original practice

- **A.** 97.1 cm
- **B.** 89.4 cm
- **C.** 96.4 cm recorded as measured standing height
- **D.** 95.7 cm

<details><summary>Answer aur detailed explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: D. 95.7 cm**

- **A. Incorrect:** Yeh correction opposite direction mein hai. Standing height recumbent length se generally slightly shorter hoti hai.
- **B. Incorrect:** 7 cm subtract karna excessive correction hai. Standard adjustment 0.7 cm hai.
- **C. Incorrect:** Actual technique recumbent thi. Appropriate adjustment aur documentation se chart interpretation accurate hoti hai.
- **D. Correct:** Age two years or older mein standing height standard hota hai. Recumbent length se 0.7 cm subtract karke 95.7 cm milta hai.

</details>

- **A.** Add an arbitrary amount to compensate for movement
- **B.** Check zeroing and repeat the measurement safely when the infant is settled
- **C.** Diagnose wasting immediately from the first reading alone
- **D.** Use the last recorded weight as today's measured value

<details><summary>Answer aur detailed explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: B. Check zeroing and repeat the measurement safely when the infant is settled**

- **A. Incorrect:** Arbitrary correction validated nahi hai. Reliable repeat measurement better hai.
- **B. Correct:** Movement aur scale zero error result distort kar sakte hain. Safe repeat se measurement reliability improve hoti hai.
- **C. Incorrect:** Unreliable single reading se classification error ho sakta hai. Accurate anthropometry aur clinical assessment chahiye.
- **D. Incorrect:** Old weight ko current measurement label karna misleading hai. Actual repeat result aur limitations document karein.

</details>

### Q5. For head circumference in an infant, where should a nonstretchable tape be positioned?

- **B.** Around the maximum occipitofrontal circumference

- **D.** Around the forehead without passing over the occiput

<details><summary>Answer aur detailed explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: B. Around the maximum occipitofrontal circumference**

- **A. Incorrect:** Neck circumference head growth measure nahi karti. Head ke widest occipitofrontal plane ki zaroorat hai.
- **B. Correct:** Tape eyebrows ke just above aur occiput ke most prominent part se maximum circumference capture karti hai. Repeat consistent technique se karein.
- **C. Incorrect:** Yeh loop standard horizontal head circumference nahi hai. Chart comparison invalid ho sakta hai.
- **D. Incorrect:** Occipital prominence exclude karne se maximum circumference miss ho sakti hai.

</details>

### Q6. A 4-year-old has height-for-age z score -2.5 and weight-for-height z score -0.5. Which anthropometric interpretation is correct?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Priority:** High | **Topic:** Child growth and anthropometry | **Type:** Original practice

- **A.** Stunting without wasting by these two indicators
- **B.** Wasting without stunting
- **C.** Both severe wasting and severe stunting
- **D.** Overweight based solely on low height-for-age

<details><summary>Answer aur detailed explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: A. Stunting without wasting by these two indicators**

- **A. Correct:** Height-for-age -2 se below chronic linear growth deficit indicate karta hai. Weight-for-height -0.5 wasting cutoff se below nahi hai.
- **B. Incorrect:** Wasting weight relative to height se identify hoti hai. Given low height-for-age stunting indicate karta hai.
- **C. Incorrect:** Neither indicator -3 se below hai. Height-for-age stunting range mein hai, weight-for-height wasting range mein nahi.
- **D. Incorrect:** Overweight weight relative to height or age-specific BMI se assess hota hai. Low height-for-age overweight prove nahi karta.

</details>

### Q7. A 20-month-old has weight-for-age z score -2.4. Which limitation of this indicator is important?

<details><summary>Answer aur detailed explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: C. It cannot by itself distinguish short stature from low weight relative to length**

- **A. Incorrect:** Anthropometry pattern suggest karti hai, exact cause history aur clinical assessment se identify hota hai.
- **B. Incorrect:** Oedema clinical examination se assess hoti hai. Weight-for-age alone oedema diagnose nahi karta.
- **C. Correct:** Weight-for-age age ke comparison mein body weight batata hai. Stunting aur wasting differentiate karne ke liye length-related indicators bhi chahiye.
- **D. Incorrect:** Weight aur length different measurements hain. Dono distinct growth dimensions describe karte hain.

</details>

### Q8. A child aged 4 years has weight-for-height z score -3.2 without oedema. Which classification applies to this indicator?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Priority:** High | **Topic:** Child growth and anthropometry | **Type:** Original practice

- **A.** Moderate wasting
- **B.** Stunting
- **C.** Normal weight-for-height
- **D.** Severe wasting

<details><summary>Answer aur detailed explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: D. Severe wasting**

- **A. Incorrect:** Moderate wasting generally -3 inclusive to below -2 range hai. -3.2 usse lower hai.
- **B. Incorrect:** Stunting height-for-age indicator se classify hoti hai. Given value weight-for-height ka hai.
- **C. Incorrect:** Value WHO median se markedly below hai. Normal category nahi hai.
- **D. Correct:** WHO threshold weight-for-height below -3 z severe wasting indicate karta hai. Full clinical assessment aur appropriate referral required hai.

</details>

### Q9. A 2-year-old has weight-for-height z score exactly -3.0, MUAC 120 mm and no nutritional oedema. Based on WHO 2023 anthropometric thresholds, which classification fits?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Priority:** High | **Topic:** Child growth and anthropometry | **Type:** Original practice

- **A.** Moderate wasting
- **B.** Severe wasting solely because WHZ equals -3.0
- **C.** No wasting because MUAC is above 115 mm
- **D.** Severe stunting

<details><summary>Answer aur detailed explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: A. Moderate wasting**

- **A. Correct:** WHZ -3 inclusive se below -2 aur MUAC 115 to below 125 mm moderate wasting range mein hain. Medical complications separately assess karein.
- **B. Incorrect:** Severe WHZ criterion strictly below -3 hai. MUAC bhi 115 se below nahi hai.
- **C. Incorrect:** MUAC 120 mm moderate wasting range mein hai. Above severe cutoff ka matlab normal nahi.
- **D. Incorrect:** Stunting height-for-age se assess hoti hai. WHZ and MUAC stunting classification establish nahi karte.

</details>

### Q10. A 19-month-old has MUAC 112 mm but weight-for-length z score -1.5. What is the appropriate interpretation?

<details><summary>Answer aur detailed explanation dekho</summary>

- **B. Incorrect:** WHO severe MUAC cutoff 115 mm hai, 100 mm nahi.
- **C. Correct:** Children 6–59 months mein MUAC below 115 mm severe wasting criterion hai. Normal-ish WLZ is independent criterion ko cancel nahi karta.
- **D. Incorrect:** MUAC arm tissue depletion screen karta hai. Stunting ke liye length-for-age chahiye.

<details><summary>Answer aur detailed explanation dekho</summary>
