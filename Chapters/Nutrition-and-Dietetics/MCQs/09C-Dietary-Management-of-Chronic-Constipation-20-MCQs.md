### Q1. A dietitian is considering the 2025 British Dietetic Association dietary guideline for chronic idiopathic constipation. Which patient most directly fits its intended population?

**Difficulty:** 🟡 Medium | **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD | **Topic:** Guideline population | **Type:** Original practice

- **A.** A neonate with delayed passage of meconium
- **B.** A pregnant patient with new constipation
- **C.** An otherwise healthy adult with assessed chronic primary constipation
- **D.** A patient with constipation caused by a recently started opioid

**Correct: C. An otherwise healthy adult with assessed chronic primary constipation**

- **A. Incorrect:** Neonate mein delayed meconium congenital obstruction jaise causes ka sign ho sakta hai. Adult dietary trial evidence is age group par directly apply nahi hota.

- **B. Incorrect:** Pregnancy mein physiological changes aur medicine safety alag considerations hain. Adult idiopathic constipation guideline ko automatically transfer nahi karte.

- **C. Correct:** Guideline adult primary constipation ke liye develop hui hai. Secondary cause assess karne ke baad is population mein dietary evidence apply karna appropriate hai.

- **D. Incorrect:** Opioid-induced constipation secondary cause hai. Drug-specific assessment aur management ki zarurat hoti hai, primary constipation trial population se directly match nahi karta.

**Exam clue:** Identify the guideline population before applying a dietary recommendation.

### Q4. A clinically assessed adult begins a clinician-approved fibre supplement and experiences bothersome gas after a rapid increase. Which modification is most consistent with BDA good practice?

**Difficulty:** 🟡 Medium | **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD | **Topic:** Gradual titration | **Type:** Original practice

- **B.** Review tolerance and increase the agreed dose more gradually if appropriate
- **C.** Stop all plant foods permanently
- **D.** Restrict all fluids to reduce intestinal contents

<details><summary>Answer aur detailed explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: B. Review tolerance and increase the agreed dose more gradually if appropriate**

- **A. Incorrect:** Dose aur rapidly badhane se symptoms worse ho sakte hain. Tolerance problem mein escalation automatic nahi hoti.

- **B. Correct:** Rapid fibre increase se gas aur discomfort ho sakta hai. Tolerance ke hisab se gradual increments se body ko adapt karne ka time milta hai, persistent symptoms review karne chahiye.

- **C. Incorrect:** All plant foods permanently avoid karna unnecessary nutritional restriction create karega. Specific supplement aur dose review better hai.

- **D. Incorrect:** Fluid restriction fibre ke safe use ko impair kar sakti hai. Fluid advice clinical condition ke hisab se individualized honi chahiye.

**Exam clue:** Titrate fibre according to tolerance rather than forcing a rapid increase.

### Q20. A dietitian reviews an agreed four-week nutrition trial. Stool frequency increased, but the adult reports more abdominal discomfort and no improvement in daily functioning. Which documentation best guides the next decision?

**Difficulty:** 🟡 Medium | **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD | **Topic:** Patient-centred outcome audit | **Type:** Original practice

- **A.** Record the frequency change, discomfort, functional impact and actual adherence before jointly reviewing the plan
- **B.** Record complete success using only bowel frequency
- **C.** Record complete failure without documenting the frequency change
- **D.** Increase the intervention automatically because one endpoint improved

<details><summary>Answer aur detailed explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: A. Record the frequency change, discomfort, functional impact and actual adherence before jointly reviewing the plan**

- **A. Correct:** Nutrition trial ke multiple outcomes document karo. Frequency improve hona useful hai, lekin discomfort aur daily functioning patient ke liye important hain, actual adherence bhi interpretation mein help karti hai.

- **B. Incorrect:** Sirf frequency se complete success declare karna tolerability aur patient priorities ignore karta hai.

- **D. Incorrect:** One endpoint improvement se dose escalation automatically justified nahi hoti. Overall benefit, harm aur preference review karo.

**Exam clue:** Evaluate outcome-specific benefit alongside tolerability and quality of life.
