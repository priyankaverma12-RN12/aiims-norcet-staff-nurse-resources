### Q1. Which measurement condition most closely approximates basal metabolic rate rather than ordinary resting expenditure?

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Priority:** High | **Topic:** Energy requirements and BMR | **Type:** Original practice

- **A.** Immediately after a large breakfast
- **B.** Awake, postabsorptive, physically rested and thermoneutral
- **C.** During moderate treadmill exercise
- **D.** During shivering in a cold room

<details><summary>Answer aur detailed explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: B. Awake, postabsorptive, physically rested and thermoneutral**

- **A. Incorrect:** Meal digestion additional thermogenesis produce karti hai, isliye value basal nahi hogi.
- **B. Correct:** BMR minimum awake energy expenditure hai. Recent meal, activity aur temperature stress remove karne se basal measurement meaningful hota hai.
- **C. Incorrect:** Exercise muscle energy expenditure increase karta hai, jo basal condition se alag hai.
- **D. Incorrect:** Shivering heat production badhati hai, isliye thermoneutral basal measurement nahi milta.

</details>

### Q2. Which component of total daily energy expenditure reflects the energy used to digest, absorb and process nutrients?

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Priority:** Medium | **Topic:** Energy requirements and BMR | **Type:** Original practice

- **A.** Basal metabolic rate
- **B.** Physical activity expenditure
- **C.** Thermic effect of food
- **D.** Energy stored in adipose tissue

<details><summary>Answer aur detailed explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: C. Thermic effect of food**

- **A. Incorrect:** BMR postabsorptive conditions mein essential functions ka expenditure hai, meal processing ka extra component nahi.
- **B. Incorrect:** Activity expenditure movement aur exercise se related hai, nutrient processing se nahi.
- **C. Correct:** Food processing mein bhi energy lagti hai. Is additional expenditure ko thermic effect of food kehte hain.
- **D. Incorrect:** Stored energy reserve hai, daily expenditure ka component nahi.

</details>

- **D.** 1,400 kcal/day

<details><summary>Answer aur detailed explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: A. 2,240 kcal/day**

- **A. Correct:** PAL total expenditure aur BMR ka ratio hai. 1400 times 1.6 se 2240 kcal/day aata hai.
- **B. Incorrect:** Yeh BMR ko PAL se divide karta hai, jabki daily expenditure ke liye multiply karte hain.
- **C. Incorrect:** PAL dimensionless multiplier hai, kcal/day mein seedha add nahi hota.
- **D. Incorrect:** Yeh basal expenditure alone hai aur daily activity ko include nahi karta.

</details>

### Q6. A metabolic cart reports VCO2 240 mL/min and VO2 300 mL/min. What is the measured respiratory exchange ratio?

- **A.** 1.25
- **B.** 0.80
- **C.** 60
- **D.** 0.24

<details><summary>Answer aur detailed explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: B. 0.80**

- **A. Incorrect:** Yeh VO2 divided by VCO2 hai, ratio ka order reverse hai.
- **B. Correct:** RER exhaled carbon dioxide divided by consumed oxygen hai. 240/300 se 0.8 milta hai.
- **C. Incorrect:** Yeh volume difference hai, gas exchange ratio nahi.
- **D. Incorrect:** VCO2 ko litres mein convert karna ratio calculation ka substitute nahi hai.

</details>

### Q7. Under steady-state conditions, which respiratory quotient is approximately associated with predominantly carbohydrate oxidation?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Priority:** Medium | **Topic:** Energy requirements and BMR | **Type:** Original practice

- **A.** 1.0
- **B.** 0.70
- **C.** 0.0
- **D.** 2.0

<details><summary>Answer aur detailed explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: A. 1.0**

- **A. Correct:** Glucose oxidation mein oxygen consumption aur carbon dioxide production roughly equal hote hain. Isliye RQ about 1 hota hai.
- **B. Incorrect:** Yeh predominantly fat oxidation se associated approximate value hai.
- **C. Incorrect:** Normal aerobic carbohydrate oxidation mein carbon dioxide produce hoti hai, isliye zero nahi.
- **D. Incorrect:** Pure carbohydrate oxidation ka stoichiometric ratio 2 nahi hai. Clinical RER nonsteady conditions mein differ kar sakta hai.

</details>

### Q8. Two adults of similar weight have different proportions of lean tissue. Which characteristic generally predicts greater resting energy expenditure?

- **D.** Identical blood group

<details><summary>Answer aur detailed explanation dekho</summary>

- **B. Incorrect:** Shoe size metabolic tissue composition ka reliable measure nahi hai.
- **C. Correct:** Organs aur lean tissues resting energy use mein major contribution karte hain. Equal body weight ke bawajood composition different expenditure de sakti hai.
- **D. Incorrect:** Blood group resting energy demand ka usual predictor nahi hai.

</details>

### Q10. A stable patient with uncontrolled hyperthyroidism reports heat intolerance. Which energy-related change is plausible?

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Priority:** Medium | **Topic:** Energy requirements and BMR | **Type:** Original practice

- **A.** Permanent abolition of basal metabolism
- **B.** Increased resting energy expenditure
- **C.** A fixed identical expenditure to every euthyroid patient
- **D.** Reduced expenditure solely because sweating occurs

<details><summary>Answer aur detailed explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: B. Increased resting energy expenditure**

- **A. Incorrect:** Vital functions continue karte hain. Hyperthyroidism metabolism abolish nahi karta.
- **B. Correct:** Excess thyroid hormone metabolic activity aur heat production badha sakta hai. Disease control ke saath nutrition needs reassess karni chahiye.
- **C. Incorrect:** Thyroid status energy expenditure influence kar sakta hai, isliye fixed equality assume nahi karte.
- **D. Incorrect:** Sweating heat dissipation response hai, increased heat production ko exclude nahi karti.

</details>

### Q14. A patient with fever is receiving nutrition support. Which general principle should guide reassessment?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Priority:** High | **Topic:** Energy requirements and BMR | **Type:** Original practice

- **A.** Fever and illness can alter energy expenditure, but the size of the effect is individual
- **B.** Always double energy provision for any fever
- **C.** Fever has no influence on energy expenditure

<details><summary>Answer aur detailed explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: A. Fever and illness can alter energy expenditure, but the size of the effect is individual**

- **A. Correct:** Illness metabolic demand badal sakti hai, lekin response severity aur treatment se differ karta hai. Universal multiplier ke bajay current assessment useful hai.
- **B. Incorrect:** Fixed doubling overfeeding risk create kar sakti hai. Clinical guidance aur measured or estimated need use karein.
- **C. Incorrect:** Temperature regulation aur inflammation energy use alter kar sakte hain. No effect assume karna incorrect hai.
- **D. Incorrect:** Nutrition need illness mein bhi remain karti hai. Route aur timing stability aur clinical plan se decide hoti hai.

</details>

### Q20. A patient receives nutrition support and develops worsening hypercapnia with suspected substantial overfeeding. Which nutrition-related mechanism warrants review?

- **B.** Only dietary calcium directly determines carbon dioxide output
- **C.** Excess energy provision can increase carbon dioxide production
- **D.** All nutrition must permanently stop whenever CO2 rises

<details><summary>Answer aur detailed explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: C. Excess energy provision can increase carbon dioxide production**

- **B. Incorrect:** Calcium physiology important hai, lekin metabolic gas production primarily substrate metabolism se linked hai.
- **C. Correct:** Overfeeding metabolic CO2 production badha sakti hai, especially excess carbohydrate oxidation and lipogenesis contexts mein. Respiratory status aur nutrition prescription team se review karein.
- **D. Incorrect:** Hypercapnia multiple causes se hoti hai. Safe individualized adjustment chahiye, permanent cessation universal rule nahi.

</details>
