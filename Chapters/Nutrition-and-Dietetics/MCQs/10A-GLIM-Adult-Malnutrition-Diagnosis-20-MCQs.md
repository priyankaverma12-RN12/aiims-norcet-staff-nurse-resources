### Q1. Which finding belongs to the etiologic rather than phenotypic domain of GLIM?

**Difficulty:** 🟢 Easy | **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD | **Topic:** Criterion classification | **Type:** Original practice

- **A.** Unintentional weight loss
- **B.** Reduced food assimilation
- **C.** Low BMI
- **D.** Low muscle mass

<details><summary>Answer aur detailed explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: B. Reduced food assimilation**

- **A. Incorrect:** Weight loss body mein observed change hai. Yeh phenotype hai, cause ka evidence alag assess hota hai.

- **B. Correct:** Assimilation kam hone se nutrients effectively absorb nahi hote. Yeh cause-related finding hai, isliye etiologic domain mein aati hai.

- **C. Incorrect:** Low BMI body size ka indicator hai, isliye phenotype hai. Isse cause directly establish nahi hota.

- **D. Incorrect:** Low muscle mass body composition ka finding hai. Yeh phenotypic criterion hai, etiologic nahi.

**Exam clue:** Phenotype describes bodily changes, etiology describes contributing causes.

**Evidence:** [GLIM Table 5](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12053077/)

**Exam clue:** Missing data need documentation, not fabricated normal values.

### Q5. An adult lost 9% of usual body weight unintentionally over 12 months, with no further loss in the most recent six months. Using the GLIM weight-loss criterion alone, what is the finding?

**Difficulty:** 🟡 Medium | **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD | **Topic:** Weight loss interval | **Type:** Original practice

- **A.** It does not reach the threshold for loss occurring beyond six months
- **B.** It meets the threshold solely because 9% is above 5%
- **C.** It proves severe malnutrition
- **D.** It meets an identical threshold for every observation interval

<details><summary>Answer aur detailed explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: A. It does not reach the threshold for loss occurring beyond six months**

- **A. Correct:** Beyond six months ke liye GLIM threshold 10% se zyada loss hai. Given history mein 9% hai aur recent six months mein loss nahi hua, to yeh particular criterion fulfill nahi hota.

- **B. Incorrect:** 5% se zyada wala cutoff recent six months ke liye hai. Usko longer interval par automatically apply nahi karna.

- **C. Incorrect:** Weight-loss criterion meet na hone par severity establish nahi hoti. Other phenotype aur etiology still assess karo.

- **D. Incorrect:** Time interval ke hisab se cutoff different hai. Accurate history lena important hai.

**Exam clue:** Match percentage loss to its observation interval.

**Evidence:** [GLIM Table 5](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12053077/)
