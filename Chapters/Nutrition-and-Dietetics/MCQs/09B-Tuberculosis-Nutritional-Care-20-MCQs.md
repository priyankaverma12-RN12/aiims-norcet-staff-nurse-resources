### Q1. A patient with pulmonary TB has an adequate baseline nutritional assessment. At the next treatment visit, the nurse proposes omitting further nutrition review because the initial assessment was satisfactory. Which approach follows WHO's 2025 recommendation 1?

**Difficulty:** 🟡 Medium | **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD | **Topic:** Longitudinal nutrition assessment | **Type:** Original practice

- **A.** Reassess only if sputum remains positive at the end of treatment
- **B.** Assess nutrition only if the patient requests a food supplement
- **C.** Continue nutrition assessment and counselling during treatment
- **D.** Replace nutrition follow-up with medication dispensing alone

<details><summary>Answer aur detailed explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: C. Continue nutrition assessment and counselling during treatment**

- **A. Incorrect:** Sputum result microbiological response batata hai. Nutritional deterioration us result se pehle bhi occur kar sakti hai, isliye assessment ko end tak delay nahi karna chahiye.
- **B. Incorrect:** Patient request useful ho sakti hai, lekin unrecognized weight loss aur intake problems detect karna nursing care ka part hai.
- **C. Correct:** TB disease, medicines ke adverse effects aur food access time ke saath change ho sakte hain. Isliye diagnosis ke baad bhi nutrition reassessment important hai. Counselling findings ke according update hoti hai.
- **D. Incorrect:** Medicine dispensing essential hai par dietary needs, intake aur food insecurity automatically assess nahi hoti. Dono components comprehensive TB care mein relevant hain.

**Exam clue:** TB mein baseline assessment normal hone par bhi nutritional status treatment ke dauran change ho sakta hai.

<details><summary>Answer aur detailed explanation dekho</summary>
