### Q1. Which naturally occurring dietary sugar is a monosaccharide?

**Difficulty:** 🟢 Easy | **Priority:** 🟢 FOUNDATION | **Topic:** Single sugar unit | **Type:** Original practice

- **A.** Sucrose
- **B.** Fructose
- **C.** Lactose
- **D.** Maltose

<details><summary>Answer aur detailed explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: B. Fructose**

- **A. Incorrect:** Sucrose mein glucose aur fructose linked hote hain, isliye yeh disaccharide hai. Isko absorb karne se pehle digestive enzyme todta hai.
- **B. Correct:** Fructose single sugar unit hai aur fruits mein naturally mil sakta hai. Isko monosaccharide kehte hain kyunki ise smaller sugar units mein hydrolyse nahi karna padta.
- **C. Incorrect:** Lactose milk sugar hai jo glucose aur galactose se bana hai. Do units hone ki wajah se yeh disaccharide hai.
- **D. Incorrect:** Maltose glucose ke do linked units se bana hai. Isliye fructose ki tarah single sugar unit nahi hai.

**Exam clue:** Fructose ek single sugar unit hai.

**Evidence:** [FAO dietary carbohydrate composition, classification and individual carbohydrate sections](https://www.fao.org/4/w8079e/w8079e0h.htm) (direct retrieved text).

### Q2. Which carbohydrate is the principal storage polysaccharide in rice grains and potato tubers?

**Difficulty:** 🟢 Easy | **Priority:** 🟢 FOUNDATION | **Topic:** Plant reserve carbohydrate | **Type:** Original practice

- **A.** Cellulose
- **B.** Lactose
- **C.** Sucrose
- **D.** Starch

<details><summary>Answer aur detailed explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: D. Starch**

- **A. Incorrect:** Cellulose plant cell walls ko structural strength deta hai. Rice aur potato mein major reserve carbohydrate starch hota hai, cellulose nahi.
- **B. Incorrect:** Lactose mainly milk mein milne wala disaccharide hai. Yeh grain ya tuber ka principal storage polysaccharide nahi hai.
- **C. Incorrect:** Sucrose plant mein sugar transport aur energy metabolism mein important ho sakta hai, lekin yeh disaccharide hai. Question reserve polysaccharide pooch raha hai.
- **D. Correct:** Starch plants ka main reserve glucose polymer hai. Rice grains aur potato tubers starch store karte hain, jo diet mein digestible carbohydrate supply kar sakta hai.

**Exam clue:** Plant storage carbohydrate starch hai.

**Evidence:** [FAO dietary carbohydrate composition, classification and individual carbohydrate sections](https://www.fao.org/4/w8079e/w8079e0h.htm) (direct retrieved text).

### Q3. Which of the two major starch polymers is normally much more highly branched?

**Difficulty:** 🟢 Easy | **Priority:** 🟢 FOUNDATION | **Topic:** Starch branching | **Type:** Original practice

- **A.** Amylopectin
- **B.** Amylose
- **C.** Cellulose
- **D.** Pectin

<details><summary>Answer aur detailed explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: A. Amylopectin**

- **A. Correct:** Amylopectin mein glucose chains ke beech alpha-1,6 branch points hote hain. Isi wajah se starch ka yeh component amylose ke comparison mein much more branched hota hai.
- **B. Incorrect:** Amylose mostly alpha-1,4 linked glucose chain hai. Ismein limited branching possible hai, lekin normally amylopectin se kaafi kam hoti hai.
- **C. Incorrect:** Cellulose bhi glucose polymer hai, par beta linkages ke saath plant cell wall ka structural material hai. Yeh starch ka major polymer component nahi hai.
- **D. Incorrect:** Pectin plant cell wall carbohydrate hai aur fibre ka part ho sakta hai. Starch ke do main polymers amylose aur amylopectin hain.

**Exam clue:** Amylopectin extensively branched hai, amylose mainly linear hai.

**Evidence:** [FAO dietary carbohydrate composition, classification and individual carbohydrate sections](https://www.fao.org/4/w8079e/w8079e0h.htm) (direct retrieved text).

### Q4. Raffinose, a carbohydrate present in pulses, contains three monosaccharide units. Which class does it belong to in the FAO dietary carbohydrate classification?

**Difficulty:** 🟢 Easy | **Priority:** 🟢 FOUNDATION | **Topic:** Oligosaccharide class | **Type:** Original practice

- **A.** Monosaccharide
- **B.** Disaccharide
- **C.** Oligosaccharide
- **D.** Polysaccharide

<details><summary>Answer aur detailed explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: C. Oligosaccharide**

- **A. Incorrect:** Monosaccharide mein ek sugar unit hota hai. Raffinose mein three units specified hain, isliye single-unit class fit nahi karti.
- **B. Incorrect:** Disaccharide mein two monosaccharide units hote hain. Raffinose ke three units hone se yeh category incorrect hai.
- **C. Correct:** FAO dietary classification mein three to nine sugar units wale carbohydrates oligosaccharides kehlate hain. Raffinose specifically trisaccharide hai aur isi group mein aata hai.
- **D. Incorrect:** FAO classification mein polysaccharides ten ya more sugar units wale group mein hain. Three units wala raffinose is group mein nahi aata.

**Exam clue:** Three to nine sugar units ko FAO classification mein oligosaccharides kehte hain.

**Evidence:** [FAO dietary carbohydrate composition, classification and individual carbohydrate sections](https://www.fao.org/4/w8079e/w8079e0h.htm) (direct retrieved text).

### Q5. Which description best identifies resistant starch in human nutrition?

**Difficulty:** 🟢 Easy | **Priority:** 🟢 FOUNDATION | **Topic:** Resistant starch | **Type:** Original practice

- **A.** Starch that escapes digestion in the small intestine
- **B.** Starch that is completely absorbed unchanged in the stomach
- **C.** A starch that contains only amino acids
- **D.** A sugar that must be digested exclusively in the mouth

<details><summary>Answer aur detailed explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: A. Starch that escapes digestion in the small intestine**

- **A. Correct:** Resistant starch ka kuch fraction normal small-intestinal digestion se bach jaata hai. Yeh lower intestine tak pahunch sakta hai, jahan gut microbes isko varying extent tak ferment karte hain.
- **B. Incorrect:** Starch intact form mein stomach se completely absorb nahi hota. Resistant fraction ka important feature small intestine mein digestion se escape karna hai.
- **C. Incorrect:** Amino acids proteins ke building blocks hain. Starch glucose units se bana carbohydrate hai, amino acid polymer nahi.
- **D. Incorrect:** Mouth mein starch digestion start ho sakti hai, lekin digestive process exclusively mouth mein nahi hota. Resistant starch ka meaning digestion se bachne wale fraction se related hai.

**Exam clue:** Resistant starch small intestine mein digestion se bach kar lower gut tak pahunch sakta hai.

**Evidence:** [FAO dietary carbohydrate composition, classification and individual carbohydrate sections](https://www.fao.org/4/w8079e/w8079e0h.htm) (direct retrieved text).

### Q6. Which non-starch polysaccharide forms a major structural component of plant cell walls and is not digested by human digestive enzymes?

**Difficulty:** 🟢 Easy | **Priority:** 🟢 FOUNDATION | **Topic:** Structural dietary fibre | **Type:** Original practice

- **A.** Glycogen
- **B.** Cellulose
- **C.** Maltose
- **D.** Amylose

<details><summary>Answer aur detailed explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: B. Cellulose**

- **A. Incorrect:** Glycogen animals mein glucose ka storage polymer hai, plant cell wall ka major structural fibre nahi. Human metabolism mein glycogen ko suitable enzymes break down kar sakte hain.
- **B. Correct:** Cellulose plant cell walls mein structural support deta hai. Human digestive enzymes iske beta-1,4 glucose linkages ko digest nahi karte, isliye yeh dietary fibre ka part hai.
- **C. Incorrect:** Maltose two glucose units wala disaccharide hai. Yeh plant cell walls ka structural polysaccharide nahi hai.
- **D. Incorrect:** Amylose starch ka mainly linear glucose polymer hai. Human amylase starch ke alpha linkages ko digest kar sakta hai, jabki cellulose ke beta linkages alag hain.

**Exam clue:** Cellulose plant cell wall ka structural glucose polymer hai.

**Evidence:** [FAO dietary carbohydrate composition, classification and individual carbohydrate sections](https://www.fao.org/4/w8079e/w8079e0h.htm) (direct retrieved text).
