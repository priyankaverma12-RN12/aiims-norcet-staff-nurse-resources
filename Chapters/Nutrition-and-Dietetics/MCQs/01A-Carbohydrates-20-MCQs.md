### Q1. A patient receives 150 g of digestible carbohydrate in a daily meal plan. Approximately how much energy does this supply?

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Priority:** Medium | **Topic:** Carbohydrates | **Type:** Original practice

- **A.** 150 kcal
- **B.** 600 kcal
- **C.** 1,350 kcal
- **D.** 300 kcal

<details><summary>Answer aur detailed explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: B. 600 kcal**

- **A. Incorrect:** Is hisaab mein gram ko calorie maan liya gaya hai, jabki conversion 4 kcal per gram hota hai.
- **B. Correct:** Har gram digestible carbohydrate se lagbhag 4 kcal milti hain. Isliye 150 ko 4 se multiply karne par 600 kcal aati hain.
- **C. Incorrect:** Yeh 9 kcal per gram wala hisaab hai, jo fat ke liye use hota hai.
- **D. Incorrect:** Ismein 2 kcal per gram lagaya gaya hai, jo digestible carbohydrate ka standard factor nahi hai.

### Q2. Which pair of monosaccharides is released when intestinal lactase hydrolyses lactose?

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Priority:** Medium | **Topic:** Carbohydrates | **Type:** Original practice

- **A.** Glucose and fructose
- **B.** Glucose and glucose
- **C.** Fructose and galactose
- **D.** Glucose and galactose

<details><summary>Answer aur detailed explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: D. Glucose and galactose**

- **A. Incorrect:** Yeh sucrose ke breakdown products hain, isliye milk sugar ke liye yeh pair fit nahi hota.
- **B. Incorrect:** Maltose mein glucose ke do units hote hain, lactose mein ek galactose hota hai.
- **C. Incorrect:** Lactose mein fructose nahi hota, isliye yeh pair enzyme ke substrate se match nahi karta.
- **D. Correct:** Lactose doodh ki sugar hai. Brush border par lactase ise glucose aur galactose mein todta hai, jisse absorption ho sake.

**Exam clue:** Is question mein Lactose doodh ki sugar hai, is baat ko option select karte waqt yaad rakho.

### Q3. A patient develops bloating and loose stools after ordinary milk but tolerates lactose-free milk. What best explains the difference?

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Priority:** High | **Topic:** Carbohydrates | **Type:** Original practice

- **A.** Unabsorbed lactose draws water into the bowel and is fermented by bacteria
- **B.** An immune response to casein is prevented by removing lactose
- **C.** Excess bile salts digest lactose into glucose
- **D.** Pancreatic protease deficiency causes selective milk sugar intolerance

<details><summary>Answer aur detailed explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: A. Unabsorbed lactose draws water into the bowel and is fermented by bacteria**

- **A. Correct:** Jab lactase kam hota hai toh lactose poori tarah absorb nahi hota. Bowel mein water khinchta hai aur bacteria gas banate hain, isliye symptoms aate hain.
- **B. Incorrect:** Lactose-free milk mein milk proteins reh sakte hain, isliye protein allergy ka mechanism alag hai.
- **C. Incorrect:** Bile fats ke digestion mein help karti hai, lactose ko todne ka kaam lactase karta hai.
- **D. Incorrect:** Protease protein ko todta hai, isliye selective lactose symptoms ko yeh mechanism explain nahi karta.

**Exam clue:** Is question mein Jab lactase kam hota hai toh lactose poori tarah absorb nahi hota, is baat ko option select karte waqt yaad rakho.

</details>

### Q4. A patient asks why thoroughly chewed plain rice begins to taste slightly sweet. Which enzyme is most directly responsible?

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Priority:** Medium | **Topic:** Carbohydrates | **Type:** Original practice

- **A.** Pepsin
- **B.** Lingual lipase
- **C.** Salivary amylase
- **D.** Lactase

<details><summary>Answer aur detailed explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: C. Salivary amylase**

- **A. Incorrect:** Pepsin acidic stomach mein proteins todta hai, rice starch par directly kaam nahi karta.
- **B. Incorrect:** Lingual lipase fats par kaam karta hai, isliye starch ki sweetness iska main result nahi hai.
- **C. Correct:** Rice mein starch hota hai. Saliva ka amylase starch ki kuch bonds todkar chhote carbohydrates banata hai, jisse sweetness feel ho sakti hai.
- **D. Incorrect:** Lactase small intestine ke brush border par lactose todta hai, mouth mein starch nahi.

**Exam clue:** Is question mein Rice mein starch hota hai, is baat ko option select karte waqt yaad rakho.

### Q5. Following a meal, which process primarily stores surplus glucose as glycogen in the liver?

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Priority:** Medium | **Topic:** Carbohydrates | **Type:** Original practice

- **A.** Glycogenolysis
- **B.** Gluconeogenesis
- **C.** Glycogenesis
- **D.** Lipolysis

<details><summary>Answer aur detailed explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: C. Glycogenesis**

- **A. Incorrect:** Is process mein stored glycogen toot ta hai, jo fasting mein glucose availability badhata hai.
- **B. Incorrect:** Ismein non-carbohydrate precursors se naya glucose banta hai, stored glycogen nahi.
- **C. Correct:** Meal ke baad insulin ka signal liver ko glucose store karne mein help karta hai. Glucose se glycogen banne ko glycogenesis kehte hain.
- **D. Incorrect:** Lipolysis mein triglycerides tootkar fatty acids release hote hain, glucose storage nahi hota.

**Exam clue:** Is question mein Meal ke baad insulin ka signal liver ko glucose store karne mein help karta hai, is baat ko option select karte waqt yaad rakho.

### Q6. During an overnight fast, which hepatic process directly releases glucose units from stored glycogen?

- **A.** Glycogenolysis
- **B.** Glycogenesis
- **C.** Protein synthesis
- **D.** Fatty acid synthesis

<details><summary>Answer aur detailed explanation dekho</summary>

- **A. Correct:** Liver glycogen ko todkar glucose availability maintain karta hai. Yeh process fasting ke shuruati phase mein important hota hai.
- **B. Incorrect:** Yeh glucose ko glycogen mein store karta hai, fasting mein glycogen release karne ka naam nahi hai.
- **C. Incorrect:** Protein synthesis amino acids ko jodta hai, glycogen se glucose nikalna iska kaam nahi hai.
- **D. Incorrect:** Is process mein fatty acids bante hain, stored hepatic glycogen directly break nahi hota.

**Exam clue:** Is question mein Liver glycogen ko todkar glucose availability maintain karta hai, is baat ko option select karte waqt yaad rakho.

### Q7. Why can skeletal muscle glycogen not directly supply free glucose to the bloodstream in the same way as liver glycogen?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Priority:** Medium | **Topic:** Carbohydrates | **Type:** Original practice

- **A.** Skeletal muscle contains no glycogen
- **B.** Liver cannot break down glycogen
- **C.** Skeletal muscle has no glucose transporters
- **D.** Skeletal muscle lacks glucose-6-phosphatase activity needed for free glucose release

<details><summary>Answer aur detailed explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: D. Skeletal muscle lacks glucose-6-phosphatase activity needed for free glucose release**

- **A. Incorrect:** Muscle mein glycogen store hota hai aur activity ke waqt use bhi hota hai.
- **B. Incorrect:** Liver glycogen breakdown fasting mein blood glucose maintain karne mein help karta hai.
- **C. Incorrect:** Muscle glucose uptake kar sakti hai, khas taur par insulin aur exercise ke signals ke saath. Problem free glucose release wale enzyme ki hai.
- **D. Correct:** Muscle apna glycogen local energy ke liye use karti hai. Liver ki tarah glucose-6-phosphate ko free glucose mein badalkar blood mein bhejne ki capacity muscle mein nahi hoti.

**Exam clue:** Is question mein Muscle apna glycogen local energy ke liye use karti hai, is baat ko option select karte waqt yaad rakho.

### Q8. Which advice best increases dietary fibre for an adult with uncomplicated constipation who has no fluid restriction?

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Priority:** High | **Topic:** Carbohydrates | **Type:** Original practice

- **A.** Immediately consume a very large fibre supplement without water
- **B.** Gradually replace refined grains with whole grains and maintain adequate fluid intake
- **C.** Replace vegetables with strained juices only
- **D.** Avoid all pulses and whole grains permanently

<details><summary>Answer aur detailed explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: B. Gradually replace refined grains with whole grains and maintain adequate fluid intake**

- **A. Incorrect:** Achanak bahut fibre se bloating ho sakti hai aur inadequate fluid se stool pass karna difficult ho sakta hai.
- **B. Correct:** Whole grains aur plant foods fibre dete hain. Dheere increase karne aur adequate fluid lene se stool consistency improve ho sakti hai aur gas kam ho sakti hai.
- **C. Incorrect:** Straining se kaafi fibre hat sakta hai, isliye whole vegetables usually better fibre source hote hain.
- **D. Incorrect:** In foods ko bina clinical reason avoid karne se fibre intake aur diet variety kam ho sakti hai.

**Exam clue:** Is question mein Whole grains aur plant foods fibre dete hain, is baat ko option select karte waqt yaad rakho.

</details>

### Q9. A nurse compares a whole apple with an equal-carbohydrate portion of sweetened apple juice. Which feature of the whole fruit most directly supports slower carbohydrate delivery?

- **B.** Its higher saturated fat content
- **C.** Its inability to provide absorbable glucose

<details><summary>Answer aur detailed explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: D. Its intact cellular structure and fibre**

- **A. Incorrect:** Apple mein natural sugars hoti hain, isliye whole fruit sugar-free nahi hota.

- **C. Incorrect:** Whole fruit ke carbohydrates digest aur absorb ho sakte hain, bas speed aur food matrix alag ho sakte hain.
- **D. Correct:** Whole fruit mein fibre aur food structure intact rehte hain. Yeh chewing aur digestion ka pattern change karte hain, jisse carbohydrate delivery juice se slower ho sakti hai.

### Q13. A patient with severe hypoglycaemia is unconscious. Which action is unsafe?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Priority:** High | **Topic:** Carbohydrates | **Type:** Original practice

- **A.** Pouring a glucose drink into the patient's mouth
- **B.** Activating the urgent clinical response
- **C.** Checking blood glucose promptly without delaying emergency care
- **D.** Using protocol-authorized non-oral rescue treatment

<details><summary>Answer aur detailed explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: A. Pouring a glucose drink into the patient's mouth**

- **A. Correct:** Unconscious patient airway protect nahi kar sakta. Oral liquid lungs mein ja sakta hai, isliye emergency response aur prescribed parenteral treatment chahiye.
- **B. Incorrect:** Severe hypoglycaemia emergency hai. Help bulana aur timely treatment arrange karna appropriate hai.
- **C. Incorrect:** Bedside glucose finding treatment guide kar sakti hai, lekin emergency care delay nahi honi chahiye.
- **D. Incorrect:** Glucagon ya IV glucose jaise rescue options setting aur protocol ke hisaab se use ho sakte hain, kyunki oral route unsafe hai.

**Exam clue:** Is question mein Unconscious patient airway protect nahi kar sakta, is baat ko option select karte waqt yaad rakho.

</details>

- **D. Incorrect:** Insoluble fibre stool bulk aur bowel function mein help kar sakta hai, though individual tolerance vary karti hai.
