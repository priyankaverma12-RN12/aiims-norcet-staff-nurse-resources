### Q2. A family's typical daily menu includes cereals, pulses and curd but no fruit or vegetables. Which change most directly fills the missing food-group gap?

- **B.** Increase cereal portions without changing other foods
- **C.** Replace curd with clarified butter only
- **D.** Add only another portion of lentils

<details><summary>Answer aur detailed explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: A. Add varied seasonal vegetables and whole fruits**

- **A. Correct:** Existing meal cereals, pulses aur dairy cover karti hai. Fruits aur vegetables add karne se food-group diversity aur nutrient coverage improve hoti hai.
- **B. Incorrect:** Cereal already present hai. Extra cereal missing fruit aur vegetable groups replace nahi karta.
- **C. Incorrect:** Ghee fat provide karta hai, dairy protein aur other nutrients equivalent nahi. Missing produce gap remain karega.
- **D. Incorrect:** Lentils useful protein source hain, lekin fruits aur vegetables ki diversity replace nahi karte.

</details>

</details>
