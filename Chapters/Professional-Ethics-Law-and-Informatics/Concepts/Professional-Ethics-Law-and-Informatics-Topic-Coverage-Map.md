# Professional Ethics, Law and Nursing Informatics — Topic coverage map

This is a curriculum-based checklist, not full theory notes. No learning completion is recorded.

- Professional accountability
- ethics and patient rights
- informed consent and confidentiality
- legal documentation
- incident reporting
- health information systems
- data privacy and safe digital practice

## Initial study explanations

### Consent

Adult patient agar decision samajhkar competent hai, toh refusal ka right generally respect hota hai. Nurse concern clarify karti hai aur team ko inform karke accurate record rakhti hai.

### Confidentiality

Patient ki health information private hoti hai. Nurse pehle check karti hai ki requesting person ko information milne ka right hai ya nahi.

### Documentation

Clinical record mein correction honest aur traceable honi chahiye. Approved process se original history aur correction ka time identify hota hai.

### Informatics

Phishing email login details chura sakti hai. Nurse credentials share nahi karti aur official security team se verify karti hai.
