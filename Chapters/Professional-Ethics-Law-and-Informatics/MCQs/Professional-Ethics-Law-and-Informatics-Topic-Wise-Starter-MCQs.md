## Consent

### Q1. A competent adult refuses a recommended treatment after receiving understandable information. What is the appropriate response?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Topic:** Consent

- **A.** Respect the decision, assess understanding and document according to policy
- **B.** Force treatment because the nurse believes it is best
- **C.** Ask a family member to override every competent refusal
- **D.** Alter the medical record to show consent

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: A. Respect the decision, assess understanding and document according to policy**

Adult patient agar decision samajhkar competent hai, toh refusal ka right generally respect hota hai. Nurse concern clarify karti hai aur team ko inform karke accurate record rakhti hai.

**Baaki options kyun galat hain?**

- **B.** Force karna autonomy violate kar sakta hai.
- **C.** Family automatically competent adult ka decision override nahi karti.
- **D.** Record alter karna unethical aur unsafe hai.

</details>

## Confidentiality

### Q2. A nurse is asked for a patient's test results by a person whose authorization is unclear. Which response is appropriate?

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Topic:** Confidentiality

- **A.** Share all results because the person knows the patient's name
- **B.** Post the results in a public group
- **C.** Send the record to a personal account without approval
- **D.** Verify authorization before sharing information

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: D. Verify authorization before sharing information**

Patient ki health information private hoti hai. Nurse pehle check karti hai ki requesting person ko information milne ka right hai ya nahi.

**Baaki options kyun galat hain?**

- **A.** Naam jaanna authorization nahi hai.
- **B.** Public sharing privacy violate karti hai.
- **C.** Personal account unsafe aur policy ke against ho sakta hai.

</details>

## Documentation

### Q3. A nurse discovers an error in a previously entered electronic record. What is the appropriate correction?

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Topic:** Documentation

- **A.** Backdate a new entry without identifying the correction
- **B.** Ask another nurse to take responsibility for the entry
- **C.** Use the approved amendment process that preserves the audit trail
- **D.** Delete the original entry secretly

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: C. Use the approved amendment process that preserves the audit trail**

Clinical record mein correction honest aur traceable honi chahiye. Approved process se original history aur correction ka time identify hota hai.

**Baaki options kyun galat hain?**

- **A.** False backdating misleading hai.
- **B.** Doosre nurse par blame shift karna inappropriate hai.
- **D.** Secret deletion record integrity harm karti hai.

</details>

## Informatics

### Q4. A nurse receives an unexpected email requesting login credentials for the hospital record system. What should the nurse do?

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Topic:** Informatics

- **A.** Use the same password on every external website
- **B.** Do not provide credentials and report through the approved security channel
- **C.** Reply with the password if the email has a logo
- **D.** Forward confidential records as proof of identity

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: B. Do not provide credentials and report through the approved security channel**

Phishing email login details chura sakti hai. Nurse credentials share nahi karti aur official security team se verify karti hai.

**Baaki options kyun galat hain?**

- **A.** Password reuse security risk badhaata hai.
- **C.** Logo fake ho sakta hai.
- **D.** Patient records share karna privacy risk hai.

</details>
