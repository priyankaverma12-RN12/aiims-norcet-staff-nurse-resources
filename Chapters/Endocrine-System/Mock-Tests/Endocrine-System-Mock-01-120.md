### Q1. A patient has low morning cortisol with a markedly elevated ACTH level. Which site of dysfunction is most likely?

**Difficulty:** Difficult

- **A.** Adrenal cortex
- **B.** Hypothalamus with absent CRH release
- **C.** Anterior pituitary with absent ACTH release
- **D.** Posterior pituitary

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation</summary>

**Correct: A. Adrenal cortex**

**Kyun sahi hai?** Cortisol kam hai par pituitary ACTH ka signal zyada bhej rahi hai. Adrenal gland signal ke baad bhi cortisol nahi bana pa rahi, isliye primary adrenal failure suspect karo.

**Baaki options kyun galat hain?**

- **B.** CRH kam ho toh ACTH bhi kam ho sakta hai
- **C.** ACTH yahan high hai
- **D.** Posterior pituitary ADH aur oxytocin release karti hai

**Exam trick:** Primary gland failure often raises the upstream hormone.
