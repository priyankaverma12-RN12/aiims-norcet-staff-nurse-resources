### Q1. Which listed endocrine gland lies at the base of the brain?

**Reported exam:** ESIC Nursing Officer 2016 Shift 2 | **Difficulty:** Easy

**Evidence:** New question-level third-party result. [Original source / reported wording](https://nprep.in/question-answer/endocrine-gland-that-is-situated-in-the-brain-is-2mHD38Y22BOnuMsvPkKn-)

- **A.** Thyroid
- **B.** Adrenal
- **C.** Pituitary
- **D.** Parathyroid

<details><summary>✅ Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: C. Pituitary** — Pituitary sella turcica mein brain ke base par.

**Baaki options kyun nahi?** A/D neck; B kidneys ke upar.

**Exam trick:** Pituitary location near optic chiasm.

</details>

### Q2. Autoimmune type 1 diabetes primarily damages insulin-producing cells in which organ?

**Reported exam:** MP CHO 2019 Shift 1 | **Difficulty:** Easy

**Evidence:** New question-level third-party result. [Original source / reported wording](https://nprep.in/question-answer/which-gland-or-organ-does-not-function-properly-in-type-1-diabetes-6c-cYmT_UMk_U_ojSGPyk)

- **A.** Kidney
- **B.** Thyroid
- **C.** Adrenal
- **D.** Pancreas

<details><summary>✅ Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: D. Pancreas** — Pancreatic beta cells autoimmune destruction se insulin deficiency.

**Baaki options kyun nahi?** A kidney EPO/renin; B T3/T4; C steroids/catecholamines.

**Exam trick:** Beta = insulin.

</details>

### Q3. In iodine-deficiency goiter, reduced production of which hormone group may drive compensatory TSH stimulation?

**Reported exam:** MP CHO 2019 Shift 1 | **Difficulty:** Medium

**Evidence:** New third-party attribution; adapted to clarify oversimplified historical stem. [Original source / reported wording](https://nprep.in/question-answer/goitre-is-caused-by-the-deficiency-of-which-hormone-WO7e4X3MF4Dre0fk3T1zC)

- **A.** Thyroid hormones T3/T4
- **B.** Insulin
- **C.** ADH
- **D.** Aldosterone

<details><summary>✅ Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: A. Thyroid hormones T3/T4** — Iodine T3/T4 synthesis ke liye; low hormone feedback TSH increase kar sakta hai.

**Baaki options kyun nahi?** B glucose; C water; D salt regulation.

**Exam trick:** Goiter itself can be euthyroid, hypo or hyper; not all caused by hormone deficiency.

</details>
