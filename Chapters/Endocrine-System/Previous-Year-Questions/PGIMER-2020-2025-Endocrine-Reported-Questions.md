### Q1. Which finding is least typical of untreated primary hypothyroidism?

**Reported exam:** PGIMER Nursing Officer 2020 | **Difficulty:** Easy

**Evidence:** New question-level third-party attribution. [Original source / reported wording](https://nprep.in/question-answer/features-of-hypothyroidism-except-1ZmMIyfLjrHgWEuSqKhgz)

- **A.** Cold intolerance
- **B.** Constipation
- **C.** Persistent heat intolerance with tachycardia
- **D.** Dry skin

<details><summary>✅ Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: C. Persistent heat intolerance with tachycardia** — Low thyroid hormone metabolism slow karta hai. Heat and tachycardia more hyperthyroid pattern.

**Baaki options kyun nahi?** A/B/D classic hypo clues.

**Exam trick:** EXCEPT question carefully read.

</details>

### Q2. Why may prescribed iodine solution be used before thyroid surgery for hyperthyroidism?

**Reported exam:** PGIMER Sangrur Nursing Officer 2023 | **Difficulty:** Medium

**Evidence:** New question-level third-party attribution. [Original source / reported wording](https://nprep.in/question-answer/a-client-is-scheduled-for-a-subtotal-thyroidectomy-iodine-solution-lugol-solution-NMPLv8Bi6UDbgvrw6uWG7)

- **A.** To increase gland blood flow
- **B.** To increase thyroid hormone production
- **C.** To cause permanent diabetes
- **D.** To reduce thyroid vascularity and operative bleeding

<details><summary>✅ Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: D. To reduce thyroid vascularity and operative bleeding** — Selected preoperative short-term iodine thyroid vascularity and hormone release reduce kar sakta hai.

**Baaki options kyun nahi?** A opposite; B not goal; C unrelated.

**Exam trick:** Lugol pre-op only prescribed short course, not all patients.

</details>

### Q3. Which symptoms suggest an early adrenergic response to low blood glucose?

**Reported exam:** PGIMER Nursing Officer 2020 Q56 | **Difficulty:** Easy

**Evidence:** Existing lead; question number from earlier register; full page failed. [Original source / reported wording](https://nextnurse.in/pyq/pgimer-nursing-officer/pgimer-nursing-officer-pgimer-no-2020)

- **A.** Sweating and trembling
- **B.** Dry skin and slow pulse
- **C.** Purple striae
- **D.** Carpal spasm

<details><summary>✅ Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: A. Sweating and trembling** — Adrenaline counter-regulation sweating and tremor produce karti hai.

**Baaki options kyun nahi?** B hypothyroid; C cortisol excess; D low calcium.

**Exam trick:** Early adrenergic symptoms may be absent in impaired awareness.

</details>

### Q4. An adult reports polyuria and polydipsia. Which laboratory finding, in the presence of these classic symptoms, supports diabetes diagnosis?

**Reported exam:** PGIMER Nursing Officer 2025 Q71 | **Difficulty:** Medium

**Evidence:** Existing lead, Q71 search index; adapted test threshold, exact original options unavailable. [Original source / reported wording](https://nextnurse.in/pyq/pgimer-nursing-officer/pgimer-nursing-officer-pgimer-nursing-officer-2025)

- **A.** Random plasma glucose 110 mg/dL
- **B.** Random plasma glucose ≥200 mg/dL
- **C.** Normal serum calcium
- **D.** Low serum sodium alone

<details><summary>✅ Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: B. Random plasma glucose ≥200 mg/dL** — Classic symptoms + random plasma glucose ≥200 diabetes criterion. Appropriate plasma test use karo.

**Baaki options kyun nahi?** A below criterion; C/D not diagnostic glucose tests.

**Exam trick:** Symptoms plus random threshold; no symptoms may need confirmation.

</details>
