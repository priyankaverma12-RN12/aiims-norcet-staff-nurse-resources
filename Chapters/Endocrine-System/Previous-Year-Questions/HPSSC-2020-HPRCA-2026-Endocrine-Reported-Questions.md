### Q1. Which prescribed substance may be used short term in selected patients with hyperthyroidism?

**Reported exam:** HPSSC Staff Nurse 2020 Series A Q19 | **Difficulty:** Medium

**Evidence:** New numbered question in opened third-party transcription. [Original source / reported wording](https://hpgeneralstudies.com/solved-paper-hp-staff-nurse-exam-2020-hpssc-hamirpur-part-1/)

- **A.** Levothyroxine replacement
- **B.** Thyroglobulin supplement
- **C.** Lugol iodine solution
- **D.** Insulin glargine

<details><summary>✅ Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: C. Lugol iodine solution** — Lugol iodine can reduce hormone release and gland vascularity in selected settings, e.g. pre-op/storm sequence.

**Baaki options kyun nahi?** A thyroid replacement; B not standard treatment; D glucose drug.

**Exam trick:** Old paper option is not first-line universal therapy.

</details>

### Q2. Deep, labored compensatory respirations are characteristic of which metabolic emergency?

**Reported exam:** HPSSC Staff Nurse 2020 Series A Q21 | **Difficulty:** Easy

**Evidence:** New numbered question in opened third-party transcription. [Original source / reported wording](https://hpgeneralstudies.com/solved-paper-hp-staff-nurse-exam-2020-hpssc-hamirpur-part-1/)

- **A.** Uncomplicated hypothyroidism
- **B.** Diabetic ketoacidosis
- **C.** Isolated hypocalcemia
- **D.** Stable goiter

<details><summary>✅ Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: B. Diabetic ketoacidosis** — Ketones metabolic acidosis cause karte hain; lungs CO2 blow off with Kussmaul breathing.

**Baaki options kyun nahi?** A severe hypo may hypoventilate; C tetany; D neck enlargement alone.

**Exam trick:** Kussmaul = metabolic acidosis compensation.

</details>

### Q3. A pheochromocytoma most commonly arises from which adrenal region?

**Reported exam:** HPSSC Staff Nurse 2020 Series A Q22 | **Difficulty:** Easy

**Evidence:** Previously recorded gland question, number now confirmed in transcription; adapted region. [Original source / reported wording](https://hpgeneralstudies.com/solved-paper-hp-staff-nurse-exam-2020-hpssc-hamirpur-part-1/)

- **A.** Cortex glomerulosa
- **B.** Anterior pituitary
- **C.** Pancreatic beta cells
- **D.** Adrenal medulla

<details><summary>✅ Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: D. Adrenal medulla** — Chromaffin cells catecholamines produce karte hain; pheochromocytoma typically medulla.

**Baaki options kyun nahi?** A aldosterone; B pituitary hormones; C insulin.

**Exam trick:** Medulla = catecholamines.

</details>

### Q4. For a standard fasting 75-g oral glucose tolerance test in a nonpregnant adult, which preparation is appropriate?

**Reported exam:** HPSSC Staff Nurse 2020 Series A Q52 | **Difficulty:** Medium

**Evidence:** New numbered transcription; historical option set ambiguous, no official key checked. [Original source / reported wording](https://hpgeneralstudies.com/solved-paper-hp-staff-nurse-exam-2020-hpssc-hamirpur-part-1/)

- **A.** Fast for at least 8 hours as instructed
- **B.** Stop all medication independently
- **C.** Avoid carbohydrate for several days
- **D.** Eat immediately before the test

<details><summary>✅ Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: A. Fast for at least 8 hours as instructed** — Standard OGTT fasting ≥8 h and adequate usual carbohydrate intake beforehand; follow lab instructions.

**Baaki options kyun nahi?** B unsafe; C inadequate carbohydrate may affect result; D invalid fasting sample.

**Exam trick:** Historical wording can be ambiguous because adequate carbohydrate preparation also matters.

</details>

### Q5. Which two hormones are released from the posterior pituitary?

**Reported exam:** HP Staff Nurse, 5 May 2026 Shift 2 (reported) | **Difficulty:** Easy

**Evidence:** Existing unverified HPRCA lead; page could not reopen; NOT a confirmed question. [Original source / reported wording](https://rojgarbee.com/himachal-pradesh/staff-nurse/old-question-papers/25026-shift-2-05-may-2026)

- **A.** GH and TSH
- **B.** ADH and oxytocin
- **C.** ACTH and prolactin
- **D.** FSH and LH

<details><summary>✅ Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: B. ADH and oxytocin** — ADH and oxytocin hypothalamus mein synthesize hote hain, posterior pituitary releases.

**Baaki options kyun nahi?** A/C/D anterior pituitary hormones.

**Exam trick:** Made vs released important.

</details>
