### Q1. A patient develops carpal spasm after thyroid removal. Which electrolyte disturbance should be suspected?

**Reported exam:** NORCET 2022, recall collection | **Difficulty:** Medium

**Evidence:** Previously recorded recall; not reopened in this pass. [Original source / reported wording](https://id.scribd.com/document/883792179/AIIMS-NORCET-2022-Memory-Based-Paper-11th-September-2022-English)

- **A.** Hypernatremia
- **B.** Hypocalcemia
- **C.** Hyperkalemia
- **D.** Hyperglycemia

<details><summary>✅ Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: B. Hypocalcemia** — Parathyroid function surgery ke baad affected ho sakta hai, Ca kam hone se tetany.

**Baaki options kyun nahi?** A sodium, C potassium aur D glucose disturbance classic carpal spasm pattern nahi.

**Exam trick:** Thyroid surgery + tetany = check calcium.

</details>

**Reported exam:** NORCET 11 Mains, 2026 | **Difficulty:** Difficult

**Evidence:** New question-level third-party result. [Original source / reported wording](https://nprep.in/question-answer/a-client-with-diabetic-ketoacidosis-has-a-serum-potassium-of-3-0-CsuoAO0ITEFoHBxOX4Bt7)

- **A.** Start infusion immediately without review
- **B.** Escalate low potassium and replace it before insulin per protocol
- **C.** Give an oral potassium-free snack only
- **D.** Stop all monitoring

<details><summary>✅ Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: B. Escalate low potassium and replace it before insulin per protocol** — Insulin K cells mein shift karta hai; 3.0 already low. Current adult consensus insulin delay if K <3.5 until >3.5.

**Baaki options kyun nahi?** A dangerous arrhythmia; C insufficient; D unsafe.

**Exam trick:** Older threshold 3.3, newer adult consensus 3.5; at 3.0 both require correction.
