### Q01. An adult has progressive acral enlargement and an IGF-1 result of 310 ng/mL. The laboratory lists different reference intervals for different age groups. Which comparison is appropriate before interpreting the result?

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Priority:** 🟠 High yield | **Type:** Original practice

A. Use the pediatric reference interval for every adult

B. Compare the result directly with serum GH in ng/mL

C. Use the same numerical upper limit for all laboratories

D. Use the age-appropriate upper limit for the laboratory assay

<details>
<summary>Show answer and explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: D. Use the age-appropriate upper limit for the laboratory assay**

- **A — Incorrect:** Bachchon aur adolescents mein growth ki wajah se IGF-1 ka level alag ho sakta hai. Unki range adult par lagane se galat interpretation ho sakti hai.
- **B — Incorrect:** GH aur IGF-1 do alag hormones hain. Inki numerical values ko seedha compare karke IGF-1 ka abnormal hona decide nahi kar sakte.
- **C — Incorrect:** Alag laboratories ki testing methods aur reference ranges mein difference ho sakta hai. Ek fixed number sab reports par fit nahi hota.
- **D — Correct:** IGF-1 ki normal range age aur test ki method ke hisaab se badalti hai. Isliye nurse report par di hui patient ki age wali range ko specialist ke saath compare karti hai.

**Exam clue:** IGF-1 ko hamesha age aur assay ke reference interval ke saath padho.

### Q20. An adult with confirmed acromegaly has a resectable pituitary adenoma and is an appropriate operative candidate. Which treatment pathway should the nurse prepare the patient to discuss with the multidisciplinary team?

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Priority:** 🟠 High yield | **Type:** Original practice

A. Routine thyroidectomy as the initial GH-directed operation

B. Lifelong insulin as the only treatment for GH excess

C. Observation alone in every symptomatic resectable case

D. Transsphenoidal pituitary surgery as a usual initial option

<details>
<summary>Show answer and explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: D. Transsphenoidal pituitary surgery as a usual initial option**

- **A — Incorrect:** Thyroidectomy thyroid gland ko treat karti hai. GH-secreting pituitary lesion ko remove nahi karti.
- **B — Incorrect:** Insulin diabetes manage kar sakta hai, lekin pituitary adenoma aur GH excess ka direct treatment nahi hai.
- **C — Incorrect:** Observation har symptomatic resectable case mein appropriate nahi hai. Active disease ki morbidity aur treatment benefit ko team assess karti hai.
- **D — Correct:** Resectable GH-secreting pituitary adenoma mein transsphenoidal surgery aksar initial treatment option hoti hai. Team lesion aur patient ki operative suitability ke hisaab se decision leti hai.

**Exam clue:** Hormone-producing lesion ki location se definitive treatment ka target samjho.
