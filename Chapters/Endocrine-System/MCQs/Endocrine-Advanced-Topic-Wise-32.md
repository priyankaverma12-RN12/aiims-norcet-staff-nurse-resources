## Hormone control and assessment

### Q1. A patient has low morning cortisol with a markedly elevated ACTH level. Which site of dysfunction is most likely?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Exam focus:** NORCET / Staff Nurse

- **A.** Adrenal cortex
- **B.** Hypothalamus with absent CRH release
- **C.** Anterior pituitary with absent ACTH release
- **D.** Posterior pituitary

<details><summary>✅ Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: A. Adrenal cortex**

**Kyun sahi hai?** Cortisol kam hai par pituitary ACTH ka signal zyada bhej rahi hai. Adrenal gland signal ke baad bhi cortisol nahi bana pa rahi, isliye primary adrenal failure suspect karo.

**Baaki options kyun galat hain?**

- **B.** CRH kam ho toh ACTH bhi kam ho sakta hai
- **C.** ACTH yahan high hai
- **D.** Posterior pituitary ADH aur oxytocin release karti hai

**Exam trick:** Primary gland failure often raises the upstream hormone.

</details>

### Q2. A pregnant patient's total T4 increases while free T4 and TSH remain appropriate for pregnancy. Which explanation is most likely?

<details><summary>✅ Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Kyun sahi hai?** Pregnancy mein hormone ko blood mein pakadkar rakhne wale proteins badh sakte hain. Total hormone badh sakta hai bina free hormone zyada hue.

- **A.** Storm clinical emergency hai, total T4 alone proof nahi
- **B.** T4 absent nahi hai

**Exam trick:** Total and free hormones are different.

</details>

### Q31. A patient taking acarbose with insulin develops hypoglycemia while awake and able to swallow. Which rapid carbohydrate is most suitable?

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Exam focus:** NORCET / Staff Nurse

- **A.** Additional acarbose
- **B.** Glucose tablets or glucose gel
- **C.** Sucrose-rich food as the only preferred option
- **D.** A high-fat meal as the only immediate treatment

<details><summary>✅ Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: B. Glucose tablets or glucose gel**

**Kyun sahi hai?** Acarbose complex carbohydrate aur sucrose breakdown slow kar sakti hai. Direct glucose jaldi absorb hota hai.

**Baaki options kyun galat hain?**

- **A.** Acarbose low sugar treat nahi karti
- **C.** Sucrose breakdown delay ho sakta hai
- **D.** Fat rapid correction slow kar sakta hai

**Exam trick:** Acarbose-associated hypoglycemia: use glucose.

</details>

### Q32. A patient using a continuous glucose monitor has a rapidly falling sensor reading that does not match symptoms. Which action is appropriate?

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Exam focus:** NORCET / Staff Nurse

- **A.** Confirm with an appropriate capillary glucose check when indicated
- **B.** Give a large correction insulin dose without checking
- **C.** Ignore symptoms because sensor readings are infallible
- **D.** Share a used lancet to confirm quickly

<details><summary>✅ Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: A. Confirm with an appropriate capillary glucose check when indicated**

**Kyun sahi hai?** CGM skin ke neeche fluid mein glucose measure karta hai. Rapid change mein blood glucose se thoda late ho sakta hai. Meter check useful hai.

**Baaki options kyun galat hain?**

- **B.** Insulin low glucose worsen kar sakta hai
- **C.** Sensor error possible
- **D.** Lancet sharing infection risk

**Exam trick:** CGM lag can occur during rapid changes.

</details>
