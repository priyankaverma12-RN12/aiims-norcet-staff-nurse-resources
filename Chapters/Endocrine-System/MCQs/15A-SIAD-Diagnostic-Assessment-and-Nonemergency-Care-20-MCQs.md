### Q01. Which term describes the inappropriate retention of water as the central process in SIAD?

B. Antidiuresis

C. Hematuria

D. Ketonuria

<summary>Show answer and explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: B. Antidiuresis**

- **A — Incorrect:** Glycosuria mein urine mein glucose milta hai. Yeh SIAD mein paani retention ki primary process nahi hai.
