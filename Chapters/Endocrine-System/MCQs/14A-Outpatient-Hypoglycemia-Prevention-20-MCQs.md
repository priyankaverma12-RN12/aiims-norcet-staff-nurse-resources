### Q01. Which supply should a workplace first-aid kit include specifically to help treat low glucose in an alert adult who can swallow?

**Difficulty:** Easy | **Priority:** 🟠 High yield | **Type:** Original practice

A. Oral glucose tablets

B. Glucose-free electrolyte tablets

C. An extra dose of basal insulin

D. A high-fiber laxative

<details>
<summary>Show answer and explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: A. Oral glucose tablets**

- **A — Correct:** Glucose tablet jaldi absorb hone wali sugar deti hai. Alert patient mein low glucose ko treat karne ke liye ise first-aid kit mein rakhna useful hai.
- **B — Incorrect:** Electrolyte tablet mein glucose na ho toh woh blood sugar ko jaldi nahi badhayegi.
- **C — Incorrect:** Basal insulin glucose ko aur gira sakti hai, isliye low sugar ka treatment nahi hai.
- **D — Incorrect:** Laxative constipation ke liye hoti hai aur low glucose ka immediate correction nahi karti.

**Exam clue:** First-aid glucose must contain usable carbohydrate.
