D. Assess sleep, mood, daily functioning and support needs rather than dismissing symptoms because biomedical markers are stable

<summary>Show answer and explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: D. Assess sleep, mood, daily functioning and support needs rather than dismissing symptoms because biomedical markers are stable**
