### Q01. A patient with suspected pheochromocytoma has episodic symptoms but feels well during the clinic visit. Why can fractionated metanephrines remain useful even between attacks?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Priority:** 🟠 High yield | **Type:** Original practice

A. Metanephrines are produced only during a hypertensive attack

B. A symptom-free interval prevents all catecholamine metabolism

C. Metanephrines measure adrenal cortisol production rather than catecholamine metabolism

D. Tumor cells can continuously metabolize catecholamines to metanephrines independently of episodic secretion

<details>
<summary>Show answer and explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: D. Tumor cells can continuously metabolize catecholamines to metanephrines independently of episodic secretion**

- **A — Incorrect:** Metanephrine formation sirf symptomatic attack tak limited nahi hai. Attack ke time sample lena always required nahi hota.
- **B — Incorrect:** Symptoms absent hone ka matlab metabolism stop hona nahi hai. Biochemical testing episodic symptoms se independently informative ho sakti hai.
- **C — Incorrect:** Metanephrines catecholamine metabolites hain. Cortisol production ka assessment different hormone tests se hota hai.
- **D — Correct:** Tumor ke andar catecholamines ka metabolism continuously ho sakta hai, even jab patient ko attack feel nahi ho raha. Isliye fractionated metanephrines useful biochemical marker hain.

**Exam clue:** Episodic catecholamine release aur continuous metabolite formation distinguish karo.
