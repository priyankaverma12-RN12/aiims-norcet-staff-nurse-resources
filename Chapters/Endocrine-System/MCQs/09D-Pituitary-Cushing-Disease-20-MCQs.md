- **D — Incorrect:** Dono adrenal glands remove hone ke baad removed glands mein new adenoma ka usual scenario nahi hai. Main concern remaining pituitary lesion hai.

**Exam clue:** Bilateral adrenalectomy ke baad adrenal replacement aur pituitary tumor surveillance dono important hain.

### Q20. A patient in remission after Cushing disease treatment asks why cardiovascular and bone-health follow-up is still planned even though cortisol has improved. Which response is most appropriate?

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Priority:** 🟠 High yield | **Type:** Original practice

A. Previous cortisol excess can leave persistent comorbidity risk that needs individualized ongoing care

B. Normal cortisol immediately reverses all vascular and skeletal changes

C. Bone and cardiovascular assessments are useful only during active pituitary surgery

D. All previous hypertension treatment should be discontinued automatically after remission

<details>
<summary>Show answer and explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: A. Previous cortisol excess can leave persistent comorbidity risk that needs individualized ongoing care**

- **A — Correct:** Chronic cortisol excess ke vascular, metabolic aur bone effects remission ke baad bhi persist kar sakte hain. Ongoing individualized assessment recovery aur prevention mein help karta hai.
- **B — Incorrect:** Har complication instantly reverse nahi hoti. Residual risk ko monitor karna zaroori hai.
- **C — Incorrect:** Follow-up surgery period tak limited nahi hai. Bone aur cardiovascular complications long-term care ka part hain.
- **D — Incorrect:** BP improve ho sakta hai, lekin medication change measured BP aur clinical review ke basis par hota hai.

**Exam clue:** Biochemical remission ke baad bhi comorbidity management complete karo.
