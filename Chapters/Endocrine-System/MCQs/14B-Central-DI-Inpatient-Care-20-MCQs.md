### Q01. Which alternative diagnostic term on a hospital alert identifies established central diabetes insipidus?

**Difficulty:** Easy | **Priority:** 🟠 High yield | **Type:** Original practice

A. Arginine vasopressin resistance

B. Arginine vasopressin deficiency

C. Type 1 diabetes mellitus

D. Primary polydipsia

<details>
<summary>Show answer and explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: B. Arginine vasopressin deficiency**

- **A — Incorrect:** Resistance mein kidney hormone ka signal theek se accept nahi karti. Yeh nephrogenic DI ka mechanism hai.
- **B — Correct:** Central DI mein body vasopressin kam banati ya release karti hai. Naya naam isi hormone ki kami ko directly batata hai.
- **C — Incorrect:** Type 1 diabetes mein insulin ki kami hoti hai. Yeh glucose control ki disease hai.
- **D — Incorrect:** Primary polydipsia mein zarurat se zyada paani peena primary problem hoti hai. Isse central hormone deficiency prove nahi hoti.
