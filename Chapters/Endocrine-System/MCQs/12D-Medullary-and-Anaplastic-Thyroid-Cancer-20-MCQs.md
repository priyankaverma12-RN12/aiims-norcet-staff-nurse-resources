### Q01. A thyroid tumor biopsy shows neuroendocrine morphology with positive calcitonin and chromogranin staining but negative thyroglobulin staining. Which tumor type does this pattern support?

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Priority:** 🟠 High yield | **Type:** Original practice

A. Conventional papillary thyroid carcinoma

B. Benign follicular adenoma

C. Medullary thyroid carcinoma

D. Conventional follicular thyroid carcinoma

<details>
<summary>Show answer and explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: C. Medullary thyroid carcinoma**

- **A — Incorrect:** Papillary carcinoma bhi follicular-derived malignancy hai. Calcitonin-positive neuroendocrine pattern medullary differentiation ko support karta hai.
- **B — Incorrect:** Follicular adenoma benign follicular cell neoplasm hai. Given immunostaining pattern alag cellular differentiation suggest karta hai.
- **C — Correct:** Calcitonin aur chromogranin ka neuroendocrine pattern parafollicular C-cell origin support karta hai. Medullary carcinoma mein thyroglobulin generally negative hota hai.
- **D — Incorrect:** Follicular carcinoma follicular epithelium se arise karti hai aur often thyroglobulin express karti hai. Given neuroendocrine pattern uske liye typical nahi hai.

**Exam clue:** Immunostaining helps distinguish C-cell from follicular-cell tumors.
