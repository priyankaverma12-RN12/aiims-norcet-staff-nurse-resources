### Q01. A patient with type 1 diabetes uses a pump containing only rapid-acting insulin. The pump unexpectedly stops delivering basal insulin. Why is this interruption especially concerning?

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Priority:** 🟠 High yield | **Type:** Original practice

A. Rapid-acting insulin continues to provide full basal coverage for several days

B. There is little sustained insulin depot, so insulin deficiency and ketone formation may develop quickly

C. The pump reservoir provides a long-acting subcutaneous insulin depot after delivery stops

D. Stopping the pump directly increases endogenous insulin secretion

<details>
<summary>Show answer and explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: B. There is little sustained insulin depot, so insulin deficiency and ketone formation may develop quickly**

- **A — Incorrect:** Rapid-acting insulin ka effect limited hours tak hota hai. Several days ki basal coverage assume karna unsafe hai.
- **B — Correct:** Pump mein rapid-acting insulin chhoti amounts mein continuously deliver hoti hai. Delivery rukne par long-acting insulin depot generally nahi hota, isliye insulin deficiency aur ketones jaldi develop ho sakte hain.
- **C — Incorrect:** Reservoir mein insulin device ke andar hoti hai, body mein long-acting depot nahi banta. Delivery stop hone par reservoir insulin automatically absorb nahi hoti.
- **D — Incorrect:** Type 1 diabetes mein endogenous insulin production inadequate hoti hai. Pump stop karna pancreas ki insulin production restore nahi karta.

**Exam clue:** Pump interruption ko long-acting insulin coverage ke absence se link karo.

### Q20. A pump and CGM user repeatedly silences alerts because of alarm fatigue and has started missing important urgent warnings. Which education strategy is safest?

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Priority:** 🟠 High yield | **Type:** Original practice

A. Assume missed alerts have no clinical consequences when an automated system is used

B. Review alert burden with the diabetes team and individualize allowed settings while preserving essential safety alerts

<details>
<summary>Show answer and explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: B. Review alert burden with the diabetes team and individualize allowed settings while preserving essential safety alerts**

- **A — Incorrect:** Automated systems bhi alerts aur user response par depend kar sakte hain. Missed warnings clinically important ho sakti hain.
- **B — Correct:** Frequent alarms se fatigue ho sakti hai aur important warnings miss ho sakti hain. Team allowed settings individualize karke essential safety alerts aur action plan preserve karti hai.
- **C — Incorrect:** All alerts permanently disable karne se important hypoglycemia ya device failure warnings miss ho sakti hain. Unsupervised blanket disabling unsafe hai.
- **D — Incorrect:** Excessive nonurgent alerts burden increase kar sakte hain. Meaningful, individualized settings and training more useful hain.

**Exam clue:** Alarm fatigue ko acknowledge karo, lekin critical safety alerts preserve karo.
