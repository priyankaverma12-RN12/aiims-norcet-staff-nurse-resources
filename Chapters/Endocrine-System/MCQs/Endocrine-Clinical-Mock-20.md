### Q1. An adult with diabetes has glucose 185 mg/dL, beta-hydroxybutyrate 4.5 mmol/L and venous pH 7.23. Which diagnosis should be considered?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Exam focus:** NORCET / Staff Nurse

- **A.** Respiratory alkalosis as the primary disorder
- **B.** Diabetic ketoacidosis despite modest glucose elevation
- **C.** HHS based solely on glucose
- **D.** Normal glucose metabolism because glucose is below 200

<details><summary>✅ Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: B. Diabetic ketoacidosis despite modest glucose elevation**

**Kyun sahi hai?** Known diabetes, ketones high aur pH low hai. DKA mein glucose har baar bahut high nahi hoti. SGLT2 use aur illness history bhi assess karo.

**Baaki options kyun galat hain?**

- **A.** pH low hai, primary metabolic acidosis possible
- **C.** HHS criteria meet nahi
- **D.** Ketones and acidosis abnormal hain

**Exam trick:** Euglycemic DKA can occur.

</details>

### Q2. An adult has sodium 145 mmol/L, glucose 810 mg/dL and BUN 28 mg/dL. What is the estimated effective serum osmolality?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Exam focus:** NORCET / Staff Nurse

- **A.** 335 mOsm/kg
- **B.** 345 mOsm/kg
- **C.** 290 mOsm/kg
- **D.** 810 mOsm/kg

<details><summary>✅ Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: A. 335 mOsm/kg**

**Kyun sahi hai?** Effective = 2 x 145 + 810/18 = 290 + 45 = 335. BUN total osmolality mein add hota hai, effective mein nahi.

**Baaki options kyun galat hain?**

- **B.** BUN add karke total value 345 aati hai
- **C.** Glucose term missing hai
- **D.** Glucose alone osmolality nahi

**Exam trick:** Effective osmolality excludes BUN.

</details>

### Q3. A patient with DKA has glucose reduced to 220 mg/dL but still has marked ketonemia and acidosis. What is the appropriate protocol principle?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Exam focus:** NORCET / Staff Nurse

- **A.** Stop insulin immediately because glucose improved
- **B.** Give only oral water and discharge
- **C.** Stop checking electrolytes
- **D.** Continue prescribed insulin with dextrose when indicated until ketoacidosis resolves

<details><summary>✅ Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: D. Continue prescribed insulin with dextrose when indicated until ketoacidosis resolves**

**Kyun sahi hai?** Insulin ketones banana rokta hai. Sugar kam hone par dextrose add kar sakte hain taaki insulin safely chale jab tak ketones clear ho.

**Baaki options kyun galat hain?**

- **A.** DKA glucose alone se resolve nahi
- **B.** Acidosis still dangerous
- **C.** K and other labs still needed

**Exam trick:** Treat ketones, not only glucose.

</details>

### Q4. A patient with HHS is becoming more drowsy during treatment and osmolality is falling rapidly. Which nursing response is most appropriate?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Exam focus:** NORCET / Staff Nurse

- **A.** Stop all neurological observations
- **B.** Assume drowsiness always indicates recovery
- **C.** Urgent neurological reassessment and review of the correction rate
- **D.** Accelerate glucose and osmolality correction independently

<details><summary>✅ Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: C. Urgent neurological reassessment and review of the correction rate**

**Kyun sahi hai?** Blood concentration bahut jaldi badle toh brain water balance affect ho sakta hai. Worsening alertness immediate review chahiye.

**Baaki options kyun galat hain?**

- **A.** Neuro checks essential
- **B.** Drowsiness danger sign hai
- **D.** Rapid correction harm kar sakti hai

**Exam trick:** HHS correction must be gradual.

</details>

### Q5. A hospitalized patient receiving long-acting insulin has repeated hypoglycemia after initial treatment. Which action is appropriate?

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Exam focus:** NORCET / Staff Nurse

- **A.** Give an additional long-acting insulin dose
- **B.** Assume recurrent hypoglycemia cannot occur
- **C.** Continue glucose surveillance and request review of the regimen and intake
- **D.** Stop all glucose checks after one normal reading

<details><summary>✅ Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: C. Continue glucose surveillance and request review of the regimen and intake**

**Kyun sahi hai?** Long-acting insulin ka effect kaafi der tak reh sakta hai. Sugar phir gir sakti hai. Repeat checks aur dose/food review chahiye.

**Baaki options kyun galat hain?**

- **A.** Extra insulin risk
- **B.** Recurrent lows possible
- **D.** One normal reading enough nahi

**Exam trick:** Treat and prevent recurrence.

</details>

### Q6. A patient with diabetes develops a red, swollen foot with spreading redness and fever despite little pain. What is the priority?

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Exam focus:** NORCET / Staff Nurse

- **A.** Reassure because severe pain is absent
- **B.** Urgent evaluation for diabetic foot infection and systemic illness
- **C.** Routine nail trimming before assessment
- **D.** Encourage barefoot walking

<details><summary>✅ Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: B. Urgent evaluation for diabetic foot infection and systemic illness**

**Kyun sahi hai?** Nerves damage hone se pain kam feel ho sakta hai. Redness aur fever infection ke warning signs hain. Urgent assessment chahiye.

**Baaki options kyun galat hain?**

- **A.** No pain does not exclude infection
- **C.** Nail care not priority
- **D.** Barefoot injury risk

**Exam trick:** Diabetic neuropathy can mask severe foot infection.

</details>

### Q7. A patient with type 2 diabetes has elevated urine albumin-to-creatinine ratio on repeat testing but preserved eGFR. What is the significance?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Exam focus:** NORCET / Staff Nurse

- **A.** Early kidney damage may be present despite preserved filtration
- **B.** Normal eGFR excludes all kidney disease
- **C.** Urine albumin proves acute thyroid storm
- **D.** Kidney screening is unnecessary until dialysis

<details><summary>✅ Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: A. Early kidney damage may be present despite preserved filtration**

**Kyun sahi hai?** Kidney filter thoda damage ho toh albumin urine mein leak ho sakta hai even filtration rate abhi normal ho. Follow-up needed.

**Baaki options kyun galat hain?**

- **B.** eGFR and albumin different markers
- **C.** Thyroid storm unrelated
- **D.** Early screening important

**Exam trick:** Albuminuria can precede reduced eGFR.

</details>

### Q8. During community diabetes screening, a patient aged 26 reports marked thirst, frequent urination and weight loss. What should the nurse do?

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Exam focus:** NORCET / Staff Nurse

- **A.** Refuse evaluation until age 30
- **B.** Give a diagnosis without glucose testing
- **C.** Advise waiting until pregnancy
- **D.** Arrange prompt clinical assessment regardless of routine screening age

<details><summary>✅ Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: D. Arrange prompt clinical assessment regardless of routine screening age**

**Kyun sahi hai?** Routine NP-NCD screening age 30+ hai, par symptoms wale kisi bhi age ke patient ko assessment chahiye.

**Baaki options kyun galat hain?**

- **A.** Age threshold routine screening ke liye
- **B.** Testing needed
- **C.** Delay unsafe

**Exam trick:** Screening age is not a barrier to symptomatic evaluation.

</details>

### Q9. A woman with previous gestational diabetes asks whether a normal postpartum glucose result means no further testing is needed. Which advice is appropriate?

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Exam focus:** NORCET / Staff Nurse

- **B.** Use thyroid tests instead of glucose tests
- **C.** Take insulin permanently without medical assessment
- **D.** Continue periodic diabetes screening because future type 2 diabetes risk remains elevated

<details><summary>✅ Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: D. Continue periodic diabetes screening because future type 2 diabetes risk remains elevated**

**Kyun sahi hai?** Pregnancy ke baad sugar normal ho sakti hai par future diabetes ka risk zyada rehta hai. Regular follow-up useful hai.

**Baaki options kyun galat hain?**

- **A.** Risk persists
- **B.** TSH glucose test nahi

</details>

### Q10. A newborn of a mother with poorly controlled diabetes develops jitteriness shortly after birth. Which assessment is particularly important?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Exam focus:** NORCET / Staff Nurse

- **A.** Only the newborn's height
- **B.** Serum adult HbA1c as the sole emergency test
- **C.** Blood glucose for neonatal hypoglycemia
- **D.** Maternal thyroid ultrasound as the immediate newborn test

<details><summary>✅ Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: C. Blood glucose for neonatal hypoglycemia**

**Kyun sahi hai?** Pregnancy mein baby zyada insulin bana sakta hai. Delivery ke baad maternal glucose supply band hoti hai, insulin effect se sugar gir sakti hai. Neonatal protocol follow karo.

**Baaki options kyun galat hain?**

- **A.** Height acute clue nahi
- **B.** A1c acute glucose nahi batata
- **D.** Mother thyroid scan newborn low sugar nahi batata

**Exam trick:** Infant of diabetic mother may develop low glucose.

</details>

### Q11. A patient with suspected myxedema emergency develops severe hypoventilation after sedative administration. What is the priority?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Exam focus:** NORCET / Staff Nurse

- **A.** Wait for routine outpatient thyroid tests
- **B.** Support airway and ventilation and obtain emergency medical assistance
- **C.** Give another sedative for comfort before assessment
- **D.** Start vigorous external heating as the only action

<details><summary>✅ Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: B. Support airway and ventilation and obtain emergency medical assistance**

**Kyun sahi hai?** Severe hypothyroid mein saans slow ho sakti hai. Sedative saans aur kam kar sakti hai. ABC first.

**Baaki options kyun galat hain?**

- **A.** Emergency wait nahi kar sakti
- **C.** More sedation dangerous
- **D.** Heating alone ventilation correct nahi

**Exam trick:** Myxedema emergency threatens breathing.

</details>

### Q12. A patient receiving methimazole reports fever, painful swallowing and mouth ulcers. What should the nurse arrange?

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Exam focus:** NORCET / Staff Nurse

- **A.** Urgent clinical assessment and blood count for possible agranulocytosis
- **B.** Routine thyroid ultrasound next year only
- **C.** An additional methimazole dose without review
- **D.** Reassurance that infection is impossible

<details><summary>✅ Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: A. Urgent clinical assessment and blood count for possible agranulocytosis**

**Kyun sahi hai?** Methimazole kabhi infection se ladne wale white cells bahut kam kar sakti hai. Fever/sore throat warning hai. Urgent review before further dosing per advice.

**Baaki options kyun galat hain?**

- **B.** Ultrasound WBC nahi batata
- **C.** Extra dose unsafe
- **D.** Infection risk badh sakta hai

**Exam trick:** Antithyroid drug plus fever = check blood count.

</details>

### Q13. After neck surgery, a patient has new perioral tingling and a prolonged QT interval. Which electrolyte abnormality should be assessed urgently?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Exam focus:** NORCET / Staff Nurse

- **A.** Hypocalcemia
- **B.** Isolated hypernatremia
- **C.** Hyperglycemia alone
- **D.** Isolated hyperphosphatemia as the only direct cause

<details><summary>✅ Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: A. Hypocalcemia**

**Kyun sahi hai?** Calcium low hone se nerves aur muscles easily activate hoti hain aur ECG QT long ho sakta hai. Neck surgery parathyroid affect kar sakti hai.

**Baaki options kyun galat hain?**

- **B.** Sodium typical pattern nahi
- **C.** Glucose tingling/QT combination explain nahi karta
- **D.** Phosphate may rise but calcium is immediate concern

**Exam trick:** Low calcium can prolong QT.

</details>

### Q14. A patient with chronic steroid use develops hypotension and hypoglycemia during a serious infection. Which endocrine emergency is a concern?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Exam focus:** NORCET / Staff Nurse

- **A.** Uncomplicated Cushing appearance alone
- **B.** Isolated hyperparathyroidism
- **C.** Stable acromegaly
- **D.** Adrenal crisis from inadequate cortisol response

<details><summary>✅ Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: D. Adrenal crisis from inadequate cortisol response**

**Kyun sahi hai?** Lambe steroid use se body apna cortisol banana kam kar sakti hai. Severe illness mein cortisol demand badhti hai. BP aur sugar girna crisis clue hai.

**Baaki options kyun galat hain?**

- **A.** Cushing appearance emergency explain nahi
- **B.** PTH calcium control
- **C.** GH excess alag

**Exam trick:** Stress may uncover adrenal suppression.

</details>

### Q15. A patient with a suspected pituitary mass reports bumping into objects on both sides while walking. Which assessment is most relevant?

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Exam focus:** NORCET / Staff Nurse

- **A.** Urine glucose alone
- **B.** Measurement of calf circumference
- **C.** Formal visual-field assessment
- **D.** Only central visual acuity using a reading card

<details><summary>✅ Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: C. Formal visual-field assessment**

**Kyun sahi hai?** Pituitary mass optic chiasm par pressure daal sakta hai. Dono taraf ki peripheral nazar kam ho sakti hai.

**Baaki options kyun galat hain?**

- **A.** Glucose unrelated
- **B.** Calf size unrelated
- **D.** Central vision normal ho tab bhi field loss possible

**Exam trick:** Bitemporal field loss suggests chiasm compression.

</details>

### Q16. A patient with MEN2 and medullary thyroid carcinoma is being prepared for thyroid surgery. Which condition should be evaluated beforehand because of perioperative risk?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Exam focus:** NORCET / Staff Nurse

- **A.** Uncomplicated iron deficiency as the defining MEN2 lesion
- **B.** Pheochromocytoma
- **C.** Isolated simple myopia
- **D.** Stable seasonal allergic rhinitis

<details><summary>✅ Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: B. Pheochromocytoma**

**Kyun sahi hai?** MEN2 mein pheochromocytoma bhi ho sakta hai. Untreated tumor surgery ke stress mein catecholamine release karke dangerous BP rise kar sakta hai.

**Baaki options kyun galat hain?**

- **A.** Iron deficiency not defining tumor
- **C.** Vision problem not MEN2 hallmark
- **D.** Rhinitis unrelated

**Exam trick:** MEN2: rule out pheochromocytoma before thyroid surgery.

</details>

### Q17. A patient has recurrent episodes of sweating and confusion during fasting that improve after glucose administration. What additional evidence completes Whipple's triad?

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Exam focus:** NORCET / Staff Nurse

- **A.** An elevated calcium level alone
- **B.** Documented low plasma glucose during the symptoms
- **C.** Elevated TSH measured a month earlier
- **D.** Normal fasting cholesterol

<details><summary>✅ Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: B. Documented low plasma glucose during the symptoms**

**Kyun sahi hai?** Whipple triad mein symptoms, usi time low glucose ka proof aur glucose dene se symptoms improve hona chahiye. Isse hypoglycemia workup guide hota hai.

**Baaki options kyun galat hain?**

- **A.** Calcium low glucose prove nahi karta
- **C.** TSH thyroid
- **D.** Cholesterol unrelated

**Exam trick:** Symptoms plus documented low glucose plus relief.

</details>

### Q18. A patient on glucocorticoid therapy has fragile skin and frequent bruising. Which nursing approach reduces injury risk?

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Exam focus:** NORCET / Staff Nurse

- **A.** Use gentle handling and skin-friendly dressing techniques
- **B.** Remove adhesive rapidly without supporting skin
- **C.** Perform vigorous massage over bruises
- **D.** Encourage unassisted activity despite severe weakness

<details><summary>✅ Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: A. Use gentle handling and skin-friendly dressing techniques**

**Kyun sahi hai?** Steroid protein breakdown badha sakta hai. Skin patli aur muscles weak ho sakti hain. Gentle handling aur fall precautions useful hain.

**Baaki options kyun galat hain?**

- **B.** Fast removal skin tear risk
- **C.** Massage bruising badha sakti hai
- **D.** Fall injury risk

**Exam trick:** Cortisol excess affects skin and bone.

</details>

### Q19. A patient with severe hypercalcemia has polyuria, dehydration and confusion. Why can dehydration worsen the problem?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Exam focus:** NORCET / Staff Nurse

- **A.** Dehydration always increases glomerular filtration
- **B.** Calcium is eliminated only through sweat
- **C.** Dehydration immediately stops all PTH production
- **D.** Reduced circulating volume can impair renal calcium clearance

<details><summary>✅ Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: D. Reduced circulating volume can impair renal calcium clearance**

**Kyun sahi hai?** Paani kam hone se kidney tak blood kam pahunch sakta hai. Kidney calcium achhe se clear nahi kar paati, calcium aur high reh sakta hai. Fluid plan clinician se.

**Baaki options kyun galat hain?**

- **A.** Filtration decrease ho sakti hai
- **B.** Urine major route hai
- **C.** PTH response cause-dependent

**Exam trick:** Hypercalcemia and dehydration may reinforce each other.

</details>

### Q20. A patient with type 1 diabetes becomes ill and is eating very little. Which teaching is most important?

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Exam focus:** NORCET / Staff Nurse

- **A.** Exercise vigorously if ketones are high
- **B.** Avoid seeking help for persistent vomiting
- **C.** Follow the prescribed sick-day plan, including basal insulin and ketone monitoring
- **D.** Stop all insulin whenever appetite is low

<details><summary>✅ Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: C. Follow the prescribed sick-day plan, including basal insulin and ketone monitoring**

**Kyun sahi hai?** Type 1 mein khana kam ho tab bhi insulin ki basic need rehti hai. Illness glucose/ketones badha sakti hai. Sick-day plan aur danger signs follow karo.

**Baaki options kyun galat hain?**

- **A.** High ketones exercise unsafe
- **B.** Vomiting dehydration and DKA risk
- **D.** All insulin stop DKA risk

**Exam trick:** Never omit basal insulin without a safe clinical plan.

</details>
