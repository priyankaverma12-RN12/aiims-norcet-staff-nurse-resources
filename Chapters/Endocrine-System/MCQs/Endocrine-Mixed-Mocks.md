## Mock 1 — 5 questions
### 1. After thyroid surgery, a patient develops stridor. Priority?

- **A.** Offer food
- **B.** Assess/support airway and call emergency help
- **C.** Walk patient
- **D.** Check weight

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Exam focus:** NORCET / Staff Nurse (original practice)

<details><summary>✅ Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: B. Assess/support airway and call emergency help** — Stridor airway narrowing ka emergency clue hai.

**Baaki options kyun nahi?** A oral food unsafe; C walking and D weight delay airway.

**Exam trick:** ABC first.

</details>

### 2. Which finding is typical in SIADH?

- **A.** Hypernatremia with dilute urine
- **B.** Hyponatremia with concentrated urine
- **C.** Hypercalcemia
- **D.** High HbA1c

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Exam focus:** NORCET / Staff Nurse (original practice)

<details><summary>✅ Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: B. Hyponatremia with concentrated urine** — ADH excess water retain karata hai, Na dilute aur urine concentrated.

**Baaki options kyun nahi?** A DI pattern; C calcium and D chronic glucose unrelated.

**Exam trick:** SIADH = water excess.

</details>

### 3. Severe hypotension and vomiting in known Addison disease suggest:

- **A.** Thyroid storm
- **B.** Adrenal crisis

- **D.** Graves disease

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Exam focus:** NORCET / Staff Nurse (original practice)

<details><summary>✅ Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: B. Adrenal crisis** — Cortisol deficiency stress mein shock kar sakti hai.

**Baaki options kyun nahi?** A thyroid storm fever/tachycardia; C GH excess; D Graves thyroid excess.

**Exam trick:** Addison + hypotension/vomiting = crisis.

</details>

### 4. An unconscious hypoglycemic patient should receive:

- **A.** Oral juice
- **B.** Oral candy

- **D.** Exercise

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Exam focus:** NORCET / Staff Nurse (original practice)

<details><summary>✅ Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Baaki options kyun nahi?** A/B aspiration risk; D exercise worsens low glucose.

**Exam trick:** Unconscious = no oral food.

</details>

### 5. Bitemporal hemianopia in pituitary tumor results from pressure on:
