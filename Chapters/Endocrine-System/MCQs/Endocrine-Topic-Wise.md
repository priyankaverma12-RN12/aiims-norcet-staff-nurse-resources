### 1. Which gland releases ADH?

- **B.** Posterior pituitary

- **D.** Adrenal cortex

**Difficulty:** Easy | **Exam focus:** NORCET / Staff Nurse (original practice)

<details><summary>✅ Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>
