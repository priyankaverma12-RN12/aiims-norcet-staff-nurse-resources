### Q01. Which type of nerve damage in diabetes affects involuntary control of internal organs?

**Difficulty:** Easy | **Priority:** 🟠 High yield | **Type:** Original practice

A. Isolated sensory neuropathy

B. Autonomic neuropathy

C. Isolated motor neuropathy

D. Optic neuritis

<details>
<summary>Show answer and explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: B. Autonomic neuropathy**

- **A — Incorrect:** Sensory nerves touch aur pain ka signal deti hain. Sirf unka damage involuntary organ control ko define nahi karta.
- **B — Correct:** Autonomic nerves heart, bowel aur bladder jaise organs ko bina conscious effort ke control karti hain. In nerves ka damage autonomic neuropathy kehlata hai.
- **C — Incorrect:** Motor nerves voluntary muscle movement mein madad karti hain. Unka isolated damage internal organ control ki primary problem nahi hai.
- **D — Incorrect:** Optic neuritis optic nerve ki inflammation hai. Yeh multiple autonomic organs ki problem nahi batati.

**Exam clue:** Automatic body functions ko autonomic nerves sambhalti hain.
