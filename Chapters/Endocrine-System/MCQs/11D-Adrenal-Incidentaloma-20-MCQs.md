B. Conservative management can never be considered for bilateral lesions

C. Bilateral adrenalectomy is generally avoided in the absence of overt Cushing syndrome, and management is individualized

D. Bilateral adrenalectomy is mandatory for all bilateral hyperplasia

<summary>Show answer and explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: C. Bilateral adrenalectomy is generally avoided in the absence of overt Cushing syndrome, and management is individualized**

- **A — Incorrect:** Dono glands remove karne se endogenous adrenal hormone production lost ho sakti hai. Replacement aur crisis safety major concerns hain.
- **B — Incorrect:** Selected patients mein conservative management aur clinical follow-up suitable ho sakte hain. Benefit-risk patient-specific hota hai.
- **C — Correct:** Bilateral adrenalectomy se lifelong adrenal insufficiency ho sakti hai. ESE overt Cushing syndrome absent hone par bilateral removal ke against suggest karti hai, with individualized management.
- **D — Incorrect:** Bilateral hyperplasia ka diagnosis automatic surgery indication nahi hai. Cortisol autonomy aur comorbidities assess hoti hain.

**Exam clue:** Bilateral MACS mein routine bilateral adrenalectomy avoid karo.
