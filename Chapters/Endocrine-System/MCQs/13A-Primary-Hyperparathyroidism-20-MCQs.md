### Q01. Which specialist treatment can definitively cure sporadic primary hyperparathyroidism caused by a removable adenoma?

**Difficulty:** Easy | **Priority:** 🟠 High yield | **Type:** Original practice

A. Oral alendronate alone

B. Vitamin D replacement alone

C. Parathyroidectomy

D. Long-term cinacalcet alone

<details>
<summary>Show answer and explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: C. Parathyroidectomy**

- **A — Incorrect:** Alendronate bone resorption kam karti hai, parathyroid adenoma ko cure nahi karti.
- **B — Incorrect:** Vitamin D deficiency correct karna useful ho sakta hai, lekin autonomous gland secretion ka definitive treatment nahi hai.
- **C — Correct:** Abnormal parathyroid tissue remove karne se excess PTH ka source eliminate ho sakta hai. Suitable patient mein surgery definitive treatment hai.
- **D — Incorrect:** Cinacalcet calcium control kar sakti hai, lekin adenoma remove nahi karti. Isliye drug control aur cure alag hain.

**Exam clue:** Definitive treatment targets the abnormal gland.

**Correct answer: A. The inherited calcium-sensing abnormality usually persists despite ordinary parathyroid surgery**

- **D — Incorrect:** All glands remove karna permanent hypoparathyroidism cause kar sakta hai. FHH alone routine indication nahi hai.
