### Q01. A patient with confirmed biochemical thyrotoxicosis has no palpable thyroid nodule. Which initial etiologic investigation does NICE NG145 recommend to help confirm Graves disease?

B. TSH receptor antibody measurement

C. Routine thyroid FNA

D. Serum calcitonin alone

<details>
<summary>Show answer and explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: B. TSH receptor antibody measurement**

- **A — Incorrect:** NICE adults mein ultrasound generally palpable nodule ho tab consider karti hai. Etiologic antibody test ka role alag hai.
- **B — Correct:** TSH receptor antibodies Graves ki autoimmune mechanism identify karne mein help karti hain. Thyrotoxicosis ka cause establish karne ke liye yeh recommended initial assessment hai.
