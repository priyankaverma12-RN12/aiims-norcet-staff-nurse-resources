A. Immediate routine removal of the entire gland is always straightforward in invasive fibrosis

B. Specialist tissue and anatomical assessment may be needed to distinguish invasive fibrosis from malignancy

C. Hard consistency alone conclusively proves Riedel thyroiditis

D. Normal thyroid function reliably excludes a significant infiltrative lesion

<details>
<summary>Show answer and explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: B. Specialist tissue and anatomical assessment may be needed to distinguish invasive fibrosis from malignancy**

- **A — Incorrect:** Invasive fibrosis surgery technically difficult bana sakti hai. Specialist individualized approach needed hota hai, not automatic total removal.
- **B — Correct:** Riedel thyroiditis mein fibrous tissue adjacent structures tak extend kar sakta hai aur malignancy mimic kar sakta hai. Tissue evaluation aur anatomical assessment se differential clarify hota hai.
- **C — Incorrect:** Hard fixed gland suspicious finding hai lekin specific diagnosis confirm nahi karti. Malignant aur fibrosing causes evaluate hote hain.
- **D — Incorrect:** Hormone production normal hone par bhi local infiltrative disease possible hai. Structure aur function separate assess hote hain.

**Exam clue:** Hard infiltrative thyroid lesion mein diagnosis aur surgical anatomy carefully clarify karo.
