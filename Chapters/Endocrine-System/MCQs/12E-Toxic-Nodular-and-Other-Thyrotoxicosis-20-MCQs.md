### Q01. A patient with toxic multinodular goiter asks why the thyroid continues producing excess hormone despite a very low TSH. Which mechanism is most relevant?

**Difficulty:** Easy | **Priority:** 🟠 High yield | **Type:** Original practice

A. Thyroid hormone excess in nodular goiter always results from follicular destruction

B. Autonomously functioning thyroid tissue can synthesize hormone without normal TSH-dependent stimulation

C. Low TSH always switches off every functioning thyroid cell

D. The pituitary must be releasing excessive biologically active TSH

<details>
<summary>Show answer and explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: B. Autonomously functioning thyroid tissue can synthesize hormone without normal TSH-dependent stimulation**

- **A — Incorrect:** Toxic nodular disease increased synthesis se related hai. Destructive thyroiditis stored hormone leakage ka different mechanism hai.
- **B — Correct:** Autonomous nodules mein hormone synthesis normal TSH feedback se relatively independent ho sakti hai. Isliye suppressed TSH ke bawajood functioning tissue excess hormone produce karta hai.
- **C — Incorrect:** Normal tissue low TSH par activity reduce karti hai, lekin autonomous areas continue kar sakte hain. Feedback suppression alone sufficient nahi hoti.
- **D — Incorrect:** Primary thyroid autonomy mein circulating TSH usually suppressed hota hai. Excess pituitary TSH ka mechanism different hai.

**Exam clue:** Autonomous synthesis can persist despite suppressed TSH.

**Clinical reference:** [Endotext thyrotoxicosis of other etiologies](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/NBK285562/)
