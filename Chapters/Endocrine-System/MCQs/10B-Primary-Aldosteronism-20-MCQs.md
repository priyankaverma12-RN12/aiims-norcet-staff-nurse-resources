### Q20. Two patients have similar clinic blood pressure readings. One has untreated primary aldosteronism and the other has primary hypertension. Why is disease-specific treatment important in the first patient?

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Priority:** 🟠 High yield | **Type:** Original practice

B. Excess aldosterone is associated with cardiovascular and renal harm beyond the blood pressure measurement alone

<details>
<summary>Show answer and explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: B. Excess aldosterone is associated with cardiovascular and renal harm beyond the blood pressure measurement alone**

- **A — Incorrect:** Overt edema primary aldosteronism mein absent ho sakta hai. Treatment indication biochemical diagnosis aur clinical risks se related hoti hai.
- **B — Correct:** Autonomous aldosterone signaling heart aur kidneys mein adverse remodeling aur injury contribute kar sakti hai. Isliye BP control ke saath underlying hormone effect target karna meaningful hai.
- **C — Incorrect:** Same clinic BP se complete cardiovascular risk equal prove nahi hota. Etiology aur organ exposure bhi relevant hain.
- **D — Incorrect:** Aldosterone renal electrolyte balance aur cardiovascular physiology influence karta hai. Iska effect cosmetic changes tak limited nahi hai.

**Exam clue:** PA mein blood pressure ke saath organ protection ka goal samjho.
