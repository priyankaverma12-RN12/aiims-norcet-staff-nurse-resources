### Q01. A patient admitted with bacterial pneumonia has no prior thyroid symptoms or clinical suspicion of thyroid disease. The nurse notices that a routine thyroid panel has been proposed solely because the patient is acutely ill. Which approach is consistent with NICE NG145?

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Priority:** 🟠 High yield | **Type:** Original practice

B. Start empirical thyroid replacement before the infection improves

C. Defer routine thyroid testing until recovery unless thyroid dysfunction is suspected as a contributor to the acute illness

D. Use the acute illness thyroid panel to establish lifelong replacement needs

<details>
<summary>Show answer and explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: C. Defer routine thyroid testing until recovery unless thyroid dysfunction is suspected as a contributor to the acute illness**

- **A — Incorrect:** Reverse T3 routine replacement decision ka recommended sole test nahi hai. Appropriate TSH aur FT4 assessment clinical context se guide hota hai.
- **B — Incorrect:** Empirical levothyroxine bina indication unnecessary hormone exposure de sakti hai. Infection ka appropriate treatment aur targeted assessment priority hain.
- **C — Correct:** Acute illness thyroid tests ko temporarily alter kar sakti hai. Agar thyroid dysfunction ka specific suspicion nahi hai toh recovery ke baad indicated testing zyada meaningful hoti hai.
- **D — Incorrect:** Acute illness mein abnormal result transient ho sakta hai. Lifelong diagnosis ke liye proper clinical context aur reassessment chahiye.

**Correct answer: B. Thyroid hormone is not indicated for weight loss in euthyroid individuals and excessive dosing may cause serious harm**

- **A — Incorrect:** Suppressed TSH excess hormone exposure ka warning ho sakta hai, weight-loss safety marker nahi. Unnecessary thyroid hormone avoid karo.
- **B — Correct:** Euthyroid patient mein thyroid hormone weight-loss treatment ke liye indicated nahi hai. Excess dose cardiac aur metabolic adverse effects cause kar sakti hai, so appropriate weight-management care discuss karo.
- **C — Incorrect:** Normal baseline function extra hormone ke adverse effects se protect nahi karti. Iatrogenic thyrotoxicosis possible hai.
- **D — Incorrect:** Weight management individualized nutrition, activity aur indicated medical support se guided hota hai. Thyroid replacement actual deficiency ke liye use hoti hai.

**Exam clue:** Levothyroxine is replacement, not a weight-loss drug.
