### Q01. A patient with confirmed hypercalcemia has markedly suppressed intact PTH. Which etiologic group should be investigated next?

**Difficulty:** Easy | **Priority:** 🟠 High yield | **Type:** Original practice

A. Familial hypocalciuric hypercalcemia with nonsuppressed PTH

B. PTH-independent hypercalcemia

C. Primary hyperparathyroidism from an autonomous adenoma as the only diagnosis

D. Tertiary hyperparathyroidism with autonomous secretion

<details>
<summary>Show answer and explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: B. PTH-independent hypercalcemia**

- **A — Incorrect:** FHH mein PTH usually inappropriate normal ya mildly raised ho sakti hai. Family context aur urinary calcium bhi chahiye.
- **B — Correct:** High calcium par normal feedback PTH suppress karti hai. Markedly low PTH mein malignancy, vitamin D-related aur other non-PTH causes investigate hote hain.
- **C — Incorrect:** Autonomous adenoma mein PTH elevated ya inappropriate normal rehti hai. Marked suppression is mechanism ko less likely banati hai.
- **D — Incorrect:** Tertiary disease mein autonomous PTH usually elevated hoti hai. Suppression different etiology suggest karti hai.

**Exam clue:** Use PTH as the initial etiologic branch.
