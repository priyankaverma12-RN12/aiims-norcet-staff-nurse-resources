### Q01. Which term describes new or worsened physical, cognitive or psychological problems following critical illness?

**Difficulty:** Easy | **Priority:** High | **Type:** Original practice | **Topic:** ICU transitional care and recovery

- **A.** Acute coronary syndrome
- **B.** Systemic inflammatory response syndrome
- **C.** Post-intensive care syndrome (PICS)
- **D.** Compartment syndrome

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: C. Post-intensive care syndrome (PICS)**

- **A.** ACS heart ki acute ischemia se related hai, ICU recovery ke combined problems ka naam nahi.
- **B.** SIRS systemic inflammation ka pattern hai, long-term functional recovery syndrome nahi.
- **C.** PICS mein critical illness ke baad physical, thinking aur mental health problems develop ya worsen ho sakti hain.
- **D.** Compartment syndrome raised tissue pressure se related emergency hai.

**Exam clue:** PICS ke three domains yaad rakho.
