### Q01. An adult with cardiogenic shock has a higher arterial pressure after initial treatment but urine output and mental status continue to worsen. Which decision is most appropriate?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Priority:** High | **Type:** Original practice | **Topic:** Non-septic shock decisions

- **A.** Reduce all monitoring because pressure has improved
- **B.** Request reassessment of shock trajectory and adequacy of organ perfusion rather than assuming stabilization
- **C.** Use the arterial pressure alone to certify recovery
- **D.** Postpone review until the next scheduled multidisciplinary meeting

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: B. Request reassessment of shock trajectory and adequacy of organ perfusion rather than assuming stabilization**

- **A.** Higher pressure se full organ recovery confirm nahi hoti. Close monitoring abhi needed hai.
- **B.** Pressure improvement ke bawajood organ perfusion inadequate ho sakti hai. Urine aur consciousness ka worsening trend ongoing shock ki reassessment maangta hai.
- **C.** Blood pressure ek variable hai. Flow aur organ function bhi clinical outcome guide karte hain.
- **D.** Worsening organ signs mein routine meeting ka wait avoidable delay hai.

**Exam clue:** Cardiogenic shock mein pressure ke saath organ response reassess karo.
