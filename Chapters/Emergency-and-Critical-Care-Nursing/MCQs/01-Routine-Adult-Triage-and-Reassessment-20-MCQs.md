### Q01. A patient with a painful swollen ankle arrives before an adult with ongoing heavy external bleeding. The registration queue is long. Which workflow is safest?

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Priority:** High | **Type:** Original practice | **Topic:** Routine adult triage

- **A.** Perform clinical triage before registration and route patients according to acuity

- **C.** Complete the ankle radiograph before assessing the second patient
- **D.** Send both directly to the same waiting queue

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: A. Perform clinical triage before registration and route patients according to acuity**

- **A.** Nurse pehle clinical urgency check karti hai. Heavy bleeding mein blood loss tez ho sakta hai, isliye registration treatment mein rukawat nahi banna chahiye.
- **B.** Arrival order se pata nahi chalta ki kiska circulation khatre mein hai. Registration baad mein ho sakta hai.
- **C.** Ankle X-ray useful ho sakta hai, lekin doosre patient ki heavy bleeding ka assessment uske liye delay nahi karna chahiye.
- **D.** Dono ki urgency alag ho sakti hai. Ek hi queue unki zaroorat ko ignore karti hai.

- **D.** Continue routine triage unless mass-casualty activation criteria are met

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: D. Continue routine triage unless mass-casualty activation criteria are met**

- **A.** Multiple arrivals apne aap mass casualty declaration nahi hote. Facility capacity aur incident plan matter karte hain.
- **B.** Triage suspend karne se urgent cases miss ho sakte hain. Increased demand mein sorting aur important hoti hai.
- **C.** Incident triage ko unrelated patients ke liye hi use karna sahi nahi hai. Activation ke baad facility protocol all arrivals par apply ho sakta hai.
- **D.** Mass casualty tab hota hai jab needs usual resources aur process se zyada ho jaati hain. Agar facility routine care safely de sakti hai toh routine triage continue hoti hai.
