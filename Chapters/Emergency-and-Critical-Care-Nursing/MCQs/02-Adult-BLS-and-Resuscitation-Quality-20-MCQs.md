### Q01. A resuscitation recording shows 150 seconds of actual compressions during a 240-second observation period. What is the chest compression fraction?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Priority:** High | **Type:** Original practice | **Topic:** Adult BLS and resuscitation quality

- **A.** 37.5%
- **B.** 80%
- **C.** 62.5%
- **D.** 160%

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: C. 62.5%**
