### Q01. An adult has just achieved ROSC after cardiac arrest. The pulse oximeter has no reliable waveform yet. Which oxygen strategy is appropriate until reliable measurement becomes available?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Priority:** High | **Type:** Original MCQ | **Topic:** Post-cardiac arrest care

- **A.** Immediately reduce inspired oxygen to room air
- **B.** Use 100% inspired oxygen initially
- **C.** Titrate oxygen using the unreliable reading
- **D.** Withhold supplemental oxygen until arterial sampling

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: B. Use 100% inspired oxygen initially**

- **A.** Room air par turant shift karne se occult hypoxemia ho sakti hai.
- **B.** ROSC ke turant baad reliable saturation nahi milne par high inspired oxygen hypoxemia ka risk kam karta hai.
- **C.** Poor waveform ke number ko reliable maan kar titration karna unsafe hai.
- **D.** ABG ka wait karte hue oxygen rokna tissue oxygen delivery compromise kar sakta hai.

**Exam clue:** Reliable measurement se pehle oxygen support, uske baad titration.

**Reference:** [AHA 2025 Post-Cardiac Arrest Care, section 4.1](https://cpr.heart.org/en/resuscitation-science/cpr-and-ecc-guidelines/post-cardiac-arrest-care).
