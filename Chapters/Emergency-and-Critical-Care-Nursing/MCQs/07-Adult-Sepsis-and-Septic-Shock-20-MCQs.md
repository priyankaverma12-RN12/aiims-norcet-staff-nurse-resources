### Q20. A survivor of sepsis is being discharged after new weakness and difficulties with memory. Which action is most consistent with SSC 2026 recovery guidance?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Priority:** High | **Type:** Original practice | **Topic:** Adult sepsis and septic shock

- **A.** Assume all symptoms resolve after infection treatment and omit follow-up
- **B.** Provide only the original culture report with no explanation of possible sequelae
- **C.** Arrange follow-up for new impairments and provide understandable written and verbal information about recovery
- **D.** Require the patient to return only if the original fever recurs

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: C. Arrange follow-up for new impairments and provide understandable written and verbal information about recovery**

- **A.** Infection control ke baad recovery immediate ya complete hona guaranteed nahi hai. New functional symptoms assess hone chahiye.
- **B.** Culture report organism batati hai, lekin patient ki weakness aur memory problems ka follow-up plan nahi deti.
- **C.** Sepsis ke baad physical aur cognitive problems persist kar sakti hain. Patient aur family ko recovery information aur appropriate follow-up se new impairments manage karne mein help milti hai.
- **D.** Fever recurrence ek concern ho sakti hai, lekin post-sepsis problems bina fever bhi present ho sakti hain. Broader follow-up needed hai.

**Reference:** [SSC 2026 adult guidelines — Hospital discharge and post-hospital evaluation](https://sccm.org/clinical-resources/guidelines/guidelines/surviving-sepsis-campaign-international-guidelines-for-management-of-sepsis-and-septic-shock-2026).
