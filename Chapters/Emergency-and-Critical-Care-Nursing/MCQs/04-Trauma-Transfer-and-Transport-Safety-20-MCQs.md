### Q01. A rural facility needs to transfer an injured adult for specialist care. The nearest hospital cannot provide the required surgery. Which destination decision is safest?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Priority:** High | **Type:** Original practice | **Topic:** Trauma transfer and transport safety

- **A.** Choose the nearest hospital solely because transport time is shorter
- **B.** Choose a hospital without checking whether the relevant service exists
- **C.** Delay all arrangements until every diagnostic test is complete
- **D.** Arrange transfer to a facility with the required capability and confirm acceptance

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: D. Arrange transfer to a facility with the required capability and confirm acceptance**

- **B.** Service confirm na karne se patient aisi jagah pahunch sakta hai jahan definitive care nahi milti. Pehle coordination karo.
- **C.** Har test ka wait time-critical referral delay kar sakta hai. Team necessary stabilization aur timely transfer ko balance karti hai.
- **D.** Transfer ka purpose required treatment tak pahunchna hai. Receiving facility ki capability aur acceptance confirm karne se unnecessary repeat transfer ka risk kam hota hai.
