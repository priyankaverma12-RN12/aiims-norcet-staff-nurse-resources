### Q01. A patient arrives with an unidentified household chemical exposure. The family has a photograph of the product label. Which information should the nurse prioritize when contacting toxicology support?

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Priority:** High | **Type:** Original practice | **Topic:** Poisoning and toxic exposure

- **A.** Only the patient's age and residential address
- **B.** The colour of the container without other details
- **C.** Exact product and concentration if known, exposure route, estimated amount, time and symptoms
- **D.** Only the current blood pressure

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: C. Exact product and concentration if known, exposure route, estimated amount, time and symptoms**

- **A.** Age clinical context deti hai, lekin address substance ya exposure ki severity identify nahi karta.
- **B.** Container colour se chemical reliably identify nahi hota. Label par ingredients aur concentration useful ho sakte hain.
- **C.** Product aur exposure ki details se specialist possible toxicity aur appropriate assessment samajh sakta hai. Symptoms aur timing bhi decision ko guide karte hain.
- **D.** BP severity ka ek parameter hai. Isse substance, route aur time ka pata nahi chalta.

**Exam clue:** Product aur exposure ki details se specialist possible toxicity aur appropriate assessment samajh sakta hai.
