### Q02. An arterial trace shows an unusually high systolic peak, low diastolic trough and repeated ringing after a dynamic response test. Which problem is most likely?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Priority:** High | **Type:** Original practice | **Topic:** Advanced ICU monitoring

- **A.** Underdamping with resonance
- **B.** Overdamping from a severely obstructed catheter
- **C.** A verified physiological rise in systemic vascular resistance
- **D.** An automatically correct waveform because the mean value appears plausible

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: A. Underdamping with resonance**

- **A.** Underdamped system pressure oscillations exaggerate karta hai. Systolic falsely high aur diastolic falsely low ho sakte hain.
- **B.** Severe obstruction usually waveform blunt karta hai, repeated ringing ka typical pattern nahi.
- **C.** Resistance patient physiology ka parameter hai. Resonant tracing se ise directly establish nahi kar sakte.
- **D.** MAP relatively less affected ho sakti hai, lekin waveform quality phir bhi verify karni chahiye.

**Exam clue:** Ringing aur widened artificial pulse pressure underdamping suggest karte hain.
