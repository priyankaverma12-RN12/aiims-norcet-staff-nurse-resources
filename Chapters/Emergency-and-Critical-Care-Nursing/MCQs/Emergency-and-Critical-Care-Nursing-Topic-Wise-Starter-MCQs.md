## Triage

### Q1. Four patients arrive simultaneously. Which patient requires immediate assessment first?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Topic:** Triage

- **A.** An adult with stridor and rapidly increasing respiratory distress
- **B.** A stable patient requesting a routine prescription refill
- **C.** An alert patient with a minor healed abrasion
- **D.** A patient awaiting a routine dressing change with stable vitals

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: A. An adult with stridor and rapidly increasing respiratory distress**

Stridor airway narrowing ka sign ho sakta hai. Saans ka rasta band hone se jaan ko turant khatra hota hai. Isliye nurse airway emergency ko pehle leti hai.

**Baaki options kyun galat hain?**

- **B.** Routine refill urgent airway se kam priority hai.
- **C.** Healed abrasion stable hai.
- **D.** Stable dressing change wait kar sakti hai.

</details>

## Shock

### Q2. A patient with suspected sepsis has low BP, confusion and falling urine output despite initial management. Which clinical concern is most important?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Topic:** Shock

- **A.** Normal adaptation to fever
- **B.** Stable chronic anemia as the only diagnosis
- **C.** A reason to stop all observations
- **D.** Inadequate organ perfusion requiring urgent escalation

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: D. Inadequate organ perfusion requiring urgent escalation**

Sepsis mein infection par body ka response circulation ko disturb kar sakta hai. Brain aur kidney tak blood kam pahunchne se confusion aur urine kam hota hai. Urgent treatment escalation chahiye.

**Baaki options kyun galat hain?**

- **A.** Ye normal fever response nahi hai.
- **B.** Anemia alone is acute pattern ko explain nahi karti.
- **C.** Frequent observations aur bhi zaroori hain.

</details>

## Poisoning

### Q3. A patient arrives after suspected corrosive ingestion with drooling and painful swallowing. Which action is appropriate?

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Topic:** Poisoning

- **A.** Give a neutralizing chemical without advice
- **B.** Insert a gastric tube blindly
- **C.** Assess airway and seek emergency toxicology guidance without inducing vomiting
- **D.** Induce vomiting immediately

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: C. Assess airway and seek emergency toxicology guidance without inducing vomiting**

Corrosive substance food pipe aur airway ko jala sakti hai. Vomiting se substance dobara tissue ko damage kar sakti hai. Nurse airway assess karke emergency guidance leti hai.

**Baaki options kyun galat hain?**

- **A.** Neutralization reaction heat aur injury kar sakti hai.
- **B.** Blind tube insertion injured tissue ko harm kar sakti hai.
- **D.** Vomiting second exposure karwa sakti hai.

</details>

## ICU line safety

### Q4. A central venous catheter dressing is loose and visibly soiled. Which action is appropriate?

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Topic:** ICU line safety

- **A.** Use the line without checking the dressing
- **B.** Replace it using aseptic technique according to the line-care protocol
- **C.** Reinforce indefinitely without assessing the insertion site
- **D.** Remove all infection precautions

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: B. Replace it using aseptic technique according to the line-care protocol**

Central line seedha large vein mein jaati hai. Loose aur dirty dressing se germs entry kar sakte hain. Nurse insertion site assess karke aseptic dressing change karti hai.

**Baaki options kyun galat hain?**

- **A.** Dressing condition ignore karna unsafe hai.
- **C.** Soiled dressing ko indefinitely reinforce karna safe nahi hai.
- **D.** Infection precautions line care mein essential hain.

</details>
