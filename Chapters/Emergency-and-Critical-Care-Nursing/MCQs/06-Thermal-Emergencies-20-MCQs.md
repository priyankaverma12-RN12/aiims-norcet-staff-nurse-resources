### Q01. A marathon runner collapses with confusion, a core temperature of 40.4°C and profuse sweating. Which interpretation should guide emergency care?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Priority:** High | **Type:** Original practice | **Topic:** Thermal emergencies

- **A.** Heat exhaustion is established because sweating persists
- **B.** Heat syncope is confirmed by the elevated temperature
- **C.** Exertional heat stroke remains likely despite sweating
- **D.** Classic heat stroke is excluded solely because the skin is wet

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: C. Exertional heat stroke remains likely despite sweating**

- **A.** Sweating heat exhaustion mein bhi hoti hai, lekin confusion aur marked hyperthermia heat stroke ka concern badhate hain.
- **B.** Heat syncope usually transient fainting hoti hai. Persistent confusion aur itna high temperature us diagnosis ko sufficient nahi banate.
- **C.** Exertional heat stroke mein sweating continue ho sakti hai. High core temperature aur brain function mein change ko saath dekhkar emergency cooling aur support chahiye.
- **D.** Skin wet ya dry hone se heat stroke reliably exclude nahi hota. Clinical context aur mental status important hain.
