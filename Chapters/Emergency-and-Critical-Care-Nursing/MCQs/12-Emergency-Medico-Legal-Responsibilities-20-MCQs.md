### Q01. An injured motorcyclist is brought to a small hospital that normally refers medico-legal cases elsewhere. The patient requires immediate resuscitative care within the facility's capability. Which principle from Parmanand Katara v Union of India is most relevant?

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Priority:** High | **Type:** Original practice | **Topic:** Emergency medico-legal responsibilities

- **A.** Refer without providing available emergency assistance because the case is medico-legal
- **B.** Await the police report before the clinical team assesses the injury
- **C.** Begin necessary emergency aid before procedural formalities and arrange appropriate further care
- **D.** Obtain a court order before starting basic emergency measures

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: C. Begin necessary emergency aid before procedural formalities and arrange appropriate further care**

- **A.** Medico-legal label available lifesaving assistance deny karne ka reason nahi hai. Patient ko transfer se pehle appropriate support chahiye.
- **B.** Police report legal process ka part ho sakti hai, lekin urgent clinical care uske wait mein nahi rukti.
- **C.** Supreme Court ne preservation of life ko priority di hai. Facility apni capability ke andar urgent care shuru karti hai aur required referral coordinate karti hai.
- **D.** Basic emergency care ke liye court order ka wait unnecessary aur dangerous delay create karega.

**Exam clue:** Emergency care first, legal formalities alongside or afterward.

**Reference:** [Supreme Court Parmanand Katara (1989) ](https://indiankanoon.org/doc/498126/?type=print).

### Q02. A police escort brings a severely injured person who is suspected of an offence. A staff member suggests lower clinical priority because of that allegation. Which principle is appropriate?

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

- **A.** Patient ki legal status se clinical need change nahi hoti. Parmanand Katara mein life preservation ka obligation sab injured patients ke liye emphasize hua hai.
- **B.** Acuity-based priority ko allegation ke basis par override karna appropriate nahi hai.
- **C.** Innocence decide karna clinical team ka role nahi hai. Emergency care required findings ke basis par hoti hai.
- **D.** Offence allegation medical contraindication nahi hai. Security arrangements aur treatment ko safely coordinate karo.

**Exam clue:** Custody or accused status does not remove emergency-care duties.
