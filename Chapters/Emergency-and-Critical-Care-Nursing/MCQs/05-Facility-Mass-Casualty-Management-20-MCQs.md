### Q01. A hospital can accommodate a sudden patient influx during daytime but not during the night shift. How should its mass-casualty activation criteria account for this?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Priority:** High | **Type:** Original practice | **Topic:** Facility mass-casualty management

- **A.** Use an identical patient count as the only trigger at all hours
- **B.** Activate only after every available bed is occupied
- **C.** Use explicit thresholds that consider acuity, anticipated arrivals and available resources at the time
- **D.** Let each clinician independently decide whether their individual patient is a mass-casualty case

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: C. Use explicit thresholds that consider acuity, anticipated arrivals and available resources at the time**

- **A.** Same patient count ka impact different shifts mein alag ho sakta hai. Sirf fixed number se actual capacity ka pata nahi chalta.
- **B.** Beds full hone se pehle bhi team overwhelmed ho sakti hai. Anticipated surge ke basis par timely activation useful hai.
- **C.** Night shift mein staff aur resources kam ho sakte hain. Activation threshold current capacity aur patient needs par depend karti hai, isliye plan mein clear criteria hone chahiye.
- **D.** Individual patient ka acuity decision aur facility activation alag processes hain. Coordinated incident plan chahiye.

**Reference:** [Guidance p. 1](https://cdn.who.int/media/docs/default-source/integrated-health-services-(ihs)/csy/mcm-triage_guidance-note.pdf?sfvrsn=d20449f5_2).
