### Q01. An ICU has an empty bed but no available critical-care-trained nurse to supervise the organ support required by a new referral. Which interpretation is most appropriate?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Priority:** High | **Type:** Original practice | **Topic:** ICU organization, monitoring and liberation

- **B.** Any available staff member can independently provide specialist organ support
- **C.** The patient should be refused without discussing escalation options
- **D.** A physical bed does not constitute safe staffed ICU capacity

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

- **C.** Referral ko bina clinical coordination reject karna safe nahi hai. Alternative capacity aur escalation assess karni chahiye.
