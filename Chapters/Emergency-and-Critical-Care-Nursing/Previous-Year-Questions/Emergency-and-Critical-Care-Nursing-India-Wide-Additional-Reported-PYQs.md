### PYQ1. In a rapid AVPU assessment, what does P represent?

**Difficulty:** Easy/Medium | **Topic:** Consciousness assessment

- **A.** Response to pain
- **B.** Pulse assessment
- **C.** Pupil diameter
- **D.** Peripheral perfusion

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation</summary>

**Correct: A. Response to pain**

AVPU alertness ka quick assessment hai. A ka matlab Alert, V response to Voice, P response to Pain aur U Unresponsive hai. Pulse, pupil aur perfusion useful assessments hain lekin P ka meaning is scale mein pain response hai.

</details>
