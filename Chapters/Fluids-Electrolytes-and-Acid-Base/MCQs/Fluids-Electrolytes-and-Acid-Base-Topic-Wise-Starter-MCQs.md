## IV calculation

### Q1. An order specifies 750 mL of fluid over 6 hours using an infusion pump. What rate is required?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Topic:** IV calculation

- **A.** 125 mL/h
- **B.** 75 mL/h
- **C.** 150 mL/h
- **D.** 450 mL/h

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: A. 125 mL/h**

Total fluid 750 mL hai aur time 6 ghante hai. 750 ko 6 se divide karne par 125 mL/h aata hai. Pump setting se pehle order aur units verify karo.

**Baaki options kyun galat hain?**

- **B.** 75 mL/h se 450 mL hi milega.
- **C.** 150 mL/h se 900 mL milega.
- **D.** 450 mL/h prescribed se bahut zyada hai.

</details>

## Potassium

### Q2. A patient receives a prescription for IV potassium chloride. Which administration principle is essential?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Topic:** Potassium

- **A.** Administer concentrated potassium as a rapid IV push
- **B.** Mix any dose without checking compatibility
- **C.** Stop ECG and laboratory monitoring in high-risk patients
- **D.** Use the prescribed diluted concentration and controlled infusion rate

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: D. Use the prescribed diluted concentration and controlled infusion rate**

Potassium heart ki electrical activity ko affect karta hai. Concentrated potassium jaldi dene se dangerous arrhythmia ho sakti hai. Nurse prescribed dilution, rate aur monitoring follow karti hai.

**Baaki options kyun galat hain?**

- **A.** Rapid IV push potassium unsafe hai.
- **B.** Compatibility aur concentration check karna zaroori hai.
- **C.** High-risk patient mein monitoring important hai.

</details>

## Sodium

### Q3. A patient with severe acute hyponatremia becomes confused and develops a seizure. What is the priority?

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Topic:** Sodium

- **A.** Delay treatment until the next routine clinic visit
- **B.** Correct sodium as fast as possible without monitoring
- **C.** Emergency seizure and airway safety with protocol-based sodium treatment
- **D.** Encourage large amounts of plain water

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: C. Emergency seizure and airway safety with protocol-based sodium treatment**

Sodium bahut kam ho toh water brain cells mein ja sakta hai aur swelling ho sakti hai. Seizure ke time airway safety aur urgent treatment chahiye. Sodium correction carefully monitor hoti hai.

**Baaki options kyun galat hain?**

- **A.** Seizure emergency hai.
- **B.** Uncontrolled correction brain injury kar sakti hai.
- **D.** Plain water sodium ko aur dilute kar sakta hai.

</details>

## Acid-base

### Q4. An ABG shows pH 7.29, bicarbonate 14 mEq/L and PaCO2 30 mmHg. Which primary disturbance is present?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Topic:** Acid-base

- **A.** Respiratory alkalosis
- **B.** Metabolic acidosis
- **C.** Respiratory acidosis
- **D.** Metabolic alkalosis

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: B. Metabolic acidosis**

pH kam hai, isliye blood acidic hai. Bicarbonate kam hai aur CO2 bhi kam hai. Main problem metabolic acidosis hai aur lungs CO2 nikaalkar compensate kar rahe hain.

**Baaki options kyun galat hain?**

- **A.** Respiratory alkalosis mein primary CO2 kam aur pH usually high hota hai.
- **C.** Respiratory acidosis mein CO2 high hota hai.
- **D.** Metabolic alkalosis mein bicarbonate high hota hai.

</details>
