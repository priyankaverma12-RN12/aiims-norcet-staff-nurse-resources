## 🔵 NCL Staff Nurse — November 2020

### PYQ 1. A patient has no documented urinary output over a complete 24-hour period. Which term describes the observed absence of urine output?

**Difficulty:** 🟢 Easy · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD

- **A.** Nocturia
- **B.** Polyuria
- **C.** Anuria
- **D.** Dysuria

<details><summary>✅ Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct answer: C. Anuria**

Anuria ka matlab urine output absent ya almost absent hona hai. Historical textbook volume definitions vary kar sakti hain. Adult AKI severity staging mein complete anuria for at least 12 hours stage 3 criterion hai. Nurse ko urine measurement confirm karke drainage obstruction aur retention assess karni chahiye aur urgent review arrange karna chahiye. Patient ko 24 hours tak wait nahi karvana hai.

- **A.** Nocturia raat ko neend se uthkar urine pass karna hai.
- **B.** Polyuria mein total urine volume zyada hota hai, absent nahi.
- **D.** Dysuria urine pass karte waqt pain ya burning hai, output absence nahi.

**Exam clue:** Anuria is absence or near absence of output. Cause aur severity urgently assess karo.
