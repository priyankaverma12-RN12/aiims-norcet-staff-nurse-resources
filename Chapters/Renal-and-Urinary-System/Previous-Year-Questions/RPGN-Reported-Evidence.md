## 🔵 JSSC Grade A Nurse — 16 July 2022

### PYQ 1. In anti-GBM disease, IgG antibodies bind directly to fixed basement membrane antigens and cause tissue inflammation. Under the Gell and Coombs classification, which hypersensitivity mechanism best explains this injury?

**Difficulty:** 🟡 Medium · **Priority:** 🔴 MOST IMPORTANT

- **A.** Type III immune-complex hypersensitivity
- **B.** Type IV delayed T-cell-mediated hypersensitivity
- **C.** Type II antibody-mediated hypersensitivity
- **D.** Type I immediate IgE-mediated hypersensitivity

<details><summary>✅ Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct answer: C. Type II antibody-mediated hypersensitivity**

Dekho, anti-GBM disease mein IgG antibody basement membrane ke fixed antigen se directly bind karti hai. Is binding ke baad complement aur inflammatory cells activate ho sakte hain. Inflammation glomerular capillaries aur lung ki alveolar basement membrane ko damage kar sakti hai. Ye antibody-mediated tissue injury Type II hypersensitivity ka example hai. Goodpasture syndrome traditionally pulmonary hemorrhage aur glomerulonephritis ke combination ko describe karta hai.

- **A.** Type III mein soluble antigen-antibody complexes tissue mein deposit hokar inflammation trigger karte hain. Anti-GBM disease ka characteristic mechanism fixed basement membrane antigen se direct binding hai.
- **B.** Type IV reaction primarily sensitized T cells se mediated hoti hai. Is clinical mechanism mein direct antibody binding important hai.
- **D.** Type I reaction IgE aur mast-cell activation se associated hoti hai. Anti-GBM disease ka characteristic mechanism IgE-mediated immediate allergy nahi hai.

**Exam clue:** Fixed tissue antigen par direct antibody binding = Type II. Deposited immune complexes = Type III.
