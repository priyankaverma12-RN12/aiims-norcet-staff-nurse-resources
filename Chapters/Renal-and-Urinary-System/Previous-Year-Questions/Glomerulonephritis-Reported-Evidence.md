## 🔵 MP Vyapam Group 5 Staff Nurse — 27 June 2023, Shift 2, reported Q82

### PYQ 1. A school-aged child is being assessed after a recent infection. Which urinary observation should particularly prompt evaluation for glomerulonephritis?

**Difficulty:** 🟢 Easy · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD

- **A.** Persistently pale urine after increased fluid intake
- **B.** Dark brown or tea-colored urine
- **C.** Clear urine with no other abnormal findings
- **D.** A transient change in urine odor alone

<details><summary>✅ Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct answer: B. Dark brown or tea-colored urine**

Glomerular capillary wall injury mein RBC urine mein leak ho sakte hain. Blood-containing urine brown, tea ya cola color ki dikh sakti hai. Recent infection ke context mein ye GN ka important clue hai. Color alone diagnosis confirm nahi karta. Urine microscopy, protein quantification, BP, renal function and clinical history assess karo.

- **A.** Pale urine increased water intake se ho sakti hai. Ye glomerular bleeding ka characteristic clue nahi hai.
- **C.** Clear urine visible hematuria show nahi karti, lekin microscopic abnormality ko exclude nahi karti.
- **D.** Odor alone GN-specific sign nahi hai. Hydration, food and infection jaise other factors bhi odor change kar sakte hain.
