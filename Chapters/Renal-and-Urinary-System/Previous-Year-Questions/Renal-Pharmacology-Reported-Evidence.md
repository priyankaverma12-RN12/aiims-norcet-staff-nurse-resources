## 🔵 RRB Nursing Superintendent — 29 April 2025, Shift 2

### PYQ 1. A patient on prescribed loop diuretic therapy develops a weak irregular pulse and generalized muscle weakness. Which electrolyte abnormality is particularly important to assess promptly?

**Difficulty:** 🟡 Medium · **Priority:** 🔴 MOST IMPORTANT

- **A.** Hyperphosphatemia alone
- **B.** Hypokalemia
- **C.** Hypernatremia as the only possible abnormality
- **D.** Hypermagnesemia as the expected effect of every loop diuretic

<details><summary>✅ Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct answer: B. Hypokalemia**

Loop diuretic thick ascending limb mein sodium transport inhibit karta hai. Distal sodium delivery aur potassium excretion badh sakti hai. Potassium low hone se muscle weakness aur cardiac rhythm changes ho sakte hain. Weak irregular pulse mein ECG, vital signs, serum potassium and magnesium assessment promptly arrange karo. Symptoms alone se exact electrolyte diagnosis nahi hota. Prescribed electrolyte replacement aur diuretic review clinical team decide karti hai.

- **A.** High phosphate CKD mein possible hai, lekin loop-related potassium loss is symptom combination ka important concern hai.
- **C.** Sodium disturbances bhi possible hain. Hypernatremia ko sole diagnosis assume nahi karte.
- **D.** Loop diuretics magnesium loss bhi cause kar sakte hain. Hypermagnesemia universal expected effect nahi hai.

[Direct third-party RRB question report](https://testbook.com/question-answer/which-symptom-should-the-nurse-assess-when-a-patie--681c797ac2c0bed03160653a). Reported answer describes weak irregular pulse and poor muscle tone. This adaptation asks the electrolyte interpretation.

## 🔵 AIIMS NORCET — 1 September 2020, memory-based

### PYQ 2. An adult on ongoing furosemide therapy develops headache, nausea and confusion. Which possible adverse effect is supported by a measured serum sodium of 124 mmol/L?

**Difficulty:** 🟡 Medium · **Priority:** 🔴 MOST IMPORTANT

- **A.** Hypernatremia
- **B.** Normal sodium concentration
- **C.** Hyponatremia
- **D.** An isolated potassium disturbance established by the sodium result

<details><summary>✅ Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct answer: C. Hyponatremia**

Sodium 124 mmol/L usual adult reference range se low hai. Furosemide renal sodium loss contribute kar sakta hai. Fluid balance, volume status, other medicines and underlying conditions bhi sodium concentration affect karte hain. Confusion ke saath clinically significant hyponatremia urgent evaluation require karti hai. Nurse independently rapid sodium correction ya arbitrary fluid restriction prescribe nahi karti.

- **A.** Hypernatremia mein serum sodium elevated hota hai.
- **B.** 124 usual normal range mein nahi hai.
- **D.** Sodium measurement se potassium concentration determine nahi hoti. Other electrolytes separately assess karo.

[Direct NORCET 2020 memory-based report](https://testbook.com/question-answer/long-term-use-of-frusemide-lasix-cause-the-follo--6177bbeb54468ab3b5c1f4c7). Source selects hyponatremia. This adaptation adds measured sodium and clinical context because diuretics may produce several adverse effects.
