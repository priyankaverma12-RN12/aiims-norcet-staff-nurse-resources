## 🔵 AIIMS Delhi Nursing Officer — 15 September 2019, memory-based report

### PYQ 1. Which dietary plan is most appropriate for a stable adult with nondialysis CKD, persistent hyperkalemia and sodium-sensitive edema, after individual renal dietitian assessment?

**Difficulty:** 🟡 Medium · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD

- **A.** Increase sodium and potassium intake without monitoring
- **B.** Use a prescribed moderate protein intake with individualized sodium and potassium restriction
- **C.** Eliminate all dietary protein permanently
- **D.** Follow an unrestricted diet regardless of laboratory results and symptoms

<details><summary>✅ Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct answer: B. Use a prescribed moderate protein intake with individualized sodium and potassium restriction**

Nondialysis CKD mein protein intake ko renal function aur nutrition status ke according plan karte hain. Edema ke saath sodium restriction useful ho sakti hai. Persistent hyperkalemia mein potassium intake, medicines aur other contributors assess karke individual advice di jaati hai. Dialysis par protein requirement change ho sakti hai.

- **A.** Extra sodium edema aur BP worsen kar sakta hai. Extra potassium persistent hyperkalemia mein unsafe ho sakta hai.
- **C.** Protein completely eliminate karne se malnutrition ka risk badhega.
- **D.** Unrestricted intake labs aur volume status ko ignore karega.

**Source caution:** Historical reported answer combines low sodium, low potassium and low protein. Isko every CKD patient ke liye blanket prescription nahi samajhna. Source ki low-potassium food examples bhi reliable clinical list nahi hain. Current dietitian guidance use karo.

## 🔵 UPSC ESIC Nursing Officer — 7 July 2024, third-party reported question

### PYQ 2. Which sustained adult eGFR category indicates kidney failure and a high risk of uremic complications, although symptoms and dialysis need must be assessed individually?

**Difficulty:** 🟢 Easy · **Priority:** 🔴 MOST IMPORTANT

- **A.** G1, at least 90 mL/min/1.73 m²
- **B.** G2, 60–89 mL/min/1.73 m²
- **C.** G3a, 45–59 mL/min/1.73 m²
- **D.** G5, below 15 mL/min/1.73 m²

<details><summary>✅ Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct answer: D. G5, below 15 mL/min/1.73 m²**

Persistent eGFR below 15 G5 kidney failure category hai. Advanced reduction mein retained solutes aur uremic symptoms ka risk increase ho sakta hai. Uremia clinical syndrome hai, fixed eGFR cutoff se automatically diagnose nahi hoti. Dialysis decision bhi symptoms, complications aur full assessment par based hota hai.

- **A.** G1 normal/high filtration category hai. CKD diagnosis ke liye other damage evidence chahiye.
- **B.** G2 mildly reduced category hai, kidney failure category nahi.
- **C.** G3a mildly to moderately reduced category hai, G5 nahi.

**Source caution:** Retrieved historical question uses below 15 as an answer to a uremia threshold question. Clinical adaptation distinguishes kidney failure category from a universal symptom threshold.
