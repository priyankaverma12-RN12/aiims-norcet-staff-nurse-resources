### 🔵 ESIC Nursing Officer — 7 July 2024 (reported) — Adaptation 1
**Difficulty: Easy | Priority: High | Pattern recognition**

Which combination of findings most strongly supports nephrotic syndrome?

A. Heavy proteinuria, hypoalbuminemia, generalized edema and hyperlipidemia  
B. Isolated glycosuria with normal serum albumin  
C. Dysuria, urgency and bacteriuria without significant proteinuria  
D. Isolated hematuria following ureteric colic  

<details>
<summary>Answer and option-wise explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: A. Heavy proteinuria, hypoalbuminemia, generalized edema and hyperlipidemia**

- **A — Correct:** Glomerular filtration barrier se protein leak hone par urine mein heavy proteinuria hoti hai. Serum albumin kam ho sakta hai, edema develop hota hai aur lipid metabolism badalne se hyperlipidemia bhi mil sakti hai. Hypertension ho sakta hai, lekin diagnosis ke liye obligatory finding nahi hai.
- **B — Incorrect:** Glycosuria mainly glucose handling ya blood glucose ki problem ka clue hai; normal serum albumin ke saath isolated glycosuria typical nephrotic pattern nahi banati.
- **C — Incorrect:** Dysuria, urgency aur bacteriuria lower urinary tract infection ke clues hain; ye heavy glomerular protein loss wala pattern establish nahi karte.
- **D — Incorrect:** Colic ke saath isolated hematuria stone ya urinary tract bleeding suggest kar sakti hai; nephrotic syndrome ke characteristic protein loss ko establish nahi karti.

**Exam clue:** Heavy proteinuria + hypoalbuminemia + edema; hyperlipidemia is common. BP normal bhi ho sakta hai.

**Reported source:** [ESIC 2024 characteristic manifestations question](https://testbook.com/question-answer/the-characteristic-manifestations-of-nephrotic-syn--668e5965b703444436975be8). Third-party reported question retrieved; official paper/key not independently authenticated. The source's inclusion of hypertension is not treated as an obligatory diagnostic feature.

**Clinical reference:** [MSD Manual — Overview of nephrotic syndrome](https://www.msdmanuals.com/professional/genitourinary-disorders/glomerular-disorders/overview-of-nephrotic-syndrome).

**Related MCQ-practice video:** [Nursing Wallah NORCET 10 renal MCQ series, 38:40](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=FzjMv67IJA8&t=2320s). Indexed description identifies nephrotic features at this chapter marker; exact question match and playback not verified.

### 🔵 RRB Staff Nurse — 21 July 2019, Shift II (reported) — Adaptation 2
**Difficulty: Medium | Priority: High | Therapeutic goal**

In a child with steroid-sensitive idiopathic nephrotic syndrome, what is the principal disease-directed objective of prescribed glucocorticoid therapy?

A. Increase urinary protein excretion  
B. Induce remission of proteinuria  
C. Replace serum albumin directly  
D. Produce immediate diuresis by blocking renal sodium reabsorption  

<details>
<summary>Answer and option-wise explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: B. Induce remission of proteinuria**

- **A — Incorrect:** Proteinuria badhana treatment ka objective nahi hai; continued protein loss edema aur complications ko worsen kar sakta hai.
- **B — Correct:** Steroid-sensitive idiopathic nephrotic syndrome mein glucocorticoids disease-directed treatment hain. Goal proteinuria ki remission achieve karna hai. Urine protein trend aur clinical status monitor karna zaroori hai. Har etiology steroid-responsive nahi hoti.
- **C — Incorrect:** Steroid albumin replacement product nahi hai; selected situations mein albumin infusion separate clinical decision hota hai.
- **D — Incorrect:** Glucocorticoid loop diuretic ki tarah direct sodium transport blockade nahi karta; edema management aur disease remission alag therapeutic objectives hain.

**Exam clue:** Steroid response ko urinary protein remission se assess karo, sirf edema kam hone se nahi.

**Reported source:** [RRB 2019 corticosteroid purpose question](https://testbook.com/question-answer/the-primary-purpose-of-administering-corticosteroi--61a9fea1157c50cd8e11686d). Retrieved third-party report; official paper/key not independently authenticated.

**Clinical reference:** [KDIGO 2025 nephrotic syndrome in children guideline](https://kdigo.org/guidelines/nephrotic-syndrome-in-children/).
