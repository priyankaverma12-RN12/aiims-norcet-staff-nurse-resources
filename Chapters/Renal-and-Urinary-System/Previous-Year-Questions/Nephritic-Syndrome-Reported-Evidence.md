## 🔵 UPSC ESIC Nursing Officer — 7 July 2024

### PYQ 1. A child is admitted with new glomerular hematuria, reduced urine output, periorbital swelling and hypertension. Which clinical pattern best integrates these findings?

**Difficulty:** 🟡 Medium · **Priority:** 🔴 MOST IMPORTANT

- **A.** Isolated lower urinary tract infection
- **B.** Acute nephritic presentation
- **C.** Isolated nephrogenic diabetes insipidus
- **D.** Uncomplicated primary polydipsia

<details><summary>✅ Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct answer: B. Acute nephritic presentation**

Glomerular inflammation se RBC urine mein leak ho sakte hain. Filtration decline aur sodium-water retention reduced urine output, edema aur elevated BP explain kar sakte hain. Ye acute nephritic pattern hai. Underlying GN cause and severity investigate karni hoti hai. AKI is presentation ke saath coexist kar sakti hai. Syndrome label alone specific etiology establish nahi karta.

- **A.** Lower UTI mein dysuria aur frequency possible hain, lekin glomerular bleeding, edema aur hypertension ka combined pattern isolated cystitis se adequately explain nahi hota.
- **C.** Diabetes insipidus mein dilute urine ka volume typically badhta hai. Oliguria and active glomerular sediment expected defining features nahi hain.
- **D.** Excess water intake alone glomerular red-cell leakage establish nahi karta.

**Exam clue:** Inflammation-dominant pattern ko blood in urine, filtration decline aur sodium-water retention ke saath identify karo.
