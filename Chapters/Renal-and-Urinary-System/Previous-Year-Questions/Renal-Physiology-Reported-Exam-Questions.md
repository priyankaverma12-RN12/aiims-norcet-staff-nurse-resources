### RP-PYQ-01 — Normal GFR

**🔵 Reported:** Haryana USHR Staff Nurse, 17 April 2022 and DSSSB Public Health Nurse 2015 | **🟠 High yield | 🟢 Easy**

A healthy young adult has a typical textbook whole-kidney filtration rate closest to:

A. 25 mL/min  
B. 125 mL/min  
C. 180 mL/min  
D. 1,800 mL/min

<details>
<summary>Answer and explanation</summary>

**Correct: B.** GFR dono kidneys mein per minute filter hone wala fluid hai. Standard physiology approximation 125 mL/min hai, jo lagbhag 180 L/day banta hai. Actual GFR age, body size aur clinical condition se change hota hai.

- A: 25 mL/min typical healthy young adult value se kaafi kam hai.
- C: 180 L/day approximate daily filtration hai, 180 mL/min nahi.
- D: 1,800 mL/min textbook GFR se bahut zyada hai.

</details>

### RP-PYQ-02 — Renin release

**🔵 Reported:** ESIC Nursing Officer 2019, Shift 1 | **🔴 Most important | 🟡 Medium**

Reduced renal perfusion activates juxtaglomerular cells. Which substance is released to initiate the compensatory RAAS pathway?

A. Aldosterone  
B. Insulin  
C. Renin  
D. Atrial natriuretic peptide

<details>
<summary>Answer and explanation</summary>

**Correct: C.** Kidney ke juxtaglomerular cells low perfusion par renin release karte hain. Renin angiotensinogen ko angiotensin I mein convert karta hai. ACE angiotensin II banata hai, jo vasoconstriction aur aldosterone secretion promote karta hai.

- A: Aldosterone adrenal cortex se aata hai, juxtaglomerular cells se nahi.
- B: Insulin pancreas ke beta cells ka hormone hai.
- D: ANP atrial stretch par release hota hai aur sodium excretion promote karta hai.

</details>

### RP-PYQ-03 — ADH and aquaporins

**🔵 Reported:** ESIC Nursing Officer 2019, Shift 1 and RRB Nursing Superintendent 20 July 2019, Shift 1 | **🔴 Most important | 🟡 Medium**

Which hormone increases collecting duct water permeability through V2 receptor mediated aquaporin-2 insertion?

A. ANP  
B. Aldosterone  
C. Erythropoietin  
D. Vasopressin

<details>
<summary>Answer and explanation</summary>

**Correct: D.** Vasopressin ko ADH bhi kehte hain. Ye collecting duct ke principal cells par V2 receptor activate karta hai. Aquaporin-2 apical membrane mein insert hota hai aur water reabsorption badhti hai.

- A: ANP sodium aur water excretion promote karta hai.
- B: Aldosterone mainly sodium reabsorption aur potassium secretion regulate karta hai, direct AQP2 insertion nahi.
- C: Erythropoietin RBC production stimulate karta hai.

</details>
