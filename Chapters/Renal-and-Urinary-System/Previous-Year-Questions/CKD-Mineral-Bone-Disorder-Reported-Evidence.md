## 🔵 AIIMS NORCET 11 Prelims — 2026, coaching memory-based Q3

### PYQ 1. An adult with advanced untreated CKD has declining renal clearance. Which combined laboratory pattern is physiologically plausible and warrants assessment?

**Difficulty:** 🟡 Medium · **Priority:** 🔴 MOST IMPORTANT

- **A.** Low bicarbonate with elevated serum potassium, phosphate and magnesium
- **B.** High bicarbonate with universal potassium and phosphate depletion
- **C.** High phosphate with obligatory severe hypomagnesemia in every patient
- **D.** Normal creatinine with obligatory metabolic alkalosis and phosphate depletion

<details><summary>✅ Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct answer: A. Low bicarbonate with elevated serum potassium, phosphate and magnesium**

Advanced CKD mein kidney acid aur electrolytes efficiently remove nahi kar paati. Isliye bicarbonate kam ho sakta hai aur potassium aur phosphate accumulate ho sakte hain. Magnesium bhi elevated ho sakta hai, especially severe renal impairment ya magnesium-containing medicines ke context mein. Ye possible pattern hai, har patient ke liye compulsory combination nahi. Diet, medications, dialysis, residual kidney function aur other illnesses actual result affect karte hain.

- **B.** Acidosis aur retention common possibilities hain. Universal potassium/phosphate depletion CKD ka defining pattern nahi hai.
- **C.** Magnesium low bhi ho sakta hai in selected situations, lekin obligatory severe hypomagnesemia unsupported hai.
- **D.** Creatinine alone CKD severity exclude nahi karta. Metabolic alkalosis and phosphate depletion obligatory CKD findings nahi hain.

## 🔵 RRB Staff Nurse — 20 July 2019, Shift III

### PYQ 2. During assessment of a renal patient with suspected mineral imbalance, gentle tapping over the facial nerve region produces ipsilateral facial muscle twitching. Which abnormality should be considered and confirmed by laboratory assessment?

**Difficulty:** 🟡 Medium · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD

- **A.** Hypernatremia
- **B.** Hypocalcemia
- **C.** Hypercalcemia
- **D.** Isolated hyperphosphatemia with no possible calcium change

<details><summary>✅ Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct answer: B. Hypocalcemia**

Is finding ko Chvostek sign kehte hain. Low ionized calcium mein nerve aur muscle excitability increase ho sakti hai. Facial muscle twitch supportive clue hai, definitive diagnostic test nahi. Clinical symptoms aur calcium measurement ke saath interpret karo. CKD patient mein calcium-lowering medicine history, magnesium aur other mineral parameters assess karo.

- **A.** Hypernatremia neurologic symptoms cause kar sakti hai, lekin ye classic facial nerve irritability sign specifically sodium excess establish nahi karta.
- **C.** Hypercalcemia usually neuromuscular excitability reduce kar sakti hai. Classic Chvostek association low calcium hai.
- **D.** Phosphate excess calcium regulation affect kar sakta hai. Is sign se isolated phosphate excess diagnose nahi hota.
