## 🔵 GMCH Nursing Officer — 29 June 2025

### PYQ 1. A maintenance hemodialysis patient needs additional high-quality protein while limiting phosphorus exposure. The patient eats eggs and the renal dietitian is reviewing breakfast options. Which choice best meets this specified goal among the following?

**Difficulty:** 🟡 Medium · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD

- **A.** A breakfast consisting only of sugar-sweetened tea
- **B.** A portion of cooked egg whites in the individualized meal plan
- **C.** A portion of egg yolks alone selected specifically for their low phosphorus content
- **D.** A processed meat product containing added phosphate selected without reviewing its label

<details><summary>✅ Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct answer: B. A portion of cooked egg whites in the individualized meal plan**

Egg whites essential amino acids ke saath high-quality protein provide karte hain aur yolk ke comparison mein phosphorus kam hota hai. Hemodialysis mein adequate protein important hai because treatment amino acid/protein losses contribute kar sakta hai. Actual quantity dietitian patient ki requirement aur mineral reports ke according decide karta hai.

- **A.** Tea with sugar protein requirement meet nahi karti.
- **C.** Egg yolk mein phosphorus relatively zyada hota hai, so specified lower-phosphorus goal ke liye whites more suitable ho sakte hain.
- **D.** Added phosphate highly absorbable ho sakta hai. Processed food label aur sodium content review karna chahiye.

**Historical source:** [GMCH Nursing Officer 29 June 2025 question report](https://testbook.com/question-answer/a-renal-patient-on-hemodialysis-is-prescribed-a-hi--68662696ee35a0d85b9473f5). Retrieved HTML identifies egg whites as the reported answer. Original options and wording are not reproduced here. The source's blanket statements about plant proteins are not adopted. Soy can provide all essential amino acids and appropriately planned vegetarian diets can meet protein requirements.
