## 🔵 AIIMS NORCET — 20 November 2021, memory-based

### PYQ 1. Which bedside finding most directly suggests elevated systemic venous filling pressure in a patient being evaluated for renal fluid overload?

**Difficulty:** 🟡 Medium · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD

- **A.** Isolated dry oral mucosa
- **B.** Elevated jugular venous pressure
- **C.** Isolated painful urination
- **D.** Flat neck veins while appropriately positioned

<details><summary>✅ Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct answer: B. Elevated jugular venous pressure**

JVP right-sided filling pressure ka clinical estimate hai. Jab systemic venous congestion hoti hai toh neck venous pressure elevated ho sakta hai. Renal patient mein fluid excess possible hai, lekin cardiac disease aur other causes bhi assess karne hote hain. JVP ko symptoms, BP, lung sounds aur weight trend ke saath interpret karo.

- **A.** Dry mucosa hydration ya local mouth dryness ka clue ho sakti hai, elevated venous filling pressure ka direct sign nahi.
- **C.** Dysuria urinary tract symptom hai, systemic congestion ka direct sign nahi.
- **D.** Flat neck veins elevated venous pressure support nahi karti hain, although measurement technique aur position important hain.

**Exam clue:** Raised JVP supports venous congestion, but is not diagnostic of renal overload alone.
