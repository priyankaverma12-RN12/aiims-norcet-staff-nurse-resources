## 🔵 RRB Nursing Superintendent — 29 April 2025, Shift 2

### PYQ 1. Which patient requires particular surveillance for potassium accumulation due to impaired renal elimination?

**Difficulty:** 🟢 Easy · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD

- **A.** An adult with isolated uncomplicated seasonal allergic rhinitis
- **B.** An adult with advanced renal dysfunction and markedly reduced urinary excretion
- **C.** An adult with normal renal function and uncomplicated myopia
- **D.** An adult with a healed superficial skin abrasion and normal renal function

<details><summary>✅ Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct answer: B. An adult with advanced renal dysfunction and markedly reduced urinary excretion**

Kidney excess potassium remove karne ka main organ hai. Jab filtration aur tubular excretion significantly impair hoti hai, toh potassium accumulate ho sakta hai. Har renal patient ka potassium high hona compulsory nahi hai, isliye serial labs aur medicine review important hain.

- **A.** Uncomplicated allergic rhinitis renal potassium excretion directly impair nahi karti.
- **C.** Myopia refractive problem hai, jo by itself renal potassium clearance reduce nahi karti.
- **D.** Healed minor abrasion ongoing cell breakdown ya renal dysfunction ka clue nahi hai.

## 🔵 AIIMS NORCET — 1 September 2020, memory-based report

### PYQ 2. During a prescribed intravenous potassium replacement infusion, a patient develops new tall peaked T waves and a rising serum potassium result. What should the nurse do?

**Difficulty:** 🟡 Medium · **Priority:** 🔴 MOST IMPORTANT

- **A.** Increase the potassium infusion rate
- **B.** Continue replacement until the entire bag is empty
- **C.** Stop the potassium infusion, urgently assess the patient and notify the treating team
- **D.** Remove cardiac monitoring because peaked T waves are always benign

<details><summary>✅ Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct answer: C. Stop the potassium infusion, urgently assess the patient and notify the treating team**

Potassium replacement ke dauran rising K aur new peaked T waves dangerous hyperkalemia ka concern raise karte hain. Potassium administration stop karke ABC, vitals, ECG aur urgent clinical review arrange karo. Prescribed emergency protocol follow karo. ECG finding alone diagnosis confirm nahi karti, lekin is scenario mein biochemical trend bhi concerning hai.

- **A.** Faster potassium administration arrhythmia risk badha sakti hai.
- **B.** Bag empty hone ka wait ongoing potassium exposure continue karega.
- **D.** Peaked T waves concerning clinical context mein monitoring remove karne ka reason nahi hain.
