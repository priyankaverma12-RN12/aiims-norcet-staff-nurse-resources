### PYQ1. Which warning sign should a kidney transplant recipient taking cyclosporine report promptly?

**Difficulty:** Easy/Medium | **Topic:** Transplant care

- **A.** Fever or other signs of infection
- **B.** Normal appetite
- **C.** Stable urine output
- **D.** Improved exercise tolerance

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation</summary>

**Correct: A. Fever or other signs of infection**

Cyclosporine immune response suppress karti hai taaki transplant rejection ka risk kam ho. Isse infection ka risk badhta hai, isliye fever ya infection ke signs report karne chahiye. Stable urine, normal appetite aur improving activity apne aap mein infection warning nahi hain.

</details>

### PYQ2. How can a nurse reduce traction on an indwelling urinary catheter in an adult female patient?

**Difficulty:** Easy/Medium | **Topic:** Catheter safety

- **A.** Pull on the catheter during each assessment
- **B.** Secure it appropriately to the thigh without tension
- **C.** Let the catheter hang freely under its own weight
- **D.** Tie the tubing tightly around the ankle

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation</summary>

**Correct: B. Secure it appropriately to the thigh without tension**

Catheter ko approved securement device se thigh par secure karne se urethra par pull kam hota hai. Tubing mein enough slack hona chahiye. Free hanging, tight tying aur pulling injury ka risk badha sakti hain.

</details>
