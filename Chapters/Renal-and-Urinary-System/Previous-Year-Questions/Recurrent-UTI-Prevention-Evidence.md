### Practice 1. A woman's symptomatic cystitis resolved after treatment of a documented E. coli infection. Six weeks later she develops new bladder symptoms and a properly collected culture grows Klebsiella pneumoniae. Which mechanism best explains the second episode?

**Difficulty:** 🟡 Medium · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD · Source-based adaptation, not an original bank slot.

- **A.** Persistence of the initial E. coli infection
- **B.** Reinfection with a different organism
- **C.** Asymptomatic bacteriuria
- **D.** Confirmed urinary obstruction causing recurrence

<details><summary>✅ Correct answer and detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: B. Reinfection with a different organism**

**A. Kyun galat hai:** Relapse mein pehle wala organism persist kar sakta hai aur symptoms aksar treatment ke jaldi baad wapas aa sakte hain. Yahan pehla episode resolve hua tha aur naya culture different species dikha raha hai. Isliye persistence of the original infection best explanation nahi hai.

**B. Kyun sahi hai:** Reinfection ka matlab hai ki patient ko ek naya infection hua hai. Yahan pehle E. coli infection ke baad symptoms resolve hue aur chhe hafte baad Klebsiella ke saath naya symptomatic episode aaya. Ye pattern reinfection support karta hai. Har recurrence mein same species milne ka matlab relapse hi ho, aisa zaroori nahi hai.

**C. Kyun galat hai:** Asymptomatic bacteriuria mein bacteria urine mein mil sakte hain, lekin attributable UTI symptoms absent hote hain. Is patient ke naye bladder symptoms hain. Isliye ASB is scenario ka best description nahi hai.

**D. Kyun galat hai:** Obstruction recurrent infection ka risk factor ho sakta hai. Lekin is scenario mein imaging ya doosra evidence obstruction confirm nahi karta. Sirf recurrence ke basis par obstruction diagnose nahi karna chahiye.

**Exam clue:** Organism identity, recovery between episodes aur symptom timing ko saath dekho. Fixed percentage ya time threshold ko har patient mein diagnostic proof mat samjho.

**Clinical reference:** [NICE evidence review for recurrent UTI prevention](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/NBK611982/?report=printable).

**YouTube MCQ practice:** [UTI Nursing MCQs with Concept Clarity](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=mtjTI100_sw) is a broad description-level question-practice lead. This exact mechanism discussion and timestamp are not verified.
