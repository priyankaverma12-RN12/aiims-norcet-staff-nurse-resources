### Related practice exercise — uncounted

Which mechanism commonly explains bacterial infection of the renal pelvis after a lower urinary tract infection?

**Difficulty:** 🟡 Medium · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD

- **A.** Immune complexes deposit in the glomerular capillaries without bacterial invasion
- **B.** Bacteria travel upward through a ureter into the upper urinary tract
- **C.** Loss of nephron filtration causes sterile inflammation in every case
- **D.** Dehydration alone directly generates bacteria in renal tissue

<details><summary>✅ Correct answer and individual explanations</summary>

**Correct answer: B. Bacteria travel upward through a ureter into the upper urinary tract**

**A kyun galat hai:** Immune complex deposition glomerular inflammation ka mechanism ho sakta hai, lekin ascending bacterial renal infection ko explain nahi karta.

**B kyun sahi hai:** Lower urinary tract mein bacteria bladder se ureter ke through renal pelvis aur kidney tissue tak ascend kar sakte hain. Is route se pyelonephritis develop ho sakti hai.

**C kyun galat hai:** Reduced filtration kidney dysfunction ka finding ho sakta hai. Har pyelonephritis case ko sterile inflammation nahi kehte, kyunki bacterial infection important mechanism hai.

**D kyun galat hai:** Dehydration urinary flow aur patient risk affect kar sakti hai, lekin khud renal tissue mein bacteria generate nahi karti. Microbial infection ka source aur route alag evaluate karte hain.

**Clinical reference:** [EAU infections](https://uroweb.org/guidelines/urological-infections/chapter/the-guideline).
