### PYQ 1. Which complete microscopic working unit performs initial filtration and subsequent tubular processing in the kidney?

**Difficulty:** 🟢 Easy · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD · Reported source-based adaptation

- **A.** Renal papilla
- **B.** Nephron
- **C.** Glomerulus alone
- **D.** Renal pelvis

<details><summary>✅ Answer aur easy explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: B. Nephron**

Nephron ko kidney ka ek complete mini filter samjho. Iske corpuscle mein blood se filtrate banta hai. Phir tubular segments paani aur useful substances ko blood mein wapas bhejne aur waste secretion mein help karte hain. Isliye complete working unit nephron hai.

**Baaki options kyun galat hain?**

- **A.** Renal papilla pyramid ka tip hai jahan collecting system ka urine drain hota hai. Ye poora filter unit nahi hai.
- **C.** Glomerulus filtration karta hai, lekin tubular processing ke bina poora nephron nahi banta.
- **D.** Renal pelvis urine collect karke ureter ki taraf bhejta hai. Ye microscopic filtration unit nahi hai.

**Exam clue:** Glomerulus sirf filter part hai. Nephron complete unit hai.

### PYQ 2. A renal histology image shows a regulatory complex where the distal tubular region contacts the glomerular arterioles. Which complex is this?

**Difficulty:** 🟡 Medium · **Priority:** 🔴 MOST IMPORTANT · Reported source-based adaptation

- **A.** Renal corpuscle
- **B.** Vasa recta
- **C.** Juxtaglomerular apparatus
- **D.** Renal papilla

<details><summary>✅ Answer aur easy explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: C. Juxtaglomerular apparatus**

JGA ko kidney ka local monitoring station samjho. Ismein macula densa, renin release karne wale granular cells aur extraglomerular mesangial cells hote hain. Ye tubular salt delivery aur local pressure signals ke basis par filtration aur renin release ko regulate karne mein help karta hai.

**Baaki options kyun galat hain?**

- **A.** Renal corpuscle glomerulus aur Bowman's capsule ka combination hai. Ye specified regulatory cell complex nahi hai.
- **B.** Vasa recta medullary blood vessels hain jo urine concentration mechanism mein help karte hain.
- **D.** Papilla pyramid ka tip hai jahan urine papillary ducts se calyx mein jaata hai.

**Exam clue:** JGA vascular pole par hota hai. Macula densa NaCl sense karti hai aur granular cells renin release karte hain.

### PYQ 3. Which nephron population makes up the majority of human nephrons and generally has relatively short loops of Henle?

**Difficulty:** 🟢 Easy · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD · Reported source-based adaptation

- **A.** Juxtamedullary nephrons
- **B.** Papillary ducts
- **C.** Vasa recta
- **D.** Cortical nephrons

<details><summary>✅ Answer aur easy explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: D. Cortical nephrons**

Cortical nephrons ke corpuscles cortex mein relatively outer side par hote hain. Inke loops of Henle short hote hain aur outer medulla tak ja sakte hain. Ye roughly 85 percent nephrons hote hain, though exact proportion vary kar sakta hai.

**Baaki options kyun galat hain?**

- **A.** Juxtamedullary nephrons ke corpuscles medulla ke paas hote hain aur inke loops deeper medulla tak jaate hain.
- **B.** Papillary ducts collecting system ka last part hain. Ye nephron type nahi hain.
- **C.** Vasa recta blood vessels hain. Ye filtering unit ki classification nahi hain.

**Exam clue:** Cortical = short loop. Juxtamedullary = long loop.

## 🔵 NHM UP Staff Nurse 2019 — retrieved third-party report

### PYQ 4. Which covering should be distinguished from Bowman's capsule because it encloses the entire kidney rather than an individual glomerulus?

**Difficulty:** 🟡 Medium · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD · Reported source-based adaptation

- **A.** Renal fibrous capsule
- **B.** Glomerular basement membrane
- **C.** Renal papilla
- **D.** Visceral layer of Bowman's capsule

<details><summary>✅ Answer aur easy explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: A. Renal fibrous capsule**

Renal fibrous capsule poori kidney ke outer tissue par direct covering banati hai. Bowman's capsule kidney ke andar ek individual glomerulus ko surround karti hai. Dono ko naam mein capsule hone ke baad bhi confuse mat karo.

**Baaki options kyun galat hain?**

- **B.** Glomerular basement membrane microscopic filtration barrier ka part hai. Ye poori kidney ko cover nahi karti.
- **C.** Papilla pyramid ka pointed tip hai. Ye protective covering nahi hai.
- **D.** Bowman's capsule ki visceral layer podocytes se banti hai. Ye individual glomerulus se related hai, poori kidney se nahi.

**Exam clue:** Renal capsule covers organ. Bowman's capsule surrounds glomerulus.
