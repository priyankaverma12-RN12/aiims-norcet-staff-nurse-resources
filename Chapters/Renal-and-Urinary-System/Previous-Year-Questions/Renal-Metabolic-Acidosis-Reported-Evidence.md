## 🔵 AIIMS NORCET 8 Prelims — 2025, memory-based

### PYQ 1. A patient with AKI has severe metabolic acidemia. The treating team has assessed ventilation, circulation and other dialysis indications and prescribed intravenous sodium bicarbonate. Which nursing precaution is appropriate during administration?

**Difficulty:** 🟡 Medium · **Priority:** 🔴 MOST IMPORTANT

- **A.** Administer repeated additional doses independently until the pH exceeds the normal range
- **B.** Monitor acid-base status, ventilation, sodium and volume status, potassium and ionized calcium according to the clinical protocol
- **C.** Discontinue clinical monitoring immediately when the infusion begins
- **D.** Assume dialysis cannot be indicated if any bicarbonate has been administered

<details><summary>✅ Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct answer: B. Monitor acid-base status, ventilation, sodium and volume status, potassium and ionized calcium according to the clinical protocol**

Selected patients with severe metabolic acidemia mein clinical team IV bicarbonate consider kar sakti hai. Is treatment se sodium load, CO₂ production aur electrolyte shifts ho sakte hain. Ionized calcium fall aur alkalinization se potassium changes possible hain. Nurse prescribed protocol ke according response aur adverse effects monitor karti hai.

- **A.** Independent additional doses overcorrection, sodium load aur electrolyte disturbance cause kar sakti hain.
- **C.** Infusion start hone par monitoring aur important ho jaati hai.
- **D.** Life-threatening acidosis refractory to appropriate treatment mein RRT urgent ho sakti hai. Bicarbonate milne se dialysis indication automatically eliminate nahi hoti.

**Historical context:** The third-party source selected bicarbonate in an AKI acidemia scenario. The original scenario did not adequately establish ventilation, volume status, cause or response to treatment to support a universal bicarbonate-versus-dialysis decision. This adaptation tests safe administration after a clinical decision rather than reproducing that ambiguity.
