## 🔵 HPSSC Staff Nurse — 1 August 2021

### PYQ 1. Which combination correctly matches methods for estimating urine specific gravity with their underlying measurement principles?

**Difficulty:** 🟡 Medium · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD

- **A.** Urinometer — buoyancy, refractometer — light refraction, reagent strip — chemical reaction
- **B.** Urinometer — ECG voltage, refractometer — blood pressure, reagent strip — ultrasound
- **C.** Urinometer — bacterial culture, refractometer — tissue biopsy, reagent strip — radiography
- **D.** All three measure urine osmolality directly and are interchangeable

<details><summary>✅ Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct answer: A. Urinometer — buoyancy, refractometer — light refraction, reagent strip — chemical reaction**

Urinometer liquid mein kitna float karta hai usse density ka idea milta hai. Refractometer light ki direction mein change ko measure karta hai. Reagent strip chemical pad ki response se SG estimate karti hai. Teeno ka result method aur solutes ke hisaab se differ kar sakta hai.

- **B.** ECG, BP aur ultrasound SG measurement ke principles nahi hain.
- **C.** Culture microorganisms identify karti hai, biopsy tissue examine karti hai aur radiography imaging karti hai.
- **D.** SG density ko reflect karti hai aur osmolality particle number concentration ko. Dono identical nahi hain.

**Exam clue:** Urinometer, refractometer aur reagent strip teeno SG assess kar sakte hain.

## 🔵 BHU Nursing Officer — 2019

### PYQ 2. When a first-morning clean-catch sample is requested for routine urine microscopy, which patient instruction is appropriate?

**Difficulty:** 🟡 Medium · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD

- **A.** Collect yesterday's urine from an open container
- **B.** Follow cleansing instructions, discard the initial stream and collect the middle stream in the specified container
- **C.** Touch the inside of the specimen container to check its cleanliness
- **D.** Add tap water to increase the specimen volume

<details><summary>✅ Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct answer: B. Follow cleansing instructions, discard the initial stream and collect the middle stream in the specified container**

First morning urine often more concentrated hota hai aur requested routine microscopy mein useful ho sakta hai. Clean-catch midstream technique contamination kam karne mein help karti hai. Har test ke liye first morning sample compulsory nahi hota. Lab instructions follow karna zaroori hai.

- **A.** Old open sample mein bacterial growth aur chemical changes ho sakte hain.
- **C.** Inner surface touch karne se specimen contamination ka risk badhta hai.
- **D.** Tap water urine ko dilute aur contaminate kar sakta hai.

**Exam clue:** Sample timing aur clean-catch technique alag concepts hain. Test-specific instructions follow karo.
