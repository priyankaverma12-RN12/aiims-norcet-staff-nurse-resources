## 🔵 RRB Nursing Superintendent — 29 April 2025, Shift 2

### PYQ 1. An adult with CKD has persistent anemia after correctable causes have been addressed. The nephrologist selects a medication that directly mimics the deficient renal signal to bone marrow. Which medication fits this purpose?

**Difficulty:** 🟡 Medium · **Priority:** 🔴 MOST IMPORTANT

- **A.** Oral ferrous fumarate
- **B.** Epoetin alfa
- **C.** Sevelamer carbonate
- **D.** Calcitriol

<details><summary>✅ Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct answer: B. Epoetin alfa**

Kidney ka erythropoietin hormone normally bone marrow ko RBC banane ka signal deta hai. CKD mein ye signal inadequate ho sakta hai. Epoetin alfa ek ESA hai jo erythropoietin action mimic karta hai. Iron deficiency aur other reversible causes ko address karne ke baad selected patients mein prescribed ESA consider hota hai. Hb response, BP aur thrombotic risks monitor karo.

- **A.** Ferrous fumarate iron supplement hai. Iron deficiency correction important ho sakti hai, lekin ye directly erythropoietin signal replace nahi karta.
- **C.** Sevelamer phosphate binder hai. Ye intestinal phosphate absorption reduce karta hai, marrow ko EPO signal nahi deta.
- **D.** Calcitriol active vitamin D hai. Calcium-phosphate and PTH management mein selected use hai, direct ESA action nahi.

## 🔵 HPSSC Staff Nurse — 1 August 2021

### PYQ 2. In an adult being evaluated for CKD anemia, which laboratory marker provides information about iron stores, although inflammation may increase its level?

**Difficulty:** 🟡 Medium · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD

- **A.** Serum creatinine
- **B.** Serum potassium
- **C.** Serum ferritin
- **D.** Hemoglobin concentration alone

<details><summary>✅ Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct answer: C. Serum ferritin**

Ferritin iron storage se related protein hai. Serum ferritin low ho toh iron stores depleted hone ka important clue milta hai. CKD aur inflammation mein ferritin high bhi ho sakta hai despite low usable iron. Isliye TSAT aur clinical findings ke saath interpret karo. Original reported question general iron deficiency par tha. Ye adaptation specifically renal context aur inflammation caveat add karti hai.

- **A.** Creatinine filtration assessment mein helpful hai. Iron stores directly establish nahi karta.
- **B.** Potassium electrolyte hai. Iron storage ka marker nahi.
- **D.** Hb anemia identify karne mein helpful hai but anemia ki iron-specific cause confirm nahi karta.
