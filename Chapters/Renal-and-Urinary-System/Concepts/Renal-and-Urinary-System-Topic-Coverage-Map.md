## Initial study explanations

### AKI

Kidney potassium ko blood se nikaalne mein fail ho sakti hai. High potassium heart ki electrical activity ko dangerous bana sakta hai. ECG changes ke saath emergency treatment chahiye.

### Hemodialysis

Fistula mein blood flow hone par halka vibration feel hota hai. Thrill gayab ho toh blockage ka doubt hota hai. Nurse us access ko use karne se pehle dialysis team ko batati hai.

### Peritoneal dialysis

Peritoneal dialysis mein pet ki lining blood se waste nikaalne mein madad karti hai. Cloudy fluid aur pet pain infection ke signs ho sakte hain. Nurse sample aur urgent review arrange karti hai.

### Nephrotic syndrome

Jab albumin urine se nikalta hai, toh blood mein paani rokne ki ability kam ho jaati hai. Fluid tissues mein jama ho sakta hai. Sodium retention bhi swelling ko badha sakti hai.
