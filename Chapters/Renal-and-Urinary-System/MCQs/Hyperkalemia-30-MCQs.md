## Potassium balance and causes

### Q1. A stable adult has confirmed potassium 5.8 mmol/L. Which UKKA severity category applies?

**Difficulty:** 🟡 Medium · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD

- **A.** Moderate hyperkalemia
- **B.** Severe hyperkalemia
- **C.** Mild hyperkalemia
- **D.** Hypokalemia

<details><summary>✅ Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct answer: C. Mild hyperkalemia**

**Kyun sahi hai?** 5.8 mmol/L UKKA mild range 5.5–5.9 mein hai. Symptoms, rate of rise aur kidney function bhi management decide karte hain.

**Baaki options kyun galat hain?**

- **A.** Moderate range 6.0–6.4 hai.
- **B.** Severe category 6.5 ya above hai.
- **D.** Hypokalemia mein potassium low hota hai.

**Exam clue:** Severity category does not replace clinical assessment.

### Q3. A patient with CKD develops hyperkalemia after starting spironolactone. Which mechanism is most relevant?

**Difficulty:** 🟡 Medium · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD

- **B.** Reduced aldosterone-mediated distal potassium secretion
- **C.** Direct destruction of all circulating platelets
- **D.** Increased glomerular filtration in every nephron

<details><summary>✅ Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Baaki options kyun galat hain?**

- **A.** Colon elimination is medicine ka primary mechanism nahi hai.
- **C.** Platelet destruction mechanism nahi hai.
- **D.** Medicine filtration universally increase nahi karti.

**Exam clue:** Mineralocorticoid blockade can retain potassium.

### Q8. A blood sample is visibly hemolyzed and potassium is unexpectedly high in a clinically stable patient. Which explanation is plausible?

**Difficulty:** 🟡 Medium · **Priority:** 🔴 MOST IMPORTANT

- **A.** Hemolysis always proves true severe hyperkalemia
- **B.** Hemolysis removes potassium from the specimen
- **C.** A hemolyzed sample is always more accurate
- **D.** Potassium released from damaged blood cells may falsely raise the measured result

<details><summary>✅ Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct answer: D. Potassium released from damaged blood cells may falsely raise the measured result**

**Kyun sahi hai?** RBC ke andar potassium high hota hai. Sample mein cells break hone par measured result falsely increase ho sakta hai. Prompt repeat sample aur clinical assessment chahiye.

**Baaki options kyun galat hain?**

- **A.** Sample hemolysis in-vivo hyperkalemia prove nahi karti.
- **B.** Cell breakdown potassium sample fluid mein release karta hai.

**Exam clue:** Consider specimen artifact but assess patient safety.
