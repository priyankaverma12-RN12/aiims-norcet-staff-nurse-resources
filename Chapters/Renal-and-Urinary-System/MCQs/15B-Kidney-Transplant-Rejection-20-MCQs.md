Isolated candidate; original practice, not historical PYQs. Rejection diagnosis and treatment require the specialist transplant team and current pathology criteria.

### Q1. What does renal allograft rejection mean?

**Difficulty:** Easy | **Topic:** Kidney allograft rejection and graft assessment | **Priority:** High | **Type:** Original practice

- **A.** Normal urine production by a healthy graft
- **B.** Mechanical obstruction of every urinary catheter
- **C.** Immune-mediated injury to the transplanted kidney
- **D.** Expected surgical wound healing

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: C. Immune-mediated injury to the transplanted kidney**

Recipient immune response graft tissue ko injure kar sakta hai, isi ko rejection kehte hain.

**Baaki options kyun galat hain?**

- **A.** Normal urine production graft function support karti hai.
- **B.** Catheter obstruction nonimmune mechanical problem hai.
- **D.** Wound healing rejection ka definition nahi hai.

**Exam clue:** Rejection is immune-mediated graft injury.
