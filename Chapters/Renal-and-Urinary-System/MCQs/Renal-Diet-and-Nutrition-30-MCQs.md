### Q1. A patient with CKD G4 has increasing edema but reports reduced food intake and loosening clothes around the arms. Which approach best evaluates nutritional change?

**Difficulty:** 🟡 Medium · **Priority:** 🔴 MOST IMPORTANT

- **A.** Conclude nutrition has improved because body weight increased
- **B.** Review edema-adjusted weight trends, muscle findings and dietary history
- **C.** Use edema severity as the sole measure of calorie adequacy
- **D.** Prescribe weight reduction solely from the current scale reading

<details><summary>✅ Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct answer: B. Review edema-adjusted weight trends, muscle findings and dietary history**

**Kyun sahi hai?** Fluid retention scale weight increase kar sakti hai even when muscle aur fat loss ho raha ho. Intake history, tissue findings and estimated dry-weight trend together assess karo.

**Baaki options kyun galat hain?**

- **A.** Weight increase fluid se ho sakta hai, nutrition improvement necessarily nahi.
- **C.** Edema energy intake directly measure nahi karta.
- **D.** Scale weight alone tissue loss ko miss kar sakta hai.

**Exam clue:** Assess nutrition using multiple findings, not one lab.
