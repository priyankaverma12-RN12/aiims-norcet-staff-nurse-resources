### Q1. Which description best distinguishes urinary retention from anuria?

**Difficulty:** Easy | **Topic:** Urinary retention and bladder emptying | **Priority:** Medium | **Type:** Original practice

- **A.** The kidneys produce almost no urine
- **B.** Urine remains in the bladder because emptying is impaired
- **C.** Urine is lost involuntarily during coughing
- **D.** Urine contains excessive protein

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: B. Urine remains in the bladder because emptying is impaired**

Retention mein bladder empty nahi hota, jabki kidney urine bana sakti hai.

**Baaki options kyun galat hain?**

- **A.** Anuria mein urine production ya measurable output bahut kam hota hai, bladder filling separately assess karni chahiye.
- **C.** Cough ke saath leakage stress incontinence ka pattern hai, retention ki definition nahi.
- **D.** Proteinuria filtration barrier ki problem bata sakti hai, bladder emptying ka measure nahi.

**Exam clue:** Retention mein bladder empty nahi hota, jabki kidney urine bana sakti hai.
