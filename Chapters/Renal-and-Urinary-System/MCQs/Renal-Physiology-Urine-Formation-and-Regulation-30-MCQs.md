### Q1. A healthy adult has a constant GFR of 125 mL/min for 24 hours. What total volume is filtered?

**Difficulty:** 🟡 Medium · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD · 🟣 [Haryana USHR Staff Nurse 17 April 2022 — related GFR concept](https://testbook.com/question-answer/normal-glomerular-filtration-rate-is--625e7968d145fa29ed42bcf7)

- **A.** 18 L
- **B.** 125 L
- **C.** 180 L
- **D.** 1.8 L

<details><summary>✅ Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct answer: C. 180 L**

**Kyun sahi hai?** Ek din mein 1,440 minutes hote hain. 125 × 1,440 = 180,000 mL, yaani 180 L filtrate banta hai. Ye final urine nahi hai.

**Baaki options kyun galat hain?**

- **A.** 18 L calculation mein factor of ten kam hai.
- **B.** 125 L tab aata agar daily time ko 1,000 minutes maan lete. Actual day 1,440 minutes ka hai.
- **D.** 1.8 L daily urine jaisa ho sakta hai, lekin question filtered volume poochh raha hai.

**Exam clue:** GFR ko mL/min se L/day convert karne ke liye ×1.44 karo.

### Q2. A patient filters 144 L/day and produces 1.44 L of urine. Assuming net water secretion is negligible, what percentage of filtered water is reabsorbed?

**Difficulty:** 🔴 Difficult · **Priority:** 🔴 MOST IMPORTANT

- **A.** 99%
- **B.** 90%
- **C.** 1%
- **D.** 98%

<details><summary>✅ Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct answer: A. 99%**

**Kyun sahi hai?** 144 − 1.44 = 142.56 L water recover hua. 142.56 ÷ 144 × 100 = 99%.

**Baaki options kyun galat hain?**

- **B.** 90% recover hota toh 14.4 L urine hota.
- **C.** 1% water excrete hua hai, reabsorb nahi.
- **D.** 98% recover hota toh 2.88 L urine hota.

**Exam clue:** Reabsorbed fraction = (filtered − excreted) ÷ filtered.

### Q3. Glomerular capillary hydrostatic pressure is 60 mmHg, capsular pressure 18 mmHg and plasma oncotic pressure 30 mmHg. Assuming negligible capsular oncotic pressure, what is net filtration pressure?

**Difficulty:** 🟡 Medium · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD

- **A.** 48 mmHg
- **B.** 72 mmHg
- **C.** 108 mmHg
- **D.** 12 mmHg

<details><summary>✅ Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct answer: D. 12 mmHg**

**Kyun sahi hai?** NFP = 60 − 18 − 30 = 12 mmHg. Capillary pressure filtration ko push karta hai aur baaki dono oppose karte hain.

**Baaki options kyun galat hain?**

- **A.** 48 mmHg mein opposing pressures ka sahi subtraction nahi hua.
- **B.** 72 mmHg positive aur negative forces ko galat combine karta hai.
- **C.** 108 mmHg teen pressures add kar deta hai, jabki do oppose karte hain.

**Exam clue:** NFP = pushing pressure minus opposing pressures.

### Q4. In a simplified filtration model, glomerular pressure is 55 mmHg and plasma oncotic pressure is 25 mmHg. Obstruction raises Bowman's space pressure from 10 to 22 mmHg. How does net filtration pressure change?

**Difficulty:** 🔴 Difficult · **Priority:** 🔴 MOST IMPORTANT

- **A.** From 20 to 32 mmHg
- **B.** From 20 to 8 mmHg
- **C.** From 30 to 42 mmHg
- **D.** It remains 20 mmHg

<details><summary>✅ Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct answer: B. From 20 to 8 mmHg**

**Kyun sahi hai?** Initially 55 − 25 − 10 = 20 mmHg. Obstruction ke baad 55 − 25 − 22 = 8 mmHg. Back pressure badhne se filtration ka driving pressure kam ho gaya.

**Baaki options kyun galat hain?**

- **A.** 32 mmHg tab milta jab extra capsular pressure add kar dete, lekin woh filtration oppose karta hai.
- **C.** 30 mmHg initial calculation mein capsular pressure ignore hua hai.
- **D.** Capsular pressure change hone se NFP bhi change hota hai.

**Exam clue:** Obstruction raises back pressure and lowers filtration.

### Q7. Urine creatinine is 120 mg/dL, plasma creatinine is 2 mg/dL and urine flow is 1 mL/min. What is the calculated creatinine clearance?

**Difficulty:** 🟡 Medium · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD

- **C.** 0.017 mL/min
- **D.** 120 mL/min

<details><summary>✅ Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

- **D.** 120 mL/min plasma concentration ko ignore karta hai.

**Exam clue:** C = UV/P.

- **C.** 30 mg/min
- **D.** 40 mg/min

<details><summary>✅ Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>
