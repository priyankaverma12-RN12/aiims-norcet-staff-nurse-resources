### Q1. What is removed during a partial nephrectomy?

**Difficulty:** Easy | **Topic:** Nephrectomy and renal-specific perioperative nursing | **Priority:** Medium | **Type:** Original practice

- **A.** Both kidneys in their entirety
- **B.** The diseased portion of the kidney while retaining remaining renal tissue
- **C.** The urinary bladder alone
- **D.** The entire ureter and bladder in every case

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: B. The diseased portion of the kidney while retaining remaining renal tissue**

Partial nephrectomy mein tumor-bearing portion remove karke functioning parenchyma preserve karte hain.

**Baaki options kyun galat hain?**

- **A.** Both kidneys remove karna partial procedure nahi hai.
- **C.** Bladder removal cystectomy hai.
- **D.** Ureter aur bladder removal partial nephrectomy ka routine component nahi.

**Exam clue:** Partial means nephron-sparing.

</details>
