### Q1. What does renal artery stenosis describe?

**Difficulty:** Easy | **Topic:** Renal artery disorders and renovascular nursing | **Priority:** Medium | **Type:** Original practice

- **A.** Narrowing of the urethral lumen
- **B.** Dilation of a renal calyx
- **C.** Obstruction of a bladder catheter
- **D.** Narrowing of an artery supplying the kidney

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: D. Narrowing of an artery supplying the kidney**

Renal artery narrowing kidney perfusion reduce kar sakti hai aur secondary hypertension ya ischemic kidney dysfunction mein contribute kar sakti hai.

**Baaki options kyun galat hain?**

- **A.** Urethral stenosis urine outlet narrowing hai, arterial supply nahi.
- **B.** Calyceal dilation collecting system issue hai, arterial stenosis nahi.
- **C.** Catheter obstruction urinary drainage problem hai.

**Exam clue:** Distinguish renal blood supply from urinary drainage.

**Exam clue:** Low perfusion can drive RAAS activation.
