### Q1. A child with otherwise typical idiopathic nephrotic syndrome undergoes biopsy for an atypical course. Light microscopy is nearly normal but electron microscopy shows diffuse podocyte foot-process effacement. Which lesion is most consistent with this description?

**Difficulty:** 🟡 Medium · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD

- **A.** Membranous nephropathy with subepithelial immune deposits
- **B.** Focal segmental glomerulosclerosis with segmental scarring
- **C.** Minimal change disease
- **D.** Postinfectious GN with proliferative changes and subepithelial humps

<details><summary>✅ Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct answer: C. Minimal change disease**

**Kyun sahi hai?** Minimal change disease mein light microscope par prominent structural change nahi dikh sakta. Electron microscope podocyte foot processes ki flattening reveal kar sakta hai. Clinical course aur full biopsy interpretation se diagnosis confirm hota hai.

**Baaki options kyun galat hain?**

- **A.** Membranous disease mein immune deposits typical clue hain.
- **B.** FSGS mein selected glomeruli ke segments mein sclerosis identify ho sakti hai.
- **D.** Postinfectious GN mein proliferative injury and immune deposits typical clues hain.

**Exam clue:** Minimal change refers to the light microscopic appearance, not an absence of barrier injury.

### Q2. In the term focal segmental glomerulosclerosis, what does segmental refer to?

**Difficulty:** 🟢 Easy · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD

- **A.** Scarring involving part of an affected glomerulus
- **B.** Scarring involving only the whole renal pelvis
- **C.** Involvement of every glomerulus in its entirety
- **D.** Inflammation limited to one ureteric segment

<details><summary>✅ Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct answer: A. Scarring involving part of an affected glomerulus**

**Kyun sahi hai?** Segmental ka matlab affected glomerulus ka ek part scarred hai. Focal ka matlab all glomeruli affected nahi hote. Dono words histological distribution describe karte hain.

**Baaki options kyun galat hain?**

- **B.** Renal pelvis urine collecting structure hai, glomerulus nahi.
- **C.** All glomeruli fully affected hone ko focal segmental nahi kehte.
- **D.** Ureter ka segment is glomerular term ka meaning nahi hai.

**Exam clue:** Focal = some glomeruli. Segmental = part of a glomerulus.
