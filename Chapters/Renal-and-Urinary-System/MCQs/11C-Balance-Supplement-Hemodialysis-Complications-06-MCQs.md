### Q1. Which protein is associated with dialysis-related amyloidosis in patients receiving long-term dialysis?

**Difficulty:** Easy | **Topic:** Hemodialysis complications and emergency nursing | **Priority:** High | **Type:** Original practice

- **A.** Serum albumin
- **B.** Beta-2 microglobulin
- **C.** Myoglobin
- **D.** Hemoglobin A

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: B. Beta-2 microglobulin**

Beta-2 microglobulin ka deposition long-term dialysis-related amyloidosis se juda hai. High-flux dialysis ne is complication ka risk kam kiya hai.

**Baaki options kyun galat hain?**

- **A.** Albumin blood ka important protein hai, lekin is specific amyloidosis ka deposited protein beta-2 microglobulin hai.
- **C.** Myoglobin muscle injury mein release ho sakta hai, is condition ka characteristic deposit nahi hai.
- **D.** Hemoglobin A red cells mein oxygen carry karta hai, dialysis amyloid ka usual protein nahi hai.

**Exam clue:** Dialysis-related amyloid ko beta-2 microglobulin se associate karo.

</details>

### Q2. Which description is typical of restless legs syndrome that can occur in patients receiving maintenance dialysis?

**Difficulty:** Easy | **Topic:** Hemodialysis complications and emergency nursing | **Priority:** High | **Type:** Original practice

- **A.** Sudden loss of a fistula thrill
- **B.** New visible blood in the dialysate waste line
- **C.** A fixed painful swollen calf with no relief on movement
- **D.** An urge to move the legs during rest that improves with movement

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: D. An urge to move the legs during rest that improves with movement**

Restless legs syndrome mein rest ke time legs hilane ka urge hota hai aur movement se temporary relief mil sakta hai.

**Baaki options kyun galat hain?**

- **A.** Fistula thrill ka loss vascular access ki problem batata hai, restless legs syndrome nahi.
- **B.** Dialysate mein blood ka doubt membrane leak ki taraf point karta hai.
- **C.** Fixed painful swollen calf ka alag urgent assessment chahiye, jaise venous thrombosis ka doubt.

**Exam clue:** Rest aur movement ke saath symptom ka pattern important hai.

</details>

### Q3. Which body system is especially affected by dialysis-related amyloid deposits causing joint and bone symptoms?

**Difficulty:** Easy | **Topic:** Hemodialysis complications and emergency nursing | **Priority:** High | **Type:** Original practice

- **A.** Musculoskeletal system
- **B.** Lower urinary tract
- **C.** Hepatobiliary tract
- **D.** Upper respiratory tract

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: A. Musculoskeletal system**

Dialysis-related amyloid joints aur bones ke aaspaas jama ho sakta hai. Isliye musculoskeletal problems mil sakti hain.

**Baaki options kyun galat hain?**

- **B.** Lower urinary tract mein bladder aur urethra aate hain, yeh typical amyloid deposition symptoms nahi batate.
- **C.** Hepatobiliary tract liver aur bile ducts ka system hai, is question ka main manifestation nahi.
- **D.** Upper respiratory tract is bone-joint manifestation ko explain nahi karta.

**Exam clue:** Long-term dialysis mein bone aur joint complaints ka cause sirf CKD-MBD nahi hota.

</details>

### Q4. A hemodialysis patient has recurrent symptomatic hypotension despite reassessment of target weight and fluid removal. Which dialysate-related option may the team consider under prescription?

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Topic:** Hemodialysis complications and emergency nursing | **Priority:** High | **Type:** Original practice

- **A.** A deliberately elevated dialysate temperature without review
- **B.** An arbitrary change in dialysate potassium concentration
- **C.** An individualized cooler dialysate temperature
- **D.** An increase in sodium concentration at every session without assessment

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: C. An individualized cooler dialysate temperature**

Cooler dialysate se kuch patients mein dialysis ke dauran BP stability improve ho sakti hai. Temperature change individualized prescription aur patient tolerance ke hisaab se hota hai.

**Baaki options kyun galat hain?**

- **A.** Bina review high temperature vasodilation aur hypotension ko worsen kar sakta hai.
- **B.** Potassium concentration electrolyte status ke hisaab se prescribe hoti hai, hypotension ke liye arbitrary change safe nahi hai.
- **D.** Routine sodium increase se thirst aur fluid gain badh sakte hain, isliye blanket change nahi karna chahiye.

**Exam clue:** Recurring hypotension ke liye dialysis prescription ka systematic review chahiye.

</details>

### Q5. After hemodialysis, a patient becomes light-headed on sitting up and wants to walk to the exit alone. What is the best immediate nursing response?

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Topic:** Hemodialysis complications and emergency nursing | **Priority:** High | **Type:** Original practice

- **A.** Allow the patient to walk alone because treatment is finished
- **B.** Assist the patient to a safe position, reassess vital signs and defer unassisted walking
- **C.** Ask the patient to stand quickly to complete discharge assessment
- **D.** Give a large unrestricted oral fluid load before checking blood pressure

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: B. Assist the patient to a safe position, reassess vital signs and defer unassisted walking**

Light-headed patient ka fall risk hai. Nurse pehle safe position mein assist karke BP aur symptoms reassess karti hai, phir safe mobilization decide karti hai.

**Baaki options kyun galat hain?**

- **A.** Dialysis complete hone se hypotension ya fall ka risk automatically khatam nahi hota.
- **C.** Jaldi khada karne se symptoms worsen ho sakte hain aur fall ho sakta hai.
- **D.** Fluid decision BP, volume status aur unit protocol se hota hai. Bina assessment large fluid load safe nahi hai.

**Exam clue:** Discharge readiness mein safe mobilization bhi assess karo.

</details>

### Q6. Which blood cell count is particularly relevant when heparin-induced thrombocytopenia is suspected in a patient receiving heparin during dialysis?

**Difficulty:** Easy | **Topic:** Hemodialysis complications and emergency nursing | **Priority:** High | **Type:** Original practice

- **A.** Eosinophil count
- **B.** Reticulocyte count
- **C.** Absolute neutrophil count
- **D.** Platelet count

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: D. Platelet count**

Heparin-induced thrombocytopenia mein platelets ka trend aur timing important hai. Suspected HIT ko promptly escalate karna chahiye kyunki thrombosis bhi ho sakta hai.

**Baaki options kyun galat hain?**

- **A.** Eosinophil count kuch allergic conditions mein helpful hai, HIT ka main count nahi.
- **B.** Reticulocyte count marrow ki red cell production assess karta hai.
- **C.** Neutrophil count infection aur marrow suppression mein useful hai, HIT ki characteristic count nahi.

**Exam clue:** Heparin exposure ke saath platelet fall ko sirf bleeding problem mat samjho.

</details>
