### Q1. What does CAKUT stand for?

**Difficulty:** Easy | **Topic:** Congenital urinary disorders foundational practice | **Priority:** Medium | **Type:** Original practice

- **A.** Chronic acquired kidney and ureteral tumors
- **B.** Congenital arterial kidney uptake test
- **C.** Congenital anomalies of the kidney and urinary tract
- **D.** Clinical assessment of ketones and urinary toxins

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: C. Congenital anomalies of the kidney and urinary tract**

CAKUT development ke dauran kidney aur urinary tract ki structural abnormalities ka group hai.

**Baaki options kyun galat hain?**

- **A.** Acquired tumors congenital anomaly group nahi hain.
- **B.** Arterial uptake test is acronym ka meaning nahi.
- **D.** Ketone/toxin assessment structural anomalies ka naam nahi.

**Exam clue:** CAKUT encompasses renal and urinary outflow development.

</details>

### Q2. What is renal hypoplasia?

**Difficulty:** Easy | **Topic:** Congenital urinary disorders foundational practice | **Priority:** Medium | **Type:** Original practice

- **A.** An abnormally small, underdeveloped kidney
- **B.** A kidney removed surgically
- **C.** A normally developed kidney with acute cystitis
- **D.** A newly acquired bladder diverticulum

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: A. An abnormally small, underdeveloped kidney**

Hypoplasia mein kidney development incomplete hota hai aur kidney chhoti ho sakti hai.

**Baaki options kyun galat hain?**

- **B.** Surgical removal nephrectomy hai, hypoplasia nahi.
- **C.** Cystitis bladder inflammation hai.
- **D.** Bladder diverticulum renal development ka term nahi.

**Exam clue:** Hypoplasia means underdevelopment, not complete absence.

</details>

### Q3. Where is a pelvic ectopic kidney located?

**Difficulty:** Easy | **Topic:** Congenital urinary disorders foundational practice | **Priority:** Medium | **Type:** Original practice

- **A.** Inside the urinary bladder lumen
- **B.** Inside the urethral canal
- **C.** Inside the thoracic aorta
- **D.** Abnormally low within the pelvis

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: D. Abnormally low within the pelvis**

Pelvic ectopia mein kidney normal ascent complete nahi karti aur pelvis mein located ho sakti hai.

**Baaki options kyun galat hain?**

- **A.** Kidney bladder lumen mein normally ectopic organ ke roop mein nahi hoti.
- **B.** Urethra urine outflow tube hai, kidney location nahi.
- **C.** Aorta blood vessel hai, renal ectopia ka usual location nahi.

**Exam clue:** Ectopia refers to abnormal organ position.

</details>

### Q4. What does the term megaureter describe?

**Difficulty:** Easy | **Topic:** Congenital urinary disorders foundational practice | **Priority:** Medium | **Type:** Original practice

- **A.** An enlarged glomerular capillary
- **B.** An abnormally widened ureter
- **C.** A widened renal vein only
- **D.** A duplicated urinary bladder

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: B. An abnormally widened ureter**

Megaureter ureter ke abnormal dilation ko describe karta hai; cause further evaluation se pata chalta hai.

**Baaki options kyun galat hain?**

- **A.** Glomerular capillary dilation is anatomical term se different hai.
- **C.** Renal vein blood drain karti hai, ureter nahi.
- **D.** Bladder duplication separate anomaly hai.

**Exam clue:** Megaureter describes caliber; it does not itself establish cause.

</details>

### Q5. Which operation reconstructs the renal pelvis–ureter junction to improve drainage in selected children with obstruction?

**Difficulty:** Easy | **Topic:** Congenital urinary disorders foundational practice | **Priority:** Medium | **Type:** Original practice

- **A.** Cystectomy
- **B.** Urethral meatotomy
- **C.** Pyeloplasty
- **D.** Orchidopexy

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: C. Pyeloplasty**

Pyeloplasty UPJ obstruction mein drainage restore karne ke liye reconstructive procedure ho sakti hai.

**Baaki options kyun galat hain?**

- **A.** Cystectomy bladder removal hota hai.
- **B.** Meatotomy urethral opening widen karne se related hai.
- **D.** Orchidopexy testis position correct karta hai.

**Exam clue:** Pyeloplasty is reconstruction, not kidney removal.

</details>

### Q6. A newborn has prenatal urinary tract dilation. The parents ask whether every affected infant needs immediate surgery. What is the appropriate explanation?

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Topic:** Congenital urinary disorders foundational practice | **Priority:** Medium | **Type:** Original practice

- **A.** Management depends on postnatal evaluation, severity, cause and kidney function
- **B.** Every infant requires nephrectomy
- **C.** All prenatal dilation proves permanent obstruction
- **D.** Postnatal follow-up is never necessary if the infant passes urine

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: A. Management depends on postnatal evaluation, severity, cause and kidney function**

Prenatal dilation transient bhi ho sakti hai; postnatal severity aur cause se monitoring ya intervention decide hoti hai.

**Baaki options kyun galat hain?**

- **B.** Nephrectomy har infant ka standard treatment nahi.
- **C.** Dilation alone permanent obstruction prove nahi karti.
- **D.** Urine pass hona unilateral ya partial problem exclude nahi karta.

**Exam clue:** Antenatal dilation is a finding, not an automatic surgical indication.

</details>
