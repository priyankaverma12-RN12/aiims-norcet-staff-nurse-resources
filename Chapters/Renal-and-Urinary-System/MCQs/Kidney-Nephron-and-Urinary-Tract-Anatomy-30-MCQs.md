## Gross kidney anatomy

### Q1. On an abdominal CT, which description correctly locates the normal kidneys?

**Difficulty:** 🟢 Easy · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD · 🟣 [RRB Staff Nurse 20 July 2019 Shift III — related tested concept](https://testbook.com/question-answer/where-is-kidney-located--61adf06df9d96c2371891b24/amp)

- **A.** Within the peritoneal cavity anterior to the stomach
- **B.** Posterior to the parietal peritoneum
- **C.** In the thoracic cavity
- **D.** Within the true pelvic cavity

<details><summary>✅ Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct answer: B. Posterior to the parietal peritoneum**

**Kyun sahi hai?** Kidneys posterior abdominal wall ke paas peritoneum ke pichhe hoti hain. Isi location ko retroperitoneal kehte hain.

**Baaki options kyun galat hain?**

- **A.** Kidneys stomach ke saamne intraperitoneal organs nahi hain.
- **C.** Diaphragm ke upar thorax mein lungs aur heart hote hain, normal kidneys nahi.
- **D.** Normal adult kidneys upper posterior abdomen mein hoti hain, pelvis mein nahi.

**Exam clue:** Retroperitoneal ka matlab peritoneum ke pichhe hai.
