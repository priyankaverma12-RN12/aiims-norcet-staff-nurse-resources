### Q1. A previously well nonpregnant adult reports new urgency, frequent small voids and suprapubic discomfort without fever or flank pain. Which process best explains the combination?

**Difficulty:** 🟡 Medium · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD · **Exam focus:** NORCET / all-India Staff Nurse · **Topic:** Localized infection

- **A.** Inflammation confined to the renal pelvis with systemic illness
- **B.** Inflammation of the bladder from a localized lower urinary tract infection
- **C.** Reduced glomerular filtration as the only explanation
- **D.** Obstruction of both ureters as an established diagnosis

<details><summary>✅ Correct answer and detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: B. Inflammation of the bladder from a localized lower urinary tract infection**

**A. Kyun galat hai:** Renal pelvis involvement mein fever, flank pain ya other systemic signs ka concern zyada hota hai. Is stem ka main pattern bladder irritation hai.

**B. Kyun sahi hai:** Bladder ki lining inflamed hone se storage ke time irritation hoti hai. Isliye urgency, frequent small voids aur suprapubic discomfort saath aa sakte hain. Systemic signs absent hone se localized cystitis ka pattern fit hota hai, lekin clinical assessment abhi bhi zaroori hai.

**C. Kyun galat hai:** Reduced GFR kidney filtration ka issue hai. Ye akela acute urgency aur suprapubic irritation ko achhi tarah explain nahi karta.

**D. Kyun galat hai:** Bilateral ureteric obstruction ek serious possibility ho sakti hai specific findings mein, lekin yahan uski confirmation ke liye evidence nahi diya gaya hai.

**Clinical reference:** [Guidance](https://uroweb.org/guidelines/urological-infections/chapter/the-guideline) · [Concept guide](../Concepts/Lower-Urinary-Tract-Infection-and-Cystitis.md).

### Q2. A patient with bacterial cystitis asks how intestinal bacteria can cause an infection in the bladder. Which explanation is most accurate?

**Difficulty:** 🟡 Medium · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD · **Exam focus:** NORCET / all-India Staff Nurse · **Topic:** Ascending mechanism

- **A.** Intestinal bacteria must first destroy the glomerular basement membrane
- **B.** Bacteria enter the bladder directly through the skin of the lower abdomen in every case
- **C.** All cystitis begins with bacteria descending from an infected kidney
- **D.** Bacteria may colonize the periurethral area and ascend through the urethra into the bladder

<details><summary>✅ Correct answer and detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: D. Bacteria may colonize the periurethral area and ascend through the urethra into the bladder**

**A. Kyun galat hai:** Glomerular basement membrane kidney filtration barrier hai. Bladder infection ke liye pehle glomerular destruction hona necessary nahi hai.

**B. Kyun galat hai:** Intact abdominal skin usual route nahi hai. Direct access special instrumentation ya abnormal communication mein possible ho sakta hai, lekin every case mein nahi.

**C. Kyun galat hai:** Ascending infection commonly bladder se upar ki taraf progress kar sakti hai. Har cystitis ka origin kidney infection nahi hota.

**D. Kyun sahi hai:** Gut mein rehne wale kuch bacteria urethral opening ke aas-paas pahunch sakte hain. Jab woh urethra se bladder tak ascend karke multiply karte hain, toh bacterial cystitis develop ho sakti hai.

### Q16. For a selected adult with localized lower UTI, the prescriber has ordered nitrofurantoin 100 mg orally twice daily for five days, consistent with the stated ICMR July 2026 course range. How many 100 mg doses are required for this written course?

**Difficulty:** 🟡 Medium · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD · **Exam focus:** NORCET / all-India Staff Nurse · **Topic:** Prescription verification

- **A.** 5 doses
- **B.** 20 doses
- **C.** 10 doses
- **D.** 2 doses

<details><summary>✅ Correct answer and detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: C. 10 doses**

**A. Kyun galat hai:** Five doses ka matlab roz ek dose hota, jo twice-daily written order se match nahi karta.

**B. Kyun galat hai:** 20 doses five days mein four doses per day banengi. Ye given twice-daily schedule se double hai.
