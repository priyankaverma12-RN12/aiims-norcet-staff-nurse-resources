### Q1. What is the function of the transfer set in a peritoneal dialysis system?

**Difficulty:** Easy | **Topic:** Peritoneal dialysis, CAPD and APD | **Priority:** High | **Type:** Original practice

- **A.** Connects an AV fistula to a blood pump
- **B.** Connects a Foley catheter to a urine meter
- **C.** Connects the permanent PD catheter to exchange tubing
- **D.** Connects an enteral tube to a feeding pump

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: C. Connects the permanent PD catheter to exchange tubing**

Transfer set permanent PD catheter aur disposable exchange tubing ke beech connection deta hai. Aseptic handling infection se bachati hai.

**Baaki options kyun galat hain?**

- **A.** AV fistula blood circuit hemodialysis mein use hota hai.
- **B.** Foley catheter urine drainage ke liye hota hai, PD exchange ke liye nahi.
- **D.** Enteral feeding ka connection gastrointestinal nutrition ke liye hota hai.

**Exam clue:** PD catheter aur transfer set alag components hain.

</details>

### Q2. During a routine gravity-assisted CAPD drain, where should the drain bag be placed according to the taught setup?

**Difficulty:** Easy | **Topic:** Peritoneal dialysis, CAPD and APD | **Priority:** High | **Type:** Original practice

- **A.** Below the level of the abdomen
- **B.** Above the patient's shoulders
- **C.** At a level above the fresh solution bag
- **D.** On top of the patient's abdomen

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: A. Below the level of the abdomen**

Drain bag abdomen se neeche rakhne par gravity fluid ko bahar nikalne mein madad karti hai. Patient trained setup follow karta hai.

**Baaki options kyun galat hain?**

- **B.** Shoulder ke upar bag rakhne se gravity drainage mein madad nahi milegi.
- **C.** Fresh solution bag se upar drain bag rakhna intended drainage setup nahi hai.
- **D.** Abdomen par bag rakhne se tubing aur drainage disturb ho sakte hain.

**Exam clue:** Gravity drain mein receiving bag ka level important hai.

</details>

### Q3. In CAPD, which term describes the introduction of fresh prescribed dialysis solution into the peritoneal cavity?

**Difficulty:** Easy | **Topic:** Peritoneal dialysis, CAPD and APD | **Priority:** High | **Type:** Original practice

- **A.** Drain phase
- **B.** Fistula maturation
- **C.** Hemostasis phase
- **D.** Fill phase

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: D. Fill phase**

Fill phase mein fresh solution peritoneal cavity mein jaata hai. Uske baad prescribed dwell aur drainage hoti hai.

**Baaki options kyun galat hain?**

- **A.** Drain phase mein used solution body se bahar nikalta hai.
- **B.** Fistula maturation HD vascular access ka process hai.
- **C.** Hemostasis bleeding control ko kehte hain, exchange ka fill step nahi.

**Exam clue:** Fill aur drain mein dialysate ki direction opposite hoti hai.

</details>

### Q4. What does a disposable cap on the PD transfer set help achieve between exchanges?

**Difficulty:** Easy | **Topic:** Peritoneal dialysis, CAPD and APD | **Priority:** High | **Type:** Original practice

- **A.** Measures the volume of dialysate removed
- **B.** Protects the closed catheter connection from contamination
- **C.** Adjusts the glucose concentration of dialysate
- **D.** Determines the patient's serum potassium

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: B. Protects the closed catheter connection from contamination**

Prescribed disposable cap connection ko closed aur protected rakhta hai. Isse outside germs ke entry ka risk kam hota hai.

**Baaki options kyun galat hain?**

- **A.** Drain volume bag ya cycler data se measure hota hai, cap se nahi.
- **C.** Glucose concentration prescribed solution bag ka feature hai.
- **D.** Serum potassium blood sample se pata chalta hai.

**Exam clue:** Exchange ke beech catheter connection ko closed rakhna important hai.

</details>

### Q5. Which access does peritoneal dialysis use to deliver therapy without routine repeated puncture of an AV fistula?

**Difficulty:** Easy | **Topic:** Peritoneal dialysis, CAPD and APD | **Priority:** High | **Type:** Original practice

- **A.** A peripheral intravenous cannula
- **B.** A bladder catheter with balloon
- **C.** An indwelling catheter entering the peritoneal cavity
- **D.** An arterial pressure-monitoring catheter

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: C. An indwelling catheter entering the peritoneal cavity**

PD catheter peritoneal cavity tak route deta hai. Routine PD exchange mein AV fistula cannulation ki zaroorat nahi hoti.

**Baaki options kyun galat hain?**

- **A.** Peripheral IV cannula veins mein fluid ya medicine ke liye hota hai, PD exchange ke liye nahi.
- **B.** Balloon wala bladder catheter urine drain karta hai, abdomen mein dialysate deliver nahi karta.
- **D.** Arterial monitoring catheter BP monitoring aur sampling ke liye hota hai.

**Exam clue:** Peritoneal cavity aur urinary bladder anatomically alag hain.

</details>

### Q6. An APD prescription includes a final daytime fill after nighttime cycling. What does this instruction mean?

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Topic:** Peritoneal dialysis, CAPD and APD | **Priority:** High | **Type:** Original practice

- **A.** A prescribed volume of fresh dialysate remains in the abdomen for the daytime dwell
- **B.** The catheter must be removed every morning
- **C.** The final drain is always discarded before any fill occurs
- **D.** All daytime fluid intake must equal the volume of the final fill

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: A. A prescribed volume of fresh dialysate remains in the abdomen for the daytime dwell**

Last fill ka matlab prescribed fresh fluid daytime dwell ke liye abdomen mein rehta hai. Schedule individualized hota hai.

**Baaki options kyun galat hain?**

- **B.** PD catheter repeated exchanges ke liye body mein rehta hai, daily remove nahi hota.
- **C.** Final fill aur final drain ka meaning alag hai. Prescription mein daytime dwell specified hai.
- **D.** Oral fluid allowance residual urine, fluid status aur UF ke hisaab se decide hoti hai, final fill se directly equal nahi hoti.

**Exam clue:** Cycler schedule mein last fill ko separate prescribed exchange parameter samjho.

</details>
