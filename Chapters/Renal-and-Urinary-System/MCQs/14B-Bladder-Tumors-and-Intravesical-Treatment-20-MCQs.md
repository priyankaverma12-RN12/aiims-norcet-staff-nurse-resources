### Q1. Which is the predominant histological type of bladder cancer?

**Difficulty:** Easy | **Topic:** Bladder tumors and intravesical treatment nursing | **Priority:** Medium | **Type:** Original practice

- **A.** Renal clear cell carcinoma

- **C.** Urothelial carcinoma
- **D.** Renal angiomyolipoma

**Correct: C. Urothelial carcinoma**

Urothelium bladder ki inner lining hai aur most bladder cancers isi se arise karte hain.

**Baaki options kyun galat hain?**

- **B.** Nephroblastoma childhood renal tumor hai.
- **D.** Angiomyolipoma renal mesenchymal mass hai.

**Exam clue:** Bladder lining = urothelium.
