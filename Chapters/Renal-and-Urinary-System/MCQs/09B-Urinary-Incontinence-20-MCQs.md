### Q1. A woman loses a small amount of urine whenever she sneezes, without preceding urgency. Which type of incontinence is most likely?

**Difficulty:** Easy | **Topic:** Urinary incontinence | **Priority:** Medium | **Type:** Original practice

- **A.** Urgency urinary incontinence
- **B.** Functional incontinence
- **C.** Stress urinary incontinence
- **D.** Continuous leakage from a fistula

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: C. Stress urinary incontinence**

Cough ya sneeze se abdominal pressure badhta hai, aur urethral closure support inadequate ho to stress leakage hoti hai.

**Baaki options kyun galat hain?**

- **A.** Urgency leakage mein sudden compelling urge pehle aata hai, jo yahan absent hai.
- **B.** Functional leakage toilet tak pahunchne ki difficulty se hoti hai.
- **D.** Fistula mein leakage sustained ho sakti hai, sirf sneeze ke waqt nahi.

**Exam clue:** Cough ya sneeze se abdominal pressure badhta hai, aur urethral closure support inadequate ho to stress leakage hoti hai.

</details>
