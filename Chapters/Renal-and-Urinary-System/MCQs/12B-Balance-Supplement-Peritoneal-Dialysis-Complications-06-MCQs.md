### Q1. Why is the external portion of a peritoneal dialysis catheter secured to avoid traction?

**Difficulty:** Easy | **Topic:** Peritoneal dialysis complications | **Priority:** High | **Type:** Original practice

- **A.** To increase the glucose concentration of dialysate
- **B.** To prevent the abdomen from expanding during a fill
- **C.** To reduce movement-related trauma at the exit site
- **D.** To measure peritoneal membrane transport

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: C. To reduce movement-related trauma at the exit site**

Catheter ko secure karne se exit site par kheenchav aur repeated injury kam hoti hai. Damaged skin infection ke liye entry point ban sakti hai.

**Baaki options kyun galat hain?**

- **A.** Glucose concentration prescribed solution se decide hoti hai, fixation se nahi.
- **B.** Fixation abdomen ki expansion ko control karne ka method nahi hai.
- **D.** Membrane transport PET jaise assessment se evaluate hota hai.

**Exam clue:** Exit site ko protect karne ke liye catheter movement minimize karo.

</details>

### Q2. Which preventive measure is recommended immediately before planned PD catheter insertion to reduce insertion-related peritonitis?

**Difficulty:** Easy | **Topic:** Peritoneal dialysis complications | **Priority:** High | **Type:** Original practice

- **A.** Prescribed systemic antibiotic prophylaxis
- **B.** Routine long-term high-dose oral steroids
- **C.** Prolonged urinary catheterization
- **D.** Starting a full-volume ambulatory PD schedule before surgery

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: A. Prescribed systemic antibiotic prophylaxis**

ISPD catheter insertion se just pehle systemic antibiotic prophylaxis recommend karta hai. Drug selection local protocol aur allergy assessment se guided hoti hai.

**Baaki options kyun galat hain?**

- **B.** Steroids infection risk badha sakte hain aur routine insertion prophylaxis nahi hain.
- **C.** Urinary catheter PD peritonitis prevention ke liye routine requirement nahi hai.
- **D.** PD exchanges catheter insertion ke baad appropriate prescription se start hote hain.

**Exam clue:** Peri-insertion prophylaxis aur later infection treatment ko alag samjho.

</details>

### Q3. Which appearance can suggest a fibrin-related obstruction in a peritoneal dialysis drainage system?

**Difficulty:** Easy | **Topic:** Peritoneal dialysis complications | **Priority:** High | **Type:** Original practice

- **A.** A consistently clear unobstructed drain
- **B.** A stable intact sterile cap before connection
- **C.** An empty unused supply bag
- **D.** Thread-like material in the drained solution or tubing

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: D. Thread-like material in the drained solution or tubing**

Fibrin dhaage jaise strands bana sakta hai jo catheter ya tubing flow mein obstruction kar sakte hain. Nurse findings ko PD team tak escalate karti hai.

**Baaki options kyun galat hain?**

- **A.** Clear unobstructed drainage se fibrin blockage ka direct evidence nahi milta.
- **B.** Intact cap between exchanges expected finding hai, fibrin obstruction ka sign nahi.
- **C.** Unused bag ka empty hona drainage catheter ki condition prove nahi karta.

**Exam clue:** PD flow problem ke mechanical aur infectious causes dono assess karo.

</details>

### Q4. Clear watery fluid appears around a newly inserted PD catheter exit site during a fill. Which mechanical complication should be considered?

**Difficulty:** Easy | **Topic:** Peritoneal dialysis complications | **Priority:** High | **Type:** Original practice

- **A.** AV graft thrombosis
- **B.** Dialysate leakage around the catheter
- **C.** Dialyzer membrane rupture
- **D.** Urethral stricture

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: B. Dialysate leakage around the catheter**

Fill ke time exit site se clear fluid aana pericatheter dialysate leak ka sign ho sakta hai. PD team ko promptly assess karna chahiye.

**Baaki options kyun galat hain?**

- **A.** AV graft thrombosis HD access ka complication hai, exit site se dialysate leak nahi.
- **C.** Dialyzer rupture extracorporeal hemodialysis circuit mein hota hai.
- **D.** Urethral stricture urine flow ko affect karta hai, PD catheter ke leak ko explain nahi karta.

**Exam clue:** Naye catheter se unusual wetness ko normal maan kar ignore mat karo.

</details>

### Q5. A PD patient develops peritonitis with systemic sepsis features. In addition to effluent sampling, what investigation should the nurse anticipate when ordered?

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Topic:** Peritoneal dialysis complications | **Priority:** High | **Type:** Original practice

- **A.** A routine sputum culture as the only microbiology sample
- **B.** A peritoneal equilibration test before emergency assessment
- **C.** Blood cultures, without delaying urgent sepsis care
- **D.** An elective bladder cystoscopy before antimicrobial treatment

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: C. Blood cultures, without delaying urgent sepsis care**

Systemic sepsis ka doubt ho toh blood cultures bloodstream infection identify karne mein madad kar sakte hain. Urgent treatment ko unnecessary delay nahi karna chahiye.

**Baaki options kyun galat hain?**

- **A.** Sputum culture respiratory source ke liye relevant ho sakta hai, lekin suspected systemic PD infection ka only sample nahi hai.
- **B.** PET membrane transport test hai, emergency infection workup ka first step nahi.
- **D.** Elective cystoscopy bladder evaluation hai, sepsis care delay karna unsafe hai.

**Exam clue:** Effluent infection ke saath systemic illness ko bhi assess karo.

</details>

### Q6. What does the term culture-negative PD peritonitis mean when the other diagnostic criteria are met?

**Difficulty:** Easy | **Topic:** Peritoneal dialysis complications | **Priority:** High | **Type:** Original practice

- **A.** Peritonitis is diagnosed despite no organism growing in the effluent culture
- **B.** The patient necessarily has no peritoneal infection
- **C.** The catheter is permanently blocked by fibrin
- **D.** The effluent culture has grown two different organisms

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: A. Peritonitis is diagnosed despite no organism growing in the effluent culture**

Clinical symptoms aur effluent cell findings diagnostic criteria meet kar sakte hain, bhale hi culture mein organism na grow ho. Team further assessment aur treatment decide karti hai.

**Baaki options kyun galat hain?**

- **B.** Negative culture alone peritonitis exclude nahi karta.
- **C.** Fibrin obstruction mechanical issue hai, culture-negative diagnosis ka meaning nahi.
- **D.** Do organisms grow karna polymicrobial culture hai, culture-negative nahi.

**Exam clue:** Culture result ko symptoms aur effluent cell count ke saath interpret karo.

</details>
