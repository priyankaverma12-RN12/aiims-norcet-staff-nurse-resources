### Q1. A patient with CKD has persistent anemia, normal MCV and an inappropriately low reticulocyte response. Which interpretation is most consistent with renal anemia?

**Difficulty:** 🟡 Medium · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD

- **A.** A brisk marrow response to acute hemorrhage
- **B.** Isolated hemoconcentration from dehydration
- **C.** Reduced effective marrow red-cell production
- **D.** An established diagnosis of vitamin B12 deficiency

<details><summary>✅ Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct answer: C. Reduced effective marrow red-cell production**

**Kyun sahi hai?** Reticulocytes naye RBC hote hain. Anemia ke bawajood inka response kam hai toh marrow production inadequate ho sakti hai. CKD mein insufficient EPO response ek important cause hai.

**Baaki options kyun galat hain?**

- **A.** Hemorrhage ke baad adequate marrow response mein reticulocytes generally badhte hain, though timing matters.
- **B.** Hemoconcentration Hb falsely high kar sakti hai, low Hb plus poor reticulocyte response explain nahi karti.
- **D.** B12 deficiency confirm karne ke liye additional assessment chahiye. Normal MCV alone diagnostic nahi hai.

### Q2. Why can chronic inflammation reduce iron availability to the marrow in a patient with kidney disease?

**Difficulty:** 🟡 Medium · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD

- **B.** Hepcidin stimulates unrestricted iron release from macrophages
- **C.** Inflammation always destroys all transferrin molecules
- **D.** Inflammation increases kidney erythropoietin production sufficiently to correct anemia

<details><summary>✅ Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Baaki options kyun galat hain?**

- **B.** Hepcidin iron release restrict karta hai, increase nahi.
- **C.** Inflammation transferrin ko affect kar sakti hai lekin all transferrin destruction mechanism nahi hai.

### Q7. Serum iron is 42 micrograms/dL and total iron-binding capacity is 280 micrograms/dL. What is the calculated transferrin saturation?

**Difficulty:** 🟡 Medium · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD

- **A.** 6.7%
- **B.** 15%
- **C.** 23.8%
- **D.** 66.7%

<details><summary>✅ Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct answer: B. 15%**

**Kyun sahi hai?** TSAT = serum iron divided by TIBC multiplied by 100. 42 ÷ 280 × 100 = 15%. Same units hone chahiye.

**Baaki options kyun galat hain?**

- **A.** 6.7 approximate reciprocal quotient hai, percentage formula nahi.
- **C.** 23.8 incorrect calculation hai.
- **D.** 66.7 ratio ko incorrect direction ya scale mein apply karta hai.
