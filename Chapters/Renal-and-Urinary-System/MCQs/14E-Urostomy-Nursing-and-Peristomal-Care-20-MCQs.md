### Q1. Which appearance is generally expected in a healthy ileal conduit stoma?

**Difficulty:** Easy | **Topic:** Urostomy nursing and peristomal care | **Priority:** Medium | **Type:** Original practice

- **A.** Dry black tissue
- **B.** Pale gray tissue with loss of moisture
- **C.** Pink to red and moist
- **D.** A deep open necrotic ulcer

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: C. Pink to red and moist**

Healthy bowel mucosa vascular hoti hai, isliye pink-red aur moist appearance expected hai.

**Baaki options kyun galat hain?**

- **A.** Black tissue ischemia ya necrosis indicate kar sakti hai.
- **B.** Gray pale dry tissue perfusion compromise suggest kar sakti hai.
- **D.** Necrotic ulcer normal healing nahi hai.

**Exam clue:** Healthy stoma resembles moist oral mucosa.
