### Q1. An adult with CKD G3b has normal serum phosphate but rising FGF23 and PTH. Which interpretation is most appropriate?

**Difficulty:** 🟡 Medium · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD

- **C.** Compensatory phosphate-regulating responses may precede overt hyperphosphatemia

**Baaki options kyun galat hain?**

- **A.** Normal phosphate CKD-MBD exclude nahi karta.
