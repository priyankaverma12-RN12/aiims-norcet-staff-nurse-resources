## Glomerular immune injury

### Q1. Which distinction between glomerulonephritis and a nephritic syndrome is most accurate?

**Difficulty:** 🟡 Medium · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD

- **A.** Both terms identify one specific bacterial infection
- **B.** Glomerulonephritis describes inflammatory glomerular injury, while nephritic syndrome describes a clinical pattern that may result
- **C.** Nephritic syndrome always establishes one histological subtype
- **D.** Every glomerular disease necessarily presents as pure nephritic syndrome

<details><summary>✅ Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct answer: B. Glomerulonephritis describes inflammatory glomerular injury, while nephritic syndrome describes a clinical pattern that may result**

**Kyun sahi hai?** GN filtration unit ki inflammatory injury describe karta hai. Nephritic syndrome clinical signs ka pattern hai, jo different diseases se ho sakta hai. Etiology separately identify karni hoti hai.

**Baaki options kyun galat hain?**

- **A.** Bacterial infection one possible trigger hai, only cause nahi.
- **C.** Clinical pattern biopsy subtype establish nahi karta.
- **D.** Nephrotic, mixed and asymptomatic presentations possible hain.

**Exam clue:** Distinguish disease mechanism from clinical syndrome.
