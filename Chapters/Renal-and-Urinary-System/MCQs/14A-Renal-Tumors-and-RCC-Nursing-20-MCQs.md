### Q1. Which malignant tumor most commonly arises from renal tubular epithelium in adults?

**Difficulty:** Easy | **Topic:** Renal tumors and RCC nursing | **Priority:** Medium | **Type:** Original practice

- **A.** Renal cell carcinoma
- **B.** Urothelial carcinoma of the bladder
- **C.** Nephroblastoma
- **D.** Renal angiomyolipoma

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: A. Renal cell carcinoma**

RCC adult renal parenchyma ke tubular epithelial cells se arise karta hai.

**Baaki options kyun galat hain?**

- **B.** Bladder urothelial tumor bladder lining se arise karta hai.
- **C.** Nephroblastoma mainly childhood embryonal tumor hai.
- **D.** Angiomyolipoma generally benign mesenchymal tumor hai.

**Exam clue:** Adult renal parenchymal malignancy = RCC.
