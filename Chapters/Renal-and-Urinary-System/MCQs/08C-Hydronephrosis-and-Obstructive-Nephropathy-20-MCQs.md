### Q1. Which structural change defines hydronephrosis?

**Difficulty:** Easy | **Topic:** Hydronephrosis and obstructive nephropathy | **Priority:** Medium | **Type:** Original practice

- **A.** Inflammation of renal glomeruli
- **B.** Accumulation of fluid in the pericardial sac
- **C.** Dilation of the renal pelvis and calyces
- **D.** Thickening of the urinary bladder alone

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: C. Dilation of the renal pelvis and calyces**

Hydronephrosis mein kidney ka urine collecting system, yani pelvis aur calyces, dilate hota hai.

**Baaki options kyun galat hain?**

- **A.** Glomerular inflammation glomerulonephritis ko describe karti hai, collecting system dilation ko nahi.
- **B.** Pericardial fluid heart ke around hota hai, urinary tract mein nahi.
- **D.** Bladder wall change lower urinary tract finding hai, hydronephrosis ki definition nahi.

**Exam clue:** Pelvis aur calyces ko renal collecting system samjho.

</details>
