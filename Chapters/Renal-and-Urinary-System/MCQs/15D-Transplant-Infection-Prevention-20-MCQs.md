# Kidney transplant infection prevention and surveillance — 20 original MCQs

Isolated candidate; original NORCET/HPRCA practice, not authenticated PYQs. Prevention, screening intervals and treatment must be individualized by the transplant team.

### Q1. Which viral infection is commonly monitored after kidney transplantation because it can cause graft nephropathy?

**Difficulty:** Easy | **Topic:** Kidney transplant infection prevention and surveillance | **Priority:** High | **Type:** Original practice

- **A.** Rotavirus alone
- **B.** Rabies virus
- **C.** Influenza virus as the defining graft nephropathy pathogen
- **D.** BK polyomavirus

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: D. BK polyomavirus**

BK polyomavirus immunosuppression mein reactivate hokar renal allograft ko injure kar sakta hai.

**Baaki options kyun galat hain?**

- **A.** Rotavirus mainly gastroenteritis cause karta hai.
- **B.** Rabies primarily nervous system affect karta hai.
- **C.** Influenza respiratory infection hai, BK-associated graft nephropathy nahi.

**Exam clue:** BK virus can injure the transplanted kidney.
