## Bedside assessment

### Q1. A patient reports voiding twelve times daily, with a total measured urine volume of 1.6 L. Which finding is documented?

**Difficulty:** 🟡 Medium · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD

- **A.** Polyuria based on the number of voids
- **B.** Urinary frequency without documented polyuria
- **C.** Anuria
- **D.** Oliguria based solely on frequency

<details><summary>✅ Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct answer: B. Urinary frequency without documented polyuria**

**Kyun sahi hai?** Patient baar-baar urine pass kar raha hai, lekin total 1.6 L/day polyuria ke usual adult threshold se kam hai. Frequency aur total volume alag assess karo.

**Baaki options kyun galat hain?**

- **A.** Polyuria volume par depend karti hai, number of voids par nahi.
- **C.** Anuria mein urine almost absent hota hai.
- **D.** Frequency se oliguria establish nahi hoti.

**Exam clue:** Frequency counts visits, polyuria counts volume.
