### Q1. A patient asks why a protective antibody can participate in kidney injury in IgA nephropathy. Which mechanism best explains this paradox?

**Difficulty:** 🟡 Medium · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD · **Exam focus:** NORCET / all-India Staff Nurse · **Topic:** mechanism

- **A.** Antibodies directly dissolve all circulating red cells
- **B.** Abnormally glycosylated IgA1 participates in immune complex formation
- **C.** IgA completely suppresses the formation of all other antibodies
- **D.** Normal IgA physically blocks the ureter

<details><summary>✅ Correct answer and detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: B. Abnormally glycosylated IgA1 participates in immune complex formation**

**A. Kyun galat hai:** IgA nephropathy mein problem circulating red cells ke widespread destruction ki nahi hoti. Glomerular filter ki injury ki wajah se red cells urine mein aa sakte hain.

**B. Kyun sahi hai:** IgA1 ki sugar chains ka pattern abnormal ho sakta hai. Jab is IgA1 ke saath dusre antibodies judte hain, toh immune complexes ban sakte hain jo glomerulus mein inflammation ko trigger karte hain.

**C. Kyun galat hai:** IgA ka normal kaam mucosal protection mein madad karna hai. Ye disease sabhi antibodies ke production ko poori tarah band karne se nahi hoti.

**D. Kyun galat hai:** Ureter kidney se bladder tak urine le jaata hai. IgA nephropathy mein primary injury glomerular filter mein hoti hai, na ki ureter ki mechanical blockage mein.

**Exam clue:** IgA1 ki abnormal glycosylation aur immune complex ko ek mechanism ke do steps ki tarah samjho.

### Q15. Which explanation best reflects the two complementary treatment aims in progressive-risk IgA nephropathy?

**Difficulty:** 🟡 Medium · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD · **Exam focus:** NORCET / all-India Staff Nurse · **Topic:** treatment mechanism

- **D.** Treat urine discoloration alone and stop follow-up

<details><summary>✅ Correct answer and detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: C. Reduce pathogenic immune-complex injury while protecting remaining nephron function**

**A. Kyun galat hai:** Bacterial UTI treatment IgA nephropathy ke main immune mechanism ko directly target nahi karta. Urine pH badhana is disease ka primary treatment goal nahi hai.

**B. Kyun galat hai:** Glomerular pressure ko unnecessarily badhana remaining nephrons ko stress de sakta hai. Thirst suppress karna disease-specific goal nahi hai.
