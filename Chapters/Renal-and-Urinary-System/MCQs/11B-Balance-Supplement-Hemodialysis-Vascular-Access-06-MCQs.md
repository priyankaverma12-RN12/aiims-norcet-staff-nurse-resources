### Q1. Which test commonly maps arm blood vessels before planned hemodialysis access surgery?

**Difficulty:** Easy | **Topic:** Hemodialysis vascular access | **Priority:** High | **Type:** Original practice

- **A.** Duplex ultrasound
- **B.** Electroencephalography
- **C.** Spirometry
- **D.** Cystoscopy

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: A. Duplex ultrasound**

Duplex ultrasound se vessel ka size aur blood flow assess kiya ja sakta hai. Isse surgeon ko suitable access plan karne mein madad milti hai.

**Baaki options kyun galat hain?**

- **B.** EEG brain ki electrical activity assess karta hai, arm vessels nahi.
- **C.** Spirometry lung function check karti hai.
- **D.** Cystoscopy bladder aur urethra ke andar dekhne ke liye hoti hai.

**Exam clue:** Access planning mein vessel anatomy aur flow dono important hote hain.

</details>

### Q2. How many needles are normally used for standard two-needle hemodialysis through a mature AV access?

**Difficulty:** Easy | **Topic:** Hemodialysis vascular access | **Priority:** High | **Type:** Original practice

- **A.** One for every dialysis hour
- **B.** Three
- **C.** Two
- **D.** None

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: C. Two**

Standard two-needle technique mein ek needle blood nikaalne aur doosri blood return karne ke liye hoti hai.

**Baaki options kyun galat hain?**

- **A.** Har hour ke liye nayi needle routine technique nahi hai.
- **B.** Standard two-needle technique mein teesri needle ki zaroorat nahi hoti.
- **D.** Catheter dialysis mein external connections use hote hain, lekin mature AV access ki standard technique mein needles lagti hain.

**Exam clue:** Question mein standard two-needle technique specify ki gayi hai.

</details>

### Q3. Before routine fistula cannulation, what should the patient do to the access skin as instructed by the unit?

**Difficulty:** Easy | **Topic:** Hemodialysis vascular access | **Priority:** High | **Type:** Original practice

- **A.** Apply moisturizer directly to the needle sites
- **B.** Wash the access area with soap and water
- **C.** Remove all scabs with fingernails
- **D.** Cover the entire access with an adhesive strip

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: B. Wash the access area with soap and water**

Skin ko soap aur water se saaf karne se surface dirt aur germs kam hote hain. Staff phir prescribed antisepsis karta hai.

**Baaki options kyun galat hain?**

- **A.** Moisturizer antiseptic skin preparation ki jagah nahi le sakta.
- **C.** Scab ko khurachne se skin injury aur infection ka risk badhta hai.
- **D.** Adhesive strip se access cover karna cleansing nahi hai aur assessment mein dikkat ho sakti hai.

**Exam clue:** Patient cleansing ke baad trained staff ki antisepsis bhi zaroori hai.

</details>

### Q4. Which feature helps distinguish a tunneled hemodialysis catheter from a typical nontunneled temporary dialysis catheter?

**Difficulty:** Easy | **Topic:** Hemodialysis vascular access | **Priority:** High | **Type:** Original practice

- **A.** An external urine collection bag
- **B.** A surgically formed artery-to-vein connection
- **C.** An inflatable balloon in the bladder
- **D.** A subcutaneous tunnel and usually a tissue-ingrowth cuff

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: D. A subcutaneous tunnel and usually a tissue-ingrowth cuff**

Tunneled catheter skin ke neeche ek tunnel se guzarta hai. Cuff tissue mein integrate hokar fixation mein madad karta hai.

**Baaki options kyun galat hain?**

- **A.** Urine bag urinary catheter ke saath hota hai, vascular dialysis catheter ke saath nahi.
- **B.** Artery aur vein ka surgical connection AV fistula hota hai.
- **C.** Bladder balloon Foley catheter ka feature hai.

**Exam clue:** Tunneled aur nontunneled vascular catheters ko urinary catheters se confuse mat karo.

</details>

### Q5. What should a hemodialysis patient do if the catheter dressing becomes wet at home?

**Difficulty:** Easy | **Topic:** Hemodialysis vascular access | **Priority:** High | **Type:** Original practice

- **A.** Contact the dialysis team promptly for appropriate dressing care
- **B.** Wait until the next scheduled session regardless of timing
- **C.** Remove the dressing and leave the site uncovered
- **D.** Open the catheter hubs to dry them

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: A. Contact the dialysis team promptly for appropriate dressing care**

Wet dressing protective barrier ko compromise kar sakti hai. Dialysis team ko turant batana chahiye taaki safe care arrange ho.

**Baaki options kyun galat hain?**

- **B.** Next session tak wait karne se contamination ka risk bana reh sakta hai.
- **C.** Site uncovered chhodne se germs ka exposure badhta hai.
- **D.** Hubs kholne se bloodstream infection ka risk ho sakta hai.

**Exam clue:** Catheter dressing clean, dry aur secure rehni chahiye.

</details>

### Q6. Which everyday clothing choice helps protect a functioning fistula from external compression?

**Difficulty:** Easy | **Topic:** Hemodialysis vascular access | **Priority:** High | **Type:** Original practice

- **A.** A tight elastic cuff over the access
- **B.** A restrictive watch strap directly over the fistula
- **C.** A loose sleeve over the access arm
- **D.** A heavy bag strap pressing on the access

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: C. A loose sleeve over the access arm**

Loose sleeve access par unnecessary pressure nahi daalti aur blood flow ko protect karne mein madad karti hai.

**Baaki options kyun galat hain?**

- **A.** Tight cuff access ko compress kar sakta hai.
- **B.** Restrictive watch strap blood flow par pressure daal sakti hai.
- **D.** Heavy bag ka strap access ko daba sakta hai.

**Exam clue:** Long periods of direct pressure over vascular access should be avoided.

</details>
