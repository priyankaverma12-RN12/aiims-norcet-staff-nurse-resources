### Q1. What is the effluent collected from the waste outlet during CRRT?

**Difficulty:** Easy | **Topic:** Continuous renal replacement therapy | **Priority:** High | **Type:** Original practice

- **A.** Fresh replacement solution before it enters the circuit
- **B.** Used fluid leaving the filter, including removed water and cleared solutes
- **C.** Whole blood waiting to return to the patient
- **D.** Urine draining through a bladder catheter

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: B. Used fluid leaving the filter, including removed water and cleared solutes**

Effluent filter se bahar nikalne wala waste fluid hota hai. Mode ke hisaab se ismein spent dialysate aur ultrafiltrate ho sakta hai.

**Baaki options kyun galat hain?**

- **A.** Fresh replacement solution prescribed fluid input hai, waste output nahi.
- **C.** Return blood patient ki circulation mein jaata hai, effluent bag mein nahi.
- **D.** Urine kidney se banta hai aur separate measure kiya jaata hai.

**Exam clue:** Effluent aur urine output ko alag document karna zaroori hai.

</details>

### Q2. Which abbreviation describes a prolonged intermittent dialysis modality often delivered over several hours rather than continuously throughout the day?

**Difficulty:** Easy | **Topic:** Continuous renal replacement therapy | **Priority:** High | **Type:** Original practice

- **A.** CAPD
- **B.** CVVHDF
- **C.** APD
- **D.** SLED

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: D. SLED**

SLED ka full form sustained low-efficiency dialysis hai. Yeh prolonged intermittent modality hai aur CRRT se schedule mein alag hai.

**Baaki options kyun galat hain?**

- **A.** CAPD abdomen mein manual dialysis exchanges ka method hai.
- **B.** CVVHDF continuous therapy ka mode hai.
- **C.** APD peritoneal dialysis mein cycler machine use karta hai.

**Exam clue:** Modality ke naam mein continuous aur prolonged intermittent ka difference dekho.

</details>

### Q3. Which nutrient component can be lost in CRRT effluent and therefore needs consideration in nutrition planning?

**Difficulty:** Easy | **Topic:** Continuous renal replacement therapy | **Priority:** High | **Type:** Original practice

- **A.** Free amino acids
- **B.** Intact red blood cells during normal filtration
- **C.** Large dietary fiber particles
- **D.** Intact platelets during normal filtration

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: A. Free amino acids**

CRRT filter se free amino acids ka loss ho sakta hai. Critical illness mein catabolism bhi hota hai, isliye dietitian ko protein needs assess karni chahiye.

**Baaki options kyun galat hain?**

- **B.** Intact red cells normal filter se waste fluid mein nahi jaate. Blood leak ek safety issue hai.
- **C.** Dietary fiber particles gut mein rehte hain aur CRRT blood filter se normally nahi nikalte.
- **D.** Intact platelets normal effluent mein expected nutritional loss nahi hain.

**Exam clue:** CRRT mein solute clearance ke saath nutrition losses bhi consider karo.

</details>

### Q4. Which electrolyte can become depleted during CRRT when a low-potassium solution is used and losses exceed replacement?

**Difficulty:** Easy | **Topic:** Continuous renal replacement therapy | **Priority:** High | **Type:** Original practice

- **A.** Albumin
- **B.** Hemoglobin
- **C.** Potassium
- **D.** Creatinine

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: C. Potassium**

Continuous clearance aur low-potassium solution ki wajah se serum potassium kam ho sakta hai. Isliye repeat lab monitoring aur prescribed replacement important hain.

**Baaki options kyun galat hain?**

- **A.** Albumin protein hai, electrolyte nahi.
- **B.** Hemoglobin oxygen-carrying protein hai, serum electrolyte nahi.
- **D.** Creatinine waste marker hai, potassium jaisa essential electrolyte nahi.

**Exam clue:** CRRT high potassium treat kar sakta hai lekin overcorrection ko bhi monitor karo.

</details>

### Q5. A patient on citrate-anticoagulated CRRT is receiving enteral feeding. What should the nurse communicate to the dietitian about energy assessment?

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Topic:** Continuous renal replacement therapy | **Priority:** High | **Type:** Original practice

- **A.** Citrate always provides the same calories as heparin
- **B.** Metabolized citrate may contribute non-nutritional calories
- **C.** The CRRT effluent volume alone gives the total daily calorie intake
- **D.** All enteral feeding should be discontinued during citrate anticoagulation

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: B. Metabolized citrate may contribute non-nutritional calories**

Citrate ka kuch hissa patient ki body mein metabolize hokar energy de sakta hai. Actual contribution prescription aur clearance par depend karta hai, isliye dietitian ko citrate therapy ki information chahiye.

**Baaki options kyun galat hain?**

- **A.** Heparin citrate ki tarah metabolizable energy source nahi hai.
- **C.** Effluent volume se total calorie intake calculate nahi hota. Feed, medicines aur CRRT fluids ki composition bhi dekhni hoti hai.
- **D.** Enteral feeding automatically stop nahi ki jaati. Nutrition plan clinical condition ke hisaab se adjust hota hai.

**Exam clue:** ICU nutrition mein feed ke alawa treatment-derived energy bhi ho sakti hai.

</details>

### Q6. In an ICU patient with severe shock and impaired lactate metabolism, which buffer is generally preferred in CRRT fluids according to ISCCM guidance?

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Topic:** Continuous renal replacement therapy | **Priority:** High | **Type:** Original practice

- **A.** Lactate as the preferred buffer in every case
- **B.** No buffer in any fluid
- **C.** An independently added concentrated sodium bicarbonate bolus in the bag
- **D.** Bicarbonate

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: D. Bicarbonate**

Bicarbonate-based dialysis aur replacement fluid acid-base correction ke liye generally preferred hai, especially jab lactate metabolism compromised ho.

**Baaki options kyun galat hain?**

- **A.** Lactate buffer ko har patient mein prefer nahi kiya jaata, shock mein handling impaired ho sakti hai.
- **B.** Buffer ke bina acid-base correction ka prescribed objective achieve karna mushkil ho sakta hai.
- **C.** Bag mein independent additive milana composition aur sterility error kar sakta hai. Prescribed ready-to-use solution follow karo.

**Exam clue:** Shock mein buffer selection clinical metabolism aur prescription se guided hota hai.

</details>
