## Diagnosis and chronicity

### Q1. A previously healthy adult has eGFR 48 during an acute febrile illness and no previous kidney results. What is the appropriate interpretation?

**Difficulty:** 🟡 Medium · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD

- **A.** CKD is confirmed from this single result
- **B.** Chronicity is not yet established and acute dysfunction must be evaluated
- **C.** The patient has stage 5 CKD
- **D.** Repeat assessment is unnecessary

<details><summary>✅ Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct answer: B. Chronicity is not yet established and acute dysfunction must be evaluated**

**Kyun sahi hai?** Ek isolated low eGFR acute illness mein AKI reflect kar sakta hai. Previous records aur appropriate reassessment se chronicity establish karni hoti hai.

**Baaki options kyun galat hain?**

- **A.** CKD ke liye persistent abnormality ka evidence chahiye.
- **C.** G5 category eGFR below 15 hai.
- **D.** Acute illness ke baad reassessment important hai.

**Exam clue:** One abnormal result does not establish chronicity.

### Q12. After loss of functioning nephrons, which adaptive response may contribute to further injury over time?

**Difficulty:** 🟡 Medium · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD

- **B.** Complete regeneration of every lost nephron
- **C.** Permanent absence of protein leakage in all patients
- **D.** Automatic normalization of blood pressure

<details><summary>✅ Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct answer: A. Increased workload and intraglomerular pressure in remaining nephrons**

**Kyun sahi hai?** Remaining nephrons filtration maintain karne ke liye adapt karte hain. Kuch settings mein hyperfiltration aur increased intraglomerular pressure further scarring contribute kar sakte hain.

- **C.** Protein leakage kidney damage ka important marker ho sakti hai.
- **D.** CKD hypertension se associated ho sakti hai.

**Exam clue:** Compensatory hyperfiltration can become harmful.
