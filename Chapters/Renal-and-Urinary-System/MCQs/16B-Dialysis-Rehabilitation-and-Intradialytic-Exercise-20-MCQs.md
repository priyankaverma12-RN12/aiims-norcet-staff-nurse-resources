# 16B — Dialysis rehabilitation and intradialytic exercise — 20 original MCQs

**Status:** Candidate original practice, staged in PR63; not published. Clinical guidance is educational; use individualized clearance and local protocol. No official PYQ attribution. Topic-specific exercise scenarios, not general mobilization or community rehabilitation.

### Q1. Which term describes planned exercise performed while a stable patient is receiving haemodialysis?

**Difficulty:** Easy | **Topic:** Dialysis rehabilitation and intradialytic exercise | **Priority:** High | **Type:** Original practice

- **A.** Intradialytic exercise
- **B.** Interdialytic exercise
- **C.** Pretransplant conditioning only
- **D.** Peritoneal exchange

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: A. Intradialytic exercise**

Intradialytic ka matlab dialysis treatment ke dauran exercise karna hai.

**Baaki options kyun galat hain?**

- **B.** Interdialytic activity dialysis sessions ke beech hoti hai.
- **C.** Pretransplant conditioning ek alag purpose hai; dialysis exercise transplant tak limited nahi hai.

**Exam clue:** Intra means during the treatment.
