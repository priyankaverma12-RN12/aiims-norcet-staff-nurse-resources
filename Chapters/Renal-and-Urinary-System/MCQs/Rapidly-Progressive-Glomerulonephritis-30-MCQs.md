### Q1. An adult with previously stable renal function develops creatinine elevation from 0.9 to 3.1 mg/dL over ten days with active glomerular urine sediment. Which clinical course requires urgent investigation?

**Difficulty:** 🟡 Medium · **Priority:** 🔴 MOST IMPORTANT

- **B.** Isolated uncomplicated cystitis
- **C.** Rapidly progressive glomerulonephritis
- **D.** Physiological age-related decline alone

**Baaki options kyun galat hain?**
