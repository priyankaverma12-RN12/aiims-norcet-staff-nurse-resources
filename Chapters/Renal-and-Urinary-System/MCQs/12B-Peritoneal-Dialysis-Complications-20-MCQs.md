### Q1. Which finding most strongly supports a peritoneal dialysis catheter exit-site infection?

**Difficulty:** Easy | **Topic:** Peritoneal dialysis infectious and mechanical complications | **Priority:** Medium | **Type:** Original practice

- **A.** Purulent drainage from the catheter-skin interface
- **B.** An intact dry exit site without tenderness
- **C.** A healed surgical scar remote from the catheter
- **D.** Stable clear dialysate alone

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: A. Purulent drainage from the catheter-skin interface**

Exit site se pus discharge infection ka strong clinical sign hai, erythema present ho ya na ho. PD team ko report karna chahiye.

**Baaki options kyun galat hain?**

- **B.** Dry intact site active infection ka positive evidence nahi hai.
- **C.** Remote healed scar catheter exit-site infection establish nahi karti.
- **D.** Clear effluent peritoneal cavity ke status ka clue hai, exit site ko separately assess karna padta hai.

**Exam clue:** Purulence is the key exit-site finding.
