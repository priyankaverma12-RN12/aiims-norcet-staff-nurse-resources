### Q1. What structural change defines an anterior urethral stricture?

**Difficulty:** Easy | **Topic:** Urethral stricture and reconstructive nursing | **Priority:** Medium | **Type:** Original practice

- **A.** Dilation of the renal collecting system
- **B.** Enlargement of the entire urinary bladder
- **C.** Inflammation limited to the renal glomeruli
- **D.** Scar-related narrowing of the urethral lumen

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: D. Scar-related narrowing of the urethral lumen**

Anterior urethral scar aur surrounding spongiofibrosis lumen narrow kar sakte hain, jisse urine flow affected hota hai.

**Baaki options kyun galat hain?**

- **A.** Collecting system dilation hydronephrosis hai, urethral narrowing nahi.
- **B.** Bladder enlarge ho sakta hai as a consequence of outlet obstruction, par stricture ka anatomical site urethra hai.
- **C.** Glomerular inflammation filtration ko affect karti hai, urethral scar define nahi karti.

**Exam clue:** Anterior urethral scar aur surrounding spongiofibrosis lumen narrow kar sakte hain, jisse urine flow affected hota hai.
