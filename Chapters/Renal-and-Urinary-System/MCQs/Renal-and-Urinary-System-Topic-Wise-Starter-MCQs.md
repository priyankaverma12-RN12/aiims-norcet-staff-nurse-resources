## AKI

### Q1. A patient with acute kidney injury has potassium 6.7 mmol/L and peaked T waves. Which action is most appropriate?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Topic:** AKI

- **A.** Activate urgent management of hyperkalemia and monitor the ECG
- **B.** Give a potassium supplement
- **C.** Delay review until the next routine blood sample
- **D.** Encourage potassium-rich fruit immediately

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: A. Activate urgent management of hyperkalemia and monitor the ECG**

Kidney potassium ko blood se nikaalne mein fail ho sakti hai. High potassium heart ki electrical activity ko dangerous bana sakta hai. ECG changes ke saath emergency treatment chahiye.

**Baaki options kyun galat hain?**

- **B.** Potassium supplement level aur badha sakta hai.
- **C.** Delay se fatal rhythm ka risk hai.
- **D.** Potassium-rich food acute problem ko badha sakta hai.

</details>

## Hemodialysis

### Q2. Before hemodialysis, a nurse cannot feel the usual thrill over an arteriovenous fistula. What should the nurse do?

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Topic:** Hemodialysis

- **A.** Apply a tight tourniquet for the rest of the day
- **B.** Insert a needle blindly until blood appears
- **C.** Use the fistula arm for routine blood pressure monitoring
- **D.** Avoid cannulation and notify the dialysis team promptly

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: D. Avoid cannulation and notify the dialysis team promptly**

Fistula mein blood flow hone par halka vibration feel hota hai. Thrill gayab ho toh blockage ka doubt hota hai. Nurse us access ko use karne se pehle dialysis team ko batati hai.

**Baaki options kyun galat hain?**

- **A.** Tight tourniquet flow ko aur rok sakta hai.
- **B.** Blind cannulation injury kar sakti hai.
- **C.** BP cuff fistula arm par avoid kiya jaata hai.

</details>

## Peritoneal dialysis

### Q3. A patient receiving peritoneal dialysis reports abdominal pain and cloudy effluent. Which complication should be suspected?

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Topic:** Peritoneal dialysis

- **A.** Uncomplicated anemia alone
- **B.** Isolated cataract
- **C.** Peritonitis
- **D.** Normal expected clear drainage

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: C. Peritonitis**

Peritoneal dialysis mein pet ki lining blood se waste nikaalne mein madad karti hai. Cloudy fluid aur pet pain infection ke signs ho sakte hain. Nurse sample aur urgent review arrange karti hai.

**Baaki options kyun galat hain?**

- **A.** Anemia cloudy fluid explain nahi karti.
- **B.** Cataract aankh ki condition hai.
- **D.** Normal effluent aam taur par clear hota hai.

</details>

## Nephrotic syndrome

### Q4. A patient has heavy proteinuria, low serum albumin and generalized edema. Which mechanism contributes to the edema?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Topic:** Nephrotic syndrome

- **A.** Excess hemoglobin binding all plasma water
- **B.** Reduced plasma oncotic pressure from protein loss
- **C.** Increased albumin production as the sole mechanism
- **D.** Complete absence of sodium retention in every case

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: B. Reduced plasma oncotic pressure from protein loss**

Jab albumin urine se nikalta hai, toh blood mein paani rokne ki ability kam ho jaati hai. Fluid tissues mein jama ho sakta hai. Sodium retention bhi swelling ko badha sakti hai.

**Baaki options kyun galat hain?**

- **A.** Hemoglobin is edema ka main mechanism nahi hai.
- **C.** Albumin yahan kam hai.
- **D.** Sodium retention bhi role play kar sakti hai.

</details>
