### Q1. Which component drives blood through the extracorporeal circuit during hemodialysis?

**Difficulty:** Easy | **Topic:** Hemodialysis principles and adequacy | **Priority:** High | **Type:** Original practice

- **A.** Blood pump
- **B.** Dialysate heater
- **C.** Venous air detector
- **D.** Dialysate conductivity monitor

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: A. Blood pump**

Blood pump patient ka blood prescribed rate se extracorporeal tubing mein circulate karta hai.

**Baaki options kyun galat hain?**

- **B.** Heater dialysate ka temperature control karta hai, blood flow nahi.
- **C.** Air detector venous line mein hawa detect karta hai, blood ko pump nahi karta.
- **D.** Conductivity monitor dialysate concentration ki safety check karta hai, circulation nahi chalata.

**Exam clue:** Blood circuit aur dialysate circuit ke components alag kaam karte hain.

</details>

### Q2. In a conventional hemodialysis circuit, what is the function of the line called the arterial blood line?

**Difficulty:** Easy | **Topic:** Hemodialysis principles and adequacy | **Priority:** High | **Type:** Original practice

- **A.** Returns treated blood to the patient
- **B.** Delivers fresh dialysate to the dialyzer
- **C.** Carries blood from the access toward the dialyzer
- **D.** Carries spent dialysate to waste

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: C. Carries blood from the access toward the dialyzer**

Arterial blood line ka naam circuit mein flow direction ko batata hai. Ismein blood patient se dialyzer ki taraf jaata hai.

**Baaki options kyun galat hain?**

- **A.** Treated blood patient tak venous blood line se lautata hai.
- **B.** Fresh dialysate apni separate fluid line se jaata hai.
- **D.** Spent dialysate waste line se nikalta hai, arterial blood line se nahi.

**Exam clue:** Arterial aur venous circuit labels ko blood ki direction se yaad rakho.

</details>

### Q3. What is the function of the venous blood chamber in a standard hemodialysis extracorporeal circuit?

**Difficulty:** Easy | **Topic:** Hemodialysis principles and adequacy | **Priority:** High | **Type:** Original practice

- **A.** Measures the patient's urine output
- **B.** Provides a space for air separation before blood returns
- **C.** Stores fresh dialysis concentrate
- **D.** Measures the patient's postdialysis weight

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: B. Provides a space for air separation before blood returns**

Venous chamber blood mein aayi hawa ko alag hone ki jagah deta hai. Air detector aur clamp bhi safety system ka hissa hain.

**Baaki options kyun galat hain?**

- **A.** Urine output urine collection se measure hota hai, blood chamber se nahi.
- **C.** Concentrate dialysate preparation system mein rehta hai.
- **D.** Patient ka weight weighing scale se liya jaata hai, chamber se nahi.

**Exam clue:** Return line mein hawa ko patient tak pahunchne se rokna zaroori hai.

</details>

### Q4. What is the usual purpose of prescribed anticoagulation in the extracorporeal blood circuit?

**Difficulty:** Easy | **Topic:** Hemodialysis principles and adequacy | **Priority:** High | **Type:** Original practice

- **A.** To increase serum hemoglobin
- **B.** To replace dialysate bicarbonate
- **C.** To increase kidney urine production
- **D.** To prevent circuit clotting

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: D. To prevent circuit clotting**

Blood tubing aur filter mein clot ban sakta hai. Prescribed anticoagulation is risk ko kam karta hai.

**Baaki options kyun galat hain?**

- **A.** Hemoglobin badhane ke liye anemia ki wajah ke hisaab se treatment diya jaata hai.
- **B.** Bicarbonate dialysate composition se diya ja sakta hai, anticoagulant se nahi.
- **C.** Anticoagulation kidney ki urine formation ko restore nahi karta.

**Exam clue:** Circuit clotting aur patient ki bleeding risk dono assess karne hote hain.

</details>

### Q5. What does the term intermittent hemodialysis describe?

**Difficulty:** Easy | **Topic:** Hemodialysis principles and adequacy | **Priority:** High | **Type:** Original practice

- **A.** Dialysis delivered in separate scheduled treatment sessions
- **B.** A continuous 24-hour ICU treatment
- **C.** Manual exchanges of fluid through the abdomen
- **D.** Continuous bladder irrigation through a urinary catheter

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: A. Dialysis delivered in separate scheduled treatment sessions**

Intermittent HD mein patient ko alag-alag scheduled sessions milte hain. Beech mein patient machine se disconnected rehta hai.

**Baaki options kyun galat hain?**

- **B.** Continuous ICU treatment CRRT ho sakta hai, routine intermittent HD nahi.
- **C.** Abdominal exchanges peritoneal dialysis mein hote hain.
- **D.** Bladder irrigation urinary tract ki procedure hai, kidney replacement nahi.

**Exam clue:** Modality ko uske route aur treatment schedule se pehchano.

</details>

### Q6. Which statement about red blood cells in an intact conventional dialyzer is correct?

**Difficulty:** Easy | **Topic:** Hemodialysis principles and adequacy | **Priority:** High | **Type:** Original practice

- **A.** They routinely pass into spent dialysate
- **B.** They are intentionally removed to lower blood viscosity
- **C.** They normally remain in the blood compartment
- **D.** They are replaced by cells in fresh dialysate

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: C. They normally remain in the blood compartment**

Intact membrane blood cells ko blood side mein rakhti hai aur chhote solutes ka exchange allow karti hai.

**Baaki options kyun galat hain?**

- **A.** Spent dialysate mein red cells normal finding nahi hai. Blood leak ka doubt ho sakta hai.
- **B.** Routine dialysis ka goal red cells nikaalna nahi hai.
- **D.** Fresh dialysate mein replacement red cells nahi hote.

**Exam clue:** Blood leak safety alarm ko ignore nahi karna chahiye.

</details>
