# Ureteroscopy, PCNL and postoperative stone nursing — 20 original MCQs

Candidate only. Original NORCET/HPRCA staff nurse practice, not historical PYQs. Device care, medication plans and emergency actions follow local protocol and individualized orders.

### Q1. What is the usual access route for retrograde ureteroscopy to treat a ureteric stone?

**Difficulty:** Easy | **Topic:** Ureteroscopy, PCNL and postoperative stone nursing | **Priority:** Medium | **Type:** Original practice

- **A.** Through the urethra and bladder into the ureter
- **B.** Through a flank puncture directly into the renal cortex
- **C.** Through a femoral artery catheter
- **D.** Through a suprapubic incision into the peritoneum

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: A. Through the urethra and bladder into the ureter**

Retrograde ureteroscopy mein scope urethra se bladder aur phir ureter mein jata hai, jahan stone ko visualize aur treat kar sakte hain.

**Baaki options kyun galat hain?**

- **B.** Flank puncture percutaneous access se related hai, routine retrograde ureteroscopy ka route nahi.
- **C.** Femoral artery vascular access deti hai, urinary tract endoscopy ka route nahi.
- **D.** Suprapubic incision routine retrograde ureteroscopic access mein required nahi hoti.

**Exam clue:** Retrograde approach urine flow ki opposite direction mein hota hai.

</details>
