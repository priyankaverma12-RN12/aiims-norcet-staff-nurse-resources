### Q1. A patient with a migrating ureteric stone reports severe intermittent flank pain extending toward the groin. Which mechanism explains the colicky pattern?

**Difficulty:** Easy | **Topic:** Ureteric obstruction and renal colic | **Priority:** Medium | **Type:** Original practice

- **A.** Reduced gastric acid secretion
- **B.** Ureteral peristaltic contractions against obstruction
- **C.** Inflammation of the middle ear
- **D.** Inhibition of skeletal muscle contraction

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: B. Ureteral peristaltic contractions against obstruction**

Ureter stone ko aage badhane ke liye contract karta hai, blockage ke against spasm se waves mein pain ho sakta hai.

**Baaki options kyun galat hain?**

- **A.** Gastric acid stomach symptoms ko affect karta hai, ureter ki colic ko explain nahi karta.
- **C.** Middle ear inflammation ear pain karati hai, flank-to-groin radiation nahi.
- **D.** Skeletal muscle inhibition ureter ke smooth muscle contraction ka mechanism nahi hai.

**Exam clue:** Colic waves ka relation ureter ki contractions se jodo.
