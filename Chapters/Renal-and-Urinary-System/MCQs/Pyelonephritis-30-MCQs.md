### Q1. Which anatomical involvement distinguishes acute pyelonephritis from infection limited to the bladder?

**Difficulty:** 🟡 Medium · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD · **Exam focus:** NORCET / all-India Staff Nurse · **Topic:** Tissue involvement

- **A.** Inflammation confined to the bladder mucosa
- **B.** Isolated immune injury of the glomerular filtration barrier
- **C.** Infection involving the renal pelvis and renal parenchyma
- **D.** Inflammation confined to the distal urethra

<details><summary>✅ Correct answer and detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: C. Infection involving the renal pelvis and renal parenchyma**

**A. Kyun galat hai:** Bladder mucosa tak limited infection cystitis se match karta hai. Pyelonephritis mein kidney involvement hoti hai.

**B. Kyun galat hai:** Immune-mediated glomerular injury nephritis ke different mechanism se related ho sakti hai. Bacterial kidney infection ko sirf glomerular disease nahi kehte.

**C. Kyun sahi hai:** Pyelonephritis mein renal pelvis aur kidney ka functional tissue, yani parenchyma, infection se affected hote hain. Isliye ye sirf lower urinary tract disease nahi hai.

**D. Kyun galat hai:** Distal urethral inflammation urethritis ka pattern hai. Isse renal tissue involvement establish nahi hoti.
