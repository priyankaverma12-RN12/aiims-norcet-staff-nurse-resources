### Q1. Which term describes impaired bladder storage or emptying caused by a neurological disorder?

**Difficulty:** Easy | **Topic:** Neurogenic bladder and spinal cord injury | **Priority:** Medium | **Type:** Original practice

- **A.** Neurogenic lower urinary tract dysfunction
- **B.** Nephrolithiasis
- **C.** Primary hyperparathyroidism
- **D.** Glomerulonephritis

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: A. Neurogenic lower urinary tract dysfunction**

Nerve pathways bladder aur sphincter ko coordinate karti hain, neurological damage se storage ya emptying affect ho sakti hai.

**Baaki options kyun galat hain?**

- **B.** Nephrolithiasis kidney stone hai, neural control disorder ki definition nahi.
- **C.** Hyperparathyroidism calcium metabolism affect karta hai, neurogenic bladder define nahi karta.
- **D.** Glomerulonephritis glomerular inflammation hai, lower tract neural dysfunction nahi.

**Exam clue:** Nerve pathways bladder aur sphincter ko coordinate karti hain, neurological damage se storage ya emptying affect ho sakti hai.
