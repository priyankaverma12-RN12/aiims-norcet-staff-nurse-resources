### Q1. Which material is found in the most common category of renal calculi?

**Difficulty:** Easy | **Topic:** Renal calculi | **Priority:** Medium | **Type:** Original practice

- **A.** Calcium salts
- **B.** Cholesterol
- **C.** Bilirubin pigment
- **D.** Keratin

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: A. Calcium salts**

Calcium oxalate aur calcium phosphate urine mein crystal bana kar sabse common stone category banate hain.

**Baaki options kyun galat hain?**

- **B.** Cholesterol stones gallbladder mein bante hain, kidney stone ki typical composition nahi hain.
- **C.** Bilirubin pigment biliary stones se related hai, urinary calcium stones se nahi.
- **D.** Keratin skin ka structural protein hai, usual renal calculus ka mineral nahi.

**Exam clue:** Calcium oxalate aur calcium phosphate urine mein crystal bana kar sabse common stone category banate hain.

**Exam clue:** Uric acid low urine pH mein kam soluble hota hai aur plain film par aam taur par nahi dikhta, jabki CT par detect ho sakta hai.
