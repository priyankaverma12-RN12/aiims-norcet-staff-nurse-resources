### Q1. A patient with oliguric CKD develops volume expansion despite serum sodium of 137 mmol/L. What explains this finding?

**Difficulty:** 🟡 Medium · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD

- **A.** Normal serum sodium excludes fluid overload
- **B.** Serum sodium directly measures total body water
- **C.** Sodium and water can both increase while their concentration ratio remains near normal
- **D.** Only intracellular dehydration is possible

<details><summary>✅ Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct answer: C. Sodium and water can both increase while their concentration ratio remains near normal**

**Kyun sahi hai?** Serum sodium water ke relative sodium concentration batata hai. Dono retain hone par concentration normal reh sakti hai.

**Baaki options kyun galat hain?**

- **A.** Normal concentration total fluid amount establish nahi karti.
- **B.** Sodium concentration total water ka direct measurement nahi hai.
- **D.** Extracellular expansion isolated intracellular dehydration prove nahi karti.
