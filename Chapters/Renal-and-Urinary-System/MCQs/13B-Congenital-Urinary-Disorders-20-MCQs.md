# Congenital urinary anomalies and renal follow-up — 20 original MCQs

Isolated candidate. Original NORCET/HPRCA nursing practice; not authenticated previous-year items. Paediatric investigations, interventions and prophylaxis must follow specialist orders and local guidance.

### Q1. What is meant by unilateral renal agenesis?

**Difficulty:** Easy | **Topic:** Congenital urinary anomalies and renal follow-up | **Priority:** Medium | **Type:** Original practice

- **A.** Congenital absence of one kidney
- **B.** Acquired removal of a kidney after trauma
- **C.** Temporary obstruction of one ureter
- **D.** Loss of bladder contractility

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: A. Congenital absence of one kidney**

Renal agenesis mein embryonic development ke dauran ek kidney form nahi hoti, jabki remaining kidney function maintain kar sakti hai.

**Baaki options kyun galat hain?**

- **B.** Surgical removal acquired solitary kidney cause hai, agenesis congenital absence hai.
- **C.** Ureter obstruction existing kidney ki drainage problem hai, organ absent nahi hota.
- **D.** Bladder contractility ka disorder kidney formation se distinct hai.

**Exam clue:** Agenesis means failure of organ development.

</details>

### Q2. Which anatomical arrangement is typical of a horseshoe kidney?

**Difficulty:** Easy | **Topic:** Congenital urinary anomalies and renal follow-up | **Priority:** Medium | **Type:** Original practice

- **A.** Complete absence of both renal arteries
- **B.** Duplication of the urinary bladder only
- **C.** Fusion of the two kidneys, usually at their lower poles
- **D.** Fusion of both ureters at the external meatus

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: C. Fusion of the two kidneys, usually at their lower poles**

Horseshoe kidney mein most commonly lower poles renal isthmus se joined hote hain, position aur drainage alter ho sakti hai.

**Baaki options kyun galat hain?**

- **A.** Both renal arteries absent hona horseshoe kidney ki defining feature nahi hai; vascular anatomy variable ho sakti hai.
- **B.** Bladder duplication alag congenital anomaly hai.
- **D.** Ureter fusion at meatus is renal fusion ka definition nahi hai.

**Exam clue:** Recognize renal fusion, not ureteric fusion.

</details>

### Q3. A child with a solitary functioning kidney has a normal serum creatinine. Which follow-up remains important?

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Topic:** Congenital urinary anomalies and renal follow-up | **Priority:** High | **Type:** Original practice

- **A.** Discontinue renal surveillance permanently
- **B.** Periodic blood pressure, urine albumin and kidney-function assessment
- **C.** Monitor urine color alone
- **D.** Perform renal biopsy routinely every month

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: B. Periodic blood pressure, urine albumin and kidney-function assessment**

Single functioning kidney compensatory filtration se normal creatinine maintain kar sakti hai; BP aur albumin surveillance early injury detect karne mein useful hai.

**Baaki options kyun galat hain?**

- **A.** Normal creatinine future hypertension ya albuminuria exclude nahi karti.
- **C.** Urine color microscopic albumin loss ya filtration decline reliably measure nahi karta.
- **D.** Routine monthly biopsy invasive hai aur asymptomatic child ke liye indicated nahi.

**Exam clue:** Preserved filtration does not remove long-term risk.

</details>

- **B.** Maternal bladder distension directly compressing fetal lungs
- **C.** Failure of fetal hepatic bile secretion alone

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: D. Marked reduction in fetal urine contributing to deficient amniotic fluid and pulmonary hypoplasia**

Later gestation mein fetal urine amniotic fluid ka major source hota hai; severe deficiency lung expansion aur development compromise kar sakti hai.

**Baaki options kyun galat hain?**

- **A.** Bilateral agenesis mein functioning kidneys absent hone se urine production reduced hoti hai, excessive nahi.
- **B.** Maternal bladder distension primary mechanism nahi hai.
- **C.** Bile secretion oligohydramnios aur pulmonary hypoplasia ka usual renal mechanism nahi hai.

**Exam clue:** Severe oligohydramnios may impair lung development.

</details>

### Q5. Which finding describes a multicystic dysplastic kidney rather than a normally functioning collecting system?

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Topic:** Congenital urinary anomalies and renal follow-up | **Priority:** High | **Type:** Original practice

- **A.** A single normally draining renal pelvis
- **B.** Uniformly normal functioning renal cortex with no cysts
- **C.** An abnormal renal unit containing multiple noncommunicating cysts and dysplastic tissue
- **D.** A stone confined to a normal distal ureter

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: C. An abnormal renal unit containing multiple noncommunicating cysts and dysplastic tissue**

MCDK developmental dysplasia se related hai, affected unit commonly nonfunctioning hota hai aur noncommunicating cysts present hote hain.

**Baaki options kyun galat hain?**

- **A.** Normally draining pelvis MCDK ki defining architecture nahi hai.
- **B.** Normal cortex with no cysts dysplastic multicystic unit ko describe nahi karta.
- **D.** Ureteric calculus acquired obstruction hai, renal dysplasia nahi.

**Exam clue:** Noncommunicating cysts in a dysplastic renal unit.

</details>

### Q6. A newborn with unilateral multicystic dysplastic kidney has a healthy opposite kidney. Which care principle is appropriate?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Topic:** Congenital urinary anomalies and renal follow-up | **Priority:** High | **Type:** Original practice

- **A.** Individualized surveillance of the functioning kidney and blood pressure rather than automatic immediate nephrectomy
- **B.** Immediate removal of the healthy kidney
- **C.** Assume dialysis is inevitable in the neonatal period
- **D.** Stop all follow-up because one normal kidney is present

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: A. Individualized surveillance of the functioning kidney and blood pressure rather than automatic immediate nephrectomy**

Unilateral MCDK mein contralateral kidney adequate function maintain kar sakti hai; conservative follow-up common hai, surgery specific indications par depend karti hai.

**Baaki options kyun galat hain?**

- **B.** Healthy kidney removal remaining filtration compromise karegi.
- **C.** Dialysis need total renal function aur clinical status par depend karti hai, inevitable nahi.
- **D.** Solitary functioning kidney mein long-term risk surveillance important hai.

**Exam clue:** Preserve functioning renal tissue and follow risk.

</details>

### Q7. What is a duplex collecting system?

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Topic:** Congenital urinary anomalies and renal follow-up | **Priority:** High | **Type:** Original practice

- **A.** Two kidneys both absent at birth
- **B.** An acquired fistula between bladder and colon
- **C.** A renal artery that divides into two normal branches
- **D.** A kidney with duplicated collecting structures and partially or completely duplicated ureters

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: D. A kidney with duplicated collecting structures and partially or completely duplicated ureters**

Duplex system mein same kidney ke upper aur lower collecting moieties ho sakte hain, ureteric drainage patterns variable hote hain.

**Baaki options kyun galat hain?**

- **A.** Bilateral agenesis mein kidneys absent hoti hain, duplicated drainage nahi.
- **B.** Enterovesical fistula acquired communication ho sakti hai, congenital duplex anatomy nahi.
- **C.** Arterial branching collecting system duplication nahi hai.

**Exam clue:** Duplex describes drainage anatomy.

</details>

### Q8. A toilet-trained girl passes normal voluntary urine but has continuous wetness between voids since infancy. Which structural cause should be investigated?

- **D.** Normal age-related daytime continence

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

Ectopic ureter sphincter ke distal drain kare to normal bladder voiding ke saath continuous leakage ho sakti hai, specialist imaging chahiye.

**Baaki options kyun galat hain?**

- **C.** Normally positioned ureter bladder mein drain karta hai, distal bypass mechanism nahi.
- **D.** Persistent continuous wetness after toilet training evaluation demand karti hai.

**Exam clue:** Normal voids plus continuous dribbling suggest ectopic drainage.

</details>

### Q9. What is a ureterocele?

- **A.** Dilation of the entire renal artery
- **B.** Cystic dilation of the distal ureter near or within its bladder insertion
- **C.** A congenital opening of the abdominal wall into the bowel
- **D.** A solid calculus inside the urethral meatus

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: B. Cystic dilation of the distal ureter near or within its bladder insertion**

Ureterocele distal ureter ka cystic dilation hai jo obstruction, duplex system ya infection ke saath associated ho sakta hai.

**Baaki options kyun galat hain?**

- **A.** Renal artery dilation vascular anomaly hai, ureterocele urinary structure hai.
- **C.** Abdominal wall opening unrelated defect hai.
- **D.** Solid calculus cystic ureteric dilation nahi hai.

**Exam clue:** Identify the dilated distal ureter.

</details>

### Q11. What does vesicoureteral reflux describe?

**Difficulty:** Easy | **Topic:** Congenital urinary anomalies and renal follow-up | **Priority:** Medium | **Type:** Original practice

- **A.** Retrograde flow of urine from the bladder into a ureter, sometimes reaching the kidney
- **B.** Normal movement of urine from kidney to bladder
- **C.** Reflux of gastric contents into the esophagus
- **D.** Retrograde flow of blood in the renal artery

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: A. Retrograde flow of urine from the bladder into a ureter, sometimes reaching the kidney**

VUR mein anti-reflux mechanism inadequate hone par bladder urine ureter aur sometimes renal collecting system ki taraf wapas ja sakta hai.

**Baaki options kyun galat hain?**

- **B.** Kidney se bladder tak antegrade flow normal drainage hai.
- **C.** Gastroesophageal reflux different organ system ka disorder hai.
- **D.** Renal arterial blood flow urinary reflux nahi hai.

**Exam clue:** VUR is backward urine flow.

</details>
