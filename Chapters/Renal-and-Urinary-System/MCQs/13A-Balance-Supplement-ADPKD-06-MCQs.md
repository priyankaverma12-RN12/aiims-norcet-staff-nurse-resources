# ADPKD foundational practice — 6 original balancing MCQs

Candidate only. Original NORCET/HPRCA practice; supplements existing originals without relabeling or replacement.

### Q1. Which description best characterizes typical polycystic kidneys in classic ADPKD?

**Difficulty:** Easy | **Topic:** ADPKD foundational practice | **Priority:** Medium | **Type:** Original practice

- **A.** A single ureteric stone without renal cysts
- **B.** An isolated congenital absence of the bladder
- **C.** Multiple fluid-filled cysts involving both kidneys
- **D.** A single solid renal arterial plaque

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: C. Multiple fluid-filled cysts involving both kidneys**

Typical ADPKD mein dono kidneys mein multiple cysts develop hote hain, though severity vary kar sakti hai.

**Baaki options kyun galat hain?**

- **A.** Ureteric stone alone ADPKD define nahi karta.
- **B.** Bladder absence alag congenital abnormality hai.
- **D.** Arterial plaque vascular disease hai, renal cystic disorder nahi.

**Exam clue:** Typical ADPKD is a bilateral cystic kidney disorder.

</details>

### Q2. What does the R in ARPKD refer to?

**Difficulty:** Easy | **Topic:** ADPKD foundational practice | **Priority:** Medium | **Type:** Original practice

- **A.** Recessive inheritance
- **B.** Renal artery stenosis
- **C.** Recurrent renal infection
- **D.** Reversible renal injury

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: A. Recessive inheritance**

ARPKD mein R autosomal recessive ko indicate karta hai, ADPKD dominant inheritance se distinct hai.

**Baaki options kyun galat hain?**

- **B.** Renal artery stenosis blood vessel narrowing hai.
- **C.** Infection genetic inheritance acronym ka meaning nahi.
- **D.** Reversible injury ARPKD expansion nahi hai.

**Exam clue:** Distinguish recessive and dominant forms of PKD.

</details>

### Q3. Which urinary complication occurs more often in people with ADPKD than in the general population?

**Difficulty:** Easy | **Topic:** ADPKD foundational practice | **Priority:** Medium | **Type:** Original practice

- **A.** Congenital absence of the urethra
- **B.** Acute bladder rupture in every patient
- **C.** Acquired duplication of the ureters
- **D.** Kidney stones

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: D. Kidney stones**

ADPKD mein altered renal anatomy aur metabolic factors stones ke risk ko badha sakte hain.

**Baaki options kyun galat hain?**

- **A.** Urethral absence ADPKD ki typical acquired complication nahi.
- **B.** Bladder rupture universal feature nahi.
- **C.** Ureter duplication congenital anomaly hoti hai, ADPKD se acquired nahi.

**Exam clue:** Cystic disease can coexist with stone disease.

</details>

### Q4. Which abdominal-wall problem is associated with ADPKD?

**Difficulty:** Easy | **Topic:** ADPKD foundational practice | **Priority:** Medium | **Type:** Original practice

- **A.** Intussusception in every adult
- **B.** Hernia
- **C.** Acute appendicitis as an inevitable complication
- **D.** Pyloric stenosis in every adult

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: B. Hernia**

ADPKD mein abdominal pressure aur connective-tissue factors hernia se associated ho sakte hain.

**Baaki options kyun galat hain?**

- **A.** Intussusception universal ADPKD feature nahi.
- **C.** Appendicitis inevitable ADPKD outcome nahi.
- **D.** Adult ADPKD ka universal complication pyloric stenosis nahi hai.

**Exam clue:** Abdominal-wall hernia is a recognized extrarenal association.

</details>

### Q5. Which term describes feeling unusually full after eating only a small amount of food, sometimes reported with marked cyst-related organ enlargement?

**Difficulty:** Easy | **Topic:** ADPKD foundational practice | **Priority:** Medium | **Type:** Original practice

- **A.** Polyuria
- **B.** Dysuria
- **C.** Early satiety
- **D.** Hemoptysis

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: C. Early satiety**

Early satiety mein thoda khana khane ke baad fullness hoti hai; enlarged cystic organs pressure create kar sakte hain.

**Baaki options kyun galat hain?**

- **A.** Polyuria increased urine volume hai.
- **B.** Dysuria painful urination hai.
- **D.** Hemoptysis cough ke saath blood aana hai.

**Exam clue:** Large cystic organs may cause pressure-related symptoms.

</details>

### Q6. Does absence of a known family history rule out ADPKD in a patient with suggestive imaging?

**Difficulty:** Easy | **Topic:** ADPKD foundational practice | **Priority:** Medium | **Type:** Original practice

- **A.** No; further assessment may still establish the diagnosis
- **B.** Yes; every affected patient knows an affected parent
- **C.** Yes; absence of family history proves a simple cyst
- **D.** Yes; genetic disease is never first recognized in one family member

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: A. No; further assessment may still establish the diagnosis**

Family history unknown ho sakti hai ya new variant ho sakta hai; imaging aur specialist evaluation important hain.

**Baaki options kyun galat hain?**

- **B.** Har patient ko affected relative ka diagnosis pata ho zaroori nahi.
- **C.** Family history alone cyst type prove nahi karti.
- **D.** Genetic condition pehli baar family mein recognize ho sakti hai.

**Exam clue:** A negative family history is not an absolute exclusion.

</details>
