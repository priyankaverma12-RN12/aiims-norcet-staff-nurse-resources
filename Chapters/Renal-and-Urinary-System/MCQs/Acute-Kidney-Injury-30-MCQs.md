### Q1. An adult's serum creatinine rises from 0.9 to 1.2 mg/dL in 36 hours. Urine output remains preserved. Which interpretation is correct under the traditional KDIGO adult criteria?

**Difficulty:** 🟡 Medium · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD

- **A.** AKI requires anuria in every patient
- **B.** A rise of 0.3 mg/dL is relevant only after three months
- **C.** The creatinine rise meets an AKI criterion
- **D.** Preserved urine output rules out AKI

<details><summary>✅ Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct answer: C. The creatinine rise meets an AKI criterion**

**Kyun sahi hai?** 0.3 mg/dL rise 48 hours ke andar AKI criterion meet karta hai. Normal urine output AKI exclude nahi karta.

**Baaki options kyun galat hain?**

- **A.** Anuria severe presentation ho sakti hai, mandatory criterion nahi.
- **B.** Acute creatinine change ka time window 48 hours hai, three months nahi.
- **D.** Nonoliguric AKI possible hai.

**Exam clue:** Creatinine and urine criteria are alternatives.

### Q2. A 60 kg adult produces 150 mL urine during six consecutive hours. What is the average output and interpretation?

**Difficulty:** 🟡 Medium · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD

- **A.** 0.42 mL/kg/hour, meeting the adult stage 1 urine-output criterion if reliable
- **B.** 2.5 mL/kg/hour, normal output
- **C.** 0.25 mL/kg/hour, automatically stage 3
- **D.** 25 mL/kg/hour, polyuria

<details><summary>✅ Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct answer: A. 0.42 mL/kg/hour, meeting the adult stage 1 urine-output criterion if reliable**

**Kyun sahi hai?** 150 ÷ 6 = 25 mL/hour. 25 ÷ 60 = 0.4167 mL/kg/hour. Six hours tak below 0.5 stage 1 urine criterion meet karta hai.

**Baaki options kyun galat hain?**

- **B.** 2.5 mL/kg/hour correct calculation nahi hai.
- **C.** 0.25 incorrect hai aur stage 3 low output ke liye duration bhi important hai.
- **D.** 25 mL/hour hai, per kg per hour nahi.

**Exam clue:** Divide by hours and weight.

### Q3. Baseline creatinine is 1.1 mg/dL and rises to 2.5 mg/dL in four days. Measured urine output is 0.2 mL/kg/hour for 26 hours. Which AKI stage is assigned using the worse criterion?

**Difficulty:** 🔴 Difficult · **Priority:** 🔴 MOST IMPORTANT

<details><summary>✅ Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Baaki options kyun galat hain?**

- **A.** Stage 1 dono findings underestimate karta hai.
- **B.** Creatinine stage 2 hai lekin urine stage 3 bhi meet karta hai.
- **C.** 4 mg/dL ek alternative criterion hai, compulsory threshold nahi.

**Exam clue:** Use the higher applicable stage.

### Q7. After severe diarrhea, a patient develops hypotension and rising creatinine. Which mechanism is most likely initially?

**Difficulty:** 🟡 Medium · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD

- **A.** Primary bladder outlet obstruction
- **B.** Reduced renal perfusion from volume depletion
- **C.** Immune glomerular injury proved by diarrhea alone
- **D.** Excess renal blood flow

<details><summary>✅ Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Kyun sahi hai?** Diarrhea se fluid loss aur BP fall kidney perfusion reduce kar sakta hai. Prerenal mechanism consider karo aur assess karo.

**Baaki options kyun galat hain?**

- **A.** Bladder obstruction ka evidence stem mein nahi hai.
- **C.** Diarrhea alone glomerulonephritis prove nahi karta.
- **D.** Hypotension usually adequate perfusion ka indication nahi hai.
