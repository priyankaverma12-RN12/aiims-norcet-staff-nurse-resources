### Q1. A man without urinary symptoms has a properly collected voided urine culture growing E. coli at 10⁵ CFU/mL. What does this result establish?

**Difficulty:** 🟡 Medium · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD · **Exam focus:** NORCET / all-India Staff Nurse · **Topic:** Culture interpretation

- **A.** Acute pyelonephritis
- **B.** Symptomatic cystitis
- **C.** Asymptomatic bacteriuria
- **D.** Bloodstream infection

<details><summary>✅ Correct answer and detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: C. Asymptomatic bacteriuria**

**A. Kyun galat hai:** Kidney infection ke liye compatible clinical features chahiye. Sirf urine growth se upper tract involvement prove nahi hoti.

**B. Kyun galat hai:** Cystitis ke liye bladder symptoms ka context chahiye. Positive culture akela symptomatic disease nahi banata.

**C. Kyun sahi hai:** Is count par, UTI se related symptoms absent hon to ASB ki definition fit hoti hai. Diagnosis aur antibiotic indication alag decisions hain.

**D. Kyun galat hai:** Urine culture se bloodstream infection establish nahi hota. Clinical assessment aur indicated blood cultures alag hain.

### Q2. A stable asymptomatic man has 10⁵ CFU/mL of a single organism in voided urine but no pyuria. Which interpretation is correct?

**Difficulty:** 🟡 Medium · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD · **Exam focus:** NORCET / all-India Staff Nurse · **Topic:** Pyuria-independent definition

- **A.** The absence of pyuria does not exclude ASB
- **B.** ASB requires both bacteriuria and pyuria
- **C.** The result establishes sterile pyuria
- **D.** The result must represent acute glomerulonephritis

<details><summary>✅ Correct answer and detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: A. The absence of pyuria does not exclude ASB**

**A. Kyun sahi hai:** ASB quantitative bacterial growth aur attributable symptoms ki absence par define hoti hai. Pyuria hona compulsory nahi hai.

**B. Kyun galat hai:** White cells inflammatory response dikha sakte hain, lekin ASB ki definition un par depend nahi karti.

**C. Kyun galat hai:** Sterile pyuria mein white cells hote hain aur routine bacterial culture negative ho sakta hai. Yahan situation ulat hai.

**D. Kyun galat hai:** Glomerulonephritis ka diagnosis compatible renal findings par hota hai, is isolated culture se nahi.
