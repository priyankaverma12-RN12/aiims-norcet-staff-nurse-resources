Isolated candidate. NORCET/HPRCA original practice, not historical exam items. Clinical eligibility and medicine changes require the treating renal team and applicable prescribing label.

### Q1. Which inheritance pattern characterizes classic adult autosomal dominant polycystic kidney disease (ADPKD)?

**Difficulty:** Easy | **Topic:** Autosomal dominant polycystic kidney disease | **Priority:** Medium | **Type:** Original practice

- **A.** X-linked recessive
- **B.** Autosomal dominant
- **C.** Mitochondrial only
- **D.** Autosomal recessive in every family

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: B. Autosomal dominant**

ADPKD mein disease-causing variant ki ek altered copy sufficient ho sakti hai, dono sexes inherit kar sakte hain.

**Baaki options kyun galat hain?**

- **A.** X-linked inheritance sex chromosome se related hoti hai, classic ADPKD autosomal hai.
- **C.** Mitochondrial inheritance exclusively maternal line se hoti hai, ADPKD ka usual pattern nahi.
- **D.** ARPKD alag inherited condition hai, ADPKD dominant pattern follow karti hai.

**Exam clue:** Dominant inheritance is not sex-linked.

</details>
