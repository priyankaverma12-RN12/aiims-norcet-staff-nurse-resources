## Dose verification

### Q1. A renally eliminated medicine is given repeatedly at its usual maintenance dose despite a sustained decline in clearance. Which pharmacokinetic change increases toxicity risk?

**Difficulty:** 🟡 Medium · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD

- **A.** A shorter half-life with complete elimination between doses
- **B.** An obligatory reduction in drug absorption from every route
- **C.** A longer elimination half-life with accumulation during repeated dosing
- **D.** A guaranteed reduction in peak concentration after every dose

<details><summary>✅ Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct answer: C. A longer elimination half-life with accumulation during repeated dosing**

**Kyun sahi hai?** Kidney drug removal slow ho toh half-life badh sakti hai. Repeated dose se previous drug body mein remain karti hai aur concentration accumulate ho sakti hai. Dose and interval review karo.

**Baaki options kyun galat hain?**

- **A.** Reduced clearance generally elimination slow karti hai, fast nahi.
- **B.** Clearance aur absorption separate processes hain.
- **D.** Accumulation peak concentration increase bhi kar sakti hai.

**Exam clue:** Reduced clearance can prolong half-life.

### Q8. Serum creatinine rises from 1.6 to 2.2 mg/dL within three weeks after an ACE inhibitor dose increase. What percentage rise is recorded and what action is appropriate under KDIGO 2024?

**Difficulty:** 🔴 Difficult · **Priority:** 🔴 MOST IMPORTANT

- **A.** 27.3% rise, requiring no further assessment
- **B.** 60% rise, mandating permanent discontinuation without evaluation
- **C.** 6% rise, proving normal kidney function
- **D.** 37.5% rise, prompting clinical review for contributing causes and treatment reassessment

<details><summary>✅ Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct answer: D. 37.5% rise, prompting clinical review for contributing causes and treatment reassessment**

**Kyun sahi hai?** Rise = (2.2 − 1.6) ÷ 1.6 × 100 = 37.5%. Four weeks mein >30% rise review warrant karta hai. Volume, drugs and other causes assess karo.

**Baaki options kyun galat hain?**

- **A.** 27.3 final creatinine ko denominator banata hai, baseline required hai.
- **B.** 60 absolute difference ko wrongly percentage treat karta hai. Permanent stop automatic nahi.
- **C.** 0.6 mg/dL difference 6% nahi hai.

**Exam clue:** Use the baseline value as denominator for percentage rise.
