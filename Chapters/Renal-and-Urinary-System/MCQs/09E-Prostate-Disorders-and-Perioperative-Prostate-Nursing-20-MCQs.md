### Q1. Which prostate condition is defined by nonmalignant enlargement of prostatic tissue?

**Difficulty:** Easy | **Topic:** Prostate disorders and perioperative prostate nursing | **Priority:** Medium | **Type:** Original practice

- **A.** Metastatic prostate carcinoma
- **B.** Benign prostatic hyperplasia
- **C.** Acute bacterial prostatitis
- **D.** Urethral stricture

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: B. Benign prostatic hyperplasia**

BPH prostate tissue ki nonmalignant growth hai, jo urethral resistance badha sakti hai.

**Baaki options kyun galat hain?**

- **A.** Carcinoma malignant growth hai, benign hyperplasia se alag.
- **C.** Acute bacterial prostatitis infection aur inflammation hai, hyperplasia ki definition nahi.
- **D.** Stricture urethral scar narrowing hai, prostate tissue growth nahi.

**Exam clue:** BPH prostate tissue ki nonmalignant growth hai, jo urethral resistance badha sakti hai.
