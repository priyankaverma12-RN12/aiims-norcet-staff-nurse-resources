## Clinical and quality caveats

- Q15 ka answer NSAID class hai, jo suitable patients mein primary dysmenorrhea ke first-line options mein aata hai. Hormonal therapy bhi first-line option ho sakti hai. Existing four choices mein NSAID best hai. [RCH dysmenorrhea guideline](https://www.rch.org.au/clinicalguide/guideline_index/Dysmenorrhoea/) aur [AAFP clinical review](https://www.aafp.org/afp/2021/0800/p164) is choice ko support karte hain.
- Q17 mein pelvic ultrasound offered choices mein appropriate hai. Lekin jab persistent intermenstrual bleeding aur intracavitary lesion ka suspicion ho, toh NICE hysteroscopy-first pathway consider karta hai. Isliye ultrasound ko har structural cause ka universal first test mat samjho. [NICE NG88](https://www.nice.org.uk/guidance/ng88/chapter/recommendations).
- Q19 ka active peptic ulcer ya significant renal disease wala caution clinically important hai. Isko existing NSAID safety practice ke saath revise karo. [NHS NSAID overview](https://www.nhs.uk/medicines/nsaids/).
