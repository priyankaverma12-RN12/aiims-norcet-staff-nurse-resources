# Gynecological and Reproductive Nursing — Topic coverage map

This is a curriculum-based checklist, not full theory notes. No learning completion is recorded.

## Topics to study

- Menstrual cycle
- infertility
- contraception and counseling
- reproductive tract infections
- gynecological cancers
- fibroids and endometriosis
- menopause
- reproductive surgery

## Initial study explanations

### Contraception

IUCD uterus ke andar pregnancy prevent karne mein help karta hai. Lekin STI exposure ko block nahi karta. Condoms aur testing ke baare mein patient ko counseling deni chahiye.

### Ectopic pregnancy

Ectopic pregnancy mein pregnancy uterus ke bahar grow karti hai. Tube rupture ho toh internal bleeding aur shoulder-tip pain ho sakta hai. Dizziness shock ka clue hai, isliye emergency review chahiye.

### Cervical screening

Screening cervix mein early cell changes ka risk identify karti hai. Abnormal result ka matlab further assessment chahiye, lekin har abnormal result cancer nahi hota.

### Endometriosis

Endometriosis mein uterus ki lining jaisa tissue uterus ke bahar mil sakta hai. Hormonal cycle ke saath inflammation aur pain ho sakta hai. Clinical evaluation diagnosis guide karti hai.
