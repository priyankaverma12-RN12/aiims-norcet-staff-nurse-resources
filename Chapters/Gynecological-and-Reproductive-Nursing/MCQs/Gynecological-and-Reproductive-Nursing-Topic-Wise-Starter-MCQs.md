## Contraception

### Q1. A patient asks whether an intrauterine contraceptive device protects against sexually transmitted infections. What is the correct counseling?

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Topic:** Contraception

- **A.** It prevents pregnancy but does not provide reliable STI protection
- **B.** It prevents all sexually transmitted infections
- **C.** It eliminates the need for condoms in every circumstance
- **D.** It permanently cures reproductive tract infections

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: A. It prevents pregnancy but does not provide reliable STI protection**

IUCD uterus ke andar pregnancy prevent karne mein help karta hai. Lekin STI exposure ko block nahi karta. Condoms aur testing ke baare mein patient ko counseling deni chahiye.

**Baaki options kyun galat hain?**

- **B.** IUCD STI prevention device nahi hai.
- **C.** Condoms infection protection mein useful hain.
- **D.** Existing infection ka treatment alag hota hai.

</details>

## Ectopic pregnancy

### Q2. A patient with a positive pregnancy test has unilateral pelvic pain, shoulder-tip pain and dizziness. Which condition requires urgent assessment?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Topic:** Ectopic pregnancy

- **A.** Uncomplicated menstrual cramps
- **B.** Normal menopause
- **C.** Stable uterine fibroid without bleeding
- **D.** Possible ruptured ectopic pregnancy

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: D. Possible ruptured ectopic pregnancy**

Ectopic pregnancy mein pregnancy uterus ke bahar grow karti hai. Tube rupture ho toh internal bleeding aur shoulder-tip pain ho sakta hai. Dizziness shock ka clue hai, isliye emergency review chahiye.

**Baaki options kyun galat hain?**

- **A.** Positive pregnancy aur shock clues routine cramps nahi hain.
- **B.** Menopause mein ongoing pregnancy expected nahi hai.
- **C.** Stable fibroid is acute pattern ko explain nahi karta.

</details>

## Cervical screening

### Q3. A patient has an abnormal cervical screening result. What should the nurse explain?

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Topic:** Cervical screening

- **A.** No follow-up is necessary if there is no pain
- **B.** Screening results should be ignored in vaccinated individuals
- **C.** Further evaluation is needed because screening alone does not establish invasive cancer
- **D.** Every abnormal result proves advanced cancer

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: C. Further evaluation is needed because screening alone does not establish invasive cancer**

Screening cervix mein early cell changes ka risk identify karti hai. Abnormal result ka matlab further assessment chahiye, lekin har abnormal result cancer nahi hota.

**Baaki options kyun galat hain?**

- **A.** Early changes painless ho sakti hain.
- **B.** Vaccination ke baad bhi recommended screening follow karni hoti hai.
- **D.** Cancer diagnosis additional evaluation se hota hai.

</details>

## Endometriosis

### Q4. A patient reports recurrent cyclic pelvic pain and painful intercourse. Which condition may explain these symptoms?

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Topic:** Endometriosis

- **A.** Primary pulmonary hypertension
- **B.** Endometriosis
- **C.** Acute otitis media
- **D.** Uncomplicated cataract

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: B. Endometriosis**

Endometriosis mein uterus ki lining jaisa tissue uterus ke bahar mil sakta hai. Hormonal cycle ke saath inflammation aur pain ho sakta hai. Clinical evaluation diagnosis guide karti hai.

**Baaki options kyun galat hain?**

- **A.** Pulmonary hypertension breathing aur circulation ko affect karti hai.
- **C.** Ear infection pelvic pain nahi deti.
- **D.** Cataract vision ko affect karta hai.

</details>
