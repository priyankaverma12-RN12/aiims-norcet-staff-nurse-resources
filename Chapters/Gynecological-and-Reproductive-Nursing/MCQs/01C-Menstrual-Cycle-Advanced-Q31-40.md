### Q31. A woman has severe menstrual pain beginning just before bleeding, with no pelvic pathology identified. Which mechanism most often explains primary dysmenorrhea?

**Difficulty:** 🟡 Medium · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD · **Evidence:** Original practice

- **A.** Excess endometrial prostaglandins causing uterine contractions
- **B.** Increased hCG from pregnancy
- **C.** Complete absence of ovulation in every cycle
- **D.** Excess prolactin causing uterine rupture

<details><summary>Answer aur four-option explanation</summary>

**Correct: A. Excess endometrial prostaglandins causing uterine contractions**

**Kyun?** Primary dysmenorrhea mein prostaglandin-mediated uterine contractions aur ischemia pain create karte hain.

**Option-wise reasoning:**

- **A.** Primary dysmenorrhea mein prostaglandin-mediated uterine contractions aur ischemia pain create karte hain.
- **B.** Pregnancy hCG dysmenorrhea ka primary mechanism nahi.
- **C.** Ovulation absence universal cause nahi.
