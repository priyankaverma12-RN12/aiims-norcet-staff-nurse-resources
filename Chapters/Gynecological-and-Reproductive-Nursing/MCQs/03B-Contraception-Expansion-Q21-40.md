### Q21. A mother is four months postpartum, has no menstrual bleeding and is fully breastfeeding. Which additional advice is appropriate when she chooses lactational amenorrhea as contraception?

**Difficulty:** 🟡 Medium · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD · **Evidence:** Original practice · **Exam focus:** NORCET / Staff Nurse / CHO

- **A.** Continue this method indefinitely
- **B.** Switch or add contraception when any LAM criterion stops being met
- **C.** Wait until her infant is one year old
- **D.** Stop breastfeeding to improve effectiveness

<details><summary>✅ Answer aur detailed A–D Hinglish explanation</summary>

**Correct: B. Switch or add contraception when any LAM criterion stops being met**

- **A kyun galat hai:** Baby ki age badhne ke saath breastfeeding ka pattern badal sakta hai. Isliye LAM hamesha reliable nahi rehta.
- **B kyun sahi hai:** LAM mein teen conditions ek saath chahiye. Baby chhe mahine se chhota ho, periods wapas na aaye hon aur feeding poori ya lagbhag poori breast milk se ho. Koi bhi condition toot jaaye toh doosra method plan karna hai.
- **C kyun galat hai:** Ek saal tak wait karne se ovulation wapas aa sakta hai aur unwanted pregnancy ho sakti hai.
- **D kyun galat hai:** Breastfeeding kam karne se ovulation ko rokne wala hormonal effect kam ho sakta hai.

**Exam clue:** LAM = age, amenorrhea, feeding. Teenon chahiye.

**Clinical reference:** [CDC 2024 contraceptive guidance](https://www.cdc.gov/contraception/hcp/usmec/lactational-amenorrhea-method.html)

### Q26. A woman taking combined oral contraceptives misses two consecutive active pills in the second week. She had taken all preceding active pills correctly. Which instruction follows CDC 2024 guidance?

**Difficulty:** 🔴 Difficult · **Priority:** 🔴 MOST IMPORTANT · **Evidence:** Original practice · **Exam focus:** NORCET / Staff Nurse / CHO

- **A.** Stop the pack until her next period
- **B.** Take both missed pills every day for one week
- **C.** Take the most recent missed pill, continue the pack and use backup for seven consecutive active-pill days
- **D.** Take only the placebo tablets

<details><summary>✅ Answer aur detailed A–D Hinglish explanation</summary>

**Correct: C. Take the most recent missed pill, continue the pack and use backup for seven consecutive active-pill days**

- **A kyun galat hai:** Pack rokne se hormone-free gap aur lamba ho sakta hai, jisse ovulation ka chance badhta hai.
- **B kyun galat hai:** Har din extra missed tablets lena standard correction nahi hai. Isse side effects bhi badh sakte hain.
- **C kyun sahi hai:** Sabse recent missed active pill ab lein aur baaki missed pill discard karein. Regular pack continue karein aur saat lagatar active-pill days tak condom ya abstinence rakhein. Week aur recent intercourse ke hisaab se emergency contraception ka assessment bhi hota hai.
- **D kyun galat hai:** Placebo mein contraceptive hormones nahi hote. Isse missed active pills ki protection restore nahi hoti.

**Exam clue:** Two or more missed active pills: recent pill plus seven active-pill days.

**Clinical reference:** [CDC 2024 contraceptive guidance](https://www.cdc.gov/contraception/hcp/usspr/combined-hormonal-contraceptives.html)

### Q40. During a community home visit, a married woman wants to postpone her next pregnancy for several years but uses no contraceptive method. Which family planning indicator best describes this situation?

**Difficulty:** 🟡 Medium · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD · **Evidence:** Original practice · **Exam focus:** NORCET / Staff Nurse / CHO

- **A.** Unmet need for family planning spacing
- **B.** Completed permanent sterilization
- **C.** Contraceptive prevalence among users
- **D.** Total fertility rate

<details><summary>✅ Answer aur detailed A–D Hinglish explanation</summary>

**Correct: A. Unmet need for family planning spacing**

- **A kyun sahi hai:** Woman future pregnancy delay karna chahti hai lekin koi method use nahi kar rahi. Programme terminology mein yeh spacing ke liye unmet need ka example hai. Nurse ko choice-based counselling deni chahiye.
- **B kyun galat hai:** Sterilization ho chuki hoti toh permanent method use ho raha hota. Yahan method use nahi ho raha.
- **C kyun galat hai:** Contraceptive prevalence population mein method use karne ka proportion batati hai. Yeh individual unmet need ki category nahi hai.
- **D kyun galat hai:** Total fertility rate population ka average births estimate hai. Yeh ek individual ki contraceptive access need ko directly describe nahi karta.

**Exam clue:** Spacing need plus no contraceptive use = assess unmet need.
