### Q31. A woman with advanced prolapse has no leakage before pessary fitting, but leaks during coughing after the prolapse is reduced. What best explains this finding?

**Difficulty:** 🔴 Difficult · **Priority:** 🔴 MOST IMPORTANT · **Evidence:** Original practice · **Exam focus:** NORCET / Staff Nurse / CHO

- **A.** A new vesicovaginal fistula is confirmed
- **B.** Previously masked stress urinary incontinence may become apparent
- **C.** The pessary has permanently destroyed the bladder
- **D.** The finding proves urinary tract infection

<details><summary>✅ Answer aur detailed A–D Hinglish explanation</summary>

**Correct: B. Previously masked stress urinary incontinence may become apparent**

- **A kyun galat hai:** Fistula se often continuous leakage hoti hai. Sirf cough ke saath leakage se fistula confirm nahi hoti.
- **B kyun sahi hai:** Advanced prolapse urethra ko kink karke stress leakage mask kar sakta hai. Support restore hone par pressure-related leakage appear ho sakti hai. Patient ko review aur continence assessment chahiye.
- **C kyun galat hai:** New leakage ka matlab permanent bladder destruction nahi hota. Fit aur symptoms assess karo.
- **D kyun galat hai:** UTI mein burning ya urgency ho sakti hai. Cough-related leakage alone infection prove nahi karti.

**Exam clue:** Reducing prolapse can uncover stress incontinence.

**Clinical reference:** [NICE NG123](https://www.nice.org.uk/guidance/ng123/chapter/recommendations)

### Q32. Before supervised pelvic floor training for urinary incontinence, a patient bears down instead of lifting the muscles. Which action is recommended?

**Difficulty:** 🟡 Medium · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD · **Evidence:** Original practice · **Exam focus:** NORCET / Staff Nurse / CHO

- **A.** Increase resistance without checking technique
- **B.** Ask her to hold her breath
- **C.** Avoid all assessment because she can learn alone
- **D.** Arrange consent-based assessment of her ability to contract and teach correct technique

<details><summary>✅ Answer aur detailed A–D Hinglish explanation</summary>

**Correct: D. Arrange consent-based assessment of her ability to contract and teach correct technique**

- **A kyun galat hai:** Wrong movement ke saath resistance badhane se pressure aur badh sakta hai.
- **B kyun galat hai:** Breath holding correct muscle activation ka substitute nahi.
- **C kyun galat hai:** NICE training se pehle pelvic floor contraction assess karne ko recommend karta hai.
- **D kyun sahi hai:** Trained clinician consent aur privacy ke saath muscle contraction check kar sakta hai. Patient ko squeeze aur lift sikhaya jaata hai, taaki downward pressure ke bajay correct muscles activate hon.

**Exam clue:** Verify contraction before prescribing a training programme.

**Clinical reference:** [NICE NG123](https://www.nice.org.uk/guidance/ng123/chapter/recommendations)

### Q33. A woman with mixed urinary incontinence has leakage on exertion and with urgency. Which minimum supervised pelvic floor training duration is recommended by NICE?

**Difficulty:** 🟡 Medium · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD · **Evidence:** Original practice · **Exam focus:** NORCET / Staff Nurse / CHO

- **A.** Three months
- **B.** Three days
- **C.** One week
- **D.** Twelve months before any review

<details><summary>✅ Answer aur detailed A–D Hinglish explanation</summary>

**Correct: A. Three months**

- **A kyun sahi hai:** NICE stress ya mixed urinary incontinence mein minimum 3 months supervised training recommend karta hai. Urgency ke liye bladder training bhi relevant hai.
- **B kyun galat hai:** Teen din strength aur coordination improve karne ke liye sufficient programme nahi hai.
- **C kyun galat hai:** Ek week guideline ke minimum duration se kam hai.
- **D kyun galat hai:** Review ko ek saal tak delay karna necessary nahi. Technique aur progress ongoing assess karte hain.

**Exam clue:** Stress/mixed UI: minimum 3 months PFMT, distinct from prolapse-specific 16 weeks.

**Clinical reference:** [NICE NG123](https://www.nice.org.uk/guidance/ng123/chapter/recommendations)

**Exam clue:** Bladder diary: at least 3 days.

### Q35. A woman wants to preserve her uterus and asks which abdominal or laparoscopic operation attaches the uterus toward the sacrum using mesh. Which procedure fits?

**Difficulty:** 🟡 Medium · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD · **Evidence:** Original practice · **Exam focus:** NORCET / Staff Nurse / CHO

- **A.** Anterior colporrhaphy
- **B.** Sacrohysteropexy
- **C.** Total vaginal hysterectomy
- **D.** Colpocleisis

<details><summary>✅ Answer aur detailed A–D Hinglish explanation</summary>

**Correct: B. Sacrohysteropexy**

- **A kyun galat hai:** Anterior colporrhaphy anterior vaginal wall support repair hai.
- **B kyun sahi hai:** Sacrohysteropexy uterus ko retain karke sacral support provide karta hai. Mesh risks, recurrence aur pregnancy plans specialist se discuss hote hain.
- **C kyun galat hai:** Hysterectomy uterus remove karti hai, preservation nahi.
- **D kyun galat hai:** Colpocleisis vaginal canal close karne wala option hai, sacral suspension nahi.

**Exam clue:** Sacrohysteropexy preserves uterus, sacrocolpopexy supports vault.

**Clinical reference:** [RCOG pelvic organ prolapse](https://www.rcog.org.uk/for-the-public/browse-our-patient-information/pelvic-organ-prolapse/)

### Q36. A patient develops persistent vaginal discharge, spotting and painful intercourse months after mesh-based prolapse repair. Which complication deserves targeted specialist assessment?

**Difficulty:** 🔴 Difficult · **Priority:** 🔴 MOST IMPORTANT · **Evidence:** Original practice · **Exam focus:** NORCET / Staff Nurse / CHO

- **A.** Expected permanent postoperative symptoms needing no assessment
- **B.** Normal menopause in every case
- **C.** New ovarian torsion established by discharge
- **D.** Vaginal mesh exposure or erosion

<details><summary>✅ Answer aur detailed A–D Hinglish explanation</summary>

**Correct: D. Vaginal mesh exposure or erosion**

- **A kyun galat hai:** Persistent bleeding aur pain ko normal bolkar ignore nahi karna chahiye.
- **B kyun galat hai:** Menopausal tissue changes possible hain, lekin mesh history relevant hai.
- **C kyun galat hai:** Ovarian torsion generally acute pain se present ho sakti hai. Discharge alone diagnosis nahi karta.
- **D kyun sahi hai:** Mesh vaginal tissue ke through expose ho sakta hai, jisse pain, bleeding aur discharge ho sakte hain. Nurse specialist review arrange kare, other causes bhi assess hon.

**Exam clue:** New bleeding and dyspareunia after mesh surgery require review.

### Q39. After uncomplicated prolapse surgery, a patient asks whether symptoms can recur despite an initially successful repair. Which response is accurate?

**Difficulty:** 🟡 Medium · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD · **Evidence:** Original practice · **Exam focus:** NORCET / Staff Nurse / CHO

- **A.** Recurrence is impossible once sutures heal
- **B.** A prolapse may return in the repaired or another compartment
- **C.** Recurrence always proves surgical negligence
- **D.** Follow-up is unnecessary if early symptoms improve

<details><summary>✅ Answer aur detailed A–D Hinglish explanation</summary>

**Correct: B. A prolapse may return in the repaired or another compartment**

- **A kyun galat hai:** Support tissues time ke saath change ho sakte hain. Initial success permanent cure guarantee nahi.
- **B kyun sahi hai:** RCOG explain karta hai ki prolapse same ya another part mein return ho sakta hai. Patient ko new symptoms report karne aur follow-up ki advice do.
- **C kyun galat hai:** Recurrence multiple patient aur tissue factors se related hai. Isse negligence automatically prove nahi hoti.
- **D kyun galat hai:** Review se recovery aur new complications assess kar sakte hain.

**Exam clue:** Symptom improvement is not a guarantee against recurrence.

**Clinical reference:** [RCOG pelvic organ prolapse](https://www.rcog.org.uk/for-the-public/browse-our-patient-information/pelvic-organ-prolapse/)

### Q40. After sacrospinous fixation, a woman has mild improving buttock discomfort but no fever, weakness or worsening pain. Which counseling is appropriate?

**Difficulty:** 🟡 Medium · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD · **Evidence:** Original practice · **Exam focus:** NORCET / Staff Nurse / CHO

- **A.** Diagnose pulmonary embolism from this symptom alone
- **B.** Recommend strenuous lifting to stretch the repair
- **C.** Assure her that severe progressive pain should never be reported
- **D.** Explain that transient buttock pain can occur and arrange review if it worsens or persists

<details><summary>✅ Answer aur detailed A–D Hinglish explanation</summary>

**Correct: D. Explain that transient buttock pain can occur and arrange review if it worsens or persists**

- **A kyun galat hai:** Pulmonary embolism usually respiratory ya circulatory symptoms se concern raise karta hai. Is isolated finding se diagnose nahi hota.
- **B kyun galat hai:** Heavy lifting healing repair par stress daal sakta hai.
- **C kyun galat hai:** Severe progressive pain, weakness ya other concerning symptoms prompt assessment require karte hain.
- **D kyun sahi hai:** Sacrospinous ligament ke nearby structures ki wajah se buttock discomfort ho sakta hai. RCOG ke hisaab se ye often settle hota hai. Pain trend aur red flags assess karo.

**Exam clue:** Sacrospinous fixation can cause transient buttock pain.
