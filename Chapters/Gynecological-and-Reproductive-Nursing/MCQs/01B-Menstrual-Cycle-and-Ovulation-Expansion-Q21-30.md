### Q21. A 23-year-old has a positive urinary LH surge test. Which counseling best describes the likely timing of ovulation?

**Difficulty:** 🟡 Medium · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD · **Evidence:** Original practice, historical exam verification pending

- **A.** Ovulation is likely within approximately 24–36 hours
- **B.** Ovulation was definitely completed one week earlier
- **C.** Menstruation must start within six hours
- **D.** Pregnancy is already confirmed

<details><summary>Answer aur chaaron options ki explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: A. Ovulation is likely within approximately 24–36 hours**

**Har option samjho:**

- **A. Ovulation is likely within approximately 24–36 hours:** A urinary LH surge generally precedes ovulation by about one to one and a half days, but it does not guarantee follicle rupture.
- **B. Ovulation was definitely completed one week earlier:** One week earlier ka claim LH surge ke time se match nahi karta.
- **C. Menstruation must start within six hours:** LH surge menstruation ka immediate signal nahi hai.
- **D. Pregnancy is already confirmed:** LH test pregnancy diagnose nahi karta, hCG pregnancy testing mein use hota hai.

**Nursing exam clue:** Ovulation ka timing, hormone action aur cycle phase ko clinical history ke saath interpret karo. Ek single observation ko definitive diagnosis mat samjho.
