### 🔵 RPSC Nursing Tutor — 2011 — reported adaptation

**Difficulty:** Medium · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD · **Evidence:** Third-party retrieved historical report

Which ovarian change best explains the decline in circulating estrogen during natural menopause?

- **A.** Increased recruitment of functioning ovarian follicles
- **B.** Loss of ovarian follicular function as the follicle pool declines
- **C.** Persistent hyperprolactinemia in every menopausal woman
- **D.** Increased production of progesterone by a persistent corpus luteum

<details><summary>✅ Answer aur detailed Hinglish explanation</summary>

**Correct: B. Loss of ovarian follicular function as the follicle pool declines**

- **A kyun galat hai:** Natural menopause ke dauran functional ovarian follicles kam hote jaate hain. Increased follicle recruitment se regular ovarian hormone production continue hone ki expectation hoti, menopause ka mechanism explain nahi hota.
- **B kyun sahi hai:** Follicles ovary ke andar egg aur hormone-producing cells wali structures hain. Age ke saath functional follicle pool kam hota hai aur ovarian response change hota hai. Isse estrogen production generally decline karti hai aur regular ovulation aur periods eventually stop ho jaate hain. Historical report ka answer low estrogen tha, lekin follicular function ka loss underlying ovarian change hai.
- **C kyun galat hai:** High prolactin se amenorrhea ho sakti hai, par har natural menopausal patient mein hyperprolactinemia nahi hoti. Yeh ek separate differential diagnosis hai.
- **D kyun galat hai:** Corpus luteum ovulation ke baad progesterone banata hai. Natural menopause mein regular ovulation stop hota hai, isliye persistent corpus luteum iska expected cause nahi hai.

**Exam clue:** Ovarian follicular function declines, estrogen feedback decreases and gonadotropins generally rise.

**Historical source:** [Testbook — RPSC Nursing Tutor 2011](https://testbook.com/question-answer/the-main-cause-of-menopause-is--67cc675f9bd81be2cb546a61). Reported answer: low estrogen. This adaptation clarifies the underlying ovarian mechanism.

**Clinical source:** [WHO menopause fact sheet](https://www.who.int/news-room/fact-sheets/detail/menopause/).

**YouTube MCQ practice:** [Menopause Gynecology MCQs — indexed topic-level lead](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Gvp062WOhGo). Specific item, creator and playback pending.

### 🔵 RRB Staff Nurse — 20 July 2019, Shift I / ESIC Staff Nurse — 19 March 2016, Shift I — reported repeated concept

**Difficulty:** Medium · **Priority:** 🔴 MOST IMPORTANT · **Evidence:** Two independently retrieved third-party historical reports, one practice adaptation to prevent duplication

During preparation for an indicated transcervical gynecological procedure, which instrument is intended to hold a cervical lip so that a trained operator can stabilize the cervix?

- **A.** Sims vaginal speculum
- **B.** Hegar dilator
- **C.** Vulsellum forceps
- **D.** Uterine sound

<details><summary>✅ Answer aur detailed Hinglish explanation</summary>

**Correct: C. Vulsellum forceps**

- **A kyun galat hai:** Sims speculum vaginal wall ko retract karta hai taaki cervix visualize ho sake. Iska main purpose cervical lip ko toothed forceps ki tarah hold karna nahi hai.
- **B kyun galat hai:** Hegar dilator cervical canal ko gradually widen karne mein use hota hai. Cervical lip grasp karna iska function nahi hai.
- **C kyun sahi hai:** Vulsellum toothed holding instrument hai jo selected gynecological procedures mein cervical tissue ko stabilize karne ke liye use kiya ja sakta hai. Trained provider patient ki indication, pain relief aur safety ke hisaab se instrument use karta hai. Nurse ka role correct instrument identification, asepsis aur assistance hai.
- **D kyun galat hai:** Uterine sound indicated procedures mein uterine direction aur depth assess karne ke liye use ho sakta hai. Yeh cervical tissue grasp karne ka instrument nahi hai.

**Exam clue:** Vulsellum holds cervical tissue. Speculum exposes, dilator opens and sound measures.

**Historical sources:** [RRB Staff Nurse 20 July 2019 Shift I](https://testbook.com/question-answer/vulsellum-forcep-is-used-for-gripping-the_______--619e13a883cf4ef0fc1b6fa9) · [ESIC Staff Nurse 19 March 2016 Shift I](https://testbook.com/question-answer/vulsellum-forceps-is-used-to-grasp--6241f89e1b7ec02a439e44a4). Both reports give cervix as the answer. Original booklet and official keys pending.

**Clinical source:** [India IUCD reference manual, WHO-hosted](https://platform.who.int/docs/default-source/mca-documents/policy-documents/operational-guidance/ind-rh-32-02-operational-guidance-2014-eng-iucd-manual.pdf), indexed extract indicates cervical traction with vulsellum. Full PDF page not inspected.

**YouTube MCQ practice:** Specific vulsellum question-solving video not verified. Do not substitute obstetric delivery forceps clips.
