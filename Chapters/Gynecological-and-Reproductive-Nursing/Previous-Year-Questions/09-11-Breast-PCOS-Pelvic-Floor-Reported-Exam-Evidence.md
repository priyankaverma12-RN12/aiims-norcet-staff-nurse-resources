### 🔵 AIIMS NORCET — 20 November 2021, Shift II — reported memory-based adaptation

**Difficulty:** 🟡 Medium · **Priority:** 🔴 MOST IMPORTANT · **Evidence:** Third-party retrieved reported question

A 48-year-old woman has a persistent suspicious palpable breast mass. Which combination represents the three components of a diagnostic triple assessment when all three are indicated?

- **A.** Clinical assessment, age-appropriate breast imaging and tissue sampling
- **B.** Clinical assessment, complete blood count and serum electrolytes
- **C.** Mammography alone repeated three times
- **D.** Breast self-awareness, routine vaccination and urine examination

<details><summary>✅ Answer aur detailed A–D Hinglish explanation</summary>

**Correct: A. Clinical assessment, age-appropriate breast imaging and tissue sampling**

- **A kyun sahi hai:** Triple assessment mein history aur physical examination, appropriate imaging aur suspicious finding ki tissue assessment ko combine karte hain. Is age group mein diagnostic mammography aur targeted ultrasound useful ho sakte hain. Biopsy se pathology confirm hoti hai. Har benign finding ko biopsy ki zarurat ho, aisa rule nahi hai.
- **B kyun galat hai:** Blood count aur electrolytes general health ya treatment preparation ke liye useful ho sakte hain. Lekin ye breast lesion ki imaging aur histology replace nahi karte.
- **C kyun galat hai:** Mammography imaging component hai. Ek hi test ko repeat karna clinical examination aur tissue sampling ko replace nahi karta.
- **D kyun galat hai:** Breast awareness changes notice karne mein help karti hai, lekin vaccination aur urine examination suspicious breast lesion ki triple assessment ke components nahi hain.

**Exam clue:** Clinical evaluation + appropriate imaging + tissue sampling when indicated. Historical report gives mammography in the sequence. Current imaging choice is age- and presentation-dependent.

**Historical source:** [AIIMS NORCET 20 November 2021 Shift II — reported question](https://testbook.com/question-answer/correct-sequence-for-assessment-of-breasts-cancer--65f84643c939d888aa785a54). Third-party reported key, not independently authenticated official answer.

**Clinical sources:** [NCI diagnosis](https://www.cancer.gov/types/breast/diagnosis) · [Breast Cancer Now triple assessment](https://breastcancernow.org/about-breast-cancer/screening-tests-and-scans/what-happens-at-a-breast-clinic-appointment).

### 🔵 DSSSB Nursing Officer — 28 August 2019, Shift 3 — reported adaptation

**Difficulty:** 🟡 Medium · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD · **Evidence:** Third-party retrieved historical report

During bladder filling, a woman experiences sudden involuntary bladder contractions associated with urgency and leakage. Which mechanism is most consistent with this observation?

- **A.** Continuous urinary leakage through a fistula alone
- **B.** Complete absence of bladder muscle activity
- **C.** Detrusor overactivity
- **D.** Isolated stress leakage due to a rise in abdominal pressure

<details><summary>✅ Answer aur detailed A–D Hinglish explanation</summary>

**Correct: C. Detrusor overactivity**

- **A kyun galat hai:** Urinary fistula abnormal communication hoti hai jisse continuous leakage ho sakti hai. Yahan bladder filling ke time involuntary muscle contraction specifically describe ki gayi hai.
- **B kyun galat hai:** Detrusor bladder wall ki smooth muscle hai. Muscle activity absent ho toh emptying problem aur retention ho sakti hai, involuntary contraction nahi.
- **C kyun sahi hai:** Detrusor normally urine store karte waqt relaxed rehti hai aur voiding par contract karti hai. Filling phase mein involuntary contractions urgency aur leakage se associated ho sakti hain. Detrusor overactivity urodynamic finding hai, jabki urgency incontinence symptom-based diagnosis hai. Har urgency patient mein demonstrable detrusor overactivity hona zaroori nahi.
- **D kyun galat hai:** Stress incontinence coughing, lifting ya exertion se associated hoti hai. Is question ka key observation bladder filling ke time involuntary contraction hai.

**Exam clue:** Detrusor overactivity can underlie urgency leakage. Symptom and urodynamic observation are not interchangeable.

**Historical source:** [DSSSB Nursing Officer 28 August 2019 Shift 3 — reported item](https://testbook.com/question-answer/what-is-the-common-cause-for-urge-incontinence--61641fcb160baf11a2b8d7b6). Third-party page gives detrusor overactivity as the key. Official booklet and key not checked.

**Clinical sources:** [NIDDK bladder control](https://www.niddk.nih.gov/health-information/urologic-diseases/bladder-control-problems/definition-facts) · [NIDDK urodynamic testing](https://www.niddk.nih.gov/health-information/diagnostic-tests/urodynamic-testing).
