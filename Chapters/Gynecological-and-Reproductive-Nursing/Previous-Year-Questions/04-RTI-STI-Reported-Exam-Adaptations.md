### 🔵 ESIC Staff Nurse — 26 February 2019, Shift II — reported question adaptation

**Difficulty:** Medium · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD · **Evidence:** Third-party retrieved historical report, not authenticated verbatim

A nurse receives a fresh vaginal sample from a patient with suspected trichomoniasis. Which low-cost bedside microscopy approach can show the motile organism directly?

- **A.** Examine a freshly prepared saline wet mount.
- **B.** Measure serum sodium and potassium.
- **C.** Examine a stained peripheral blood smear for malaria.
- **D.** Perform a routine cervical cytology screening test.

<details><summary>✅ Answer aur detailed Hinglish explanation</summary>

**Correct: A. Examine a freshly prepared saline wet mount.**

- **A kyun sahi hai:** Trichomonas vaginalis ek motile protozoan parasite hai. Fresh vaginal sample ko saline ke saath microscope mein dekhne par moving organisms dikh sakte hain. Wet mount low-cost method hai, lekin sensitivity limited hoti hai. Negative result infection exclude nahi karta. NAAT available ho toh zyada sensitive test useful hai.
- **B kyun galat hai:** Sodium aur potassium electrolyte balance assess karte hain. Yeh test parasite ko directly identify nahi karta.
- **C kyun galat hai:** Malaria smear blood mein Plasmodium parasite detect karta hai. Vaginal trichomoniasis ke liye yeh appropriate sample ya test nahi hai.
- **D kyun galat hai:** Pap smear cervical cell changes screen karta hai. Trichomonas kabhi incidental dikh sakta hai, lekin Pap smear diagnostic test nahi hai. Suspected infection ke liye sensitive confirmatory test chahiye.

**Exam clue:** Traditional exam answer wet mount ho sakta hai. Modern practice mein negative wet mount ke baad sensitive testing important hai.

**Historical source:** [Testbook — ESIC 26 Feb 2019 Shift II](https://testbook.com/question-answer/the-test-used-for-the-diagnosis-of-trichomoniasis--6232242dbd5130cee4b9ca44). Reported question tests a wet mount for diagnosis. Official key and question number unavailable.

**Clinical reference:** [CDC trichomoniasis guidance](https://www.cdc.gov/std/treatment-guidelines/trichomoniasis.htm).

### 🔵 AIIMS Jodhpur Nursing Officer — 10 December 2019 — memory-based adaptation

**Difficulty:** Medium · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD · **Evidence:** Retrieved memory-based report, not official key

During a community counseling visit, a patient asks how to reduce irritation around the vulva while receiving care for recurrent vaginal symptoms. Which statement indicates appropriate understanding?

- **A.** I should wash inside the vagina daily with scented products.
- **B.** I can choose breathable cotton underwear, avoid irritating products and seek assessment for persistent symptoms.
- **C.** I should use leftover antibiotics whenever itching occurs.
- **D.** I can stop prescribed treatment whenever discomfort improves.

<details><summary>✅ Answer aur detailed Hinglish explanation</summary>

**Correct: B. I can choose breathable cotton underwear, avoid irritating products and seek assessment for persistent symptoms.**

- **A kyun galat hai:** Vagina ka natural environment protective bacteria maintain karte hain. Internal washing aur scented products irritation kar sakte hain aur normal flora ko disturb kar sakte hain. Douching ko treatment nahi samajhna chahiye.
- **B kyun sahi hai:** Breathable clothing aur gentle external hygiene se moisture aur irritation manage karne mein help mil sakti hai. Ye infection cure ya STI prevention ki guarantee nahi hai. Recurrent symptoms mein provider assessment zaroori hai, kyunki yeast, BV aur STI ke treatments alag hote hain.
- **C kyun galat hai:** Har itching bacterial infection nahi hoti. Unnecessary antibiotics normal bacteria ko disturb kar sakti hain aur correct diagnosis delay kar sakti hain.
- **D kyun galat hai:** Symptoms kam hone ka matlab infection completely clear hona zaroori nahi hai. Prescribed treatment ko clinician ki instructions ke hisaab se complete karna chahiye.

**Exam clue:** Hygiene counseling aur actual infection treatment alag cheezein hain. Condoms aur indicated STI testing ki bhi apni importance hai.

**Historical source:** [Testbook — AIIMS Jodhpur 10 Dec 2019 memory-based](https://testbook.com/question-answer/which-statement-by-the-client-indicates-an-underst--625ef00ba8cbd66c4748e065/amp). Retrieved report tests breathable underwear and RTI prevention counseling. Original question number and official key unavailable.

**Clinical reference:** [CDC vaginal discharge assessment](https://www.cdc.gov/std/treatment-guidelines/vaginal-discharge.htm) and [CDC VVC guidance](https://www.cdc.gov/std/treatment-guidelines/candidiasis.htm).
