### 🔵 UPSC ESIC Nursing Officer — 7 July 2024 — reported adaptation

**Difficulty:** Medium · **Priority:** 🔴 MOST IMPORTANT · **Evidence:** Third-party retrieved historical report

A nurse encounters the classical gynecology term procidentia in an older examination paper. Which description best represents this term?

- **A.** Isolated anterior vaginal wall descent
- **B.** Cervical ectropion without descent
- **C.** Complete uterine prolapse outside the vaginal opening
- **D.** Isolated posterior vaginal wall descent

<details><summary>✅ Answer aur detailed A–D Hinglish explanation</summary>

**Correct: C. Complete uterine prolapse outside the vaginal opening**

- **A kyun galat hai:** Anterior wall descent commonly bladder support defect se related hai. Uterus ka complete external descent necessary nahi hai.
- **B kyun galat hai:** Ectropion cervical epithelium ka finding hai. Ismein complete uterine descent define nahi hota.
- **C kyun sahi hai:** Procidentia classical terminology mein complete uterine prolapse ko describe karta hai. Uterus vaginal opening ke outside descend karta hai. Contemporary examination mein POP-Q anatomical measurements use kiye ja sakte hain. Classical degree labels aur POP-Q stages ko automatically interchangeable mat samjho.
- **D kyun galat hai:** Posterior wall descent commonly rectal support defect se related hai. Isolated posterior bulge complete uterine prolapse nahi hai.

**Exam clue:** Procidentia means complete uterine prolapse in classical terminology. POP-Q uses measured anatomical landmarks.
