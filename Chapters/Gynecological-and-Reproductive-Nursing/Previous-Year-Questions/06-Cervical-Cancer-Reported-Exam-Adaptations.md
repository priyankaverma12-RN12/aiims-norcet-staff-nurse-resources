### 🔵 HPSSC Hamirpur Staff Nurse — 5 August 2021 — reported adaptation

**Difficulty:** Medium · **Priority:** 🔴 MOST IMPORTANT · **Evidence:** Third-party reported historical question, official key pending

A patient is receiving temporary intracavitary brachytherapy for cervical cancer. While a radioactive source remains in place, which action best applies the radiation safety principle of time?

- **A.** Limit unnecessary visitor exposure according to the radiation safety team's instructions.
- **B.** Allow unlimited visits because all radiation remains inside the tumor.
- **C.** Withhold all essential nursing care until the source is removed.
- **D.** Use the same visitor restriction permanently after every type of radiotherapy.

<details><summary>✅ Answer aur detailed Hinglish explanation</summary>

**Correct: A. Limit unnecessary visitor exposure according to the radiation safety team's instructions.**

- **A kyun sahi hai:** Radiation exposure ko kam karne ke basic principles time, distance aur shielding hain. Temporary radioactive source present ho toh unnecessary exposure time limit karte hain. Staff essential care continue kare aur facility ki source-specific instructions follow kare. Historical reported answer mein ten-minute visit restriction diya gaya tha. Yeh sab modern brachytherapy techniques ke liye universal fixed limit nahi hai.
- **B kyun galat hai:** Source present hone par exposure risk source aur technique par depend karta hai. Unlimited visitation automatically safe assume nahi karni chahiye.
- **C kyun galat hai:** Essential nursing care rokna patient ko harm kar sakta hai. Radiation safety precautions ke saath necessary care continue hoti hai.
- **D kyun galat hai:** External beam treatment aur temporary internal source ki precautions different hoti hain. Temporary source removal ke baad patient mein us source ki radiation nahi rehti.

**Exam clue:** Time, distance and shielding. Specific restrictions depend on the source and local protocol.

**Historical report:** [Testbook — HPSSC Hamirpur 5 August 2021](https://testbook.com/question-answer/which-of-the-following-is-an-appropriate-nursing-a--697b13df63fd3d90d63923dd/amp). Reported key limits visitor time. Official booklet and key pending.

**Clinical source:** [NCI brachytherapy](https://www.cancer.gov/about-cancer/treatment/types/radiation-therapy/brachytherapy).

### 🔵 Haryana HSSC Staff Nurse — 2019 — reported adaptation

**Difficulty:** Medium · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD · **Evidence:** Third-party report, official paper/key not independently authenticated

A community health nurse organizes cervical cytology screening for asymptomatic eligible women to detect early cervical cell abnormalities. Which level of prevention does this represent?

- **A.** Primary prevention through immunization.
- **B.** Secondary prevention through early detection.
- **C.** Tertiary prevention through rehabilitation.
- **D.** Primordial prevention through broad social policy alone.

<details><summary>✅ Answer aur detailed Hinglish explanation</summary>

**Correct: B. Secondary prevention through early detection.**

- **A kyun galat hai:** Primary prevention disease develop hone se pehle risk reduce karti hai. HPV vaccination iska example hai. Cytology screening already present abnormal changes ko detect karti hai.
- **B kyun sahi hai:** Secondary prevention mein disease ya precancer ko early stage par detect karke timely management arrange karte hain. Pap cytology asymptomatic women mein abnormal cells identify kar sakti hai. Abnormal result ke baad appropriate follow-up zaroori hai.
- **C kyun galat hai:** Tertiary prevention established disease ke complications aur disability kam karne par focus karti hai. Rehabilitation iska example ho sakta hai.
- **D kyun galat hai:** Primordial prevention population mein risk factors develop hone se rokne wale broad measures par focus karti hai. Individual screening is category ka main example nahi hai.

**Exam clue:** Vaccination = primary prevention. Screening = secondary prevention.

**Historical report:** [Testbook — HSSC Staff Nurse 2019](https://testbook.com/question-answer/pap-smear-examination-procedure-to-detect-carcinom--698d74ac1e7b6b9a34ef83ed). Reported key: secondary prevention. Official authentication pending.
