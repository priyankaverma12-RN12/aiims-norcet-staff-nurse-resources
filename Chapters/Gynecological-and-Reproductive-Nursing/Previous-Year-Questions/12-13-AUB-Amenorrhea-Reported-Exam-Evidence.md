### 🔵 AIIMS NORCET 9 Mains — 27 September 2025 — reported memory-based adaptation

**Difficulty:** 🟡 Medium · **Priority:** 🔴 MOST IMPORTANT · **Evidence:** Retrieved third-party exam report

A patient has previously menstruated normally but develops dense intrauterine adhesions after a procedure. Which change in menstrual flow is most consistent with loss of functional endometrial surface?

- **A.** Increased menstrual volume in every cycle
- **B.** Scanty or absent menstrual flow
- **C.** Predictably increased menstrual frequency
- **D.** Normal menstrual flow guaranteed by the presence of adhesions

<details><summary>✅ Answer aur detailed A–D Hinglish explanation</summary>

**Correct: B. Scanty or absent menstrual flow**

- **A kyun galat hai:** Jab uterine cavity mein scar tissue ban jaata hai, toh functional lining ka area kam ho sakta hai. Is wajah se har cycle mein blood loss increase hona typical expectation nahi hai. Heavy bleeding ho toh other causes bhi assess karne hain.
- **B kyun sahi hai:** Asherman syndrome mein uterine cavity ke andar adhesions yani scar tissue ke bands ban sakte hain. Normal period ke time functional endometrial lining shed hoti hai. Jab lining ka area damage ho ya cavity partly obstruct ho, toh periods bahut light ho sakte hain ya band bhi ho sakte hain. Historical report ne scanty flow ke liye hypomenorrhagia term use kiya hai. Current clinical evaluation mein uterine instrumentation history aur anatomical assessment important hain.
- **C kyun galat hai:** Frequent menstruation cycle start dates ke beech short interval ko describe karti hai. Uterine cavity scarring ka typical effect flow kam hona ya absent hona hai, na ki predictable short intervals.
- **D kyun galat hai:** Adhesions normal cavity function disturb kar sakti hain. Normal flow guarantee nahi hota. Fertility difficulties ya cyclic pain jaise associated symptoms bhi ho sakte hain.

**Exam clue:** Asherman syndrome mein intrauterine scarring ki wajah se menstrual flow kam ho sakta hai. History aur diagnostic hysteroscopy jaise indicated investigations diagnosis guide karte hain.
