### Q1. During a menstrual-health teaching session, a student describes a cluster of recurrent physical and emotional complaints linked to the days preceding a period. The complaints subside after menstruation begins. What is the most appropriate syndrome name in this examination context?

**A.** Primary amenorrhea

**B.** Premenstrual syndrome

**C.** Primary dysmenorrhea

**D.** Abnormal uterine bleeding

<details>
<summary>Show answer and Hinglish explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: B. Premenstrual syndrome**

**A kyun correct nahi hai:** Primary amenorrhea mein expected age tak first menstruation start nahi hoti. Yahan established menstrual cycles ke saath recurrent mood aur physical complaints describe ki gayi hain.

**B kyun correct hai:** PMS mein physical aur emotional symptoms cyclically period se pehle recur karte hain aur period start hone ke baad improve hote hain. Real clinical diagnosis mein daily prospective records aur functional impact assess karna chahiye. Har mild premenstrual symptom clinically significant PMS nahi hota.

**C kyun correct nahi hai:** Primary dysmenorrhea mainly menstruation ke around prostaglandin-mediated painful cramps se related hai. Question ka central finding recurrent premenstrual symptom cluster hai.

**D kyun correct nahi hai:** Abnormal uterine bleeding mein bleeding frequency, regularity, duration ya amount abnormal hota hai. Yahan abnormal bleeding nahi, cycle-related physical aur emotional symptoms central finding hain.
