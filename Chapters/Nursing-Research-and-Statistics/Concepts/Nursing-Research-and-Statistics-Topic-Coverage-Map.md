This is a curriculum-based checklist, not full theory notes. No learning completion is recorded.

- Research process and designs
- variables and hypothesis
- sampling
- data collection and tools
- validity and reliability
- descriptive and inferential statistics
- ethics
- evidence-based practice

## Initial study explanations

### Sampling

Researcher pehle random starting point choose karta hai aur phir fixed gap se records select karta hai. Ise systematic sampling kehte hain. List mein hidden pattern ho toh bias possible hai.

### Statistics

Median ordered numbers ka beech wala value hai. Yahan 5 values hain aur third value 4 hai. Mean 25/5 yani 5 hota hai.

### Study design

Cohort study mein groups ko exposure ke basis par observe kiya jaata hai aur baad mein outcome compare hota hai. Isse incidence aur association study ho sakte hain.

### Research ethics

Research participation voluntary hoti hai. Consent sign karne ke baad bhi participant withdraw kar sakta hai. Researcher procedure samjhata hai aur routine care ko punishment ke roop mein nahi rokta.
