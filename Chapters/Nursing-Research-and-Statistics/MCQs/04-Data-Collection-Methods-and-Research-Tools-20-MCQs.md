### Q1. A researcher wants to learn why patients choose not to use an inhaler as prescribed, without restricting them to predetermined reasons. Which question format is most suitable?

**Difficulty:** 🟡 Medium · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD · Original exam-style practice

- **A.** Open-ended question inviting a response in the patient's own words
- **B.** A forced yes-or-no item
- **C.** A checklist of only the investigator's suspected barriers
- **D.** A numerical satisfaction score without follow-up

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: A. Open-ended question inviting a response in the patient's own words**

**A.** Open-ended question patient ko apne experience aur barriers khud explain karne deti hai. Unexpected reasons bhi mil sakte hain jo predefined options mein nahi the.

**Baaki options kyun galat hain?**

- **B.** Yes-no item quick coding mein useful hai, lekin patient ko reason explain karne ki space nahi deta. Is objective mein detailed explanation important hai.
- **C.** Checklist known barriers count kar sakti hai. Lekin unlisted reasons miss ho sakte hain, isliye exploratory aim ke liye alone less suitable hai.
- **D.** Score satisfaction ka level summarize kar sakta hai. Lekin patient inhaler kyun use nahi karta, iska explanation score alone se nahi milta.

### Q2. A multicentre survey asks every respondent the same questions in the same order using predefined answer categories. What is the main advantage of this structure?

**Difficulty:** 🟡 Medium · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD · Original exam-style practice

- **A.** It guarantees that every respondent interprets every item identically
- **B.** It eliminates all nonresponse
- **C.** Responses are more directly comparable across respondents and sites
- **D.** It provides unrestricted narratives for every question

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: C. Responses are more directly comparable across respondents and sites**

**C.** Same wording aur order se administration differences kam ho sakte hain. Predefined categories coding aur group comparison ko easier banati hain.

**Baaki options kyun galat hain?**

- **A.** Same wording ke bawajood language aur context se meaning differ ho sakta hai. Pilot testing aur clear wording phir bhi important hain.
- **B.** Structured survey participation guarantee nahi karta. Some participants time, access ya privacy concerns ki wajah se respond nahi kar sakte.
- **D.** Predefined response categories narrative freedom limit karti hain. Open-ended follow-up add karne se detailed experiences collect kiye ja sakte hain.

### Q4. A questionnaire asks, 'How satisfied are you with the nurse's communication and the cleanliness of the ward?' What is the principal wording defect?

**Difficulty:** 🔴 Difficult · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD · Original exam-style practice

- **A.** It is necessarily an invalid nominal measurement because it uses words
- **B.** It is a randomization error
- **C.** It is an example of a neutral probe
- **D.** It is a double-barrelled item asking about two distinct issues

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**D.** Patient communication se satisfied aur cleanliness se dissatisfied ho sakta hai. Ek combined answer dono experiences ko separately identify nahi kar payega, isliye items split karne chahiye.

**Baaki options kyun galat hain?**

- **A.** Words meaningful ordered categories bhi represent kar sakte hain. Main problem do separate concepts ko ek response mein combine karna hai.
- **B.** Randomization group assignment ya selection se related hai. Item wording ka problem measurement stage mein hai.
- **C.** Neutral probe participant ke response ko bina direction diye clarify karta hai. Yeh item two topics ko combine kar raha hai, probe ka function nahi kar raha.

### Q8. A trained observer notices that staff improve their usual hand-hygiene behavior when they realize they are being watched. Which threat should the researcher acknowledge?

**Difficulty:** 🔴 Difficult · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD · Original exam-style practice

- **A.** Selection of the sample by stratified randomization
- **B.** A guaranteed increase in instrument validity
- **C.** Observer-induced reactivity, often called a Hawthorne effect
- **D.** A statistical calculation error in the sample size

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: C. Observer-induced reactivity, often called a Hawthorne effect**

**C.** Jab participant ko observation ka pata hota hai, toh behavior usual routine se change ho sakta hai. Findings interpret karte waqt is reactivity ko consider karna chahiye.

**Baaki options kyun galat hain?**

- **A.** Stratification selection ya assignment ka process hai. Yahan already observed staff ka behavior observation ki wajah se change ho raha hai.
- **B.** Behavior change actual usual practice ke measurement ko distort kar sakta hai. Isse validity automatically improve nahi hoti.
- **D.** Sample-size calculation alag planning process hai. Observation awareness se behavior change hona measurement setting ka issue hai.

**Baaki options kyun galat hain?**

- **A.** Random sampling variation sample estimates mein chance differences la sakti hai. Yahan answers ka systematic favorable direction social context se related ho sakta hai.
- **C.** Carryover earlier experimental treatment ka residual effect hota hai. Questionnaire administration mein crossover sequence describe nahi hui.
- **D.** Clinician presence response influence kar sakti hai. Presence alone truthful reporting ya validity guarantee nahi karti.
