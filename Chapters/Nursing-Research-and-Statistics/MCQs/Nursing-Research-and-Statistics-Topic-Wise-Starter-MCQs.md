## Sampling

### Q1. A researcher selects every tenth eligible record after a randomly chosen starting record. Which sampling method is used?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Topic:** Sampling

- **A.** Systematic random sampling
- **B.** Convenience sampling
- **C.** Purposive sampling
- **D.** Snowball sampling

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: A. Systematic random sampling**

Researcher pehle random starting point choose karta hai aur phir fixed gap se records select karta hai. Ise systematic sampling kehte hain. List mein hidden pattern ho toh bias possible hai.

**Baaki options kyun galat hain?**

- **B.** Convenience sampling mein aasani se available people liye jaate hain.
- **C.** Purposive sampling specific judgment se hoti hai.
- **D.** Snowball mein participants doosre participants refer karte hain.

</details>

## Statistics

### Q2. Five observations are 2, 4, 4, 6 and 9. Which value is the median?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Topic:** Statistics

- **A.** 5
- **B.** 6
- **C.** 9
- **D.** 4

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: D. 4**

Median ordered numbers ka beech wala value hai. Yahan 5 values hain aur third value 4 hai. Mean 25/5 yani 5 hota hai.

**Baaki options kyun galat hain?**

- **A.** 5 mean hai, median nahi.
- **B.** 6 fourth value hai.
- **C.** 9 largest value hai.

</details>

## Study design

### Q3. A study follows exposed and unexposed groups over time to compare the occurrence of disease. Which design is most consistent?

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Topic:** Study design

- **A.** Cross-sectional snapshot only
- **B.** Laboratory calibration study
- **C.** Cohort study
- **D.** Case report

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: C. Cohort study**

Cohort study mein groups ko exposure ke basis par observe kiya jaata hai aur baad mein outcome compare hota hai. Isse incidence aur association study ho sakte hain.

**Baaki options kyun galat hain?**

- **A.** Cross-sectional design ek time point par assessment karti hai.
- **B.** Calibration study instrument performance check karti hai.
- **D.** Case report single ya few cases describe karti hai.

</details>

## Research ethics

### Q4. A research participant decides to withdraw after enrollment. Which response is ethically appropriate?

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Topic:** Research ethics

- **A.** Remove the participant's privacy protections
- **B.** Respect voluntary withdrawal and explain any applicable procedures
- **C.** Force continued participation because consent was signed
- **D.** Threaten loss of routine medical care

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: B. Respect voluntary withdrawal and explain any applicable procedures**

Research participation voluntary hoti hai. Consent sign karne ke baad bhi participant withdraw kar sakta hai. Researcher procedure samjhata hai aur routine care ko punishment ke roop mein nahi rokta.

**Baaki options kyun galat hain?**

- **A.** Privacy protect karna zaroori hai.
- **C.** Consent permanent obligation nahi hai.
- **D.** Threat coercion hai.

</details>
