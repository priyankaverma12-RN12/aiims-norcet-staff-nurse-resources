### Q1. A nursing student is preparing a research proposal rather than a completed research report. Which statement best distinguishes the proposal?

**Difficulty:** 🟡 Medium · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD · Original exam-style practice

- **A.** It describes the planned investigation and its justification before the results are known
- **B.** It must contain final observed outcomes from the proposed participants
- **C.** It consists only of the final reference list
- **D.** It is identical to a consent form

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: A. It describes the planned investigation and its justification before the results are known**

**A.** Proposal future study ka reason, objectives, methods and practical plan describe karti hai. Actual findings data collection and analysis ke baad report hote hain.

**Baaki options kyun galat hain?**

- **B.** Planning stage mein future observations available nahi hain. Invented final results proposal mein include nahi karne chahiye.
- **C.** References background support karti hain but complete proposal mein study rationale, methods and implementation plan bhi chahiye.
- **D.** Consent form participant information and choice document karta hai. Proposal entire research plan explain karti hai.
