### Q1. A participant signs a research consent form but cannot explain what procedures the study involves. What should the nurse researcher do before enrollment?

**Difficulty:** 🟡 Medium · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD · Original exam-style practice

- **A.** Repeat the signature procedure without checking understanding
- **B.** Give the participant the written form again and enroll immediately
- **C.** Ask a family member to consent despite the participant retaining capacity
- **D.** Re-explain the study in understandable language and check comprehension

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: D. Re-explain the study in understandable language and check comprehension**

**D.** Research purpose, procedures, risks, possible benefits, alternatives aur refusal ka right simple language mein explain karo. Teach-back jaise neutral method se understanding check karo. Voluntary informed decision ke baad hi enrollment karo.

**Baaki options kyun galat hain?**

- **A.** Signature repeat karne se documentation milti hai, understanding establish nahi hoti. Agar participant procedure explain nahi kar pa raha, toh researcher ko communication aur comprehension improve karni chahiye.
- **B.** Written information helpful hai, lekin sirf form dena informed consent complete nahi karta. Enrollment se pehle questions answer karo aur participant ki understanding assess karo.
- **C.** Family support useful ho sakta hai, lekin capable adult ki own decision replace nahi karni chahiye. Capacity concern ho toh approved assessment process follow karo.

**Exam clue:** Consent signature se zyada understanding aur free choice important hai.

### Q3. A participant who cannot read the consent document is otherwise capable of making an informed decision. What is the appropriate consent approach?

**Difficulty:** 🟡 Medium · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD · Original exam-style practice

- **A.** Assume that inability to read means incapacity to consent
- **B.** Ask a relative to decide without consulting the participant
- **C.** Explain the information orally in a language understood and document consent with an impartial witness as required
- **D.** Obtain a thumb impression without explaining the study

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: C. Explain the information orally in a language understood and document consent with an impartial witness as required**

**C.** ICMR ke hisaab se jab participant padh nahi sakta, tab researcher ko information samjhani chahiye aur poore consent process mein ek impartial literate witness present hona chahiye. Yeh witness participant ka relative nahi hona chahiye aur research team se connected nahi hona chahiye. Agar participant sign nahi kar sakta, toh applicable documentation process mein thumb impression liya jata hai. Sirf oral consent ko routine substitute mat samjho. Uske liye exceptional justification aur ethics committee approval chahiye.

**Baaki options kyun galat hain?**

- **A.** Agar koi person padh nahi sakta, iska matlab yeh nahi ki woh research ke baare mein decision nahi le sakta. Researcher ko information aisi language aur tareeke se samjhani chahiye jise participant samajh sake.
- **B.** Family member participant ko information samajhne mein support kar sakta hai. Lekin jab adult khud informed decision le sakta hai, tab family member uski permission ko replace nahi kar sakta.
- **D.** Thumb impression consent ko document karne mein madad karta hai, lekin pehle participant ko study samajhni aur apni marzi se agree karna zaroori hai. Bina explanation ke impression lene se informed consent complete nahi hota.

**Exam clue:** Exam mein yaad rakho ki inability to read aur inability to decide do alag baatein hain.

### Q4. Two scientifically equivalent methods are available to collect a research measurement. One requires an invasive procedure, while the other is noninvasive and imposes less burden. Which ethical decision is preferable?

**Difficulty:** 🟡 Medium · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD · Original exam-style practice

- **A.** Select the invasive procedure solely because it is more familiar
- **B.** Choose the less burdensome method that preserves scientific validity
- **C.** Assign the invasive method to participants with fewer resources
- **D.** Choose whichever method generates the most publishable images

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: B. Choose the less burdensome method that preserves scientific validity**

**B.** Nonmaleficence aur risk minimization ke under unnecessary procedural burden reduce karna chahiye. Scientifically equivalent safer method participant protection strengthen karta hai.

**Baaki options kyun galat hain?**

- **A.** Familiarity operational convenience de sakti hai, but avoidable participant harm justify nahi karti. Equal scientific validity mein safer method prefer karo.
- **C.** Economic disadvantage ko more invasive burden assign karna unjust and potentially exploitative hai. Fair risk distribution and scientific justification required hain.
- **D.** Publication attractiveness participant welfare ke upar priority nahi honi chahiye. Method selection research question and proportional risk ke according karo.

**Exam clue:** Scientifically equivalent less burdensome method choose karo.

### Q6. A research study offers a very large payment to low-income participants for a risky nontherapeutic procedure. What should the ethics committee assess particularly carefully?

### Q7. A mature school-age child understands a minimal-risk study and refuses participation despite parental permission. There is no prospect of essential direct clinical benefit. What should the researcher ordinarily do?

**Difficulty:** 🔴 Difficult · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD · Original exam-style practice

- **C.** Tell the child the procedure is routine treatment even though it is research
- **D.** Respect the child's dissent and do not enroll

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: D. Respect the child's dissent and do not enroll**

**D.** Assent ka matlab hai ki bachcha apni age aur understanding ke hisaab se research mein participate karne ke liye agree kare. ICMR ke child-research guidance mein meaningful dissent respect karna chahiye. Narrow lifesaving exception tab consider ho sakti hai jab intervention sirf study mein available ho aur prior ethics approval aur parental permission ho. Is question mein aisa benefit nahi hai, isliye child ko enroll nahi karna chahiye.

**Baaki options kyun galat hain?**

- **A.** Parents ki permission important hai, lekin is nonessential research mein bachche ki samajh ke saath di hui refusal ko bhi respect karna chahiye. Parental consent har situation mein child dissent ko override nahi karta.
- **B.** Teacher school-level permission ya support de sakta hai. Lekin teacher ka role parent ya legally authorized representative ki permission ke barabar nahi hota, jab tak applicable legal arrangement aisa na ho.
- **C.** Agar investigator research ko routine treatment batata hai, toh bachche ko sachchi information nahi milti. Isliye age-appropriate explanation deni chahiye aur pressure ya deception se bachna chahiye.

**Exam clue:** Silence ko assent mat samjho. Nonessential research mein explicit dissent ko respect karo.

- **A.** Only the leader's signature is needed in every study
- **B.** Consent only from households that disagree with the leader
- **C.** Their own voluntary informed consent unless a specific approved lawful exception applies
- **D.** Mandatory enrollment because community approval was obtained

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: C. Their own voluntary informed consent unless a specific approved lawful exception applies**

**C.** Community engagement trust and local acceptability support karti hai, lekin capable individual ki decision separate hai. Participation freely decline karne ka option hona chahiye.

**Baaki options kyun galat hain?**

- **A.** Leader permission individual autonomy replace nahi karti. Consent requirements research type aur applicable approved process par depend karte hain.
- **B.** Consent all eligible individuals ke liye consistent hona chahiye. Agreement presume karke selection mat karo.
- **D.** Community approval compulsory individual participation ka authority nahi hai. Research voluntary rehni chahiye.

**Exam clue:** Community permission individual voluntary consent replace nahi karti.

### Q19. A study plans an experimental procedure with possible research-related injury. Which issue should be clarified during ethical review and consent under applicable Indian requirements?

**Difficulty:** 🔴 Difficult · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD · Original exam-style practice

- **A.** Arrangements for appropriate medical management and compensation for research-related injury where required
- **B.** An agreement that participants waive all rights to injury management
- **C.** A guarantee that no participant can ever experience harm
- **D.** A policy that only participants with favorable outcomes receive care

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: A. Arrangements for appropriate medical management and compensation for research-related injury where required**

**A.** Participant ko pehle batana chahiye ki research se injury hone par treatment kaise milega aur applicable compensation ka kya arrangement hai. ICMR section 5.3.12 free treatment for research-related injury aur zaroorat ke hisaab se additional compensation ka provision batata hai. Specific regulatory rules study category par depend karte hain, isliye approved protocol aur applicable requirements dono check karo.

**Baaki options kyun galat hain?**

- **B.** Consent form ka purpose participant ke rights samjhana hai, unhe blanket waiver se hatana nahi hai. Applicable medical management aur compensation duties ko waiver se avoid nahi karna chahiye.
- **C.** Researcher risk ko kam kar sakta hai, lekin har possible injury ko eliminate karne ki guarantee nahi de sakta. Isliye realistic risks explain karna aur injury response plan banana zaroori hai.
- **D.** Agar participant research-related injury experience karta hai, toh appropriate care ka decision favorable outcome par depend nahi karna chahiye. Safety aur applicable protections sab eligible participants ke liye consistent honi chahiye.

**Exam clue:** Research-related injury ka treatment aur applicable compensation study shuru hone se pehle plan karo.

### Q20. A nurse researcher invites patients to a randomized trial, and a patient assumes the clinician will select whichever treatment is best for that individual rather than follow the random assignment. What should the researcher address?

**Difficulty:** 🟡 Medium · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD · Original exam-style practice

- **A.** Assure the patient that randomization always selects the personally optimal treatment
- **B.** Use the misunderstanding to improve enrollment
- **C.** Explain only that the study is free of cost
- **D.** Therapeutic misconception about the distinction between research and individualized care

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: D. Therapeutic misconception about the distinction between research and individualized care**

**D.** Patient study procedures ko personalized treatment decision samajh raha hai. Randomization, study purpose, alternatives and clinical care relationship accurately explain karo.

**Baaki options kyun galat hain?**

- **A.** Random allocation scientific comparison support karti hai, not individualized treatment optimization. Aisa assurance misleading hai.
- **B.** Misunderstanding exploit karna informed consent undermine karta hai. Comprehension correct karna ethical responsibility hai.
- **C.** Cost information relevant ho sakti hai but allocation misunderstanding resolve nahi karti. Research and clinical care difference discuss karo.

**Exam clue:** Research aur individualized clinical treatment ka distinction explain karo.
