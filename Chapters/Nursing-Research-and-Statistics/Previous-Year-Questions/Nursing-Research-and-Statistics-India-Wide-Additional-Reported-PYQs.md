### PYQ1. Which statistic describes how far observations typically vary around their mean?

**Difficulty:** Easy/Medium | **Topic:** Dispersion

- **A.** Standard deviation
- **B.** Median
- **C.** Mode
- **D.** Sample size

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation</summary>

**Correct: A. Standard deviation**

Standard deviation data ke spread ka measure hai. Values mean ke paas hon toh standard deviation kam hota hai. Median middle value hai, mode most frequent value hai aur sample size observations ki count hai.

</details>

### PYQ2. In an experiment, what is the factor deliberately changed by the investigator called?

**Difficulty:** Easy/Medium | **Topic:** Variables

- **A.** Confounding variable
- **B.** Independent variable
- **C.** Dependent variable
- **D.** Outcome measure

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation</summary>

**Correct: B. Independent variable**

Independent variable researcher change karta hai taaki outcome par effect observe kar sake. Dependent variable aur outcome measure result ko represent karte hain. Confounder exposure aur outcome ke relationship ko distort kar sakta hai.

</details>
