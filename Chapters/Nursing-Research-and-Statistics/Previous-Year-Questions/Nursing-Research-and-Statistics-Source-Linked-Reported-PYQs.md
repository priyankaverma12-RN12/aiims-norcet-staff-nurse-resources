### PYQ1. Systematic sampling is primarily a method used for which research activity?

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Topic:** Research sampling

- **A.** Selecting a sample from a population
- **B.** Calculating a drug infusion rate
- **C.** Sterilizing instruments
- **D.** Diagnosing a cardiac rhythm

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: A. Selecting a sample from a population**

Systematic sampling mein fixed interval se participants ya records select kiye jaate hain. Iska use research sample banane mein hota hai.

**Baaki options kyun galat hain?**

- **B.** Infusion rate arithmetic calculation hai.
- **C.** Sterilization infection control ka process hai.
- **D.** ECG rhythm diagnosis sampling ka meaning nahi hai.

</details>
