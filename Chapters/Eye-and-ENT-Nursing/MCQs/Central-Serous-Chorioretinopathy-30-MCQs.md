### Q1. Which anatomical compartment contains the characteristic fluid in active central serous chorioretinopathy?

**Difficulty:** 🟢 Easy · **Priority:** 🔴 MOST IMPORTANT · Original clinical practice

- **A.** Between the corneal epithelium and Bowman's layer
- **B.** Between the neurosensory retina and the retinal pigment epithelium
- **C.** Within the anterior chamber angle
- **D.** Within the crystalline lens nucleus

<details><summary>✅ Answer aur detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: B. Between the neurosensory retina and the retinal pigment epithelium**

**Kyun sahi hai:** CSC mein neural retina ke neeche fluid collect hota hai. Isse photoreceptor layer RPE se lift ho sakti hai aur central vision disturb hoti hai.

**Baaki options kyun galat hain?**

- **A.** Cornea anterior eye mein hai. CSC macular retina ko affect karti hai.
- **C.** Anterior chamber angle aqueous drainage ka region hai. CSC fluid posterior segment mein hota hai.
- **D.** Lens nucleus opacity cataract se related hai, subretinal fluid se nahi.
