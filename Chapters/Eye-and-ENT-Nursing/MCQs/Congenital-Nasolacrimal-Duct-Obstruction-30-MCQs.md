### Q1. Which embryological remnant most commonly causes simple congenital nasolacrimal duct obstruction?

**Difficulty:** 🟢 Easy · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD · Original practice

- **A.** Persistent pupillary membrane
- **B.** Persistent hyaloid artery
- **C.** Persistent membrane at the valve of Hasner
- **D.** Incomplete closure of the optic fissure

<details><summary>✅ Correct answer and detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: C. Persistent membrane at the valve of Hasner**

**A kyun galat:** Ye iris ki developmental remnant hai, tear duct ki nahi.

**B kyun galat:** Ye intraocular vascular structure hai, nasal tear outlet nahi.

**C kyun sahi:** Duct ke nasal end par membrane open na ho to tears drain nahi kar pati. Ye simple congenital obstruction ka common mechanism hai.

**D kyun galat:** Isse coloboma ho sakta hai. Distal tear duct membrane alag hai.
