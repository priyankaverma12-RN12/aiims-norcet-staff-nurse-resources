### Q1. Which imbalance most directly predisposes to an anomalous posterior vitreous detachment with traction?

**Difficulty:** 🟡 Medium · **Priority:** 🔴 MOST IMPORTANT · Original clinical practice

- **A.** Adhesion disappears completely before gel separation
