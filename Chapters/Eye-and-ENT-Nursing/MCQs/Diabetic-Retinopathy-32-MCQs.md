### Q1. Which retinal lesion is usually the earliest clinically visible sign of diabetic retinopathy?

**Difficulty:** 🟢 Easy · **Priority:** 🔴 MOST IMPORTANT · **Topic:** staging

- **C.** Microaneurysms
