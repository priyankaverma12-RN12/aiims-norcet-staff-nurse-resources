## Glaucoma

### Q1. A patient develops sudden severe eye pain, blurred vision, halos and nausea. Which condition requires urgent evaluation?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Topic:** Glaucoma

- **A.** Acute angle-closure glaucoma
- **B.** Stable refractive error
- **C.** Uncomplicated chronic cataract
- **D.** Presbycusis

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: A. Acute angle-closure glaucoma**

Aankh ke andar fluid ka outflow block ho toh pressure achanak badh sakta hai. Severe pain aur halos ke saath vision ko permanent harm ho sakta hai. Emergency eye review chahiye.

**Baaki options kyun galat hain?**

- **B.** Refractive error usually severe acute pain nahi deti.
- **C.** Cataract generally dheere-dheere vision kam karta hai.
- **D.** Presbycusis age-related hearing loss hai.

</details>

## Retinal detachment

### Q2. A patient reports flashes of light, new floaters and a curtain over part of the visual field. What is the best response?

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Topic:** Retinal detachment

- **A.** Recommend only a new spectacle prescription
- **B.** Reassure that these symptoms always resolve
- **C.** Delay until routine annual examination
- **D.** Arrange urgent ophthalmic assessment for retinal detachment

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: D. Arrange urgent ophthalmic assessment for retinal detachment**

Retina aankh ke andar light ko signal mein badalti hai. Agar retina detach ho, toh flashes aur curtain jaisa shadow aa sakta hai. Jaldi treatment vision bachane mein help kar sakta hai.

**Baaki options kyun galat hain?**

- **A.** Spectacles retina ko attach nahi karte.
- **B.** Symptoms serious ho sakte hain.
- **C.** Delay permanent vision loss ka risk badha sakta hai.

</details>

## Epistaxis

### Q3. An alert patient has an uncomplicated anterior nosebleed. Which first-aid instruction is appropriate?

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Topic:** Epistaxis

- **A.** Lie flat with the head lower than the body
- **B.** Insert an unsterile sharp object into the nostril
- **C.** Sit forward and pinch the soft part of the nose continuously
- **D.** Tilt the head backward and swallow blood

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: C. Sit forward and pinch the soft part of the nose continuously**

Nose ki front vessels se bleeding ho toh soft part press karne se clot banne mein help milti hai. Aage jhukne se blood throat mein jaane ka risk kam hota hai.

**Baaki options kyun galat hain?**

- **A.** Flat head-down position aspiration risk badha sakti hai.
- **B.** Sharp object injury badha sakta hai.
- **D.** Peeche tilt karne se blood nigla ja sakta hai.

</details>

## Post-tonsillectomy

### Q4. A child after tonsillectomy has repeated swallowing, pallor and tachycardia. Which complication is concerning?

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Topic:** Post-tonsillectomy

- **A.** Routine constipation
- **B.** Postoperative hemorrhage
- **C.** Normal recovery without bleeding risk
- **D.** Expected improvement in hearing

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: B. Postoperative hemorrhage**

Tonsil ki surgery ke baad blood throat mein ja sakta hai aur child baar-baar swallow kar sakta hai. Pallor aur fast pulse blood loss ke clues hain. Nurse emergency review leti hai.

**Baaki options kyun galat hain?**

- **A.** Constipation acute blood loss signs nahi deti.
- **C.** Repeated swallowing with circulatory changes normal assume nahi karna chahiye.
- **D.** Hearing improvement is pattern ko explain nahi karti.

</details>
