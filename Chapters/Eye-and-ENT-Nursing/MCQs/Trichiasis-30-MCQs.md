### Q1. A patient with a single abnormal upper-lid lash experiences scratching chiefly while blinking. Which observation best links the symptom to the lash?

**Difficulty:** 🟡 Medium · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD · Original practice

- **A.** The pupil constricts in bright light
- **B.** The lash tip sweeps across the ocular surface during blinking
- **C.** The lower punctum is patent
- **D.** The lens has an age-related opacity

<details><summary>✅ Correct answer and detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: B. The lash tip sweeps across the ocular surface during blinking**

**A kyun galat:** Pupil reflex nerve pathway assess karta hai, lash contact nahi.

**B kyun sahi:** Blink ke saath actual contact observe hone se mechanical rubbing aur symptom ka link samajh aata hai.

**C kyun galat:** Punctum tear drainage ka opening hai. Iski patency rubbing ka cause confirm nahi karti.

**D kyun galat:** Lens opacity vision affect kar sakti hai, lekin blink-related scratching explain nahi karti.

**Exam clue:** Lash ki location aur actual contact separately record karo.
