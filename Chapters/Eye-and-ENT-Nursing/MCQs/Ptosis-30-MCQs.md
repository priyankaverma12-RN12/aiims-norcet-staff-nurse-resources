### Q1. Which muscle generates most active elevation of the upper eyelid during ordinary eye opening?

**Difficulty:** 🟢 Easy · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD · Original practice

- **A.** Orbicularis oculi
- **B.** Levator palpebrae superioris
- **C.** Superior rectus
- **D.** Inferior oblique

<details><summary>✅ Correct answer and detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: B. Levator palpebrae superioris**

**A kyun galat:** Ye eyelid close karne mein help karti hai, open karne mein nahi.

**B kyun sahi:** Ye muscle upper lid ko actively lift karti hai. Iski weakness se lid neeche aa sakta hai.

**C kyun galat:** Ye globe ko elevate karti hai, upper lid ki principal elevator nahi hai.

**D kyun galat:** Ye globe ke movement mein help karti hai, upper lid ko primarily lift nahi karti.

**Exam clue:** Opening aur closing muscles ki nerve supply alag yaad rakho.

### Q4. In primary gaze, the upper eyelid margin lies 1 mm above the corneal light reflex and the lower margin lies 5 mm below it. What is the vertical palpebral fissure height?

**Difficulty:** 🔴 Difficult · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD · Original practice

- **A.** 4 mm
- **B.** 5 mm
- **C.** 6 mm
- **D.** 10 mm

<details><summary>✅ Correct answer and detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: C. 6 mm**

**A kyun galat:** 5 - 1 karne se distance ka difference milta hai, complete opening nahi.

**B kyun galat:** Ye sirf lower margin ka distance hai. Upper segment bhi include karna hai.

**C kyun sahi:** MRD1 1 mm aur MRD2 5 mm hai. Dono same vertical reference line par hain. Fissure height = 1 + 5 = 6 mm. Reverse check mein 6 - 5 = 1 mm milta hai.

**D kyun galat:** Is calculation mein kisi given distance ko double karne ka reason nahi hai.

**Exam clue:** Lid height, brow compensation aur actual movement ko separately measure karo.
