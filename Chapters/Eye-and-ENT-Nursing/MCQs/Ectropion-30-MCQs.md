### Q1. Which finding most directly identifies ectropion rather than lower eyelid retraction alone?

**Difficulty:** 🟡 Medium · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD · Original clinical practice

- **A.** Inferior displacement of a normally oriented lid margin
- **B.** Outward rotation of the lid margin
- **C.** Incomplete closure without visible margin eversion
- **D.** An extra row of lashes contacting the globe

<details><summary>✅ Answer aur detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: B. Outward rotation of the lid margin**

**Kyun sahi hai:** Ectropion mein lid margin evert hota hai. Retraction mein lid lower position mein ho sakta hai without margin eversion. Dono coexist kar sakte hain.

**Baaki options kyun galat hain?**

- **A.** Ye lower lid retraction describe karta hai, eversion necessary nahi.
- **C.** Ye lagophthalmos hai, ectropion prove nahi karta.
- **D.** Ye distichiasis se related hai.

### Q2. A patient with ectropion has epiphora although the lacrimal drainage tract is patent. Which mechanism explains this?
