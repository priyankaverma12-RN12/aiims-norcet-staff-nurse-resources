### Q1. Which pathological process primarily produces a chalazion?

**Difficulty:** 🟡 Medium · **Priority:** 🔴 MOST IMPORTANT · Original clinical practice

- **A.** Acute purulent infection in every case
- **B.** Neovascularization of the iris
- **C.** Lipogranulomatous inflammation following retention and leakage of glandular lipid
- **D.** Deposition of calcium in the crystalline lens

<details><summary>✅ Answer aur detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: C. Lipogranulomatous inflammation following retention and leakage of glandular lipid**

**Kyun sahi hai:** Meibomian duct block hone par meibum adjacent tissue mein leak kar sakta hai. Macrophages aur giant cells chronic inflammatory nodule banate hain. Ye primary acute bacterial abscess se different hai.

**Baaki options kyun galat hain?**

- **A.** Primary chalazion generally sterile hai. Secondary infection possible hai but mandatory nahi.
- **B.** Iris neovascularization intraocular vascular disorder hai, eyelid nodule mechanism nahi.
