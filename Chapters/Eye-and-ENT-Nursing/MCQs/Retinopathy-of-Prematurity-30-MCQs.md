### Q1. Why is the peripheral retina of a very premature infant particularly susceptible to ROP?

**Difficulty:** 🟡 Medium · **Priority:** 🔴 MOST IMPORTANT · Original clinical practice

- **A.** All premature infants have congenital absence of the retina
- **B.** Normal retinal vascular development may still be incomplete at birth
- **C.** The lens has already undergone age-related degeneration
- **D.** The cornea normally contains a dense network of blood vessels

<details><summary>✅ Answer aur detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: B. Normal retinal vascular development may still be incomplete at birth**

**Kyun sahi hai:** Retinal vessels optic disc se peripheral retina tak grow karti hain. Early birth mein avascular peripheral tissue remain kar sakta hai, jahan later abnormal vessel growth develop ho sakti hai.

**Baaki options kyun galat hain?**

- **A.** Prematurity vascular immaturity ka risk hai, retina absent hone ka proof nahi.
- **C.** Age-related lens degeneration ROP ka primary mechanism nahi.
- **D.** Normal cornea avascular hai aur ROP retina ki vascular condition hai.
