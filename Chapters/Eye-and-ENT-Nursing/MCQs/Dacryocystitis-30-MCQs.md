### Q1. Which structure is primarily infected in dacryocystitis?

**Difficulty:** 🟢 Easy · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD · Original practice

- **A.** Lacrimal gland
- **B.** Lacrimal sac
- **C.** Meibomian gland
- **D.** Trabecular meshwork

<details><summary>✅ Correct answer and detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: B. Lacrimal sac**

**A kyun galat:** Gland tears banati hai. Iski inflammation dacryoadenitis hai.

**B kyun sahi:** Lacrimal sac tears ko nose tak drain hone se pehle collect karta hai. Is sac ki infection ko dacryocystitis kehte hain.

**C kyun galat:** Ye lid margin ki oil-producing gland hai. Iska disease lacrimal sac infection nahi hai.

**D kyun galat:** Ye intraocular aqueous drainage ka structure hai, tear drainage ka nahi.

**Exam clue:** Tear production aur tear drainage ko alag samjho.
