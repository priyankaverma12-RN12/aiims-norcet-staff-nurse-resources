### Q1. Which structure is primarily infected in a typical external hordeolum at the base of an eyelash?

**Difficulty:** 🟢 Easy · **Priority:** 🔴 MOST IMPORTANT · Original clinical practice

- **C.** Lacrimal sac below the medial canthus
