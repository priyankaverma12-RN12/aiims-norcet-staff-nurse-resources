### Q1. Which vessel is obstructed in central retinal vein occlusion?

**Difficulty:** 🟢 Easy · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD · **Topic:** mechanism

- **A.** The central retinal artery supplying the inner retina
- **B.** The ophthalmic artery before its orbital branches
- **C.** The main venous drainage vessel of the retina
- **D.** The canal of Schlemm draining aqueous humor

<details><summary>✅ Answer aur detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: C. The main venous drainage vessel of the retina**

**Kyun sahi hai:** Central retinal vein retina se blood bahar le jaati hai. Iski obstruction se venous blood ka outflow slow hota hai aur retina mein congestion ho sakti hai.

**Baaki options kyun galat hain?**

- **A.** Central retinal artery blockage arterial ischemia cause karti hai, venous occlusion nahi.
- **B.** Ophthalmic artery ki proximal obstruction wider arterial supply affect karti hai.
- **D.** Canal of Schlemm aqueous humor drain karta hai, retinal blood nahi.
