### Q1. Which tissue location characterizes an epiretinal membrane?

**Difficulty:** 🟢 Easy · **Priority:** 🔴 MOST IMPORTANT · Original clinical practice

- **A.** Opacity in the lens nucleus
- **B.** New vessels beneath the retinal pigment epithelium
- **C.** Fibrocellular tissue on the inner retinal surface

<details><summary>✅ Answer aur detailed explanation</summary>

**Kyun sahi hai:** Retina ki vitreous-facing surface par extra cellular layer ban jaati hai. Ye contract hokar macula ki shape change kar sakti hai.
