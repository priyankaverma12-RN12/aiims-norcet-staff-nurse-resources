### Q1. Which tear-film component is principally supplied by the lacrimal gland?

**Difficulty:** 🟢 Easy · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD · Original practice

- **A.** Lipid component
- **B.** Aqueous component
- **C.** Retinal photopigment
- **D.** Vitreous gel

<details><summary>✅ Correct answer and detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: B. Aqueous component**

**A kyun galat:** Lipid ka main source meibomian glands hain. Ye evaporation reduce karta hai.

**B kyun sahi:** Gland tear film ka watery component banane mein main role rakhti hai. Is fluid se ocular surface moist aur protected rehti hai.

**C kyun galat:** Photopigment retinal photoreceptors mein hota hai, tear film mein nahi.

**D kyun galat:** Vitreous globe ke andar hota hai. Lacrimal gland ise secrete nahi karti.
