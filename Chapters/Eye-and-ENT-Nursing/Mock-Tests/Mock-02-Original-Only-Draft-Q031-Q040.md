### Q31. Two days after epithelium-off corneal cross-linking, the prescribed bandage contact lens falls onto the patient's pillow. Which telephone instruction is most appropriate?

**Difficulty:** 🟡 Medium · **Priority:** 🔴 MOST IMPORTANT · Original mock-exclusive practice

- **A.** Rinse the lens under tap water and reinsert it
- **B.** Do not reinsert the lens and contact the treating eye service for advice
- **C.** Replace it with the patient's usual rigid contact lens
- **D.** Leave the eye untreated until the next annual appointment

<details><summary>✅ Answer aur detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: B. Do not reinsert the lens and contact the treating eye service for advice**

- **A. Kyun galat hai:** Tap water saaf dikh sakta hai, lekin usmein germs ho sakte hain. Healing cornea par contaminated lens lagane se infection ka risk badhta hai. Isliye gire hue lens ko paani se dhokar wapas nahi lagana chahiye.

- **B. Kyun sahi hai:** Cornea aankh ke aage ka transparent hissa hai. Epithelium-off cross-linking mein outer cell layer hataayi jaati hai aur woh dobara heal hoti hai. Bandage lens is surface ko eyelid ki rubbing se protect karta hai. Lens girne par khud se reinsert na karein aur treating eye service se advice lein. Prescribed drops instructions ke mutabik continue karein. Badhta pain, redness ya achanak vision loss urgent assessment ke signs hain.

- **C. Kyun galat hai:** Normal rigid lens vision correction ke liye fit hota hai, healing wound ke liye nahi. Bina clearance use lagane se surface par pressure aur irritation ho sakti hai. Isliye woh therapeutic bandage lens ka substitute nahi hai.

- **D. Kyun galat hai:** Annual appointment tak wait karne se healing problem ya infection miss ho sakta hai. Early postoperative period mein lens girne ki information team ko deni chahiye, taaki woh decide kare ki examination ya replacement chahiye ya nahi.

**Exam clue:** Therapeutic lens girne par self-reinsertion nahi karni hai.

**Clinical reference:** [Clinical guidance](https://moorfields.nhs.uk/eye-conditions/keratoconus/corneal-cross-linking). · [Moorfields adult aftercare](https://www.moorfields.nhs.uk/eye-conditions/keratoconus/cross-linking-aftercare)

### Q32. A patient scheduled for elective corneal cross-linking reports a previous episode of herpes simplex keratitis that is absent from the preoperative history. What should the nurse do before the procedure?

**Difficulty:** 🔴 Difficult · **Priority:** 🔴 MOST IMPORTANT · Original mock-exclusive practice

- **A.** Proceed without documentation because the infection occurred in the past
- **B.** Advise the patient to start leftover steroid drops
- **C.** Inform the corneal specialist because previous herpetic disease may affect the treatment plan
- **D.** Cancel every future ophthalmic procedure permanently

<details><summary>✅ Answer aur detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: C. Inform the corneal specialist because previous herpetic disease may affect the treatment plan**

- **A. Kyun galat hai:** Herpes simplex keratitis mein virus cornea ko infect karta hai. Infection theek hone ke baad bhi virus nerves mein inactive reh sakta hai aur baad mein reactivate ho sakta hai. Isliye past episode ki history ignore nahi karni chahiye.

- **B. Kyun galat hai:** Steroid drops inflammation kam karti hain, lekin active epithelial herpes mein inappropriate steroid use infection worsen kar sakta hai. Kuch stromal disease mein specialist antiviral ke saath steroid prescribe karta hai. Patient ko leftover drops khud se start nahi karni chahiye.

- **C. Kyun sahi hai:** Corneal cross-linking ke baad herpes reactivation ka concern ho sakta hai. Nurse nayi history document karke specialist ko procedure se pehle batayegi. Specialist suitability, precautions aur antiviral plan ki zaroorat assess karega.

- **D. Kyun galat hai:** Past herpes infection ka matlab ye nahi hai ki patient kabhi koi eye procedure nahi karwa sakta. Har procedure ka risk alag hota hai. Isliye permanent cancellation ki jagah individualized specialist assessment chahiye.

**Exam clue:** Past infection history elective corneal procedure ki safety ko change kar sakti hai.

**Clinical reference:** [Clinical guidance](https://eyewiki.aao.org/Corneal_Collagen_Cross-Linking). · [EyeWiki epi-off and epi-on techniques](https://eyewiki.aao.org/Techniques_for_Corneal_Collagen_Crosslinking:_Epi-off_vs_Epi-on) · [AAO herpes keratitis](https://www.aao.org/education/topic-detail/herpes-simplex-keratitis--europe)

### Q33. At a serial corneal tomography visit for keratoconus, the patient reports wearing rigid contact lenses until immediately before today's scan despite a previous lens-free preparation instruction. Why should this be documented?

**Difficulty:** 🔴 Difficult · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD · Original mock-exclusive practice

- **A.** Recent lens wear can temporarily influence corneal shape measurements and complicate comparison
- **B.** Contact lenses permanently stop all corneal progression
- **C.** Tomography measures only retinal blood flow
- **D.** Recent lens wear proves that cross-linking has failed

<details><summary>✅ Answer aur detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: A. Recent lens wear can temporarily influence corneal shape measurements and complicate comparison**

- **A. Kyun sahi hai:** Tomography se cornea ki front aur back surfaces aur thickness ka map banta hai. Rigid lens corneal shape temporarily change kar sakta hai, isliye scan purane lens-free scan se directly compare karna misleading ho sakta hai. Nurse actual wear document karegi aur specialist repeat scan ya lens-free preparation decide karega. Universal interval established nahi hai.

- **B. Kyun galat hai:** Rigid contact lens irregular cornea ke optical effect ko correct karke vision improve kar sakta hai. Lekin achha dikhna aur corneal disease ki progression rukna alag cheezein hain. Lens permanent cure ki guarantee nahi deta.

- **C. Kyun galat hai:** Corneal tomography cornea ke structure ka map banati hai. Retinal blood flow assess karne ke liye alag tests hote hain. Lens wear ki history yahan corneal measurement ke liye important hai.

- **D. Kyun galat hai:** Recent lens wear temporary measurement change ka reason ho sakta hai. Ye cross-linking fail hone ka proof nahi hai. Specialist ko comparable serial scans aur clinical findings assess karni chahiye.

**Exam clue:** Serial measurements ko comparable conditions mein lena important hai. Lens-free interval individualized hota hai.

**Clinical reference:** [Clinical guidance](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11621536/). · [Eye clinician survey](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41433-024-03352-2)

### Q34. Before conventional ultraviolet-A corneal cross-linking, a patient asks why the surgeon measures corneal thickness rather than deciding solely from spectacle power. Which explanation is appropriate?

**Difficulty:** 🔴 Difficult · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD · Original mock-exclusive practice

- **A.** Thickness establishes the patient's colour vision
- **B.** Spectacle power alone specifies the safe ultraviolet exposure
- **C.** The measurement is intended to calculate tear production
- **D.** Corneal thickness helps determine procedural safety for deeper ocular structures

<details><summary>✅ Answer aur detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: D. Corneal thickness helps determine procedural safety for deeper ocular structures**

- **A. Kyun galat hai:** Colour vision retina ke cone cells aur visual pathways se related hai. Corneal thickness se patient ki colours distinguish karne ki ability measure nahi hoti. Colour vision ke liye separate assessment hoti hai.

- **B. Kyun galat hai:** Spectacle power clear focus ke liye optical correction batati hai. Ye actual corneal thickness ya UV-A exposure se deeper cells ki safety ka measurement nahi hai. Isliye spectacle power alone se procedural safety decide nahi kar sakte.

- **C. Kyun galat hai:** Pachymetry ka matlab cornea ki thickness measure karna hai. Tears assess karne ke liye Schirmer jaise alag tests use hote hain. Yahan thickness procedural safety ke liye measure hoti hai.

- **D. Kyun sahi hai:** Cross-linking mein riboflavin aur UV-A light corneal fibres ko strengthen karte hain. Agar cornea bahut patli ho, toh deeper endothelial cells ko harmful exposure ka risk ho sakta hai. Endothelium cornea ki inner cell layer hai jo clarity maintain karne mein help karti hai. Specialist chosen protocol ki safety conditions check karta hai. Nurse khud UV exposure decide nahi karti.

**Exam clue:** Pachymetry tissue safety assessment hai, sirf refraction test nahi. Specialist-level detail enrichment hai.

**Clinical reference:** [Clinical guidance](https://eyewiki.aao.org/Corneal_Collagen_Cross-Linking). · [EyeWiki corneal cross-linking](https://eyewiki.aao.org/Corneal_Cross-Linking)

### Q35. After enucleation, a caregiver notices a clear temporary shell in the healing socket and asks why the definitive artificial eye has not yet been fitted. What is the primary function of the temporary device?

**Difficulty:** 🟡 Medium · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD · Original mock-exclusive practice

- **A.** It restores retinal light perception
- **B.** It helps preserve socket shape and conjunctival fornices while healing occurs
- **C.** It replaces the optic nerve with an artificial nerve
- **D.** It permanently eliminates the need for an ocularist

<details><summary>✅ Answer aur detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: B. It helps preserve socket shape and conjunctival fornices while healing occurs**

- **A. Kyun galat hai:** Enucleation mein poora eyeball remove hota hai. Jab retina remove ho gayi hai, toh socket mein rakha shell light ko nerve signals mein convert nahi kar sakta. Isliye conformer vision restore nahi karta.

- **B. Kyun sahi hai:** Conformer ek temporary smooth shell hai jo healing socket mein jagah maintain karta hai. Conjunctival fornices eyelid aur socket lining ke beech ke folds hote hain. Inko preserve karne se baad mein artificial eye fit karna aasaan ho sakta hai. Healing ke baad ocularist, yani artificial eye fitting specialist, suitable prosthesis fit karta hai.

- **C. Kyun galat hai:** Conformer socket ko physical support deta hai. Ismein aisa biological ya electrical system nahi hai jo optic nerve ke signals brain tak bhej sake. Isliye ye artificial nerve nahi hai.

- **D. Kyun galat hai:** Temporary conformer aur customized artificial eye ke roles alag hain. Healing ke baad socket shape aur comfort assess karke professional fitting chahiye. Conformer ocularist ke follow-up ko replace nahi karta.

**Exam clue:** Conformer ka role temporary healing support hai.

**Clinical reference:** [Clinical guidance](https://eyewiki.aao.org/Ocular_Prostheses,_Scleral_Shells,_and_Conformers).

### Q36. A patient has a severely disfigured nonseeing eye that remains anatomically present. The specialist discusses a cosmetic prosthetic option fitted over the residual globe without removing it. Which device matches that description?

**Difficulty:** 🔴 Difficult · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD · Original mock-exclusive practice

- **A.** An intraocular lens implanted after cataract extraction
- **B.** A temporary postoperative wound drain
- **C.** A scleral shell prosthesis
- **D.** A definitive prosthesis intended for an empty enucleated socket

<details><summary>✅ Answer aur detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: C. A scleral shell prosthesis**

- **A. Kyun galat hai:** Intraocular lens cataract surgery ke baad natural lens ki optical function replace karne ke liye aankh ke andar lagta hai. Ye residual eyeball par fitted cosmetic cover nahi hai. Dono devices ka purpose aur placement alag hai.

- **B. Kyun galat hai:** Wound drain jama fluid ko bahar nikalne ke liye use hota hai. Long-term cosmetic restoration ke liye customized prosthetic device chahiye. Drain uska substitute nahi hai.

- **C. Kyun sahi hai:** Scleral shell ek customized cosmetic cover ho sakta hai jo selected disfigured nonseeing eye ke upar fit hota hai jab globe present ho. Iska purpose appearance improve karna hai, vision restore karna nahi. Ophthalmologist aur ocularist surface condition aur comfort assess karte hain.

- **D. Kyun galat hai:** Enucleated socket mein eyeball remove ho chuka hota hai aur prosthesis socket mein fit hoti hai. Is question mein globe present hai, isliye residual eye par fitted scleral shell appropriate description hai. Exact device specialist decide karta hai.

**Exam clue:** Device selection residual globe ki presence par depend kar sakti hai.

**Clinical reference:** [Clinical guidance](https://eyewiki.aao.org/Ocular_Prostheses,_Scleral_Shells,_and_Conformers).

### Q37. A long-term artificial-eye user reports new rubbing discomfort and recurrent discharge. The external prosthesis looks scratched and has not been professionally serviced for years. Which plan is appropriate?

**Difficulty:** 🟡 Medium · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD · Original mock-exclusive practice

- **A.** Arrange ocularist review of fit and surface condition together with clinical socket assessment
- **B.** Advise the patient to sand the prosthesis at home
- **C.** Assume recurrent discharge is always normal in an artificial socket
- **D.** Recommend leaving symptoms unreported until the prosthesis breaks

<details><summary>✅ Answer aur detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: A. Arrange ocularist review of fit and surface condition together with clinical socket assessment**

- **A. Kyun sahi hai:** Artificial eye smooth honi chahiye taaki socket lining aur eyelid par rubbing kam ho. Scratched surface ya changed fit irritation aur discharge cause kar sakte hain. Ocularist polishing, repair ya replacement ki need assess karega. New discharge mein eye service ko infection aur doosri socket problems ke liye bhi examination karna chahiye.

- **B. Kyun galat hai:** Ghar par sandpaper se prosthesis ghisne se shape aur surface damage ho sakti hai. Rough spots socket ko aur irritate kar sakte hain. Isliye polishing trained ocularist se karwani chahiye.

- **C. Kyun galat hai:** Artificial eye users mein kuch discharge common ho sakta hai. Lekin naya ya recurrent discharge jo discomfort ke saath hai, use automatically normal nahi samajhna chahiye. Clinical assessment se infection aur mechanical irritation distinguish karne mein help milti hai.

- **D. Kyun galat hai:** Prosthesis tootne ka wait karna zaroori nahi hai. Scratches, discomfort aur repeated discharge review ki wajah hain. Timely assessment se possible socket complication identify ho sakti hai.

**Exam clue:** Ocularist device assess karta hai aur eye service socket ki health assess karti hai.

**Clinical reference:** [Clinical guidance](https://www.cuh.nhs.uk/patient-information/removal-of-an-eye-enucleation-and-evisceration/). · [NHS National Artificial Eye Service](https://www.naes.nhs.uk/health-professionals)

### Q38. A patient with a healed unilateral enucleation socket returns to a workshop job where flying debris is possible. Which counselling addresses the most important long-term visual safety issue?

**Difficulty:** 🟡 Medium · **Priority:** 🔴 MOST IMPORTANT · Original mock-exclusive practice

- **A.** Use ordinary cosmetic spectacles instead of appropriate workplace eye protection
- **B.** Assume the artificial eye makes injury to the seeing eye less important
- **C.** Avoid regular examination of the remaining eye if it is currently asymptomatic
- **D.** Use appropriate impact-resistant protection for the remaining seeing eye and maintain ophthalmic follow-up

<details><summary>✅ Answer aur detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: D. Use appropriate impact-resistant protection for the remaining seeing eye and maintain ophthalmic follow-up**

- **A. Kyun galat hai:** Ordinary cosmetic spectacles har workshop hazard ke liye certified protection nahi dete. Flying debris ke risk ke hisaab se impact-rated safety glasses, side protection ya goggles chahiye ho sakte hain. Sirf regular chashme par depend nahi karna chahiye.

- **B. Kyun galat hai:** Artificial eye appearance improve kar sakti hai, lekin sight wapas nahi laati. Patient ki functional vision remaining eye par depend karti hai. Us eye ki injury independence par serious impact daal sakti hai.

- **C. Kyun galat hai:** Kuch eye diseases early stage mein bina noticeable symptoms ke develop ho sakti hain. Regular assessment se treatable problems time par mil sakti hain. Isliye asymptomatic eye ka follow-up avoid nahi karna chahiye.

- **D. Kyun sahi hai:** Patient ki remaining seeing eye ko injury se bachana bahut important hai. Workshop mein appropriate impact-rated eye protection use karein aur workplace safety requirements follow karein. Regular eye follow-up se remaining vision monitor hoti hai. Injury ya sudden vision change par prompt assessment chahiye.

**Exam clue:** Monocular patient ki remaining vision ko actively protect karo.

**Clinical reference:** [Clinical guidance](https://eyewiki.aao.org/Ocular_Prostheses,_Scleral_Shells,_and_Conformers). · [AAO living with one seeing eye](https://www.aao.org/eye-health/tips-prevention/living-with-vision-loss-in-one-eye)

### Q39. A patient with low vision can independently organize medicines but has accidentally confused two similarly shaped prescription bottles because their small labels are unreadable. Which discharge intervention most directly reduces this medication error risk?

**Difficulty:** 🔴 Difficult · **Priority:** 🔴 MOST IMPORTANT · Original mock-exclusive practice

- **A.** Tell the patient to identify both medicines solely by bottle colour
- **B.** Coordinate accessible pharmacy labels and verify that the patient can identify each medicine and its dosing instructions
- **C.** Remove the original labels and rely on memory
- **D.** Assume that all patients with low vision must transfer medication responsibility to a relative

<details><summary>✅ Answer aur detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: B. Coordinate accessible pharmacy labels and verify that the patient can identify each medicine and its dosing instructions**

- **A. Kyun galat hai:** Bottle ka colour manufacturer ya pharmacy change kar sakti hai. Low vision mein similar colours distinguish karna bhi difficult ho sakta hai. Colour alone par depend karne se wrong medicine ya dose lene ka risk hai.

- **B. Kyun sahi hai:** Patient medicine organize kar sakta hai lekin labels nahi padh pa raha hai. Nurse pharmacy se usable large-print, audio ya doosra accessible label arrange karegi. Phir patient se har medicine identify karwa kar dose aur timing apne words mein batane aur safe use demonstrate karne ko kahegi. Isse actual safety verify hogi.

- **C. Kyun galat hai:** Original label hataane se medicine name, strength, dose aur safety information lose ho sakti hai. Similar bottles ke saath memory-only strategy risky hai. Accessible information correct bottle se reliably matched rehni chahiye.

- **D. Kyun galat hai:** Low vision ka matlab ye nahi hai ki har patient independently medicine manage nahi kar sakta. Nurse actual ability, preference aur aids assess karegi. Accessible labeling se safe use establish ho toh independence support karein, aur need ke hisaab se assistance arrange karein.

**Exam clue:** Accessible labels ke saath actual safe-use demonstration verify karo.

**Clinical reference:** [Clinical guidance](https://www.afb.org/blindness-and-low-vision/your-rights/rx-label-enable-campaign/guidelines-prescription-labeling). · [AFB accessible medication information](https://www.afb.org/blindness-and-low-vision/using-technology/prescription-health-and-fitness/accessible-prescription)

### Q40. A child with confirmed red-green colour vision deficiency can read ordinary text clearly but repeatedly misinterprets school worksheets that distinguish two instructions only by red and green ink. Which adaptation best addresses this specific difficulty?

**Difficulty:** 🟡 Medium · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD · Original mock-exclusive practice

- **A.** Use still smaller red and green print
- **B.** Increase spectacle power without an eye assessment
- **C.** Add distinct words or symbols to the two colour-coded instructions
- **D.** Exclude the child from all written activities

<details><summary>✅ Answer aur detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: C. Add distinct words or symbols to the two colour-coded instructions**

- **A. Kyun galat hai:** Chhota print reading ko aur difficult bana sakta hai. Red aur green distinguish karne ki original difficulty phir bhi rahegi. Isliye smaller coloured text useful adaptation nahi hai.

- **B. Kyun galat hai:** Spectacle power focusing error correct karti hai. Red-green colour vision deficiency aam taur par retina ke colour sensing system se related hai. Bina assessment lens power badhane se colour discrimination restore nahi hoti.

- **C. Kyun sahi hai:** Child ordinary text padh sakta hai lekin red aur green ko distinguish nahi kar pa raha. Teacher har instruction ke saath alag word, shape ya symbol add kare, toh child colour par depend kiye bina instruction pehchaan sakta hai. Isse same learning activity mein participation possible hoti hai.

- **D. Kyun galat hai:** Child ki ordinary reading ability preserved hai. Actual barrier colour-only instructions hain, jise accessible format se address kiya ja sakta hai. Sab written activities se exclude karna unnecessary hai.

**Exam clue:** Information sirf colour par dependent mat rakho.

**Clinical reference:** [Clinical guidance](https://www.nei.nih.gov/eye-health-information/eye-conditions-and-diseases/color-blindness). · [Tamanash Eye Foundation school adaptation](https://www.tamanash.in/eye_foundation/School/ColorBlindness1.aspx)
