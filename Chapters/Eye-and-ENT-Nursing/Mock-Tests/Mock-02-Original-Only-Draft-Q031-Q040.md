### Q31. Two days after epithelium-off corneal cross-linking, the prescribed bandage contact lens falls onto the patient's pillow. Which telephone instruction is most appropriate?

**Difficulty:** 🟡 Medium · **Priority:** 🔴 MOST IMPORTANT · Original mock-exclusive practice

- **A.** Rinse the lens under tap water and reinsert it
- **B.** Do not reinsert the lens and contact the treating eye service for advice
- **C.** Replace it with the patient's usual rigid contact lens
- **D.** Leave the eye untreated until the next annual appointment

<details><summary>✅ Answer aur detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: B. Do not reinsert the lens and contact the treating eye service for advice**

- **A. Kyun galat hai:** Tap water sterile nahi hota. Healing corneal surface par contaminated lens lagane se infection ka risk badh sakta hai.

- **B. Kyun sahi hai:** Epithelium-off cross-linking ke baad cornea ki outer cell layer heal kar rahi hoti hai. Bandage lens isi surface ko temporarily protect karta hai. Gira hua lens khud se wapas mat lagao aur treating eye service ko inform karo. Increasing pain, redness ya vision change mein urgent assessment chahiye.

- **C. Kyun galat hai:** Regular rigid contact lens therapeutic bandage lens ka substitute nahi hai. Healing surface par pressure aur irritation ho sakti hai.

- **D. Kyun galat hai:** Scheduled annual appointment tak wait karna safe nahi hai. Early postoperative surface healing ko timely review ki zaroorat ho sakti hai.

**Exam clue:** Therapeutic lens girne par self-reinsertion nahi karni hai.

**Clinical reference:** [Clinical guidance](https://moorfields.nhs.uk/eye-conditions/keratoconus/corneal-cross-linking).

### Q32. A patient scheduled for elective corneal cross-linking reports a previous episode of herpes simplex keratitis that is absent from the preoperative history. What should the nurse do before the procedure?

**Difficulty:** 🔴 Difficult · **Priority:** 🔴 MOST IMPORTANT · Original mock-exclusive practice

- **A.** Proceed without documentation because the infection occurred in the past
- **B.** Advise the patient to start leftover steroid drops
- **C.** Inform the corneal specialist because previous herpetic disease may affect the treatment plan
- **D.** Cancel every future ophthalmic procedure permanently

<details><summary>✅ Answer aur detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: C. Inform the corneal specialist because previous herpetic disease may affect the treatment plan**

- **A. Kyun galat hai:** Previous herpetic keratitis clinically relevant history hai. Purana episode resolve hone par bhi virus reactivate ho sakta hai.

- **B. Kyun galat hai:** Leftover steroid drops khud se start karna unsafe hai. Active viral epithelial disease mein inappropriate steroid use corneal damage worsen kar sakta hai.

- **C. Kyun sahi hai:** Cross-linking ke baad herpetic reactivation ka concern ho sakta hai. Specialist ko history verify karke suitability, precautions aur treatment plan decide karna chahiye. Nurse ko information procedure se pehle communicate karni hai.

- **D. Kyun galat hai:** Har future eye procedure ko permanently cancel karna justified nahi hai. Individual risk assessment aur specialist decision zaroori hai.

**Exam clue:** Past infection history elective corneal procedure ki safety ko change kar sakti hai.

### Q33. At a serial corneal tomography visit for keratoconus, the patient reports wearing rigid contact lenses until immediately before today's scan despite a previous lens-free preparation instruction. Why should this be documented?

**Difficulty:** 🔴 Difficult · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD · Original mock-exclusive practice

- **A.** Recent lens wear can temporarily influence corneal shape measurements and complicate comparison
- **B.** Contact lenses permanently stop all corneal progression
- **C.** Tomography measures only retinal blood flow
- **D.** Recent lens wear proves that cross-linking has failed

<details><summary>✅ Answer aur detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: A. Recent lens wear can temporarily influence corneal shape measurements and complicate comparison**

- **A. Kyun sahi hai:** Tomography corneal shape aur thickness assess karti hai. Rigid lens wear se temporary shape alteration ho sakti hai. Different preparation conditions mein serial readings compare karna misleading ho sakta hai. Treating team repeat scan ya appropriate lens-free interval decide karegi.

- **B. Kyun galat hai:** Contact lens vision correction mein help karta hai, lekin ectatic progression ko permanently stop karne ki guarantee nahi deta.

- **C. Kyun galat hai:** Corneal tomography anterior aur posterior corneal structure assess karti hai. Retinal blood flow iska primary measurement nahi hai.

- **D. Kyun galat hai:** Ek preparation difference ya ek scan alone treatment failure establish nahi karta. Serial specialist interpretation ki need hai.

**Exam clue:** Serial measurements ko comparable conditions mein lena important hai. Lens-free interval individualized hota hai.

**Clinical reference:** [Clinical guidance](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11621536/).

### Q34. Before conventional ultraviolet-A corneal cross-linking, a patient asks why the surgeon measures corneal thickness rather than deciding solely from spectacle power. Which explanation is appropriate?

**Difficulty:** 🔴 Difficult · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD · Original mock-exclusive practice

- **A.** Thickness establishes the patient's colour vision
- **B.** Spectacle power alone specifies the safe ultraviolet exposure
- **C.** The measurement is intended to calculate tear production
- **D.** Corneal thickness helps determine procedural safety for deeper ocular structures

<details><summary>✅ Answer aur detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: D. Corneal thickness helps determine procedural safety for deeper ocular structures**

- **A. Kyun galat hai:** Colour vision retina aur visual pathways se related hai. Corneal thickness se colour discrimination assess nahi hoti.

- **B. Kyun galat hai:** Spectacle power focusing error batati hai, lekin corneal tissue thickness aur ultraviolet transmission ka reliable substitute nahi hai.

- **C. Kyun galat hai:** Tear production ko separate tests se assess kiya ja sakta hai. Pachymetry corneal thickness measure karti hai.

- **D. Kyun sahi hai:** Cross-linking mein riboflavin aur ultraviolet-A energy use hoti hai. Adequate corneal thickness aur protocol selection deeper cells, especially endothelium, ko excessive exposure se protect karne mein important hain. Specialist protocol-specific suitability decide karta hai.

**Exam clue:** Pachymetry tissue safety assessment hai, sirf refraction test nahi. Specialist-level detail enrichment hai.

### Q35. After enucleation, a caregiver notices a clear temporary shell in the healing socket and asks why the definitive artificial eye has not yet been fitted. What is the primary function of the temporary device?

**Difficulty:** 🟡 Medium · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD · Original mock-exclusive practice

- **A.** It restores retinal light perception
- **B.** It helps preserve socket shape and conjunctival fornices while healing occurs
- **C.** It replaces the optic nerve with an artificial nerve
- **D.** It permanently eliminates the need for an ocularist

<details><summary>✅ Answer aur detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: B. It helps preserve socket shape and conjunctival fornices while healing occurs**

- **A. Kyun galat hai:** Enucleation mein eyeball remove hota hai. Transparent conformer retina ya light perception restore nahi karta.

- **B. Kyun sahi hai:** Conformer temporary shell hai jo healing socket mein space aur conjunctival fornices maintain karne mein help karta hai. Fornices eyelid aur socket lining ke folds hote hain. Healing ke baad ocularist suitable prosthesis fit kar sakta hai.

- **C. Kyun galat hai:** Conformer mechanical support provide karta hai. Ye optic nerve function ko electrically ya biologically replace nahi karta.

- **D. Kyun galat hai:** Definitive prosthesis ke liye fitting aur follow-up ki need rehti hai. Conformer permanent customized artificial eye ka replacement nahi hai.

**Exam clue:** Conformer ka role temporary healing support hai.

### Q36. A patient has a severely disfigured nonseeing eye that remains anatomically present. The specialist discusses a cosmetic prosthetic option fitted over the residual globe without removing it. Which device matches that description?

**Difficulty:** 🔴 Difficult · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD · Original mock-exclusive practice

- **A.** An intraocular lens implanted after cataract extraction
- **B.** A temporary postoperative wound drain
- **C.** A scleral shell prosthesis
- **D.** A definitive prosthesis intended for an empty enucleated socket

<details><summary>✅ Answer aur detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: C. A scleral shell prosthesis**

- **A. Kyun galat hai:** Intraocular lens natural lens ki optical function replace karne ke liye eye ke andar lagta hai. Ye residual disfigured globe ke upar cosmetic shell nahi hai.

- **B. Kyun galat hai:** Wound drain fluid removal ke liye use hota hai. Iska purpose long-term cosmetic restoration nahi hai.

- **C. Kyun sahi hai:** Scleral shell residual globe ke upar fitted cosmetic prosthesis ho sakta hai. Globe present rehne aur socket empty hone mein distinction important hai. Ocularist aur ophthalmologist suitability decide karte hain.

- **D. Kyun galat hai:** Enucleated socket mein globe absent hota hai. Uske liye fitted ocular prosthesis aur residual globe ke upar fitted scleral shell ka anatomical context alag hai.

**Exam clue:** Device selection residual globe ki presence par depend kar sakti hai.

### Q37. A long-term artificial-eye user reports new rubbing discomfort and recurrent discharge. The external prosthesis looks scratched and has not been professionally serviced for years. Which plan is appropriate?

**Difficulty:** 🟡 Medium · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD · Original mock-exclusive practice

- **A.** Arrange ocularist review of fit and surface condition together with clinical socket assessment
- **B.** Advise the patient to sand the prosthesis at home
- **C.** Assume recurrent discharge is always normal in an artificial socket
- **D.** Recommend leaving symptoms unreported until the prosthesis breaks

<details><summary>✅ Answer aur detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: A. Arrange ocularist review of fit and surface condition together with clinical socket assessment**

- **A. Kyun sahi hai:** Rough ya poorly fitting prosthesis socket lining ko irritate kar sakta hai. Professional cleaning, polishing ya replacement ki need assess hoti hai. New discharge aur discomfort mein infection ya other socket problem ka clinical assessment bhi important hai.

- **B. Kyun galat hai:** Home sanding se surface aur fit damage ho sakte hain. Prosthesis servicing trained ocularist karega.

- **C. Kyun galat hai:** Kuch mild discharge common ho sakta hai, lekin new recurrent discharge aur discomfort ko automatically normal nahi maana chahiye.

- **D. Kyun galat hai:** Symptoms mechanical irritation ya infection ka clue ho sakte hain. Prosthesis tootne tak wait karna appropriate nahi hai.

**Exam clue:** Ocularist device assess karta hai aur eye service socket ki health assess karti hai.

**Clinical reference:** [Clinical guidance](https://www.cuh.nhs.uk/patient-information/removal-of-an-eye-enucleation-and-evisceration/).

### Q38. A patient with a healed unilateral enucleation socket returns to a workshop job where flying debris is possible. Which counselling addresses the most important long-term visual safety issue?

**Difficulty:** 🟡 Medium · **Priority:** 🔴 MOST IMPORTANT · Original mock-exclusive practice

- **C.** Avoid regular examination of the remaining eye if it is currently asymptomatic
- **D.** Use appropriate impact-resistant protection for the remaining seeing eye and maintain ophthalmic follow-up

<details><summary>✅ Answer aur detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: D. Use appropriate impact-resistant protection for the remaining seeing eye and maintain ophthalmic follow-up**

- **A. Kyun galat hai:** Ordinary cosmetic spectacle lenses har occupational hazard ke liye certified protection provide nahi karte. Workplace hazard ke hisaab se suitable protective eyewear chahiye.

- **B. Kyun galat hai:** Artificial eye visual function restore nahi karta. Remaining seeing eye ki injury ka functional impact particularly serious ho sakta hai.

- **C. Kyun galat hai:** Asymptomatic eye mein bhi routine examination se treatable problems identify ho sakti hain. Follow-up skip karna safe strategy nahi hai.

- **D. Kyun sahi hai:** Remaining seeing eye patient ki functional vision ke liye crucial hai. Suitable impact-resistant eye protection aur regular examination injury prevention aur early detection mein help karte hain.

**Exam clue:** Monocular patient ki remaining vision ko actively protect karo.

### Q39. A patient with low vision can independently organize medicines but has accidentally confused two similarly shaped prescription bottles because their small labels are unreadable. Which discharge intervention most directly reduces this medication error risk?

**Difficulty:** 🔴 Difficult · **Priority:** 🔴 MOST IMPORTANT · Original mock-exclusive practice

- **A.** Tell the patient to identify both medicines solely by bottle colour
- **B.** Coordinate accessible pharmacy labels and verify that the patient can identify each medicine and its dosing instructions
- **C.** Remove the original labels and rely on memory
- **D.** Assume that all patients with low vision must transfer medication responsibility to a relative

<details><summary>✅ Answer aur detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: B. Coordinate accessible pharmacy labels and verify that the patient can identify each medicine and its dosing instructions**

- **A. Kyun galat hai:** Bottle colour change ho sakta hai aur low vision mein similar colours distinguish karna difficult ho sakta hai. Colour alone reliable medication identifier nahi hai.

- **B. Kyun sahi hai:** Pharmacy se readable large-print ya audio label jaise accessible format arrange karo jo patient ke liye actually usable ho. Patient se correct medicine, dose aur timing identify karwa kar understanding aur practical safety verify karo.

- **C. Kyun galat hai:** Original labels hataane se medicine identity, expiry aur dose information lose ho sakti hai. Memory-only strategy error risk badhati hai.

- **D. Kyun galat hai:** Low vision ka matlab automatically independence lose hona nahi hai. Patient ki ability, preference aur available aids assess karke individualized support do.

**Exam clue:** Accessible labels ke saath actual safe-use demonstration verify karo.

**Clinical reference:** [Clinical guidance](https://www.afb.org/blindness-and-low-vision/your-rights/rx-label-enable-campaign/guidelines-prescription-labeling).

### Q40. A child with confirmed red-green colour vision deficiency can read ordinary text clearly but repeatedly misinterprets school worksheets that distinguish two instructions only by red and green ink. Which adaptation best addresses this specific difficulty?

**Difficulty:** 🟡 Medium · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD · Original mock-exclusive practice

- **A.** Use still smaller red and green print
- **B.** Increase spectacle power without an eye assessment
- **C.** Add distinct words or symbols to the two colour-coded instructions
- **D.** Exclude the child from all written activities

<details><summary>✅ Answer aur detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: C. Add distinct words or symbols to the two colour-coded instructions**

- **A. Kyun galat hai:** Small print se reading aur difficult ho sakti hai. Colour-only distinction ki problem solve nahi hoti.

- **B. Kyun galat hai:** Colour vision deficiency aur refractive focusing error alag problems ho sakti hain. Extra lens power bina assessment ke colour recognition restore nahi karti.

- **C. Kyun sahi hai:** Words, shapes ya symbols se information colour ke bina bhi distinguish ho sakti hai. Child ko same activity mein accessible way se participate karne mein help milti hai.

- **D. Kyun galat hai:** Child ki ordinary reading ability preserved hai. Appropriate accommodation se participation support karna chahiye, unnecessary exclusion nahi.

**Exam clue:** Information sirf colour par dependent mat rakho.

**Clinical reference:** [Clinical guidance](https://www.nei.nih.gov/eye-health-information/eye-conditions-and-diseases/color-blindness).
