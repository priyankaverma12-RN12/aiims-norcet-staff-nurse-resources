### Q91. During prescribed amblyopia patching, a child repeatedly tilts the chin to see around the edge of a spectacle-mounted patch. What is the most useful immediate adjustment?

**Difficulty:** 🟡 Medium · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD · Original mock-exclusive practice

- **A.** Increase the patching hours without checking whether the patch works
- **B.** Shift the patch to the weaker eye without an order
- **C.** Remove the child's prescribed corrective spectacles permanently
- **D.** Check patch coverage and fit so the child cannot peek around it

<details><summary>✅ Answer aur detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: D. Check patch coverage and fit so the child cannot peek around it**

- **A. Kyun galat hai:** Extra time bhi ineffective ho sakta hai agar child stronger eye se dekh raha hai.

- **B. Kyun galat hai:** Weaker eye ko occlude karne se intended treatment mechanism reverse ho sakta hai.

- **C. Kyun galat hai:** Refractive correction amblyopia care ka important part ho sakti hai. Patch fitting problem ki wajah se spectacles discontinue nahi karne chahiye.

- **D. Kyun sahi hai:** Patching ka purpose stronger eye ka input temporarily restrict karna hai. Agar child patch ke side se dekh raha hai toh prescribed therapy ka effective exposure kam ho jaata hai. Fit ko verify karke team ki recommended patching method follow karo.

### Q93. A caregiver records 90 minutes of effective occlusion on a day when the clinician prescribed two hours. The child also wore the patch loosely for another 30 minutes while peeking around it. What percentage of the prescribed effective duration was achieved?

**Difficulty:** 🟡 Medium · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD · Original mock-exclusive practice

- **A.** 75 percent
- **B.** 50 percent
- **C.** 100 percent
- **D.** 125 percent

<details><summary>✅ Answer aur detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: A. 75 percent**

- **A. Kyun sahi hai:** Do hours 120 minutes hote hain. Effective occlusion 90 minutes hai. Adherence 90 divided by 120 multiplied by 100 se 75 percent hai. Peeking wale ineffective minutes ko effective dose mat count karo.

- **B. Kyun galat hai:** Fifty percent ka matlab 60 effective minutes hota, jabki actual 90 minutes hain.

- **C. Kyun galat hai:** Total wearing time 120 minutes hai lekin 30 minutes mein occlusion effective nahi thi.

- **D. Kyun galat hai:** 125 percent ke liye 150 effective minutes chahiye hote. Actual duration prescribed duration se kam hai.
