### Q61. A four-year-old has painless middle-ear effusion for six weeks after a respiratory infection and no identified developmental risk. The family requests immediate ear tube surgery. Which plan best reflects the 2022 pediatric guideline?

**Difficulty:** 🟡 Medium · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD · Original mock-exclusive practice

- **A.** Arrange bilateral tubes solely because fluid is present
- **B.** Continue observation and reassess rather than insert tubes solely for this short episode
- **C.** Begin indefinite oral antibiotics to maintain drainage
- **D.** Cancel all follow-up because the child has no earache

<details><summary>✅ Answer aur detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: B. Continue observation and reassess rather than insert tubes solely for this short episode**

- **A. Kyun galat hai:** Fluid ki presence alone short-duration episode mein surgery justify nahi karti.

- **B. Kyun sahi hai:** Middle ear mein fluid infection resolve hone ke baad bhi reh sakta hai. Single episode three months se chhota hai aur child at-risk nahi hai. Immediate tube insertion ke benefits clear nahi hain, isliye observation appropriate hai.

- **C. Kyun galat hai:** Effusion without acute infection mein indefinite antibiotic use appropriate nahi hai. Adverse effects aur resistance ka risk hai.

- **D. Kyun galat hai:** Painless effusion bhi hearing affect kar sakti hai. Persistence aur hearing concerns reassess karne chahiye.
