### Q1. An adult reports that telephone speech became indistinct in one ear overnight. No wax or middle-ear fluid is seen. Which information should the nurse record most precisely for the specialist?

**Difficulty:** 🟡 Medium · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD · Original mock-exclusive practice

- **B.** The time when hearing was last normal and when the change began
- **C.** The patient's favourite music
- **D.** The next routine annual appointment only

<details><summary>✅ Answer aur detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: B. The time when hearing was last normal and when the change began**

- **A. Kyun galat hai:** Brand se symptom onset establish nahi hota.

- **B. Kyun sahi hai:** Sudden hearing loss mein onset ka time assessment aur treatment ki urgency decide karne mein help karta hai. Last normal hearing aur symptoms ka start dono note karo.

- **C. Kyun galat hai:** Music preference clinical urgency determine nahi karti.

**Clinical reference:** [Guideline](https://www.entnet.org/resource/aao-hnsf-updated-cpg-shl-press-release-fact-sheet/).

**Referral safety:** NICE NG98 recommends immediate assessment (within 24 hours) for unexplained sudden hearing loss arising within the last 30 days. [NICE NG98](https://www.nice.org.uk/guidance/ng98/chapter/Recommendations). Onset record karna urgent referral ko delay nahi karna chahiye.

**Clinical audit note (27 Nov 2026):** Onset ko exact duration aur last-known-normal hearing ke saath record karo.
