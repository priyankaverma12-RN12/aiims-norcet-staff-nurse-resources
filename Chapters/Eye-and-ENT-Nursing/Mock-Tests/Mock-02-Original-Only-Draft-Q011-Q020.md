### Q11. A patient initially treated for a mildly irritated red eye now describes a deep boring pain that wakes them at night and radiates toward the brow. Which revision of the initial assessment is most appropriate?

**Difficulty:** 🟡 Medium · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD · Original mock-exclusive practice

- **A.** Continue routine treatment for uncomplicated episcleritis
- **B.** Arrange prompt ophthalmic assessment for possible scleritis
- **C.** Record the pain as normal age-related near-vision loss
- **D.** Assume that the redness is caused by a painless subconjunctival hemorrhage

<details><summary>✅ Answer aur detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: B. Arrange prompt ophthalmic assessment for possible scleritis**

- **A. Kyun galat hai:** Episcleritis superficial layer ki inflammation hai. Isme mild irritation ho sakti hai, lekin naya deep pain initial assessment ko doubtful banata hai.

- **B. Kyun sahi hai:** Sclera eye ki strong outer wall hai. Jab isme deep inflammation hoti hai, pain severe ho sakta hai aur brow tak ja sakta hai. Isliye nurse ko prompt specialist review arrange karna chahiye, bina pain ko simple redness maan kar ignore kiye.

- **C. Kyun galat hai:** Presbyopia mein near focus gradually weak hota hai. Isse deep painful red eye explain nahi hoti.

- **D. Kyun galat hai:** Subconjunctival hemorrhage mein superficial blood patch usually painless hota hai. Ye pain pattern us diagnosis ke liye reassuring nahi hai.

**Exam clue:** Deep pain aur superficial irritation mein difference pehchano.

### Q12. A patient with longstanding rheumatoid arthritis has a newly noticed area of marked scleral thinning. There is surprisingly little pain. Which assessment conclusion is safest?

**Difficulty:** 🔴 Difficult · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD · Original mock-exclusive practice

- **A.** The lack of pain excludes destructive scleral inflammation
- **B.** The finding is expected from ordinary presbyopia
- **C.** The sclera should be pressed to assess the depth of the thinning
- **D.** Painless necrotizing scleral disease remains possible and requires prompt specialist assessment

<details><summary>✅ Answer aur detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: D. Painless necrotizing scleral disease remains possible and requires prompt specialist assessment**

- **A. Kyun galat hai:** Necrotizing disease ka ek rare form kam pain ke saath bhi ho sakta hai. Isliye painless presentation ko exclusion criterion mat banao.

- **B. Kyun galat hai:** Presbyopia lens ki accommodation se related hai. Isse sclera ki localized thinning nahi hoti.

- **C. Kyun galat hai:** Thinned sclera structurally weak ho sakti hai. Nurse ko pressure daal kar depth check nahi karni chahiye. Specialist safe examination decide karega.

- **D. Kyun sahi hai:** Scleromalacia perforans necrotizing anterior scleritis ka painless form ho sakta hai. Rheumatoid arthritis ke context mein new thinning important red flag hai. Eye ko protect karke prompt ophthalmology assessment arrange karo.

**Exam clue:** Painless ka matlab hamesha harmless nahi hota.

**Clinical reference:** [Clinical guidance](https://eyewiki.aao.org/Scleritis).

### Q13. During admission for confirmed scleritis, a patient mentions persistent bloody nasal crusting and recent blood-stained sputum. Which action best addresses the significance of these symptoms?

**Difficulty:** 🔴 Difficult · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD · Original mock-exclusive practice

- **A.** Communicate the combined ocular, upper-airway and pulmonary findings for urgent systemic assessment
- **B.** Document the symptoms only in the routine nasal hygiene record
- **C.** Reassure the patient that scleritis cannot be associated with disease outside the eye
- **D.** Wait for the ocular redness to resolve before reporting the respiratory symptoms

<details><summary>✅ Answer aur detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: A. Communicate the combined ocular, upper-airway and pulmonary findings for urgent systemic assessment**

- **A. Kyun sahi hai:** Scleritis kabhi systemic vasculitis ke saath ho sakti hai. Vasculitis mein blood vessel walls inflamed hoti hain. Nasal crusting aur blood-stained sputum granulomatosis with polyangiitis jaise disorder ka suspicion raise karte hain. Nurse ko combined findings escalate karni hain aur breathing, circulation aur bleeding severity assess karni hai.

- **B. Kyun galat hai:** Nasal hygiene record useful ho sakta hai, lekin ye systemic pattern ka medical review replace nahi karta.

- **C. Kyun galat hai:** Scleritis sirf local eye disease nahi hoti. Autoimmune aur infectious systemic conditions ka assessment relevant ho sakta hai.

- **D. Kyun galat hai:** Blood-stained sputum ko eye treatment complete hone tak delay nahi karna chahiye. Respiratory status ke hisaab se urgent escalation zaroori hai.

**Exam clue:** Eye inflammation ke saath extraocular red flags ko ek hi handover mein jodo.

**Clinical reference:** [Clinical guidance](https://eyewiki.aao.org/Scleritis).

### Q14. A patient has experienced several clinician-confirmed episodes of episcleritis. At follow-up the patient reports persistent diarrhea and intermittent bloody stools. Which nursing action adds the most useful information to the care plan?

**Difficulty:** 🟡 Medium · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD · Original mock-exclusive practice

- **A.** Treat the bowel symptoms as an expected adverse effect of the red eye
- **B.** Arrange repeat refraction without communicating the bowel history
- **C.** Communicate the bowel symptoms for assessment of a possible associated inflammatory disorder
- **D.** Advise the patient to independently start systemic corticosteroids

<details><summary>✅ Answer aur detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: C. Communicate the bowel symptoms for assessment of a possible associated inflammatory disorder**

- **A. Kyun galat hai:** Episcleritis ka bowel bleeding se direct normal side effect wala relationship nahi hai. Is symptom ko separate assessment ki need hai.

- **B. Kyun galat hai:** Refraction focusing problem assess karti hai. Ye systemic inflammation ke clues ko address nahi karti.

- **C. Kyun sahi hai:** Recurrent episcleritis kuch systemic inflammatory conditions, including inflammatory bowel disease, ke saath occur kar sakti hai. Bloody stools aur diarrhea cause establish nahi karte, lekin medical team ko history report karna zaroori hai.

- **D. Kyun galat hai:** Systemic corticosteroid khud se start karna unsafe hai. Infection aur doosre causes assess karne ke baad treating clinician plan decide karta hai.

**Exam clue:** Recurrent superficial inflammation mein systemic history ka value samjho.

**Clinical reference:** [Clinical guidance](https://eyewiki.aao.org/Episcleritis).

### Q15. A clinician suspects inflammation of the posterior sclera in an adult with deep ocular pain and reduced vision despite limited visible anterior redness. Which ordered investigation should the nurse recognize as particularly helpful for assessing posterior scleral thickening?

**Difficulty:** 🔴 Difficult · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD · Original mock-exclusive practice

- **A.** A Schirmer tear-production test
- **B.** B-scan ocular ultrasonography
- **C.** A colour plate test alone
- **D.** An objective refraction measurement

<details><summary>✅ Answer aur detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: B. B-scan ocular ultrasonography**

- **A. Kyun galat hai:** Schirmer strip tear production assess karti hai. Ye posterior wall ki structure nahi dikhati.

- **B. Kyun sahi hai:** B-scan ultrasound sound waves se posterior ocular structures assess kar sakta hai. Posterior scleritis mein wall thickening aur surrounding fluid jaise findings diagnosis support karte hain. Specialist ko overall picture interpret karni hoti hai.

- **C. Kyun galat hai:** Colour plate test colour perception assess karta hai. Ye wall thickening ka structural image nahi deta.

- **D. Kyun galat hai:** Refraction spectacle power estimate karti hai. Deep scleral inflammation assess karne ke liye ye primary investigation nahi hai.

**Exam clue:** Posterior wall disease mein front ki redness se severity decide mat karo. Specialist investigation detail enrichment hai.

### Q16. An adult treated for herpes zoster involving the ophthalmic division of the trigeminal nerve now has crusted skin lesions. The original eye examination was normal. Which follow-up instruction is most appropriate?

**Difficulty:** 🟡 Medium · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD · Original mock-exclusive practice

- **A.** Cancel ophthalmic follow-up once the skin rash has crusted
- **B.** Use any remaining steroid eye drops whenever redness develops
- **C.** Wait for the skin vesicles to recur before reporting visual symptoms
- **D.** Keep planned ophthalmic follow-up and report new eye pain, redness or visual changes promptly

<details><summary>✅ Answer aur detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: D. Keep planned ophthalmic follow-up and report new eye pain, redness or visual changes promptly**

- **A. Kyun galat hai:** Skin healing se delayed ocular complication exclude nahi hoti. Isliye planned eye assessment cancel nahi karni chahiye.

- **B. Kyun galat hai:** Steroid drops ka indication clinician decide karta hai. Unsupervised steroid use infection ya pressure-related problem worsen kar sakta hai.

- **C. Kyun galat hai:** Eye symptoms skin vesicles ke recur hone ke bina bhi develop ho sakte hain. Visual symptoms report karne mein wait karna unsafe hai.

- **D. Kyun sahi hai:** Varicella-zoster virus eye ki different layers ko affect kar sakta hai, kabhi skin rash settle hone ke baad bhi. Planned follow-up aur new visual symptoms ki prompt reporting complications jaldi identify karne mein madad karti hai.

**Exam clue:** Rash recovery aur ocular recovery ko ek hi cheez mat samjho.

**Clinical reference:** [Clinical guidance](https://eyewiki.aao.org/Herpes_Zoster_Ophthalmicus).

### Q17. A patient with established thyroid eye disease has normal thyroid hormone results at review but increasing orbital swelling and discomfort. Which interpretation should guide the nurse's handover?

**Difficulty:** 🔴 Difficult · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD · Original mock-exclusive practice

- **A.** Normal thyroid results do not establish that the orbital inflammatory disease is inactive
- **B.** The eye disease must have resolved because thyroid hormones are normal
- **C.** The swelling establishes bacterial conjunctivitis without further assessment
- **D.** The nurse should increase the patient's thyroid medicine until the swelling subsides

<details><summary>✅ Answer aur detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: A. Normal thyroid results do not establish that the orbital inflammatory disease is inactive**

- **A. Kyun sahi hai:** Thyroid eye disease mein immune-mediated orbital tissue inflammation hoti hai. Thyroid hormone control important hai, lekin eye disease activity ka separate assessment chahiye. New swelling aur discomfort specialist review mein communicate karo.

- **B. Kyun galat hai:** Hormone level aur orbital inflammatory activity ek hi measurement nahi hain. Normal blood report se eye disease resolution establish nahi hota.

- **C. Kyun galat hai:** Bacterial conjunctivitis usually ocular surface disease hai. Orbital swelling ki cause clinical examination se assess karni hoti hai.

- **D. Kyun galat hai:** Nurse ko thyroid dose independently change nahi karni chahiye. Thyroid clinician labs aur clinical findings ke basis par dosing decide karega.

**Exam clue:** Biochemical thyroid control aur orbital disease activity ko alag assess karo.

### Q18. A patient considering orbital decompression for thyroid eye disease expects the operation to guarantee normal binocular alignment. Which preoperative teaching is accurate?

**Difficulty:** 🟡 Medium · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD · Original mock-exclusive practice

- **A.** The operation is performed solely to change the lens power
- **B.** Every patient is guaranteed complete resolution of double vision
- **C.** Double vision can persist or newly develop and should be discussed with the surgical team
- **D.** A new postoperative alignment problem should never be reported

<details><summary>✅ Answer aur detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: C. Double vision can persist or newly develop and should be discussed with the surgical team**

- **A. Kyun galat hai:** Orbital decompression eye socket mein extra space create karne ki procedure hai. Lens power change karna iska main purpose nahi hai.

- **B. Kyun galat hai:** Alignment recovery guarantee nahi hoti. Orbital tissues aur eye muscles ki position change hone se double vision persist kar sakti hai.

- **C. Kyun sahi hai:** Decompression selected patients mein optic nerve pressure ya bulging reduce kar sakti hai. Surgery ke baad alignment outcome variable ho sakta hai, isliye possible persistent ya new diplopia ko preoperative counselling mein discuss karna appropriate hai.

- **D. Kyun galat hai:** New double vision report karni chahiye, especially agar visual acuity ya pain mein bhi change ho. Surgical team severity assess karegi.

**Exam clue:** Operation ka intended benefit aur possible functional adverse outcome dono samjho.

### Q19. A patient develops scleral inflammation with a focal purulent nodule near a previous eye-surgery site. A new immunosuppressive treatment order is being considered. Which concern should the nurse communicate before administration?

**Difficulty:** 🔴 Difficult · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD · Original mock-exclusive practice

- **A.** The surgical site makes an infectious cause impossible
- **B.** Possible infectious scleritis requires clinician clarification before immunosuppression
- **C.** Purulence is a reliable sign that the disease is purely autoimmune
- **D.** The patient can diagnose the cause by trying leftover steroid drops at home

<details><summary>✅ Answer aur detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: B. Possible infectious scleritis requires clinician clarification before immunosuppression**

- **A. Kyun galat hai:** Previous surgery scleral infection ka possible risk context ho sakta hai. Surgical history infection exclude nahi karti.

- **B. Kyun sahi hai:** Purulent nodule infection ka concern raise karta hai. Noninfectious scleritis aur infectious scleritis ka treatment different ho sakta hai. Nurse ko prescribing team se diagnosis aur treatment plan clarify karna chahiye, taaki possible infection untreated na rahe.

- **C. Kyun galat hai:** Purulence autoimmune disease ka specific proof nahi hai. Microbial assessment aur specialist examination required ho sakte hain.

- **D. Kyun galat hai:** Leftover steroid drops se infection mask ya worsen ho sakti hai. Patient ko ghar par trial treatment se diagnosis confirm karne ki advice nahi deni chahiye.

**Exam clue:** Immunosuppression se pehle possible infection ka concern communicate karo.

### Q20. An adult undergoing assessment for uveitis asks why the clinician requests a detailed dilated retinal examination when the front of the eye looks relatively quiet. Which explanation is most appropriate?

**Difficulty:** 🟡 Medium · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD · Original mock-exclusive practice

- **A.** Uveitis always remains limited to the conjunctiva
- **B.** A quiet anterior surface excludes inflammation within the eye
- **C.** Dilation measures the amount of tears produced by the lacrimal gland
- **D.** Inflammation can involve the retina and choroid or vitreous without prominent anterior surface signs

<details><summary>✅ Answer aur detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: D. Inflammation can involve the retina and choroid or vitreous without prominent anterior surface signs**

- **A. Kyun galat hai:** Conjunctiva eye ki superficial covering hai. Uveitis internal tissues ki inflammation hai aur superficial conjunctivitis se alag hai.

- **B. Kyun galat hai:** Front surface quiet hone se posterior ocular inflammation exclude nahi hoti. Patient ki visual symptoms important hain.

- **C. Kyun galat hai:** Tear production Schirmer jaise assessment se evaluate ho sakti hai. Pupil dilation ka purpose internal structures ko better examine karna hai.

- **D. Kyun sahi hai:** Posterior uveitis retina aur choroid ko affect kar sakti hai. Intermediate uveitis mein vitreous involvement hoti hai. Dilated examination clinician ko in deeper structures assess karne mein help karta hai, chahe anterior redness prominent na ho.

**Exam clue:** Internal inflammation ko sirf front redness se judge mat karo.

**Clinical reference:** [Clinical guidance](https://www.nei.nih.gov/eye-health-information/eye-conditions-and-diseases/uveitis).
