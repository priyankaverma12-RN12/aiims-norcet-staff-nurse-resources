### Q11. A patient initially treated for a mildly irritated red eye now describes a deep boring pain that wakes them at night and radiates toward the brow. Which revision of the initial assessment is most appropriate?

**Difficulty:** 🟡 Medium · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD · Original mock-exclusive practice

- **A.** Continue routine treatment for uncomplicated episcleritis
- **B.** Arrange prompt ophthalmic assessment for possible scleritis
- **C.** Record the pain as normal age-related near-vision loss
- **D.** Assume that the redness is caused by a painless subconjunctival hemorrhage

<details><summary>✅ Answer aur detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: B. Arrange prompt ophthalmic assessment for possible scleritis**

- **A. Kyun galat hai:** Is condition mein sclera ke upar wali patli layer, episclera, mein sujan hoti hai. Aam taur par halki jalan ya discomfort hota hai. Lekin jab dard aankh ke andar gehra mehsoos ho aur neend se jaga de, to purane diagnosis par dobara vichar karna zaroori hai. Routine treatment jaari rakhne se serious disease ki pehchan mein deri ho sakti hai.

- **B. Kyun sahi hai:** Sclera aankh ki majboot safed bahari deewar hai. Jab is deewar mein gehri sujan hoti hai, to use scleritis kehte hain. Ismein dard bahut tez ho sakta hai, raat ko neend kharab kar sakta hai aur bhaun tak phail sakta hai. Is patient ke naye symptoms is condition ka suspicion badhate hain. Nurse visual symptoms aur dard assess karegi aur bina deri ophthalmology review arrange karegi. Sirf symptoms se final diagnosis confirm nahi kiya jaata.

- **C. Kyun galat hai:** Presbyopia umr badhne ke saath paas ki cheez par focus karne ki kshamata kam hone ko kehte hain. Patient ko chhote letters padhne mein dikkat hoti hai. Yeh lens ke focusing mechanism se sambandhit hai, isliye achanak gehre dard aur laal aankh ko iske naam par ignore nahi karna chahiye.

- **D. Kyun galat hai:** Subconjunctival hemorrhage mein aankh ki transparent covering ke neeche thoda khoon jama ho jaata hai. Aksar ek saaf laal patch dikhta hai aur dard nahi hota. Is patient ka neend se jagane wala gehra dard is aam pattern se match nahi karta, isliye further assessment zaroori hai.

**Exam clue:** Deep pain aur superficial irritation mein difference pehchano.

**Clinical reference:** [Clinical guidance](https://eyewiki.aao.org/Scleritis). [Independent clinical rereview source](https://doi.org/10.1093/ecco-jcc/jjad108).

### Q12. A patient with longstanding rheumatoid arthritis has a newly noticed area of marked scleral thinning. There is surprisingly little pain. Which assessment conclusion is safest?

**Difficulty:** 🔴 Difficult · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD · Original mock-exclusive practice

- **A.** The lack of pain excludes destructive scleral inflammation
- **B.** The finding is expected from ordinary presbyopia
- **C.** The sclera should be pressed to assess the depth of the thinning
- **D.** Painless necrotizing scleral disease remains possible and requires prompt specialist assessment

<details><summary>✅ Answer aur detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: D. Painless necrotizing scleral disease remains possible and requires prompt specialist assessment**

- **A. Kyun galat hai:** Har destructive scleral disease mein tez dard hona zaroori nahi hai. Scleromalacia perforans mein sclera dheere dheere patli aur kamzor ho sakti hai, lekin dard bahut kam ho sakta hai. Isliye sirf pain score kam hone se nurse serious disease ko rule out nahi karegi.

- **B. Kyun galat hai:** Presbyopia mein lens aur uske focusing mechanism ki umr se sambandhit kshamata kam hoti hai. Isse padhne ke liye chashme ki zaroorat ho sakti hai, lekin sclera ki ek jagah par marked thinning expected nahi hoti. Is finding ke liye alag assessment chahiye.

- **C. Kyun galat hai:** Sclera aankh ki protective wall hai. Jab yeh patli ho jaati hai, to uski structural strength kam ho sakti hai. Us jagah ko dabakar thinning ki depth check karna unsafe hai. Nurse pressure ya unnecessary manipulation se bachegi aur specialist ko examination karne degi.

- **D. Kyun sahi hai:** Scleromalacia perforans necrotizing anterior scleritis ka ek uncommon form hai, jisme sclera ke tissue ko damage hone ke bawajood pain kam ho sakta hai. Longstanding rheumatoid arthritis wale patient mein nayi thinning ko serious warning sign samajhna chahiye. Nurse aankh par pressure nahi dalegi aur urgent ophthalmic assessment arrange karegi. Specialist disease ki extent aur systemic treatment ki zaroorat decide karega.

**Exam clue:** Painless ka matlab hamesha harmless nahi hota.

**Clinical reference:** [Clinical guidance](https://eyewiki.aao.org/Scleritis). [Independent clinical rereview source](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/NBK499944/?report=reader).

### Q13. During admission for confirmed scleritis, a patient mentions persistent bloody nasal crusting and recent blood-stained sputum. Which action best addresses the significance of these symptoms?

**Difficulty:** 🔴 Difficult · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD · Original mock-exclusive practice

- **A.** Communicate the combined ocular, upper-airway and pulmonary findings for urgent systemic assessment
- **B.** Document the symptoms only in the routine nasal hygiene record
- **C.** Reassure the patient that scleritis cannot be associated with disease outside the eye
- **D.** Wait for the ocular redness to resolve before reporting the respiratory symptoms

<details><summary>✅ Answer aur detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: A. Communicate the combined ocular, upper-airway and pulmonary findings for urgent systemic assessment**

- **A. Kyun sahi hai:** Scleritis kabhi poore sharir mein chal rahi inflammatory disease ka ek sign ho sakti hai. Granulomatosis with polyangiitis, ya GPA, mein chhoti aur medium blood vessels mein sujan ho sakti hai aur aankh, naak, lungs aur kidneys affect ho sakte hain. Naak mein khoon wali crust aur sputum mein khoon ko saath dekhkar urgent systemic assessment karwana chahiye. Nurse breathing, oxygen saturation, vital signs aur bleeding ki severity check karegi. Agar patient unstable hai to emergency response activate karegi. Yeh findings GPA ka suspicion badhati hain, lekin diagnosis confirm nahi karti.

- **B. Kyun galat hai:** Naak ke symptoms record karna useful hai, lekin sirf nasal hygiene record mein likhne se lungs aur aankh ke symptoms ka connection medical team tak nahi pahunchta. Sputum mein khoon potentially serious finding hai. Nurse ko in sab findings ko ek saath handover karna chahiye.

- **C. Kyun galat hai:** Sclera ki sujan local infection ya immune-mediated disease ke kaaran ho sakti hai. Kuch patients mein aankh ke bahar joints, blood vessels ya doosre organs ki disease bhi hoti hai. Isliye yeh kehna galat hai ki scleritis kabhi systemic disease se related nahi ho sakti.

- **D. Kyun galat hai:** Aankh ki redness theek hone ka intezar karna unsafe hai, kyunki sputum mein khoon lungs ya airway ki serious problem ka sign ho sakta hai. Nurse respiratory assessment aur escalation abhi karegi. Eye treatment ke response se pulmonary risk ka faisla nahi kiya ja sakta.

**Exam clue:** Eye inflammation ke saath extraocular red flags ko ek hi handover mein jodo.

**Clinical reference:** [Clinical guidance](https://eyewiki.aao.org/Scleritis). [Independent clinical rereview source](https://www.merckmanuals.com/professional/musculoskeletal-and-connective-tissue-disorders/vasculitis/granulomatosis-with-polyangiitis-gpa).

### Q14. A patient has experienced several clinician-confirmed episodes of episcleritis. At follow-up the patient reports persistent diarrhea and intermittent bloody stools. Which nursing action adds the most useful information to the care plan?

**Difficulty:** 🟡 Medium · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD · Original mock-exclusive practice

- **A.** Treat the bowel symptoms as an expected adverse effect of the red eye
- **B.** Arrange repeat refraction without communicating the bowel history
- **C.** Communicate the bowel symptoms for assessment of a possible associated inflammatory disorder
- **D.** Advise the patient to independently start systemic corticosteroids

<details><summary>✅ Answer aur detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: C. Communicate the bowel symptoms for assessment of a possible associated inflammatory disorder**

- **A. Kyun galat hai:** Episcleritis mein aankh ke upar wali layer inflamed hoti hai. Persistent loose stools aur stool mein khoon uska normal local side effect nahi hai. In symptoms ka independent medical assessment chahiye, chahe aankh ki problem mild hi kyon na ho.

- **B. Kyun galat hai:** Refraction test aankh ki focusing power aur chashme ke number ko assess karta hai. Yeh intestine ki sujan ya bleeding ka cause nahi batata. Agar nurse sirf refraction arrange kare aur bowel history report na kare, to important systemic clue miss ho jayega.

- **C. Kyun sahi hai:** Inflammatory bowel disease mein intestine ki chronic sujan hoti hai, jaise Crohn disease ya ulcerative colitis mein. Kuch patients mein aankh ki episclera bhi inflamed ho sakti hai. Repeated episcleritis ke saath diarrhea aur bloody stools milna ek important history hai. Nurse frequency, bleeding aur associated symptoms note karegi, dehydration ya instability assess karegi aur clinician ko report karegi. Isse possible associated disease ki timely investigation ho sakti hai, lekin nurse sirf in findings se IBD diagnose nahi karegi.

- **D. Kyun galat hai:** Systemic corticosteroids immune response aur sujan ko kam karte hain, lekin inke important side effects hain aur infection ko worsen ya mask kar sakte hain. Stool mein khoon ka cause pehle assess karna zaroori hai. Patient ko apni marzi se steroid shuru karne ki salah dena unsafe hai.

**Exam clue:** Recurrent superficial inflammation mein systemic history ka value samjho.

**Clinical reference:** [Clinical guidance](https://eyewiki.aao.org/Episcleritis). [Independent clinical rereview source](https://doi.org/10.1093/ecco-jcc/jjad108).

### Q15. A clinician suspects inflammation of the posterior sclera in an adult with deep ocular pain and reduced vision despite limited visible anterior redness. Which ordered investigation should the nurse recognize as particularly helpful for assessing posterior scleral thickening?

**Difficulty:** 🔴 Difficult · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD · Original mock-exclusive practice

- **A.** A Schirmer tear-production test
- **B.** B-scan ocular ultrasonography
- **C.** A colour plate test alone
- **D.** An objective refraction measurement

<details><summary>✅ Answer aur detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: B. B-scan ocular ultrasonography**

- **A. Kyun galat hai:** Schirmer test mein ek special paper strip se aansu banne ki quantity ka estimate liya jaata hai. Yeh dry eye assessment mein kaam aa sakta hai, lekin aankh ki pichhli deewar kitni moti ya inflamed hai, iska structural image nahi banata.

- **B. Kyun sahi hai:** B-scan ultrasonography sound waves se aankh ke andar aur pichhle structures ka image banati hai. Posterior scleritis mein sclera aur paas ke tissues mote dikh sakte hain aur aaspaas fluid bhi jama ho sakta hai. Kabhi optic nerve ke paas fluid ka pattern T-sign ke roop mein dikhta hai. Yeh findings specialist ko diagnosis assess karne mein madad karti hain. Nurse ordered investigation ko facilitate karegi aur reduced vision ko urgent clinical finding samjhegi. Ultrasound ki ek finding akeli final diagnosis nahi hoti.

- **C. Kyun galat hai:** Colour plate test se patient ki rang pehchanne ki kshamata assess ki ja sakti hai. Yeh visual function ke baare mein information deta hai, lekin posterior sclera ki thickness ka image nahi banata. Isliye structural inflammation ke liye sirf colour test sufficient nahi hai.

- **D. Kyun galat hai:** Objective refraction se aankh ki optical focusing power ka estimate milta hai. Iska main purpose refractive error assess karna hai. Posterior sclera ke inflamed aur thick hone ka suspicion ho to refractive measurement ki jagah appropriate structural investigation chahiye.

**Exam clue:** Posterior wall disease mein front ki redness se severity decide mat karo. Specialist investigation detail enrichment hai.

**Clinical reference:** [Clinical guidance](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6080445/). [Independent clinical rereview source](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/NBK499944/?report=reader).

### Q16. An adult treated for herpes zoster involving the ophthalmic division of the trigeminal nerve now has crusted skin lesions. The original eye examination was normal. Which follow-up instruction is most appropriate?

**Difficulty:** 🟡 Medium · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD · Original mock-exclusive practice

- **A.** Cancel ophthalmic follow-up once the skin rash has crusted
- **B.** Use any remaining steroid eye drops whenever redness develops
- **C.** Wait for the skin vesicles to recur before reporting visual symptoms
- **D.** Keep planned ophthalmic follow-up and report new eye pain, redness or visual changes promptly

<details><summary>✅ Answer aur detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: D. Keep planned ophthalmic follow-up and report new eye pain, redness or visual changes promptly**

- **A. Kyun galat hai:** Skin par crust banne ka matlab rash heal ho raha hai, lekin aankh ke andar ya cornea mein complication baad mein bhi develop ho sakti hai. Initial normal eye examination future disease ko guarantee ke saath rule out nahi karta. Isliye planned follow-up cancel karna theek nahi hai.

- **B. Kyun galat hai:** Steroid eye drops kuch specific inflammatory complications mein specialist ki supervision mein useful ho sakti hain. Lekin bina examination purani drops shuru karne se infection, corneal problem ya raised eye pressure ka risk ho sakta hai. Nayi redness hone par patient ko prescribed follow-up ya urgent review lena chahiye.

- **C. Kyun galat hai:** Herpes zoster se related ocular inflammation nayi skin vesicles ke bina bhi develop ho sakti hai. Agar vision change ho raha hai, to skin rash dobara aane ka wait karna diagnosis aur treatment mein unnecessary delay karega.

- **D. Kyun sahi hai:** Herpes zoster ophthalmicus mein chickenpox wala varicella-zoster virus trigeminal nerve ki eye-related branch mein reactivate hota hai. Skin rash settle hone ke baad bhi cornea ya aankh ke andar inflammation develop ho sakti hai. Isliye nurse patient ko planned eye follow-up maintain karne aur naya pain, redness, light sensitivity ya vision change promptly report karne ki instruction degi. Sudden ya marked vision loss mein urgent assessment chahiye.

**Exam clue:** Rash recovery aur ocular recovery ko ek hi cheez mat samjho.

**Clinical reference:** [Clinical guidance](https://eyewiki.aao.org/Herpes_Zoster_Ophthalmicus). [Independent clinical rereview source](https://www.aao.org/eyenet/article/herpes-zoster-ophthalmicus-is-a-growing-risk).

### Q17. A patient with established thyroid eye disease has normal thyroid hormone results at review but increasing orbital swelling and discomfort. Which interpretation should guide the nurse's handover?

**Difficulty:** 🔴 Difficult · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD · Original mock-exclusive practice

- **A.** Normal thyroid results do not establish that the orbital inflammatory disease is inactive
- **B.** The eye disease must have resolved because thyroid hormones are normal
- **C.** The swelling establishes bacterial conjunctivitis without further assessment
- **D.** The nurse should increase the patient's thyroid medicine until the swelling subsides

<details><summary>✅ Answer aur detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: A. Normal thyroid results do not establish that the orbital inflammatory disease is inactive**

- **A. Kyun sahi hai:** Thyroid eye disease mein immune system aankh ke socket ke muscles aur fat tissues ko affect kar sakta hai, jisse swelling, discomfort aur kabhi double vision hoti hai. Thyroid hormone normal rakhna important hai, lekin blood tests se orbital inflammation ki activity directly measure nahi hoti. Nurse increasing swelling aur discomfort report karegi aur vision ya colour perception change jaise warning signs assess karegi, taaki specialist eye disease ki activity alag se evaluate kar sake.

- **B. Kyun galat hai:** Normal thyroid report se pata chalta hai ki tested hormone levels control mein hain. Isse yeh confirm nahi hota ki aankh ke socket mein immune-related inflammation khatam ho gayi hai. Naye symptoms ko report karna ab bhi zaroori hai.

- **C. Kyun galat hai:** Bacterial conjunctivitis mein aam taur par aankh ki superficial conjunctiva inflamed hoti hai aur discharge ho sakta hai. Orbital swelling ke kai causes hote hain. Thyroid eye disease wale patient mein swelling ko bina proper examination bacterial conjunctivitis declare karna galat hoga.

- **D. Kyun galat hai:** Thyroid medicine ki dose hormone levels, prescribed treatment aur clinician ke assessment par depend karti hai. Orbital swelling apne aap mein dose badhane ka order nahi hai. Nurse treatment independently change nahi karegi aur specialist ko symptoms report karegi.

**Exam clue:** Biochemical thyroid control aur orbital disease activity ko alag assess karo.

**Clinical reference:** [Clinical guidance](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9807259/). [Independent clinical rereview source](https://www.thyroid.org/thyroid-eye-disease/).

### Q18. A patient considering orbital decompression for thyroid eye disease expects the operation to guarantee normal binocular alignment. Which preoperative teaching is accurate?

**Difficulty:** 🟡 Medium · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD · Original mock-exclusive practice

- **A.** The operation is performed solely to change the lens power
- **B.** Every patient is guaranteed complete resolution of double vision
- **C.** Double vision can persist or newly develop and should be discussed with the surgical team
- **D.** A new postoperative alignment problem should never be reported

<details><summary>✅ Answer aur detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: C. Double vision can persist or newly develop and should be discussed with the surgical team**

- **A. Kyun galat hai:** Orbital decompression mein eye socket ke andar swollen tissues ke liye extra jagah banayi jaati hai. Iska purpose selected patients mein protruding eye ya optic nerve par pressure ko kam karna ho sakta hai. Yeh cataract ya chashme ki lens power correct karne wali procedure nahi hai.

- **B. Kyun galat hai:** Double vision tab ho sakti hai jab dono aankhen ek direction mein theek se align nahi hoti hain. Surgery se socket ke tissues ki position badal sakti hai, isliye alignment ka result har patient mein same nahi hota. Decompression se double vision poori tarah khatam hone ki guarantee nahi di ja sakti.

- **C. Kyun sahi hai:** Diplopia ka matlab ek hi object ki do images dikhna hai. Thyroid eye disease mein swollen ya stiff eye muscles alignment disturb kar sakte hain. Orbital decompression se selected patients mein pressure aur protrusion kam ho sakta hai, lekin muscles aur orbital tissues ki position badalne se pehle wali diplopia reh sakti hai ya nayi diplopia develop ho sakti hai. Nurse patient ko benefit aur is risk ke baare mein informed counselling ke liye surgical team se baat karne mein madad karegi.

- **D. Kyun galat hai:** Operation ke baad nayi double vision ko report karna chahiye, kyunki surgical team ko alignment aur healing assess karni hoti hai. Agar saath mein vision kam ho, severe pain ho ya doosra acute warning sign ho, to urgent assessment chahiye. Is symptom ko ignore karne ki salah nahi deni chahiye.

**Exam clue:** Operation ka intended benefit aur possible functional adverse outcome dono samjho.

**Clinical reference:** [Clinical guidance](https://www.thyroid.org/thyroid-eye-disease/). [Independent clinical rereview source](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9727317/).

### Q19. A patient develops scleral inflammation with a focal purulent nodule near a previous eye-surgery site. A new immunosuppressive treatment order is being considered. Which concern should the nurse communicate before administration?

**Difficulty:** 🔴 Difficult · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD · Original mock-exclusive practice

- **A.** The surgical site makes an infectious cause impossible
- **B.** Possible infectious scleritis requires clinician clarification before immunosuppression
- **C.** Purulence is a reliable sign that the disease is purely autoimmune
- **D.** The patient can diagnose the cause by trying leftover steroid drops at home

<details><summary>✅ Answer aur detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: B. Possible infectious scleritis requires clinician clarification before immunosuppression**

- **A. Kyun galat hai:** Eye surgery ke baad sclera mein infection develop hona possible hai. Surgical site par inflammation immune-mediated bhi ho sakti hai, lekin previous surgery infection ko exclude nahi karti. Localized pus wali finding ko clinician tak pahunchana important hai.

- **B. Kyun sahi hai:** Purulent nodule ka matlab pus wala localized swelling hai. Yeh infection ka suspicion badhata hai, khaaskar previous surgical site ke paas. Infectious aur immune-mediated scleritis ki management alag ho sakti hai, aur untreated infection ke saath immunosuppression se nuksan ho sakta hai. Nurse nayi order ko administer karne se pehle prescribing clinician se urgently clarify karegi, aur institutional medication-safety policy follow karegi. Specialist zaroorat ke hisaab se microbiology aur examination decide karega.

- **C. Kyun galat hai:** Pus immune-mediated inflammation ka reliable proof nahi hai. Bacterial ya doosre infectious causes ko consider karna zaroori hai, aur infection aur immune-mediated disease ek saath bhi ho sakti hain. Sirf nodule ki appearance se final cause decide nahi kiya ja sakta.

- **D. Kyun galat hai:** Purani steroid drops ko trial ke roop mein use karke diagnosis nahi kiya ja sakta. Steroids sujan ke signs kam dikha sakti hain aur kuch infections ko worsen kar sakti hain. Patient ko leftover medicine use karne ki jagah urgent clinician review lena chahiye.

**Exam clue:** Immunosuppression se pehle possible infection ka concern communicate karo.

**Clinical reference:** [Clinical guidance](https://eyewiki.aao.org/Scleritis). [Independent clinical rereview source](https://www.aao.org/eyenet/article/surgically-induced-necrotizing-scleritis).

### Q20. An adult undergoing assessment for uveitis asks why the clinician requests a detailed dilated retinal examination when the front of the eye looks relatively quiet. Which explanation is most appropriate?

**Difficulty:** 🟡 Medium · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD · Original mock-exclusive practice

- **A.** Uveitis always remains limited to the conjunctiva
- **B.** A quiet anterior surface excludes inflammation within the eye
- **C.** Dilation measures the amount of tears produced by the lacrimal gland
- **D.** Inflammation can involve the retina and choroid or vitreous without prominent anterior surface signs

<details><summary>✅ Answer aur detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: D. Inflammation can involve the retina and choroid or vitreous without prominent anterior surface signs**

- **A. Kyun galat hai:** Conjunctiva aankh ke safed hisse aur eyelids ke andar ki superficial covering hai. Conjunctivitis us layer ki sujan hai, jabki uveitis aankh ke andar ki inflammatory disease hai. Uveitis ko sirf conjunctiva tak limited samajhne se deeper structures ki disease miss ho sakti hai.

- **B. Kyun galat hai:** Aankh ka front bahar se zyada laal na dikhe, tab bhi andar inflammation ho sakti hai. Posterior ya intermediate uveitis mein floaters aur blurred vision important symptoms ho sakte hain. Isliye quiet anterior appearance deeper disease ko rule out nahi karti.

- **C. Kyun galat hai:** Pupil dilation mein drops se pupil ko bada kiya jaata hai, taaki doctor aankh ke andar ke structures ko achhi tarah examine kar sake. Aansu ki production check karne ke liye Schirmer jaise tests use ho sakte hain. Dilation ka main purpose tear production measure karna nahi hai.

- **D. Kyun sahi hai:** Uveitis ki location alag alag ho sakti hai. Intermediate uveitis mein vitreous, ya aankh ke andar ka gel, mainly involve ho sakta hai. Posterior uveitis mein retina aur choroid affect ho sakte hain. In structures mein inflammation hone par front surface par prominent redness zaroori nahi hai. Dilated retinal examination specialist ko pichhle structures dekhne aur vision ko threaten karne wali changes assess karne mein madad karta hai. Nurse patient ko test ka purpose samjhayegi aur visual deterioration promptly report karegi.

**Exam clue:** Internal inflammation ko sirf front redness se judge mat karo.

**Clinical reference:** [Clinical guidance](https://www.nei.nih.gov/eye-health-information/eye-conditions-and-diseases/uveitis). [Independent clinical rereview source](https://www.nei.nih.gov/eye-health-information/eye-conditions-and-diseases/uveitis).
