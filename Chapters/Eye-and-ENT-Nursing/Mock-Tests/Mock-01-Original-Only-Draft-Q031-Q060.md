### Q31. After an isolated alkali splash, the ocular surface pH becomes physiological during irrigation but rises again after a short pause. What should the nurse anticipate?

**Difficulty:** 🔴 Difficult · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD · Original mock-exclusive practice

- **A.** Discharge because one normal reading proves decontamination
- **B.** Resume irrigation and arrange trained assessment for residual chemical
- **C.** Instil a concentrated acid to counteract the alkali
- **D.** Record the second reading as equipment error without checking

<details><summary>✅ Answer aur detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: B. Resume irrigation and arrange trained assessment for residual chemical**

- **A. Kyun galat hai:** Ek normal reading stable pH prove nahi karti. Chemical residue baad mein release ho sakta hai.

- **B. Kyun sahi hai:** Chemical material tissue ya conjunctival folds mein bacha ho sakta hai. Irrigation band karne ke baad pH dobara badhna continuing exposure ka clue hai. Protocol ke hisab se irrigation resume aur specialist assessment zaroori hai.

- **C. Kyun galat hai:** Acid se neutralize karne ki koshish additional chemical damage aur heat produce kar sakti hai. Safe irrigation preferred hai.

- **D. Kyun galat hai:** Unexplained abnormal reading verify karni chahiye. Ignore karne se exposure continue ho sakta hai.

**Clinical reference:** [Clinical guidance](https://eyewiki.aao.org/Chemical_(Alkali_and_Acid)_Injury_of_the_Conjunctiva_and_Cornea).
