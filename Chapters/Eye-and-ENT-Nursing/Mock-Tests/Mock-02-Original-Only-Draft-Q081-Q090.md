### Q81. After drainage of an auricular hematoma, an athlete asks why the clinician has placed a shaped pressure dressing on the pinna. Which explanation is most appropriate?

**Difficulty:** 🔴 Difficult · **Priority:** 🔴 MOST IMPORTANT · Original mock-exclusive practice

- **A.** It permanently increases cochlear sensitivity
- **B.** It prevents blood from collecting again between the cartilage and its covering
- **C.** It closes the eustachian tube to prevent infection
- **D.** It restores the tympanic membrane after perforation

<details><summary>✅ Answer aur detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: B. It prevents blood from collecting again between the cartilage and its covering**

- **A. Kyun galat hai:** Cochlea inner ear mein hoti hai aur sound vibrations ko nerve signals mein convert karne mein help karti hai. Pinna bahar ka ear hai. Us par pressure dressing lagane ka purpose inner ear ki sensitivity badhana nahi, balki local blood collection ko control karna hai.

- **B. Kyun sahi hai:** Pinna ka cartilage apni nutrition ke liye uski covering, yani perichondrium, par depend karta hai. Jab trauma se inke beech blood jama hota hai, toh cartilage ki nutrition disturb ho sakti hai. Drainage ke baad fitted pressure dressing in layers ko paas rakhkar blood dobara jama hone ka risk kam karti hai. Nurse dressing ke neeche excessive pain, skin damage aur recurrence ke signs monitor karegi aur planned follow-up reinforce karegi.

- **C. Kyun galat hai:** Eustachian tube middle ear ko nasopharynx se connect karti hai aur pressure equalize karne mein help karti hai. Pinna ki dressing external cartilage par kaam karti hai. Isse tube close karna na treatment ka aim hai aur na infection prevention ka mechanism.

- **D. Kyun galat hai:** Tympanic membrane, yani eardrum, ear canal ke andar hoti hai. Pinna ke haematoma ki dressing eardrum ke hole ko repair nahi karti. Agar eardrum injury ka suspicion ho, toh uska separate examination chahiye.

**Exam clue:** Drainage ke baad reaccumulation prevent karna bhi care ka important part hai.

**Clinical reference:** [Clinical guidance](https://www.merckmanuals.com/professional/injuries-poisoning/facial-trauma/external-ear-trauma). · [2025 emergency medicine review](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/39904638/)

### Q82. A patient presents with a human bite wound involving the auricular cartilage. Bleeding is controlled and the wound looks small. Which concern should remain prominent in the nursing handover?

**Difficulty:** 🟡 Medium · **Priority:** 🔴 MOST IMPORTANT · Original mock-exclusive practice

- **A.** The wound is harmless because bleeding has stopped
- **B.** The injury can be managed only by checking hearing acuity
- **C.** A small bite wound carries no microbial contamination risk
- **D.** Cartilage infection risk requires prompt wound and prophylaxis assessment

<details><summary>✅ Answer aur detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: D. Cartilage infection risk requires prompt wound and prophylaxis assessment**

- **A. Kyun galat hai:** Bleeding rukne ka matlab sirf itna hai ki active blood loss control hua hai. Human bite ke through bacteria tissue mein enter kar sakte hain aur cartilage involvement se complication ka risk badh sakta hai. Isliye chhote wound ko bhi appropriately assess karna chahiye.

- **B. Kyun galat hai:** Hearing assessment ear function ke baare mein useful information deti hai. Lekin bite wound mein skin aur cartilage ki injury, contamination aur immunization history bhi assess karni hoti hai. Sirf hearing check karne se infection risk address nahi hota.

- **C. Kyun galat hai:** Human mouth mein naturally bacteria hote hain. Bite se skin tootne par ye bacteria chhote wound mein bhi pahunch sakte hain. Wound ka external size infection risk ko reliably exclude nahi karta.

- **D. Kyun sahi hai:** Cartilage ko nutrition mainly perichondrium se milti hai, isliye infection aur tissue damage serious ho sakte hain. Nurse bite ki timing, depth, contamination aur vaccine history document karke prompt wound assessment arrange karegi. Clinician cleaning, repair aur indicated antibiotic prophylaxis decide karega. Tetanus aur relevant blood-borne exposure assessment local protocol ke hisaab se ki jaayegi.

**Exam clue:** Bleeding rukna aur bite infection risk khatam hona alag baat hain.

**Clinical reference:** [Clinical guidance](https://www.merckmanuals.com/professional/injuries-poisoning/facial-trauma/external-ear-trauma).

### Q83. Several days after upper-ear cartilage piercing, a patient develops painful red swelling of the cartilaginous pinna. The soft lobule is relatively unaffected. Which assessment should this distribution support?

**Difficulty:** 🔴 Difficult · **Priority:** 🔴 MOST IMPORTANT · Original mock-exclusive practice

- **A.** Possible auricular perichondritis requiring prompt clinical assessment
- **B.** Isolated tympanic membrane perforation
- **C.** Uncomplicated middle-ear effusion
- **D.** Normal healing that requires no reassessment

<details><summary>✅ Answer aur detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: A. Possible auricular perichondritis requiring prompt clinical assessment**

- **A. Kyun sahi hai:** Perichondrium cartilage ki covering hai. Jab upper-ear piercing ke baad is area mein inflammation ya infection hoti hai, toh cartilage-bearing pinna red, painful aur swollen ho sakti hai. Soft lobule mein cartilage nahi hoti, isliye uska relatively unaffected rehna useful clue hai. Nurse prompt assessment arrange karegi, kyunki untreated infection cartilage damage kar sakti hai. Ye finding alone final diagnosis nahi hai.

- **B. Kyun galat hai:** Tympanic membrane perforation ear canal ke andar eardrum mein hole hota hai. Isse pain, hearing change ya discharge ho sakta hai. Lekin upper pinna mein selective redness aur swelling ko ye finding explain nahi karti.

- **C. Kyun galat hai:** Middle-ear effusion mein eardrum ke peeche fluid jama hota hai. Patient ko fullness ya conductive hearing difficulty ho sakti hai. Piercing ke baad outer cartilage ki painful swelling is mechanism se match nahi karti.

- **D. Kyun galat hai:** Piercing ke baad halka local discomfort ho sakta hai, lekin badhta pain aur redness ko normal healing assume nahi karna chahiye. Clinician ko infection aur abscess ke signs check karne chahiye, taaki tissue damage ko time par address kiya ja sake.

**Exam clue:** Pinna aur lobule ka distribution diagnosis mein helpful clue hai, absolute proof nahi.

**Clinical reference:** [Clinical guidance](https://www.merckmanuals.com/professional/ear-nose-and-throat-disorders/external-ear-disorders/perichondritis-of-the-ear). · [StatPearls pinna perichondritis](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/NBK572081/)

### Q84. A patient receiving treatment for infectious auricular perichondritis develops a localized fluctuant collection over the cartilage. What should the nurse anticipate discussing urgently with the ENT team?

**Difficulty:** 🔴 Difficult · **Priority:** 🔴 MOST IMPORTANT · Original mock-exclusive practice

- **A.** Discontinuing observation because swelling is now localized
- **B.** Using hearing amplification as definitive treatment
- **C.** Possible drainage and reassessment of antimicrobial management
- **D.** Waiting for the pinna to develop a permanent deformity

<details><summary>✅ Answer aur detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: C. Possible drainage and reassessment of antimicrobial management**

- **A. Kyun galat hai:** Fluctuant swelling ka matlab palpation par andar fluid collection jaisi feeling milna hai. Infection ke dauran ye pus collection ka sign ho sakta hai. Sirf swelling localized hone se ye prove nahi hota ki condition improve ho gayi hai.

- **B. Kyun galat hai:** Hearing aid sound ko amplify karta hai, lekin cartilage ke paas jama pus ko remove nahi karta. Is patient ko infection aur possible abscess ka assessment chahiye, hearing amplification se source control nahi hoga.

- **C. Kyun sahi hai:** Abscess mein pus jama ho sakta hai aur perichondrium cartilage se separate ho sakta hai. Isse cartilage ko nutrition milna disturb ho sakta hai. Nurse worsening pain, swelling, fever aur systemic illness report karegi. ENT team prompt drainage, specimen culture aur antimicrobial plan reassess karegi, jab indicated ho. Nurse khud se abscess puncture nahi karegi.

- **D. Kyun galat hai:** Agar cartilage ko infection se permanent damage ho jaaye, toh pinna ki shape change ho sakti hai. Early treatment ka aim isi complication ko prevent karna hai. Deformity hone ka wait karna unsafe hai.

**Exam clue:** Fluctuant infection mein source control ki need reassess karo.

**Clinical reference:** [Clinical guidance](https://www.merckmanuals.com/professional/ear-nose-and-throat-disorders/external-ear-disorders/perichondritis-of-the-ear).

### Q85. An adult in a humid climate has prominent ear itching and thick canal debris. The clinician sees filamentous material on otoscopy rather than a simple wax plug. Which condition should be specifically considered?

**Difficulty:** 🟡 Medium · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD · Original mock-exclusive practice

- **A.** Otosclerosis
- **B.** Otomycosis
- **C.** Presbycusis
- **D.** Isolated eustachian tube obstruction

<details><summary>✅ Answer aur detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: B. Otomycosis**

- **A. Kyun galat hai:** Otosclerosis mein middle ear ke stapes bone ki movement restrict ho sakti hai, jisse conductive hearing loss hota hai. Ye external canal mein fungal-looking debris ya prominent itching ka usual cause nahi hai.

- **B. Kyun sahi hai:** Otomycosis outer ear canal ki fungal infection hai. Moist aur humid environment fungal growth ko favour kar sakta hai. Aspergillus aur Candida common organisms hain, lekin unki appearance alag ho sakti hai. Itching aur filamentous debris fungal infection ka suspicion raise karte hain. Clinician otoscopy aur zaroorat ke hisaab se further tests se diagnosis assess karega.

- **C. Kyun galat hai:** Presbycusis age ke saath develop hone wali sensorineural hearing difficulty hai. Ismein external canal mein fungal material produce nahi hota. Is patient ke itching aur debris ko local canal disease se explain karna zyada appropriate hai.

- **D. Kyun galat hai:** Eustachian tube dysfunction middle ear ki ventilation aur pressure balance affect karti hai. Isse blocked-ear sensation ho sakti hai, lekin outer canal mein filamentous material iska characteristic sign nahi hai.

**Exam clue:** Itching aur fungal-looking debris ko ordinary wax se distinguish karo.

**Clinical reference:** [Clinical guidance](https://www.icmr.gov.in/icmrobject/uploads/STWs/1725952350_ent_otorrhoea.pdf). · [Merck acute external otitis, May 2026](https://www.merckmanuals.com/professional/ear-nose-and-throat-disorders/external-ear-disorders/external-otitis-acute)

### Q86. A patient with clinician-confirmed otomycosis asks why professional canal cleaning has been scheduled in addition to prescribed topical antifungal treatment. Which explanation is accurate?

**Difficulty:** 🟡 Medium · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD · Original mock-exclusive practice

- **A.** Cleaning replaces the need for any prescribed antifungal
- **B.** Cleaning permanently changes the auditory nerve threshold
- **C.** Cleaning is intended to create a tympanic membrane perforation
- **D.** Removal of obstructing fungal debris can improve access of topical treatment to affected canal skin

<details><summary>✅ Answer aur detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: D. Removal of obstructing fungal debris can improve access of topical treatment to affected canal skin**

- **A. Kyun galat hai:** Professional cleaning fungal debris ko remove karne mein help karti hai. Lekin remaining infection ke liye prescribed antifungal ki zaroorat ho sakti hai. Patient ko treatment khud se stop nahi karna chahiye, aur clinician response assess karega.

- **B. Kyun galat hai:** Auditory nerve sound information brain tak carry karti hai. Canal cleaning ka main aim outer canal se obstructing material remove karna hai. Agar blockage ki wajah se hearing difficulty hai, toh woh improve ho sakti hai, lekin nerve threshold permanently change karna cleaning ka aim nahi hai.

- **C. Kyun galat hai:** Tympanic membrane perforation eardrum mein injury hoti hai. Cleaning ka purpose infection manage karna hai, eardrum ko damage karna nahi. Trained clinician safe technique choose karega aur membrane ki condition consider karega.

- **D. Kyun sahi hai:** Jab fungal debris canal ki skin ko cover kar leta hai, toh topical medicine affected area tak achhi tarah nahi pahunch sakti. Professional cleaning se debris kam hota hai aur prescribed medicine ka contact improve ho sakta hai. Clinician eardrum ki condition dekhkar suitable antifungal select karega. Patient ko cotton bud ya ghar par irrigation se canal clean nahi karni chahiye.

**Exam clue:** Topical therapy ka access improve karna cleaning ka ek useful role hai.

**Clinical reference:** [Clinical guidance](https://www.merckmanuals.com/professional/ear-nose-and-throat-disorders/external-ear-disorders/external-otitis-acute).

### Q87. A hearing-aid user has recurrent itchy scaling confined to the ear canal where the earmold contacts the skin. Symptoms began after a new earmold was fitted. Which addition to the assessment is useful?

**Difficulty:** 🟡 Medium · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD · Original mock-exclusive practice

- **A.** Review possible contact irritation or allergy and arrange device-fit and skin assessment
- **B.** Assume the hearing aid has caused a middle-ear bone fracture
- **C.** Prescribe indefinite systemic antibiotics without examination
- **D.** Advise the patient to scrape the affected skin each evening

<details><summary>✅ Answer aur detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: A. Review possible contact irritation or allergy and arrange device-fit and skin assessment**

- **A. Kyun sahi hai:** Contact dermatitis mein skin kisi material ya irritant ke contact mein aakar inflamed ho sakti hai. New earmold ke baad itching aur contact area mein scaling hona useful history hai. Nurse onset, device fit aur skin changes document karegi. Audiology aur clinical team material, pressure irritation, allergy aur possible infection assess kar sakti hain.

- **B. Kyun galat hai:** Middle ear ki small bones sound transmit karti hain aur unki fracture usually trauma se related hoti hai. New earmold ke contact area mein itching aur scaling fracture ke typical signs nahi hain. Yahan skin aur device contact assess karna zyada useful hai.

- **C. Kyun galat hai:** Dermatitis bacterial infection ke bina bhi ho sakti hai. Bina examination indefinite antibiotics se side effects aur antimicrobial resistance ka risk hota hai. Clinician diagnosis ke hisaab se treatment decide karega.

- **D. Kyun galat hai:** Skin ko scrape karne se protective barrier damage ho sakta hai. Isse inflammation badh sakti hai aur secondary infection ka risk ho sakta hai. Patient ko rubbing aur scraping avoid karke clinician-directed care follow karni chahiye.

**Exam clue:** Device-contact pattern ko document karo, infection aur dermatitis ko assess karo.

**Clinical reference:** [Clinical guidance](https://www.merckmanuals.com/home/ear-nose-and-throat-disorders/outer-ear-disorders/dermatitis-of-the-ear-canal). · [Merck professional canal dermatitis](https://www.merckmanuals.com/professional/ear%2C-nose%2C-and-throat-disorders/external-ear-disorders/dermatitis-of-the-ear-canal-chronic-otitis-externa?ruleredirectid=31)

### Q88. A cold-water swimmer has bilateral hard bony narrowing of the external auditory canals on ENT examination and recurrent trapping of water after swimming. Which mechanism best explains the conductive component of hearing difficulty?

**Difficulty:** 🟡 Medium · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD · Original mock-exclusive practice

- **A.** Primary degeneration of auditory cortical neurons
- **B.** A congenital failure of the optic nerve to develop
- **C.** External canal narrowing can impede transmission of sound and trap debris
- **D.** Excessive opening of the nasolacrimal duct

<details><summary>✅ Answer aur detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: C. External canal narrowing can impede transmission of sound and trap debris**

- **A. Kyun galat hai:** Auditory cortex brain mein sound ko interpret karne mein help karti hai. Uski disease central auditory processing ko affect kar sakti hai, lekin external ear canal mein visible bony narrowing sound passage ko physically obstruct karti hai. Isliye yahan conductive mechanism important hai.

- **B. Kyun galat hai:** Optic nerve aankh se brain tak vision ki information le jaati hai. Uska developmental defect external ear canal mein bone growth ya water trapping ko explain nahi karta.

- **C. Kyun sahi hai:** Exostoses benign bony growths hoti hain jo repeated cold-water exposure ke saath develop ho sakti hain. Jab canal narrow ho jaati hai, toh sound eardrum tak pahunchne mein rukawat aa sakti hai aur wax ya debris phans sakta hai. Water trapping se infection ka risk bhi badh sakta hai. ENT examination aur hearing assessment se severity aur suitable management decide hoga.

- **D. Kyun galat hai:** Nasolacrimal duct aansuon ko aankh se nose tak drain karta hai. Iska ear canal ke bony narrowing ya sound conduction se direct relation nahi hai.

**Exam clue:** Conductive loss mein sound transmission ka anatomical barrier socho.

**Clinical reference:** [Clinical guidance](https://www.enthealth.org/conditions/conductive-hearing-loss/). · [StatPearls surfer's ear](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/NBK534874/)

### Q89. A child has a dried bean lodged in the external auditory canal. A staff member suggests routine water irrigation. Which concern should the nurse communicate?

**Difficulty:** 🔴 Difficult · **Priority:** 🔴 MOST IMPORTANT · Original mock-exclusive practice

- **A.** Water may swell the organic object and worsen impaction, so an alternative clinician-selected removal method is needed
- **B.** A dried bean always dissolves instantly in water
- **C.** Irrigation is mandatory for all visible ear foreign bodies
- **D.** The bean will migrate through an intact eardrum into the nose

<details><summary>✅ Answer aur detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: A. Water may swell the organic object and worsen impaction, so an alternative clinician-selected removal method is needed**

- **A. Kyun sahi hai:** Dried bean organic material hai aur water absorb karke expand ho sakti hai. Jab bean ear canal mein aur badi ho jaati hai, toh woh tightly phans sakti hai aur removal difficult ho sakta hai. Isliye vegetable matter mein water irrigation contraindicated hai. Nurse unsafe irrigation ko question karegi aur trained clinician se object position aur eardrum safety ke hisaab se alternative removal arrange karwayegi.

- **B. Kyun galat hai:** Dried bean paani mein turant dissolve nahi hoti. Woh moisture absorb karke swell ho sakti hai. Isliye water use karne se object chhota hone ke bajay aur tightly lodge ho sakta hai.

- **C. Kyun galat hai:** Ear foreign body ke liye removal method object ki type aur position par depend karta hai. Vegetable matter, button battery aur suspected eardrum perforation mein irrigation avoid karni chahiye. Har visible object ko irrigate karna safe rule nahi hai.

- **D. Kyun galat hai:** Intact eardrum external ear canal aur middle ear ko separate karti hai. Bean ka eardrum cross karke nose tak pahunchna normal anatomical route nahi hai. Actual concern expansion aur impaction ka hai.

**Exam clue:** Organic expandable foreign body mein irrigation se problem worsen ho sakti hai.

**Clinical reference:** [Clinical guidance](https://www.childrens.health.qld.gov.au/for-health-professionals/queensland-paediatric-emergency-care-qpec/queensland-paediatric-clinical-guidelines/foreign-body-ear).

### Q90. After successful removal of a small nonhazardous ear foreign body in a preschool child, the caregiver confirms that the child had been playing with several identical beads. Which further assessment should be facilitated?

**Difficulty:** 🟡 Medium · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD · Original mock-exclusive practice

- **A.** Discharge without examining any other site
- **B.** Arrange a clinician examination for additional foreign bodies in the other ear and nasal passages
- **C.** Insert cotton into the unaffected ear to prevent further play
- **D.** Order a routine MRI for every asymptomatic child

<details><summary>✅ Answer aur detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: B. Arrange a clinician examination for additional foreign bodies in the other ear and nasal passages**

- **A. Kyun galat hai:** Ek bead nikalne se ye confirm nahi hota ki child ne sirf wahi ek bead insert ki thi. Jab caregiver batata hai ki several beads available the, toh doosre accessible sites mein additional object ho sakta hai. Discharge se pehle appropriate examination karwana chahiye.

- **B. Kyun sahi hai:** Small children ek se zyada objects ear ya nose mein insert kar sakte hain. Clinician affected ear ko dobara inspect karega, taaki residual object ya canal injury miss na ho. Doosre ear aur nasal passages bhi examine karne chahiye. Nurse caregiver ko small objects safe jagah rakhne aur pain, discharge ya hearing change par review karwane ki advice degi.

- **C. Kyun galat hai:** Unaffected ear mein cotton insert karne se khud ek naya foreign body create ho sakta hai. Prevention ke liye age-appropriate toys aur small objects ko reach se door rakhna zyada useful hai.

- **D. Kyun galat hai:** Uncomplicated foreign body removal ke baad har asymptomatic child ko MRI ki zaroorat nahi hoti. Pehle clinical examination se remaining objects aur injury assess hoti hai. Imaging tabhi consider ki jaati hai jab specific clinical indication ho.

**Exam clue:** Removal ke baad additional objects aur procedural trauma assess karo.

**Clinical reference:** [Clinical guidance](https://www.childrens.health.qld.gov.au/for-health-professionals/queensland-paediatric-emergency-care-qpec/queensland-paediatric-clinical-guidelines/foreign-body-ear). · [CHOP pediatric ear foreign body pathway](https://www.chop.edu/clinical-pathway/ear-foreign-body-clinical-pathway)
