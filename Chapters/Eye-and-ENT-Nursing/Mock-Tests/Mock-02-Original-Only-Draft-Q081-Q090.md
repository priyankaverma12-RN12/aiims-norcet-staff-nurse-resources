### Q81. After drainage of an auricular hematoma, an athlete asks why the clinician has placed a shaped pressure dressing on the pinna. Which explanation is most appropriate?

**Difficulty:** 🔴 Difficult · **Priority:** 🔴 MOST IMPORTANT · Original mock-exclusive practice

- **A.** It permanently increases cochlear sensitivity
- **B.** It prevents blood from collecting again between the cartilage and its covering
- **C.** It closes the eustachian tube to prevent infection
- **D.** It restores the tympanic membrane after perforation

<details><summary>✅ Answer aur detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: B. It prevents blood from collecting again between the cartilage and its covering**

- **A. Kyun galat hai:** Cochlea inner ear ki hearing structure hai. Pinna par dressing ka purpose cochlear sensitivity change karna nahi hai.

- **B. Kyun sahi hai:** Perichondrium cartilage ki covering hai jo nutrition supply mein madad karti hai. Hematoma mein blood is covering ko cartilage se separate kar sakta hai. Drainage ke baad fitted pressure dressing dead space kam karti hai, jisse blood dobara collect hone ka risk kam hota hai. Follow-up zaroori hai, kyunki recurrence aur skin pressure injury dono assess karne hote hain.

- **C. Kyun galat hai:** Eustachian tube middle ear ko nasopharynx se connect karti hai. External pinna dressing is tube ko therapeutic tarike se close nahi karti.

- **D. Kyun galat hai:** Tympanic membrane ear canal ke end par hoti hai. Pinna hematoma treatment perforated eardrum repair ka substitute nahi hai.

**Exam clue:** Drainage ke baad reaccumulation prevent karna bhi care ka important part hai.

**Clinical reference:** [Clinical guidance](https://www.merckmanuals.com/professional/injuries-poisoning/facial-trauma/external-ear-trauma).

### Q82. A patient presents with a human bite wound involving the auricular cartilage. Bleeding is controlled and the wound looks small. Which concern should remain prominent in the nursing handover?

**Difficulty:** 🟡 Medium · **Priority:** 🔴 MOST IMPORTANT · Original mock-exclusive practice

- **A.** The wound is harmless because bleeding has stopped
- **B.** The injury can be managed only by checking hearing acuity
- **C.** A small bite wound carries no microbial contamination risk
- **D.** Cartilage infection risk requires prompt wound and prophylaxis assessment

<details><summary>✅ Answer aur detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: D. Cartilage infection risk requires prompt wound and prophylaxis assessment**

- **A. Kyun galat hai:** Bleeding control se bacterial contamination exclude nahi hoti. Bite injury ka depth aur infection risk separately assess karna chahiye.

- **B. Kyun galat hai:** Hearing assessment useful ho sakta hai, lekin wound care, infection prevention aur immunization history ka review bhi important hai.

- **C. Kyun galat hai:** Human mouth mein bacteria hote hain jo small wound mein bhi enter kar sakte hain. Cartilage involvement infection ko serious bana sakta hai.

- **D. Kyun sahi hai:** Bite wound mein clinician cleaning, damaged tissue assessment aur indicated antibiotic prophylaxis decide karega. Tetanus aur blood-borne exposure risk bhi appropriate protocol ke according review karo. Cartilage ki blood supply limited hone se complications ho sakti hain.

**Exam clue:** Bleeding rukna aur bite infection risk khatam hona alag baat hain.

**Clinical reference:** [Clinical guidance](https://www.merckmanuals.com/professional/injuries-poisoning/facial-trauma/external-ear-trauma).

### Q83. Several days after upper-ear cartilage piercing, a patient develops painful red swelling of the cartilaginous pinna. The soft lobule is relatively unaffected. Which assessment should this distribution support?

**Difficulty:** 🔴 Difficult · **Priority:** 🔴 MOST IMPORTANT · Original mock-exclusive practice

- **A.** Possible auricular perichondritis requiring prompt clinical assessment
- **B.** Isolated tympanic membrane perforation
- **C.** Uncomplicated middle-ear effusion
- **D.** Normal healing that requires no reassessment

<details><summary>✅ Answer aur detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: A. Possible auricular perichondritis requiring prompt clinical assessment**

- **A. Kyun sahi hai:** Lobule mein cartilage nahi hoti. Cartilage-bearing pinna ki inflammation aur relative lobule sparing perichondritis ka clue hai. Piercing infection risk badha sakti hai. Early clinical treatment cartilage damage aur deformity ka risk kam karne mein help karta hai.

- **B. Kyun galat hai:** Eardrum perforation mein hearing change, pain ya discharge ho sakta hai, lekin pinna cartilage ka selective inflammatory pattern explain nahi hota.

- **C. Kyun galat hai:** Middle-ear effusion eardrum ke peeche fluid hota hai. Ye outer cartilage ko is tarah red aur painful nahi banata.

- **D. Kyun galat hai:** Increasing redness aur pain ko normal healing assume nahi karna chahiye. Infection aur abscess ke liye prompt evaluation chahiye.

**Exam clue:** Pinna aur lobule ka distribution diagnosis mein helpful clue hai, absolute proof nahi.

### Q84. A patient receiving treatment for infectious auricular perichondritis develops a localized fluctuant collection over the cartilage. What should the nurse anticipate discussing urgently with the ENT team?

**Difficulty:** 🔴 Difficult · **Priority:** 🔴 MOST IMPORTANT · Original mock-exclusive practice

- **A.** Discontinuing observation because swelling is now localized
- **B.** Using hearing amplification as definitive treatment
- **C.** Possible drainage and reassessment of antimicrobial management
- **D.** Waiting for the pinna to develop a permanent deformity

<details><summary>✅ Answer aur detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: C. Possible drainage and reassessment of antimicrobial management**

- **A. Kyun galat hai:** Fluctuance fluid collection ka clue ho sakti hai. Localized swelling ko improvement maan kar observation stop nahi karni chahiye.

- **B. Kyun galat hai:** Hearing aid sound amplify karta hai. Ye infected cartilage ke paas pus collection treat nahi karta.

- **C. Kyun sahi hai:** Abscess mein pus perichondrium aur cartilage ke beech accumulate ho sakta hai. Cartilage ki nutrition compromise ho sakti hai. ENT team drainage aur appropriate antibiotics ka plan decide karegi. Nurse pain, fever aur worsening swelling report karegi.

- **D. Kyun galat hai:** Permanent deformity avoid karne ke liye early treatment important hai. Cartilage destruction hone tak wait unsafe hai.

**Exam clue:** Fluctuant infection mein source control ki need reassess karo.

### Q85. An adult in a humid climate has prominent ear itching and thick canal debris. The clinician sees filamentous material on otoscopy rather than a simple wax plug. Which condition should be specifically considered?

**Difficulty:** 🟡 Medium · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD · Original mock-exclusive practice

- **A.** Otosclerosis
- **B.** Otomycosis
- **C.** Presbycusis
- **D.** Isolated eustachian tube obstruction

<details><summary>✅ Answer aur detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: B. Otomycosis**

- **A. Kyun galat hai:** Otosclerosis mein middle-ear bone movement affect hoti hai. Itching aur filamentous canal debris uske typical findings nahi hain.

- **B. Kyun sahi hai:** Otomycosis external ear canal ki fungal infection hai. Aspergillus ya Candida jaise fungi humid environment mein grow kar sakte hain. Itching aur fungal-looking debris suspicion raise karte hain. Examination se diagnosis confirm karna zaroori hai.

- **C. Kyun galat hai:** Presbycusis age-related hearing decline hai. Ye fungal debris produce nahi karti.

- **D. Kyun galat hai:** Eustachian tube obstruction middle-ear pressure affect karti hai. Ye external canal mein fungal material explain nahi karti.

**Exam clue:** Itching aur fungal-looking debris ko ordinary wax se distinguish karo.

**Clinical reference:** [Clinical guidance](https://www.icmr.gov.in/icmrobject/uploads/STWs/1725952350_ent_otorrhoea.pdf).

### Q86. A patient with clinician-confirmed otomycosis asks why professional canal cleaning has been scheduled in addition to prescribed topical antifungal treatment. Which explanation is accurate?

**Difficulty:** 🟡 Medium · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD · Original mock-exclusive practice

- **A.** Cleaning replaces the need for any prescribed antifungal
- **B.** Cleaning permanently changes the auditory nerve threshold
- **C.** Cleaning is intended to create a tympanic membrane perforation
- **D.** Removal of obstructing fungal debris can improve access of topical treatment to affected canal skin

<details><summary>✅ Answer aur detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: D. Removal of obstructing fungal debris can improve access of topical treatment to affected canal skin**

- **A. Kyun galat hai:** Cleaning debris remove karti hai, lekin prescribed antifungal ki need clinician decide karta hai. Ek procedure se har infection cure hone ki guarantee nahi hai.

- **B. Kyun galat hai:** Auditory nerve threshold inner-ear function se related hai. Canal cleaning ka direct aim affected canal surface se debris remove karna hai.

- **C. Kyun galat hai:** Eardrum perforation complication ho sakti hai, intended treatment outcome nahi. Trained clinician safe technique select karega.

- **D. Kyun sahi hai:** Fungal debris canal surface cover kar sakta hai. Professional cleaning se affected skin tak topical medicine pahunchne mein madad mil sakti hai. Eardrum status aur drug suitability clinician confirm karega.

**Exam clue:** Topical therapy ka access improve karna cleaning ka ek useful role hai.

### Q87. A hearing-aid user has recurrent itchy scaling confined to the ear canal where the earmold contacts the skin. Symptoms began after a new earmold was fitted. Which addition to the assessment is useful?

**Difficulty:** 🟡 Medium · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD · Original mock-exclusive practice

- **A.** Review possible contact irritation or allergy and arrange device-fit and skin assessment
- **B.** Assume the hearing aid has caused a middle-ear bone fracture
- **C.** Prescribe indefinite systemic antibiotics without examination
- **D.** Advise the patient to scrape the affected skin each evening

<details><summary>✅ Answer aur detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: A. Review possible contact irritation or allergy and arrange device-fit and skin assessment**

- **A. Kyun sahi hai:** Contact dermatitis mein skin kisi material ya irritant ke contact se inflamed ho sakti hai. New earmold aur symptom location useful history hai. Device fit, material aur possible infection assess karne ke liye audiology aur clinical review arrange karo.

- **B. Kyun galat hai:** Middle-ear bone fracture trauma se ho sakta hai. Localized scaling aur new earmold contact history ko fracture explain nahi karta.

- **C. Kyun galat hai:** Dermatitis har baar bacterial infection nahi hoti. Bina assessment long-term antibiotics dena unnecessary harm create kar sakta hai.

- **D. Kyun galat hai:** Scraping skin barrier damage kar sakti hai. Isse secondary infection ka risk badhta hai. Gentle clinician-directed care follow karni chahiye.

**Exam clue:** Device-contact pattern ko document karo, infection aur dermatitis ko assess karo.

### Q88. A cold-water swimmer has bilateral hard bony narrowing of the external auditory canals on ENT examination and recurrent trapping of water after swimming. Which mechanism best explains the conductive component of hearing difficulty?

**Difficulty:** 🟡 Medium · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD · Original mock-exclusive practice

- **A.** Primary degeneration of auditory cortical neurons
- **B.** A congenital failure of the optic nerve to develop
- **C.** External canal narrowing can impede transmission of sound and trap debris
- **D.** Excessive opening of the nasolacrimal duct

<details><summary>✅ Answer aur detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: C. External canal narrowing can impede transmission of sound and trap debris**

- **A. Kyun galat hai:** Auditory cortex brain mein sound interpret karti hai. External canal ki bony narrowing ka conductive effect is mechanism se different hai.

- **B. Kyun galat hai:** Optic nerve vision signals carry karta hai. Ear canal narrowing se optic nerve development ka relation nahi hai.

- **C. Kyun sahi hai:** Exostoses benign bony growths hain jo cold-water exposure se associated ho sakti hain. Narrow canal sound passage aur wax clearance affect kar sakta hai. Water trapping infection ko predispose kar sakti hai. ENT assessment se severity aur management decide hoga.

- **D. Kyun galat hai:** Nasolacrimal duct eye tears drain karta hai. Iska external auditory canal sound conduction se relation nahi hai.

**Exam clue:** Conductive loss mein sound transmission ka anatomical barrier socho.

**Clinical reference:** [Clinical guidance](https://www.enthealth.org/conditions/conductive-hearing-loss/).

### Q89. A child has a dried bean lodged in the external auditory canal. A staff member suggests routine water irrigation. Which concern should the nurse communicate?

**Difficulty:** 🔴 Difficult · **Priority:** 🔴 MOST IMPORTANT · Original mock-exclusive practice

- **A.** Water may swell the organic object and worsen impaction, so an alternative clinician-selected removal method is needed
- **B.** A dried bean always dissolves instantly in water
- **C.** Irrigation is mandatory for all visible ear foreign bodies
- **D.** The bean will migrate through an intact eardrum into the nose

<details><summary>✅ Answer aur detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: A. Water may swell the organic object and worsen impaction, so an alternative clinician-selected removal method is needed**

- **A. Kyun sahi hai:** Vegetable matter water absorb karke swell ho sakta hai. Isse canal mein object aur tightly lodge ho sakta hai. Irrigation contraindicated hai. Clinician object position, cooperation aur eardrum safety ke according alternative removal method choose karega.

- **B. Kyun galat hai:** Dried bean instantly dissolve nahi hota. Water absorb karne se size badh sakta hai.

- **C. Kyun galat hai:** Irrigation har object ke liye safe nahi hai. Expandable object, battery aur suspected perforation mein avoid karna chahiye.

- **D. Kyun galat hai:** Intact tympanic membrane external canal aur middle ear ke beech barrier hai. Bean ka normal route eardrum cross karke nose tak jana nahi hai.

**Exam clue:** Organic expandable foreign body mein irrigation se problem worsen ho sakti hai.

**Clinical reference:** [Clinical guidance](https://www.childrens.health.qld.gov.au/for-health-professionals/queensland-paediatric-emergency-care-qpec/queensland-paediatric-clinical-guidelines/foreign-body-ear).

### Q90. After successful removal of a small nonhazardous ear foreign body in a preschool child, the caregiver confirms that the child had been playing with several identical beads. Which further assessment should be facilitated?

**Difficulty:** 🟡 Medium · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD · Original mock-exclusive practice

- **A.** Discharge without examining any other site
- **B.** Arrange a clinician examination for additional foreign bodies in the other ear and nasal passages
- **C.** Insert cotton into the unaffected ear to prevent further play
- **D.** Order a routine MRI for every asymptomatic child

<details><summary>✅ Answer aur detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: B. Arrange a clinician examination for additional foreign bodies in the other ear and nasal passages**

- **A. Kyun galat hai:** Ek bead remove hone se additional objects exclude nahi hote. Multiple beads available the, isliye further examination appropriate hai.

- **B. Kyun sahi hai:** Children ek se zyada accessible orifices mein objects insert kar sakte hain. Other ear aur nasal passages ko clinician assess karega. Removed side ki injury aur residual object bhi check honi chahiye.

- **C. Kyun galat hai:** Cotton ko ear canal mein insert karna naya foreign body create kar sakta hai. Prevention education safer approach hai.

- **D. Kyun galat hai:** Routine MRI har uncomplicated foreign body removal ke baad necessary nahi hai. Examination aur clinical indication ke basis par investigation decide hoti hai.

**Exam clue:** Removal ke baad additional objects aur procedural trauma assess karo.

**Clinical reference:** [Clinical guidance](https://www.childrens.health.qld.gov.au/for-health-professionals/queensland-paediatric-emergency-care-qpec/queensland-paediatric-clinical-guidelines/foreign-body-ear).
