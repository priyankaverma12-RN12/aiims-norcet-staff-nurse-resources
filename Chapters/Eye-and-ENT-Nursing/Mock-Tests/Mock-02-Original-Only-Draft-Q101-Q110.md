### Q101. During aircraft descent, a passenger with a recent upper respiratory infection develops ear pain. Otoscopy later shows a retracted but intact tympanic membrane. Which pressure relationship best explains the finding?

**Difficulty:** 🔴 Difficult · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD · Original mock-exclusive practice

- **A.** Middle-ear pressure remained higher than cabin pressure during descent
- **B.** Middle-ear and cabin pressures remained equal throughout descent
- **C.** Middle-ear pressure became lower than the surrounding cabin pressure because equalization was impaired
- **D.** The tympanic membrane retracted because the external canal was filled with cerebrospinal fluid

<details><summary>✅ Answer aur detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: C. Middle-ear pressure became lower than the surrounding cabin pressure because equalization was impaired**

- **A. Kyun galat hai:** Jab middle ear ka pressure bahar ke pressure se zyada hota hai, toh eardrum bahar ki taraf move kar sakti hai. Is case mein eardrum inward retract hui hai, isliye opposite pressure difference ko consider karna chahiye.

- **B. Kyun galat hai:** Normally Eustachian tube middle ear aur nasopharynx ke beech pressure equalize karne mein help karti hai. Agar dono taraf pressure equal rehta, toh descent se pressure difference ke karan retraction nahi hoti. Recent cold mein tube ki opening impair ho sakti hai.

- **C. Kyun sahi hai:** Aircraft neeche aata hai toh cabin ka surrounding pressure badhta hai. Cold mein Eustachian tube properly open na ho, toh middle ear ka pressure bahar ke comparison mein kam reh sakta hai. Bahar ka higher pressure eardrum ko andar ki taraf push karta hai. Isse pain aur barotrauma, yani pressure change se tissue injury, ho sakti hai. Nurse persistent pain, hearing change ya vertigo par clinical review arrange karegi.

- **D. Kyun galat hai:** CSF brain aur spinal cord ke around protective fluid hai. Ear mein CSF leak ka concern usually relevant trauma ya doosri skull-base pathology mein hota hai. Yahan descent aur recent cold ka pressure-related mechanism retracted eardrum ko zyada directly explain karta hai.

**Exam clue:** Descent mein external pressure badhne aur middle-ear pressure equal na hone ka relation samjho.

**Clinical reference:** [Clinical guidance](https://www.merckmanuals.com/professional/ear%2C-nose%2C-and-throat-disorders/middle-ear-and-tympanic-membrane-disorders/otic-barotrauma).

### Q102. A scuba diver reports inability to equalize ear pressure during descent. After a forceful attempt, severe vertigo and new hearing loss develop. Which management direction is safest?

**Difficulty:** 🔴 Difficult · **Priority:** 🔴 MOST IMPORTANT · Original mock-exclusive practice

- **A.** Stop diving and arrange urgent medical assessment for possible inner-ear injury or another diving-related emergency
- **B.** Repeat forceful pressure equalization until hearing improves
- **C.** Treat the episode as uncomplicated cerumen impaction
- **D.** Return to diving immediately when ear pain decreases

<details><summary>✅ Answer aur detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: A. Stop diving and arrange urgent medical assessment for possible inner-ear injury or another diving-related emergency**

- **A. Kyun sahi hai:** Diving mein surrounding pressure change hota hai. Agar equalization difficult ho aur forceful effort ke baad sudden hearing loss aur severe vertigo aaye, toh inner-ear injury ka concern hota hai. Decompression illness bhi similar symptoms de sakti hai, isliye exact diagnosis khud assume nahi karna hai. Dive safely abort karke assistance aur urgent emergency ya diving-medicine assessment arrange karni chahiye. Further diving specialist clearance tak avoid karein.

- **B. Kyun galat hai:** Forceful equalization inner ear par additional pressure daal sakti hai aur existing injury worsen kar sakti hai. New severe vertigo aur hearing loss mein repeat self-treatment ke bajay urgent clinical assessment chahiye.

- **C. Kyun galat hai:** Cerumen ear canal ka wax hai aur blockage se conductive hearing difficulty ho sakti hai. Lekin forceful pressure change ke immediately baad severe vertigo aur sudden hearing loss ko simple wax blockage maan kar emergency miss nahi karni chahiye.

- **D. Kyun galat hai:** Ear pain kam hona inner-ear function normal hone ka proof nahi hai. Hearing aur balance injury pain ke bina bhi persist kar sakti hai. Diving dobara start karne se pehle appropriate specialist assessment aur clearance chahiye.

**Exam clue:** Pressure exposure plus new vestibular and auditory deficit is a high-risk pattern.

**Clinical reference:** [Clinical guidance](https://www.merckmanuals.com/professional/injuries-poisoning/injury-during-diving-or-work-in-compressed-air/ear-and-sinus-barotrauma). · [Divers Alert Network: Inner-ear barotrauma](https://dan.org/health-medicine/health-resources/diseases-conditions/inner-ear-barotrauma-iebt/)

### Q103. A patient with a traumatic tympanic membrane perforation is scheduled for audiometry before treatment and again after recovery. Why is documenting hearing at both times useful?

**Difficulty:** 🟡 Medium · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD · Original mock-exclusive practice

- **A.** It guarantees that an injured ossicle will heal
- **B.** It helps distinguish hearing impairment already caused by the injury from a subsequent treatment-associated change
- **C.** It directly measures middle-ear bacterial load
- **D.** It proves that every perforation requires surgery

<details><summary>✅ Answer aur detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: B. It helps distinguish hearing impairment already caused by the injury from a subsequent treatment-associated change**

- **A. Kyun galat hai:** Audiometry sound sunne ki ability measure karti hai. Isse injured ossicle, yani middle ear ki small sound-transmitting bone, mechanically repair nahi hoti. Healing aur need for intervention specialist assessment se decide hoti hai.

- **B. Kyun sahi hai:** Treatment se pehle audiogram record karne par pata chalta hai ki injury ke baad hearing kis level par hai. Recovery ke baad same type ki hearing assessment se change compare kar sakte hain. Isse trauma ki wajah se treatment se pehle hi present deficit aur treatment ke baad develop hue change ko distinguish karne mein help milti hai. Comparison alone exact cause prove nahi karta aur persistent loss mein further assessment zaroori ho sakti hai.

- **C. Kyun galat hai:** Audiometry mein sounds ki perception aur hearing thresholds assess kiye jaate hain. Ye bacteria count ya identify nahi karti. Agar infection ka concern hai, toh clinician appropriate microbiological assessment decide karega.

- **D. Kyun galat hai:** Bahut se uncomplicated traumatic eardrum perforations apne aap heal ho sakte hain. Audiometry hearing outcome monitor karti hai, lekin abnormal result ka matlab har perforation mein surgery mandatory nahi hai. Specialist persistent defect aur associated injury assess karega.

**Exam clue:** Pre-treatment audiogram = baseline, post-treatment audiogram = comparison of functional outcome.

**Clinical reference:** [Merck Manual: Traumatic perforation of the tympanic membrane](https://www.merckmanuals.com/professional/ear-nose-and-throat-disorders/middle-ear-and-tympanic-membrane-disorders/traumatic-perforation-of-the-tympanic-membrane). · [MSD Manual traumatic perforation](https://www.msdmanuals.com/professional/ear%2C-nose%2C-and-throat-disorders/middle-ear-and-tympanic-membrane-disorders/traumatic-perforation-of-the-tympanic-membrane?ruleredirectid=749)

### Q104. A patient with a healing traumatic eardrum perforation asks how to avoid shower water entering the injured ear. Which practical instruction is most appropriate?

**Difficulty:** 🟡 Medium · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD · Original mock-exclusive practice

- **A.** Direct shower water into the canal to wash away debris
- **B.** Pack dry cotton deeply inside the ear canal
- **C.** Use a cotton ball coated with petroleum jelly at the external opening during bathing, without pushing it into the canal, if consistent with the clinician's advice
- **D.** Use a cotton swab after each shower to dry the middle ear

<details><summary>✅ Answer aur detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: C. Use a cotton ball coated with petroleum jelly at the external opening during bathing, without pushing it into the canal, if consistent with the clinician's advice**

- **A. Kyun galat hai:** Perforated eardrum mein water middle ear tak enter kar sakta hai. Direct shower stream dene se infection aur irritation ka risk badh sakta hai. Isliye healing period mein clinician ki dry-ear instructions follow karni chahiye.

- **B. Kyun galat hai:** Dry cotton ko canal ke andar deep push karne se injury ya blockage ho sakti hai. Water-resistant protective barrier sirf outer opening par gently lagana chahiye, jab clinician ne aisa advise kiya ho.

- **C. Kyun sahi hai:** Petroleum jelly cotton ball ko water-resistant barrier banane mein help karti hai. Bathing ke waqt cotton ko external opening par gently rakhne se water entry kam ho sakti hai. Cotton ko deep canal mein nahi push karna hai aur bathing ke baad outer barrier hata dena hai. Clinician ki instructions ke mutabik swimming bhi avoid karni chahiye jab tak healing confirm na ho.

- **D. Kyun galat hai:** Cotton swab eardrum ko injure kar sakta hai aur debris ko aur andar push kar sakta hai. Isse middle ear safely dry nahi hota. Ear canal mein khud instruments insert nahi karne chahiye.

**Exam clue:** Dry-ear precaution ka practical method external barrier hai, deep canal packing nahi.

**Clinical reference:** [Clinical guidance](https://www.merckmanuals.com/professional/ear-nose-and-throat-disorders/middle-ear-and-tympanic-membrane-disorders/traumatic-perforation-of-the-tympanic-membrane). · [MSD Manual traumatic perforation](https://www.msdmanuals.com/professional/ear%2C-nose%2C-and-throat-disorders/middle-ear-and-tympanic-membrane-disorders/traumatic-perforation-of-the-tympanic-membrane?ruleredirectid=749)

### Q105. A patient has persistent conductive hearing loss after a temporal bone fracture. The middle-ear blood has resolved on follow-up and the tympanic membrane is intact. Which further cause should be considered?

**Difficulty:** 🔴 Difficult · **Priority:** 🔴 MOST IMPORTANT · Original mock-exclusive practice

- **A.** An isolated increase in tear production
- **B.** Expected persistence of hemotympanum despite confirmed resolution
- **C.** A complete recovery of ossicular function proved by the intact membrane
- **D.** An ossicular chain injury disrupting sound transmission

<details><summary>✅ Answer aur detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: D. An ossicular chain injury disrupting sound transmission**

- **A. Kyun galat hai:** Tears lacrimal gland se produce hoti hain aur eye surface ko moist rakhti hain. Temporal bone fracture ke baad conductive hearing loss ko explain karne ke liye sound transmission pathway assess karna chahiye, tear production nahi.

- **B. Kyun galat hai:** Hemotympanum ka matlab eardrum ke peeche blood jama hona hai. Isse conductive loss ho sakta hai, lekin question mein blood resolve ho chuka hai. Agar hearing loss phir bhi hai, toh doosri injury evaluate karna zaroori hai.

- **C. Kyun galat hai:** Intact eardrum sound pathway ka sirf ek part hai. Uske peeche malleus, incus aur stapes bones bhi properly connected honi chahiye. Eardrum intact hone se ossicular chain normal prove nahi hoti.

- **D. Kyun sahi hai:** Malleus, incus aur stapes eardrum ki vibrations inner ear tak transmit karte hain. Temporal bone fracture se in bones ki continuity ya joints damage ho sakte hain. Middle-ear blood resolve hone ke baad bhi conductive hearing loss persist ho, toh ossicular injury consider karni chahiye. Specialist audiometry aur appropriate CT imaging se further assessment kar sakta hai.

**Exam clue:** Intact eardrum ka matlab intact ossicular chain zaroori nahi.

**Clinical reference:** [Clinical guidance](https://www.merckmanuals.com/professional/injuries-poisoning/facial-trauma/temporal-bone-fractures).

### Q106. A patient after significant head trauma develops clear watery ear discharge. The trauma team is evaluating a possible cerebrospinal fluid leak. Which nursing handling of the discharge is appropriate?

**Difficulty:** 🔴 Difficult · **Priority:** 🔴 MOST IMPORTANT · Original mock-exclusive practice

- **A.** Seal the ear tightly with a deep occlusive pack
- **B.** Avoid ear irrigation or deep packing, document the drainage and escalate immediately within the trauma pathway
- **C.** Instil nonprescribed ear drops to determine whether the fluid changes colour
- **D.** Use a bedside glucose strip as definitive proof that the fluid is cerebrospinal fluid

<details><summary>✅ Answer aur detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: B. Avoid ear irrigation or deep packing, document the drainage and escalate immediately within the trauma pathway**

- **A. Kyun galat hai:** Suspected CSF leak mein ear canal ko deep tightly pack karna routine care nahi hai. Isse unnecessary manipulation ho sakti hai aur drainage assessment difficult ho sakti hai. Agar severe bleeding ho toh separate trauma-team-directed management chahiye.

- **B. Kyun sahi hai:** CSF brain aur spinal cord ke around protective fluid hota hai. Significant head trauma ke baad clear watery ear discharge skull-base injury aur CSF leak ka possible sign hai. Nurse airway, breathing, circulation aur neurologic monitoring continue karegi, drainage ki timing aur appearance document karegi aur trauma team ko promptly inform karegi. Ear ko irrigate ya deep pack nahi karna hai. Specialist appropriate testing aur imaging decide karega.

- **C. Kyun galat hai:** Nonprescribed ear drops fluid identify karne ka validated test nahi hain. Possible skull-base ya tympanic membrane injury mein unnecessary instillation se risk badh sakta hai. Nurse ear ko manipulate karne ke bajay trauma pathway follow karegi.

- **D. Kyun galat hai:** Glucose strips CSF ko reliably identify nahi karti, kyunki doosre fluids mein bhi glucose ho sakta hai aur results misleading ho sakte hain. Lab mein beta-2 transferrin jaise suitable test aur imaging specialist select kar sakte hain. Bedside strip ko definitive proof nahi maanna chahiye.

**Exam clue:** Watery otorrhoea after head trauma ko routine ear discharge ki tarah irrigate mat karo.

**Clinical reference:** [Clinical guidance](https://www.icmr.gov.in/icmrobject/uploads/STWs/1725952350_ent_otorrhoea.pdf). · [Merck temporal bone fractures](https://www.merckmanuals.com/professional/injuries-poisoning/facial-trauma/temporal-bone-fractures) · [ARUP cranial CSF leak testing](https://arupconsult.com/content/csf-leak-beta-2-transferrin)

### Q107. In a conscious patient with temporal bone fracture, facial movement was documented as symmetrical on admission but complete unilateral weakness appeared the next day. Why is the timing of onset important to record?

**Difficulty:** 🔴 Difficult · **Priority:** 🔴 MOST IMPORTANT · Original mock-exclusive practice

- **A.** It helps specialists distinguish possible delayed nerve edema from an immediately severe nerve injury
- **B.** Delayed weakness proves that the facial nerve has completely regenerated
- **C.** The timing is irrelevant once the fracture is confirmed
- **D.** Delayed weakness establishes that the patient has a bacterial ear canal infection

<details><summary>✅ Answer aur detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: A. It helps specialists distinguish possible delayed nerve edema from an immediately severe nerve injury**

- **A. Kyun sahi hai:** Facial nerve face ki muscles ko movement signals deti hai. Agar severe complete palsy injury ke turant baad ho, toh direct nerve damage ka concern hota hai. Initially normal movement ke baad delayed weakness nerve edema se related ho sakti hai. Nurse onset aur severity record karke urgent specialist assessment arrange karegi. Timing useful clue hai, lekin nerve ki actual condition additional assessment se decide hoti hai.

- **B. Kyun galat hai:** Nayi complete facial weakness nerve function worsen hone ka sign hai, regeneration ka proof nahi hai. Recovery ko serial clinical findings aur specialist tests se assess kiya jaata hai.

- **C. Kyun galat hai:** Fracture confirm hone ke baad bhi nerve injury ki severity aur onset treatment planning ke liye important hain. Isliye admission ka baseline aur baad mein change ka exact clinical documentation useful hai.

- **D. Kyun galat hai:** Traumatic facial palsy temporal bone ke andar nerve injury ya swelling se ho sakti hai. Delayed facial weakness alone external canal ki bacterial infection establish nahi karti.

**Exam clue:** Serial documentation of onset is clinically useful, not merely recording presence of palsy.

**Clinical reference:** [Clinical guidance](https://www.merckmanuals.com/professional/injuries-poisoning/facial-trauma/temporal-bone-fractures).

### Q108. An adult hears their own voice and breathing unusually loudly in one ear, especially when upright. The symptoms improve while lying down. Which mechanism should the nurse mention as a possible explanation during ENT referral?

**Difficulty:** 🟡 Medium · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD · Original mock-exclusive practice

- **A.** A tympanic membrane that is always completely perforated
- **B.** A permanently fixed stapes in every case
- **C.** An abnormally open eustachian tube allowing internal sounds to transmit to the middle ear
- **D.** A complete obstruction of the optic nerve

<details><summary>✅ Answer aur detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: C. An abnormally open eustachian tube allowing internal sounds to transmit to the middle ear**

- **A. Kyun galat hai:** Eardrum perforation hearing ko affect kar sakti hai, lekin apni breathing aur voice unusually loud sunne ka posture-related pattern perforation ka pakka proof nahi hai. ENT examination se eardrum aur other causes assess honge.

- **B. Kyun galat hai:** Stapes fixation middle ear ki sound transmission restrict karke conductive hearing loss cause kar sakti hai. Upright posture mein breathing aur own voice ki loud perception, jo lying down par improve hoti hai, patulous tube ki possibility suggest karti hai. Stapes fixation assume nahi karna chahiye.

- **C. Kyun sahi hai:** Eustachian tube normally mostly closed rehti hai aur zaroorat ke waqt open hoti hai. Patulous dysfunction mein tube inappropriate tareeke se open reh sakti hai, jisse nasopharynx ki breathing aur voice sounds middle ear tak transmit ho jaati hain. Apni voice unusually loud sunna autophony kehlata hai. Nurse posture-related pattern document karegi aur ENT assessment arrange karegi, kyunki doosri conditions bhi autophony cause kar sakti hain.

- **D. Kyun galat hai:** Optic nerve visual signals brain tak le jaati hai. Ear mein voice aur breathing sounds unusually loud sunna auditory symptom hai, optic nerve obstruction ka result nahi.

**Exam clue:** Autophony ke posture-related pattern ko record karo.

**Clinical reference:** [Clinical guidance](https://stanfordhealthcare.org/medical-conditions/ear-nose-and-throat/patulous-eustachian-tube-dysfunction.html).

### Q109. An adult with severe ear pain develops vesicles inside the external canal and new unilateral facial weakness. Which suspected condition best accounts for this combination?

**Difficulty:** 🟡 Medium · **Priority:** 🔴 MOST IMPORTANT · Original mock-exclusive practice

- **A.** Uncomplicated bacterial otitis media
- **B.** Benign paroxysmal positional vertigo
- **C.** Isolated allergic contact dermatitis of the pinna
- **D.** Herpes zoster oticus with facial palsy

<details><summary>✅ Answer aur detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: D. Herpes zoster oticus with facial palsy**

- **A. Kyun galat hai:** Bacterial otitis media mein middle ear infected hota hai aur ear pain ya fever aa sakta hai. Lekin painful ear-canal vesicles aur new facial weakness ka combination zoster-related nerve involvement ka important clue hai. Isliye uncomplicated infection assume nahi karna chahiye.

- **B. Kyun galat hai:** BPPV head position change ke saath brief spinning episodes cause karti hai. Ye painful vesicular rash aur peripheral facial palsy ka usual cause nahi hai. In symptoms ka prompt separate clinical assessment chahiye.

- **C. Kyun galat hai:** Contact dermatitis kisi irritant ya allergen ke response mein itching, redness aur scaling cause kar sakti hai. Painful vesicles ke saath new facial weakness ko simple allergy maan lena safe nahi hai. Nerve-related infection consider karni chahiye.

- **D. Kyun sahi hai:** Varicella-zoster virus chickenpox ke baad nerve tissue mein dormant reh sakta hai aur baad mein reactivate ho sakta hai. Jab ear ke region mein zoster aur facial nerve involvement saath hote hain, toh painful vesicles aur facial palsy Ramsay Hunt syndrome ka characteristic pattern hain. Nurse prompt clinical assessment arrange karegi, eye closure assess karegi aur prescribed eye protection aur treatment follow karegi. New facial weakness ke alternate urgent causes bhi clinician assess karega.

**Exam clue:** Ear vesicles plus facial palsy is an important zoster pattern.

**Clinical reference:** [Clinical guidance](https://www.merckmanuals.com/professional/ear%2C-nose%2C-and-throat-disorders/inner-ear-disorders/herpes-zoster-oticus).

### Q110. During review of a patient with diagnosed herpes zoster oticus, the nurse documents new tinnitus, hearing difficulty and prolonged vertigo in addition to facial palsy. Which interpretation best explains why an auditory and vestibular assessment is needed?

**Difficulty:** 🔴 Difficult · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD · Original mock-exclusive practice

- **A.** The eighth cranial nerve may also be involved in the zoster process
- **B.** The facial nerve is the only nerve that can be affected
- **C.** The symptoms prove that the patient's eustachian tube is permanently obstructed
- **D.** The vestibular complaints establish uncomplicated cerumen impaction

<details><summary>✅ Answer aur detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: A. The eighth cranial nerve may also be involved in the zoster process**

- **A. Kyun sahi hai:** Eighth cranial nerve ko vestibulocochlear nerve kehte hain. Iska cochlear part hearing aur vestibular part balance se related hai. Herpes zoster oticus mein seventh facial nerve ke saath eighth nerve bhi involved ho sakti hai. Isliye new tinnitus, hearing difficulty aur vertigo ko promptly report karke auditory aur vestibular assessment arrange karna important hai.

- **B. Kyun galat hai:** Facial nerve ki involvement se facial movement affect hoti hai, lekin zoster nearby auditory aur balance nerve structures ko bhi affect kar sakta hai. Sirf seventh nerve assess karne se patient's additional deficits miss ho sakte hain.

- **C. Kyun galat hai:** Eustachian tube middle ear ke pressure ko regulate karne mein help karti hai. Hearing aur balance symptoms se permanent tube obstruction prove nahi hota, especially jab zoster diagnosis already present hai. Specialist inner-ear involvement evaluate karega.

- **D. Kyun galat hai:** Cerumen blockage outer canal mein conductive hearing difficulty cause kar sakta hai. Lekin diagnosed zoster ke saath prolonged vertigo, tinnitus aur facial palsy ko ordinary wax se explain karna unsafe hai. Patient ko hearing aur balance assessment chahiye.

**Exam clue:** CN VII facial movement aur CN VIII hearing/balance symptoms ko separate document karo.

**Clinical reference:** [Clinical guidance](https://www.merckmanuals.com/professional/ear%2C-nose%2C-and-throat-disorders/inner-ear-disorders/herpes-zoster-oticus).
