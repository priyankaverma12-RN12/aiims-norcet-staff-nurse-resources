### Q101. During aircraft descent, a passenger with a recent upper respiratory infection develops ear pain. Otoscopy later shows a retracted but intact tympanic membrane. Which pressure relationship best explains the finding?

**Difficulty:** 🔴 Difficult · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD · Original mock-exclusive practice

- **A.** Middle-ear pressure remained higher than cabin pressure during descent
- **B.** Middle-ear and cabin pressures remained equal throughout descent
- **C.** Middle-ear pressure became lower than the surrounding cabin pressure because equalization was impaired
- **D.** The tympanic membrane retracted because the external canal was filled with cerebrospinal fluid

<details><summary>✅ Answer aur detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: C. Middle-ear pressure became lower than the surrounding cabin pressure because equalization was impaired**

- **A. Kyun galat hai:** Middle-ear pressure bahar ke pressure se zyada ho toh eardrum outward bulge kar sakta hai. Retraction ke liye opposite pressure difference relevant hai.

- **B. Kyun galat hai:** Equal pressure hone par pressure difference ki wajah se eardrum displace nahi hota. Eustachian tube obstruction equalization ko impair kar sakti hai.

- **C. Kyun sahi hai:** Aircraft descent mein ambient pressure badhta hai. Cold ke dauran eustachian tube open na ho paye toh middle ear mein pressure comparatively kam reh sakta hai. Higher external pressure eardrum ko inward displace kar sakta hai, jisse pain aur barotrauma develop hota hai.

- **D. Kyun galat hai:** CSF leak trauma-related condition ho sakti hai. Is scenario mein descent aur impaired pressure equalization retraction ko better explain karte hain.

**Exam clue:** Descent mein external pressure badhne aur middle-ear pressure equal na hone ka relation samjho.

**Clinical reference:** [Clinical guidance](https://www.merckmanuals.com/professional/ear%2C-nose%2C-and-throat-disorders/middle-ear-and-tympanic-membrane-disorders/otic-barotrauma).

### Q102. A scuba diver reports inability to equalize ear pressure during descent. After a forceful attempt, severe vertigo and new hearing loss develop. Which management direction is safest?

**Difficulty:** 🔴 Difficult · **Priority:** 🔴 MOST IMPORTANT · Original mock-exclusive practice

- **A.** Stop diving and arrange urgent medical assessment for possible inner-ear injury or another diving-related emergency
- **B.** Repeat forceful pressure equalization until hearing improves
- **C.** Treat the episode as uncomplicated cerumen impaction
- **D.** Return to diving immediately when ear pain decreases

<details><summary>✅ Answer aur detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: A. Stop diving and arrange urgent medical assessment for possible inner-ear injury or another diving-related emergency**

- **A. Kyun sahi hai:** Pressure change ke baad sudden hearing loss aur severe vertigo inner-ear barotrauma, perilymph leak ya other diving emergency ka clue ho sakte hain. Further pressure exposure avoid karo aur urgent diving-medicine or emergency assessment arrange karo. Symptoms se exact diagnosis independently establish nahi karna hai.

- **B. Kyun galat hai:** Forceful repeated equalization existing injury worsen kar sakti hai. Severe new vestibular symptoms mein self-treatment continue karna unsafe hai.

- **C. Kyun galat hai:** Cerumen external canal mein wax hai. Acute pressure event ke immediately baad severe vertigo aur hearing loss ko wax assume karna unsafe hai.

- **D. Kyun galat hai:** Pain reduce ho jaane se inner-ear injury exclude nahi hoti. Medical clearance ke bina diving resume nahi karni chahiye.

**Exam clue:** Pressure exposure plus new vestibular and auditory deficit is a high-risk pattern.

**Clinical reference:** [Clinical guidance](https://www.merckmanuals.com/professional/injuries-poisoning/injury-during-diving-or-work-in-compressed-air/ear-and-sinus-barotrauma).

### Q103. A patient with a traumatic tympanic membrane perforation is scheduled for audiometry before treatment and again after recovery. Why is documenting hearing at both times useful?

**Difficulty:** 🟡 Medium · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD · Original mock-exclusive practice

- **A.** It guarantees that an injured ossicle will heal
- **B.** It helps distinguish hearing impairment already caused by the injury from a subsequent treatment-associated change
- **C.** It directly measures middle-ear bacterial load
- **D.** It proves that every perforation requires surgery

<details><summary>✅ Answer aur detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: B. It helps distinguish hearing impairment already caused by the injury from a subsequent treatment-associated change**

- **A. Kyun galat hai:** Audiometry hearing function measure karti hai. Test karne se ossicular injury heal hone ki guarantee nahi milti.

- **B. Kyun sahi hai:** Treatment se pehle hearing threshold record karne se baseline milta hai. Follow-up audiogram se change compare kar sakte hain. Is comparison se initial trauma ki wajah se present deficit aur treatment ke baad aaye change ko distinguish karne mein help milti hai. Persistent loss mein specialist further assessment kar sakta hai.

- **C. Kyun galat hai:** Audiometry sound perception aur hearing threshold evaluate karti hai. Bacteria identify ya quantify karne ke liye microbiological tests alag hote hain.

- **D. Kyun galat hai:** Bahut se uncomplicated traumatic perforations spontaneously heal hote hain. Surgery ki need persistent defect, associated injury aur specialist assessment par depend karti hai.

**Exam clue:** Pre-treatment audiogram = baseline, post-treatment audiogram = comparison of functional outcome.

**Clinical reference:** [Merck Manual: Traumatic perforation of the tympanic membrane](https://www.merckmanuals.com/professional/ear-nose-and-throat-disorders/middle-ear-and-tympanic-membrane-disorders/traumatic-perforation-of-the-tympanic-membrane).

### Q104. A patient with a healing traumatic eardrum perforation asks how to avoid shower water entering the injured ear. Which practical instruction is most appropriate?

**Difficulty:** 🟡 Medium · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD · Original mock-exclusive practice

- **A.** Direct shower water into the canal to wash away debris
- **B.** Pack dry cotton deeply inside the ear canal
- **C.** Use a cotton ball coated with petroleum jelly at the external opening during bathing, without pushing it into the canal, if consistent with the clinician's advice
- **D.** Use a cotton swab after each shower to dry the middle ear

<details><summary>✅ Answer aur detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: C. Use a cotton ball coated with petroleum jelly at the external opening during bathing, without pushing it into the canal, if consistent with the clinician's advice**

- **A. Kyun galat hai:** Perforation ke through water middle ear mein ja sakta hai aur infection risk badh sakta hai. Direct shower stream avoid karo.

- **B. Kyun galat hai:** Deep packing canal trauma aur obstruction cause kar sakti hai. Protective material external opening par rehna chahiye.

- **C. Kyun sahi hai:** Petroleum jelly water-resistant barrier provide kar sakti hai. Cotton ball ko external opening par gently place karna bathing ke time water entry reduce kar sakta hai. Isko canal mein deep insert nahi karna hai. Clinician-specific instructions follow karo.

- **D. Kyun galat hai:** Cotton swab injury worsen kar sakta hai aur middle ear ko safely dry nahi karta. Ear ke andar instrumentation avoid karo.

**Exam clue:** Dry-ear precaution ka practical method external barrier hai, deep canal packing nahi.

**Clinical reference:** [Clinical guidance](https://www.merckmanuals.com/professional/ear-nose-and-throat-disorders/middle-ear-and-tympanic-membrane-disorders/traumatic-perforation-of-the-tympanic-membrane).

### Q105. A patient has persistent conductive hearing loss after a temporal bone fracture. The middle-ear blood has resolved on follow-up and the tympanic membrane is intact. Which further cause should be considered?

**Difficulty:** 🔴 Difficult · **Priority:** 🔴 MOST IMPORTANT · Original mock-exclusive practice

- **A.** An isolated increase in tear production
- **B.** Expected persistence of hemotympanum despite confirmed resolution
- **C.** A complete recovery of ossicular function proved by the intact membrane
- **D.** An ossicular chain injury disrupting sound transmission

<details><summary>✅ Answer aur detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: D. An ossicular chain injury disrupting sound transmission**

- **A. Kyun galat hai:** Tear production hearing pathway ko directly affect nahi karti. Conductive deficit ke liye external aur middle-ear structures assess hote hain.

- **B. Kyun galat hai:** Hemotympanum ka matlab middle ear mein blood hai. Is stem mein blood resolve ho chuka hai, isliye persistent deficit ka additional cause investigate karna chahiye.

- **C. Kyun galat hai:** Intact tympanic membrane middle-ear bones ki continuity establish nahi karti. Ossicular chain separately injured ho sakti hai.

- **D. Kyun sahi hai:** Malleus, incus aur stapes sound vibrations transfer karte hain. Fracture se chain disrupt ho toh blood resolve hone ke baad bhi conductive loss persist kar sakta hai. Audiometry aur specialist-directed imaging relevant hain.

**Exam clue:** Intact eardrum ka matlab intact ossicular chain zaroori nahi.

**Clinical reference:** [Clinical guidance](https://www.merckmanuals.com/professional/injuries-poisoning/facial-trauma/temporal-bone-fractures).

### Q106. A patient after significant head trauma develops clear watery ear discharge. The trauma team is evaluating a possible cerebrospinal fluid leak. Which nursing handling of the discharge is appropriate?

**Difficulty:** 🔴 Difficult · **Priority:** 🔴 MOST IMPORTANT · Original mock-exclusive practice

- **A.** Seal the ear tightly with a deep occlusive pack
- **B.** Avoid ear irrigation or deep packing, document the drainage and escalate immediately within the trauma pathway
- **C.** Instil nonprescribed ear drops to determine whether the fluid changes colour
- **D.** Use a bedside glucose strip as definitive proof that the fluid is cerebrospinal fluid

<details><summary>✅ Answer aur detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: B. Avoid ear irrigation or deep packing, document the drainage and escalate immediately within the trauma pathway**

- **A. Kyun galat hai:** Deep occlusive packing suspected CSF leak mein safe routine intervention nahi hai. Intracranial injury aur infection risk ke context mein specialist management chahiye.

- **B. Kyun sahi hai:** CSF brain aur spinal cord ke around protective fluid hai. Head trauma ke baad watery ear drainage skull-base injury ka possible clue hai. Drainage ko document karo, canal manipulate mat karo aur urgent trauma team assessment ensure karo. Airway aur other trauma priorities continue rehni chahiye.

- **C. Kyun galat hai:** Nonprescribed drops diagnostic test nahi hain. Possible eardrum ya skull-base injury mein unnecessary instillation avoid karni chahiye.

- **D. Kyun galat hai:** Glucose strips fluid source ko reliably confirm nahi karte. Specialist appropriate laboratory test aur imaging select karega.

**Exam clue:** Watery otorrhoea after head trauma ko routine ear discharge ki tarah irrigate mat karo.

**Clinical reference:** [Clinical guidance](https://www.icmr.gov.in/icmrobject/uploads/STWs/1725952350_ent_otorrhoea.pdf).

### Q107. In a conscious patient with temporal bone fracture, facial movement was documented as symmetrical on admission but complete unilateral weakness appeared the next day. Why is the timing of onset important to record?

**Difficulty:** 🔴 Difficult · **Priority:** 🔴 MOST IMPORTANT · Original mock-exclusive practice

- **A.** It helps specialists distinguish possible delayed nerve edema from an immediately severe nerve injury
- **B.** Delayed weakness proves that the facial nerve has completely regenerated
- **C.** The timing is irrelevant once the fracture is confirmed
- **D.** Delayed weakness establishes that the patient has a bacterial ear canal infection

<details><summary>✅ Answer aur detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: A. It helps specialists distinguish possible delayed nerve edema from an immediately severe nerve injury**

- **A. Kyun sahi hai:** Facial nerve face muscles ko movement signal deta hai. Immediate complete palsy severe direct nerve injury ka concern raise kar sakti hai, jabki delayed weakness nerve swelling ke saath occur kar sakti hai. Timing specialist assessment ko guide karti hai, lekin alone injury type confirm nahi karti.

- **B. Kyun galat hai:** Complete weakness recovery ka evidence nahi hai. Nerve function aur prognosis specialist assessment se evaluate hote hain.

- **C. Kyun galat hai:** Onset ka timing treatment decisions mein useful ho sakta hai. Baseline aur serial facial movement document karna important hai.

- **D. Kyun galat hai:** Ear canal infection aur traumatic facial palsy alag mechanisms se ho sakte hain. Delayed weakness alone bacterial otitis externa establish nahi karti.

**Exam clue:** Serial documentation of onset is clinically useful, not merely recording presence of palsy.

**Clinical reference:** [Clinical guidance](https://www.merckmanuals.com/professional/injuries-poisoning/facial-trauma/temporal-bone-fractures).

### Q108. An adult hears their own voice and breathing unusually loudly in one ear, especially when upright. The symptoms improve while lying down. Which mechanism should the nurse mention as a possible explanation during ENT referral?

**Difficulty:** 🟡 Medium · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD · Original mock-exclusive practice

- **A.** A tympanic membrane that is always completely perforated
- **B.** A permanently fixed stapes in every case
- **C.** An abnormally open eustachian tube allowing internal sounds to transmit to the middle ear
- **D.** A complete obstruction of the optic nerve

<details><summary>✅ Answer aur detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: C. An abnormally open eustachian tube allowing internal sounds to transmit to the middle ear**

- **A. Kyun galat hai:** Eardrum perforation hearing change cause kar sakti hai, lekin position-dependent autophony ke basis par perforation assume nahi karna chahiye.

- **B. Kyun galat hai:** Stapes fixation conductive loss cause kar sakti hai. Own breathing aur voice ki unusually loud perception with posture change patulous tube ka clue ho sakti hai.

- **C. Kyun sahi hai:** Patulous eustachian tube mein tube inappropriate time par open reh sakti hai. Nasopharynx ki breathing aur voice sounds middle ear tak transmit ho sakti hain. Autophony ka meaning apni voice unusually loud sunna hai. ENT specialist alternate causes bhi assess karega.

- **D. Kyun galat hai:** Optic nerve vision se related hai. Is auditory symptom ka mechanism optic nerve obstruction nahi hai.

**Exam clue:** Autophony ke posture-related pattern ko record karo.

**Clinical reference:** [Clinical guidance](https://stanfordhealthcare.org/medical-conditions/ear-nose-and-throat/patulous-eustachian-tube-dysfunction.html).

### Q109. An adult with severe ear pain develops vesicles inside the external canal and new unilateral facial weakness. Which suspected condition best accounts for this combination?

**Difficulty:** 🟡 Medium · **Priority:** 🔴 MOST IMPORTANT · Original mock-exclusive practice

- **A.** Uncomplicated bacterial otitis media
- **B.** Benign paroxysmal positional vertigo
- **C.** Isolated allergic contact dermatitis of the pinna
- **D.** Herpes zoster oticus with facial palsy

<details><summary>✅ Answer aur detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: D. Herpes zoster oticus with facial palsy**

- **A. Kyun galat hai:** Otitis media middle ear ki infection hai. Ear canal vesicles aur peripheral facial weakness ka combination zoster-related nerve involvement suggest kar sakta hai.

- **B. Kyun galat hai:** BPPV positional spinning episodes cause karti hai. Vesicular rash aur facial weakness uski typical features nahi hain.

- **C. Kyun galat hai:** Contact dermatitis itching, scaling ya redness cause kar sakti hai. New facial palsy ke saath painful vesicles ko sirf allergy nahi maanna chahiye.

- **D. Kyun sahi hai:** Varicella-zoster virus reactivate hokar facial nerve ke geniculate ganglion ko involve kar sakta hai. Painful ear vesicles ke saath facial palsy Ramsay Hunt syndrome ka important pattern hai. Prompt clinical assessment aur prescribed treatment ki need hoti hai.

**Exam clue:** Ear vesicles plus facial palsy is an important zoster pattern.

**Clinical reference:** [Clinical guidance](https://www.merckmanuals.com/professional/ear%2C-nose%2C-and-throat-disorders/inner-ear-disorders/herpes-zoster-oticus).

### Q110. During review of a patient with diagnosed herpes zoster oticus, the nurse documents new tinnitus, hearing difficulty and prolonged vertigo in addition to facial palsy. Which interpretation best explains why an auditory and vestibular assessment is needed?

**Difficulty:** 🔴 Difficult · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD · Original mock-exclusive practice

- **A.** The eighth cranial nerve may also be involved in the zoster process
- **B.** The facial nerve is the only nerve that can be affected
- **C.** The symptoms prove that the patient's eustachian tube is permanently obstructed
- **D.** The vestibular complaints establish uncomplicated cerumen impaction

<details><summary>✅ Answer aur detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: A. The eighth cranial nerve may also be involved in the zoster process**

- **A. Kyun sahi hai:** Vestibulocochlear nerve eighth cranial nerve hai. Iske cochlear part ka hearing aur vestibular part ka balance se relation hai. Zoster oticus mein seventh ke saath eighth nerve involvement possible hai. New symptoms document karke specialist hearing aur balance assessment arrange karo.

- **B. Kyun galat hai:** Facial nerve involvement prominent ho sakti hai, lekin disease nearby auditory aur vestibular nerve structures ko bhi affect kar sakti hai.

- **C. Kyun galat hai:** Eustachian tube obstruction middle-ear pressure affect karti hai. Zoster ke context mein new hearing aur balance symptoms se permanent obstruction prove nahi hota.

- **D. Kyun galat hai:** Cerumen impaction conductive hearing difficulty cause kar sakti hai, lekin zoster-related prolonged vertigo aur facial palsy ko wax se explain karna unsafe hai.

**Exam clue:** CN VII facial movement aur CN VIII hearing/balance symptoms ko separate document karo.

**Clinical reference:** [Clinical guidance](https://www.merckmanuals.com/professional/ear%2C-nose%2C-and-throat-disorders/inner-ear-disorders/herpes-zoster-oticus).
