### 🔵 HPSSC 2015 — reported IOP reference-range question

[NPrep report](https://nprep.in/question-answer/normal-intraocular-pressure-is-TCbVly4vcYJSzOGTBpMnw), retrieved 27 November 2026. The historical report selects the less-than-20 option among its choices. The usual reference interval is more accurately expressed as about 10–21 mmHg. Exact booklet, question number and official key pending.

#### PYQ1. Which interval is commonly used as the approximate statistical reference range for adult intraocular pressure?

**Difficulty:** 🟢 Easy · **Priority:** 🔴 MOST IMPORTANT · Reported-concept adaptation

- **A.** 0–5 mmHg
- **B.** 10–21 mmHg
- **C.** 25–40 mmHg
- **D.** 60–80 mmHg

<details><summary>✅ Answer aur detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: B. 10–21 mmHg**

Adult IOP ka commonly taught statistical reference approximately 10 se 21 mmHg hai. Historical report ka less-than-20 option approximate best choice tha, universal definition nahi. Target pressure disease stage aur individual risk ke hisaab se lower bhi ho sakta hai. High IOP alone glaucoma prove nahi karta aur normal IOP disease exclude nahi karti.

- **A.** Is range mein pressure unusually low ho sakta hai. Ye usual adult reference interval nahi hai.
- **C.** Ye values conventional upper reference se above hain. Clinical assessment required ho sakti hai.
- **D.** Ye markedly high values hain, normal adult reference nahi.

**Clinical reference:** [NEI glaucoma and eye pressure](https://www.nei.nih.gov/eye-health-information/eye-conditions-and-diseases/glaucoma/glaucoma-and-eye-pressure), [AAFP review](https://www.aafp.org/afp/2023/0300/glaucoma).

**YouTube MCQ practice lead:** [Vineet Sehgal — Glaucoma MCQ Series](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ZaRq7crTKig). Indexed description only, exact IOP question and playback pending.

### 🔵 AIIMS Raipur Nursing Officer — 9 September 2017 recall / OSSSC Nursing Officer — 19 March 2023 reported paper

[AIIMS Raipur memory-based report](https://testbook.com/question-answer/which-of-the-following-condition-is-associated-wit--62d68855270283416bf4c61c) and [OSSSC reported question](https://testbook.com/question-answer/which-of-the-following-condition-is-manifested-by--6a3baa8144f1b97a8255acd9) select glaucoma from their choices. One shared concept family. Modern definition below avoids equating all high IOP with glaucoma.

#### PYQ2. In a patient with confirmed glaucomatous optic neuropathy, which risk factor is a major modifiable target of treatment?

**Difficulty:** 🟡 Medium · **Priority:** 🔴 MOST IMPORTANT · Reported adaptation

- **A.** The patient's inherited family history
- **B.** The patient's chronological age
- **C.** The patient's original optic disc anatomy
- **D.** Intraocular pressure

<details><summary>✅ Answer aur detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: D. Intraocular pressure**

Glaucoma mein optic nerve damage hota hai. Raised IOP important modifiable risk factor hai. Pressure reduce karne se remaining visual function preserve karne mein help mil sakti hai. Normal-tension glaucoma mein bhi individualized pressure-lowering therapy useful ho sakti hai.

- **A.** Family history risk stratification mein important hai, lekin inherited history ko medicine se change nahi kar sakte.
- **B.** Age risk factor ho sakti hai, lekin chronological age treatment target nahi.
- **C.** Baseline optic disc anatomy examination mein useful hai, par pressure-lowering intervention ka direct target nahi.

**Clinical reference:** [NEI glaucoma](https://www.nei.nih.gov/eye-health-information/eye-conditions-and-diseases/glaucoma).

**YouTube MCQ practice lead:** [Glaucoma MCQ series](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ZaRq7crTKig), description-level lead, no exact item authentication.

</details>

### 🔵 AIIMS Rishikesh Nursing Officer — 2019 — reported pilocarpine mechanism question

[NPrep report](https://nprep.in/question-answer/what-is-the-mechanism-of-action-of-pilocarpine-in-acute-closed-angle-Lu1b-zmIKm7osfFOvtkG2). Source selects miosis and improved drainage. Adaptation adds appropriate clinical context rather than a blanket emergency administration recommendation.

#### PYQ3. Once pressure and mechanism have been assessed, how can prescribed pilocarpine help in an appropriate pupillary-block angle-closure eye?

**Difficulty:** 🟡 Medium · **Priority:** 🔴 MOST IMPORTANT · Reported-concept adaptation

- **A.** Muscarinic stimulation produces miosis and can relieve peripheral iris obstruction
- **B.** Adrenergic stimulation produces maximal pupil dilation
- **C.** Carbonic anhydrase inhibition directly suppresses aqueous formation
- **D.** Systemic osmotic diuresis removes vitreous water

<details><summary>✅ Answer aur detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: A. Muscarinic stimulation produces miosis and can relieve peripheral iris obstruction**

Pilocarpine muscarinic agonist hai. Suitable pupillary-block mechanism mein iris sphincter contraction se pupil smaller ho sakti hai aur drainage access improve ho sakta hai. Very high IOP mein ischemic sphincter poor response de sakta hai. Topiramate-associated ya lens-related non-pupillary-block closure mein miotic harmful ho sakti hai. Isliye clinical mechanism aur prescribed sequence follow karo.

- **B.** Maximal dilation opposite action hai aur susceptible angle mein closure risk badha sakti hai.
- **C.** Carbonic anhydrase inhibition acetazolamide ya dorzolamide ka mechanism hai.
- **D.** Hyperosmotic action mannitol jaise agent se related hai, pilocarpine ka mechanism nahi.

**Clinical reference:** [Royal College angle closure pathway, printed p22](https://www.rcophth.ac.uk/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/Management-of-Angle-Closure-Glaucoma.pdf), [AAFP clinical review](https://www.aafp.org/afp/2023/0300/glaucoma).

**YouTube MCQ practice lead:** [Essentials in Acute Angle Closure Glaucoma MCQ](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=3M07VcgIZvo). UKMLA description-level lead. Exact mechanism question, creator and playback pending.

</details>

#### PYQ4. A patient using a topical alpha-2 adrenergic agonist for glaucoma reports persistent dry mouth. Which prescribed drug is most consistent with this recognized adverse effect?

**Difficulty:** 🟡 Medium · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD · Official-paper-concept adaptation, medically reasoned key

- **A.** Latanoprost
- **B.** Brimonidine
- **C.** Dorzolamide
- **D.** Pilocarpine

<details><summary>✅ Answer aur detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: B. Brimonidine**

Brimonidine alpha-2 adrenergic agonist hai. Topical administration ke baad bhi systemic absorption ho sakta hai. Dry mouth aur drowsiness recognized adverse effects hain. Persistent symptoms report karo aur prescribed therapy independently discontinue mat karo.

- **A.** Latanoprost prostaglandin analogue hai. Iris pigmentation aur eyelash growth change important effects hain.
- **C.** Dorzolamide carbonic anhydrase inhibitor hai. Ye alpha-2 agonist nahi hai.
- **D.** Pilocarpine muscarinic agonist hai aur miosis produce karti hai. Ye alpha-2 agonist nahi hai.

**Clinical support:** [DailyMed brimonidine/timolol label](https://dailymed.nlm.nih.gov/dailymed/drugInfo.cfm?setid=038cf2ba-ad08-4981-a3cc-bff0e4ba5dfb). Official historical answer key unavailable.
