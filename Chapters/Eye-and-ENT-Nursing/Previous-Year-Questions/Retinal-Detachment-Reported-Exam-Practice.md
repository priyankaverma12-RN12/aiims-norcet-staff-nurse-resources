#### PYQ1. In the absence of recent trauma, which age-associated change commonly predisposes an older adult to rhegmatogenous retinal detachment?

**Difficulty:** 🟡 Medium · **Priority:** 🔴 MOST IMPORTANT · Reported adaptation

- **A.** Progressive stiffening of the crystalline lens alone
- **B.** Declining tear production alone
- **C.** Degeneration and liquefaction of the vitreous with possible retinal traction
- **D.** Physiological slowing of pupillary constriction alone

<details><summary>✅ Answer aur detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: C. Degeneration and liquefaction of the vitreous with possible retinal traction**

Age ke saath vitreous ki consistency change aur gel shrink ho sakti hai. Posterior vitreous detach hote waqt firm retinal adhesion par traction generate ho sakti hai. Retinal break se fluid retina ke neeche enter karke rhegmatogenous detachment cause kar sakti hai. Har PVD retinal tear nahi banati.

- **A.** Lens stiffening presbyopia se related hai, retinal traction ka primary mechanism nahi.
- **B.** Reduced tear production ocular surface dryness cause kar sakti hai.
- **D.** Pupil response change alone retinal tear ka common cause nahi.

**Clinical reference:** [NEI types and causes](https://www.nei.nih.gov/eye-health-information/eye-conditions-and-diseases/retinal-detachment/types-and-causes-retinal-detachment).

#### PYQ2. A patient being assessed for a retinal tear reports the sudden appearance of numerous dark floating spots and hazy vision. Which associated finding should be investigated?

**Difficulty:** 🟡 Medium · **Priority:** 🔴 MOST IMPORTANT · Official-paper-concept adaptation, medically reasoned key

- **A.** Vitreous hemorrhage
- **B.** Isolated physiological presbyopia
- **C.** Uncomplicated external ear obstruction
- **D.** Stable hyperopia without other ocular pathology

<details><summary>✅ Answer aur detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: A. Vitreous hemorrhage**

Retinal tear ke saath retinal blood vessel injure ho sakti hai aur blood vitreous cavity mein enter kar sakta hai. Patient sudden dark floaters ya haze notice kar sakta hai. Symptoms alone hemorrhage confirm nahi karte. Urgent dilated retinal examination aur view obscured ho toh appropriate specialist investigation chahiye.

- **B.** Presbyopia gradually near accommodation affect karti hai. Sudden dark floaters typical feature nahi.
- **C.** Ear obstruction ocular vitreous symptoms explain nahi karta.
- **D.** Stable refractive mismatch suddenly appearing moving opacities explain nahi karta.

**Clinical reference:** [AAO PVD and retinal breaks preferred practice pattern](https://www.aao.org/education/preferred-practice-pattern/posterior-vitreous-detachment-retinal-breaks-latti).
