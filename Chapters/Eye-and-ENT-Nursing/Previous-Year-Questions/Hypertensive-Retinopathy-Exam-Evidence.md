### Source-based adaptation (not verbatim)

A patient arrives with markedly elevated BP and confusion. Which newly documented eye finding most strongly supports acute hypertensive target-organ damage?

- **A.** New bilateral retinal flame hemorrhages with optic disc swelling
- **B.** Stable age-related presbyopia
- **C.** An unchanged old corneal scar
- **D.** A previously corrected refractive error

<details><summary>Answer aur detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct: A. New bilateral retinal flame hemorrhages with optic disc swelling**

**Kyun sahi hai:** Severe hypertension se retinal vessels acutely injure ho sakti hain aur optic disc swell kar sakti hai. Confusion ke saath ye emergency systemic assessment require karta hai. BP ko clinician-directed monitored plan se manage karna hai.

**Baaki options:**

- **B.** Presbyopia age-related accommodation loss hai. Ye acute vascular injury ka sign nahi hai.
- **C.** Old unchanged corneal scar acute hypertensive retinal damage establish nahi karta.
- **D.** Corrected refractive error optical focusing se related hai. Acute target-organ injury ka evidence nahi hai.
