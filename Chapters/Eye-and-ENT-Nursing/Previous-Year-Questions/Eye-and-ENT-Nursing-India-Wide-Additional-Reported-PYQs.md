### PYQ1. In which inner-ear structure are the sensory hair cells responsible for hearing situated?

**Difficulty:** Easy/Medium | **Topic:** Hearing physiology

- **A.** Cochlea
- **B.** Utricle
- **C.** Saccule
- **D.** Semicircular canals

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation</summary>

**Correct: A. Cochlea**

Cochlea ke organ of Corti mein hearing ke sensory hair cells hote hain. Utricle aur saccule linear acceleration aur head position sense karte hain. Semicircular canals angular acceleration sense karte hain.
