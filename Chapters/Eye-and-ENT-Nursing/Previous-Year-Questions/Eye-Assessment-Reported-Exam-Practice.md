#### PYQ1. Which sensory function is assessed using a standard distance Snellen chart?

**Difficulty:** 🟢 Easy · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD · Reported adaptation

- **A.** Auditory threshold
- **B.** Distance visual acuity
- **C.** Olfactory discrimination
- **D.** Tactile localization

<details><summary>✅ Answer aur detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: B. Distance visual acuity**

Snellen chart progressively smaller letters ya symbols se vision ki sharpness assess karta hai. Patient ko specified distance par ek eye se chart read karne ko kehte hain. Isse nurse vision reduction screen kar sakti hai.

- **A.** Auditory threshold hearing sensitivity hai aur audiometry se assess ki ja sakti hai.
- **C.** Olfactory discrimination smell identify karne ki ability hai, eye chart se measure nahi hoti.
- **D.** Tactile localization touch stimulus ki location identify karna hai.

**Exam trick:** Snellen = distance vision. Jaeger = near vision.

#### PYQ2. A clinician wants to quantify pressure inside the eyeball. Which examination is appropriate?

**Difficulty:** 🟢 Easy · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD · Reported adaptation

- **A.** Perimetry
- **B.** Gonioscopy
- **C.** Tonometry
- **D.** Amsler grid testing

<details><summary>✅ Answer aur detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: C. Tonometry**

Tonometry aankh ke andar ka pressure estimate karti hai aur result mmHg mein record hota hai. Goldmann applanation ek established method hai. Pressure alone glaucoma establish ya exclude nahi karta. Suspected open-globe injury mein tonometry avoid ki jaati hai.

- **A.** Perimetry visual field map karti hai, pressure measure nahi karti.
- **B.** Gonioscopy iris aur cornea ke beech drainage angle inspect karti hai.
- **D.** Amsler grid central vision distortion screen karti hai.

**Exam trick:** Tonometry pressure, gonioscopy angle, perimetry field.

[Retrieved Testbook paper attribution and question](https://testbook.com/question-answer/all-are-the-examination-methods-for-assessment-of--668e5fb048fdcf6e24fe3a2a). Official booklet and key not independently inspected.

#### PYQ3. Which test belongs primarily to neurological balance assessment rather than routine ophthalmic examination?

**Difficulty:** 🟡 Medium · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD · Reported adaptation

- **A.** Slit-lamp examination
- **B.** Refraction
- **C.** Romberg test
- **D.** Tonometry

<details><summary>✅ Answer aur detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: C. Romberg test**

Romberg test standing balance aur sensory contribution to postural stability assess karta hai. Ismein visual input remove karke balance observe kiya jaata hai, lekin iska main purpose eye pathology examine karna nahi hai. Fall precautions zaroori hain.

- **A.** Slit lamp magnified eye structures examine karne mein help karta hai.
- **B.** Refraction optical focusing error aur corrective lens requirement assess karti hai.
- **D.** Tonometry IOP measure karti hai.

**Exam trick:** Balance ka test aur eye structure/function ka test alag rakho.
