### Provisional related-concept practice — not the original paper

**Difficulty:** 🟢 Easy · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD · Independent practice based on an indexed lead. Not counted as a historical adaptation pending direct retrieval and inventory reconciliation.

A nursing record uses the term blepharoptosis. Which clinical observation is consistent with this term?

- **A.** Outward rotation of the lower eyelid margin
- **B.** Abnormally low position of the upper eyelid margin
- **C.** Additional row of eyelashes
- **D.** Incomplete closure of the eyelids

<details><summary>✅ Correct answer and detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: B. Abnormally low position of the upper eyelid margin**

**A kyun galat:** Lower lid margin bahar rotate hone ko ectropion kehte hain. Ismein upper lid height ka abnormal low position hona required nahi hai.

**B kyun sahi:** Ptosis mein upper lid margin normal position se neeche hoti hai. Muscle, nerve, aponeurosis ya mechanical cause is position ko affect kar sakta hai.

**C kyun galat:** Additional lash row ko distichiasis kehte hain. Ye lash origin ka disorder hai, lid height ka definition nahi.

**D kyun galat:** Incomplete lid closure ko lagophthalmos kehte hain. Ptosis elevation problem hai, jabki lagophthalmos closure problem hai.

**Exam clue:** Ptosis = upper lid droop, ectropion = outward lid rotation.

[Clinical reference — EyeWiki](https://eyewiki.aao.org/Blepharoptosis). Medically reasoned answer. Official key not verified.
