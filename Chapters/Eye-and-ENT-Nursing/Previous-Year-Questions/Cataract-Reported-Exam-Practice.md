### 🔵 Central Institute of Psychiatry — 9 July 2022 — reported clinical manifestation question

[Retrieved NPrep report](https://nprep.in/question-answer/early-sign-of-cataract-include-bdJdJqrdohQiZVYVuuO_M). Source selects blurred vision from its options. The adaptation avoids claiming a universal first symptom, because glare can also occur early.

#### PYQ1. Which symptom pattern is most consistent with an uncomplicated age-related cataract rather than an acute ocular emergency?

**Difficulty:** 🟡 Medium · **Priority:** 🔴 MOST IMPORTANT · Reported adaptation

- **A.** Slowly progressive painless cloudy vision
- **B.** Sudden severe eye pain with vomiting
- **C.** Abrupt curtain-like loss of the visual field
- **D.** Acute penetrating ocular trauma

<details><summary>✅ Answer aur detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: A. Slowly progressive painless cloudy vision**

Natural lens ki transparency gradually kam hone se light scatter hoti hai. Isliye patient ko foggy ya blurry vision, glare aur colours dull lag sakte hain. Typical age-related cataract painless hoti hai. Symptom onset aur clinical assessment se other conditions differentiate karo.

- **B.** Severe pain aur vomiting acute pressure-related emergency ka clue ho sakte hain.
- **C.** Abrupt curtain-like shadow retinal detachment ka possible red flag hai.
- **D.** Penetrating trauma urgent ocular injury assessment maangti hai, routine cataract pattern nahi.

**Clinical reference:** [NEI cataract and the eye](https://www.nei.nih.gov/eye-health-information/eye-conditions-and-diseases/cataracts/cataracts-and-eye).

### 🔵 ESIC Nursing Officer — 7 July 2024 / GMCH Nursing Officer — 2019 — reported perioperative advice family

[ESIC postoperative care report](https://nprep.in/question-answer/a-nurse-caring-the-patient-after-cataract-surgery-should-include-which-of-MABjg-upSFPzu-59veyxg) selects topical antibiotic, corticosteroid and activity advice rather than routine sedatives. [GMCH 2019 preoperative teaching report](https://testbook.com/question-answer/a-nurse-performs-pre-operative-teaching-for-a-clie--625ff7b7ba6dfaedb9ec1ead) selects advice against bending at the waist. Shared activity-safety concept is consolidated to avoid duplicate historical counts. Exact postoperative regimen and activity duration vary.

#### PYQ2. Which discharge plan best supports uncomplicated cataract surgery recovery?

**Difficulty:** 🟡 Medium · **Priority:** 🔴 MOST IMPORTANT · Reported-concept adaptation

- **A.** Give routine sedatives to every patient and omit recovery teaching
- **B.** Follow the prescribed anti-infective and anti-inflammatory drop regimen, explain individual activity restrictions, and provide emergency contact instructions
- **C.** Allow immediate unrestricted heavy lifting regardless of the surgeon's instructions
- **D.** Discontinue all prescribed eye drops when mild irritation resolves

<details><summary>✅ Answer aur detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: B. Follow the prescribed anti-infective and anti-inflammatory drop regimen, explain individual activity restrictions, and provide emergency contact instructions**

Surgery ke baad infection prevention, inflammation control aur operated eye protection important hain. Har patient ka exact regimen surgeon ke according hota hai. Antibiotic ya anti-inflammatory drops prescribed hain toh use sikhana hai. Activity restrictions wound aur surgery type ke according explain karo. Routine sedatives har patient ke liye required nahi hain. Increasing pain, redness aur vision loss mein urgent contact instructions do.

- **A.** Sedation individual indication par depend karti hai. Routine sedation fall risk badha sakti hai aur education omit karna unsafe hai.
- **C.** Heavy lifting ke restrictions treating surgeon decide karta hai. Blanket unrestricted advice appropriate nahi hai.
- **D.** Mild irritation resolve hone se prescribed course complete nahi ho jaata. Medicines prescribed duration ke hisaab se follow karo.

**Clinical reference:** [NEI surgery and recovery](https://www.nei.nih.gov/eye-health-information/eye-conditions-and-diseases/cataracts/cataract-surgery).

### 🔵 GMCH Nursing Officer — 2025 — reported postoperative analgesia question

[Retrieved NPrep report](https://nprep.in/question-answer/which-of-the-following-advice-given-by-nurse-to-a-postoperative-patient-ctmG6tmvzXN3Bl-O0jL8z) tests inappropriate aspirin advice after extracapsular extraction and IOL implantation. The reported answer is aspirin for pain. Clinical adaptation distinguishes starting aspirin independently for pain from a patient already taking prescribed antiplatelet therapy.

#### PYQ3. A patient who has undergone cataract surgery wants to start aspirin for postoperative pain without consulting the surgical team. What is the safest advice?

**Difficulty:** 🟡 Medium · **Priority:** 🔴 MOST IMPORTANT · Reported adaptation

- **A.** Start additional aspirin for every episode of pain without review
- **B.** Stop all prescribed antiplatelet therapy immediately even if used for cardiovascular prevention
- **C.** Use only the approved postoperative pain plan and clarify any aspirin or antiplatelet changes with the treating team
- **D.** Ignore severe new eye pain if the patient is taking an analgesic

<details><summary>✅ Answer aur detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: C. Use only the approved postoperative pain plan and clarify any aspirin or antiplatelet changes with the treating team**

Aspirin platelet function affect karti hai. Postoperative pain ke liye additional aspirin independently start karna appropriate nahi hai. Jo patient cardiovascular reason se aspirin already leta hai, uski prescribed medicine independently stop bhi nahi karni. Surgeon aur prescribing clinician bleeding aur clotting risks assess karke individual plan decide karte hain. Severe new eye pain urgent review maangti hai.

- **A.** Unsupervised additional aspirin bleeding risk aur other medication issues create kar sakti hai.
- **B.** Abrupt prescribed antiplatelet cessation thrombosis risk badha sakti hai. Individual plan zaroori hai.
- **D.** Severe postoperative pain complication ka sign ho sakti hai. Analgesic use emergency assessment replace nahi karta.

**Clinical references:** [NEI surgery](https://www.nei.nih.gov/eye-health-information/eye-conditions-and-diseases/cataracts/cataract-surgery) and [AAO cataract benchmark](https://www.aao.org/education/summary-benchmark-detail/cataract-anterior-segment-summary-benchmark-2020).
