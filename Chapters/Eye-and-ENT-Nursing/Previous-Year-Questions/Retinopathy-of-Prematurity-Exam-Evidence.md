A nurse explains why excessive oxygen exposure in a premature infant requires careful prevention. Which retinal complication is particularly associated with this exposure?

- **A.** Retinopathy of prematurity
- **B.** Congenital color vision deficiency
- **C.** Age-related macular degeneration
- **D.** Presbyopia

<details><summary>Answer aur detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct: A. Retinopathy of prematurity**

Premature retina ki vascular development incomplete ho sakti hai. Unregulated excessive oxygen aur oxygen fluctuations normal vessel growth disturb kar sakte hain. Later abnormal neovascularization develop ho sakti hai. Prescribed oxygen lifesaving hai, isliye correct monitoring and titration chahiye, abrupt withdrawal nahi.

- **B.** Congenital color deficiency usually inherited photopigment-related problem hai, oxygen-associated premature retinal vasculopathy nahi.
- **C.** AMD age-associated macular disorder hai, premature infant vascular development disorder nahi.
- **D.** Presbyopia age-associated accommodation loss hai.
