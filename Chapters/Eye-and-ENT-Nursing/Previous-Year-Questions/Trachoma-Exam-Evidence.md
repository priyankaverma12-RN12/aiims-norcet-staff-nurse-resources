### Independently worded related exam adaptation

A community health worker is organizing a SAFE intervention. Which activity represents the S component rather than case detection?

**Difficulty:** Medium · **Priority:** MOST IMPORTANT

- A. Identifying children with conjunctival follicles during a survey
- B. Arranging corrective eyelid surgery for trachomatous trichiasis
- C. Supporting safe water access in underserved villages
- D. Administering prescribed azithromycin to eligible residents

<details><summary>✅ Answer aur detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: B. Arranging corrective eyelid surgery for trachomatous trichiasis.**

SAFE ka S surgical management hai, jisse inward lashes ka corneal contact reduce hota hai. Early detection important hai but acronym mein S screening nahi.

- A. Screening disease identify karta hai, SAFE ka literal S component nahi.
- C. Safe water environmental improvement ka component hai.
- D. Azithromycin antibiotic component hai.
