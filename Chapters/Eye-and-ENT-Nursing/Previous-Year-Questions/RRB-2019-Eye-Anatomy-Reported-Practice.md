### PYQ1. Which tissue converts incoming light into neural signals that can be transmitted toward the brain?

**Difficulty:** 🟢 Easy · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD · Reported adaptation

- **A.** Iris
- **B.** Lens
- **C.** Retina
- **D.** Sclera

<details><summary>✅ Answer aur detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: C. Retina**

**Kyun sahi hai:** Retina mein rods aur cones light ko detect karte hain. Retinal cells information ko process karte hain aur ganglion cell axons optic nerve ke through signal brain ki taraf le jaate hain. Isliye retina signal banane aur optic nerve signal le jaane mein important hai.

**A kyun galat:** Iris pupil ka size control karti hai aur light ki quantity adjust karti hai.

**B kyun galat:** Lens light ko retina par focus karne mein help karta hai, lekin neural signals nahi banata.

**D kyun galat:** Sclera aankh ko shape aur protection deti hai, photoreceptor tissue nahi hai.

**Exam trick:** Retina light ko signal mein badalti hai. Optic nerve signal brain tak le jaati hai.
