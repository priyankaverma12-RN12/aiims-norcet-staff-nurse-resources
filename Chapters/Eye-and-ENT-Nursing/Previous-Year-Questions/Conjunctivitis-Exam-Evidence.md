### Related source-based clinical adaptation (not verbatim)

A patient has bilateral itchy red eyes and unspecified discharge. Which additional history would most strongly support allergic conjunctivitis rather than infectious disease?

- A. Recurrent episodes immediately after pollen exposure with associated sneezing
- B. A recent cluster of contagious red-eye illness among close contacts
- C. Rapidly reaccumulating copious purulent discharge
- D. Fever and sore throat accompanying a new outbreak of conjunctivitis

<details><summary>Answer aur detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct: A. Recurrent episodes immediately after pollen exposure with associated sneezing.**

Allergen exposure ke baad repeated ocular itching aur nasal allergy symptoms allergic mechanism support karte hain. Clinical diagnosis still examination aur complete history par depend karti hai.

- B. Close contact outbreak infectious transmission support karta hai.
- C. Copious purulence hyperacute bacterial infection ka red flag ho sakti hai.
- D. Fever aur sore throat adenoviral pharyngoconjunctival fever ka clue ho sakte hain.
