#### PYQ1. Which finding in a patient with advanced age-related macular degeneration is most characteristic of the affected retinal region?

**Difficulty:** 🟡 Medium · **Priority:** 🔴 MOST IMPORTANT · Reported adaptation

- **A.** Selective loss of fine central detail, with potentially useful peripheral vision
- **B.** A primary corneal opacity causing diffuse optical scattering
- **C.** Isolated early glaucomatous peripheral field loss without central symptoms
- **D.** A conductive hearing deficit caused by middle-ear effusion

<details><summary>✅ Answer aur detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: A. Selective loss of fine central detail, with potentially useful peripheral vision**

Macula sharp central vision mein important role play karti hai. Jab macula damage hoti hai toh patient ko face recognition aur reading mein difficulty ho sakti hai, even if side vision remains useful. Original reported question central visual loss test karti hai. Dry AMD generally wet form se more common hai, lekin fixed prevalence percentages ko universal current fact assume nahi karna.

**Baaki options kyun galat hain?**

- **B.** Corneal opacity light transmission affect karti hai, jabki AMD retinal macula ko primarily affect karti hai.
- **C.** Peripheral field damage glaucoma mein common early pattern ho sakta hai. AMD ka characteristic functional effect central detail loss hai.
- **D.** Middle-ear effusion sound conduction affect karti hai, macular vision nahi.
