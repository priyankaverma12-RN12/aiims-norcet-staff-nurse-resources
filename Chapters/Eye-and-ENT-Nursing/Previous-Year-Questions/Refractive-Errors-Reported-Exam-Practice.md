#### PYQ1. A spherical refractive error causes distant objects to focus in front of the retina when accommodation is relaxed. Which type of spectacle lens is used to compensate?

**Difficulty:** 🟡 Medium · **Priority:** 🔴 MOST IMPORTANT · Reported adaptation

- **A.** Positive spherical lens
- **B.** Negative spherical lens
- **C.** Plano lens
- **D.** Prism alone

<details><summary>✅ Answer aur detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: B. Negative spherical lens**

Myopic eye mein distance light retina se pehle focus karti hai. Negative ya concave lens light ko eye mein enter hone se pehle spread karta hai. Isse optical focus retina ki taraf shift hota hai. Correct power refraction se decide hoti hai.

- **A.** Positive lens convergence badhati hai, jo simple myopic distance correction ke liye suitable nahi hai.
- **C.** Plano lens ki spherical corrective power zero hoti hai.
- **D.** Prism light ki direction change kar sakta hai lekin simple spherical myopia ki required focusing power provide nahi karta.

**Exam trick:** Myopia = minus. Source question family ka direction of correction clinically consistent hai. Source ke additional summaries mein presbyopia ko dono distances par inevitable blur batana accurate nahi hai.

#### PYQ2. In simple hypermetropia, which spherical spectacle correction helps compensate for insufficient resting refractive power?

**Difficulty:** 🟡 Medium · **Priority:** 🔴 MOST IMPORTANT · Reported adaptation

- **A.** Diverging minus lens
- **B.** Plano protective lens
- **C.** Converging plus lens
- **D.** Prism alone

<details><summary>✅ Answer aur detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: C. Converging plus lens**

Hypermetropic eye mein relaxed focus retina ke peeche hota hai. Convex ya plus lens incoming rays ko converge karta hai. Isse required optical power milti hai aur focus retina ki taraf aata hai. Young patients accommodation se kuch hyperopia compensate kar sakte hain, isliye symptoms aur measured refraction dono important hain.

- **A.** Minus lens divergence badhata hai aur simple hypermetropia ki insufficient power compensate nahi karta.
- **B.** Plano lens optical power add nahi karta.
- **D.** Prism alone spherical focusing error correct nahi karta.

**Exam trick:** Hyperopia = plus. Presbyopia bhi near plus addition use kar sakti hai, lekin mechanism alag hai.

#### PYQ3. A school screening record uses the term nearsightedness for a child's refractive problem. Which medical term has the same meaning?

**Difficulty:** 🟢 Easy · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD · Reported adaptation

- **A.** Hyperopia
- **B.** Nyctalopia
- **C.** Myopia
- **D.** Presbyopia

<details><summary>✅ Answer aur detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: C. Myopia**

Myopia mein relaxed eye distance light ko retina se pehle focus karti hai. Isliye door ki cheezein blur ho sakti hain aur near vision relatively better ho sakti hai. Nearsightedness isi refractive condition ka common English name hai.

- **A.** Hyperopia mein relaxed eye ki optical power insufficient ho sakti hai. Iska common name farsightedness hai.
- **B.** Nyctalopia dim light mein dekhne ki difficulty hai. Ye near ya distance refractive focus ka synonym nahi hai.
- **D.** Presbyopia age-related near accommodation loss hai.

**Exam trick:** Nearsightedness aur myopia same term hain. Concave correction alag reported question family mein already covered hai.

**Clinical source:** [NEI myopia](https://www.nei.nih.gov/eye-health-information/eye-conditions-and-diseases/nearsightedness-myopia).

#### PYQ4. Which finding distinguishes astigmatic refraction from a purely spherical refractive error?

**Difficulty:** 🟡 Medium · **Priority:** 🔴 MOST IMPORTANT · Reported adaptation

- **A.** Uniform refractive power in every meridian
- **B.** Unequal refractive power across meridians
- **C.** Complete absence of the natural lens
- **D.** Loss of hearing sensitivity with aging

<details><summary>✅ Answer aur detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: B. Unequal refractive power across meridians**

Astigmatism mein eye ke optical meridians ki power different hoti hai. Isliye light ek sharp point par focus nahi karti. Cornea ya lens ki shape is optical mismatch ka cause ho sakti hai. Ye concept original integrated Q13 se related hai, historical practice count alag hai.

- **A.** Uniform meridional power spherical optics ko describe karti hai.
- **C.** Natural lens absent hona aphakia hai. Ye astigmatism ki definition nahi.
- **D.** Age-related hearing loss presbycusis hai, refractive condition nahi.

**Clinical source:** [NEI refractive errors](https://www.nei.nih.gov/eye-health-information/eye-conditions-and-diseases/refractive-errors/types-refractive-errors).

#### PYQ5. In a standard elementary optics example, which distance is conventionally taken as the least distance of distinct vision of a normal young adult eye?

**Difficulty:** 🟢 Easy · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD · Reported adaptation

- **A.** 2.5 cm
- **B.** 2.5 m
- **C.** 25 cm
- **D.** 25 m

<details><summary>✅ Answer aur detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: C. 25 cm**

Elementary optics mein normal near-point example 25 cm use hota hai. Near point closest clear viewing distance hai. Actual value accommodation aur measurement method par depend karti hai. Presbyopia mein near point usually door shift karta hai. Is numerical convention ko universal physiological measurement mat samjho.

- **A.** 2.5 cm is conventional example se ten times closer hai.
- **B.** 2.5 m near-point textbook example se ten times farther hai.
- **D.** 25 m reading distance nahi hai aur standard near-point convention se match nahi karta.

**Clinical source:** [NEI presbyopia](https://www.nei.nih.gov/eye-health-information/eye-conditions-and-diseases/presbyopia).

#### PYQ6. When focal length is expressed in metres, which relationship gives the signed spherical power of a thin lens?

**Difficulty:** 🟢 Easy · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD · Reported adaptation

- **A.** P = f
- **B.** P = 1/f
- **C.** P = f²
- **D.** P = −f

<details><summary>✅ Answer aur detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: B. P = 1/f**

Lens power focal length ka reciprocal hai. Focal length metres mein use karne se power diopters mein milti hai. Convex lens ka focal length positive aur concave lens ka negative hota hai in the usual thin-lens convention. Ye general-science concept hai. Actual spectacle prescription clinical refraction se decide hoti hai.

- **A.** Focal length aur power directly equal nahi hote.
- **C.** Focal length square karne se optical power nahi milti.
- **D.** Sirf sign change karne se reciprocal relation nahi milta.

#### PYQ7. A converging thin lens produces a real image 24 cm from the lens when an object is placed 40 cm from it. What is the focal length?

**Difficulty:** 🟡 Medium · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD · Reported-concept adaptation

- **A.** 64 cm
- **B.** 16 cm
- **C.** 15 cm
- **D.** 24 cm

<details><summary>✅ Answer aur detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: C. 15 cm**

Real object aur real image ke liye positive distance convention use karo. Formula 1/f = 1/d_o + 1/d_i hai. 1/f = 1/40 + 1/24 = 3/120 + 5/120 = 8/120 = 1/15. Isliye f = +15 cm. Source report ke original numbers 30 aur 20 cm hain, jinka answer +12 cm hai. Dono ko mix mat karo.

- **A.** 64 cm distances ka sum hai, focal length nahi.
- **B.** 16 cm distances ka difference hai, lens equation ka answer nahi.
- **D.** 24 cm image distance hai, focal length nahi.

**Physics reference:** [OpenStax thin lenses](https://openstax.org/books/university-physics-volume-3/pages/2-4-thin-lenses).

#### PYQ8. For a finite real object viewed through an ideal diverging thin lens in air, how does the image size normally compare with the object?

**Difficulty:** 🟢 Easy · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD · Reported adaptation

- **A.** Always greater than the object
- **B.** Smaller than the object
- **C.** Exactly twice the object
- **D.** Always identical to the object

<details><summary>✅ Answer aur detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: B. Smaller than the object**

Concave lens real object se aane wali rays ko diverge karti hai. Ideal lens mein rays ko peeche extend karne par virtual upright diminished image milti hai. Ye isolated lens ka result hai, poore corrected human eye ki retinal image ka direct description nahi.

- **A.** Real object ke liye ideal diverging lens ka image magnification magnitude 1 se kam hota hai.
- **C.** Double size is optical condition ke saath compatible nahi.
- **D.** Equal size bhi finite real object aur ideal diverging lens ke liye expected nahi.

**Physics reference:** [OpenStax image formation](https://openstax.org/books/college-physics-2e/pages/25-6-image-formation-by-lenses).
