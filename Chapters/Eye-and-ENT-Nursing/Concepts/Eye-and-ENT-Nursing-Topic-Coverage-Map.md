# Eye and ENT Nursing — Topic coverage map

This is a curriculum-based checklist, not full theory notes. No learning completion is recorded.

- Eye anatomy and assessment
- cataract
- glaucoma and retinal emergencies
- eye injury and postoperative care
- ear disorders and hearing loss
- epistaxis
- tonsil and laryngeal conditions

## Initial study explanations

### Glaucoma

Aankh ke andar fluid ka outflow block ho toh pressure achanak badh sakta hai. Severe pain aur halos ke saath vision ko permanent harm ho sakta hai. Emergency eye review chahiye.

### Retinal detachment

Retina aankh ke andar light ko signal mein badalti hai. Agar retina detach ho, toh flashes aur curtain jaisa shadow aa sakta hai. Jaldi treatment vision bachane mein help kar sakta hai.

### Epistaxis

Nose ki front vessels se bleeding ho toh soft part press karne se clot banne mein help milti hai. Aage jhukne se blood throat mein jaane ka risk kam hota hai.

### Post-tonsillectomy

Tonsil ki surgery ke baad blood throat mein ja sakta hai aur child baar-baar swallow kar sakta hai. Pallor aur fast pulse blood loss ke clues hain. Nurse emergency review leti hai.
