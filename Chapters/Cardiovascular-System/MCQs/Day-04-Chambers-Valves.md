## Chambers and internal structures — Very High priority

### Q1. During a cardiac anatomy assessment, a nurse identifies a vessel that drains most of the venous blood from the myocardium. Into which cardiac chamber does this vessel open?

- **A.** Left atrium
- **B.** Right ventricle
- **C.** Left ventricle
- **D.** Right atrium

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Exam focus:** NORCET / HPRCA / PGIMER

<details><summary>Hint dekho</summary>

Heart muscle ki venous drainage aur chambers ke receiving vessels recall karo.

</details>

<details><summary>✅ Answer aur detailed explanation dekho</summary>

**D. Right atrium**

**Kyun sahi?** Coronary sinus myocardium se majority venous blood collect karta hai aur directly right atrium mein open hota hai. RA mein SVC, IVC aur coronary sinus main venous openings hain.

**Baaki options kyun nahi?** A LA mainly pulmonary veins se oxygenated blood receive karta hai. B RV RA se tricuspid valve ke through receive karta hai. C LV LA se mitral valve ke through receive karta hai. Coronary sinus in chambers mein normally open nahi hota.

**Exam trick:** Coronary sinus → RA; pulmonary veins → LA.

</details>
