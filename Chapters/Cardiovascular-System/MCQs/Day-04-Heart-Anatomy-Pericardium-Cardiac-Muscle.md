### Q1. During cardiovascular assessment, a nurse palpates the normal apex beat of an adult patient. At which location should it normally be felt?

- **A.** Right 2nd intercostal space, sternal border
- **B.** Left 4th intercostal space, midaxillary line
- **C.** Left 5th intercostal space, midclavicular line
- **D.** Left 2nd intercostal space, sternal border

**Difficulty:** Easy | **Exam focus:** NORCET / HPRCA / All-India Staff Nurse

<details><summary>✅ Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**C. Left 5th intercostal space, midclavicular line**

**Kyun sahi?** Normal adult PMI left 5th ICS MCL mein feel hota hai. Apex LV se formed hai.

**Baaki options kyun nahi?** A aortic area hai; B normal PMI se lateral aur upper hai; D pulmonary area hai.

**Exam trick:** Apex = Left + 5th ICS + MCL.

</details>

### Q2. A patient is diagnosed with inflammation of the muscular layer of the heart responsible for contraction and pumping of blood. Which layer is affected?

- **A.** Endocardium
- **B.** Fibrous pericardium
- **C.** Epicardium
- **D.** Myocardium

**Difficulty:** Easy | **Exam focus:** NORCET / HPRCA / All-India Staff Nurse

<details><summary>✅ Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**D. Myocardium**

**Kyun sahi?** Myocardium muscular middle layer hai; inflammation myocarditis hai.

**Baaki options kyun nahi?** A inner lining hai; B tough outer sac hai; C outer wall hai, pumping muscle nahi.

**Exam trick:** Myo = muscle.

</details>

### Q3. Which of the following structures forms the major part of the base of the heart?

- **A.** Right atrium
- **B.** Left atrium
- **C.** Right ventricle
- **D.** Left ventricle

**Difficulty:** Easy | **Exam focus:** NORCET / HPRCA / All-India Staff Nurse

<details><summary>✅ Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**B. Left atrium**

**Kyun sahi?** Base broad posterior part hai, mainly LA.

**Baaki options kyun nahi?** A right border banata hai; C anterior surface; D apex.

**Exam trick:** Base LA, apex LV.

</details>

### Q4. The pericardial cavity is located between which two layers?

- **A.** Fibrous pericardium and parietal serous pericardium
- **B.** Epicardium and myocardium
- **C.** Parietal and visceral layers of serous pericardium
- **D.** Myocardium and endocardium

**Difficulty:** Easy | **Exam focus:** NORCET / HPRCA / All-India Staff Nurse

<details><summary>✅ Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**C. Parietal and visceral layers of serous pericardium**

**Kyun sahi?** Serous layers ke beech potential cavity hai, approximately 15–50 mL fluid.

**Baaki options kyun nahi?** A outer sac ki attached layers hain; B aur D heart wall ke adjacent layers hain, cavity nahi.

**Exam trick:** Visceral serous = epicardium.

</details>

### Q5. A patient develops excessive accumulation of fluid in the pericardial cavity. The fluid compresses the heart and interferes with ventricular filling. Which condition is most likely present?

- **A.** Cardiac tamponade
- **B.** Myocarditis
- **C.** Endocarditis
- **D.** Myocardial infarction

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Exam focus:** NORCET / HPRCA / All-India Staff Nurse

<details><summary>✅ Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**A. Cardiac tamponade**

**Kyun sahi?** Fluid compression → reduced filling → reduced cardiac output. Yeh tamponade hai.

**Baaki options kyun nahi?** B muscle inflammation hai; C inner lining inflammation hai; D ischemic muscle injury/death hai.

**Exam trick:** Tamponade = pressure on heart; effusion alone is not tamponade.

</details>

### Q6. Which chamber of the heart has the thickest muscular wall, and why?

- **A.** Right atrium – receives systemic venous blood
- **B.** Right ventricle – pumps blood to the lungs
- **C.** Left ventricle – pumps blood into systemic circulation
- **D.** Left atrium – receives oxygenated blood

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Exam focus:** NORCET / HPRCA / All-India Staff Nurse

<details><summary>✅ Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**C. Left ventricle – pumps blood into systemic circulation**

**Kyun sahi?** LV systemic circulation ke liye high pressure generate karta hai, isliye thickest muscle.

**Baaki options kyun nahi?** A aur D receiving chambers hain; B pulmonary circulation lower pressure par pump karta hai.

**Exam trick:** More workload → more pressure → thicker muscle.

</details>

### Q7. Which structure prevents sudden excessive distension (overstretching) of the heart and helps maintain its position within the mediastinum?

- **A.** Endocardium
- **B.** Fibrous pericardium
- **C.** Myocardium
- **D.** Pericardial fluid

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Exam focus:** NORCET / HPRCA / All-India Staff Nurse

<details><summary>✅ Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**B. Fibrous pericardium**

**Kyun sahi?** Fibrous pericardium tough outer covering hai; stretching limit karta hai aur position maintain karta hai.

**Baaki options kyun nahi?** A smooth lining hai; C contraction/pumping karta hai; D friction reduce karta hai.

**Exam trick:** Fibrous = firm covering.

</details>

### Q8. Which layer of the heart provides a smooth internal lining to the cardiac chambers and covers the heart valves?

- **A.** Epicardium
- **B.** Myocardium
- **C.** Parietal pericardium
- **D.** Endocardium

**Difficulty:** Easy | **Exam focus:** NORCET / HPRCA / All-India Staff Nurse

<details><summary>✅ Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**D. Endocardium**

**Kyun sahi?** Endocardium smooth internal lining hai; valves ko bhi cover karta hai.

**Baaki options kyun nahi?** A outer wall hai; B muscular middle layer hai; C sac ki lining hai.

**Exam trick:** Endo = inside.

</details>
