## Hemodynamics and assessment

### Q1. A patient has a heart rate of 88/min and stroke volume of 55 mL. What is the estimated cardiac output?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Exam focus:** NORCET / Staff Nurse

- **A.** 4.84 L/min
- **B.** 1.60 L/min
- **C.** 0.484 L/min
- **D.** 48.4 L/min

<details><summary>✅ Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: A. 4.84 L/min**

**Kyun sahi hai?** Heart har minute 88 x 55 = 4840 mL blood pump karta hai. 1000 se divide karo toh 4.84 L/min.

**Baaki options kyun galat hain?**

- **B.** Galat multiplication hai
- **C.** Decimal ek place galat hai
- **D.** 10 guna zyada hai

**Exam trick:** CO = HR x SV.

</details>

### Q2. A patient's blood pressure is 84/48 mmHg. What is the approximate mean arterial pressure using the usual resting formula?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Exam focus:** NORCET / Staff Nurse

- **A.** 66 mmHg
- **B.** 36 mmHg
- **C.** 132 mmHg
- **D.** 60 mmHg

<details><summary>✅ Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: D. 60 mmHg**

**Kyun sahi hai?** MAP = (SBP + 2 x DBP)/3. (84 + 96)/3 = 60. Low pressure se organs ko blood kam mil sakta hai.

**Baaki options kyun galat hain?**

- **A.** Simple average hai, MAP nahi
- **B.** Pulse pressure hai
- **C.** Sum hai, divide nahi kiya

**Exam trick:** MAP formula weights diastolic pressure twice.

</details>

### Q3. An echocardiogram shows an end-diastolic volume of 150 mL and end-systolic volume of 90 mL. What is the ejection fraction?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Exam focus:** NORCET / Staff Nurse

- **A.** 90%
- **B.** 150%
- **C.** 40%
- **D.** 60%

<details><summary>✅ Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: C. 40%**

**Kyun sahi hai?** Stroke volume = 150 minus 90 = 60 mL. EF = 60/150 x 100 = 40%.

**Baaki options kyun galat hain?**

- **A.** 90 mL residual volume hai
- **B.** 150 mL filling volume hai
- **D.** 60 mL stroke volume hai

**Exam trick:** EF is percentage ejected, not volume.

</details>

## ECG and rhythm

### Q7. At standard ECG speed of 25 mm/s, a PR interval spans six small squares. How should it be interpreted?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Exam focus:** NORCET / Staff Nurse

- **C.** 0.06 seconds, short
- **D.** 0.24 seconds, prolonged

<details><summary>✅ Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: D. 0.24 seconds, prolonged**

**Kyun sahi hai?** Ek small square 0.04 sec. 6 x 0.04 = 0.24 sec. Normal PR usually 0.12–0.20 sec.

**Baaki options kyun galat hain?**

- **A.** Teen squares ka time hai
- **B.** Calculation galat
- **C.** Ek se bhi kam/lagbhag 1.5 square

**Exam trick:** Small ECG square = 0.04 seconds.

</details>

### Q8. An irregular rhythm has no consistent P waves and varying R-R intervals. What is the most likely interpretation?

- **C.** Atrial fibrillation
- **D.** Sinus rhythm with a fixed rate

<details><summary>✅ Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Kyun sahi hai?** Atria organized beat nahi karti. P waves clear nahi aur ventricles irregular beat karte hain.

- **D.** Sinus P waves expected

**Exam trick:** Irregularly irregular plus absent organized P.

</details>

### Q12. An ECG shows progressive PR lengthening followed by a dropped QRS, repeating in groups. Which conduction disorder fits?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Exam focus:** NORCET / Staff Nurse

- **A.** First-degree AV block
- **B.** Second-degree AV block Mobitz II with fixed PR
- **C.** Ventricular fibrillation
- **D.** Second-degree AV block Mobitz I

<details><summary>✅ Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: D. Second-degree AV block Mobitz I**

**Kyun sahi hai?** Har beat mein AV node signal dheere bhejta hai jab tak ek QRS miss ho. Ye Wenckebach pattern hai.

**Baaki options kyun galat hain?**

- **B.** Mobitz II mein conducted PR usually fixed
- **C.** VF organized P/QRS nahi

**Exam trick:** Longer, longer, dropped = Wenckebach.

</details>

- **B.** Coronary obstruction not defining mechanism
- **C.** Bradycardia universal nahi
- **D.** SVR often low in distributive shock

**Exam trick:** Early septic shock can be warm.
