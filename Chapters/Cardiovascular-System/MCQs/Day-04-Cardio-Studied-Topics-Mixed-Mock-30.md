### Q1. On a normal axial thoracic image, the heart is seen within the pericardial sac between the lungs. Which mediastinal subdivision contains it?

- **A.** Posterior mediastinum
- **B.** Anterior mediastinum
- **C.** Superior mediastinum
- **D.** Middle mediastinum

**Difficulty:** Easy | **Exam focus:** NORCET / HPRCA / PGIMER / All-India Staff Nurse

<details><summary>✅ Answer aur detailed explanation dekho</summary>

**D. Middle mediastinum**

**Kyun sahi?** Heart pericardium ke andar middle mediastinum mein hai, inferior mediastinum ka subdivision.

**Baaki options kyun nahi?** C great vessels/trachea ka region; A esophagus/thoracic aorta; B sternum aur pericardium ke beech.

**Exam trick:** Heart = middle mediastinum.

</details>

### Q2. A nurse hears a new systolic murmur following acute myocardial infarction, accompanied by breathlessness from pulmonary congestion. If mitral support has failed, where does the regurgitant blood enter?

- **A.** Pulmonary trunk
- **B.** Left atrium
- **C.** Right atrium
- **D.** Aorta

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Exam focus:** NORCET / HPRCA / PGIMER / All-India Staff Nurse

<details><summary>✅ Answer aur detailed explanation dekho</summary>

**B. Left atrium**

**Kyun sahi?** MR mein systole ke dauran LV se LA leak hota hai; LA/pulmonary venous pressure rise se congestion possible.

**Baaki options kyun nahi?** C tricuspid leak destination; A RV outlet; D normal LV forward ejection.

**Exam trick:** MR: LV → LA in systole.

</details>

</details>

</details>

### Q6. A valve is unable to open sufficiently during left ventricular ejection. The obstruction is at the outlet into the systemic arterial circulation. Which disorder is consistent with this mechanism?

- **A.** Aortic stenosis

<details><summary>✅ Answer aur detailed explanation dekho</summary>

**A. Aortic stenosis**

**Kyun sahi?** LV outflow aorta ke through, narrow aortic opening forward ejection obstruct karta hai.

**Baaki options kyun nahi?** B LA→LV filling obstruction; C reverse aorta→LV leak; D LV→LA leak.

**Exam trick:** Stenosis = forward obstruction.

</details>

### Q9. Which physiological feature distinguishes cardiac muscle from skeletal muscle when exposed to repeated rapid stimulation?

- **A.** Cardiac muscle normally cannot sustain tetanus because relaxation must occur
- **B.** Cardiac muscle has no electrical excitability
- **C.** Cardiac muscle is always voluntarily controlled
- **D.** Cardiac muscle lacks actin

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Exam focus:** NORCET / HPRCA / PGIMER / All-India Staff Nurse

<details><summary>✅ Answer aur detailed explanation dekho</summary>

**A. Cardiac muscle normally cannot sustain tetanus because relaxation must occur**

**Kyun sahi?** Long refractory period prevents sustained tetanus, filling ke liye relaxation necessary.

**Baaki options kyun nahi?** D actin present; C involuntary; B excitable.

**Exam trick:** No sustained tetanus = time for filling.

</details>

### Q10. In an adult with uncomplicated VSD and normal relative ventricular pressures, which initial direction of shunting is most likely?

- **A.** Left ventricle to right ventricle
- **B.** Right ventricle to left atrium
- **C.** Aorta to right atrium
- **D.** Right atrium to left atrium

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Exam focus:** NORCET / HPRCA / PGIMER / All-India Staff Nurse

<details><summary>✅ Answer aur detailed explanation dekho</summary>

**A. Left ventricle to right ventricle**

**Kyun sahi?** LV pressure usually RV se higher; ventricular septal hole se left-to-right flow.

**Baaki options kyun nahi?** D atrial pathway; B cross-chamber not VSD; C unrelated abnormal connection.

**Exam trick:** VSD = ventricular shunt, usually L→R initially.

</details>

### Q18. Which mechanism explains why an acute small pericardial collection may impair filling more severely than a larger slowly accumulating collection?

- **A.** Rapid accumulation allows less time for the pericardium to stretch
- **B.** The amount of fluid never affects pressure
- **C.** Rapid accumulation improves ventricular relaxation
- **D.** Slow accumulation always causes myocardial infarction

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Exam focus:** NORCET / HPRCA / PGIMER / All-India Staff Nurse

<details><summary>✅ Answer aur detailed explanation dekho</summary>

**A. Rapid accumulation allows less time for the pericardium to stretch**

**Kyun sahi?** Rate matters: acute volume rise sac pressure rapidly badha sakta; gradual collection accommodation allow kar sakti hai.

**Baaki options kyun nahi?** C opposite; D ischemic process separate; B pressure depends volume and compliance.

**Exam trick:** Tamponade depends rate and pressure, not volume alone.

</details>

### Q24. Which sequence correctly describes the first events of ventricular contraction before blood is ejected?

- **A.** All valves open together, then AV closure
- **B.** AV opening, semilunar closure, then ejection
- **C.** AV closure, brief all-valves-closed interval, then semilunar opening
- **D.** Semilunar opening, then AV closure

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Exam focus:** NORCET / HPRCA / PGIMER / All-India Staff Nurse

<details><summary>✅ Answer aur detailed explanation dekho</summary>

**C. AV closure, brief all-valves-closed interval, then semilunar opening**

**Kyun sahi?** Ventricular pressure atrial se exceed → AV closes; arterial pressure exceed hone tak all closed → semilunar opens.

**Baaki options kyun nahi?** D reversed; A no normal all-open stage; B filling/relaxation events.

**Exam trick:** Closure before opening.

</details>
