### Q1. A monitor shows organized electrical activity at 70/min, but the patient is unresponsive, not breathing normally and has no definite pulse. What is the correct response?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Exam focus:** NORCET / Staff Nurse

- **A.** Give oral fluids
- **B.** Begin CPR and manage as pulseless electrical activity
- **C.** Perform synchronized cardioversion for a stable rhythm
- **D.** Wait because an ECG rate is present

<details><summary>✅ Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: B. Begin CPR and manage as pulseless electrical activity**

**Kyun sahi hai?** ECG signal hona mechanical heartbeat prove nahi karta. Pulse absent hai toh arrest care shuru karo.

**Baaki options kyun galat hain?**

- **A.** Unsafe swallow
- **C.** No pulse = not stable tachycardia
- **D.** Waiting delays CPR

**Exam trick:** PEA is nonshockable, CPR and reversible causes.

</details>

### Q2. During CPR, the compressor reports fatigue and compression depth is falling. Which action supports high-quality CPR?

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Exam focus:** NORCET / Staff Nurse

- **A.** Switch compressors with minimal interruption
- **B.** Stop compressions for several minutes
- **C.** Continue shallow compressions without change
- **D.** Increase ventilation to compensate for shallow compressions

<details><summary>✅ Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: A. Switch compressors with minimal interruption**

**Kyun sahi hai?** Thakan se pressure aur depth kam hoti hai. Team jaldi switch kare taaki blood flow maintain rahe.

**Baaki options kyun galat hain?**

- **B.** Long pause perfusion giraati hai
- **C.** Shallow compressions inadequate
- **D.** Extra ventilation compression replace nahi karti

**Exam trick:** Rotate about every two minutes or sooner if fatigued.

</details>

### Q3. An intubated cardiac arrest patient has an abrupt sustained rise in end-tidal CO2 during CPR. What should the team consider?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Exam focus:** NORCET / Staff Nurse

- **A.** Certain equipment failure without assessment
- **B.** A reason to stop all monitoring immediately
- **C.** Proof that no blood flow is present
- **D.** Possible return of spontaneous circulation, confirm at appropriate rhythm and pulse check

<details><summary>✅ Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: D. Possible return of spontaneous circulation, confirm at appropriate rhythm and pulse check**

**Kyun sahi hai?** CO2 lungs tak blood flow badhne par increase ho sakta hai. Sudden rise ROSC ka clue hai, confirm required.

**Baaki options kyun galat hain?**

- **A.** Equipment cause possible but assume nahi
- **B.** Monitoring continue
- **C.** Flow return possible

**Exam trick:** Sudden ETCO2 rise may signal ROSC.

</details>

### Q4. A patient with atrial fibrillation is receiving an anticoagulant. Which symptom should prompt urgent assessment?

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Exam focus:** NORCET / Staff Nurse

- **A.** Long-standing corrected myopia
- **B.** An old healed scar
- **C.** New focal weakness or speech difficulty
- **D.** Mild hunger before lunch

<details><summary>✅ Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: C. New focal weakness or speech difficulty**

**Kyun sahi hai?** AF mein clot brain artery block kar sakta hai. Anticoagulant risk kam karta hai, zero nahi. New weakness/speech change stroke concern.

**Baaki options kyun galat hain?**

- **A.** Old vision issue acute stroke sign nahi
- **B.** Healed scar unrelated
- **D.** Hunger nonspecific

**Exam trick:** AF and new focal deficit = stroke emergency.

</details>

### Q5. A patient has crushing chest pain and an initial normal troponin shortly after symptom onset. What is the safest interpretation?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Exam focus:** NORCET / Staff Nurse

- **A.** The patient definitely has no cardiac disease
- **B.** Repeat ECGs are never useful
- **C.** Early myocardial infarction is not excluded and serial evaluation is needed
- **D.** One normal troponin rules out all acute coronary syndromes

<details><summary>✅ Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: C. Early myocardial infarction is not excluded and serial evaluation is needed**

**Kyun sahi hai?** Troponin injury ke baad rise hone mein time le sakta hai. Timing, ECG aur serial high-sensitivity test protocol matter.

**Baaki options kyun galat hain?**

- **A.** No cardiac disease prove nahi
- **B.** ECG changes evolve ho sakte hain
- **D.** Single early test insufficient

**Exam trick:** Early negative troponin needs context.

</details>

### Q6. A patient with heart failure is receiving IV fluids and develops new crackles and increasing breathlessness. Which action is appropriate?

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Exam focus:** NORCET / Staff Nurse

- **A.** Document only at the end of the shift
- **B.** Reassess oxygenation and fluid status and alert the team
- **C.** Increase fluids without reassessment
- **D.** Place the patient flat and leave alone

<details><summary>✅ Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: B. Reassess oxygenation and fluid status and alert the team**

**Kyun sahi hai?** Weak heart extra fluid lungs mein jama kar sakta hai. Saans aur SpO2 check karo, upright if tolerated and urgent review.

**Baaki options kyun galat hain?**

- **A.** Delay unsafe
- **C.** More fluid overload worsen kar sakta hai
- **D.** Flat position breathlessness badha sakti hai

**Exam trick:** New crackles during infusion may indicate overload.

</details>

### Q7. A patient with severe hypotension has distended neck veins, muffled heart sounds and a large pericardial effusion. Which condition is likely?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Exam focus:** NORCET / Staff Nurse

- **A.** Cardiac tamponade
- **B.** Uncomplicated systemic hypertension
- **C.** Isolated chronic anemia
- **D.** Stable mitral valve prolapse

<details><summary>✅ Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: A. Cardiac tamponade**

**Kyun sahi hai?** Heart ke bahar fluid pressure se chambers fill nahi ho paate. BP girta, neck veins distend ho sakti hain. Emergency drainage assessment chahiye.

**Baaki options kyun galat hain?**

- **B.** BP low hai
- **C.** Anemia triad explain nahi
- **D.** Stable prolapse shock pattern nahi

**Exam trick:** Tamponade obstructs cardiac filling.

</details>

### Q8. A patient receiving IV heparin develops a major platelet fall and a new thrombosis. What should the nurse suspect?

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Exam focus:** NORCET / Staff Nurse

- **A.** Normal desired heparin effect
- **B.** A reason to give extra heparin independently
- **C.** Iron deficiency as the only cause
- **D.** Heparin-induced thrombocytopenia requiring urgent review

<details><summary>✅ Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: D. Heparin-induced thrombocytopenia requiring urgent review**

**Kyun sahi hai?** HIT immune reaction platelet activate kar sakti hai. Platelets girne ke bawajood clot ban sakta hai. Urgent stop/review per protocol.

**Baaki options kyun galat hain?**

- **A.** Not expected therapeutic response
- **B.** Extra heparin dangerous
- **C.** Iron deficiency new thrombosis with heparin pattern explain nahi

**Exam trick:** HIT can cause thrombosis despite low platelets.

</details>

### Q9. A patient prescribed sublingual nitroglycerin asks why dizziness can occur. Which explanation is best?

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Exam focus:** NORCET / Staff Nurse

- **A.** It always raises blood pressure sharply
- **B.** It directly raises hemoglobin within seconds
- **C.** It permanently closes the coronary arteries
- **D.** Blood vessels relax and blood pressure can fall

<details><summary>✅ Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: D. Blood vessels relax and blood pressure can fall**

**Kyun sahi hai?** Nitrate vessels relax karta hai. Heart par load kam hota hai par BP girne se chakkar aa sakte hain.

**Baaki options kyun galat hain?**

- **A.** BP generally fall ho sakta hai
- **B.** Hemoglobin immediate change nahi
- **C.** Coronary closure mechanism nahi

**Exam trick:** Check BP before nitrate per protocol.

</details>

### Q10. A patient has ST elevation in leads II, III and aVF. Which myocardial region is most commonly represented?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Exam focus:** NORCET / Staff Nurse

- **A.** Anterior septum only
- **B.** Right atrium only
- **C.** Inferior wall
- **D.** High lateral wall only

<details><summary>✅ Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: C. Inferior wall**

**Kyun sahi hai?** II, III, aVF heart ki inferior surface dekhte hain. Clinical setting mein inferior STEMI evaluate karo.

**Baaki options kyun galat hain?**

- **A.** V1–V4 anterior/septal
- **B.** These leads not right atrium only
- **D.** I/aVL high lateral

**Exam trick:** II, III, aVF = inferior.

</details>

### Q11. A patient with new unilateral weakness arrives with blood pressure 196/110 mmHg. Which action is safest?

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Exam focus:** NORCET / Staff Nurse

- **A.** Ask the patient to walk alone
- **B.** Activate stroke evaluation and follow the stroke-specific BP plan
- **C.** Rapidly normalize BP independently with extra home tablets
- **D.** Delay assessment until BP becomes normal

<details><summary>✅ Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: B. Activate stroke evaluation and follow the stroke-specific BP plan**

**Kyun sahi hai?** Stroke mein BP management type aur treatment plan par depend karti hai. Sudden BP drop brain blood flow kam kar sakta hai. Urgent assessment chahiye.

**Baaki options kyun galat hain?**

- **A.** Fall risk
- **C.** Independent rapid lowering unsafe
- **D.** Delay time-sensitive stroke care

**Exam trick:** Neurologic emergency BP plan is specific.

</details>

### Q12. A patient after femoral cardiac catheterization develops back pain, tachycardia and falling BP without obvious groin bleeding. What complication is concerning?

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Exam focus:** NORCET / Staff Nurse

- **A.** Occult retroperitoneal hemorrhage
- **B.** Routine postprocedure hunger
- **C.** Expected improvement in perfusion
- **D.** Isolated mild skin irritation

<details><summary>✅ Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: A. Occult retroperitoneal hemorrhage**

**Kyun sahi hai?** Femoral puncture ke paas bleeding body ke andar ho sakti hai. Back pain aur shock signs important hain.

**Baaki options kyun galat hain?**

- **B.** Hunger shock explain nahi
- **C.** BP falling poor perfusion
- **D.** Skin irritation systemic signs nahi

**Exam trick:** Hidden bleeding after femoral access.

</details>

### Q13. An adult in shock has a heart rate of 125/min, low BP and new confusion. Which finding most directly supports impaired end-organ perfusion?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Exam focus:** NORCET / Staff Nurse

- **A.** New confusion and decreasing urine output
- **B.** Normal hair texture
- **C.** A healed surgical scar
- **D.** Stable shoe size

<details><summary>✅ Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: A. New confusion and decreasing urine output**

**Kyun sahi hai?** Brain aur kidney ko blood kam milta hai toh confusion aur urine kam ho sakta hai. Shock mein serial checks important hain.

**Baaki options kyun galat hain?**

- **B.** Hair unrelated
- **C.** Scar not acute perfusion
- **D.** Shoe size unrelated

**Exam trick:** Look at organs, not only pressure.

</details>

### Q15. An ECG shows a regular ventricular rate of 30/min with P waves and QRS complexes marching independently. Which rhythm is most likely?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Exam focus:** NORCET / Staff Nurse

- **A.** First-degree AV block
- **B.** Normal atrial paced rhythm
- **C.** Complete AV block
- **D.** Sinus tachycardia

<details><summary>✅ Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: C. Complete AV block**

**Kyun sahi hai?** Atria aur ventricles independent beat kar rahe hain. AV signal pass nahi ho raha, escape rate slow hai.

**Baaki options kyun galat hain?**

- **A.** First-degree mein every P followed by QRS
- **B.** AV dissociation normal paced atrial pattern nahi
- **D.** Rate fast nahi

**Exam trick:** P and QRS independent = third-degree block.

</details>

### Q16. A patient on a beta blocker reports wheezing and marked bradycardia. Which response is appropriate?

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Exam focus:** NORCET / Staff Nurse

- **A.** Stop all cardiac medicines permanently without consultation
- **B.** Assess respiratory status and pulse and seek medication review
- **C.** Give extra beta blocker without assessment
- **D.** Ignore the symptoms if blood pressure is normal

<details><summary>✅ Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: B. Assess respiratory status and pulse and seek medication review**

**Kyun sahi hai?** Beta blocker pulse slow karta hai. Kuch agents airway narrowing badha sakte hain. Symptoms aur pulse assess karke clinician review lo.

**Baaki options kyun galat hain?**

- **A.** All drugs abruptly stop unsafe
- **C.** Extra dose worsen
- **D.** Normal BP doesn't exclude side effects

**Exam trick:** Check pulse and respiratory effects.

</details>

### Q17. A patient with acute heart failure has severe hypertension, pulmonary edema and distress. Which general priority is appropriate?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Exam focus:** NORCET / Staff Nurse

- **A.** Delay review until a daily weight is recorded
- **B.** Rapid assessment and protocol-directed oxygenation, ventilatory and hemodynamic treatment
- **C.** Routine exercise tolerance testing
- **D.** Give unrestricted oral fluids

<details><summary>✅ Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: B. Rapid assessment and protocol-directed oxygenation, ventilatory and hemodynamic treatment**

**Kyun sahi hai?** Lungs mein fluid se oxygen kam ho sakti hai. High BP heart workload badhata hai. Emergency care team support kare.

**Baaki options kyun galat hain?**

- **A.** Weight not emergency priority
- **C.** Exercise unsafe
- **D.** Fluid overload risk

**Exam trick:** Acute pulmonary edema needs urgent stabilization.

</details>

### Q18. A patient with suspected acute limb ischemia develops a cold foot, severe pain and new loss of sensation. Which action is most appropriate?

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Exam focus:** NORCET / Staff Nurse

- **A.** Urgent vascular assessment
- **B.** Apply tight compression and elevate high without assessment
- **C.** Massage vigorously
- **D.** Delay review until the patient can walk

<details><summary>✅ Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: A. Urgent vascular assessment**

**Kyun sahi hai?** Artery block se foot ko blood nahi mil sakta. Numbness tissue danger ka sign hai. Time-sensitive vascular review chahiye.

**Baaki options kyun galat hain?**

- **B.** Compression perfusion worse
- **C.** Massage inappropriate
- **D.** Delay tissue loss

**Exam trick:** Acute limb ischemia: pain, pallor, pulselessness, paresthesia, paralysis, poikilothermia.

</details>

### Q19. A patient with septic shock remains hypotensive after appropriate initial fluids. Which prescribed drug is commonly the first-line vasopressor in adults?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Exam focus:** NORCET / Staff Nurse

- **A.** Oral digoxin
- **B.** Sublingual nitroglycerin
- **C.** Oral furosemide as sole treatment
- **D.** Norepinephrine

<details><summary>✅ Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: D. Norepinephrine**

**Kyun sahi hai?** Norepinephrine vessels ko constrict karke BP support karti hai. ICU monitoring aur protocol se titrate hoti hai.

**Baaki options kyun galat hain?**

- **A.** Digoxin first-line pressor nahi
- **B.** Nitrate BP aur gira sakta hai
- **C.** Diuretic shock resuscitation nahi

**Exam trick:** Norepinephrine is usual first-line septic shock pressor.

</details>

### Q20. A patient with a recent coronary stent wants to stop the prescribed P2Y12 inhibitor before a dental visit. What is the best nursing advice?

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Exam focus:** NORCET / Staff Nurse

- **A.** Double aspirin instead without advice
- **B.** Replace the drug with an herbal supplement
- **C.** Contact the cardiology and dental teams before any interruption
- **D.** Stop immediately because the stent is already placed

<details><summary>✅ Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: C. Contact the cardiology and dental teams before any interruption**

**Kyun sahi hai?** Stent par clot banne ka risk ho sakta hai. Antiplatelet interrupt karne ka decision doctors coordinate karte hain.

**Baaki options kyun galat hain?**

- **A.** Independent substitution unsafe
- **B.** Herbal not equivalent
- **D.** Early stop thrombosis risk

**Exam trick:** Never independently stop post-stent antiplatelet therapy.

</details>
