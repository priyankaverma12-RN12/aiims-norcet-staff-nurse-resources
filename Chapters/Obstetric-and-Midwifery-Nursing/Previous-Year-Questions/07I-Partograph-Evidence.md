## Separate reported adaptation

### Reported adaptation R01 — RRB Nursing Superintendent, 29 April 2025 Shift 3 (third-party reported)

In the historical Indian partograph, which symbol records the maternal pulse when the routine charting interval is 30 minutes?

- **A.** Cervical cross (X)
- **B.** Dot (•)
- **C.** Descent circle (O)
- **D.** Vertical blood-pressure arrow

<details><summary>✅ Sahi answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct answer: B. Dot (•)**

- **A (Incorrect):** X cervical dilatation in centimetres ko plot karta hai. Maternal pulse separate maternal panel mein rate ke against record hota hai.
- **B (Correct):** Historical NHM partograph maternal pulse ko half-hourly dot se plot karta hai. Yeh chart-specific convention hai, current universal monitoring schedule nahi.
- **C (Incorrect):** O abdominal head descent in fifths ka marker hai. Maternal pulse ko descent symbol se plot karna incorrect chart interpretation hoga.
- **D (Incorrect):** Vertical arrow systolic aur diastolic BP ke paired values preserve karta hai. Single pulse measurement ke liye dot marker appropriate hai.

**Clinical reference:** [NHM 2024 chart instructions](https://nhm.gov.in/images/pdf/programmes/maternal-health/guidelines/Revised_CEmONC_curriculum_2024-Part_1.pdf).

**Reported source:** [Third-party report](https://testbook.com/question-answer/in-partograph-pulse-is-recorded-every-30-minutes--681dd41387038c33ef445759). Independently worded adaptation, not verbatim official question. Official RRB booklet and final key not authenticated.
