### Q01. On a CTG, the contraction peaks at 40 seconds and ends at 65 seconds. The gradual fetal rate dip reaches its nadir at 58 seconds and recovers after the contraction. Which pattern is most consistent?

**Difficulty:** 🟡 Medium · **Priority:** 🔴 MOST IMPORTANT

- **A.** Early deceleration
- **B.** Late deceleration
- **C.** Variable acceleration
- **D.** Baseline variability

<details><summary>✅ Sahi answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct answer: B. Late deceleration**

- **A (Incorrect):** Early deceleration usually contraction ko mirror karti hai, isliye nadir peak ke around hota hai. Yahan nadir clearly delayed hai.
- **B (Correct):** Gradual dip ka nadir peak se 18 seconds baad hai aur recovery contraction ke baad hoti hai. Ye late timing suggest karti hai. Current NICE is finding ko red feature classify karta hai aur clinical review chahiye.
- **C (Incorrect):** Acceleration rate ka temporary rise hai. Yahan rate fall describe hua hai.
- **D (Incorrect):** Baseline variability small ongoing fluctuations hai. Ye contraction-associated gradual dip ka label nahi hai.
