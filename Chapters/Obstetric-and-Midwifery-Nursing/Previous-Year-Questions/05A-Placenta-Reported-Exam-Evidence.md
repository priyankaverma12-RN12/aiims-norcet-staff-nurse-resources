**Evidence status:** third-party question page retrieved. Official paper and official key not authenticated. Independently worded educational adaptation, not an authenticated verbatim question.

**Q1. Which approximate weight is commonly described for an uncomplicated term placenta?**

A. 50 g

B. 500 g

C. 1500 g

D. 3000 g

<details>
<summary>Answer and individual explanations</summary>

**Correct answer: B. 500 g**

- A: 50 g term placenta ke commonly described weight se bahut kam hai.
- B: 500 g ek conventional approximate exam value hai. Reviews mein approximately 500–600 g bhi describe hota hai; trimming, gestation aur measurement method se variation possible hai.
- C: 1500 g typical term placental weight se considerably greater hai; individual pathology assessment context-dependent hai.
- D: 3000 g newborn ke possible birth weight ke closer hai, typical placental weight nahi.

**Source:** [Reported UPPSC question, Testbook](https://testbook.com/question-answer/the-average-weight-of-placenta-is--625be7de670ea0a43f7c7588). [Clinical cross-check, 2026 review](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s00404-026-08382-7).
