### PYQ1. At an antenatal registration clinic, which gestational limit represents the target for completing the initial visit under Indian standards?

**Difficulty:** 🟡 Medium | **Priority:** 🔴 High | **Type:** Source-based adaptation

- **A.** By 12 weeks
- **B.** By 28 weeks
- **C.** By 36 weeks
- **D.** Only after labour begins

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: A. By 12 weeks**

NHM early registration aur first trimester ANC recommend karta hai. Early visit se baseline tests aur risk assessment timely ho sakte hain.

**Baaki options kyun galat hain?**

- **B.** 28 weeks tak delay se early screening miss ho sakti hai.
- **C.** 36 weeks first trimester nahi hai.
- **D.** Labour tak wait karna antenatal booking ka purpose defeat karta hai.

**Exam trick:** First ANC within 12 weeks.

</details>

### PYQ2. A primary centre without comprehensive emergency obstetric capability detects a pregnancy likely to require high-risk delivery. Which service plan is most appropriate?

**Difficulty:** 🟡 Easy | **Priority:** 🔴 High | **Type:** Source-based adaptation

- **A.** Omit counselling and laboratory screening
- **B.** Arrange referral to a suitably equipped obstetric facility
- **C.** Plan high-risk delivery locally despite absent capability
- **D.** Stop routine maternal follow-up

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: B. Arrange referral to a suitably equipped obstetric facility**

PHC risk ko identify karke initial assessment aur suitable referral coordinate karta hai. Actual delivery site capability aur clinical need par depend karti hai.

**Baaki options kyun galat hain?**

- **A.** Counselling aur investigations ANC package ka part hain.
- **C.** Absent emergency capacity mein high-risk delivery plan unsafe hai.
- **D.** Referral ke saath appropriate ongoing follow-up important hai.

**Exam trick:** Match referral facility to obstetric risk.

</details>
