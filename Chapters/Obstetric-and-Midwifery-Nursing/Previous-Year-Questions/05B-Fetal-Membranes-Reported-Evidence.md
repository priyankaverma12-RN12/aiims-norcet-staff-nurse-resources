**Q1. During established labor, spontaneous membrane rupture occurs. No prolapsed cord is visible. Which immediate fetal assessment should the nurse prioritize?**

A. Measurement of fetal femur length

B. Documentation of the estimated fetal weight only

C. Assessment of fetal heart rate

D. Routine newborn hearing screening

<details>
<summary>Answer and individual explanations</summary>

**Correct answer: C. Assessment of fetal heart rate**

- A: Femur length antenatal growth biometry hai; acute post-rupture fetal wellbeing assess nahi karta.
- B: Weight documentation useful background hai, but immediate oxygenation concern assess nahi karti.
- C: Rupture ke baad cord compression/prolapse possibility consider karo. FHR assess karo aur abnormal finding par urgently maternity team ko escalate karo. Visible cord absent hone se occult prolapse exclude nahi hota.
- D: Hearing screening birth ke baad hoti hai, intrapartum fetal assessment nahi.

**Sources:** [BHU attributed report](https://testbook.com/question-answer/a-clients-membranes-spontaneously-rupture-dur--619cab44684563454ef7c093) · [RCOG cord prolapse guidance](https://www.rcog.org.uk/guidance/browse-all-guidance/green-top-guidelines/umbilical-cord-prolapse-green-top-guideline-no-50). Monitoring modality is based on clinical risk and local guidance; third-party explanation's blanket continuous-monitoring statement is not adopted.
