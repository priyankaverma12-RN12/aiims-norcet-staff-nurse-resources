### Q01. Which statement about the reliability of a completed biophysical profile is clinically defensible when counseling a woman?

**Difficulty:** 🟡 Medium · **Priority:** 🔴 MOST IMPORTANT

- **A.** A reassuring result reduces concern but cannot guarantee that a later acute event will not occur.
- **B.** A normal result guarantees that no stillbirth can occur before the next visit.
- **C.** The test directly measures fetal arterial blood pH.
- **D.** False-positive findings never occur.

<details><summary>✅ Sahi answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct answer: A. A reassuring result reduces concern but cannot guarantee that a later acute event will not occur.**

- **A (Correct):** BPP current wellbeing ka indirect assessment hai. New acute event predict nahi ho sakta, isliye new symptoms par reassessment chahiye.
- **B (Incorrect):** Normal surveillance future adverse event ko completely rule out nahi karti.
- **C (Incorrect):** BPP observed behaviors, fluid aur heart rate assess karta hai, direct arterial pH nahi.
- **D (Incorrect):** False-positive results possible hain aur unnecessary intervention ka risk bhi consider hota hai.

**Clinical reference:** [NCBI Antenatal Fetal Surveillance](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/sites/books/NBK537123/) · [ACOG patient guidance](https://www.acog.org/womens-health/faqs/special-tests-for-monitoring-fetal-well-being).
