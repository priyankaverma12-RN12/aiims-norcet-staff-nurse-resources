## Reported adaptation 1 — RRB Staff Nurse, 20 July 2019 Shift II

**Q1. On examination of a delivered placenta, the cord attaches to the membranes outside the placental disc, and vessels travel through the membranes before reaching the disc. Which insertion is described?**

A. Central insertion

B. Marginal or battledore insertion

C. Velamentous insertion

D. Eccentric direct disc insertion

<details>
<summary>Answer and individual explanations</summary>

**Correct answer: C. Velamentous insertion**

- A: Central cord directly placental disc center ke near insert hota hai.
- B: Marginal insertion disc edge ke near direct attachment hai; free membranes mein insertion definition nahi.
- C: Velamentous cord membranes mein insert hota hai; connecting fetal vessels usual Wharton jelly protection ke bina travel karte hain.
- D: Eccentric insertion central se off-center hai, but still directly disc mein hota hai.

**Sources:** [Retrieved RRB attributed question](https://testbook.com/question-answer/the-cord-that-is-inserted-into-the-membranes-some--61a8bc5f7cedc78a4924b655) · [EAPM position statement](https://www.ejog.org/article/S0301211524002197/pdf). Third-party unrelated placental dimensions contain obvious errors; those figures were not adopted.

</details>

## Reported adaptation 2 — RRB Staff Nurse, 21 July 2019 Shift I

**Q2. Which term describes unprotected fetal vessels crossing the membranes over the internal cervical opening?**

A. Vasa previa

B. Cord presentation

C. Occult cord prolapse

D. Placenta accreta

<details>
<summary>Answer and individual explanations</summary>

**Correct answer: A. Vasa previa**

- A: Vasa previa mein exposed fetal vessels cervical os over/near course karte hain; rupture potentially fetal hemorrhage cause kar sakta hai.
- B: Cord presentation whole cord presenting part and cervix ke between hone ko describe karti hai, isolated exposed membranous vessel course nahi.
- C: Occult prolapse ruptured membranes ke presence mein cord alongside presenting part through cervix descends karta hai.
- D: Accreta spectrum abnormal placental attachment/invasion hai, exposed fetal vessel location term nahi.

**Sources:** [Retrieved RRB attributed question](https://testbook.com/question-answer/which-of-the-following-is-termed-as-the-fetal-bloo--61a9b507e5d090f85124c30d) · [SMFM vasa previa guidance](https://publications.smfm.org/publications/215-society-for-maternal-fetal-medicine-consult-series-37/) · [RCOG definitions](https://www.rcog.org.uk/guidance/browse-all-guidance/green-top-guidelines/umbilical-cord-prolapse-green-top-guideline-no-50).

</details>
