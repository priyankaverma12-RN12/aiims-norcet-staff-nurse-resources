## 🔵 DSSSB Nursing Officer — 29 August 2019, Shift 3

**Evidence status:** Third-party question page directly retrieved. Official booklet, question number and key not independently authenticated. The source associates morula with 16 cells. This adaptation is independently worded.

### Q01. In conventional embryology teaching, an approximately 16-cell compact solid conceptus is most commonly described as which stage?

**Difficulty:** 🟢 Easy · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD

- **A.** Zygote
- **B.** Morula
- **C.** Trilaminar embryonic disc
- **D.** Late fetal stage

<details><summary>✅ Sahi answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct answer: B. Morula**

- **A (Incorrect):** Zygote fertilization ke baad single diploid cell hota hai. Repeated cleavage se multiple blastomeres bante hain, isliye 16-cell cluster ko single-cell zygote nahi bolte.
- **B (Correct):** Morula early cleavage ke baad compact solid cluster hoti hai. Conventional exam teaching mein approximately 16 cells ek common association hai. Actual morula ko sirf exact cell count se define nahi karte, kyunki compaction aur developmental timing bhi matter karti hain.
- **C (Incorrect):** Trilaminar disc gastrulation ke dauran develop hoti hai. Ismein ectoderm, mesoderm aur endoderm establish hote hain. Yeh earlier compact morula stage se different hai.
- **D (Incorrect):** Late fetal stage mein organ growth aur maturation ongoing hoti hai. Yeh early cleavage stage nahi hai.

**Source:** [DSSSB Nursing Officer 29 August 2019 Shift 3 — retrieved Testbook report](https://testbook.com/question-answer/morula-is-a-stage-of-_______--615d37f4b6032ddfc59ec2c3). Third-party selected answer 16 cells. Official key not verified.

**Clinical cross-check:** [OpenStax embryonic development](https://openstax.org/books/anatomy-and-physiology-2e/pages/28-2-embryonic-development) and [NCBI fertilization](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/NBK542186/). Cell-count descriptions differ, so compact morphology is the more useful clinical distinction.

## 🔵 ESIC Staff Nurse — 19 March 2016, Shift I

**Evidence status:** Third-party question page directly retrieved. The reported answer is 12 weeks. Clinical texts give varying onset/detection estimates. The following independent adaptation avoids pretending that one exact week applies to every fetus.

### Q02. During normal prenatal development, fetal urine production is generally established by which broad stage?

**Difficulty:** 🟡 Medium · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD

- **A.** Before fertilization
- **B.** During the first cleavage divisions
- **C.** Around the end of the first trimester
- **D.** Only after birth

<details><summary>✅ Sahi answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct answer: C. Around the end of the first trimester**

- **A (Incorrect):** Fertilization se pehle fetus ya fetal kidney develop nahi hui hoti. Isliye fetal urine production us stage par possible nahi hai.
- **B (Incorrect):** Cleavage early cell divisions ka stage hai. Functional kidney structures later develop hoti hain, toh cleavage ko urine production ka stage nahi bolte.
- **C (Correct):** Fetal renal structures first trimester mein develop hoti hain aur urine production roughly late first trimester ke aas paas establish ho sakti hai. References onset aur ultrasound detection ko different weeks mein describe kar sakti hain. Later gestation mein fetal urine amniotic fluid ka major source ban jati hai.
- **D (Incorrect):** Fetal kidneys birth se pehle urine produce kar sakti hain. Birth ke baad renal clearance aur other functions further mature hote hain, lekin urine production ki beginning birth par nahi hoti.

**Source:** [ESIC Staff Nurse 19 March 2016 Shift I — retrieved Testbook report](https://testbook.com/question-answer/the-fetal-kidneys-start-producing-urine-at--6241c84a97faa0de329cd029). The reported answer is 12 weeks. Official key not verified.

**Clinical cross-check:** [NCBI amniotic fluid](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/NBK541089/) reports production around 10 weeks and [fetal genitourinary sonography](https://obgyn.mhmedical.com/content.aspx?bookid=1414&sectionid=76393006) gives approximately 10–11 gestational weeks. [Renal development review](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5387761/) distinguishes early urine production from its becoming a major fluid contributor around 20 weeks.

**YouTube MCQ practice:** Specific verified video mapping pending. The [Aarogyam Infinity Part 6](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=jRqFUX0xdCM) published description includes amniotic fluid and placenta questions, but urine-onset item coverage and playback were not independently verified.
