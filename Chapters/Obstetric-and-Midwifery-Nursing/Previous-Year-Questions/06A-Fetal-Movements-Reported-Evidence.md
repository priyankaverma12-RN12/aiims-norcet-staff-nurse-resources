### Q01. Which interval is commonly taught in older nursing examination texts for the first perception of fetal movements?

**Difficulty:** 🟢 Easy · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD

- **A.** 2–4 weeks
- **B.** 8–10 weeks
- **C.** 16–20 weeks
- **D.** 34–36 weeks

<details><summary>✅ Sahi answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct answer: C. 16–20 weeks**

- **A (Incorrect):** Pregnancy ke 2–4 weeks par conceptus bahut early developmental stage mein hota hai. Mother ko fetal kicks is interval mein feel hone ki expectation nahi hoti.
- **B (Incorrect):** Fetal movements ultrasound par pehle identify ho sakti hain, lekin mother ko unka sensation recognize hone mein aur time lagta hai. Isliye 8–10 weeks conventional quickening interval nahi hai.
- **C (Correct):** Retrieved exam report 16–20 weeks ko conventional answer batata hai. Clinical counseling mein NHS 16–24 weeks ka broader range use karta hai. First pregnancy mein 20 weeks ke baad bhi first movement feel ho sakti hai. Agar 24 weeks tak kabhi movement feel nahi hui, toh maternity assessment arrange karni chahiye.
- **D (Incorrect):** 34–36 weeks usual first perception se kaafi late hai. Agar movement kabhi feel nahi hui, toh assessment ko is gestation tak delay nahi karna chahiye.
