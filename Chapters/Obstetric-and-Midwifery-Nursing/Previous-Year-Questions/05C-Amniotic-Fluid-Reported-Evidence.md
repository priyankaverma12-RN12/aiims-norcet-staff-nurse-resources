## Reported adaptation 1 — RRB JPR Staff Nurse, 6 June 2018

**Q1. Which component of the fetal environment most directly provides a liquid cushion against external mechanical pressure?**

A. Corpus luteum

B. Amniotic fluid

C. Maternal decidual cells

D. Vitelline duct

<details>
<summary>Answer and individual explanations</summary>

**Correct answer: B. Amniotic fluid**

- A: Corpus luteum progesterone secretion se early pregnancy support karta hai, liquid cushioning nahi.
- B: Amniotic fluid fetus ko surround karke pressure distribute aur mechanical cushioning provide karta hai. It does not eliminate all injury risk.
- C: Decidua pregnancy-modified endometrium hai, liquid cushion nahi.
- D: Vitelline duct midgut–yolk sac connection hai, cushioning fluid compartment nahi.

**Sources:** [Retrieved RRB attributed report](https://testbook.com/question-answer/which-structure-is-responsible-for-protecting-the--61a0be815a5a11a6a6ce9671) · [Clinical physiology](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/NBK541089/).

</details>

## Reported adaptation 2 — GMCH Chandigarh Staff Nurse, 28 August 2022

**Q2. An older examination convention describes an amniotic fluid volume exceeding 2 liters. Which term denotes this excess-fluid condition?**

A. Polyhydramnios

B. Oligohydramnios

C. Amnionitis

D. Placental abruption

<details>
<summary>Answer and individual explanations</summary>

**Correct answer: A. Polyhydramnios**

- A: Polyhydramnios excess fluid ko describe karta hai. Two-liter criterion historical teaching convention hai; actual clinical ultrasound evaluation usually AFI/DVP thresholds use karta hai.
- B: Oligohydramnios insufficient fluid hai, excess nahi.
- C: Amnionitis inflammatory/infectious condition describe karta hai, high-volume definition nahi.
- D: Abruption placenta ke premature separation se related hai, fluid-volume term nahi.

**Sources:** [Retrieved GMCH attributed report](https://testbook.com/question-answer/when-the-amount-of-amniotic-fluid-exceeds-two-lite--630cef5b5dfb1bf61dd30496) · [SMFM Consult #46](https://publications.smfm.org/publications/263-society-for-maternal-fetal-medicine-consult-series-46/).

**Clinical distinction:** Modern SMFM singleton definition uses AFI at least 24 cm or DVP at least 8 cm. The older volumetric convention is not taught as a modern ultrasound cutoff.

</details>
