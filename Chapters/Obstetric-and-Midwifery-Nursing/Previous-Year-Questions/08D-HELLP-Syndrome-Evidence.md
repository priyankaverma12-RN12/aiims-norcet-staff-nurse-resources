### 🔵 RRB Staff Nurse 20 July 2019 Shift III — reported adaptation

During a high-risk pregnancy teaching session, which obstetric disorder should the nurse identify as a characteristic association of HELLP syndrome?

**Difficulty:** 🟢 Easy · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD

- **A.** Placenta previa
- **B.** Pre-eclampsia
- **C.** Hyperemesis gravidarum
- **D.** Rh isoimmunization

<details>
<summary>✅ Answer aur detailed explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct answer: B. Pre-eclampsia**

- **A (Incorrect):** Placenta previa mein placenta lower uterine segment mein cervical opening ke near ya over ho sakta hai. Bleeding iska important concern hai, lekin HELLP ke characteristic hypertensive pregnancy association ko ye describe nahi karta.
- **B (Correct):** HELLP mein hemolysis, elevated liver enzymes aur low platelets milte hain. Ye pre-eclampsia ke spectrum se commonly associated serious disorder hai. Kuch cases obvious hypertension ya proteinuria ke bina bhi present kar sakte hain, isliye appropriate lab aur clinical assessment important hai.
- **C (Incorrect):** Hyperemesis gravidarum severe pregnancy nausea-vomiting se related condition hai jo dehydration aur metabolic abnormalities produce kar sakti hai. HELLP ka characteristic microvascular association pre-eclampsia se hai, not isolated hyperemesis.
- **D (Incorrect):** Rh isoimmunization mein maternal antibodies fetal red cells ko affect kar sakti hain. Iska mechanism maternal endothelial dysfunction aur platelet consumption wale HELLP syndrome se different hai.
