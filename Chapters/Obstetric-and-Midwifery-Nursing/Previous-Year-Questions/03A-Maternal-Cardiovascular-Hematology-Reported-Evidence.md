### 🔵 BTSC Bihar ANM — 12 January 2024, Shift 3 — reported adaptation

**Difficulty:** 🟡 Medium · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD · **Evidence:** Retrieved third-party report

During antenatal teaching, a nurse lists changes of pregnancy under their primary physiological systems. Which finding belongs primarily to gastrointestinal or appetite-related changes rather than maternal cardiovascular adaptation?

- **A.** Increased maternal stroke volume
- **B.** Expansion of circulating plasma volume
- **C.** Reduced systemic vascular resistance
- **D.** Reduced appetite associated with nausea

<details><summary>✅ Answer aur detailed A–D Hinglish explanation</summary>

**Correct: D. Reduced appetite associated with nausea**

- **A kyun galat hai:** Stroke volume ka matlab ek heartbeat mein ventricle se pump hone wala blood hai. Pregnancy mein cardiac filling aur output ke changes ki wajah se stroke volume badh sakta hai. Isliye yeh cardiovascular adaptation ka example hai.
- **B kyun galat hai:** Plasma blood ka liquid portion hai. Pregnancy mein plasma volume badhta hai, jisse maternal organs aur placental circulation ki increased demand support hoti hai. Yeh blood aur circulatory system ka adaptation hai.
- **C kyun galat hai:** Systemic vascular resistance ka matlab systemic blood vessels mein flow ke against resistance hai. Pregnancy mein vasodilation se yeh resistance generally kam hota hai. Isliye yeh cardiovascular change hai.
- **D kyun sahi hai:** Nausea ke saath appetite kam ho sakti hai. Yeh symptom mainly gastrointestinal, hormonal aur appetite-related changes se associated ho sakta hai, na ki heart ki pumping ya blood vessels ki resistance se. Persistent vomiting ya dehydration par clinical assessment zaroori hai. Yeh finding har pregnant woman mein hona required nahi hai.

**Exam clue:** Jab exam mein cardiovascular changes poochhe jaayein, toh cardiac output, pulse, stroke volume, blood volume aur vascular resistance ko appetite-related symptoms se alag samjho.
