### Q01. Which potential fetal problem is the primary concern investigated by a contraction stress test?

**Difficulty:** 🟢 Easy · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD

- **A.** Inadequate fetal oxygen reserve during uterine contractions
- **B.** Placental tissue covering the cervical opening
- **C.** An umbilical cord lying below the presenting part
- **D.** Excessive amniotic fluid volume

<details><summary>✅ Sahi answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct answer: A. Inadequate fetal oxygen reserve during uterine contractions**

- **A (Correct):** CST contraction ke response mein fetal heart rate assess karta hai. Repeated late slowing limited oxygen reserve ka concern raise kar sakti hai. Ye indirect test hai, direct oxygen measurement nahi.
- **B (Incorrect):** Placental tissue cervical opening cover kare toh placenta previa ka concern hota hai. Placental location ultrasound se assess hoti hai aur previa CST stimulation ke liye contraindication ho sakti hai.
- **C (Incorrect):** Cord prolapse ek obstetric emergency ho sakti hai. Isko detect karne ke liye deliberately CST stimulation nahi karte.
- **D (Incorrect):** Excessive fluid ko polyhydramnios kehte hain aur ultrasound fluid measurements se assess karte hain. CST fluid volume directly measure nahi karta.
