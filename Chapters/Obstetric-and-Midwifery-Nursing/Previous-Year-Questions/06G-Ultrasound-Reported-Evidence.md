### Q01. At which of the following gestations would a routine detailed mid-trimester fetal anatomical survey ordinarily be appropriate?

**Difficulty:** 🟢 Easy · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD

- **A.** 19 weeks
- **B.** 5 weeks
- **C.** 8 weeks
- **D.** 39 weeks as the first planned routine anatomy scan

<details><summary>✅ Sahi answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct answer: A. 19 weeks**

- **A (Correct):** 19 weeks reported 18–20 range aur broader ISUOG mid-trimester window dono mein aata hai.
- **B (Incorrect):** 5 weeks detailed fetal organ survey ke liye too early hai. Early pregnancy location assessment different purpose hai.
- **C (Incorrect):** 8 weeks embryonic development ongoing hai. Standard comprehensive mid-trimester survey later hota hai.
- **D (Incorrect):** Term imaging specific indications par useful hai but routine planned mid-trimester anatomical survey postpone karna appropriate nahi.

**Clinical reference:** [ISUOG](https://www.isuog.org/static/4e2ed89e-fa8a-42c2-9c0929cd89cb58ff/ISUOG-Practice-Guidelines-routine-mid-trimester-fetal-ultrasound.pdf).
