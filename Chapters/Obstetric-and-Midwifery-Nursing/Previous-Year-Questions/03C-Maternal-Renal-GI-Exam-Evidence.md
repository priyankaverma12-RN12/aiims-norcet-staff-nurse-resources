### Sample practice — hormone and bowel motility

Which hormone contributes to slower intestinal transit during pregnancy through its smooth muscle effects?

**Difficulty:** 🟢 Easy · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD · Coaching-source adaptation, not a recruitment PYQ

- **A.** Insulin
- **B.** Progesterone
- **C.** Glucagon
- **D.** Oxytocin

<details><summary>✅ Sahi answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct answer: B. Progesterone**

- **A (Incorrect):** Insulin cells ko glucose uptake aur metabolism regulate karne mein help karta hai. Yeh pregnancy ke slow intestinal transit ka main hormone mechanism nahi hai.
- **B (Correct):** Progesterone smooth muscle relaxation mein contribute karta hai. Bowel ki movement slow ho sakti hai aur stool se water absorption zyada ho sakta hai. Is wajah se constipation develop ho sakti hai.
- **C (Incorrect):** Glucagon blood glucose regulation mein role play karta hai. Normal pregnancy mein bowel motility slow hone ka best explanation yeh nahi hai.
- **D (Incorrect):** Oxytocin labour contractions aur milk ejection mein important hai. Yeh asked smooth muscle relaxation ka main explanation nahi hai.
