### PYQ1. What is the intended uterine effect of carboprost in selected patients with atonic postpartum hemorrhage?

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Topic:** PPH medicines

- **A.** Increase uterine contractions
- **B.** Relax the uterus to increase bleeding
- **C.** Prevent all maternal infections
- **D.** Replace lost red blood cells

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: A. Increase uterine contractions**

Carboprost prostaglandin medicine hai jo uterus ko contract karne mein help karti hai. Firm contraction se blood vessels compress ho sakti hain. Asthma history aur contraindications check karna zaroori hai.

**Baaki options kyun galat hain?**

- **B.** Uterine relaxation PPH ko worsen kar sakti hai.
- **C.** Ye antibiotic nahi hai.
- **D.** Ye blood replacement nahi hai.

</details>
