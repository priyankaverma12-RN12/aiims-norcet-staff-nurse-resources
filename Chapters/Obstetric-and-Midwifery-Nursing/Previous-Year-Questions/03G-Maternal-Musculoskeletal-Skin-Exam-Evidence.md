### Q01. Which listed hormonal signal is associated with increased melanocyte activity contributing to pregnancy-related darkening of the abdominal midline?

**Difficulty:** 🟡 Medium · **Priority:** 🔴 MOST IMPORTANT

- **A.** Human placental lactogen
- **B.** Melanocyte-stimulating hormone
- **C.** Oxytocin
- **D.** Relaxin

<details><summary>✅ Sahi answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct answer: B. Melanocyte-stimulating hormone**

- **A (Incorrect):** Human placental lactogen maternal metabolic adaptation aur mammary preparation se associated hai. Yeh listed signals mein direct melanocyte stimulation ka best answer nahi.
- **B (Correct):** Melanocyte-stimulating hormone melanocytes ki melanin production stimulate kar sakta hai. Pregnancy mein estrogen aur progesterone bhi pigmentation contribute karte hain. Isliye MSH exam context mein best listed answer hai, exclusive single cause nahi.
- **C (Incorrect):** Oxytocin uterine contraction aur milk ejection mein important hai. Abdominal pigmentation ka direct best explanation nahi.
- **D (Incorrect):** Relaxin connective tissue aur reproductive adaptations contribute karta hai, melanocyte stimulation ka best listed signal nahi.

**Clinical cross-check:** [NCBI Bookshelf antenatal care](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/sites/books/NBK615334/?report=printable) supports multifactorial hormonal contribution. [OpenStax maternal physiological changes](https://openstax.org/books/maternal-newborn-nursing/pages/10-1-physiologic-changes-due-to-pregnancy) explains linea nigra and melasma.

**Allocation:** Hormonal mechanism task is distinct from the existing HPSSC 2022 linea nigra identification task. Count once as reported adaptation, not as an original.
