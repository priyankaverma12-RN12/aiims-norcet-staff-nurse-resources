### 🔵 HPSSSB Staff Nurse — October 2020 — reported adaptation

In the conventional sequence that separates restitution from the subsequent visible head turn, which named movement precedes complete fetal expulsion?

**Difficulty:** 🟡 Medium · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD

- **A.** Engagement
- **B.** Flexion
- **C.** External rotation
- **D.** Cervical effacement

<details><summary>✅ Sahi answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct answer: C. External rotation**

- **A (Incorrect):** Engagement mein head pelvic inlet se pass karta hai. Ye comparatively early adaptation hai, entire baby ke birth se just pehle hone wala visible head turn nahi hai.
- **B (Incorrect):** Flexion mein chin chest ke paas aata hai aur head canal ke liye adjust karta hai. Ye head delivery se pehle ka movement hai.
- **C (Correct):** Conventional sequence mein head birth aur restitution ke baad visible external rotation shoulder alignment ko reflect karti hai. Uske baad shoulders aur body deliver hote hain. Kuch texts dono turns ko ek combined movement ke roop mein list karte hain.
- **D (Incorrect):** Effacement maternal cervix ka thinning hai. Ye fetal head birth ke baad ka named cardinal movement nahi hai.
