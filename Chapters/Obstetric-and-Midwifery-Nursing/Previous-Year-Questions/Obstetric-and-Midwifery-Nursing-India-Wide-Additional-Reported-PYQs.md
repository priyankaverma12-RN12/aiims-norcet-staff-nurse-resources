### PYQ1. What is the dark vertical abdominal line that may develop during pregnancy called?

**Difficulty:** Easy/Medium | **Topic:** Physiological pregnancy changes

- **A.** Linea nigra
- **B.** Chloasma
- **C.** Striae gravidarum
- **D.** Linea alba

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation</summary>

**Correct: A. Linea nigra**

Pregnancy ke hormonal changes se abdominal midline dark ho sakti hai, jise linea nigra kehte hain. Chloasma face pigmentation hai. Striae stretch marks hain. Linea alba normal midline connective tissue hai.
