### Q01. A nurse explains NST preparation. Which instruction correctly distinguishes the heart-rate sensor from the contraction sensor?

**Difficulty:** 🟡 Medium · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD

- **A.** The heart-rate sensor is attached where fetal cardiac sounds can be detected clearly.
- **B.** The heart-rate sensor is always placed at the uterine fundus regardless of fetal position.
- **C.** The heart-rate sensor requires a puncture into the amniotic cavity.
- **D.** The heart-rate sensor requires the induction of contractions to function.

<details><summary>✅ Sahi answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct answer: A. The heart-rate sensor is attached where fetal cardiac sounds can be detected clearly.**

- **A (Correct):** Fetal cardiac signal jahan clearly milta hai wahan external ultrasound sensor position kiya jata hai. Contraction sensor uterine activity separately record karta hai.
- **B (Incorrect):** Fundal area contraction sensor ke liye useful ho sakti hai, lekin fetal heart sensor fetal position aur clear cardiac signal ke according adjust hota hai.
- **C (Incorrect):** Routine NST external noninvasive test hai. Amniotic cavity puncture is procedure ka part nahi hai.
- **D (Incorrect):** NST deliberately contractions induce nahi karta. CST ka purpose contraction ke saath fetal response assess karna hai.

**Clinical reference:** [NCBI Antenatal Fetal Surveillance](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/sites/books/NBK537123/).
