### Q01. During early pregnancy, which hormone acts directly on the uterine lining to support its maintenance rather than primarily signalling the corpus luteum to continue steroid production?

**Difficulty:** 🟡 Medium · **Priority:** 🔴 MOST IMPORTANT · Reported source-based adaptation

- **A.** Human chorionic gonadotropin
- **B.** Progesterone
- **C.** Follicle-stimulating hormone
- **D.** Oxytocin

<details><summary>✅ Sahi answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct answer: B. Progesterone**

- **A (Incorrect):** hCG developing trophoblast se release hota hai aur early pregnancy mein corpus luteum ko support karta hai. Iska important indirect effect progesterone secretion maintain karna hai, lekin yahan uterus ki lining par direct steroid support poocha gaya hai.
- **B (Correct):** Progesterone endometrium ko pregnancy support karne wali condition mein maintain karne mein help karta hai. Early pregnancy mein corpus luteum iska important source hota hai aur gradually placenta progesterone production sambhalta hai.
- **C (Incorrect):** FSH ovarian follicle growth ko stimulate karta hai. Established pregnancy mein cyclic follicle stimulation generally suppress ho jati hai. Yeh asked uterine steroid support nahi deta.
- **D (Incorrect):** Oxytocin uterine contraction aur milk ejection mein important hai. Yeh early pregnancy ke endometrial maintenance ka primary hormone nahi hai.

**Reported source:** [GMCH Nursing Officer, 29 June 2025 — Testbook](https://testbook.com/question-answer/the-primary-hormone-responsible-for-maintaining-th--68662695f1b9dcd4585eb980). The page selects progesterone. [RMLIMS Lucknow, 7 September 2025 memory-based report](https://testbook.com/question-answer/which-hormone-maintains-pregnancy--68c9415f887a0d86a44d73ac) tests a closely related learning task and is not counted as a second adaptation.

**Clinical reference:** [Endotext pregnancy endocrinology](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/sites/books/NBK278962/). Do not infer that every pregnancy requires prescribed progesterone supplementation. The treating clinician determines indications.

**Video practice:** [Pregnancy physiology MCQ session](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=1ZTmqJVWtQk&t=492s). Published hCG marker is related to the distinction in the question, but exact progesterone item coverage and playback remain pending.
