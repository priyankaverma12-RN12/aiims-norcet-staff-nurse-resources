### Q01. Which range was the reported expected fetal heart rate answer in the cited older ESIC item?

**Difficulty:** 🟢 Easy · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD

- **A.** 80–100 beats/min
- **B.** 120–160 beats/min
- **C.** 170–200 beats/min
- **D.** 40–80 beats/min

<details><summary>✅ Sahi answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct answer: B. 120–160 beats/min**

- **A (Incorrect):** Ye rate range cited reported key nahi hai. Current labor assessment mein is range ka sustained fetal rate concern raise kar sakta hai.
- **B (Correct):** Retrieved ESIC report mein 120–160 option expected answer diya gaya tha. Lekin current NICE normal baseline 110–160 hai. Isliye historical exam key ko current clinical definition nahi banana chahiye.
- **C (Incorrect):** Ye range modern normal baseline se upar hai aur retrieved historical answer bhi nahi hai. Persistent high rate ko clinical context ke saath evaluate karna chahiye.
- **D (Incorrect):** Ye fetal heart rate ke usual baseline se kaafi neeche hai. Fetal aur maternal signal differentiation aur urgent assessment zaroori ho sakti hai.
