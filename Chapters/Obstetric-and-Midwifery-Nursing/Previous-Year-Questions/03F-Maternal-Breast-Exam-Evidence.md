### Q01. During antenatal breast assessment, where should the nurse identify the small Montgomery gland openings that may become more prominent during pregnancy?

**Difficulty:** 🟢 Easy · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD · Reported adaptation

- **A.** Within the deep milk-producing alveoli
- **B.** At the openings of the main milk ducts on the nipple tip only
- **C.** In the areolar skin surrounding the nipple
- **D.** In the axillary lymph nodes

<details><summary>✅ Sahi answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct answer: C. In the areolar skin surrounding the nipple**

- **A (Incorrect):** Alveoli breast ke deeper glandular secretory units hain. Montgomery glands areolar skin mein located hoti hain, na ki deep milk-producing alveoli mein.
- **B (Incorrect):** Main milk ducts nipple ki surface par open hote hain aur milk transport karte hain. Montgomery glands areola ki separate glands hain.
- **C (Correct):** Areola nipple ke around pigmented skin area hai. Montgomery glands isi area mein small bumps ke form mein dikh sakti hain. Pregnancy mein ye more prominent ho sakti hain aur protective oily secretion release karti hain.
- **D (Incorrect):** Axillary lymph nodes underarm region mein lymph drainage ka part hain. Inka location nipple ke surrounding areola se alag hai.

**Reported source:** [RRB Staff Nurse 20 July 2019 Shift II, Testbook](https://testbook.com/question-answer/which-part-of-the-breast-is-the-montgomery-glands--61a8c0eaa468cbb793fc9b9f). The reported answer describes the area near the nipple base. The source explanation incorrectly implies that the glands are visible only during pregnancy. They exist outside pregnancy too. Clinical clarification is based on [NCBI breast milk physiology](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/sites/books/NBK539790/).

**Video:** [NORCET 10 countdown breast changes and Montgomery tubercles, 01:37:45](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=1rUuZ7EtVFE&t=5865s). Published topic marker only. Exact playback pending.
