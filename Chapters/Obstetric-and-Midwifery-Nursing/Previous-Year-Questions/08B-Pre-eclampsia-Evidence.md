### 🔵 AIIMS NORCET 7 Prelims 2024 — reported adaptation

A woman with pre-eclampsia at 35 weeks suddenly develops constant abdominal pain, a tender tense uterus and vaginal bleeding. Which placental complication should the nurse urgently suspect?

**Difficulty:** 🟡 Medium · **Priority:** 🔴 MOST IMPORTANT

- **A.** Placental abruption
- **B.** Placenta previa as the most likely explanation of the painful tense uterus
- **C.** Physiological placental maturation without complication
- **D.** Placenta accreta spectrum as the most likely acute explanation

<details>
<summary>✅ Answer aur detailed explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct answer: A. Placental abruption**

- **A (Correct):** Placental abruption mein normally implanted placenta delivery se pehle uterine wall se separate hone lagta hai. Pre-eclampsia associated risk factor hai aur constant pain, tender tense uterus aur bleeding concerning clues hain. Bleeding concealed bhi ho sakti hai. Nurse urgent maternal-fetal assessment aur obstetric emergency escalation facilitate karti hai.
- **B (Incorrect):** Placenta previa classically painless bleeding se associated ho sakti hai. Pain aur tender tense uterus abruption ka concern zyada raise karte hain, lekin symptoms alone exact diagnosis establish nahi karte. Placental location clarify hone tak unsafe digital examination avoid karo.
- **C (Incorrect):** Placental maturation normal gestational process hai. Sudden constant pain aur bleeding ko normal maturation bolna potentially serious haemorrhage miss kara sakta hai.
- **D (Incorrect):** Placenta accreta spectrum abnormal placental attachment/invasion se related hai, often delivery ke time separation difficulty aur haemorrhage concern hota hai. Given acute painful tense uterus with bleeding mein abruption more likely concern hai.

**Reported source:** [Testbook NORCET 7 Prelims Memory Based Paper 2024](https://testbook.com/question-answer/a-nurse-is-assessing-a-pregnant-woman-with-gestati--67a219028c5d90c4ccda25f6) directly retrieved, reported key placental abruption. Original report includes preterm labour as another alternative, which can also be associated with pre-eclampsia and makes the broad risk stem less discriminating.
