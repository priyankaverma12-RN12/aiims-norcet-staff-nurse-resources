### Reported adaptation 01

A pregnant woman's red cell mass increases, but her hematocrit decreases compared with her pre-pregnancy value. Which physiological mechanism best explains this finding?

- A. Plasma volume increases proportionately more than red cell mass
- B. All erythropoiesis stops during pregnancy
- C. Plasma volume decreases while red cell mass remains unchanged
- D. Maternal blood volume always decreases as pregnancy progresses

<details><summary>✅ Sahi answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct answer: A. Plasma volume increases proportionately more than red cell mass**

- **A (Correct):** Pregnancy mein plasma aur red blood cell mass dono increase karte hain. Plasma ka proportionately greater increase blood ko dilute karta hai, jisse hematocrit kam dikh sakta hai.
- **B (Incorrect):** Erythropoiesis yani red blood cell production completely stop nahi hoti. Red cell mass actually badh sakti hai.
- **C (Incorrect):** Plasma volume generally badhta hai. Plasma kam hone par dilution wala mechanism explain nahi hota.
- **D (Incorrect):** Maternal blood volume generally expand hota hai. Yeh growing fetus aur maternal organs ki circulation support karta hai.

Low hematocrit ko automatically physiological nahi maanna chahiye. Hemoglobin, gestational stage, iron status aur clinical context assess karein.
