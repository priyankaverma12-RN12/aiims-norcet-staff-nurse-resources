### Q01. In a conventional 28-day cycle, an embryo is estimated to be 6 completed weeks after fertilization. What is the approximate obstetric gestational age?

**Difficulty:** 🟡 Medium · **Priority:** 🔴 MOST IMPORTANT

- **A.** 4 weeks
- **B.** 8 weeks
- **C.** 6 weeks
- **D.** 10 weeks

<details><summary>✅ Sahi answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct answer: B. 8 weeks**

- **A (Incorrect):** 4 weeks tab milenge jab aap 6 mein se 2 subtract karenge. Lekin yahan conception age se LMP-based age nikalni hai, isliye add karna hai.
- **B (Correct):** Clinical gestational age LMP ke first day se count hoti hai. Agar ovulation approximately day 14 par hua tha, toh fertilization ke 6 weeks mein 2 weeks add karke 8 weeks milte hain.
- **C (Incorrect):** 6 weeks fertilization-based age hai. Standard obstetric clock approximately do weeks pehle start hoti hai.
- **D (Incorrect):** 10 weeks ke liye 4 weeks add karne padenge. Typical 28-day cycle mein difference approximately 2 weeks hota hai, although actual ovulation timing vary kar sakti hai.

**Exam clue:** Fertilization age mein approximately do weeks add karke obstetric age milti hai.

### Q02. Which change best explains why early cleavage increases cell number without a comparable increase in the overall size of the conceptus?

**Difficulty:** 🟡 Medium · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD

- **A.** The maternal endometrium replaces each blastomere with a larger maternal cell
- **B.** Each division doubles both the cell diameter and total conceptus diameter
- **C.** Meiosis repeatedly halves chromosome number in the embryonic cells
- **D.** Repeated mitotic division partitions existing cytoplasm into smaller blastomeres

<details><summary>✅ Sahi answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct answer: D. Repeated mitotic division partitions existing cytoplasm into smaller blastomeres**

- **A (Incorrect):** Blastomeres conceptus ki own daughter cells hain. Maternal lining implantation support karti hai, lekin embryonic cells ko replace nahi karti.
- **B (Incorrect):** Agar har cell ka diameter bhi double ho, toh total size rapidly badhega. Early cleavage ka usual pattern iske opposite hai.
- **C (Incorrect):** Fertilization ke baad embryo mitosis se divide karta hai. Meiosis gamete formation ka process hai, embryonic cleavage ka nahi.
- **D (Correct):** Cleavage mein zygote repeatedly mitosis karta hai. Har division ke baad existing cytoplasm chhoti daughter cells mein distribute hota hai, isliye early embryo ka overall size proportionately nahi badhta.

**Exam clue:** Cleavage cell number badhata hai aur blastomeres gradually chhote hote hain.

### Q03. An embryology report describes a compact solid cluster of cells becoming a structure with a distinct fluid-filled cavity. Which transition has occurred?

**Difficulty:** 🟡 Medium · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD

- **A.** Morula to blastocyst
- **B.** Blastocyst to zygote
- **C.** Fetus to morula
- **D.** Gastrula to unfertilized oocyte

<details><summary>✅ Sahi answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct answer: A. Morula to blastocyst**

- **A (Correct):** Morula compact cell cluster hoti hai. Jab fluid-filled cavity, jise blastocoel kehte hain, develop hoti hai aur inner cell mass separate dikhti hai, toh blastocyst stage aati hai.
- **B (Incorrect):** Zygote fertilization ke baad single-cell stage hai. Development normally blastocyst se reverse hokar zygote nahi banta.
- **C (Incorrect):** Fetal stage mein developed organ systems grow aur mature hote hain. Morula early cleavage ki stage hai, isliye sequence reverse hai.
- **D (Incorrect):** Gastrulation mein embryonic germ layers form hoti hain. Unfertilized oocyte is process se pehle ki reproductive cell hai.

**Exam clue:** Morula solid hoti hai aur blastocyst mein fluid cavity develop hoti hai.

### Q04. What is the principal developmental significance of blastocyst hatching from the zona pellucida?

- **D (Incorrect):** Hatching existing conceptus ka event hai. Yeh ovary mein naya ovulation trigger nahi karta.

### Q17. Which event defines the change from a bilaminar to a trilaminar embryonic disc?

**Difficulty:** 🟡 Medium · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD

- **A.** Cell migration through the primitive streak during gastrulation
- **B.** Completion of umbilical cord clamping
- **C.** Loss of the maternal decidua at delivery
- **D.** Maturation of fetal pulmonary surfactant

<details><summary>✅ Sahi answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct answer: A. Cell migration through the primitive streak during gastrulation**

- **A (Correct):** Early embryonic disc mein epiblast aur hypoblast associated layers hoti hain. Gastrulation ke dauran epiblast cells primitive streak se migrate karke definitive endoderm aur mesoderm form karti hain aur remaining epiblast ectoderm banti hai.
- **B (Incorrect):** Cord clamping birth ke around hota hai. Bilaminar disc se trilaminar disc ka change early embryogenesis mein hota hai.
- **C (Incorrect):** Decidua maternal tissue hai aur delivery ke baad separate hoti hai. Embryonic germ layer formation ke liye primitive streak important hai.
