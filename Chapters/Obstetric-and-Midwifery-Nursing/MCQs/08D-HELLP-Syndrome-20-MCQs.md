### Q01. A peripheral blood film from a pregnant patient with suspected HELLP syndrome contains numerous schistocytes. Which process best explains their formation?

**Difficulty:** 🟡 Medium · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD · Original practice

- **A.** Antibody-mediated destruction of red cells as the sole mechanism
- **B.** Failure of the bone marrow to release platelets
- **C.** Mechanical fragmentation of red cells in damaged small vessels
- **D.** Increased absorption of dietary iron

<details>
<summary>✅ Answer aur detailed explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct answer: C. Mechanical fragmentation of red cells in damaged small vessels**

- **A (Incorrect):** Immune hemolysis mein antibodies red cells ko target kar sakti hain. HELLP ka characteristic red cell damage microvascular mechanical fragmentation hai, isliye immune mechanism ko sole explanation nahi maante.
- **B (Incorrect):** Bone marrow platelet production kam kare toh platelet count low ho sakta hai. Is mechanism se circulating red cells ke mechanically fragmented pieces directly explain nahi hote.
- **C (Correct):** Schistocytes red blood cells ke toote hue fragments hain. Jab red cells damaged small vessels aur fibrin-rich narrow channels se guzarte hain, toh mechanical stress unhe tod sakta hai. Ye microangiopathic hemolysis ka useful clue hai, lekin HELLP ke alawa doosri microangiopathies mein bhi mil sakta hai.
- **D (Incorrect):** Dietary iron absorption hemoglobin production ko support karti hai. Iska damaged vessels mein red cell fragmentation se direct causal relation nahi hai.

**Exam clue:** Schistocyte ko mechanical hemolysis ka clue samjho, disease ka exclusive marker nahi.

### Q02. A woman has anemia without evident external bleeding and suspected HELLP syndrome. Which laboratory change most specifically supports intravascular hemolysis in this context?

**Difficulty:** 🟡 Medium · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD · Original practice

- **A.** Raised serum haptoglobin due to binding of free hemoglobin
- **B.** Increased platelet count due to endothelial injury
- **C.** Isolated increase in serum alkaline phosphatase
- **D.** Reduced serum haptoglobin due to binding and clearance of released hemoglobin

<details>
<summary>✅ Answer aur detailed explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct answer: D. Reduced serum haptoglobin due to binding and clearance of released hemoglobin**

- **A (Incorrect):** Haptoglobin free hemoglobin bind karta hai. Jab intravascular hemolysis mein hemoglobin release hota hai, toh complex clear hone se circulating haptoglobin kam ho sakta hai. Raised value alone expected evidence nahi hai.
- **B (Incorrect):** HELLP mein endothelial injury se platelets activate aur consume ho sakte hain. Platelet increase ko intravascular hemolysis ka characteristic specific marker nahi maante.
- **C (Incorrect):** Alkaline phosphatase pregnancy mein placenta ki wajah se bhi increase ho sakta hai. Isolated ALP se intravascular red cell destruction establish nahi hota.
- **D (Correct):** Red cells circulation ke andar tootne par free hemoglobin blood mein aata hai. Haptoglobin ise bind karta hai aur complex clear ho jata hai. Isliye reduced haptoglobin smear, LDH, bilirubin aur clinical findings ke saath hemolysis support karta hai.

**Exam clue:** Haptoglobin free hemoglobin ko bind karta hai, isliye hemolysis mein low ho sakta hai.

### Q03. A patient with suspected HELLP has biochemical evidence of red cell destruction. Which bilirubin pattern can support hemolysis when interpreted with the other investigations?

**Difficulty:** 🟡 Medium · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD · Original practice

- **A.** An increase in unconjugated bilirubin
- **B.** An isolated fall in unconjugated bilirubin
- **C.** An isolated rise in alkaline phosphatase with unchanged bilirubin
- **D.** A bilirubin pattern that proves biliary obstruction in every case

<details>
<summary>✅ Answer aur detailed explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct answer: A. An increase in unconjugated bilirubin**

- **A (Correct):** Jab red cells toot te hain, toh hemoglobin ka heme component bilirubin formation mein contribute karta hai. Unconjugated bilirubin rise hemolysis ko support kar sakta hai. HELLP mein liver injury bhi ho sakti hai, isliye bilirubin fractions aur doosre tests saath mein dekho.
- **B (Incorrect):** Red cell destruction se heme breakdown increase hota hai. Isse unconjugated bilirubin badh sakta hai, isliye isolated fall hemolysis ka expected supportive change nahi hai.
- **C (Incorrect):** ALP liver ya placenta se related ho sakta hai. Unchanged bilirubin aur isolated ALP rise red cell breakdown ka direct evidence nahi hai.
- **D (Incorrect):** Bilirubin badhne ke kai reasons hain. HELLP mein hemolysis aur hepatic dysfunction coexist kar sakte hain, isliye bilirubin pattern alone se biliary obstruction confirm nahi hota.

**Exam clue:** Bilirubin ko smear aur hemolysis markers ke saath interpret karo.

### Q04. Which mechanism best accounts for the low platelet count in HELLP syndrome?

**Difficulty:** 🟡 Medium · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD · Original practice

- **A.** Increased platelet destruction due solely to dietary folate deficiency
- **B.** Platelet activation and consumption in injured microvasculature
- **C.** Dilution from normal pregnancy plasma expansion as the complete explanation
- **D.** Reduced platelet consumption because small vessels are protected

<details>
<summary>✅ Answer aur detailed explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct answer: B. Platelet activation and consumption in injured microvasculature**

- **A (Incorrect):** Folate deficiency bone marrow blood cell production disturb kar sakti hai. HELLP ki characteristic thrombocytopenia ko sirf nutritional deficiency se explain nahi karte.
- **B (Correct):** Endothelium blood vessels ki inner lining hai. Jab ye injured hoti hai, toh platelets activate aur aggregate hote hain. Small vessels mein platelet consumption badhne se circulating count gir sakta hai aur organ perfusion bhi compromise ho sakti hai.
- **C (Incorrect):** Normal pregnancy mein plasma volume increase ho sakta hai aur mild gestational thrombocytopenia bhi possible hai. HELLP mein pathological vascular injury aur platelet consumption important mechanisms hain.
- **D (Incorrect):** Damaged microvasculature platelet activation ko promote kar sakti hai. Reduced consumption wala explanation syndrome ke typical low count ko support nahi karta.

**Exam clue:** HELLP mein platelets ka consumption badh sakta hai, sirf dilution nahi.

### Q06. Using the classic Mississippi triple-class system, a woman has platelets 42 × 10⁹/L, AST 112 U/L and LDH 900 U/L with evidence of hemolysis. Which class fits her laboratory findings?

- **B.** Class II
- **C.** No HELLP class because the platelet unit is × 10⁹/L
- **D.** Class I

<details>
<summary>✅ Answer aur detailed explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct answer: D. Class I**

- **A (Incorrect):** Class III mein platelet count 100 se 150 × 10⁹/L ke interval mein hota hai aur liver enzyme criterion lower ho sakta hai. Forty-two is range se kaafi kam hai.
- **B (Incorrect):** Class II ka platelet range 50 se greater aur 100 × 10⁹/L tak hai. Forty-two Class II range mein nahi aata.
- **C (Incorrect):** Platelets × 10⁹/L ek standard reporting unit hai. Forty-two × 10⁹/L equals 42,000/µL, isliye units correctly convert karke class determine kar sakte hain.
- **D (Correct):** Mississippi system mein Class I ka platelet count 50 × 10⁹/L ya usse kam hota hai. Given elevated AST aur LDH bhi named class ke laboratory criteria support karte hain. Ye severe thrombocytopenia ka concern hai, automatic treatment order nahi.

**Exam clue:** Mississippi class ko platelet count aur associated laboratory thresholds se determine karo.

### Q08. A patient with HELLP has a platelet count of 75,000/µL, but her PT, aPTT and fibrinogen are currently within the laboratory reference ranges. What does this pattern suggest?

**Difficulty:** 🟡 Medium · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD · Original practice

- **A.** HELLP is excluded by normal coagulation tests
- **B.** Disseminated intravascular coagulation is already proven
- **C.** Platelet consumption can occur in HELLP before overt clotting-factor consumption is evident
- **D.** The platelet count must be ignored because PT is normal

<details>
<summary>✅ Answer aur detailed explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct answer: C. Platelet consumption can occur in HELLP before overt clotting-factor consumption is evident**

- **A (Incorrect):** HELLP mein platelet activation aur hemolysis ho sakte hain jabki coagulation factors initially relatively preserved hote hain. Normal PT ya aPTT syndrome exclude nahi karta.
- **B (Incorrect):** DIC mein widespread coagulation activation ke saath clotting factors consume ho sakte hain. Current normal tests overt DIC prove nahi karte, lekin early evolving disease ko rule out bhi nahi karte.
- **C (Correct):** Platelet consumption HELLP ka core feature hai. PT aur aPTT clotting factor pathways assess karte hain, platelet count ko directly measure nahi karte. Nurse bleeding signs aur serial coagulation results monitor karti hai.
- **D (Incorrect):** Platelet count bleeding risk assessment mein relevant hai. Normal PT platelet deficiency ko cancel nahi karta aur invasive procedure se pehle team ko count pata hona chahiye.

**Exam clue:** Normal clotting times ke saath thrombocytopenia possible hai.

### Q15. A hemodynamically stable woman with HELLP develops severe persistent upper abdominal pain. The team suspects hepatic bleeding. Which investigation plan is appropriate?

**Difficulty:** 🟡 Medium · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD · Original practice

- **A.** Arrange prompt maternal liver imaging under specialist direction while continuing close assessment
- **B.** Use only routine fetal biometry to exclude hepatic injury
- **C.** Wait for visible vaginal bleeding before any imaging
- **D.** Perform repeated deep abdominal palpation to determine the hematoma size

<details>
<summary>✅ Answer aur detailed explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct answer: A. Arrange prompt maternal liver imaging under specialist direction while continuing close assessment**

- **A (Correct):** Bedside ultrasound rapid assessment mein help kar sakta hai aur clinical team CT ya MRI select kar sakti hai when needed. Nurse maternal status closely monitor karti hai aur deterioration par resuscitation delay nahi karti.
- **B (Incorrect):** Fetal biometry baby ke size ka estimate deta hai. Is test se maternal liver capsule aur bleeding reliably assess nahi hoti.
- **C (Incorrect):** Liver bleeding abdominal cavity mein concealed ho sakti hai. Vaginal bleeding absent hone par bhi maternal imaging aur assessment indicated ho sakte hain.
