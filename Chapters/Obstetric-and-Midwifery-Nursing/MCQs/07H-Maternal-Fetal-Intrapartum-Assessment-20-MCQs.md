- **C.** 13:00
- **D.** Only after full dilatation

<details><summary>✅ Sahi answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct answer: B. 10:00**

- **A (Incorrect):** Fifteen-minute interval kuch higher-risk situations mein indicated ho sakta hai. Question reassuring low-risk woman ke routine NICE pulse schedule ke baare mein hai.
- **B (Correct):** NICE routine first-stage schedule mein maternal pulse har ghante record karte hain. 09:00 ke ek ghante baad 10:00 hua, lekin concern aaye toh pehle bhi measure karna hai.

### Q20. A woman has several new mildly abnormal maternal findings during labour, each individually less dramatic than a severe emergency threshold. What principle should guide escalation?

**Difficulty:** 🟡 Medium · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD

- **A.** Assess each finding in isolation and never combine concerns
- **B.** Wait until one observation reaches the most extreme threshold
- **C.** Use cervical dilatation alone to decide whether the woman is safe
- **D.** Consider the cumulative risk and clinical trend rather than dismissing each finding separately

<details><summary>✅ Sahi answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct answer: D. Consider the cumulative risk and clinical trend rather than dismissing each finding separately**

- **A (Incorrect):** Individual observations useful hain, lekin combined abnormalities deterioration ka stronger signal de sakti hain. Inko separate karke ignore karna clinical picture miss kar sakta hai.

- **D (Correct):** NICE multiple risk factors ka cumulative effect consider karne ko kehta hai. Nurse repeated observations, symptoms aur fetal condition ko saath assess karti hai aur urgency accordingly communicate karti hai.
