## Hypertensive disorders

### Q1. A pregnant patient with severe preeclampsia develops a respiratory rate of 8/min and absent deep tendon reflexes during magnesium sulfate therapy. Which action is appropriate?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Topic:** Hypertensive disorders

- **A.** Stop the infusion and urgently assess for magnesium toxicity
- **B.** Increase the magnesium infusion rate
- **C.** Give additional magnesium without checking the order
- **D.** Wait for the next routine antenatal appointment

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: A. Stop the infusion and urgently assess for magnesium toxicity**

Magnesium sulfate seizures prevent karne mein help karta hai. Lekin level zyada ho toh reflexes aur saans kam ho sakti hain. Nurse infusion stop karke emergency assessment aur antidote protocol follow karti hai.

**Baaki options kyun galat hain?**

- **B.** Extra magnesium toxicity aur badha sakta hai.
- **C.** Bina assessment dose dena unsafe hai.
- **D.** Slow breathing emergency hai.

</details>

## Postpartum hemorrhage

### Q2. After vaginal delivery, a patient has heavy bleeding and a soft boggy uterus. Which cause is most likely?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Topic:** Postpartum hemorrhage

- **A.** Normal uterine contraction
- **B.** Isolated urinary infection
- **C.** Routine breast engorgement
- **D.** Uterine atony

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: D. Uterine atony**

Delivery ke baad uterus contract hokar blood vessels ko press karta hai. Agar uterus soft aur relaxed rahe, toh bleeding ho sakti hai. Nurse PPH emergency response aur uterine assessment karti hai.

**Baaki options kyun galat hain?**

- **A.** Firm contracted uterus expected hota hai.
- **B.** UTI is heavy bleeding ko explain nahi karti.
- **C.** Breast engorgement uterus bleeding ka cause nahi hai.

</details>

## Fetal monitoring

### Q3. Recurrent late decelerations are observed during labor. Which underlying problem should be considered?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Topic:** Fetal monitoring

- **A.** Improved fetal oxygenation
- **B.** Maternal hearing impairment
- **C.** Reduced uteroplacental oxygen transfer
- **D.** Normal fetal head compression as the usual explanation

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: C. Reduced uteroplacental oxygen transfer**

Late deceleration contraction ke baad fetal heart rate girne ka pattern hai. Placenta se baby ko oxygen kam milne ka doubt hota hai. Nurse maternal position, contraction pattern aur fetal status assess karke team ko batati hai.

**Baaki options kyun galat hain?**

- **A.** Late pattern reassuring nahi hota.
- **B.** Hearing impairment fetal oxygenation explain nahi karti.
- **D.** Head compression usually early decelerations se linked hai.

</details>

## Antepartum bleeding

### Q4. A pregnant patient in the third trimester presents with painless bright red vaginal bleeding. What should be avoided until placental location is assessed?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Topic:** Antepartum bleeding

- **A.** Prompt obstetric referral
- **B.** Digital vaginal examination
- **C.** Maternal vital-sign assessment
- **D.** Fetal heart rate assessment

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: B. Digital vaginal examination**

Placenta previa mein placenta cervix ke paas ho sakta hai. Digital examination se placenta disturb hokar severe bleeding badh sakti hai. Isliye placental location confirm hone tak exam avoid karte hain.

**Baaki options kyun galat hain?**

- **A.** Urgent obstetric referral zaroori hai.
- **C.** Vital signs blood loss assess karne ke liye chahiye.
- **D.** Fetal heart assessment baby ki condition batata hai.

</details>
