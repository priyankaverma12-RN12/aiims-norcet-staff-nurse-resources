### Q01. In early pregnancy, which hormonal influence particularly promotes growth and branching of the mammary duct system?

**Difficulty:** 🟡 Medium · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD

- **B.** Rising estrogen
- **C.** Suppressed estrogen production
- **D.** An isolated increase in antidiuretic hormone

<details><summary>✅ Sahi answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct answer: B. Rising estrogen**

- **A (Incorrect):** Pregnancy mein prolactin usually badhta hai, girta nahi hai. Yeh secretory tissue differentiation mein contribute karta hai, jabki early ductal growth mein estrogen ka prominent role hai.
- **B (Correct):** Estrogen breast ke ducts ki growth mein important role play karta hai. Ducts woh small pathways hain jo later milk ko nipple ki taraf transport karenge. Doosre hormones bhi breast development mein contribute karte hain.
- **C (Incorrect):** Estrogen concentration pregnancy mein generally rise karti hai. Iski suppression expected ductal expansion ko explain nahi karti.
- **D (Incorrect):** ADH primarily water balance regulate karta hai. Mammary duct growth ka principal hormonal signal isko nahi maana jata.

- **D (Correct):** Progesterone prolactin aur other hormonal signals ke saath milk-producing lobules aur alveoli develop karne mein contribute karta hai. Alveoli small glandular units hain jahan secretory cells milk components banati hain.

### Q03. At approximately mid-pregnancy, mammary epithelial cells acquire the ability to synthesize early milk components. Which physiological stage does this describe?

**Difficulty:** 🔴 Difficult · **Priority:** 🔴 MOST IMPORTANT

- **A.** Secretory differentiation, also called lactogenesis I
- **B.** Secretory activation, or lactogenesis II, fully completed before birth
- **C.** Mammary involution after weaning
- **D.** Failure of mammogenesis

<details><summary>✅ Sahi answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct answer: A. Secretory differentiation, also called lactogenesis I**

- **A (Correct):** Pregnancy ke dauran milk-producing epithelial cells mature hoti hain aur limited early secretion bana sakti hain. Is preparation ko secretory differentiation ya lactogenesis I kehte hain. Copious postpartum milk secretion ki stage alag hai.
- **B (Incorrect):** Lactogenesis II mein milk volume substantially increase hota hai, jo usually placenta delivery ke baad hormonal changes se associated hai. Mid-pregnancy differentiation is stage ke samaan nahi hai.
- **C (Incorrect):** Involution breastfeeding kam ya band hone ke baad glandular tissue ke remodelling ko kehte hain. Mid-pregnancy mein tissue lactation ke liye prepare ho raha hota hai.
- **D (Incorrect):** Mammogenesis breast tissue growth aur development hai. Secretory ability develop hona preparation ka sign hai, failure ka proof nahi hai.

### Q17. Which distinction best describes the roles of developing mammary lactocytes and surrounding myoepithelial cells?

<details><summary>✅ Sahi answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct answer: A. Lactocytes synthesize milk components, while myoepithelial contraction helps move milk into ducts**

- **A (Correct):** Lactocytes alveoli ki secretory epithelial cells hain. Yeh milk components banati hain. Myoepithelial cells alveoli ke around contractile cells hain jo milk ko ducts ki taraf push karne mein help karti hain.
- **B (Incorrect):** Skin pigmentation melanocytes se related hai. Lactocytes aur myoepithelial cells lactation-related glandular structures mein different functions perform karti hain.
