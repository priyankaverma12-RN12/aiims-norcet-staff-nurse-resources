### Q01. A woman at 29 weeks has pain over the sacroiliac region and anterior pelvis on walking. Which pregnancy-related pattern is most consistent?

**Difficulty:** 🟡 Medium · **Priority:** 🔴 MOST IMPORTANT

- **A.** Isolated wrist neuropathy
- **B.** Pelvic girdle pain
- **C.** Physiological breast tenderness
- **D.** Normal fetal movement alone

<details><summary>✅ Sahi answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct answer: B. Pelvic girdle pain**

- **A (Incorrect):** Wrist nerve compression mein hand symptoms hote hain, pelvic joints ka movement pain nahi.
- **B (Correct):** Pelvis ke front aur back joints par load badhne se movement ke saath pain ho sakta hai. Is pattern mein pelvic girdle assessment useful hai.
- **C (Incorrect):** Breast tissue mein hormonal changes se tenderness ho sakti hai, par yeh pelvic load-related pain explain nahi karta.
- **D (Incorrect):** Fetal movement transient sensation de sakta hai, lekin persistent joint-related walking pain ko sirf fetal movement nahi bolna chahiye.

<details><summary>✅ Sahi answer aur explanation dekho</summary>
