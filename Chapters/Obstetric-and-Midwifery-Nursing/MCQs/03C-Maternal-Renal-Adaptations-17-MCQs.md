### Q01. At 10 weeks of an uncomplicated pregnancy, which renal circulatory change helps meet the increased filtration demand?

**Difficulty:** 🟡 Medium · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD

- **A.** All nephrons temporarily stop filtration
- **B.** Renal artery constriction reduces renal perfusion
- **C.** Renal vasodilation increases renal plasma flow
- **D.** Renal blood supply is replaced by placental blood

<details><summary>✅ Sahi answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct answer: C. Renal vasodilation increases renal plasma flow**

- **A (Incorrect):** Nephrons pregnancy mein kaam karte rehte hain. Filtration band hona serious abnormality hai.
- **B (Incorrect):** Renal artery narrow hone se blood supply ghat sakti hai. Yeh expected early adaptation ki opposite direction hai.
- **C (Correct):** Kidney ki blood vessels relax hoti hain. Isse kidney tak plasma ka flow badhta hai aur early pregnancy mein filtration badhne mein madad milti hai.
- **D (Incorrect):** Placenta fetal exchange mein help karta hai. Maternal kidneys ko blood maternal circulation se hi milta hai.

### Q02. A woman's measured prepregnancy GFR was 100 mL/min. In a simplified exercise, it rises by 45% during pregnancy. What is the new GFR?

**Difficulty:** 🟡 Medium · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD

- **A.** 145 mL/min
- **B.** 45 mL/min
- **C.** 100 mL/min
- **D.** 55 mL/min

<details><summary>✅ Sahi answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

- **A (Correct):** 100 ka 45% 45 mL/min hai. Baseline mein 45 add karne par 145 mL/min milta hai.
- **B (Incorrect):** 45 sirf increase hai. Yeh final filtration rate nahi hai.
- **C (Incorrect):** Yeh baseline hai. Ismein pregnancy adaptation ka increase include nahi hua.
- **D (Incorrect):** 55 tab milta agar baseline se 45% subtract karte. Question mein increase diya hai.

### Q03. A healthy pregnant woman has a lower blood urea nitrogen concentration than before conception. Which explanation fits the expected adaptation?

**Difficulty:** 🟡 Medium · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD

- **A.** A low BUN independently proves liver failure
- **B.** The kidneys normally stop excreting urea
- **C.** Maternal protein metabolism ceases
- **D.** Increased renal filtration and altered volume balance can lower BUN

<details><summary>✅ Sahi answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct answer: D. Increased renal filtration and altered volume balance can lower BUN**

- **A (Incorrect):** Low BUN ke kai causes ho sakte hain. Is single result se liver failure diagnose nahi karte.
- **B (Incorrect):** Agar urea excretion ruk jaaye toh blood mein waste accumulate ho sakta hai. Yeh normal adaptation nahi hai.
- **C (Incorrect):** Mother aur fetus dono ko protein metabolism ki zaroorat rehti hai. Yeh process band nahi hota.
- **D (Correct):** Urea protein metabolism ka waste hai. Pregnancy mein filtration badhne aur plasma volume expand hone se measured BUN kam ho sakta hai.

### Q04. A renal laboratory report automatically prints a CKD-EPI eGFR for a pregnant woman. Which assessment principle should the nurse communicate?

- **C (Incorrect):** Pregnancy mein kidney dysfunction detect karna important hai. Tests ko delivery tak postpone karna unsafe ho sakta hai.
- **D (Incorrect):** CKD ke liye chronicity aur kidney damage ki assessment bhi chahiye. Pregnancy mein single automated estimate se stage decide nahi karte.

### Q05. A well pregnant woman has mildly lower serum sodium and plasma osmolality than her prepregnancy values. Which physiological change contributes?

**Difficulty:** 🔴 Difficult · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD

- **A.** The body loses the ability to sense plasma osmolality
- **B.** The osmotic thresholds for thirst and vasopressin release shift downward
- **C.** Vasopressin production permanently stops
- **D.** Every mild sodium reduction proves severe renal failure

<details><summary>✅ Sahi answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct answer: B. The osmotic thresholds for thirst and vasopressin release shift downward**

- **A (Incorrect):** Osmoregulation band nahi hoti. Body ek adjusted set point ke aas paas water balance maintain karti hai.
- **B (Correct):** Body pregnancy mein thirst aur ADH release ko lower osmolality par regulate kar sakti hai. Isse water retention aur slightly lower concentration develop ho sakti hai.
- **C (Incorrect):** ADH water conservation mein important rehta hai. Permanent absence expected physiological change nahi hai.
- **D (Incorrect):** Pregnancy-specific reference aur clinical context important hain. Marked ya symptomatic hyponatremia ko phir bhi evaluate karna chahiye.

### Q06. How can total maternal body sodium increase while serum sodium concentration is slightly lower in healthy pregnancy?

**Difficulty:** 🔴 Difficult · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD

- **A.** Pregnancy prevents any increase in total body water
- **B.** The placenta removes all sodium from maternal blood
- **C.** Serum sodium concentration always equals total body sodium content
- **D.** Water retention proportionately exceeds sodium retention

<details><summary>✅ Sahi answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct answer: D. Water retention proportionately exceeds sodium retention**

- **A (Incorrect):** Maternal body water pregnancy mein expand hota hai. Yeh volume adaptation ka important part hai.

- **C (Incorrect):** Concentration water volume par bhi depend karti hai. Isliye dono measurements ek hi cheez nahi batate.
- **D (Correct):** Serum sodium ek concentration hai, total body store nahi. Jab water proportionately zyada retain hota hai toh sodium store badhne ke bawajood concentration thodi kam ho sakti hai.

### Q07. A student notices increased renin and aldosterone activity in healthy pregnancy despite a lower systemic vascular resistance. Which interpretation is most appropriate?

**Difficulty:** 🔴 Difficult · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD

- **A.** The renin–angiotensin–aldosterone system helps support maternal volume expansion
- **B.** Renin elevation means all maternal arteries must be severely constricted
- **C.** Aldosterone normally removes all sodium from the urine without reabsorption

<details><summary>✅ Sahi answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct answer: A. The renin–angiotensin–aldosterone system helps support maternal volume expansion**

- **A (Correct):** Vasodilation ke bawajood body sodium aur water conserve karke circulating volume expand karti hai. RAAS is adaptation mein contribute karta hai.
- **B (Incorrect):** RAAS activity aur vascular sensitivity pregnancy mein change hoti hain. Increased renin se severe generalized vasoconstriction automatically prove nahi hoti.
- **C (Incorrect):** Aldosterone distal nephron mein sodium reabsorption promote karta hai. Sodium ko urine mein hi chhod dena iske usual effect ki opposite direction hai.
- **D (Incorrect):** Aldosterone sodium conservation mein help karta hai. Iski absence normal volume expansion ki requirement nahi hai.

### Q09. A routine ultrasound at 28 weeks shows mild right-sided renal collecting-system dilatation in an asymptomatic woman. Which interpretation is best?

<details><summary>✅ Sahi answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct answer: D. Right-sided physiological dilatation is common, but symptoms and obstruction still require assessment**

- **A (Incorrect):** Dilatation urine drainage se related hai. Isse complete cessation of filtration prove nahi hoti.

### Q12. A woman at 32 weeks asks why coughing occasionally causes a few drops of urine to leak. Which pregnancy-specific explanation is most relevant?

### Q13. A pregnant woman has a 24-hour urine protein result of 420 mg. Which interpretation is safest?

**Difficulty:** 🔴 Difficult · **Priority:** 🔴 MOST IMPORTANT

<details><summary>✅ Sahi answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct answer: A. The result exceeds the usual significant-proteinuria threshold and needs obstetric evaluation**

- **C (Incorrect):** Proteinuria ke multiple causes hain. Preeclampsia diagnosis ke liye gestational timing, blood pressure aur organ findings ka context chahiye.
- **D (Incorrect):** Urine mein protein detect hona zero filtration ka proof nahi hai. Result ko renal and obstetric context mein interpret karte hain.
