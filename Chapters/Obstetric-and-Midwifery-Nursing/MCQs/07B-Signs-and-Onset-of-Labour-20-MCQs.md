### Q01. At term, painful tightenings occur without progressive cervical change on two clinically indicated assessments. What is the best interpretation?

**Difficulty:** 🟡 Medium · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD

- **A.** Established labour is confirmed by pain
- **B.** The pain must be imaginary
- **C.** Pain alone does not establish progressive labour
- **D.** Immediate surgery is mandatory

<details><summary>✅ Sahi answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct answer: C. Pain alone does not establish progressive labour**

- **A (Incorrect):** Sirf pain se established labour confirm nahi hota, kyunki painful contractions bina cervical progression ke bhi ho sakti hain.
- **B (Incorrect):** Patient ki takleef ko imaginary kehna galat hai. Nurse symptoms ko seriously leti hai aur suitable relief arrange karti hai.
- **C (Correct):** Dard real ho sakta hai, lekin labour ki progress ke liye cervix aur contractions dono assess karne padte hain. Nurse support aur reassessment arrange karti hai.
- **D (Incorrect):** Cervical progression abhi establish na hona apne aap surgery ka indication nahi hai. Puri clinical situation assess karni hoti hai.
