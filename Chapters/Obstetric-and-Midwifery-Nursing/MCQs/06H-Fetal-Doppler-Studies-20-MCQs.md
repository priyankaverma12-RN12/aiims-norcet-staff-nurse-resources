### Q03. A waveform has peak systolic velocity 60 cm/s and end-diastolic velocity 20 cm/s. What is the S/D ratio?

**Difficulty:** 🟡 Medium · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD

- **A.** 3
- **B.** 0.33
- **C.** 2
- **D.** 40

<details><summary>✅ Sahi answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct answer: A. 3**

- **A (Correct):** S/D = peak systolic divided by end-diastolic velocity, 60/20 = 3. Units cancel ho jati hain.
- **B (Incorrect):** 0.33 diastolic/systolic ka inverse ratio hai.
- **C (Incorrect):** 2 yahan correct division nahi hai.
- **D (Incorrect):** 40 velocity difference hai, ratio nahi.

### Q04. A Doppler waveform shows systolic velocity 50 cm/s and end-diastolic velocity 10 cm/s. What is its resistance index (RI)?

**Difficulty:** 🟡 Medium · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD

- **A.** 5
- **B.** 0.2
- **C.** 0.8
- **D.** 4

<details><summary>✅ Sahi answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct answer: C. 0.8**

- **A (Incorrect):** 5 S/D ratio hai, RI nahi.
- **B (Incorrect):** 0.2 D/S hai, RI ke numerator mein difference chahiye.
- **C (Correct):** RI = (S − D)/S, so (50 − 10)/50 = 0.8. Ye dimensionless ratio hai.
- **D (Incorrect):** 4 (S − D)/D hai, standard RI formula nahi.
