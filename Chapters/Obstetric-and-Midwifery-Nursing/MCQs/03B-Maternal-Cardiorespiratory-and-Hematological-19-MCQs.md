## Cardiovascular

### Q01. A woman at 36 weeks has a reassuring cardiovascular examination. The nurse finds the apical impulse slightly superior and lateral to its prepregnancy position. Which anatomical adaptation best explains this?

**Difficulty:** 🟡 Medium · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD

- **A.** Displacement of the heart toward the right iliac fossa
- **B.** Descent of the diaphragm into the pelvis
- **C.** Elevation of the diaphragm by the enlarging uterus
- **D.** Closure of the pulmonary valve throughout diastole

<details><summary>✅ Sahi answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct answer: C. Elevation of the diaphragm by the enlarging uterus**

- **A (Incorrect):** Heart thorax ke andar hi rehta hai. Right iliac fossa abdomen ke lower right region mein hai, aur yeh normal cardiac displacement nahi hai.
- **B (Incorrect):** Diaphragm pelvis mein descend nahi karta. Growing uterus uski resting position ko upar push karta hai.
- **C (Correct):** Jab uterus bada hota hai, toh diaphragm upar uthta hai aur heart ki position thodi upar aur left ki taraf shift ho sakti hai. Nurse auscultation ki jagah ko actual anatomy ke hisab se adjust karti hai.
- **D (Incorrect):** Pulmonary valve normally diastole mein closed rehta hai, lekin is normal valve action se heart ka anatomical position shift explain nahi hota.

### Q02. A normotensive woman's blood pressure was 118/76 mmHg before pregnancy, 104/65 at 22 weeks and 116/74 at 37 weeks. She has no concerning symptoms. Which interpretation best fits the expected longitudinal trend?

**Difficulty:** 🟡 Medium · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD

- **A.** Blood pressure may fall in mid-pregnancy and approach baseline near term
- **B.** Progressive hypotension is obligatory until birth
- **C.** The final reading establishes gestational hypertension
- **D.** The mid-pregnancy reading alone establishes circulatory shock

<details><summary>✅ Sahi answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct answer: A. Blood pressure may fall in mid-pregnancy and approach baseline near term**

- **A (Correct):** Mid-pregnancy mein blood pressure thoda gir sakta hai aur term ke paas baseline ke kareeb aa sakta hai. Diye gaye observations is trend se compatible hain, lekin routine monitoring phir bhi zaroori hai.
- **B (Incorrect):** Normal pregnancy mein vascular resistance kam ho sakta hai, lekin blood pressure delivery tak lagatar girna zaroori nahi hai. Late pregnancy mein woh baseline ke paas aa sakta hai.
- **C (Incorrect):** 116/74 mmHg se gestational hypertension establish nahi hota. Diagnosis ke liye actual hypertension criteria aur clinical assessment zaroori hote hain.
- **D (Incorrect):** 104/65 mmHg ki ek reading shock prove nahi karti. Shock mein tissue perfusion aur symptoms jaise altered consciousness, tachycardia ya reduced urine output bhi assess karte hain.

### Q03. A clinically well pregnant woman has a higher erythrocyte sedimentation rate than her prepregnancy baseline. Which interpretation is most appropriate?

**Difficulty:** 🟡 Medium · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD

- **A.** An elevated ESR independently confirms sepsis
- **B.** Pregnancy always suppresses ESR to zero
- **C.** An elevated ESR proves that the maternal platelet count is low
- **D.** Pregnancy can raise ESR through changes in plasma proteins and hematocrit, so the result is nonspecific

<details><summary>✅ Sahi answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct answer: D. Pregnancy can raise ESR through changes in plasma proteins and hematocrit, so the result is nonspecific**

- **A (Incorrect):** ESR ek nonspecific test hai. Sirf iski elevation se sepsis diagnose nahi karte, kyunki infection ke bina bhi ESR badh sakta hai.
- **B (Incorrect):** Pregnancy ESR ko zero nahi karti. Plasma proteins aur hematocrit mein changes se ESR increase ho sakta hai.
- **C (Incorrect):** ESR red blood cell sedimentation measure karta hai. Platelet count ke liye CBC ka platelet result dekhte hain.
- **D (Correct):** ESR batata hai ki red cells ek fixed time mein kitni tezi se neeche settle hote hain. Pregnancy mein fibrinogen jaise proteins aur hematocrit ke changes is rate ko badha sakte hain. Isliye symptoms aur other tests ke saath interpretation karte hain.

### Q11. In a simplified maternal physiology exercise, tidal volume is 600 mL, anatomical dead space is 150 mL and respiratory rate is 16/min. What is the calculated alveolar ventilation?

**Difficulty:** 🟡 Medium · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD

- **B.** 2.4 L/min
- **C.** 9.6 L/min
- **D.** 0.45 L/min

<details><summary>✅ Sahi answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct answer: A. 7.2 L/min**

- **A (Correct):** Har breath mein gas exchange ke liye estimated volume 600 − 150 = 450 mL hai. Isko 16 se multiply karne par 7200 mL/min yani 7.2 L/min milta hai. Yeh simplified calculation hai.

- **C (Incorrect):** 9.6 L/min total minute ventilation hai, jisme conducting airway ka dead space bhi include hai.
- **D (Incorrect):** 0.45 L ek breath ka estimated alveolar volume hai. Per minute value ke liye respiratory rate se multiply karna padega.

### Q18. A pregnant woman with chronic hypertension reports new loud snoring, witnessed breathing pauses during sleep and daytime sleepiness. Which response is appropriate?

### Q19. A healthy pregnant woman at sea level has a modest rise in arterial oxygen tension compared with her prepregnancy baseline. Which mechanism best explains this expected change?

**Difficulty:** 🟡 Medium · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD

- **A.** Increased alveolar ventilation can raise alveolar and arterial oxygen tension modestly
- **B.** Maternal lungs cease carbon dioxide elimination
- **C.** Reduced ventilation causes oxygen to accumulate in arterial blood
- **D.** Placental oxygen transfer stops in healthy pregnancy

<details><summary>✅ Sahi answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct answer: A. Increased alveolar ventilation can raise alveolar and arterial oxygen tension modestly**

- **A (Correct):** Alveolar ventilation badhne se alveolar carbon dioxide kam hota hai aur alveolar oxygen tension modestly rise kar sakti hai. Arterial oxygen tension bhi thoda badh sakta hai. Exact values altitude, posture aur clinical condition par depend karte hain.
- **B (Incorrect):** Maternal lungs carbon dioxide eliminate karte rehte hain. Pregnancy mein ventilation increase se PaCO₂ usually lower hota hai.
- **C (Incorrect):** Reduced ventilation se alveolar oxygen generally gir sakti hai. Yeh expected modest rise ka mechanism nahi hai.
- **D (Incorrect):** Placenta fetal oxygen exchange ke liye important hai. Transfer stop hona healthy adaptation nahi hai.
