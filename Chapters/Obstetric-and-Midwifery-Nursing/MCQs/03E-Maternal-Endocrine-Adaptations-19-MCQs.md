### Q01. At 10 weeks of pregnancy, a woman has a mildly reduced TSH and free T4 within her laboratory's pregnancy-specific interval. Which mechanism may explain this pattern?

**Difficulty:** 🟡 Medium · **Priority:** 🔴 MOST IMPORTANT

- **A.** TSH necessarily becomes undetectable throughout every pregnancy
- **B.** Placental progesterone destroys all pituitary thyrotrophs
- **C.** hCG can stimulate the thyroid receptor and reduce pituitary TSH through feedback
- **D.** Maternal thyroid hormone synthesis stops until delivery

<details><summary>✅ Sahi answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct answer: C. hCG can stimulate the thyroid receptor and reduce pituitary TSH through feedback**

- **A (Incorrect):** TSH ka level gestational stage aur individual response ke hisab se vary karta hai. Har pregnant woman mein undetectable TSH expected nahi hai.
- **B (Incorrect):** Thyrotrophs TSH banane wali pituitary cells hain. Pregnancy mein inka destruction expected nahi hai, aur low TSH ke liye hCG-mediated stimulation zyada relevant hai.
- **C (Correct):** Early pregnancy mein hCG ka level badhta hai. Yeh TSH receptor ko mildly stimulate kar sakta hai, jisse thyroid hormone production par effect padta hai aur feedback ke through TSH kam ho sakta hai. Result ko symptoms aur gestational reference range ke saath review karna chahiye.
- **D (Incorrect):** Maternal thyroid pregnancy mein hormones banata rehta hai. Early fetal development ke liye maternal thyroid hormone availability important hai.

<details><summary>✅ Sahi answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct answer: A. Use the applicable laboratory and trimester-specific interval and review the assay used**

- **C (Incorrect):** Nonpregnant range pregnancy-related changes ko account nahi kar sakti. Pregnancy-specific interval available ho toh use prefer karte hain.
- **D (Incorrect):** Gestational age reference interpretation mein help karta hai, lekin disease establish karne ke liye thyroid results, symptoms aur clinical context chahiye.

- **D (Correct):** Lipolysis ka matlab stored fat ka breakdown hai. Late pregnancy mein maternal body fatty acids ko fuel ke roop mein zyada use kar sakti hai, jisse glucose availability support hoti hai. Prolonged fasting ya illness mein ketosis risk bhi assess karna chahiye.

### Q19. Which change in the source of growth hormone activity occurs as pregnancy advances?

**Difficulty:** 🔴 Difficult · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD

- **A.** The maternal pancreas becomes the principal source of placental growth hormone
- **B.** Placental growth hormone increasingly contributes to maternal circulating growth hormone activity
- **C.** All maternal growth hormone activity disappears at the end of the first trimester
- **D.** Fetal red blood cells become the only growth hormone-producing cells

<details><summary>✅ Sahi answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct answer: B. Placental growth hormone increasingly contributes to maternal circulating growth hormone activity**

- **A (Incorrect):** Pancreas insulin aur glucagon jaise hormones banata hai. Placental growth hormone ka source placenta hai.
- **B (Correct):** Placenta growth hormone ka variant produce karta hai jo gestation ke saath maternal circulation mein important ho jata hai. Yeh maternal metabolic adaptation aur IGF-I signalling mein contribute karta hai.
- **C (Incorrect):** Maternal pituitary growth hormone secretion change ho sakti hai, lekin placental growth hormone activity increase hoti hai. Total activity ka disappear hona expected nahi hai.
- **D (Incorrect):** Red cells oxygen transport karte hain. Yeh maternal placental growth hormone ka source nahi hain.
