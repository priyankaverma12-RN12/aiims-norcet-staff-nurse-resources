### Q01. A woman is confirmed to be in the active first stage at 5 cm with intact membranes. The unit uses the WHO Labour Care Guide. What should the midwife do?

**Difficulty:** 🟡 Medium · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD

- **A.** Wait for membrane rupture before opening the guide
- **B.** Start LCG documentation now and continue appropriate assessments
- **C.** Wait until 6 cm because that is the WHO LCG starting point
- **D.** Use only an old alert-line chart until full dilatation

<details><summary>✅ Sahi answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct answer: B. Start LCG documentation now and continue appropriate assessments**

- **A (Incorrect):** Membranes intact hone se active labour documentation delay nahi hoti. WHO LCG ko active first stage confirm hone par use kiya ja sakta hai.
- **B (Correct):** WHO LCG mein active first stage ka starting point 5 cm hai. Membranes intact hon ya ruptured, appropriate assessment ke baad guide initiate ki jati hai.
- **C (Incorrect):** Kuch other guidelines active phase ke liye 6 cm use karti hain. Named WHO guide ka criterion 5 cm hai, isliye in definitions ko mix mat karo.
- **D (Incorrect):** Traditional alert-line chart ek different monitoring tool hai. WHO LCG use karne wale unit mein full dilatation tak documentation postpone nahi karte.
