### Q01. A pregnant woman's heart rate is 90/min and stroke volume is 80 mL. What is her cardiac output?

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Priority:** High

- A. 5.25 L/min
- B. 7.2 L/min
- C. 9 L/min
- D. 0.72 L/min

<details><summary>✅ Sahi answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct answer: B. 7.2 L/min**

- **Option A (Incorrect):** 5.25 L/min tab aayega jab heart rate 75/min aur stroke volume 70 mL ho. Given values alag hain.
- **Option B (Correct):** 90 × 80 = 7200 mL/min hota hai. Isko 1000 se divide karne par 7.2 L/min milta hai.
- **Option C (Incorrect):** 9 L/min ke liye 90/min heart rate par stroke volume 100 mL hona chahiye.
- **Option D (Incorrect):** 7200 mL ko litre mein convert karne par 7.2 L milta hai, 0.72 L nahi.

### Q02. Why may blood pressure decrease in mid-pregnancy even though cardiac output increases?

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Priority:** High

- A. Systemic vascular resistance decreases
- B. Maternal blood volume falls markedly
- C. The placenta stops receiving blood
- D. Stroke volume necessarily falls below baseline

<details><summary>✅ Sahi answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct answer: A. Systemic vascular resistance decreases**

- **Option A (Correct):** Vasodilation se vascular resistance kam hota hai. Isliye cardiac output badhne ke bawajood blood pressure thoda gir sakta hai.
- **Option B (Incorrect):** Normal pregnancy mein blood volume badhta hai, markedly kam nahi hota.
- **Option C (Incorrect):** Placental circulation growing fetus ki needs poori karti hai. Placental perfusion band hona normal adaptation nahi hai.
- **Option D (Incorrect):** Stroke volume badh sakta hai. Blood pressure ka fall necessarily stroke volume girne se nahi hota.

**Concept:** [Cardiovascular and hematological changes](../Concepts/03-Physiological-Changes-During-Pregnancy.md#1-cardiovascular-and-hematological-changes)

### Q03. At 34 weeks, a conscious woman becomes dizzy and hypotensive while lying flat for examination. Her pulse is present and there is no trauma concern. What should the nurse do immediately?

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Priority:** Very high

- A. Ask her to remain supine
- B. Give oral iron and wait
- C. Turn her to the left lateral position and reassess ABC and vital signs
- D. Ask her to walk without assistance

<details><summary>✅ Sahi answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct answer: C. Turn her to the left lateral position and reassess ABC and vital signs**

- **Option A (Incorrect):** Supine position mein uterus vena cava ko compress kar sakta hai, isliye isi position mein rakhna symptoms badha sakta hai.
- **Option B (Incorrect):** Iron acute positional hypotension ko correct nahi karta. Assessment delay nahi karni chahiye.
- **Option C (Correct):** Left lateral position vena cava compression kam kar sakti hai. Airway, breathing, circulation aur vital signs reassess karein aur zarurat par emergency help bulayein.
- **Option D (Incorrect):** Dizziness ke waqt bina assistance walk karna fall ka risk badhata hai.

### Q08. Tidal volume increases from 450 mL to 600 mL while respiratory rate remains 16/min. How much does minute ventilation increase?

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Priority:** High

- A. 0.15 L/min
- B. 1.6 L/min
- C. 7.2 L/min
- D. 2.4 L/min

<details><summary>✅ Sahi answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct answer: D. 2.4 L/min**

- **Option A (Incorrect):** 0.15 L ek breath ka tidal volume increase hai, ek minute ka nahi.
- **Option B (Incorrect):** Respiratory rate 16/min hai. 1.6 L/min given difference se calculate nahi hota.
- **Option C (Incorrect):** 7.2 L/min original minute ventilation hai, increase nahi.
- **Option D (Correct):** Original ventilation 450 × 16 = 7200 mL/min hai. New ventilation 600 × 16 = 9600 mL/min hai. Difference 2400 mL/min yani 2.4 L/min hai.

### Q20. At 32 weeks, a woman reports intense itching of the palms and soles without a primary rash. What is the priority nursing response?

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Priority:** Very high

- A. Reassure her that all itching is caused by linea nigra
- B. Advise her to wait until delivery without evaluation
- C. Recommend vigorous scratching of the affected areas
- D. Arrange prompt maternity assessment for possible intrahepatic cholestasis of pregnancy

<details><summary>✅ Sahi answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct answer: D. Arrange prompt maternity assessment for possible intrahepatic cholestasis of pregnancy**

- **Option A (Incorrect):** Linea nigra abdominal pigmentation hai. Intense palm aur sole itching ka explanation nahi hai.
- **Option B (Incorrect):** Cholestasis possibility ko delivery tak ignore karna unsafe ho sakta hai. Prompt assessment chahiye.
- **Option C (Incorrect):** Scratching underlying cause address nahi karta aur skin injury ho sakti hai.
- **Option D (Correct):** Palms aur soles par intense itching without primary rash cholestasis ka possible sign hai. Maternity team evaluation aur appropriate tests arrange kare.
