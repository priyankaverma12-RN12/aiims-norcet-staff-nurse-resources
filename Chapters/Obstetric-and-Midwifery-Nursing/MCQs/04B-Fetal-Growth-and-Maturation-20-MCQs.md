### Q01. An antenatal educator describes fetal growth after the major embryonic organ-forming period. Which statement best differentiates growth from functional maturation?

**Difficulty:** 🟡 Medium · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD

- **A.** Functional maturation always precedes the formation of every organ
- **B.** All fetal organs function at adult capacity as soon as they are anatomically visible
- **C.** An organ may increase in size while its specialized functions continue to mature
- **D.** Growth and maturation stop at the end of the first trimester

<details><summary>✅ Sahi answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct answer: C. An organ may increase in size while its specialized functions continue to mature**

- **A (Incorrect):** Organ ki structural organization aur functional development related processes hain. Har organ ke fully form hone se pehle complete functional maturity expected nahi hoti.
- **B (Incorrect):** Anatomical structure pehle dikh sakti hai, lekin uski functional capacity gradual develop hoti hai. Lungs aur brain ke example mein yeh difference especially important hai.
- **C (Correct):** Fetal period mein pehle se established organs size aur cell number mein grow karte hain. Saath hi specialized functions develop hote rehte hain. Isliye ultrasound par organ ka dikhna uski full functional maturity prove nahi karta.
- **D (Incorrect):** First trimester ke baad fetal length, weight aur organ function mein major changes continue hote hain. Isliye later prenatal care bhi important hai.

**Exam clue:** Organ size badhna aur organ ka functional mature hona alag processes hain.

### Q02. A fetal skin examination identifies a waxy coating. What is its principal developmental role before birth?

<details><summary>✅ Sahi answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

- **A (Correct):** Vernix caseosa skin secretions aur shed cells se related waxy coating hai. Yeh fetal skin ko surrounding fluid environment mein protect karne aur hydration maintain karne mein contribute karti hai.

**Exam clue:** Vernix skin ka protective coating hai, respiratory surfactant nahi.

### Q04. Why can a very preterm infant have relatively thin, translucent skin compared with a term infant?

- **D (Incorrect):** Genetics pigmentation influence kar sakti hai, lekin gestational maturity skin thickness aur barrier development ko independently affect karti hai.

- **A.** Birth weight alone always replaces gestational age in counseling
- **B.** Every newborn at one specified week has the same survival outcome
- **C.** Survival depends on multiple factors including gestational age, fetal condition and available neonatal care
- **D.** Availability of trained neonatal care has no effect on outcomes

<details><summary>✅ Sahi answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct answer: C. Survival depends on multiple factors including gestational age, fetal condition and available neonatal care**

- **A (Incorrect):** Birth weight useful factor hai, lekin gestational maturity aur other clinical information ko replace nahi karta. Multiple factors together consider karte hain.
- **B (Incorrect):** Similar gestational age par bhi individual risks aur available care different ho sakti hain. Fixed identical outcome ka promise accurate nahi hai.
- **C (Correct):** Gestational age survival estimate ke liye important hai, lekin weight, antenatal treatment, congenital conditions aur NICU resources bhi matter karte hain. Maternity aur neonatal team individualized counselling provide karti hai.
- **D (Incorrect):** Very preterm newborns ko respiratory, thermal aur feeding support ki need ho sakti hai. Appropriate neonatal resources survival aur complications par effect daal sakte hain.

**Exam clue:** Viability aur neonatal survival ko ek universal fixed guarantee na samjhein.

### Q18. A woman at 30 weeks is assessed as likely to give birth within seven days and the obstetric team prescribes antenatal corticosteroids. What developmental benefit is a key purpose?

**Difficulty:** 🟡 Medium · **Priority:** 🔴 MOST IMPORTANT

- **A.** To remove the need for any assessment of maternal condition
- **B.** To permanently stop fetal growth until term
- **C.** To guarantee that every preterm newborn will breathe without respiratory support
- **D.** To accelerate fetal lung maturation and reduce respiratory morbidity associated with preterm birth

<details><summary>✅ Sahi answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct answer: D. To accelerate fetal lung maturation and reduce respiratory morbidity associated with preterm birth**

- **A (Incorrect):** Maternal condition aur obstetric indications treatment decision ka part hain. Medicine dene ke baad monitoring unnecessary nahi hoti.
- **B (Incorrect):** Treatment ka goal fetal growth stop karna nahi hai. Fetal organ readiness improve karna key purpose hai.
- **C (Incorrect):** Corticosteroids risk reduce kar sakte hain, lekin individual infant ko phir bhi respiratory support chahiye ho sakta hai. Guarantee nahi deni chahiye.
- **D (Correct):** Corticosteroids fetal lung maturation ko promote karte hain, including surfactant-related readiness. Indicated use se prematurity-related respiratory morbidity aur kuch other complications reduce ho sakti hain. Nurse prescribed timing aur clinical plan verify kare.

**Exam clue:** Antenatal corticosteroids fetal lung maturation aur other prematurity outcomes improve karne ke liye use hote hain.

### Q19. A reliably dated fetus has an estimated weight at the 7th percentile at 31 weeks. Under SMFM guidance, which interpretation is appropriate?

**Difficulty:** 🔴 Difficult · **Priority:** 🔴 MOST IMPORTANT

- **A.** It meets the size threshold used for fetal growth restriction and warrants the appropriate obstetric evaluation
- **B.** A percentile below 10 always proves a chromosomal anomaly
- **C.** The gestational age should automatically be reduced until the weight is average
- **D.** No follow-up is needed because a percentile is not a measured weight

<details><summary>✅ Sahi answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct answer: A. It meets the size threshold used for fetal growth restriction and warrants the appropriate obstetric evaluation**

- **A (Correct):** EFW estimated fetal weight hai. SMFM gestational age ke liye EFW ya abdominal circumference below 10th percentile ko FGR ki definition mein use karta hai. Doppler, serial growth aur clinical review management guide karte hain.
- **B (Incorrect):** Small size ke multiple causes ho sakte hain, including placental insufficiency aur constitutionally small fetus. Chromosomal cause assessment se identify hota hai, percentile alone prove nahi karta.
- **C (Incorrect):** Reliable dating ko small size se automatically change karna growth restriction hide kar sakta hai. Current growth ko established gestational age ke against interpret karte hain.
- **D (Incorrect):** EFW estimate hone ke bawajood clinically useful hai. Low percentile appropriate evaluation warrant karta hai.

**Exam clue:** EFW ya AC 10th percentile se below ho toh FGR criteria meet kar sakta hai, clinical review zaroori hai.

### Q20. A woman requests an elective delivery at 38 weeks without a medical indication. Which fetal maturation consideration should the nurse communicate to the care team?

**Difficulty:** 🟡 Medium · **Priority:** 🔴 MOST IMPORTANT

- **A.** All infants at 38 and 40 weeks have identical organ maturity and neonatal risks
- **B.** Early-term infants can have greater neonatal morbidity than full-term infants, so elective timing should follow clinical guidance
- **C.** Delivery must never occur before 39 weeks even when a serious indication is present
- **D.** A normal fetal size estimate alone guarantees complete lung maturity

<details><summary>✅ Sahi answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct answer: B. Early-term infants can have greater neonatal morbidity than full-term infants, so elective timing should follow clinical guidance**

- **A (Incorrect):** Maturity within term period bhi change ho sakti hai. Early-term aur full-term categories clinically meaningful hain.
- **B (Correct):** 38 weeks early term hai. Respiratory aur other neonatal outcomes 39 weeks ki comparison mein less favorable ho sakte hain. Isliye nonmedically indicated early delivery avoid karne ki guidance hai, jabki genuine maternal ya fetal indication ho toh appropriate earlier birth possible hai.
- **C (Incorrect):** Medical indication ho toh pregnancy continue karne ka risk earlier birth se greater ho sakta hai. Timing treating team individualize karti hai.
- **D (Incorrect):** Weight estimate fetal size ka measure hai. Yeh complete pulmonary functional maturity ya neonatal outcome guarantee nahi karta.

**Exam clue:** Nonmedically indicated delivery before 39 weeks generally avoid ki jati hai.
