### Q01. Which feature distinguishes the historical modified WHO partograph from the earlier composite chart?

**Difficulty:** 🟡 Medium · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD

- **A.** The fetal monitoring section is omitted
- **B.** The latent-phase plotting section is omitted
- **C.** The maternal observations are removed
- **D.** The time axis is removed

<details><summary>✅ Sahi answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct answer: B. The latent-phase plotting section is omitted**

- **A (Incorrect):** Fetal wellbeing chart ka important component rehta hai. Latent phase section remove hone ka matlab fetal heart aur liquor observations remove hona nahi hai.
- **B (Correct):** Modified chart mein latent phase ka plotting section remove kiya gaya tha aur active labour ki plotting use hoti thi. Latent labour assessment phir bhi case record mein continue rehta hai.
- **C (Incorrect):** Maternal pulse, BP aur temperature jaise observations modified chart mein bhi relevant rehte hain. Chart ka purpose mother aur baby dono ko monitor karna hai.
- **D (Incorrect):** Time axis chart ko longitudinal record banata hai. Iske bina cervical progression aur observations ke timing compare nahi kar sakte.
