### Q01. A first-time pregnant woman at 19 weeks has not yet recognized fetal movements. She has no other concerning symptoms. Which explanation is most appropriate?

**Difficulty:** 🟡 Medium · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD

- **A.** Fetal movement never begins before 28 weeks
- **B.** Absence of perceived movement at 19 weeks establishes fetal death
- **C.** Fetal movements may first be recognized after 20 weeks in a first pregnancy
- **D.** Maternal perception must start exactly at 16 weeks

<details><summary>✅ Sahi answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct answer: C. Fetal movements may first be recognized after 20 weeks in a first pregnancy**

- **A (Incorrect):** Movement ki perception often 28 weeks se kaafi pehle start ho jati hai. Isliye 28 weeks ko universal starting point nahi maana jata.
- **B (Incorrect):** Sirf 19 weeks par movement feel nahi hona fetal death prove nahi karta. Clinical concerns aur gestation ke hisab se fetal assessment decide hoti hai.
- **C (Correct):** Pehli pregnancy mein woman ko movement ka sensation pehchanne mein thoda time lag sakta hai. NHS ke hisab se movements usually 16 se 24 weeks ke beech feel hote hain. Agar 24 weeks tak bilkul movement feel na ho, toh maternity team ko batana chahiye.
- **D (Incorrect):** Har woman ki perception same date par start nahi hoti. Placental location aur individual familiarity bhi difference create kar sakti hain.

**Exam clue:** First perception varies and may occur after 20 weeks in a first pregnancy.

- **D (Incorrect):** Hearing screening birth ke baad hoti hai. Yeh prenatal movement perception aur fetal cardiac activity evaluate nahi karti.

### Q03. Why might an anterior placenta reduce a woman's perception of some fetal movements?

- **A.** It establishes fetal hypoxia in every pregnancy

<details><summary>✅ Sahi answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct answer: D. Placental tissue lies between some fetal movements and the anterior abdominal wall**

- **A (Incorrect):** Anterior location normal variation ho sakti hai. Isse fetal hypoxia automatically diagnose nahi hoti, aur concern ho toh appropriate assessment chahiye.
- **B (Incorrect):** Placental position fetal skeletal muscles ko normally form hone se nahi rokti. Is option mein perception aur actual movement ko mix kiya gaya hai.

### Q04. At 37 weeks, a woman reports that fetal movements feel more like rolling and stretching than sharp kicks, with her usual overall activity preserved. What developmental explanation is reasonable?

### Q20. Which triage documentation best supports subsequent clinical assessment of a woman reporting altered fetal movements?

**Difficulty:** 🟡 Medium · **Priority:** 🔴 MOST IMPORTANT

- **A.** Usual pattern, onset and nature of the change, associated symptoms and relevant risk factors
- **B.** Only a single numerical kick count without timing or baseline
- **C.** Only a statement that fetal movements are subjective
- **D.** Only the estimated date of delivery

<details><summary>✅ Sahi answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct answer: A. Usual pattern, onset and nature of the change, associated symptoms and relevant risk factors**

- **A (Correct):** Nurse usual pattern, change kab start hua, strength ya frequency ka difference aur associated bleeding, pain ya other symptoms record kare. Is information se clinician urgency aur additional investigations decide kar sakta hai.
- **B (Incorrect):** Single count context ke bina misleading ho sakta hai. Baseline aur timing se symptom ka actual meaning samajh aata hai.
- **C (Incorrect):** Subjective perception clinically meaningful ho sakti hai. Sirf subjective bolkar detailed history omit karna appropriate nahi hai.
- **D (Incorrect):** Expected delivery date gestational context deta hai, lekin movement change ki severity, onset aur associated symptoms describe nahi karta.

**Exam clue:** Document the change from the individual baseline and associated symptoms.
