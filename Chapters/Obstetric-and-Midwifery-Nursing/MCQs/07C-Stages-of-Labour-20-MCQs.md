- **A.** Third stage
- **B.** Second stage
- **C.** Fourth stage
- **D.** Latent first stage

<details><summary>✅ Sahi answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct answer: B. Second stage**

- **A (Incorrect):** Third stage baby ke complete birth ke baad shuru hota hai. Abhi baby birth canal mein hai, isliye placental stage shuru nahi hua.
- **B (Correct):** Cervix poori tarah khulne ke baad second stage shuru hota hai aur baby ke complete birth tak chalta hai. Ab fetal descent aur wellbeing assess karte hain.
- **C (Incorrect):** Fourth stage placenta deliver hone ke baad early recovery observation ko describe karta hai. Abhi delivery ka process chal raha hai.

### Q20. Forty minutes after placental delivery, a woman has increasing dizziness, tachycardia and ongoing blood loss. Which fourth-stage principle takes priority?

**Difficulty:** 🔴 Difficult · **Priority:** 🔴 MOST IMPORTANT

- **A.** Delay assessment until the routine observation period ends
- **B.** Activate urgent postpartum haemorrhage assessment and response
- **C.** Prioritize routine birth-certificate paperwork
- **D.** Reassure because the placenta is already delivered

<details><summary>✅ Sahi answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct answer: B. Activate urgent postpartum haemorrhage assessment and response**

- **A (Incorrect):** Fourth stage observation ka purpose complications early identify karna hai. Routine period khatam hone tak wait karna unsafe hai.
- **B (Correct):** Early recovery mein dizziness, tachycardia aur ongoing bleeding deterioration ke signs ho sakte hain. Nurse emergency PPH response activate karti hai aur team maternal condition aur bleeding cause assess karti hai.
- **C (Incorrect):** Birth documentation important hai, lekin acute maternal instability mein treatment aur escalation first priority hain. Paperwork baad mein complete hota hai.
- **D (Incorrect):** Placenta deliver ho jaane se haemorrhage risk khatam nahi hota. Atony, trauma, retained tissue aur clotting problems evaluate karni pad sakti hain.
