### Q01. A woman at 35 weeks has a first generalized convulsion. Her antenatal records contain no previous diagnosis of hypertension. Which interpretation is safest?

**Difficulty:** 🟡 Medium · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD · Original practice

- **A.** Exclude eclampsia because hypertension was not previously documented
- **B.** Wait for a positive urine protein result before escalating
- **C.** Diagnose chronic epilepsy from this single event
- **D.** Consider eclampsia urgently while evaluating alternative causes of seizures

<details>
<summary>✅ Answer aur detailed explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct answer: D. Consider eclampsia urgently while evaluating alternative causes of seizures**

- **A (Incorrect):** Pehle hypertension record na hona eclampsia ko exclude nahi karta. Kuch women mein seizure pehla recognized manifestation hota hai, isliye emergency assessment zaroori hai.
- **B (Incorrect):** Urine protein result diagnosis mein help kar sakta hai, lekin emergency response uske liye wait nahi karta. Eclampsia without documented proteinuria bhi possible hai.
- **C (Incorrect):** Epilepsy recurrent unprovoked seizures se related diagnosis hai. Ek pregnancy-associated seizure se automatically chronic epilepsy establish nahi hoti.
- **D (Correct):** Pregnancy mein new seizure eclampsia ka concern raise karta hai even without previous hypertension documentation. Team airway aur circulation stabilize karte hue BP, glucose aur other possible causes assess karti hai.

**Exam clue:** Pehle se diagnosis absent hone par emergency ko exclude mat karo.

### Q02. During an active generalized convulsion in a maternity ward, which bedside action is safest?

**Difficulty:** 🟡 Medium · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD · Original practice

- **A.** Call for emergency help, protect the head and support lateral positioning when safe
- **B.** Hold both arms and legs firmly against the bed
- **C.** Insert a rigid object between the teeth
- **D.** Give oral water to prevent tongue injury

<details>
<summary>✅ Answer aur detailed explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct answer: A. Call for emergency help, protect the head and support lateral positioning when safe**

- **A (Correct):** Emergency team ko call karke nurse patient ko injury se protect karti hai. Jab safely possible ho, lateral position airway drainage aur maternal circulation support karti hai. Airway aur breathing promptly assess karo.
- **B (Incorrect):** Limbs forcefully restrain karne se muscle aur joint injury ho sakti hai. Nurse surrounding hazards remove karti hai aur head protect karti hai.
- **C (Incorrect):** Convulsion ke dauran teeth ke beech object insert karne se dental injury, airway obstruction aur staff injury ho sakti hai. Jaw ko force se open nahi karte.
- **D (Incorrect):** Active seizure mein swallowing safe nahi hoti. Water dene se aspiration ka risk badh sakta hai, isliye oral intake avoid karte hain.

**Exam clue:** Seizure mein mouth mein kuch mat daalo aur limbs ko forcefully restrain mat karo.

**Exam clue:** Total mass aur route ko confuse mat karo. Four plus ten equals fourteen grams.

### Q09. An IV magnesium prescription follows the NICE Collaborative Eclampsia Trial regimen. A pharmacy-prepared bag contains 10 g in a final volume of 100 mL. What pump rate delivers the prescribed maintenance dose of 1 g/hour?

- **C.** 20 mL/hour

<details>
<summary>✅ Answer aur detailed explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct answer: D. 10 mL/hour**

- **A (Incorrect):** Forty mL/hour se 4 g/hour deliver hoga. Loading mass ko maintenance hourly rate samajhna serious medication error ho sakta hai.
