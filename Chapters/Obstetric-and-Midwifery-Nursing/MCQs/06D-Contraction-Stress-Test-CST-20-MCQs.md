### Q01. Why can a uterine contraction reveal limited fetal oxygen reserve during a contraction stress test?

**Difficulty:** 🟡 Medium · **Priority:** 🔴 MOST IMPORTANT

- **A.** It permanently closes the umbilical arteries in every fetus
- **B.** It directly measures fetal arterial oxygen saturation
- **C.** It prevents the placenta from functioning for the remainder of pregnancy
- **D.** It temporarily reduces uteroplacental perfusion and challenges the fetus's ability to tolerate that reduction

<details><summary>✅ Sahi answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct answer: D. It temporarily reduces uteroplacental perfusion and challenges the fetus's ability to tolerate that reduction**

- **A (Incorrect):** Umbilical arteries har contraction mein permanently close nahi hoti. Aisa mechanism normal contraction ka description nahi hai.
- **B (Incorrect):** CST fetal rate pattern record karta hai. Direct arterial oxygen saturation measure nahi hoti.
- **C (Incorrect):** Temporary perfusion change aur permanent placental failure alag conditions hain. Normal contractions permanent failure cause nahi karte.
- **D (Correct):** Contraction ke waqt uteroplacental flow temporarily kam ho sakta hai. Agar reserve limited hai toh fetal rate recovery mein late slowing dikh sakti hai. CST isi response ko indirectly assess karta hai.

### Q02. Why is CST less commonly used than NST and BPP in contemporary antenatal surveillance?

**Difficulty:** 🟡 Medium · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD

- **A.** It may require contraction stimulation, has more contraindications and can produce equivocal or false-positive results
- **B.** It cannot record fetal heart rate
- **C.** It requires fetal surgery in every case
- **D.** It provides a definitive diagnosis of every fetal anomaly

<details><summary>✅ Sahi answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct answer: A. It may require contraction stimulation, has more contraindications and can produce equivocal or false-positive results**

- **A (Correct):** CST mein contractions assess ya induce karne pad sakte hain. Iske safety restrictions aur false-positive limitations ki wajah se many settings mein NST aur BPP preferred hain.
- **B (Incorrect):** FHR recording CST ka essential part hai. Limitation recording ki absence nahi hai.
- **C (Incorrect):** Routine CST fetal surgery nahi hai. Monitoring external ho sakti hai, lekin stimulation ka risk assess karna hota hai.
- **D (Incorrect):** CST anatomical anomalies ka definitive test nahi hai. Ye contraction-related fetal response assess karta hai.

### Q06. Four adequate contractions occur during CST, with late decelerations after three. What classification follows the conventional late-deceleration threshold?
