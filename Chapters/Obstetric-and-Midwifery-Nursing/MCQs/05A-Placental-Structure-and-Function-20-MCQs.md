### Q01. Which pair correctly identifies the principal fetal and maternal components of the mature placenta?

**Difficulty:** 🟡 Medium · **Priority:** 🔴 MOST IMPORTANT

- **A.** Amnion and decidua parietalis
- **B.** Chorion laeve and decidua capsularis
- **C.** Chorion frondosum and decidua basalis
- **D.** Yolk sac and myometrium

<details><summary>✅ Sahi answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct answer: C. Chorion frondosum and decidua basalis**

- **A (Incorrect):** Amnion inner fetal membrane hai. Decidua parietalis remaining uterine lining hai, implantation site ke directly neeche ka maternal placental component nahi.
- **B (Incorrect):** Chorion laeve relatively smooth region hai jahan villi regress hote hain. Decidua capsularis conceptus ko uterine cavity ki taraf cover karti hai, main placental bed nahi banati.
- **C (Correct):** Chorion frondosum villi wala fetal region hai. Decidua basalis implantation site par modified maternal endometrium hai. Dono tissues milkar functional placenta ka interface establish karte hain, lekin unka genetic origin alag rehta hai.
- **D (Incorrect):** Yolk sac early development support karta hai. Myometrium uterine muscle hai. Normal mature placental interface ki primary fetal-maternal pairing yeh nahi hai.

**Exam clue:** Fetal component chorion frondosum hai aur maternal component decidua basalis hai.

### Q02. During gross examination of an intact delivered placenta, which appearance identifies the fetal surface?

**Difficulty:** 🟢 Easy · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD

- **A.** Smooth, shiny surface with branching vessels radiating from the cord insertion
- **B.** Rough, dark-red surface divided into visible cotyledons
- **C.** Exposed uterine muscle with contractile fascicles
- **D.** A continuous surface lined by endometrial glands only

<details><summary>✅ Sahi answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct answer: A. Smooth, shiny surface with branching vessels radiating from the cord insertion**

- **A (Correct):** Fetal side par chorionic plate amnion se covered hoti hai. Isliye surface shiny aur smooth dikhti hai. Cord insertion se fetal vessels placental disc par branch karte dikh sakte hain.
- **B (Incorrect):** Rough lobulated surface maternal side hoti hai jo uterine wall se attached thi. Isse fetal surface identify nahi karte.
- **C (Incorrect):** Placenta ke fetal side par uterine muscle nahi honi chahiye. Myometrium maternal uterus ka layer hai.
- **D (Incorrect):** Endometrial tissue maternal side se related hai. Fetal side ka characteristic amnion-covered chorionic plate aur branching vessels hai.

**Exam clue:** Fetal surface amnion se covered hoti hai aur cord vessels nazar aa sakti hain.

### Q04. A student describes the human placenta as hemochorial. What does this classification mean?

**Difficulty:** 🟡 Medium · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD

- **C.** Maternal blood is separated from trophoblast by an intact uterine epithelial layer everywhere
- **D.** Only fetal blood enters the intervillous space

<details><summary>✅ Sahi answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct answer: B. Maternal blood directly bathes the chorionic trophoblast surface**

- **A (Incorrect):** Exchange ke liye blood pools ko openly mix hona zaroori nahi hai. Dono circulations normally distinct rehti hain, though limited cell transfer ho sakta hai.

- **D (Incorrect):** Intervillous space primarily maternal blood ka compartment hai. Fetal blood villous vessels mein flow karta hai.

**Exam clue:** Hemochorial ka matlab maternal blood fetal trophoblast ke direct contact mein aata hai, fetal blood ke direct contact mein nahi.
