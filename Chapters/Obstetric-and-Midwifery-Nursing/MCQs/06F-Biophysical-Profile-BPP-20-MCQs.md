### Q01. Which BPP component most directly reflects fetal urine production and longer-term placental function in a pregnancy with intact membranes?

**Difficulty:** 🟡 Medium · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD

- **A.** Fetal breathing movements
- **B.** Fetal tone
- **C.** Amniotic fluid volume
- **D.** NST accelerations

<details><summary>✅ Sahi answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct answer: C. Amniotic fluid volume**

- **A (Incorrect):** Breathing movement short-term fetal behavior assess karta hai. Ye directly urine output nahi dikhata.
- **B (Incorrect):** Tone fetal neurological behavior ka clue hai, fluid production ka direct marker nahi.
- **C (Correct):** Later pregnancy mein fetal urine fluid volume ka major contributor hota hai. Placental insufficiency renal perfusion reduce kar sakti hai, isliye fluid assessment relatively longer-term clue deta hai.
- **D (Incorrect):** NST accelerations current autonomic reactivity assess karti hain. Fluid se complementary information milti hai.
