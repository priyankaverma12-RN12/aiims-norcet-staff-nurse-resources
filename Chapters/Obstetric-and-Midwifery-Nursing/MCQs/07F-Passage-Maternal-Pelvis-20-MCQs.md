### Q01. On a pelvic model, which boundary separates the greater pelvis from the lesser pelvis?

**Difficulty:** 🟢 Easy · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD

- **A.** Ischial tuberosities alone
- **B.** Pelvic brim
- **C.** Sacrococcygeal joint alone
- **D.** Perineal skin

<details><summary>✅ Sahi answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct answer: B. Pelvic brim**

- **A (Incorrect):** Ischial tuberosities outlet ke lateral landmarks hain. Ye akelay greater aur lesser pelvis ke beech ki boundary nahi banate.
- **B (Correct):** Pelvic brim ek bony boundary hai jo false pelvis aur true pelvis ko separate karti hai. True pelvis ka upper opening isi level par hota hai.
- **C (Incorrect):** Sacrococcygeal joint pelvis ke lower posterior region mein hai. Greater aur lesser pelvis ko separate karne wali complete line brim hai.
- **D (Incorrect):** Perineal skin soft tissue ka part hai jo outlet region mein hota hai. Bony pelvic divisions skin se define nahi hote.
