### Q01. Why can pregnancy-related reflux become more noticeable as gestation progresses even if gastric emptying is not consistently delayed?

**Difficulty:** 🟡 Medium · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD

- **A.** The esophageal sphincter becomes permanently sealed
- **B.** Reduced lower esophageal sphincter tone and pressure from the enlarging uterus facilitate reflux
- **C.** The uterus directly produces gastric acid
- **D.** Reflux requires complete cessation of gastric emptying

<details><summary>✅ Sahi answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct answer: B. Reduced lower esophageal sphincter tone and pressure from the enlarging uterus facilitate reflux**

- **A (Incorrect):** Sphincter seal ho jaaye toh swallowing obstruct ho sakti hai. Reflux mein barrier ka tone kam hona relevant hai.
- **B (Correct):** Lower esophageal sphincter food pipe aur stomach ke beech ka muscular valve hai. Iska tone kam hone aur uterus ka pressure badhne se acid wapas upar aa sakta hai.
- **C (Incorrect):** Gastric acid stomach ke cells banate hain. Uterus mechanical pressure contribute karta hai, acid nahi banata.
- **D (Incorrect):** Stomach emptying stop hona reflux ki necessary condition nahi hai. Sphincter relaxation aur mechanical pressure sufficient contributors ho sakte hain.

### Q02. Which statement best distinguishes intestinal transit from gastric emptying in uncomplicated pregnancy?

**Difficulty:** 🔴 Difficult · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD

- **A.** Slow intestinal transit proves gastric outlet obstruction
- **B.** Both processes invariably cease during the third trimester
- **C.** Gastric emptying is always twice as fast at term
- **D.** Intestinal transit may slow, while evidence does not show uniform slowing of gastric emptying throughout pregnancy

<details><summary>✅ Sahi answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct answer: D. Intestinal transit may slow, while evidence does not show uniform slowing of gastric emptying throughout pregnancy**

- **A (Incorrect):** Intestine aur stomach alag regions hain. Slow bowel transit se gastric outlet obstruction diagnose nahi hota.
- **B (Incorrect):** Digestion aur intestinal transit continue rehte hain. Complete cessation serious abnormality hogi.
- **C (Incorrect):** Evidence fixed doubling support nahi karti. Timing, food aur labour-related factors matter karte hain.
- **D (Correct):** Intestinal transit ka matlab food ka intestine se aage badhna hai. Yeh slow ho sakta hai, lekin healthy pregnancy mein gastric emptying har trimester mein consistently slow prove nahi hui hai.

- **B (Incorrect):** Excess saliva ke multiple causes hain. Fever, swelling aur pain jaise findings infection assessment mein help karte hain.
- **C (Incorrect):** Saliva amount se circulating fluid status confirm nahi hota. Intake aur hydration separately assess karte hain.
