### Q01. A pregnant woman develops symmetrical brown patches over the cheeks, forehead and upper lip. Which finding is most consistent?

**Difficulty:** 🟡 Medium · **Priority:** 🔴 MOST IMPORTANT

- **A.** Palmar erythema
- **B.** Linea nigra
- **C.** Melasma
- **D.** Striae gravidarum

<details><summary>✅ Sahi answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct answer: C. Melasma**

- **A (Incorrect):** Palmar erythema palms ki redness hai, facial brown pigmentation nahi.

- **C (Correct):** Pregnancy-related pigmentation face par symmetrical brown patches bana sakti hai, jise melasma ya chloasma bolte hain.
- **D (Incorrect):** Striae dermal stretching se linear marks hote hain, diffuse facial patches nahi.

### Q03. Why do striae gravidarum commonly develop on the expanding abdomen and breasts?

- **B.** Dermal connective tissue changes from mechanical stretching and hormonal influences
- **C.** Loss of all epidermal cells at once
- **D.** Obstruction of every superficial artery

<details><summary>✅ Sahi answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

- **C (Incorrect):** Striae mein complete epidermal destruction nahi hota.
