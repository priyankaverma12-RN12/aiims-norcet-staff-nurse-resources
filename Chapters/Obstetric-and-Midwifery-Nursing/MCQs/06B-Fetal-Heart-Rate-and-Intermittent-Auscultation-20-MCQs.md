**Difficulty:** 🟢 Easy · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD

- **C.** 160–190 beats/min
- **D.** 120–180 beats/min

<details><summary>✅ Sahi answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct answer: B. 110–160 beats/min**

- **A (Incorrect):** Is range ka bada hissa usual lower limit se neeche hai. Persistent low rate ko context ke saath evaluate karna padta hai.
- **B (Correct):** Ye usual baseline range hai. Baaki features aur clinical risks bhi assess karne chahiye, kyunki sirf normal rate se fetal wellbeing prove nahi hoti.
- **C (Incorrect):** Is range ke higher rates fetal tachycardia ka concern raise karte hain. Inko normal range nahi maana jata.
