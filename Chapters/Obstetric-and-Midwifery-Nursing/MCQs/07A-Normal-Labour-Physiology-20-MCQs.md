### Q01. Which endocrine mechanism explains how human labour can begin despite sustained circulating progesterone?

**Difficulty:** 🟡 Medium · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD

- **A.** Functional progesterone withdrawal
- **B.** Total disappearance of progesterone
- **C.** Permanent estrogen suppression
- **D.** Complete loss of oxytocin receptors

<details><summary>✅ Sahi answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct answer: A. Functional progesterone withdrawal**

- **A (Correct):** Term par receptor aur local signalling change se progesterone ka uterus-relaxing effect kam ho sakta hai, even if circulating level high hai. Isi ko functional withdrawal kehte hain.
- **B (Incorrect):** Human placenta progesterone produce karta rehta hai. Blood level ka completely disappear hona normal labour onset ke liye necessary nahi hai.
- **C (Incorrect):** Estrogen-associated signalling uterine activation ko support kar sakti hai. Permanent suppression is physiological preparation ka explanation nahi hai.
- **D (Incorrect):** Oxytocin responsiveness labour readiness mein contribute karti hai. Sab receptors lose hone se responsiveness kam hogi, coordinated activation explain nahi hogi.
