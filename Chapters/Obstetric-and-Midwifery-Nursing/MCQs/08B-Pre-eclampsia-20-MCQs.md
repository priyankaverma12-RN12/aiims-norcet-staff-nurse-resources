### Q01. In the classic placental model of pre-eclampsia, which abnormal change limits the normal increase in uteroplacental perfusion?

**Difficulty:** 🟡 Medium · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD · Original practice

- **A.** Excessive dilation of the maternal spiral arteries
- **B.** Incomplete remodeling of the maternal spiral arteries
- **C.** Increased capacity of the intervillous space alone
- **D.** Normal conversion of spiral arteries into low-resistance vessels

<details>
<summary>✅ Answer aur detailed explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct answer: B. Incomplete remodeling of the maternal spiral arteries**

- **A (Incorrect):** Normal spiral arteries widen aur low resistance wali vessels banti hain. Excessive dilation ko classic placental mechanism ka main defect nahi maana jata.
- **B (Correct):** Early pregnancy mein trophoblast cells maternal spiral arteries ko remodel karte hain. Jab ye process incomplete rahe, toh vessels relatively narrow aur high resistance wali reh sakti hain. Placenta ko adequate perfusion milne mein dikkat hoti hai.
- **C (Incorrect):** Intervillous space mein maternal blood fetal exchange ke liye aata hai. Uski capacity alone badhne se classic high-resistance placental circulation explain nahi hoti.
- **D (Incorrect):** Low-resistance conversion normal placental adaptation hai. Pre-eclampsia ke classic model mein isi conversion ki kami relevant hoti hai.

**Exam clue:** Normal low-resistance remodeling aur incomplete remodeling ka difference samjho.

### Q02. A teaching chart shows placental release of excess soluble fms-like tyrosine kinase-1 (sFlt-1). Which subsequent effect best explains maternal vascular dysfunction?

**Difficulty:** 🔴 Difficult · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD · Original practice

- **A.** Increased availability of free VEGF and PlGF
- **B.** Increased placental production of insulin
- **C.** Sequestration of VEGF and PlGF with impaired endothelial signaling
- **D.** Reduced binding of circulating angiogenic factors

<details>
<summary>✅ Answer aur detailed explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct answer: C. Sequestration of VEGF and PlGF with impaired endothelial signaling**

- **A (Incorrect):** Free VEGF aur PlGF vascular endothelial health ko support karte hain. Excess sFlt-1 in factors ko bind karke unki availability kam kar sakta hai.
- **B (Incorrect):** Insulin glucose regulation ka hormone hai. Is chart mein placental antiangiogenic signaling discuss ho rahi hai, na ki insulin production.
- **C (Correct):** sFlt-1 soluble receptor ki tarah VEGF aur PlGF ko bind kar sakta hai. Isse vascular lining ki normal signaling disturb hoti hai aur maternal endothelial dysfunction mein contribution ho sakta hai.
- **D (Incorrect):** Excess sFlt-1 ki wajah se VEGF aur PlGF ki binding badh sakti hai. Reduced binding us mechanism ka opposite hai.

**Exam clue:** sFlt-1 ko antiangiogenic factor samjho, universal single cause nahi.

### Q03. A patient with pre-eclampsia develops renal protein leakage. Which structural change is characteristic of the renal lesion associated with the disorder?

**Difficulty:** 🟡 Medium · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD · Original practice

- **A.** Glomerular endothelial swelling and injury
- **B.** Isolated obstruction of the ureter by a stone
- **C.** Destruction of renal pelvis by ascending bacteria
- **D.** Physiological widening of the collecting system alone

<details>
<summary>✅ Answer aur detailed explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct answer: A. Glomerular endothelial swelling and injury**

- **A (Correct):** Glomerulus kidney ka microscopic filter hai. Pre-eclampsia mein uski endothelial cells swell aur injured ho sakti hain, jise glomerular endotheliosis kehte hain. Filtering barrier disturb hone se protein urine mein leak ho sakta hai.
- **B (Incorrect):** Ureteric stone urine flow obstruct kar sakta hai aur colicky pain de sakta hai. Ye pre-eclampsia-associated glomerular lesion ka characteristic mechanism nahi hai.
- **C (Incorrect):** Ascending bacterial infection pyelonephritis produce kar sakti hai. Fever aur urinary findings ke context mein evaluate karte hain, lekin ye vascular glomerular lesion nahi hai.
- **D (Incorrect):** Pregnancy mein collecting system physiological dilatation common ho sakti hai. Is normal anatomical change se characteristic endothelial injury explain nahi hoti.

**Exam clue:** Glomerular endotheliosis ko renal vascular injury se connect karo.
