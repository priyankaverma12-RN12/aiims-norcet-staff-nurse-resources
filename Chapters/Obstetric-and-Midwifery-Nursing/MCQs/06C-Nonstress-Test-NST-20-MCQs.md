### Q01. Which physiological response does an antenatal nonstress test primarily assess?

**Difficulty:** 🟢 Easy · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD

- **A.** Placental location relative to the cervix
- **B.** Fetal lung maturity from amniotic fluid
- **C.** Fetal heart rate acceleration associated with fetal activity without deliberately inducing contractions
- **D.** Maternal glucose response to an oral glucose load

<details><summary>✅ Sahi answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct answer: C. Fetal heart rate acceleration associated with fetal activity without deliberately inducing contractions**

- **A (Incorrect):** Placental location ultrasound imaging se determine hoti hai. NST us location ko directly nahi dikhata.
- **B (Incorrect):** Lung maturity ki laboratory assessment alag investigation hai. NST heart rate response evaluate karta hai.
- **C (Correct):** NST mein fetal activity ke saath heart rate ka response observe hota hai. Deliberately contractions induce nahi karte, isliye ise nonstress test kehte hain.
- **D (Incorrect):** Glucose load maternal glucose tolerance assess karta hai. Ye fetal heart rate reactivity ka test nahi hai.

### Q04. A woman becomes pale and dizzy while lying flat during NST. Which immediate positioning action is appropriate?

**Difficulty:** 🟡 Medium · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD

<details><summary>✅ Sahi answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct answer: B. Assist her into a lateral position and assess maternal observations and fetal status**

- **A (Incorrect):** Flat position continue karne se possible aortocaval compression worsen ho sakti hai. Symptoms ignore nahi karne chahiye.

- **C (Incorrect):** Dizzy woman ko unassisted stand karane se fall risk hai. Safe positioning aur assessment pehle chahiye.

### Q20. Which finding illustrates why an antenatal NST and ultrasound growth assessment answer different clinical questions?

**Difficulty:** 🔴 Difficult · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD

- **A.** A reactive NST directly calculates fetal weight
- **B.** A fetus can have reassuring short-term heart-rate reactivity while remaining growth restricted
- **C.** Ultrasound biometry always measures heart-rate acceleration duration
- **D.** A nonreactive NST identifies the exact fetal weight percentile

<details><summary>✅ Sahi answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct answer: B. A fetus can have reassuring short-term heart-rate reactivity while remaining growth restricted**

- **A (Incorrect):** Fetal weight estimate ultrasound measurements se calculate hota hai. NST se direct weight nahi milta.
- **B (Correct):** NST current autonomic heart response evaluate karta hai. Growth restriction fetal size aur growth pattern ka issue hai jo ultrasound aur indicated Doppler se assess hota hai. Dono results ek saath exist kar sakte hain.
- **C (Incorrect):** Biometry fetal body dimensions measure karti hai. Acceleration duration fetal rate recording se evaluate hoti hai.
- **D (Incorrect):** Nonreactivity se exact percentile determine nahi hota. Growth assessment separate data require karta hai.
