### Q01. At 10 weeks of pregnancy, a woman's blood pressure is normal while she takes a prescribed antihypertensive started two years before conception. How should her hypertension history be classified?

**Difficulty:** 🟡 Medium · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD · Original practice

- **A.** Transient gestational hypertension
- **B.** Chronic hypertension with currently controlled blood pressure
- **C.** Resolved gestational hypertension
- **D.** White-coat hypertension established by the current reading

<details>
<summary>✅ Answer aur detailed explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct answer: B. Chronic hypertension with currently controlled blood pressure**

- **A (Incorrect):** Transient gestational hypertension pregnancy ke 20 weeks ya uske baad clinic mein aane wali elevation hai jo repeated readings par resolve hoti hai. Yahan treatment pregnancy se pehle shuru hua tha.
- **B (Correct):** Pregnancy se pehle hypertension diagnosed tha aur ab medicine se BP control mein hai. Isliye chronic hypertension ka history relevant rehta hai. Nurse treatment history aur baseline findings document karti hai.
- **C (Incorrect):** Gestational hypertension ka onset pregnancy ke latter half mein hota hai. Pehle se diagnosed disease ko ek normal reading ke basis par resolved gestational disease nahi bolte.
- **D (Incorrect):** White-coat pattern establish karne ke liye clinic aur outside-clinic readings ka comparison chahiye. Ek normal treated reading is pattern ka evidence nahi hai.

**Exam clue:** Controlled BP ka matlab underlying chronic diagnosis disappear hona nahi hai.
