**Difficulty:** 🟢 Easy · **Priority:** 🔴 MOST IMPORTANT

- **B.** Only during active labor
- **C.** Only after birth
- **D.** Before 24 weeks of gestation

<details><summary>✅ Sahi answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct answer: D. Before 24 weeks of gestation**

- **A (Incorrect):** 37 weeks tak wait karne se early assessment ka benefit miss hota hai.

- **C (Incorrect):** Postnatal imaging antenatal screening nahi hai.
