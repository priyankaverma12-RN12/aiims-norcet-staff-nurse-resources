### Q01. Which feature of Wharton's jelly is most relevant to preserving umbilical blood flow during normal fetal movement?

**Difficulty:** 🟡 Medium · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD

- **A.** A separate epithelial duct carries fetal urine to the placenta
- **B.** Dense bone matrix fixes the cord in one position
- **C.** A contractile muscle layer pumps maternal blood
- **D.** Hydrated gelatinous connective tissue cushions vessels against mechanical compression

<details><summary>✅ Sahi answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct answer: D. Hydrated gelatinous connective tissue cushions vessels against mechanical compression**

- **A (Incorrect):** Fetal urine amniotic cavity mein excrete hoti hai; jelly urinary duct nahi.
- **B (Incorrect):** Cord flexible connective tissue hai, bony rigid structure nahi.
- **C (Incorrect):** Maternal blood fetal cord vessels mein routinely flow nahi karta; jelly pumping muscle nahi.
- **D (Correct):** Wharton's jelly hydrated extracellular matrix hai, rich in glycosaminoglycans; vessels ko mechanical compression aur bending se protect karne mein help karti hai.
