### Q01. Which force is classified as the primary power of labour?

**Difficulty:** 🟢 Easy · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD

- **A.** Voluntary abdominal bearing down
- **B.** Involuntary myometrial contractions
- **C.** Maternal pelvic bone resistance
- **D.** Fetal head moulding

<details><summary>✅ Sahi answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct answer: B. Involuntary myometrial contractions**

- **A (Incorrect):** Abdominal muscles second stage mein additional force generate kar sakti hain. Isliye bearing down ko secondary power kehte hain.
- **B (Correct):** Uterus ki smooth muscle apne aap rhythmic contractions generate karti hai. Ye primary power cervix par pressure aur fetal descent mein help karti hai.
- **C (Incorrect):** Pelvic bones birth passage ka structural part hain. Resistance force generate karne wali primary power nahi hai.
- **D (Incorrect):** Moulding mein fetal skull bones ek doosre ke relative adjust hoti hain. Ye passenger ka adaptation hai, maternal uterine force nahi.

### Q02. A fully dilated woman bears down with a spontaneous urge during a contraction. Which muscle action contributes the secondary power?

**Difficulty:** 🟡 Medium · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD

- **A.** Contraction of abdominal musculature that raises intra-abdominal pressure
- **B.** Passive cervical collagen remodelling
- **C.** Involuntary contraction of the fetal diaphragm
- **D.** Relaxation of all maternal abdominal muscles

<details><summary>✅ Sahi answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct answer: A. Contraction of abdominal musculature that raises intra-abdominal pressure**

- **A (Correct):** Jab woman bear down karti hai, toh abdominal muscles intra-abdominal pressure badhati hain. Ye force uterine contraction ke saath milkar descent mein help kar sakti hai.
- **B (Incorrect):** Cervical collagen ka remodelling cervix ko compliant banata hai. Ye abdominal expulsion force ka source nahi hai.
- **C (Incorrect):** Fetal diaphragm baby ki respiratory muscle hai. Maternal bearing-down pressure us muscle se generate nahi hota.
- **D (Incorrect):** Abdominal relaxation rest ke waqt useful ho sakti hai. Lekin bearing down ke waqt active abdominal effort secondary force provide karta hai.

### Q03. Why should the uterus relax adequately between labour contractions?

**Difficulty:** 🟡 Medium · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD

- **A.** To ensure the fetal head returns to its original station
- **B.** To interrupt all placental circulation
- **C.** To allow recovery of uteroplacental blood flow between periods of compression
- **D.** To reverse cervical effacement

<details><summary>✅ Sahi answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct answer: C. To allow recovery of uteroplacental blood flow between periods of compression**

- **A (Incorrect):** Relaxation ka purpose fetal descent undo karna nahi hai. Fetal position aur station ko progress ke context mein assess kiya jata hai.
- **B (Incorrect):** Contraction ke dauran uterine blood flow temporarily reduce ho sakta hai. Rest ka purpose perfusion recover hone ka opportunity dena hai, circulation permanently stop karna nahi.
- **C (Correct):** Jab uterus relax karta hai, toh placental circulation ko recover karne ka time milta hai. Bahut kam rest milne par fetal oxygen reserve compromise ho sakta hai.
- **D (Incorrect):** Cervical effacement tissue ke progressive thinning se related hai. Har contraction ke baad usko reverse karna physiological objective nahi hai.

### Q05. Three consecutive 10-minute windows contain 6, 5 and 7 contractions. Under the more-than-five-per-10-minutes averaged-over-30-minutes definition, how is the pattern classified?

**Difficulty:** 🔴 Difficult · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD

- **B.** Normal because one window contains five
- **C.** Hypertonus proven by contraction count alone
- **D.** Uninterpretable because the windows are equal

<details><summary>✅ Sahi answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct answer: A. Tachysystole, because the average is six**

- **A (Correct):** Total 6 + 5 + 7 = 18 contractions hain. Three equal windows se divide karne par average six aata hai, jo specified definition ke threshold se above hai.
- **B (Incorrect):** Ek window mein five hone se complete average normal nahi ho jata. Puri 30-minute activity ko consider karna hai.
- **C (Incorrect):** Hypertonus resting tone se related hai. Sirf contraction count se elevated baseline pressure prove nahi kar sakte.
- **D (Incorrect):** Equal observation windows calculation ko straightforward banati hain. Fetal response aur clinical condition separately assess karna zaroori hai.
