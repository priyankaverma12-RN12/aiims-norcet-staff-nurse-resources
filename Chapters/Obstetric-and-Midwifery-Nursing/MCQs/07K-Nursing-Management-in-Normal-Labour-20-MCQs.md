### Q01. Before a planned uncomplicated vaginal birth, the neonatal resuscitation bag is present but its function has not been checked. What should the assigned nurse do?

**Difficulty:** 🟡 Medium · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD · Original practice

- **A.** Leave testing until ventilation is required
- **B.** Check equipment readiness and arrange skilled assistance before birth
- **C.** Replace equipment checking with a normal fetal heart rate entry
- **D.** Move all newborn equipment away from the birth area

<details>
<summary>✅ Answer aur detailed explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct answer: B. Check equipment readiness and arrange skilled assistance before birth**

- **A (Incorrect):** Baby ko ventilation ki need ho toh us waqt equipment test karne se emergency care delay ho sakti hai. Readiness pehle check karo.
- **B (Correct):** Normal labour mein bhi baby ko unexpected breathing support chahiye ho sakta hai. Nurse working bag, suitable mask aur trained help ki availability birth se pehle confirm karti hai.
- **C (Incorrect):** Normal fetal heart rate fetus ki current condition ka clue deta hai. Isse ventilation bag ka function prove nahi hota.
- **D (Incorrect):** Equipment ko door rakhne se emergency mein use karne mein delay ho sakta hai. Newborn care area accessible aur ready hona chahiye.

**Exam clue:** Equipment readiness birth se pehle confirm karo.

### Q02. A nurse removes gloves after handling contaminated maternity linen and is about to prepare a clean delivery tray. Which action prevents transfer of organisms?

**Difficulty:** 🟡 Medium · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD · Original practice

- **D.** Put clean instruments on the contaminated linen

<details>
<summary>✅ Answer aur detailed explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct answer: C. Perform hand hygiene and use appropriate fresh protective equipment**

- **A (Incorrect):** Intact gloves par bhi organisms ho sakte hain. Dirty task ke gloves clean task mein use karne se contamination transfer ho sakti hai.

- **D (Incorrect):** Contaminated linen par clean instruments rakhne se organisms instrument tak pahunch sakte hain. Clean aur dirty supplies separate rakho.

**Exam clue:** Gloves hand hygiene ka substitute nahi hain.

### Q20. A healthy term newborn is warm and stable after delivery. The family requests a bath within the first hour. Which response reflects WHO postnatal guidance?

**Difficulty:** 🟢 Easy · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD · Original practice

- **A.** Explain that the first bath should be delayed for at least 24 hours
- **B.** Bathe immediately to improve thermoregulation
- **C.** Bathe before skin-to-skin contact to make breastfeeding safe
- **D.** Use a cold bath whenever the temperature is normal

<details>
<summary>✅ Answer aur detailed explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct answer: A. Explain that the first bath should be delayed for at least 24 hours**

- **A (Correct):** WHO healthy term newborn ka first bath at least 24 hours delay recommend karta hai. Isse heat loss kam karne aur early mother-baby contact protect karne mein help milti hai.
- **B (Incorrect):** Immediate bath se wet skin ke through heat loss badh sakta hai. Warm stable newborn ko bhi thermal protection chahiye.
- **C (Incorrect):** Breastfeeding ya skin-to-skin se pehle bath mandatory nahi hai. Drying aur appropriate hygiene ke saath early contact support karo.
- **D (Incorrect):** Cold bath normal-temperature newborn ki routine care nahi hai. Newborn ki temperature regulation immature hoti hai, isliye unnecessary cooling avoid karo.

**Exam clue:** Healthy term newborn mein first bath delay karna thermal care ka part hai.
