### Q01. Abdominal palpation identifies the fetal head near the maternal left flank and buttocks near the right flank. Which relationship is most likely?

**Difficulty:** 🟡 Medium · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD

- **A.** Longitudinal lie
- **B.** Complete flexion
- **C.** Transverse lie
- **D.** Occiput anterior position

<details><summary>✅ Sahi answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct answer: C. Transverse lie**

- **A (Incorrect):** Longitudinal lie mein baby ka long axis mother ke long axis ke parallel hota hai. Head aur buttocks opposite flanks mein hona us pattern se match nahi karta.
- **B (Incorrect):** Flexion fetal body parts ke aapas ke relationship ko batati hai. Head aur buttocks ki maternal abdomen mein location se flexion directly establish nahi hoti.
- **C (Correct):** Baby ka long axis mother ke long axis ke across hai. Isliye transverse lie suspect hoti hai aur ultrasound se presentation confirm karna chahiye.
- **D (Incorrect):** Occiput anterior position vertex ke denominator ki direction batati hai. Sirf fetal poles ki location se occiput ki direction establish nahi hoti.

### Q05. Why does good flexion of a vertex-presenting fetal head usually favour passage through the pelvis?

**Difficulty:** 🟡 Medium · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD

- **B.** It presents a smaller anteroposterior head diameter

- **D.** It permanently enlarges the maternal pelvic inlet

<details><summary>✅ Sahi answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct answer: B. It presents a smaller anteroposterior head diameter**

- **A (Incorrect):** Flexion se biparietal transverse diameter disappear nahi hota. Skull ke dimensions aur maternal pelvis dono delivery mechanics mein relevant rehte hain.

- **C (Incorrect):** Skull sutures aur fontanelles limited moulding allow karte hain. Flexion skull ko rigid banane ka process nahi hai.
- **D (Incorrect):** Flexion baby ke head ki attitude change karti hai. Isse maternal bony inlet permanently enlarge nahi hota.

- **C.** Repeat forceful palpation until a definite answer is obtained
- **D.** Choose the delivery route solely from estimated fetal weight

<details><summary>✅ Sahi answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct answer: B. Arrange ultrasound confirmation and clinical review**

- **A (Incorrect):** Uncertain findings ko certain cephalic presentation record karna unsafe hai. Documentation mein uncertainty clearly mention karni chahiye.
- **B (Correct):** Maternal body habitus aur examiner experience palpation ki accuracy influence karte hain. Uncertain presentation mein ultrasound confirmation aur obstetric review safer approach hai.
- **C (Incorrect):** Forceful palpation discomfort aur harm kar sakti hai aur diagnostic certainty guarantee nahi karti. Gentle trained examination aur imaging appropriate hain.
- **D (Incorrect):** Estimated weight bhi error-prone hai. Delivery route presentation, maternal-fetal condition aur full obstetric evaluation par depend karta hai.
