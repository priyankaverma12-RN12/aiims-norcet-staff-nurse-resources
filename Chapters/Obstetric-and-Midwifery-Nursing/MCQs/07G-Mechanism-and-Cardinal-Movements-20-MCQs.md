### Q01. Why are the cardinal movements described as fetal adaptations rather than seven independent voluntary actions?

**Difficulty:** 🟡 Medium · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD

- **A.** They are voluntary fetal exercises that begin on command
- **B.** They reflect changing relationships between the fetus, maternal pelvic geometry and expulsive forces
- **C.** They occur only after the entire infant is born
- **D.** They are seven separate uterine contraction frequencies

<details><summary>✅ Sahi answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct answer: B. They reflect changing relationships between the fetus, maternal pelvic geometry and expulsive forces**

- **A (Incorrect):** Fetus ko delivery ke liye voluntary exercise karne ki instruction nahi di jati. Uterine force aur pelvic resistance se fetal alignment change hota hai.
- **B (Correct):** Baby jaise hi curved birth canal mein move karta hai, toh head aur shoulders available space ke according adapt karte hain. Isliye movements ek connected mechanical process hain.
- **C (Incorrect):** Head aur body birth se pehle aur delivery ke dauran ye adaptations karte hain. Entire infant birth ke baad cardinal movements complete ho chuke hote hain.
- **D (Incorrect):** Contraction frequency maternal uterine activity ki measurement hai. Cardinal movements fetal position aur orientation ke changes describe karte hain.

### Q02. A nulliparous woman has an engaged fetal head at a routine term visit but no regular painful contractions. What should the nurse explain about the mechanism of labour?

**Difficulty:** 🟡 Medium · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD

- **A.** Engagement can precede established labour and does not alone establish its onset
- **B.** Engagement means the cervix must already be fully dilated
- **C.** The head must already have completed extension
- **D.** The infant will invariably be born within one hour

<details><summary>✅ Sahi answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct answer: A. Engagement can precede established labour and does not alone establish its onset**

- **A (Correct):** Head pelvic inlet mein enter ho sakta hai before labour begins, especially first pregnancy mein. Labour onset ko contraction pattern aur clinical cervical progression ke context mein assess karte hain.
- **B (Incorrect):** Engagement aur cervical dilatation separate findings hain. Bony head pelvis mein enter kar sakta hai jab cervix abhi fully dilated na ho.
- **C (Incorrect):** Extension outlet ke paas head delivery se related movement hai. Inlet engagement se extension complete hone ka conclusion nahi nikalta.
- **D (Incorrect):** Ek engagement finding exact birth timing predict nahi karti. Woman ko appropriate assessment aur individualized return advice dena chahiye.

### Q18. After the fetal head is delivered, it retracts tightly against the perineum and the shoulders fail to follow normally. What should the nurse recognize?

**Difficulty:** 🔴 Difficult · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD

- **A.** A reassuring sign that the third stage has started

<details><summary>✅ Sahi answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct answer: B. Possible shoulder dystocia requiring immediate emergency team response**

- **A (Incorrect):** Entire baby abhi born nahi hua, isliye third stage start nahi hua. Head retraction aur shoulder difficulty ko reassuring normal finding nahi bol sakte.
- **B (Correct):** Turtle sign aur shoulder delivery failure shoulder dystocia ka concern raise karte hain. Team ko immediately alert karke unit emergency protocol follow karo aur timing document karo.
