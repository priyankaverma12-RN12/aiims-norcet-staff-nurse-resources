## Part 1 — Routine ANC and assessment

### Q1. Which schedule matches the WHO routine antenatal contact model after the initial first-trimester contact?

**Difficulty:** 🟡 Medium | **Priority:** 🔴 High | **Type:** Original practice

- **A.** 20, 26, 30, 34, 36, 38 and 40 weeks
- **B.** 16, 24, 32 and 40 weeks
- **C.** 28, 32 and 36 weeks only
- **D.** Every day after 20 weeks

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: A. 20, 26, 30, 34, 36, 38 and 40 weeks**

WHO model mein first contact ke baad saat planned contacts hote hain. Isse risk ko jaldi identify karne aur follow-up ka mauka milta hai.

**Baaki options kyun galat hain?**

- **B.** Ye WHO ke eight-contact model ka schedule nahi hai.
- **C.** Sirf teen late contacts se total eight contacts nahi bante.
- **D.** Daily visit routine low-risk schedule nahi hai.

**Exam trick:** WHO ka model 8 contacts hai.

</details>

### Q3. A woman asks whether she should skip all remaining scheduled visits because her first examination was normal. What is the best response?

**Difficulty:** 🟡 Medium | **Priority:** 🔴 High | **Type:** Original practice

- **A.** Replace visits with a home pregnancy test
- **B.** Stop monitoring if fetal movement begins
- **C.** Continue scheduled antenatal follow-up
- **D.** Attend only after labour starts

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: C. Continue scheduled antenatal follow-up**

Pregnancy mein anemia, BP aur growth related problems baad mein bhi develop ho sakti hain. Regular follow-up se inhe detect karne ka chance milta hai.

**Baaki options kyun galat hain?**

- **A.** Home pregnancy test ongoing maternal ya fetal assessment nahi karta.
- **B.** Fetal movement hone ke baad bhi maternal assessment chahiye.
- **D.** Labour tak rukne se treatable problems miss ho sakti hain.

**Exam trick:** Normal booking does not rule out later complications.

</details>

## Part 2 — Booking laboratory screening

- **B.** Haemoglobin concentration
- **C.** Serum amylase
- **D.** Creatine kinase

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: B. Haemoglobin concentration**

Hb oxygen carry karne wale haemoglobin ka concentration batata hai. Low Hb anemia ka clue hai aur cause evaluate karna padta hai.

**Baaki options kyun galat hain?**

- **A.** Bilirubin akela anemia screen nahi karta.
- **C.** Amylase pancreatic problems mein useful ho sakta hai.
- **D.** CK muscle injury se related marker hai.

**Exam trick:** Hb is an anemia screen.

</details>

### Q7. Why is ABO and Rh typing included in early pregnancy assessment?

- **D.** To measure fetal lung maturity

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: C. To identify blood-group information and potential Rh sensitization risk**

Blood group aur Rh status transfusion planning aur Rh-negative mother ki further assessment mein useful hain. Rh-negative result par antibody status aur anti-D indication evaluate karte hain.

**Baaki options kyun galat hain?**

- **A.** GDM ke liye glucose testing hoti hai.
- **B.** Presentation abdominal examination ya ultrasound se assess hoti hai.
- **D.** Lung maturity blood grouping se pata nahi chalti.

**Exam trick:** Rh type informs prevention of sensitization.

</details>

## Part 3 — Prevention and fetal assessment

### Q11. What daily elemental iron and folic acid dose does WHO recommend for routine pregnancy supplementation?

**Difficulty:** 🟡 Easy | **Priority:** 🔴 High | **Type:** Original practice

- **A.** 300 mg iron and 4 mg folic acid for every woman
- **B.** 400 mg folic acid alone
- **C.** 30–60 mg iron and 400 micrograms folic acid
- **D.** 3 mg iron and 40 micrograms folic acid

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: C. 30–60 mg iron and 400 micrograms folic acid**

WHO routine prevention ke liye 30 se 60 mg elemental iron aur 400 micrograms folic acid recommend karta hai. Diagnosed anemia ya special risk ke liye treatment regimen alag ho sakta hai.

**Baaki options kyun galat hain?**

- **A.** Ye universal prophylactic dose nahi hai. Special indications alag hote hain.
- **B.** Folic acid ki unit aur dose galat hain aur iron bhi absent hai.
- **D.** Dono quantities WHO routine dose se kam hain.

**Exam trick:** 400 micrograms = 0.4 mg.

</details>

</details>

## Part 4 — Warning signs and Indian programmes

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: D. Arrange urgent obstetric assessment including blood pressure evaluation**

Severe headache aur visual symptoms hypertensive disorder ka warning sign ho sakte hain. Urgent evaluation se maternal aur fetal risk assess hota hai.

**Baaki options kyun galat hain?**

- **C.** Prescribed treatment independently stop karna safe triage nahi hai.

**Exam trick:** Severe headache plus vision changes is a danger sign.

</details>
