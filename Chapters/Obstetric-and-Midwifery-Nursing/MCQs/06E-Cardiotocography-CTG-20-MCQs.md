### Q01. Which four fetal heart tracing features should be reviewed alongside contraction activity on CTG?

**Difficulty:** 🟡 Medium · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD

- **A.** Cervical dilation, effacement, station and lie
- **B.** Baseline, variability, accelerations and decelerations
- **C.** Placental grade, fetal weight, fluid index and presentation
- **D.** Maternal pulse, temperature, height and weight

<details><summary>✅ Sahi answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct answer: B. Baseline, variability, accelerations and decelerations**

- **A (Incorrect):** Ye obstetric examination findings hain. CTG directly cervix aur fetal station measure nahi karta.
- **B (Correct):** CTG mein baseline rate, uske around fluctuations, temporary rises aur temporary falls assess hote hain. Contractions aur whole clinical picture ke saath inko interpret karte hain.
- **C (Incorrect):** Ye ultrasound aur clinical assessment se milne wali findings hain. CTG ke tracing features alag hote hain.
- **D (Incorrect):** Maternal observations zaroori context hain, lekin fetal tracing ke features ko replace nahi karti.

### Q02. Which CTG segment is used to estimate the baseline fetal heart rate?

**Difficulty:** 🟡 Medium · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD

- **A.** The highest acceleration peak in ten minutes
- **B.** The deepest deceleration in the entire recording
- **C.** The maternal pulse recorded at admission
- **D.** A stable mean over a ten-minute period, excluding accelerations and decelerations

<details><summary>✅ Sahi answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct answer: D. A stable mean over a ten-minute period, excluding accelerations and decelerations**

- **A (Incorrect):** Acceleration peak temporary rise hai. Isko baseline bolne se actual sustained rate falsely high dikhega.
- **B (Incorrect):** Deceleration nadir temporary lowest point hai. Ye average stable fetal rate nahi hai.
- **C (Incorrect):** Maternal pulse separate signal hai. Fetal baseline ke liye fetal tracing identify karna chahiye.
- **D (Correct):** Baseline usual rate ka estimate hai jo stable portion se milta hai. Temporary rises aur falls exclude karte hain, taaki sustained trend samajh aaye.

### Q20. Which external CTG limitation matters when a contraction looks small on the printed tracing?

**Difficulty:** 🟡 Medium · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD

- **A.** A small printed wave proves uterine pressure is below a precise mmHg value
- **B.** External tocodynamometry records timing and relative activity but does not reliably measure absolute contraction pressure

<details><summary>✅ Sahi answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct answer: B. External tocodynamometry records timing and relative activity but does not reliably measure absolute contraction pressure**

- **A (Incorrect):** Printed wave height precise intrauterine pressure ka reliable measurement nahi hai. Timing aur clinical assessment use karo.
- **B (Correct):** External sensor abdominal wall changes sense karta hai. Placement aur maternal body factors wave height affect karte hain, isliye absolute pressure reliably infer nahi karte.
- **C (Incorrect):** CTG heart rate aur uterine activity record karta hai, blood pH direct measure nahi karta.
- **D (Incorrect):** Cervical dilation appropriate examination se assess hoti hai. Uterine activity channel dilation measure nahi karta.
