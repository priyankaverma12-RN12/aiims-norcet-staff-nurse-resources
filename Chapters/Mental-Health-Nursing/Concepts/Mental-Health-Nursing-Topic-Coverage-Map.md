## Initial study explanations

### Therapeutic communication

Hallucination mein patient ko aisi awaaz sunai de sakti hai jo bahar actually nahi hai. Nurse patient ki feeling respect karti hai aur safety assess karti hai, lekin awaaz ko real confirm nahi karti.

### Suicide safety

Jab patient immediate plan batata hai, toh safety priority ban jaati hai. Nurse patient ko safe environment mein rakhti hai aur urgent mental health team assessment arrange karti hai.

### Psychotropic drugs

Antipsychotic medicine se rare severe reaction ho sakti hai. Fever, muscle rigidity aur confusion neuroleptic malignant syndrome ke clues hain. Emergency medical assessment chahiye.

### Substance withdrawal

Alcohol ke lambe use ke baad body uske effect ki aadat bana leti hai. Achanak stop karne par nervous system overactive ho sakta hai. Severe withdrawal seizures aur delirium tak ja sakti hai, isliye urgent protocol chahiye.
