### Q1. A patient reports feeling cheerful but looks tearful during the interview. Which entry best separates the two findings?

**Difficulty:** 🟡 Medium · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD · Original nursing exam practice

- **A.** Both mood and affect cheerful
- **B.** Mood tearful, affect cheerful
- **C.** Mood cheerful by report, affect tearful
- **D.** Neither can be assessed

<details><summary>✅ Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct answer: C. Mood cheerful by report, affect tearful**

**Kyun sahi hai?** Mood patient ki batayi hui feeling hai aur affect emotion ka observed expression hai. Dono findings ko alag likhne se team ko mismatch samajh aata hai.

**Baaki options kyun galat hain?**

- **A.** Patient ki report cheerful hai lekin expression tearful hai, isliye dono ko cheerful nahi likhna chahiye.
- **B.** Ismein reported feeling aur observed expression ulat diye gaye hain.
- **D.** Nurse ko patient ki report aur apni observation dono mil chuki hain.

**Exam clue:** Mood ko patient batata hai aur affect ko nurse observe karti hai.
