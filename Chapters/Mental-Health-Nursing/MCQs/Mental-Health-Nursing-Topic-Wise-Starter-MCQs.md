## Therapeutic communication

### Q1. A patient says that voices are threatening them. Which nursing response is most therapeutic?

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Topic:** Therapeutic communication

- **A.** Acknowledge the fear and assess what the voices are saying and immediate safety
- **B.** Agree that the threatening people are definitely present
- **C.** Mock the patient for hearing voices
- **D.** Tell the patient to stop talking about the experience

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: A. Acknowledge the fear and assess what the voices are saying and immediate safety**

Hallucination mein patient ko aisi awaaz sunai de sakti hai jo bahar actually nahi hai. Nurse patient ki feeling respect karti hai aur safety assess karti hai, lekin awaaz ko real confirm nahi karti.

**Baaki options kyun galat hain?**

- **B.** Delusion ya hallucination ko reinforce karna helpful nahi hai.
- **C.** Mocking trust ko damage karti hai.
- **D.** Conversation rokne se safety information miss ho sakti hai.

</details>

## Suicide safety

### Q2. A patient with severe depression states an intention to die and describes an immediate plan. Which nursing action is appropriate?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Topic:** Suicide safety

- **A.** Promise to keep the plan secret from the care team
- **B.** Leave the patient alone to reflect
- **C.** Delay assessment until the next routine appointment
- **D.** Ensure immediate safety, stay with the patient as appropriate and activate urgent risk assessment

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: D. Ensure immediate safety, stay with the patient as appropriate and activate urgent risk assessment**

Jab patient immediate plan batata hai, toh safety priority ban jaati hai. Nurse patient ko safe environment mein rakhti hai aur urgent mental health team assessment arrange karti hai.

**Baaki options kyun galat hain?**

- **A.** Safety risk ko confidential rakhkar team se hide karna unsafe hai.
- **B.** Alone chhodna risk badha sakta hai.
- **C.** Routine appointment ka wait nahi karna chahiye.

</details>

## Psychotropic drugs

### Q3. A patient taking an antipsychotic develops high fever, marked muscle rigidity and altered consciousness. Which emergency should be suspected?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Topic:** Psychotropic drugs

- **A.** Uncomplicated seasonal allergy
- **B.** Expected mild dry mouth
- **C.** Neuroleptic malignant syndrome
- **D.** Normal therapeutic sedation

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: C. Neuroleptic malignant syndrome**

Antipsychotic medicine se rare severe reaction ho sakti hai. Fever, muscle rigidity aur confusion neuroleptic malignant syndrome ke clues hain. Emergency medical assessment chahiye.

**Baaki options kyun galat hain?**

- **A.** Allergy is pattern ko usually explain nahi karti.
- **B.** Dry mouth mild side effect hai, yahan severe signs hain.
- **D.** Normal sedation mein high fever aur severe rigidity expected nahi hain.

</details>

## Substance withdrawal

### Q4. A patient who abruptly stopped heavy alcohol use develops tremor, sweating, agitation and hallucinations. What is the priority?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Topic:** Substance withdrawal

- **A.** Ignore the hallucinations if the patient is awake
- **B.** Urgent assessment for severe alcohol withdrawal and seizure risk
- **C.** Discharge because withdrawal is always harmless
- **D.** Give unsupervised sedatives without assessment

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: B. Urgent assessment for severe alcohol withdrawal and seizure risk**

Alcohol ke lambe use ke baad body uske effect ki aadat bana leti hai. Achanak stop karne par nervous system overactive ho sakta hai. Severe withdrawal seizures aur delirium tak ja sakti hai, isliye urgent protocol chahiye.

**Baaki options kyun galat hain?**

- **A.** Hallucinations severe withdrawal ka sign ho sakti hain.
- **C.** Withdrawal life-threatening ho sakti hai.
- **D.** Sedatives clinical monitoring ke saath deni hoti hain.

</details>
