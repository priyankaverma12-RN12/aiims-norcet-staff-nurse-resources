### Q1. A patient lives alone but regularly receives practical and emotional help from friends. Which conclusion is supported?

**Difficulty:** 🟡 Medium · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD · Original nursing exam practice

- **A.** The patient has no support network
- **B.** The patient is necessarily dependent in self-care
- **C.** Living alone does not establish social isolation
- **D.** The patient has no psychosocial needs

<details><summary>✅ Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct answer: C. Living alone does not establish social isolation**

**Kyun sahi hai?** Living alone ghar ki arrangement batata hai. Friends ki regular help se actual social connections ka evidence milta hai.

**Baaki options kyun galat hain?**

- **A.** Support network absent nahi hai kyunki friends help karte hain.
- **B.** Help milna basic self-care dependency prove nahi karta.
- **D.** Support hone ke bawajood other needs assess karni hoti hain.

**Exam clue:** Living arrangement aur social isolation ko alag rakho.
