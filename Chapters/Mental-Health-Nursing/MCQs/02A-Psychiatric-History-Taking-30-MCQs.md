### Q1. A patient gives a two-month history of insomnia. Which additional detail best establishes the onset?

**Difficulty:** 🟡 Medium · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD · Original nursing exam practice

- **A.** The date or circumstances when sleep first changed
- **B.** The nurse's preferred diagnosis
- **C.** The name of the ward
- **D.** The patient's current room number

<details><summary>✅ Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct answer: A. The date or circumstances when sleep first changed**

**Kyun sahi hai?** Jab nurse pehli baar sleep change hone ka time samajhti hai, toh symptom ki shuruaat aur possible trigger ko timeline mein rakh sakti hai.

**Baaki options kyun galat hain?**

- **B.** Diagnosis pehle choose karne se assessment biased ho sakta hai.
- **C.** Ward ka naam illness ki shuruaat nahi batata.
- **D.** Room number administrative information hai, symptom ka time nahi.

**Exam clue:** Onset ka matlab symptom kab shuru hua.
