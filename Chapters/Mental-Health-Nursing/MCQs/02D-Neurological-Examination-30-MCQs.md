### Q1. Which nerve carries the afferent signal in the pupillary light reflex?

**Difficulty:** 🟢 Easy · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD · Original nursing exam practice

- **A.** Oculomotor nerve (III)
- **B.** Trigeminal nerve (V)
- **C.** Optic nerve (II)
- **D.** Facial nerve (VII)

<details><summary>✅ Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct answer: C. Optic nerve (II)**

**Kyun sahi hai?** Optic nerve light ki sensory information brain tak le jaati hai. Is signal se reflex pathway start hota hai.

**Baaki options kyun galat hain?**

- **A.** Oculomotor nerve pupil constriction ka motor signal le jaati hai, light ka incoming signal nahi.
- **B.** Trigeminal nerve facial sensation aur chewing se related hai.
- **D.** Facial nerve facial expression control karti hai.

**Exam clue:** Afferent signal brain ki taraf aata hai.
