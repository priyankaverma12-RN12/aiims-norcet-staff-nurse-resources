### PYQ1. A patient taking haloperidol has severe inner restlessness and cannot remain seated. Which adverse effect is most likely?

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Topic:** Antipsychotic adverse effects

- **A.** Akathisia
- **B.** Aphasia
- **C.** Anosmia
- **D.** Asterixis

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: A. Akathisia**

Akathisia mein patient ko andar se restlessness hoti hai aur baithna mushkil lagta hai. Antipsychotic medicine se ye side effect ho sakta hai. Nurse medicine review arrange karti hai.

**Baaki options kyun galat hain?**

- **B.** Aphasia language impairment hai.
- **C.** Anosmia smell loss hai.
- **D.** Asterixis flapping movement hai.

</details>
