### 🔵 AIIMS NORCET 8 Prelims 2025 — reported memory-based MMSE item

#### Q1. An older adult obtains an MMSE score of 20/30. No information about education, baseline function or current illness is available. Which interpretation is most defensible for clinical nursing practice?

**Difficulty:** 🔴 Difficult · **Priority:** 🔴 MOST IMPORTANT

- **A.** The result establishes advanced Alzheimer's disease.
- **B.** The result raises concern for cognitive impairment and requires further contextual evaluation.
- **C.** The result proves the patient lacks capacity for every decision.
- **D.** No further evaluation is required because the score is within the maximum of 30.

<details><summary>✅ Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct answer: B. The result raises concern for cognitive impairment and requires further contextual evaluation.**

**Kyun sahi hai?** Score 20/30 commonly used screening thresholds ke neeche hai. Lekin MMSE score ko education, language, current health aur daily functioning ke saath interpret karna zaruri hai. Isliye further assessment chahiye. Sirf score se disease subtype ya universal severity category confirm nahi hoti.

- **A.** Alzheimer's disease ki diagnosis ke liye additional clinical evaluation chahiye. Raw score alone advanced disease establish nahi karta.
- **C.** Decision-making capacity specific decision ke context mein assess ki jaati hai. Cognitive score se automatic global incapacity declare nahi karte.
- **D.** Score ka possible range 0–30 hai. Range ke andar hona normal cognition ka proof nahi hai.

**Exam clue:** Historical exam scoring bands aur current clinical interpretation ko alag samjho.
