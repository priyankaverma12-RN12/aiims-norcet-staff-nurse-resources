### 🔵 NORCET 5 Mains — third-party reported coping outcome

#### Q1. After a rehabilitation programme teaches a patient alternative ways to manage distress, which finding most directly demonstrates that the patient can apply the newly learned coping skill?

**Difficulty:** 🟡 Medium · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD

- **A.** The patient can name the session facilitator.
- **B.** The patient states a general intention to manage stress better.
- **C.** The patient independently uses a rehearsed relaxation technique when a new everyday stressor occurs.
- **D.** The patient attends the education session without demonstrating the technique.

<details><summary>✅ Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct answer: C. The patient independently uses a rehearsed relaxation technique when a new everyday stressor occurs.**

**Kyun sahi hai?** Coping skill ka goal sirf uske baare mein information yaad karna nahi hai. Jab patient real situation mein safe learned technique use karta hai, toh nurse ko skill application ka direct evidence milta hai. Isse poori recovery ya future success guarantee nahi hoti.

- **A.** Facilitator ka naam yaad hona memory aur attendance se related hai. Ye stress manage karne ki ability ka direct evidence nahi hai.
- **B.** Intention positive starting point ho sakta hai, lekin patient ne skill actual situation mein use ki ya nahi, ye abhi pata nahi chala.
- **D.** Session attend karna learning opportunity hai. Bina demonstration ya application ke skill outcome confirm nahi hota.

**Exam clue:** Jab question actual coping skill ke outcome par ho, toh practical use ka evidence dekho.
