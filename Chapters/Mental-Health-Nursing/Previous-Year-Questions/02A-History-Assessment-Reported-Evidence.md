### 🔵 RRB Staff Nurse — 21 July 2019, Shift II, reported Q70

#### Q1. A nurse has recorded a client's past psychiatric admissions and symptom timeline. Which additional assessment systematically describes current emotional expression, thought and cognition?

**Difficulty:** 🟡 Medium · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD

- **A.** Medication dispensing record
- **B.** Mental status examination
- **C.** Family medical pedigree
- **D.** Admission billing summary

<details><summary>✅ Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct answer: B. Mental status examination**

**Kyun sahi hai?** History patient ki past aur recent journey batati hai. MSE mein nurse current mental condition ke important areas systematically assess karti hai. Dono assessments ko combine karke team patient ki current need samajhti hai.

- **A.** Dispensing record se medicines milne ki information mil sakti hai. Ye current thought, emotion aur cognition ka systematic assessment nahi hai.
- **C.** Family pedigree family relationships aur relevant illness pattern dikhata hai. Ye patient ke current mental functions ka examination nahi hai.
- **D.** Billing summary administrative record hai. Isse clinical mental status establish nahi hota.

**Exam clue:** History ko journey aur MSE ko current snapshot samjho.
