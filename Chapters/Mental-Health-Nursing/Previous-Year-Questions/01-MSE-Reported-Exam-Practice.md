### 🔵 NORCET 9 Prelims — retrieved third-party report

#### Q1. During successive unrelated assessment questions, a patient keeps giving the same previous response. Which term describes this pattern?

**Difficulty:** 🟡 Medium · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD

- **A.** Echolalia
- **B.** Perseveration
- **C.** Clang association
- **D.** Word salad

<details><summary>✅ Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct answer: B. Perseveration**

Perseveration mein question ya task change hone ke baad bhi patient apni pehle wali response repeat karta rehta hai. Nurse exact example aur context record karti hai. Ye finding different neurological aur psychiatric conditions mein ho sakti hai, isliye alone diagnosis nahi karti.

- **A.** Echolalia mein patient doosre person ke words repeat karta hai. Yahan patient apni pehli response repeat kar raha hai.
- **C.** Clang association mein words meaning ke bajay sound ya rhyme se connect hote hain. Yahan repetition main clue hai.
- **D.** Word salad mein words ka meaningful connection samajhna mushkil hota hai. Stem mein same response repeat hone ka pattern diya hai.

**Exam clue:** Question change hone ke baad bhi purani response repeat ho toh perseveration socho.

### 🔵 NORCET 3 Shift 2 — retrieved third-party report

#### Q2. The nurse asks a patient to describe a common category for a bicycle and a bus. Which cognitive ability is being assessed?

**Difficulty:** 🟡 Medium · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD

- **A.** Delayed recall
- **B.** Orientation to time
- **C.** Abstraction
- **D.** Speech articulation

<details><summary>✅ Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct answer: C. Abstraction**

Abstraction mein patient alag examples ke common concept ko samajhta hai. Bicycle aur bus different objects hain, lekin dono transport ke means hain. Nurse culturally familiar objects use karti hai aur patient ka exact response record karti hai.

- **A.** Delayed recall mein patient short delay ke baad pehle di hui information yaad karta hai. Yahan nayi category identify karni hai.
- **B.** Orientation to time mein date, month ya year ki awareness assess hoti hai. Transport category time orientation test nahi hai.
- **D.** Articulation speech ki pronunciation hai. Common meaning identify karna pronunciation se alag cognitive skill hai.

**Exam clue:** Common category ya figurative meaning samajhna abstract thinking se related hai.

### 🔵 DSSSB 13 August 2024 — retrieved third-party report

#### Q3. Which assessment primarily belongs to the physical neurological examination rather than the mental status examination?

**Difficulty:** 🟢 Easy · **Priority:** 🟡 IMPORTANT

- **A.** Affect range
- **B.** Thought organization
- **C.** Orientation
- **D.** Pupillary light response

<details><summary>✅ Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct answer: D. Pupillary light response**

Pupillary light response mein nurse dekhti hai ki light stimulus par pupil ka response kaisa hai. Ye physical neurological assessment ka finding hai. MSE aur neurological examination dono patient assessment ke useful parts ho sakte hain.

- **A.** Affect range emotional expression ka range hai, jo MSE mein observe hota hai.
- **B.** Thought organization mein nurse soch ka flow aur connection assess karti hai. Ye MSE ka component hai.
- **C.** Orientation mein person, place, time aur situation ka awareness assess hota hai. Ye cognitive assessment ka part hai.

**Exam clue:** Pupillary reaction physical neurological sign hai, jabki mood, thought aur orientation mental status domains hain.
