### 🔵 NORCET 5 Prelims Shift 2 — retrieved third-party GCS question

#### Q1. While preparing a standard GCS observation chart, a nurse also records pupil reactivity. Which interpretation is correct?

**Difficulty:** 🟡 Medium · **Priority:** 🔴 MOST IMPORTANT

- **A.** Pupil reactivity replaces the verbal component.
- **B.** Pupil reactivity is added as a fourth standard GCS component.
- **C.** Pupil reactivity is a separate neurological observation alongside eye opening, verbal and motor responses.
- **D.** A normal pupil response eliminates the need to record motor response.

<details><summary>✅ Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct answer: C. Pupil reactivity is a separate neurological observation alongside eye opening, verbal and motor responses.**

**Kyun sahi hai?** Standard GCS mein E, V aur M separately record karte hain. Pupillary reactivity bhi neurological observation ke liye useful hai lekin standard three-component GCS mein usko extra points ke roop mein add nahi karte.

- **A.** Verbal response standard GCS ka separate component hai. Pupil response uski replacement nahi hai.
- **B.** Standard GCS mein three components hote hain. Separate GCS-P method ko standard GCS total se confuse nahi karna hai.
- **D.** Pupil response normal hone ke bawajood motor response important information de sakta hai. Dono ko alag record karo.

**Exam clue:** Standard GCS EVM hai. Pupils separate check hote hain.
