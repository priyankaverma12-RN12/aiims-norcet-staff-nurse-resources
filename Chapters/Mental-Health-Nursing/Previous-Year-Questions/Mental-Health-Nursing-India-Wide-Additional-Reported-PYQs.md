### PYQ1. Imipramine belongs to which antidepressant class?

**Difficulty:** Easy/Medium | **Topic:** Antidepressants

- **A.** Tricyclic antidepressants
- **B.** Selective serotonin reuptake inhibitors
- **C.** Monoamine oxidase inhibitors
- **D.** Atypical antipsychotics

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation</summary>

**Correct: A. Tricyclic antidepressants**

Imipramine tricyclic antidepressant hai. Iska anticholinergic aur cardiac adverse effect profile important hai. SSRIs, MAOIs aur atypical antipsychotics separate medicine classes hain.

</details>

### PYQ2. Who is commonly associated with the development of the therapeutic community approach in psychiatry?

**Difficulty:** Easy/Medium | **Topic:** Milieu therapy

- **A.** Virginia Henderson
- **B.** Maxwell Jones
- **C.** Dorothea Orem
- **D.** Sister Callista Roy

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation</summary>

**Correct: B. Maxwell Jones**

Maxwell Jones therapeutic community approach se associated hain, jahan patient participation aur group interactions treatment ka hissa bante hain. Orem self-care theory, Roy adaptation model aur Henderson basic nursing needs se related hain.

</details>
