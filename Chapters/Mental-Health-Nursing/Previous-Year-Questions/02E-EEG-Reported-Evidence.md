### 🔵 OSSSC Nursing Officer 2023 — reported investigation question

#### Q1. A patient with recurrent episodes of impaired awareness is being investigated for possible seizure activity. Which investigation directly records cerebral electrical activity to support the clinical assessment?

**Difficulty:** 🟡 Medium · **Priority:** 🔴 MOST IMPORTANT

- **A.** Cranial CT
- **B.** Electroencephalogram
- **C.** Structural brain MRI
- **D.** Plain skull radiograph

<details><summary>✅ Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct answer: B. Electroencephalogram**

**Kyun sahi hai?** EEG electrical activity record karta hai. Suspected seizure mein electrical recording clinical history ke saath interpret ki ja sakti hai. Normal result alone epilepsy exclude nahi karta.

- **A.** CT brain ki structure aur selected acute abnormalities evaluate kar sakta hai, lekin direct electrical trace record nahi karta.
- **C.** Structural MRI detailed anatomical information de sakta hai, lekin standard EEG ki tarah electrical recording nahi karta.
- **D.** Plain skull radiograph mainly bone structure show karta hai. Ye cerebral electrical activity record nahi karta.

**Exam clue:** Electrical activity ke liye EEG aur structural changes ke liye selected imaging yaad rakho.
