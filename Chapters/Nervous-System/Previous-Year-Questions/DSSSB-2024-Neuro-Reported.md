## 13 August Q2 — parietal lobe (indexed excerpt)

[Source question listing](https://nextnurse.in/pyq/dsssb-nursing-officer/dsssb-nursing-officer-dsssb-13-august-2024)

Which lobe contains the primary somatosensory cortex?

**Difficulty:** Easy

- A. Parietal lobe
- B. Occipital lobe
- C. Temporal lobe
- D. Cerebellum

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation</summary>

**A. Parietal lobe**

Parietal lobe body sensation process karta hai. Occipital vision, temporal auditory processing aur cerebellum coordination se associated hai.

</details>

## 12 August Q57 — pathetic nerve (indexed excerpt)

[Source question listing](https://nextnurse.in/pyq/dsssb-nursing-officer/dsssb-nursing-officer-dsssb-12-august-2024)

Which cranial nerve has historically been called the pathetic nerve?

**Difficulty:** Easy

- A. Trochlear nerve
- B. Optic nerve
- C. Facial nerve
- D. Accessory nerve

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation</summary>

**A. Trochlear nerve**

Trochlear nerve CN IV hai aur superior oblique muscle supply karti hai. Optic vision, facial expression aur accessory shoulder/head movement se related hain.

</details>
