## 20 July Shift 1 Q52 — meninges (indexed excerpt)

[Source question listing](https://nextnurse.in/pyq/rrb-nursing-superintendent/rrb-nursing-superintendent-rrb-nsg-superintendent-20-july-2019-shift-1)

Which collective term describes the membranes surrounding the brain and spinal cord?

**Difficulty:** Easy

- A. Meninges
- B. Pleura
- C. Pericardium
- D. Peritoneum

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation</summary>

**A. Meninges**

Meninges dura, arachnoid aur pia ka collective name hai. Pleura lungs, pericardium heart aur peritoneum abdomen se related hain.

</details>
