## Source Q97 — Seizure nursing care

A child develops a convulsive seizure in a ward. What is the safest immediate supportive action?

A. Force a spoon between the teeth

B. Hold the limbs down

C. Protect the head and support airway positioning when safe

D. Give water by mouth

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation</summary>

**C. Protect the head and support airway positioning when safe.** Nurse injury avoid karti hai aur breathing assess karti hai. A se injury, B se physical harm aur D se aspiration ka risk hai. Seizure time karo aur prolonged event mein emergency protocol activate karo. [Concept](../Concepts/05-Seizures-Headache-Neuro-Infections.md).

</details>

## Source Q103 — Extrapyramidal adverse effect

A patient receiving haloperidol cannot sit still and repeatedly walks around. Which adverse effect is most likely?

A. Akathisia

B. Aphasia

C. Ataxia

D. Amnesia

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation</summary>

**A. Akathisia.** Patient ko restlessness aur move karne ki strong need hoti hai. Aphasia language problem hai. Ataxia coordination problem hai. Amnesia memory loss hai. Medication adverse effect report karo.

</details>
