## 2023 Sangrur Q34 — auditory cortex

[Source question listing](https://nextnurse.in/pyq/pgimer-nursing-officer/pgimer-nursing-officer-pgimer-sangrur-no-2023)

Which cerebral lobe contains the primary auditory cortex?

**Difficulty:** Easy

- A. Temporal lobe
- B. Frontal lobe
- C. Occipital lobe
- D. Cerebellum

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation</summary>

**A. Temporal lobe**

Temporal lobe auditory information process karta hai. Frontal planning/motor function se, occipital vision se aur cerebellum coordination se associated hai.

</details>

## 2025 Q26 — ventilation and ICP

[Source question listing](https://nextnurse.in/pyq/pgimer-nursing-officer/pgimer-nursing-officer-pgimer-nursing-officer-2025)

Which physiological change explains a temporary fall in ICP during controlled rescue hyperventilation?

**Difficulty:** Medium

- A. Cerebral vasoconstriction from reduced PaCO2
- B. Cerebral vasodilation from elevated PaCO2
- C. Increased CSF secretion
- D. Increased venous congestion

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation</summary>

**A. Cerebral vasoconstriction from reduced PaCO2**

PaCO2 girne par cerebral vessels constrict ho sakti hain. Isse cerebral blood volume aur ICP temporary kam ho sakte hain. Elevated CO2 vasodilation kar sakti hai, CSF secretion aur venous congestion desired mechanism nahi hain. Routine prophylactic hyperventilation avoid hoti hai kyunki ischemia risk hai. Rescue use emergency specialist protocol se hota hai.

</details>

## 2025 Q72 — secondary stroke prevention

[Source question listing](https://nextnurse.in/pyq/pgimer-nursing-officer/pgimer-nursing-officer-pgimer-nursing-officer-2025)

Which medication class is commonly used for secondary prevention of noncardioembolic ischemic stroke when appropriate?

**Difficulty:** Easy

- A. Antiplatelet drugs
- B. Antifungal drugs
- C. Antihistamines
- D. Antacids

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation</summary>

**A. Antiplatelet drugs**

Antiplatelet drugs platelet aggregation kam karti hain. Other choices clot prevention ka standard mechanism nahi hain. Cardioembolic stroke mein anticoagulation as indicated alag decision hai.

</details>
