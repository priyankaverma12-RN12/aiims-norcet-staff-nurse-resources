## Source Q35 — Autonomic response

Which cardiovascular response is expected during sympathetic activation?

A. Slower heart rate

B. Faster heart rate

C. No heart activity

D. Complete AV block

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation</summary>

**B. Faster heart rate.** Sympathetic activation heart ko faster beat karne ka signal deti hai. A parasympathetic effect ka clue hai. C aur D normal sympathetic response nahi hain. [Concept](../Concepts/02-Spinal-Cord-Cranial-Nerves-ANS.md).

</details>

## Source Q50 — GCS lower limit

What is the lowest possible total when all three Glasgow Coma Scale components are testable?

A. 0

B. 1

C. 3

D. 6

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation</summary>

**C. 3.** Eye, verbal aur motor mein minimum 1 point each hai. A aur B teen components ka sum nahi hain. D possible score hai par minimum nahi. [Concept](../Concepts/03-Neuro-Assessment-GCS-Diagnostics.md).

</details>
