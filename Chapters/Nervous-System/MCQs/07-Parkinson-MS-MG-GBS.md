## Parkinson disease

### Q1. A hospitalized patient with Parkinson disease becomes markedly rigid before a delayed levodopa dose. What nursing practice is most important?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Exam focus:** NORCET / Staff Nurse / CHO-relevant practice | **Topic:** Parkinson disease

- **A.** Administer time-critical medication according to the individualized schedule
- **B.** Combine all doses at bedtime
- **C.** Withhold every dose when mobility declines
- **D.** Replace prescribed levodopa with a sedative

<details><summary>✅ Sahi answer aur detailed explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct answer: A. Administer time-critical medication according to the individualized schedule**

**Kyun sahi hai:** Levodopa effect wear off hone par rigidity aur swallowing worsen ho sakte hain. Individual timing maintain karna important hai.

**Baaki options kyun galat hain?**

- **B.** Motor control throughout day affect ho sakta hai.
- **C.** Delay symptoms worsen kar sakta hai.

**Exam trick:** Parkinson medication is time-critical.

### Q2. Why is carbidopa combined with levodopa?

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Exam focus:** NORCET / Staff Nurse / CHO-relevant practice | **Topic:** Parkinson disease

- **A.** It prevents all dyskinesias
- **B.** It reduces peripheral conversion of levodopa
- **C.** It directly replaces all central dopamine neurons
- **D.** It blocks dopamine receptors in the brain

<details><summary>✅ Sahi answer aur detailed explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct answer: B. It reduces peripheral conversion of levodopa**

**Kyun sahi hai:** Carbidopa peripheral decarboxylation inhibit karti hai. Zyada levodopa brain tak pahunch sakti hai aur peripheral adverse effects kam ho sakte hain.

**Baaki options kyun galat hain?**

- **A.** Motor complications still possible hain.
- **C.** Lost neurons regenerate nahi hote.
- **D.** Ye levodopa benefit oppose karega.

**Exam trick:** Carbidopa protects levodopa outside brain.

## Multiple sclerosis

### Q4. A patient with MS experiences transient worsening of vision after a hot bath. Which phenomenon is most consistent?

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Exam focus:** NORCET / Staff Nurse / CHO-relevant practice | **Topic:** Multiple sclerosis

- **A.** Spinal shock
- **B.** Tardive dyskinesia
- **C.** Uhthoff phenomenon
- **D.** Cushing response

<details><summary>✅ Sahi answer aur detailed explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct answer: C. Uhthoff phenomenon**

**Kyun sahi hai:** Heat demyelinated fibers mein conduction temporarily worsen kar sakti hai. Cooling se symptoms improve ho sakte hain.

**Baaki options kyun galat hain?**

- **A.** Ye acute cord injury ke baad reflex loss hai.
- **B.** Ye drug-associated involuntary movement hai.
- **D.** Ye raised ICP ka late pattern hai.

**Exam trick:** Heat can worsen MS symptoms.

## Guillain-Barré syndrome

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Exam focus:** NORCET / Staff Nurse / CHO-relevant practice | **Topic:** Guillain-Barré syndrome

- **B.** Migraine with aura
- **C.** Guillain-Barré syndrome
- **D.** Parkinson disease

<details><summary>✅ Sahi answer aur detailed explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct answer: C. Guillain-Barré syndrome**

**Kyun sahi hai:** Infection ke baad immune response peripheral nerves damage kar sakta hai. Ascending weakness aur reduced reflexes GBS clue hain.

**Baaki options kyun galat hain?**

- **A.** MS CNS disorder hai aur UMN signs ho sakte hain.
- **B.** Ye episodic neurological symptoms de sakta hai, progressive areflexia typical nahi hai.
- **D.** Ye slow movement aur rigidity deta hai.
