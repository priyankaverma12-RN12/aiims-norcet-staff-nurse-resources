## ICP

### Q7. A patient has MAP 82 mmHg and ICP 24 mmHg. What is the cerebral perfusion pressure?

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Exam focus:** NORCET / Staff Nurse / CHO-relevant practice | **Topic:** ICP

- **A.** 106 mmHg
- **B.** 24 mmHg
- **C.** 82 mmHg
- **D.** 58 mmHg

<details><summary>✅ Sahi answer aur detailed explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct answer: D. 58 mmHg**

**Kyun sahi hai:** CPP = MAP minus ICP. 82 minus 24 = 58 mmHg. Perfusion concern ko clinical target ke according escalate karo.

**Baaki options kyun galat hain?**

- **A.** Ismein values add ki gayi hain.
- **B.** Ye ICP alone hai.
- **C.** Ye MAP alone hai.

**Exam trick:** CPP = MAP − ICP.
