## Tumors

### Q1. A patient with a brain tumor receives prescribed dexamethasone. What is the intended neurological effect in selected cases?

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Exam focus:** NORCET / Staff Nurse / CHO-relevant practice | **Topic:** Tumors

- **A.** Dissolve all tumor tissue immediately
- **B.** Increase CSF production
- **C.** Repair peripheral nerve myelin
- **D.** Reduce tumor-associated vasogenic edema

<details><summary>✅ Sahi answer aur detailed explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct answer: D. Reduce tumor-associated vasogenic edema**

**Kyun sahi hai:** Tumor ke around leaky vessels edema kar sakti hain. Dexamethasone selected vasogenic edema reduce karne mein help karta hai.

**Baaki options kyun galat hain?**

- **A.** Steroid tumor cure nahi karta.
- **B.** Ye therapeutic goal nahi hai.
- **C.** Ye CNS tumor edema indication se unrelated hai.

**Exam trick:** Steroid for selected tumor edema.
