## Spinal cord

### Q1. A dorsal spinal nerve root is damaged. Which function is most directly disrupted?

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Exam focus:** NORCET / Staff Nurse / CHO-relevant practice | **Topic:** Spinal cord

- **A.** Sensory input to the spinal cord
- **B.** Motor output through ventral roots
- **C.** CSF production
- **D.** Pituitary hormone secretion

<details><summary>✅ Sahi answer aur detailed explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct answer: A. Sensory input to the spinal cord**

**Kyun sahi hai:** Dorsal root body se sensory messages cord tak laati hai. Ventral root motor signal bahar le jaati hai.

**Baaki options kyun galat hain?**

- **B.** Ye ventral roots ka kaam hai.
- **C.** Ye choroid plexus se related hai.
- **D.** Ye endocrine control se related hai.

**Exam trick:** Dorsal sensory, ventral motor.

### Q3. Which finding most strongly supports an established upper motor neuron lesion?

- **C.** Ye LMN damage se zyada related hain.
- **D.** Isse UMN damage prove nahi hota.

### Q15. Why is the L4–L5 interspace commonly suitable for adult lumbar puncture?

**Baaki options kyun galat hain?**

- **A.** Lumbar cistern mein CSF hota hai.
- **B.** Ventricles brain mein hote hain.
