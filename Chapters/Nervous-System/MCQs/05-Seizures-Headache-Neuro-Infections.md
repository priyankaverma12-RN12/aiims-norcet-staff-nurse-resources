## Seizures

### Q1. A patient has a convulsive seizure lasting six minutes. What is the priority?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Exam focus:** NORCET / Staff Nurse / CHO-relevant practice | **Topic:** Seizures

- **A.** Hold both arms tightly
- **B.** Activate the status epilepticus emergency pathway
- **C.** Wait until 30 minutes before responding
- **D.** Give an oral tablet during jerking

<details><summary>✅ Sahi answer aur detailed explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct answer: B. Activate the status epilepticus emergency pathway**

**Kyun sahi hai:** Convulsion 5 minutes ya zyada ho toh spontaneous termination less likely ho sakti hai. ABC, glucose aur ordered rescue medicine ke liye urgent response chahiye.

**Baaki options kyun galat hain?**

- **A.** Restraint injury kar sakta hai.
- **C.** Five minutes par prolonged convulsion emergency hai.
- **D.** Aspiration ka risk hai.

**Exam trick:** Convulsive status threshold 5 minutes.
