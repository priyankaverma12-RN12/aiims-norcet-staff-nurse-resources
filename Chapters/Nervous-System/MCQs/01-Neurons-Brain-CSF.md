## Neuron and neuroglia

### Q1. Which cell forms myelin around an axon in the peripheral nervous system?

**Difficulty:** Easy | **Exam focus:** NORCET / Staff Nurse / CHO-relevant practice | **Topic:** Neuron and neuroglia

- **A.** Schwann cell
- **B.** Oligodendrocyte
- **C.** Microglial cell
- **D.** Astrocyte

<details><summary>✅ Sahi answer aur detailed explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct answer: A. Schwann cell**

**Kyun sahi hai:** Schwann cell peripheral axon ke around insulating layer banati hai. Isse signal tezi se travel karta hai.

**Baaki options kyun galat hain?**

- **B.** Ye CNS myelin banati hai.
- **C.** Ye immune defence karti hai.
- **D.** Ye metabolic support aur barrier maintenance mein help karti hai.

**Exam trick:** PNS = Schwann.

**Baaki options kyun galat hain?**

- **A.** Ye membrane traffic ka process hai.
- **C.** Ye mainly potassium exit se hoti hai.

### Q3. Which change explains slower conduction after a central axon loses myelin?

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Exam focus:** NORCET / Staff Nurse / CHO-relevant practice | **Topic:** Neuron and neuroglia

- **A.** Increased axon diameter in every fiber
- **B.** Increased neurotransmitter storage
- **C.** Impaired saltatory conduction
- **D.** Increased insulation

<details><summary>✅ Sahi answer aur detailed explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct answer: C. Impaired saltatory conduction**

**Kyun sahi hai:** Normally signal nodes ke beech jump karta hai. Myelin damage hone par current leak aur conduction delay ho sakta hai.

**Baaki options kyun galat hain?**

- **A.** Demyelination diameter badhne ka rule nahi hai.
- **B.** Vesicle storage is mechanism ka direct cause nahi hai.
- **D.** Myelin loss se insulation kam hoti hai.

**Exam trick:** Myelin speeds conduction.
