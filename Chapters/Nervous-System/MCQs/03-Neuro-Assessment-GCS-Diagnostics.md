## GCS

### Q1. An adult opens the eyes to sound, is confused and localizes pressure. What is the Glasgow Coma Scale total?

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Exam focus:** NORCET / Staff Nurse / CHO-relevant practice | **Topic:** GCS

- **A.** 10
- **B.** 13
- **C.** 15
- **D.** 12

<details><summary>✅ Sahi answer aur detailed explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct answer: D. 12**

**Kyun sahi hai:** Eye 3, verbal 4 aur motor 5 milkar 12 hote hain. Components separately bhi document karo.

**Baaki options kyun galat hain?**

- **A.** Ye given component scores ka sum nahi hai.
- **B.** Ismein ek point extra add ho gaya hai.
- **C.** Ye fully normal responses ka score hai.

**Exam trick:** E3 V4 M5 = 12.

### Q6. A patient stands steadily with eyes open but sways markedly after closing the eyes. What does this finding suggest?

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Exam focus:** NORCET / Staff Nurse / CHO-relevant practice | **Topic:** Assessment

- **A.** Definite isolated cerebellar disease
- **B.** Normal motor power proves normal balance
- **C.** Complete visual cortex failure
- **D.** Dependence on visual input for balance

<details><summary>✅ Sahi answer aur detailed explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct answer: D. Dependence on visual input for balance**

**Kyun sahi hai:** Romberg testing mein eyes close karne se visual compensation remove hota hai. Sensory ya vestibular impairment unmask ho sakta hai.

**Baaki options kyun galat hain?**

- **A.** Cerebellar ataxia eyes open par bhi ho sakti hai.
- **B.** Balance ke liye sensory aur vestibular input bhi chahiye.
- **C.** Eyes open mein compensation ho raha hai.

**Exam trick:** Romberg is not purely cerebellar.

### Q10. Which CSF pattern most strongly supports typical acute bacterial meningitis?

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Exam focus:** NORCET / Staff Nurse / CHO-relevant practice | **Topic:** Diagnostics

- **A.** High glucose with no cells in all cases
- **B.** Isolated increased red cells with no other changes
- **C.** Neutrophilic pleocytosis, increased protein and low glucose
- **D.** Normal cells with isolated low protein

<details><summary>✅ Sahi answer aur detailed explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct answer: C. Neutrophilic pleocytosis, increased protein and low glucose**

**Kyun sahi hai:** Bacterial inflammation neutrophils attract karti hai. Barrier disruption protein badhata hai aur glucose low ho sakta hai.

**Baaki options kyun galat hain?**

- **A.** CSF glucose blood glucose se linked hai, infection mein often low hota hai.
- **B.** Ye bleeding ya traumatic tap ka clue ho sakta hai.
- **D.** Ye typical bacterial pattern nahi hai.

**Exam trick:** Bacterial CSF = neutrophils, high protein, low glucose.
