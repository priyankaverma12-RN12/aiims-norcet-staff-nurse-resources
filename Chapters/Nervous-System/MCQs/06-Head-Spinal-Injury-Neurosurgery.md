### Q1. A trauma patient has a lens-shaped extra-axial collection on CT. Which hematoma is most characteristic?

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Exam focus:** NORCET / Staff Nurse / CHO-relevant practice | **Topic:** Head injury

- **A.** Subarachnoid hemorrhage
- **B.** Epidural hematoma
- **C.** Subdural hematoma
- **D.** Intraventricular hemorrhage

<details><summary>✅ Sahi answer aur detailed explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct answer: B. Epidural hematoma**

**Kyun sahi hai:** Epidural blood dura aur skull ke beech collect hota hai. Dural attachments ki wajah se biconvex shape mil sakti hai.

**Baaki options kyun galat hain?**

- **A.** Ye sulci aur cisterns mein blood de sakta hai.
- **C.** Ye classically crescent-shaped hota hai.
- **D.** Ye ventricles ke andar hota hai.

**Exam trick:** Epidural = lens.

### Q5. A patient with acute high cervical spinal injury has hypotension and a heart rate of 44/min. Which shock pattern is most suggestive after bleeding is considered?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Exam focus:** NORCET / Staff Nurse / CHO-relevant practice | **Topic:** Spinal injury

- **A.** Neurogenic shock
- **B.** Hypovolemic shock with expected compensatory tachycardia
- **C.** Anaphylactic shock as the only possibility
- **D.** Autonomic dysreflexia

<details><summary>✅ Sahi answer aur detailed explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct answer: A. Neurogenic shock**

**Kyun sahi hai:** Sympathetic tone lose hone se vessels dilate aur heart rate slow ho sakta hai. Urgent hemodynamic support needed hai.

**Baaki options kyun galat hain?**

- **B.** Bleeding exclude karna hai, relative bradycardia neurogenic clue hai.
- **C.** Allergen exposure aur other signs nahi diye hain.
- **D.** Ye typically severe hypertension deta hai.

**Exam trick:** Neurogenic = hypotension plus bradycardia.
