## Neurons, Brain, Meninges and CSF

- Dendrites → cell body → axon
- Myelin → faster conduction
- Ventricles → subarachnoid CSF
- Arachnoid granulations → venous blood

## Spinal cord, Cranial nerves, Reflexes and ANS

- Receptor → sensory nerve
- Spinal cord → interneuron
- Motor nerve → muscle
- Sympathetic: fight or flight

## Neurological assessment, GCS and investigations

- Eye E: 1–4
- Verbal V: 1–5
- Motor M: 1–6
- E + V + M = 3–15 if testable

## Stroke, TIA, Raised ICP and Hydrocephalus

- Sudden deficit → stroke alert
- ABC + glucose + last known well
- CT → stroke type
- Swallow screen before oral intake

[Detail mein padho](../Concepts/04-Stroke-TIA-ICP-Hydrocephalus.md)

## Seizures, Headache, Meningitis and Encephalitis

- Seizure starts → note time
- Protect head, remove hazards
- Side position when safe
- 5 min or repeated without recovery → emergency

[Detail mein padho](../Concepts/05-Seizures-Headache-Neuro-Infections.md)

## Head injury, Spinal cord injury and Neurosurgery

- Spinal shock → reflex loss
- Neurogenic shock → low BP + slow HR
- Autonomic dysreflexia → high BP
- Check bladder trigger and escalate

[Detail mein padho](../Concepts/06-Head-Spinal-Injury-Neurosurgery.md)

## Parkinson disease, Multiple sclerosis, Myasthenia gravis and Guillain-Barré syndrome

- MS → CNS myelin
- GBS → peripheral nerves
- MG → neuromuscular junction
- Parkinson → dopamine loss

[Detail mein padho](../Concepts/07-Parkinson-MS-MG-GBS.md)

## Brain tumors, Dementia, Peripheral disorders and Community nursing

- Community screening → BP + diabetes
- Recognize stroke or seizure
- Urgent referral when indicated
- Rehabilitation + family education

[Detail mein padho](../Concepts/08-Tumors-Dementia-Neuropathy-Community.md)

## Must remember

GCS E4 V5 M6. CPP = MAP minus ICP. Stroke mein oral intake se pehle swallow screen. Seizure mein mouth mein kuch mat daalo. GBS mein respiratory function monitor karo. SCI mein sudden severe hypertension autonomic dysreflexia ho sakta hai.
