### Q01. An adult has irreversible lower-limb ischemia with extensive nonviable tissue despite attempted revascularization. What is a principal therapeutic aim of major amputation in this setting?

**Difficulty:** 🟡 Medium · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD · Original practice

- **A.** Restore blood flow to every removed tissue
- **B.** Remove unsalvageable tissue and permit healing at a viable level
- **C.** Treat only the patient's appearance
- **D.** Avoid assessing perfusion of the remaining limb

<details><summary>✅ Correct answer and detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: B. Remove unsalvageable tissue and permit healing at a viable level**

**A kyun galat hai:** Removed tissue ko revascularize karna amputation ka objective nahi hai. Viable remaining tissue ki perfusion important hai.

**B kyun sahi hai:** Jab tissue irreversible necrosis mein chala jaata hai, limb salvage possible nahi ho sakta. Surgical team viable tissue level par nonviable tissue remove karke healing aur clinical recovery support karti hai.

**C kyun galat hai:** Appearance secondary concern ho sakta hai, lekin ischemic necrosis mein medical aur functional goals priority hain.

**D kyun galat hai:** Remaining limb ki perfusion healing determine karne mein important hai. Is assessment ko avoid nahi karna chahiye.

**Exam clue:** Amputation level decide karte waqt viable tissue aur healing potential consider karo.
