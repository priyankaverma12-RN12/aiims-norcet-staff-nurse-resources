### Q01. A patient twists the ankle. Imaging excludes fracture and the clinician diagnoses a grade II ligament sprain. Which tissue injury is expected?

**Difficulty:** 🟡 Medium · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD · Original practice

- **A.** Stretching with minimal microscopic injury
- **B.** Complete separation of joint surfaces
- **C.** Partial ligament tearing
- **D.** Complete ligament rupture

<details><summary>✅ Correct answer and detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: C. Partial ligament tearing**

**A kyun galat hai:** Grade I mein fibers stretch ya microscopic damage hota hai aur stability generally preserved rehti hai. Grade II mein injury isse greater hoti hai.

**B kyun galat hai:** Complete joint surface separation dislocation kehlata hai. Sprain grading ligament injury ko describe karti hai, articular displacement ko nahi.

**C kyun sahi hai:** Grade II sprain mein ligament ke kuch fibers tear hote hain. Swelling, pain aur functional limitation ho sakti hai, isliye prescribed protection aur rehabilitation zaroori hain.

**D kyun galat hai:** Complete ligament rupture grade III ke saath fit karta hai. Grade II mein ligament continuity ka kuch part retained hota hai.

**Exam clue:** Grade II = partial ligament tear.
