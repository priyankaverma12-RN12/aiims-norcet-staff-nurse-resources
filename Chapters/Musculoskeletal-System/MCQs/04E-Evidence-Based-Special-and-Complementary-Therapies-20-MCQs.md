### Q01. A patient with knee osteoarthritis asks to substitute passive manual therapy sessions for all therapeutic exercise. Which approach is consistent with NICE NG226?

**Difficulty:** 🟡 Medium · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD · Original practice

- **A.** Consider manual therapy, if suitable, alongside therapeutic exercise rather than as its sole replacement
- **B.** Use manual therapy alone as the preferred treatment for all joints
- **C.** Stop all movement whenever passive treatment is available
- **D.** Guarantee that manipulation regenerates articular cartilage

<details><summary>✅ Correct answer and detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: A. Consider manual therapy, if suitable, alongside therapeutic exercise rather than as its sole replacement**

**A kyun sahi hai:** Hip ya knee OA mein manual techniques selected patients ke exercise plan ka adjunct ho sakti hain. Exercise muscle capacity aur functional recovery address karti hai, so passive treatment usko routinely replace nahi karta.

**B kyun galat hai:** NG226 manual therapy ko hip/knee OA mein exercise ke saath consider karta hai, all joints ke liye standalone preferred therapy nahi.

**C kyun galat hai:** Movement ka suitable graded plan functional benefit de sakta hai. Passive modality available hone se active rehabilitation unnecessary nahi ho jaati.

**D kyun galat hai:** Manual therapy symptom or movement effects de sakti hai, but complete cartilage regeneration guarantee evidence support nahi karti.

**Exam clue:** Manual therapy is an adjunct, not a replacement for exercise in this guideline scenario.

**Clinical reference:** [NICE NG226 osteoarthritis](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/NBK588843/). Direct full guideline retrieved.

### Q20. A patient with uncomplicated symptomatic knee osteoarthritis requests arthroscopic joint washing solely to treat degenerative pain. Which response follows NICE NG226?

**Difficulty:** 🔴 Difficult · **Priority:** 🔴 MOST IMPORTANT · Original practice

- **A.** Arthroscopic lavage or debridement is not recommended as treatment for osteoarthritis alone
- **B.** Arthroscopic washing guarantees permanent restoration of normal cartilage

<details><summary>✅ Correct answer and detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: A. Arthroscopic lavage or debridement is not recommended as treatment for osteoarthritis alone**

**A kyun sahi hai:** NG226 OA treatment ke liye arthroscopic lavage/debridement recommend nahi karta. Other specific surgical indications separate assessment require karti hain. Patient ko suitable alternatives explain karo.

**B kyun galat hai:** Lavage structural cartilage degeneration ko permanently reverse nahi karti. Benefit aur indication disease-specific evidence par depend karte hain.

**C kyun galat hai:** Suspected infection ka aspiration or lavage source-control indication different hai. Degenerative OA aur septic joint management interchange nahi karna chahiye.

**D kyun galat hai:** Routine rehabilitation se pehle invasive wash necessary nahi hai. Evidence-based conservative options patient needs ke according start kiye ja sakte hain.

**Exam clue:** Do not confuse degenerative OA lavage with procedures for infection or another indication.
