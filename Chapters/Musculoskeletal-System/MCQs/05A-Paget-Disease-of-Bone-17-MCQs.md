### Q01. A radiograph of an older adult's tibia shows expansion, cortical thickening and coarse trabeculae in the same segment. Which interpretation is most appropriate?

**Difficulty:** 🟡 Medium · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD · Original practice

- **A.** The pattern establishes generalized osteoporosis
- **B.** The combined pattern supports Paget disease in the appropriate clinical setting
- **C.** The pattern is diagnostic of nutritional rickets
- **D.** The pattern establishes an acute bacterial infection

<details><summary>✅ Correct answer and detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: B. The combined pattern supports Paget disease in the appropriate clinical setting**

**A kyun galat hai:** Osteoporosis mein bone mass kam hota hai. Local expansion aur coarse thick trabeculae typical generalized osteoporosis pattern nahi hain.

**B kyun sahi hai:** Paget disease mein abnormal remodeling ke karan bone ka size aur internal architecture dono badal sakte hain. In radiographic features ko saath interpret karne se diagnosis support hota hai.

**C kyun galat hai:** Rickets growing bone ki mineralization problem hai. Adult tibia ka yeh remodeling pattern uska specific evidence nahi hai.

**D kyun galat hai:** Infection mein bone destruction ya reactive changes ho sakte hain, lekin in findings alone se bacterial cause establish nahi hota.

**Exam clue:** Radiographic pattern matters more than an isolated sign.
