## Fracture care

### Q1. A patient with a leg fracture has severe increasing pain despite analgesia and pain on passive toe stretch. Which complication should be suspected?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Topic:** Fracture care

- **A.** Acute compartment syndrome
- **B.** Normal cast adjustment
- **C.** Stable osteoarthritis
- **D.** Expected muscle soreness only

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: A. Acute compartment syndrome**

Muscle ke aas-paas ki closed space mein pressure badh sakta hai. Isse nerves aur blood vessels dab sakti hain. Severe pain aur passive stretch par pain early clues hain, isliye urgent surgical assessment chahiye.

**Baaki options kyun galat hain?**

- **B.** Increasing severe pain normal adjustment nahi hai.
- **C.** Osteoarthritis acute fracture ke baad ye signs explain nahi karti.
- **D.** Expected soreness maan kar wait karna unsafe hai.

</details>

## Traction

### Q2. During skeletal traction, the weights are found resting on the floor. What should the nurse do?

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Topic:** Traction

- **A.** Add extra weights independently
- **B.** Remove the weights permanently
- **C.** Tie the weights to the bed rail
- **D.** Restore the ordered free-hanging position and alignment

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: D. Restore the ordered free-hanging position and alignment**

Traction ka pull tabhi continuous rahega jab weights freely hang karein. Nurse prescribed alignment maintain karti hai aur weight ka amount apne aap nahi badalti.

**Baaki options kyun galat hain?**

- **A.** Extra weights tissue injury kar sakte hain.
- **B.** Weights hatane se prescribed pull ruk jaata hai.
- **C.** Bed rail se tie karne se free pull nahi milega.

</details>

## Orthopedic surgery

### Q3. After hip arthroplasty, a patient develops sudden shortening and abnormal rotation of the operated leg with severe pain. What should the nurse suspect?

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Topic:** Orthopedic surgery

- **A.** Normal muscle strengthening
- **B.** Uncomplicated constipation
- **C.** Possible hip dislocation
- **D.** Expected postoperative alignment

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: C. Possible hip dislocation**

Artificial hip joint apni jagah se nikal sakta hai. Sudden pain aur leg ki position badalna warning signs hain. Nurse limb ko force se move nahi karti aur urgent review leti hai.

**Baaki options kyun galat hain?**

- **A.** Muscle strengthening se ye change expected nahi hai.
- **B.** Constipation leg rotation explain nahi karti.
- **D.** Sudden deformity expected nahi hai.

</details>

## Arthritis

### Q4. A patient has symmetrical small-joint swelling and prolonged morning stiffness. Which disease pattern is most suggestive?

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Topic:** Arthritis

- **A.** Acute tendon rupture as the only explanation
- **B.** Rheumatoid arthritis
- **C.** Simple traumatic fracture
- **D.** Uncomplicated osteoporosis

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: B. Rheumatoid arthritis**

Rheumatoid arthritis mein body ki defense system joints ki lining ko inflame karti hai. Dono haathon mein similar swelling aur lambi morning stiffness iske clues ho sakte hain.

**Baaki options kyun galat hain?**

- **A.** Tendon rupture usually local acute injury hoti hai.
- **C.** Fracture generally injury aur localized pain se linked hota hai.
- **D.** Osteoporosis bone strength ki problem hai.

</details>
