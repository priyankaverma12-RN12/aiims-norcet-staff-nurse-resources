### Q01. A 62-year-old patient has knee pain that increases during walking and brief stiffness after sitting. Which pathological process most closely explains the usual osteoarthritis pattern?

**Difficulty:** 🟡 Medium · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD · Original practice

- **A.** Immune-mediated synovial destruction as the defining primary process
- **B.** Progressive changes in articular cartilage and other joint structures
- **C.** Bacterial invasion of marrow
- **D.** Loss of bone mineral alone without joint changes

<details><summary>✅ Correct answer and detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: B. Progressive changes in articular cartilage and other joint structures**

**A kyun galat hai:** Immune-mediated synovitis rheumatoid arthritis ka important mechanism hai. Osteoarthritis mein inflammation ho sakti hai, lekin ye uska defining primary mechanism nahi hai.

**B kyun sahi hai:** Osteoarthritis mein articular cartilage aur poore joint ke structures mein degenerative changes develop hote hain. Weight-bearing activity pain ko provoke kar sakti hai, kyunki joint par mechanical load badhta hai.

**C kyun galat hai:** Marrow mein microbial infection osteomyelitis ka concern hai. Chronic activity-related knee pain ka usual OA mechanism ye nahi hai.

**D kyun galat hai:** Bone mineral loss osteoporosis ka feature hai. Osteoarthritis joint cartilage, subchondral bone aur other structures ko affect karti hai.

**Correct answer: B. Use a structured disease-activity assessment and adjust treatment toward remission or low disease activity**
