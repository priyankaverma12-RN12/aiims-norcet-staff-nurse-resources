### Q01. A patient asks how a total hip replacement differs from a conventional hip hemiarthroplasty. Which explanation is correct?

**Difficulty:** 🟡 Medium · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD · Original practice

- **A.** Both procedures always replace the entire acetabular socket
- **B.** Hemiarthroplasty replaces only the acetabular socket
- **C.** Total hip replacement replaces both femoral and acetabular articulating components, while hemiarthroplasty retains the native acetabulum
- **D.** Total hip replacement is a procedure performed without prosthetic components

<details><summary>✅ Correct answer and detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: C. Total hip replacement replaces both femoral and acetabular articulating components, while hemiarthroplasty retains the native acetabulum**

**A kyun galat hai:** Hemiarthroplasty mein native acetabulum retained hota hai. Dono procedures mein acetabular component replace hona zaroori nahi hai.

**B kyun galat hai:** Hemiarthroplasty primarily femoral head-side replacement hai. Sirf acetabular replacement iska standard description nahi hai.

**C kyun sahi hai:** Total hip replacement mein femoral head-side component aur acetabular cup dono reconstruct hote hain. Hemiarthroplasty femoral side replace karti hai, native socket retained rehta hai.

**D kyun galat hai:** Arthroplasty joint reconstruction hai aur total hip replacement mein artificial components implant hote hain. Yeh implant-free procedure nahi hai.

**Exam clue:** Total hip replacement versus hemiarthroplasty ka key difference acetabular replacement hai.

### Q20. Before discharge after primary knee replacement, a patient can walk the required distance but cannot explain the prescribed wound-warning signs or whom to contact. Which action completes education most effectively?

**Difficulty:** 🟡 Medium · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD · Original practice

- **A.** Require the patient to memorize implant brand names instead
- **B.** Hand the patient a leaflet and assume understanding without checking
- **C.** Discharge without addressing the knowledge gap because walking is adequate
- **D.** Use teach-back for wound red flags, contact pathways and the individualized rehabilitation plan

<details><summary>✅ Correct answer and detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: D. Use teach-back for wound red flags, contact pathways and the individualized rehabilitation plan**

**A kyun galat hai:** Implant details clinical record mein useful ho sakte hain. Brand memorization immediate warning-sign education replace nahi karta.

**B kyun galat hai:** Written information helpful hai, but teach-back patient understanding verify karta hai aur misunderstandings correct karne ka opportunity deta hai.

**C kyun galat hai:** Mobility discharge readiness ka ek component hai. Wound safety, medication, support aur follow-up understanding bhi important hain.

**D kyun sahi hai:** Patient apne words mein warning signs, contact plan aur exercises explain kare toh nurse education effectiveness assess kar sakti hai. Unresolved barriers discharge se pehle address karo.

**Exam clue:** Joint replacement discharge readiness includes actionable safety-net understanding.
