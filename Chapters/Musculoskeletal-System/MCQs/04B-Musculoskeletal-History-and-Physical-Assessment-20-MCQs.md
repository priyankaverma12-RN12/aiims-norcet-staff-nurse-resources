### Q01. A patient describes a painful shoulder and says the arm is 'stiff.' Which follow-up question best differentiates weakness from restricted joint movement?

**Difficulty:** 🟡 Medium · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD · Original practice

- **A.** Does the pain occur on your dominant side?
- **B.** Can you move the arm farther when someone supports it, or does the joint itself stop moving?
- **C.** Is your pain score above five?
- **D.** Have you ever taken an analgesic?

<details><summary>✅ Correct answer and detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: B. Can you move the arm farther when someone supports it, or does the joint itself stop moving?**

**A kyun galat hai:** Dominant side functional impact batati hai, but weakness versus mechanical restriction ko directly differentiate nahi karti.

**B kyun sahi hai:** Patient ke word stiffness ka meaning actual movement block, weakness ya pain avoidance ho sakta hai. Supported motion aur own movement ka difference clarify karne se assessment focused hoti hai.

**C kyun galat hai:** Pain score severity quantify karta hai, lekin movement limitation ka mechanism identify nahi karta.

**D kyun galat hai:** Analgesic history relevant hai but active aur assisted movement ke difference ka answer nahi deti.

**Exam clue:** Clarify what the patient means by stiffness.

### Q02. A patient with chronic unilateral shoulder symptoms cannot actively raise the arm fully. Gentle passive examination by the clinician reveals nearly preserved motion. Which interpretation is most appropriate?

**D kyun sahi hai:** Active motion muscle force aur motor control par depend karti hai. Passive range preserved ho toh weakness ya pain inhibition consider karo, but structural diagnosis ke liye further assessment needed hai.

### Q04. A patient undergoing routine nontraumatic joint examination has tolerated inspection and palpation. Which range-of-motion sequence is usually appropriate?

**Difficulty:** 🟡 Medium · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD · Original practice

- **A.** Skip voluntary movement if the examiner can move the joint

<details><summary>✅ Correct answer and detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: C. Assess voluntary active movement first, then gentle passive movement if indicated and safe**

**A kyun galat hai:** Passive movement se patient ki own motor capacity assess nahi hoti. Dono findings clinical context mein useful hain.

**D kyun galat hai:** Forced passive movement painful tissue aggravate kar sakti hai. Baseline voluntary function aur safety assessment pehle useful hain.

**Exam clue:** Active ROM first in a safe routine examination.

### Q06. An elbow extends only to a position 20 degrees short of neutral and flexes to 120 degrees using a neutral-zero convention. What is the measured movement arc?

**Difficulty:** 🔴 Difficult · **Priority:** 🔴 MOST IMPORTANT · Original practice

- **A.** 100 degrees
- **B.** 20 degrees
- **C.** 140 degrees
- **D.** 120 degrees

<details><summary>✅ Correct answer and detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: A. 100 degrees**

**A kyun sahi hai:** Extension endpoint 20 degrees flexion par hai aur maximum flexion 120 degrees hai. Difference 120 minus 20 = 100 degrees actual excursion hai.

**B kyun galat hai:** 20 degrees extension deficit hai. Complete movement arc ke liye maximum flexion se deficit subtract karo.

**C kyun galat hai:** 140 degrees tab milega jab endpoints incorrectly add kiye jayen. Dono endpoints flexion side par hain.

**D kyun galat hai:** 120 degrees maximum flexion endpoint hai, not total available arc because neutral extension achieve nahi hoti.

**Exam clue:** Movement arc is the difference between the two measured endpoints.
