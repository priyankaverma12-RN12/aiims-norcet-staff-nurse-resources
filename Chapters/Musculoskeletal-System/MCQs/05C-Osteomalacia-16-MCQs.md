### Q01. An adult with suspected osteomalacia has narrow transverse radiolucent lines along the pubic rami, with sclerotic margins. Which term describes these characteristic insufficiency lesions?

**Difficulty:** 🟡 Medium · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD · Original practice

- **A.** Sequestra
- **B.** Involucrum
- **C.** Osteophytes
- **D.** Looser zones

<details><summary>✅ Correct answer and detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: D. Looser zones**

**A kyun galat hai:** Sequestrum infected ya devitalized bone ka separated fragment hai. Yeh osteomyelitis terminology hai, mineralization defect ki transverse lucency nahi.

### Q02. A patient has histologically confirmed osteomalacia. The report describes excessive unmineralized osteoid. What is osteoid?

**Difficulty:** 🟡 Medium · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD · Original practice

- **A.** The same structure as mature compact bone regardless of mineral content
- **B.** The organic bone matrix before normal mineral deposition
- **C.** A detached mineralized cartilage fragment from a joint
- **D.** A collection of pus within the medullary cavity

<details><summary>✅ Correct answer and detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: B. The organic bone matrix before normal mineral deposition**

**A kyun galat hai:** Mature mineralized bone aur unmineralized osteoid mechanical properties mein differ karte hain. Dono equivalent nahi hain.

**B kyun sahi hai:** Osteoblasts pehle organic matrix banate hain, jise osteoid kehte hain. Jab calcium-phosphate mineral deposition inadequate hota hai, toh osteoid accumulate ho sakta hai.

**C kyun galat hai:** Articular cartilage fragment joint pathology represent kar sakta hai. Osteoid bone-forming matrix ka specific term hai.

**D kyun galat hai:** Pus inflammatory cells and debris ka collection hai. Osteoid normal bone formation ka precursor matrix hai.

**Exam clue:** Osteoid is formed before adequate mineralization.
