### Q01. MRI describes a tumor arising within spinal cord tissue and expanding the cord from inside. Which anatomical classification is correct?

**Difficulty:** 🟡 Medium · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD · Original practice

- **A.** Intradural extramedullary
- **B.** Extradural
- **C.** Intramedullary
- **D.** Isolated paravertebral

<details><summary>✅ Correct answer and detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: C. Intramedullary**

**A kyun galat hai:** Intradural extramedullary lesion dura ke andar lekin cord tissue ke bahar hoti hai. Given lesion cord ke andar hai.

**B kyun galat hai:** Extradural lesion dura ke bahar hoti hai aur cord ko outside se compress kar sakti hai.

**C kyun sahi hai:** Tumor location spinal cord ke parenchyma ke andar hai. Intramedullary lesion cord ko expand ya damage kar sakti hai, isliye neurological assessment important hai.

**D kyun galat hai:** Paravertebral lesion vertebral column ke nearby soft tissue mein hoti hai, cord parenchyma mein nahi.

**Exam clue:** Medulla here refers to spinal cord tissue, not bone marrow.
