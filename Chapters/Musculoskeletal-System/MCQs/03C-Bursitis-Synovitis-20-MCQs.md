### Q01. A patient asks how a bursa differs from the synovial cavity of a joint. Which explanation is correct?

**Difficulty:** 🟡 Medium · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD · Original practice

- **A.** A bursa is the entire joint capsule in every joint
- **B.** A bursa is a tendon connecting muscle to bone
- **C.** A bursa is a mineralized bone structure
- **D.** A bursa is a small fluid-containing sac that reduces friction between moving tissues

<details><summary>✅ Correct answer and detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: D. A bursa is a small fluid-containing sac that reduces friction between moving tissues**

**A kyun galat hai:** Joint capsule articulation ko enclose karti hai. Bursa separate periarticular cushioning sac ho sakti hai, although kuch bursae joints se communicate kar sakti hain.

**B kyun galat hai:** Tendon muscle force bone tak transmit karta hai. Bursa tendon movement ko facilitate kar sakti hai, lekin tendon khud nahi hai.

**C kyun galat hai:** Bursa soft tissue sac hai. Iska primary function mineralized support provide karna nahi hai.

**D kyun sahi hai:** Bursa tendon, skin aur bone jaise moving surfaces ke beech friction reduce karti hai. Excess pressure ya inflammation se bursa mein fluid aur pain increase ho sakte hain.

**Exam clue:** Bursa friction reduction karti hai, joint capsule se distinguish karo.
