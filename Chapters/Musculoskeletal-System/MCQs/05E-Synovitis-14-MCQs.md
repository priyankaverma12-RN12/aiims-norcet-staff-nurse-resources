### Q01. An adult with chronic knee swelling has ultrasonographic evidence of thickened hypoechoic synovial tissue but very little free joint fluid. Which distinction is important?

**Difficulty:** 🟡 Medium · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD · Original practice

- **A.** A large effusion is mandatory for the ultrasound definition of synovitis
- **B.** Synovial hypertrophy can exist even without a large effusion
- **C.** Synovial tissue thickening and free fluid are mechanically identical
- **D.** The finding proves that every joint in the body is inflamed

<details><summary>✅ Correct answer and detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: B. Synovial hypertrophy can exist even without a large effusion**

**A kyun galat hai:** Large fluid collection absent hone se synovial hypertrophy exclude nahi hoti. Effusion aur lining changes separately describe kiye ja sakte hain.

**B kyun sahi hai:** Synovial lining inflammation ke saath tissue thickening ho sakti hai even when joint fluid volume low ho. Ultrasound definition mein synovial hypertrophy important component hai.

**C kyun galat hai:** Free fluid aur thickened tissue imaging aur mechanical properties mein differ karte hain. Sonographer relevant characteristics distinguish karta hai.

**D kyun galat hai:** One knee ka finding systemic distribution establish nahi karta. Other joints clinical indication ke basis par assess hote hain.

**Exam clue:** Synovial thickening and effusion are different findings.

**D kyun galat hai:** Positive joint culture direct infection suggest kar sakti hai. Classic reactive process ke liye viable triggering organism ka joint culture mandatory nahi hai.
