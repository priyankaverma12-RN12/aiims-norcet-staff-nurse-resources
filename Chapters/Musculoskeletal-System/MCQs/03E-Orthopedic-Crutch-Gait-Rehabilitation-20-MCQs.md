### Q01. Following lower-limb fracture fixation, an adult correctly reproduces the prescribed partial load during stationary scale practice, but the therapist suspects excessive loading during walking. Which additional assessment method most directly evaluates the loading problem during actual gait?

**Difficulty:** 🟡 Medium · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD · Original practice

- **A.** Repeat only the stationary scale measurement after each rest period
- **B.** Use therapist-directed dynamic load measurement, such as a validated instrumented insole with real-time feedback where available
- **C.** Calculate the prescribed percentage again from body weight without assessing gait
- **D.** Infer exact affected-limb loading from the number of steps walked

<details><summary>✅ Correct answer and detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: B. Use therapist-directed dynamic load measurement, such as a validated instrumented insole with real-time feedback where available**

**A kyun galat hai:** Stationary scale practice se patient ko prescribed load ka feel samajh aa sakta hai. Lekin walking ke dauran actual load change hota hai, isliye repeated static reading dynamic problem ko directly measure nahi karti.

**B kyun sahi hai:** Instrumented insole jaisa suitable validated system walking ke dauran load measure kar sakta hai aur excessive loading par feedback de sakta hai. Therapist is information ko supervised gait assessment mein use karke training adjust kar sakta hai. Biofeedback se har patient ki perfect adherence guarantee nahi hoti, aur surgeon ki loading restriction independently change nahi karni chahiye.

**C kyun galat hai:** Percentage calculation se prescribed target milta hai, actual walking load nahi. Agar patient stationary target samajh bhi gaya hai, tab bhi gait ke dauran us target ko follow karne ki ability alag se assess karni padti hai.

**D kyun galat hai:** Step count walking ki quantity batata hai. Har step mein affected limb par kitna load gaya, yeh step count se accurately determine nahi hota.

**Exam clue:** Static load target aur actual dynamic gait load alag measurements hain. Therapist-directed real-time feedback dynamic assessment mein help kar sakta hai.

**Clinical reference:** [The Impact of Real-Time Biofeedback on Partial Weightbearing Training: A Comparative Study](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11872552/) and [Audio-biofeedback versus the scale method in healthy older adults, randomized trial](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/39154064/). Research evidence, not a universal equipment requirement.

### Q02. In a standardized gait-speed assessment after fracture rehabilitation, a medically cleared patient covers the measured 10-metre segment in 20 seconds. What is the walking speed?

**Difficulty:** 🟢 Easy · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD · Original practice

- **A.** 2.0 m/s
- **B.** 0.2 m/s
- **C.** 0.5 m/s
- **D.** 20 m/s

<details><summary>✅ Correct answer and detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: C. 0.5 m/s**

**A kyun galat hai:** 2 m/s tab milta jab patient 10 metre ko 5 seconds mein cover karta. Yahan elapsed time 20 seconds diya hai.

**B kyun galat hai:** 0.2 m/s ke liye 10 metre cover karne mein 50 seconds lagte. Given values se yeh speed nahi milti.

**C kyun sahi hai:** Speed distance divided by time hoti hai. 10 metre ko 20 seconds se divide karne par 0.5 m/s aata hai. Serial assessment mein prescribed aid aur testing protocol bhi record karo.

**D kyun galat hai:** 20 seconds elapsed time hai, speed nahi. Time ko distance ke saath relate karna zaroori hai.

**Exam clue:** Gait speed = measured distance / elapsed time.

**A kyun sahi hai:** Crutches pehle support base create karti hain. Affected leg prescribed amount load leti hai aur unaffected leg advance karti hai while upper limbs unload as taught. Exact step length therapist guide karta hai.

**D kyun galat hai:** Simultaneous jump controlled partial-weight-bearing gait nahi hai. It increases balance and impact demands.

**Exam clue:** Three-point partial-weight-bearing gait coordinates crutches with the restricted limb.

### Q19. A patient with a prescribed non-weight-bearing gait is preparing to enter an escalator using crutches. Which advice is safest?

### Q20. During follow-up after a lower-limb fracture, the rehabilitation team records Lower Extremity Functional Scale (LEFS) scores of 28/80 at baseline and 45/80 later. What is the correct interpretation of the score direction?

**Difficulty:** 🟢 Easy · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD · Original practice

- **A.** The patient reports worse lower-limb function
- **B.** The score proves that the fracture has united radiographically
- **C.** The patient reports better lower-limb function
- **D.** The score alone authorizes unrestricted weight bearing

<details><summary>✅ Correct answer and detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: C. The patient reports better lower-limb function**

**A kyun galat hai:** LEFS mein higher score generally better reported function represent karta hai. Yahan score badha hai, isliye direction deterioration nahi dikhati.

**B kyun galat hai:** LEFS patient ki reported activity difficulty assess karta hai. Bone union confirm karne ke liye appropriate clinical aur radiographic assessment chahiye.

**C kyun sahi hai:** LEFS ka total score 0 se 80 tak hota hai aur higher score better reported lower-limb function dikhata hai. 28 se 45 tak increase improvement ki direction batata hai. Clinical significance aur individual recovery ko doosri findings ke saath interpret karna chahiye.

**D kyun galat hai:** Functional score better hone se surgical loading restriction automatically cancel nahi hoti. Weight-bearing progression treating team ke documented plan se decide hoti hai.

**Exam clue:** LEFS mein higher score better patient-reported function hota hai. Yeh bone union ya weight-bearing clearance test nahi hai.
