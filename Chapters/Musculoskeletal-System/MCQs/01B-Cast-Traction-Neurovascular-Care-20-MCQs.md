### Q01. A fresh plaster cast is being supported while it sets. Which handling technique minimizes the risk of focal pressure under the cast?

**Difficulty:** 🟡 Medium · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD · Original practice

- **A.** Grip the cast firmly with the fingertips
- **B.** Rest the cast edge directly on a hard rail
- **C.** Support the wet cast with the palms
- **D.** Press a thumb into the plaster over each bony prominence

<details><summary>✅ Correct answer and detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: C. Support the wet cast with the palms**

**A kyun galat hai:** Fingertips se chhote area par pressure padta hai aur plaster mein dents ban sakte hain.

**B kyun galat hai:** Hard rail cast par localized pressure aur deformation create kar sakti hai.

**C kyun sahi hai:** Palms se pressure large area par distribute hota hai. Wet plaster par fingers ka indentation baad mein skin ko compress kar sakta hai.

**D kyun galat hai:** Bony prominence par indentation banane se underlying skin injury ka risk aur badhta hai.

**Exam clue:** Wet cast ko palm support se handle karo.

**Difficulty:** 🟡 Medium · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD · Original practice

- **A.** Use prescribed pressure redistribution and heel offloading without altering traction alignment
- **B.** Massage reddened skin vigorously every hour
- **C.** Place a rigid object directly beneath the heel
- **D.** Avoid skin assessment until traction is discontinued

<details><summary>✅ Correct answer and detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: A. Use prescribed pressure redistribution and heel offloading without altering traction alignment**

**A kyun sahi hai:** Heel bony prominence hai jahan prolonged pressure tissue perfusion ko impair kar sakta hai. Alignment preserve karke appropriate offloading aur regular skin assessment pressure injury risk kam karte hain.

**B kyun galat hai:** Reddened skin ko vigorous massage karna tissue injury worsen kar sakta hai.

**C kyun galat hai:** Rigid object pressure ko aur concentrate kar sakta hai. Suitable support pressure redistribute karta hai.

**D kyun galat hai:** Traction ke dauran hi skin risk monitor karna important hai. End tak wait karna prevention nahi hai.

**Exam clue:** Heel offloading aur prescribed alignment dono maintain karo.

### Q20. One week after a lower-leg fracture was casted, swelling has subsided and the patient reports excessive movement of the limb inside the intact cast. What is the most appropriate response?

**Difficulty:** 🟡 Medium · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD · Original practice

- **A.** Tell the patient that any amount of looseness is desirable
- **B.** Arrange review of cast fit and fracture stability
- **C.** Advise full weight bearing to seat the cast
- **D.** Insert towels deeply inside the cast to tighten it

<details><summary>✅ Correct answer and detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: B. Arrange review of cast fit and fracture stability**

**A kyun galat hai:** Excess looseness protective immobilization reduce kar sakti hai; intact cast ka fit bhi assess karna hota hai.

**B kyun sahi hai:** Swelling reduce hone se cast loose ho sakta hai. Excess movement fracture support compromise kar sakta hai; orthopedic service fit aur alignment review kare.

**C kyun galat hai:** Weight bearing prescribed restrictions par depend karta hai; loose cast ko seat karne ke liye weight bearing unsafe hai.

**D kyun galat hai:** Towels insert karne se focal pressure aur skin injury ho sakti hai; cast fitting professional task hai.

**Exam clue:** Cast intact hona aur cast properly fitting hona different assessments hain.
