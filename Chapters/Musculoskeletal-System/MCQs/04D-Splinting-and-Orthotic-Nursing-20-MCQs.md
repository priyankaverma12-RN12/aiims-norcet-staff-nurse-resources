### Q01. A clinician prescribes an initial noncircumferential splint for a recently fractured extremity with anticipated swelling. Which structural advantage explains this choice?

**Difficulty:** 🟡 Medium · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD · Original practice

- **A.** A splint eliminates the need to monitor swelling
- **B.** A noncircumferential splint guarantees definitive reduction in every fracture
- **C.** A splint automatically allows unrestricted weight bearing
- **D.** A properly applied splint can accommodate changing swelling more readily than a complete rigid circumferential cast

<details><summary>✅ Correct answer and detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: D. A properly applied splint can accommodate changing swelling more readily than a complete rigid circumferential cast**

**A kyun galat hai:** Splint bhi pressure create kar sakti hai. Swelling aur distal condition monitoring still important hai.

**B kyun galat hai:** Alignment fracture pattern aur reduction technique se depend karti hai. Splint choice alone satisfactory reduction guarantee nahi karti.

**C kyun galat hai:** Weight-bearing permission injury stability aur clinician order se decide hoti hai. Splint presence full load clearance nahi hai.

**D kyun sahi hai:** Noncircumferential support mein swelling ke liye relative space ho sakti hai. Isliye initial injury phase mein splint useful hoti hai, although outer wrap too tight ho toh compression still possible hai.

**Exam clue:** Noncircumferential support can accommodate swelling but still requires monitoring.
