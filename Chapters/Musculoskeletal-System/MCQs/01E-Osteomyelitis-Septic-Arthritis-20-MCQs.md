### Q01. A child develops acute metaphyseal bone infection after bacteria enter the bloodstream from a distant focus. Which route of infection is described?

**Difficulty:** 🟡 Medium · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD · Original practice

- **A.** Hematogenous dissemination
- **B.** Direct inoculation through an open fracture
- **C.** Contiguous spread from an adjacent ulcer
- **D.** Sterile autoimmune synovitis

<details><summary>✅ Correct answer and detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: A. Hematogenous dissemination**

**A kyun sahi hai:** Hematogenous ka matlab blood ke through spread hona hai. Bacteria distant source se circulation mein aakar bone mein settle kar sakte hain, especially growing long bone ke metaphysis mein.

**B kyun galat hai:** Direct inoculation mein bacteria injury ya surgery ke through directly bone tak pahunchte hain. Yahan bloodborne route describe hua hai.

**C kyun galat hai:** Contiguous spread nearby infected tissue se bone tak direct extension hoti hai. Distant bloodborne source alag route hai.

**D kyun galat hai:** Autoimmune synovitis noninfectious immune inflammation hai. Yahan bacterial bone infection diya gaya hai.

**Exam clue:** Bloodstream se bone infection hematogenous spread hai.

**Clinical reference:** [Merck osteomyelitis](https://www.merckmanuals.com/professional/musculoskeletal-and-connective-tissue-disorders/infections-of-joints-and-bones/osteomyelitis). Direct page retrieved.

### Q05. A patient with chronic osteomyelitis has a detached fragment of nonviable bone within the infected area. Which term describes this fragment?

**Difficulty:** 🟢 Easy · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD · Original practice

- **A.** Sequestrum
- **B.** Involucrum
- **C.** Synovium
- **D.** Epiphysis

<details><summary>✅ Correct answer and detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: A. Sequestrum**

**A kyun sahi hai:** Infection blood supply compromise karke bone tissue ko dead bana sakti hai. Jab nonviable fragment surrounding viable bone se separate hota hai, usse sequestrum kehte hain.

**B kyun galat hai:** Involucrum reactive new bone hai jo infected area ya sequestrum ko surround kar sakti hai. Ye dead fragment khud nahi hai.

**C kyun galat hai:** Synovium joint lining hai. Bone infection mein dead fragment ko synovium nahi kehte.

**D kyun galat hai:** Epiphysis long bone ka end region hai. Ye anatomical location term hai, dead bone fragment ka naam nahi.

**Exam clue:** Sequestrum = devitalized bone fragment.
