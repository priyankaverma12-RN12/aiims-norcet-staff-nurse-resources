### Q01. An adult has a newly painful swollen native knee, but the first blood CRP is within the laboratory reference range. Which interpretation is safest?

**Difficulty:** 🟡 Medium · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD · Original practice

- **A.** The normal CRP establishes a noninfectious cause
- **B.** Aspiration is unnecessary until CRP exceeds a fixed threshold
- **C.** The normal CRP cannot independently exclude septic arthritis
- **D.** The result identifies the responsible organism

<details><summary>✅ Correct answer and detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: C. The normal CRP cannot independently exclude septic arthritis**

**A kyun galat hai:** CRP normal hone se bacterial cause reliably rule out nahi hota. Noninfectious diagnosis ke liye appropriate assessment chahiye.

**B kyun galat hai:** Fixed CRP threshold ka wait time-sensitive infection diagnosis delay kar sakta hai. Aspiration indication clinical context se guide hoti hai.

**C kyun sahi hai:** CRP early disease ya individual inflammatory response ke according normal ho sakta hai. Clinical suspicion ke basis par urgent joint assessment still necessary hai.

**D kyun galat hai:** CRP inflammation marker hai, microbial species identification nahi karta.

**Exam clue:** Normal blood markers do not safely rule out native-joint infection.
