### Q01. A patient with a lumbar disc herniation asks which normal disc structure has displaced through a defect in its surrounding fibrous ring. Which structure is being described?

**Difficulty:** 🟡 Medium · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD · Original practice

- **A.** Ligamentum flavum
- **B.** Facet joint articular cartilage
- **C.** Nucleus pulposus
- **D.** Posterior longitudinal ligament

<details><summary>✅ Correct answer and detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: C. Nucleus pulposus**

**A kyun galat hai:** Ligamentum flavum posterior spinal canal region ka supporting ligament hai. Yeh disc ka central material nahi hai.

**B kyun galat hai:** Facet cartilage posterior synovial joint surfaces cover karti hai. Intervertebral disc ka nucleus usse separate structure hai.

**C kyun sahi hai:** Nucleus pulposus disc ka relatively soft central core hai. Annulus fibrosus mein tear hone par central material displace ho kar nearby root irritate kar sakta hai.

**D kyun galat hai:** Posterior longitudinal ligament vertebral bodies ke posterior surface along hota hai. Disc herniation mein adjacent ligament involved ho sakta hai, but displaced central disc material nucleus hai.

**Exam clue:** Annulus surrounds the nucleus pulposus.

### Q02. A patient with a confirmed L4–L5 posterolateral disc herniation develops weakness of ankle and great-toe dorsiflexion with altered dorsal-foot sensation. Which nerve root is classically implicated?

**Difficulty:** 🔴 Difficult · **Priority:** 🔴 MOST IMPORTANT · Original practice

<details><summary>✅ Correct answer and detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: A. L5**

**D kyun galat hai:** L2 involvement proximal thigh and hip-flexion findings se more consistent ho sakta hai. Given distal dorsiflexion pattern lower lumbar root suggest karta hai.

**Exam clue:** For a typical posterolateral lumbar herniation, the traversing root often lies one level below the upper vertebra.
