### Q01. Which component of a lower-limb prosthesis receives the residual limb and transfers load between the body and the artificial limb?

**Difficulty:** 🟢 Easy · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD · Original practice

- **A.** Socket
- **B.** Cosmetic outer cover
- **C.** Prosthetic foot alone
- **D.** External walking frame

<details><summary>✅ Correct answer and detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: A. Socket**

**A kyun sahi hai:** Socket residual limb ke shape ke according fit hota hai aur body se prosthesis tak force transfer karta hai. Isliye socket fit comfort aur safety ke liye essential hai.

**B kyun galat hai:** Cosmetic cover artificial limb ki appearance aur selected components ki protection ke liye hota hai. Ye primary load interface nahi hai.

**C kyun galat hai:** Foot ground contact aur gait mein role nibhata hai, lekin residual limb ko directly receive karne wala part socket hai.

**D kyun galat hai:** Walking frame external assistive device hai. Ye residual limb aur prosthesis ke beech component nahi hai.

**Exam clue:** Socket ko body aur prosthesis ka primary load interface samjho.
