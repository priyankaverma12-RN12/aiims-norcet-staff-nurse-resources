## Initial study explanations

### Fracture care

Muscle ke aas-paas ki closed space mein pressure badh sakta hai. Isse nerves aur blood vessels dab sakti hain. Severe pain aur passive stretch par pain early clues hain, isliye urgent surgical assessment chahiye.

### Traction

Traction ka pull tabhi continuous rahega jab weights freely hang karein. Nurse prescribed alignment maintain karti hai aur weight ka amount apne aap nahi badalti.

### Orthopedic surgery

Artificial hip joint apni jagah se nikal sakta hai. Sudden pain aur leg ki position badalna warning signs hain. Nurse limb ko force se move nahi karti aur urgent review leti hai.

### Arthritis

Rheumatoid arthritis mein body ki defense system joints ki lining ko inflame karti hai. Dono haathon mein similar swelling aur lambi morning stiffness iske clues ho sakte hain.
