### PYQ1. A child's bone bends and breaks incompletely on one side. Which fracture pattern fits?

**Difficulty:** Easy/Medium | **Topic:** Pediatric fractures

- **A.** Greenstick fracture
- **B.** Comminuted fracture
- **C.** Transverse complete fracture
- **D.** Pathological fracture

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation</summary>

**Correct: A. Greenstick fracture**

Bachchon ki haddiyan relatively flexible hoti hain. Greenstick fracture mein bone ek taraf toot sakti hai aur doosri taraf bend hoti hai. Comminuted fracture mein multiple fragments hote hain. Complete fracture poori width cross karta hai. Pathological fracture weakened bone mein hota hai.
