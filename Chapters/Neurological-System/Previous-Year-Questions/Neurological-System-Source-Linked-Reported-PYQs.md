### PYQ1. Which assessment trend is essential during early care following an acute thrombotic stroke?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Topic:** Stroke monitoring

- **A.** Serial neurological status including level of consciousness and motor function
- **B.** Only daily body weight
- **C.** Weekly hearing screening
- **D.** Routine dental examination

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: A. Serial neurological status including level of consciousness and motor function**

Stroke ke baad brain function change ho sakta hai. Nurse consciousness, pupils aur movement baar-baar assess karti hai aur deterioration report karti hai.

**Baaki options kyun galat hain?**

- **B.** Weight neurological deterioration detect nahi karta.
- **C.** Hearing screening acute stroke priority nahi hai.
- **D.** Dental examination brain function monitor nahi karti.

</details>
