## Initial study explanations

### Stroke

Stroke mein brain ke kisi part ko blood kam mil sakta hai ya bleeding ho sakti hai. Last known well ka time treatment options decide karne mein madad karta hai. Nurse emergency stroke pathway activate karti hai.

### Seizures

Seizure mein muscles bina control ke contract kar sakti hain. Nurse injury se bachati hai, time note karti hai aur saans assess karti hai. Muh mein kuch nahi daalna chahiye.

### Raised intracranial pressure

Skull ke andar pressure badhne par brainstem dab sakta hai. BP ka pattern, slow pulse aur irregular saans Cushing response ke clues hain. Is situation mein emergency escalation chahiye.

### Neuromuscular disease

Guillain-Barre mein nerves weak ho sakti hain aur breathing muscles bhi affect ho sakti hain. Vital capacity girna saans ki failure ka early clue hai. Nurse respiratory support ke liye jaldi help leti hai.
