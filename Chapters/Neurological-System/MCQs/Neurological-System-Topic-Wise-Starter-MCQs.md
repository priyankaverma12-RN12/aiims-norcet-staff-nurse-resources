## Stroke

### Q1. A patient with new facial droop and unilateral arm weakness arrives at the emergency department. Which information is essential for time-sensitive stroke treatment?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Topic:** Stroke

- **A.** Time last known well
- **B.** Time of the last routine dental visit
- **C.** Usual shoe size
- **D.** Date of the last eye examination

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: A. Time last known well**

Stroke mein brain ke kisi part ko blood kam mil sakta hai ya bleeding ho sakti hai. Last known well ka time treatment options decide karne mein madad karta hai. Nurse emergency stroke pathway activate karti hai.

**Baaki options kyun galat hain?**

- **B.** Dental visit treatment window nahi batata.
- **C.** Shoe size stroke assessment se related nahi hai.
- **D.** Eye exam ki date acute symptom onset nahi batati.

</details>

## Seizures

### Q2. A patient begins a generalized tonic-clonic seizure while in bed. Which nursing action is appropriate?

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Topic:** Seizures

- **A.** Force a spoon between the teeth
- **B.** Restrict all limb movements forcefully
- **C.** Give oral water during the convulsion
- **D.** Protect the head, clear hazards and time the seizure

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: D. Protect the head, clear hazards and time the seizure**

Seizure mein muscles bina control ke contract kar sakti hain. Nurse injury se bachati hai, time note karti hai aur saans assess karti hai. Muh mein kuch nahi daalna chahiye.

**Baaki options kyun galat hain?**

- **A.** Spoon se teeth aur airway ko injury ho sakti hai.
- **B.** Forceful restraint se injury ho sakti hai.
- **C.** Convulsion ke time water lungs mein ja sakta hai.

</details>

## Raised intracranial pressure

### Q3. A head-injured patient develops bradycardia, rising systolic pressure and irregular respirations. What is the most concerning explanation?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Topic:** Raised intracranial pressure

- **A.** Stable recovery from concussion
- **B.** Isolated hypoglycemia as the only possible cause
- **C.** Severe raised intracranial pressure with possible brainstem compression
- **D.** Uncomplicated peripheral neuropathy

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: C. Severe raised intracranial pressure with possible brainstem compression**

Skull ke andar pressure badhne par brainstem dab sakta hai. BP ka pattern, slow pulse aur irregular saans Cushing response ke clues hain. Is situation mein emergency escalation chahiye.

**Baaki options kyun galat hain?**

- **A.** Ye signs stable recovery ke nahi hain.
- **B.** Glucose bhi check ho sakta hai par is pattern ko ignore nahi karna chahiye.
- **D.** Peripheral neuropathy is combination ko explain nahi karti.

</details>

## Neuromuscular disease

### Q4. A patient with Guillain-Barre syndrome has progressive weakness and a declining vital capacity. Which priority is most appropriate?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Topic:** Neuromuscular disease

- **A.** Focus only on sensory symptoms
- **B.** Urgent assessment for impending respiratory failure
- **C.** Increase walking exercises without reassessment
- **D.** Wait until oxygen saturation becomes zero

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: B. Urgent assessment for impending respiratory failure**

Guillain-Barre mein nerves weak ho sakti hain aur breathing muscles bhi affect ho sakti hain. Vital capacity girna saans ki failure ka early clue hai. Nurse respiratory support ke liye jaldi help leti hai.

**Baaki options kyun galat hain?**

- **A.** Motor aur breathing weakness ko bhi assess karna hai.
- **C.** Exercise respiratory fatigue badha sakti hai.
- **D.** SpO2 girne ka wait dangerous hai.

</details>
