### Q1. Which mosquito transmits human malaria?

**Difficulty:** Easy | **Priority:** High | **Topic:** Malaria and vector control | **Type:** Original practice

- **A.** Female Anopheles
- **B.** Male Anopheles
- **C.** Female Aedes aegypti
- **D.** Female Culex as the usual malaria vector

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: A. Female Anopheles**

Female Anopheles blood meal ke waqt malaria parasite transmit kar sakti hai.

**Baaki options kyun galat hain?**

- **B.** Male mosquito blood meal nahi leta.
- **C.** Aedes aegypti dengue jaise infections transmit karti hai.
- **D.** Culex kuch other vector-borne diseases transmit karti hai, human malaria ka usual vector nahi hai.

**Exam clue:** Female Anopheles blood meal ke waqt malaria parasite transmit kar sakti hai.
