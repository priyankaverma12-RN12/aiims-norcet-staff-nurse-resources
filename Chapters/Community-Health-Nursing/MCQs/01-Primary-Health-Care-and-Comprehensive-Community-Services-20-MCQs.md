### Q1. Which statement best distinguishes primary health care from primary care?

**Difficulty:** Easy | **Priority:** Medium | **Topic:** Primary Health Care and Comprehensive Community Services | **Type:** Original practice

- **A.** Primary health care includes community participation and action beyond clinical services
- **B.** Primary health care means only the first consultation
- **C.** Primary health care is limited to district hospitals
- **D.** Primary health care excludes prevention

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: A. Primary health care includes community participation and action beyond clinical services**

- **A.** PHC mein clinical care ke saath community ke decisions aur health ko affect karne wali conditions par bhi kaam hota hai.
- **B.** First consultation primary care ka hissa hai, lekin PHC ka scope isse bada hai.
- **C.** District hospital referral care deta hai, jabki PHC community ke paas bhi services organize karta hai.
- **D.** Disease ko hone se rokna PHC ka important part hai.

**Exam clue:** PHC ka scope primary care se bada hota hai.

</details>
