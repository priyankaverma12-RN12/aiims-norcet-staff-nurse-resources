### Q1. Which family structure consists of parents and their dependent children living as one unit?

**Difficulty:** Easy | **Priority:** High | **Topic:** Family health nursing | **Type:** Original practice

- **A.** Nuclear family
- **B.** Joint family
- **C.** Extended family
- **D.** Single-person household

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: A. Nuclear family**

Nuclear family aam taur par parents aur dependent children ka unit hota hai.

**Baaki options kyun galat hain?**

- **B.** Joint family mein multiple related generations ya family units saath ho sakte hain.
- **C.** Extended family mein wider relatives included hote hain.
- **D.** Single-person household mein ek resident hota hai.

**Exam clue:** Recognize the basic household structure.

</details>

### Q3. Which contraceptive method also helps reduce sexually transmitted infection risk when correctly used?

**Difficulty:** Easy | **Priority:** High | **Topic:** Family health nursing | **Type:** Original practice

- **A.** Copper intrauterine device
- **B.** Oral contraceptive pill
- **C.** Fertility awareness method
- **D.** Condom

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: D. Condom**

Condom barrier provide karke pregnancy aur many STIs ka risk reduce kar sakta hai.

**Baaki options kyun galat hain?**

- **A.** Copper IUD contraception deta hai, STI protection nahi.
- **B.** Pill pregnancy prevention ke liye hai, STI barrier nahi.
- **C.** Fertility awareness STI transmission prevent nahi karti.

**Exam clue:** Condoms offer dual protection.

</details>
