## Epidemiology

### Q1. In a village of 2,000 people at risk, 40 new cases of a disease occur during one year. What is the cumulative incidence over that year?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Topic:** Epidemiology

- **A.** 2%
- **B.** 20%
- **C.** 0.2%
- **D.** 40%

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: A. 2%**

Incidence naye cases ko measure karti hai. Yahan 40 ko 2,000 se divide karke 100 se multiply karo, toh 2% aata hai.

**Baaki options kyun galat hain?**

- **B.** 20% ke liye 400 cases hote.
- **C.** 0.2% ke liye 4 cases hote.
- **D.** 40% case count ko percentage samajhne ki galti hai.

</details>

## Screening

### Q2. A community screening test is positive in an asymptomatic person. What should the nurse explain?

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Topic:** Screening

- **A.** The screening result always proves disease
- **B.** Treatment should be started without any applicable diagnostic assessment
- **C.** The result should be ignored because symptoms are absent
- **D.** Further diagnostic evaluation is required

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: D. Further diagnostic evaluation is required**

Screening ka kaam disease ka risk jaldi identify karna hai. Positive result ke baad diagnostic test ki need ho sakti hai. Nurse referral aur follow-up arrange karti hai.

**Baaki options kyun galat hain?**

- **A.** Screening mein false positive bhi ho sakta hai.
- **B.** Treatment decision diagnosis aur programme protocol par depend karta hai.
- **C.** Screening ka purpose asymptomatic people mein risk pakadna bhi hai.

</details>

## Cold chain

### Q3. A freeze-sensitive adsorbed vaccine is suspected to have been frozen during transport. What is the safest action?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Topic:** Cold chain

- **A.** Warm it in boiling water
- **B.** Mix it with another vaccine to restore potency
- **C.** Quarantine it and follow the programme's assessment and disposal guidance
- **D.** Administer it immediately because freezing improves every vaccine

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: C. Quarantine it and follow the programme's assessment and disposal guidance**

Kuch vaccines freeze hone se permanently damage ho sakti hain. Nurse vaccine ko use se alag rakhti hai aur cold-chain supervisor ke protocol se assess karti hai.

**Baaki options kyun galat hain?**

- **A.** Boiling heat se potency aur damage ho sakti hai.
- **B.** Mixing potency restore nahi karti.
- **D.** Freezing har vaccine ke liye safe nahi hai.

</details>

## Prevention levels

### Q4. Which intervention is an example of secondary prevention in a community?

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Topic:** Prevention levels

- **A.** Palliative care for advanced disease
- **B.** Screening asymptomatic adults for hypertension
- **C.** Health education to prevent initiation of tobacco use
- **D.** Stroke rehabilitation after established disability

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: B. Screening asymptomatic adults for hypertension**

Secondary prevention disease ko early stage mein pakadne aur treatment shuru karne se related hai. BP screening isi liye useful hai.

**Baaki options kyun galat hain?**

- **A.** Palliative care established disease mein comfort support karti hai.
- **C.** Tobacco initiation rokna primary prevention hai.
- **D.** Rehabilitation tertiary prevention hai.

</details>
