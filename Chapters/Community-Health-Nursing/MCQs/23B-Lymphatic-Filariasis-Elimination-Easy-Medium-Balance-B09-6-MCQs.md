### Q1. Which mosquito is the principal vector of bancroftian lymphatic filariasis in many Indian settings?

**Difficulty:** Easy | **Priority:** High | **Topic:** Lymphatic filariasis elimination | **Type:** Original practice

- **A.** Culex quinquefasciatus
- **B.** Phlebotomus argentipes
- **C.** Anopheles stephensi as the principal vector everywhere
- **D.** Aedes aegypti as the principal vector everywhere

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: A. Culex quinquefasciatus**

Culex quinquefasciatus India mein bancroftian filariasis ka major vector hai.

**Baaki options kyun galat hain?**

- **B.** Phlebotomus sandfly kala-azar transmit karta hai.
- **C.** Anopheles species kuch settings mein filarial vectors ho sakti hain, lekin yeh India ka principal general answer nahi hai.
- **D.** Aedes kuch global settings mein LF transmit kar sakta hai, par Indian bancroftian LF ka main vector yahan Culex hai.

**Exam clue:** Match disease and local vector ecology.

</details>
