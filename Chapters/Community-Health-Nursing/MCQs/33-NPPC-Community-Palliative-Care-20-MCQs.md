### Q1. What is the central aim of the National Programme for Palliative Care?

**Difficulty:** Easy | **Priority:** High | **Topic:** NPPC community palliative care | **Type:** Original practice

- **A.** Exclusive provision of intensive care beds
- **B.** Accessible quality symptom relief and palliative care across health-care levels
- **C.** Cessation of all disease-directed treatment
- **D.** Universal admission to specialist hospices

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: B. Accessible quality symptom relief and palliative care across health-care levels**

NPPC ka aim pain aur other distress relief ko health system mein accessible banana hai.

**Baaki options kyun galat hain?**

- **A.** ICU beds alone palliative care access establish nahi karte.
- **C.** Palliative care disease-directed treatment ke saath bhi di ja sakti hai.
- **D.** Har patient ko hospice admission ki need nahi hoti.

**Exam clue:** Palliative care belongs across the care continuum.

</details>
