### Q1. What is the other name for kala-azar affecting internal organs?

**Difficulty:** Easy | **Priority:** High | **Topic:** Kala-azar elimination and surveillance | **Type:** Original practice

- **A.** Visceral leishmaniasis
- **B.** Cutaneous larva migrans
- **C.** Lymphatic filariasis
- **D.** Japanese encephalitis

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: A. Visceral leishmaniasis**

Kala-azar ko visceral leishmaniasis bhi kehte hain kyunki infection internal organs ko affect karta hai.

**Baaki options kyun galat hain?**

- **B.** Cutaneous larva migrans skin migration syndrome hai.
- **C.** Filariasis lymphatic nematode infection hai.
- **D.** Japanese encephalitis viral neurological infection hai.

**Exam clue:** Visceral means involving internal organs.

</details>

### Q3. What does PKDL stand for?

**Difficulty:** Easy | **Priority:** High | **Topic:** Kala-azar elimination and surveillance | **Type:** Original practice

- **A.** Primary kidney disease lesion
- **B.** Post-kala-azar dermal leishmaniasis
- **C.** Peripheral kala-azar diagnostic laboratory
- **D.** Post-kala-azar dietary loss

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: B. Post-kala-azar dermal leishmaniasis**

PKDL kala-azar ke baad develop ho sakne wali dermal leishmaniasis ko describe karta hai.

**Baaki options kyun galat hain?**

- **A.** Kidney disease iska expansion nahi hai.
- **C.** Diagnostic laboratory PKDL ka full form nahi hai.
- **D.** Dietary loss PKDL ka meaning nahi hai.

**Exam clue:** Dermal refers to the skin.

</details>

### Q4. Which organ enlargement is called splenomegaly?

**Difficulty:** Easy | **Priority:** High | **Topic:** Kala-azar elimination and surveillance | **Type:** Original practice

- **A.** Enlargement of the liver
- **B.** Enlargement of the thyroid
- **C.** Enlargement of the heart
- **D.** Enlargement of the spleen

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: D. Enlargement of the spleen**

Splenomegaly spleen ke size badhne ko kehte hain, jo kala-azar mein common finding ho sakti hai.

**Baaki options kyun galat hain?**

- **A.** Liver enlargement hepatomegaly kehlata hai.
- **B.** Thyroid enlargement goitre ho sakta hai.
- **C.** Heart enlargement cardiomegaly kehlata hai.

**Exam clue:** Recognize the clinical terminology.

</details>

### Q6. Which frontline community worker can help identify and refer suspected kala-azar cases in endemic villages?

**Difficulty:** Easy | **Priority:** High | **Topic:** Kala-azar elimination and surveillance | **Type:** Original practice

- **A.** ASHA
- **B.** Radiotherapy technician
- **C.** Dental laboratory technician
- **D.** Blood bank storekeeper

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: A. ASHA**

ASHA village-level case identification aur designated facility referral mein support kar sakti hai.

**Baaki options kyun galat hain?**

- **C.** Dental technician dental appliances banane mein involved hota hai.

**Exam clue:** Community detection uses frontline link workers.

</details>
