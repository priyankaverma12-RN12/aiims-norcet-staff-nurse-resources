### Q1. What is the full form of RKSK?

**Difficulty:** Easy | **Priority:** High | **Topic:** RKSK adolescent health services | **Type:** Original practice

- **A.** Rashtriya Kishor Swasthya Karyakram
- **B.** Rashtriya Kusth Suraksha Kendra
- **C.** Rural Kidney Screening Karyakram
- **D.** Rashtriya Krishi Shiksha Karyakram

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: A. Rashtriya Kishor Swasthya Karyakram**

RKSK Rashtriya Kishor Swasthya Karyakram hai, adolescent health ke liye national programme.

**Baaki options kyun galat hain?**

- **B.** Kusth-related name leprosy scope suggest karta hai, RKSK expansion nahi.
- **C.** Kidney screening separate clinical service hai.
- **D.** Krishi shiksha agriculture education hai, adolescent health programme nahi.

**Exam clue:** Kishor refers to adolescents.

</details>

### Q5. Which life stage is characterised by puberty and the transition from childhood toward adulthood?
