### Q1. Which bacterium causes tuberculosis?

**Difficulty:** Easy | **Priority:** High | **Topic:** NTEP tuberculosis programme | **Type:** Original practice

- **A.** Mycobacterium tuberculosis
- **B.** Mycobacterium leprae
- **C.** Vibrio cholerae
- **D.** Clostridium tetani

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: A. Mycobacterium tuberculosis**

TB ka main causative organism Mycobacterium tuberculosis hai.

**Baaki options kyun galat hain?**

- **B.** M. leprae leprosy cause karta hai.
- **C.** V. cholerae cholera cause karta hai.
- **D.** C. tetani tetanus cause karta hai.

**Exam clue:** Match pathogen to disease.

</details>

### Q2. Which vaccine helps protect young children against severe forms of tuberculosis?

**Difficulty:** Easy | **Priority:** High | **Topic:** NTEP tuberculosis programme | **Type:** Original practice

- **A.** OPV
- **B.** Hepatitis B vaccine
- **C.** MR vaccine
- **D.** BCG

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: D. BCG**

BCG childhood severe TB, especially meningeal aur disseminated TB, ke against protection provide karti hai.

**Baaki options kyun galat hain?**

- **A.** OPV poliomyelitis prevention ke liye hai.
- **B.** Hepatitis B vaccine HBV infection se protect karti hai.
- **C.** MR measles aur rubella ke liye hai.

**Exam clue:** BCG is a TB vaccine, not guaranteed prevention of every TB case.

</details>

### Q3. What does TPT stand for in TB prevention?

**Difficulty:** Easy | **Priority:** High | **Topic:** NTEP tuberculosis programme | **Type:** Original practice

- **A.** Total pulmonary testing
- **B.** Tuberculosis preventive treatment
- **C.** Temporary patient transfer
- **D.** Therapeutic pulse therapy

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: B. Tuberculosis preventive treatment**

TPT eligible at-risk people mein TB disease develop hone ka risk reduce karne ke liye preventive treatment hai.

**Baaki options kyun galat hain?**

- **A.** Pulmonary testing TPT ka expansion nahi hai.
- **C.** Patient transfer administrative process hai.
- **D.** Pulse therapy iska meaning nahi hai.

**Exam clue:** Differentiate prevention from active disease treatment.

</details>

### Q4. Which body site is most commonly affected by tuberculosis disease?

**Difficulty:** Easy | **Priority:** High | **Topic:** NTEP tuberculosis programme | **Type:** Original practice

- **A.** Lungs
- **B.** Hair shaft
- **C.** Tooth enamel
- **D.** Nail plate

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: A. Lungs**

Pulmonary TB sabse common form hai, though TB other organs mein bhi ho sakti hai.

**Baaki options kyun galat hain?**

- **B.** Hair shaft TB ka usual primary site nahi hai.
- **C.** Tooth enamel common TB site nahi hai.
- **D.** Nail plate typical primary site nahi hai.

**Exam clue:** Pulmonary means lung-related.

</details>

### Q5. Which finding is a recognized symptom that may prompt evaluation for TB?

**Difficulty:** Easy | **Priority:** High | **Topic:** NTEP tuberculosis programme | **Type:** Original practice

- **A.** Normal appetite with stable weight
- **B.** Healed superficial scratch
- **C.** Unexplained weight loss
- **D.** Normal nail growth

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: C. Unexplained weight loss**

Unexplained weight loss TB ke possible symptoms mein se hai, diagnosis testing se confirm hota hai.

**Baaki options kyun galat hain?**

- **A.** Stable weight aur normal appetite alone TB suspicion nahi banate.
- **B.** Healed scratch characteristic TB symptom nahi hai.
- **D.** Normal nail growth TB indicator nahi hai.

**Exam clue:** Symptoms guide evaluation, not diagnosis.

</details>

### Q6. What is meant by drug-susceptible tuberculosis?

**Difficulty:** Easy | **Priority:** High | **Topic:** NTEP tuberculosis programme | **Type:** Original practice

- **A.** TB that requires no medicines
- **B.** TB transmitted exclusively through food
- **C.** TB caused by organisms susceptible to the relevant anti-TB medicines
- **D.** TB limited to the skin

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: C. TB caused by organisms susceptible to the relevant anti-TB medicines**

Drug-susceptible ka matlab relevant drugs organism par effective ho sakte hain, treatment phir bhi needed hai.

**Baaki options kyun galat hain?**

- **A.** Susceptibility treatment ki need eliminate nahi karti.
- **B.** Food-only transmission drug susceptibility define nahi karta.
- **D.** Skin location resistance status establish nahi karti.

**Exam clue:** Susceptibility describes drug response.

</details>
