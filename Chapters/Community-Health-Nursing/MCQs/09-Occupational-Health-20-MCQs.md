### Q1. Which term describes a workplace condition with potential to cause harm?

**Difficulty:** Easy | **Priority:** High | **Topic:** Occupational health | **Type:** Original practice

- **A.** Health insurance claim
- **B.** Clinical diagnosis only
- **C.** Treatment outcome
- **D.** Occupational hazard

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: D. Occupational hazard**

Hazard aisi condition ya agent hai jo harm cause kar sakta hai.

**Baaki options kyun galat hain?**

- **A.** Insurance claim financial documentation hai, hazard nahi.
- **B.** Diagnosis disease identify karta hai, potential harmful source nahi.
- **C.** Treatment outcome care ke baad result batata hai.

**Exam clue:** Hazard aisi condition ya agent hai jo harm cause kar sakta hai.

</details>
