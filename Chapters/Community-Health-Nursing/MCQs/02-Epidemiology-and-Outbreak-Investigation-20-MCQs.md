### Q1. Which measure describes all existing cases of a disease at a specified point in time?

**Difficulty:** Easy | **Priority:** Medium | **Topic:** Epidemiology and Outbreak Investigation | **Type:** Original practice

- **A.** Attack rate
- **B.** Point prevalence
- **C.** Incidence density
- **D.** Case fatality proportion

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: B. Point prevalence**

- **A.** Attack rate outbreak period ke naye cases ko population at risk se relate karta hai.
- **B.** Point prevalence ek particular time par existing cases ka proportion batati hai.
- **C.** Incidence density naye cases ko person-time se divide karti hai.
- **D.** Case fatality cases mein disease se deaths ka proportion hai.

**Exam clue:** Existing cases ke liye prevalence yaad rakho.

</details>

### Q2. A chronic disease becomes less fatal, but new cases continue at the same rate. Which change in prevalence is most likely, other factors remaining stable?

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Priority:** High | **Topic:** Epidemiology and Outbreak Investigation | **Type:** Original practice

- **A.** Prevalence remains zero regardless of incidence
- **B.** Prevalence becomes identical to case fatality
- **C.** Prevalence increases because disease duration increases
- **D.** Prevalence falls because survival increases

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: C. Prevalence increases because disease duration increases**

- **A.** Naye cases continue ho rahe hain, isliye prevalence zero nahi hogi.
- **B.** Case fatality severity ka indicator hai, prevalence disease burden ka.
- **C.** Patients zyada time disease ke saath jeete hain, isliye existing cases ka pool badh sakta hai.
- **D.** Better survival se existing cases turant kam nahi hote.

**Exam clue:** Duration badhe toh stable incidence ke saath prevalence badh sakti hai.

</details>

### Q4. In a cohort of 120 exposed people, 36 develop illness. Among 180 unexposed people, 18 develop illness. What is the risk ratio?

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

### Q6. A case-control study includes 60 exposed cases, 20 unexposed cases, 30 exposed controls and 40 unexposed controls. What is the exposure odds ratio?

- **B.** 1.5
- **C.** 4
- **D.** 2

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: C. 4**

- **A.** 0.25 correct odds ratio ka reciprocal hai.
- **B.** 1.5 exposed aur unexposed controls ko galat direction mein compare karta hai.

### Q10. An epidemic curve shows most cases appearing within one incubation period after a single shared meal. Which pattern is most likely?

### Q18. A study reports a risk ratio of 1.8 with a 95% confidence interval of 0.9 to 3.6. Which interpretation is best?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Priority:** High | **Topic:** Epidemiology and Outbreak Investigation | **Type:** Original practice

- **A.** The effect is definitely causal
- **B.** The point estimate proves statistical significance regardless of the interval
- **C.** The interval includes the null value of 1, so statistical significance at the usual 5% two-sided level is not established
- **D.** The exposure is proven protective

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: C. The interval includes the null value of 1, so statistical significance at the usual 5% two-sided level is not established**

- **A.** Association aur statistical uncertainty se causation establish nahi hoti.
- **B.** Point estimate alone precision aur uncertainty nahi batata.
- **C.** Risk ratio ka null value 1 hai aur interval usko include karta hai, isliye usual criterion par significance establish nahi hui.
- **D.** Point estimate 1 se bada hai aur interval protective effect ko prove nahi karta.

**Exam clue:** Ratio measure ke confidence interval mein null value 1 dekho.

</details>
