### Q1. What was the original name of NIDDCP before its renaming in 1992?

**Difficulty:** Easy | **Priority:** High | **Topic:** NIDDCP iodized salt and surveillance | **Type:** Original practice

- **A.** National Nutritional Anaemia Control Programme
- **B.** National Fluorosis Control Programme
- **C.** National Oral Health Programme
- **D.** National Goitre Control Programme

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: D. National Goitre Control Programme**

NIDDCP ka earlier name National Goitre Control Programme tha, jo 1992 mein broaden hua.

**Baaki options kyun galat hain?**

- **A.** Anaemia programme iron aur other causes of anaemia se related hai.
- **B.** Fluorosis programme excess fluoride exposure address karta hai.
- **C.** Oral health programme dental services strengthen karta hai.

**Exam clue:** Know the predecessor programme.

</details>

### Q5. What does TGR stand for in historical iodine-deficiency surveillance?

### Q6. Which chemical form is commonly added to edible salt in the Indian iodated-salt programme?

**Difficulty:** Easy | **Priority:** High | **Topic:** NIDDCP iodized salt and surveillance | **Type:** Original practice

- **A.** Potassium permanganate
- **B.** Sodium fluoride
- **C.** Potassium iodate
- **D.** Ferrous sulphate

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: C. Potassium iodate**

Potassium iodate salt fortification mein iodine source ke roop mein use kiya jaata hai.

**Baaki options kyun galat hain?**

- **A.** Potassium permanganate iodine source nahi hai.
- **B.** Sodium fluoride fluoride supply karta hai, iodine nahi.
- **D.** Ferrous sulphate iron source hai, iodine source nahi.

**Exam clue:** Match the fortificant to iodine delivery.

</details>
