### Q1. What are the two main pillars of India's lymphatic filariasis elimination strategy?

**Difficulty:** Easy | **Priority:** High | **Topic:** Lymphatic filariasis elimination and community care | **Type:** Original practice

- **A.** Routine vaccination and universal surgery
- **B.** Mass drug administration and morbidity management with disability prevention
- **C.** Case isolation and contact quarantine
- **D.** Antibiotic distribution and school closure

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: B. Mass drug administration and morbidity management with disability prevention**

MDA transmission kam karti hai aur MMDP existing disability ke saath jeene wale patients ko support karti hai.

**Baaki options kyun galat hain?**

- **A.** LF ke liye routine vaccine pillar nahi hai aur sab patients ko surgery nahi chahiye.
- **C.** Mosquito-borne LF mein routine contact quarantine strategy nahi.
- **D.** School closure aur indiscriminate antibiotics elimination pillars nahi hain.

**Exam clue:** Separate transmission interruption from disability care.

</details>
