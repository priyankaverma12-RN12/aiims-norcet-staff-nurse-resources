### Q1. What does the term dental caries mean?

**Difficulty:** Easy | **Priority:** High | **Topic:** NOHP community oral health programme | **Type:** Original practice

- **A.** Gum recession alone
- **B.** Tooth decay
- **C.** Loss of hearing
- **D.** Inflammation of the tonsils

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: B. Tooth decay**

Dental caries tooth structure ki decay ko kehte hain, jo biofilm aur sugar exposure se associated hai.

**Baaki options kyun galat hain?**

- **A.** Gum recession gum tissue position se related hai.
- **C.** Hearing loss ear function issue hai.
- **D.** Tonsillitis tonsil inflammation hai.

**Exam clue:** Caries is tooth decay.

</details>

### Q2. What is gingivitis?

**Difficulty:** Easy | **Priority:** High | **Topic:** NOHP community oral health programme | **Type:** Original practice

- **A.** Inflammation of the retina
- **B.** Inflammation of the middle ear
- **C.** Fracture of the tooth root
- **D.** Inflammation of the gums

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: D. Inflammation of the gums**

Gingivitis gingiva ya gums ki inflammation hai.

**Baaki options kyun galat hain?**

- **A.** Retinal inflammation different eye disorder hai.
- **B.** Middle ear inflammation otitis media hai.
- **C.** Root fracture traumatic dental injury hai, gingivitis nahi.

**Exam clue:** Gingiva means gums.

</details>

### Q3. What does edentulism mean?

**Difficulty:** Easy | **Priority:** High | **Topic:** NOHP community oral health programme | **Type:** Original practice

- **A.** Loss of all natural teeth
- **B.** Presence of extra teeth
- **C.** Tooth enamel discoloration alone
- **D.** Bleeding gums alone

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: A. Loss of all natural teeth**

Complete edentulism natural teeth ke total loss ko kehte hain.

**Baaki options kyun galat hain?**

- **B.** Extra teeth supernumerary teeth ho sakte hain.
- **C.** Discoloration alone total tooth loss nahi hai.
- **D.** Bleeding gums periodontal issue suggest kar sakte hain, edentulism define nahi karte.

**Exam clue:** Recognize complete tooth loss.

</details>
