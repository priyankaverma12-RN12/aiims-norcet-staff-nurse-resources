### Q1. Which date marks the launch of the PMNDP national IT portal, distinct from the earlier clinical programme rollout?

**Difficulty:** Easy | **Priority:** Medium | **Topic:** PMNDP service access | **Type:** Original practice

- **A.** 15 August 1947
- **B.** 26 January 1950
- **C.** 5 May 2022
- **D.** 7 April 2016

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: C. 5 May 2022**

- **A.** 15 August 1947 Indian independence date hai, portal launch nahi.
- **B.** 26 January 1950 Republic Day ka historical date hai, dialysis portal launch nahi.
- **C.** PMNDP introduction ke according national IT portal 5 May 2022 ko launch hua, clinical programme isse pehle start ho chuka tha.
- **D.** 7 April 2016 clinical programme rollout date hai, IT portal launch nahi.

**Exam clue:** Differentiate the 2016 programme and the 2022 portal.

### Q4. In the PMNDP health-record linkage pathway, what is necessary before sharing a patient's records across authorized systems?

**Difficulty:** Easy | **Priority:** Medium | **Topic:** PMNDP service access | **Type:** Original practice

- **A.** Payment to an unrelated private agent
- **B.** Informed consent of the patient
- **C.** Public display of the patient's identity
- **D.** Permission from another dialysis patient

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: B. Informed consent of the patient**

- **A.** Unrelated agent ko payment informed consent nahi hai.
- **B.** Patient ka informed consent health records ke authorised exchange ke liye important hai.
- **C.** Public identity display confidentiality violate kar sakta hai.
- **D.** Another patient apne behalf par consent de sakta hai, is patient ke behalf par nahi.

**Exam clue:** Health-record exchange requires patient consent.

</details>

### Q5. Which practical condition must be checked when a registered dialysis patient seeks a slot at another participating centre?

**Difficulty:** Easy | **Priority:** Medium | **Topic:** PMNDP service access | **Type:** Original practice

- **A.** The patient's preferred political party
- **B.** Whether the patient owns the dialysis machine
- **C.** Availability of a suitable dialysis slot
- **D.** The patient's school examination marks

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: C. Availability of a suitable dialysis slot**

- **A.** Political affiliation care eligibility ka clinical factor nahi hai.
- **B.** Patient ko dialysis machine own karna programme service ke liye necessary nahi hai.
- **C.** Receiving centre mein suitable available slot hona continuity arrange karne ke liye essential hai.
- **D.** School marks treatment booking se relevant nahi hain.

**Exam clue:** Portability still depends on suitable capacity.

</details>

- **C.** Essential sanitation reporting district

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: D. End-stage renal disease**
