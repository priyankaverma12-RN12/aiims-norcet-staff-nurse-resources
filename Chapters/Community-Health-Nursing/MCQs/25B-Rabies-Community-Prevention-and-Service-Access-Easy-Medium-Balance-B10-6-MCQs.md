### Q1. Rabies primarily affects which organ system?

**Difficulty:** Easy | **Priority:** High | **Topic:** Rabies community prevention and service access | **Type:** Original practice

- **A.** Skeletal system alone
- **B.** Endocrine glands alone
- **C.** Central nervous system
- **D.** Urinary collecting system alone

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: C. Central nervous system**

Rabies virus central nervous system ko infect karta hai aur severe neurological disease cause karta hai.

**Baaki options kyun galat hain?**

- **A.** Skeleton iska primary target nahi hai.
- **B.** Endocrine glands alone rabies ka main target nahi hain.
- **D.** Urinary collecting system rabies ka characteristic site nahi hai.

**Exam clue:** Rabies is a neurotropic viral disease.

</details>

### Q3. Which animal is responsible for the vast majority of human rabies cases worldwide?

- **A.** Cow
- **B.** Goat
- **C.** Chicken
- **D.** Dog

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: D. Dog**

Globally infected dogs human rabies transmission ka main source hain.

**Baaki options kyun galat hain?**

- **A.** Cattle rabies contract kar sakti hain, par major global human source nahi.
- **B.** Goats bhi mammals hain, lekin main reservoir source nahi.
- **C.** Chickens birds hain aur typical rabies source nahi hain.

**Exam clue:** Dog-mediated rabies is the principal public-health concern.

</details>

### Q6. What is the name of the global strategy aiming to end human deaths from dog-mediated rabies by 2030?

- **B.** End TB Strategy
- **C.** Global Polio Eradication Initiative
- **D.** Global Malaria Technical Strategy

**Correct: A. Zero by 30**

**Baaki options kyun galat hain?**

**Exam clue:** A target is not proof of achievement.
