### Q1. Which system provides continuous legal registration of births and deaths in India?

**Difficulty:** Easy | **Priority:** Medium | **Topic:** Demography and Vital Statistics | **Type:** Original practice

- **A.** Sample Registration System alone
- **B.** National Family Health Survey
- **C.** Decennial census alone
- **D.** Civil Registration System

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: D. Civil Registration System**

- **A.** SRS sample areas se vital rates estimate karta hai, legal registration system nahi hai.
- **B.** NFHS household survey hai jo health aur family indicators collect karta hai.
- **C.** Census population enumeration hai, continuous event registration nahi.
- **D.** CRS births aur deaths ko continuous legal registration ke through record karta hai.

**Exam clue:** CRS legal registration hai aur SRS sample estimates deta hai.

</details>

### Q3. A block has 720 live births during a year and a mid-year population of 48,000. What is the crude birth rate per 1,000 population?

- **B.** 15
- **C.** 1.5
- **D.** 66.7

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: B. 15**

- **A.** 72 per 1,000 ke liye 3,456 births chahiye.
- **B.** 720 ko 48,000 se divide karke 1,000 se multiply karne par 15 milta hai.
- **C.** 1.5 per 1,000 correct scaling se tenfold kam hai.
- **D.** 66.7 population-to-births ratio ke kareeb hai, birth rate nahi.

**Exam clue:** CBR ka denominator mid-year total population hai.

</details>

### Q5. A district records a crude birth rate of 19 and crude death rate of 7 per 1,000 population per year. Ignoring migration, what is the natural increase as a percentage per year?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Priority:** High | **Topic:** Demography and Vital Statistics | **Type:** Original practice

- **A.** 12%
- **B.** 2.6%
- **C.** 0.12%
- **D.** 1.2%

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: D. 1.2%**

- **A.** 12 per 1,000 ko 12% samajhna unit error hai.
- **B.** 2.6% birth aur death rates ko add karne se related hai, natural increase nahi.
- **C.** 0.12% correct result se tenfold kam hai.
- **D.** 19 minus 7 se 12 per 1,000 milta hai, jo 1.2 per 100 yani 1.2% hai.

**Exam clue:** Natural increase is births minus deaths, not including migration.

</details>

### Q20. Two districts have the same estimated infant mortality rate but one has very incomplete death registration. Which interpretation is safest?

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Priority:** High | **Topic:** Demography and Vital Statistics | **Type:** Original practice

- **A.** Declare the district with incomplete records has no infant deaths
- **B.** Use only the number of registered deaths without considering births
- **C.** Assess data quality and estimation methods before concluding the districts have equal underlying risk
- **D.** Assume equal data quality because the numerical estimates match

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: C. Assess data quality and estimation methods before concluding the districts have equal underlying risk**

- **A.** Incomplete records ka matlab missing events ho sakte hain, zero deaths nahi.
- **B.** Birth denominator ke bina death counts comparable nahi hain.
- **C.** Incomplete registration se raw counts aur estimates ki uncertainty badh sakti hai, isliye methods compare karna important hai.
- **D.** Matching numbers data quality equality prove nahi karte.

**Exam clue:** Vital indicators ke saath completeness aur uncertainty dekho.

</details>
