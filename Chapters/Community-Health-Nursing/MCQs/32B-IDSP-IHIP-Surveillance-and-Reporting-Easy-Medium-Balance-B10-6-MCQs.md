### Q1. What is the full form of IHIP in India's disease surveillance system?

**Difficulty:** Easy | **Priority:** High | **Topic:** IDSP and IHIP surveillance reporting | **Type:** Original practice

- **A.** Indian Hospital Insurance Programme
- **B.** Integrated Health Information Platform
- **C.** Integrated Household Immunization Project
- **D.** International Health Inspection Protocol

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: B. Integrated Health Information Platform**

IHIP digital platform health surveillance information integrate aur report karne mein help karta hai.

**Baaki options kyun galat hain?**

- **A.** Hospital insurance programme IHIP ka full form nahi hai.
- **C.** Household immunization project iska official expansion nahi hai.
- **D.** Inspection protocol IHIP ka meaning nahi hai.

**Exam clue:** IHIP is a digital information platform.

</details>

### Q3. What does SSU stand for in the IDSP organisational structure?

- **B.** Special Surgical Unit
- **C.** School Screening Unit
- **D.** Salt Sampling Utility

### Q6. Which IDSP laboratory tier supports referral testing of outbreak-related samples beyond district laboratory capacity?

**Difficulty:** Easy | **Priority:** High | **Topic:** IDSP and IHIP surveillance reporting | **Type:** Original practice

- **A.** Anganwadi centre
- **B.** District pharmacy store
- **C.** Village nutrition committee
- **D.** State Referral Laboratory

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: D. State Referral Laboratory**

State Referral Laboratory district capacity ke beyond appropriate outbreak sample testing support karti hai.

**Baaki options kyun galat hain?**

- **A.** Anganwadi nutrition aur early childhood services provide karta hai.
- **B.** Pharmacy store medicines manage karta hai, reference testing nahi.

</details>
