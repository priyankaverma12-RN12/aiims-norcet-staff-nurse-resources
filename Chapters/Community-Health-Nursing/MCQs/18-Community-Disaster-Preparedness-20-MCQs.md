### Q1. Which phase of disaster management includes conducting evacuation drills before a predicted hazard season?

**Difficulty:** Easy | **Priority:** High | **Topic:** Community disaster preparedness | **Type:** Original practice

- **A.** Preparedness
- **B.** Response
- **C.** Recovery
- **D.** Post-event rehabilitation

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: A. Preparedness**

Drill pehle se roles aur routes practice karne mein help karti hai, isliye preparedness activity hai.

**Baaki options kyun galat hain?**

- **B.** Response actual emergency ke dauran immediate action hai.
- **C.** Recovery disrupted services restore karne se related hai.
- **D.** Post-event rehabilitation disaster ke baad functioning restore karti hai.

**Exam clue:** Preparedness builds readiness before impact.

</details>
