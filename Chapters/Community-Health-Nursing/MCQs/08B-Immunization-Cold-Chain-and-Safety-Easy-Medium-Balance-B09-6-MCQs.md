### Q1. What is the usual temperature range for routine refrigerated vaccines at service-level storage?

**Difficulty:** Easy | **Priority:** High | **Topic:** Immunization cold chain and safety | **Type:** Original practice

- **A.** +15°C to +25°C
- **B.** -40°C to -30°C
- **C.** +30°C to +40°C
- **D.** +2°C to +8°C

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: D. +2°C to +8°C**

Routine refrigerated vaccines ko aam taur par 2 se 8 degree Celsius range mein rakhte hain, specific product guidance follow karte hue.

**Baaki options kyun galat hain?**

- **A.** Room temperature routine refrigerated storage range nahi hai.
- **B.** Deep freezing many vaccines ko damage kar sakti hai.
- **C.** High heat potency loss cause kar sakti hai.

**Exam clue:** Remember the routine refrigerated range.

</details>

### Q4. Which routine vaccine is freeze-sensitive?

**Difficulty:** Easy | **Priority:** High | **Topic:** Immunization cold chain and safety | **Type:** Original practice

- **A.** OPV
- **B.** Unreconstituted lyophilized BCG vaccine
- **C.** Hepatitis B vaccine
- **D.** Unreconstituted lyophilized measles vaccine

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: C. Hepatitis B vaccine**

Hepatitis B adsorbed vaccine freezing se damage ho sakti hai.

**Baaki options kyun galat hain?**

- **A.** OPV heat-sensitive hoti hai aur formulation guidance ke mutabik freezing tolerate karti hai.
- **B.** Unreconstituted freeze-dried BCG freezing-sensitive liquid adsorbed vaccine jaisi nahi hai.
- **D.** Unreconstituted freeze-dried measles vaccine same freeze-sensitive category mein nahi aati; diluent rules alag hain.

**Exam clue:** Differentiate adsorbed liquid from freeze-dried vaccines.

</details>

### Q5. What does AEFI stand for?

**Difficulty:** Easy | **Priority:** High | **Topic:** Immunization cold chain and safety | **Type:** Original practice

- **A.** Approved Emergency Facility Inspection
- **B.** Annual Evaluation of Fever Incidence
- **C.** Adverse Event Following Immunization
- **D.** Antigen Effectiveness Follow-up Index

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: C. Adverse Event Following Immunization**

AEFI vaccine administration ke baad occur hone wala adverse medical event hai, causality automatically prove nahi hoti.

**Baaki options kyun galat hain?**

- **A.** Facility inspection iska meaning nahi hai.
- **B.** Fever incidence survey AEFI nahi hai.
- **D.** Effectiveness index AEFI ka official expansion nahi hai.

**Exam clue:** Following does not necessarily mean caused by.

</details>

- **D.** Uninsulated shopping bag
