### Q1. Which unit is the primary focus of family health nursing?

**Difficulty:** Easy | **Priority:** High | **Topic:** Family Health Nursing | **Type:** Original practice

- **A.** Only the person admitted to hospital
- **B.** The family as a unit of care
- **C.** Only the oldest male member
- **D.** Only individuals with communicable disease

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: B. The family as a unit of care**

Family ke members ek dusre ki health aur care ko affect karte hain, isliye pura family unit assessment ka hissa hai.

**Baaki options kyun galat hain?**

- **A.** Hospital patient important hai par family context bhi care ko influence karta hai.
- **C.** Age aur gender se family assessment ko ek member tak restrict nahi karte.
- **D.** Chronic disease aur preventive care bhi family nursing mein aate hain.

**Exam clue:** Family ke members ek dusre ki health aur care ko affect karte hain, isliye pura family unit assessment ka hissa hai.

</details>
