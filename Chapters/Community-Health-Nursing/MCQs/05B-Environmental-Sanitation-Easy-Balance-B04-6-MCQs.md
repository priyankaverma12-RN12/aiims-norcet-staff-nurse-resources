### Q2. In the WHO/UNICEF monitoring framework, which feature is required for safely managed household drinking-water service in addition to an improved source and absence of specified contamination?

**Difficulty:** Easy | **Priority:** Medium | **Topic:** Environmental sanitation and safe water | **Type:** Original practice

- **A.** Water collected from an unprotected pond
- **B.** Water on the premises and available when needed
- **C.** A source more than one hour away
- **D.** Water supplied only on examination days

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: B. Water on the premises and available when needed**

- **A.** Unprotected pond improved water source nahi hai.
- **B.** Safely managed service ke liye ghar ke premises par water available hona aur zaroorat ke samay milna chahiye.
- **C.** Door source safely managed access ki on-premises condition fulfill nahi karta.
- **D.** Occasional supply availability when needed establish nahi karti.

**Exam clue:** Improved source, on premises, available and safe.

</details>

### Q3. Which chemical occurring naturally in some groundwater can cause chronic poisoning after prolonged excessive exposure?

**Difficulty:** Easy | **Priority:** Medium | **Topic:** Environmental sanitation and safe water | **Type:** Original practice

- **A.** Nitrogen gas
- **B.** Water vapour
- **C.** Arsenic
- **D.** Dissolved oxygen

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: C. Arsenic**

- **A.** Nitrogen gas arsenic jaisi toxic groundwater contaminant nahi hai.
- **B.** Water vapour paani ka gaseous form hai, arsenic nahi.
- **C.** Arsenic kuch groundwater sources mein naturally mil sakta hai aur long-term excessive intake health damage kar sakta hai.
- **D.** Dissolved oxygen normal water quality component hai, arsenic poisoning ka cause nahi.

**Exam clue:** Arsenic is a priority chemical groundwater hazard.

</details>

### Q4. What does the acronym WASH represent in community health?

**Difficulty:** Easy | **Priority:** Medium | **Topic:** Environmental sanitation and safe water | **Type:** Original practice

- **A.** Waste, antibiotics and surgical handover
- **B.** Water, air, soil and housing only
- **C.** Ward assessment and safety hierarchy
- **D.** Water, sanitation and hygiene

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: D. Water, sanitation and hygiene**

- **A.** Antibiotics aur surgical handover WASH ki expansion nahi hain.
- **B.** Air aur housing environmental health mein important hain par WASH acronym alag hai.
- **C.** Ward assessment phrase WASH ka recognised full form nahi hai.
- **D.** WASH ka matlab water, sanitation aur hygiene hai, jo infection prevention aur health ke liye important hain.

**Exam clue:** WASH combines three basic environmental health services.

</details>

### Q5. Which indicator classifies drinking water from an improved source taking more than 30 minutes for a round trip to collect under the WHO/UNICEF service ladder?

**Difficulty:** Easy | **Priority:** Medium | **Topic:** Environmental sanitation and safe water | **Type:** Original practice

- **A.** Limited drinking-water service
- **B.** Safely managed drinking-water service
- **C.** Surface-water service
- **D.** Basic drinking-water service

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: A. Limited drinking-water service**

- **A.** Improved source se water collection round trip 30 minutes se zyada ho to limited service classify hoti hai.
- **B.** Safely managed service mein on-premises aur availability criteria bhi chahiye.
- **C.** Surface-water category untreated rivers, lakes aur similar sources ko describe karti hai.
- **D.** Basic service mein improved source tak round trip 30 minutes ya kam hota hai.

**Exam clue:** Basic versus limited: 30-minute collection threshold.

</details>

### Q6. Which stage is necessary for safe management of faecal sludge removed from a household pit latrine?

**Difficulty:** Easy | **Priority:** Medium | **Topic:** Environmental sanitation and safe water | **Type:** Original practice

- **A.** Mixing sludge directly with drinking-water supplies
- **B.** Safe treatment or disposal after emptying
- **C.** Dumping into an open stream
- **D.** Leaving untreated sludge beside a playground

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: B. Safe treatment or disposal after emptying**

- **A.** Drinking water mein sludge milana faecal contamination ka serious hazard hai.
- **B.** Pit empty karne ke baad sludge ko safe transport aur treatment ya disposal chahiye, warna infection risk shift ho jata hai.
- **C.** Open stream mein dumping water contamination create kar sakti hai.
- **D.** Playground ke paas untreated sludge se direct exposure risk hota hai.

**Exam clue:** Sanitation extends beyond the toilet to safe sludge management.

</details>
