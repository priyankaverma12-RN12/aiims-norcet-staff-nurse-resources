### Q1. What does HMIS stand for?

**Difficulty:** Easy | **Priority:** High | **Topic:** Community health records and HMIS | **Type:** Original practice

- **A.** Hospital Medicine Inspection Service
- **B.** Household Mortality Intervention Scheme
- **C.** Health Management Information System
- **D.** Health Manpower Immunization Schedule

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: C. Health Management Information System**

HMIS routine health data collection, reporting aur management support karta hai.

**Baaki options kyun galat hain?**

- **A.** Medicine inspection iska full form nahi hai.
- **B.** Mortality scheme HMIS ka expansion nahi hai.
- **D.** Immunization schedule separate programme tool hai.

**Exam clue:** Recall the routine information system acronym.

</details>

### Q2. What does RHIS stand for in WHO health information guidance?

**Difficulty:** Easy | **Priority:** High | **Topic:** Community health records and HMIS | **Type:** Original practice

- **A.** Rapid Hospital Infection Survey
- **B.** Regional Health Insurance Scheme
- **C.** Routine Health Information System
- **D.** Rural Household Immunization Session

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: C. Routine Health Information System**

RHIS routine facility aur community service data collect aur use karta hai.

**Baaki options kyun galat hain?**

- **A.** Infection survey ek specific activity hai, RHIS expansion nahi.
- **B.** Insurance scheme financial protection tool hai.
- **D.** Immunization session service delivery activity hai.

**Exam clue:** Routine means repeatedly collected data.

</details>

- **C.** Multiplier
- **D.** Unit of time

**Baaki options kyun galat hain?**

### Q4. Which simple graphical format is best for showing monthly clinic attendance over a year?

**Difficulty:** Easy | **Priority:** High | **Topic:** Community health records and HMIS | **Type:** Original practice

- **A.** Pie chart of one month's categories
- **B.** Single undated photograph
- **C.** Unlabelled scatter of patient names
- **D.** Line graph

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: D. Line graph**

Line graph chronological months ke across trend clearly show karta hai.

**Baaki options kyun galat hain?**

- **A.** Pie chart ek time point ki composition show kar sakta hai, monthly trend nahi.
- **B.** Photograph quantitative trend nahi batati.
- **C.** Patient names graph variables nahi hain aur privacy concern create karte hain.

**Exam clue:** Use line graphs for time trends.

</details>

### Q6. Which data-quality property refers to closeness of a recorded value to the true value?

**Difficulty:** Easy | **Priority:** High | **Topic:** Community health records and HMIS | **Type:** Original practice

- **A.** Accuracy
- **B.** Timeliness alone
- **C.** Completeness alone
- **D.** Accessibility alone

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: A. Accuracy**

Accuracy recorded value ke factual correctness ko describe karti hai.

**Baaki options kyun galat hain?**

- **B.** Timeliness report time par available hone se related hai.
- **C.** Completeness required data filled hone se related hai.
- **D.** Accessibility authorized users ki availability describe karti hai.

**Exam clue:** Differentiate correctness from other quality dimensions.

</details>
