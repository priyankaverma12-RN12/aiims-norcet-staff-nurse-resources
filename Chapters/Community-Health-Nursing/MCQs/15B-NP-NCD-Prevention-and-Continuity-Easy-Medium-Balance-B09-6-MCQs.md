### Q1. Which common behavioural risk factor is shared by several major noncommunicable diseases?

**Difficulty:** Easy | **Priority:** High | **Topic:** NP-NCD prevention and continuity | **Type:** Original practice

- **A.** Blood group O
- **B.** Physical inactivity
- **C.** Hand dominance
- **D.** Birth order

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: B. Physical inactivity**

Physical inactivity diabetes aur cardiovascular disease jaise NCDs ka modifiable risk factor hai.

**Baaki options kyun galat hain?**

- **A.** Blood group lifestyle intervention ka target nahi hai.
- **C.** Hand dominance common modifiable NCD risk factor nahi hai.
- **D.** Birth order routine behavioural NCD risk factor nahi hai.

**Exam clue:** Identify a modifiable behaviour.

</details>

### Q6. Which condition is a noncommunicable disease rather than an infectious disease?
