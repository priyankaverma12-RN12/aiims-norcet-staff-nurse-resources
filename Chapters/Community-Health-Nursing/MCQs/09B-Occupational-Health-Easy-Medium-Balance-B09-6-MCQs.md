### Q1. Which term describes the likelihood and severity of harm from a workplace hazard?

**Difficulty:** Easy | **Priority:** High | **Topic:** Occupational health | **Type:** Original practice

- **A.** Exposure limit alone
- **B.** Risk
- **C.** Rehabilitation
- **D.** Health promotion

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: B. Risk**

Risk hazard se harm ki probability aur severity consider karta hai.

**Baaki options kyun galat hain?**

- **A.** Exposure limit sirf ek reference value hai, risk ka complete concept nahi.
- **C.** Rehabilitation function recovery se related hai.
- **D.** Health promotion wellbeing improve karta hai, risk ka definition nahi hai.

**Exam clue:** Hazard is potential; risk considers likelihood and consequences.

</details>

### Q6. What is the purpose of an occupational incident report?

**Difficulty:** Easy | **Priority:** High | **Topic:** Occupational health | **Type:** Original practice

- **A.** Automatically punish every injured worker
- **B.** Replace immediate medical treatment
- **C.** Keep all hazards permanently secret
- **D.** Document the event for investigation and prevention

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: D. Document the event for investigation and prevention**

Incident report facts preserve karta hai taaki investigation aur prevention improve ho sake.

**Baaki options kyun galat hain?**

- **A.** Automatic punishment learning culture ko damage kar sakta hai.
- **B.** Medical care separately timely provide karni hoti hai.
- **C.** Hazards hide karna prevention ko hinder karta hai.

**Exam clue:** Reporting supports learning and prevention.

</details>
