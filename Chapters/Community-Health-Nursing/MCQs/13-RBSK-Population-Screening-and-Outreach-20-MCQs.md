### Q14. A district has 800 eligible children in a target group, but only 600 have been screened. Of those screened, 48 have a suspected condition. Which pair of measures is correct?

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Exam clue:** Use the correct denominator for each programme indicator.
