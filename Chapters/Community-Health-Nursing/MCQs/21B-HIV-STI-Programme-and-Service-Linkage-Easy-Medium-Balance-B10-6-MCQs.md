### Q1. What does HIV stand for?

**Difficulty:** Easy | **Priority:** High | **Topic:** HIV-STI programme and service linkage | **Type:** Original practice

- **A.** Human influenza variant
- **B.** Hepatic infection virus
- **C.** Human intestinal vibrio
- **D.** Human immunodeficiency virus

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: D. Human immunodeficiency virus**

HIV ka full form human immunodeficiency virus hai, jo immune system ko affect karta hai.

**Baaki options kyun galat hain?**

- **A.** Influenza respiratory virus group hai, HIV ka expansion nahi.
- **B.** Hepatic infection virus HIV ka full form nahi hai.
- **C.** Vibrio bacteria ka genus hai, HIV virus hai.

**Exam clue:** Remember the full name of the pathogen.

</details>

### Q2. Which immune cells are particularly affected by HIV infection?

**Difficulty:** Easy | **Priority:** High | **Topic:** HIV-STI programme and service linkage | **Type:** Original practice

- **A.** Mature red blood cells
- **B.** Platelets alone
- **C.** CD4 T lymphocytes
- **D.** Bone osteocytes

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: C. CD4 T lymphocytes**

HIV mainly CD4 T lymphocytes ko infect karta hai, jisse immune defence weaken ho sakti hai.

**Baaki options kyun galat hain?**

- **A.** Mature RBC oxygen carry karti hain, HIV ka main target nahi.
- **B.** Platelets clotting support karte hain, primary viral target nahi.
- **D.** Osteocytes bone cells hain, HIV ka characteristic target nahi.

**Exam clue:** CD4 count relates to immune function.

</details>

### Q3. What does AIDS represent in the natural course of untreated HIV infection?

**Difficulty:** Easy | **Priority:** High | **Topic:** HIV-STI programme and service linkage | **Type:** Original practice

- **A.** The most advanced stage of HIV infection
- **B.** Every recent exposure before infection
- **C.** A separate bacterial infection
- **D.** An ordinary vaccine reaction

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: A. The most advanced stage of HIV infection**

AIDS HIV infection ka advanced stage hai, jisme immune damage severe ho sakta hai.

**Baaki options kyun galat hain?**

- **B.** Exposure ka matlab infection ya AIDS establish hona nahi.
- **C.** AIDS bacterial disease nahi hai.
- **D.** Vaccine reaction se AIDS define nahi hota.

**Exam clue:** HIV infection and AIDS are not synonymous.

</details>

### Q4. Which global HIV programme target refers to 95% of people living with HIV knowing their HIV status?

**Difficulty:** Easy | **Priority:** High | **Topic:** HIV-STI programme and service linkage | **Type:** Original practice

- **A.** The first 95 of the 95-95-95 targets
- **B.** The second 95
- **C.** The third 95
- **D.** The tuberculosis treatment success target

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: A. The first 95 of the 95-95-95 targets**

First 95 ka focus HIV status diagnosis aur awareness par hai.

**Baaki options kyun galat hain?**

**Exam clue:** The treatment cascade begins with diagnosis.

</details>

### Q6. What does ICTC stand for in India's HIV service network?

**Difficulty:** Easy | **Priority:** High | **Topic:** HIV-STI programme and service linkage | **Type:** Original practice

- **A.** Intensive Cardiac Treatment Centre
- **B.** Integrated Counselling and Testing Centre
- **C.** Infection Control Training Committee
- **D.** Integrated Childhood Tuberculosis Clinic

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: B. Integrated Counselling and Testing Centre**

ICTC Integrated Counselling and Testing Centre hai; yahan HIV counselling, testing aur appropriate referral services provide ki jaati hain.

**Baaki options kyun galat hain?**

- **A.** Cardiac treatment centre HIV testing service ka official expansion nahi hai.
- **C.** Infection control training committee alag health-system function describe karti hai.
- **D.** Childhood TB clinic TB services se related hai, ICTC ka meaning nahi hai.

**Exam clue:** Identify the HIV counselling and testing service point.

</details>
