### Q16. Five equally weighted applicable NQAS checkpoints score 2, 1, 2, 0 and 1, each with a maximum of 2. What is the percentage score for this illustrative group?

- **A.** 30%
- **B.** 80%
- **C.** 100%
- **D.** 60%

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: D. 60%**

- **A.** 30% incorrect denominator se aa sakta hai.
- **B.** 80% ke liye 8 points chahiye.
- **C.** 100% ke liye all checkpoints full hone chahiye.
- **D.** Total score 6 hai aur maximum 10 hai, isliye 6 divided by 10 times 100 se 60% aata hai.
