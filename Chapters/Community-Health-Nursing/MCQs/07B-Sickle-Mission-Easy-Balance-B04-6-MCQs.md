### Q1. Which blood component primarily contains haemoglobin affected in sickle cell disease?

**Difficulty:** Easy | **Priority:** Medium | **Topic:** Sickle cell mission | **Type:** Original practice

- **A.** Neutrophils
- **B.** Plasma albumin
- **C.** Red blood cells
- **D.** Platelets

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: C. Red blood cells**

- **A.** Neutrophils infection defense ke white blood cells hain.
- **B.** Albumin plasma protein hai, red cell haemoglobin nahi.
- **C.** Haemoglobin red blood cells ke andar oxygen carry karta hai aur sickle cell disorder isi protein se related hai.
- **D.** Platelets blood clotting mein help karte hain, haemoglobin ka main location nahi hain.

**Exam clue:** Sickle cell disease affects red-cell haemoglobin.

</details>

### Q2. Which abnormal haemoglobin is characteristic of sickle cell disorders?

**Difficulty:** Easy | **Priority:** Medium | **Topic:** Sickle cell mission | **Type:** Original practice

- **A.** HbA1c
- **B.** HbF alone
- **C.** Normal adult HbA alone
- **D.** HbS

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: D. HbS**

- **A.** HbA1c glycated haemoglobin hai jo glucose exposure assess karta hai.
- **B.** HbF fetal haemoglobin hai, sickle variant nahi.
- **C.** Normal HbA alone sickle haemoglobin ko represent nahi karta.
- **D.** HbS sickle haemoglobin variant hai jo sickle cell disorders mein important hota hai.

**Exam clue:** HbS is the sickle variant.

</details>

### Q3. Which laboratory method can help distinguish haemoglobin variants during confirmatory evaluation after community screening?

**Difficulty:** Easy | **Priority:** Medium | **Topic:** Sickle cell mission | **Type:** Original practice

- **A.** High-performance liquid chromatography
- **B.** Urine glucose dipstick
- **C.** Chest radiography
- **D.** Sputum acid-fast staining

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: A. High-performance liquid chromatography**

- **A.** HPLC haemoglobin fractions separate karke variant identification mein help karta hai, applicable confirmatory pathway follow karna chahiye.
- **B.** Urine glucose dipstick glucose detect karti hai, haemoglobin variants nahi.
- **C.** Chest X-ray lungs aur chest structures evaluate karta hai.
- **D.** AFB staining mycobacteria investigation ke liye hai.

**Exam clue:** HPLC is a haemoglobin-variant evaluation method.

</details>

### Q4. Which common genetic pattern is relevant to counselling for HbSS disease?

**Difficulty:** Easy | **Priority:** Medium | **Topic:** Sickle cell mission | **Type:** Original practice

- **A.** Transmission through respiratory droplets
- **B.** Autosomal recessive inheritance
- **C.** X-linked dominant inheritance
- **D.** Maternal mitochondrial inheritance only

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: B. Autosomal recessive inheritance**

- **A.** Respiratory droplets inherited haemoglobin disorder transmit nahi karte.
- **B.** HbSS mein HbS allele dono parents se milta hai aur classical pattern autosomal recessive hai.
- **C.** X-linked dominant pattern HbSS ka typical inheritance nahi hai.
- **D.** HbS gene mitochondrial DNA se exclusively inherit nahi hota.

**Exam clue:** HbSS is inherited, not contagious.

</details>
