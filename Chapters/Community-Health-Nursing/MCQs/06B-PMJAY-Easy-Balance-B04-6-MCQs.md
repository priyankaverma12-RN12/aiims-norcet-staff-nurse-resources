### Q1. What does SHA stand for in state-level PM-JAY administration?

**Difficulty:** Easy | **Priority:** Medium | **Topic:** PM-JAY financial protection | **Type:** Original practice

- **A.** Surgical Health Association
- **B.** State Health Agency
- **C.** School Health Assistant
- **D.** State Hospital Admission

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: B. State Health Agency**

- **A.** Surgical Health Association SHA ka official expansion nahi hai.
- **B.** State Health Agency state mein PM-JAY implementation aur service delivery coordinate karti hai.
- **C.** School Health Assistant scheme authority ka full form nahi hai.
- **D.** State Hospital Admission koi designated implementing agency nahi hai.

**Exam clue:** NHA nationally, SHA at state level.

</details>

### Q5. In the standard PM-JAY family-floater benefit, what does family floater mean?

**Difficulty:** Easy | **Priority:** Medium | **Topic:** PM-JAY financial protection | **Type:** Original practice

- **A.** The cover is divided into equal compulsory monthly cash payments
- **B.** Eligible family members share the applicable annual cover
- **C.** Every member automatically receives an unrelated full individual cover
- **D.** Only the family head can use the benefit

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: B. Eligible family members share the applicable annual cover**

- **A.** Scheme hospital care benefit hai, compulsory monthly cash transfer nahi.
- **B.** Family floater mein applicable annual amount eligible family members ke covered care ke liye shared hota hai.
- **C.** Har member ko separate full cover standard floater ka meaning nahi hai, specific additional entitlements alag ho sakte hain.
- **D.** Benefit sirf household head tak restricted nahi hai.

**Exam clue:** Floater means shared family cover.

</details>

### Q6. What is the purpose of pre-authorization when a PM-JAY package requires it?

**Difficulty:** Easy | **Priority:** Medium | **Topic:** PM-JAY financial protection | **Type:** Original practice

- **A.** Guarantee that all unrelated future admissions are free
- **B.** Allow any staff member to change the diagnosis for reimbursement
- **C.** Review the proposed covered treatment before approval under applicable package rules
- **D.** Replace the patient's clinical consent

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: C. Review the proposed covered treatment before approval under applicable package rules**

- **A.** Ek approval future unrelated care ko automatically cover nahi karta.
- **B.** Diagnosis clinical evidence se record hota hai, reimbursement ke liye change nahi karna chahiye.
- **C.** Pre-authorization applicable package aur proposed treatment ka approval process hai.
- **D.** Scheme approval informed clinical consent ka substitute nahi hai.

**Exam clue:** Pre-authorization is package approval, not patient consent.

</details>
