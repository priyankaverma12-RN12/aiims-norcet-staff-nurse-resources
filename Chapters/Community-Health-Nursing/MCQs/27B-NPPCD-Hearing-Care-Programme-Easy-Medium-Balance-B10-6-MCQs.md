### Q1. According to the WHO definition, disabling hearing loss is hearing loss greater than how many decibels in the better-hearing ear?

**Difficulty:** Easy | **Priority:** High | **Topic:** NPPCD community hearing-care programme | **Type:** Original practice

- **A.** 10 dB
- **B.** 20 dB
- **C.** 35 dB
- **D.** 100 dB

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: C. 35 dB**

WHO disabling hearing loss ko better-hearing ear mein 35 dB se greater hearing loss ke roop mein describe karta hai.

**Baaki options kyun galat hain?**

- **A.** 10 dB specified disabling threshold nahi hai.
- **B.** 20 dB normal hearing threshold reference se associated hai.
- **D.** 100 dB profound loss range mein ho sakta hai, minimum disabling threshold nahi.

**Exam clue:** Use the WHO better-ear threshold.

</details>

### Q3. Which term describes medicines that may damage the auditory or vestibular system?

**Difficulty:** Easy | **Priority:** High | **Topic:** NPPCD community hearing-care programme | **Type:** Original practice

- **A.** Ototoxic medicines
- **B.** Antipyretic medicines as a class
- **C.** Antacid medicines as a class
- **D.** Topical emollients as a class

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: A. Ototoxic medicines**

Ototoxic medicines hearing ya balance structures ko harm kar sakti hain; appropriate monitoring important hai.

**Baaki options kyun galat hain?**

- **B.** Antipyretic label fever reduction describe karta hai, ototoxicity define nahi karta.
- **C.** Antacid label gastric acidity treatment describe karta hai.
- **D.** Emollient label skin moisturizing action describe karta hai.

**Exam clue:** Ototoxic refers to ear-related toxicity.

</details>

- **C.** Dysphagia
- **D.** Dysarthria

Presbycusis age-related hearing decline ka term hai.

**Baaki options kyun galat hain?**

### Q5. Which maternal immunization-preventable infection can lead to congenital hearing impairment when acquired during pregnancy?

**Difficulty:** Easy | **Priority:** High | **Topic:** NPPCD community hearing-care programme | **Type:** Original practice

- **A.** Tetanus
- **B.** Rabies
- **C.** Cholera
- **D.** Rubella

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: D. Rubella**

Maternal rubella infection fetal hearing damage cause kar sakti hai; rubella prevention pregnancy se pehle immunization planning mein important hai.

**Baaki options kyun galat hain?**

- **A.** Tetanus toxin-mediated disease hai aur classic congenital hearing-loss cause nahi.
- **B.** Rabies neurological zoonosis hai, classic congenital hearing-loss infection nahi.
- **C.** Cholera enteric infection hai, congenital rubella syndrome nahi.

**Exam clue:** Connect rubella prevention to congenital hearing health.

</details>

### Q6. Which device or service converts spoken audio in a public health video into readable on-screen text?

**Difficulty:** Easy | **Priority:** High | **Topic:** NPPCD community hearing-care programme | **Type:** Original practice

- **A.** Captions
- **B.** Pure-tone audiometer
- **C.** Otoscope
- **D.** Tympanometer

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: A. Captions**

Captions spoken information ko readable text mein provide karti hain, hearing difficulties wale viewers ke liye access improve ho sakta hai.

**Baaki options kyun galat hain?**

- **B.** Audiometer hearing thresholds measure karta hai.
- **C.** Otoscope ear canal aur tympanic membrane examination ke liye hai.
- **D.** Tympanometer middle-ear function assess karta hai.

**Exam clue:** Captioning is an accessibility measure.

</details>
