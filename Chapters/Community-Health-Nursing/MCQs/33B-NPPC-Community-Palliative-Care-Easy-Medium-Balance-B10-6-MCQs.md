### Q1. What does NPPC stand for in the national health programme context?

**Difficulty:** Easy | **Priority:** High | **Topic:** NPPC community palliative care | **Type:** Original practice

- **A.** National Programme for Polio Certification
- **B.** National Programme for Primary Cataract Correction
- **C.** National Programme for Palliative Care
- **D.** National Programme for Pulmonary Culture

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: C. National Programme for Palliative Care**

NPPC National Programme for Palliative Care hai, jo accessible symptom relief aur supportive care strengthen karta hai.

**Baaki options kyun galat hain?**

- **A.** Polio certification NPPC ka full form nahi hai.
- **B.** Cataract correction eye-care programme ka scope hai.
- **D.** Pulmonary culture laboratory testing hai, programme name nahi.

**Exam clue:** Recall the national palliative programme.

</details>

### Q2. Which term describes care provided to improve comfort by relieving distressing symptoms?

**Difficulty:** Easy | **Priority:** High | **Topic:** NPPC community palliative care | **Type:** Original practice

- **A.** Symptom palliation
- **B.** Disease notification
- **C.** Contact tracing
- **D.** Mass screening

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: A. Symptom palliation**

Symptom palliation ka aim distressing symptoms ko kam karke comfort improve karna hai.

**Baaki options kyun galat hain?**

- **B.** Notification public health reporting process hai.
- **C.** Contact tracing exposed people identify karti hai.
- **D.** Mass screening possible disease ya risk identify karti hai.

**Exam clue:** Palliation focuses on relief of suffering.

</details>

### Q3. Which class of essential medicines is particularly important for relief of severe pain in palliative care when clinically indicated?

**Difficulty:** Easy | **Priority:** High | **Topic:** NPPC community palliative care | **Type:** Original practice

- **A.** Anthelmintics
- **B.** Antimalarial prophylactics
- **C.** Opioid analgesics
- **D.** Routine vaccines

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: C. Opioid analgesics**

Opioid analgesics severe pain relief ke liye indicated ho sakte hain, prescribed assessment aur monitoring ke saath.

**Baaki options kyun galat hain?**

- **A.** Anthelmintics worm infections treat karte hain.
- **B.** Antimalarial prophylaxis malaria prevention ke liye hai.
- **D.** Vaccines infectious disease prevention ke liye hain, established severe pain relief ke liye nahi.

**Exam clue:** Recognize a core symptom-control medicine class.

</details>

### Q6. Which term describes care delivered by professionals from several disciplines working toward shared patient goals?

- **B.** Multidisciplinary care
- **C.** Case notification
- **D.** Random service allocation
