### Q1. What does NHM stand for in the name of India's umbrella mission containing NUHM?

**Difficulty:** Easy | **Priority:** High | **Topic:** NUHM urban health services | **Type:** Original practice

- **A.** National Hospital Mapping
- **B.** National Hearing Monitoring
- **C.** National Health Mission
- **D.** National Housing Ministry

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: C. National Health Mission**

NHM National Health Mission hai, jiske under rural aur urban health sub-missions operate karte hain.

**Baaki options kyun galat hain?**

- **A.** Hospital mapping ek planning activity ho sakti hai, NHM ka full form nahi.
- **B.** Hearing monitoring NPPCD se related service ho sakti hai, NHM expansion nahi.
- **D.** Housing ministry health mission ka official full form nahi hai.

**Exam clue:** Distinguish the umbrella mission from its sub-missions.

</details>

### Q5. Which national urban scheme is especially relevant for convergence with NUHM on sanitation?

### Q6. Which local administrative division commonly serves as a geographical unit for urban health planning?
