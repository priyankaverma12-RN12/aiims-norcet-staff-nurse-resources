### Q1. What does IEC stand for in community health promotion?

**Difficulty:** Easy | **Priority:** High | **Topic:** Community health education and communication | **Type:** Original practice

- **A.** Individual Evaluation and Consultation
- **B.** Information, Education and Communication
- **C.** Integrated Emergency Care
- **D.** Institutional Examination and Certification

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: B. Information, Education and Communication**

IEC information aur education ko communication ke through community tak pahunchata hai.

**Baaki options kyun galat hain?**

- **A.** Evaluation aur consultation useful activities hain par IEC ka full form nahi.
- **C.** Emergency care acute illness management se related hai.
- **D.** Certification IEC ka main meaning nahi hai.

**Exam clue:** IEC = knowledge and communication.

</details>

### Q10. A health education campaign achieves high attendance but few households adopt the recommended action. Which evaluation is most useful next?

**Exam clue:** Measure behavioural outcomes separately from reach.

</details>

### Q12. A vaccine-related rumour spreads through a community messaging group. What is the best communication response?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Priority:** High | **Topic:** Community health education and communication | **Type:** Original practice

- **A.** Listen to concerns, verify information and share a clear evidence-based response through trusted channels
- **B.** Dismiss every question as ignorance
- **C.** Repeat the rumour widely without context
- **D.** Promise that no vaccine can ever cause an adverse reaction

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: A. Listen to concerns, verify information and share a clear evidence-based response through trusted channels**

Concern ko respectfully sun kar verified facts share karna trust build karta hai aur misinformation reduce kar sakta hai.

**Baaki options kyun galat hain?**

- **B.** Dismissal distrust badha sakta hai.
- **C.** Uncontextualized repetition false claim amplify kar sakti hai.
- **D.** Absolute safety guarantee inaccurate hai.

**Exam clue:** Correct misinformation without dismissing concerns.

</details>
