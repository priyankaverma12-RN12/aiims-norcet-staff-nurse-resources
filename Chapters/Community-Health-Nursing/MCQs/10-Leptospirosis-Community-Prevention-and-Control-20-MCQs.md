### Q1. Which organism causes leptospirosis?

**Difficulty:** Easy | **Priority:** Medium | **Topic:** Programme for Prevention and Control of Leptospirosis | **Type:** Original practice

- **A.** Pathogenic Leptospira bacteria
- **B.** Plasmodium parasites
- **C.** Dengue virus
- **D.** Entamoeba histolytica

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: A. Pathogenic Leptospira bacteria**

- **A.** Leptospirosis pathogenic Leptospira bacteria ki wajah se hoti hai, jo infected animals ke urine se environment tak pahunch sakte hain.
- **B.** Plasmodium malaria cause karta hai aur mosquito transmission se associated hai.
- **C.** Dengue viral infection hai aur Aedes mosquitoes se spread hota hai.
- **D.** Entamoeba amoebiasis cause karti hai, leptospirosis nahi.

**Exam clue:** Leptospirosis is a bacterial zoonosis.

</details>

### Q2. Which animal is an important reservoir of leptospirosis in many settings?

**Difficulty:** Easy | **Priority:** Medium | **Topic:** Programme for Prevention and Control of Leptospirosis | **Type:** Original practice

- **A.** Housefly
- **B.** Human louse
- **C.** Rodent
- **D.** Mosquito

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: C. Rodent**

- **A.** Housefly mechanical food contamination kar sakti hai, important Leptospira reservoir nahi hai.
- **B.** Louse kuch other infections transmit karta hai, leptospirosis ka major reservoir nahi.
- **C.** Rodents infected hote hue bhi urine mein bacteria shed kar sakte hain aur surroundings contaminate kar sakte hain.
- **D.** Mosquito dengue ya malaria vectors ho sakte hain, leptospirosis ka typical reservoir nahi.

**Exam clue:** Rodent urine is an important environmental source.

</details>

- **B.** Entry through damaged skin or exposed mucous membranes
- **C.** Transmission solely through mosquito bites
- **D.** Inheritance of a bacterial gene from parents

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: B. Entry through damaged skin or exposed mucous membranes**

- **A.** Coughing exclusive transmission route nahi hai.
- **B.** Contaminated water mein bacteria cuts, abrasions ya mucous membranes ke through enter kar sakte hain.

**Exam clue:** Contaminated water and skin exposure are important clues.

</details>
