### Q1. What does BCC stand for in public health communication?

**Difficulty:** Easy | **Priority:** High | **Topic:** Community health education and communication | **Type:** Original practice

- **A.** Behaviour change communication
- **B.** Basic clinical calculation
- **C.** Blood collection centre
- **D.** Birth certificate correction

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: A. Behaviour change communication**

BCC planned communication se healthy behaviour change support karta hai.

**Baaki options kyun galat hain?**

- **B.** Clinical calculation BCC ka official expansion nahi hai.
- **C.** Blood collection centre separate clinical service hai.
- **D.** Birth certificate correction vital records work hai.

**Exam clue:** Recognize the behaviour-focused abbreviation.

</details>
