### Q1. Which document outlines India's national health adaptation strategies for climate-sensitive illnesses?

**Difficulty:** Easy | **Priority:** Medium | **Topic:** Climate change and human health | **Type:** Original practice

- **A.** National Tuberculosis Elimination Strategic Plan
- **B.** National Mental Health Programme
- **C.** National Oral Health Programme
- **D.** National Action Plan for Climate Change and Human Health

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: D. National Action Plan for Climate Change and Human Health**

- **A.** TB plan tuberculosis control goals par focus karta hai.
- **B.** Mental health programme mental health service delivery se related hai.
- **C.** Oral health programme dental aur oral health par focus karta hai.
- **D.** NAPCCHH climate-sensitive health risks ke liye national adaptation strategies outline karta hai.

**Exam clue:** NAPCCHH is the climate-health action plan.

### Q3. Which population characteristic can increase sensitivity to climate-related health hazards?

**Difficulty:** Easy | **Priority:** Medium | **Topic:** Climate change and human health | **Type:** Original practice

- **A.** Using a different spelling of a person's name
- **B.** Existing chronic illness
- **C.** Having a different hospital registration number
- **D.** Owning an identical school uniform

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: B. Existing chronic illness**

- **A.** Name spelling climate-related sensitivity ko determine nahi karti.
- **B.** Chronic illness physiological reserve reduce kar sakti hai aur climate hazards se health risk badha sakti hai.
- **C.** Registration number vulnerability ka biological determinant nahi hai.
- **D.** School uniform physiological susceptibility determine nahi karti.

**Exam clue:** Underlying health affects climate vulnerability.

</details>

### Q4. Which health problem can worsen when drought disrupts food production and access?

**Difficulty:** Easy | **Priority:** Medium | **Topic:** Climate change and human health | **Type:** Original practice

- **A.** Congenital colour blindness
- **B.** Genetically determined eye colour change
- **C.** Undernutrition
- **D.** Inherited ABO blood group change

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: C. Undernutrition**

- **A.** Congenital colour blindness inherited ya developmental condition ho sakti hai, food production disruption ka direct result nahi.
- **B.** Genetically determined eye colour drought exposure se change nahi hota.
- **C.** Drought food availability aur affordability affect karke undernutrition risk badha sakta hai.
- **D.** ABO blood group drought se change nahi hota.

**Exam clue:** Climate hazards can affect food security.

</details>

### Q5. Which term describes a health facility's ability to maintain essential care despite climate-related disruption?

**Difficulty:** Easy | **Priority:** Medium | **Topic:** Climate change and human health | **Type:** Original practice

- **A.** Climate neutrality of every individual patient
- **B.** Genetic immunity to heat
- **C.** Permanent absence of seasonal hazards
- **D.** Climate resilience

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: D. Climate resilience**

- **A.** Facility resilience aur individual carbon neutrality different concepts hain.
- **B.** Genetic immunity to heat facility performance define nahi karti.
- **C.** Resilience ka meaning hazards ka complete disappearance nahi hai.
- **D.** Climate resilience ka aim shocks ke bawajood essential health services ko maintain aur recover karna hai.

**Exam clue:** Resilience is the capacity to cope and recover.

</details>

### Q6. Which weather hazard other than heat is explicitly addressed by NPCCHH public-health advisories?

**Difficulty:** Easy | **Priority:** Medium | **Topic:** Climate change and human health | **Type:** Original practice

- **A.** Cold waves
- **B.** Only ordinary sunrise
- **C.** Only normal nightfall
- **D.** Only the lunar phase

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: A. Cold waves**

- **A.** NCDC cold wave aur frost ke liye public health advisory list karta hai, jo extreme weather preparedness ka part hai.
- **B.** Ordinary sunrise extreme weather hazard nahi hai.
- **C.** Normal nightfall routine daily cycle hai.
- **D.** Lunar phase NPCCHH extreme weather advisory category nahi hai.

**Exam clue:** Cold waves also require health preparedness.

</details>
