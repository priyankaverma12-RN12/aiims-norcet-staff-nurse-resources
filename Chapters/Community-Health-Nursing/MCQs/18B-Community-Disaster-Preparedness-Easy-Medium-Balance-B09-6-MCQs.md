### Q1. Who chairs India's National Disaster Management Authority?

**Difficulty:** Easy | **Priority:** High | **Topic:** Community disaster preparedness | **Type:** Original practice

- **A.** Union Health Secretary
- **B.** District Chief Medical Officer
- **C.** Prime Minister
- **D.** Chief Justice of India

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: C. Prime Minister**

NDMA Prime Minister ke chairmanship mein national apex disaster management body hai.

**Baaki options kyun galat hain?**

- **A.** Health Secretary NDMA ka statutory chairperson nahi hai.
- **B.** District medical officer local health coordination role nibhata hai.
- **D.** Chief Justice judiciary lead karta hai, NDMA chairperson nahi.

**Exam clue:** Identify national disaster governance leadership.
