### Q1. What is the purpose of the vaccine cold chain?

**Difficulty:** Easy | **Priority:** High | **Topic:** Immunization logistics and safety | **Type:** Original practice

- **A.** Maintain vaccine quality at recommended temperatures from manufacture to administration

**Baaki options kyun galat hain?**

- **C.** Vaccine dose product protocol se decide hoti hai, temperature se nahi.

**Exam clue:** Cold chain recommended temperature maintain karke vaccine ki potency preserve karti hai.
