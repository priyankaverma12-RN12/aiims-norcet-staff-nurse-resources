### Q1. Which part of the eye becomes cloudy in cataract?

**Difficulty:** Easy | **Priority:** High | **Topic:** NPCBVI community eye care | **Type:** Original practice

- **A.** Optic disc
- **B.** Lens
- **C.** Iris alone
- **D.** Conjunctival sac

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: B. Lens**

Cataract mein natural lens ki transparency kam ho jaati hai.

**Baaki options kyun galat hain?**

- **A.** Optic disc nerve head hai, cataract ka anatomical site nahi.
- **C.** Iris eye colour aur pupil size regulate karti hai.
- **D.** Conjunctival sac ocular surface region hai, lens opacity nahi.

**Exam clue:** Cataract is lens opacity.

</details>

### Q2. What is the term for age-related difficulty focusing on nearby objects?

**Difficulty:** Easy | **Priority:** High | **Topic:** NPCBVI community eye care | **Type:** Original practice

- **A.** Myopia
- **B.** Colour blindness
- **C.** Night blindness
- **D.** Presbyopia

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: D. Presbyopia**

Presbyopia age ke saath near focusing ability kam hone ko kehte hain.

**Baaki options kyun galat hain?**

- **A.** Myopia mein usually distance vision blurry hoti hai.
- **B.** Colour blindness colour discrimination ko affect karti hai.
- **C.** Night blindness dim-light vision difficulty hai.

**Exam clue:** Presbyopia particularly affects near vision.

</details>

### Q4. Which eye condition is associated with damage to the optic nerve and can cause irreversible vision loss?

**Difficulty:** Easy | **Priority:** High | **Topic:** NPCBVI community eye care | **Type:** Original practice

- **A.** Glaucoma
- **B.** Simple conjunctival redness alone
- **C.** Presbyopia
- **D.** Uncomplicated myopia

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: A. Glaucoma**

Glaucoma optic nerve damage se associated hai aur untreated disease permanent vision loss cause kar sakti hai.

**Baaki options kyun galat hain?**

- **B.** Redness alone optic nerve damage prove nahi karti.
- **C.** Presbyopia near focusing problem hai.
- **D.** Uncomplicated myopia refractive error hai, characteristic glaucoma damage nahi.

**Exam clue:** Glaucoma involves the optic nerve.

</details>

### Q6. Which layer is affected by diabetic retinopathy?

- **B.** Lens capsule alone
- **C.** Retina
- **D.** External ear canal

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>
