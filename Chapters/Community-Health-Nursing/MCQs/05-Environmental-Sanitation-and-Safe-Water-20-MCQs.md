### Q1. Which transmission route is most directly interrupted by safe disposal of human faeces and handwashing after toilet use?

**Difficulty:** Easy | **Priority:** Medium | **Topic:** Environmental Sanitation and Safe Water | **Type:** Original practice

- **A.** Transmission through inherited genetic variants
- **B.** Faecal-oral transmission
- **C.** Airborne transmission of tuberculosis
- **D.** Transmission through an incompatible blood transfusion

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: B. Faecal-oral transmission**

- **A.** Inherited genetic variants parents se pass ho sakte hain, sanitation unko prevent nahi karti.
- **B.** Faeces se germs hands, water ya food ke through mouth tak pahunch sakte hain. Sanitation aur handwashing is route ko break karte hain.
- **C.** Tuberculosis mainly infectious airborne particles se spread hoti hai, faecal disposal us route ka direct control nahi hai.
- **D.** Blood transfusion safety blood compatibility aur screening se related hai.

**Exam clue:** Faecal-oral route mein contaminated hands, food aur water important hain.

</details>
