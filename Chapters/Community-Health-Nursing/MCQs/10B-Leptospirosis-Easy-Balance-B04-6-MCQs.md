### Q1. What is the usual incubation period for leptospirosis after exposure?

**Difficulty:** Easy | **Priority:** Medium | **Topic:** Leptospirosis community prevention | **Type:** Original practice

- **A.** 5–14 days
- **B.** 5–14 minutes
- **C.** 6–12 months in every case
- **D.** Exactly one year

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: A. 5–14 days**

- **A.** Leptospirosis mein symptoms usually exposure ke 5–14 din baad develop hote hain, possible range 2–30 din hai.
- **B.** Minutes wala interval typical incubation ke liye bahut short hai.
- **C.** Months ka fixed interval leptospirosis ki usual incubation nahi hai.
- **D.** Exactly one year ka fixed interval evidence se supported nahi hai.

**Exam clue:** Usual 5–14 days, possible 2–30 days.

</details>

### Q2. Which statement about human-to-human transmission of leptospirosis is correct?

**Difficulty:** Easy | **Priority:** Medium | **Topic:** Leptospirosis community prevention | **Type:** Original practice

- **A.** It is always transmitted by casual handshakes
- **B.** It is uncommon
- **C.** It is the usual dominant route through routine coughing
- **D.** Every household contact becomes infected

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: B. It is uncommon**

- **A.** Casual handshake ko universal route maanna incorrect hai.
- **B.** Human-to-human spread rare hai, common risk infected animal fluids ya contaminated environment se hai.
- **C.** Routine cough spread leptospirosis ka usual route nahi hai.
- **D.** Household contact alone inevitable infection establish nahi karta.

**Exam clue:** Most transmission is zoonotic or environmental.

</details>

### Q3. Which eye finding can support clinical suspicion of leptospirosis in a compatible exposure setting?

**Difficulty:** Easy | **Priority:** Medium | **Topic:** Leptospirosis community prevention | **Type:** Original practice

- **A.** A lifelong refractive error
- **B.** An isolated old corneal scar
- **C.** Conjunctival suffusion
- **D.** An isolated congenital cataract

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: C. Conjunctival suffusion**

- **A.** Lifelong refractive error light focusing problem hai, acute infectious sign nahi.
- **B.** Old corneal scar past injury ya infection ka result ho sakta hai.
- **C.** Conjunctival suffusion yani conjunctival redness compatible illness mein clue ho sakti hai, lekin diagnosis alone confirm nahi karti.
- **D.** Congenital cataract birth-related lens opacity hai, acute leptospirosis clue nahi.

**Exam clue:** Conjunctival suffusion is a clue, not a definitive diagnosis.

</details>

### Q4. Can an infected animal transmit Leptospira even if it appears healthy?

**Difficulty:** Easy | **Priority:** Medium | **Topic:** Leptospirosis community prevention | **Type:** Original practice

- **A.** No, every infected animal must look severely ill
- **B.** No, only animals with visible skin ulcers can transmit it
- **C.** No, only dead animals carry Leptospira
- **D.** Yes, an animal without obvious symptoms may shed the bacteria

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: D. Yes, an animal without obvious symptoms may shed the bacteria**

- **A.** Severe visible illness shedding ke liye required nahi hai.
- **B.** Visible ulcers transmission ka required sign nahi hain.
- **C.** Living infected animals bhi bacteria shed kar sakte hain.
- **D.** Infected animals sometimes asymptomatic hote hain aur urine mein bacteria shed kar sakte hain.

**Exam clue:** Healthy appearance does not rule out shedding.

</details>

### Q5. What does a biphasic clinical course mean in a possible leptospirosis illness?

**Difficulty:** Easy | **Priority:** Medium | **Topic:** Leptospirosis community prevention | **Type:** Original practice

- **A.** Symptoms may improve temporarily and then recur
- **B.** Symptoms must end permanently after the first fever
- **C.** Symptoms always occur only once for a few minutes
- **D.** Symptoms are present only before exposure

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: A. Symptoms may improve temporarily and then recur**

- **A.** Biphasic course mein initial illness ke baad temporary improvement ho sakta hai aur symptoms phir recur kar sakte hain.
- **B.** Initial fever ke baad permanent recovery har patient mein guaranteed nahi hai.
- **C.** Brief single episode biphasic pattern nahi describe karta.
- **D.** Exposure se pehle symptoms ka hona biphasic infection ka meaning nahi hai.

**Exam clue:** Some cases have two phases of illness.

</details>

### Q6. Which protective action is appropriate for a person with a skin scratch who must work near potentially contaminated water?

**Difficulty:** Easy | **Priority:** Medium | **Topic:** Leptospirosis community prevention | **Type:** Original practice

- **A.** Remove all skin protection before work
- **B.** Seal the scratch with a waterproof covering and use suitable protective equipment
- **C.** Keep the scratch uncovered to dry in the water
- **D.** Scrub the scratch with contaminated mud

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: B. Seal the scratch with a waterproof covering and use suitable protective equipment**

- **A.** Protection remove karna preventable exposure risk badhata hai.
- **B.** Waterproof covering aur appropriate PPE contaminated water ko damaged skin tak pahunchne se rokne mein help karte hain.
- **C.** Uncovered scratch se contaminated water ka direct contact ho sakta hai.
- **D.** Contaminated mud wound exposure badha sakta hai.

**Exam clue:** Cover skin breaks with waterproof protection.

</details>
