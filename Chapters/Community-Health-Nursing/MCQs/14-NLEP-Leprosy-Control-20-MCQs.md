### Q1. Which organism is the principal cause of Hansen disease?

**Difficulty:** Easy | **Priority:** High | **Topic:** National Leprosy Eradication Programme | **Type:** Original practice

- **A.** Mycobacterium tuberculosis

- **C.** Treponema pallidum

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

M leprae leprosy cause karta hai aur skin peripheral nerves ko affect kar sakta hai.

**Baaki options kyun galat hain?**

- **B.** P vivax malaria parasite hai.
