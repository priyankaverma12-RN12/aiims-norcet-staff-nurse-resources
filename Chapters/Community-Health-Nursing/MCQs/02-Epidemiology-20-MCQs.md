### Q1. Which measure describes the proportion of residents with hypertension on a specified survey date?

**Difficulty:** Easy | **Priority:** High | **Topic:** Epidemiology | **Type:** Original practice

- **A.** Incidence density
- **B.** Case fatality ratio
- **C.** Point prevalence
- **D.** Risk ratio

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: C. Point prevalence**

Us date par existing cases ka proportion point prevalence hai.

**Baaki options kyun galat hain?**

- **A.** Incidence density naye cases ko person-time se divide karti hai.
- **B.** Case fatality diagnosed cases mein deaths ka proportion hai.
- **D.** Risk ratio do groups ke risks compare karta hai.

**Exam clue:** Us date par existing cases ka proportion point prevalence hai.

</details>

- **D.** Longer duration aam taur par prevalence ko badha sakti hai.

**Exam clue:** Jab log disease ke saath zyada time jeete hain toh existing cases jama ho sakte hain.

</details>

### Q10. Which feature of randomization in a clinical trial reduces baseline confounding on average?

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Priority:** High | **Topic:** Epidemiology | **Type:** Original practice

- **A.** Allocation by chance
- **B.** Selecting only patients with severe disease
- **C.** Allowing participants to choose treatment
- **D.** Measuring outcomes only in the intervention group

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: A. Allocation by chance**

Chance allocation known aur unknown confounders ko average mein balance karne mein madad karta hai.

**Baaki options kyun galat hain?**

- **B.** Severe cases select karna random allocation nahi hai.
- **C.** Patient choice groups ko systematically different bana sakti hai.
- **D.** Dono groups ke outcomes compare karna trial ke liye zaruri hai.

**Exam clue:** Chance allocation known aur unknown confounders ko average mein balance karne mein madad karta hai.

</details>

### Q11. A disease is more frequent in smokers. Age is associated with smoking and disease but is not on the causal pathway. What role may age play?

- **C.** Confounder
- **D.** Random measurement error

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Baaki options kyun galat hain?**

- **A.** Mediator exposure aur outcome ke beech causal pathway mein hota hai.
- **B.** Outcome yahan disease hai, age nahi.
- **D.** Systematic association ko random measurement error nahi kehte.

**Exam clue:** Age exposure aur outcome dono se associated hai aur causal pathway mein nahi hai, toh confounding ho sakti hai.

</details>
