### Q1. What does ANM stand for?

**Difficulty:** Easy | **Priority:** High | **Topic:** Health workers and referral | **Type:** Original practice

- **A.** Assistant Nutrition Manager
- **B.** Advanced Neonatal Medic
- **C.** Auxiliary Nurse Midwife
- **D.** Area Nursing Monitor

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: C. Auxiliary Nurse Midwife**

ANM ka full form Auxiliary Nurse Midwife hai, jo community maternal-child services mein important role nibhati hai.

**Baaki options kyun galat hain?**

- **A.** Nutrition manager ANM ka official expansion nahi hai.
- **B.** Neonatal medic ANM designation nahi hai.
- **D.** Area monitoring ek task ho sakta hai, lekin full form nahi.

**Exam clue:** Recall the standard cadre name.

</details>

### Q6. In a village meeting, whose participation should the VHSNC actively encourage?

**Difficulty:** Easy | **Priority:** High | **Topic:** Health workers and referral | **Type:** Original practice

- **A.** Only wealthy landowners
- **B.** Only hospital specialists
- **C.** Women and marginalized community members
- **D.** Only elected state legislators

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: C. Women and marginalized community members**

Vulnerable aur marginalized groups ki participation community needs ko better represent karti hai.

**Baaki options kyun galat hain?**

- **A.** Sirf landowners se many households exclude ho sakte hain.
- **B.** Specialists alone local community voice replace nahi karte.
- **D.** State legislators alone village participation ensure nahi karte.

**Exam clue:** Community representation must be inclusive.

</details>
