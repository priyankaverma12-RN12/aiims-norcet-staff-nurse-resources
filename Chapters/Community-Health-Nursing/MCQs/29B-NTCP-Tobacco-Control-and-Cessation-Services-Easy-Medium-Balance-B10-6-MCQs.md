### Q1. Which section of COTPA 2003 prohibits smoking in public places, subject to applicable statutory provisions?

**Difficulty:** Easy | **Priority:** High | **Topic:** NTCP tobacco control and cessation services | **Type:** Original practice

- **A.** Section 4
- **B.** Section 5
- **C.** Section 6
- **D.** Section 7

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: A. Section 4**

Section 4 public places mein smoking prohibition se related hai; applicable rules aur exceptions separately check karne chahiye.

**Baaki options kyun galat hain?**

- **B.** Section 5 tobacco advertisement prohibition se related hai.
- **C.** Section 6 minor sales aur educational institution distance restrictions cover karta hai.
- **D.** Section 7 specified package warnings aur related requirements se linked hai.

**Exam clue:** Match the law section to public smoking.

</details>

### Q6. Which cessation approach replaces tobacco-derived nicotine with an approved therapeutic product to manage withdrawal under appropriate guidance?

**Difficulty:** Easy | **Priority:** High | **Topic:** NTCP tobacco control and cessation services | **Type:** Original practice

- **A.** Routine antibiotic prophylaxis
- **B.** Insulin replacement therapy
- **C.** Oral rehydration therapy
- **D.** Nicotine replacement therapy

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: D. Nicotine replacement therapy**

Nicotine replacement therapy controlled therapeutic nicotine supply se withdrawal manage karne mein help kar sakti hai; suitability clinician assess karta hai.

**Baaki options kyun galat hain?**

- **A.** Antibiotics nicotine withdrawal treat nahi karte.
- **B.** Insulin diabetes treatment mein use hota hai.
- **C.** ORS fluid-electrolyte replacement ke liye hai.

**Exam clue:** NRT is an evidence-based cessation aid.

</details>
