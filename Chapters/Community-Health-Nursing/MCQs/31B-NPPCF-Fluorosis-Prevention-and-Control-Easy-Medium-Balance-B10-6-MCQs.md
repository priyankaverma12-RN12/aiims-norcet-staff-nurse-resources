### Q1. What does NPPCF stand for in Indian public health?

**Difficulty:** Easy | **Priority:** High | **Topic:** NPPCF fluorosis prevention and control | **Type:** Original practice

- **A.** National Programme for Prevention and Control of Filariasis
- **B.** National Programme for Primary Cardiac Facilities
- **C.** National Programme for Prevention of Food Contamination
- **D.** National Programme for Prevention and Control of Fluorosis

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: D. National Programme for Prevention and Control of Fluorosis**

NPPCF excessive fluoride exposure se associated fluorosis ki prevention aur control ke liye national programme hai.

**Baaki options kyun galat hain?**

- **A.** Filariasis ek parasitic infection hai aur iska separate elimination programme hai.
- **B.** Cardiac facility development NPPCF ka full form nahi hai.
- **C.** Food contamination prevention iska official expansion nahi hai.

**Exam clue:** Recall the programme's environmental health focus.

</details>

### Q3. Which tissue is predominantly affected in skeletal fluorosis?

**Difficulty:** Easy | **Priority:** High | **Topic:** NPPCF fluorosis prevention and control | **Type:** Original practice

- **A.** Retinal tissue alone
- **B.** Middle-ear mucosa alone
- **C.** Bone
- **D.** Pancreatic islet cells alone

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: C. Bone**

Skeletal fluorosis chronic excessive fluoride exposure se bones aur related skeletal structures ko affect karti hai.

**Baaki options kyun galat hain?**

- **A.** Retina skeletal tissue nahi hai.
- **B.** Middle-ear mucosa skeletal fluorosis ka characteristic tissue nahi hai.
- **D.** Pancreatic islets skeletal structures nahi hain.

**Exam clue:** Skeletal refers to bones and related structures.

</details>

### Q6. Which term describes treatment intended to restore function in people with advanced deformities due to fluorosis?

**Difficulty:** Easy | **Priority:** High | **Topic:** NPPCF fluorosis prevention and control | **Type:** Original practice

- **A.** Reconstructive surgery
- **B.** Routine mass deworming
- **C.** Universal salt iodization
- **D.** Measles vaccination

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: A. Reconstructive surgery**

NPPCF mein selected advanced deformity cases ke management ke liye reconstructive surgery aur rehabilitation support included hain.

**Baaki options kyun galat hain?**

- **B.** Deworming intestinal worm burden reduce karta hai, established skeletal deformity repair nahi karta.
- **C.** Salt iodization iodine deficiency prevent karti hai, skeletal deformity repair nahi.
- **D.** Measles vaccine measles prevention ke liye hai.

**Exam clue:** Distinguish prevention from corrective care.

</details>
