### Q1. What does CBR stand for in the context of disability and community health?

**Difficulty:** Easy | **Priority:** High | **Topic:** Community based rehabilitation and inclusion | **Type:** Original practice

- **A.** Community-based rehabilitation
- **B.** Central blood regulation
- **C.** Clinical birth registration
- **D.** Community bacteriology reporting

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: A. Community-based rehabilitation**

CBR community-based rehabilitation ka abbreviation hai.

**Baaki options kyun galat hain?**

- **B.** Blood regulation CBR ka meaning nahi hai.
- **C.** Birth registration vital records ka function hai.
- **D.** Bacteriology reporting lab surveillance se related hai.

**Exam clue:** Recall the CBR expansion.

### Q6. Which communication language uses conventional hand movements, facial expressions and visual signs?

**Difficulty:** Easy | **Priority:** High | **Topic:** Community based rehabilitation and inclusion | **Type:** Original practice

- **A.** Chemical notation
- **B.** Morse code alone
- **C.** Numerical tallying
- **D.** Sign language

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: D. Sign language**

Sign languages visual-gestural communication systems hain jinmein hand movements aur facial expressions meaningful hote hain.

**Baaki options kyun galat hain?**

- **A.** Chemical notation substances represent karti hai.
- **B.** Morse code signals encode karta hai, sign language ka synonym nahi.
- **C.** Tallying counts record karti hai.

**Exam clue:** Sign language is a visual language.

</details>
