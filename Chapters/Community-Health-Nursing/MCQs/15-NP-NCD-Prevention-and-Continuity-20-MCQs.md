### Q1. What does NCD stand for in the Indian national prevention and control programme?

**Difficulty:** Easy | **Priority:** High | **Topic:** National NCD programme and prevention | **Type:** Original practice

- **A.** Noncommunicable disease
- **B.** National communicable diagnosis
- **C.** Nutritional contamination disorder
- **D.** Neonatal communicable deficiency

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: A. Noncommunicable disease**

NCD aisi disease category hai jo generally ek person se dusre person ko directly transmit nahi hoti.

**Baaki options kyun galat hain?**

- **B.** Communicable diagnosis infection se related ho sakti hai, NCD ka full form nahi hai.
- **C.** Nutritional contamination iska official full form nahi hai.
- **D.** Neonatal communicable deficiency NCD ka programme term nahi hai.

**Exam clue:** NCD aisi disease category hai jo generally ek person se dusre person ko directly transmit nahi hoti.

</details>
