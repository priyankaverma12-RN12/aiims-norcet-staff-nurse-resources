### Q1. Which mosquito is the principal vector of both dengue and chikungunya in urban India?

**Difficulty:** Easy | **Priority:** High | **Topic:** Dengue and chikungunya community vector programme | **Type:** Original practice

- **A.** Anopheles stephensi
- **B.** Culex tritaeniorhynchus
- **C.** Aedes aegypti
- **D.** Phlebotomus argentipes

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: C. Aedes aegypti**

Aedes aegypti dono arboviruses transmit kar sakta hai.

**Baaki options kyun galat hain?**

- **A.** Anopheles malaria se associated hai.
- **B.** Culex tritaeniorhynchus JE se associated hai.

### Q11. A community worker visits an ill dengue patient at home. Which transmission-prevention advice is useful during the viremic period?

- **B.** Prevent mosquitoes from biting the patient using appropriate barriers
- **C.** Use antibiotics to prevent mosquito infection
- **D.** Remove only the patient's drinking glass

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: B. Prevent mosquitoes from biting the patient using appropriate barriers**

Mosquito patient se virus acquire karke dusron tak transmit kar sakta hai; bite prevention cycle interrupt karti hai.

**Baaki options kyun galat hain?**

- **A.** Meal sharing ordinary dengue transmission route nahi.
- **C.** Antibiotics viral transmission prevent nahi karte.
- **D.** Glass removal mosquito transmission address nahi karta.

**Exam clue:** Protect infected people from mosquito bites too.

</details>
