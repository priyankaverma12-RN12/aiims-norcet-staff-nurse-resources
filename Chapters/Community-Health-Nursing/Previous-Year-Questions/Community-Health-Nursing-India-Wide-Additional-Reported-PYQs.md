### PYQ1. How is the BCG vaccine classified by organism viability?

**Difficulty:** Easy/Medium | **Topic:** Vaccines

- **A.** Live attenuated bacterial vaccine
- **B.** Inactivated viral vaccine
- **C.** Toxoid vaccine
- **D.** Recombinant protein vaccine

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation</summary>

**Correct: A. Live attenuated bacterial vaccine**

BCG mein weakened Mycobacterium bovis use hota hai. Isliye ye live attenuated bacterial vaccine hai. Toxoid inactivated toxin hota hai. Inactivated viral aur recombinant protein vaccines ka preparation alag hota hai.

</details>

### PYQ2. Which description best represents a community in community health nursing?

**Difficulty:** Easy/Medium | **Topic:** Community concept

- **A.** An individual without any social context
- **B.** People connected by shared locality, interests or social relationships
- **C.** Only patients admitted to a single ICU
- **D.** Only employees of a hospital pharmacy

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation</summary>

**Correct: B. People connected by shared locality, interests or social relationships**

Community logon ka group hota hai jisme locality, interests ya social relationships common ho sakte hain. Community nursing group ki needs aur environment ko assess karti hai. ICU patients ya pharmacy employees narrower groups hain, aur ek individual alone community ka full concept represent nahi karta.

</details>
