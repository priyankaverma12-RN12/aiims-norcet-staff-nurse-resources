### PYQ1. Which field test may help assess suspected freeze damage in eligible adsorbed vaccines according to the cold-chain protocol?

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Topic:** Cold chain

- **A.** Shake test
- **B.** Mantoux test
- **C.** Widal test
- **D.** Schick test

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: A. Shake test**

Kuch adsorbed vaccines freeze hone par particles jaldi settle ho sakte hain. Trained staff shake test ko eligible vaccine aur control sample ke saath protocol se use kar sakta hai.

**Baaki options kyun galat hain?**

- **B.** Mantoux TB infection assessment mein use hota hai.
- **C.** Widal enteric fever testing se related hai.
- **D.** Schick historical diphtheria susceptibility test hai.

</details>

### PYQ2. Which of these is not a dedicated vaccine cold-chain equipment item?

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Topic:** Cold chain equipment

- **A.** Vaccine carrier
- **B.** Ice-lined refrigerator
- **C.** Cold box
- **D.** Ordinary cotton wool

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: D. Ordinary cotton wool**

Cold chain vaccine ko recommended temperature par rakhne ka system hai. Cotton wool apne aap temperature-maintaining cold-chain equipment nahi hai.

**Baaki options kyun galat hain?**

- **A.** Vaccine carrier transport mein use hota hai.
- **B.** Ice-lined refrigerator storage mein use hota hai.
- **C.** Cold box transport aur storage ke liye use ho sakta hai.

</details>
