# CSSD workflow and quality assurance

**Priority High. Study-ready, not studied.** [30 original MCQs](../MCQs/02A-CSSD-Workflow-Quality-Assurance-30-MCQs.md)

CSSD Central Sterile Supply/Services Department hai. Iska purpose reusable medical devices ko safe, reproducible manner mein process karna hai. Sterilizer alone poori process nahi hai. Agar instrument ke hinge mein blood residue reh gaya, toh correct-looking cycle bhi poor preparation ko compensate nahi kar sakti. Har stage quality gate hai.

 A[Point-of-use pretreatment] --> B[Contained dirty transport]

## Workflow and engineering

Dirty receiving/decontamination separate rakho from clean inspection/assembly and sterile storage. Negative pressure relative to adjacent clean areas in decontamination and positive pressure in clean storage are typical engineered arrangements. Actual standards and facility engineering requirements apply. Used instruments ko closed leak-resistant transport mein bhejo, sharps ke liye puncture resistance as appropriate. Point of use par approved pretreatment dried organic material prevent karta hai. Saline universal soaking solution nahi hai, corrosion risk ho sakta hai.

Decontamination staff heavy-duty utility gloves, splash-resistant clothing and eye/face protection task risk ke according use karte hain. Manual cleaning suitable devices ke liye approved detergent, water quality, brush and time use karti hai. Underwater brushing splash reduce kar sakti hai. Multipart instrument IFU ke according disassemble karo. Hinges open, lumens specified connectors se irrigate karo. Washer-disinfector completion visible residue ko override nahi karti. Clean items inspect for cleanliness, corrosion and function. Defective instruments qualified repair or replacement ke liye segregate karo.

## Packaging, loading and release

Compatible packaging sterilant penetration and later microbial barrier support karti hai. Pouch seal complete hona chahiye. Tray mass, layout, delicate instrument protection, load arrangement all validated requirements hain. Sterilizer ko overload mat karo. Physical cycle data and appropriate chemical/biological indicators review karo. Positive control from same BI lot must demonstrate expected viability. Failed control invalidates interpretation. Major repair or relocation ke baad specified qualification cycles before routine production. Implant loads require BI monitoring and quarantine until acceptable BI result whenever possible. Emergency exceptions strictly authorized documented protocol se. Routine immediate-use steam sterilization inventory shortage solve karne ka default nahi. Critical items from liquid peracetic acid immersion systems generally immediate use require karte hain rather than long-term sterile storage.

## Storage, recall and patient safety

Storage clean, dry and protected hona chahiye. Event-related sterility package integrity and handling par depend karti hai. Assigned expiry policy where applicable follow karo. Dropped package evaluate according to system and facility policy. Wet, compromised or uncertain package release mat karo. Stock rotation first-expiring suitable stock first. Clean distribution protected and dirty return separate rakho. Load ID, instrument set and patient/procedure linkage recall ke liye vital hain. Confirmed process failure mein affected stock quarantine, investigate, document, and authorized clinical/IPC/risk team se used devices exposure assessment coordinate karo. Never hide failures. Staff competency observation and balancing indicators such as rejected trays and turnaround time review karo.
