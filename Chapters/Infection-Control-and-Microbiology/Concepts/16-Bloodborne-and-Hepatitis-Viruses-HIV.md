# Bloodborne and hepatitis viruses, HIV

**Priority High. Study-ready, not studied.** [40 original MCQs](../MCQs/16-Bloodborne-and-Hepatitis-Viruses-HIV-40-MCQs.md)

## Hepatitis ko basic level se samjho

Hepatitis liver inflammation hai. Liver bilirubin process karta hai, clotting proteins banata hai aur metabolism mein role rakhta hai. Inflammation se fatigue, nausea, dark urine aur jaundice ho sakte hain. Har infected person symptomatic nahi hota. Acute liver failure mein altered consciousness and coagulopathy urgent warning signs hain. Nurse mental status, hydration, intake/output, bleeding and ordered laboratory trends monitor karti hai. Viral hepatitis mein cause identify karna essential hai because transmission and treatment differ karte hain.

| Virus | Main route | Genome and key clue |
|---|---|---|
| HAV | Fecal-oral | RNA, generally no chronic disease. IgM supports acute infection. |
| HBV | Blood, sexual, perinatal | Partially double-stranded DNA, reverse transcription involved. Chronicity possible. |
| HCV | Mainly blood | RNA, chronic disease possible, direct-acting antiviral cure achievable. |
| HDV | Blood/body fluids in HBV context | HBV surface antigen dependent. Coinfection or superinfection. |
| HEV | Often contaminated water, also foodborne routes | RNA. Severe disease risk in pregnancy, chronicity possible in immunosuppression. |

## HBV serology — exam favourite

| HBsAg | Total anti-HBc | Anti-HBs | Typical interpretation |
|---|---|---|---|
| Negative | Negative | Negative | Susceptible if no other evidence |
| Negative | Negative | Positive | Vaccine-induced immunity |
| Negative | Positive | Positive | Resolved natural infection |
| Positive | Positive | Negative | Current infection, assess IgM and duration |
| Negative | Positive | Negative | Isolated core antibody, further context needed |

HBsAg current infection marker ho sakta hai. Anti-HBs immunity marker hai. Total anti-HBc natural exposure indicate karta hai because vaccine surface antigen contain karti hai. IgM anti-HBc acute infection mein useful hai, especially window period. Chronic HBV mein HBsAg six months or longer persist kar sakta hai. HBeAg replication association hai but negative result active replication exclude nahi karta. HBV DNA directly viral replication assess karne mein useful hai. ALT, fibrosis and other clinical factors treatment decisions guide karte hain.

**Case:** Patient vaccinated hai, HBsAg negative, total anti-HBc negative aur anti-HBs positive hai. Is pattern mein natural infection evidence nahi, vaccine immunity likely hai. Agar core antibody bhi positive hoti toh resolved natural infection consider karte. Immunosuppression before therapy prior HBV exposure assess karna useful hai because reactivation possible hai.

## HBV and HCV management

WHO HBV birth dose preferably 24 hours ke andar recommend karta hai. HBsAg-positive mother ke newborn ko timely prophylaxis and indicated HBIG local policy ke according arrange karo. Infant infection chronicity risk high hoti hai. Chronic HBV antiviral treatment eligibility guideline-directed hai. Cirrhosis and liver cancer surveillance clinician assess karta hai.

HCV antibody exposure marker hai. Reactive antibody ke baad RNA current infection determine karne ke liye needed hai. Recent exposure mein antibody negative ho sakti hai. Modern DAA treatment cure achieve kar sakta hai, but reinfection possible hai. Routine available HCV vaccine nahi hai. Standard precautions and safe injection practices essential hain.

## HIV mechanism

HIV CD4 helper T cells aur other susceptible cells infect kar sakta hai. gp120 CD4/coreceptor bind karta hai aur gp41 membrane fusion support karta hai. Reverse transcriptase RNA se DNA banata hai. Integrase host genome mein viral DNA insert karta hai. Protease viral proteins maturation ke liye process karta hai.

## HIV testing and nursing care

Fourth-generation assay p24 antigen and antibodies detect karti hai. Reactive screen confirmatory algorithm se resolve karo. Negative test immediately after exposure rule-out nahi hai. PEP evaluation time-sensitive hai and national protocol follow karti hai. CD4 immune status assess karta hai. Viral load ART response assess karta hai. ART viral replication suppress karti hai but routine cure nahi hai. Sustained undetectable viral load sexual transmission prevent karta hai (U=U), other routes ke liye same guarantee assume mat karo.

Advanced immunosuppression mein opportunistic infections possible hain. Pneumocystis pneumonia dyspnea, dry cough and hypoxemia de sakti hai. Severe headache may indicate CNS infection and needs urgent evaluation. Nurse adherence barriers, adverse effects, interactions, nutrition, mental health and stigma-free care address karti hai. Pregnancy mein maternal ART, infant prophylaxis and feeding guidance national protocol-based hain. HBV coinfection mein HBV-active ART changes carefully plan hote hain because withdrawal flare cause kar sakta hai.
