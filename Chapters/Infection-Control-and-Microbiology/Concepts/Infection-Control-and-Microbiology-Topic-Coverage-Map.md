## Initial study explanations

### Transmission precautions

TB ke germs chhote particles ke through hawa mein travel kar sakte hain. Isliye staff ko suitable respirator aur airborne precautions follow karne hote hain.

### Sterilization

Sterilization ka matlab microbial life aur spores ko bhi khatam karna hai. Heat-resistant instruments ke liye validated steam cycle useful hoti hai.

### Needlestick exposure

Used needle se blood-borne infection ka risk ho sakta hai. Nurse site wash karti hai aur jaldi report karti hai taaki HIV, HBV aur HCV exposure ka assessment ho sake.

### Biomedical waste

Contaminated recyclable plastic ko Indian biomedical waste rules mein red category mein segregate kiya jaata hai. Isse treatment aur recycling ka safe process follow ho sakta hai.
