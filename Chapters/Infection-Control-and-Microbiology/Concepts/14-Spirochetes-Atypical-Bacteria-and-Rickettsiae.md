# Spirochetes, atypical bacteria and rickettsiae

## Spirochetes

Spirochetes slender spiral bacteria hain. Periplasmic flagella corkscrew motility support karte hain. Treponema pallidum syphilis cause karta hai. Primary stage mein painless chancre possible hai. Secondary stage mein systemic symptoms and palms/soles rash ho sakta hai. Latent stage mein clinical manifestations absent hote hain but serologic evidence present hota hai. Late disease cardiovascular or neurological complications de sakti hai. Ocular and neurological involvement any stage mein possible hai. Pregnancy mein fetal transmission risk important hai. Prompt guideline-directed penicillin treatment and antenatal follow-up essential hain.

RPR and VDRL nontreponemal tests hain. TP-PA and other treponemal tests more specific antibody response detect karte hain. Dono types and clinical history diagnosis guide karte hain. Treponemal antibodies past treated infection ke baad persist kar sakti hain. Quantitative nontreponemal titers follow-up mein useful hote hain. Prozone antibody excess se false-negative reaction possible hai. Treatment ke baad Jarisch–Herxheimer inflammatory reaction ho sakti hai. Isko anaphylaxis automatically mat samjho, but allergy and serious symptoms assess karo.

Leptospira infected animal urine se contaminated water or soil mein present ho sakta hai. Broken skin and mucosa entry routes hain. Flood exposure, fever, calf myalgia and conjunctival suffusion suggestive clues hain. Severe disease renal dysfunction, jaundice, bleeding or pulmonary hemorrhage cause kar sakti hai. Nurse fluid balance, urine output, respiratory status and organ dysfunction monitor karti hai. PCR or serology timing depends on illness stage. Early clinical assessment and treatment matter karte hain. Protective footwear and wound protection useful hain.

Borrelia species relapsing fever or Lyme disease cause kar sakti hain depending on species. Antigenic variation recurrent fever mechanism hai. Lyme disease tick exposure in relevant geography and expanding erythema ke context mein consider hoti hai. All tick bites Lyme disease nahi hote.

## Wall-less and intracellular organisms

Mycoplasma pneumoniae lacks peptidoglycan wall. Isliye beta-lactams ka target absent hai. Membrane sterols stability support karte hain. Atypical pneumonia mein persistent cough and systemic symptoms possible hain. Cold agglutinin-related hemolysis extrapulmonary association hai. Molecular detection useful ho sakti hai because culture slow and specialized hai. Treatment age, severity and resistance considerations ke according clinician decide karta hai.

Chlamydia obligate intracellular bacteria hain. Elementary body infectious form hai. Host cell ke andar reticulate body replicative form banta hai. C. trachomatis genital infection, neonatal conjunctivitis or pneumonia and trachoma cause kar sakta hai depending on strain. Partner evaluation, safe sexual health counselling and antenatal screening where indicated important hain. Trachoma repeated inflammation se eyelid scarring and corneal damage de sakta hai. SAFE strategy surgery, antibiotics, facial cleanliness and environmental improvement cover karti hai. C. psittaci bird exposure-associated pneumonia cause kar sakta hai. Exposure history clinician ko inform karo.

## Rickettsial and related febrile illnesses

| Illness | Organism | Typical exposure |
|---|---|---|
| Scrub typhus | Orientia tsutsugamushi | Chigger bite |
| Epidemic typhus | Rickettsia prowazekii | Body lice |
| Murine typhus | Rickettsia typhi | Fleas |
| Spotted fever group | Several Rickettsia species | Often ticks |
| Q fever | Coxiella burnetii | Inhalation of contaminated animal-associated aerosols |

Scrub typhus fever, headache, eschar and systemic illness de sakta hai. Eschar har patient mein present nahi hota. Severe disease respiratory failure, renal injury, encephalitis or shock cause kar sakti hai. Early negative serology rule-out nahi hai. High clinical suspicion mein urgent clinician-directed therapy and supportive management delay nahi honi chahiye. Prevention includes bite avoidance, protective clothing, and exposure awareness. Q fever mein valvular disease persistent focal infection risk increase kar sakti hai. Specialist follow-up may be needed.
