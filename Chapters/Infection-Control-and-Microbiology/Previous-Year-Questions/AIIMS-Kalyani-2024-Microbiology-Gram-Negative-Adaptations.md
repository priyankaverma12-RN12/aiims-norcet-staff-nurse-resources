## Source Section C, question 6 — selective medium

Which culture medium is suitable for selective recovery of Shigella from feces?

- A. Sabouraud agar
- B. XLD agar
- C. Lowenstein–Jensen medium
- D. CLED agar as the standard selective enteric pathogen medium

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation</summary>

**B. XLD agar.** XLD enteric pathogens select aur differentiate karta hai. Sabouraud fungi ke liye hai. Lowenstein–Jensen mycobacteria ke liye hai. CLED commonly urine culture ke liye use hota hai. Source question same topic test karti hai, options yahan independently authored hain.

</details>

## Source Section A, question 2 — urinary microbiology case (essay-to-MCQ adaptation)

In a community-acquired uncomplicated urinary tract infection, which organism is a common leading bacterial cause?

- A. Neisseria meningitidis
- B. Vibrio cholerae
- C. Escherichia coli
- D. Corynebacterium diphtheriae

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation</summary>

**C. Escherichia coli.** Intestinal E. coli urinary tract mein ascend karke infection cause kar sakti hai. Meningococcus invasive meningococcal disease, Vibrio cholera aur Corynebacterium diphtheria se associated hain. Original source ek case-based essay hai, is MCQ format ko actual recruitment PYQ mat samjho.
