### 1. UPPSC Staff Nurse, 3 October 2021 (reported)

Which route is normally used for routine hepatitis B vaccine?

- A. Oral
- B. Intramuscular
- C. Intravenous
- D. Intrathecal

<details><summary>Answer</summary>

**B. Intramuscular.** HBV vaccine IM di jaati hai. Oral route OPV/rotavirus products ke liye use hota hai. IV and intrathecal HBV vaccine routes nahi hain. Birth dose preferably within 24 hours per WHO, do not follow the source's older 48-hour statement. ### 2. Haryana USHR Staff Nurse, 17 April 2022 (reported)

Which infection is primarily enteric rather than a typical bloodborne occupational sharps hazard?

- A. HBV
- B. HCV
- C. HIV
- D. HAV

<details><summary>Answer</summary>

**D. HAV.** HAV primarily fecal-oral spread karta hai. HBV, HCV and HIV recognized bloodborne exposure concerns hain. Rare blood transmission possibilities mean this is a primary-route distinction, not an absolute impossibility. ### 3. AIIMS NORCET, 20 November 2021, shift II (reported memory-based)

Which disease is suggested by a febrile child with cough, coryza, conjunctivitis and characteristic buccal spots?

- A. Measles
- B. Mumps
- C. Varicella
- D. Rubella

<details><summary>Answer</summary>

**A. Measles.** Koplik spots and three Cs characteristic hain. Mumps parotitis, varicella vesicular rash and rubella lymphadenopathy with rash se associated hain. Airborne precautions promptly arrange karo. ### 4. MP NHM CHO, 17 April 2022 (reported)

Which site should be examined for the characteristic Koplik enanthem?

- A. Nail bed
- B. Buccal mucosa
- C. Sole of foot
- D. External ear canal

<details><summary>Answer</summary>

**B. Buccal mucosa.** Small oral mucosal lesions measles ka classic sign hain. Nail bed, sole and ear canal typical site nahi hain. This item overlaps conceptually with item 3 but assesses anatomical site rather than disease identification, so use as PYQ revision, not additional original bank count.
