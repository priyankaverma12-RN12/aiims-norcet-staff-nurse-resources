# Biomedical waste and occupational exposure — reported exam practice

Two source-linked independently written adaptations. Third-party reported questions, not authenticated official papers or official answer keys.

### PYQ1. Which Indian waste category is used for a contaminated plastic urinary drainage bag without a sharp attachment?

**Reported exam:** AIIMS NORCET 2025. Cycle and shift not verified. [Question source](https://previousyearquestions.app/aiims-norcet/2025/)

- A. Yellow
- B. Red
- C. White translucent
- D. Blue

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: B. Red**

Urinary drainage bag contaminated recyclable plastic hai. Red category iske liye listed hai. Yellow soiled aur anatomical waste ke liye hai. White metal sharps ke liye hai aur blue specified glassware aur metallic implants ke liye hai. [Clinical reference](https://cpcb.nic.in/uploads/Projects/Bio-Medical-Waste/Guidelines_healthcare_June_2018.pdf)

</details>

### PYQ2. Immediately after a used needle punctures a nurse's skin, which initial first-aid action should be taken?

**Reported exam:** NORCET 3, 2022, Shift 1, third-party attribution. [Question source](https://nprep.in/question-answer/the-first-priority-nursing-intervention-in-a-patient-with-needle-sticks-injury-vqfjAcdoC828lsjlrTG-5)

- A. Wait for the source result before first aid
- B. Squeeze the wound forcefully
- C. Wash gently with soap and running water
- D. Apply concentrated bleach

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: C. Wash gently with soap and running water**

Pehle wound clean karo aur phir immediately report karke urgent occupational assessment karao. Indicated HIV PEP ko unnecessary delay nahi karna hai. Forceful squeezing aur concentrated bleach tissue damage kar sakte hain. Source result ka wait first aid ko delay karta hai. [Clinical reference](https://ncdc.mohfw.gov.in/wp-content/uploads/2024/09/NGIPC.pdf)

</details>
