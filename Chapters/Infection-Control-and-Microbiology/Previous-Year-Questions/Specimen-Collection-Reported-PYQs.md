### OSSSC Nursing Officer — 7 February 2021 (reported)

An ambulatory adult is asked to provide a clean-catch urine sample for bacterial culture. Which portion is preferable?

- A. First few drops
- B. Midstream urine
- C. Last drops mixed with the first portion
- D. Urine collected from a toilet receptacle

<details><summary>Answer and explanation</summary>

**B.** Midstream urine external contamination reduce karne mein help karti hai. A initial stream urethral organisms carry kar sakti hai. C mixed portions standard clean-catch approach nahi hain. D contaminated collection environment result unreliable bana sakta hai. Clinical symptoms aur sample type culture count interpretation guide karte hain. A universal colony count cutoff should not be inferred from the source explanation.
