### 1. DSSSB Nursing Officer — 27 August 2019, shift III (reported)

Which intervention confers passive rather than active adaptive protection?

- A. Recovery from infection
- B. Administration of specific preformed immunoglobulin
- C. Vaccination
- D. Immune response after subclinical infection

<details><summary>Answer and explanation</summary>

**B.** Ready-made antibodies passive temporary protection provide karti hain. Infection and vaccination host-generated active responses induce kar sakte hain. [Source](https://testbook.com/question-answer/which-of-the-following-is-not-the-way-to-acquire-a--61d2c50b7373bbd9af4957f0)

### 2. DSSSB Nursing Officer — 28 August 2019, shift II (reported)

Which immunoglobulin is particularly important in mucosal secretions including saliva and colostrum?

- A. IgD
- B. IgM
- C. IgA
- D. IgE

<details><summary>Answer and explanation</summary>

**C.** Secretory IgA mucosal protection support karti hai. IgD naive B-cell receptor, IgM early humoral response, IgE allergy and helminth response se associated hain. [Source](https://testbook.com/question-answer/identify-an-immunoglobulin-present-in-milk-saliva--61cecde93b89a1f31c471269) · [Related NORCET 2021 indexed lead](https://testbook.com/question-answer/the-immunoglobulin-that-is-found-in-breast-milk-is--61ce8b5e0ea17652377bd430/amp)

</details>
