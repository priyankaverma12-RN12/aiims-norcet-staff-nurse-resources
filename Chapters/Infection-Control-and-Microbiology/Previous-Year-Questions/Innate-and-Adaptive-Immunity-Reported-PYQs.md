### 1. ESIC Staff Nurse — 26 February 2019, shift II (reported)

Which innate lymphocyte can kill abnormal host cells without using an antigen-specific T cell receptor?

- A. Memory B lymphocyte
- B. Natural killer cell
- C. Plasma cell
- D. CD4 helper T lymphocyte

<details><summary>Answer and explanation</summary>

**B.** NK cells innate cytotoxic defense mein role play karte hain. A specific B cell recall response se related hai. C antibody secretion karta hai. D adaptive response coordinate karta hai. NK cell abnormal cells ko activating aur inhibitory signals ke balance se recognize kar sakta hai.

### 2. UP NHM Staff Nurse — 24 January 2021, shift I (search-indexed)

After recovery from an infection, a patient's own lymphocytes produce antigen-specific protection. Which category describes this response?

- A. Artificial passive
- B. Natural passive
- C. Natural active
- D. Physical barrier alone

<details><summary>Answer and explanation</summary>

**C.** Natural infection body ka own adaptive response stimulate kar sakti hai. A administered ready-made antibodies describe karta hai. B maternal antibody transfer jaise mechanism se related hai. D innate barrier hai. Duration of active protection variable hoti hai.
