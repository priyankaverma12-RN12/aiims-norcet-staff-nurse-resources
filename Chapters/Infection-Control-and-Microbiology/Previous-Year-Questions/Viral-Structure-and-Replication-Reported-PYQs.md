### 1. HPSSC Hamirpur Staff Nurse — 5 August 2021 (reported)

Which statement about the genome of a conventional virion is correct?

- A. It must always be double-stranded DNA.
- B. It consists of DNA or RNA, depending on the virus.
- C. It must contain both DNA and RNA genomes simultaneously.
- D. It consists only of protein.

<details><summary>Answer and explanation</summary>

**B.** Virus genome DNA ya RNA ho sakta hai. A sirf kuch DNA viruses par fit hota hai. C genome classification ko replication intermediates ke saath confuse karta hai. D protein-only prion ke concept se related hai, conventional virus genome se nahi.

### 2. RRB Nursing Superintendent — 29 April 2025, shift 1 (reported)

Which hepatitis virus has a partially double-stranded DNA genome and uses a reverse transcription step?

- A. HAV
- B. HCV
- C. HEV
- D. HBV

<details><summary>Answer and explanation</summary>

**D.** HBV DNA virus hai jo replication mein RNA intermediate use karta hai. HAV, HCV aur HEV RNA viruses hain. Reverse transcription alone HIV jaisa RNA genome prove nahi karta.
