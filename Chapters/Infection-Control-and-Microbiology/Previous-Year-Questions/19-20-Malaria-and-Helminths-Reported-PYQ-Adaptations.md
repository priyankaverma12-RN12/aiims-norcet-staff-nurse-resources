### 1. AIIMS NORCET, 20 November 2021 (memory-based)

Which listed organism causes malaria?

- A. Entamoeba histolytica
- B. Plasmodium falciparum
- C. Salmonella Typhi
- D. HIV

<details><summary>Answer</summary>

**B.** P. falciparum one of several human malaria species hai. Entamoeba amebiasis, Salmonella enteric fever and HIV immunodeficiency cause karte hain. [Source](https://testbook.com/question-answer/malaria-is-caused-by--61bb19c74b8db0806d377385)

### 2. RRB Nursing Superintendent, 29 April 2025 shift III (reported)

Which reference parasitological method supports malaria species identification and density estimation?

- A. Antibody serology only
- B. Fever pattern alone
- C. Thick and thin blood-film microscopy
- D. Routine bacterial culture

<details><summary>Answer</summary>

**C.** Blood films parasite detection and morphology assessment support karte hain. Serology and fever alone current disease reliably establish nahi karte. Bacterial culture malaria test nahi hai. RDT bhi WHO-approved diagnostic option hai. [Source](https://testbook.com/question-answer/which-diagnostic-method-is-recommended-as-the-gold--681db2e40d2952f73802fb25)

</details>

### Provisional — UPPSC Staff Nurse, 10 April 2022

Indexed question page concerns helminth classification. Independent related practice: Which disease is caused by a parasitic roundworm?

- A. Measles
- B. Giardiasis
- C. Ascariasis
- D. Diphtheria

<details><summary>Answer</summary>

**C.** Ascaris nematode hai. Measles virus, Giardia protozoan and diphtheria bacterium se associated hain. [Indexed lead](https://testbook.com/question-answer/which-of-the-following-disease-is-caused-by-helmin--625e16b89c295dc670cb4175/amp). Not counted as verified reported adaptation.
