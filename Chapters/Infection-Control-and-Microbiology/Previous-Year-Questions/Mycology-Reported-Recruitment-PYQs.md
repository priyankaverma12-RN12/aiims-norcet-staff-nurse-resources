### 1. AIIMS NORCET — 11 September 2022 (memory-based image item)

A patient has an itchy, circular, scaly lesion on the trunk with a raised active border. Which diagnosis is most consistent?

- A. Pressure injury
- B. Tinea corporis
- C. Thermal injury
- D. Herpes zoster

<details><summary>Answer and explanation</summary>

**B.** Tinea corporis dermatophyte infection hai jo annular scaly lesions cause kar sakta hai. A pressure aur shear se related tissue injury hai. C heat exposure ke baad develop hoti hai. D commonly painful dermatomal vesicles cause karta hai. Original recalled image ko inspect nahi kiya gaya aur yahan reproduce nahi kiya gaya. Text-based adaptation reported diagnosis aur source explanation par based hai.

### 2. AIIMS Bhubaneswar Nursing Officer — 15 September 2019 (memory-based)

Which organism is least typical as a cause of common infectious vaginal symptoms?

- A. Candida albicans
- B. Trichomonas vaginalis
- C. Salmonella enterica
- D. Bacterial vaginosis-associated Gardnerella

<details><summary>Answer and explanation</summary>

**C.** Salmonella generally enteric infection se related hai. A vulvovaginal candidiasis ka important cause hai. B trichomoniasis cause karta hai. D bacterial vaginosis se associated organism hai, although BV polymicrobial dysbiosis hai. Symptoms alone specific cause confirm nahi karte.

### 3. SGPGI Staff Nurse — 28 February 2024, shift 1 (reported)

A patient has marked nocturnal itching with thin burrows between the fingers. Which condition should be distinguished from dermatophyte infection?

- A. Scabies
- B. Tinea corporis
- C. Pityriasis versicolor
- D. Candidemia

<details><summary>Answer and explanation</summary>

**A.** Scabies mite infestation hai aur interdigital burrows important clue hain. B dermatophyte infection mein annular scaling ho sakti hai. C Malassezia-associated superficial patches cause karta hai. D bloodstream Candida infection hai. Ye differential diagnosis practice hai, fungal organism identification question nahi hai. Count only once if reused in skin or parasitology study.

</details>
