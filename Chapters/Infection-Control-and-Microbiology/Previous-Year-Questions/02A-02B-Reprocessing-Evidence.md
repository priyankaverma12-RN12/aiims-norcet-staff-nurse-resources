### RRB JPR Staff Nurse, 6 June 2018 — reported adaptation

Which validated industrial process is a recognized option for sterilizing compatible disposable plastic medical devices?

- A. Atmospheric boiling
- B. Routine UV surface exposure
- C. Ethylene oxide gas
- D. Low-level wiping

<details><summary>Answer and explanation</summary>

**C.** Ethylene oxide compatible heat-sensitive devices ke liye validated industrial sterilization option hai. Boiling does not reliably kill spores. UV limited penetration rakhta hai and routine surface wiping sterilization nahi hai. Gamma or other ionizing radiation is also a recognized industrial method when compatible and validated. Do not assume all disposable syringes use one universal manufacturing method.
