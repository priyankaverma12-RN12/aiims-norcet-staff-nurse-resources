### AIIMS NORCET, 1 September 2020 (memory-based)

A used needle punctures a healthcare worker's finger. Which immediate wound-care action is appropriate?

- A. Make an incision at the puncture site.
- B. Apply suction to the wound.
- C. Wash with soap and running water.
- D. Aggressively squeeze the wound.

<details><summary>Answer and explanation</summary>

**C.** Washing appropriate initial first aid hai. A additional injury cause kar sakta hai. B safe decontamination method nahi hai. D tissue injury badha sakti hai. Prompt occupational assessment aur indicated PEP evaluation bhi essential hain.

### JIPMER Staff Nurse, 4 June 2013 (reported)

A historical examination asks for the approximate HIV transmission risk following percutaneous exposure to HIV-infected blood. Which answer best represents its reported conventional estimate?

- A. Around one in three
- B. Around one in thirty
- C. Around one in three hundred
- D. Around one in three thousand

<details><summary>Answer and explanation</summary>

**C.** Historical exam approximately 0.3% estimate use karta hai. A aur B risk ko considerably overestimate karte hain. D conventional exam estimate se tenfold lower hai. Individual risk fixed nahi hai. Current 2025 US PHS guideline reports an average estimate of 0.23% for percutaneous exposure to HIV-infected blood. Source viral load, route, injury severity and prophylaxis matter. Risk estimate ko urgent evaluation delay karne ke liye use mat karo.

[Retrieved JIPMER report](https://testbook.com/question-answer/nurse-working-in-an-operationtheatre-must-kn--6221dd924a77825a7eaa564f) · [Current US PHS guidance](https://stacks.cdc.gov/view/cdc/258795).

</details>
