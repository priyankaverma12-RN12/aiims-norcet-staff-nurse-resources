### AIIMS Bhubaneswar Nursing Officer — 15 September 2019 (memory-based)

Which assumption is inconsistent with safe care under historical universal precautions?

- A. Blood exposure may transmit infection even when a carrier has not been identified.
- B. Barrier practices can reduce occupational exposure.
- C. Patient blood may be treated as harmless without assessing exposure risk.
- D. Infection-prevention measures help reduce cross-transmission.

<details><summary>Answer and explanation</summary>

**C.** Blood ko harmless assume nahi karna chahiye. A unrecognized infection ka important risk describe karta hai. B appropriate barriers occupational exposure reduce karte hain. D IPC cross-transmission reduce karti hai. Modern standard precautions historical universal precautions se broader hain.

### RRB Staff Nurse — 20 July 2019, shift I (reported)

What was a major original purpose of universal precautions in healthcare?

- A. Eliminate the need for sterile equipment.
- B. Protect healthcare workers from occupational bloodborne exposure.
- C. Replace all transmission-specific isolation.
- D. Restrict protective measures to confirmed HIV cases.

<details><summary>Answer and explanation</summary>

**B.** Historical universal precautions occupational bloodborne exposure prevention par focused thi. A sterile equipment still necessary hai. C additional isolation disease-specific assessment par depend karti hai. D recognized aur unrecognized sources dono se protection needed hai. Modern standard precautions patients and workers dono ko protect karti hain.
