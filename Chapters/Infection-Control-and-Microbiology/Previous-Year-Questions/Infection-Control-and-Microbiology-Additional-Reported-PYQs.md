### PYQ1. Before putting on indicated PPE for isolation care, which preparatory action is essential?

**Reported exam:** NORCET 5 Mains, 2023

[Question source](https://nprep.in/question-answer/what-is-the-first-step-of-donning-WuyEnojXbvFcIaKH9zf1T)

- **A.** Hand hygiene
- **B.** Put on gloves before cleaning hands
- **C.** Touch the isolation-room door handle with clean gloves
- **D.** Adjust contaminated eye protection before washing

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: A. Hand hygiene**

PPE pehenne se pehle hands clean karne se clean equipment contaminate hone ka risk kam hota hai. Uske baad indicated sequence follow karna chahiye. Gloves pehle pehenna, contaminated eye protection touch karna aur clean gloves se door handle touch karna asepsis compromise kar sakta hai.

</details>

### PYQ2. Which process is preferred to sterilize a reusable heat- and moisture-resistant critical surgical instrument after cleaning?

**Reported exam:** NORCET 2, 2021 Shift 2

[Question source](https://nprep.in/question-answer/best-method-described-for-the-asepsis-of-any-surgical-instrument-flsRERgAEX_C-krb2kRxf)

- **A.** Low-level disinfection
- **B.** Routine boiling
- **C.** Alcohol hand rub
- **D.** Validated steam sterilization

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: D. Validated steam sterilization**

Autoclave mein pressurized steam appropriate temperature aur time par resistant spores ko bhi inactivate kar sakti hai. Heat-sensitive devices ko alternative compatible method chahiye. Low-level disinfectant aur boiling critical instrument ke liye reliable sterilization nahi hain. Hand rub skin ke liye hota hai.

</details>

### PYQ5. Which type of immunity develops when a vaccine stimulates the recipient's own immune response?

**Reported exam:** PGIMER Nursing Officer, 2025

- **A.** Innate immunity alone
- **B.** Artificially acquired active immunity
- **C.** Naturally acquired passive immunity
- **D.** Artificially acquired passive immunity

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: B. Artificially acquired active immunity**

Vaccine immune system ko specific antigen recognize karna sikhati hai. Body khud antibodies aur memory response develop karti hai, isliye active immunity hai. Placental IgG natural passive aur administered immunoglobulin artificial passive immunity examples hain. Innate immunity antigen-specific vaccine memory ko describe nahi karti.

### PYQ13. Which maternal immunoglobulin can cross the placenta and provide temporary passive fetal immunity?

**Reported exam:** HPSSC Staff Nurse 2021, Series A, post code 892, Q13

[Question source](https://hpgeneralstudies.com/solved-paper-hp-staff-nurse-exam-2021-part-1-hpssc-hamirpur/)

- **A.** IgM
- **B.** IgE
- **C.** Secretory IgA
- **D.** IgG

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: D. IgG**

IgG placental transport ke through fetus tak pahunch sakti hai. Ye temporary passive protection deti hai. IgM generally placenta cross nahi karti. IgE allergy aur parasite responses mein important hai. Secretory IgA mucosal protection aur breast milk se associated hai.

</details>
