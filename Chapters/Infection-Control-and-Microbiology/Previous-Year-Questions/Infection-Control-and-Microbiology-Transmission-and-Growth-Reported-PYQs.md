Original wording and official keys are not authenticated. Two exam years are unavailable and have not been guessed. The clinical explanations use independent reference material.

### PYQ1. Which routine action most directly interrupts contact transmission during bedside nursing care?

**Reported exam:** NORCET 2, 2021 Shift 2

[Question source](https://nprep.in/question-answer/what-are-the-best-things-to-break-the-chain-of-infection-k3QjA7VxC2wrmE7X0xqxp)

- **A.** Using the same gloves throughout a round
- **B.** Hand hygiene at indicated moments
- **C.** Routine antibiotic prophylaxis for every patient
- **D.** Sterilizing the entire ward after every round

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: B. Hand hygiene at indicated moments**

Jab nurse ek patient ko touch karti hai, toh germs hands par aa sakte hain. Correct time par hand hygiene karne se germs doosre patient tak pahunchne ka risk kam hota hai. Har patient ko antibiotics dena unnecessary hai. Puri ward ko sterilize karna routine hand hygiene ka replacement nahi hai. Same gloves se germs transfer ho sakte hain.

</details>

### PYQ2. Which order describes the movement of a pathogen from its source to a new vulnerable person?

**Reported exam:** Rajasthan CHO, 2025

[Question source](https://nprep.in/question-answer/what-is-the-correct-sequence-of-the-chain-of-infection-pQcZu3rneSDbiEXbv33cb)

- **A.** Agent → reservoir → exit → transmission → entry → susceptible host
- **B.** Agent → entry → exit → reservoir → host → transmission
- **C.** Reservoir → susceptible host → agent → exit → entry → transmission
- **D.** Host → entry → transmission → agent → exit → reservoir

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: A. Agent → reservoir → exit → transmission → entry → susceptible host**

Pehle germ apne reservoir mein rehta hai. Phir woh exit route se bahar aata hai, kisi transmission route se travel karta hai aur naye susceptible person ke body mein entry karta hai. Baaki teen options mein entry, exit aur host ka order galat hai.

</details>

### PYQ3. A bacterial population doubles at regular intervals under favorable conditions. Which phase does this represent?

**Reported exam:** AIIMS Bhopal Nursing Officer, year not visible in indexed result

[Question source](https://nprep.in/question-answer/the-division-of-bacterial-cells-and-the-exponential-increase-in-their-number-2JnfoJMVctBGjqB1VGL_g)

- **A.** Lag phase
- **B.** Stationary phase
- **C.** Death phase
- **D.** Exponential phase

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: D. Exponential phase**

Exponential ya log phase mein bacteria actively divide karte hain aur population rapidly increase hoti hai. Lag phase mein bacteria new environment mein adjust karte hain. Stationary phase mein formation aur death roughly balance ho sakti hain. Death phase mein viable cells kam hote hain.

</details>

### PYQ4. What process produces two daughter cells from a typical dividing bacterium?

**Reported exam:** HPSSSB Staff Nurse, year not visible in indexed result

[Question source](https://nprep.in/question-answer/the-most-common-method-of-reproduction-of-bacteria-is-qMBdu-1igyn6ug4fOicqD)

- **A.** Conjugation
- **B.** Endospore formation
- **C.** Binary fission
- **D.** Meiosis

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: C. Binary fission**

Binary fission mein bacterium apna DNA copy karke do cells mein divide hota hai. Meiosis typical bacterial reproduction nahi hai. Conjugation genetic material transfer karti hai, cell number increase karna iska direct purpose nahi hai. Endospore adverse conditions mein survival ke liye banta hai.

</details>

### PYQ5. Which organism lacks the peptidoglycan target of conventional beta-lactam antibiotics?

**Reported exam:** AIIMS Raipur, 2017 Shift 3

[Question source](https://nprep.in/question-answer/which-among-the-following-bacteria-lack-a-cell-wall-N4pXCPeQtZCgkBndCIt3k)

- **A.** Streptococcus pyogenes
- **B.** Mycoplasma pneumoniae
- **C.** Staphylococcus aureus
- **D.** Escherichia coli

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: B. Mycoplasma pneumoniae**

Mycoplasma ke paas peptidoglycan cell wall nahi hoti. Isliye beta-lactam medicine ka usual target absent hota hai. Staphylococcus aur Streptococcus Gram-positive walls rakhte hain. E. coli Gram-negative hai lekin usmein bhi thin peptidoglycan layer hoti hai.

</details>

### PYQ6. Which temperature is near the optimum for many common human bacterial pathogens?

**Reported exam:** BHU Nursing Officer, 2015

[Question source](https://nprep.in/question-answer/the-optimum-temperature-for-the-growth-of-the-most-pathogenic-bacteria-is-FXb8zCP5A_LPK5QHWjIkd)

- **A.** 37°C
- **B.** 4°C
- **C.** 70°C
- **D.** 100°C

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: A. 37°C**

Human body ki normal temperature approximately 37°C hai. Many human pathogens iske aas-paas growth ke liye adapted hote hain. 4°C par many bacteria slow grow karte hain. 70°C aur 100°C many common pathogens ki normal optimum growth temperatures nahi hain.

</details>

### PYQ7. Which bacterial morphology is described by the general term bacilli?

**Reported exam:** KPSC Staff Nurse, 2021

[Question source](https://nprep.in/question-answer/bacilli-are-the-term-used-for-JQCdFZMh56_meMzhsYreG)

- **A.** Spherical cells
- **B.** Flexible spiral cells
- **C.** Comma-shaped curved rods only
- **D.** Rod-shaped cells

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: D. Rod-shaped cells**

Bacilli ka general meaning rod-shaped bacteria hai. Spherical cells cocci kehlate hain. Flexible spiral bacteria spirochetes ho sakte hain. Comma-shaped curved rods vibrios ki characteristic morphology hain.

</details>
