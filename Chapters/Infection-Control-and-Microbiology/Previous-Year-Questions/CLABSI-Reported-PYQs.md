### AIIMS Bhopal Nursing Officer — 23 May 2018, shift II (reported)

Which central-line practice should be avoided when the catheter is functioning and still indicated?

- A. Perform hand hygiene before access.
- B. Use appropriate skin antisepsis.
- C. Review the optimal insertion site.
- D. Replace the catheter every day solely to prevent infection.

<details><summary>Answer and explanation</summary>

**D.** Routine daily catheter replacement unnecessary invasive procedure hai aur infection prevention ke liye recommended nahi hai. A contamination reduce karti hai. B skin microbes reduce karti hai. C mechanical aur infection risks balance karne mein help karti hai.
