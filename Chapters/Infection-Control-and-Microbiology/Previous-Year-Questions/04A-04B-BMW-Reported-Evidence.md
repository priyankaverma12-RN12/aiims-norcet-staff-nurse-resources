### AIIMS Raipur Nursing Officer, 31 August 2019, shift II (memory-based)

Which colour stream is appropriate for discarded human anatomical tissue and blood-soiled absorbent cotton?

- A. Red
- B. Blue
- C. Yellow
- D. White translucent

<details><summary>Answer and explanation</summary>

**C.** Anatomical aur specified soiled material yellow stream mein aata hai. A contaminated recyclable plastics ke liye hai. B specified glassware aur metallic implants ke liye hai. D contaminated metal sharps ke liye hai. [Retrieved recall](https://testbook.com/question-answer/human-and-animal-wastes-microbial-and-biological--62c53a63c6e14093ce29f83c). Source's additional black-bin glove and broad red-fluid explanations are not suitable as modern rules. Microbiological waste requires applicable pretreatment.

</details>

### AIIMS NORCET, 20 November 2021, shift II (memory-based)

Which routine practice increases needlestick risk during disposal?

- A. Using the designated sharps container.
- B. Manually bending or recapping a used needle.
- C. Segregating blood-soiled gauze in yellow.
- D. Separating specified contaminated glassware into blue-marked packaging.

<details><summary>Answer and explanation</summary>

**B.** Manual needle bending aur recapping avoid karo. A safe containment hai. C aur D current material-specific segregation examples hain. [Retrieved recall and reported key](https://testbook.com/question-answer/which-technique-used-for-biomedical-waste-manageme--65f84d4fc57a0e3e356268d6).

</details>
