### 1. OSSSC Nursing Officer — 7 February 2021 (reported, question page retrieved)

A DPT vial is confirmed to have been damaged by freezing. Which action is safest?

- A. Allow the product to thaw and administer it routinely
- B. Exclude it from use and follow the programme disposal procedure
- C. Shake the product to restore its original potency
- D. Combine it with a fresh vaccine vial

<details><summary>Answer and explanation</summary>

**B.** DPT freeze-sensitive adsorbed vaccine hai. Confirmed freeze damage restore nahi hoti. A thawing alone safety restore nahi karti. C shake test assessment hai, treatment nahi. D mixing unsafe hai. Suspected temperature breach aur confirmed damage mein difference yaad rakho.

[Retrieved third-party question and key](https://testbook.com/question-answer/ques--627a3a870bd0e650e5933431) · [WHO cold-chain guidance](https://www.who.int/teams/immunization-vaccines-and-biologicals/essential-programme-on-immunization/supply-chain/guidanceandtools)
