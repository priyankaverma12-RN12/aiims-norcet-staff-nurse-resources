### GMCH Nursing Officer — 29 June 2025 (reported)

A steam sterilization cycle has apparently satisfactory chemical indicators, but a correctly processed biological indicator is positive. What should the supervisor conclude initially?

- A. Chemical results establish that all instruments are sterile.
- B. The biological finding is a possible process failure and requires investigation before routine release.
- C. The result proves only that the wrapping material was defective.
- D. A longer storage period can correct the failed process.

<details><summary>Answer and rationale</summary>

**B.** Positive BI serious process warning hai. A chemical indicator direct microbial survival assess nahi karti. C failure ka exact cause investigation se establish hota hai. D storage failed sterilization correct nahi karta. Follow current method-specific quarantine, investigation, testing and recall procedure.
