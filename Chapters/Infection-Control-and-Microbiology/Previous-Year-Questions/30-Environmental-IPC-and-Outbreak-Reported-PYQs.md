# Environmental IPC and outbreak investigation — reported exam adaptation

### MPESB Nursing Officer, 25 November 2022, shift II (third-party reported)

Which action most directly represents the nurse's epidemiological investigation role during an outbreak?

- A. Administering an ordered antipyretic
- B. Comparing case histories and exposures to help identify the infection source
- C. Providing routine wound dressing to an unrelated patient
- D. Ordering meals for patients

<details><summary>Answer and explanation</summary>

**B.** Epidemiological investigation case histories, exposures and time/place patterns analyze karke source hypothesis test karti hai. A is clinical symptom management. C and D routine care hain, source investigation directly represent nahi karte. Nurses also contribute to education, case finding and control.
