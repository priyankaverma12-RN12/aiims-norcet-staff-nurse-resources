### RRB Nursing Superintendent — 12 March 2026 shift II (reported)

Which nursing practice best preserves the protective barrier against intraluminal contamination during maintenance of an indwelling urinary catheter?

- A. Disconnect the drainage tubing for every routine hygiene procedure.
- B. Preserve a closed sterile drainage pathway.
- C. Replace sterile insertion technique with clean gloves alone in acute care.
- D. Reuse insertion equipment between patients after a quick rinse.

<details><summary>Answer and explanation</summary>

**B.** Closed system preserve karne se microorganisms ke entry routes reduce hote hain. A unnecessary disconnection contamination risk create karti hai. C acute-care indwelling insertion ki aseptic requirements meet nahi karta. D unvalidated reuse cross-transmission cause kar sakta hai.
