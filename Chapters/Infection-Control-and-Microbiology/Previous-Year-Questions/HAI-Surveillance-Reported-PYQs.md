### 1. AIIMS Delhi Nursing Officer — 16 September 2018 (memory-based)

In a traditional nursing examination asking for the approximate elapsed-time threshold used to suspect hospital-acquired infection, which answer is commonly expected?

- A. 2 hours
- B. 48 hours

<details><summary>Answer and explanation</summary>

### 2. HSSC Staff Nurse — 31 December 2023 shift II (reported)

Which epidemiological measure focuses on newly occurring cases rather than all existing cases?

- A. Point prevalence
- B. Mortality
- C. Incidence
- D. Existing morbidity burden alone

<details><summary>Answer and explanation</summary>

**C.** Incidence new cases par focus karti hai. A specified point par existing cases count karti hai. B deaths se related hai. D broad illness burden describe karta hai. Incidence proportion at-risk population use karti hai aur incidence rate person-time denominator use karti hai.
