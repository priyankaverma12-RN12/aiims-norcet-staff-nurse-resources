### PYQ1. Which routine practice is central to interrupting transmission of organisms between patients?

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Topic:** Hand hygiene

- **A.** Hand hygiene at indicated moments
- **B.** Wearing one pair of gloves for the entire shift
- **C.** Cleaning only at the end of the week
- **D.** Avoiding all patient contact

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: A. Hand hygiene at indicated moments**

Hands ke through germs ek surface ya patient se doosre tak ja sakte hain. Sahi time par hand hygiene transmission ko kam karti hai.

**Baaki options kyun galat hain?**

- **B.** Same gloves se contamination spread ho sakti hai.
- **C.** Weekly cleaning alone sufficient nahi hai.
- **D.** Safe patient contact nursing care ka part hai.

</details>

### PYQ2. Which Indian biomedical waste category is used for contaminated recyclable plastic infusion tubing without a sharp?

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Topic:** Biomedical waste

- **A.** White translucent
- **B.** Blue
- **C.** General waste
- **D.** Red

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: D. Red**

Indian segregation system mein contaminated recyclable plastic red category mein jaata hai. Sharps ko alag puncture-proof container mein daalte hain.

**Baaki options kyun galat hain?**

- **A.** White sharps ke liye hai.
- **B.** Blue specified glass items ke liye hai.
- **C.** Contaminated plastic general waste nahi hai.

</details>
