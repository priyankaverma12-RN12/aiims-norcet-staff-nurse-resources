### 1. AIIMS NORCET — 20 November 2021 (memory-based)

Which organism is a cause of human malaria?

- A. Salmonella Typhi
- B. Plasmodium falciparum
- C. Entamoeba histolytica
- D. Human immunodeficiency virus

<details><summary>Answer and explanation</summary>

**B.** P. falciparum malaria cause kar sakta hai. Ye malaria ki only species nahi hai. A enteric fever se related bacterium hai. C amebiasis cause kar sakta hai. D HIV infection cause karta hai.

### 2. RRB Nursing Superintendent — 29 April 2025, shift 3 (reported)

Which conventional laboratory method can identify malaria parasites, determine species and quantify parasitemia?

- A. Widal agglutination
- B. Urine dipstick
- C. Microscopy of stained thick and thin blood films
- D. Routine serum electrolyte measurement

<details><summary>Answer and explanation</summary>

**C.** Thick film parasite detection mein helpful hai. Thin film species aur parasitemia assessment mein useful hai. A enteric fever-associated serology hai aur malaria microscopy replace nahi karti. B urine chemistry assess karti hai. D electrolyte status batata hai lekin parasite identify nahi karta. RDT bhi rapid diagnosis mein useful hai aur current programme guidance follow karni chahiye.

[Retrieved third-party question and reported answer](https://testbook.com/question-answer/which-diagnostic-method-is-recommended-as-the-gold--681db2e40d2952f73802fb25)

</details>
