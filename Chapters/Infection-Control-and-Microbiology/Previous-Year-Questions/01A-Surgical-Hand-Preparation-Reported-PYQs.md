### AIIMS Delhi Nursing Officer, 16 September 2018 — memory-based report

A historical examination source reports three minutes as the expected minimum surgical scrub time. Which approach should a nurse use in current practice when selecting the actual duration?

- A. Use ten minutes for all products.
- B. Follow the validated product-specific instructions.
- C. Use routine ward handwashing duration for all surgical products.
- D. Select duration solely from the operation's expected length.

<details><summary>Answer and explanation</summary>

**B.** Current WHO guidance describes a typical antimicrobial soap scrub of 2–5 minutes, while CDC describes usual 2–6 minutes. Manufacturer instructions determine the actual validated duration. A unnecessary long scrub skin irritation cause kar sakta hai. C routine aur surgical preparation ko confuse karta hai. D operation duration alone product application time decide nahi karti.
