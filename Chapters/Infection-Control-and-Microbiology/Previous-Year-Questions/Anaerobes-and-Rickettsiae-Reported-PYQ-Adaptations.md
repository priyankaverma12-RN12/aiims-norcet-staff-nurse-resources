### 1. HPSSC Hamirpur Staff Nurse — 5 August 2021 (reported)

Foodborne botulism is primarily due to which mechanism?

- A. Immune destruction of red cells
- B. Ingestion of preformed bacterial neurotoxin
- C. Mosquito-transmitted protozoa
- D. Fungal allergy

<details><summary>Answer and explanation</summary>

**B.** Foodborne botulism toxin-mediated hai. Red-cell destruction, protozoal transmission aur fungal allergy different mechanisms hain. Infant and wound forms bhi exist karte hain, so botulism ko sirf food poisoning mat samjho. [Source](https://testbook.com/question-answer/botulism-is-a--697b4a368364567eb189cd9f)

</details>

### 2. UPRVUNL Staff Nurse — 5 July 2021 (reported)

A patient develops painful jaw stiffness and generalized stimulus-induced muscle spasms. Which toxin-mediated illness is most consistent?

- A. Botulism
- B. Cholera
- C. Tetanus
- D. Typhoid

<details><summary>Answer and explanation</summary>

**C.** Tetanus inhibitory motor signaling disrupt karta hai. Botulism flaccid weakness, cholera watery diarrhea aur typhoid systemic enteric fever se associated hain. [Source](https://testbook.com/question-answer/trismus-risus-sardonicus-and-opisthotonus-are-the--619dccf46c38186d064a4d56)

</details>

### 3. ESIC Staff Nurse — 26 February 2019, shift II (reported)

Which organism should be considered in a patient with acute febrile illness following infected chigger exposure?

- A. Rickettsia typhi
- B. Coxiella burnetii
- C. Rickettsia prowazekii
- D. Orientia tsutsugamushi

<details><summary>Answer and explanation</summary>

**D.** Orientia scrub typhus cause karti hai. R. typhi murine typhus, Coxiella Q fever aur R. prowazekii epidemic typhus se associated hain. [Source](https://testbook.com/question-answer/ques--62321057bd5130cee4b76a51)

</details>

### 4. HPSSC Staff Nurse — 1 August 2021 (reported)

Which duration best approximates the usual incubation period of tetanus among these choices?

- A. One hour
- B. Ten days
- C. Six months
- D. Two years

<details><summary>Answer and explanation</summary>

**B.** Tetanus incubation generally days se weeks mein hoti hai. Third-party reported historical answer ten days hai. CDC generally 3–21 days aur average around eight days describe karta hai. A typical incubation ke liye bahut short hai. C aur D typical window se bahut long hain. Exact duration har patient mein same nahi hoti.

[Retrieved reported question and key](https://testbook.com/question-answer/what-is-the-incubation-period-of-tetanus--697c40bea38b204d8c6c5e52) · [CDC clinical overview](https://www.cdc.gov/tetanus/hcp/clinical-overview/index.html)

</details>

### 5. UPPSC Staff Nurse — 3 October 2021 (reported)

Which bacterial species most commonly causes traumatic clostridial myonecrosis?

- A. C. perfringens
- B. C. tetani
- C. C. botulinum
- D. C. difficile

<details><summary>Answer and explanation</summary>

**A.** C. perfringens gas gangrene ka common cause hai aur toxin muscle tissue ko damage kar sakta hai. B tetanus mein rigidity aur spasms cause karta hai. C botulism mein flaccid paralysis cause karta hai. D antibiotic-associated colitis se linked hai. Suspected gas gangrene mein urgent surgical assessment chahiye.

[Indexed reported question](https://testbook.com/question-answer/the-most-predominant-causative-agent-of-gas-gangre--6166b54b164a82529bf048e5) · [IDSA clinical reference](https://www.idsociety.org/practice-guideline/skin-and-soft-tissue-infections/)

</details>
