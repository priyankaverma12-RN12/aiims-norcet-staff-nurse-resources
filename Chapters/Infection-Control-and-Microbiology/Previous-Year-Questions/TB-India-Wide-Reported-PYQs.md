### 1. NVS Staff Nurse, 12 January 2018

Which infection does an intradermal tuberculin test assess?

- A. Cholera
- B. Tuberculosis
- C. Typhoid
- D. Malaria

<details><summary>Answer and explanation</summary>

B. Tuberculosis. Mantoux immune sensitization assess karta hai, active disease independently confirm nahi karta. Cholera and typhoid enteric infections hain, malaria parasite disease hai. Source explanation redness and induration mix karti hai. Correct reading palpable induration hai.

### 2. NCL Staff Nurse, November 2020

What is the primary transmission route of infectious pulmonary TB?

- A. Vector bite
- B. Fecal-oral
- C. Airborne inhalation
- D. Routine skin contact

<details><summary>Answer and explanation</summary>

C. Airborne inhalation. Infectious particles breathe in karne se spread ho sakta hai. Vector, fecal-oral aur skin contact primary routes nahi hain.

### 3. OSSSC Nursing Officer, 19 March 2023

Historical DOTS strategy is associated with which disease?

- A. Leprosy
- B. Malaria
- C. Measles
- D. Tuberculosis

<details><summary>Answer and explanation</summary>

D. Tuberculosis. DOTS historical directly observed treatment short-course approach hai. Leprosy MDT, malaria antimalarial therapy aur measles supportive management/prevention se associated hai. Current NTEP guidance follow karo.

### 4. AIIMS Raipur Nursing Officer, 31 August 2019 shift I, memory-based

Which first-line TB drug is classically associated with peripheral neuropathy?

- A. Ethambutol
- B. Isoniazid
- C. Rifampicin
- D. Pyrazinamide

<details><summary>Answer and explanation</summary>

B. Isoniazid. B6 metabolism alter ho sakta hai. Ethambutol optic toxicity, rifampicin interactions and discoloration, pyrazinamide hyperuricemia se associated hain.

[Source question](https://testbook.com/question-answer/which-of-the-following-antitubercular-drug-causes--625e7b72d566e342841d8a23)

</details>
