# VAP and SSI — practice source and evidence gaps

[Undated VAP source](https://testbook.com/question-answer/the-nurse-is-implementing-a-ventilator-associated--697871002187e3fe5cbf01bd) has no exam attribution. This independent adaptation is practice, not verified PYQ.

Which care plan is least appropriate for a stable ventilated adult without mobility contraindications?

- A. Regular oral hygiene
- B. Unnecessary strict immobilization
- C. Appropriate head elevation
- D. Team-directed mobility

<details><summary>Answer and explanation</summary>

**B.** Unnecessary immobilization does not prevent VAP and can worsen deconditioning. Oral hygiene, positioning and safe mobility are appropriate. Current SHEA guidance favors toothbrushing without routine oral chlorhexidine solely for VAP prevention.
