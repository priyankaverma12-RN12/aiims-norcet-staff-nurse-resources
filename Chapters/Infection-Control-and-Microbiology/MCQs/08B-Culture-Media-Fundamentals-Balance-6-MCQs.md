### Q1. What distinguishes a chemically defined culture medium?

**Difficulty:** Easy | **Priority:** High

- **A.** It is identified only by its container color
- **B.** Every chemical component and its quantity are known
- **C.** It cannot contain any nutrient
- **D.** It must always contain blood

<details><summary>Sahi answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct answer: B. Every chemical component and its quantity are known**

- **A.** Container color medium composition establish nahi karta.
- **B.** Chemically defined medium mein chemical constituents aur unki quantities known hoti hain. Isse nutritional requirements systematically study ki ja sakti hain.
- **C.** Culture medium growth ke liye required nutrients contain kar sakta hai.
- **D.** Blood complex biological material hai, defined medium ki mandatory ingredient nahi.

**Exam clue:** Defined medium = exact known chemical composition.

**Reference:** [OpenStax Microbiology 9.6](https://openstax.org/books/microbiology/pages/9-6-media-used-for-bacterial-growth) (retrieved 28 November 2026).

### Q2. Which of these is a conventional example of a complex culture medium?

**Difficulty:** Easy | **Priority:** High

- **A.** Pure distilled water
- **B.** A solution containing only specified pure glucose and salts
- **C.** Nutrient broth
- **D.** An empty sterile Petri dish

<details><summary>Sahi answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct answer: C. Nutrient broth**

- **A.** Distilled water alone conventional nutrient-rich complex medium nahi hai.
- **B.** Exactly specified pure chemicals se prepared suitable nutrient mixture chemically defined ho sakta hai.
- **C.** Nutrient broth mein extracts ya peptones jaise components ho sakte hain jinki complete molecular composition exactly defined nahi hoti. Isliye complex medium kehlata hai.
- **D.** Empty dish container hai, culture medium nahi.

**Exam clue:** Nutrient broth is a complex medium.

**Reference:** [OpenStax Microbiology 9.6](https://openstax.org/books/microbiology/pages/9-6-media-used-for-bacterial-growth) (retrieved 28 November 2026).

</details>

### Q3. Which medium type is designed to favor growth of a desired microbial group while suppressing other organisms?

**Difficulty:** Easy | **Priority:** High

- **A.** A transport medium by definition
- **B.** A fixed stained slide
- **C.** A nonnutritive microscope immersion oil
- **D.** A selective medium

<details><summary>Sahi answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct answer: D. A selective medium**

- **A.** Transport medium ka main goal specimen viability preserve karna hota hai, desired organism selection by definition nahi.
- **B.** Fixed slide microscopy ke liye hai, microbial growth medium nahi.
- **C.** Immersion oil optical use ke liye hai, nutrient medium nahi.
- **D.** Selective medium mein aise conditions ya substances hote hain jo desired organisms ko grow karne dete hain aur other groups ko inhibit karte hain.

**Exam clue:** Selective medium selects a group by inhibiting others.

**Reference:** [OpenStax Microbiology 9.6](https://openstax.org/books/microbiology/pages/9-6-media-used-for-bacterial-growth) (retrieved 28 November 2026).

</details>

### Q4. What is the purpose of a differential culture medium?

**Difficulty:** Easy | **Priority:** High

- **A.** To distinguish organisms by visible differences in their biochemical activity
- **B.** To guarantee that only one species can ever grow
- **C.** To sterilize every specimen during incubation
- **D.** To prevent all metabolic activity

<details><summary>Sahi answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct answer: A. To distinguish organisms by visible differences in their biochemical activity**

- **A.** Differential medium biochemical differences ko visible changes jaise colony ya surrounding medium color ke through indicate kar sakta hai.
- **B.** Single species guarantee differential medium ki property nahi hai. Multiple organisms grow kar sakte hain.
- **C.** Culture medium growth support karta hai, sterilization process nahi.
- **D.** Differentiation often microbial metabolic activity par depend karti hai, usko completely stop karne par nahi.

**Exam clue:** Differential media reveal biochemical differences.

**Reference:** [OpenStax Microbiology 9.6](https://openstax.org/books/microbiology/pages/9-6-media-used-for-bacterial-growth) (retrieved 28 November 2026).

</details>

### Q5. What is the main purpose of adding nutrients such as blood or serum to an enriched culture medium?

**Difficulty:** Easy | **Priority:** High

- **A.** To make the medium unsuitable for every microorganism
- **B.** To support growth of organisms with demanding nutritional needs
- **C.** To convert the medium into a microscope stain
- **D.** To replace all clinical specimen identification

<details><summary>Sahi answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct answer: B. To support growth of organisms with demanding nutritional needs**

- **A.** Additional nutrients ka purpose growth support karna hota hai, universal inhibition nahi.
- **B.** Fastidious organisms ko extra nutrients chahiye ho sakte hain. Enriched medium nutritional support provide karta hai.
- **C.** Culture medium ka role growth hai, staining reagent ka role contrast provide karna.
- **D.** Specimen identity aur laboratory interpretation ab bhi important hai.

**Exam clue:** Enriched medium supports fastidious organisms.

**Reference:** [OpenStax Microbiology 9.6](https://openstax.org/books/microbiology/pages/9-6-media-used-for-bacterial-growth) (retrieved 28 November 2026).

</details>

### Q6. Which culture-medium property helps maintain conditions suitable for oxygen-sensitive anaerobes?

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Priority:** High

- **A.** A fluorescent dye alone
- **B.** An increased concentration of dissolved oxygen
- **C.** Reducing substances that lower available oxygen
- **D.** A larger label on the container

<details><summary>Sahi answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct answer: C. Reducing substances that lower available oxygen**

- **A.** Fluorescent dye alone low-oxygen growth conditions establish nahi karta.
- **B.** Increased oxygen oxygen-sensitive organisms ki growth interfere kar sakti hai.
- **C.** Reducing agents oxygen availability kam karne mein help karte hain. Anaerobic processing ke liye laboratory validated atmosphere aur procedure follow karti hai.
- **D.** Container label specimen identification mein helpful hai, oxygen control nahi karta.

**Exam clue:** Reducing media support low-oxygen conditions.

**Reference:** [OpenStax Microbiology 9.2](https://openstax.org/books/microbiology/pages/9-2-oxygen-requirements-for-microbial-growth) (retrieved 28 November 2026).
