### Q1. An occupational exposure is limited to nonbloody tears splashing into a nurse's eye. Under the 2025 US PHS HIV guidance, what is the usual interpretation?

**Difficulty:** Easy | **Priority:** High

- **A.** Nonbloody tears are not considered a potentially infectious fluid for HIV
- **B.** All tears contain infectious blood
- **C.** HIV infection is confirmed
- **D.** A hepatitis B vaccine diagnoses HIV

<details><summary>Sahi answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct answer: A. Nonbloody tears are not considered a potentially infectious fluid for HIV**

- **A.** Nonbloody tears HIV exposure assessment mein potentially infectious nahi maane jate. Eye splash first aid aur other infectious hazards ka assessment phir bhi situation ke according ho sakta hai.
- **B.** Har tear mein infectious blood hona zaroori nahi hai. Visible blood aur exposure context important hain.
- **C.** Splash se HIV infection confirm nahi hoti. Risk aur testing alag clinical decisions hain.
- **D.** HBV vaccine HIV diagnosis nahi karti.

**Exam clue:** HIV risk assessment mein fluid type aur exposure route dono dekho.

**Reference:** [2025 US PHS occupational HIV guideline](https://www.cambridge.org/core/journals/infection-control-and-hospital-epidemiology/article/2025-us-public-health-service-guidelines-for-the-management-of-occupational-exposures-to-human-immunodeficiency-virus-and-recommendations-for-postexposure-prophylaxis-in-healthcare-settings/A410E90C8C13C8B9FB417C7F42051D2A).

### Q2. What does PrEP stand for in HIV prevention terminology?

**Difficulty:** Easy | **Priority:** High

- **A.** Primary renal examination procedure
- **B.** Pre-exposure prophylaxis
- **C.** Postoperative respiratory exercise plan
- **D.** Peripheral rehabilitation evaluation protocol

<details><summary>Sahi answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct answer: B. Pre-exposure prophylaxis**

- **A.** Renal examination kidney assessment se related hai, HIV prevention abbreviation nahi.
- **B.** PrEP potential exposure se pehle use hone wali prophylaxis ko kehte hain. PEP exposure ke baad indicated prevention hai.
- **C.** Respiratory exercise postoperative care ka part ho sakta hai, lekin PrEP ka meaning nahi.
- **D.** Rehabilitation protocol HIV prevention medicine ka abbreviation nahi hai.

**Exam clue:** PrEP before exposure, PEP after exposure.

**Reference:** [2025 US PHS occupational HIV guideline](https://www.cambridge.org/core/journals/infection-control-and-hospital-epidemiology/article/2025-us-public-health-service-guidelines-for-the-management-of-occupational-exposures-to-human-immunodeficiency-virus-and-recommendations-for-postexposure-prophylaxis-in-healthcare-settings/A410E90C8C13C8B9FB417C7F42051D2A).

### Q3. A worker is injured by an unknown-source discarded needle. What does the 2025 US PHS guideline say about testing that needle itself for HIV?

**Difficulty:** Easy | **Priority:** High

- **A.** A negative needle test always excludes exposure risk
- **B.** Needle testing must occur before clinical review
- **C.** Do not test the needle for HIV
- **D.** The needle test replaces worker follow-up

<details><summary>Sahi answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct answer: C. Do not test the needle for HIV**

- **A.** Needle ke test se reliable negative assurance nahi milti. Approved method available nahi hai.
- **B.** Needle testing ke liye clinical review delay nahi karna chahiye.
- **C.** Guideline unknown-source sharps ko HIV ke liye test karne se mana karti hai. Clinical risk assessment aur expert advice ke according care plan banaya jata hai.
- **D.** Worker follow-up clinical exposure assessment par depend karta hai, needle test par nahi.

**Exam clue:** Unknown needle ko test karne ke bajay exposure assess karo.

**Reference:** [2025 US PHS occupational HIV guideline](https://www.cambridge.org/core/journals/infection-control-and-hospital-epidemiology/article/2025-us-public-health-service-guidelines-for-the-management-of-occupational-exposures-to-human-immunodeficiency-virus-and-recommendations-for-postexposure-prophylaxis-in-healthcare-settings/A410E90C8C13C8B9FB417C7F42051D2A).

### Q4. Which pair of components does a fourth-generation HIV combination immunoassay detect?

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Priority:** High

- **A.** Hemoglobin and platelets
- **B.** HBsAg and anti-HBs
- **C.** HCV RNA and bilirubin
- **D.** HIV p24 antigen and HIV antibodies

<details><summary>Sahi answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct answer: D. HIV p24 antigen and HIV antibodies**

- **A.** Hemoglobin aur platelets blood count parameters hain, HIV combination immunoassay targets nahi.
- **B.** HBsAg aur anti-HBs hepatitis B serology ke markers hain.
- **C.** HCV RNA hepatitis C testing ka target hai, bilirubin liver function se related hai.
- **D.** Fourth-generation HIV combination test p24 antigen aur HIV antibodies detect karta hai. Exposure ke baad baseline aur indicated follow-up clinical guideline ke mutabik hota hai.

**Exam clue:** Fourth generation HIV assay antigen plus antibody detect karta hai.

**Reference:** [2025 US PHS occupational HIV guideline](https://www.cambridge.org/core/journals/infection-control-and-hospital-epidemiology/article/2025-us-public-health-service-guidelines-for-the-management-of-occupational-exposures-to-human-immunodeficiency-virus-and-recommendations-for-postexposure-prophylaxis-in-healthcare-settings/A410E90C8C13C8B9FB417C7F42051D2A).

### Q5. Which group of medicines is used for HIV PEP?

**Difficulty:** Easy | **Priority:** High

- **A.** Antiretroviral medicines
- **B.** Antifungal creams alone
- **C.** Antipyretics alone
- **D.** Oral iron supplements alone

<details><summary>Sahi answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct answer: A. Antiretroviral medicines**

- **A.** Antiretroviral medicines HIV replication ke stages ko target karti hain. Clinician indicated exposure ke liye suitable regimen choose karta hai.
- **B.** Antifungal cream fungal infection treat karti hai, HIV prophylaxis nahi.
- **C.** Antipyretic fever reduce kar sakta hai lekin HIV prevention regimen nahi hai.
- **D.** Iron deficiency treatment HIV prophylaxis provide nahi karta.

**Exam clue:** HIV PEP antiretroviral medicines se hoti hai.

**Reference:** [WHO HIV PEP 2024](https://www.who.int/publications/i/item/9789240095137).

</details>

### Q6. In WHO's 2024 HIV PEP guidance, what is the ideal time window for starting PEP when indicated?

**Difficulty:** Easy | **Priority:** High

- **A.** After seven days
- **B.** Within 24 hours, as soon as possible
- **C.** Only after symptoms appear
- **D.** After the final follow-up test

<details><summary>Sahi answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct answer: B. Within 24 hours, as soon as possible**

- **A.** Seven days usual initiation limit se beyond hai. Urgent specialist evaluation delay nahi karni chahiye.
- **B.** WHO earliest possible start ko emphasize karta hai, ideally 24 hours ke andar aur no later than 72 hours.
- **C.** Symptoms ka wait karne se prevention ka important window miss ho sakta hai.
- **D.** Final follow-up test prevention course start hone ke baad ka assessment hai, initiation ka prerequisite nahi.

**Exam clue:** Ideal start within 24 hours aur maximum usual window 72 hours ko distinguish karo.

**Reference:** [WHO HIV PEP 2024](https://www.who.int/publications/i/item/9789240095137).

</details>
