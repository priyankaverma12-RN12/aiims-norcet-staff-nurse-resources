### Q1. What is the principal lethal mechanism of dry-heat sterilization?

**Difficulty:** Easy | **Priority:** High

- **A.** Oxidative damage to microbial cell constituents
- **B.** Freezing of microbial cytoplasm
- **C.** Stimulation of bacterial metabolism
- **D.** Removal of organisms by a membrane filter

<details><summary>Sahi answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct answer: A. Oxidative damage to microbial cell constituents**

- **A.** Dry heat microbial cell ke important constituents ko oxidation se damage karti hai, jis wajah se essential functions fail ho jate hain.
- **B.** Freezing ka mechanism dry-heat sterilizer mein nahi hota. Dry heat elevated temperature use karti hai.
- **C.** Metabolism stimulate karna sterilization ka goal nahi hai. Process ka goal viable microorganisms eliminate karna hai.
- **D.** Filtration physical separation ka method hai, jabki dry heat microorganisms ko thermal damage se kill karti hai.

**Exam clue:** Dry heat ko oxidation aur moist heat ko protein denaturation se associate karo.

**Reference:** [CDC other sterilization methods](https://www.cdc.gov/infection-control/hcp/disinfection-sterilization/other-sterilization-methods.html) (retrieved 28 November 2026).

### Q2. Which feature is a common disadvantage of dry-heat sterilization compared with steam for compatible items?

**Difficulty:** Easy | **Priority:** High

- **A.** It needs no validated cycle
- **B.** Heat penetration and microbial killing are relatively slow
- **C.** It works only by adding liquid disinfectant
- **D.** It cannot reach high temperatures

<details><summary>Sahi answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct answer: B. Heat penetration and microbial killing are relatively slow**

- **A.** Dry heat ke liye bhi validated temperature aur exposure time required hote hain.
- **B.** Dry heat mein heat penetration aur microbial killing comparatively slow ho sakti hai, isliye process time longer ho sakta hai.
- **C.** Dry-heat sterilizer hot air use karta hai. Liquid disinfectant add karna iska basic principle nahi hai.
- **D.** Dry-heat sterilizers high temperatures achieve karte hain. Limitation slow heat transfer aur material compatibility ki hoti hai.

**Exam clue:** Dry heat aksar steam ke comparison mein longer processing demand karti hai.

**Reference:** [CDC other sterilization methods](https://www.cdc.gov/infection-control/hcp/disinfection-sterilization/other-sterilization-methods.html) (retrieved 28 November 2026).

</details>

### Q3. Which component circulates heated air in a forced-air dry-heat sterilizer?

**Difficulty:** Easy | **Priority:** High

- **A.** A gravity-fed water reservoir
- **B.** A biological indicator incubator
- **C.** A motor-driven blower
- **D.** An ultraviolet lamp

<details><summary>Sahi answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct answer: C. A motor-driven blower**

- **A.** Water reservoir dry-heat forced circulation provide nahi karta. Is method mein heated air circulate hoti hai.
- **B.** Incubator biological indicator ke readout process mein use ho sakta hai, lekin hot air circulate nahi karta.
- **C.** Motor-driven blower chamber mein heated air circulate karta hai. Isse static-air oven ke comparison mein heat transfer faster aur more uniform ho sakta hai.
- **D.** UV lamp radiation emit karta hai, heated air circulation ka mechanical component nahi hai.

**Exam clue:** Forced-air oven mein blower hot air circulate karta hai.

**Reference:** [CDC other sterilization methods](https://www.cdc.gov/infection-control/hcp/disinfection-sterilization/other-sterilization-methods.html) (retrieved 28 November 2026).

### Q4. According to CDC guidance, a routine Bowie-Dick air-removal test for a prevacuum steam sterilizer is performed in which condition?

**Difficulty:** Easy | **Priority:** High

- **A.** With the largest available surgical load
- **B.** With wet patient linen packed inside
- **C.** Only after a positive biological result
- **D.** In an empty chamber using the specified test system

<details><summary>Sahi answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct answer: D. In an empty chamber using the specified test system**

- **A.** Routine air-removal test ke liye ordinary full surgical load specified test condition nahi hai.
- **B.** Wet patient linen chamber mein rakhna standardized Bowie-Dick test ka method nahi hai.
- **C.** Bowie-Dick test regular daily process check hai, sirf positive biological indicator ke baad perform nahi hota.
- **D.** CDC empty dynamic-air-removal sterilizer mein daily test describe karta hai. Approved test pack aur device instructions follow karne se air removal assess hoti hai.

**Exam clue:** Bowie-Dick test empty chamber mein specified test system se hota hai.

**Reference:** [CDC sterilizing practices](https://www.cdc.gov/infection-control/hcp/disinfection-sterilization/sterilizing-practices.html) (retrieved 28 November 2026).

### Q5. Which environmental parameter is particularly important for the effectiveness of gaseous sterilants such as ethylene oxide?

**Difficulty:** Easy | **Priority:** High

- **A.** Relative humidity
- **B.** The colour of room walls
- **C.** The number of beds in the ward
- **D.** The weight of the operator

<details><summary>Sahi answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct answer: A. Relative humidity**

- **A.** Relative humidity gaseous sterilant ki activity affect karti hai. Ethylene oxide cycle mein approved humidity conditions validated process ka hissa hoti hain.
- **B.** Wall colour gas sterilant ki microbial killing parameter nahi hai.
- **C.** Ward bed count sterilization cycle ka physical parameter nahi hai.
- **D.** Operator ka body weight chamber mein sterilant efficacy decide nahi karta.

**Exam clue:** Ethylene oxide process mein humidity ko important parameter samjho.

### Q6. What is used to remove air from the chamber before steam enters a conventional prevacuum sterilizer?

**Difficulty:** Easy | **Priority:** High

- **A.** A dry cloth
- **B.** A vacuum pump or ejector
- **C.** A chemical indicator strip
- **D.** An instrument lubricant

<details><summary>Sahi answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct answer: B. A vacuum pump or ejector**

- **A.** Dry cloth chamber se air extract nahi kar sakta. Air removal ke liye designed mechanical system chahiye.
- **B.** Prevacuum system pump ya ejector use karta hai, taaki steam admit hone se pehle air remove ho aur steam load mein better penetrate kare.
- **C.** Chemical indicator processing exposure assess karne mein help karta hai, lekin air mechanically remove nahi karta.
- **D.** Lubricant instrument maintenance mein useful ho sakta hai, chamber air removal ke liye nahi.

**Exam clue:** Prevacuum sterilizer mein vacuum pump air removal karta hai.

**Reference:** [CDC steam sterilization](https://www.cdc.gov/infection-control/hcp/disinfection-sterilization/steam-sterilization.html) (retrieved 28 November 2026).
