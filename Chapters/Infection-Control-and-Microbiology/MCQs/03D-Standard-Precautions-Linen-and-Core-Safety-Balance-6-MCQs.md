### Q1. How should a nurse handle used bed linen to reduce the spread of contamination?

**Difficulty:** Easy | **Priority:** High

- **A.** Shake it vigorously before folding
- **B.** Hold it against the uniform
- **C.** Handle it with as little agitation as possible
- **D.** Place it on the floor for sorting

<details><summary>Sahi answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct answer: C. Handle it with as little agitation as possible**

- **A.** Vigorous shaking se contaminated particles air aur nearby surfaces par disperse ho sakte hain.
- **B.** Uniform ke contact se contamination kapdon tak transfer ho sakti hai.
- **C.** Minimum agitation se contaminated particles ki unnecessary dispersal kam hoti hai. Nurse indicated PPE aur facility laundry procedure follow karti hai.
- **D.** Floor par linen rakhne se environmental contamination aur extra handling badh sakti hai.

**Exam clue:** Used linen ko gently handle karo, shake mat karo.

**Reference:** [CDC isolation recommendations](https://www.cdc.gov/infection-control/hcp/isolation-precautions/recommendations.html) (retrieved 28 November 2026).

### Q2. Where should wet contaminated linen that could leak be placed for transport?

**Difficulty:** Easy | **Priority:** High

- **A.** An open basket without any liner
- **B.** A clean patient's bedside cabinet
- **C.** An ordinary paper tray that allows leakage
- **D.** A designated leak-resistant laundry container

<details><summary>Sahi answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct answer: D. A designated leak-resistant laundry container**

- **A.** Open unlined basket se fluid leak ho sakta hai aur route par contamination ho sakti hai.
- **B.** Clean patient's cabinet contaminated linen ke liye designated area nahi hai.
- **C.** Paper tray fluid leak hone se prevent nahi karegi.
- **D.** Leak-resistant designated container wet linen ko safely contain karta hai. Facility ke approved laundry system ke according transport karo.

**Exam clue:** Wet contaminated linen ke liye leak-resistant containment zaroori hai.

**Reference:** [CDC isolation recommendations](https://www.cdc.gov/infection-control/hcp/isolation-precautions/recommendations.html) (retrieved 28 November 2026).

### Q3. Which body fluid is excluded from the general Standard Precautions assumption that body fluids may contain transmissible infectious agents, unless visibly contaminated with blood?

**Difficulty:** Easy | **Priority:** High

- **A.** Sweat
- **B.** Semen
- **C.** Pleural fluid
- **D.** Cerebrospinal fluid

<details><summary>Sahi answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct answer: A. Sweat**

- **A.** CDC Standard Precautions framework mein sweat ko general potentially infectious body fluid assumption se exclude kiya jata hai. Agar blood contamination ho ya koi specific indication ho toh appropriate precautions phir bhi chahiye.
- **B.** Semen infectious agents transmit kar sakta hai, isliye sweat wali exception is par apply nahi hoti.
- **C.** Pleural fluid clinical exposure mein potentially infectious material maana jata hai.
- **D.** Cerebrospinal fluid exposure mein bhi infection risk assess karke appropriate precautions follow karte hain.

**Exam clue:** Standard Precautions ka common exam exception sweat hai, with blood contamination caveat.

**Reference:** [CDC isolation recommendations](https://www.cdc.gov/infection-control/hcp/isolation-precautions/recommendations.html) (retrieved 28 November 2026).

### Q5. Which action supports occupational infection prevention for healthcare personnel before they start patient-care duties?

**Difficulty:** Easy | **Priority:** High

- **A.** Discourage staff vaccination
- **B.** Use antibiotics routinely for every healthy worker
- **C.** Assess vaccination and immunity needs according to occupational health guidance
- **D.** Wait until an outbreak before discussing immunization

<details><summary>Sahi answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct answer: C. Assess vaccination and immunity needs according to occupational health guidance**

- **A.** Vaccination indicated infections ke against protection provide kar sakti hai. Isko discourage karna prevention ko weaken karta hai.
- **B.** Healthy workers ko routine antibiotics dena vaccination ka replacement nahi hai aur unnecessary adverse effects aur resistance ka risk hai.
- **C.** Occupational health assessment se worker ki indicated immunizations aur immunity requirements identify ki ja sakti hain. Local policy follow karni chahiye.
- **D.** Outbreak ka wait karna preventive protection delay karta hai.

**Exam clue:** Occupational immunization needs exposure se pehle assess karo.

**Reference:** [CDC core infection prevention practices](https://www.cdc.gov/infection-control/hcp/core-practices/) (retrieved 28 November 2026).

</details>

### Q6. A nurse has symptoms of a potentially transmissible illness before the shift. What is the appropriate first administrative action?

**Difficulty:** Easy | **Priority:** High

- **A.** Hide the symptoms from the supervisor
- **B.** Take an antibiotic without assessment and work as usual
- **C.** Wear a second uniform and ignore the illness
- **D.** Report the illness through the facility's occupational health procedure

<details><summary>Sahi answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct answer: D. Report the illness through the facility's occupational health procedure**

- **A.** Symptoms hide karne se coworkers aur patients ko preventable exposure ho sakta hai.
- **B.** Bina clinical assessment antibiotic lena appropriate nahi hai, aur har transmissible illness bacterial nahi hoti.
- **C.** Second uniform respiratory ya other transmission risk ko adequately manage nahi karta.
- **D.** Reporting se occupational health team illness, work restriction aur return-to-work guidance assess kar sakti hai. Nurse ko facility procedure follow karna chahiye.

**Exam clue:** Healthcare worker illness reporting patient safety ka important part hai.

**Reference:** [CDC core infection prevention practices](https://www.cdc.gov/infection-control/hcp/core-practices/) (retrieved 28 November 2026).
