### Q1. Which route is specified for HBIG when indicated for occupational HBV post-exposure prophylaxis under CDC guidance?

**Difficulty:** Easy | **Priority:** High

- **A.** Intradermal
- **B.** Intramuscular
- **C.** Oral
- **D.** Inhalational

<details><summary>Sahi answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct answer: B. Intramuscular**

- **A.** Intradermal route HBIG occupational prophylaxis ki specified route nahi hai.
- **B.** HBIG ready-made protective antibodies provide karta hai aur indicated occupational exposure mein intramuscularly diya jata hai. Clinician dose aur timing decide karta hai.
- **C.** Oral administration se required antibody delivery nahi hoti.
- **D.** Inhalation HBIG administration ka specified method nahi hai.

**Exam clue:** Indicated HBIG is given intramuscularly.

**Reference:** [CDC Pink Book hepatitis B](https://www.cdc.gov/pinkbook/hcp/table-of-contents/chapter-10-hepatitis-b.html).

</details>

### Q2. Which muscle is the preferred site for routine intramuscular hepatitis B vaccination in an adult healthcare worker?

**Difficulty:** Easy | **Priority:** High

- **A.** Masseter
- **B.** Intercostal muscle
- **C.** Deltoid
- **D.** Extraocular muscle

<details><summary>Sahi answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct answer: C. Deltoid**

- **A.** Masseter chewing muscle hai aur routine adult vaccination site nahi hai.
- **B.** Intercostal muscle ribs ke beech hota hai aur routine vaccination site nahi hai.
- **C.** Adult HepB vaccination ke liye upper arm deltoid preferred site hai. Correct needle aur administration technique follow karni chahiye.
- **D.** Extraocular muscles eye movements control karte hain, vaccination site nahi hain.

**Exam clue:** Adult HepB vaccination mein deltoid IM yaad rakho.

**Reference:** [CDC hepatitis B vaccine administration](https://www.cdc.gov/hepatitis-b/hcp/vaccine-administration/index.html).

</details>

### Q3. Which hepatitis B viral antigen is used in the conventional recombinant HepB vaccine?

**Difficulty:** Easy | **Priority:** High

- **A.** HCV core protein
- **B.** HIV p24
- **C.** HBV polymerase alone
- **D.** Hepatitis B surface antigen

<details><summary>Sahi answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct answer: D. Hepatitis B surface antigen**

- **A.** HCV core protein different virus ka component hai.
- **B.** HIV p24 HIV ka antigen hai, HBV vaccine antigen nahi.
- **C.** HBV polymerase replication enzyme hai, conventional recombinant vaccine ka antigen nahi.
- **D.** Recombinant HepB vaccine surface antigen se immune response stimulate karti hai. Ismein infectious HBV nahi diya jata.

**Exam clue:** Recombinant HepB vaccine uses HBsAg.

**Reference:** [CDC hepatitis B diagnosis](https://www.cdc.gov/hepatitis-b/hcp/diagnosis-testing/).

</details>

### Q4. Which history is a contraindication to giving another dose of the same HepB vaccine without specialist assessment?

**Difficulty:** Easy | **Priority:** High

- **A.** Anaphylaxis after a previous dose
- **B.** A healed paper cut
- **C.** Wearing spectacles
- **D.** A remote uncomplicated ankle sprain

<details><summary>Sahi answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct answer: A. Anaphylaxis after a previous dose**

- **A.** Previous vaccine dose se severe allergic reaction further dose ka contraindication hai. Occupational clinician suitable alternative protection assess karega.
- **B.** Healed paper cut vaccine component allergy nahi hai.
- **C.** Spectacles use ka vaccine contraindication se relation nahi hai.
- **D.** Old uncomplicated ankle sprain severe vaccine allergy nahi hai.

**Exam clue:** Previous vaccine anaphylaxis ko serious contraindication samjho.

**Reference:** [CDC Pink Book hepatitis B](https://www.cdc.gov/pinkbook/hcp/table-of-contents/chapter-10-hepatitis-b.html).

</details>

### Q5. Which factor is associated with a lower likelihood of responding adequately to hepatitis B vaccination?

**Difficulty:** Easy | **Priority:** High

- **A.** A short surname
- **B.** Smoking
- **C.** Left-handedness
- **D.** Wearing a blue uniform

<details><summary>Sahi answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct answer: B. Smoking**

- **A.** Surname length immune response ko determine nahi karti.
- **B.** Smoking vaccine nonresponse se associated host factors mein include hota hai. Individual protection documented serology aur vaccination history se assess hoti hai.
- **C.** Hand dominance ko established vaccine nonresponse factor nahi maana jata.
- **D.** Uniform color immune response ka predictor nahi hai.

**Exam clue:** Smoking aur older age jaise host factors vaccine response ko affect kar sakte hain.

**Reference:** [CDC Pink Book hepatitis B](https://www.cdc.gov/pinkbook/hcp/table-of-contents/chapter-10-hepatitis-b.html).

### Q6. A new healthcare employee has persistent lack of protective antibody after two properly administered complete HepB vaccine series. Before classifying the worker as susceptible, which investigation helps exclude existing HBV infection?

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Priority:** High

- **A.** Chest radiography alone
- **B.** Blood grouping alone
- **C.** HBsAg and anti-HBc testing
- **D.** Urine glucose alone

<details><summary>Sahi answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct answer: C. HBsAg and anti-HBc testing**

- **A.** Chest radiography HBV serology establish nahi karti.
- **B.** Blood group ABO compatibility batata hai, HBV infection status nahi.
- **C.** CDC guidance persistent vaccine nonresponse mein HBsAg aur anti-HBc testing describe karti hai. Existing infection aur true susceptible nonresponse ko distinguish karna zaroori hai.
- **D.** Urine glucose se HBV infection status determine nahi hota.

**Exam clue:** Persistent vaccine nonresponse mein existing infection ko bhi assess karo.

**Reference:** [CDC Pink Book hepatitis B](https://www.cdc.gov/pinkbook/hcp/table-of-contents/chapter-10-hepatitis-b.html).

</details>
