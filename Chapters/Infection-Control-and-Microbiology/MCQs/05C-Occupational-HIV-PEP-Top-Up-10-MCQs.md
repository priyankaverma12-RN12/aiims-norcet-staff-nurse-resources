### Q1. A worker received an appropriate first HIV PEP dose shortly after an occupational exposure. Which early clinical review does the 2025 US PHS guideline recommend?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Priority:** High

- **A.** Reevaluate within 72 hours after exposure for counselling and PEP tolerability
- **B.** Wait until completion of the full course before any review
- **C.** Repeat only source testing at six months
- **D.** Require daily HIV antibody tests

<details><summary>✅ Sahi answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct answer: A. Reevaluate within 72 hours after exposure for counselling and PEP tolerability**

**Kyun sahi hai:** Early review helps reassess the indication, counsel the worker and manage adverse effects before they disrupt adherence.

**Baaki options kyun galat hain?**

- **B.** Early adverse effects and adherence barriers may be missed.
- **C.** This does not address early worker needs.
- **D.** Repeated daily testing does not address the diagnostic window or tolerability.

</details>

### Q10. Why are antiretroviral medicines with complementary targets used together in standard HIV PEP?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Priority:** High

- **A.** To disinfect the puncture wound directly
- **B.** To inhibit viral replication at different stages before infection becomes established
- **C.** To supply ready-made vaccine antibodies
- **D.** To guarantee eradication of all latent HIV reservoirs

<details><summary>✅ Sahi answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct answer: B. To inhibit viral replication at different stages before infection becomes established**

**Kyun sahi hai:** Combination prophylaxis targets viral replication. It is not lifelong immunity or a replacement for primary prevention.

**Baaki options kyun galat hain?**

- **A.** Local wound cleansing serves a separate purpose.
- **C.** Antiretroviral medicines are not immunoglobulins.
- **D.** PEP does not establish such a guarantee.

</details>
