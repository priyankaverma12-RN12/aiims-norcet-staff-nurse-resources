### Q1. Which waste stream includes an experimental animal carcass from a veterinary research laboratory under Schedule I?

**Difficulty:** Easy | **Priority:** High

- **A.** Yellow
- **B.** Red
- **C.** White translucent
- **D.** Blue

<details><summary>Sahi answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct answer: A. Yellow**

- **A.** Animal anatomical waste ko yellow stream mein segregate karte hain kyunki ismein biological tissue hota hai.
- **B.** Red stream contaminated recyclable items ke liye hai, animal tissue ke liye nahi.
- **C.** White translucent container sharps ke liye hai, carcass ke liye nahi.
- **D.** Blue stream specified glassware aur metallic implants ke liye hai.

**Exam clue:** Animal anatomical waste bhi yellow category mein aata hai.

**Reference:** [CPCB, Bio-Medical Waste Management Rules 2016, Schedule I](https://cpcb.nic.in/uploads/Projects/Bio-Medical-Waste/Bio-medical_Waste_Management_Rules_2016.pdf).

</details>

### Q2. A contaminated mattress is permanently discarded rather than sent for laundry. Which Schedule I category applies?

**Difficulty:** Easy | **Priority:** High

- **A.** Red
- **B.** Yellow
- **C.** Blue
- **D.** White translucent

<details><summary>Sahi answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct answer: B. Yellow**

- **A.** Red category recyclable contaminated items ke liye hai, discarded contaminated mattress ke liye nahi.
- **B.** Blood ya body fluid se contaminated discarded bedding aur mattress yellow category mein aate hain. Suitable packing aur approved treatment chahiye.
- **C.** Blue stream glassware aur specified metallic implants ke liye hai.
- **D.** White translucent stream puncture aur cut karne wale sharps ke liye hai.

**Exam clue:** Discarded contaminated bedding ko reusable laundry se alag samjho.

**Reference:** [CPCB, Bio-Medical Waste Management Rules 2016, Schedule I](https://cpcb.nic.in/uploads/Projects/Bio-Medical-Waste/Bio-medical_Waste_Management_Rules_2016.pdf).

</details>

### Q3. Which receptacle property is needed for discarded live vaccine material awaiting required on-site laboratory pretreatment?

**Difficulty:** Easy | **Priority:** High

- **A.** An open paper tray
- **B.** A reusable food basket
- **C.** A container suitable for the validated autoclave process
- **D.** An ordinary municipal waste bag

<details><summary>Sahi answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct answer: C. A container suitable for the validated autoclave process**

- **A.** Open tray contaminated material ko safely contain nahi karti.
- **B.** Food basket biomedical laboratory waste processing ke liye suitable nahi hai.
- **C.** Live ya attenuated vaccine waste laboratory waste group mein aata hai. Schedule I autoclave-safe bags ya containers specify karta hai aur approved pretreatment follow karna chahiye.
- **D.** Municipal bag required laboratory waste pretreatment aur containment provide nahi karta.

**Exam clue:** Live vaccine waste ko approved laboratory pretreatment pathway mein rakho.

**Reference:** [CPCB, Bio-Medical Waste Management Rules 2016, Schedule I](https://cpcb.nic.in/uploads/Projects/Bio-Medical-Waste/Bio-medical_Waste_Management_Rules_2016.pdf).

### Q4. Under Schedule I, which waste stream includes discarded solid chemicals used in the production of biologicals?

### Q5. What is the appropriate disposal category for a discarded contaminated plastic urine collection bag without a sharp?

**Difficulty:** Easy | **Priority:** High

- **A.** Red
- **B.** Yellow
- **C.** White translucent
- **D.** Blue

<details><summary>Sahi answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct answer: A. Red**

- **A.** Used plastic urine bag Schedule I ke contaminated recyclable items mein listed hai. Liquid content ko facility policy ke according safely manage karke bag red stream mein jata hai.
- **B.** Yellow stream anatomical, soiled aur other specified wastes ke liye hai. Urine bag specifically red mein listed hai.
- **C.** White translucent container sharps ke liye hai, needleless plastic bag ke liye nahi.
- **D.** Blue stream specified glass aur metallic implants ke liye hai.

**Exam clue:** Urine collection bag without sharps is a red-category item.

**Reference:** [CPCB, Bio-Medical Waste Management Rules 2016, Schedule I](https://cpcb.nic.in/uploads/Projects/Bio-Medical-Waste/Bio-medical_Waste_Management_Rules_2016.pdf).

### Q6. Which category receives a discarded unused metal needle that is still capable of causing a puncture?

**Difficulty:** Easy | **Priority:** High

- **A.** Red
- **B.** White translucent
- **C.** Yellow soiled waste
- **D.** Blue glass waste

<details><summary>Sahi answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct answer: B. White translucent**

- **A.** Red bag mein sharp needle rakhne se puncture hazard create hota hai.
- **B.** Schedule I sharps mein used aur discarded unused needles bhi include karta hai. Inhe puncture-resistant designated white translucent container mein rakho.
- **C.** Yellow soiled waste bag needle puncture se adequate protection nahi deti.
- **D.** Blue glass stream metal needle ke liye specified sharps pathway nahi hai.

**Exam clue:** Unused discarded needle bhi sharp hai.

**Reference:** [CPCB, Bio-Medical Waste Management Rules 2016, Schedule I](https://cpcb.nic.in/uploads/Projects/Bio-Medical-Waste/Bio-medical_Waste_Management_Rules_2016.pdf).

</details>
