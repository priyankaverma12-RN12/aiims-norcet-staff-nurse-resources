### Q1. Where should the cuffs of examination gloves lie when worn with a conventional isolation gown?

**Difficulty:** Easy | **Priority:** High

- **A.** Below the gown cuffs, leaving bare wrist exposed
- **B.** Inside the uniform pocket
- **C.** Over the wrist cuffs of the gown
- **D.** Only over the fingertips

<details><summary>Sahi answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct answer: C. Over the wrist cuffs of the gown**

- **A.** Bare wrist exposed rehne se sleeve aur glove ke beech protective coverage gap ban sakta hai.
- **B.** Pocket mein cuff rakhne se hand aur wrist protect nahi hote.
- **C.** Gloves gown ke wrist cuffs ko cover karte hain, jisse protective coverage continue rehti hai. Task ke liye appropriate PPE select karo.
- **D.** Sirf fingertips cover karne se rest of hand aur wrist expose reh sakte hain.

**Exam clue:** Glove cuff ko isolation gown cuff ke upar extend karo.

### Q2. In the CDC conventional isolation-gown example, where are the gown ties fastened?

**Difficulty:** Easy | **Priority:** High

- **A.** On the front of the face
- **B.** Around the ankles
- **C.** On the gloves
- **D.** At the back of the neck and waist

<details><summary>Sahi answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct answer: D. At the back of the neck and waist**

- **A.** Face ke front par gown ties fasten karna described design nahi hai aur breathing ya vision interfere ho sakti hai.
- **B.** Ankles par ties neck aur torso coverage secure nahi karti.
- **C.** Gloves par gown ties fasten karna standard donning step nahi hai.
- **D.** Neck aur waist ke back par ties fasten karne se conventional isolation gown ka wrap secure hota hai. Actual gown design ke instructions follow karo.

**Exam clue:** Conventional tie-back gown neck aur waist par secure hota hai.

**Reference:** [CDC PPE donning and removal illustration](https://www.cdc.gov/infection-control/hcp/isolation-precautions/appendix-a-figure.html) (retrieved 28 November 2026).

### Q3. Which part of a conventional medical mask is adjusted to fit the bridge of the nose?

**Difficulty:** Easy | **Priority:** High

- **A.** The flexible nose band
- **B.** The lower gown tie
- **C.** The glove cuff
- **D.** The face shield strap

<details><summary>Sahi answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct answer: A. The flexible nose band**

- **A.** Flexible nose band nose bridge ke shape ke according adjust hota hai, jisse mask face par properly fit ho sakta hai.
- **B.** Gown tie torso protection secure karta hai, medical mask fit nahi.
- **C.** Glove cuff hand aur wrist coverage ke liye hota hai.

**Exam clue:** Mask donning mein nose band ko nose bridge par adjust karo.

### Q4. While providing care in contaminated gloves, which action helps avoid self-inoculation?

**Difficulty:** Easy | **Priority:** High

- **A.** Rub the eyes with a gloved finger
- **B.** Keep gloved hands away from the face
- **C.** Adjust personal contact lenses repeatedly
- **D.** Touch the mouth to test glove integrity

<details><summary>Sahi answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct answer: B. Keep gloved hands away from the face**

- **A.** Gloved finger se eye rub karne par contamination conjunctiva tak transfer ho sakti hai.
- **B.** Contaminated gloved hands ko face se door rakhne se eyes, nose aur mouth ke mucous membranes tak germs transfer hone ka risk kam hota hai.
- **C.** Contact lenses adjust karne se gloved hand eye ke near aata hai. Pehle safe glove removal aur hand hygiene chahiye.
- **D.** Glove integrity check karne ke liye mouth touch karna contamination exposure create karta hai.

**Exam clue:** Gloves pehenkar face touch karne se self-contamination ho sakti hai.

**Reference:** [CDC PPE donning and removal illustration](https://www.cdc.gov/infection-control/hcp/isolation-precautions/appendix-a-figure.html) (retrieved 28 November 2026).

### Q5. After safe removal, where should reusable protective goggles be placed?

**Difficulty:** Easy | **Priority:** High

- **A.** On the clean medication preparation surface
- **B.** In a pocket alongside unused gloves
- **C.** In the designated receptacle for reprocessing
- **D.** Directly on the next patient's bedside table

<details><summary>Sahi answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct answer: C. In the designated receptacle for reprocessing**

- **A.** Used goggles ki external surface contaminated ho sakti hai, isliye medication preparation surface par rakhna inappropriate hai.
- **B.** Clean gloves ke saath pocket mein rakhne se clean supplies contaminate ho sakti hain.
- **C.** Designated receptacle reprocessing ke liye contaminated goggles ko safely contain karta hai. Manufacturer instructions ke according cleaning aur disinfection zaroori hai.
- **D.** Next patient's bedside table par unprocessed goggles rakhne se contamination transfer ho sakti hai.

**Exam clue:** Reusable eye protection ko designated reprocessing receptacle mein rakho.

**Reference:** [CDC PPE donning and removal illustration](https://www.cdc.gov/infection-control/hcp/isolation-precautions/appendix-a-figure.html) (retrieved 28 November 2026).

### Q6. When removing a conventional contaminated tie-on surgical mask using the CDC example, which part should be handled?

**Difficulty:** Easy | **Priority:** High

- **A.** The front surface
- **B.** The inner filter material
- **C.** The nose band from the contaminated front
- **D.** The ties at the back, avoiding the front

<details><summary>Sahi answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct answer: D. The ties at the back, avoiding the front**

- **A.** Mask front contaminated ho sakta hai. Isko grasp karne se hand contamination ka risk hota hai.
- **B.** Filter material ko handle karna mask removal ka safe method nahi hai.
- **C.** Contaminated front ke nose band ko touch karna unnecessary contamination risk create karta hai.
- **D.** CDC conventional mask removal example mein back ties handle karke front se contact avoid karte hain. Product aur facility procedure follow karna chahiye.

**Exam clue:** Mask removal mein contaminated front avoid karke ties ya straps handle karo.

**Reference:** [CDC PPE donning and removal illustration](https://www.cdc.gov/infection-control/hcp/isolation-precautions/appendix-a-figure.html) (retrieved 28 November 2026).
