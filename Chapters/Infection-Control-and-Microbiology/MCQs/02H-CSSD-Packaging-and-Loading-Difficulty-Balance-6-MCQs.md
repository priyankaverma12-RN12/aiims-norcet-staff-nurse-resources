### Q1. Which packaging option is designed to hold a single small reusable instrument during compatible sterilization and subsequent sterile storage?

**Difficulty:** Easy | **Priority:** High

- **A.** An approved peel-open sterilization pouch
- **B.** An ordinary unsealed paper envelope
- **C.** A used patient linen bag
- **D.** An open medication cup

<details><summary>Sahi answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct answer: A. An approved peel-open sterilization pouch**

- **A.** Approved peel-open pouch sterilant ko penetrate karne deta hai aur processing ke baad microbial barrier provide karta hai, jab pouch intact aur properly sealed ho.
- **B.** Ordinary envelope sterilization ke liye validated nahi hota aur uski barrier quality establish nahi hoti.
- **C.** Used linen bag contaminated ho sakta hai aur sterile packaging ke liye suitable nahi hai.
- **D.** Open cup instrument ko environmental contamination se protect nahi karta.

**Exam clue:** Peel pouch compatible instrument aur sterilization method ke liye approved hona chahiye.

**Reference:** [CDC sterilizing practices](https://www.cdc.gov/infection-control/hcp/disinfection-sterilization/sterilizing-practices.html) (retrieved 28 November 2026).

### Q2. A compatible concave basin is being arranged for steam processing. How should it be positioned?

**Difficulty:** Easy | **Priority:** High

- **A.** With a puddle of water trapped inside
- **B.** To allow condensate to drain
- **C.** Under a sealed impermeable cover
- **D.** With a dirty cloth inside the basin

<details><summary>Sahi answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct answer: B. To allow condensate to drain**

- **A.** Trapped water drying aur process quality ko affect kar sakta hai. Basin ko aise arrange karna chahiye ki water drain ho sake.
- **B.** Concave surface mein steam condensation jama ho sakti hai. Validated loading instructions ke mutabik drainage facilitate karna important hai.
- **C.** Impermeable cover sterilant penetration ko interfere kar sakta hai aur approved loading method nahi hai.
- **D.** Dirty cloth organic contamination introduce karega. Instrument aur accessories clean aur compatible hone chahiye.

**Exam clue:** Concave devices ko drainage facilitate karne wali position mein arrange karo.

**Reference:** [CDC sterilizing practices](https://www.cdc.gov/infection-control/hcp/disinfection-sterilization/sterilizing-practices.html) (retrieved 28 November 2026).

### Q3. Under CDC's general steam sterilizer loading guidance, how are compatible peel packs arranged in a perforated rack?

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Priority:** High

- **A.** Compressed flat under heavy metal trays
- **B.** Tightly rolled together
- **C.** On their edge, with adequate circulation
- **D.** Sealed inside an additional impermeable plastic bag

<details><summary>Sahi answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct answer: C. On their edge, with adequate circulation**

- **A.** Heavy trays pouch ko damage ya compress kar sakte hain aur circulation interfere kar sakti hai.
- **B.** Tightly rolling packs se sterilant access restrict ho sakta hai.
- **C.** CDC general loading guidance mein peel packs ko perforated rack mein edge par arrange karna describe karta hai. Device aur pouch instructions ko priority deni hai.
- **D.** Extra impermeable bag sterilant passage ko block kar sakta hai aur validated process ko change kar deta hai.

**Exam clue:** Peel packs ke liye edge placement aur circulation yaad rakho, with approved load instructions.

**Reference:** [CDC sterilizing practices](https://www.cdc.gov/infection-control/hcp/disinfection-sterilization/sterilizing-practices.html) (retrieved 28 November 2026).

### Q4. What is an appropriate general loading arrangement for compatible perforated instrument trays in a steam sterilizer?

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Priority:** High

- **A.** Standing unsupported against the chamber door
- **B.** Wedged tightly against the steam outlet
- **C.** Stacked without any regard for the validated load
- **D.** Placed parallel to the shelf according to the validated configuration

<details><summary>Sahi answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct answer: D. Placed parallel to the shelf according to the validated configuration**

- **B.** Steam outlet obstruct karne se sterilant distribution affect ho sakti hai.
- **C.** Unvalidated stacking se steam penetration aur drying affect ho sakti hai.
- **D.** CDC general guidance perforated trays ko shelf ke parallel rakhne ka principle deta hai. Specific sterilizer aur tray instructions follow karni chahiye.

### Q5. Which practice reduces unnecessary handling of sterile supplies while they are in storage?

**Difficulty:** Easy | **Priority:** High

- **A.** Retrieve only the supplies needed for the task
- **B.** Repeatedly open intact packs to count instruments
- **C.** Move every pack between shelves at each shift change
- **D.** Ask visitors to handle packages during stock checks

<details><summary>Sahi answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct answer: A. Retrieve only the supplies needed for the task**

- **A.** Sirf required supplies retrieve karne se baaki sterile packages ki unnecessary handling kam hoti hai. Isse barrier damage ka chance bhi kam ho sakta hai.
- **B.** Pack kholne se sterile barrier break ho jata hai, isliye counting ke liye intact packs open nahi karne chahiye.
- **C.** Har shift mein unnecessary movement handling related damage ka chance badhata hai.
- **D.** Visitors ko sterile stock handling karwana controlled storage practices maintain nahi karta.

**Exam clue:** Sterile packages ko unnecessary touch aur movement se bachao.

**Reference:** [CDC sterilizing practices](https://www.cdc.gov/infection-control/hcp/disinfection-sterilization/sterilizing-practices.html) (retrieved 28 November 2026).

### Q6. What does event-related sterility refer to in the storage of processed instruments?

**Difficulty:** Easy | **Priority:** High

- **A.** Sterility is determined only by the weekday
- **B.** A contamination event can compromise an otherwise intact sterile barrier
- **C.** A package is sterile forever regardless of damage
- **D.** A label colour establishes indefinite sterility

<details><summary>Sahi answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct answer: B. A contamination event can compromise an otherwise intact sterile barrier**

- **A.** Weekday se sterile integrity determine nahi hoti. Packaging aur storage conditions important hain.
- **B.** Event-related approach mein package damage, moisture ya seal failure jaisi events sterile integrity compromise kar sakti hain. Manufacturer expiry aur facility policy bhi follow karte hain.
- **C.** Damaged package sterile nahi maana ja sakta. Event-related approach indefinite guarantee nahi hai.
- **D.** Label colour package integrity establish nahi karta. Approved identification aur inspection zaroori hain.

**Exam clue:** Event-related shelf life mein barrier compromise karne wali event important hai.

**Reference:** [CDC sterilizing practices](https://www.cdc.gov/infection-control/hcp/disinfection-sterilization/sterilizing-practices.html) (retrieved 28 November 2026).
