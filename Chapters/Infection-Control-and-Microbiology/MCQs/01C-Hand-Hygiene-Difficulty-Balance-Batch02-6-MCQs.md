### Q1. A health centre uses small bars of soap for handwashing. Which soap holder is appropriate under WHO guidance?

**Difficulty:** Easy | **Priority:** High

- **A.** A closed bowl filled with stagnant water
- **B.** A rack that allows water to drain away
- **C.** A shared damp cloth wrapped around the soap
- **D.** A sealed container holding the wet bar

<details><summary>Sahi answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct answer: B. A rack that allows water to drain away**

- **A.** Stagnant water mein bar continuously wet rehta hai aur isse clean storage maintain karna mushkil hota hai.
- **B.** Drainage wali rack se excess water nikal jata hai aur bar ko dry hone ka mauka milta hai. WHO bar soap use hone par isi tarah ki storage recommend karta hai.
- **C.** Shared damp cloth moisture retain karta hai aur alag logon ke contact se contamination ka risk badh sakta hai.
- **D.** Wet bar ko sealed container mein band karne se drying nahi ho pati, isliye yeh recommended drainage arrangement nahi hai.

**Exam clue:** Bar soap ke saath drainage aur drying ko yaad rakho.

**Reference:** [WHO hand hygiene consensus, section 2](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/NBK144035/) (directly retrieved 28 November 2026).

### Q2. Which drying technique is preferred to reduce friction on the skin after frequent handwashing?

**Difficulty:** Easy | **Priority:** High

- **A.** Scrub the skin with a rough cloth
- **B.** Use a hot-air blower at maximum heat
- **C.** Gently pat the hands dry with a disposable towel
- **D.** Wipe the hands on the apron

<details><summary>Sahi answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct answer: C. Gently pat the hands dry with a disposable towel**

- **A.** Rough cloth se repeatedly scrub karne par skin barrier irritate ho sakta hai.
- **B.** Maximum heat ki zaroorat nahi hai aur extra heat dry skin ko aur uncomfortable bana sakti hai.
- **C.** Gentle patting se water remove hota hai bina skin par unnecessary friction dale. CDC frequent handwashing mein is technique ko skin care ke liye recommend karta hai.
- **D.** Apron clean disposable towel nahi hota aur hands par dobara germs transfer ho sakte hain.

**Exam clue:** Dry hands thoroughly, lekin skin ko aggressively rub mat karo.

**Reference:** [CDC clinical hand hygiene and skin care](https://www.cdc.gov/clean-hands/hcp/clinical-safety/) (directly retrieved 28 November 2026).

</details>

### Q3. According to CDC, what is the minimum recommended alcohol concentration for an alcohol-based hand sanitizer?

**Difficulty:** Easy | **Priority:** High

- **A.** At least 20%
- **B.** At least 30%
- **C.** At least 40%
- **D.** At least 60%

<details><summary>Sahi answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct answer: D. At least 60%**

- **A.** 20 percent alcohol CDC ki minimum recommendation se kam hai, isliye is concentration ko suitable maan kar select nahi karna chahiye.
- **B.** 30 percent bhi recommended minimum se kam hai aur product ki antimicrobial efficacy ko assume nahi kar sakte.
- **C.** 40 percent alcohol bhi specified minimum ko meet nahi karta.
- **D.** CDC kam se kam 60 percent alcohol recommend karta hai. Clinical setting mein approved formulation aur manufacturer ki instructions bhi zaroor follow karni hain.

**Exam clue:** Alcohol concentration ka minimum 60 percent yaad rakho, lekin hospital-approved product bhi check karo.

**Reference:** [CDC clinical hand hygiene and skin care](https://www.cdc.gov/clean-hands/hcp/clinical-safety/) (directly retrieved 28 November 2026).

</details>

### Q4. What is the main role of glycerol when it is included as a humectant in an alcohol handrub formulation?

**Difficulty:** Easy | **Priority:** High

- **A.** To help retain skin moisture
- **B.** To act as the main sterilizing agent
- **C.** To replace the alcohol completely
- **D.** To increase skin abrasion

<details><summary>Sahi answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct answer: A. To help retain skin moisture**

- **A.** Glycerol ek humectant hai, jo skin mein moisture maintain karne mein madad karta hai. Isse repeated handrub use ki wajah se hone wali dryness kam ho sakti hai.
- **B.** Glycerol formulation mein primary sterilizing chemical ke roop mein nahi dala jata, aur routine handrub skin ko sterile nahi banata.
- **C.** Alcohol antimicrobial activity ka important component hota hai. Glycerol usko completely replace nahi karta.
- **D.** Skin abrasion se protective barrier damage ho sakta hai, jabki humectant ka purpose skin tolerance improve karna hota hai.

**Exam clue:** Humectant ka kaam moisture maintain karna hota hai.

**Reference:** [WHO review of hand hygiene preparations](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/NBK144041/) (directly retrieved 28 November 2026).

</details>

### Q5. Why should the container of a volatile alcohol-based handrub remain properly closed when not in use?

**Difficulty:** Easy | **Priority:** High

- **A.** To convert alcohol into soap
- **B.** To reduce alcohol evaporation and preserve its concentration
- **C.** To increase the amount of water in the formulation
- **D.** To make the product sterile after opening

<details><summary>Sahi answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct answer: B. To reduce alcohol evaporation and preserve its concentration**

- **A.** Container close karne se alcohol chemically soap mein convert nahi hota, kyunki dono alag types ke products hain.
- **B.** Alcohol volatile hota hai, yani hawa mein evaporate ho sakta hai. Properly closed container se concentration ko preserve karne mein madad milti hai.
- **C.** Lid close karne se formulation mein water add nahi hota. Product ko dilute karna bhi approved concentration ko change kar sakta hai.
- **D.** Container band karne se sterility automatically establish nahi hoti. Product handling aur storage instructions alag se follow karni hoti hain.

**Exam clue:** Volatile alcohol ki concentration protect karne ke liye container instructions follow karo.

**Reference:** [WHO review of hand hygiene preparations](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/NBK144041/) (directly retrieved 28 November 2026).

</details>

### Q6. A nurse is selecting a new routine hand hygiene product. Which staff feedback is particularly useful in addition to confirmed antimicrobial efficacy?

**Difficulty:** Easy | **Priority:** High

- **A.** Whether the bottle matches the ward decoration
- **B.** Whether it has the highest price
- **C.** Whether it is tolerated well by staff skin
- **D.** Whether it is sold in the largest container

<details><summary>Sahi answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct answer: C. Whether it is tolerated well by staff skin**

- **A.** Bottle ka colour ya decoration product ki clinical suitability establish nahi karta.
- **B.** Price zyada hone se efficacy ya tolerability automatically improve nahi hoti.
- **C.** Agar effective product skin par repeatedly irritation karta hai, toh staff ke liye regular use difficult ho sakta hai. Isliye WHO efficacy ke saath skin tolerance aur acceptability bhi consider karta hai.
- **D.** Container size supply planning mein useful ho sakta hai, lekin staff skin tolerance ka substitute nahi hai.

**Exam clue:** Effective aur skin-tolerable product regular hand hygiene mein help karta hai.

**Reference:** [WHO hand hygiene consensus, section 2](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/NBK144035/) (directly retrieved 28 November 2026).

</details>
