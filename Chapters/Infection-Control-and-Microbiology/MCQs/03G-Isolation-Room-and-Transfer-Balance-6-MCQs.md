### Q1. What does the abbreviation AIIR stand for in hospital infection control?

**Difficulty:** Easy | **Priority:** High

- **A.** Acute intravenous infusion room
- **B.** Airborne infection isolation room
- **C.** Automatic instrument inspection register
- **D.** Ambulatory injury intervention room

<details><summary>Sahi answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct answer: B. Airborne infection isolation room**

- **A.** Intravenous infusion medication aur fluids dene se related hai. AIIR iska abbreviation nahi hai.
- **B.** Airborne infection isolation room airborne infectious particles ke spread ko limit karne ke liye specially controlled patient room hota hai.
- **C.** Instrument inspection sterile services ka kaam ho sakta hai, lekin AIIR ka meaning yeh nahi hai.
- **D.** Ambulatory injury care ka AIIR abbreviation se sambandh nahi hai.

**Exam clue:** AIIR ka full form Airborne Infection Isolation Room hai.

**Reference:** [CDC isolation glossary](https://www.cdc.gov/infection-control/hcp/isolation-precautions/glossary.html) (retrieved 28 November 2026).

### Q2. What is the primary purpose of assigning dedicated nursing staff to a cohort of infected patients during an outbreak?

**Difficulty:** Easy | **Priority:** High

- **A.** Increase the number of patients each worker visits across all wards
- **B.** Remove the requirement for hand hygiene
- **C.** Reduce opportunities for transmission to other groups of patients
- **D.** Ensure that all patients receive the same medications

<details><summary>Sahi answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct answer: C. Reduce opportunities for transmission to other groups of patients**

- **A.** Har ward mein unnecessary patient contacts badhane se infectious agent ke transfer ke opportunities badh sakti hain.
- **B.** Staff cohorting hand hygiene ko replace nahi karta. Standard aur indicated additional precautions continue rehti hain.
- **C.** Dedicated staff ek defined cohort ki care karte hain, jisse unke through susceptible patients tak transmission ke opportunities kam ho sakte hain.
- **D.** Cohorting infection-control arrangement hai. Har patient's medicines individual clinical needs ke mutabik decide hoti hain.

**Exam clue:** Patient cohorting ke alawa staff cohorting bhi transmission control mein help kar sakti hai.

**Reference:** [CDC isolation glossary](https://www.cdc.gov/infection-control/hcp/isolation-precautions/glossary.html) (retrieved 28 November 2026).

### Q4. An occupied AIIR has an electronic pressure monitor. According to CDC, which additional monitoring practice is recommended?

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Priority:** High

- **A.** Perform a daily visual airflow or pressure-direction check using an appropriate indicator
- **B.** Assume the electronic reading makes every other check unnecessary
- **C.** Measure pressure only after the patient leaves hospital
- **D.** Check the direction by propping the door open for the entire shift

<details><summary>Sahi answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct answer: A. Perform a daily visual airflow or pressure-direction check using an appropriate indicator**

- **A.** CDC occupied AIIR mein electronic differential pressure monitor ke saath bhi appropriate visual indicator se daily check recommend karta hai. Trained staff facility engineering protocol follow karte hain.
- **B.** Electronic reading useful hai, lekin CDC ki specified daily visual check recommendation ko replace nahi karti.
- **C.** Discharge tak wait karne se current containment failure identify karna delay ho sakta hai.
- **D.** Door continuously open rakhne se designed pressure relationship disturb ho sakta hai. Approved indicator aur safe checking method use karo.

**Exam clue:** Occupied AIIR mein daily visual pressure-direction verification bhi important hai.

### Q5. Two patients require Droplet Precautions, but only one single room is available. Other clinical factors are equal. Who should be prioritized for the room under CDC placement guidance?

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Priority:** High

- **A.** The patient who requests a room with a better view
- **B.** The patient with excessive cough and sputum production
- **C.** The patient whose relative arrives first
- **D.** The patient with the longer surname

<details><summary>Sahi answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct answer: B. The patient with excessive cough and sputum production**

- **A.** Room view personal preference hai, transmission risk ka clinical indicator nahi hai.
- **B.** Excessive cough aur sputum se respiratory secretions disperse hone ka chance badh sakta hai. CDC single-room shortage mein aise patients ko prioritize karta hai, with overall risk assessment.
- **C.** Relative ka arrival order infection-control placement ka appropriate criterion nahi hai.
- **D.** Surname se infectious transmission risk assess nahi hota.

**Exam clue:** Limited single rooms mein respiratory secretion burden ko placement risk assessment mein consider karo.

**Reference:** [CDC isolation recommendations, section V](https://www.cdc.gov/infection-control/hcp/isolation-precautions/recommendations.html) (retrieved 28 November 2026).

### Q6. Before transferring a patient who requires transmission-based precautions to another healthcare facility, which information should the sending nurse communicate?

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Priority:** High

- **A.** Only the patient's meal preference
- **B.** Only the name of the previous ward
- **C.** The required infection-control precautions to the receiving facility and transport team
- **D.** No infection-control information until after arrival

<details><summary>Sahi answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct answer: C. The required infection-control precautions to the receiving facility and transport team**

- **A.** Meal preference routine care ke liye useful ho sakti hai, lekin safe transfer precautions ki information replace nahi karti.
- **B.** Ward name se required PPE aur placement arrangements automatically pata nahi chalte.
- **C.** Receiving facility aur transport team ko timely precautions communicate karne se appropriate PPE, patient placement aur safe transfer plan prepare ho sakta hai. Necessary clinical information confidential handover mein share karo.
- **D.** Arrival tak information delay hone se staff aur other patients ko avoidable exposure ho sakta hai.

**Exam clue:** Transfer se pehle infection-control precautions communicate karo.

**Reference:** [CDC core practices, section 6](https://www.cdc.gov/infection-control/hcp/core-practices/) (retrieved 28 November 2026).
