### Q1. What does the term bioburden describe on a medical device before sterilization?

**Difficulty:** Easy | **Priority:** High

- **A.** The total weight of its packaging
- **B.** The number of viable microorganisms on or in the item
- **C.** The amount of steam in the chamber
- **D.** The number of staff handling it

<details><summary>Sahi answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct answer: B. The number of viable microorganisms on or in the item**

- **A.** Packaging ka weight load preparation mein relevant ho sakta hai, lekin bioburden microbial quantity ko refer karta hai.
- **B.** Bioburden item par ya uske andar present viable microorganisms ki population ko kehte hain. Cleaning se is burden ko reduce karne mein madad milti hai.
- **C.** Chamber steam sterilization process ka part hai, lekin bioburden steam quantity nahi hai.
- **D.** Handling se contamination affect ho sakti hai, lekin staff count bioburden ki definition nahi hai.

**Exam clue:** Bioburden ka matlab viable microbial load hai.

**Reference:** [CDC reprocessing glossary](https://www.cdc.gov/infection-control/hcp/disinfection-sterilization/glossary.html) (retrieved 28 November 2026).

### Q2. Which enzyme in an instrument-cleaning formulation helps break down protein residues?

**Difficulty:** Easy | **Priority:** High

- **A.** Amylase
- **B.** Lipase
- **C.** Protease
- **D.** Lactase

<details><summary>Sahi answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct answer: C. Protease**

- **A.** Amylase starch jaise carbohydrate ko break down karta hai, protein residue uska main target nahi hai.
- **B.** Lipase fats ko break down karta hai. Blood aur tissue ke protein component ke liye protease relevant hai.
- **C.** Protease proteins ko smaller components mein break karne mein help karta hai. Compatible enzyme cleaner instrument ki instructions ke mutabik use karna chahiye.
- **D.** Lactase lactose sugar ko break down karta hai, protein residues ko nahi.

**Exam clue:** Protease protein ko break down karne wala enzyme hai.

**Reference:** [CDC cleaning guideline](https://www.cdc.gov/infection-control/hcp/disinfection-sterilization/cleaning.html) (retrieved 28 November 2026).

</details>

### Q3. An enzymatic cleaning solution contains lipase. Which type of residue does this enzyme help remove?

**Difficulty:** Easy | **Priority:** High

- **A.** Mineral scale only
- **B.** Bacterial spores by sterilization
- **C.** Metal corrosion
- **D.** Fat and oily organic residue

<details><summary>Sahi answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct answer: D. Fat and oily organic residue**

- **A.** Mineral scale inorganic deposit hai. Lipase ka principal substrate fat hota hai.
- **B.** Lipase cleaning enzyme hai, validated sporicidal sterilization process nahi hai.
- **C.** Metal corrosion ko enzyme se reverse nahi kiya ja sakta. Damaged instruments ko appropriate inspection chahiye.
- **D.** Lipase fats par act karta hai aur oily organic soil ko remove karne mein help kar sakta hai. Final disinfection ya sterilization phir bhi required ho sakti hai.

**Exam clue:** Lipase ka substrate lipid ya fat hota hai.

**Reference:** [CDC cleaning guideline](https://www.cdc.gov/infection-control/hcp/disinfection-sterilization/cleaning.html) (retrieved 28 November 2026).

### Q4. Which statement about an enzymatic instrument cleaner is correct?

**Difficulty:** Easy | **Priority:** High

- **A.** It supports cleaning but does not automatically sterilize instruments
- **B.** It guarantees sterility after a brief soak
- **C.** It replaces every chemical and biological indicator
- **D.** It is suitable for every device regardless of instructions

<details><summary>Sahi answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct answer: A. It supports cleaning but does not automatically sterilize instruments**

- **A.** Enzyme cleaner organic soil remove karne mein help karta hai. Critical device ke liye cleaning ke baad validated sterilization required hoti hai.
- **B.** Brief enzyme soak se sterility establish nahi hoti. Cleaning aur sterilization alag steps hain.
- **C.** Indicators sterilization process ki monitoring karte hain. Enzyme cleaning inko replace nahi karti.
- **D.** Cleaner device material aur manufacturer instructions ke compatible hona chahiye. Har device ke liye ek hi formulation assume nahi kar sakte.

**Exam clue:** Enzymatic cleaning ko sterilization samajhne ki galti mat karo.

**Reference:** [CDC cleaning guideline](https://www.cdc.gov/infection-control/hcp/disinfection-sterilization/cleaning.html) (retrieved 28 November 2026).

### Q5. What does the term sporicidal mean when describing a validated antimicrobial process?

**Difficulty:** Easy | **Priority:** High

- **A.** It only removes visible dust
- **B.** It can kill bacterial spores under specified conditions
- **C.** It only improves the smell of the equipment
- **D.** It encourages bacteria to form spores

<details><summary>Sahi answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct answer: B. It can kill bacterial spores under specified conditions**

- **A.** Dust removal cleaning ka physical part ho sakta hai, lekin sporicidal ka meaning microbial activity se related hai.
- **B.** Sporicidal process specified conditions mein resistant bacterial spores ko kill kar sakta hai. Actual effectiveness approved concentration, time aur application par depend karti hai.
- **C.** Pleasant smell se sporicidal efficacy establish nahi hoti.
- **D.** Sporicidal action ka goal spores kill karna hai, unki formation encourage karna nahi hai.

**Exam clue:** Sporicidal ka matlab spore-killing activity hai.

**Reference:** [CDC reprocessing glossary](https://www.cdc.gov/infection-control/hcp/disinfection-sterilization/glossary.html) (retrieved 28 November 2026).

### Q6. Which device is specifically designed to examine the interior of a hollow organ or body canal?

**Difficulty:** Easy | **Priority:** High

- **A.** A sphygmomanometer
- **B.** A pulse oximeter
- **C.** An endoscope
- **D.** A clinical weighing scale

<details><summary>Sahi answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct answer: C. An endoscope**

- **A.** Sphygmomanometer blood pressure measure karta hai aur hollow organ ke interior ko visualize nahi karta.
- **B.** Pulse oximeter oxygen saturation estimate karta hai. Yeh internal organ viewing instrument nahi hai.
- **C.** Endoscope body canal ya hollow organ ke andar visualize karne ke liye designed hota hai. Reuse ke liye uske actual contact type aur manufacturer instructions ke hisaab se reprocessing chahiye.
- **D.** Weighing scale body weight measure karta hai, internal visualization nahi karta.

**Exam clue:** Endoscope ko internal visualization se associate karo, aur reprocessing uske intended use par depend karti hai.

**Reference:** [CDC reprocessing glossary](https://www.cdc.gov/infection-control/hcp/disinfection-sterilization/glossary.html) (retrieved 28 November 2026).
