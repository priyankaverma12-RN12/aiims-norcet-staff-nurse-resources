### Q1. A patient says their body has become resistant to an antibiotic. Which explanation is most accurate?

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Priority:** High

- **A.** Microorganisms can become resistant to the drug

- **C.** Resistance is the same as drug allergy
- **D.** Resistance means the patient has permanent immunity

<details><summary>Sahi answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Kyun sahi hai:** Resistance microorganism ki drug susceptibility mein change hai, patient ki body ka antibiotic-resistant hona nahi.

</details>

### Q38. A patient with resistant bacterial colonization is admitted for unrelated surgery. Which approach is appropriate?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Priority:** High

### Q40. A stewardship programme reduces antibiotic use but sepsis treatment delays increase. What should the programme do?
