### Q1. What does CSSD commonly stand for in a hospital?

**Difficulty:** Easy | **Priority:** High

- **A.** Central Sterile Services Department
- **B.** Clinical Surgical Screening Division
- **C.** Central Staff Scheduling Desk
- **D.** Community Sanitation Survey Department

<details><summary>Sahi answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct answer: A. Central Sterile Services Department**

- **A.** CSSD central sterile services department hota hai jahan reusable instruments ki cleaning, processing aur sterile supply management coordinated tareeke se hoti hai.
- **B.** Surgical screening patient assessment ka hissa ho sakta hai, lekin CSSD ka standard expansion yeh nahi hai.
- **C.** Staff scheduling duty roster se related hai, sterile instrument processing se nahi.
- **D.** Community sanitation public health ka kaam ho sakta hai, lekin hospital CSSD ka meaning nahi hai.

**Exam clue:** CSSD ko central instrument reprocessing aur sterile supply se jodo.

**Reference:** [CDC sterilizing practices](https://www.cdc.gov/infection-control/hcp/disinfection-sterilization/sterilizing-practices.html) (retrieved 28 November 2026).

### Q2. In a central processing department, where are cleaned instruments inspected and assembled before sterilization?

**Difficulty:** Easy | **Priority:** High

- **A.** The contaminated receiving bay
- **B.** The clean packaging area
- **C.** The hospital kitchen
- **D.** The dirty linen store

<details><summary>Sahi answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct answer: B. The clean packaging area**

- **A.** Contaminated receiving bay mein used items aate hain. Wahan clean instruments assemble karne se cross contamination ho sakti hai.
- **B.** Clean packaging area mein cleaned instruments inspect aur assemble kiye jate hain, phir suitable package mein sterilization ke liye prepare hote hain.
- **C.** Kitchen food preparation ke liye hai aur medical device packaging ka controlled area nahi hai.
- **D.** Dirty linen store mein contaminated textiles ho sakte hain. Clean device preparation wahan appropriate nahi hai.

**Exam clue:** Inspection aur assembly clean packaging zone mein hoti hai.

**Reference:** [CDC sterilizing practices](https://www.cdc.gov/infection-control/hcp/disinfection-sterilization/sterilizing-practices.html) (retrieved 28 November 2026).

</details>

### Q3. Why should detergent residue be adequately rinsed off a reusable instrument after cleaning?

**Difficulty:** Easy | **Priority:** High

- **A.** To make the instrument heavier
- **B.** To replace the subsequent sterilization cycle
- **C.** To prevent residues from interfering with later processing
- **D.** To make the detergent more concentrated

<details><summary>Sahi answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct answer: C. To prevent residues from interfering with later processing**

- **A.** Rinsing ka purpose instrument ka weight badhana nahi hai.
- **B.** Cleaning aur rinsing ke baad required sterilization ya disinfection ab bhi perform karni hoti hai.
- **C.** Detergent residue subsequent disinfectant ya sterilant ke kaam mein interfere kar sakta hai. Isliye manufacturer ki rinse instructions follow karte hain.
- **D.** Rinsing detergent ko remove karne ke liye hoti hai, concentrate karne ke liye nahi.

**Exam clue:** Cleaning agent residue hatao, phir required reprocessing complete karo.

**Reference:** [CDC disinfection and sterilization recommendations](https://www.cdc.gov/infection-control/hcp/disinfection-sterilization/summary-recommendations.html) (retrieved 28 November 2026).

</details>

### Q4. Which storage furniture is ideal for protecting sterile packages from routine dust exposure, when available?

**Difficulty:** Easy | **Priority:** High

- **A.** An uncovered trolley in a public corridor
- **B.** A damp shelf under a sink
- **C.** A basket on the floor
- **D.** A closed or covered cabinet in the sterile storage area

<details><summary>Sahi answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct answer: D. A closed or covered cabinet in the sterile storage area**

- **A.** Public corridor mein handling aur dust exposure zyada ho sakta hai. Uncovered trolley ideal storage nahi hai.
- **B.** Sink ke neeche water leakage se package wet ho sakta hai, isliye aisi storage avoid karni chahiye.
- **C.** Floor par packages rakhne se cleaning difficult hoti hai aur contamination exposure badhta hai.
- **D.** Controlled sterile store mein closed ya covered cabinet packages ko dust aur unnecessary contact se protect karne mein help karta hai.

**Exam clue:** Sterile stock ko protected controlled storage mein rakho.

**Reference:** [CDC sterilizing practices](https://www.cdc.gov/infection-control/hcp/disinfection-sterilization/sterilizing-practices.html) (retrieved 28 November 2026).

</details>

### Q5. Why is clearance maintained between sterile supply shelving and the floor?

**Difficulty:** Easy | **Priority:** High

- **A.** To permit effective floor cleaning and air circulation
- **B.** To make sterile supplies fall more easily
- **C.** To allow splash water to reach packages
- **D.** To eliminate the need to inspect package integrity

<details><summary>Sahi answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct answer: A. To permit effective floor cleaning and air circulation**

- **A.** Floor se adequate clearance rakhne par cleaning aur air circulation easy hoti hai. Facility standards ke mutabik spacing maintain karni chahiye.
- **B.** Shelves ka purpose packs ko stable rakhna hai, unhe girana nahi.
- **C.** Water splash sterile packaging compromise kar sakta hai, isliye usko prevent karna chahiye.
- **D.** Shelf clearance useful hai, lekin use se pehle package integrity inspect karna phir bhi zaroori hai.

**Exam clue:** Sterile shelving mein floor clearance cleanliness aur airflow ke liye hoti hai.

**Reference:** [CDC sterilizing practices](https://www.cdc.gov/infection-control/hcp/disinfection-sterilization/sterilizing-practices.html) (retrieved 28 November 2026).

</details>

### Q6. A CSSD technician demonstrated competency at appointment two years ago. Which plan best maintains competency for current reprocessing tasks?

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Priority:** High

- **A.** No further assessment unless the technician resigns
- **B.** Periodic documented competency reassessment under facility policy
- **C.** Only asking the technician to read a poster
- **D.** Replacing all training with production speed targets

<details><summary>Sahi answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct answer: B. Periodic documented competency reassessment under facility policy**

- **A.** Initial competency future practice ki permanent guarantee nahi hai. Equipment aur instructions change ho sakti hain.
- **B.** Periodic competency testing se verify hota hai ki technician current procedure safely perform kar raha hai. CDC regular reassessment ka example annual testing deta hai.
- **C.** Poster reminder useful ho sakta hai, lekin actual task competency demonstrate nahi karta.
- **D.** Speed target safe reprocessing technique assess nahi karta aur shortcuts ka risk badha sakta hai.

**Exam clue:** Competency sirf joining ke time nahi, regular intervals par bhi check hoti hai.

**Reference:** [CDC disinfection and sterilization recommendations](https://www.cdc.gov/infection-control/hcp/disinfection-sterilization/summary-recommendations.html) (retrieved 28 November 2026).
