### Q1. Which chemical is the active ingredient in ordinary liquid household bleach?

**Difficulty:** Easy | **Priority:** High

- **A.** Sodium chloride
- **B.** Sodium hypochlorite
- **C.** Sodium bicarbonate
- **D.** Calcium carbonate

<details><summary>Sahi answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct answer: B. Sodium hypochlorite**

- **A.** Sodium chloride common salt hai aur bleach ka active chlorine releasing compound nahi hai.
- **B.** Liquid household bleach mein aam taur par sodium hypochlorite hota hai. Healthcare use mein appropriate approved formulation aur label instructions follow karna zaroori hai.
- **C.** Sodium bicarbonate baking soda hai. Yeh sodium hypochlorite ka equivalent disinfectant nahi hai.
- **D.** Calcium carbonate chalk aur antacid formulations mein mil sakta hai, lekin ordinary liquid bleach ka active ingredient nahi hai.

**Exam clue:** Liquid bleach ka common active ingredient sodium hypochlorite hai.

**Reference:** [CDC chemical disinfectants](https://www.cdc.gov/infection-control/hcp/disinfection-sterilization/chemical-disinfectants.html) (retrieved 28 November 2026).

### Q2. What is a principal antimicrobial action of alcohol in chemical disinfection?

**Difficulty:** Easy | **Priority:** High

- **A.** Stimulating bacterial cell division
- **B.** Producing a protective bacterial capsule
- **C.** Denaturing microbial proteins
- **D.** Supplying glucose to microorganisms

<details><summary>Sahi answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct answer: C. Denaturing microbial proteins**

- **A.** Alcohol ka purpose microbes ko multiply karana nahi hai. Effective disinfection unki viability reduce karti hai.
- **B.** Protective capsule kuch bacteria ki structure hoti hai, alcohol ka primary action capsule banana nahi hai.
- **C.** Alcohol microbial proteins ki normal structure disrupt karta hai, jise denaturation kehte hain. Isse essential cellular functions disturb hote hain.
- **D.** Glucose microbial energy source ho sakta hai, lekin alcohol disinfection glucose supply karke kaam nahi karti.

**Exam clue:** Alcohol ki antimicrobial activity mein protein denaturation important hai.

**Reference:** [CDC chemical disinfectants](https://www.cdc.gov/infection-control/hcp/disinfection-sterilization/chemical-disinfectants.html) (retrieved 28 November 2026).

</details>

### Q3. Why can blood reduce the effectiveness of a chlorine disinfectant applied to a contaminated surface?

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Priority:** High

- **A.** Blood increases the labelled shelf life
- **B.** Blood converts every bacterium into a spore
- **C.** Blood makes contact time unnecessary
- **D.** Organic material can consume available active chlorine

<details><summary>Sahi answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct answer: D. Organic material can consume available active chlorine**

- **A.** Blood contamination product ki shelf life improve nahi karti. Product expiry aur storage label se determine hote hain.
- **B.** Blood contact se har bacterium spore nahi ban jata. Spore formation specific organisms ki characteristic hai.
- **C.** Organic contamination present hone par bhi required contact time important hai.
- **D.** Blood mein organic compounds active chlorine ke saath react kar sakte hain, jisse microorganisms par act karne ke liye available chlorine kam ho jati hai. Spill management mein approved procedure follow karo.

**Exam clue:** Chlorine efficacy organic soil se reduce ho sakti hai.

**Reference:** [CDC efficacy factors](https://www.cdc.gov/infection-control/hcp/disinfection-sterilization/efficacy-factors.html) (retrieved 28 November 2026).

### Q4. Which water constituents are responsible for hardness that may interfere with some disinfectants?

**Difficulty:** Easy | **Priority:** High

- **A.** Calcium and magnesium ions
- **B.** Oxygen and nitrogen gases
- **C.** Glucose and fructose
- **D.** Hemoglobin and albumin

<details><summary>Sahi answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct answer: A. Calcium and magnesium ions**

- **A.** Calcium aur magnesium ions hard water ki main wajah hote hain. Kuch disinfectants ke saath interaction se unki activity kam ho sakti hai.
- **B.** Dissolved oxygen aur nitrogen hardness ke principal ions nahi hain.
- **C.** Glucose aur fructose sugars hain, jo mineral water hardness define nahi karte.
- **D.** Hemoglobin aur albumin proteins hain, water hardness ke usual mineral constituents nahi hain.

**Exam clue:** Hard water mein calcium aur magnesium ions yaad rakho.

**Reference:** [CDC efficacy factors](https://www.cdc.gov/infection-control/hcp/disinfection-sterilization/efficacy-factors.html) (retrieved 28 November 2026).

### Q5. Why can a rapidly evaporating alcohol disinfectant be unsuitable for maintaining prolonged wet contact on a large surface?

**Difficulty:** Easy | **Priority:** High

- **A.** It permanently seals the surface
- **B.** The surface may dry before the required contact time is met
- **C.** It turns into steam sterilization
- **D.** It always forms a residual sterile film

<details><summary>Sahi answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct answer: B. The surface may dry before the required contact time is met**

- **A.** Alcohol evaporation permanent seal nahi banati. Surface phir se contaminate ho sakti hai.
- **B.** Alcohol jaldi evaporate ho sakta hai, isliye required wet contact time maintain karna difficult ho sakta hai. Surface aur approved product ki instructions ke hisaab se method select karo.
- **C.** Evaporation steam sterilization nahi hai. Steam sterilization mein validated cycle aur specific conditions chahiye.
- **D.** Alcohol use se permanent sterile film establish nahi hoti. Disinfection ke baad handling precautions important rehte hain.

**Exam clue:** Wet contact time aur evaporation ko product selection mein consider karo.

**Reference:** [CDC chemical disinfectants](https://www.cdc.gov/infection-control/hcp/disinfection-sterilization/chemical-disinfectants.html) (retrieved 28 November 2026).

</details>

### Q6. A nurse suggests changing the pH of a ready-to-use disinfectant to make it stronger. What is the safest response?

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Priority:** High

- **A.** Acidify every disinfectant because low pH always improves activity
- **B.** Alkalinize every disinfectant because high pH always improves activity
- **C.** Do not alter the formulation outside its validated instructions
- **D.** Change pH according to the bottle colour

<details><summary>Sahi answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct answer: C. Do not alter the formulation outside its validated instructions**

- **A.** Different disinfectants ka response pH par alag hota hai. Acid add karne se activity kam ho sakti hai ya dangerous reaction ho sakti hai.
- **B.** High pH bhi har agent ke liye beneficial nahi hai. Unapproved change efficacy aur safety compromise kar sakta hai.
- **C.** Disinfectant ka pH aur formulation validated conditions ka part hote hain. Label aur manufacturer ke instructions follow karo aur khud se chemicals add mat karo.
- **D.** Bottle colour se chemical compatibility ya suitable pH pata nahi chalta.

**Exam clue:** Disinfectant formulation ko khud modify mat karo.

**Reference:** [CDC efficacy factors](https://www.cdc.gov/infection-control/hcp/disinfection-sterilization/efficacy-factors.html) (retrieved 28 November 2026).
