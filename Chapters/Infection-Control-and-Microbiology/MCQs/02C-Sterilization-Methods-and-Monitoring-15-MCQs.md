### Q1. Two steam sterilizer cycles reach the same chamber temperature, but one contains trapped air within a porous load. Why is the second cycle less reliable?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Priority:** High

- **A.** Chamber temperature alone establishes equivalent microbial killing
- **B.** Air pockets improve penetration into porous materials
- **C.** Trapped air interferes with direct saturated-steam contact and heat transfer
- **D.** Trapped air increases the latent heat released by steam

<details><summary>Sahi answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct answer: C. Trapped air interferes with direct saturated-steam contact and heat transfer**

**Kyun sahi hai:** Air pockets steam contact aur heat transfer ko impair kar sakti hain. Chamber reading local load conditions guarantee nahi karti.

**Baaki options kyun galat hain?**

- **A.** Temperature alone full cycle efficacy establish nahi karti.
- **B.** Air pockets penetration obstruct kar sakti hain.
- **D.** Air latent heat delivery improve nahi karti.

</details>
