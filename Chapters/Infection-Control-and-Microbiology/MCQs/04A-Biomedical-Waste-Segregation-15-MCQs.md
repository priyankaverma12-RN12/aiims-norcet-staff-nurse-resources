### Q1. A dialysis unit discards a used dialyzer without attached sharps. Which Indian biomedical waste stream applies?

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Priority:** High

- **A.** White sharps waste
- **B.** Ordinary dry municipal waste
- **C.** Red contaminated recyclable waste
- **D.** Blue glassware waste

<details><summary>Sahi answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct answer: C. Red contaminated recyclable waste**

**Kyun sahi hai:** Used dialyzer contaminated recyclable plastic stream mein listed hai. Authorized treatment zaroori hai.

**Baaki options kyun galat hain?**

- **A.** White contaminated metal sharps ke liye hai.
