### Q1. Which chemical is classified as an iodophor used in suitable surgical hand antiseptic preparations?

**Difficulty:** Easy | **Priority:** High

- **A.** Chlorhexidine gluconate
- **B.** Povidone-iodine
- **C.** Isopropyl alcohol
- **D.** Glycerol

<details><summary>Sahi answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct answer: B. Povidone-iodine**

- **A.** Chlorhexidine gluconate ek alag antiseptic compound hai aur iodine carrier complex nahi hai.
- **B.** Povidone-iodine mein iodine ek carrier ke saath combined hota hai. Free iodine antimicrobial activity mein important role play karta hai.
- **C.** Isopropyl alcohol alcohol group ka antiseptic hai aur iodophor nahi hai.
- **D.** Glycerol humectant ke roop mein formulation mein use ho sakta hai, lekin yeh iodine-based antiseptic nahi hai.

**Exam clue:** Povidone-iodine ko iodophor category se jod kar yaad rakho.

**Reference:** [WHO review of hand hygiene preparations](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/NBK144041/) (directly retrieved 28 November 2026).

### Q2. Which structure of a bacterial cell is primarily disrupted by chlorhexidine during hand antisepsis?

**Difficulty:** Easy | **Priority:** High

- **A.** The bacterial flagellum only
- **B.** The bacterial capsule only
- **C.** The cytoplasmic membrane
- **D.** The chromosome through ionizing radiation

<details><summary>Sahi answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct answer: C. The cytoplasmic membrane**

- **A.** Flagellum bacteria ko movement mein help kar sakta hai, lekin chlorhexidine ka primary action sirf flagellum par nahi hota.
- **B.** Capsule kuch bacteria ki outer protective layer hoti hai, par chlorhexidine ka main target cytoplasmic membrane hai.
- **C.** Chlorhexidine bacterial cell membrane se attach hokar uski integrity disturb karta hai. Isse cell ke normal contents aur functions maintain nahi ho pate.
- **D.** Ionizing radiation ka mechanism alag hai. Chlorhexidine radiation emit karke chromosome damage nahi karta.

**Exam clue:** CHG ka important antimicrobial mechanism membrane disruption hai.

**Reference:** [WHO review of hand hygiene preparations](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/NBK144041/) (directly retrieved 28 November 2026).

</details>

### Q3. In the NHS Scotland surgical rubbing sequence, how is an elbow-operated dispenser activated without using the fingers?

**Difficulty:** Easy | **Priority:** High

- **A.** With the fingertips
- **B.** With the palm of the prepared hand
- **C.** With the sterile glove already worn
- **D.** With the opposite elbow

<details><summary>Sahi answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct answer: D. With the opposite elbow**

- **A.** Fingertips se dispenser ko touch karne par hand contact hota hai, jise elbow-operated design avoid karne ke liye banaya gaya hai.
- **B.** Palm use karne se hand-operated contact hota hai, jabki given dispenser opposite elbow se operate kiya ja sakta hai.
- **C.** Sterile gloves surgical hand preparation complete hone ke baad pehne jate hain, is step se pehle nahi.
- **D.** NHS Scotland ke illustrated sequence mein opposite elbow se dispenser activate kiya jata hai, taaki hand direct contact na kare.

**Exam clue:** Elbow-operated dispenser ka purpose hands ka unnecessary surface contact avoid karna hai.

**Reference:** [NHS Scotland surgical rubbing, Appendix 4](https://www.nipcm.hps.scot.nhs.uk/appendices/appendix-4-surgical-rubbing/) (directly retrieved 28 November 2026).

### Q4. In the NHS Scotland alcohol-based surgical rubbing sequence, which part is dipped into the collected handrub at the beginning of preparation for each hand?

**Difficulty:** Easy | **Priority:** High

- **A.** The fingertips
- **B.** The back of the elbow
- **C.** The wristwatch area while wearing the watch
- **D.** The cuff of the scrub uniform

<details><summary>Sahi answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct answer: A. The fingertips**

- **A.** Illustrated sequence mein fingertips ko collected handrub mein dip kiya jata hai, taaki nail ke neeche wale area tak antiseptic pahunch sake.
- **B.** Elbow par product cover karna forearm application ka hissa ho sakta hai, lekin initial dipping step fingertips ke liye hai.
- **C.** Wristwatch surgical preparation se pehle remove hoti hai. Watch pehenkar us area ko prepare karna appropriate nahi hai.
- **D.** Scrub uniform skin antisepsis ka target nahi hai, isliye cuff ko handrub mein dip nahi kiya jata.

**Exam clue:** Fingertip dipping ka aim nail ke neeche antiseptic coverage improve karna hai.

**Reference:** [NHS Scotland surgical rubbing, Appendix 4](https://www.nipcm.hps.scot.nhs.uk/appendices/appendix-4-surgical-rubbing/) (directly retrieved 28 November 2026).

### Q5. Which movement in the NHS Scotland surgical rubbing sequence helps cover the entire circumference of a forearm?

**Difficulty:** Easy | **Priority:** High

- **A.** Rubbing only one narrow strip along the forearm
- **B.** Circular rubbing movements around the forearm
- **C.** Keeping the forearm untouched while rubbing the palms
- **D.** Rubbing the scrub sleeve instead of exposed skin

<details><summary>Sahi answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct answer: B. Circular rubbing movements around the forearm**

- **A.** Ek narrow strip par rubbing karne se forearm ke baaki surface par product nahi pahunch sakta.
- **B.** Circular movement se forearm ke charon taraf exposed skin ko cover karne mein madad milti hai. Product aur local protocol ki application instructions follow karni hain.
- **C.** Palms clean karna zaroori hai, lekin surgical rubbing mein forearms bhi prepare hote hain.
- **D.** Scrub sleeve ko rub karne se exposed forearm skin ki antisepsis nahi hoti.

**Exam clue:** Surgical rub mein forearm ki complete circumferential coverage important hai.

**Reference:** [NHS Scotland surgical rubbing, Appendix 4](https://www.nipcm.hps.scot.nhs.uk/appendices/appendix-4-surgical-rubbing/) (directly retrieved 28 November 2026).

### Q6. Which group of bacteria commonly forms part of the resident skin flora of the surgical team's hands?

**Difficulty:** Easy | **Priority:** High

- **A.** Plasmodium species
- **B.** Ascaris species
- **C.** Coagulase-negative staphylococci
- **D.** Influenza viruses

<details><summary>Sahi answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct answer: C. Coagulase-negative staphylococci**

- **A.** Plasmodium malaria cause karne wala protozoan parasite hai, normal resident skin bacterium nahi hai.
- **B.** Ascaris ek intestinal helminth hai, jo normal hand skin bacterial flora ka part nahi hota.
- **C.** Coagulase-negative staphylococci commonly skin par rehte hain. Surgical hand preparation inki quantity kam karne mein madad karti hai, kyunki foreign material ya wound mein pahunchne par kuch organisms infection cause kar sakte hain.
- **D.** Influenza virus respiratory infection cause karta hai aur resident bacterial flora ki category mein nahi aata.

**Exam clue:** Resident skin flora mein coagulase-negative staphylococci common hote hain.

**Reference:** [WHO surgical hand preparation](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/NBK144036/) (directly retrieved 28 November 2026).

</details>
