### Q1. An infant is admitted with respiratory syncytial virus bronchiolitis. Which additional precaution is listed in CDC Appendix A for infants and young children?

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Priority:** High

- **A.** Contact precautions
- **B.** Airborne precautions alone
- **C.** Protective isolation only
- **D.** No additional precautions

<details><summary>✅ Sahi answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct answer: A. Contact precautions**

**Kyun sahi hai:** RSV secretions aur contaminated hands se spread ho sakta hai. Contact plus standard precautions apply karo, local policy may add droplet protection.

**Baaki options kyun galat hain?**

- **B.** RSV in this setting does not routinely require airborne isolation.
- **C.** This protects selected vulnerable patients rather than controlling RSV transmission.
- **D.** CDC lists contact precautions for this group.
