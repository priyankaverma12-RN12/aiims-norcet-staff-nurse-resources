### Q1. A hospital has an authorized common biomedical waste treatment service 50 km away. The administrator proposes a new captive final-disposal plant solely for convenience. Which rule applies?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Priority:** High

- **A.** Stop required on-site laboratory pretreatment
- **B.** Do not establish an on-site final-treatment facility when the prescribed common service is available within 75 km
- **C.** Build the captive plant without authorization
- **D.** Use municipal landfill for untreated waste instead

<details><summary>Sahi answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct answer: B. Do not establish an on-site final-treatment facility when the prescribed common service is available within 75 km**

**Kyun sahi hai:** Rule 7 common service available within 75 km hone par captive disposal plant establish karne ko restrict karti hai. Required pretreatment alag duty hai.

**Baaki options kyun galat hain?**

- **A.** Required laboratory pretreatment continue hoti hai.
- **C.** Authorization requirements ignore nahi kar sakte.
- **D.** Untreated BMW ordinary landfill mein nahi bhejna hai.

</details>
