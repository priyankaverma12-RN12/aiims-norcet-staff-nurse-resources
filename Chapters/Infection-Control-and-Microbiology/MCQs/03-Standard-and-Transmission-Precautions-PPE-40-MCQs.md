**Baaki options kyun sahi nahi hain?** Needle change syringe contamination eliminate nahi karta. Two-handed recapping aur open tray injuries badha sakte hain.

</details>

### Q40. An isolation patient develops a sudden life-threatening deterioration. What is the appropriate principle?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Priority:** High

- **A.** Activate emergency response promptly while using indicated infection-control measures as rapidly and safely as possible
- **B.** Delay all clinical assessment until every administrative form is completed
- **C.** Abandon all infection-control measures routinely
- **D.** Wait for the isolation period to end

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: A. Activate emergency response promptly while using indicated infection-control measures as rapidly and safely as possible**

Emergency mein patient ki immediate care priority hai, lekin staff protection bhi important hai. Trained response aur rapidly available PPE dono support karte hain.

**Baaki options kyun sahi nahi hain?** Administrative delay aur isolation end ka wait unsafe hai. Unnecessary exposure bhi avoid karna chahiye.

</details>
