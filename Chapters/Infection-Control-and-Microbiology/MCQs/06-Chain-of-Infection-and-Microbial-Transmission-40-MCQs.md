**Original NORCET-style and Indian staff nurse practice.** Difficulty and priority are editorial estimates. Open answers only after attempting. Not official PYQs.

### Q1. Which sequence correctly represents the six links of the chain of infection?

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Priority:** High

- **A.** Exit, entry, agent, reservoir, host, transmission
- **B.** Agent, reservoir, exit, transmission, entry, susceptible host
- **C.** Reservoir, entry, agent, host, exit, transmission
- **D.** Agent, host, entry, reservoir, exit, transmission

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: B. Agent, reservoir, exit, transmission, entry, susceptible host**

Sabse pehle germ kisi jagah rehta hai. Phir woh bahar nikalta hai, doosri jagah pahunchta hai aur naye person ke body mein enter karta hai. Agar us person ki protection kam hai toh infection ho sakti hai.

**Baaki options kyun sahi nahi hain?** Baaki sequences mein germ ke bahar nikalne aur naye host mein enter karne ka order galat hai.

</details>

- **D.** 6%

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: D. 6%**

Attack proportion nikalne ke liye new cases ko at-risk logon se divide karke 100 se multiply karo. 18 divided by 300 multiplied by 100 = 6%.

**Baaki options kyun sahi nahi hain?** 18 raw case count hai. 16.7 aur 60 wrong calculation hain.

</details>
