- **B.** Improvisation risk create kar sakti hai.
- **C.** Measurement alone shortage solve nahi karta.
- **D.** Patients cause nahi hain.

</details>

### Q40. A patient asks whether a healthcare-associated infection proves a nurse was negligent. Which response is most accurate?
