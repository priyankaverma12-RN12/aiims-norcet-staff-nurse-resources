### Q1. How should a sharps disposal container be positioned while in use?

**Difficulty:** Easy | **Priority:** High

- **A.** Upright and stable
- **B.** Lying loosely on its side
- **C.** Inverted on a shelf
- **D.** Suspended from a patient's IV tubing

<details><summary>Sahi answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct answer: A. Upright and stable**

- **A.** Upright stable container contents ko spill hone se bachane mein help karta hai aur safe disposal allow karta hai.
- **B.** Side par loose container se sharps spill ho sakte hain.
- **C.** Inverted container ka intended disposal opening safely use nahi ho payega.
- **D.** IV tubing se container suspend karna unstable aur patient care ke liye unsafe hai.

**Exam clue:** Sharps container upright aur stable hona chahiye.

**Reference:** [CDC sharps disposal](https://archive.cdc.gov/www_cdc_gov/nora/councils/hcsa/stopsticks/sharpsdisposal.html).

</details>

### Q2. A used sharp becomes stuck at the disposal opening. Which action should the nurse avoid?

**Difficulty:** Easy | **Priority:** High

- **A.** Secure the area and seek trained assistance
- **B.** Pushing the sharp down with bare fingers
- **C.** Following the approved container replacement procedure
- **D.** Preventing other people from using the unsafe opening

<details><summary>Sahi answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct answer: B. Pushing the sharp down with bare fingers**

- **A.** Area secure karke trained assistance lena further exposure ko prevent karta hai.
- **B.** Fingers opening ke andar le jane se hidden sharps puncture kar sakte hain. Kabhi hand se contents force mat karo.
- **C.** Approved replacement procedure damaged ya obstructed container safely manage karne mein help karta hai.
- **D.** Unsafe opening se others ko door rakhna appropriate interim protection hai.

**Exam clue:** Sharps container mein fingers insert mat karo.

**Reference:** [CDC sharps disposal](https://archive.cdc.gov/www_cdc_gov/nora/councils/hcsa/stopsticks/sharpsdisposal.html).

</details>

### Q3. What does percutaneous exposure mean in occupational infection control?

**Difficulty:** Easy | **Priority:** High

- **A.** Exposure through an intact uniform alone
- **B.** Exposure through hearing a patient cough
- **C.** Exposure through a break or puncture in the skin
- **D.** Exposure by viewing a laboratory result

<details><summary>Sahi answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct answer: C. Exposure through a break or puncture in the skin**

- **A.** Uniform touch alone skin penetration establish nahi karta.
- **B.** Cough sunna percutaneous route nahi hai.
- **C.** Needle ya sharp skin penetrate karta hai toh percutaneous exposure ho sakta hai. Material aur injury details ka assessment zaroori hai.
- **D.** Lab result dekhne se infectious material skin penetrate nahi karta.

**Exam clue:** Percutaneous ka matlab skin ke through penetration hai.

**Reference:** [CDC NIOSH bloodborne risks](https://www.cdc.gov/niosh/healthcare/risk-factors/bloodborne-infectious-diseases.html).

</details>

### Q4. Which body fluid is collected from a joint and is considered potentially infectious in bloodborne exposure assessment?

**Difficulty:** Easy | **Priority:** High

- **A.** Tears without blood
- **B.** Sweat without blood
- **C.** Saliva without blood in ordinary non-dental care
- **D.** Synovial fluid

<details><summary>Sahi answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct answer: D. Synovial fluid**

- **A.** Nonbloody tears usual occupational HIV risk fluid category mein nahi aate. Other infection hazards alag assess hote hain.
- **B.** Sweat without blood bloodborne exposure risk fluid generally nahi hai.
- **C.** Nonbloody saliva ordinary non-dental setting mein occupational HIV risk fluid generally nahi maana jata.
- **D.** Synovial fluid joints mein hota hai aur potentially infectious fluid list mein included hai. Exposure route ke according assessment karo.

**Exam clue:** Synovial fluid joint fluid hai aur exposure assessment mein consider hota hai.

**Reference:** [CDC NIOSH bloodborne risks](https://www.cdc.gov/niosh/healthcare/risk-factors/bloodborne-infectious-diseases.html).

</details>

### Q5. A laboratory worker has unprotected direct contact with concentrated HIV culture material. What is appropriate?

**Difficulty:** Easy | **Priority:** High

- **A.** Prompt specialized clinical exposure evaluation
- **B.** Wait for visible jaundice
- **C.** No assessment unless a used needle was involved
- **D.** Only routine annual vaccination review

<details><summary>Sahi answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct answer: A. Prompt specialized clinical exposure evaluation**

- **A.** Concentrated virus se direct unprotected contact ko clinical evaluation chahiye. Specialist route aur material ke according risk assess karta hai.
- **B.** Symptoms ka wait karna time-sensitive assessment delay karta hai.
- **C.** Needlestick ke alawa mucosal ya other direct contact bhi relevant ho sakta hai.
- **D.** Vaccination review useful ho sakta hai lekin acute HIV exposure evaluation ka substitute nahi hai.

**Exam clue:** Concentrated virus contact mein needle injury hona mandatory nahi hai.

**Reference:** [2025 US PHS occupational HIV guideline](https://www.cambridge.org/core/journals/infection-control-and-hospital-epidemiology/article/2025-us-public-health-service-guidelines-for-the-management-of-occupational-exposures-to-human-immunodeficiency-virus-and-recommendations-for-postexposure-prophylaxis-in-healthcare-settings/A410E90C8C13C8B9FB417C7F42051D2A).

### Q6. A patient bites a nurse and blood is present in the wound and mouth. Whose possible bloodborne exposure needs evaluation?

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Priority:** High

- **A.** Only the nurse, irrespective of the patient's exposure
- **B.** Both the bitten person and the person who inflicted the bite
- **C.** Only witnesses who saw the event
- **D.** Neither person because bites never transmit bloodborne infection

<details><summary>Sahi answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct answer: B. Both the bitten person and the person who inflicted the bite**

- **A.** Nurse ka assessment zaroori hai, lekin patient's mouth bhi blood contact mein aaya ho sakta hai.
- **B.** Bleeding bite mein dono directions mein exposure possible hai. Clinician dono individuals ke blood contact aur injury ko assess karta hai.
- **C.** Witnesses sirf dekhne se bloodborne exposure experience nahi karte.
- **D.** Bites mein transmission uncommon ho sakti hai, lekin blood present ho toh risk assessment zaroori hai.

**Exam clue:** Bleeding human bite mein dono persons ka exposure consider karo.

**Reference:** [2025 US PHS occupational HIV guideline](https://www.cambridge.org/core/journals/infection-control-and-hospital-epidemiology/article/2025-us-public-health-service-guidelines-for-the-management-of-occupational-exposures-to-human-immunodeficiency-virus-and-recommendations-for-postexposure-prophylaxis-in-healthcare-settings/A410E90C8C13C8B9FB417C7F42051D2A).
