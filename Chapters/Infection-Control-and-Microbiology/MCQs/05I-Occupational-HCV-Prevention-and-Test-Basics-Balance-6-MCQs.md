# Occupational hepatitis C — preventive and testing basics

### Q1. Which vaccine is currently available to specifically prevent HCV infection in an occupationally exposed nurse?

**Difficulty:** Easy | **Priority:** High

- **A.** A routine HCV vaccine given annually
- **B.** A combined HCV and HIV vaccine
- **C.** A hepatitis B vaccine that specifically prevents HCV
- **D.** No licensed HCV vaccine is available

<details><summary>Sahi answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct answer: D. No licensed HCV vaccine is available**

- **A.** Routine annual HCV vaccine available nahi hai.
- **B.** Combined HCV aur HIV vaccine routine licensed occupational prevention option nahi hai.
- **C.** Hepatitis B vaccine HBV ko target karti hai, HCV ko specifically prevent nahi karti.
- **D.** HCV prevention ke liye vaccine available nahi hai. Safe sharps practice aur indicated exposure follow-up important hain.

**Exam clue:** HBV vaccine available hai, HCV vaccine nahi.

**Reference:** [CDC HCV clinical testing](https://www.cdc.gov/hepatitis-c/hcp/diagnosis-testing/index.html).

### Q2. Which statement about ordinary immune globulin as prophylaxis after occupational hepatitis C exposure is correct?

**Difficulty:** Easy | **Priority:** High

- **A.** It is not effective for preventing HCV infection after exposure
- **B.** It is routinely prescribed for all HCV exposures
- **C.** It replaces all HCV laboratory tests
- **D.** It provides lifelong HCV protection

<details><summary>Sahi answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct answer: A. It is not effective for preventing HCV infection after exposure**

- **A.** Immune globulin HCV exposure ke baad infection prevention ke liye effective nahi maana jata. Indicated testing aur clinical follow-up follow karo.
- **B.** Har HCV exposure mein immune globulin routine recommendation nahi hai.
- **C.** Immune globulin laboratory follow-up replace nahi kar sakta.
- **D.** Ordinary immune globulin lifelong HCV protection establish nahi karta.

**Exam clue:** HCV exposure ke liye routine immune globulin prophylaxis effective nahi hai.

**Reference:** [CDC HCV treatment guidelines](https://www.cdc.gov/std/treatment-guidelines/hcv.htm).

</details>

### Q3. In HCV laboratory terminology, what does NAT stand for?

**Difficulty:** Easy | **Priority:** High

- **A.** Nursing assessment technique
- **B.** Nucleic acid test
- **C.** Neutral antibody transfer
- **D.** New antibiotic therapy

<details><summary>Sahi answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct answer: B. Nucleic acid test**

- **A.** Nursing assessment clinical care ka part hai, HCV test abbreviation nahi.
- **B.** NAT nucleic acid test hai jo HCV RNA detect kar sakta hai. RNA detection current infection assessment mein useful hai.
- **C.** Neutral antibody transfer NAT ka standard laboratory expansion nahi.
- **D.** Antibiotics bacterial infection treat karte hain, NAT diagnostic test hai.

**Exam clue:** NAT detects nucleic acid, for HCV the target is viral RNA.

**Reference:** [CDC HCV clinical testing](https://www.cdc.gov/hepatitis-c/hcp/diagnosis-testing/index.html).

</details>

### Q4. Approximately how soon can HCV RNA usually become detectable after acquisition, according to CDC clinical testing guidance?

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Priority:** High

- **A.** Immediately in every person within seconds
- **B.** Only after one year
- **C.** Around one to two weeks
- **D.** Only when jaundice appears

<details><summary>Sahi answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct answer: C. Around one to two weeks**

- **A.** Immediately har person mein RNA detect hona guaranteed nahi hai. Exposure ke baad viral replication ke liye time lag sakta hai.
- **B.** One year tak wait karna necessary nahi hai. RNA early infection mein detectable ho sakta hai.
- **C.** CDC ke according RNA usually one to two weeks mein detectable ho sakta hai. Yeh occupational follow-up schedule ko replace nahi karta.
- **D.** Jaundice absent hone par bhi RNA detect ho sakta hai.

**Exam clue:** RNA may appear early, but use the indicated testing schedule.

**Reference:** [CDC HCV clinical testing](https://www.cdc.gov/hepatitis-c/hcp/diagnosis-testing/index.html).

</details>

### Q5. A worker previously cured of hepatitis C asks whether cure guarantees protection from another infection. What is correct?

**Difficulty:** Easy | **Priority:** High

- **A.** Cure produces guaranteed lifelong protection
- **B.** Only a hepatitis B booster is needed to prevent reinfection
- **C.** A positive HCV antibody test proves immunity
- **D.** HCV reinfection remains possible

<details><summary>Sahi answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct answer: D. HCV reinfection remains possible**

- **A.** Cure current infection ko resolve kar sakti hai lekin future exposure se lifelong protection guarantee nahi karti.
- **B.** HBV booster hepatitis B prevention ke liye hai, HCV reinfection prevention ke liye nahi.
- **C.** Positive antibody prior exposure indicate kar sakta hai, protective immunity ka proof nahi.
- **D.** HCV reinfection possible hai. Prior cure ke baad bhi sharps safety aur new exposure assessment zaroori hai.

**Exam clue:** HCV cure does not establish immunity to reinfection.

**Reference:** [CDC HCV treatment guidelines](https://www.cdc.gov/std/treatment-guidelines/hcv.htm).

</details>

### Q6. What does the abbreviation DAA refer to in hepatitis C treatment?

**Difficulty:** Easy | **Priority:** High

- **A.** Direct-acting antiviral
- **B.** Delayed antibody assessment
- **C.** Daily autoclave adjustment
- **D.** Dermal allergy antigen

<details><summary>Sahi answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct answer: A. Direct-acting antiviral**

- **A.** DAA viral replication ke specific steps target karne wali medicines ko kehte hain. Confirmed HCV infection mein appropriate regimen clinician select karta hai.
- **B.** Antibody assessment diagnosis aur follow-up se related hai, DAA drug category nahi.
- **C.** Autoclave adjustment sterilization process se related hai, HCV treatment nahi.
- **D.** Allergy antigen ka DAA antiviral abbreviation se relation nahi.

**Exam clue:** DAA means direct-acting antiviral, used for treatment rather than routine exposure prophylaxis.

**Reference:** [CDC HCV occupational guidance 2020](https://www.cdc.gov/mmwr/volumes/69/rr/rr6906a1.htm).

</details>
