### Q38. Why should even a hepatitis B immune worker report a used-needle injury?

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Priority:** High

- **A.** Other pathogens such as HIV and HCV still require assessment
- **B.** HBV immunity protects against every bloodborne pathogen
- **C.** Reporting is only required when symptoms develop
- **D.** A vaccine makes all needlestick wounds sterile

HBV vaccine specific hepatitis B protection deti hai. HIV aur HCV ke liye exposure assessment separately karna hota hai.
