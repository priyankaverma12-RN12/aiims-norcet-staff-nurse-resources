### Q1. What is the usual shape of a bacterial coccobacillus?

**Difficulty:** Easy | **Priority:** High

- **A.** Long rigid spiral
- **B.** Short oval rod
- **C.** A multicellular flat worm
- **D.** A branching fungal hypha

<details><summary>Sahi answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct answer: B. Short oval rod**

- **A.** Rigid spiral shape spirillum se associated hoti hai, short oval rod se nahi.
- **B.** Coccobacillus short rod hota hai jo oval ya round-ended dikh sakta hai. Isliye naam cocci aur bacilli ke beech ki morphology indicate karta hai.
- **C.** Flat worm multicellular parasite hai, bacterial cell shape nahi.
- **D.** Fungal hypha elongated fungal structure hai, coccobacillus morphology nahi.

**Exam clue:** Coccobacillus = short oval rod.

**Reference:** [OpenStax Microbiology 3.3](https://openstax.org/books/microbiology/pages/3-3-unique-characteristics-of-prokaryotic-cells) (retrieved 28 November 2026).

### Q2. What name is given to a pair of rod-shaped bacterial cells remaining attached after division?

**Difficulty:** Easy | **Priority:** High

- **A.** Tetrad
- **B.** Staphylococcus arrangement
- **C.** Diplobacilli
- **D.** Diplococci

<details><summary>Sahi answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct answer: C. Diplobacilli**

- **A.** Tetrad four cells ki arrangement describe karta hai, two rods ki nahi.
- **B.** Staphylococcal arrangement usually cocci ke clusters ko describe karti hai.
- **C.** Diplo ka meaning pair hai aur bacilli rods hain. Isliye attached rods ki pair diplobacilli kehlati hai.
- **D.** Diplococci round cells ki pair hai, rod-shaped cells ki nahi.

**Exam clue:** Diplo = pair, bacilli = rods.

**Reference:** [OpenStax Microbiology 3.3](https://openstax.org/books/microbiology/pages/3-3-unique-characteristics-of-prokaryotic-cells) (retrieved 28 November 2026).

</details>

### Q3. A bacterial smear shows four spherical cells arranged in a square. Which term describes this grouping?

**Difficulty:** Easy | **Priority:** High

- **A.** Palisade
- **B.** Streptobacillus
- **C.** Vibrio
- **D.** Tetrad

<details><summary>Sahi answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct answer: D. Tetrad**

- **A.** Palisade rod cells ki side-by-side ya angular arrangement ko describe kar sakti hai.
- **B.** Streptobacillus rods ki chain hoti hai, four spherical cells ki square arrangement nahi.
- **C.** Vibrio curved rod shape hai, grouping of four cocci nahi.
- **D.** Four spherical cells ki square grouping ko tetrad kehte hain. Morphology preliminary identification mein help karti hai.

**Exam clue:** Tetrad = four cells arranged together.

**Reference:** [OpenStax Microbiology 3.3](https://openstax.org/books/microbiology/pages/3-3-unique-characteristics-of-prokaryotic-cells) (retrieved 28 November 2026).

### Q4. Where is the main chromosomal DNA located in a typical bacterial cell?

**Difficulty:** Easy | **Priority:** High

- **A.** Nucleoid
- **B.** Mitochondrial matrix
- **C.** Membrane-bound nucleus
- **D.** Golgi apparatus

<details><summary>Sahi answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct answer: A. Nucleoid**

- **A.** Bacterial chromosome nucleoid region mein concentrated hota hai. Yeh membrane-bound nucleus nahi hota.
- **B.** Typical bacteria mitochondria possess nahi karte, isliye main chromosome mitochondrial matrix mein nahi hota.
- **C.** Membrane-bound nucleus eukaryotic cells ki feature hai.
- **D.** Golgi apparatus eukaryotic protein processing organelle hai aur typical bacteria mein nahi hota.

**Exam clue:** Bacterial chromosomal DNA nucleoid mein hota hai.

**Reference:** [OpenStax Microbiology 3.3](https://openstax.org/books/microbiology/pages/3-3-unique-characteristics-of-prokaryotic-cells) (retrieved 28 November 2026).

### Q5. What is the main function of many bacterial cytoplasmic storage inclusions?

**Difficulty:** Easy | **Priority:** High

- **A.** Producing a membrane-bound nucleus
- **B.** Storing reserve nutrients or energy-rich substances
- **C.** Becoming a complete multicellular organ
- **D.** Carrying oxygen as human red blood cells do

<details><summary>Sahi answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct answer: B. Storing reserve nutrients or energy-rich substances**

- **A.** Storage inclusion membrane-bound nucleus create nahi karti. Bacterial DNA nucleoid region mein hota hai.
- **B.** Many inclusions reserve substances jaise carbon compounds ya phosphate store kar sakti hain, jo cell baad mein use kar sakta hai.
- **C.** Bacteria single-cell organisms hote hain aur storage granules multicellular organs nahi banate.
- **D.** Human red cell oxygen transport hemoglobin se karta hai, bacterial inclusion ka usual function yeh nahi hai.

**Exam clue:** Inclusion granules may store metabolic reserves.

**Reference:** [OpenStax Microbiology 3.3](https://openstax.org/books/microbiology/pages/3-3-unique-characteristics-of-prokaryotic-cells) (retrieved 28 November 2026).

</details>

### Q6. Which term describes a bacterial flagellum situated at one pole of the cell when only one flagellum is present?

**Difficulty:** Easy | **Priority:** High

- **A.** Peritrichous
- **B.** Amphitrichous
- **C.** Monotrichous
- **D.** Atrichous

<details><summary>Sahi answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct answer: C. Monotrichous**

- **A.** Peritrichous arrangement mein flagella cell surface ke around distributed hote hain.
- **B.** Amphitrichous arrangement mein opposite poles par flagella hote hain.
- **C.** Monotrichous arrangement mein single flagellum ek pole par present hota hai.
- **D.** Atrichous cell flagella absent hone ko describe karta hai.

**Exam clue:** Mono = one, monotrichous = one polar flagellum.

**Reference:** [OpenStax Microbiology 3.3](https://openstax.org/books/microbiology/pages/3-3-unique-characteristics-of-prokaryotic-cells) (retrieved 28 November 2026).
