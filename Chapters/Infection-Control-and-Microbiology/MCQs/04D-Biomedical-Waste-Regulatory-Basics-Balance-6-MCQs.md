### Q1. Under Rule 9 of the Indian BMW rules, which body is the prescribed authority for an ordinary civilian hospital in Himachal Pradesh?

**Difficulty:** Easy | **Priority:** High

- **A.** The State Pollution Control Board
- **B.** The district transport office
- **C.** The Indian Nursing Council
- **D.** The hospital pharmacy committee

<details><summary>Sahi answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct answer: A. The State Pollution Control Board**

- **A.** State mein biomedical waste rules implement karne ki prescribed authority State Pollution Control Board hoti hai.
- **B.** Transport office vehicle administration se related hai, BMW authorization ka prescribed authority nahi hai.
- **C.** Indian Nursing Council nursing education standards se related hai.
- **D.** Pharmacy committee hospital medicines manage kar sakti hai, lekin statutory BMW authority nahi hai.

**Exam clue:** States mein SPCB aur Union Territories mein applicable PCC yaad rakho.

**Reference:** [CPCB, Bio-Medical Waste Management Rules 2016, Rules 9–16](https://cpcb.nic.in/uploads/Projects/Bio-Medical-Waste/Bio-medical_Waste_Management_Rules_2016.pdf).

### Q3. Which form represents the authorization issued under Rule 10 of the BMW rules?

**Difficulty:** Easy | **Priority:** High

- **B.** Form II
- **C.** Form III
- **D.** Form IV

<details><summary>Sahi answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct answer: C. Form III**

- **A.** Form I major accident reporting ke liye hai.
- **B.** Form II authorization ke application ke liye hai, issued authorization nahi.

### Q4. Under the original Rule 15 reporting framework, after immediately notifying the authority of a major biomedical waste accident, within what time is the written report required?

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Priority:** High

- **A.** Seven days
- **B.** Thirty days
- **C.** One year
- **D.** Twenty-four hours

<details><summary>Sahi answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct answer: D. Twenty-four hours**

- **A.** Seven days written major accident reporting ke specified interval se zyada hai.
- **B.** Thirty days is emergency reporting ke liye specified deadline nahi hai.
- **C.** Annual reporting immediate major accident reporting ka replacement nahi hai.
- **D.** Rule 15 immediate intimation ke baad 24 hours ke andar written remedial report specify karta hai. Facility current applicable reporting requirements bhi follow kare.

**Exam clue:** Major accident ki immediate intimation aur 24-hour written report alag steps hain.

**Reference:** [CPCB, Bio-Medical Waste Management Rules 2016, Rules 9–16](https://cpcb.nic.in/uploads/Projects/Bio-Medical-Waste/Bio-medical_Waste_Management_Rules_2016.pdf).

### Q5. In biomedical waste administration, who is the occupier of a healthcare establishment?

**Difficulty:** Easy | **Priority:** High

- **A.** The person with administrative control over the waste-generating institution and premises
- **B.** Every visitor entering the hospital
- **C.** Only the patient receiving an injection
- **D.** The driver of any passing vehicle

<details><summary>Sahi answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct answer: A. The person with administrative control over the waste-generating institution and premises**

- **A.** Occupier institution aur premises par administrative control rakhne wala person hota hai. Rules usko waste management duties assign karte hain.
- **B.** Visitor hospital enter karta hai lekin uske paas administrative control nahi hota.
- **C.** Patient waste generate karne wali activity se associated ho sakta hai, lekin administrative occupier nahi hota.
- **D.** Passing vehicle ka driver healthcare premises control nahi karta.

**Exam clue:** Occupier ka meaning administrative control se related hai.

**Reference:** [CPCB, Bio-Medical Waste Management Rules 2016, Rules 9–16](https://cpcb.nic.in/uploads/Projects/Bio-Medical-Waste/Bio-medical_Waste_Management_Rules_2016.pdf).

### Q6. A facility changes an authorized biomedical waste treatment activity. What does Rule 10 require regarding authorization conditions?

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Priority:** High

- **A.** No action because the previous permission covers every change
- **B.** Notify the prescribed authority and apply for modification
- **C.** Only tell the hospital canteen staff
- **D.** Wait until the equipment is permanently closed

<details><summary>Sahi answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct answer: B. Notify the prescribed authority and apply for modification**

- **A.** Existing authorization specific conditions par based hoti hai. Changed activity ko automatically covered assume nahi karna chahiye.
- **B.** Rule 10 variation ko prescribed authority ke notice mein lane aur modification ke liye fresh Form II application ka provision deta hai.
- **C.** Canteen staff statutory authorization modify nahi kar sakta.
- **D.** Closure tak wait karne se changed activity bina applicable permission operate ho sakti hai.

**Exam clue:** Authorized activity mein change ho toh authority ko inform karke modification process follow karo.

**Reference:** [CPCB, Bio-Medical Waste Management Rules 2016, Rules 9–16](https://cpcb.nic.in/uploads/Projects/Bio-Medical-Waste/Bio-medical_Waste_Management_Rules_2016.pdf).
