## Transmission precautions

### Q1. A patient with suspected pulmonary tuberculosis requires urgent assessment. Which protection is most appropriate for staff entering the airborne isolation area?

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Topic:** Transmission precautions

- **A.** A fit-tested particulate respirator according to policy
- **B.** Only sterile surgical gloves
- **C.** A face shield without respiratory protection
- **D.** A cloth mask worn loosely

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: A. A fit-tested particulate respirator according to policy**

TB ke germs chhote particles ke through hawa mein travel kar sakte hain. Isliye staff ko suitable respirator aur airborne precautions follow karne hote hain.

**Baaki options kyun galat hain?**

- **B.** Gloves saans ke through exposure nahi rokte.
- **C.** Face shield droplets se help karti hai par airborne filtration nahi deti.
- **D.** Loose cloth mask adequate respiratory protection nahi deta.

</details>

## Sterilization

### Q2. Which method is most appropriate for reusable heat-resistant surgical instruments that require sterilization?

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Topic:** Sterilization

- **A.** Wiping with plain water
- **B.** Brief exposure to room air
- **C.** Routine low-level surface disinfection
- **D.** Validated steam sterilization in an autoclave

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: D. Validated steam sterilization in an autoclave**

Sterilization ka matlab microbial life aur spores ko bhi khatam karna hai. Heat-resistant instruments ke liye validated steam cycle useful hoti hai.

**Baaki options kyun galat hain?**

- **A.** Paani se cleaning ho sakti hai par sterilization nahi.
- **B.** Hawa mein rakhne se spores nahi marte.
- **C.** Low-level disinfection critical surgical instruments ke liye enough nahi hai.

</details>

## Needlestick exposure

### Q3. A nurse sustains a needlestick injury from a used hollow-bore needle. Which immediate action is appropriate?

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Topic:** Needlestick exposure

- **A.** Apply concentrated bleach to the puncture
- **B.** Wait for symptoms before reporting
- **C.** Wash the site with soap and water and report promptly for exposure assessment
- **D.** Squeeze the wound aggressively until it bleeds

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: C. Wash the site with soap and water and report promptly for exposure assessment**

Used needle se blood-borne infection ka risk ho sakta hai. Nurse site wash karti hai aur jaldi report karti hai taaki HIV, HBV aur HCV exposure ka assessment ho sake.

**Baaki options kyun galat hain?**

- **A.** Bleach tissue ko damage kar sakti hai.
- **B.** Post-exposure treatment ka time important hota hai.
- **D.** Wound ko force se squeeze karna recommended nahi hai.

</details>

## Biomedical waste

### Q4. Under Indian biomedical waste segregation rules, where should a used contaminated disposable plastic IV tube without a sharp be placed?

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Topic:** Biomedical waste

- **A.** Ordinary general-waste bin
- **B.** Red category container or bag
- **C.** White puncture-proof sharps container
- **D.** Blue glassware container

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: B. Red category container or bag**

Contaminated recyclable plastic ko Indian biomedical waste rules mein red category mein segregate kiya jaata hai. Isse treatment aur recycling ka safe process follow ho sakta hai.

**Baaki options kyun galat hain?**

- **A.** Contaminated tube general waste nahi hai.
- **C.** White category sharps ke liye hai.
- **D.** Blue category specified glassware ke liye hai.

</details>
