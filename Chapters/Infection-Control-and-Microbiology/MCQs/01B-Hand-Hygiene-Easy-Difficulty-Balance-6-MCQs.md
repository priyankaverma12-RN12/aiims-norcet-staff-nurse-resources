### Q1. Which hand hygiene method should a ward nurse use immediately after using the restroom?

**Difficulty:** Easy | **Priority:** High

- **A.** Wash with soap and water
- **B.** Rinse briefly with water alone
- **C.** Wipe the hands on the uniform
- **D.** Wait until the next patient contact

<details><summary>Sahi answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct answer: A. Wash with soap and water**

- **A.** Restroom use ke baad nurse soap aur water se hands wash karti hai, kyunki germs hands par reh sakte hain aur unhe achhi tarah remove karna zaroori hai.
- **B.** Sirf water se jaldi rinse karne par soap ke saath hone wali proper cleaning nahi milti.
- **C.** Uniform par hands ponchne se germs kapdon par transfer ho sakte hain aur hands properly clean nahi hote.
- **D.** Agar nurse agle patient tak wait karegi, toh beech mein touched surfaces contaminate ho sakti hain.

**Exam clue:** Restroom ke baad soap aur water ko yaad rakho, chahe hands visibly dirty na dikh rahe hon.

**Reference:** [CDC hand hygiene guidance](https://www.cdc.gov/clean-hands/hcp/clinical-safety/) (27 February 2024, directly retrieved).

### Q2. Which water temperature practice helps protect the skin during frequent routine handwashing?

**Difficulty:** Easy | **Priority:** High

- **A.** Use very hot water each time
- **B.** Avoid hot water and use comfortably warm or cool running water
- **C.** Use only ice-cold water
- **D.** Use boiling water for final rinsing

<details><summary>Sahi answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct answer: B. Avoid hot water and use comfortably warm or cool running water**

- **A.** Bahut hot water se skin ke natural oils kam ho sakte hain, jis wajah se dryness aur irritation badh sakti hai.
- **B.** Comfortable temperature ka running water use karne se soap ke saath cleaning ho sakti hai aur unnecessary heat se skin ko bachaya ja sakta hai.
- **C.** Ice-cold water zaroori nahi hai aur uncomfortable temperature ki wajah se nurse proper washing jaldi khatam kar sakti hai.
- **D.** Boiling water skin ko burn kar sakta hai aur routine hand hygiene mein iski koi zaroorat nahi hoti.

**Exam clue:** Routine washing mein soap aur technique important hain, excessive heat nahi.

**Reference:** [CDC hand hygiene guidance](https://www.cdc.gov/clean-hands/hcp/clinical-safety/) (27 February 2024, directly retrieved).

</details>

### Q3. A patient believes that ordinary alcohol-based hand sanitizer causes antibiotic resistance. Which response is correct?

**Difficulty:** Easy | **Priority:** High

- **A.** It makes every bacterium resistant to antibiotics
- **B.** It must be replaced with an oral antibiotic
- **C.** It does not cause antibiotic resistance through recommended hand hygiene use
- **D.** It should be used only when an antibiotic is prescribed

<details><summary>Sahi answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct answer: C. It does not cause antibiotic resistance through recommended hand hygiene use**

- **A.** Antibiotic resistance ek specific process hai, aur recommended alcohol hand hygiene se har bacterium antibiotic-resistant nahi ban jata.
- **B.** Oral antibiotic patient ke infection ke liye indication ke hisaab se diya jata hai, hands clean karne ke liye nahi.
- **C.** Alcohol-based sanitizer germs ko antibiotics se alag tareeke se affect karta hai. CDC ke mutabik recommended hand hygiene use antimicrobial resistance ka cause nahi hai.
- **D.** Hand sanitizer ka use hand hygiene indication par depend karta hai, antibiotic prescription par nahi.

**Exam clue:** Hand sanitizer aur antibiotic treatment ke purpose ko alag samjho.

**Reference:** [CDC hand hygiene guidance](https://www.cdc.gov/clean-hands/about/hand-hygiene-for-healthcare.html) (27 February 2024, directly retrieved).

</details>

### Q4. Before inserting their own contact lens, a hospital visitor should first do which of the following?

**Difficulty:** Easy | **Priority:** High

- **A.** Rub the eyes with a used tissue
- **B.** Touch the bedside table to steady the hand
- **C.** Put on a used examination glove
- **D.** Clean their hands

<details><summary>Sahi answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct answer: D. Clean their hands**

- **A.** Used tissue se germs fingers ya eyes tak transfer ho sakte hain, isliye yeh safe preparation nahi hai.
- **B.** Bedside table par germs ho sakte hain aur usse touch karna hand hygiene ka substitute nahi hai.
- **C.** Used glove contaminated ho sakta hai aur aankh ko touch karne se pehle clean hands ki zaroorat ko remove nahi karta.
- **D.** Contact lens lagate waqt fingers eyes ke paas jati hain. Pehle proper hand cleaning aur drying karne se germs transfer hone ka risk kam hota hai.

**Exam clue:** Eyes, nose ya mouth touch karne se pehle hand hygiene important hai.

**Reference:** [CDC hand hygiene guidance](https://www.cdc.gov/clean-hands/about/hand-hygiene-for-healthcare.html) (27 February 2024, directly retrieved).

### Q5. A patient politely asks a nurse to clean her hands again before examination. What is the appropriate response?

**Difficulty:** Easy | **Priority:** High

- **A.** Welcome the reminder and perform hand hygiene
- **B.** Tell the patient that reminders are prohibited
- **C.** Ask the patient to leave the examination area
- **D.** Clean only the patient's hands instead

<details><summary>Sahi answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct answer: A. Welcome the reminder and perform hand hygiene**

- **A.** Patient ko infection prevention mein participate karne ka haq hai. Nurse reminder ko respectfully accept karke required hand hygiene kar sakti hai.
- **B.** Patient ke concern ko prohibit karna safety communication ko discourage karta hai aur CDC patient participation ko encourage karta hai.
- **C.** Polite safety reminder ki wajah se examination rokne ya patient ko bahar bhejne ki zaroorat nahi hai.
- **D.** Patient ke hands clean karna useful ho sakta hai, lekin nurse ki apni hand hygiene requirement phir bhi rehti hai.

**Exam clue:** Patient ka safety reminder teamwork ka part hai, misconduct nahi.

**Reference:** [CDC hand hygiene guidance](https://www.cdc.gov/clean-hands/about/hand-hygiene-for-healthcare.html) (27 February 2024, directly retrieved).

</details>

### Q6. Which statement about normal hand skin microorganisms after alcohol hand hygiene is correct?

**Difficulty:** Easy | **Priority:** High

- **A.** The skin stays permanently sterile
- **B.** Normal resident microorganisms can return after hand hygiene
- **C.** All normal skin microorganisms must be eliminated for life
- **D.** The return of normal microorganisms proves that the hand rub was expired

<details><summary>Sahi answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct answer: B. Normal resident microorganisms can return after hand hygiene**

- **A.** Routine alcohol hand hygiene living skin ko permanently sterile nahi banati hai. Skin par normal microorganisms phir se mil sakte hain.
- **B.** Hamari skin ki normal microbial population hand hygiene ke baad dobara recover ho sakti hai. Iska matlab yeh nahi hai ki hand cleaning useless thi.
- **C.** Normal skin microbiota ko lifetime ke liye eliminate karna routine hand hygiene ka goal nahi hai. Goal harmful germ transmission ko kam karna hai.
- **D.** Normal microorganisms ke dobara milne se product expiry prove nahi hoti. Expiry product label aur approved storage instructions se check ki jati hai.

**Exam clue:** Hand hygiene germ transmission ko kam karti hai, permanent skin sterilization nahi karti.

**Reference:** [CDC hand hygiene guidance](https://www.cdc.gov/clean-hands/about/hand-hygiene-for-healthcare.html) (27 February 2024, directly retrieved).
