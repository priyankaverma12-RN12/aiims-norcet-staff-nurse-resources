### Q1. Which type of staining uses a single dye to display the general shape and arrangement of bacterial cells?

**Difficulty:** Easy | **Priority:** High

- **A.** Simple staining
- **B.** Differential Gram staining
- **C.** Acid-fast differential staining
- **D.** Endospore differential staining

<details><summary>Sahi answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct answer: A. Simple staining**

- **A.** Simple stain ek dye se cells ko visible banata hai, jisse shape aur arrangement observe ki ja sakti hai.
- **B.** Gram staining multiple reagents use karke organisms ko differential groups mein distinguish karti hai.
- **C.** Acid-fast staining differential staining technique hai, single dye simple stain nahi.
- **D.** Endospore stain spores aur vegetative cells ko distinguish karta hai, isliye simple stain se different hai.

**Exam clue:** Simple stain uses one dye for general morphology.

### Q2. What is the main purpose of fixing a routine bacterial smear to a microscope slide before staining?

**Difficulty:** Easy | **Priority:** High

- **A.** To allow the cells to swim freely
- **B.** To attach the cells to the slide and preserve their morphology
- **C.** To increase the bacterial population
- **D.** To convert every organism into an endospore

<details><summary>Sahi answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct answer: B. To attach the cells to the slide and preserve their morphology**

- **A.** Fixed smear motility assess karne ke liye nahi hota, kyunki cells immobilize ho jate hain.
- **B.** Fixation cells ko slide par attach aur morphology preserve karne mein help karti hai, taaki staining ke dauran sample wash off na ho. Fixation ko guaranteed sterilization mat samjho.
- **C.** Fixation growth promote nahi karti. Routine fixed smear mein multiplication observe nahi ki jati.
- **D.** Endospore formation biological process hai aur fixation se every organism spore nahi banata.

**Exam clue:** Fixation helps retain cells on the slide, not prove sterility.

**Reference:** [OpenStax Microbiology 2.4](https://openstax.org/books/microbiology/pages/2-4-staining-microscopic-specimens) (retrieved 28 November 2026).

</details>

### Q3. What is the charge of the colored ion in a basic microbiological dye?

**Difficulty:** Easy | **Priority:** High

- **A.** Always neutral
- **B.** Always negative
- **C.** Positive
- **D.** Alternating between positive and negative each second

<details><summary>Sahi answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct answer: C. Positive**

- **A.** Basic dye ka chromophore neutral nahi, positively charged hota hai.
- **B.** Negative chromophore acidic dye ki defining feature hai.
- **C.** Basic dye ka colored ion positively charged hota hai. Yeh bacterial surfaces ke negatively charged components se interact kar sakta hai.
- **D.** Charge har second alternate karna basic dye ki definition nahi hai.

**Exam clue:** Basic dye = positively charged chromophore.

**Reference:** [OpenStax Microbiology 2.4](https://openstax.org/books/microbiology/pages/2-4-staining-microscopic-specimens) (retrieved 28 November 2026).

### Q4. Which part of a compound light microscope focuses illumination onto the specimen?

**Difficulty:** Easy | **Priority:** High

- **A.** Eyepiece
- **B.** Mechanical stage clip
- **C.** Coarse adjustment knob
- **D.** Condenser

<details><summary>Sahi answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct answer: D. Condenser**

- **A.** Eyepiece image magnify karta hai, specimen par light focus karne wala part nahi.
- **B.** Stage clip slide ko hold karta hai.
- **C.** Coarse adjustment specimen aur objective ke relative focus position ko change karta hai, illumination focus nahi.
- **D.** Condenser light ko specimen par concentrate karta hai, jisse appropriate illumination milti hai.

**Exam clue:** Condenser focuses illumination on specimen.

**Reference:** [OpenStax Microbiology 2.3](https://openstax.org/books/microbiology/pages/2-3-instruments-of-microscopy) (retrieved 28 November 2026).

### Q5. Which microscope component regulates the aperture through which illumination passes toward the specimen?

**Difficulty:** Easy | **Priority:** High

- **A.** Iris diaphragm
- **B.** Nosepiece
- **C.** Stage clip
- **D.** Eyepiece tube

<details><summary>Sahi answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

- **A.** Iris diaphragm aperture change karke illuminating light cone ko regulate karta hai. Proper setting image contrast aur resolution affect karti hai.
- **B.** Nosepiece objective lenses switch karne ke liye rotate hota hai.
- **C.** Stage clip slide position secure karta hai, light aperture control nahi.
- **D.** Eyepiece tube optical path hold karta hai, adjustable illumination aperture nahi.

**Exam clue:** Iris diaphragm controls illumination aperture.

### Q6. Which microscopy method produces a bright specimen against a dark background by excluding direct illumination from the objective?

**Difficulty:** Easy | **Priority:** High

- **A.** Ordinary brightfield microscopy
- **B.** Darkfield microscopy
- **C.** Routine unaided visual inspection
- **D.** Macroscopic photography alone

<details><summary>Sahi answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct answer: B. Darkfield microscopy**

- **A.** Brightfield mein background generally bright hota hai aur specimen comparatively darker ya stained dikh sakta hai.
- **B.** Darkfield direct light ko objective se exclude karta hai aur specimen se scattered light collect karta hai. Isse bright specimen dark background par visible hota hai.
- **C.** Unaided inspection microscopic detail resolve nahi kar sakti.
- **D.** Macroscopic photography alone darkfield optical contrast principle use nahi karti.

**Exam clue:** Darkfield = bright object against dark background.

**Reference:** [OpenStax Microbiology 2.3](https://openstax.org/books/microbiology/pages/2-3-instruments-of-microscopy) (retrieved 28 November 2026).

</details>
