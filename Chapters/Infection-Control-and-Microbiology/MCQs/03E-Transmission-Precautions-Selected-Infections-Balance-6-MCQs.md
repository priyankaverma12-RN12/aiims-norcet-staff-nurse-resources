### Q1. According to CDC Appendix A, which additional precaution is indicated for a hospitalized patient with Mycoplasma pneumonia?

**Difficulty:** Easy | **Priority:** High

- **A.** Droplet precautions
- **B.** Airborne precautions for all cases
- **C.** Contact precautions alone
- **D.** No Standard Precautions

<details><summary>Sahi answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct answer: A. Droplet precautions**

- **A.** Mycoplasma pneumoniae respiratory secretions ke through spread ho sakta hai. CDC Appendix A illness ke duration mein Droplet plus Standard Precautions list karta hai.
- **B.** Routine Mycoplasma pneumonia ke liye Appendix A Airborne Precautions list nahi karta, unless koi separate indication ho.
- **C.** Contact alone listed respiratory transmission precaution ko cover nahi karta.
- **D.** Standard Precautions har patient care mein continue rehti hain.

**Exam clue:** Mycoplasma pneumonia ko CDC Droplet category se jodo.

**Reference:** [CDC Appendix A: precautions by infection](https://www.cdc.gov/infection-control/hcp/isolation-precautions/appendix-a-type-duration.html) (retrieved 28 November 2026).

### Q2. Under CDC Appendix A, which precaution is added to Standard Precautions for poliomyelitis during illness?

**Difficulty:** Easy | **Priority:** High

- **A.** Droplet only
- **B.** Contact
- **C.** Airborne only
- **D.** Protective environment

<details><summary>Sahi answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct answer: B. Contact**

- **A.** Poliovirus ke important transmission route mein fecal contamination include hoti hai. Listed added category Droplet alone nahi hai.
- **B.** CDC poliomyelitis ke liye illness ke duration mein Contact plus Standard Precautions list karta hai. Public health coordination bhi important hai.
- **C.** Airborne alone polio ke listed added precaution ko match nahi karta.
- **D.** Protective environment selected immunocompromised patients ko environmental exposure se protect karne ke liye hota hai.

**Exam clue:** Polio ke clinical isolation question mein Contact plus Standard yaad rakho.

**Reference:** [CDC Appendix A: precautions by infection](https://www.cdc.gov/infection-control/hcp/isolation-precautions/appendix-a-type-duration.html) (retrieved 28 November 2026).

### Q3. Which additional precaution does CDC Appendix A list for respiratory parainfluenza infection in infants and young children?

**Difficulty:** Easy | **Priority:** High

- **A.** Airborne only
- **B.** Droplet only
- **C.** Contact
- **D.** No precautions of any kind

<details><summary>Sahi answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct answer: C. Contact**

- **A.** CDC is specified group ke liye routine Airborne category list nahi karta.
- **B.** Droplet alone Appendix A mein is particular pediatric infection ke liye listed category nahi hai. Local respiratory policies additional measures require kar sakti hain.
- **C.** Parainfluenza infected infants aur young children ke secretions se contact spread ho sakta hai. CDC Contact plus Standard Precautions list karta hai.
- **D.** Har patient mein Standard Precautions applicable hain aur specified parainfluenza infection mein Contact bhi add hoti hai.

**Exam clue:** CDC pediatric parainfluenza classification ko Contact se associate karo.

**Reference:** [CDC Appendix A: precautions by infection](https://www.cdc.gov/infection-control/hcp/isolation-precautions/appendix-a-type-duration.html) (retrieved 28 November 2026).

### Q4. An inpatient has confirmed adenovirus pneumonia. Which CDC Appendix A combination is indicated in addition to Standard Precautions?

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Priority:** High

- **A.** Airborne alone
- **B.** Protective isolation alone
- **C.** Contact alone
- **D.** Droplet and Contact

<details><summary>Sahi answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct answer: D. Droplet and Contact**

- **A.** CDC adenovirus pneumonia ke liye Airborne alone list nahi karta.
- **B.** Protective isolation immunocompromised patient protection ka special approach hai, adenovirus transmission category nahi.
- **C.** Contact important hai, lekin adenovirus pneumonia mein CDC Droplet bhi add karta hai.
- **D.** Adenovirus respiratory secretions aur contaminated contact ke through spread ho sakta hai. CDC dono Droplet aur Contact list karta hai.

**Exam clue:** Adenovirus pneumonia mein Droplet plus Contact plus Standard yaad rakho.

**Reference:** [CDC Appendix A: precautions by infection](https://www.cdc.gov/infection-control/hcp/isolation-precautions/appendix-a-type-duration.html) (retrieved 28 November 2026).

### Q5. Which additional precaution is listed by CDC for pneumonic plague during its infectious period?

**Difficulty:** Easy | **Priority:** High

- **A.** Droplet
- **B.** Contact only
- **C.** Protective environment
- **D.** No precautions

<details><summary>Sahi answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct answer: A. Droplet**

- **A.** Pneumonic plague respiratory droplets se transmit ho sakta hai. CDC effective antibiotics shuru hone ke baad initial 48 hours tak Droplet plus Standard Precautions list karta hai, with public health guidance.
- **B.** Contact alone pneumonic plague ki listed respiratory precaution nahi hai.
- **C.** Protective environment ka purpose patient ko external pathogens se protect karna hai, infectious respiratory source control nahi.
- **D.** Pneumonic plague serious transmissible disease hai. Standard aur indicated additional precautions zaroori hain.

**Exam clue:** Pneumonic plague ko Droplet category se associate karo.

**Reference:** [CDC Appendix A: precautions by infection](https://www.cdc.gov/infection-control/hcp/isolation-precautions/appendix-a-type-duration.html) (retrieved 28 November 2026).

### Q6. For an adult inpatient with Legionella pneumonia and no other isolation indication, what does CDC Appendix A list?

**Difficulty:** Easy | **Priority:** High

- **A.** Airborne and Contact
- **B.** Standard Precautions
- **C.** Droplet for all cases
- **D.** Protective environment for all cases

<details><summary>Sahi answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct answer: B. Standard Precautions**

- **A.** Legionella usually contaminated water systems ke aerosols se acquire hota hai. Routine person-to-person Airborne plus Contact category CDC list nahi karta.
- **B.** CDC Legionella pneumonia ke liye Standard Precautions list karta hai. Facility water source investigation ya other infection-control measures alag se required ho sakte hain.
- **C.** Legionella pneumonia ke liye routine Droplet category listed nahi hai, unless another diagnosis requires it.
- **D.** Har Legionella patient ke liye protective environment indicated nahi hota.

**Exam clue:** Legionella ka water-associated source aur Standard Precautions classification yaad rakho.

**Reference:** [CDC Appendix A: precautions by infection](https://www.cdc.gov/infection-control/hcp/isolation-precautions/appendix-a-type-duration.html) (retrieved 28 November 2026).
