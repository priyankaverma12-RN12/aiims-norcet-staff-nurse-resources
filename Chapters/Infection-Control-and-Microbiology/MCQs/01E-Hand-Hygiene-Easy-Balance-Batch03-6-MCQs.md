### Q1. A nurse is about to eat lunch during a shift. Which method does CDC specifically recommend before eating?

**Difficulty:** Easy | **Priority:** High

- **A.** Soap and water handwashing
- **B.** A dry tissue wipe
- **C.** An examination glove without hand cleaning
- **D.** A rinse with saline only

<details><summary>Sahi answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct answer: A. Soap and water handwashing**

- **A.** Meal se pehle soap aur water se hands wash karne chahiye, kyunki hands se germs food ke through mouth tak pahunch sakte hain.
- **B.** Dry tissue se surface moisture hat sakti hai, lekin yeh soap aur water wali cleaning provide nahi karta.
- **C.** Glove pehenna eating se pehle proper handwashing ka replacement nahi hai.
- **D.** Saline se rinse karna soap ke saath proper friction aur cleaning ka substitute nahi hai.

**Exam clue:** Khana khane se pehle soap aur water yaad rakho.

**Reference:** [CDC clinical hand hygiene](https://www.cdc.gov/clean-hands/hcp/clinical-safety/) (retrieved 28 November 2026).

</details>

### Q2. The alcohol handrub dispenser is unavailable, but a handwashing station is working. What should the nurse do when routine hand hygiene is indicated?

**Difficulty:** Easy | **Priority:** High

- **A.** Wait for the dispenser to be repaired
- **B.** Wash with soap and water
- **C.** Use a dry sterile gauze alone
- **D.** Skip hygiene if no dirt can be seen

<details><summary>Sahi answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct answer: B. Wash with soap and water**

- **A.** Dispenser repair ka wait karne se required hand hygiene delay hogi aur transmission ka risk rahega.
- **B.** WHO ke according jab alcohol handrub available na ho toh soap aur water se handwashing kar sakte hain. Isse indicated cleaning ko skip nahi karna padta.
- **C.** Sterile gauze khud clean ho sakti hai, lekin dry wiping proper handwashing nahi hai.
- **D.** Germs naked eye se nahi dikhte, isliye visible dirt na hona hand hygiene skip karne ka reason nahi hai.

**Exam clue:** ABHR available na ho toh indicated hand hygiene ke liye soap aur water use karo.

**Reference:** [WHO consensus recommendations, sections 1–2](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/NBK144035/) (retrieved 28 November 2026).

</details>

### Q3. What is the initial step in routine soap-and-water handwashing before applying liquid soap?

**Difficulty:** Easy | **Priority:** High

- **A.** Dry the palms with a towel
- **B.** Put on sterile gloves
- **C.** Wet the hands with water
- **D.** Apply hand lotion

<details><summary>Sahi answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct answer: C. Wet the hands with water**

- **A.** Towel se drying washing ke end mein hoti hai, jab soap rinse ho chuka hota hai.
- **B.** Gloves se bare skin par soap properly apply nahi ho payega. Routine washing gloves ke bina ki jati hai.
- **C.** Pehle hands wet karne se soap ko skin par spread karna easy hota hai. Phir soap apply karke sab surfaces rub aur rinse karte hain.
- **D.** Lotion skin care ke liye useful ho sakta hai, lekin washing ka first step nahi hai.

**Exam clue:** Wet, soap, rub, rinse aur dry ka order yaad rakho.

**Reference:** [CDC clinical hand hygiene](https://www.cdc.gov/clean-hands/hcp/clinical-safety/) (retrieved 28 November 2026).

</details>

### Q4. A trainee mixes liquid soap and alcohol handrub together in the palm for routine hand hygiene. What should the trainer explain?

**Difficulty:** Easy | **Priority:** High

- **A.** Mixing always doubles germ killing
- **B.** Mixing is needed for every hand hygiene opportunity
- **C.** Mixing produces a sterile glove barrier
- **D.** Use the indicated method separately instead of mixing the products

<details><summary>Sahi answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct answer: D. Use the indicated method separately instead of mixing the products**

- **A.** Do products mix karne se antimicrobial activity automatically double nahi hoti. Formulation ki performance change ho sakti hai.
- **B.** Har opportunity par dono products mix karna WHO ka recommended routine method nahi hai.
- **C.** Soap aur handrub ka mixture glove jaisa physical barrier create nahi karta.
- **D.** WHO soap aur alcohol handrub ko ek saath use na karne ki recommendation deta hai. Clinical indication ke hisaab se suitable method choose karo.

**Exam clue:** Routine hand hygiene mein soap aur handrub ko ek saath mix mat karo.

**Reference:** [WHO consensus recommendations, sections 1–2](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/NBK144035/) (retrieved 28 November 2026).

</details>

### Q5. What determines the amount of routine alcohol-based hand sanitizer a nurse should dispense for effective coverage?

**Difficulty:** Easy | **Priority:** High

- **A.** The product instructions and sufficient volume to cover all hand surfaces
- **B.** A fixed single drop regardless of hand size
- **C.** The colour of the sanitizer bottle
- **D.** The patient's age

<details><summary>Sahi answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct answer: A. The product instructions and sufficient volume to cover all hand surfaces**

- **A.** Product instructions ke mutabik enough volume use karna chahiye taaki sab hand surfaces adequately cover ho sakein. Bahut kam product se areas miss ho sakte hain.
- **B.** Ek fixed drop har hand size aur product ke liye enough nahi hota. Coverage important hai.
- **C.** Bottle colour volume ya effectiveness decide nahi karta.
- **D.** Patient ki age nurse ke hands par sanitizer ki required coverage ko determine nahi karti.

**Exam clue:** Handrub ka volume complete surface coverage ke liye sufficient hona chahiye.

**Reference:** [CDC clinical hand hygiene](https://www.cdc.gov/clean-hands/hcp/clinical-safety/) (retrieved 28 November 2026).

</details>

### Q6. Which historical physician linked hand decontamination with reduced puerperal fever in a Vienna maternity clinic in 1847?

**Difficulty:** Easy | **Priority:** High

- **A.** Louis Pasteur
- **B.** Ignaz Semmelweis
- **C.** Robert Koch
- **D.** Edward Jenner

<details><summary>Sahi answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct answer: B. Ignaz Semmelweis**

- **A.** Pasteur ka important contribution germ theory aur pasteurization se related hai, lekin Vienna maternity intervention Semmelweis ka tha.
- **B.** Semmelweis ne doctors ke contaminated hands aur maternal fever ka link observe kiya. Unki historical hand cleansing intervention ke baad maternal deaths kam hui.
- **C.** Koch specific microorganisms aur diseases ke relationship ki research ke liye famous hain, lekin yeh intervention unka nahi tha.
- **D.** Jenner smallpox vaccination ke historical development se associated hain, na ki Vienna maternity hand hygiene intervention se.

**Exam clue:** Semmelweis ko puerperal fever prevention aur hand hygiene history se associate karo. Historical chemical use modern practice recommendation nahi hai.

**Reference:** [WHO historical perspective](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/NBK144018/) (retrieved 28 November 2026).

</details>
