### Q1. A nurse checks a patient's pulse, then prepares to palpate another patient's abdomen. Which action is required between these contacts?

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Priority:** High

- **A.** Perform hand hygiene
- **B.** Change only the stethoscope cover
- **C.** Wear gloves over the unclean hands
- **D.** Clean hands only at the end of rounds

Hand hygiene improve karne ke liye supply, knowledge, reminders, feedback aur safety culture together address karna useful hai. WHO multimodal strategy isi approach ko support karti hai.

**Baaki options kyun sahi nahi hain?** Ye sterilization ya antibiotic program nahi hai. Sirf product procurement se zyada components present hain.

### Q40. A nurse cleans hands before and after a patient contact, but repeatedly touches the keyboard during an aseptic dressing change. Which improvement is most important?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Priority:** High

- **A.** Finish the dressing without changing contaminated gloves
- **B.** Clean the keyboard only at the end of the week
- **C.** Avoid contaminating the clean field, repeat hand hygiene and replace contaminated supplies as needed
- **D.** Count the initial and final hand hygiene as sufficient

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: C. Avoid contaminating the clean field, repeat hand hygiene and replace contaminated supplies as needed**

Aseptic task ke beech contamination ho toh immediate correction chahiye. Nurse hands, gloves aur sterile supplies ki integrity restore karti hai.

**Baaki options kyun sahi nahi hain?** Start-end compliance alone midprocedure contamination prevent nahi karti. Delayed equipment cleaning bhi insufficient hai.

</details>
