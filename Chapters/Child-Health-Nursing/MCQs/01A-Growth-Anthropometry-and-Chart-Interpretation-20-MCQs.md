### Q1. An 18-month-old's length was recorded standing at the clinic. Which action best improves comparability with the age-appropriate WHO length chart?

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Priority:** 🟠 Important | **Topic:** Growth anthropometry | **Type:** Original

- **B.** Estimate length from the mother's height
- **C.** Measure recumbent length with a length board
- **D.** Plot the standing measurement on a weight-for-age chart

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

- **C.** Is age mein length board par bachche ko lita kar naapna standard technique hai. Sahi position se chart par comparison reliable banta hai.
