### Q1. An infant with a long hospital stay is repeatedly comforted when hungry or distressed. Which Erikson developmental task does this nursing approach primarily support?

**Difficulty:** Easy | **Priority:** 🟠 Important | **Topic:** Developmental theories and play | **Type:** Original

- **A.** Autonomy versus shame and doubt
- **B.** Industry versus inferiority
- **C.** Identity versus role confusion
- **D.** Trust versus mistrust

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: D. Trust versus mistrust**

- **A.** Autonomy ka matlab apne chhote decisions khud lena hai. Yeh toddler stage ka main theoretical task hai. Infant ki hunger aur distress ka consistent response independence se pehle security ko support karta hai.
- **B.** Industry ka matlab useful skills seekhkar competence feel karna hai. School-age child schoolwork aur practical tasks se yeh experience le sakta hai. Infant ko reliable feeding aur comfort dena is task ka direct example nahi hai.
- **C.** Identity mein adolescent apne interests, values aur future role ko explore karta hai. Infant ke basic comfort ki care se yeh adolescent task directly assess nahi hota.
- **D.** Erikson ke model mein infant baar-baar experience karta hai ki jab use bhookh lagti hai ya woh distressed hota hai, toh caregiver uski need samajhkar respond karta hai. Is predictable care se security aur basic trust ko support milta hai. Isko future personality ka guaranteed outcome nahi samajhna chahiye.

**Exam clue:** Infant ki basic needs ka predictable response Erikson ke trust task se juda hai.

### Q2. A stable two-year-old refuses to put on the hospital gown. Which intervention best supports Erikson's autonomy task while maintaining safety?

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Priority:** 🔴 High | **Topic:** Developmental theories and play | **Type:** Original

- **A.** Ask the parent to dress the child without participation every time
- **B.** Offer a choice of two acceptable gowns and let the child attempt dressing
- **C.** Make the child choose whether clinically essential treatment is given
- **D.** Reward compliance by withholding comfort after refusal

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: B. Offer a choice of two acceptable gowns and let the child attempt dressing**

- **A.** Parent ki help zaroori ho sakti hai jab child tired ho ya movement restricted ho. Lekin har baar bina participation dressing karne se toddler ko apne kapde choose aur manage karne ki practice kam milti hai.
- **B.** Toddler ko do suitable gowns dikhane se decision manageable rehta hai. Khud dressing try karne do aur zaroorat par help do. Isse child control aur independence experience karta hai, while nurse safety aur treatment needs maintain karti hai.
- **C.** Essential treatment ko aise present karna jaise child usse freely cancel kar sakta hai, false choice create karta hai. Age-appropriate explanation, assent aur legitimate choices jaise position ya gown offer karo, aur consent rules follow karo.
- **D.** Refusal ke baad comfort withdraw karne se child shame ya fear experience kar sakta hai. Nurse ko safe boundaries maintain karte hue distress ko address karna chahiye, na ki basic comfort ko compliance ki condition banana chahiye.

**Exam clue:** Autonomy support karne ke liye safe, genuine choices do.

### Q6. A baby searches for a toy after a nurse covers it with a cloth. In Piaget's theory, which developing concept explains this behavior?

**Difficulty:** Easy | **Priority:** 🟠 Important | **Topic:** Developmental theories and play | **Type:** Original

- **A.** Conservation of mass
- **B.** Hypothetical deduction
- **C.** Object permanence
- **D.** Class inclusion

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: C. Object permanence**

- **A.** Conservation ka matlab shape ya container badalne ke bawajood quantity same samajhna hai. Is case mein toy ki quantity change nahi hui, balki toy nazar se chhupa diya gaya hai.
- **B.** Hypothetical deduction mein person imaginary situation se logical consequence nikalta hai. Infant ka hidden toy search karna concrete object ke existence ko recognise karna hai, abstract reasoning nahi.
- **C.** Object permanence ka matlab yeh samajhna hai ki cheez nazar na aaye tab bhi woh exist kar sakti hai. Cloth ke neeche toy search karna is emerging understanding ko show karta hai. Piaget ke sensorimotor model mein yeh important concept hai, lekin ek task se overall development diagnose nahi hota.
- **D.** Class inclusion mein child samajhta hai ki subgroup larger category ka hissa hai, jaise roses flowers ka hissa hain. Toy chhupne ke baad search karna category relationship ka assessment nahi hai.

**Exam clue:** Hidden toy ko search karna object permanence ka classic example hai.
