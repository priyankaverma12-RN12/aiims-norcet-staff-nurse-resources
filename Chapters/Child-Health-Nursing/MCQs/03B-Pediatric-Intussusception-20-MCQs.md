### Q1. Which anatomical change defines ileocolic intussusception in an infant?

**Difficulty:** Easy | **Priority:** 🟠 Important | **Topic:** Pediatric intussusception | **Type:** Original

- **A.** The pyloric muscle thickens and obstructs the gastric outlet
- **B.** The sigmoid colon twists about its mesenteric axis
- **C.** The distal colon lacks enteric ganglion cells
- **D.** A segment of ileum invaginates into the downstream colon through the ileocecal valve

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: D. A segment of ileum invaginates into the downstream colon through the ileocecal valve**

- **A.** Pyloric muscle hypertrophy gastric outlet obstruction cause karti hai. Ismein bowel segment doosre bowel lumen mein telescope nahi hota.
- **B.** Mesenteric axis ke around bowel twisting volvulus ka mechanism hai. Intussusception mein invagination hoti hai, twisting primary defining change nahi hai.
- **C.** Absent enteric ganglion cells Hirschsprung disease ka mechanism hai. Functional obstruction ho sakta hai, lekin anatomical telescoping nahi.
- **D.** Intussusception mein bowel ka ek segment agle segment ke lumen ke andar telescope ho jaata hai. Ileocolic form mein ileum ileocecal valve se colon mein enter karta hai, jisse obstruction aur vascular compromise ho sakte hain.

**Exam clue:** Ileocolic intussusception is telescoping across the ileocecal junction.

### Q2. Why can an initially uncomplicated intussusception progress to bowel ischemia if reduction is delayed?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Priority:** 🔴 High | **Topic:** Pediatric intussusception | **Type:** Original

- **A.** The proximal stomach loses all acid secretion, causing diffuse mucosal injury
- **B.** Mesenteric vessels are drawn into the invaginated segment, impairing venous drainage and eventually arterial perfusion
- **C.** The colon initially loses its enteric ganglion cells after the episode begins
- **D.** The appendix becomes obstructed independently of the telescoped bowel

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: B. Mesenteric vessels are drawn into the invaginated segment, impairing venous drainage and eventually arterial perfusion**

- **A.** Acid secretion gastric physiology se related hai. Intussusception mein ischemia ka direct mechanism trapped bowel ki vascular compression hai, acid loss nahi.
- **B.** Invaginated segment ke saath mesentery bhi compress ho sakti hai. Venous return impair hone se congestion aur edema badhte hain, aur ongoing compression arterial perfusion compromise kar sakti hai. Isliye timely reduction or surgery important hai.
- **C.** Congenital aganglionosis Hirschsprung disease mein present hoti hai. Acute telescoping ke baad ganglion cells initially absent ho jaana described vascular mechanism nahi hai.
- **D.** Appendiceal obstruction appendicitis ka mechanism ho sakta hai. Intussusception ka ischemic risk invaginated bowel aur its mesenteric supply se directly related hai.

**Exam clue:** Obstruction plus mesenteric vascular compromise explains progression to ischemia.
