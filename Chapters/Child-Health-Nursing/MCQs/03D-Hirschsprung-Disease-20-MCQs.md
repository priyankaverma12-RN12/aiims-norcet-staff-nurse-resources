### Q1. What is the defining histological abnormality in Hirschsprung disease?

**Difficulty:** Easy | **Priority:** 🟠 Important | **Topic:** Hirschsprung disease | **Type:** Original

- **A.** Hypertrophy of the pyloric circular muscle
- **B.** Inflammation confined to the appendiceal lumen
- **C.** Absence of enteric ganglion cells in an affected distal bowel segment
- **D.** Telescoping of one normally innervated bowel segment into another

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: C. Absence of enteric ganglion cells in an affected distal bowel segment**

- **A.** Pyloric muscle hypertrophy stomach outlet narrowing cause karti hai. Hirschsprung disease distal intestinal innervation ka disorder hai.
- **B.** Appendiceal inflammation appendicitis ka feature hai. Congenital lack of bowel ganglion cells is condition ka defining abnormality hai.
- **C.** Enteric ganglion cells intestinal movement coordinate karte hain. Hirschsprung disease mein affected bowel mein ye cells congenitally absent hote hain, jisse functional obstruction develop ho sakti hai.
- **D.** Bowel telescoping intussusception ka mechanism hai. Hirschsprung mein motility problem aganglionic segment ki wajah se hoti hai.

**Exam clue:** Aganglionosis is the defining abnormality.

### Q2. Why does bowel proximal to the aganglionic segment become dilated in untreated Hirschsprung disease?

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Priority:** 🟠 Important | **Topic:** Hirschsprung disease | **Type:** Original

- **A.** The poorly relaxing distal segment obstructs passage, causing accumulation upstream
- **B.** Ganglion cells in the proximal bowel are always absent too
- **C.** The distal segment actively pumps stool faster than the proximal bowel
- **D.** The gastric outlet is the primary site of obstruction

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: A. The poorly relaxing distal segment obstructs passage, causing accumulation upstream**

- **A.** Aganglionic segment coordinated relaxation aur propulsion effectively nahi kar pata. Stool aur gas usse pehle accumulate hote hain, jisse proximal bowel distend ho sakta hai.
- **B.** Proximal dilated bowel usually ganglionated ho sakta hai. Dilation alone usmein ganglion cells absent hone ka proof nahi hai.
- **C.** Distal segment mein effective passage impaired hai. Faster distal pumping proximal accumulation ka mechanism explain nahi karta.
- **D.** Gastric outlet obstruction pyloric stenosis mein hoti hai. Hirschsprung ka functional obstruction affected distal intestine mein hai.

**Exam clue:** The narrow dysfunctional segment and dilated proximal bowel are different parts.
