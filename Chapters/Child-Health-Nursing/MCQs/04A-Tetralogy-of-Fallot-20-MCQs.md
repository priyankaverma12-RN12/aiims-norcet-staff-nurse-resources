### Q1. Which combination constitutes the four anatomical findings of tetralogy of Fallot?

**Difficulty:** Easy | **Priority:** 🟠 Important | **Topic:** Tetralogy of Fallot | **Type:** Original

- **A.** Atrial septal defect, mitral stenosis, overriding aorta and left ventricular hypertrophy
- **B.** Patent ductus arteriosus, coarctation, pulmonary venous obstruction and atrial dilation
- **C.** Ventricular septal defect, overriding aorta, right ventricular outflow obstruction and right ventricular hypertrophy
- **D.** Transposed great arteries, intact septum, aortic stenosis and left ventricular hypertrophy

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: C. Ventricular septal defect, overriding aorta, right ventricular outflow obstruction and right ventricular hypertrophy**

- **A.** ASD atrial opening hai, jabki TOF ka defining septal defect ventricular hota hai. Mitral stenosis aur LV hypertrophy classical four findings nahi hain.
- **B.** PDA aur coarctation separate congenital lesions hain. Pulmonary venous obstruction bhi TOF ki defining tetrad mein nahi aati.
- **C.** TOF mein ventricular septum ka defect, aorta ka septum par override karna, RV se pulmonary artery ki taraf obstruction aur RV muscle thickening milte hain. Yeh combination pulmonary blood flow aur systemic oxygenation ko affect karta hai.
- **D.** Transposition mein great arteries ki connections abnormal hoti hain. TOF mein overriding aorta aur RV outflow obstruction ka combination important hai.

**Exam clue:** TOF ki tetrad mein VSD aur RV outflow obstruction zaroor yaad rakho.

### Q2. Two infants have similarly large malalignment VSDs in tetralogy of Fallot, but one is markedly cyanotic at rest. Which anatomical difference most directly explains the different oxygenation?

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Priority:** 🟠 Important | **Topic:** Tetralogy of Fallot | **Type:** Original

- **A.** Greater severity of right ventricular outflow tract obstruction
- **B.** A larger left atrial appendage
- **C.** A wider inferior vena cava
- **D.** A slightly thicker mitral valve leaflet without obstruction

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: A. Greater severity of right ventricular outflow tract obstruction**

- **A.** RV outflow zyada narrow ho toh lungs tak blood kam pahunchta hai aur VSD ke through deoxygenated blood systemic circulation mein zyada ja sakta hai. Isi liye same VSD size ke bawajood cyanosis differ kar sakti hai.
- **B.** Left atrial appendage ka size pulmonary outflow obstruction ko directly determine nahi karta. Question mein main comparison TOF physiology ka hai.
- **C.** IVC systemic venous blood return karti hai. Uska diameter alone shunt direction aur severe cyanosis explain nahi karta.
- **D.** Nonobstructive mitral leaflet change TOF mein reduced pulmonary flow ka primary determinant nahi hai. RV outflow severity zyada relevant hai.

**Exam clue:** Severity of RVOTO determines much of the clinical phenotype.

### Q3. An infant's echocardiogram shows a large VSD and relatively mild pulmonary outflow narrowing in tetralogy of Fallot. The infant has little visible cyanosis. Which interpretation is physiologically possible?

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: D. Net left-to-right flow may occur when outflow obstruction is mild**

- **A.** Shunt direction relative resistance aur pressure conditions se change hoti hai. VSD alone fixed right-to-left flow prove nahi karta.

- **C.** Ductal dependence severe obstruction ke selected infants mein concern hai. Mild obstruction wale har child mein pulmonary flow entirely ductus se aana necessary nahi.
- **D.** Mild RV outflow obstruction mein lungs ka pathway relatively accessible ho sakta hai aur net flow left-to-right bhi ho sakta hai. TOF anatomy hone ka matlab har time severe cyanosis hona zaroori nahi.

**Exam clue:** Pink TOF can occur with mild outflow obstruction.

### Q4. During an acute cyanotic episode in unrepaired tetralogy of Fallot, the previously prominent systolic ejection murmur becomes faint while oxygen saturation falls. What does this change suggest?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Priority:** 🔴 High | **Topic:** Tetralogy of Fallot | **Type:** Original

- **A.** Successful spontaneous correction of the VSD
- **B.** Reduced flow through the obstructed right ventricular outflow tract
- **C.** Improvement in pulmonary perfusion because the murmur is quieter
- **D.** A new increase in left-to-right flow with normal oxygenation

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: B. Reduced flow through the obstructed right ventricular outflow tract**

- **A.** VSD ka spontaneous closure auscultation ke is acute pattern se establish nahi hota. TOF ka murmur mainly pulmonary outflow se related hai.
- **B.** Murmur blood ke narrowed pulmonary outflow se passage par depend karta hai. Severe obstruction mein flow kam ho sakta hai aur murmur soft ho jaata hai even as cyanosis worsens. Yeh reassuring finding nahi hai.
- **C.** Quieter murmur ko automatically improvement mat samjho. Falling saturation ke saath kam pulmonary flow serious concern hai.
- **D.** Oxygenation fall aur quieter outflow murmur reduced pulmonary flow se compatible hain. Normal oxygenation wala interpretation clinical evidence contradict karta hai.

**Exam clue:** A softer murmur during deterioration can mean less pulmonary flow.

### Q10. A child with unrepaired tetralogy of Fallot develops a cyanotic spell after vomiting and poor fluid intake. Perfusion is reduced and dehydration is suspected. Which mechanism makes volume assessment particularly relevant?

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

### Q19. In a child with unrepaired tetralogy of Fallot, oxygen administration improves the inspired oxygen concentration but arterial saturation remains below normal. Which explanation is most appropriate?

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Priority:** 🔴 High | **Topic:** Tetralogy of Fallot | **Type:** Original

- **A.** Some venous blood bypasses pulmonary oxygenation through the right-to-left intracardiac shunt

- **C.** An oxygen cylinder directly corrects the ventricular septal defect

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

- **D.** Low arterial saturation many mechanisms se ho sakti hai. Intracardiac shunt mein functioning alveoli ke bawajood systemic hypoxemia persist ho sakti hai.

### Q20. A child with repaired tetralogy of Fallot is advised to continue congenital cardiology follow-up despite having no current symptoms. Which late issue provides a disease-specific reason for surveillance?

- **D.** A guaranteed absence of future cardiac rhythm abnormalities

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: C. Pulmonary valve regurgitation with progressive right ventricular dilation**

- **D.** Repair future rhythm problems eliminate nahi karti. Asymptomatic child mein bhi periodic follow-up se residual lesions aur rhythm concerns detect ho sakte hain.

**Exam clue:** Repaired TOF still requires long-term right ventricular and valve surveillance.
