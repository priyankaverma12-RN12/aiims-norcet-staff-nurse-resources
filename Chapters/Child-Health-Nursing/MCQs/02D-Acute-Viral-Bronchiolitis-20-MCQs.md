### Q1. In acute bronchiolitis, which pathological change most directly explains the wheezing and air trapping?

**Difficulty:** Easy | **Priority:** 🟠 Important | **Topic:** Acute viral bronchiolitis | **Type:** Original

- **A.** Isolated inflammation of the vocal cords
- **B.** Fluid accumulation only in the pleural cavity
- **C.** Inflammation, edema and mucus within the small bronchioles
- **D.** Isolated inflammation of the epiglottis

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: C. Inflammation, edema and mucus within the small bronchioles**

- **A.** Vocal cords hamare upper airway mein hote hain. Jab inmein swelling hoti hai, toh awaaz bhaari ho sakti hai ya stridor sunai de sakta hai. Bronchiolitis mein dikkat vocal cords ke bajay lungs ke andar ki chhoti airways mein hoti hai.

- **B.** Pleural cavity lungs aur chest wall ke beech ki jagah hai. Jab ismein fluid jama hota hai, toh lung ko expand karne mein dikkat ho sakti hai. Lekin isse bronchiolitis mein bronchioles ke andar hone wali blockage explain nahi hoti.

- **C.** Bronchioles lungs ke andar hawa le jaane wali bahut chhoti tubes hain. Viral infection ki wajah se inki lining mein swelling hoti hai aur mucus aur damaged cells jama ho sakte hain. Bachche ko hawa bahar nikalne mein zyada dikkat ho sakti hai, isliye wheeze aur air trapping hoti hai.

- **D.** Epiglottis throat mein hota hai aur swallowing ke waqt airway ko protect karta hai. Iski swelling upper airway ko obstruct kar sakti hai. Bronchiolitis mein primary blockage chhoti lower airways ke andar hoti hai.

**Exam clue:** Yaad rakho ki bronchiolitis mein chhoti airways ki swelling aur mucus ki wajah se hawa ka raasta tang ho jaata hai.

**Reference:** [RCH clinical guideline: bronchiolitis](https://www.rch.org.au/clinicalguide/guideline_index/Bronchiolitis/). [MSD Manual: bronchiolitis](https://www.msdmanuals.com/professional/pediatrics/respiratory-disorders-in-young-children/bronchiolitis).

### Q2. Which pathogen is most commonly associated with seasonal acute bronchiolitis in infants?

**Difficulty:** Easy | **Priority:** 🟠 Important | **Topic:** Acute viral bronchiolitis | **Type:** Original

- **A.** Respiratory syncytial virus
- **B.** Human metapneumovirus
- **C.** Human rhinovirus
- **D.** Parainfluenza virus

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: A. Respiratory syncytial virus**

- **A.** RSV ka full form respiratory syncytial virus hai. Yeh infants mein bronchiolitis ka sabse common classic viral cause hai. Har bachche mein RSV hi ho, aisa zaroori nahi hai.

- **B.** Human metapneumovirus bhi lungs ki chhoti airways ko infect kar sakta hai. Isse milte-julte symptoms aa sakte hain, lekin seasonal infant bronchiolitis ke commonest classic cause ke liye RSV better answer hai.

- **C.** Rhinovirus aksar common cold karta hai, lekin infants mein bronchiolitis bhi kar sakta hai. Isliye yeh possible cause hai, par given question mein RSV leading classic association hai.

- **D.** Parainfluenza virus bhi respiratory infection karta hai aur croup ke saath iska classic association hai. Bronchiolitis bhi ho sakti hai, lekin RSV is question ka best answer hai.

**Exam clue:** RSV ko commonest classic cause samjho, lekin ek negative RSV test se bronchiolitis ko exclude mat karo.

**Reference:** [RCH clinical guideline: bronchiolitis](https://www.rch.org.au/clinicalguide/guideline_index/Bronchiolitis/). [MSD Manual: bronchiolitis](https://www.msdmanuals.com/professional/pediatrics/respiratory-disorders-in-young-children/bronchiolitis).

### Q3. A 5-month-old develops rhinorrhea, followed by cough, diffuse fine crackles and wheeze over two days. Which diagnosis is most consistent with this sequence?

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Priority:** 🟠 Important | **Topic:** Acute viral bronchiolitis | **Type:** Original

- **A.** Isolated viral croup
- **B.** Uncomplicated otitis externa
- **C.** Isolated allergic rhinitis
- **D.** Acute viral bronchiolitis

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: D. Acute viral bronchiolitis**

- **A.** Croup mein upper airway ke aas-paas swelling hoti hai. Isliye barking cough, bhaari awaaz aur inspiratory stridor zyada expected hote hain. Yahan lungs mein diffuse crackles aur wheeze lower-airway illness ko support karte hain.

- **B.** Otitis externa ka matlab outer ear canal mein inflammation hai. Isse ear pain ho sakta hai, lekin yeh dono lungs mein wheeze aur crackles ka reason nahi batata.

- **C.** Allergic rhinitis mein naak beh sakti hai ya chheenk aa sakti hai. Lekin is chhote infant mein acute cold ke baad diffuse lower-airway findings ko isolated nasal allergy se explain karna theek nahi hai.

- **D.** Pehle naak behna aur phir cough, wheeze aur fine crackles aana infant bronchiolitis ka typical pattern hai. Viral infection chhoti airways tak pahunchne par breathing difficult ho sakti hai. Nurse ko breathing effort, oxygenation aur feeding bhi assess karni chahiye.

**Exam clue:** Jab cold ke baad infant mein diffuse wheeze aur crackles aayein, toh bronchiolitis ko consider karo aur severity alag se assess karo.

**Reference:** [RCH clinical guideline: bronchiolitis](https://www.rch.org.au/clinicalguide/guideline_index/Bronchiolitis/).

### Q4. A 5-week-old infant with mild coryza has recurrent witnessed pauses in breathing but little audible wheeze. Which interpretation is most appropriate?

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Priority:** 🔴 High | **Topic:** Acute viral bronchiolitis | **Type:** Original

- **D.** The findings confirm asthma without further assessment

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: B. Apnea can be a presentation of bronchiolitis in very young infants and warrants urgent assessment**

- **A.** Wheeze har infant mein sunai dena zaroori nahi hai. Bahut chhote bachche mein breathing pauses pehle notice ho sakte hain. Isliye sirf wheeze absent hone par bronchiolitis ko exclude nahi kar sakte.

- **B.** Apnea ka matlab saans rukne ka episode hai. Bahut chhote infants mein bronchiolitis kabhi prominent wheeze se pehle apnea ke saath present ho sakti hai. Recurrent witnessed pauses par urgent pediatric assessment aur zaroorat ke hisaab se emergency support chahiye. Serious alternative causes ko bhi evaluate karna hai.

- **C.** Har viral cold mein apnea normal nahi hoti. Recurrent pauses se oxygenation aur circulation par asar pad sakta hai. Isliye caregiver ko ghar par sirf wait karne ke liye kehna unsafe hoga.

- **D.** Asthma ka diagnosis sirf is history se confirm nahi hota. Is age mein recurrent apnea ke multiple serious causes ho sakte hain, isliye appropriate assessment pehle zaroori hai.

**Exam clue:** Bahut chhote infant mein apnea ko urgent warning sign samjho, chahe wheeze kam sunai de.

### Q5. An infant born at 28 weeks' gestation has suspected bronchiolitis. Which history is particularly relevant when assessing risk of severe disease?

- **C.** Bottle ka nipple type feeding technique samajhne mein madad kar sakta hai, lekin isse prematurity ya chronic lung disease ka respiratory risk pata nahi chalta. Nurse ko current intake aur safe feeding alag se assess karni chahiye.

- **D.** Pehle hua uncomplicated mild jaundice jo poori tarah resolve ho gaya ho, woh akela RCH ke major bronchiolitis severity risk factors mein nahi aata. Current cardiopulmonary history aur infant ki age par dhyan dena zyada relevant hai.

### Q6. In the RCH bronchiolitis guideline, an otherwise healthy infant has a brief self-resolving SpO2 dip to 88% while asleep, followed by sustained readings of 94% with comfortable breathing. What is the best approach?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Priority:** 🔴 High | **Topic:** Acute viral bronchiolitis | **Type:** Original

- **A.** Start high-flow oxygen solely because of one brief dip
- **B.** Discharge immediately without considering feeding or other findings
- **C.** Treat the brief dip with routine nebulized adrenaline
- **D.** Assess the child and trend; brief isolated dips alone do not justify oxygen

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: D. Assess the child and trend; brief isolated dips alone do not justify oxygen**

- **A.** High-flow oxygen tab consider ki jaati hai jab bachche ko clinically uski zaroorat ho. Sirf neend mein ek chhota dip aane se ye pata nahi chalta ki usual oxygen support fail ho gaya hai.

- **B.** Oxygen ki reading dobara normal hona achhi baat hai, lekin discharge se pehle nurse ko saans ki mehnat, feeding, age aur apnea ka risk bhi check karna hai. Sirf ek reading se discharge decide nahi karte.

- **C.** Adrenaline blood vessels aur airway par effect karti hai, lekin typical bronchiolitis mein routine nebulized adrenaline se aise short dip ko treat nahi karte. Pehle reading aur bachche ki condition assess karo.

- **D.** Kabhi-kabhi bronchiolitis mein oxygen saturation thodi der girkar khud normal ho sakti hai. RCH ke hisaab se akele brief dip ki wajah se oxygen shuru nahi karte. Nurse reading ki reliability, trend, saans lene ki mehnat, feeding aur apnea ka risk dekhti hai. Agar saturation lagatar target se neeche rahe ya bachcha deteriorate kare, toh urgent reassessment karo.

**Exam clue:** Ek brief dip aur lagatar hypoxemia ko alag samjho. Bachche ki age aur clinical condition bhi dekho.

**Reference:** [RCH clinical guideline: bronchiolitis](https://www.rch.org.au/clinicalguide/guideline_index/Bronchiolitis/). [PREDICT Australasian bronchiolitis guideline 2025](https://predict.org.au/bronchiolitis-guideline/).

### Q7. Under the RCH bronchiolitis guideline, an otherwise healthy 4-month-old infant without a separate individualized oxygen target has repeatedly sustained SpO2 readings below 90%. Which intervention should the nurse anticipate?

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Priority:** 🔴 High | **Topic:** Acute viral bronchiolitis | **Type:** Original

- **A.** No oxygen until cyanosis is visible
- **B.** Routine oral corticosteroid instead of oxygen
- **C.** Prescribed supplemental oxygen with reassessment
- **D.** Chest physiotherapy as the sole correction of hypoxemia

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: C. Prescribed supplemental oxygen with reassessment**

- **A.** Cyanosis ka matlab skin ya lips ka neela dikhna hai. Ye oxygen ki kami ka late ya unreliable sign ho sakta hai, isliye nurse ko cyanosis ka wait nahi karna chahiye jab reliable readings lagatar low hain.

- **B.** Corticosteroids inflammation par kaam karte hain, lekin typical bronchiolitis mein routine steroids recommended nahi hain. Ye blood mein oxygen ki kami ko correct karne ke liye oxygen support ka substitute bhi nahi hain.

- **C.** Jab bachche ki oxygen saturation lagatar target se neeche rehti hai, toh blood mein oxygen ki kami ho sakti hai. RCH ke usual pathway mein persistent SpO2 90% se neeche ho toh prescribed oxygen di jaati hai. Nurse saturation, saans ki mehnat aur alertness dobara check karti hai. PREDICT 2025 mein otherwise healthy 6 weeks ya usse bade infants ke liye 90% threshold hai, lekin 6 weeks se chhote ya 12 months se chhote underlying disease wale infants ke liye 92% threshold diya gaya hai. Individual cardiopulmonary plan aur local protocol ko follow karo.

- **D.** Chest physiotherapy ka goal kuch conditions mein secretions mobilize karna hota hai. Typical bronchiolitis mein ye routine treatment nahi hai aur persistent hypoxemia ko oxygen support ke bina correct karne ka reliable tareeka nahi hai.

**Exam clue:** Is 4-month-old healthy infant mein named RCH threshold apply hota hai. Chhote ya high-risk infants mein doosre guidelines ke targets alag ho sakte hain.

### Q10. A bronchiolitis patient cannot safely receive enteral fluids and the team prescribes IV maintenance fluid. Which formulation is consistent with the RCH guideline's usual approach?

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Priority:** 🔴 High | **Topic:** Acute viral bronchiolitis | **Type:** Original

- **B.** A markedly hypotonic solution without monitoring
- **C.** An isotonic glucose-containing solution with prescribed electrolyte monitoring
- **D.** Concentrated glucose alone for all maintenance needs

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: C. An isotonic glucose-containing solution with prescribed electrolyte monitoring**

- **A.** Sterile water mein zaroori electrolytes nahi hote aur ise akela IV infusion dena unsafe hai. Iski bahut kam tonicity cells aur blood ko harm kar sakti hai. Nurse ko aisi prescription ko verify aur escalate karna chahiye.

- **C.** RCH guideline mein jab enteral hydration possible nahi hoti, toh isotonic fluid mein glucose dene ka usual approach hai. Isotonic fluid sodium dilution ke risk ko kam karne mein madad karta hai, lekin risk poori tarah khatam nahi hota. Rate, glucose, electrolytes aur monitoring treating team ke prescribed plan se decide hote hain.

- **D.** Concentrated glucose akela routine maintenance ke liye required water aur electrolytes ka balanced replacement nahi hai. Glucose ki special concentration ka use specific indications aur prescription par depend karta hai.
