### Q1. In acute bronchiolitis, which pathological change most directly explains the wheezing and air trapping?

**Difficulty:** Easy | **Priority:** Important

- **A.** Isolated inflammation of the vocal cords
- **B.** Fluid accumulation only in the pleural cavity
- **C.** Inflammation, edema and mucus within the small bronchioles
- **D.** Isolated inflammation of the epiglottis

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: C. Inflammation, edema and mucus within the small bronchioles**

**A.** Vocal cord inflammation hoarseness aur upper-airway symptoms de sakti hai. Bronchiolitis ka primary site small lower airways hai.

**B.** Pleural fluid lung expansion restrict karta hai. Yeh bronchiolitis ke small-airway obstruction ka mechanism nahi hai.

**C.** Bronchiolitis mein tiny bronchioles swollen ho jaate hain aur mucus lumen ko narrow karta hai. Expiration ke waqt airflow aur restrict ho sakta hai, jis se wheeze aur air trapping hoti hai.

**D.** Epiglottic inflammation upper-airway problem hai. Bronchiolitis mein small bronchioles affected hote hain.

**Exam clue:** Bronchiolitis = small-airway edema and mucus.

**Reference:** [RCH clinical guideline: bronchiolitis](https://www.rch.org.au/clinicalguide/guideline_index/Bronchiolitis/).
