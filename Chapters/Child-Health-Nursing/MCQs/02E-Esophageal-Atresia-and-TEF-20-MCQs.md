### Q1. Which description defines esophageal atresia?

**Difficulty:** Easy | **Priority:** Important

- **A.** A patent esophagus with isolated narrowing of the pylorus
- **B.** Congenital absence of the entire trachea
- **C.** A defect in the diaphragm allowing abdominal organs into the chest
- **D.** Congenital interruption of the esophageal lumen, commonly forming a blind upper pouch

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: D. Congenital interruption of the esophageal lumen, commonly forming a blind upper pouch**

**A.** Pyloric narrowing stomach outlet problem hai. Esophagus continuous reh sakta hai, so this is a distinct anomaly.

**B.** Tracheal agenesis airway malformation hai. Esophageal atresia specifically esophageal continuity ko affect karti hai.

**C.** Diaphragmatic hernia diaphragm defect hai, not an interruption of esophageal lumen.

**D.** Esophageal atresia mein food pipe ki continuity interrupted hoti hai aur upper segment blind pouch mein end kar sakta hai. Isliye swallowed saliva or milk stomach tak normally nahi pahunchte.

**Exam clue:** Atresia means interruption; fistula means abnormal communication.

**Reference:** [CAHS neonatal EA/TEF guideline](https://www.cahs.health.wa.gov.au/-/media/HSPs/CAHS/Documents/Health-Professionals/Neonatology-guidelines/Oesophageal-Atresia-Tracheoesophageal-Fistula.pdf); [MSD Manual: esophageal atresia](https://www.msdmanuals.com/professional/pediatrics/congenital-gastrointestinal-anomalies/esophageal-atresia).

### Q2. Which anatomical pattern is most common among the congenital esophageal atresia/tracheoesophageal fistula variants?

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Priority:** Important

- **A.** Continuous esophagus with an isolated H-type fistula
- **B.** Blind proximal esophagus with distal esophagus connected to trachea
- **C.** Pure esophageal atresia without any fistula
- **D.** Esophageal atresia with both proximal and distal fistulas

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: B. Blind proximal esophagus with distal esophagus connected to trachea**

**A.** H-type mein esophagus continuous hota hai. It is a recognized but much less common configuration.

**B.** Most common pattern Gross type C hai: proximal esophagus blind pouch mein ends aur distal esophagus trachea se abnormal connection rakhta hai. Yeh aspiration aur gastric air entry explain karta hai.

**C.** Pure atresia mein fistula absent hai. Yeh possible variant hai but type C se less common hai.

**D.** Double fistula rare variant hai. Both connections present hone se anatomy different hoti hai.

**Exam clue:** Most common: EA with distal TEF (type C).

**Reference:** [CAHS neonatal EA/TEF guideline](https://www.cahs.health.wa.gov.au/-/media/HSPs/CAHS/Documents/Health-Professionals/Neonatology-guidelines/Oesophageal-Atresia-Tracheoesophageal-Fistula.pdf); [MSD Manual: esophageal atresia](https://www.msdmanuals.com/professional/pediatrics/congenital-gastrointestinal-anomalies/esophageal-atresia).

### Q6. A radiograph shows a radiopaque enteric tube coiled in the upper esophageal pouch and air throughout the stomach and bowel. Which anatomical inference is most likely?

### Q7. In an infant with radiographically confirmed esophageal atresia, absence of gas in the stomach and bowel most directly suggests which anatomical possibility?

- **A.** A wide distal fistula allowing free passage of airway gas

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: C. No patent distal tracheoesophageal communication supplying bowel air**

**Exam clue:** Gasless abdomen suggests absence of a functioning distal fistula.

### Q18. A postoperative EA/TEF infant appears hungry but the current surgical order is no oral feeds pending reassessment. What is the most appropriate nursing response?

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Priority:** High

- **A.** Begin oral milk because hunger demonstrates anastomotic healing
- **B.** Maintain the ordered feeding restriction and clarify readiness with the surgical team
- **C.** Give a trial oral feed before the prescribed reassessment
- **D.** Withhold all ordered parenteral or tube nutrition as well

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: B. Maintain the ordered feeding restriction and clarify readiness with the surgical team**

**A.** Hunger is not a test of surgical healing. Unapproved feed aspiration or leak risk create kar sakti hai.

**B.** Hunger feeding readiness ya anastomosis healing confirm nahi karti. Oral feeding timing depends on repair, clinical status and surgeon-directed evaluation. Ordered alternative nutrition should continue as appropriate.

**C.** Trial feeding without surgical authorization could disrupt postoperative management. Clarification and reassessment safer hain.
