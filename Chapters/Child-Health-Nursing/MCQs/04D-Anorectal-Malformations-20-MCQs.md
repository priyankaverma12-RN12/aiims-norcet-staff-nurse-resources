### Q1. A newborn passes meconium through an opening on the perineal skin anterior to the expected anal site. The normal anal opening is absent. Which anatomical variant should the pediatric surgical team consider?

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Priority:** 🟠 Important | **Topic:** Pediatric anorectal malformations | **Type:** Original

- **A.** Hirschsprung disease with an anatomically normal anus
- **B.** Isolated pyloric stenosis
- **C.** Anorectal malformation with a perineal fistula
- **D.** Rectourethral fistula with meconium emerging only through the urethra

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: C. Anorectal malformation with a perineal fistula**

- **A.** Hirschsprung disease mein bowel innervation abnormal hoti hai aur anus structurally normal ho sakta hai. Visible abnormal perineal outlet separate anatomical concern hai.
- **B.** Pyloric stenosis gastric outlet disorder hai. Abnormal perineal meconium outlet ko explain nahi karti.
- **C.** Perineal fistula mein distal rectum abnormal perineal opening se communicate kar sakta hai. Stool ka exit site observe aur document karo. Exact anatomy surgeon confirm karega.
- **D.** Rectourethral communication mein stool urinary passage se emerge ho sakta hai. Stem skin opening specify karta hai.

**Exam clue:** Locate the actual meconium exit site.

**Exam clue:** Hydrocolpos can threaten upper urinary tract drainage.
