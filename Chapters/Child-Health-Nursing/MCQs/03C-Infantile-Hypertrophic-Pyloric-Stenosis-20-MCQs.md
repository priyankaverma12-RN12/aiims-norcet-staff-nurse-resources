### Q1. Which structural abnormality directly produces infantile hypertrophic pyloric stenosis?

**Difficulty:** Easy | **Priority:** 🟠 Important | **Topic:** Infantile hypertrophic pyloric stenosis | **Type:** Original

- **A.** Absence of enteric ganglion cells in the distal rectum
- **B.** Thickened pyloric muscle narrows the gastric outlet
- **C.** Invagination of ileum into the colon
- **D.** A fistulous opening between the trachea and distal esophagus

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: B. Thickened pyloric muscle narrows the gastric outlet**

- **A.** Distal bowel aganglionosis Hirschsprung disease ka mechanism hai. Ismein stomach outlet ki muscular hypertrophy primary lesion nahi hoti.
- **B.** Pylorus stomach aur duodenum ke junction par muscular opening hai. Muscle thickening se outlet narrow ho jata hai aur gastric contents ka aage passage difficult hota hai.
- **C.** Ileum ka colon mein telescope hona intussusception hai. Pyloric stenosis mein obstruction gastric outlet par muscle thickening se hota hai.
- **D.** Airway aur food pipe ki abnormal communication TEF hai. Pyloric stenosis separate gastric outlet disorder hai.

**Exam clue:** Pyloric stenosis is gastric outlet obstruction caused by muscular hypertrophy.

### Q2. Why is vomiting in typical infantile hypertrophic pyloric stenosis non-bilious?

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Priority:** 🟠 Important | **Topic:** Infantile hypertrophic pyloric stenosis | **Type:** Original

- **A.** Bile cannot be produced by a healthy young infant
- **B.** The obstruction lies distal to the terminal ileum
- **C.** The pyloric muscle normally secretes bile
- **D.** The obstruction is proximal to the point where bile enters the duodenum

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: D. The obstruction is proximal to the point where bile enters the duodenum**

- **A.** Young infant ka liver bile produce karta hai. Non-bilious vomiting ko absence of bile production se explain nahi karte.
- **B.** Terminal ileum distal small intestine mein hai. Pyloric stenosis ka obstruction much more proximal gastric outlet par hota hai.
- **C.** Pylorus muscle regulates outlet passage. Bile liver mein banti hai aur biliary tract se duodenum mein enter karti hai.
- **D.** Gastric outlet duodenum ke us region se pehle hai jahan bile enter hoti hai. Typical pyloric obstruction mein vomited gastric contents mein bile usually absent hoti hai.

**Exam clue:** Location proximal to biliary entry explains typical non-bilious emesis.

- **B.** Twelve months
- **C.** Four years

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

### Q12. Which biochemical pattern is classically associated with prolonged vomiting from hypertrophic pyloric stenosis?

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Priority:** 🔴 High | **Topic:** Infantile hypertrophic pyloric stenosis | **Type:** Original

- **A.** Hyperchloremic metabolic acidosis with elevated bicarbonate
- **B.** Respiratory acidosis caused primarily by increased gastric acid secretion
- **C.** Isolated hypernatremia as the obligatory pattern
- **D.** Hypochloremic hypokalemic metabolic alkalosis

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: D. Hypochloremic hypokalemic metabolic alkalosis**

- **A.** Hyperchloremic metabolic acidosis mein bicarbonate typically reduced hota hai, elevated nahi. Diarrheal bicarbonate loss is different mechanism from repeated gastric acid loss.
- **B.** Respiratory acidosis carbon dioxide retention se related hai. Initial pyloric vomiting ka classic disturbance metabolic acid loss se hota hai, increased gastric secretion alone se nahi.
- **C.** Sodium level hydration and intake se vary kar sakta hai. Isolated hypernatremia obligatory diagnostic pattern nahi hai.
- **D.** Repeated gastric losses mein hydrochloric acid aur chloride lose ho sakte hain, volume contraction ke saath potassium disturbance bhi develop ho sakti hai. Classical pattern low chloride, low potassium aur metabolic alkalosis hai. Early cases mein labs normal bhi ho sakte hain.

**Exam clue:** Classic prolonged pyloric vomiting causes chloride-depletion metabolic alkalosis.

### Q13. In prolonged pyloric obstruction with repeated gastric vomiting, why can potassium depletion worsen even though gastric hydrochloric acid loss is the main initiating event?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Priority:** 🔴 High | **Topic:** Infantile hypertrophic pyloric stenosis | **Type:** Original

- **A.** The pylorus directly converts serum potassium into gastric acid
- **B.** Bile secretion always removes all circulating potassium
- **C.** Volume depletion activates aldosterone-mediated renal potassium loss
- **D.** An increase in glomerular filtration alone explains every electrolyte change

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: C. Volume depletion activates aldosterone-mediated renal potassium loss**

- **A.** Pyloric muscle gastric outlet movement control karti hai, serum potassium ko acid mein convert nahi karti. Electrolyte changes GI losses aur renal compensation se related hain.
- **B.** Bile secretion potassium depletion ka stated mechanism nahi hai. Pyloric vomiting typically non-bilious hoti hai and volume-related renal responses important hain.
- **C.** Repeated vomiting circulating volume reduce kar sakti hai. Body renin-angiotensin-aldosterone system activate karti hai to retain sodium and volume, and aldosterone renal potassium excretion increase kar sakta hai. This contributes to classic hypokalemia.
- **D.** Glomerular filtration volume depletion mein change ho sakti hai, but this alone complete electrolyte pattern explain nahi karti. Aldosterone-mediated renal handling relevant mechanism hai.

**Exam clue:** Vomiting plus volume contraction can promote renal potassium loss.
