### Q1. At a two-month visit, which observation most directly assesses emerging reciprocal social interaction?

**Difficulty:** Easy | **Priority:** 🟠 Important | **Topic:** Developmental surveillance | **Type:** Original

- **A.** The infant has symmetric leg movements
- **B.** The infant smiles in response to the caregiver's smile
- **C.** The infant briefly opens a hand
- **D.** The infant lifts the head during supervised tummy time

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: B. The infant smiles in response to the caregiver's smile**

- **A.** Dono legs ka symmetric movement dekhkar nurse motor function aur possible asymmetry assess kar sakti hai. Lekin is movement se yeh pata nahi chalta ki baby caregiver ke expression ko social signal ki tarah respond kar raha hai.
- **B.** Jab caregiver smile karta hai aur baby bhi smile se jawab deta hai, toh dono ke beech early social interaction dikh raha hota hai. Isse reciprocal interaction kehte hain, yani ek insaan ka action doosre ke response se connect hota hai.
- **C.** Hand opening se finger aur hand movement observe hota hai. Yeh physical development ka part hai, lekin caregiver ke expression ka social response directly nahi batata.
- **D.** Tummy time mein head uthane se neck aur upper body ki muscles ka control assess hota hai. Isse gross motor development ka idea milta hai, reciprocal social response ka nahi.

**Exam clue:** Social interaction mein baby doosre insaan ke expression ka response deta hai.

### Q2. A caregiver reports that a two-month-old never reacts to sudden loud everyday sounds. What is the most appropriate next step?

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Priority:** 🟠 Important | **Topic:** Developmental surveillance | **Type:** Original

- **A.** Wait for the child to begin reading

- **C.** Assume the baby has normal hearing if feeding well
- **D.** Arrange assessment of auditory responsiveness and hearing

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: D. Arrange assessment of auditory responsiveness and hearing**

- **A.** Reading age tak wait karne se hearing difficulty identify karne ka important early window miss ho sakta hai. Bachcha sounds ko sun kar speech patterns seekhta hai, isliye concern present ho toh timely assessment karna chahiye.
- **B.** Visual tracking mein nurse dekhti hai ki baby aankhon se moving object follow kar pa raha hai ya nahi. Yeh hearing pathway ka test nahi hai. Normal visual tracking se auditory difficulty exclude nahi hoti.
- **C.** Feeding ability sucking aur swallowing ke coordination se related hai. Baby achhi tarah feed kar sakta hai aur phir bhi hearing difficulty ho sakti hai. Isliye feeding alone hearing status ka proof nahi hai.
- **D.** Caregiver ne repeatedly sound response absent observe kiya hai, jo further assessment ka reason hai. Nurse hearing history aur newborn screening record check karne mein help kare aur appropriate hearing assessment arrange kare. Ek observation se deafness confirm nahi hoti.

**Exam clue:** Sound response na mile toh early hearing assessment arrange karo, diagnosis assume mat karo.
