### Q1. During a pediatric ward round, the mother reports that her usually interactive child is unusually quiet, although the latest observations are within the documented range. What should the nurse do?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Priority:** 🔴 High | **Topic:** Hospitalized child and family-centered care | **Type:** Original

- **A.** Reassure the mother solely on the basis of the recorded observations
- **B.** Ask the mother to report only numerical measurements
- **C.** Take the concern seriously and reassess the child, escalating if indicated
- **D.** Wait until the next scheduled ward round before assessing

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: C. Take the concern seriously and reassess the child, escalating if indicated**

- **A.** Recorded observations reassuring ho sakti hain, lekin behavior change ko exclude nahi karti.
- **B.** Parent observation subjective hone ke bawajood valuable history hai. Numerical data uska replacement nahi hai.
- **C.** Parent baseline behavior ko achhi tarah jaanta hai. New quietness clinical change ho sakti hai, isliye reassessment aur zaroorat par escalation important hai.
- **D.** Next round tak wait karne se deterioration ka early sign miss ho sakta hai.
