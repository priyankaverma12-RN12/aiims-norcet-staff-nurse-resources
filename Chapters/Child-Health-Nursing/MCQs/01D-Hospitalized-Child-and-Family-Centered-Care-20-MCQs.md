### Q1. During a pediatric ward round, the mother reports that her usually interactive child is unusually quiet, although the latest observations are within the documented range. What should the nurse do?

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Priority:** 🔴 High | **Topic:** Hospitalized child and family-centered care | **Type:** Original

- **A.** Reassure the mother solely on the basis of the recorded observations
- **B.** Ask the mother to report only numerical measurements
- **C.** Take the concern seriously and reassess the child, escalating if indicated
- **D.** Wait until the next scheduled ward round before assessing

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: C. Take the concern seriously and reassess the child, escalating if indicated**

- **A.** Pehle ki readings us samay ki condition batati hain. Ab child usual behaviour se alag dikh raha hai, toh nurse ko nayi assessment karni chahiye. Sirf previous observations se reassure karna early deterioration miss kar sakta hai.
- **B.** Mother daily child ke expressions, activity aur responses observe karti hai. Uski report numerical reading nahi hai, lekin baseline se change identify karne mein useful hai. Nurse dono tarah ki information combine kare.
- **C.** Usual interactive child ka unusually quiet ho jaana pain, fatigue ya illness worsen hone ka sign ho sakta hai. Nurse promptly airway, breathing, circulation, alertness aur relevant findings reassess kare. Concern ya deterioration ho toh local escalation pathway activate kare.
- **D.** Agar new concern present hai, toh next scheduled round ka wait assessment delay kar sakta hai. Nurse current condition evaluate karke decide kare ki routine review enough hai ya urgent help chahiye.

**Exam clue:** Caregiver ki new concern ko normal previous readings ki wajah se ignore mat karo.

**Reference:** [Open RN planning care for the ill child](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/NBK615330/) · [RCH nursing assessment](https://www.rch.org.au/rchcpg/hospital_clinical_guideline_index/Nursing_Assessment/) · [AAP child life services](https://doi.org/10.1542/peds.2020-040261)

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Priority:** 🟠 Important | **Topic:** Hospitalized child and family-centered care | **Type:** Original

- **D.** Use an unfamiliar sedative as a substitute for the routine

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

- **D.** Sedative medicine ke apne adverse effects, contraindications aur monitoring requirements hote hain. Normal bedtime routine replace karne ke liye bina appropriate indication aur prescription sedative use nahi karna chahiye.

**Exam clue:** Home routine ke safe parts hospital care ke saath maintain karo.
