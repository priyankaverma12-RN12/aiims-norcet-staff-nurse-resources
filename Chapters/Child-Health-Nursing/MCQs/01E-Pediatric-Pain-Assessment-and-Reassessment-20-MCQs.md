### Q1. A preverbal 16-month-old is recovering after surgery. Which assessment tool is most appropriate for routine behavioral pain scoring?

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Priority:** 🟠 Important | **Topic:** Pediatric pain assessment | **Type:** Original

- **A.** Adult verbal 0–10 numerical rating requiring self-report
- **B.** FLACC scale
- **C.** Glasgow Coma Scale alone
- **D.** Apgar score

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: B. FLACC scale**

- **A.** Verbal numerical score ke liye number aur pain intensity ka relation samajhna padta hai.
- **B.** FLACC face, legs, activity, cry aur consolability ko observe karta hai. Preverbal child apna pain number mein nahi bata sakta, isliye behavior-based tool useful hai.
- **C.** GCS consciousness assess karta hai. Ye pain intensity measurement ka replacement nahi hai.
- **D.** Apgar birth ke baad newborn adaptation assess karta hai, postoperative toddler pain nahi.

**Reference:** [RCH pediatric acute pain](https://www.rch.org.au/clinicalguide/guideline_index/Acute_pain_management/) · [RCH pain assessment](https://www.rch.org.au/rchcpg/hospital_clinical_guideline_index/Pain_assessment_and_measurement/)

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: B. Assess likely pain sources, anxiety, context and response to comfort, using self-report if feasible**

- **A.** Painless planned exam ke bawajood existing pain ho sakta hai, score ignore nahi karna chahiye.
- **B.** Behavioral score pain aur fear dono se influence ho sakta hai. Clinical context, possible pain cause aur comfort response assess karne se management individualized hota hai.
- **C.** Medication decision mein pain cause, prescription aur safety assessment chahiye.
- **D.** Pain aur anxiety ek saath present ho sakte hain aur ek doosre ko intensify kar sakte hain.
