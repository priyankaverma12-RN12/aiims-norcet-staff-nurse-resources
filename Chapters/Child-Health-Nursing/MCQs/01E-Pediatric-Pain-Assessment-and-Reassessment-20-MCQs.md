### Q1. A preverbal 16-month-old is recovering after surgery. Which assessment tool is most appropriate for routine behavioral pain scoring?

**Difficulty:** Easy | **Priority:** 🟠 Important | **Topic:** Pediatric pain assessment | **Type:** Original

- **A.** Adult verbal 0–10 numerical rating requiring self-report
- **B.** FLACC scale
- **C.** Glasgow Coma Scale alone
- **D.** Apgar score

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: B. FLACC scale**

- **A.** Numerical scale mein child ko pain ki intensity ko ordered numbers se express karna hota hai. Sixteen-month-old aam taur par is concept ko reliably use nahi kar sakta, isliye verbal number maangna suitable nahi hai.
- **B.** FLACC mein nurse face, legs, activity, cry aur consolability observe karti hai. Har domain ko 0–2 score karke total 0–10 milta hai. Preverbal toddler mein yeh useful behavioral assessment hai, lekin clinical context aur caregiver observations bhi include karo.
- **C.** GCS eye, verbal aur motor responses se consciousness assess karta hai. Isse pain intensity ka validated score nahi milta. Postoperative child mein consciousness aur pain dono independently assess karne pad sakte hain.
- **D.** Apgar newborn ki birth ke baad immediate adaptation assess karta hai. Sixteen-month-old postoperative child ke pain behavior ke liye yeh tool design nahi hua hai.

**Exam clue:** Preverbal child ke liye suitable validated behavioral tool choose karo.

**Reference:** [RCH pediatric acute pain](https://www.rch.org.au/clinicalguide/guideline_index/Acute_pain_management/) · [RCH pain assessment](https://www.rch.org.au/rchcpg/hospital_clinical_guideline_index/Pain_assessment_and_measurement/)

- **D.** The physician based only on the diagnosis

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

- **D.** Diagnosis pain ka potential cause batata hai, lekin same diagnosis wale children alag intensity feel kar sakte hain. Doctor ka estimate reliable child self-report ko replace nahi karta.

**Exam clue:** Faces scale child ke andar ke pain ka self-report hai, facial expression matching test nahi.

### Q6. A child remains very still after abdominal surgery and refuses to move despite not crying. What is the most appropriate nursing interpretation?

- **D.** Crying ka wait karne se pain unnecessarily untreated reh sakta hai. Current immobility aur refusal to move assessment ke liye enough concern hain, chahe child vocalize na kare.
