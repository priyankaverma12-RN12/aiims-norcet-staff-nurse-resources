### Q1. At an immunization clinic following the cited Indian National Immunization Schedule, a healthy infant received the routine 6-week doses on time and now attends at 10 completed weeks. Which set is ordinarily due at this visit?

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Priority:** 🟠 Important | **Topic:** Pediatric UIP vaccination and AEFI | **Type:** Original

- **A.** bOPV-2, fIPV-2 and PCV-2
- **B.** bOPV-2, pentavalent-2 and rotavirus-2
- **C.** MR-1, PCV booster and fIPV-3
- **D.** DPT booster-1, MR-2 and bOPV booster

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: B. bOPV-2, pentavalent-2 and rotavirus-2**

- **A.** Is option mein bOPV-2 correct timing par hai, lekin fIPV-2 aur PCV-2 routine 14-week visit mein due hoti hain. Ek vaccine correct hone se poora combination correct nahi ho jata.
- **B.** Six weeks par multiple primary doses start hoti hain, lekin har vaccine ki next dose ka timing same nahi hota. Ten weeks ke routine visit mein bOPV, pentavalent aur rotavirus ki second doses aati hain. Age aur previous doses verify karke eligible vaccines administer karte hain.
- **C.** MR-1, PCV booster aur fIPV-3 later infancy mein 9–11 months wale visit par aati hain. Ten-week infant ke routine schedule mein yeh set due nahi hai.
- **D.** DPT aur bOPV boosters aur MR-2 toddler-age visit ke components hain. Primary series ke beech inhe substitute nahi karna chahiye.

**Exam clue:** Ten-week routine visit mein teen listed vaccine doses yaad rakho.
