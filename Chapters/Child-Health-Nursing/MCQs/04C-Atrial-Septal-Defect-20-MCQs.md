### Q1. Which chamber enlargement is most directly expected from a sustained, significant left-to-right atrial shunt in a child?

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Priority:** 🟠 Important | **Topic:** Pediatric atrial septal defect | **Type:** Original

- **A.** Isolated left ventricular pressure hypertrophy
- **B.** Right atrial and right ventricular volume enlargement
- **C.** Isolated left atrial enlargement without right-sided loading
- **D.** Concentric hypertrophy of both ventricles from fixed outflow stenosis

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: B. Right atrial and right ventricular volume enlargement**

- **A.** LV pressure hypertrophy generally systemic outflow resistance se linked hoti hai. ASD extra volume primarily right side par bhejta hai.
- **B.** Extra blood left atrium se right atrium mein jaakar RV aur pulmonary circulation ka volume load badhata hai. Echo par right-sided enlargement shunt significance ka useful clue hai.
- **C.** Left atrial enlargement alone typical sustained ASD shunt load reflect nahi karta. Right-sided receiving chambers evaluate karo.
- **D.** Fixed ventricular outflow stenosis pressure loading create karti hai. ASD ka initial major effect volume loading hai.

**Exam clue:** ASD shunt significance: look for right-sided volume overload.

### Q2. A schoolchild with ASD has a widely split second heart sound that changes little with respiration. Which mechanism explains the fixed character?

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: D. Persistently increased right ventricular flow with relatively little respiratory variation in pulmonic closure timing**

- **D.** ASD mein RV flow respiratory cycle ke dono phases mein elevated reh sakta hai. Pulmonic closure relative to aortic closure delayed aur variation limited hoti hai, jisse fixed splitting milti hai.

### Q3. In a child with a significant ASD, a midsystolic ejection murmur is best heard at the upper left sternal border. What usually produces this sound?
