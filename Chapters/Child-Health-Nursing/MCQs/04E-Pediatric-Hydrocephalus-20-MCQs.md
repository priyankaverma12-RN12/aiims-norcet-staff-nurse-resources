### Q1. An infant with progressive ventricular enlargement develops widening cranial sutures and increasing head circumference. Why may this pattern be more prominent than in an older child with hydrocephalus?

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Priority:** 🟠 Important | **Topic:** Pediatric hydrocephalus | **Type:** Original

- **A.** CSF production stops in all older children
- **B.** The older child's skull is more expandable than an infant's
- **C.** Infant cranial bones prevent any change in head size
- **D.** The infant skull can expand at open sutures and fontanelles

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: D. The infant skull can expand at open sutures and fontanelles**

- **A.** CSF production older children mein continue hoti hai. Presentation difference mainly skull compliance aur pathology se related hai.
- **B.** Older skull relatively fixed hota hai. Infant skull expansion ability more hoti hai, not the reverse.
- **C.** Open sutures head growth permit karte hain. Progressive abnormal expansion ko clinical findings ke saath evaluate karna chahiye.
- **D.** Infant mein sutures aur fontanelles open hote hain. Ventricular enlargement se skull expand kar sakta hai, isliye serial head growth important clue hai. Older child ke relatively rigid skull mein pressure symptoms more prominent ho sakte hain.

**Exam clue:** Age changes the clinical presentation of raised intracranial pressure.

### Q3. An infant with suspected hydrocephalus has persistent downward deviation of the eyes and visible sclera above the iris. Which term describes the finding?

### Q20. A family is preparing to travel with a child who has a programmable VP shunt. Which information is most useful to keep available for an unfamiliar emergency team?

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Priority:** 🟠 Important | **Topic:** Pediatric hydrocephalus | **Type:** Original

- **A.** Device model, current prescribed setting, relevant neurosurgical history and the child's usual clinical baseline
- **B.** Only the date of the child's most recent school examination
- **C.** A statement that all shunt models have identical settings
- **D.** Only the brand of the child's oral vitamins

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: A. Device model, current prescribed setting, relevant neurosurgical history and the child's usual clinical baseline**

- **A.** Device model and setting imaging safety and specialist evaluation guide karte hain. Previous operations, baseline symptoms and treating team contact help unfamiliar staff interpret new concerns. Family should know emergency review pathway.
- **B.** School information useful in educational context hai but urgent device identification provide nahi karti.
- **C.** Shunt valves differ. Identical setting assumption inappropriate treatment decisions cause kar sakti hai.
- **D.** Vitamins complete medication history ka part ho sakte hain but device-specific information essential hai.

**Exam clue:** Keep a device record and individualized baseline available.
