### Q1. Which finding identifies hypospadias rather than epispadias during newborn examination?

**Difficulty:** Easy | **Priority:** 🟠 Important | **Topic:** Hypospadias | **Type:** Original

- **A.** Urethral meatus on the dorsal aspect of the penis
- **B.** Normally positioned meatus with isolated testicular maldescent
- **C.** Normal meatus with a communicating hydrocele
- **D.** Urethral meatus on the ventral aspect of the penis

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: D. Urethral meatus on the ventral aspect of the penis**

- **A.** Dorsal opening epispadias se associated hai. Ventral aur dorsal displacement ko correctly distinguish karna important hai.
- **B.** Undescended testis associated anomaly ho sakti hai but meatus normal ho toh alone hypospadias establish nahi hoti.
- **C.** Hydrocele scrotal fluid collection hai. Urethral opening ki location isse define nahi hoti.
- **D.** Hypospadias mein urethral opening usual tip se neeche ventral surface par hoti hai. Location aur associated curvature document karke pediatric urology evaluation arrange karte hain.

**Exam clue:** Ventral meatus is the defining anatomical clue.
