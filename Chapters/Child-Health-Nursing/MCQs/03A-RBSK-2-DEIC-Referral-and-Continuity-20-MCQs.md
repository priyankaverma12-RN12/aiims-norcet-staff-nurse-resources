### Q1. A four-year-old has a suspected motor delay identified during an RBSK 2.0 screening visit. The child is clinically stable and requires coordinated assessment by several disciplines. Which facility is the primary district-level hub for this purpose?

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Priority:** 🟠 Important | **Topic:** RBSK 2.0 pediatric DEIC referral and continuity | **Type:** Original

- **A.** Nutrition Rehabilitation Centre (NRC) as the default service for all developmental delays
- **B.** Special Newborn Care Unit (SNCU) for all children with motor concerns
- **C.** District Early Intervention Centre (DEIC)
- **D.** Adolescent Friendly Health Clinic (AFHC) as the sole referral facility

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: C. District Early Intervention Centre (DEIC)**

- **A.** NRC primarily indicated severe acute malnutrition care se related hai. Motor delay alone ke liye NRC ko default multidisciplinary centre maanna correct nahi hai. Nutritional concern ho toh usko separately assess karo.
- **B.** SNCU newborns ki specialized facility care ke liye hoti hai. Four-year-old ke stable motor concern ke liye developmental assessment aur rehabilitation pathway more relevant hai.
- **C.** DEIC mein different disciplines milkar child ki developmental aur health needs assess karte hain. RBSK ke under stable suspected delay ko further evaluation aur appropriate intervention se link karne ka yeh district-level hub hai. Individual referral urgency clinical findings par depend karegi.
- **D.** AFHC adolescents ki health aur counselling needs address karta hai. Preschool child ke multidisciplinary developmental concern ke liye DEIC designated hub hai.

**Exam clue:** Stable developmental concern under RBSK: coordinate district early intervention rather than automatically choosing a disease-specific ward.
