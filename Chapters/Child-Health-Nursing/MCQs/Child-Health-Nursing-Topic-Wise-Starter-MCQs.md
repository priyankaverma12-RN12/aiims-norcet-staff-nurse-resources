## Dehydration

### Q1. A child with diarrhea is lethargic, has sunken eyes and drinks poorly. Which classification should be considered under IMNCI assessment?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Topic:** Dehydration

- **A.** Severe dehydration
- **B.** No dehydration
- **C.** Normal hydration because diarrhea is common
- **D.** Mild dehydration based solely on stool frequency

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: A. Severe dehydration**

Diarrhea se child ka paani aur salts nikal sakte hain. Lethargy, sunken eyes aur poor drinking severe dehydration ke important signs hain. Nurse urgent protocol-based care arrange karti hai.

**Baaki options kyun galat hain?**

- **B.** Ye signs normal hydration ke nahi hain.
- **C.** Common diarrhea bhi severe ho sakta hai.
- **D.** Classification sirf stool count se nahi hoti.

</details>

## Pediatric dosing

### Q2. A child weighs 16 kg. An ordered medicine is prescribed at 15 mg/kg per dose. What is the calculated dose before checking the maximum and order?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Topic:** Pediatric dosing

- **A.** 160 mg
- **B.** 31 mg
- **C.** 1,500 mg
- **D.** 240 mg

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: D. 240 mg**

Har kilogram ke liye 15 mg prescribed hai. 16 ko 15 se multiply karne par 240 mg milta hai. Nurse maximum dose, medicine concentration aur order bhi check karti hai.

**Baaki options kyun galat hain?**

- **A.** 160 mg 10 mg/kg ke barabar hai.
- **B.** 31 kg aur mg/kg ko add karne se aata hai, calculation galat hai.
- **C.** 1,500 mg prescribed calculation se zyada hai.

</details>

## Respiratory distress

### Q3. An infant with a respiratory infection develops chest retractions and poor feeding. What should the nurse prioritize?

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Topic:** Respiratory distress

- **A.** Wait until cyanosis becomes obvious
- **B.** Assess only the number of teeth
- **C.** Assessment of breathing effort, oxygenation and hydration
- **D.** Force large oral feeds during distress

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: C. Assessment of breathing effort, oxygenation and hydration**

Infant ki breathing muscles jaldi thak sakti hain. Chest retractions ka matlab saans lene mein zyada mehnat ho rahi hai. Poor feeding dehydration ka risk bhi badhati hai.

**Baaki options kyun galat hain?**

- **A.** Cyanosis late sign ho sakta hai.
- **B.** Teeth count acute distress assess nahi karta.
- **D.** Force feeding aspiration risk badha sakti hai.

</details>

## Development

### Q4. A parent reports loss of previously acquired language and social skills in a toddler. Which response is most appropriate?

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Topic:** Development

- **A.** Focus only on the child's weight
- **B.** Arrange developmental assessment because regression is a warning sign
- **C.** Reassure that loss of skills is always normal
- **D.** Wait until school age without assessment

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: B. Arrange developmental assessment because regression is a warning sign**

Child development mein naye skills aate hain. Agar pehle se aayi skill gayab hone lage, toh regression kehte hain. Iski timely developmental aur medical assessment important hai.

**Baaki options kyun galat hain?**

- **A.** Weight alone language aur social development assess nahi karta.
- **C.** Skill loss ko normal assume nahi karna chahiye.
- **D.** Early assessment support jaldi de sakti hai.

</details>
