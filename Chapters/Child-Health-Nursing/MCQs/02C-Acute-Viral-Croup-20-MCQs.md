### Q1. A 2-year-old has a barking cough, hoarseness and inspiratory stridor following coryza. Which anatomical site is chiefly responsible for the characteristic obstruction?

**Difficulty:** Easy | **Priority:** 🟠 Important | **Topic:** Acute viral croup | **Type:** Original

- **A.** Terminal bronchioles
- **B.** Subglottic upper airway
- **C.** Alveolar sacs
- **D.** Pleural space

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: B. Subglottic upper airway**

- **A.** Bronchioles ki inflammation mein wheeze aur crackles common hain. Barking cough aur hoarseness upper airway involvement ko support karte hain.

- **B.** Croup mein larynx ke neeche subglottic mucosa mein swelling hoti hai. Chhote bachche ka airway pehle se narrow hota hai, isliye thodi swelling bhi turbulent airflow aur stridor produce karti hai.

- **C.** Alveoli gas exchange karte hain. Alveolar disease hypoxemia ya crackles de sakti hai, lekin typical barking cough ka primary site nahi hai.

- **D.** Pleural space mein air ya fluid lung expansion ko affect kar sakta hai. Croup mein obstruction pleural cavity mein nahi hota.

**Exam clue:** Barking cough + hoarseness + inspiratory stridor = upper airway.

**Reference:** [RCH croup guideline](https://www.rch.org.au/clinicalguide/guideline_index/Croup_Laryngotracheobronchitis/); [CPS acute croup management](https://cps.ca/en/documents/position/acute-management-of-croup).

**Exam clue:** Classic association, not an exclusive cause.

### Q4. A toddler with croup has stridor only when crying. When settled on the parent's lap, there is no stridor at rest and minimal work of breathing. Which observation most supports the lower severity category?

### Q5. A child with severe croup initially has loud stridor. Later the stridor becomes faint, but the child becomes drowsy with poorer air entry. How should this change be interpreted?

- **D.** Antipyretic fever discomfort relieve kar sakti hai but airway inflammation treat nahi karti. It may be appropriate supportive care.

**Exam clue:** Routine viral croup is not an antibiotic indication.

### Q19. A child with a known congenital airway narrowing develops viral croup symptoms. Why should the pre-existing condition influence escalation decisions?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Priority:** 🔴 High | **Topic:** Acute viral croup | **Type:** Original

### Q20. A child is discharged after meeting croup recovery criteria. Which caregiver report warrants urgent reassessment rather than waiting for a scheduled visit?

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Priority:** 🔴 High | **Topic:** Acute viral croup | **Type:** Original

- **A.** Stridor at rest returns with increasing chest recession
- **B.** The child has an occasional barking cough but breathes comfortably
- **C.** Mild hoarseness persists without breathing difficulty
- **D.** The child drinks normally but requests a familiar food

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: A. Stridor at rest returns with increasing chest recession**

- **D.** Normal drinking aur familiar food request reassuring context ho sakte hain. Isse urgent airway warning sign identify nahi hota.

**Exam clue:** Return urgently for stridor at rest or increasing work of breathing.
