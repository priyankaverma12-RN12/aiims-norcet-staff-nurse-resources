### PYQ1. A child with diarrhea has sunken eyes and drinks eagerly because of thirst but is not lethargic. Which IMNCI classification is supported by the two stated signs?

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Topic:** IMNCI dehydration

- **A.** Some dehydration
- **B.** No dehydration
- **C.** Severe dehydration based on these signs alone
- **D.** Normal hydration because the child can drink

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: A. Some dehydration**

IMNCI mein sunken eyes aur eager drinking some dehydration ke signs hain. Nurse baaki signs bhi assess karti hai aur prescribed rehydration plan follow karti hai.

**Baaki options kyun galat hain?**

- **B.** Do dehydration signs present hain.
- **C.** Severe category ke specific signs alag hain.
- **D.** Drinking ability dehydration ko exclude nahi karti.

</details>
