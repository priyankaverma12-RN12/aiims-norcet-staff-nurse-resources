# Neonatal and Newborn Nursing — Topic coverage map

This is a curriculum-based checklist, not full theory notes. No learning completion is recorded.

- Normal newborn assessment
- thermal care and feeding
- newborn resuscitation
- prematurity and low birth weight
- jaundice and sepsis
- neonatal hypoglycemia
- congenital disorders and KMC

## Initial study explanations

### Newborn resuscitation

Newborn ki heart rate low hone ka common reason lungs mein air na pahunchna hai. Apnea aur HR 100 se kam ho toh effective ventilation priority hoti hai. Current neonatal protocol follow karna hai.

### Thermal care

Skin-to-skin contact se mother ki body heat baby ko warm rakhne mein help karti hai. Kangaroo mother care feeding aur bonding support kar sakti hai. Baby ka monitoring phir bhi zaroori hai.

### Neonatal jaundice

Birth ke pehle 24 ghante mein jaundice early warning sign hai. Hemolysis ya doosri disease possible ho sakti hai. Bilirubin age in hours aur risk factors ke hisaab se assess hota hai.

### Neonatal hypoglycemia

Pregnancy mein baby insulin zyada bana sakta hai. Delivery ke baad maa se glucose supply rukti hai aur baby ki sugar gir sakti hai. Jitteriness aur poor feeding ke saath glucose check zaroori hai.
