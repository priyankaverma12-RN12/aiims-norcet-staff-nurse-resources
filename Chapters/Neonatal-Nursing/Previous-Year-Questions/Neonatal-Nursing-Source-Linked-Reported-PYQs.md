### PYQ1. Which areas should a correctly sized neonatal ventilation mask cover?

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Topic:** Newborn bag-mask ventilation

- **A.** Chin, mouth and nose without covering the eyes
- **B.** Eyes and forehead only
- **C.** Neck and lower jaw only
- **D.** One nostril only

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: A. Chin, mouth and nose without covering the eyes**

Mask ko mouth aur nose cover karna hai aur chin par seal banana hai. Sahi fit se air lungs tak pahunchne mein help milti hai.

**Baaki options kyun galat hain?**

- **B.** Eyes cover karne se ventilation seal nahi banegi.
- **C.** Neck cover karna gas delivery nahi karta.
- **D.** Sirf ek nostril cover karna adequate seal nahi hai.

</details>
