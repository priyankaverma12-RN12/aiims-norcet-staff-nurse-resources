### Q01. Which tissue is primarily injured in necrotizing enterocolitis (NEC)?

**Difficulty:** 🟢 Easy · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD · Original practice

- **A.** Retinal vascular tissue
- **B.** Intestinal wall
- **C.** Renal glomerulus
- **D.** Myocardial valve

<details><summary>✅ Correct answer and detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: B. Intestinal wall**

- **A — Incorrect:** Retinal vascular injury ROP ka focus hai, NEC ka nahi.
- **B — Correct:** NEC mein intestinal wall inflamed aur necrotic ho sakti hai. Injury mucosa se deeper layers tak extend kar sakti hai.
- **C — Incorrect:** Glomerulus kidney filtration unit hai. NEC bowel disease hai.
- **D — Incorrect:** Cardiac valve ka structural injury NEC ka defining feature nahi hai.

**Exam clue:** NEC = intestinal necrosis.

- **B.** Gas confined to the stomach lumen
- **C.** Fluid in the pleural space

<details><summary>✅ Correct answer and detailed explanation</summary>

### Q17. A 1.5-kg infant with NEC produces 18 mL of gastric drainage in six hours. What is the average drainage rate in mL/kg/hour?

**Difficulty:** 🔴 Difficult · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD · Original practice

- **A.** 2 mL/kg/hour
- **B.** 3 mL/kg/hour
- **C.** 12 mL/kg/hour
- **D.** 18 mL/kg/hour

<details><summary>✅ Correct answer and detailed explanation</summary>

**Exam clue:** Reduced intestinal length can reduce nutrient and fluid absorption.
