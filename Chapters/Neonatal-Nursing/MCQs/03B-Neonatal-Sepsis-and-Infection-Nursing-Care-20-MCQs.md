### Q04. What does C-reactive protein (CRP) represent in neonatal infection assessment?

**Difficulty:** 🟢 Easy · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD · Original practice

- **A.** A direct count of viable bacteria
- **B.** A measure of serum bilirubin only
- **C.** A marker of inflammation
- **D.** A measure of oxygen saturation

<details><summary>✅ Correct answer and detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: C. A marker of inflammation**

- **A — Incorrect:** CRP bacteria count nahi karta. Organism detect karne ke liye culture ya appropriate microbiological test chahiye.
- **B — Incorrect:** Bilirubin jaundice evaluation ke liye separate blood test hai. CRP inflammation marker hai.
- **C — Correct:** CRP inflammation ke response mein rise ho sakta hai. Infection assessment mein clinical signs, culture aur serial trends ke saath use hota hai.
- **D — Incorrect:** Oxygen saturation pulse oximetry se assess hoti hai. CRP gas exchange measure nahi karta.

**Exam clue:** CRP is a marker, not proof of an organism.

### Q09. A clinician decides at 09:20 that a newborn requires antibiotics for suspected neonatal infection. Under NICE timing guidance, by what time should treatment be given at the latest?

**Difficulty:** 🟡 Medium · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD · Original practice

- **A.** 11:20
- **B.** 12:20
- **C.** 10:20
- **D.** At the next routine medication round regardless of its time

<details><summary>✅ Correct answer and detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: C. 10:20**

- **A — Incorrect:** 11:20 two hours baad hai. Yeh stated maximum interval exceed karta hai.
- **B — Incorrect:** 12:20 three hours baad hai. Suspected serious infection mein delay unsafe ho sakti hai.
- **C — Correct:** NICE decision to treat ke baad antibiotics as soon as possible aur within one hour recommend karta hai. 09:20 plus one hour = 10:20.
- **D — Incorrect:** Routine round timing urgent antibiotic deadline ko override nahi karti.
