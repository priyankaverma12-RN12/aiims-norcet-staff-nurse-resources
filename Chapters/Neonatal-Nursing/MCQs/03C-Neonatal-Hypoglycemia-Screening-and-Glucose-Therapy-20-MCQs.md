### Q01. Which hormone lowers circulating glucose by promoting cellular glucose uptake?

**Difficulty:** 🟢 Easy · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD · Original practice

- **A.** Insulin
- **B.** Glucagon
- **C.** Adrenaline
- **D.** Growth hormone

<details><summary>✅ Correct answer and detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: A. Insulin**

- **A — Correct:** Insulin glucose ko cells ke andar use aur storage ke liye pahunchane mein help karta hai. Excess insulin se blood glucose low ho sakta hai.
- **B — Incorrect:** Glucagon liver se glucose release badhata hai. Iska effect insulin ke opposite hai.
- **C — Incorrect:** Adrenaline stress mein glucose mobilize kar sakta hai. Yeh primary glucose-lowering hormone nahi hai.
- **D — Incorrect:** Growth hormone counter-regulatory effect rakhta hai. Insulin glucose uptake ka direct regulator hai.

**Exam clue:** Insulin lowers glucose.

### Q16. A 2-kg newborn receives prescribed 10% dextrose at 6 mL/hour. What is the glucose infusion rate?

**Difficulty:** 🔴 Difficult · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD · Original practice

- **B.** 10 mg/kg/min
- **C.** 5 mg/kg/min
- **D.** 30 mg/kg/min

<details><summary>✅ Correct answer and detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: C. 5 mg/kg/min**

- **A — Incorrect:** 3 mL/kg/hour fluid rate hai, GIR nahi. Glucose concentration aur minute conversion chahiye.
- **B — Incorrect:** 10 mg/min total glucose delivery hai. Per kg calculation mein 2 se divide karna hai.
- **C — Correct:** 10% solution mein 100 mg/mL glucose hai. 6 times 100 = 600 mg/hour. 600 divide 60 divide 2 = 5 mg/kg/min.

**Exam clue:** GIR = mg/mL × mL/hour ÷ 60 ÷ kg.
