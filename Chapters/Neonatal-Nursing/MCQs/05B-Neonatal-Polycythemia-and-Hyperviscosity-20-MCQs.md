### Q01. What does hematocrit represent in a neonatal blood sample?

**Difficulty:** 🟢 Easy · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD · Original practice

- **A.** The fraction occupied by circulating platelets only
- **B.** The serum glucose concentration
- **C.** The fraction of blood volume occupied by red blood cells
- **D.** The volume of plasma proteins alone

<details><summary>✅ Correct answer and detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: C. The fraction of blood volume occupied by red blood cells**

- **A — Incorrect:** Platelets clotting mein role rakhte hain, hematocrit platelet fraction nahi hai.
- **B — Incorrect:** Glucose dissolved sugar concentration hai; hematocrit cell volume fraction hai.
- **C — Correct:** Hematocrit RBC volume ka whole blood ke comparison mein proportion hai. Percent mein report ho sakta hai.
- **D — Incorrect:** Plasma protein concentration alag measurement hai.

**Exam clue:** Hct measures red cell volume fraction.

### Q02. What term describes excessive resistance to blood flow due to increased blood viscosity?

**Difficulty:** 🟢 Easy · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD · Original practice

- **A.** Hyperviscosity
- **B.** Hypocalcemia
- **C.** Hypovolemia
- **D.** Hemodilution

<details><summary>✅ Correct answer and detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: A. Hyperviscosity**

- **A — Correct:** Hyperviscosity mein blood flow ke against resistance badhti hai. Microcirculation compromise ho sakti hai.
- **B — Incorrect:** Hypocalcemia calcium low hona hai, blood viscosity ka term nahi.
- **C — Incorrect:** Hypovolemia circulating volume kam hona hai; viscosity concept different hai.
- **D — Incorrect:** Hemodilution mein cell concentration dilute hoti hai, excessive viscosity ka naam nahi.

**Exam clue:** Viscosity means resistance to flow.

### Q05. What is the main objective of partial exchange transfusion for neonatal polycythemia?

- **B.** Reduce excessive red cell concentration and viscosity

### Q17. For a 3-kg term infant, use the prescribed UCSF calculation inputs: initial Hct 75%, target 50%, estimated blood volume 90 mL/kg. What is the calculated partial exchange volume?

**Difficulty:** 🔴 Difficult · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD · Original practice

- **C.** 135 mL
