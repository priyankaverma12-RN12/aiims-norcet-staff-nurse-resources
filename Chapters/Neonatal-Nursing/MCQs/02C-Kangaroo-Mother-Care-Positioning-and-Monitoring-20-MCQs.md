### Q01. Which two elements form kangaroo mother care as defined in the WHO 2025 clinical practice guide?

**Difficulty:** 🟢 Easy · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD · Original practice

- **A.** Intermittent incubator warming and scheduled formula feeding
- **B.** Prolonged skin-to-skin contact with the mother and breast-milk feeding
- **C.** Maternal rooming-in and routine bottle feeding alone
- **D.** Wrapped holding and daily weighing alone

<details><summary>✅ Correct answer and detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: B. Prolonged skin-to-skin contact with the mother and breast-milk feeding**

- **A — Incorrect:** Incubator clinically useful ho sakta hai, lekin yeh KMC ke dono core elements ko replace nahi karta.
- **B — Correct:** KMC mein maa ke saath lambe samay tak seedha skin-to-skin contact aur breast-milk feeding dono essential hain. Sirf paas rakhna complete KMC nahi hota.
- **C — Incorrect:** Rooming-in bonding mein madad karta hai, lekin contact aur milk feeding ka KMC component zaroori hai.
- **D — Incorrect:** Wrapped holding mein seedha skin contact nahi milta; weighing monitoring hai, treatment ka substitute nahi.

**Exam clue:** KMC = prolonged SSC + breast milk.

### Q03. During kangaroo positioning, how should the newborn's head generally be placed?

**Difficulty:** 🟢 Easy · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD · Original practice

- **A.** Turned to one side with the neck slightly extended
- **B.** Chin pressed firmly against the chest
- **C.** Neck maximally hyperextended
- **D.** Face pressed flat against the mother's chest

<details><summary>✅ Correct answer and detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: A. Turned to one side with the neck slightly extended**

- **A — Correct:** Head side mein aur neck slightly extended rakhne se airway open rehta hai; face visible aur nose-mouth unobstructed rehne chahiye.
- **B — Incorrect:** Chin chest par dabne se airway narrow ho sakta hai.
- **C — Incorrect:** Hyperextension bhi unsafe hai; neutral se slight extension preferred hai.
- **D — Incorrect:** Face chest par flat press hone se nose aur mouth block ho sakte hain.

**Exam clue:** Visible face and slight neck extension.

- **C — Incorrect:** Poor gain mein follow-up aur assessment important hain; duration discharge criterion ka substitute nahi hai.
- **D — Correct:** Poor growth ke multiple causes ho sakte hain. Milk intake, transfer, growth trend aur illness assess karke neonatal team individualized plan banati hai.

**Exam clue:** Good contact duration does not exclude feeding or medical problems.
