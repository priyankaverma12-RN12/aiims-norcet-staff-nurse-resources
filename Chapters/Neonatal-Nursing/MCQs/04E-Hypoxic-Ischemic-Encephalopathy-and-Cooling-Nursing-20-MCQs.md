### Q01. What does HIE stand for in neonatal neurology?

**Difficulty:** 🟢 Easy · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD · Original practice

- **A.** Hereditary intestinal enteropathy
- **B.** Hyperinsulinemic endocrine excess
- **C.** Hypoxic-ischemic encephalopathy
- **D.** Hemolytic immune erythropoiesis

<details><summary>✅ Correct answer and detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: C. Hypoxic-ischemic encephalopathy**

- **A — Incorrect:** Intestinal enteropathy gut disease hai. HIE neurological condition hai.
- **B — Incorrect:** Hyperinsulinemia metabolic hypoglycemia cause kar sakti hai, HIE ka expansion nahi.
- **C — Correct:** HIE mein oxygen delivery aur cerebral blood flow inadequate hone ke baad brain dysfunction develop ho sakta hai.
- **D — Incorrect:** Immune red cell disease bilirubin risk badha sakti hai, hypoxic-ischemic encephalopathy se alag hai.

**Exam clue:** Hypoxia plus ischemia can injure the brain.
