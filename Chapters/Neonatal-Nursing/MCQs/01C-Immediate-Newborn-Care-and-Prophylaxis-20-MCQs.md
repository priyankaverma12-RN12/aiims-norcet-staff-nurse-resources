### Q01. Which vitamin is routinely administered to newborns to reduce the risk of vitamin deficiency bleeding?

**Difficulty:** 🟢 Easy · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD · Original practice

- **A.** Vitamin C
- **B.** Vitamin K1
- **C.** Vitamin B12
- **D.** Vitamin D

<details><summary>✅ Correct answer and detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: B. Vitamin K1**

- **A — Incorrect:** Vitamin C collagen formation mein useful hai, lekin vitamin K dependent clotting factors ko activate nahi karta.
- **B — Correct:** Vitamin K clotting factors ko functional banane mein help karta hai. Birth par reserves kam hote hain, isliye prescribed prophylaxis bleeding ka risk kam karti hai.
- **C — Incorrect:** Vitamin B12 red blood cell aur nerve function mein role rakhta hai. Yeh routine bleeding prophylaxis ka substitute nahi hai.
- **D — Incorrect:** Vitamin D calcium metabolism aur bone health ke liye important hai, lekin vitamin K deficiency bleeding prevent nahi karta.

**Exam clue:** Clotting factor function ko vitamin K se connect karo.

### Q20. A newborn has marked eyelid swelling and copious purulent eye discharge. A caregiver requests routine prophylactic drops instead of evaluation. Which response is safest?

**Difficulty:** 🔴 Difficult · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD · Original practice

- **A.** Treat the condition as normal vernix around the eyes
- **B.** Use only routine dry cord care to address the discharge
- **C.** Delay clinical evaluation until the next scheduled immunization
- **D.** Arrange prompt clinical assessment for neonatal conjunctivitis and organism-directed management

<details><summary>✅ Correct answer and detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: D. Arrange prompt clinical assessment for neonatal conjunctivitis and organism-directed management**

- **A — Incorrect:** Vernix skin ki natural coating hai, purulent discharge aur swollen eyelid ka explanation nahi hai.
- **B — Incorrect:** Cord care umbilical infection prevention ke liye hai. Eye disease ka assessment aur treatment alag hota hai.
- **C — Incorrect:** Immunization visit tak delay infection complications ka risk badha sakta hai. Prompt review chahiye.
- **D — Correct:** Purulent discharge aur swelling neonatal conjunctivitis ke signs ho sakte hain. Serious pathogens possible hain, isliye prompt assessment aur prescribed treatment chahiye.

**Exam clue:** Prophylaxis prevention hai, symptomatic infection ko diagnostic assessment chahiye.
