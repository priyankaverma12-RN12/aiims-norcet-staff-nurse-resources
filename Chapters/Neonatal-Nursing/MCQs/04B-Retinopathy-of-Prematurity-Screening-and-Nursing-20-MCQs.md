### Q01. Which ocular structure is primarily affected by retinopathy of prematurity (ROP)?

**Difficulty:** 🟢 Easy · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD · Original practice

- **A.** Corneal epithelium alone
- **B.** Crystalline lens alone
- **C.** Developing retinal vessels
- **D.** Lacrimal drainage duct

<details><summary>✅ Correct answer and detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: C. Developing retinal vessels**

- **A — Incorrect:** Cornea anterior transparent surface hai. ROP primarily retinal vascular disease hai.
- **B — Incorrect:** Lens opacity cataract hai; ROP ka primary site developing retina hai.
- **C — Correct:** ROP immature retina ki vascular development ka disorder hai. Abnormal vessels vision ko threaten kar sakti hain.
- **D — Incorrect:** Lacrimal duct tear drainage karta hai. Iska ROP mechanism alag hai.

**Exam clue:** ROP = developing retinal vasculature.

- **B.** Set oxygen at 100% throughout the admission
- **C.** Stop all oxygen despite hypoxemia

<details><summary>✅ Correct answer and detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: A. Review oxygen delivery and monitoring to minimize avoidable fluctuations within the prescribed target**

- **A — Correct:** Repeated hypoxic aur hyperoxic episodes harmful ho sakte hain. Reliable probe aur prescribed titration se avoidable excursions reduce karo.
- **B — Incorrect:** Continuous 100% oxygen unnecessary hyperoxia risk de sakti hai.

- **D — Incorrect:** Alarms disable karne se saturation deterioration miss ho sakti hai.
