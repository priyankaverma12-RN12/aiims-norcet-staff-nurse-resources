### Q01. What does CCHD stand for in newborn screening?

**Difficulty:** 🟢 Easy · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD · Original practice

- **A.** Chronic childhood hearing deficit
- **B.** Congenital cerebral hematoma disorder
- **C.** Critical calcium homeostasis disturbance
- **D.** Critical congenital heart disease

<details><summary>✅ Correct answer and detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: D. Critical congenital heart disease**

- **A — Incorrect:** Hearing deficit separate sensory screening target hai.
- **B — Incorrect:** Cerebral hematoma intracranial bleeding hai, cardiac defect nahi.
- **C — Incorrect:** Calcium homeostasis metabolic issue hai, CCHD ka meaning nahi.
- **D — Correct:** CCHD serious structural heart conditions ko describe karta hai jinke timely detection aur treatment ki need ho sakti hai.

**Exam clue:** CCHD is a cardiac screening target.

### Q08. A healthy newborn is 26 hours old, off supplemental oxygen and ready for the 2025 CDC/AAP pulse-ox screening algorithm. Which initial result meets the pass criteria?

**Difficulty:** 🟡 Medium · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD · Original practice

- **A.** Right hand 96%, foot 93%
- **B.** Right hand 99%, foot 95%
- **C.** Right hand 94%, foot 94%
- **D.** Right hand 97%, foot 95%

<details><summary>✅ Correct answer and detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: D. Right hand 97%, foot 95%**

- **A — Incorrect:** Foot 93% threshold se low hai. Initial nonpass result repeat pathway demand karta hai.
- **B — Incorrect:** Difference 4 points hai, allowed 3 se zyada.
- **C — Incorrect:** Dono readings 95% se kam hain, pass nahi.
- **D — Correct:** Dono readings at least 95% hain aur difference 2 percentage points hai, pass criteria meet hote hain.

**Exam clue:** Both sites at least 95%; difference at most 3.
