### Q01. At every birth, which staffing arrangement supports readiness for neonatal resuscitation under the 2025 AHA/AAP guidance?

**Difficulty:** 🟢 Easy · **Priority:** 🔴 MOST IMPORTANT · Original practice

- **A.** One staff member responsible only for maternal vital signs
- **B.** At least one trained person dedicated to newborn care who can provide ventilation
- **C.** A pediatric team called only after the five-minute Apgar score
- **D.** A person trained only in infant weighing

<details><summary>✅ Correct answer and detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: B. At least one trained person dedicated to newborn care who can provide ventilation**

- **A — Incorrect:** Maternal monitoring important hai, lekin newborn ventilation ke liye dedicated trained person ka role separate hai.
- **B — Correct:** Har birth par newborn care ke liye dedicated trained person hona chahiye jo ventilation de sake. Unexpected breathing problem mein delay kam hoti hai.
- **C — Incorrect:** Five-minute Apgar ka wait resuscitation readiness nahi hai. Team birth se pehle prepared honi chahiye.
- **D — Incorrect:** Weighing skill ventilation competency ka replacement nahi hai.

**Exam clue:** Team readiness birth se pehle establish karo.

### Q20. A term newborn has recovered after prolonged ventilation and epinephrine. The heart rate is stable but the infant is less responsive than expected. What is the appropriate post-resuscitation plan?

**Difficulty:** 🔴 Difficult · **Priority:** 🔴 MOST IMPORTANT · Original practice

- **A.** Routine unmonitored rooming-in because heart rate has recovered
- **B.** Assume reduced responsiveness is harmless and omit glucose assessment
- **C.** Begin uncontrolled cooling in the ward without eligibility assessment
- **D.** Close monitored care including glucose and neurological assessment with urgent evaluation for hypoxic-ischemic injury

<details><summary>✅ Correct answer and detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: D. Close monitored care including glucose and neurological assessment with urgent evaluation for hypoxic-ischemic injury**

- **A — Incorrect:** Heart rate recovery important hai lekin later deterioration exclude nahi karti. Close monitoring indicated hai.
- **B — Incorrect:** Reduced responsiveness hypoglycemia ya neurological injury ka sign ho sakta hai. Glucose assessment omit karna unsafe hai.
- **C — Incorrect:** Therapeutic hypothermia selected babies ke liye defined protocol aur capable facility mein hoti hai. Uncontrolled cooling harmful ho sakti hai.
- **D — Correct:** Advanced resuscitation ke baad metabolic aur neurological injury ka risk rehta hai. Glucose, breathing, circulation, temperature aur neurological status monitor karo. Selected infants mein specialist HIE eligibility assessment timely zaroori hai.

**Exam clue:** ROSC ke baad monitoring aur HIE evaluation bhi resuscitation care ka part hain.
