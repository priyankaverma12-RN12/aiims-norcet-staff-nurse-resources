### Q01. A term newborn is left wet with amniotic fluid for several minutes after birth in a warm delivery room. Which heat-loss mechanism is most directly increased by the wet skin?

**Difficulty:** 🟡 Medium · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD · Original practice

- **A.** Conduction
- **B.** Radiation
- **C.** Evaporation
- **D.** Convection

<details><summary>✅ Correct answer and detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: C. Evaporation**

- **A — Incorrect:** Conduction tab hoti hai jab baby colder surface ko directly touch karta hai. Wet skin par liquid ka vapor banna alag process hai.
- **B — Incorrect:** Radiation mein nearby cold object ko bina touch kiye heat jaati hai. Yahan liquid ka evaporation main clue hai.
- **C — Correct:** Skin par liquid jab vapor banta hai toh body se heat leta hai. Isliye birth ke turant baad wet skin ko dry karna important hai.
- **D — Incorrect:** Convection mein moving air heat le jaati hai. Wetness specifically liquid se vapor conversion ko indicate karti hai.

**Exam clue:** Wet skin aur vapor formation ko evaporation se jodo.

### Q02. The nurse places a dry newborn on an unheated metal weighing scale. Which mechanism should be prevented by warming the contact surface?

**Difficulty:** 🟡 Medium · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD · Original practice

- **A.** Conduction
- **B.** Evaporation
- **C.** Radiation
- **D.** Nonshivering thermogenesis

- **D — Incorrect:** Nonshivering thermogenesis body ke andar heat banane ka process hai. Yeh heat loss ka mode nahi hai.

**Exam clue:** Direct contact wali heat loss conduction hoti hai.

### Q19. A newborn's axillary temperature is measured immediately after a long period of skin exposure during an examination. Which technique improves the reliability of the repeat measurement?

**Difficulty:** 🟡 Medium · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD · Original practice

- **A.** Place the thermometer over clothing to avoid skin contact
- **B.** Read the device before its measurement cycle finishes
- **C.** Use the cot mattress temperature as the repeat axillary value
- **D.** Use the approved axillary device with the probe positioned against skin and the arm held gently closed

<details><summary>✅ Correct answer and detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: D. Use the approved axillary device with the probe positioned against skin and the arm held gently closed**

- **A — Incorrect:** Clothing probe aur skin ke contact ko interfere kar sakti hai. Reading actual axillary value represent nahi karegi.
- **B — Incorrect:** Device cycle complete hone se pehle reading lene par measurement incomplete ho sakti hai.
- **C — Incorrect:** Mattress ka temperature baby ke axillary temperature se different ho sakta hai. Dono measurements interchangeable nahi hain.
- **D — Correct:** Axilla mein probe skin ke contact mein ho aur arm gently close ho toh device body temperature assess kar sakta hai. Approved device instructions follow karna chahiye.

**Exam clue:** Temperature measurement mein correct site, contact aur device method important hai.

### Q20. A neonatal ward reviews repeated low admission temperatures in otherwise stable infants. Which quality-improvement measure best identifies where the warm chain is failing?

**Difficulty:** 🔴 Difficult · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD · Original practice

- **A.** Record only the final temperature after all infants have been rewarmed
- **B.** Compare the ward's adult room temperature with the adult fever threshold
- **C.** Record temperatures and thermal-care steps at birth, transfer and admission, then examine gaps
- **D.** Remove temperature documentation to shorten the transfer process

<details><summary>✅ Correct answer and detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: C. Record temperatures and thermal-care steps at birth, transfer and admission, then examine gaps**

- **A — Incorrect:** Final rewarming ke baad normal reading earlier cold exposure hide kar sakti hai. Process failure identify karna mushkil hoga.
- **B — Incorrect:** Adult fever threshold neonatal transport safety measure nahi hai. Neonatal temperature target aur actual care steps use karo.
- **C — Correct:** Different points par measured temperature aur actual thermal steps record karne se pata chal sakta hai ki heat loss kahan ho raha hai. Team us specific gap ko correct karke outcomes reassess kar sakti hai.
- **D — Incorrect:** Documentation remove karne se trend aur intervention effect assess nahi hoga. Safety improvement ke liye reliable records chahiye.

**Exam clue:** Warm chain ek process hai. Har transition ka data failure location identify karta hai.
