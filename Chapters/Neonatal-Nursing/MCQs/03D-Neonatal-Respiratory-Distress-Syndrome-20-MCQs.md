### Q01. Which older term refers to neonatal respiratory distress syndrome caused by surfactant deficiency?

**Difficulty:** 🟢 Easy · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD · Original practice

- **A.** Transient tachypnea of the newborn
- **B.** Meconium aspiration syndrome
- **C.** Hyaline membrane disease
- **D.** Persistent pulmonary hypertension of the newborn

<details><summary>✅ Correct answer and detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: C. Hyaline membrane disease**

- **A — Incorrect:** TTN delayed fetal lung fluid clearance se related hai. Yeh RDS ka older name nahi hai.
- **B — Incorrect:** Meconium aspiration mein airway aur lungs mein meconium-related injury hoti hai. Mechanism alag hai.
- **C — Correct:** Hyaline membrane disease surfactant deficiency-related neonatal RDS ka older name hai. Collapsed alveoli aur tissue injury ke saath characteristic membranes develop ho sakti hain.
- **D — Incorrect:** PPHN pulmonary vascular resistance persist karne ki condition hai. Yeh RDS ke saath coexist kar sakti hai, par synonym nahi hai.

**Exam clue:** Hyaline membrane disease is neonatal RDS.

- **B — Incorrect:** Pyloric stenosis gastric outlet disorder hai. Oxygen exposure ka direct characteristic lung outcome nahi hai.
- **C — Correct:** Immature lungs mein inflammation, ventilation-related injury aur oxygen exposure chronic lung disease risk se associated hain. BPD assessment age-specific criteria aur support history par depend karti hai.
- **D — Incorrect:** Hormone withdrawal bleeding neonatal genital finding hai, chronic respiratory complication nahi.

**Exam clue:** Prolonged respiratory support in prematurity: consider BPD risk.
