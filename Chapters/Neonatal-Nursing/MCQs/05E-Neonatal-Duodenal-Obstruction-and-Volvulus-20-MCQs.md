### Q01. Which portion of the gastrointestinal tract is affected by duodenal atresia?

**Difficulty:** 🟢 Easy · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD · Original practice

- **A.** The terminal rectum
- **B.** The esophagus above the stomach
- **C.** The first part of the small intestine after the stomach
- **D.** The descending colon

<details><summary>✅ Correct answer and detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: C. The first part of the small intestine after the stomach**

- **A — Incorrect:** Rectum distal large intestine ka part hai.
- **B — Incorrect:** Esophagus mouth se stomach tak food carry karta hai; duodenum uske baad hai.
- **C — Correct:** Duodenum stomach ke immediately baad small intestine ka proximal part hai. Atresia mein lumen obstructed hota hai.
- **D — Incorrect:** Descending colon large bowel ka segment hai.

**Exam clue:** Duodenum is proximal small bowel.

### Q02. Which developmental mechanism commonly explains congenital duodenal atresia?
