### Q01. What is meconium?

**Difficulty:** 🟢 Easy · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD · Original practice

- **A.** The first intestinal contents passed as neonatal stool
- **B.** The first secretion produced by maternal breast tissue
- **C.** The fluid surrounding the fetal brain
- **D.** The protective white coating on neonatal skin

<details><summary>✅ Correct answer and detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: A. The first intestinal contents passed as neonatal stool**

- **A — Correct:** Meconium fetal intestine mein accumulated material hai jo first stool ke roop mein pass hota hai.
- **B — Incorrect:** First breast secretion colostrum hai, meconium nahi.
- **C — Incorrect:** Brain ke around cerebrospinal fluid hota hai, intestinal contents nahi.
- **D — Incorrect:** White skin coating vernix caseosa hai. Yeh stool se different hai.

**Exam clue:** Meconium = first stool.

### Q09. An aspirated meconium plug completely obstructs a small bronchus. What change is expected in the distal lung region?

**Difficulty:** 🟡 Medium · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD · Original practice

- **A.** Uniform hyperinflation from unobstructed expiration
- **B.** Increased distal ventilation despite complete obstruction
- **C.** Atelectasis from loss of ventilation
- **D.** Immediate normal gas exchange in the isolated region

<details><summary>✅ Correct answer and detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: C. Atelectasis from loss of ventilation**

- **A — Incorrect:** Hyperinflation partial ball-valve obstruction ke saath more typical hai, complete blockage ke saath nahi.
- **B — Incorrect:** Complete obstruction mein fresh gas distal alveoli tak effectively nahi pahunchti.
- **C — Correct:** Complete blockage se distal air space ventilation lose kar sakta hai aur collapse ho sakta hai.

### Q19. A ventilated infant with severe MAS has FiO2 0.80, mean airway pressure 15 cm H2O and arterial PaO2 60 mmHg. Using oxygenation index = FiO2 × mean airway pressure × 100 / PaO2, what is the index?

**Difficulty:** 🔴 Difficult · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD · Original practice

- **A.** 2
- **B.** 12
- **C.** 80
- **D.** 20

<details><summary>✅ Correct answer and detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: D. 20**

- **A — Incorrect:** 2 calculation mein tenfold error hai. FiO2 fraction aur factor 100 check karo.
- **B — Incorrect:** 12 FiO2 × pressure ka product hai. Factor 100 aur PaO2 division missing hai.
- **C — Incorrect:** 80 inspired oxygen percentage hai, calculated index nahi.
- **D — Correct:** 0.80 × 15 × 100 ÷ 60 = 20. Trend aur clinical context specialist review karta hai; number alone treatment order nahi hai.

**Exam clue:** Use FiO2 as a fraction in the given formula.
