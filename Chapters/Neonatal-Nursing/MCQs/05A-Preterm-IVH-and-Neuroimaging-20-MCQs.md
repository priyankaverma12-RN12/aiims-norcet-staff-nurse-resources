### Q01. Which vascular-rich developmental region is the usual origin of preterm germinal matrix hemorrhage?

**Difficulty:** 🟢 Easy · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD · Original practice

- **A.** Choroid of the eye
- **B.** Germinal matrix near the lateral ventricle
- **C.** Subcutaneous scalp
- **D.** Cerebellar vermis exclusively

<details><summary>✅ Correct answer and detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: B. Germinal matrix near the lateral ventricle**

- **A — Incorrect:** Eye choroid retina ko blood supply karti hai, cerebral germinal matrix nahi hai.
- **B — Correct:** Germinal matrix immature vascular tissue hai aur lateral ventricle ke paas bleed kar sakti hai.
- **C — Incorrect:** Scalp bleeding extracranial hai, GMH brain ke andar hoti hai.
- **D — Incorrect:** Cerebellar bleed possible hai par usual GMH origin nahi.

**Exam clue:** Germinal matrix is a vulnerable preterm brain region.
