### Q01. Which finding describes central apnea in a preterm infant?

**Difficulty:** 🟢 Easy · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD · Original practice

- **A.** Persistent respiratory effort against a blocked airway
- **B.** Rapid breathing with uninterrupted airflow
- **C.** Isolated peripheral cyanosis during normal breathing
- **D.** Absent respiratory effort during the pause

<details><summary>✅ Correct answer and detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: D. Absent respiratory effort during the pause**

- **A — Incorrect:** Obstructed airway ke against ongoing effort obstructive apnea ka feature hai. Central type mein effort pause hoti hai.
- **B — Incorrect:** Rapid continuous breathing tachypnea ho sakti hai. Yeh respiratory pause nahi hai.
- **C — Incorrect:** Peripheral cyanosis skin color finding hai. Respiratory effort absent hone ka evidence nahi hai.
- **D — Correct:** Central apnea mein brain ka respiratory drive temporarily pause hota hai. Isliye breathing effort absent hoti hai.

**Exam clue:** Central apnea means no respiratory effort.

### Q02. Which finding distinguishes obstructive apnea from central apnea?

**Difficulty:** 🟢 Easy · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD · Original practice

- **A.** Respiratory effort persists despite absent or inadequate airflow
- **B.** Respiratory effort and airflow both continue normally
- **C.** Respiratory effort is absent throughout every event
- **D.** The infant has only a faster heart rate with normal breathing

<details><summary>✅ Correct answer and detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: A. Respiratory effort persists despite absent or inadequate airflow**

- **A — Correct:** Obstructive apnea mein infant breathe karne ki koshish kar sakta hai, lekin airway se effective airflow nahi milti. Airway assessment zaroori hai.
- **B — Incorrect:** Normal effort aur airflow apnea ko describe nahi karte. Actual interruption identify karo.
- **C — Incorrect:** Absent effort central mechanism suggest karta hai. Obstructive apnea mein effort persist kar sakti hai.
- **D — Incorrect:** Fast heart rate alone obstructive apnea diagnose nahi karta. Effort aur airflow pattern assess hote hain.

**Exam clue:** Effort without airflow suggests obstruction.

- **C — Correct:** Home monitor SIDS prevention ka proven replacement nahi hai. Safe sleep guidance aur clinical discharge assessment important rehte hain. Specific monitoring indication specialist decide karta hai.
- **D — Incorrect:** Home device inpatient clinical readiness establish nahi karta. Required observation aur caregiver plan complete karo.

**Exam clue:** Home monitoring and safe sleep are separate safety issues.
