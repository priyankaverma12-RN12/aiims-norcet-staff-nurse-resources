## Newborn resuscitation

### Q1. A newborn remains apneic after the initial steps of neonatal resuscitation and has a heart rate of 80/min. Which intervention is generally indicated next?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Topic:** Newborn resuscitation

- **A.** Effective positive-pressure ventilation
- **B.** Immediate chest compressions before ventilation is attempted
- **C.** Routine oral feeding
- **D.** Delay care until the Apgar score at five minutes

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: A. Effective positive-pressure ventilation**

Newborn ki heart rate low hone ka common reason lungs mein air na pahunchna hai. Apnea aur HR 100 se kam ho toh effective ventilation priority hoti hai. Current neonatal protocol follow karna hai.

**Baaki options kyun galat hain?**

- **B.** Compressions generally effective ventilation ke baad HR 60 se kam rahe toh consider hoti hain.
- **C.** Apneic baby ko feed dena unsafe hai.
- **D.** Resuscitation Apgar score ka wait nahi karti.

</details>

## Thermal care

### Q2. A stable low-birth-weight infant is placed skin-to-skin with the mother under an appropriate care plan. What is a key benefit?

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Topic:** Thermal care

- **A.** Permanent elimination of all infection risk
- **B.** A need to stop monitoring the infant
- **C.** Prevention of every congenital disorder
- **D.** Improved thermal stability and breastfeeding support

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: D. Improved thermal stability and breastfeeding support**

Skin-to-skin contact se mother ki body heat baby ko warm rakhne mein help karti hai. Kangaroo mother care feeding aur bonding support kar sakti hai. Baby ka monitoring phir bhi zaroori hai.

**Baaki options kyun galat hain?**

- **A.** Infection risk zero nahi hota.
- **B.** Monitoring continue karni hoti hai.
- **C.** Congenital disorders isse prevent nahi hote.

</details>

## Neonatal jaundice

### Q3. A newborn develops visible jaundice within the first 24 hours after birth. What is the appropriate response?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Topic:** Neonatal jaundice

- **A.** Delay assessment until one month of age
- **B.** Give unprescribed herbal treatment
- **C.** Prompt bilirubin assessment and evaluation for pathological causes
- **D.** Assume it is always physiological

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: C. Prompt bilirubin assessment and evaluation for pathological causes**

Birth ke pehle 24 ghante mein jaundice early warning sign hai. Hemolysis ya doosri disease possible ho sakti hai. Bilirubin age in hours aur risk factors ke hisaab se assess hota hai.

**Baaki options kyun galat hain?**

- **A.** Delay bilirubin-related harm ka risk badha sakti hai.
- **B.** Herbal remedy proper assessment ka substitute nahi hai.
- **D.** Early jaundice routine physiological assume nahi karna chahiye.

</details>

## Neonatal hypoglycemia

### Q4. A newborn of a diabetic mother develops jitteriness and poor feeding. Which assessment is particularly important?

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Topic:** Neonatal hypoglycemia

- **A.** A delayed dental examination
- **B.** Blood glucose assessment according to neonatal protocol
- **C.** Only maternal fasting glucose
- **D.** Routine adult HbA1c as the sole emergency test

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: B. Blood glucose assessment according to neonatal protocol**

Pregnancy mein baby insulin zyada bana sakta hai. Delivery ke baad maa se glucose supply rukti hai aur baby ki sugar gir sakti hai. Jitteriness aur poor feeding ke saath glucose check zaroori hai.

**Baaki options kyun galat hain?**

- **A.** Dental exam unrelated hai.
- **C.** Maa ka glucose baby ka current level nahi batata.
- **D.** HbA1c acute neonatal glucose nahi batata.

</details>
