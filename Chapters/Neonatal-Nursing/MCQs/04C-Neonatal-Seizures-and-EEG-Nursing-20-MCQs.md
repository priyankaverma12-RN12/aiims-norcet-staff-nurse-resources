### Q01. Which investigation confirms neonatal epileptic seizure activity?

**Difficulty:** 🟢 Easy · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD · Original practice

- **A.** Electrocardiography
- **B.** Pulse oximetry alone
- **C.** Cranial ultrasound alone
- **D.** Electroencephalography

<details><summary>✅ Correct answer and detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: D. Electroencephalography**

- **A — Incorrect:** ECG heart electrical activity record karta hai, cerebral activity nahi.
- **B — Incorrect:** Oximeter desaturation detect karta hai, seizure discharge confirm nahi karta.
- **C — Incorrect:** Ultrasound structural brain findings de sakta hai par electrical seizure prove nahi karta.
- **D — Correct:** EEG brain electrical activity record karta hai. Neonatal events ko confirm karne ke liye EEG essential hai.

**Exam clue:** EEG confirms electrical seizures.

### Q08. A newborn's limb tremor begins after handling and stops with gentle support of the limb. What does this finding suggest?

**Difficulty:** 🔴 Difficult · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD · Original practice
