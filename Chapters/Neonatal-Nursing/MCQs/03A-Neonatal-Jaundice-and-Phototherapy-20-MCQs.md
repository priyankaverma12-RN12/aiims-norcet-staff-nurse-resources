### Q01. Which pigment is responsible for the yellow discoloration in neonatal jaundice?

**Difficulty:** 🟢 Easy · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD · Original practice

- **A.** Melanin
- **B.** Myoglobin
- **C.** Bilirubin
- **D.** Carotene as the usual neonatal cause

<details><summary>✅ Correct answer and detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: C. Bilirubin**

- **A — Incorrect:** Melanin normal skin pigmentation deta hai. Neonatal jaundice ka yellow color bilirubin se hota hai.
- **B — Incorrect:** Myoglobin muscle oxygen storage protein hai. Yeh usual neonatal jaundice pigment nahi hai.
- **C — Correct:** Red cells ke haemoglobin breakdown se bilirubin banta hai. Is pigment ke accumulate hone se skin aur sclera yellow dikhte hain.
- **D — Incorrect:** Carotene skin ko yellow kar sakta hai, lekin neonatal bilirubin accumulation ka cause nahi hai.

**Exam clue:** Yellow sclera ko bilirubin se jodo.

### Q02. Which organ normally conjugates bilirubin to facilitate its excretion?

**Difficulty:** 🟢 Easy · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD · Original practice

- **A.** Liver
- **B.** Spleen
- **C.** Pancreas
- **D.** Kidney

<details><summary>✅ Correct answer and detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: A. Liver**

- **A — Correct:** Liver bilirubin ko conjugate karke water soluble banata hai. Newborn mein yeh processing immature ho sakti hai.
- **B — Incorrect:** Spleen old red cells ke breakdown mein role rakhti hai, conjugation ka primary organ nahi hai.
- **C — Incorrect:** Pancreas digestive enzymes aur hormones banata hai. Bilirubin conjugation liver karta hai.
- **D — Incorrect:** Kidney water-soluble substances excrete karti hai, par bilirubin conjugation ka main organ nahi hai.

**Exam clue:** Production aur conjugation alag steps hain.

- **B.** Cephalhematoma

- **D.** Caput succedaneum

<details><summary>✅ Correct answer and detailed explanation</summary>

### Q15. A bilirubin result rises from 180 to 234 micromol/L over six hours during initial phototherapy. Which interpretation follows the NICE rapid-rise criterion?

**Difficulty:** 🔴 Difficult · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD · Original practice

- **A.** The rise is 54 micromol/L/hour and can be ignored
- **B.** The rise is 3 micromol/L/hour and proves treatment success
- **C.** The bilirubin has fallen by 9 micromol/L/hour
- **D.** The rise is 9 micromol/L/hour and requires prompt review for intensified treatment

<details><summary>✅ Correct answer and detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: D. The rise is 9 micromol/L/hour and requires prompt review for intensified treatment**

- **A — Incorrect:** 54 total change hai, hourly rate nahi. Rising bilirubin ignore nahi karna chahiye.
- **B — Incorrect:** 3 per hour calculation wrong hai. Treatment ke dauran rise concern hai.
- **C — Incorrect:** Final value initial value se higher hai, isliye fall nahi hua.
- **D — Correct:** Difference 54 hai aur six hours se divide karne par 9 per hour milta hai. NICE ka rapid-rise criterion 8.5 se zyada hai. Team ko prompt escalation review chahiye.
