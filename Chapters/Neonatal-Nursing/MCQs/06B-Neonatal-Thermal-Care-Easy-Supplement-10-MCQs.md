### Q01. Under the ICMR thermal-care workflow, how is a neonatal temperature of 34.0°C classified?

**Difficulty:** 🟢 Easy · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD · Original practice

- **A.** Mild hypothermia
- **B.** Moderate hypothermia
- **C.** Severe hypothermia
- **D.** Normal temperature

<details><summary>✅ Correct answer and detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: B. Moderate hypothermia**

- **A — Incorrect:** Mild range 36–36.4°C hai, 34 nahi.
- **B — Correct:** 34°C stated 32–35.9 range mein hai. Moderate hypothermia clinical care demand karti hai.
- **C — Incorrect:** Severe range below 32°C hai.
- **D — Incorrect:** Normal range 36.5–37.5°C hai.

**Exam clue:** ICMR moderate range: 32–35.9°C.

### Q09. What feeding status does the ICMR severe hypothermia pathway specify during initial stabilization?

**Difficulty:** 🟢 Easy · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD · Original practice

- **A.** Nil per oral with clinician-directed IV support
- **B.** Force a full bottle feed despite instability
- **C.** Give plain water instead of prescribed support
- **D.** Continue unrestricted oral feeds in every severe case

<details><summary>✅ Correct answer and detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: A. Nil per oral with clinician-directed IV support**

- **A — Correct:** Severe hypothermia mein NPO aur prescribed IV fluids pathway ka part hain. Stability aur nutrition plan team review kare.
- **B — Incorrect:** Force feeding unstable infant mein aspiration risk create karta hai.
- **C — Incorrect:** Plain water IV metabolic support substitute nahi hai.
- **D — Incorrect:** Unrestricted oral feeding severe category ke stated pathway se match nahi karta.

**Exam clue:** Severe hypothermia: stabilize before oral feeding.
