### Q01. What is the name of the first milk produced around the time of birth?

**Difficulty:** 🟢 Easy · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD · Original practice

- **A.** Colostrum
- **B.** Mature milk
- **C.** Transitional milk
- **D.** Foremilk

<details><summary>✅ Correct answer and detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: A. Colostrum**

- **A — Correct:** Colostrum first milk hota hai. Yeh small volume mein milta hai aur protective components provide karta hai.
- **B — Incorrect:** Mature milk lactation establish hone ke baad produce hota hai. Birth ke aas paas first milk ka naam colostrum hai.
- **C — Incorrect:** Transitional milk colostrum aur mature milk ke beech ka stage hai.
- **D — Incorrect:** Foremilk ek feed ke early portion ke liye use hone wala term hai, birth ke first milk ka naam nahi hai.

**Exam clue:** First milk aur feed ke first portion ko confuse mat karo.

### Q04. Which observation is characteristic of a good breast attachment?

**Difficulty:** 🟢 Easy · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD · Original practice

- **A.** The lower lip is tightly tucked inward
- **B.** The lower lip is turned outward
- **C.** The mouth is nearly closed around the nipple
- **D.** The chin remains well away from the breast

<details><summary>✅ Correct answer and detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: B. The lower lip is turned outward**

- **A — Incorrect:** Inward tucked lip shallow attachment ka clue ho sakti hai aur latch review chahiye.
- **B — Correct:** Lower lip outward turn aur wide mouth deep attachment ke signs hain. Isse effective milk transfer mein help milti hai.
- **C — Incorrect:** Nearly closed mouth se baby nipple ko shallow hold kar sakta hai. Deep latch ke liye wide opening useful hai.
- **D — Incorrect:** Good attachment mein chin breast ko touch kar sakti hai. Chin ka far hona ideal sign nahi hai.

**Exam clue:** Lower lip everted, mouth wide, chin close yaad rakho.

### Q15. On day 4, a term infant's birth weight was 3200 g and current weight is 2848 g. The infant has few wet diapers and shallow attachment. What is the most appropriate interpretation?

<details><summary>✅ Correct answer and detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: B. Use available mother's milk first, then appropriate donor human milk, then formula if needed while supporting milk production**

- **B — Correct:** Medical indication ke saath mother ka available milk first preference hai. Screened donor milk available ho toh next option ho sakta hai, aur formula needed ho toh clinician plan follow karo. Milk expression support continue rakho.
- **C — Incorrect:** Unscreened milk mein infection aur handling risks ho sakte hain. Approved safe donor milk process chahiye.
- **D — Incorrect:** Glucose water complete nutrition nahi provide karta. Required supplement appropriate nutritional source hona chahiye.

**Exam clue:** Medical supplementation aur ongoing breastfeeding support ek saath plan karo.
