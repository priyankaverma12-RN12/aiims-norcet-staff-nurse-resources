### Q01. Which measurement records the newborn's recumbent body length?

**Difficulty:** 🟢 Easy · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD · Original practice

- **A.** Crown to umbilicus
- **B.** Crown to heel
- **C.** Shoulder to wrist
- **D.** Heel to knee

<details><summary>✅ Correct answer and detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: B. Crown to heel**

- **A — Incorrect:** Umbilicus tak measurement trunk ka partial length hai.
- **B — Correct:** Crown se heel tak full body length milti hai. Growth chart mein actual length plot karo.
- **C — Incorrect:** Shoulder-wrist arm segment hai, whole body length nahi.
- **D — Incorrect:** Heel-knee lower leg ka segment hai.

**Exam clue:** Length = crown to heel.

### Q02. Which resting heart rate is within the MSD reference range for a quiet newborn?

**Difficulty:** 🟢 Easy · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD · Original practice

- **A.** 68 beats/min
- **B.** 190 beats/min
- **C.** 220 beats/min
- **D.** 132 beats/min

<details><summary>✅ Correct answer and detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: D. 132 beats/min**

- **A — Incorrect:** 68/min expected resting range se low hai; clinical review chahiye.
- **B — Incorrect:** 190/min quiet infant mein high hai; context aur causes assess karo.
- **C — Incorrect:** 220/min marked tachycardia hai, normal resting finding nahi.
- **D — Correct:** 132/min cited 100–160 resting range mein hai. Rhythm aur perfusion bhi assess karo.

**Exam clue:** Quiet newborn reference: 100–160/min.

### Q06. What does cryptorchidism describe?

**Difficulty:** 🟢 Easy · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD · Original practice

- **A.** Fluid around a descended testis
- **B.** An undescended testis
- **C.** A displaced urethral opening
- **D.** Twisting of the spermatic cord

<details><summary>✅ Correct answer and detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: B. An undescended testis**

- **A — Incorrect:** Fluid collection hydrocele ho sakta hai.
- **B — Correct:** Testis scrotum mein descend na kare toh cryptorchidism kehte hain. Follow-up arrange karo.
- **C — Incorrect:** Displaced urethral opening hypospadias/epispadias ka feature hai.
- **D — Incorrect:** Cord twisting testicular torsion hai, urgent condition.

**Exam clue:** Cryptorchidism = undescended testis.

### Q07. Which finding is expected when a typical hydrocele is examined by transillumination?

**Difficulty:** 🟢 Easy · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD · Original practice

- **A.** A hard bony ridge becomes visible
- **B.** No light can ever pass through the swelling
- **C.** An electrical waveform is recorded
- **D.** Light passes through the fluid-filled swelling

<details><summary>✅ Correct answer and detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: D. Light passes through the fluid-filled swelling**

- **A — Incorrect:** Bony ridge transillumination ka usual hydrocele result nahi.
- **B — Incorrect:** Opaque mass different pathology suggest kar sakti hai; all hydroceles opaque nahi.
- **C — Incorrect:** Electrical waveform is examination ka output nahi.

**Exam clue:** Hydrocele often transilluminates.

### Q10. Which newborn physical examination finding is consistent with possible acute testicular torsion rather than a simple hydrocele?

**Difficulty:** 🟢 Easy · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD · Original practice

- **A.** A soft translucent painless fluid swelling
- **B.** A firm discolored scrotal swelling
- **C.** A normally descended testis with no swelling
- **D.** A small transient hymenal tag

<details><summary>✅ Correct answer and detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: B. A firm discolored scrotal swelling**

- **A — Incorrect:** Soft transilluminating fluid hydrocele se fit ho sakta hai; still assess.
- **B — Correct:** Firm ecchymotic swelling torsion concern raise karti hai. Urgent clinician assessment chahiye.
- **C — Incorrect:** Normal exam torsion ka specific warning finding nahi.
- **D — Incorrect:** Hymenal tag female genital finding hai, scrotal pathology nahi.

**Exam clue:** Firm discoloration is a surgical warning.
