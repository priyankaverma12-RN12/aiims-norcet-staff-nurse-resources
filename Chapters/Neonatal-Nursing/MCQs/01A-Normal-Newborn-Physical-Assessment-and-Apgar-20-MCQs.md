### Q01. A quiet term newborn has visibly irregular respiratory intervals but no chest retractions. Which method gives the most reliable bedside respiratory rate?

**Difficulty:** 🟡 Medium · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD · Original practice

- **A.** Count for 10 seconds and multiply by six
- **B.** Observe abdominal or chest movement for a full minute
- **C.** Use the heart rate as a surrogate respiratory rate
- **D.** Count only the deepest breaths for 30 seconds

<details><summary>✅ Correct answer and detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: B. Observe abdominal or chest movement for a full minute**

- **A — Incorrect:** Chhota sample irregular rhythm mein misleading ho sakta hai. Multiply karne se actual missed breaths wapas nahi aate.
- **B — Correct:** Newborn ki breathing ka rhythm regular adult jaisa nahi hota. Ek poora minute dekhne se chhote pauses aur rate ki variation bhi count ho jaati hai.
- **C — Incorrect:** Heart rate circulation ka measure hai. Isse har breath ka number nahi milta.
- **D — Incorrect:** Shallow breaths bhi actual breaths hain. Sirf deep breaths count karne se rate kam record ho sakta hai.

**Exam clue:** Irregular neonatal breathing mein observation ka full minute yaad rakho.

### Q02. A warm term newborn has blue hands and feet but a pink tongue, normal breathing and good activity. Which finding would change the nurse's assessment to urgent evaluation for central cyanosis?

**Difficulty:** 🟡 Medium · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD · Original practice

- **A.** Transient bluish fingers during a diaper change
- **B.** Symmetrical bluish toes with a pink trunk
- **C.** Pink lips with cool extremities
- **D.** Blue discoloration of the tongue and oral mucosa

<details><summary>✅ Correct answer and detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: D. Blue discoloration of the tongue and oral mucosa**

- **A — Incorrect:** Sirf fingers ka temporary blue hona peripheral circulation ki wajah se ho sakta hai. Phir bhi baby ki overall condition dekhni hai.
- **B — Incorrect:** Toes ka symmetric blue color aur pink trunk acrocyanosis se fit kar sakta hai. Yeh central cyanosis ka proof nahi hai.
- **C — Incorrect:** Cool extremities ko warm karke reassess karna chahiye. Pink lips ke saath yeh finding akeli central cyanosis nahi batati.
- **D — Correct:** Tongue aur oral mucosa ka blue hona central cyanosis ka clue hai. Blood oxygenation ki problem ho sakti hai, isliye urgent clinical assessment zaroori hai.

**Exam clue:** Peripheral color aur tongue ka color alag se assess karo.

### Q03. During a routine newborn examination, the brachial pulses are easily felt but femoral pulses remain weak on repeated examination when the infant is calm. Which abnormality is most important to exclude?

**Difficulty:** 🔴 Difficult · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD · Original practice

- **A.** Coarctation of the aorta
- **B.** Isolated patent foramen ovale
- **C.** Physiological peripheral acrocyanosis
- **D.** Uncomplicated caput succedaneum

<details><summary>✅ Correct answer and detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: A. Coarctation of the aorta**

- **A — Correct:** Coarctation mein aorta ka ek hissa narrow hota hai. Lower body tak blood flow kam pahunch sakta hai, isliye arm ke comparison mein femoral pulse weak milne par clinician ko inform karo.
- **B — Incorrect:** Patent foramen ovale atria ke beech fetal opening ka persisting rehna hai. Yeh pattern of weak leg pulses ko usually explain nahi karta.
- **C — Incorrect:** Acrocyanosis peripheral color change hai. Brachial aur femoral pulse ka difference structural obstruction ka clue ho sakta hai.
- **D — Incorrect:** Caput scalp ka swelling hai. Isse lower limb arterial pulse selectively weak nahi hota.

**Exam clue:** Upper aur lower limb pulses compare karna cardiac screening ka important hissa hai.

### Q05. A well newborn develops a localized parietal swelling after delivery. The swelling is confined by cranial sutures. Which anatomical explanation best fits the finding?

**Difficulty:** 🟡 Medium · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD · Original practice

- **D.** Cerebrospinal fluid has accumulated within enlarged brain ventricles

<details><summary>✅ Correct answer and detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: C. Blood has accumulated beneath the periosteum of one skull bone**

- **A — Incorrect:** Superficial scalp edema caput ke saath fit karta hai. Woh suture lines ko cross kar sakta hai.

- **C — Correct:** Cephalhematoma mein skull bone aur usko cover karne wali periosteum ke beech blood collect hota hai. Periosteum sutures par attached hoti hai, isliye swelling ek bone tak limited rehti hai.
- **D — Incorrect:** Hydrocephalus brain ke andar fluid spaces ke enlargement ko kehte hain. Yeh localized external scalp collection nahi hai.

**Exam clue:** Suture boundary se scalp swelling ka anatomical plane samajh aata hai.

### Q19. An infant's five-minute Apgar score is 5 while the team continues indicated support. Which documentation plan follows the standard repeat-scoring recommendation?

**Difficulty:** 🟡 Medium · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD · Original practice

- **A.** Stop scoring because the five-minute assessment is the final assessment in every infant
- **B.** Repeat Apgar at five-minute intervals through 20 minutes as indicated
- **C.** Calculate a new score every 30 seconds instead of recording clinical observations
- **D.** Use the one-minute score alone for all future clinical records

<details><summary>✅ Correct answer and detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: B. Repeat Apgar at five-minute intervals through 20 minutes as indicated**

- **A — Incorrect:** Five-minute score sab babies mein final nahi hota. Low score mein repeat documentation important hai.
- **B — Correct:** Five-minute score 7 se kam ho toh standard additional scoring har five minute par 20 minute tak ki jaati hai. Actual clinical support aur ongoing assessment separate continue hoti hai.
- **C — Incorrect:** Thirty-second scoring standard schedule nahi hai. Clinical observations continuously zaroori ho sakti hain, lekin unhe Apgar ke schedule se confuse mat karo.
- **D — Incorrect:** One-minute score sirf ek time point hai. Isse later response ya condition accurately document nahi hoti.

**Exam clue:** Apgar documentation treatment ka substitute nahi hai.

### Q20. Two newborns have the same low one-minute Apgar score. One was born preterm and the other received maternal sedating medication before birth. Which interpretation is valid?

**Difficulty:** 🔴 Difficult · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD · Original practice

- **A.** The score alone proves identical causes of neonatal depression
- **B.** The score alone establishes the same long-term neurological prognosis
- **C.** The score excludes medication and gestational-age effects
- **D.** The score describes early clinical status but cannot establish intrapartum asphyxia by itself

<details><summary>✅ Correct answer and detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: D. The score describes early clinical status but cannot establish intrapartum asphyxia by itself**

- **A — Incorrect:** Same score alag causes se aa sakta hai. Isliye sirf number se cause identify nahi hota.
- **B — Incorrect:** Long-term prognosis ek single early score se fix nahi kiya ja sakta. Clinical course aur further assessment important hain.
- **C — Incorrect:** Gestational age aur medications score ke components ko affect kar sakte hain. Unhe interpretation se exclude karna galat hai.
- **D — Correct:** Apgar score observed condition ko summarize karta hai. Prematurity aur maternal medicines tone, breathing aur response ko affect kar sakte hain. Asphyxia ki diagnosis ke liye additional clinical evidence chahiye.

**Exam clue:** Apgar ek descriptive score hai, standalone etiological diagnosis nahi hai.
