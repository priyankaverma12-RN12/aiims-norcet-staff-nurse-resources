- **D.** It replaces the patient's priorities with age-based targets

**Baaki options kyun galat hain?**

- **A.** Normal BP se meal access ya isolation ka problem solve nahi hota. Social factors bhi health aur independence ko affect karte hain.
- **C.** Diagnosis list zaroori hai, lekin CGA ka goal assessment se practical coordinated interventions tak pahunchna hai. Sirf list incomplete rahegi.
- **D.** Patient ki priorities care plan ko meaningful banati hain. Age-based assumptions se patient ke actual goals ignore ho sakte hain.
