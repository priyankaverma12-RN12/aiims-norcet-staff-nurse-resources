### Q1. A dependent older adult has repeatedly been left without prescribed meals by a person entrusted with daily care. Which safeguarding concern is most directly suggested?

**Difficulty:** 🟡 Medium · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD · NORCET / Staff Nurse / CHO — Original practice

- **A.** Financial exploitation
- **B.** Age-related appetite change alone
- **C.** Neglect
- **D.** Self-neglect alone

<details><summary>✅ Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct answer: C. Neglect**

**Kyun sahi hai?** Neglect mein responsible caregiver zaroori care nahi deta. Meals provide na karna patient ki nutrition aur health ko harm kar sakta hai.

**Baaki options kyun galat hain?**

- **A.** Financial exploitation mein paise ya property ka unauthorized use hota hai. Yahan food care miss hone ka concern hai.
- **B.** Appetite kam ho sakti hai, lekin yahan meals provide hi nahi kiye gaye. Isliye appetite alone se situation explain nahi hoti.
- **D.** Self-neglect mein patient apni needs meet nahi kar paata ya nahi karta. Yahan entrusted caregiver ki responsibility ka concern hai.
