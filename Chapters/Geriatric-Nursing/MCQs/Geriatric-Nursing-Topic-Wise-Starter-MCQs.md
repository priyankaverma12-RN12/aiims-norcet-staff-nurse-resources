## Delirium

### Q1. An older inpatient develops sudden fluctuating confusion and inattention after an infection. Which condition is most likely?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Topic:** Delirium

- **A.** Delirium
- **B.** Stable chronic dementia as the only explanation
- **C.** Normal aging
- **D.** Uncomplicated hearing loss alone

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: A. Delirium**

Delirium brain function ka sudden change hai jo infection, medicine ya metabolic problem se trigger ho sakta hai. Attention fluctuate kar sakti hai. Nurse underlying cause aur safety urgently assess karti hai.

**Baaki options kyun galat hain?**

- **B.** Dementia usually gradual hoti hai, delirium saath bhi ho sakta hai.
- **C.** Acute confusion normal aging nahi hai.
- **D.** Hearing loss alone fluctuating attention explain nahi karti.

</details>

## Falls

### Q2. An older adult has dizziness after starting a new antihypertensive and reports a fall. What should the nurse prioritize?

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Topic:** Falls

- **A.** Encourage walking alone immediately
- **B.** Assume all falls are unavoidable in older age
- **C.** Stop all medicines permanently without review
- **D.** Review orthostatic blood pressure, medicines and fall risks

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: D. Review orthostatic blood pressure, medicines and fall risks**

Dawai se BP khade hone par gir sakta hai. Nurse lying-standing BP safely check karti hai aur medicine review arrange karti hai. Falls ko prevent karne ke practical steps bhi chahiye.

**Baaki options kyun galat hain?**

- **A.** Walking alone injury risk badha sakti hai.
- **B.** Falls aging ka inevitable part nahi hain.
- **C.** Medication changes clinician ke saath hone chahiye.

</details>

## Polypharmacy

### Q3. An older patient takes several prescriptions and over-the-counter products from different providers. Which intervention is useful?

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Topic:** Polypharmacy

- **A.** Discard the medication list
- **B.** Recommend duplicate doses whenever a dose is missed
- **C.** Perform medication reconciliation and arrange interaction review
- **D.** Assume supplements cannot interact

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: C. Perform medication reconciliation and arrange interaction review**

Polypharmacy ka matlab kai medicines lena hai. Medicines aapas mein interact kar sakti hain. Complete list banane se duplication aur unsafe combinations identify karne mein help milti hai.

**Baaki options kyun galat hain?**

- **A.** List safe review ke liye chahiye.
- **B.** Missed dose ka plan drug-specific hota hai.
- **D.** Supplements bhi interact kar sakte hain.

</details>

## Function

### Q4. Which assessment best helps determine an older adult's need for assistance with daily self-care?

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Topic:** Function

- **A.** Date of birth alone
- **B.** Ability to perform activities of daily living
- **C.** Hair color alone
- **D.** Shoe size alone

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: B. Ability to perform activities of daily living**

Activities of daily living mein bathing, dressing, toileting aur feeding jaise kaam aate hain. Nurse dekhti hai ki patient kaunsa kaam khud kar sakta hai aur kahan help chahiye.

**Baaki options kyun galat hain?**

- **A.** Age alone care need decide nahi karti.
- **C.** Hair color functional ability nahi batata.
- **D.** Shoe size independence assess nahi karta.

</details>
