### Q1. A community-dwelling older adult reports two falls in six months and is afraid of falling again. Which next action best follows a comprehensive fall-prevention pathway?

**Baaki options kyun galat hain?**

- **B.** Balance score ek domain batata hai, lekin medication, environmental ya cardiovascular contributors miss ho sakte hain. Multifactorial assessment broader hai.
- **C.** Exercise ka benefit individualized prescription se milta hai. Physical capacity aur safety evaluate kiye bina fixed intensity prescribe karna appropriate nahi hai.
