### Q1. An independent 78-year-old copes well at rest but develops marked fatigue during a minor febrile illness. Which age-associated principle best explains the disproportionate functional change?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Priority:** High | **Topic:** Reduced physiological reserve | **Type:** Original practice

- **A.** A universal loss of consciousness with aging
- **B.** Increased maximal cardiac output
- **C.** Reduced physiological reserve
- **D.** An inevitable complete loss of immunity

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: C. Reduced physiological reserve**

Hamare organs rest ke samay kaam kar sakte hain, lekin stress aane par extra capacity ki zaroorat hoti hai. Umar ke saath yeh reserve kam ho sakta hai, isliye chhoti illness bhi function par bada asar daal sakti hai.

**Baaki options kyun galat hain?**

- **A.** Aging se consciousness ka complete loss hona expected nahi hai. Aisa change ho toh nurse ko acute illness assess karni chahiye.
- **B.** Maximal cardiac output badhne ki jagah exercise ke samay aksar kam ho jaata hai. Isliye yeh explanation physiological reserve ke opposite hai.
- **D.** Immune response mein changes hote hain, lekin immunity poori tarah khatam nahi hoti. Patient ki fatigue ko ek hi system ki failure maan lena sahi nahi hai.

### Q2. An older reader moves a book farther away to make small print clear but has unchanged distance vision. Which mechanism best fits this pattern?

- **C.** Loss of the corneal blink reflex

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

Lens ko paas ki cheez focus karne ke liye apna shape badalna padta hai. Umar ke saath lens ki flexibility kam ho jaati hai, isliye presbyopia mein paas ka print dhundhla lagta hai.

**Baaki options kyun galat hain?**

- **B.** Retinal artery occlusion mein aam taur par sudden significant visual loss hota hai. Sirf kitab ko door rakhne se theek hone wala pattern usse match nahi karta.
- **C.** Blink reflex aankh ki surface ko protect karta hai. Yeh lens ki near focusing ability ko control nahi karta.
- **D.** Optic nerve cut hone par affected eye ka visual signal brain tak nahi jaata. Is patient ki distance vision unchanged hai, isliye yeh explanation fit nahi hota.

- **D.** Itching common ho sakti hai, lekin persistent symptoms ki underlying cause bhi ho sakti hai. Nurse ko skin dekhni aur zaroorat par review arrange karna chahiye.

- **C.** Rubbing skin ko strong nahi banata aur fragile tissue ko damage kar sakta hai. Nurse ko pressure aur friction reduce karna chahiye.

### Q12. An older adult has persistently widened pulse pressure on repeated measurements. Which age-associated vascular mechanism can contribute to this finding?

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Priority:** High | **Topic:** Vascular stiffness | **Type:** Original practice

- **A.** Reduced elasticity of large arteries

- **C.** Increased aortic compliance
- **D.** Universal severe mitral stenosis

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: A. Reduced elasticity of large arteries**

Large arteries jab flexible hoti hain, toh heart ki ejected blood volume ko accommodate karti hain. Stiff arteries mein systolic pressure badh sakta hai aur pulse pressure widen ho sakta hai. Persistent abnormal reading ka clinical review phir bhi zaroori hai.

**Baaki options kyun galat hain?**

- **B.** Severe circulating volume loss acute instability cause kar sakta hai. Yeh stable widened pulse pressure ka usual age-related mechanism nahi hai.
- **C.** Compliance ka matlab artery ki expand hone ki ability hai. Increased compliance stiff artery ke effect ke opposite hai.
- **D.** Mitral stenosis specific valve disease hai aur normal aging ka universal feature nahi hai. Pulse pressure ka pattern alone is diagnosis ko confirm nahi karta.

- **D.** Bone remodeling adulthood mein bhi continue hoti hai. Aging mein balance badal sakta hai, na ki remodeling ka complete stop hona.
