### Q1. Which age-associated combination can increase susceptibility to urinary leakage without making incontinence an inevitable consequence of ageing?

**Difficulty:** 🟡 Medium · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD · NORCET / Staff Nurse — Original practice

- **A.** Increased bladder capacity with stronger contractility
- **B.** Reduced ability to postpone voiding and weaker bladder contractility
- **C.** Complete loss of urine production
- **D.** Universal destruction of bladder sensory nerves

<details><summary>✅ Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct answer: B. Reduced ability to postpone voiding and weaker bladder contractility**

**Kyun sahi hai?** Aging ke saath bladder ki extra capacity aur emptying ability kam ho sakti hai. Isse leakage ka risk badhta hai, lekin har older person incontinent nahi hota.

**Baaki options kyun galat hain?**

- **A.** Badi capacity aur strong emptying se described age-associated problem explain nahi hoti. Bladder function individual patient mein assess karo.
- **C.** Urine production kidney ka kaam hai. Complete loss serious problem hai, normal aging nahi.
- **D.** Har older person ke bladder nerves destroy nahi hote. Isliye age ke basis par neurological diagnosis mat lagao.
