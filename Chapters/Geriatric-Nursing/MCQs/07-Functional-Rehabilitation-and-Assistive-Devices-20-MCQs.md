### Q1. Which statement best distinguishes rehabilitation from a cure-only approach in an older adult with permanent joint damage?

**Difficulty:** 🟡 Medium · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD · NORCET / Staff Nurse — Original practice

- **A.** It is useful only after normal joint anatomy is restored
- **B.** It replaces all medical management of the joint disease
- **C.** It can optimize function through task adaptation, symptom control and assistive products even when structural damage persists
- **D.** It is restricted to people who can recover without equipment

<details><summary>✅ Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct answer: C. It can optimize function through task adaptation, symptom control and assistive products even when structural damage persists**

**Kyun sahi hai?** Rehabilitation ka goal daily function improve karna hai. Joint damage permanent ho tab bhi pain management, adapted tasks aur suitable equipment se patient zyada independent ho sakta hai.

**Baaki options kyun galat hain?**

- **A.** Normal anatomy restore hona har case mein possible nahi hai. Function improve karne ke liye cure ka wait zaroori nahi hai.
- **B.** Medical treatment aur rehabilitation ek doosre ko complement karte hain. Rehabilitation se necessary disease treatment automatically stop nahi hota.
- **D.** Assistive products rehabilitation ka useful part ho sakte hain. Equipment ki need se patient ineligible nahi hota.

- **B.** Unchanged score har functional domain mein no improvement prove nahi karta. Other measures bhi use karo.
