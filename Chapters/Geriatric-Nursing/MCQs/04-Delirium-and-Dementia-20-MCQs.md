**Baaki options kyun galat hain?**

- **B.** Postoperative tiredness possible hai, lekin new attention ya activity change ko routine recovery maan lena underlying illness miss kar sakta hai. Further observations aur assessment chahiye.
- **C.** Depression differential ho sakti hai, lekin one-day acute change se diagnosis establish nahi hota. Acute medical contributors pehle assess karne chahiye.
