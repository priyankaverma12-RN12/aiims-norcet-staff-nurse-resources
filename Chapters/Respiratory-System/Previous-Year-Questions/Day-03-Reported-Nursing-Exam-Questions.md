### 🔵 BHU Staff Nurse, 22 October 2019 — retrieved report

**Adaptation 1 · Difficulty:** 🟡 Medium · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD · Source-based practice

A patient receiving controlled oxygen through a 28% Venturi adapter has a respiratory rate of 32/min. The prescribed concentration remains unchanged. Which principle should guide the nurse's review of the gas supply?

- **A.** Reduce the oxygen source flow below the adapter instructions to limit carbon dioxide retention.
- **B.** Assume every oxygen interface must be limited to 4 L/min in COPD.
- **C.** Check the adapter's specified flow and whether total delivered gas flow meets inspiratory demand, then reassess saturation and breathing.
- **D.** Choose a different adapter solely according to the colour of the patient's oxygen tubing.

<details><summary>✅ Answer and detailed Hinglish explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: C. Check the adapter's specified flow and whether total delivered gas flow meets inspiratory demand, then reassess saturation and breathing.**

**Kyun sahi hai?** Venturi adapter oxygen ke saath room air mix karta hai. Rapid breathing mein patient ki inspiratory demand badh sakti hai. Manufacturer ke specified source flow aur total gas flow ko check karo. Kuch devices mein recommended minimum se upar source flow badhane se total gas flow badhta hai, intended oxygen concentration zaroori nahi badle. Prescribed target, clinical condition aur ABG ke according trained team oxygen adjust kare. Severe hypoxemia ya deterioration mein urgent help lo.

**Baaki options kyun galat hain?**

- **A.** Minimum flow se neeche device operate karne par intended delivery unreliable ho sakti hai.
- **B.** COPD ke liye universal 4 L/min maximum valid rule nahi hai. Device aur clinical need matter karte hain.
- **D.** Tubing colour concentration ya patient need ka reliable indicator nahi hai.

**Historical source and evidence:** [BHU reported question](https://testbook.com/question-answer/for-a-chronic-obstructive-pulmonary-disease-patien--619ba1d1c03703fc10d7edde). Source selects Venturi mask. This is a newly worded device-management application, not the source stem or options. Source's blanket 4 L/min cap and sole hypoxic-drive explanation are outdated. Related reports: [OSSSC Nursing Officer, 19 March 2023](https://testbook.com/question-answer/the-nurse-must-know-that-the-most-accurate-oxygen--6a3baaa5e864f40b5f6620b7), [HPSSC Hamirpur Staff Nurse, 5 August 2021](https://testbook.com/question-answer/the-nurse-must-know-that-the-most-accurate-oxygen--697b2d68bfca0b8e8a516498/amp), [PGIMER Chandigarh Staff Nurse Grade II, 2015](https://testbook.com/question-answer/the-most-accurate-oxygen-delivery-system-is-the--62b70a73a5c731008e242be9/amp). Their indexed question text supports the same device-selection concept; direct page reopens failed, official keys pending. No separate question slot per repeated report.

[Clinical reference](https://bmjopenrespres.bmj.com/content/4/1/e000170)

### 🔵 AIIMS NORCET, 1 September 2020 — retrieved memory-based report

**Adaptation 2 · Difficulty:** 🟡 Medium · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD · Source-based practice

During a routine oxygen-device check, a patient reports mild nasal dryness. A repeat assessment then shows a new decline in responsiveness. Which finding should determine the immediate nursing priority?

- **A.** The nasal discomfort because it was reported first.
- **B.** The new change in responsiveness because it may indicate respiratory or systemic deterioration.
- **C.** The need to replace a clean oxygen mask on its routine schedule.
- **D.** The patient's preference for a warm drink before reassessment.

<details><summary>✅ Answer and detailed Hinglish explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: B. The new change in responsiveness because it may indicate respiratory or systemic deterioration.**

**Kyun sahi hai?** New responsiveness decline serious warning sign ho sakta hai. Airway, breathing, circulation, oxygen saturation, ventilation aur mental status promptly assess karo aur emergency escalation karo. Hypercapnia, hypoxemia aur doosre causes evaluate hone chahiye. Nasal dryness bhi address karo, lekin patient stable hone ke baad. Source ki ambiguous priority ko clinical emergency mein copy nahi karna hai.

**Baaki options kyun galat hain?**

- **A.** Dryness important comfort concern hai, lekin acute altered responsiveness se lower priority hai.
- **C.** Routine equipment schedule emergency assessment se pehle nahi hai.
- **D.** Reduced alertness mein oral drink aspiration risk create kar sakta hai.

**Historical source and evidence:** [NORCET 2020 reported question and key](https://testbook.com/question-answer/in-copd-patients-given-o2-by-venturi-mask-then-nur--6177bd978d130f22861a0e17). The publisher's answer is nasal dryness assessment; the reported question does not establish the patient's acute status. No official key retrieved. Our distinct scenario explicitly adds new altered responsiveness, so B is the clinical answer, not a correction to an authenticated historical key.

[Clinical reference](https://bmjopenrespres.bmj.com/content/4/1/e000170)

### 🔵 HPSSSB Staff Nurse, October 2020 — retrieved report

**Adaptation 3 · Difficulty:** 🟡 Medium · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD · Source-based practice

A stable patient with chronic bronchitis practises abdominal breathing during supervised rehabilitation. Which observation best suggests the intended diaphragmatic technique?

- **A.** The shoulders rise maximally with every inhalation.
- **B.** The patient performs repeated prolonged breath-holds.
- **C.** The upper chest remains rigid while the patient strains forcefully.
- **D.** The abdomen gently rises during inspiration while the upper chest stays relatively relaxed.

<details><summary>✅ Answer and detailed Hinglish explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: D. The abdomen gently rises during inspiration while the upper chest stays relatively relaxed.**

**Kyun sahi hai?** Diaphragm inspiration mein contract karke neeche move karta hai. Relaxed abdominal breathing mein pet gently bahar move kar sakta hai. Patient ko comfortable posture, slow breathing aur relaxed shoulders samjhao. Severe hyperinflation wale kuch COPD patients mein technique uncomfortable ho sakti hai. Pulmonary rehabilitation professional individual suitability assess kare aur distress badhne par technique stop karke reassess kare.

**Baaki options kyun galat hain?**

- **A.** Maximum shoulder elevation accessory muscle overuse ho sakti hai, intended relaxed diaphragmatic technique nahi.
- **B.** Prolonged breath-hold diaphragmatic breathing ka essential step nahi hai aur dizziness ho sakti hai.
- **C.** Forceful strain comfort aur breathing efficiency worsen kar sakta hai.

**Historical source and evidence:** [HPSSSB October 2020 reported question](https://testbook.com/question-answer/howa-nurse-will-teach-breathing-exercise-for--61b9a1aec3c5dc3695351680). Retrieved source identifies diaphragmatic breathing; this independent return-demonstration scenario uses new wording and options. Official question number and key unverified.

[Clinical reference](https://www.lung.org/lung-health-diseases/wellness/breathing-exercises)

**YouTube MCQ practice: verification pending**. Specific diaphragmatic-breathing question discussion not verified; teaching-only resources do not count.

### 🔵 RRB Staff Nurse, 20 July 2019, Shift I — retrieved report

**Adaptation 4 · Difficulty:** 🟡 Medium · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD · Source-based practice

A stable COPD patient asks why gently narrowing the lips during a prolonged expiration can ease breathlessness. Which physiological explanation is most appropriate?

- **A.** It can create modest expiratory back pressure that helps limit premature small-airway collapse.
- **B.** It permanently restores destroyed alveolar walls.
- **C.** It prevents all pulmonary blood flow during expiration.
- **D.** It forces the patient to exhale at the highest possible speed.

<details><summary>✅ Answer and detailed Hinglish explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: A. It can create modest expiratory back pressure that helps limit premature small-airway collapse.**

**Kyun sahi hai?** Gently pursed lips se exhalation ke waqt halka positive pressure ban sakta hai. Isse small airways jaldi collapse hone ki tendency kam ho sakti hai aur air ko bahar nikalne ke liye more time milta hai. Patient ko relaxed exhalation aur comfortable pace sikhana hai. Har patient ka response same nahi hota. Acute emergency mein breathing exercise alone treatment nahi hai.

**Baaki options kyun galat hain?**

- **B.** Destroyed alveolar walls breathing exercise se regenerate nahi hoti.
- **C.** Pulmonary perfusion ko stop karna technique ka purpose nahi hai.
- **D.** Forceful rapid exhalation air trapping ya fatigue badha sakti hai.

**Historical source and evidence:** [RRB Staff Nurse reported question](https://testbook.com/question-answer/which-is-an-intervention-for-a-patient-with-chroni--619e1e567a81b38e9a40f07b). Source selects pursed-lip breathing as an intervention. This is a new mechanism-based adaptation, not the original stem. Official key unverified.

[Clinical reference](https://www.cochrane.org/evidence/CD008250_breathing-exercises-chronic-obstructive-pulmonary-disease)
