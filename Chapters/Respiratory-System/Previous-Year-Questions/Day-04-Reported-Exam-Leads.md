### 🔵 ESIC Staff Nurse — 26 February 2019, Shift III — retrieved third-party report

**Adaptation 1 · Difficulty:** 🟡 Medium · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD · Source-based practice

A patient living with HIV has a tuberculin skin test read at 54 hours. The transverse induration is 6 mm. Under the CDC risk-based interpretation criteria, which conclusion is appropriate?

- **A.** The reaction is negative because every patient requires at least 15 mm of induration.
- **B.** The reaction meets the positive threshold for this risk category, and further evaluation is needed to exclude TB disease.
- **C.** The reaction confirms infectious pulmonary tuberculosis without any additional investigation.
- **D.** The result must be interpreted using the diameter of redness rather than induration.

<details><summary>✅ Answer and detailed Hinglish explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: B. The reaction meets the positive threshold for this risk category, and further evaluation is needed to exclude TB disease.**

**Kyun sahi hai?** HIV mein TB disease develop hone ka risk zyada hota hai. CDC risk-based criteria mein 5 mm ya usse zyada induration positive hai. Yahan 6 mm hai aur reading 48–72 hour window mein hui hai. Positive test immune sensitization support karta hai, active disease ka proof nahi. Further clinical evaluation aur appropriate investigations zaroori hain. Local TB programme criteria bhi follow karo.

**Baaki options kyun galat hain?**

- **A.** 15 mm threshold no known risk factors wali CDC category ke liye hai. Har patient ke liye same threshold nahi.
- **C.** Positive TST alone infectious pulmonary TB confirm nahi karta. Disease evaluation chahiye.
- **D.** Redness ko measurement mein use nahi karte. Firm induration ka transverse diameter measure hota hai.

**Exam trick:** Risk category pehle, induration threshold baad mein.

**Historical evidence:** [Retrieved report](https://testbook.com/question-answer/mantoux-test-purified-protein-derivative-is-sign--619cf0e7ecc1b27849a283c6). Retrieved report gives a 15 mm erythema answer. It confuses redness with induration and lacks an explicit risk category. Do not copy that clinical interpretation. This new scenario uses CDC criteria explicitly, not the source's original answer. Official paper/key not independently authenticated.

[Clinical reference](https://www.cdc.gov/tb/hcp/testing-diagnosis/tuberculin-skin-test.html)

### 🔵 JIPMER Staff Nurse — 4 June 2013 — retrieved third-party report

**Adaptation 2 · Difficulty:** 🟡 Medium · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD · Source-based practice

Before teaching gravity-assisted airway clearance to a stable adult with bronchiectasis, which information should guide selection of the drainage position?

- **A.** A fixed prone position for every patient, regardless of the affected lung region.
- **B.** Only the patient's height, without reviewing respiratory findings.
- **C.** The affected bronchopulmonary segments, clinical contraindications, and individual tolerance.
- **D.** A rule that the head must always be lower than the chest.

<details><summary>✅ Answer and detailed Hinglish explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: C. The affected bronchopulmonary segments, clinical contraindications, and individual tolerance.**

**Kyun sahi hai?** Postural drainage ka goal gravity ki help se involved airway segments ki secretions mobilize karna hai. Har patient mein affected lung region aur clinical condition alag ho sakti hai. Imaging aur respiratory physiotherapy assessment se appropriate position decide hoti hai. Reflux ya intolerance mein modified technique useful ho sakti hai.

**Baaki options kyun galat hain?**

- **A.** Prone position selected segments mein useful ho sakti hai, lekin universally correct nahi.
- **B.** Height alone involved segment ya safety decide nahi karti.
- **D.** Head-down tilt har patient ke liye safe ya necessary nahi hota.

**Exam trick:** Drainage posture is individualized.

**Historical evidence:** [Retrieved report](https://testbook.com/question-answer/to-do-percussion-and-postural-drainage-of-lungs-in--6221df731082657f8ea987d8). Retrieved third-party report selects prone position without specifying a segment. Indexed OSSSC Nursing Officer 19 March 2023 report appears to repeat the same theme, but direct retrieval failed. [OSSSC lead](https://testbook.com/question-answer/to-do-percussion-and-postural-drainage-of-lungs-in--6a3baa939f60eca7e76828b7). Consolidate as one related learning concept. Official keys unverified.

[Clinical reference](https://thorax.bmj.com/content/74/Suppl_1/1)

### 🔵 JSSC Grade A Nurse — 16 July 2022 — retrieved third-party report

**Adaptation 3 · Difficulty:** 🟡 Medium · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD · Source-based practice

A respiratory clinician requests FEV1 and FVC for diagnostic assessment, while a postoperative patient needs visual feedback for sustained inspiration. Which device allocation is correct?

- **A.** Diagnostic spirometer for the first task and incentive spirometer for the second task.
- **B.** Incentive spirometer for both tasks because it directly measures FEV1 and FVC.
- **C.** Pulse oximeter for the first task and peak flow meter for sustained inspiration.
- **D.** Nebulizer for both tasks because aerosol flow measures lung volumes.

<details><summary>✅ Answer and detailed Hinglish explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: A. Diagnostic spirometer for the first task and incentive spirometer for the second task.**

**Kyun sahi hai?** Diagnostic spirometry mein standardized forced expiration se FEV1 aur FVC assess kiye ja sakte hain. Incentive spirometer ka purpose slow sustained inspiration encourage karna hai, especially selected postoperative care mein. Dono ke naam similar hain lekin purpose aur technique different hain. Nurse patient ko correct device aur technique samjhaaye.

**Baaki options kyun galat hain?**

- **B.** Ordinary incentive spirometer diagnostic FEV1 aur FVC measure nahi karta.
- **C.** Pulse oximeter oxygen saturation estimate karta hai. Peak flow meter expiratory flow assess karta hai, sustained inspiration feedback nahi.
- **D.** Nebulizer liquid medicine ko aerosol mein convert karta hai. Ye diagnostic spirometry device nahi hai.

**Exam trick:** Incentive = inspiration training. Diagnostic spirometry = measured expiratory indices.

**Historical evidence:** [Retrieved report](https://testbook.com/question-answer/which-of-the-following-chest-physiotherapy-devices--62d97503b03e936ff40fcaff). Retrieved report labels incentive spirometry as the device measuring FEV1 and FVC, and its explanation describes forced expiration. This is a clinical/device conflation. The corrected independent scenario is not the reported original question or key. Official paper/key not independently authenticated.

[Clinical reference](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/sites/books/NBK572114/)

### 🔵 RRB Nursing Superintendent — 29 April 2025, Shift I — retrieved third-party report

**Adaptation 4 · Difficulty:** 🟡 Medium · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD · Source-based practice

After a myocardial infarction, a patient develops new orthopnea and falling oxygen saturation. Which auscultatory finding most strongly supports pulmonary congestion in this clinical context?

- **A.** A localized upper-airway inspiratory stridor.
- **B.** A normal vesicular pattern without other respiratory findings.
- **C.** An isolated pleural rub as the only expected finding.
- **D.** Bilateral inspiratory crackles over the dependent lung regions.

<details><summary>✅ Answer and detailed Hinglish explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: D. Bilateral inspiratory crackles over the dependent lung regions.**

**Kyun sahi hai?** Left-heart dysfunction se pulmonary venous aur capillary pressure rise ho sakta hai. Fluid interstitium aur alveoli mein accumulate hone par small airways ke reopening se inspiratory crackles sunai de sakte hain. Is scenario mein orthopnea aur falling saturation ke saath ye pulmonary congestion support karte hain. Crackles alone edema-specific diagnosis nahi hain. Nurse urgent assessment aur escalation kare.

**Baaki options kyun galat hain?**

- **A.** Stridor upper-airway narrowing ka clue hai. Ye pulmonary congestion ka typical auscultatory sign nahi.
- **B.** Normal breath sounds congestion rule out nahi karte, lekin listed findings mein bilateral crackles stronger support dete hain.
- **C.** Pleural rub pleural surface inflammation se associated ho sakta hai. Ye cardiogenic congestion ka expected sole sign nahi.

**Exam trick:** Post-MI congestion can produce bilateral crackles.

**Historical evidence:** [Retrieved report](https://testbook.com/question-answer/a-client-with-a-myocardial-infarction-develops-rap--681c8d678b9dc497585feb02). Retrieved report links post-MI pulmonary edema symptoms and crackles. This is independently worded related practice, not authenticated exact paper wording. Official key unverified.

[Clinical reference](https://www.merckmanuals.com/professional/cardiovascular-disorders/heart-failure/pulmonary-edema)
