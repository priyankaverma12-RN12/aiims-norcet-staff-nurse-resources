## Hinglish explanations (question text original paper mein hi padho)

### Question 3 — Alveolar macrophages / wandering phagocytes

**[Open original question 3, page 3 — read EXACT English stem and A/B/C/D options](https://www.snowstudy.in/wp-content/uploads/2022/09/Staff-Nurse-933-Post-Code-Question-Paper.pdf#page=3)**

<details><summary>✅ Correct answer + detailed Hinglish explanation</summary>

**A. Alveolar macrophages**

Alveolar macrophages **phagocytosis** ke through dust, germs aur foreign particles ko engulf karke remove karte hain. Type I pneumocytes gas-exchange barrier provide karte hain aur Type II surfactant banate hain. Goblet cells mucus se related hain; alveoli ke wandering phagocytes nahi.

**Exam trick:** Same concept ko alag clinical context mein poochh sakte hain. Original exam question ke option wording ko change mat karo.

### Question 20 — Normal adult tidal volume

**[Open original question 20, page 6 — read EXACT English stem and A/B/C/D options](https://www.snowstudy.in/wp-content/uploads/2022/09/Staff-Nurse-933-Post-Code-Question-Paper.pdf#page=6)**

<details><summary>✅ Correct answer + detailed Hinglish explanation</summary>

**D. 500 mL**

Ek **normal quiet breath** mein inspired/expired air ko tidal volume (TV) kehte hain. Adult exam questions mein typical teaching value **approximately 500 mL** hoti hai. Ye ek example hai, real patient's tidal volume age, size aur condition ke according change hoti hai.

**Exam trick:** Same concept ko alag clinical context mein poochh sakte hain. Original exam question ke option wording ko change mat karo.

### Question 35 — External respiration / alveolar gas exchange

**[Open original question 35, page 8 — read EXACT English stem and A/B/C/D options](https://www.snowstudy.in/wp-content/uploads/2022/09/Staff-Nurse-933-Post-Code-Question-Paper.pdf#page=8)**

<details><summary>✅ Correct answer + detailed Hinglish explanation</summary>

**A. External respiration**

**Alveoli aur pulmonary capillaries** ke beech O₂–CO₂ exchange ko external respiration kehte hain. Internal respiration **systemic tissues aur blood** ke beech hota hai. Ventilation ka matlab sirf air ka movement hai.

**Exam trick:** Same concept ko alag clinical context mein poochh sakte hain. Original exam question ke option wording ko change mat karo.

### Question 67 — Definition of tidal volume

**[Open original question 67, page 13 — read EXACT English stem and A/B/C/D options](https://www.snowstudy.in/wp-content/uploads/2022/09/Staff-Nurse-933-Post-Code-Question-Paper.pdf#page=13)**

<details><summary>✅ Correct answer + detailed Hinglish explanation</summary>

**B. Tidal volume**

Normal breathing mein ek breath se andar-bahar move hone wali air **TV** hai. Vital capacity (VC) maximum voluntarily moved air hai; inspiratory capacity (IC) = TV + IRV. **Normal single breath** clue se TV answer milta hai.

**Exam trick:** Same concept ko alag clinical context mein poochh sakte hain. Original exam question ke option wording ko change mat karo.

### Question 113 — Bronchioles / narrow airways of the lungs

**[Open original question 113, page 20 — read EXACT English stem and A/B/C/D options](https://www.snowstudy.in/wp-content/uploads/2022/09/Staff-Nurse-933-Post-Code-Question-Paper.pdf#page=20)**

<details><summary>✅ Correct answer + detailed Hinglish explanation</summary>

**B. Bronchioles**

Bronchioles chhoti branching airways hoti hain jo bronchi se aage air conduct karti hain. **Alveoli** air sacs hain; **bronchi** relatively large airways hain. Sequence: Trachea → Bronchi → Bronchioles → Alveoli.

**Exam trick:** Same concept ko alag clinical context mein poochh sakte hain. Original exam question ke option wording ko change mat karo.
