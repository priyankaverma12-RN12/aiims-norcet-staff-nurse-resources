## English question as published by the memory-based source

Source: [Careers360 — NORCET 10 Answer Key 2026, memory-based questions](https://medicine.careers360.com/articles/aiims-norcet-10-answer-key-2026).

**Normal respiration rate in adults?**

- **A.** 8–10
- **B.** 12–20
- **C.** 20–30
- **D.** 30–40

> **Fidelity:** Question aur choices **Careers360 ke English memory-based listing** ke according hain; ye **verified exact AIIMS exam paper wording** hone ka claim nahi hai. Options ke units originally mention nahi the, isliye unhe add nahi kiya.

<details><summary>✅ Answer and detailed Hinglish explanation</summary>

**B. 12–20**

**Reason:** Adult patient ka normal **resting respiratory rate** generally **12–20 breaths/min** padhaya jata hai. A mein mostly very low rate, aur C/D mein adult resting rate se high numbers diye hain.

**Question ko kaise samjho:** 'Adults' aur 'normal' important words hain; children/infants ke RR ranges alag hote hain. Patient exercise, fever, altitude ya distress mein ho toh value change kar sakti hai.

**Memory trick:** Adult resting **RR = 12–20/min**.
