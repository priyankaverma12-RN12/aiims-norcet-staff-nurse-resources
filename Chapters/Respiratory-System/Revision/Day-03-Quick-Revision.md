## 🔴 Current guideline revision — 27 November 2026

| Exam situation | Current clinical distinction |
|---|---|
| Acute asthma, age ≥6 years | GINA 2026: supplemental oxygen generally indicated when SpO2 <92%, titrate to 92–95% when given |
| Child ≤5 years with severe acute wheeze/asthma | GINA 2026: oxygen target ≥92% when given, follow pediatric protocol |
| COPD at risk of hypercapnic failure | Initial 88–92% pending ABG and individual assessment. Emergency resuscitation must not be delayed |
| Asthma maintenance | ICS-containing regimen, not SABA-only or LABA-only |
| AIR/MART | Prescribed anti-inflammatory reliever or maintenance-and-reliever ICS-formoterol regimen, not salmeterol rescue |
| COPD diagnostic ratio | Post-bronchodilator FEV1/FVC <0.70 with clinical context |
| GOLD 2027 A/B/E | ≥1 moderate or severe exacerbation in previous year = E. With none, symptoms distinguish A/B |
| LTOT | Stable confirmed severe resting hypoxemia. PaO2 ≤55 mmHg or selected patients above 55 and below 60 with qualifying complications |
| Montelukast | New neuropsychiatric symptoms: stop medication and contact healthcare professional immediately. Not acute rescue |

**Dhyan de:** GINA ka 92–95% acute asthma target aur hypercapnia-risk COPD ka 88–92% target different situations ke liye hain. Normal SpO2 se normal PaCO2 prove nahi hoti. Patient ki consciousness, breathing effort aur clinical condition assess karo. GOLD 1–4 airflow obstruction grades hain, A/B/E symptoms aur exacerbations par based hai. Older exam keys aur current clinical guidelines ko alag samjho.

## Extra high-yield exam recall

| Clinical clue | Answer |
|---|---|
| PEFR zones (personal best) | Green ≥80%, yellow 50–<80%, red <50% |
| Three peak-flow attempts | Record highest |
| Early asthma ABG | Low PaCO2, respiratory alkalosis |
| Severe asthma, rising/normal PaCO2 despite distress | Fatigue warning |
| Adult bronchodilator response | ≥12% and ≥200 mL FEV1 or FVC |
| COPD post-BD FEV1 % predicted, confirmed COPD | GOLD 1 ≥80, 2 50–<80, 3 30–<50, 4 <30% |
| Chronic hypoxia, pedal edema and raised JVP | Cor pulmonale |
| Chronic hypoxia, increased RBCs | Secondary polycythemia |
| COPD severe chronic resting hypoxemia | LTOT eligibility assessment |
| Smoking / A1AT deficiency | Centriacinar / panacinar |
| Paraseptal emphysema | Pneumothorax risk |
| Theophylline toxicity | Vomiting, arrhythmias, seizures |
| Early hypoxia sign | Restlessness |
| HFNC | Heated humidified high-flow oxygen |
| Apnea / inadequate ventilation | BVM by trained staff |
| Acute hypercapnic respiratory failure with acidosis | NIV/BiPAP when appropriate |
| NRBM collapsed bag | Check supply/flow/device and assess patient |
| Venturi mask | Air entrainment ports open |

**Flow:** Cannula 1–6 L/min, simple mask 5–10 L/min (some protocols 6–10), NRBM 10–15 L/min. Venturi flow adapter-specific hai. Actual FiO2 variable devices mein estimate hai. Prescribed target aur device instructions follow karo.
