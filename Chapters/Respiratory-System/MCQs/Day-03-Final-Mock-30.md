### Q1. A patient with asthma has PEF 180 L/min, personal best 450 L/min, and difficulty speaking. Which action best fits both findings?

**Difficulty:** 🔴 Difficult · **Priority:** 🔴 MOST IMPORTANT · NORCET / Staff Nurse (original exam-style)

- **A.** Treat as green zone
- **B.** Advise routine follow-up because PEF exceeds 30%
- **C.** Follow emergency action plan and urgently escalate respiratory assessment
- **D.** Ask patient to perform exercise before assessment

<details><summary>✅ Correct answer and detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: C. Follow emergency action plan and urgently escalate respiratory assessment**

**Kyun sahi hai?** 180 ÷ 450 × 100 = 40%. Common traffic-light action plan mein 50% se kam red zone hota hai. Speech difficulty severe distress ka additional clue hai. ABC assessment aur prescribed emergency plan ke saath immediate escalation karo. PEF measure karne ke liye emergency care delay mat karo.

**Baaki options kyun galat hain?**

- **A.** 40% green zone nahi hai.
- **B.** 30% se upar hona safe follow-up ka criterion nahi hai.
- **D.** Exercise acute respiratory distress worsen kar sakti hai.

**Exam trick:** Red zone plus severe symptoms = urgent.

### Q2. An adult's FVC rises from 2.00 L to 2.22 L after bronchodilator. Does it meet the taught 12% and 200 mL criterion?

**Difficulty:** 🔴 Difficult · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD · NORCET / Staff Nurse (original exam-style)

- **A.** Yes, both criteria are met
- **B.** Yes, because any increase confirms asthma
- **C.** No, because improvement is only 22 mL
- **D.** No, 220 mL improvement but only 11%

<details><summary>✅ Correct answer and detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: D. No, 220 mL improvement but only 11%**

**Kyun sahi hai?** FVC change = 2.22 − 2.00 = 0.22 L, yaani 220 mL. Percentage = 0.22 ÷ 2.00 × 100 = 11%. Adult GINA criterion mein kam se kam 12% aur 200 mL dono chahiye. Volume condition meet hui, percentage nahi. Ek negative response se asthma exclude nahi hota.

**Baaki options kyun galat hain?**

- **A.** Dono criteria meet nahi hue.
- **B.** Koi bhi rise asthma confirm nahi karta.
- **C.** 0.22 L = 220 mL, 22 mL nahi.

**Exam trick:** Check percent and absolute change separately.

### Q3. A severely breathless asthma patient has pH 7.26 and PaCO2 58 mmHg. Which interpretation guides priority?

**Difficulty:** 🟡 Medium · **Priority:** 🔴 MOST IMPORTANT · NORCET / Staff Nurse (original exam-style)

- **B.** Expected early respiratory alkalosis
- **C.** Primary alkalemia due to excess bicarbonate
- **D.** CO2 retention with acidemia suggests ventilatory deterioration requiring urgent escalation

<details><summary>✅ Correct answer and detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: D. CO2 retention with acidemia suggests ventilatory deterioration requiring urgent escalation**

**Kyun sahi hai?** pH 7.26 acidemia aur PaCO2 58 mmHg elevated hai. Severe asthma ke context mein CO2 retain hona inadequate ventilation aur muscle fatigue ka warning ho sakta hai. Immediate respiratory assessment aur emergency escalation chahiye. HCO3 ke bina exact mixed disorder assess nahi kar sakte.

**Baaki options kyun galat hain?**

- **A.** Acidemia aur raised CO2 symptom relief ka evidence nahi hain.
- **B.** Early hyperventilation mein CO2 usually low ho sakti hai.
- **C.** pH acidic hai, alkaline nahi. Bicarbonate bhi given nahi hai.

**Exam trick:** Acidotic severe asthma is dangerous.

### Q4. Which finding most directly reflects acute bronchospasm rather than alveolar wall destruction?

**Difficulty:** 🟡 Medium · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD · NORCET / Staff Nurse (original exam-style)

- **A.** Permanent loss of alveolar septa
- **B.** Chronic hyperinflation from lost recoil alone
- **C.** Subpleural bullae on CT alone
- **D.** Variable wheeze and improved airflow after prescribed bronchodilator

<details><summary>✅ Correct answer and detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: D. Variable wheeze and improved airflow after prescribed bronchodilator**

**Kyun sahi hai?** Bronchospasm mein smooth muscle constriction variable airflow obstruction produce kar sakti hai. Prescribed bronchodilator se airflow improve hona reversible component ka clue hai. Ye finding alone asthma confirm ya COPD exclude nahi karti.

**Baaki options kyun galat hain?**

- **A.** Permanent alveolar septal loss emphysema se related hai.
- **B.** Lost recoil structural emphysema mechanism hai.
- **C.** Subpleural bullae structural changes hain, acute bronchospasm ka direct sign nahi.

**Exam trick:** Spasm can relax; destroyed walls don't regrow.

### Q7. A COPD patient has post-bronchodilator FEV1 0.9 L and FVC 1.8 L; FEV1 is 45% predicted. Which combined classification fits?

**Correct answer: D. Ratio 0.50, GOLD 3 spirometric grade if COPD confirmed**

**Kyun sahi hai?** FEV1/FVC = 0.9 ÷ 1.8 = 0.50. Post-bronchodilator low ratio obstruction support karta hai. Confirmed COPD mein FEV1 45% predicted GOLD 3 grade hai, range 30 se below 50%. Ratio aur percent predicted alag measurements hain.

**Baaki options kyun galat hain?**

- **A.** 2.0 inverse division ka result hai aur GOLD 1 ke liye FEV1 kam se kam 80% predicted hota hai.
