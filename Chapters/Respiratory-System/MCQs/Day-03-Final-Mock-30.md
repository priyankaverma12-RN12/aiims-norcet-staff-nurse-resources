### Q2. An adult's FVC rises from 2.00 L to 2.22 L after bronchodilator. Does it meet the taught 12% and 200 mL criterion?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Exam focus:** NORCET / HP Staff Nurse / Nursing Officer (original exam-style)

- **A.** Yes, both criteria are met
- **B.** Yes, because any increase confirms asthma
- **C.** No, because improvement is only 22 mL
- **D.** No, 220 mL improvement but only 11%

<details><summary>✅ Correct answer and detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: D. No, 220 mL improvement but only 11%**

**Kyun sahi hai:** 0.22 L=220 mL; 0.22/2=11%, percentage below threshold.

**Baaki options kyun nahi:** Both criteria not met; unit conversion incorrect; response alone not diagnosis.

**Exam trick:** Check percent and absolute change separately.

### Q3. A severely breathless asthma patient has pH 7.26 and PaCO2 58 mmHg. Which interpretation guides priority?

### Q4. Which finding most directly reflects acute bronchospasm rather than alveolar wall destruction?

<details><summary>✅ Correct answer and detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: D. Variable wheeze and improved airflow after prescribed bronchodilator**

**Kyun sahi hai:** Reversible smooth muscle narrowing can improve after bronchodilator.
