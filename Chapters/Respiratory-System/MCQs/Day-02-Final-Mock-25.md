### Q1. During inspection, inward movement between the ribs during inspiration suggests what?

**Difficulty:** 🟡 Medium · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD · Original nursing exam-style

- **A.** Normal percussion
- **B.** Increased work of breathing
- **C.** Normal quiet breathing in every adult
- **D.** Confirmed normal oxygenation

<details><summary>✅ Correct answer and detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: B. Increased work of breathing**

**Kyun sahi hai?** Inspiration ke time intercostal spaces andar kheenchna retractions kehlata hai. Patient ko normal se zyada effort lag raha ho sakta hai. RR, SpO2, consciousness aur respiratory effort saath assess karo.

**Baaki options kyun galat hain?**

- **A.** Percussion chest ko tap karke note assess karna hai, inspection nahi.
- **C.** Prominent retractions quiet adult breathing mein expected nahi hain.
- **D.** Retractions se oxygenation normal prove nahi hoti.

**Exam trick:** Retractions = effort.

### Q2. One side of the chest expands less on palpation. What is the best interpretation?

**Difficulty:** 🟡 Medium · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD · Original nursing exam-style

- **A.** Asymmetric expansion requiring further assessment
- **B.** Normal regardless of symptoms
- **C.** Direct arterial oxygen measurement
- **D.** Definite asthma diagnosis

<details><summary>✅ Correct answer and detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: A. Asymmetric expansion requiring further assessment**

**Kyun sahi hai?** Palpation mein dono sides ki chest movement compare karte hain. Ek side less expansion ho toh pleural effusion, pneumothorax, pain ya doosri pathology possible hai. Ye finding further examination maangti hai, diagnosis alone confirm nahi karti.

**Baaki options kyun galat hain?**

- **B.** Asymmetry ko symptoms ignore karke normal assume nahi karna chahiye.
- **C.** Palpation arterial oxygen pressure measure nahi karti.
- **D.** Asthma is single sign se establish nahi hota.

**Exam trick:** Compare both sides.

### Q3. Reduced fremitus, dull percussion and diminished breath sounds at one base suggest which condition?

**Difficulty:** 🔴 Difficult · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD · Original nursing exam-style

- **A.** Pleural effusion
- **B.** Simple bronchospasm
- **C.** Excess pleural air
- **D.** Normal aeration

<details><summary>✅ Correct answer and detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: A. Pleural effusion**

**Kyun sahi hai?** Pleural space mein fluid collect ho toh voice vibrations chest wall tak kam pahunch sakti hain. Fluid percussion ko dull banata hai aur breath sounds diminish kar sakta hai. Combination pleural effusion support karta hai, confirm karne ke liye clinical assessment aur imaging chahiye.

**Baaki options kyun galat hain?**

- **B.** Bronchospasm commonly wheeze aur prolonged expiration se associated hota hai.
- **C.** Pleural air mein reduced fremitus ke saath hyperresonance ho sakti hai, dullness typical nahi.
- **D.** Normally aerated lung mein expected resonance aur breath sounds hote hain.

**Exam trick:** Fluid = dull and decreased fremitus.

[Clinical reference 1](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/sites/books/NBK499838/)

### Q5. A low-pitched snoring breath sound changes after coughing. What is it?

**Difficulty:** 🟡 Medium · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD · Original nursing exam-style

- **A.** Rhonchi
- **B.** Vesicular sound
- **C.** Pleural friction rub
- **D.** Stridor

<details><summary>✅ Correct answer and detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: A. Rhonchi**

**Kyun sahi hai?** Rhonchi low-pitched continuous snoring-type sound hoti hai jo larger airways mein secretions ya narrowing se related ho sakti hai. Cough ke baad sound change ho sakti hai kyunki secretions move hoti hain.

**Baaki options kyun galat hain?**

- **B.** Vesicular sound expected soft peripheral breath sound hai.
- **C.** Pleural rub inflamed pleural surfaces ki grating sound hai, cough se usually clear nahi hoti.
- **D.** Stridor harsh upper airway sound hai, low-pitched snoring ka usual explanation nahi.

**Exam trick:** Snore + secretions = rhonchi.

### Q9. ABG pH 7.25, PaCO2 59 mmHg, HCO3 25 mEq/L. Which mechanism drives the disturbance?

**Difficulty:** 🟡 Medium · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD · Original nursing exam-style

- **A.** Inadequate CO2 elimination
- **B.** Primary bicarbonate excess
- **C.** Excess CO2 elimination
- **D.** Primary bicarbonate loss

<details><summary>✅ Correct answer and detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: A. Inadequate CO2 elimination**

**Kyun sahi hai?** pH low aur PaCO2 high hain, jo respiratory acidosis ka pattern hai. Inadequate alveolar ventilation se CO2 accumulate ho sakti hai. Given bicarbonate 25 ke saath calculated pH approximately 7.25 hai. Patient ki ventilation aur underlying cause assess karo.

**Baaki options kyun galat hain?**

- **B.** Primary bicarbonate excess usually pH raise karta hai.
- **C.** Excess CO2 removal PaCO2 low karega, high nahi.
- **D.** Primary bicarbonate loss mein bicarbonate reduced hota hai, yahan primary clue CO2 elevation hai.

**Exam trick:** CO2 up, pH down.
