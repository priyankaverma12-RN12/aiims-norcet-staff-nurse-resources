### Q1. A ventilated patient triggers an additional breath and receives the full preset support. Which mode best matches?

**Difficulty:** 🟡 Medium · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD · Original exam-style

- **A.** CPAP alone
- **B.** Unassisted spontaneous breathing
- **C.** Assist-control
- **D.** Postural drainage

<details><summary>✅ Correct answer and detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: C. Assist-control**

**Kyun sahi hai?** Assist-control (A/C) mode mein patient ki effective inspiratory effort machine ko trigger karti hai. Machine har triggered ya time-triggered mandatory breath ko preset volume ya pressure ke according deliver karti hai. Minimum rate bhi set hota hai. Pressure-controlled A/C mein delivered volume variable ho sakta hai.

**Baaki options kyun galat hain?**

- **A.** CPAP spontaneous breathing ke dauran baseline positive pressure maintain karta hai. Har trigger par full mandatory breath deliver nahi karta.
- **B.** Unassisted breathing mein machine preset inspiratory assistance nahi deti.
- **D.** Postural drainage secretions clear karne ki positioning technique hai, ventilator mode nahi.

**Exam trick:** A/C patient-triggered breath ko preset support deta hai.

### Q4. A patient has IPAP 14 and EPAP 5 cm H2O on bilevel NIV. What is the pressure support difference?

**Difficulty:** 🟡 Medium · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD · Original exam-style

- **A.** 19 cm H2O
- **B.** 5 cm H2O
- **C.** 14 cm H2O
- **D.** 9 cm H2O

<details><summary>✅ Correct answer and detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: D. 9 cm H2O**

**Kyun sahi hai?** IPAP inspiratory positive airway pressure aur EPAP expiratory positive airway pressure hai. Pressure support = IPAP − EPAP = 14 − 5 = 9 cm H2O. Bilevel NIV settings clinician patient ki condition ke according decide karta hai.

**Baaki options kyun galat hain?**

- **A.** 19 cm H2O dono pressures ko add karne se aata hai, difference nahi.
- **B.** 5 cm H2O EPAP alone hai.
- **C.** 14 cm H2O IPAP alone hai, pressure support difference nahi.

**Exam trick:** IPAP minus EPAP = 14−5=9.
