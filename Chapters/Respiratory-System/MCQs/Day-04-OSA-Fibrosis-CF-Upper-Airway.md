### Q1. A sleeping patient has recurrent airflow obstruction despite persistent thoracoabdominal effort. Which disorder fits?

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Exam focus:** NORCET / HP Staff Nurse / PGIMER (original)

- **A.** Central apnea with absent effort
- **B.** Pulmonary contusion
- **C.** Obstructive sleep apnea
- **D.** Pleural empyema

<details><summary>✅ Correct answer and detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: C. Obstructive sleep apnea**

**Kyun sahi hai aur baaki options kyun galat:** OSA effort continues against blockage; central effort absent, contusion trauma, empyema pus.

**Exam trick:** OSA effort continues against blockage.

</details>

- **B.** Moderate OSA

<details><summary>✅ Correct answer and detailed explanation</summary>

- **C.** Ask the child to lie flat and wait alone

**Kyun sahi hai aur baaki options kyun galat:** Manipulation can worsen obstruction; airway team urgent; food/flat waiting unsafe.

**Exam trick:** Manipulation can worsen obstruction.

</details>
