## Obstructive sleep apnea

### Q1. A sleeping patient has recurrent airflow obstruction despite persistent thoracoabdominal effort. Which disorder fits?

**Difficulty:** 🟡 Medium · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD · Original nursing exam-style

- **A.** Central apnea with absent effort
- **B.** Pulmonary contusion
- **C.** Obstructive sleep apnea
- **D.** Pleural empyema

<details><summary>✅ Correct answer and detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: C. Obstructive sleep apnea**

**Kyun sahi hai?** Obstructive sleep apnea (OSA) mein sleep ke dauran upper airway baar-baar partially ya completely collapse ho sakti hai. Chest aur abdomen ki breathing effort continue hoti hai, lekin airflow kam ya band ho jaata hai. Oxygen desaturation aur sleep arousal ho sakte hain. Central apnea mein respiratory effort absent hoti hai. Sleep study mein airflow aur respiratory effort dono compare karte hain.

**Baaki options kyun galat hain?**

- **A.** Central apnea mein respiratory effort absent hoti hai, jabki question mein effort continue hai.
- **B.** Pulmonary contusion traumatic lung tissue injury hai, sleep-related repeated upper airway collapse nahi.
- **D.** Pleural empyema infected pleural fluid ka collection hai, obstructive sleep apnea ka mechanism nahi.

**Exam trick:** OSA effort continues against blockage.

### Q2. An adult sleep study reports AHI 22 events/hour. What is the conventional severity category?

**Difficulty:** 🟡 Medium · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD · Original nursing exam-style

- **A.** Mild OSA
- **B.** Moderate OSA
- **C.** Severe OSA
- **D.** No events

<details><summary>✅ Correct answer and detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: B. Moderate OSA**

**Kyun sahi hai?** AHI ka full form apnea-hypopnea index hai. Polysomnography mein ye sleep ke har hour mein apnea aur hypopnea events ki average count batata hai. Conventional adult scale mein 5 se 15 se kam mild, 15 se 30 se kam moderate aur 30 ya zyada severe hai. AHI 22 moderate range mein hai. Symptoms, oxygenation aur comorbidities bhi care planning mein important hain.

**Baaki options kyun galat hain?**

- **A.** 22 events/hour mild range se zyada hain.
- **C.** 22 conventional severe threshold 30 tak nahi pahunchta.
- **D.** 22 ka matlab events present hain, zero nahi.

**Exam trick:** 15–<30 moderate.

[Clinical reference 1](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10359192/) · [Clinical reference 2](https://jamanetwork.com/journals/jama/fullarticle/2598777)

## Fibrosis and occupational disease

### Q3. A patient has progressive dry cough, fine inspiratory crackles and reduced lung compliance. Which disorder fits best?

**Difficulty:** 🟡 Medium · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD · Original nursing exam-style

- **A.** Pulmonary fibrosis
- **B.** Uncomplicated nasal bleeding
- **C.** Acute upper airway foreign body alone
- **D.** Normal healthy lungs

<details><summary>✅ Correct answer and detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: A. Pulmonary fibrosis**

**Kyun sahi hai?** Pulmonary fibrosis mein lung tissue mein scarring se lungs stiff ho jaate hain. Compliance ka matlab hai pressure change ke response mein lung kitna expand karta hai. Stiff lung ko expand karna difficult hota hai, jis se exertional dyspnea aur restrictive physiology ho sakti hai. Dry cough aur fine inspiratory crackles typical clues hain. HRCT, lung function aur cause assessment chahiye. Low FVC alone restriction confirm nahi karti.

**Baaki options kyun galat hain?**

- **B.** Epistaxis nose se bleeding hai, progressive lung stiffness explain nahi karti.
- **C.** Upper airway foreign body usually acute airway symptoms deta hai, chronic interstitial scarring ka typical pattern nahi.
- **D.** Healthy lungs mein progressive dry cough, fine crackles aur low compliance expected nahi hain.

**Exam trick:** Fibrotic scar stiffens lungs.

[Clinical reference 1](https://www.nhlbi.nih.gov/health/idiopathic-pulmonary-fibrosis/symptoms)

### Q4. A stone-cutting worker has long-term silica exposure. Which additional infection risk is important?

**Difficulty:** 🟡 Medium · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD · Original nursing exam-style

- **A.** Only acute otitis externa
- **B.** Only dental caries
- **C.** No increased respiratory infection risk
- **D.** Tuberculosis

<details><summary>✅ Correct answer and detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: D. Tuberculosis**

**Kyun sahi hai?** Respirable crystalline silica stone cutting aur mining jaise kaam mein lungs tak pahunch sakti hai. Silica lung defense ko impair kar sakti hai aur fibrosis cause kar sakti hai. Silica-exposed workers, especially silicosis wale patients, mein TB ka risk increased hota hai. Occupational history lo, persistent cough, fever, night sweats aur weight loss assess karo aur appropriate TB evaluation ke liye refer karo.

**Baaki options kyun galat hain?**

- **A.** Otitis externa outer ear ki infection hai, silica exposure se linked main additional respiratory risk nahi.
- **B.** Dental caries tooth decay hai, question mein silica-related infection risk poochha gaya hai.
- **C.** TB risk increase documented hai, isliye no increased risk galat hai.

**Exam trick:** Silicosis increases TB risk.

[Clinical reference 1](https://www.cdc.gov/niosh/silica/symptoms/index.html)

### Q5. Which occupational exposure is particularly associated with malignant mesothelioma?

**Difficulty:** 🟢 Easy · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD · Original nursing exam-style

- **A.** Cotton dust alone
- **B.** Mouldy hay alone
- **C.** Iron oxide alone
- **D.** Asbestos fibers

<details><summary>✅ Correct answer and detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: D. Asbestos fibers**

**Kyun sahi hai?** Asbestos fibers se exposure mesothelioma ka major known cause hai. Mesothelioma organ lining, commonly pleura, ka malignancy hai. Asbestos asbestosis aur lung cancer se bhi associated hai. Disease exposure ke decades baad develop ho sakti hai. Exposure prevention aur occupational history important hain.

**Baaki options kyun galat hain?**

- **A.** Cotton dust classically byssinosis se associated hai, mesothelioma ka main exposure nahi.
- **B.** Mouldy hay farmer's lung naam ke hypersensitivity pneumonitis se associated hai.
- **C.** Iron oxide dust siderosis se associated hai, mesothelioma ka classic cause nahi.

**Exam trick:** Asbestos mesothelioma.

[Clinical reference 1](https://www.cdc.gov/mesothelioma/about/index.html)

### Q6. A textile worker reports chest tightness particularly on returning to work after a weekend. Which occupational disorder is classically associated?

**Difficulty:** 🟡 Medium · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD · Original nursing exam-style · **🟣 AIIMS Bhopal Nursing Officer 23 May 2018 Shift II — related reported concept**

- **A.** Asbestosis
- **B.** Coal workers' pneumoconiosis
- **C.** Byssinosis
- **D.** Sarcoidosis

<details><summary>✅ Correct answer and detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: C. Byssinosis**

**Kyun sahi hai?** Byssinosis cotton, flax ya hemp dust exposure se associated airway disease hai. Work break ke baad first day chest tightness aur breathlessness zyada hona classic pattern hai. Isko traditional exam wording mein Monday symptoms ya Monday fever kehte hain, lekin fever hona essential nahi. Exposure history aur spirometry diagnosis mein help karte hain.

**Baaki options kyun galat hain?**

- **A.** Asbestosis asbestos-related interstitial fibrosis hai, cotton dust ke first-day pattern ka classic cause nahi.
- **B.** Coal workers' pneumoconiosis coal mine dust exposure se associated hai.
- **D.** Sarcoidosis granulomatous multisystem disease hai, textile work ke baad recurring chest tightness ka classic occupational pattern nahi.

**Exam trick:** Cotton dust Monday symptoms byssinosis.

## Pulmonary hypertension and sarcoidosis

### Q7. A patient has echo evidence suggesting pulmonary hypertension. Which test establishes pulmonary hemodynamics definitively?

**Difficulty:** 🟡 Medium · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD · Original nursing exam-style

- **A.** Peak flow alone
- **B.** Right heart catheterization
- **C.** Mantoux test
- **D.** Nasal examination

<details><summary>✅ Correct answer and detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: B. Right heart catheterization**

**Kyun sahi hai?** Echocardiography pulmonary hypertension ki probability estimate karti hai. Right heart catheterization (RHC) directly pulmonary arterial pressure, wedge pressure aur cardiac output measure karke hemodynamic classification mein help karta hai. ESC/ERS 2022 mein resting mean pulmonary arterial pressure 20 mmHg se zyada PH define karta hai. Cause aur subtype decide karne ke liye additional parameters aur specialist assessment chahiye.

**Baaki options kyun galat hain?**

- **A.** Peak flow forced expiratory airflow assess karta hai, pulmonary vascular pressure nahi.
- **C.** Mantoux test TB infection evaluation ka test hai, pulmonary pressure measure nahi karta.
- **D.** Nasal examination upper airway assess karti hai, pulmonary hemodynamics nahi.

**Exam trick:** RHC measures pressures.

[Clinical reference 1](https://publications.ersnet.org/lookup/doi/10.1183/13993003.00879-2022)

### Q8. Bilateral hilar lymphadenopathy and noncaseating granulomas suggest which condition in an appropriate clinical context?

**Difficulty:** 🟡 Medium · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD · Original nursing exam-style

- **A.** Sarcoidosis
- **B.** Simple epistaxis
- **C.** Primary lung abscess in every case
- **D.** Uncomplicated asthma alone

<details><summary>✅ Correct answer and detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: A. Sarcoidosis**

**Kyun sahi hai?** Sarcoidosis mein immune-cell collections, yani granulomas, lungs aur hilar lymph nodes mein ho sakte hain. Bilateral hilar lymphadenopathy aur nonnecrotizing granulomas compatible clinical picture mein suspicion support karte hain. Granuloma appearance alone diagnostic proof nahi hai. TB, fungal infection aur other granulomatous causes ko evaluate karna important hai, especially India mein. Eye, skin aur cardiac involvement ke symptoms bhi assess karo.

**Baaki options kyun galat hain?**

- **B.** Simple epistaxis nasal bleeding hai, hilar nodes aur granulomas ka typical explanation nahi.
- **C.** Lung abscess necrotic infected cavity hoti hai. Har bilateral hilar granuloma abscess nahi hai.
- **D.** Asthma variable airway obstruction hai, typical nonnecrotizing granulomatous lymphadenopathy nahi.

**Exam trick:** Sarcoid typical pattern.

[Clinical reference 1](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7159433/)

## Cystic fibrosis and upper airway emergencies

### Q9. Why may a child with cystic fibrosis require pancreatic enzyme replacement?

**Difficulty:** 🟡 Medium · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD · Original nursing exam-style

- **A.** Thick secretions obstruct pancreatic ducts and cause malabsorption
- **B.** The CFTR mutation removes the entire liver
- **C.** It prevents every respiratory infection directly
- **D.** It replaces the sweat chloride test

<details><summary>✅ Correct answer and detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: A. Thick secretions obstruct pancreatic ducts and cause malabsorption**

**Kyun sahi hai?** Cystic fibrosis (CF) mein CFTR chloride channel dysfunction ki wajah se secretions thick ho sakti hain. Pancreatic ducts obstruct hone par digestive enzymes intestine tak adequately nahi pahunchte. Pancreatic insufficiency mein fat aur other nutrients ka absorption kam ho sakta hai, greasy stool aur poor weight gain aa sakte hain. Prescribed pancreatic enzyme replacement meals aur snacks ke saath digestion mein help karti hai. Dose CF team decide karti hai.

**Baaki options kyun galat hain?**

- **B.** CFTR mutation liver ko remove nahi karta, ye ion transport dysfunction cause karta hai.
- **C.** Pancreatic enzymes digestion support karti hain. Ye direct respiratory infection prevention ka replacement nahi.
- **D.** Enzymes treatment hain. Sweat chloride diagnostic evaluation ka replacement nahi.

**Exam trick:** Pancreatic insufficiency impairs digestion.

[Clinical reference 1](https://www.cff.org/managing-cf/enzymes)

### Q10. A child has drooling, dysphagia and severe respiratory distress with suspected epiglottitis. Which action is safest?

**Difficulty:** 🟡 Medium · **Priority:** 🔴 MOST IMPORTANT · Original nursing exam-style

- **A.** Use a tongue depressor forcefully
- **B.** Offer food immediately
- **C.** Ask the child to lie flat and wait alone
- **D.** Avoid forced throat examination and obtain emergency airway help

<details><summary>✅ Correct answer and detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: D. Avoid forced throat examination and obtain emergency airway help**

**Kyun sahi hai?** Suspected epiglottitis mein swollen upper airway suddenly obstruct ho sakti hai. Drooling aur dysphagia suggest karte hain ki child secretions swallow nahi kar paa raha. Child ko calm rakho, preferred position mein parent ke saath rehne do, unnecessary examination avoid karo aur urgent experienced airway team bulao. Severe distress mein throat inspection ya investigations ke liye airway support delay nahi karna hai.

**Baaki options kyun galat hain?**

- **A.** Forceful tongue depressor examination child ko distress kar sakti hai aur airway obstruction worsen kar sakti hai.
- **B.** Food dena swallowing difficulty aur aspiration risk mein unsafe hai.
- **C.** Child ko forced supine position mein rakhna ya akela chhodna unsafe hai. Immediate emergency help chahiye.

**Exam trick:** Manipulation can worsen obstruction.

[Clinical reference 1](https://www.rch.org.au/clinicalguide/guideline_index/acute_upper_airway_obstruction/)
