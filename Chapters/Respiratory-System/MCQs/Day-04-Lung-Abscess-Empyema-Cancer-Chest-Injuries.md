## Lung abscess, empyema and pleurisy

### Q1. A lung image shows an intraparenchymal cavity with an air-fluid level in a patient with fever and foul sputum. What is suspected?

**Difficulty:** 🟡 Medium · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD · Original nursing exam-style

- **A.** Simple rhinitis
- **B.** Isolated central sleep apnea
- **C.** Lung abscess
- **D.** Uncomplicated dry pleurisy

<details><summary>✅ Correct answer and detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: C. Lung abscess**

**Kyun sahi hai?** Lung abscess mein infection lung tissue ko damage karta hai aur pus se bhari cavity ban sakti hai. Jab cavity bronchus se communicate karti hai toh air aur fluid ek saath dikh sakte hain. Fever aur foul-smelling sputum is suspicion ko support karte hain. Lekin air-fluid level alone diagnosis confirm nahi karta. CT, microbiology aur TB ya malignancy jaise alternative causes ka assessment bhi zaroori hai.

**Baaki options kyun galat hain?**

- **A.** Rhinitis nasal mucosa ki inflammation hai. Ye lung parenchyma mein pus cavity explain nahi karti.
- **B.** Central sleep apnea breathing drive ki problem hai. Fever aur infected cavity uska typical finding nahi hai.
- **D.** Dry pleurisy pleural inflammation hai. Ismein lung tissue ke andar pus cavity zaroori nahi hoti.

**Exam trick:** Pus cavity inside lung abscess.

[Clinical reference](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/NBK555920/)

### Q2. Why may antibiotics alone be insufficient in a patient with established pleural empyema?

**Difficulty:** 🟡 Medium · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD · Original nursing exam-style

- **A.** Antibiotics never reach any lung tissue
- **B.** Infected pleural fluid may require drainage for source control
- **C.** Every pleural effusion requires pneumonectomy
- **D.** Empyema is always a viral nasal disease

<details><summary>✅ Correct answer and detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: B. Infected pleural fluid may require drainage for source control**

**Kyun sahi hai?** Empyema pleural space mein pus ka collection hai. Antibiotics infection treat karti hain, lekin infected fluid ke collection ko drain karna source control ke liye zaroori ho sakta hai. Nurse prescribed antibiotics, drain output, temperature, breathing aur sepsis signs monitor kare. Established pus collection ko uncomplicated effusion mat samjho.

**Baaki options kyun galat hain?**

- **A.** Antibiotics lung tissue tak pahunch sakti hain. Problem undrained infected collection aur infection control ki hoti hai.
- **C.** Pneumonectomy poore lung ki removal hai. Har effusion ya empyema mein iski zarurat nahi hoti.
- **D.** Empyema pleural infection hai, viral nasal disease nahi.

**Exam trick:** Pus collection drainage may needed.

### Q3. A patient reports sharp chest pain that worsens on deep inspiration. Which mechanism is consistent with pleurisy?

**Difficulty:** 🟢 Easy · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD · Original nursing exam-style

- **A.** Inflamed pleural surfaces moving during respiration
- **B.** Optic nerve injury
- **C.** Gastric acid alone in every case
- **D.** CFTR gene mutation as the immediate cause

<details><summary>✅ Correct answer and detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: A. Inflamed pleural surfaces moving during respiration**

**Kyun sahi hai?** Parietal pleura pain-sensitive hoti hai. Jab pleura inflamed hoti hai toh breathing aur cough ke dauran movement se sharp pain ho sakta hai. Pleuritic pain sirf pleurisy mein nahi hota. Sudden severe pain ya breathlessness mein PE aur pneumothorax jaise urgent causes assess karne chahiye.

**Baaki options kyun galat hain?**

- **B.** Optic nerve injury vision ko affect karti hai, respiration-linked pleural pain explain nahi karti.
- **C.** Gastric acid reflux chest discomfort de sakta hai, lekin har inspiratory sharp pain reflux nahi hota.
- **D.** CFTR mutation cystic fibrosis ka underlying cause hai, acute pleural pain ka direct mechanism nahi.

**Exam trick:** Pleural friction causes pain.

[Clinical reference](https://thorax.bmj.com/content/78/Suppl_3/s1)

## Lung cancer and surgery

### Q4. A patient with squamous cell lung carcinoma develops hypercalcemia. Which paraneoplastic mediator is classically implicated?

**Difficulty:** 🟡 Medium · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD · Original nursing exam-style

- **A.** Insulin alone
- **B.** Surfactant protein alone
- **C.** Pyridoxine
- **D.** PTH-related peptide

<details><summary>✅ Correct answer and detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: D. PTH-related peptide**

**Kyun sahi hai?** Squamous cell lung carcinoma kabhi PTH-related peptide (PTHrP) produce karta hai. Ye calcium regulation par PTH jaisa effect daal kar blood calcium badha sakta hai. Hypercalcemia mein weakness, constipation, dehydration aur confusion ho sakte hain. Nurse calcium, hydration aur mental status assess kare. Bone metastases bhi hypercalcemia ka cause ho sakte hain.

**Baaki options kyun galat hain?**

- **A.** Insulin mainly glucose uptake aur blood glucose regulation karta hai. Ye classic tumor-related hypercalcemia mediator nahi hai.
- **B.** Surfactant alveolar surface tension reduce karta hai, PTH-like calcium effect explain nahi karta.
- **C.** Pyridoxine vitamin B6 hai, classic paraneoplastic hypercalcemia mediator nahi.

**Exam trick:** PTHrP raises calcium.

### Q5. A patient with small cell lung cancer develops dilutional hyponatremia. Which association is most likely?

**Difficulty:** 🟡 Medium · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD · Original nursing exam-style

- **A.** Primary hyperventilation alone
- **B.** Ethambutol toxicity
- **C.** Chylothorax
- **D.** SIADH

<details><summary>✅ Correct answer and detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: D. SIADH**

**Kyun sahi hai?** SIADH ka matlab syndrome of inappropriate antidiuretic hormone secretion hai. Small cell lung cancer ectopic ADH produce kar sakta hai. Kidney extra water retain karti hai aur serum sodium dilute ho sakta hai. Confusion, headache aur seizures severe hyponatremia ke signs ho sakte hain. Serum/urine osmolality, volume status aur alternative causes evaluate karne hote hain. Sodium correction clinician-directed monitoring ke saath honi chahiye.

**Baaki options kyun galat hain?**

- **A.** Hyperventilation CO2 aur acid-base status change karti hai, classic dilutional hyponatremia association nahi.
- **B.** Ethambutol toxicity classically optic neuropathy se related hai.
- **C.** Chylothorax pleural space mein lymph collection hai. Ye small cell cancer ka classic ADH-mediated syndrome nahi.

**Exam trick:** SIADH water retention dilutes Na.

### Q6. Shoulder pain with ptosis and miosis in a patient with a lung apex mass suggests what association?

**Difficulty:** 🟡 Medium · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD · Original nursing exam-style

- **A.** Uncomplicated allergic rhinitis
- **B.** Central sleep apnea
- **C.** Pancoast tumor with sympathetic involvement
- **D.** An isolated lower-lobe effusion only

<details><summary>✅ Correct answer and detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: C. Pancoast tumor with sympathetic involvement**

**Kyun sahi hai?** Pancoast tumor lung apex par hota hai. Brachial plexus ya nearby tissues involve hone par shoulder aur arm pain ho sakta hai. Sympathetic pathway damage hone par Horner syndrome ke signs jaise ptosis, miosis aur kabhi anhidrosis aa sakte hain. Imaging aur biopsy se tumor evaluate hota hai. Har Horner syndrome lung cancer nahi hota.

**Baaki options kyun galat hain?**

- **A.** Allergic rhinitis nasal symptoms deti hai, apical mass aur sympathetic signs ka explanation nahi.
- **B.** Central sleep apnea respiratory drive ki disorder hai, localized ptosis aur miosis ka classic cause nahi.
- **D.** Lower-lobe effusion alone apex ke sympathetic structures ko involve karne wala pattern explain nahi karta.

**Exam trick:** Apex mass can affect sympathetic chain causing Horner.

[Clinical reference](https://www.msdmanuals.com/professional/oncology/tumors-of-the-lungs/lung-carcinoma)

### Q7. What is removed during a lobectomy?

**Difficulty:** 🟢 Easy · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD · Original nursing exam-style

- **A.** Both entire lungs
- **B.** One lung lobe
- **C.** Only the pleural fluid
- **D.** The entire trachea

<details><summary>✅ Correct answer and detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: B. One lung lobe**

**Kyun sahi hai?** Lobectomy mein lung ka ek anatomical lobe remove hota hai. Wedge resection mein smaller piece aur pneumonectomy mein ek entire lung remove hota hai. Postoperative nursing care mein breathing, pain control, prescribed drain monitoring aur mobilization important hain.

**Baaki options kyun galat hain?**

- **A.** Dono entire lungs remove karna lobectomy nahi hai.
- **C.** Pleural fluid removal thoracentesis ya drainage se related hai, lobe resection nahi.
- **D.** Trachea ki removal ko lobectomy nahi kehte.

**Exam trick:** Lobe removal.

[Clinical reference](https://www.cancer.gov/types/lung/hp/non-small-cell-lung-treatment-pdq?redirect=true)

## Chest trauma and hemoptysis

### Q8. Following blunt trauma, a chest wall segment moves inward on inspiration and outward on expiration. What is this called?

**Difficulty:** 🟡 Medium · **Priority:** 🔴 MOST IMPORTANT · Original nursing exam-style

- **A.** Paradoxical motion of a flail segment
- **B.** Normal diaphragmatic motion of the entire chest
- **C.** Normal water-seal tidaling
- **D.** A positive Mantoux test

<details><summary>✅ Correct answer and detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: A. Paradoxical motion of a flail segment**

**Kyun sahi hai?** Jab teen ya zyada adjacent ribs do ya zyada jagah fracture ho jaati hain toh free flail segment ban sakta hai. Spontaneous inspiration mein segment andar aur expiration mein bahar move kar sakta hai. Isse paradoxical movement kehte hain. Pain aur associated pulmonary contusion ventilation impair kar sakte hain. ABC assessment, oxygenation aur urgent trauma care priority hain. Tight chest binding avoid karo.

**Baaki options kyun galat hain?**

- **B.** Normal inspiration mein chest expand hota hai. Localized inward motion trauma ke baad abnormal hai.
- **C.** Water-seal tidaling chest drain ke chamber mein fluid level movement hai, chest wall segment ki motion nahi.
- **D.** Mantoux TB infection evaluation ka skin test hai, trauma sign nahi.

**Exam trick:** Flail segment moves opposite chest.

[Clinical reference](https://www.msdmanuals.com/professional/injuries-poisoning/thoracic-trauma/overview-of-thoracic-trauma)

### Q9. Respiratory status worsens several hours after major blunt chest trauma due to lung tissue hemorrhage and edema. What is suspected?

**Difficulty:** 🟡 Medium · **Priority:** 🔴 MOST IMPORTANT · Original nursing exam-style

- **A.** Pulmonary contusion
- **B.** Latent TB infection
- **C.** Allergic rhinitis
- **D.** Uncomplicated tonsillitis

<details><summary>✅ Correct answer and detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: A. Pulmonary contusion**

**Kyun sahi hai?** Pulmonary contusion mein blunt injury se lung tissue bruised ho jaata hai. Alveoli mein bleeding aur edema gas exchange impair karte hain. Initial findings mild ho sakte hain aur respiratory distress agle hours mein worsen ho sakta hai. Serial SpO2, breathing effort aur hemodynamic assessment important hain. Associated pneumothorax ya hemothorax bhi exclude karna chahiye.

**Baaki options kyun galat hain?**

- **B.** Latent TB acute traumatic lung hemorrhage aur edema explain nahi karta.
- **C.** Allergic rhinitis upper airway inflammation hai, blunt trauma lung bruise nahi.
- **D.** Tonsillitis throat infection hai, chest trauma ke baad worsening alveolar injury ka cause nahi.

**Exam trick:** Bruised lung edema/bleeding can evolve.

[Clinical reference](https://www.msdmanuals.com/professional/injuries-poisoning/thoracic-trauma/overview-of-thoracic-trauma)

### Q10. A patient coughs a large amount of blood and cannot maintain oxygenation. Which nursing priority is correct?

**Difficulty:** 🟡 Medium · **Priority:** 🔴 MOST IMPORTANT · Original nursing exam-style

- **A.** Wait for routine sputum microscopy before assessment
- **B.** Encourage a large meal
- **C.** Treat only as mild epistaxis without examination
- **D.** Emergency airway protection and urgent escalation

<details><summary>✅ Correct answer and detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: D. Emergency airway protection and urgent escalation**

**Kyun sahi hai?** Life-threatening hemoptysis mein blood airway block karke asphyxia aur severe hypoxemia cause kar sakta hai. Quantity ka fixed cutoff alone sufficient nahi hai. Airway, breathing aur circulation turant assess karo, emergency help bulao aur oxygenation support arrange karo. Agar bleeding side known hai toh trained team bleeding lung down positioning consider karti hai. Bronchoscopy ya embolization clinician decide karte hain.

**Baaki options kyun galat hain?**

- **A.** Sputum microscopy cause evaluation mein useful ho sakti hai, lekin unstable patient mein emergency support delay nahi karna.
- **B.** Meal dena airway compromise aur aspiration risk mein unsafe hai.
- **C.** Blood ko epistaxis assume karna bina assessment respiratory tract bleeding miss kar sakta hai.

**Exam trick:** Life-threatening hemoptysis airway obstruction/asphyxia risk.

[Clinical reference](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10289864/)
