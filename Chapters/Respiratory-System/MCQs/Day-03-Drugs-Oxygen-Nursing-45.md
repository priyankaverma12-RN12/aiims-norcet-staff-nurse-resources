### Q1. Which receptor is primarily stimulated by salbutamol to produce bronchodilation?

**Difficulty:** 🟢 Easy · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD · NORCET / Staff Nurse (original exam-style)

- **A.** Alpha1 receptor
- **B.** Muscarinic receptor activation
- **C.** Beta2 adrenergic receptor
- **D.** Histamine H1 receptor activation

<details><summary>✅ Correct answer and detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: C. Beta2 adrenergic receptor**

**Kyun sahi hai?** Salbutamol ko albuterol bhi kehte hain. Ye beta2 receptors stimulate karke bronchial smooth muscle relax karta hai. Isse airway khulti hai aur airflow improve ho sakta hai.

**Baaki options kyun galat hain?**

- **A.** Alpha1 stimulation mainly vascular smooth muscle contraction se linked hai.
- **B.** Muscarinic activation bronchoconstriction badha sakti hai.
- **D.** H1 activation allergy-related effects se linked hai, salbutamol ka target nahi.

**Exam trick:** Beta2 = bronchodilation.

### Q2. A patient receives repeated prescribed salbutamol treatments. Which adverse effects should the nurse particularly monitor?

**Difficulty:** 🟡 Medium · **Priority:** 🔴 MOST IMPORTANT · NORCET / Staff Nurse (original exam-style)

- **A.** Oral candidiasis as the primary acute effect
- **B.** Permanent deafness
- **C.** Bradycardia and hyperkalemia as typical effects
- **D.** Tremor, tachycardia and hypokalemia

<details><summary>✅ Correct answer and detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: D. Tremor, tachycardia and hypokalemia**

**Kyun sahi hai?** Repeated beta2 agonist doses se tremor aur heart rate badh sakte hain. Potassium blood se cells ke andar shift ho sakta hai, jisse hypokalemia hoti hai. Nurse symptoms, pulse aur indicated electrolyte results monitor kare.

**Baaki options kyun galat hain?**

- **A.** Oral candidiasis inhaled corticosteroids ka typical local adverse effect hai.
- **B.** Permanent deafness salbutamol ka typical acute effect nahi hai.
- **C.** Tachycardia aur low potassium expected concerns hain, opposite findings nahi.

**Exam trick:** SABA: shaky, fast pulse, low K.

### Q6. A patient wants to stop the controller inhaler whenever symptoms settle. Which response is best?

**Difficulty:** 🟡 Medium · **Priority:** 🔴 MOST IMPORTANT · NORCET / Staff Nurse (original exam-style) · Related revision

- **A.** Use only a rapid bronchodilator indefinitely
- **B.** Follow the prescribed ICS-containing plan because inflammation may persist
- **C.** Stop every medicine whenever wheezing stops
- **D.** Double all doses independently

<details><summary>✅ Correct answer and detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: B. Follow the prescribed ICS-containing plan because inflammation may persist**

**Kyun sahi hai?** Symptoms settle hone ke baad bhi asthma inflammation present ho sakti hai. Prescribed ICS-containing plan future attacks ka risk kam karne mein help karta hai. Step-down clinician ke saath plan karo, apne aap treatment stop mat karo.

**Baaki options kyun galat hain?**

- **A.** SABA-only long-term treatment asthma risk control ke liye inadequate hai.
- **C.** Wheeze band hone se disease cure prove nahi hoti.
- **D.** Independent dose doubling adverse effects badha sakti hai.

**Exam trick:** Control inflammation even between symptoms.

### Q7. Which statement about formoterol and salmeterol in asthma is correct?

**Difficulty:** 🟡 Medium · **Priority:** 🔴 MOST IMPORTANT · NORCET / Staff Nurse (original exam-style)

- **A.** LABA-only asthma treatment is preferred
- **B.** They are LABAs and should not be used without ICS in asthma
- **C.** They are both short-acting muscarinic antagonists
- **D.** They are both inhaled corticosteroids

<details><summary>✅ Correct answer and detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: B. They are LABAs and should not be used without ICS in asthma**

**Kyun sahi hai?** Formoterol aur salmeterol long-acting beta2 agonists hain. Asthma mein LABA ko ICS ke bina use nahi karna chahiye. Formoterol rapid onset ki wajah se specific prescribed ICS-formoterol reliever plans mein use hota hai, salmeterol uska rescue substitute nahi hai.

**Baaki options kyun galat hain?**

- **A.** LABA-only asthma management recommended nahi hai.
- **C.** Ye antimuscarinic drugs nahi hain.
- **D.** Ye corticosteroids nahi, bronchodilators hain.

**Exam trick:** LABA plus ICS for asthma.
