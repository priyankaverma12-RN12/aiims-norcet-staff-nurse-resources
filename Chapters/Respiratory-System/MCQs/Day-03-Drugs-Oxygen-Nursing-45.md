### Q1. Which receptor is primarily stimulated by salbutamol to produce bronchodilation?

**Difficulty:** Easy | **Exam focus:** NORCET / HP Staff Nurse / Nursing Officer (original exam-style)

- **A.** Alpha1 receptor
- **B.** Muscarinic receptor activation
- **C.** Beta2 adrenergic receptor
- **D.** Histamine H1 receptor activation

<details><summary>✅ Correct answer and detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: C. Beta2 adrenergic receptor**

**Kyun sahi hai:** Beta2 activation bronchial smooth muscle relax karti hai.

**Baaki options kyun nahi:** Alpha1 vascular effect; muscarinic activation bronchoconstriction; H1 activation allergy response.

**Exam trick:** Beta2 = bronchodilation.

### Q2. A patient receives repeated prescribed salbutamol treatments. Which adverse effects should the nurse particularly monitor?

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Exam focus:** NORCET / HP Staff Nurse / Nursing Officer (original exam-style)

- **A.** Oral candidiasis as the primary acute effect
- **B.** Permanent deafness
- **C.** Bradycardia and hyperkalemia as typical effects
- **D.** Tremor, tachycardia and hypokalemia

<details><summary>✅ Correct answer and detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: D. Tremor, tachycardia and hypokalemia**

**Kyun sahi hai:** Beta agonist tremor, pulse rise and potassium shift cause kar sakta hai.

**Baaki options kyun nahi:** Bradycardia/hyperkalemia opposite classic effects; deafness unrelated; thrush ICS-related.

**Exam trick:** SABA: shaky, fast pulse, low K.

### Q6. A patient wants to stop the controller inhaler whenever symptoms settle. Which response is best?

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Exam focus:** NORCET / HP Staff Nurse / Nursing Officer (original exam-style)

- **A.** Use only a rapid bronchodilator indefinitely
- **B.** Follow the prescribed ICS-containing plan because inflammation may persist
- **C.** Stop every medicine whenever wheezing stops
- **D.** Double all doses independently

<details><summary>✅ Correct answer and detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: B. Follow the prescribed ICS-containing plan because inflammation may persist**

**Kyun sahi hai:** Symptom relief underlying inflammation disappearance prove nahi karti; prescribed plan follow.

**Baaki options kyun nahi:** SABA-only long-term inadequate; abrupt independent changes unsafe; double dose without advice unsafe.

**Exam trick:** Control inflammation even between symptoms.

### Q7. Which statement about formoterol and salmeterol in asthma is correct?

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Exam focus:** NORCET / HP Staff Nurse / Nursing Officer (original exam-style)

- **A.** LABA-only asthma treatment is preferred
- **B.** They are LABAs and should not be used without ICS in asthma
- **C.** They are both short-acting muscarinic antagonists
- **D.** They are both inhaled corticosteroids

<details><summary>✅ Correct answer and detailed explanation</summary>

**Kyun sahi hai:** LABA prolonged bronchodilation; asthma safety ke liye ICS-containing regimen chahiye.
