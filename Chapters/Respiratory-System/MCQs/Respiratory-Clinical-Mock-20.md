### Q1. An adult has sudden breathlessness after central venous catheter insertion, unilateral absent breath sounds and rapidly falling blood pressure. What is the priority?

**Difficulty:** 🟡 Medium · **Priority:** 🔴 MOST IMPORTANT · NORCET / Staff Nurse

- **A.** Teach breathing exercises first
- **B.** Activate emergency response for suspected tension pneumothorax
- **C.** Wait for routine morning imaging
- **D.** Give a large meal

<details><summary>✅ Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: B. Activate emergency response for suspected tension pneumothorax**

**Kyun sahi hai?** Central venous catheter insertion ke baad sudden breathlessness, ek side breath sounds absent aur BP girna tension pneumothorax ka strong warning hai. Pleural pressure badhne se lung compress aur venous return kam ho sakta hai. ABC assess karo, emergency response activate karo aur authorized clinician ke immediate treatment ki preparation karo. Unstable patient mein routine imaging ka wait mat karo.

**Baaki options kyun galat hain?**

- **A.** Breathing exercises pressure ko relieve nahi karte aur emergency care delay karte hain.
- **C.** Routine imaging ka wait shock ko worsen kar sakta hai.
- **D.** Meal dene se acute respiratory aur circulatory problem solve nahi hoti.

**Exam trick:** Unstable tension pneumothorax needs immediate escalation.

[Clinical reference 1](https://www.merckmanuals.com/en-ca/professional/multimedia/table/mechanisms-of-cardiogenic-and-obstructive-shock) · [Clinical reference 2](https://openstax.org/books/medical-surgical-nursing/pages/11-6-disorders-of-the-lower-respiratory-system-pneumothorax)

</details>

### Q2. A chest drain collection chamber is accidentally raised above the patient's chest during transport. What should be corrected?

**Difficulty:** 🟢 Easy · **Priority:** 🔴 MOST IMPORTANT · NORCET / Staff Nurse

- **A.** Return the drainage system below chest level and assess the patient
- **B.** Keep it above the chest for faster drainage
- **C.** Clamp the tube routinely for the rest of transport
- **D.** Disconnect the patient from the drainage system

<details><summary>✅ Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: A. Return the drainage system below chest level and assess the patient**

**Kyun sahi hai?** Chest drainage bottle insertion site se neeche aur upright rehni chahiye. Isse gravity drainage maintain hoti hai aur fluid backflow ka risk kam hota hai. Patient ki breathing aur tubing connections assess karo aur drain transport protocol follow karo.

**Baaki options kyun galat hain?**

- **B.** Bottle upar rakhne se backflow ka risk ho sakta hai, drainage faster nahi hoti.
- **C.** Routine clamping air escape rok kar tension pneumothorax ka risk badha sakti hai.
- **D.** System disconnect karne se air entry aur contamination ka risk hota hai.

**Exam trick:** Chest drain below chest.

[Clinical reference 1](https://thorax.bmj.com/content/78/Suppl_3/s43)

</details>

### Q3. A postoperative patient has shallow respirations and basal atelectasis after prolonged bed rest. Which combined intervention is most useful if stable?

**Difficulty:** 🟡 Medium · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD · NORCET / Staff Nurse

- **A.** Prolonged strict bed rest
- **B.** Routine fluid restriction regardless of condition
- **C.** Suppress every cough
- **D.** Adequate pain control, deep breathing and early mobilization

<details><summary>✅ Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: D. Adequate pain control, deep breathing and early mobilization**

**Kyun sahi hai?** Postoperative pain aur prolonged bed rest ki wajah se patient shallow breaths le sakta hai. Alveoli collapse hone ka risk badhta hai. Adequate prescribed analgesia, supported deep breathing, coughing aur safe early mobilization lung expansion aur secretion clearance mein help karte hain. Patient unstable ho toh pehle assessment aur medical review karo.

**Baaki options kyun galat hain?**

- **A.** Prolonged bed rest shallow ventilation aur secretion retention badha sakta hai.
- **B.** Fluid restriction har postoperative patient ke liye indicated nahi hai.
- **C.** Effective cough suppress karne se secretions retain ho sakti hain.

**Exam trick:** Prevent atelectasis by lung expansion.

[Clinical reference 1](https://openstax.org/books/medical-surgical-nursing/pages/27-3-hospitalized-postoperative-patient)

</details>

### Q4. A patient with acute pulmonary embolism develops severe hypotension and cool extremities. What does this suggest?

**Difficulty:** 🟡 Medium · **Priority:** 🔴 MOST IMPORTANT · NORCET / Staff Nurse

- **A.** Stable chronic bronchitis
- **B.** Isolated mild anemia
- **C.** High-risk PE with obstructive shock
- **D.** Uncomplicated seasonal allergy

<details><summary>✅ Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: C. High-risk PE with obstructive shock**

**Kyun sahi hai?** Pulmonary embolism pulmonary vascular flow obstruct kar sakta hai. Right ventricle ko sudden pressure load milta hai aur left heart filling aur cardiac output gir sakte hain. Severe hypotension aur cool extremities shock suggest karte hain. Emergency assessment aur escalation karo.

**Baaki options kyun galat hain?**

- **A.** Stable chronic bronchitis acute PE ke saath sudden shock explain nahi karti.
- **B.** Mild anemia isolated finding ke roop mein is sudden obstructive picture ko explain nahi karti.
- **D.** Uncomplicated seasonal allergy severe circulatory collapse ka typical explanation nahi hai.

**Exam trick:** PE plus hypotension is high risk.

[Clinical reference 1](https://www.merckmanuals.com/en-ca/professional/multimedia/table/mechanisms-of-cardiogenic-and-obstructive-shock)

</details>

### Q5. A patient has an acute asthma attack and cannot complete a sentence in one breath. Which response is appropriate?

**Difficulty:** 🟡 Medium · **Priority:** 🔴 MOST IMPORTANT · NORCET / Staff Nurse

- **A.** Encourage strenuous exercise
- **B.** Delay treatment until spirometry is available
- **C.** Treat as severe distress and arrange urgent assessment and prescribed rescue therapy
- **D.** Ask the patient to finish a long questionnaire

<details><summary>✅ Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: C. Treat as severe distress and arrange urgent assessment and prescribed rescue therapy**

**Kyun sahi hai?** Acute asthma mein poora sentence bolne mein difficulty significant respiratory distress ka clue hai. Patient ki breathing, oxygenation, mental status aur response assess karo. Urgent help arrange karke prescribed rescue treatment aur oxygen protocol follow karo. Spirometry ya lengthy history ke liye emergency treatment delay mat karo.

**Baaki options kyun galat hain?**

- **A.** Strenuous exercise distress aur oxygen demand badha sakti hai.
- **B.** Spirometry emergency care se pehle compulsory nahi hai.
- **D.** Long questionnaire se priority treatment delay ho sakta hai.

**Exam trick:** Speech difficulty is severity clue.

### Q6. A patient with ARDS is ventilated with a tidal volume based on actual body weight despite obesity. Which principle should be reviewed?

**Difficulty:** 🔴 Difficult · **Priority:** 🟡 IMPORTANT — critical care enrichment · NORCET / Staff Nurse

- **A.** Avoid monitoring plateau pressure
- **B.** Use predicted body weight based on height and sex for lung-protective tidal volume
- **C.** Increase tidal volume in proportion to body fat
- **D.** Use actual body weight for every patient

<details><summary>✅ Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: B. Use predicted body weight based on height and sex for lung-protective tidal volume**

**Kyun sahi hai?** ARDS mein lung-protective ventilation ke liye tidal volume predicted body weight par calculate kiya jaata hai, jo height aur sex-based formula se estimate hota hai. Obesity actual weight badhati hai, lekin lung size us proportion mein nahi badhta. Team ko tidal volume aur plateau pressure review karna chahiye. Ventilator settings trained team aur protocol ke according adjust hoti hain.

**Baaki options kyun galat hain?**

- **A.** Plateau pressure lung stress assess karne mein important hai.
- **C.** Body fat ke proportion mein tidal volume badhana overdistension ka risk badha sakta hai.
- **D.** Actual body weight har patient ke liye lung-protective tidal volume ka appropriate basis nahi hai.

**Exam trick:** ARDS lung-protective ventilation uses predicted weight.

### Q7. A patient on CPAP for obstructive sleep apnea asks why it helps. What is the best explanation?

**Difficulty:** 🟢 Easy · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD · NORCET / Staff Nurse

- **A.** Positive airway pressure helps keep the upper airway open during sleep
- **B.** CPAP permanently removes the tonsils
- **C.** CPAP provides insulin during sleep
- **D.** CPAP stops all respiratory muscle activity

<details><summary>✅ Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: A. Positive airway pressure helps keep the upper airway open during sleep**

**Kyun sahi hai?** OSA mein sleep ke dauran upper airway repeatedly collapse ho sakti hai. CPAP positive pressure se airway ko open rakhne mein help karta hai. Isse obstructive breathing interruptions kam ho sakte hain. Patient ko correct mask fit aur prescribed nightly use samjhao.

**Baaki options kyun galat hain?**

- **B.** CPAP tonsils surgically remove nahi karta.
- **C.** CPAP insulin deliver nahi karta.
- **D.** CPAP spontaneous respiratory muscle activity ko completely stop nahi karta.

**Exam trick:** CPAP splints the airway.

### Q8. A patient with flail chest has increasing respiratory fatigue despite analgesia. What is the priority?

**Difficulty:** 🟡 Medium · **Priority:** 🔴 MOST IMPORTANT · NORCET / Staff Nurse

<details><summary>✅ Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

Increasing respiratory fatigue ka matlab patient breathing maintain karne mein struggle kar raha ho sakta hai. Oxygenation, ventilation, respiratory effort aur consciousness reassess karo aur urgent respiratory support ke liye escalate karo.

**Baaki options kyun galat hain?**

- **A.** Restrictive binding chest expansion aur ventilation limit kar sakti hai.
- **B.** Heavy lifting respiratory distress mein unsafe hai.
- **C.** Pain kam hona adequate ventilation ka proof nahi hai.

**Exam trick:** Flail chest needs respiratory reassessment.

Foul-smelling sputum supportive clue ho sakta hai, lekin diagnosis history, assessment aur imaging se confirm hota hai.

**Baaki options kyun galat hain?**

- **A.** Ankle sprain aspiration risk ka direct clue nahi hai.

### Q10. A patient has severe hypoxemia that improves little despite high inspired oxygen in extensive alveolar consolidation. What mechanism best explains this?

**Difficulty:** 🔴 Difficult · **Priority:** 🟡 IMPORTANT — physiology enrichment · NORCET / Staff Nurse

- **A.** Excess ventilation of all perfused alveoli
- **B.** Increased upper-airway humidification
- **C.** Intrapulmonary shunt through poorly ventilated but perfused alveoli
- **D.** Increased oxygen content from anemia

<details><summary>✅ Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: C. Intrapulmonary shunt through poorly ventilated but perfused alveoli**

**Kyun sahi hai?** Extensive consolidation mein kuch alveoli fluid ya inflammatory material se fill ho jaate hain. Agar blood un units se pass ho raha hai jahan ventilation bahut kam ya absent hai, toh oxygenation poor rahegi. True shunt mein oxygen dene par bhi shunted blood adequately oxygenate nahi hota. Ye severe hypoxemia ka mechanism explain karta hai. Actual patient mein V/Q mismatch bhi coexist kar sakta hai.

**Baaki options kyun galat hain?**

- **A.** Adequately ventilated perfused alveoli mein oxygen transfer possible hota hai, jo shunt pattern nahi hai.
- **B.** Humidification consolidated alveoli ke perfusion without ventilation ko explain nahi karti.
- **D.** Anemia oxygen content reduce kar sakti hai, increase nahi karti.

**Exam trick:** Shunt responds poorly to oxygen alone.

[Clinical reference 1](https://www.merckmanuals.com/professional/critical-care-medicine/respiratory-failure-and-mechanical-ventilation/acute-hypoxemic-respiratory-failure-ahrf-including-acute-respiratory-distress-syndrome-ards)

</details>

### Q11. A patient using a metered-dose inhaler with a spacer fires three puffs into the chamber before inhaling. Which teaching is best?

**Difficulty:** 🟡 Medium · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD · NORCET / Staff Nurse

- **A.** Wash the device with boiling water before every puff
- **B.** Administer one puff at a time and inhale according to device instructions
- **C.** Fire all doses together to save time
- **D.** Exhale into the chamber immediately after firing

<details><summary>✅ Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: B. Administer one puff at a time and inhale according to device instructions**

**Kyun sahi hai?** Spacer mein multiple puffs ek saath release karne se medicine chamber ki walls par deposit ho sakti hai aur delivered dose unpredictable ho sakti hai. Ek actuation karo aur device-specific technique ke according inhale karo. Agla prescribed puff recommended interval ke baad separately lo.

**Baaki options kyun galat hain?**

- **A.** Har puff se pehle boiling water use karna zaroori nahi hai aur device damage ho sakta hai.
- **C.** Saare puffs ek saath release karna reliable delivery nahi deta.
- **D.** Actuation ke turant baad chamber mein forcefully exhale karna appropriate inhalation technique nahi hai.

**Exam trick:** Spacer one actuation at a time.

[Clinical reference 1](https://www.asthmaandlung.org.uk/living-with/inhaler-videos/spacer-single-breath)

</details>

### Q12. A patient with respiratory alkalosis from hyperventilation reports tingling around the mouth. Which mechanism can contribute?

**Difficulty:** 🔴 Difficult · **Priority:** 🟡 IMPORTANT — physiology enrichment · NORCET / Staff Nurse

- **A.** Reduced ionized calcium from increased protein binding
- **B.** A rapid increase in total body calcium
- **C.** Complete loss of thyroid function
- **D.** Increased ketone production as the sole cause

<details><summary>✅ Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: A. Reduced ionized calcium from increased protein binding**

**Kyun sahi hai?** Hyperventilation se CO2 kam aur blood pH high ho sakta hai. Alkalemia mein albumin calcium ko zyada bind kar sakta hai. Isse biologically active ionized calcium kam ho sakta hai, chahe total calcium unchanged ho. Tingling aur neuromuscular irritability ho sakti hai. Patient ki hyperventilation ka underlying cause assess karna zaroori hai.

**Baaki options kyun galat hain?**

- **B.** Tingling ke liye total body calcium ka rapid increase required nahi hai.
- **C.** Thyroid function ki complete loss acute alkalemia ka mechanism nahi hai.
- **D.** Ketone accumulation generally metabolic acidosis se related hai, is alkalosis mechanism ka sole cause nahi hai.

**Exam trick:** Alkalosis may lower ionized calcium.

[Clinical reference 1](https://www.merckmanuals.com/professional/nephrology/acid-base-regulation-and-disorders/respiratory-alkalosis)

</details>

### Q14. A patient receiving home oxygen asks whether smoking near the concentrator is safe. Which teaching is correct?

**Difficulty:** 🟢 Easy · **Priority:** 🔴 MOST IMPORTANT · NORCET / Staff Nurse

- **A.** Smoking is safe at low oxygen flow
- **B.** Cover the concentrator with a blanket
- **C.** Use petroleum jelly freely around the cannula
- **D.** No smoking or open flames near oxygen equipment

<details><summary>✅ Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: D. No smoking or open flames near oxygen equipment**

**Kyun sahi hai?** Oxygen khud fuel nahi hai, lekin combustion ko accelerate karti hai. Oxygen-enriched environment mein spark ya flame se serious fire aur burn ho sakte hain. Smoking aur open flames strictly avoid karo. Concentrator ventilation openings clear rakho aur approved water-based skin products use karo.

**Baaki options kyun galat hain?**

- **A.** Low oxygen flow par bhi smoking safe nahi hoti.
- **B.** Blanket ventilation block karke equipment overheating ka risk badha sakti hai.
- **C.** Petroleum-based products oxygen ke saath fire risk badha sakte hain, isliye freely use mat karo.

**Exam trick:** Oxygen supports combustion.

[Clinical reference 1](https://medlineplus.gov/ency/patientinstructions/000049.htm)

</details>

### Q15. A patient with a persistent air leak has continuous bubbling in the water-seal chamber. Which interpretation is most appropriate?

**Difficulty:** 🟡 Medium · **Priority:** 🔴 MOST IMPORTANT · NORCET / Staff Nurse

- **A.** Clamp the drain indefinitely
- **B.** Strip the tube forcefully every hour
- **C.** Assess for a patient or system air leak according to the drainage system
- **D.** Assume continuous bubbling always proves normal function

<details><summary>✅ Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: C. Assess for a patient or system air leak according to the drainage system**

**Kyun sahi hai?** Water-seal chamber mein continuous bubbling patient ke lung se ya drainage system connection se air leak suggest kar sakti hai. Patient ki respiratory status, tubing aur connections assess karo aur local protocol follow karo. Wet suction-control chamber ka expected bubbling pattern water seal se alag hota hai. Routine prolonged clamping mat karo.

**Baaki options kyun galat hain?**

- **A.** Indefinite clamp air ko pleural space mein trap kar sakta hai.

**Exam trick:** Water seal bubbling differs from suction chamber.

### Q17. A patient with worsening pneumonia has lactate elevation, oliguria and cool mottled skin. What is the common concern?

**Difficulty:** 🟡 Medium · **Priority:** 🔴 MOST IMPORTANT · NORCET / Staff Nurse

- **A.** Normal renal filtration
- **B.** Poor tissue perfusion during sepsis
- **C.** Improved oxygen delivery
- **D.** Isolated uncomplicated rhinitis

<details><summary>✅ Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: B. Poor tissue perfusion during sepsis**

**Kyun sahi hai?** Worsening pneumonia ke saath elevated lactate, oliguria aur cool mottled skin tissue perfusion compromise ke clues hain. Sepsis mein circulation aur cellular metabolism dono affect ho sakte hain. Lactate alone se shock diagnose nahi hota, lekin combined signs urgent ABC assessment, escalation aur sepsis protocol ki need dikhate hain.

**Baaki options kyun galat hain?**

- **A.** Oliguria normal renal filtration ka reassuring sign nahi hai.
- **C.** Mottling aur oliguria improved oxygen delivery ke supportive findings nahi hain.
- **D.** Uncomplicated rhinitis systemic hypoperfusion explain nahi karti.

**Exam trick:** Lactate and urine output are perfusion clues.

### Q18. An intubated patient is being assessed for a spontaneous breathing trial. Which finding supports readiness?

**Difficulty:** 🟡 Medium · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD · NORCET / Staff Nurse

- **A.** Improving underlying disease and stable oxygenation and circulation
- **B.** Escalating vasopressor requirements and severe hypoxemia
- **C.** Uncontrolled intracranial hypertension
- **D.** Deep sedation with no spontaneous effort

<details><summary>✅ Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: A. Improving underlying disease and stable oxygenation and circulation**

Team standardized readiness criteria follow kare. SBT readiness aur extubation readiness same nahi hain, airway protection aur secretion management bhi evaluate karne hain.

**Baaki options kyun galat hain?**

- **B.** Worsening vasopressor requirement aur severe hypoxemia instability suggest karte hain.
- **C.** Uncontrolled intracranial hypertension mein respiratory changes harmful ho sakte hain aur specialist assessment chahiye.
- **D.** Spontaneous effort absent ho toh spontaneous breathing trial readiness establish nahi hoti.

**Exam trick:** Weaning requires stability.

### Q19. A patient with a high cervical spinal cord injury develops weak diaphragmatic movement. Which nerve supply explains this risk?

- **A.** Vagus nerve only
- **B.** Femoral nerve roots L2 to L4
- **C.** Optic nerve
- **D.** Phrenic nerve roots C3 to C5

<details><summary>✅ Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Baaki options kyun galat hain?**

- **A.** Vagus nerve diaphragm ki main motor supply nahi hai.
- **B.** Femoral nerve lower limb muscles se related hai.
- **C.** Optic nerve vision se related hai, diaphragm movement se nahi.

### Q20. A patient with pulmonary hypertension and chronic lung disease develops ankle edema and raised neck veins. Which complication should be considered?

**Difficulty:** 🟡 Medium · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD · NORCET / Staff Nurse

- **A.** Uncomplicated sinusitis
- **B.** Increased renal calcium excretion alone
- **C.** Right-sided heart failure from chronic pulmonary pressure load
- **D.** Isolated left wrist fracture

<details><summary>✅ Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: C. Right-sided heart failure from chronic pulmonary pressure load**

**Kyun sahi hai?** Chronic lung disease aur pulmonary hypertension mein right ventricle ko pulmonary circulation mein blood bhejne ke liye zyada pressure overcome karna padta hai. Long-term pressure load right-sided heart dysfunction aur systemic venous congestion cause kar sakta hai. Ankle edema aur raised neck veins is complication ke clues hain, lekin clinical assessment zaroori hai.

**Baaki options kyun galat hain?**

- **A.** Uncomplicated sinusitis systemic venous congestion explain nahi karti.
- **B.** Isolated calcium excretion right ventricular pressure load ka explanation nahi hai.
- **D.** Wrist fracture chronic pulmonary hypertension ke saath edema aur JVP pattern explain nahi karta.

**Exam trick:** Cor pulmonale affects right heart.

[Clinical reference 1](https://openstax.org/books/medical-surgical-nursing/pages/11-5-disorders-of-the-lower-respiratory-system-chronic-obstructive-pulmonary-disease)

</details>
