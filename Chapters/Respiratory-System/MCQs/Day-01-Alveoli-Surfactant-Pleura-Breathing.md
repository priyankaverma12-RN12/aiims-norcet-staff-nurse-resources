## Alveolar cells, gas exchange & surfactant

#### Q1. Which cell is the principal surfactant producer?

- **A.** Type I pneumocyte
- **B.** Type II pneumocyte
- **C.** Macrophage
- **D.** Capillary endothelial cell

<details><summary>✅ Answer & explanation</summary>

**B.** Type II pneumocytes synthesize and secrete surfactant.

</details>

#### Q2. What is the main job of Type I pneumocytes?

- **A.** Produce mucus
- **B.** Fight infection by phagocytosis
- **C.** Provide thin gas-exchange surface
- **D.** Make surfactant

<details><summary>✅ Answer & explanation</summary>

**C.** Thin Type I alveolar epithelium supports diffusion.

</details>

#### Q3. Which cell engulfs inhaled microbes in alveoli?

- **A.** Alveolar macrophage
- **B.** Type I pneumocyte
- **C.** Type II pneumocyte
- **D.** Ciliated tracheal cell

<details><summary>✅ Answer & explanation</summary>

**A.** Macrophages remove particles and germs by phagocytosis.

</details>

#### Q4. Surfactant deficiency most directly leads to:

- **A.** Lower surface tension
- **B.** Higher surface tension and collapse tendency
- **C.** More alveolar oxygen production
- **D.** Expansion without effort

<details><summary>✅ Answer & explanation</summary>

**B.** Reduced surfactant increases surface tension and atelectasis risk.

</details>

#### Q5. Oxygen primarily crosses the alveolar–capillary membrane by:

- **A.** Osmosis
- **B.** Active transport
- **C.** Filtration
- **D.** Diffusion

<details><summary>✅ Answer & explanation</summary>

**D.** Gases follow partial-pressure gradients through diffusion.

</details>

## Pleura, diaphragm & inspiration/expiration

#### Q1. Which pleural layer directly touches lung surface?

- **A.** Parietal
- **B.** Visceral
- **C.** Pericardial
- **D.** Mucosal

<details><summary>✅ Answer & explanation</summary>

**B.** Visceral pleura adheres to the lungs.

</details>

#### Q2. What does pleural fluid primarily do?

- **A.** Create air pressure
- **B.** Make surfactant
- **C.** Reduce friction
- **D.** Carry oxygen

<details><summary>✅ Answer & explanation</summary>

**C.** The fluid lubricates pleural surfaces.

</details>

#### Q3. During quiet inspiration, the diaphragm:

- **A.** Contracts and descends
- **B.** Relaxes and ascends
- **C.** Contracts and ascends
- **D.** Remains stationary

<details><summary>✅ Answer & explanation</summary>

**A.** Its descent increases thoracic volume and promotes airflow inward.

</details>

#### Q4. Quiet expiration in a resting adult is usually:

- **A.** A forceful abdominal contraction
- **B.** Caused by diaphragm contraction
- **C.** Impossible without coughing
- **D.** Passive due to elastic recoil

<details><summary>✅ Answer & explanation</summary>

**D.** Inspiratory muscles relax and elastic recoil moves air out.

</details>

#### Q5. The main diaphragm motor nerve arises from:

- **A.** T1–T3
- **B.** C3–C5
- **C.** T8–T12
- **D.** L1–L3

<details><summary>✅ Answer & explanation</summary>

**B.** Phrenic nerve originates primarily from C3, C4, and C5.

</details>
