## Atelectasis and incentive spirometry

### Q1. After abdominal surgery, a patient avoids deep breaths because of pain. Which mechanism most directly predisposes to atelectasis?

**Difficulty:** 🟡 Medium · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD · Original exam-style practice

- **A.** Increased alveolar expansion from deep breathing
- **B.** Permanent dilation of all bronchi
- **C.** Poor alveolar expansion from shallow ventilation
- **D.** Airborne infection is inevitable

<details><summary>✅ Correct answer and detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: C. Poor alveolar expansion from shallow ventilation**

**Kyun sahi hai?** Surgery ke baad incision pain ki wajah se patient shallow breaths le sakta hai. Jab alveoli adequately expand nahi hoti, lung ke kuch regions mein aeration kam ho sakti hai aur alveoli collapse ho sakti hain. Ise atelectasis kehte hain. Nurse pain, breathing effort aur oxygenation assess kare. Prescribed pain relief aur tolerated mobilization se lung expansion mein help mil sakti hai.

**Baaki options kyun galat hain?**

- **A.** Deep breaths alveolar expansion support karte hain. Ye collapse ka proposed mechanism nahi hai.
- **B.** Permanent abnormal bronchial dilation bronchiectasis ka feature hai, alveolar collapse ka mechanism nahi.
- **D.** Surgery ke baad infection possible hai, lekin unavoidable airborne infection atelectasis ka direct cause nahi hai.

**Exam trick:** Pain → shallow breathing → reduced expansion.

### Q2. A postoperative chest image shows opacity accompanied by loss of lung volume. Which process is most compatible?

**Difficulty:** 🟡 Medium · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD · Original exam-style practice

- **A.** Uncomplicated emphysema with hyperinflation
- **B.** Atelectasis
- **C.** Isolated rhinitis
- **D.** Normal inspiration

<details><summary>✅ Correct answer and detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: B. Atelectasis**

**Kyun sahi hai?** Atelectasis mein lung tissue ki aeration aur volume kam ho sakte hain. Isliye X-ray par opacity ke saath volume loss ek important clue hai. Mucus plug ya postoperative shallow breathing possible causes hain. Imaging ko symptoms aur other findings ke saath interpret karna chahiye.

**Baaki options kyun galat hain?**

- **A.** Emphysema mein hyperinflation common hai, jo given volume-loss clue ke opposite hai.
- **C.** Rhinitis nasal mucosa ki inflammation hai. Ye lung opacity with volume loss explain nahi karti.
- **D.** Normal inspiration lung expansion badhati hai. New postoperative opacity with volume loss ko normal assume nahi karte.

**Exam trick:** Opacity plus volume loss suggests atelectasis.

### Q3. Which instruction is appropriate when using an incentive spirometer?

**Difficulty:** 🟢 Easy · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD · Original exam-style practice

- **A.** Inhale slowly and deeply through the mouthpiece
- **B.** Blow out as rapidly as possible into it
- **C.** Use only while lying flat with eyes closed
- **D.** Stop all coughing exercises permanently

<details><summary>✅ Correct answer and detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: A. Inhale slowly and deeply through the mouthpiece**

**Kyun sahi hai?** Incentive spirometer slow sustained inspiration encourage karta hai. Patient comfortable upright position mein mouthpiece seal karke slowly deep breath leta hai. Device instruction aur patient tolerance ke according brief breath hold aur rest kare. Nurse return demonstration se technique check kare. Ye postoperative care bundle ka adjunct hai, standalone guarantee nahi.

**Baaki options kyun galat hain?**

- **B.** Forceful expiration kuch pulmonary function tests mein use hoti hai. Incentive spirometer ka intended maneuver inspiration hai.
- **C.** Flat position mandatory nahi hai. Upright posture as tolerated often lung expansion mein help karta hai.
- **D.** Directed cough secretion clearance mein help kar sakti hai. Ise permanently stop karna appropriate nahi.

**Exam trick:** Incentive spirometry = slow inhale, not forced exhale.

### Q4. Which intervention best complements incentive spirometry after surgery?

**Difficulty:** 🟡 Medium · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD · Original exam-style practice

- **A.** Prolonged immobility
- **B.** Avoid all deep breathing
- **C.** Suppress every cough regardless of secretions
- **D.** Adequate pain management and early mobilization as tolerated

<details><summary>✅ Correct answer and detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: D. Adequate pain management and early mobilization as tolerated**

**Kyun sahi hai?** Pain control se patient deep breathe aur effective cough kar sakta hai. Early mobilization as tolerated ventilation aur recovery support karti hai. Incentive spirometry akela postoperative pulmonary complications prevent karne ki guarantee nahi deta. Individualized bundle, prescribed analgesia aur reassessment zaroori hain.

**Baaki options kyun galat hain?**

- **A.** Prolonged immobility shallow ventilation aur secretion retention ko badha sakti hai.
- **B.** Deep breathing avoid karne se lung expansion ka goal compromise hota hai.
- **C.** Har cough suppress karne se retained secretions clear nahi ho sakti.

**Exam trick:** Pain management + movement + breathing exercises.

## Chest physiotherapy

### Q5. What is the purpose of cupped-hand percussion in chest physiotherapy?

**Difficulty:** 🟢 Easy · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD · Original exam-style practice

- **A.** Directly measure arterial oxygen pressure
- **B.** Confirm pulmonary artery thrombus
- **C.** Drain blood from the pleural cavity by itself
- **D.** Mobilize retained airway secretions

<details><summary>✅ Correct answer and detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: D. Mobilize retained airway secretions**

**Kyun sahi hai?** Chest percussion mein trained person cupped hands se chest wall par appropriate rhythmic tapping karta hai. Isse retained mucus mobilize ho sakta hai. Ye technique selected patients mein cough ya other airway-clearance methods ke saath use hoti hai. Significant bleeding ya instability mein technique reassess karni chahiye.

**Baaki options kyun galat hain?**

- **A.** PaO2 arterial blood gas se assess hota hai, percussion se directly measure nahi hota.
- **B.** Pulmonary arterial thrombus ke liye suitable imaging aur diagnostic pathway chahiye.
- **C.** Pleural blood ko chest percussion se independently drain nahi kiya ja sakta.

**Exam trick:** Cupped-hand percussion helps move mucus.

### Q6. Which principle is used in postural drainage?

**Difficulty:** 🟢 Easy · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD · Original exam-style practice · **🟣 JIPMER Staff Nurse 2013 — related reported concept**

- **A.** Gravity removes all pulmonary emboli
- **B.** Positive cuff pressure clears mucus automatically
- **C.** Gravity assists movement of secretions toward larger airways
- **D.** Negative pressure is applied to the skin to measure FEV1

<details><summary>✅ Correct answer and detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: C. Gravity assists movement of secretions toward larger airways**

**Kyun sahi hai?** Postural drainage mein selected body position gravity ki madad se affected airway ki secretions ko larger airways ki taraf move karne mein help kar sakti hai. Position involved lung segment, reflux, tolerance aur physiotherapist ki assessment se decide hoti hai. Prone ya head-down posture har patient ke liye compulsory nahi hai.

**Baaki options kyun galat hain?**

- **A.** Gravity-assisted drainage airway mucus ke liye hai, vascular clot removal ke liye nahi.
- **B.** Cuff pressure airway seal se related hai. Ye mucus clearance automatically achieve nahi karta.
- **D.** FEV1 spirometry se measure hota hai. Skin par suction ka is calculation se relation nahi hai.

**Exam trick:** Gravity assists secretion drainage. Position individualized.

### Q7. Why should head-down drainage not be used indiscriminately after a large meal?

**Difficulty:** 🟡 Medium · **Priority:** 🔴 MOST IMPORTANT · Original exam-style practice

- **A.** It always increases renal bicarbonate
- **B.** It may increase reflux and aspiration risk
- **C.** It destroys alveolar walls immediately
- **D.** It confirms tuberculosis

<details><summary>✅ Correct answer and detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: B. It may increase reflux and aspiration risk**

**Kyun sahi hai?** Large meal ke baad stomach full hota hai. Head-down posture se reflux ka risk badh sakta hai. Agar gastric contents airway mein chale jaayen toh aspiration ho sakti hai. Reflux, swallowing risk aur patient tolerance ke hisaab se modified drainage ya sitting technique choose karne mein respiratory physiotherapist ki advice lo.

**Baaki options kyun galat hain?**

- **A.** Renal bicarbonate mein automatic increase postural drainage ka expected effect nahi hai.
- **C.** Meal ke baad head-down hone se alveolar walls immediately destroy hona expected nahi hai.
- **D.** Body positioning TB confirm nahi karti. TB diagnosis appropriate investigations se hoti hai.

**Exam trick:** Meal and reflux risk: avoid indiscriminate head-down positioning.

## Pulmonary edema

### Q8. A patient with acute left ventricular failure develops bilateral crackles and severe orthopnea. Which mechanism is most likely?

**Difficulty:** 🟡 Medium · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD · Original exam-style practice

- **A.** Elevated pulmonary capillary hydrostatic pressure
- **B.** Reduced pulmonary capillary pressure as the main mechanism
- **C.** Loss of bronchial wall elasticity alone
- **D.** Air entering the pleural cavity

<details><summary>✅ Correct answer and detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: A. Elevated pulmonary capillary hydrostatic pressure**

**Kyun sahi hai?** Left ventricle adequately blood forward pump nahi kar paata toh left atrium aur pulmonary venous pressure rise ho sakta hai. Pulmonary capillary hydrostatic pressure badhne se fluid interstitium aur alveoli mein move kar sakta hai. Ye cardiogenic pulmonary edema ka mechanism hai. Severe orthopnea aur crackles mein immediate assessment zaroori hai.

**Baaki options kyun galat hain?**

- **B.** Reduced hydrostatic pressure given left-heart-failure mechanism ke opposite hai.
- **C.** Bronchial wall elasticity alone cardiogenic alveolar fluid accumulation explain nahi karti.
- **D.** Pleural air pneumothorax ka mechanism hai, hydrostatic edema ka nahi.

**Exam trick:** Left-heart failure → raised pulmonary vascular pressure → fluid.

### Q9. Which finding is classically associated with acute pulmonary edema?

**Difficulty:** 🟢 Easy · **Priority:** 🔴 MOST IMPORTANT · Original exam-style practice

- **A.** Pink frothy sputum with severe breathlessness
- **B.** Isolated itchy nose without respiratory distress
- **C.** Painless chronic nail changes alone
- **D.** Normal breath sounds with no symptoms in every case

<details><summary>✅ Correct answer and detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: A. Pink frothy sputum with severe breathlessness**

**Kyun sahi hai?** Alveoli mein fluid hawa ke saath mix hokar frothy sputum bana sakta hai. Blood admixture se pink color aa sakta hai. Severe dyspnea, crackles aur orthopnea bhi mil sakte hain. Pink froth har patient mein nahi hota aur uske absent hone se edema rule out nahi hota. Acute distress mein ABC aur urgent escalation karo.

**Baaki options kyun galat hain?**

- **B.** Itchy nose isolated allergic upper-airway symptom ho sakta hai.
- **C.** Chronic nail changes acute alveolar fluid accumulation ka specific clue nahi hain.
- **D.** Acute edema symptomatic ho sakta hai. Normal breath sounds in every case ka claim incorrect hai.

**Exam trick:** Pink froth is a classic clue, not a required finding.

### Q10. A patient receives prescribed IV diuretic for cardiogenic pulmonary edema. Which monitoring pair is especially relevant?

**Difficulty:** 🟡 Medium · **Priority:** 🔴 MOST IMPORTANT · Original exam-style practice

- **A.** Hair color and shoe size
- **B.** Mantoux induration and hearing acuity
- **C.** Only the number of meals eaten
- **D.** Urine output and blood pressure

<details><summary>✅ Correct answer and detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: D. Urine output and blood pressure**

**Kyun sahi hai?** IV diuretic urine output badhakar congestion reduce karne mein help kar sakti hai. Fluid loss se blood pressure gir sakta hai, isliye output aur BP important hain. Renal function, electrolytes, fluid balance, weight aur respiratory response bhi prescribed monitoring plan mein assess karo. Significant hypotension ya poor response promptly report karo.

**Baaki options kyun galat hain?**

- **A.** Hair color aur shoe size diuretic response monitor nahi karte.
- **B.** Mantoux TB infection testing hai. Hearing assessment selected drug safety mein relevant ho sakti hai, lekin given pair acute diuretic response ka best measure nahi hai.
- **C.** Meal count alone fluid response ya hypotension identify nahi karta.

**Exam trick:** Monitor diuresis and perfusion, not urine alone.

## Bronchiectasis

### Q11. A patient with repeated chest infections has HRCT showing permanently widened bronchi. Which diagnosis is supported?

**Difficulty:** 🟡 Medium · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD · Original exam-style practice

- **A.** Isolated pleural effusion
- **B.** Acute laryngitis
- **C.** Bronchiectasis
- **D.** Uncomplicated sleep apnea

<details><summary>✅ Correct answer and detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: C. Bronchiectasis**

**Kyun sahi hai?** Bronchiectasis mein bronchi abnormally aur permanently widened hoti hain. Damaged airway ki secretion clearance impaired ho sakti hai, jisse chronic productive cough aur recurrent infections develop hote hain. Compatible clinical symptoms ke saath thin-section CT diagnosis support karti hai. Signet-ring appearance ek imaging clue hai, isolated finding ko clinical context mein interpret karo.

**Baaki options kyun galat hain?**

- **A.** Pleural effusion lung ke bahar pleural space mein fluid hai.
- **B.** Laryngitis larynx ki inflammation hai, bronchi ki permanent dilation nahi.
- **D.** Sleep apnea sleep ke dauran breathing disturbance hai. Widened bronchi iska defining imaging finding nahi hai.

**Exam trick:** Bronchiectasis = permanent structural bronchial dilation.

### Q12. Why is airway clearance an important part of bronchiectasis care?

**Difficulty:** 🟡 Medium · **Priority:** 🔴 MOST IMPORTANT · Original exam-style practice

- **A.** It reverses all structural dilation permanently
- **B.** Damaged dilated bronchi retain mucus and predispose to infection
- **C.** It replaces antibiotics in every acute infection
- **D.** It eliminates the need to assess hemoptysis

<details><summary>✅ Correct answer and detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: B. Damaged dilated bronchi retain mucus and predispose to infection**

**Kyun sahi hai?** Damaged dilated bronchi mein mucus retain ho sakta hai. Mucus retention infection aur inflammation ke cycle ko maintain kar sakti hai. Individualized airway clearance se secretions remove karne mein help milti hai. ERS 2025 guideline most adults ke liye airway-clearance training support karti hai. Acute infection aur new hemoptysis ka separate assessment zaroori hai.

**Baaki options kyun galat hain?**

- **A.** Airway clearance structural dilation permanently reverse nahi karti.
- **C.** Airway clearance indicated antibiotic treatment ka universal replacement nahi hai.
- **D.** Hemoptysis ka clinical assessment zaroori hai. Significant bleeding mein urgent escalation aur technique review chahiye.

**Exam trick:** Clear secretions, not cure structural damage.
