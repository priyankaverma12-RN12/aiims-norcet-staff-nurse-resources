## Atelectasis and incentive spirometry

### Q1. After abdominal surgery, a patient avoids deep breaths because of pain. Which mechanism most directly predisposes to atelectasis?

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Exam focus:** NORCET / HP Staff Nurse / PGIMER (original)

- **A.** Increased alveolar expansion from deep breathing
- **B.** Permanent dilation of all bronchi
- **C.** Poor alveolar expansion from shallow ventilation
- **D.** Airborne infection is inevitable

<details><summary>✅ Correct answer and detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: C. Poor alveolar expansion from shallow ventilation**

**Kyun sahi hai aur baaki options kyun galat:** Pain reduces inspiration and alveolar expansion; deep breathing helps; dilation bronchiectasis, infection not inevitable.

**Exam trick:** Pain reduces inspiration and alveolar expansion.

</details>

### Q2. A postoperative chest image shows opacity accompanied by loss of lung volume. Which process is most compatible?

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Exam focus:** NORCET / HP Staff Nurse / PGIMER (original)

- **A.** Uncomplicated emphysema with hyperinflation
- **B.** Atelectasis
- **C.** Isolated rhinitis
- **D.** Normal inspiration

<details><summary>✅ Correct answer and detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: B. Atelectasis**

**Kyun sahi hai aur baaki options kyun galat:** Collapse causes volume loss; emphysema hyperinflates, rhinitis upper airway, normal inspiration expands.

**Exam trick:** Collapse causes volume loss.

</details>

### Q3. Which instruction is appropriate when using an incentive spirometer?

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Exam focus:** NORCET / HP Staff Nurse / PGIMER (original)

- **A.** Inhale slowly and deeply through the mouthpiece
- **B.** Blow out as rapidly as possible into it
- **C.** Use only while lying flat with eyes closed
- **D.** Stop all coughing exercises permanently

<details><summary>✅ Correct answer and detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: A. Inhale slowly and deeply through the mouthpiece**

**Kyun sahi hai aur baaki options kyun galat:** Incentive spirometry encourages inspiration; forceful expiration different PFT, lying flat not ideal, coughing part of bundle.

**Exam trick:** Incentive spirometry encourages inspiration.

</details>

### Q4. Which intervention best complements incentive spirometry after surgery?

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Exam focus:** NORCET / HP Staff Nurse / PGIMER (original)

- **A.** Prolonged immobility
- **B.** Avoid all deep breathing
- **C.** Suppress every cough regardless of secretions
- **D.** Adequate pain management and early mobilization as tolerated

<details><summary>✅ Correct answer and detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: D. Adequate pain management and early mobilization as tolerated**

**Kyun sahi hai aur baaki options kyun galat:** Analgesia and mobility aid ventilation; immobility and suppressing clearance increase risk.

**Exam trick:** Analgesia and mobility aid ventilation.

</details>

## Chest physiotherapy

- **B.** Confirm pulmonary artery thrombus
- **C.** Drain blood from the pleural cavity by itself
- **D.** Mobilize retained airway secretions

<details><summary>✅ Correct answer and detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: D. Mobilize retained airway secretions**

**Kyun sahi hai aur baaki options kyun galat:** Percussion loosens mucus; ABG pressure, CTPA thrombus, chest drain pleural blood.

</details>

## Pulmonary edema

### Q8. A patient with acute left ventricular failure develops bilateral crackles and severe orthopnea. Which mechanism is most likely?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Exam focus:** NORCET / HP Staff Nurse / PGIMER (original)

- **A.** Elevated pulmonary capillary hydrostatic pressure
- **B.** Reduced pulmonary capillary pressure as the main mechanism
- **C.** Loss of bronchial wall elasticity alone
- **D.** Air entering the pleural cavity

<details><summary>✅ Correct answer and detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: A. Elevated pulmonary capillary hydrostatic pressure**

**Kyun sahi hai aur baaki options kyun galat:** LV failure increases upstream pressure causing edema; lower pressure opposite, bronchial dilation and pleural air different.

**Exam trick:** LV failure increases upstream pressure causing edema.

</details>

### Q9. Which finding is classically associated with acute pulmonary edema?

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Exam focus:** NORCET / HP Staff Nurse / PGIMER (original)

- **A.** Pink frothy sputum with severe breathlessness
- **B.** Isolated itchy nose without respiratory distress
- **C.** Painless chronic nail changes alone
- **D.** Normal breath sounds with no symptoms in every case

<details><summary>✅ Correct answer and detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: A. Pink frothy sputum with severe breathlessness**

**Kyun sahi hai aur baaki options kyun galat:** Fluid plus air can produce pink froth; other clues do not support acute edema.

**Exam trick:** Fluid plus air can produce pink froth.

</details>

## Bronchiectasis

### Q11. A patient with repeated chest infections has HRCT showing permanently widened bronchi. Which diagnosis is supported?

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Exam focus:** NORCET / HP Staff Nurse / PGIMER (original)

- **A.** Isolated pleural effusion
- **B.** Acute laryngitis
- **C.** Bronchiectasis
- **D.** Uncomplicated sleep apnea

<details><summary>✅ Correct answer and detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: C. Bronchiectasis**

**Kyun sahi hai aur baaki options kyun galat:** Permanent bronchial dilatation bronchiectasis; pleural fluid, larynx inflammation, sleep obstruction different.

**Exam trick:** Permanent bronchial dilatation bronchiectasis.

</details>

### Q12. Why is airway clearance an important part of bronchiectasis care?

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Exam focus:** NORCET / HP Staff Nurse / PGIMER (original)

- **A.** It reverses all structural dilation permanently
- **B.** Damaged dilated bronchi retain mucus and predispose to infection
- **C.** It replaces antibiotics in every acute infection
- **D.** It eliminates the need to assess hemoptysis

<details><summary>✅ Correct answer and detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: B. Damaged dilated bronchi retain mucus and predispose to infection**

**Kyun sahi hai aur baaki options kyun galat:** Clearance reduces retained secretions; not cure structural change or antibiotic replacement; bleeding needs assessment.

**Exam trick:** Clearance reduces retained secretions.

</details>
