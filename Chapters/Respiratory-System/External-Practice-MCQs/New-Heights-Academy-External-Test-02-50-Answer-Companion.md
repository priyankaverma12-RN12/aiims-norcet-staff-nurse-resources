## Respiratory failure

### Q31 — ABG classification

<details>
<summary>Suggested answer and explanation</summary>

**Suggested key: A**

**A ka reason:** Low PaO2 ke saath PaCO2 high nahi hai, isliye hypoxemic Type I failure best answer hai.

**B ka reason:** B mein hypercapnia expected hai.

**C ka reason:** C pH aur bicarbonate ke bina metabolic alkalosis conclude nahi kar sakte.

**D ka reason:** D metabolic acidosis is classification ka best answer nahi hai.

**Exam clue:** Type I = oxygenation failure.

</details>

### Q32 — Hypercapnic failure

<details>

**D ka reason:** D bicarbonate ke bina metabolic alkalosis conclude nahi kar sakte.

</details>
