### Q1. A patient has an anatomical dead space of 150 mL, tidal volume of 400 mL and respiratory rate of 25/min. What is alveolar ventilation?

**Difficulty:** Difficult

- **A.** 6.25 L/min
- **B.** 10 L/min
- **C.** 3.75 L/min
- **D.** 13.75 L/min

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation</summary>

**Correct: A. 6.25 L/min**

**Kyun sahi hai?** Har breath mein 400 minus 150 = 250 mL fresh air alveoli tak jaati hai. 250 x 25 = 6250 mL/min.

**Baaki options kyun galat hain?**

- **B.** Total minute ventilation hai
- **C.** Sirf dead space ventilation hai
- **D.** Dead space ko galat joda hai

**Exam trick:** Alveolar = (TV minus dead space) x RR

### Q3. An aspirated foreign body more commonly enters which main bronchus?

**Difficulty:** Medium

- **A.** Left
- **B.** Right
- **C.** Both equally
- **D.** Neither

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation</summary>

**B.** **Right main bronchus** relatively shorter, wider aur more vertical hota hai, isliye aspirated object is taraf zyada ja sakta hai.

**Baaki options kyun nahi?** Left main bronchus comparatively longer aur less vertical hota hai. Isliye typical adult aspiration question mein **right** choose karte hain, dono equally nahi.

**Exam trick:** Right: shorter + wider + more vertical.

### Q4. Tactile fremitus may increase with:

**Difficulty:** Difficult

- **A.** Pleural effusion
- **B.** Pneumothorax
- **C.** Lung consolidation with open bronchus
- **D.** Large airway obstruction

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation</summary>

**C.** **Consolidation** mein agar bronchus open ho toh voice vibrations better transmit ho sakti hain.

**Baaki options kyun nahi?** Consolidated lung tissue, agar bronchus patent/open hai, vibration ko better conduct kar sakta hai. Pleural effusion/pneumothorax mein pleural space vibration ko insulate karta hai, toh fremitus often **decreased** hoti hai.

**Exam trick:** Increased = consolidation (open airway). decreased = fluid/air around lung.

### Q17. Blood returning oxygenated from the lungs first enters which heart chamber?

**Difficulty:** Medium

- **B.** Right ventricle
- **C.** Left ventricle
- **D.** Left atrium

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation</summary>

**Baaki options kyun nahi?** Right atrium systemic venous blood leta hai. right ventricle se blood lungs jaata hai. left ventricle mein blood left atrium ke baad aata hai. **Pulmonary veins pehle left atrium** mein open hoti hain.

**Exam trick:** Pathway: RV → lungs → LA.
