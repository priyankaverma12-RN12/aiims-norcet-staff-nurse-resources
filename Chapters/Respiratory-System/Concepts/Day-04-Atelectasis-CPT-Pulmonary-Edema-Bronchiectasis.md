## Atelectasis

### 🟠 Kya hota hai aur kyun?

Atelectasis ka matlab hai ki lung ke kuch alveoli collapse ho jaate hain ya unmein normal air nahi pahunchti. Alveoli chhoti air sacs hain jahan gas exchange hota hai. Jab unmein air kam hoti hai, toh lung ke affected part ka volume kam ho sakta hai aur oxygenation disturb ho sakti hai.

Surgery ke baad incision pain se patient deep breath lene aur cough karne se bach sakta hai. Immobility, mucus plug, airway obstruction aur external compression bhi lung expansion ko kam kar sakte hain. Nurse respiratory rate, breath sounds, SpO2, effort aur pain assess kare. Large volume loss mein trachea affected side ki taraf shift ho sakti hai, lekin har patient mein ye sign nahi milta. Chest X-ray par opacity ke saath volume loss important clue hai.

**Clinical example:** Abdominal surgery ke baad patient pain ki wajah se shallow breaths le raha hai. Prescribed analgesia, incision ko support karke cough, early mobilization as tolerated aur deep-breathing care bundle mein help karte hain. New severe hypoxemia ya sudden chest pain mein atelectasis assume karne ke bajay urgent assessment karo.

### Incentive spirometry

Incentive spirometer patient ko slow sustained INHALATION karne ke liye visual feedback deta hai. Comfortable upright position mein patient normally exhale karta hai, mouthpiece seal karta hai aur slowly deep breath leta hai. Device instructions ke according 3–5 seconds breath hold as tolerated karke relax karta hai. Dizziness ya distress ho toh rest aur reassessment chahiye. Frequency prescribed postoperative plan ke according hoti hai. Nurse demonstration ke baad return demonstration le.

**Exam distinction:** Incentive spirometer aur diagnostic spirometry alag hain. Diagnostic spirometry forced expiration se FEV1 aur FVC measure kar sakti hai. Ordinary incentive spirometer ye diagnostic values measure nahi karta. Incentive spirometry alone postoperative complications prevent karne ki guarantee nahi deta.

[MedlinePlus technique](https://medlineplus.gov/ency/patientinstructions/000451.htm) · [Postoperative pulmonary care](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/sites/books/NBK572114/) · [Practice Q1–4](../MCQs/Day-04-Atelectasis-CPT-Pulmonary-Edema-Bronchiectasis.md).

## Chest physiotherapy

### 🟠 Secretion clearance ka mechanism

CPT ka full form chest physiotherapy hai. Jab mucus airways mein retain hota hai, toh patient ke liye effective cough se usko bahar nikalna difficult ho sakta hai. Selected airway-clearance techniques mucus ko larger airways ki taraf move karne mein help karti hain.

| Technique | Kya hota hai? | Nursing safety |
|---|---|---|
| Percussion | Cupped hands se appropriate rhythmic tapping mucus mobilize kar sakti hai | Trained technique use karo. Painful/injured areas aur contraindications assess karo |
| Vibration | Expiration ke dauran trained person chest wall movement support karta hai | Patient comfort aur clinical condition assess karo |
| Postural drainage | Selected position mein gravity secretions ko move karne mein help kar sakti hai | Affected lung segment aur tolerance ke hisaab se position choose hoti hai |
| Active cycle of breathing | Breathing control, chest expansion aur huffing ka individualized sequence | Respiratory physiotherapist patient ko technique sikhata hai |

Bronchiectasis aur cystic fibrosis mein airway clearance useful ho sakti hai. Har uncomplicated pneumonia ya COPD patient ko routine percussion ki need nahi hoti. Large meal ke immediately baad head-down drainage se reflux aur aspiration risk badh sakta hai. Significant hemoptysis, untreated pneumothorax, unstable circulation, raised intracranial pressure aur recent injury mein specific methods or positions contraindicated ho sakte hain. Patient ko forcefully head-down mat karo.

**Example:** Reflux wale patient ko drainage position mein gastric contents throat tak aate hain. Respiratory physiotherapist se modified drainage without head-down tilt ya sitting technique review karwao. New blood in sputum ya worsening oxygenation mein exercise continue karne ke bajay clinical assessment karo.

**🟣 [JIPMER Staff Nurse, 4 June 2013 — related reported concept](https://testbook.com/question-answer/to-do-percussion-and-postural-drainage-of-lungs-in--6221df731082657f8ea987d8):** Source selects prone positioning. Actual position har patient mein prone nahi hoti. Affected bronchopulmonary segment, contraindications aur tolerance matter karte hain. Official key unverified.

## Pulmonary edema

### 🔴 Fluid lungs mein kaise aata hai?

Pulmonary edema ka matlab hai ki fluid lung ke interstitium aur alveoli mein accumulate ho jaata hai. Interstitium lung cells aur small vessels ke beech ka tissue space hai. Fluid oxygen transfer ko difficult bana sakta hai.

| Cardiogenic pulmonary edema | Non-cardiogenic permeability edema |
|---|---|
| Left ventricular failure mein upstream pulmonary venous aur capillary hydrostatic pressure rise ho sakta hai | ARDS jaise disorders mein alveolar-capillary barrier injured ho sakta hai |
| Increased pressure fluid ko vessels ke bahar push karta hai | Barrier leak hone se fluid tissue aur alveoli mein aa sakta hai |
| Cause-directed cardiac treatment aur indicated diuresis important hain | Underlying injury treatment aur appropriate lung-protective respiratory support important hain |

Severe dyspnea, orthopnea (lying flat mein breathlessness), bilateral crackles, sweating aur kabhi pink frothy sputum mil sakta hai. Pink froth classic clue hai, compulsory finding nahi. Crackles edema-specific diagnosis nahi hain.

**Nursing priority:** New severe respiratory distress mein ABC assess karo, urgent help lo aur upright position as tolerated do. Oxygen jab indicated ho tab prescribed target ke according do. Clinical team BP, perfusion aur cause ke hisaab se NIV, diuretic ya vasodilator decide karti hai. Hypotension mein treatment ko blindly apply nahi karna.

**Clinical example:** Prescribed IV diuretic ke baad nurse urine output aur blood pressure monitor karti hai. Weight trend, fluid balance, renal function, electrolytes aur breathing response bhi important hain. Sudden low BP, worsening confusion ya poor urine response report karo.

**🟣 [RRB Nursing Superintendent, 29 April 2025 Shift I — related reported concept](https://testbook.com/question-answer/a-client-with-a-myocardial-infarction-develops-rap--681c8d678b9dc497585feb02):** Retrieved report tests crackles in a patient with pink frothy sputum after MI. ## Bronchiectasis

### 🟠 Definition, causes aur vicious cycle

Bronchiectasis mein bronchi permanently abnormally widen ho jaati hain. Damaged wide pipe mein mucus collect hone jaisa samjho. Jab airway wall damaged hoti hai, normal mucus clearance weak ho sakti hai. Retained mucus se infection aur inflammation badh sakte hain. Inflammation further airway damage kar sakti hai. Is cycle ko todne ke liye secretion clearance aur underlying cause dono manage karne hote hain.

Past severe infections including TB, cystic fibrosis, immune deficiency aur other airway disorders possible causes hain. Patient ko chronic productive cough, purulent sputum, recurrent infections, dyspnea, crackles aur kabhi hemoptysis ya clubbing ho sakti hai. Thin-section chest CT clinical context mein diagnosis confirm karne mein help karti hai. Signet ring sign mein bronchus accompanying pulmonary artery se wider dikh sakta hai. Imaging clue ko history aur symptoms ke saath interpret karo.

**Chronic bronchitis vs bronchiectasis:** Chronic bronchitis clinical productive-cough syndrome hai. Bronchiectasis structural bronchial dilation hai. Dono mein sputum ho sakta hai, lekin dono definitions interchangeable nahi hain.

### ERS 2025 guideline — nursing care

ERS 2025 guideline most adult patients ke liye individualized airway-clearance teaching support karti hai. Exercise capacity impaired ho toh pulmonary rehabilitation useful hai. Sputum sample exacerbation ke time ideally antibiotics se pehle collect karo jab feasible ho, lekin emergency treatment delay mat karo. Antibiotics clinician prior cultures, resistance aur severity ke hisaab se select karta hai. Hydration patient ki fluid restrictions ke according aur vaccination local eligibility ke according discuss karo. Bronchiectasis alone mein routine inhaled corticosteroids recommended nahi hain. Coexisting asthma ya another established indication alag context hai.

**Red flags:** New or increasing hemoptysis, significant dyspnea, hypoxemia, fever with deterioration aur confusion prompt assessment maangte hain. Significant bleeding ya unstable patient mein forceful airway-clearance exercises continue mat karo. Rapid response aur individualized escalation follow karo.

[ERS 2025 guideline](https://doi.org/10.1183/13993003.01126-2025) · [European Lung Foundation clinical summary](https://europeanlung.org/en/information-hub/guidelines/managing-bronchiectasis-in-adults-2025/) · [Practice Q11–12](../MCQs/Day-04-Atelectasis-CPT-Pulmonary-Edema-Bronchiectasis.md).

| Finding | Think |
|---|---|
| Volume loss after shallow breathing | Atelectasis |
| Inflammatory exudate in infected alveoli | Pneumonia |
| Pleural air | Pneumothorax |
| Pleural fluid | Pleural effusion |
| Interstitial/alveolar fluid due to pressure or permeability changes | Pulmonary edema |
| Permanently dilated bronchi with retained secretions | Bronchiectasis |
