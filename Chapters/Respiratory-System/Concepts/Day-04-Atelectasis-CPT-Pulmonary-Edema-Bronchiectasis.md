## Atelectasis
Alveoli collapse. Surgery ke baad pain se deep inspiration kam, mucus plug/immobility/obstruction/compression; lung volume loss, tachypnea, dyspnea, diminished breath sounds, decreased SpO2; large volume loss may shift trachea toward affected side. CXR volume loss. Early mobilization, analgesia, cough/deep breathing, incentive spirometry as part of bundle. Incentive spirometer mein slow deep INHALATION, hold 3–5 s as tolerated, relax; use as ordered. Pneumonia = exudate in alveoli; pneumothorax = pleural air; effusion = pleural fluid.
## Chest physiotherapy
CPT mucus loosen/clear karta hai. Percussion = cupped hands rhythmic tapping; vibration during expiration; postural drainage = gravity-assisted positions. Particularly bronchiectasis/CF. Avoid immediately after heavy meals; significant bleeding, untreated pneumothorax, unstable circulation or raised ICP may contraindicate specific techniques/positions. Not routine for every pneumonia/COPD case.
## Pulmonary edema
Alveoli/interstitium mein fluid. Cardiogenic: LV pump failure → pulmonary hydrostatic pressure rises → fluid leaks; noncardiogenic: barrier permeability increases (ARDS). Severe dyspnea, orthopnea, bilateral crackles, sweating, sometimes pink frothy sputum. ABC, upright if tolerated, urgent escalation, prescribed oxygen/NIV/diuretics as appropriate; monitor urine and fluid balance. No blanket treatment without cause/BP assessment.
## Bronchiectasis
Permanent abnormal bronchial dilation. Damaged wide pipe mein mucus collect hone jaisa. Recurrent infections, past TB, CF, immunodeficiency causes. Purulent sputum, chronic productive cough, recurrent infections, hemoptysis, crackles, sometimes clubbing. HRCT diagnostic imaging; signet ring sign. Airway clearance, prescribed antibiotics, sputum culture, hydration if appropriate, vaccination. Chronic bronchitis = excessive mucus/inflammation; bronchiectasis = irreversible structural dilation.
