## Spirometry — basic volumes, ratios and interpretation

### Spirometry basics
Spirometry measures **air movement** in and out during forced breathing manoeuvres. Correct coaching and a good seal matter.

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **FVC** | Forced vital capacity: total forcibly exhaled after maximal inspiration |
| **FEV₁** | Air forcibly exhaled in first **1 second** |
| **FEV₁/FVC** | Proportion exhaled in first second |
| **PEF** | Peak expiratory flow (maximal instantaneous flow) |

**Obstruction:** FEV₁/FVC is reduced, suggesting airflow limitation. **Restriction suspicion:** FVC may be reduced with normal/high FEV₁/FVC, but **restriction requires confirmation with TLC**, not spirometry alone.

**Do not confuse:** **RV** cannot be measured using ordinary simple spirometry because it cannot be exhaled.

#### Example
FEV₁ = 2.1 L and FVC = 3.0 L → FEV₁/FVC = 2.1/3.0 × 100 = **70%**.

#### Technique
Explain procedure, upright posture when possible, maximal inspiration, explosive forced expiration sustained to completion. Interpretation uses quality criteria and appropriate reference equations.

**NORCET memory:** FEV₁ = **first 1 second**; FVC = **all forced exhaled air**.

## Respiratory investigations — introductory overview ONLY

> **Learning status: introductory topic**, not completed advanced diagnostic interpretation.

### Common respiratory tests and their basic purposes

| Test | What it can help assess |
|---|---|
| **Pulse oximetry** | Non-invasive estimated oxygen saturation (SpO₂) |
| **Chest X-ray** | Gross lung and chest abnormalities, such as infiltrates, effusion or pneumothorax |
| **ABG** | Oxygenation and acid-base balance |
| **Spirometry** | Airflow and forced expiratory volumes |
| **Sputum examination** | Microscopic or microbiologic evaluation when indicated |

#### Nursing reminders
Confirm correct patient and test, give a clear explanation, follow local infection-prevention requirements and check any special preparation/monitoring requested.

**Boundary:** CT, bronchoscopy, detailed imaging signs, microbiology protocols and special contraindications were not comprehensively taught in the reviewed Day 2 study. They should be added only when studied.
