## Airway anatomy & conducting/respiratory zones

![Airway diagram](../Assets/respiratory-tract.svg)

### Air route
**Nose → pharynx → larynx → trachea → bronchi → bronchioles → respiratory bronchioles → alveolar ducts/sacs → alveoli.**

- **Nose:** warms, filters and humidifies inspired air (hawa ko garam, saaf aur moist karta hai).
- **Pharynx:** common passage for air and food.
- **Larynx:** voice box; **epiglottis** helps protect laryngeal inlet during swallowing (airway ki safety).
- **Trachea:** windpipe supported by C-shaped cartilage rings.
- **Bronchi:** carry air into lungs. **Bronchioles:** progressively smaller airways.
- **Conducting zone:** passage of air; ends at **terminal bronchioles**.
- **Respiratory zone:** starts at **respiratory bronchioles** and includes alveolar ducts/sacs/alveoli, where exchange can occur.

**NORCET memory:** Terminal = last *conducting* part; respiratory bronchiole = first *respiratory* part.

## Lung lobes, bronchi & pulmonary blood flow

### Lung anatomy

| Feature | Right | Left |
|---|---|---|
| Lobes | **3**: superior, middle, inferior | **2**: superior, inferior |
| Fissures | Horizontal and oblique | Oblique |
| Distinctive detail | Right main bronchus shorter, wider and more vertical | **Cardiac notch** accommodates heart |

**Exam link:** Aspirated foreign material more often enters the right main bronchus.

### Pulmonary circulation

**Right ventricle → pulmonary arteries (deoxygenated blood) → lungs (oxygenation) → pulmonary veins (oxygenated blood) → left atrium**.

> **Memory:** Pulmonary **A**rtery = away from heart **to lungs**; pulmonary **V**eins = back to **left atrium**. Unlike most systemic veins, pulmonary veins contain oxygenated blood.
