## OSA
Sleep mein upper airway collapse, breathing effort continues (central apnea effort absent). Obesity/enlarged tonsils/anatomy risk; snoring, witnessed apnea, daytime sleepiness, morning headache. Polysomnography reference diagnostic test; selected adults home sleep test. CPAP splints airway. AHI 5–<15 mild, 15–<30 moderate, ≥30 severe.
## Fibrosis, occupational diseases, pulmonary hypertension, sarcoidosis
Fibrosis = scarring, stiff lungs, restrictive pattern, dry cough, dyspnea, fine inspiratory crackles, clubbing. Silica → silicosis and higher TB risk; asbestos → asbestosis/mesothelioma/lung cancer; coal → coal workers' pneumoconiosis (anthracotic pigment is not always clinical disease); cotton → byssinosis (Monday symptoms); sugarcane bagasse → bagassosis; mouldy hay → farmer's lung; iron oxide → siderosis. Bagassosis/farmer's lung hypersensitivity pneumonitis, not all classical pneumoconioses. Exposure prevention/PPE important.
Pulmonary hypertension = elevated pulmonary arterial pressure, RV overload, exertional dyspnea/fatigue/syncope, advanced raised JVP and edema. Echo estimates probability; right heart catheterization confirms hemodynamics. Sarcoidosis = noncaseating granulomas, lungs/hilar nodes common; bilateral hilar lymphadenopathy, cough/dyspnea. TB classically caseating but histology alone not definitive.
## Cystic fibrosis
Autosomal recessive CFTR gene chromosome 7, thick secretions, recurrent infection/bronchiectasis and pancreatic insufficiency. Sweat chloride ≥60 mmol/L supports diagnosis in appropriate clinical context; airway clearance, nutrition, prescribed pancreatic enzymes, fat-soluble A D E K vitamins.
## Upper respiratory disorders
Rhinitis nasal mucosa; sinusitis sinuses; pharyngitis pharynx; tonsillitis tonsils; laryngitis hoarseness. Epistaxis: sit leaning forward, continuously pinch soft nose 10–15 min; not head backward. Croup usually viral, barking cough and stridor; epiglottitis drooling/dysphagia/airway emergency; no forceful throat examination. Detailed pediatric treatment in child health nursing.

## 📚 Detailed nursing concepts — corrective extension

### 1. OSA: obstruction, sleep study aur CPAP

Sleep ke dauran throat ki muscles relax hoti hain. Kuch patients mein upper airway collapse ho jaati hai. Is wajah se air lungs tak nahi pahunch paati, lekin chest aur abdomen breathing ki koshish karte rehte hain. Brain sleep se briefly arouse ho kar airway reopen kar sakta hai. Ye cycle baar-baar hone se sleep fragmented ho jaati hai. Patient ko loud snoring, witnessed pauses, morning headache aur din mein neend aa sakti hai. Central sleep apnea mein respiratory drive temporarily absent hoti hai, isliye effort bhi rukti hai.

AHI (apnea-hypopnea index) polysomnography mein apnea aur hypopnea events per hour of sleep ki average count hai. Home test ka REI monitoring time par based ho sakta hai. Dono ko bina context exactly interchangeable mat samjho.

| Conventional adult AHI | Category |
|---|---|
| Below 5 | Below usual OSA diagnostic threshold by index alone |
| 5 to below 15 | Mild |
| 15 to below 30 | Moderate |
| 30 or more | Severe |

For example, agar AHI 22 hai toh adult conventional moderate range hai. Child ka diagnostic scale different ho sakta hai. Sirf snoring, STOP-BANG score ya pulse oximeter se definitive OSA diagnose mat karo. Appropriate clinical assessment aur sleep testing chahiye. Selected uncomplicated adults mein home sleep apnea testing acceptable hai. Agar home test negative ya inconclusive hai aur suspicion remains, polysomnography ki zarurat ho sakti hai.

CPAP ko ek pneumatic splint samjho. Ye spontaneous breathing ke dauran positive airway pressure maintain karke upper airway collapse reduce karta hai. Nurse prescribed device ka fit, leak, skin irritation, dryness, adherence aur daytime sleepiness assess kare. Patient ko regular use aur follow-up explain karo. Sedating drugs aur alcohol airway obstruction worsen kar sakte hain. Prescribed medicines khud stop ya change mat karao. Weight management aur driving safety counseling individualized care ka part hain.

[International OSA consensus](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10359192/) · [AASM diagnostic testing guidance](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/28162150/) · [Adult AHI categories](https://jamanetwork.com/journals/jama/fullarticle/2598777).

### 2. Pulmonary fibrosis and occupational lung disease

Fibrosis mein normal elastic lung tissue ki jagah scar tissue develop hota hai. Isse lung ko expand karne ke liye zyada pressure chahiye. Patient ko exertional dyspnea, progressive dry cough aur fine inspiratory crackles ho sakte hain. Kabhi clubbing bhi hoti hai. Restrictive pattern suspicion deta hai, lekin true restriction confirm karne ke liye total lung capacity (TLC) reduced hona chahiye. HRCT aur cause-directed clinical evaluation important hain. Idiopathic pulmonary fibrosis (IPF) ek specific diagnosis hai. Har pulmonary fibrosis IPF nahi hoti.

Nurse baseline aur activity-related breathlessness, SpO2, fatigue, functional ability aur oxygen requirement assess kare. Pulmonary rehabilitation, vaccination, prescribed oxygen aur specialist-directed therapy support karo. Sudden worsening mein infection, pulmonary embolism ya other acute complications assess karna chahiye.

| Exposure | Classic disease or association | Nursing relevance |
|---|---|---|
| Respirable crystalline silica (stone cutting, mining) | Silicosis, increased TB risk | Ask about dust exposure, assess TB symptoms, refer for appropriate testing |
| Asbestos fibers | Asbestosis, lung cancer, mesothelioma | Record past exposure even decades earlier, support smoking cessation |
| Coal mine dust | Coal workers' pneumoconiosis | Occupational history and surveillance |
| Cotton, flax or hemp dust | Byssinosis | First workday after break may trigger chest tightness |
| Mouldy hay | Farmer's lung (hypersensitivity pneumonitis) | Immune reaction to inhaled organic antigens |
| Mould-contaminated sugarcane bagasse | Bagassosis (hypersensitivity pneumonitis) | Exposure reduction and clinical assessment |
| Iron oxide dust | Siderosis | Exposure history, distinguish pigment deposition from symptomatic disease |

Byssinosis ko traditional exam language mein Monday fever bhi kehte hain. Fever essential symptom nahi hai. Main clue chest tightness aur breathlessness on returning to dusty work hai. Har occupational lung disease pneumoconiosis nahi hoti. Farmer's lung aur bagassosis hypersensitivity pneumonitis hain, jabki byssinosis airway disease hai.

Prevention mein sirf mask par depend mat karo. Hazard elimination/substitution, engineering controls jaise wet cutting aur local exhaust ventilation, exposure monitoring, training aur appropriately selected respiratory protection important hain. Asbestos-containing material ko khud disturb ya remove nahi karna chahiye.

[NHLBI fibrosis symptoms](https://www.nhlbi.nih.gov/health/idiopathic-pulmonary-fibrosis/symptoms) · [CDC NIOSH silica and worker health](https://www.cdc.gov/niosh/silica/about/index.html) · [Silica and TB risk](https://www.cdc.gov/niosh/silica/symptoms/index.html) · [CDC mesothelioma](https://www.cdc.gov/mesothelioma/about/index.html) · [CDC pneumoconioses](https://www.cdc.gov/niosh/pneumoconioses/about/index.html) · [MSD byssinosis](https://www.msdmanuals.com/home/lung-and-airway-disorders/environmental-and-occupational-lung-diseases/byssinosis?ruleredirectid=742).

### 3. Pulmonary hypertension (PH) aur sarcoidosis

Pulmonary hypertension ka matlab lung circulation mein pressure elevated hona hai. Right ventricle ko blood lungs tak pump karne ke liye extra work karna pad sakta hai. Isliye advanced cases mein raised JVP, peripheral edema, exertional dyspnea aur fatigue aa sakte hain. Exertional syncope, chest pain ya rapid deterioration prompt assessment maangte hain.

Echo PH ki probability assess karta hai. Right heart catheterization specialist setting mein pressures aur blood flow directly measure karke hemodynamic confirmation aur classification mein help karta hai. ESC/ERS 2022 definition mein mean pulmonary arterial pressure (mPAP) at rest 20 mmHg se zyada hai. Older teaching 25 mmHg threshold use kar sakti hai. Pulmonary arterial hypertension (PAH) ek particular subgroup hai, PH ke har patient ko PAH nahi kehte. Precapillary pattern mein mPAP 20 se zyada, pulmonary arterial wedge pressure 15 mmHg ya kam aur pulmonary vascular resistance 2 Wood units se zyada hoti hai. Ye specialist-level classification enrichment hai, exact exam depth verified nahi hai. Nurse symptoms, oxygenation, BP, edema, weight aur prescribed therapy monitor kare. PH-specific medicines cause aur classification ke bina interchange nahi karni chahiye.

Sarcoidosis mein granulomas, yani organized immune-cell collections, different organs mein form ho sakte hain. Lungs aur hilar lymph nodes common sites hain. Bilateral hilar lymphadenopathy aur nonnecrotizing granulomas suspicion support karte hain, lekin TB ya fungal infection ko sirf histology se rule out mat karo. Diagnosis compatible presentation aur alternative causes ke exclusion par based hai. Biopsy har situation mein mandatory nahi hoti. India mein TB evaluation particularly important hai before immunosuppression. Eye pain, blurred vision, palpitations, syncope aur skin findings ko report karo. Detailed cardiac aur ophthalmic management respective chapter mein rahega.

[ESC/ERS PH guideline 2022](https://publications.ersnet.org/lookup/doi/10.1183/13993003.00879-2022) · [ATS sarcoidosis guideline 2020](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7159433/).

### 4. Cystic fibrosis (CF) aur nutrition

CF autosomal recessive inherited disease hai jisme CFTR protein ke dysfunction se ion transport abnormal ho sakta hai. Airways mein thick secretions collect hone se infection aur bronchiectasis ka risk badhta hai. Pancreas ke ducts obstruct hone par digestive enzymes intestine tak sufficient amount mein nahi pahunch paate. Isse fat malabsorption, bulky greasy stools aur poor growth ho sakti hai.

Sweat chloride 60 mmol/L ya zyada appropriate diagnostic context mein CF ke consistent hai. 30–59 mmol/L intermediate range mein further evaluation chahiye. 30 mmol/L se kam CF less likely banata hai, lekin clinical suspicion ke hisaab se specialist evaluation required ho sakti hai. Test result ko clinical findings, newborn screening aur CFTR testing ke context mein interpret karo.

Pancreatic insufficiency wale patient mein prescribed enzymes meals aur snacks ke saath dene ki teaching important hai. Dose independently change mat karo. Growth, weight, stool pattern, adherence aur prescribed fat-soluble vitamin supplementation assess karo. Airway clearance, infection-control precautions, nutrition aur individualized CF specialist care essential hain. Some patients qualify for mutation-specific CFTR modulators, lekin har patient ke liye same therapy appropriate nahi hoti.

[Cystic Fibrosis Foundation sweat test guideline](https://www.cff.org/medical-professionals/sweat-test-clinical-care-guidelines) · [Pancreatic enzyme guidance](https://www.cff.org/medical-professionals/pancreatic-enzymes-clinical-care-guidelines) · [Patient enzyme education](https://www.cff.org/managing-cf/enzymes).

### 5. Upper airway safety: croup aur epiglottitis

Croup mein usually viral inflammation larynx aur trachea ko involve karti hai. Barking cough, hoarseness aur stridor typical ho sakte hain. Epiglottitis mein child drooling, swallowing difficulty, muffled voice aur severe distress ke saath aa sakta hai. Symptoms overlap kar sakte hain, isliye stridor ko automatically uncomplicated croup assume mat karo.

Suspected severe upper airway obstruction mein child ko calm rehne do aur preferred position mein parent ke paas rakho. Forceful throat examination, unnecessary procedures, oral food aur forced lying flat avoid karo. Rapid airway assessment aur senior airway team escalation priority hai. Severe distress mein routine investigations ke liye airway care delay mat karo. Hib vaccination epiglottitis ke important cause ka risk reduce karti hai, lekin vaccinated child mein disease impossible nahi hai. Detailed drug doses aur pediatric management Child Health Nursing chapter mein cover hone chahiye. Ear/nose/throat procedure details Eye and ENT chapter ka scope hain, is file mein respiratory emergency recognition retained hai.

[Royal Children's Hospital acute upper airway obstruction](https://www.rch.org.au/clinicalguide/guideline_index/acute_upper_airway_obstruction/).
