## Lung abscess and pleural disorders
Abscess = pus cavity inside lung tissue; fever, foul sputum, air-fluid level; prescribed antibiotics. Empyema = pus in pleural space; antibiotics and drainage often needed. Pleurisy = inflamed pleura, sharp inspiratory pain. Hemothorax = blood in pleura, shock possible; chylothorax = lymph/chyle, milky fluid. See existing Day 4 pleural guide for chest drain safety.
## Cancer and surgery
Adenocarcinoma most common overall, often peripheral; squamous often central, PTHrP hypercalcemia; small cell aggressive/early metastasis, SIADH and ectopic ACTH; large cell NSCLC type. SIADH → hyponatremia. Pancoast apex tumor → shoulder/arm pain; Horner = ptosis, miosis, anhidrosis. Persistent cough, hemoptysis, weight loss, hoarseness; imaging suspicion, tissue diagnosis. Wedge removes small piece, lobectomy one lobe, pneumonectomy entire lung. Postop respiratory assessment, analgesia, breathing exercise, mobilization, chest drain if prescribed; positioning surgery-specific.
## Flail chest and contusion
Three or more adjacent ribs broken in at least two places can create a flail segment. During inspiration segment moves inward, expiration outward = paradoxical movement. Pain and associated lung contusion impair ventilation. Pulmonary contusion is trauma-induced bruising, hemorrhage and edema; oxygenation can worsen over time. ABC, oxygenation, prescribed pain relief, urgent escalation; check associated pneumothorax/hemothorax. No tight chest binding.
## Hemoptysis
Coughing blood from respiratory tract, often bright red/frothy; hematemesis is vomiting GI blood (may be coffee-ground). TB, bronchiectasis, PE, cancer causes. Life-threatening bleeding threatens airway: urgent airway protection/team; if known side, trained team may position bleeding lung down to protect healthy lung. Assess vitals, amount and respiratory status, do not delay emergency care.

## 📚 Detailed nursing concepts — corrective extension

### 1. Lung abscess, empyema aur pleurisy ko kaise distinguish karein?

Lung abscess ko lung tissue ke andar infection se bani pus-filled cavity samjho. Aspiration, especially jab swallowing ya consciousness impaired ho, ek important risk factor hai. Poor oral health aur obstructed bronchus bhi risk badha sakte hain. Infection tissue ko destroy karta hai. Jab cavity airway se communicate kare toh air-fluid level dikh sakta hai. Patient mein fever, cough, foul sputum, weakness aur kabhi hemoptysis ho sakti hai. Ye sab findings suspicion badhati hain lekin akeli X-ray finding diagnosis confirm nahi karti. CT se location aur cavity ko evaluate karte hain. India mein TB aur malignancy ko bhi differential mein rakhna important hai.

Empyema mein infection lung tissue ke andar nahi balki pleural space mein hota hai. Pleural space ko lung aur chest wall ke beech ki patli potential space samjho. Jab pus collect ho jaata hai toh lung expansion affect ho sakta hai. Antibiotics ke saath established infected fluid ka drainage important hai. Clinician ultrasound-guided sampling aur drain ki zarurat decide karta hai. BTS guidance mein agar non-purulent fluid ka pH 7.2 ya usse kam hai, toh appropriate accessible fluid aur clinical context mein drainage consider karna important hai. Frank pus milne par pH ka wait nahi karte. Complex loculated collections mein further specialist treatment ki zarurat ho sakti hai.

| Feature | Lung abscess | Empyema | Pleurisy |
|---|---|---|---|
| Site | Lung parenchyma | Pleural space | Inflamed pleura |
| Common clues | Fever, productive cough, cavity | Fever, pleural fluid, breathlessness | Sharp pain with inspiration or cough |
| Care approach | Prescribed antibiotics, monitor response, selected drainage | Antibiotics and pleural source control | Evaluate cause, prescribed analgesia and treatment |

**Nursing care:** RR, SpO2, temperature, pain, hydration, intake-output aur sputum changes monitor karo. Antibiotic ko prescribed timing par administer karo. Sepsis, new hypoxemia, shock aur worsening pain ko promptly report karo. Aspiration risk assess karo aur swallowing safety plan follow karo. Drain prescribed hai toh system upright aur insertion level ke neeche rakho. Routine clamp ya stripping mat karo. [Lung abscess clinical review](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/NBK555920/) · [BTS pleural disease 2023](https://thorax.bmj.com/content/78/Suppl_3/s1).

### 2. Lung cancer aur paraneoplastic syndromes

Paraneoplastic syndrome ka matlab tumor ke local pressure ya direct metastasis ke bina tumor-related hormones ya other substances se door ke organs mein effects aana hai.

| Association | Mechanism | Nurse kya assess kare |
|---|---|---|
| Squamous cell carcinoma | PTHrP can raise calcium | Constipation, thirst, weakness, confusion, hydration and calcium |
| Small cell carcinoma | Inappropriate ADH can retain water and dilute sodium | Headache, confusion, seizures, sodium and prescribed fluid plan |
| Apical tumor (Pancoast) | Nearby nerve involvement | Shoulder/arm pain and Horner signs |

Ye associations characteristic hain, diagnostic proof nahi hain. Hypercalcemia bone metastases se bhi ho sakti hai. SIADH ko confirm karne se pehle other causes aur volume status evaluate karte hain. Severe symptomatic sodium disturbance emergency assessment maangti hai. Rapid sodium correction bhi harmful ho sakti hai. Nurse prescribed monitoring aur therapy follow kare.

Lung cancer mein persistent cough, unexplained weight loss, hemoptysis ya hoarseness ko ignore mat karo. Smoking cessation aur occupational exposure reduction important prevention measures hain. CT imaging suspicion evaluate karne mein help karti hai, tissue diagnosis aur staging treatment guide karte hain. Surgery, radiotherapy, systemic therapy aur supportive care cancer type, stage aur patient fitness par depend karte hain. Lobectomy ek lobe remove karti hai, pneumonectomy ek entire lung. Wedge resection smaller nonanatomical piece remove karti hai. Postoperative breathing assessment, adequate pain control, safe mobilization aur drain monitoring surgical protocol ke according karo. Pneumonectomy ke baad positioning aur drainage instructions procedure-specific hain. Universal side-position rule mat apply karo. [MSD lung carcinoma](https://www.msdmanuals.com/professional/oncology/tumors-of-the-lungs/lung-carcinoma) · [NCI NSCLC treatment](https://www.cancer.gov/types/lung/hp/non-small-cell-lung-treatment-pdq?redirect=true).

### 3. Flail chest aur pulmonary contusion

Flail segment mein chest wall ka part adjacent rib fractures ke kaaran unstable ho sakta hai. Spontaneous inspiration mein normal chest bahar expand hoti hai, lekin flail segment andar move kar sakta hai. Expiration mein ulta movement ho sakta hai. Positive-pressure ventilation, splinting ya severe pain ki wajah se visible paradoxical movement absent bhi ho sakti hai. Isliye absence se injury exclude mat karo.

Pulmonary contusion mein blunt force se alveolar-capillary injury hoti hai. Blood aur edema alveoli mein collect hote hain. Ventilation-perfusion mismatch ki wajah se hypoxemia develop hoti hai. Patient pehle relatively stable dikh sakta hai, phir hours mein deteriorate kar sakta hai. Serial assessment important hai.

**Nursing priorities:** ABC assessment, oxygenation, urgent trauma-team escalation, prescribed analgesia, repeated respiratory assessment aur associated pneumothorax/hemothorax ke signs monitor karo. Chest ko tightly bind mat karo. Ventilatory support aur surgical stabilization selected cases mein team decide karti hai. Sudden shock ya respiratory collapse mein routine imaging ka wait mat karo. [MSD thoracic trauma](https://www.msdmanuals.com/professional/injuries-poisoning/thoracic-trauma/overview-of-thoracic-trauma) · [Victorian trauma guidance](https://trauma.reach.vic.gov.au/specialist/thoracic-trauma/immediately-life-threatening-injuries/).

### 4. Hemoptysis — nursing emergency recognition

Hemoptysis lower respiratory tract se cough ke saath blood aana hai. Hematemesis gastrointestinal blood vomiting hai. Nose ya mouth bleeding bhi cough ke saath appear ho sakti hai. Color aur froth clues hain, final source proof nahi. TB, bronchiectasis, malignancy aur other causes evaluate karo.

Blood quantity ka ek fixed cutoff har patient ke liye safe rule nahi hai. Agar airway obstruct ho rahi hai, oxygenation maintain nahi ho rahi, ya circulation unstable hai toh bleeding life-threatening hai, chahe measured quantity kam ho. ABC assess karo, emergency help activate karo, prescribed emergency oxygenation support arrange karo aur patient ko monitor karo. Known bleeding side mein trained team bleeding lung down position consider kar sakti hai taaki healthy lung protect ho. Unstable patient ko routine sputum testing ke liye delay mat karo. Definitive investigation aur source control specialist team karti hai. [Indian SCII consensus 2023](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10289864/) · [Lung India review 2026](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42377159/).
