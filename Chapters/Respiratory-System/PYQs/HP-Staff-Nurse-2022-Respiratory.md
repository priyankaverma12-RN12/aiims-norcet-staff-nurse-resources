## Q3 (Post Code 933) — Alveoli ke roaming phagocytes

**Paper ka topic (Hinglish paraphrase):** Alveoli mein ghoomkar dust aur microbes remove karne wali cells kaun hain?

- **A.** Alveolar macrophages
- **B.** Pulmonary cells
- **C.** Goblet cells
- **D.** Chalice cells

<details><summary>✅ Answer + detailed Hinglish reasoning</summary>

**A. Alveolar macrophages.**

**Kyun?** Alveolar macrophages immune cells hain. Ye alveoli tak pahunchne wale dust particles aur microorganisms ko **phagocytosis** se engulf karke clean karte hain. Type I pneumocytes gas-exchange lining ka part hain; Type II surfactant banate hain. Goblet cells ka main role mucus secretion hota hai, na ki alveoli ke dust particles ko phagocytose karna.

**NORCET memory:** **Type I = Exchange; Type II = Surfactant; Macrophage = Safai**.

## Q20 (Post Code 933) — Normal tidal volume

**Paper ka topic:** Textbook adult normal tidal volume ki approximate value kya hai?

- **A.** 100 mL
- **B.** 400 mL
- **C.** 6000 mL
- **D.** 500 mL

<details><summary>✅ Answer + detailed Hinglish reasoning</summary>

**D. ~500 mL.**

**Kyun?** **Tidal volume (TV)** = ek normal, quiet breath mein inspired/expired air. Typical teaching example **500 mL** liya jata hai. 6000 mL ek normal quiet breath nahi hota; ye magnitude total lung capacity ke typical adult male example se mil sakta hai. 100 mL aur 400 mL options question ke commonly used textbook average ke liye best choice nahi hain.

**Important:** Real TV body size, age, breathing pattern aur illness se vary karti hai. MCQ ka **500 mL** value exam-oriented adult approximation hai, sabhi patients ke liye mandatory fixed value nahi.

## Q35 (Post Code 933) — External respiration

**Paper ka topic:** Alveoli aur unke surrounding capillaries ke beech gas exchange ko kya bolte hain?

- **A.** External respiration
- **B.** Internal respiration
- **C.** Intrinsic respiration
- **D.** Both external and internal

<details><summary>✅ Answer + detailed Hinglish reasoning</summary>

**A. External respiration.**

**Kyun?** Lungs ke **alveoli ↔ pulmonary capillary blood** ke beech O₂/CO₂ exchange ko **external respiration** kehte hain. **Internal respiration** blood aur **body tissues** ke beech gas exchange hota hai. **Ventilation** air ka andar-bahar movement hai; gas exchange khud nahi.

**Easy trick:** *External = lungs mein exchange; Internal = body tissues mein exchange*.

</details>

## Q67 (Post Code 933) — Air during normal breathing

**Paper ka topic:** Normal respiration mein ek breath ke saath jo volume andar aata hai, usko kya kehte hain?

- **A.** Vital capacity
- **B.** Tidal volume
- **C.** Inspiratory capacity
- **D.** Lung capacity

<details><summary>✅ Answer + detailed Hinglish reasoning</summary>

**B. Tidal volume.**

**Kyun?** Quiet normal inspiration mein andar liya gaya air volume **TV** hai. **VC** total maximum voluntarily movable air hai; **IC** normal expiration ke end se maximum inspire hone wali air ka sum (TV + IRV) hai. **“Lung capacity”** yahan precise volume term nahi.

**NORCET trap:** “Normal single breath” likha ho toh **TV**, “normal expiration ke baad remaining” ho toh **FRC**.

</details>

## Q113 (Post Code 933) — Narrow air tubes in lungs

**Paper ka topic:** Lungs mein bahut saari narrow conducting airways ko kya kehte hain?

- **A.** Alveoli
- **B.** Bronchioles
- **C.** Bronchi
- **D.** Alveolar ducts

<details><summary>✅ Answer + detailed Hinglish reasoning</summary>

**B. Bronchioles.**

**Kyun?** **Bronchioles** bronchi se chhoti tubular airways hain jo air ko lungs ke deeper parts tak le jaati hain. **Alveoli** gas-exchange sacs hain; bronchi comparatively larger airways hain; **alveolar ducts** respiratory region ke passages hain. Narrow branching airways ka best answer bronchioles.

**Pathway yaad:** Trachea → Bronchi → **Bronchioles** → Respiratory bronchioles → Alveoli.
