## Reported question theme — Adult resting respiratory rate

**Hinglish paraphrase:** Normal adult person ka resting respiratory rate approximately kitna hota hai?

- **A.** 8–10/min
- **B.** 12–20/min
- **C.** 20–30/min
- **D.** 30–40/min

<details><summary>✅ Answer + explanation</summary>

**B. 12–20 breaths/min.**

**Kyun sahi?** Adult patient ke basic nursing vital signs mein normal **resting RR** ka commonly taught range **12–20 breaths per minute** hai. Isliye NORCET-style MCQ mein B best answer hota hai. **8–10** adult resting range se usually slow hai; **20–30** aur **30–40** ranges ka most portion adult rest range se high hai.

**Exam trap:** Ye **adult resting** range hai. Child/infant mein normal RR alag ho sakta hai. Fever, pain, exertion, altitude aur illness se RR change hota hai.
