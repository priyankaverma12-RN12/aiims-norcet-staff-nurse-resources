## Listening with a stethoscope
Ask for slow, slightly deeper breaths through mouth while listening **side-to-side** over matching chest sites.

### Normal sounds
- **Vesicular:** soft, low-pitched; heard over most peripheral lung fields.
- **Bronchial:** louder, higher-pitched; normally heard over trachea/manubrium, not peripheral lung.
- **Bronchovesicular:** intermediate; near main bronchi (anterior upper sternum, between scapulae).

### Adventitious sounds
| Sound | Description | Typical association (not a diagnosis) |
|---|---|---|
| **Wheeze** | Musical, usually high-pitched | Narrowed airways |
| **Crackles (rales)** | Discontinuous popping | Fluid, collapsed/reopening small airways |
| **Rhonchi** | Low-pitched snoring | Secretions/large airway narrowing |
| **Stridor** | Harsh, often inspiratory | **Upper airway obstruction — urgent assessment** |
| **Pleural friction rub** | Grating/creaking | Inflamed pleural surfaces |

**Sequence:** Inspection → Palpation → Percussion → Auscultation (**IPPA**).
