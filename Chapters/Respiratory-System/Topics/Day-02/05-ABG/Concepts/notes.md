# Arterial blood gases — basics & interpretation

[← Day-02-Assessment-Diagnostics index](../../../README.md) · [Respiratory chapter](../../../../README.md)

## ABG kya batata hai?
Arterial blood gas evaluates acid-base balance and oxygenation. Read **pH → PaCO₂ → HCO₃⁻**.

| Measurement | Common teaching range |
|---|---|
| **pH** | 7.35–7.45 |
| **PaCO₂** (respiratory) | 35–45 mmHg |
| **HCO₃⁻** (metabolic) | ~22–26 mEq/L |
| **PaO₂** | ~80–100 mmHg near sea level |

### Three-step simplified exam method
1. **pH <7.35 = acidemia**; **pH >7.45 = alkalemia**.
2. **PaCO₂ opposite direction to pH?** Think **respiratory** primary cause.
3. **HCO₃⁻ same direction as pH?** Think **metabolic** primary cause.

| Uncompensated sample pattern | Primary disorder |
|---|---|
| pH **low**, PaCO₂ **high** | Respiratory acidosis |
| pH **high**, PaCO₂ **low** | Respiratory alkalosis |
| pH **low**, HCO₃⁻ **low** | Metabolic acidosis |
| pH **high**, HCO₃⁻ **high** | Metabolic alkalosis |

**Example:** pH 7.30, PaCO₂ 52, HCO₃⁻ 24 → **uncompensated respiratory acidosis**. Increased CO₂ drives acidity.

**Note:** Real patient interpretation must evaluate compensation, oxygenation, clinical history and lab reference ranges. These are introductory exam patterns, not complete clinical algorithms.
