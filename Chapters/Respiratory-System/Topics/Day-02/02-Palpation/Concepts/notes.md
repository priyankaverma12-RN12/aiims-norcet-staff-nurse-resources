## Touch (haath se feel karna)
Palpation checks:
- **Chest expansion:** compare both sides for symmetric movement.
- **Tracheal position:** usually midline.
- **Tenderness**, lumps, local swelling.
- **Tactile fremitus:** vibration felt on chest wall as patient speaks (e.g., “ninety-nine”).

### Fremitus memory
- **Increased fremitus** may occur when dense lung tissue transmits vibrations better, such as **consolidation** with a patent bronchus.
- **Decreased fremitus** may occur with pleural effusion, pneumothorax or airway obstruction.

Interpret with all other findings. Palpation does **not** replace imaging or clinical evaluation.
