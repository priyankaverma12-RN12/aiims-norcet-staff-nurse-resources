## Common respiratory tests and their basic purposes

| Test | What it can help assess |
|---|---|
| **Pulse oximetry** | Non-invasive estimated oxygen saturation (SpO₂) |
| **Chest X-ray** | Gross lung and chest abnormalities, such as infiltrates, effusion or pneumothorax |
| **ABG** | Oxygenation and acid-base balance |
| **Spirometry** | Airflow and forced expiratory volumes |
| **Sputum examination** | Microscopic or microbiologic evaluation when indicated |

### Nursing reminders
Confirm correct patient and test, give a clear explanation, follow local infection-prevention requirements and check any special preparation/monitoring requested.

**Boundary:** CT, bronchoscopy, detailed imaging signs, microbiology protocols and special contraindications were not comprehensively taught in the reviewed Day 2 study. They should be added only when studied.
