### Q1. How many lobes does the healthy adult right lung have?

- **A.** Two
- **B.** Three
- **C.** Four
- **D.** One

<details><summary>✅ Answer & explanation</summary>

**B.** Right = three; left = two.

</details>

### Q2. Which left-lung structure accommodates the heart?

- **A.** Horizontal fissure
- **B.** Carina
- **C.** Cardiac notch
- **D.** Tracheal ring

<details><summary>✅ Answer & explanation</summary>

**C.** Cardiac notch is an indentation on the anterior border of the left lung.

</details>

### Q3. An aspirated foreign body more commonly enters which main bronchus?

- **A.** Left
- **B.** Right
- **C.** Both equally
- **D.** Neither

<details><summary>✅ Answer & explanation</summary>

**B.** The right main bronchus is shorter, wider and more vertical.

</details>

### Q4. Blood returning oxygenated from the lungs first enters which heart chamber?

- **A.** Right atrium
- **B.** Right ventricle
- **C.** Left ventricle
- **D.** Left atrium

<details><summary>✅ Answer & explanation</summary>

**D.** Pulmonary veins drain into the left atrium.

</details>

### Q5. Pulmonary arteries normally carry blood that is:

- **A.** Deoxygenated to the lungs
- **B.** Oxygenated to the body
- **C.** Oxygenated to the right atrium
- **D.** Deoxygenated to the left atrium

<details><summary>✅ Answer & explanation</summary>

**A.** They carry blood from right ventricle toward lungs.

</details>
