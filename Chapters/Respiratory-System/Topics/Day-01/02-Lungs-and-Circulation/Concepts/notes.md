## Lung anatomy

| Feature | Right | Left |
|---|---|---|
| Lobes | **3**: superior, middle, inferior | **2**: superior, inferior |
| Fissures | Horizontal and oblique | Oblique |
| Distinctive detail | Right main bronchus shorter, wider and more vertical | **Cardiac notch** accommodates heart |

**Exam link:** Aspirated foreign material more often enters the right main bronchus.

## Pulmonary circulation

**Right ventricle → pulmonary arteries (deoxygenated blood) → lungs (oxygenation) → pulmonary veins (oxygenated blood) → left atrium**.

> **Memory:** Pulmonary **A**rtery = away from heart **to lungs**; pulmonary **V**eins = back to **left atrium**. Unlike most systemic veins, pulmonary veins contain oxygenated blood.
