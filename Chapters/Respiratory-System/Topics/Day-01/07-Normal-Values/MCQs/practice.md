### Q1. Which value is outside usual normal arterial pH range?

- **A.** 7.38
- **B.** 7.42
- **C.** 7.46
- **D.** 7.40

<details><summary>✅ Answer & explanation</summary>

**C.** Typical arterial pH is 7.35–7.45.

</details>

### Q2. Which is a normal resting adult RR?

- **A.** 9/min
- **B.** 16/min
- **C.** 24/min
- **D.** 28/min

<details><summary>✅ Answer & explanation</summary>

**B.** Standard adult resting exam range is about 12–20/min.

</details>

### Q3. Which value is within usual PaCO₂ reference range?

- **A.** 31 mmHg
- **B.** 48 mmHg
- **C.** 52 mmHg
- **D.** 39 mmHg

<details><summary>✅ Answer & explanation</summary>

**D.** Common PaCO₂ range is 35–45 mmHg.

</details>

### Q4. For healthy adults near sea level, which SpO₂ is within usual exam range?

- **A.** 87%
- **B.** 91%
- **C.** 93%
- **D.** 98%

<details><summary>✅ Answer & explanation</summary>

**D.** SpO₂ of 98% is within commonly taught 95–100%.

</details>

### Q5. SpO₂ 97% means PaO₂ must be exactly 97 mmHg.

- **A.** True
- **B.** False
- **C.** Always if breathing normally
- **D.** Only after exercise

<details><summary>✅ Answer & explanation</summary>

**B.** SpO₂ is saturation %, while PaO₂ is partial pressure mmHg; they are not numerically interchangeable.

</details>
