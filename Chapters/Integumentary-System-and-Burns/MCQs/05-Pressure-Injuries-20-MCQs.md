### Q1. An immobile patient has intact skin over the sacrum with persistent nonblanchable erythema. Which pressure injury category best fits?

**Difficulty:** 🟢 Easy · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD · Original NORCET / Staff Nurse practice

- **A.** Stage 2
- **B.** Stage 3
- **C.** Stage 1
- **D.** Unstageable pressure injury

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: C. Stage 1**

Stage 1 mein skin intact rehti hai lekin localized nonblanchable erythema pressure-related damage indicate karti hai. Pressure offload karke site reassess karni chahiye.

**Baaki options kyun galat hain?**

### Q20. A patient has diffuse superficial skin damage across the buttocks after frequent diarrhea without a clear pressure-point pattern. What is the best approach?

**Difficulty:** 🔴 Difficult · **Priority:** 🔴 MOST IMPORTANT · Original NORCET / Staff Nurse practice

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: B. Assess moisture-associated skin damage and pressure or shear contributions before assigning a pressure stage**

Moisture-associated damage diffuse distribution mein ho sakti hai, jabki pressure damage commonly localized loading sites se linked hoti hai. Dono mechanisms coexist kar sakte hain, isliye cause aur morphology assess karna chahiye.

**Baaki options kyun galat hain?**

- **A.** Stage 2 term moisture-associated dermatitis ke liye use nahi hoti. Pressure etiology establish karna staging se pehle important hai.
- **C.** Incontinence aur immobility dono risk increase kar sakte hain. Same patient mein moisture damage aur pressure injury coexist kar sakti hain.
- **D.** Drainage alone pressure injury stage determine nahi karti. Tissue depth, etiology aur other wound findings assess karne chahiye.
