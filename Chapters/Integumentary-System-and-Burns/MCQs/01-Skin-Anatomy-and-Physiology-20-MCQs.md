### Q1. A superficial abrasion causes no bleeding. Which anatomical feature best explains this finding?

**Difficulty:** 🟡 Medium · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD · Original NORCET / Staff Nurse practice

- **A.** The papillary dermis has no capillaries
- **B.** The hypodermis contains only adipocytes
- **C.** The epidermis lacks its own blood vessels
- **D.** Hair follicles cannot receive a blood supply

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: C. The epidermis lacks its own blood vessels**

Epidermis ko nutrients neeche dermis se diffusion ke through milte hain. Agar injury sirf epidermis tak rahe, toh blood vessels directly nahi tootengi.

**Baaki options kyun galat hain?**

- **A.** Papillary dermis mein capillary loops hote hain jo epidermis ko support karte hain. Is layer tak injury pahunchne par bleeding ho sakti hai.
- **B.** Hypodermis mein fat ke saath vessels aur connective tissue bhi hote hain. Yeh superficial abrasion ka level nahi hai.
- **D.** Hair follicle ko surrounding dermal vessels support karte hain. Follicle ki blood supply is abrasion ka explanation nahi hai.
