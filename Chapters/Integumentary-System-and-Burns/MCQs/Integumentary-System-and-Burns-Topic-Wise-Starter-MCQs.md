## Burn airway

### Q1. A patient rescued from an enclosed-space fire has hoarseness, facial burns and soot around the mouth. Which priority is most appropriate?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Topic:** Burn airway

- **A.** Urgent airway assessment for inhalation injury
- **B.** Focus only on topical burn dressing
- **C.** Give oral fluids before evaluating swallowing
- **D.** Delay airway review until swelling is obvious

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: A. Urgent airway assessment for inhalation injury**

Smoke aur heat se airway ki lining swell ho sakti hai. Hoarse voice aur soot early clues hain. Nurse airway emergency ke liye help leti hai kyunki swelling ke baad tube lagana mushkil ho sakta hai.

**Baaki options kyun galat hain?**

- **B.** Skin dressing se airway obstruction prevent nahi hota.
- **C.** Swallowing unsafe ho sakti hai.
- **D.** Obvious swelling ka wait dangerous hai.

</details>

## Burn resuscitation

### Q2. An adult with a major burn has decreasing hourly urine output during fluid resuscitation. Which response is appropriate?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Topic:** Burn resuscitation

- **A.** Automatically double fluids without assessing the patient
- **B.** Ignore urine output if a formula was used
- **C.** Give a diuretic independently to reach the target
- **D.** Reassess perfusion and report the trend for protocol-based fluid adjustment

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: D. Reassess perfusion and report the trend for protocol-based fluid adjustment**

Burn ke baad fluid blood vessels se tissues mein leak ho sakta hai. Urine kam hona kidney ko blood kam milne ka sign ho sakta hai. Nurse vitals aur fluid balance check karke treatment team ko batati hai.

**Baaki options kyun galat hain?**

- **A.** Blind fluid increase overload kar sakti hai.
- **B.** Formula starting estimate hoti hai, response bhi dekhna hai.
- **C.** Diuretic apne aap dena cause ko solve nahi karta.

</details>

## Wound healing

### Q3. A postoperative wound has increasing redness, warmth, purulent drainage and fever. What should the nurse suspect?

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Topic:** Wound healing

- **A.** A pressure injury unrelated to the incision in every case
- **B.** Normal scar maturation
- **C.** Surgical site infection
- **D.** Uncomplicated expected healing

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: C. Surgical site infection**

Jab germs wound mein grow karte hain, toh body inflammation karti hai. Pus, fever aur badhti redness infection ke signs ho sakte hain. Nurse wound assess karti hai aur prescribed review arrange karti hai.

**Baaki options kyun galat hain?**

- **A.** Pressure injury ka mechanism alag ho sakta hai.
- **B.** Scar maturation mein pus expected nahi hai.
- **D.** Badhti redness aur pus normal healing nahi hai.

</details>

## Skin infection

### Q4. A patient with a rapidly spreading painful red skin area has fever. Which nursing action is appropriate?

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Topic:** Skin infection

- **A.** Delay evaluation until all skin changes disappear
- **B.** Mark and assess the affected area as appropriate and arrange prompt evaluation
- **C.** Apply an unprescribed irritant to the skin
- **D.** Massage the inflamed area vigorously

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: B. Mark and assess the affected area as appropriate and arrange prompt evaluation**

Skin aur neeche ke tissues mein infection spread ho sakta hai. Nurse redness ki extent, pain aur vitals check karti hai. Rapid spread aur fever ko jaldi report karna chahiye.

**Baaki options kyun galat hain?**

- **A.** Delay infection ko worsen hone de sakta hai.
- **C.** Irritant skin damage badha sakta hai.
- **D.** Massage painful tissue ko hurt kar sakti hai.

</details>
