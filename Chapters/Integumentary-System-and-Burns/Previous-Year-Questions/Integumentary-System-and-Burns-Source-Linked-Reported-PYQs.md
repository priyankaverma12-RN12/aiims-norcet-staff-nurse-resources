### PYQ1. Which bedside trend is particularly useful when evaluating ongoing fluid resuscitation in a major burn?

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Topic:** Burn fluid monitoring

- **A.** Hourly urine output along with perfusion assessment
- **B.** Hair growth
- **C.** Weekly height
- **D.** Eye color

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: A. Hourly urine output along with perfusion assessment**

Major burn mein fluid shift hone se circulation kam ho sakti hai. Hourly urine output aur vitals kidney perfusion ka useful clue dete hain. Treatment response ko saath mein assess karna chahiye.

**Baaki options kyun galat hain?**

- **B.** Hair growth acute fluid status nahi batati.
- **C.** Height acute perfusion measure nahi hai.
- **D.** Eye color fluid resuscitation response nahi batata.

</details>
