### PYQ1. What is the expanded biochemical name of ATP?

**Difficulty:** Easy/Medium | **Topic:** Cellular energy

- **A.** Adenosine triphosphate
- **B.** Adenine tetraphosphate
- **C.** Adenosine monophosphate
- **D.** Adenine diphosphate

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation</summary>

**Correct: A. Adenosine triphosphate**

ATP ka full form adenosine triphosphate hai. Cell ATP ki chemical energy ko muscle contraction, transport aur doosre processes ke liye use karta hai. Monophosphate aur diphosphate mein phosphate groups kam hote hain, aur adenine alone nucleoside nahi hai.

</details>
