### Q1. Pleural fluid LDH is 180 U/L and the laboratory upper reference limit for serum LDH is 240 U/L. The other two Light criteria are negative. Which classification follows the LDH upper-limit criterion?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Priority:** High | **Topic:** Serous cavity fluid laboratory interpretation | **Exam focus:** NORCET-style original

- **A.** Exudate because pleural LDH exceeds two-thirds of the serum upper reference limit
- **B.** Transudate because both other criteria are negative
- **C.** Transudate because pleural LDH is below serum upper limit
- **D.** Indeterminate because three positive criteria are mandatory

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: A. Exudate because pleural LDH exceeds two-thirds of the serum upper reference limit**

- **A.** 240 ka two-thirds 160 U/L hai. Pleural LDH 180 hai, jo is limit se greater hai. Etiology separately investigate karte hain.
- **B.** Light criteria mein ek positive criterion bhi exudate classification support karta hai. Do negative criteria third ko cancel nahi karte.
- **C.** Full serum upper limit nahi, uska two-thirds relevant cutoff hai.
- **D.** Teen positive criteria zaroori nahi hain. Koi ek fulfill ho toh classification exudative ho sakti hai.

**Exam clue:** Light criteria mein ek positive criterion sufficient hai.
