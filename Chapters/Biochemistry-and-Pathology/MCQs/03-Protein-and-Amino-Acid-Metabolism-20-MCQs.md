## Part 1

### Q1. Amino nitrogen is transferred from alanine to alpha-ketoglutarate without immediate release of free ammonia. What type of reaction has occurred?

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Priority:** High | **Topic:** Protein and amino acid metabolism | **Exam focus:** NORCET-style original

- **A.** Oxidative decarboxylation
- **B.** Hydrolysis of glycogen
- **C.** Beta-oxidation
- **D.** Transamination

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: D. Transamination**

- **A.** Oxidative decarboxylation carbon dioxide release karti hai, amino transfer iska defining feature nahi hai.
- **B.** Glycogen hydrolysis glucose storage polymer ko todti hai.
- **C.** Beta-oxidation fatty acid chain ko chhota karti hai.
- **D.** Transamination mein amino group doosre carbon skeleton par transfer hota hai. Isse glutamate ban sakta hai aur nitrogen collect hota hai.

</details>

### Q12. A newborn develops lethargy, vomiting and marked hyperammonemia with increased urinary orotic acid. Which urea cycle defect should be considered?

- **B.** Phenylalanine hydroxylase deficiency
- **C.** Ornithine transcarbamylase deficiency
- **D.** Glucose-6-phosphatase deficiency

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

</details>

### Q17. Which clinical condition most directly favors negative nitrogen balance?

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Priority:** High | **Topic:** Protein and amino acid metabolism | **Exam focus:** NORCET-style original

- **A.** Healthy childhood growth with adequate nutrition
- **B.** Recovery with net protein tissue synthesis
- **C.** Severe catabolic illness with inadequate protein intake
- **D.** Pregnancy with adequate nutrition and normal tissue growth

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: C. Severe catabolic illness with inadequate protein intake**

- **A.** Normal growth mein net protein retention expected hoti hai.
- **B.** Net tissue synthesis positive balance favor karti hai.
