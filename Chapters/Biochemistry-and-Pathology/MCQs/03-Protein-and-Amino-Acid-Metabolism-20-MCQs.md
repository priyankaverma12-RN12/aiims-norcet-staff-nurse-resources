### Q1. Amino nitrogen is transferred from alanine to alpha-ketoglutarate without immediate release of free ammonia. What type of reaction has occurred?

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Priority:** High | **Topic:** Protein and amino acid metabolism | **Exam focus:** NORCET-style original

- **A.** Oxidative deamination
- **B.** Amino acid decarboxylation
- **C.** Glutamine synthesis
- **D.** Transamination

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: D. Transamination**

- **A.** Oxidative deamination mein glutamate jaise amino acid se free ammonia nikal sakta hai. Yahan nitrogen doosre carbon skeleton par ja raha hai, isliye yeh reaction alag hai.
- **B.** Decarboxylation mein amino acid ka carboxyl group carbon dioxide ki form mein nikalta hai. Is reaction mein amino group transfer hona zaroori nahi hai.
- **C.** Glutamine synthetase free ammonia ko glutamate ke saath jodta hai. Sawal mein alanine se seedha amino group transfer ho raha hai.
- **D.** Jab alanine apna amino group alpha-ketoglutarate ko deta hai, toh pyruvate aur glutamate bante hain. Isko transamination kehte hain aur is step mein free ammonia seedha release nahi hota.

**Exam clue:** Amino transfer aur free ammonia release ko alag pehchano.
