### Q1. During physiological heme degradation, which enzyme catalyzes the opening of the porphyrin ring to form biliverdin?

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Priority:** High | **Topic:** Heme catabolism and bilirubin biochemistry | **Exam focus:** NORCET-style original

- **A.** Biliverdin reductase
- **B.** Heme oxygenase
- **C.** Ferrochelatase
- **D.** Glucokinase

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: B. Heme oxygenase**

- **A.** Yeh biliverdin ko bilirubin mein convert karta hai. Ring opening iske pehle hoti hai.
- **B.** Heme oxygenase heme ring open karke biliverdin, iron aur carbon monoxide generate karta hai. Yeh heme catabolism ka initial enzymatic step hai.
- **C.** Ferrochelatase heme synthesis mein iron insert karta hai, heme degradation mein ring open nahi karta.
- **D.** Glucokinase glucose phosphorylation karta hai, heme degradation enzyme nahi.

</details>

### Q4. Before hepatic uptake, what is the principal transport form of newly generated unconjugated bilirubin in plasma?

- **B.** Covalent attachment to erythrocyte membrane cholesterol

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

- **C.** Unconjugated bilirubin poorly water-soluble hai. Albumin binding blood mein transport facilitate karti hai, phir liver uptake aur conjugation hoti hai.

</details>

</details>
