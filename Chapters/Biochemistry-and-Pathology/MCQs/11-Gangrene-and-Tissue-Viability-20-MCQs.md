### Q1. A patient with longstanding arterial insufficiency has a slowly evolving, shriveled, black distal toe without clinical evidence of infection. What process best explains the dry appearance?

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Priority:** High | **Topic:** Gangrene and tissue viability | **Exam focus:** NORCET-style original

- **A.** Acute pus production as the defining process
- **B.** Ischemic tissue death with progressive dehydration
- **C.** Excess arterial perfusion
- **D.** Isolated epidermal melanin production

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: B. Ischemic tissue death with progressive dehydration**

- **A.** Pus neutrophilic infection se associated hota hai. Dry ischemic appearance ke liye suppuration essential nahi hai.
- **B.** Arterial supply kam hone par tissue necrotic ho sakta hai. Moisture loss se dead segment dry aur shriveled dikh sakta hai.
- **C.** Increased perfusion ischemic tissue death ko explain nahi karti. Yahan supply inadequate hai.
- **D.** Melanin pigmentation change kar sakta hai, lekin dead shriveled tissue ka mechanism nahi hai.

</details>

</details>
