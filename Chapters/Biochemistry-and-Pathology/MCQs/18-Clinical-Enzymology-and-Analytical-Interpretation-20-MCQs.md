## Part 1

### Q1. A clinically well adult has persistent isolated hyperamylasemia, normal renal function and no evidence of pancreatic inflammation. A large amylase-immunoglobulin complex is suspected. Why can serum amylase remain elevated?

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Priority:** High | **Topic:** Clinical enzymology | **Exam focus:** NORCET-style original

- **A.** The complex necessarily increases pancreatic amylase synthesis
- **B.** Immunoglobulins directly hydrolyze dietary starch in the blood
- **C.** Macroamylase is freely filtered faster than ordinary amylase
- **D.** The high-molecular-weight complex has reduced renal clearance

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: D. The high-molecular-weight complex has reduced renal clearance**

- **A.** Macroenzyme formation altered clearance se serum activity increase kar sakti hai, increased pancreatic production required nahi.
- **B.** Immunoglobulins immune proteins hain. Complex mein attached amylase catalytic activity retain kar sakta hai, antibody itself starch digestion ka main enzyme nahi.
- **C.** Large complex ka filtration generally reduced hota hai, faster clearance typical explanation nahi.
- **D.** Macroamylase free amylase se larger complex hota hai, so glomerular filtration aur clearance kam ho sakti hai. Persistent serum elevation pancreatitis prove nahi karti.

</details>

</details>

### Q3. A patient's amylase-creatinine clearance ratio is being evaluated for possible macroamylasemia. The values are urine amylase 120 U/L, serum amylase 600 U/L, serum creatinine 1 mg/dL and urine creatinine 100 mg/dL. What is the ratio expressed as a percentage?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Priority:** High | **Topic:** Clinical enzymology | **Exam focus:** NORCET-style original

- **A.** 2%
- **B.** 20%
- **C.** 0.2%
- **D.** 0.002%

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: C. 0.2%**

- **A.** Yeh tenfold calculation error hai. Given creatinine ratio 0.01 hai, 0.1 nahi.
- **B.** Yeh amylase concentration ratio ko directly percentage banata hai aur creatinine correction miss karta hai.
- **C.** Formula urine amylase divided by serum amylase, multiplied by serum creatinine divided by urine creatinine and then 100 hai. 120/600 times 1/100 times 100 equals 0.2%. Low ratio supportive ho sakta hai, definitive diagnosis nahi.
- **D.** Yeh fractional result ko percentage mein convert karna bhool gaya hai. Percentage ke liye 100 se multiply karte hain.

</details>

### Q6. A patient has suspected acute pancreatitis, but repeated lipase results remain elevated after clinical improvement. Why is serial enzyme normalization alone a poor measure of disease severity?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Priority:** High | **Topic:** Clinical enzymology | **Exam focus:** NORCET-style original

- **A.** The highest lipase value always determines the exact amount of pancreatic necrosis
- **B.** Lipase normalization guarantees every complication has resolved
- **C.** Every patient requires daily lipase testing until discharge
- **D.** Enzyme concentration does not reliably quantify ongoing organ failure or complication burden

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: D. Enzyme concentration does not reliably quantify ongoing organ failure or complication burden**

- **A.** Serum activity se exact tissue necrosis volume infer nahi hota. Imaging aur clinical severity assessment separate hain.
- **B.** Enzyme decline useful context ho sakta hai, but complications persist kar sakti hain. Normalization alone recovery prove nahi karti.
- **C.** Repeat testing clinical indication aur protocol par depend karti hai. Routine daily enzyme tracking severity ka reliable universal requirement nahi.
- **D.** Lipase release aur clearance disease course ke different aspects reflect karte hain. Clinical stability, organ dysfunction aur complications severity assessment mein more relevant hain.

</details>
