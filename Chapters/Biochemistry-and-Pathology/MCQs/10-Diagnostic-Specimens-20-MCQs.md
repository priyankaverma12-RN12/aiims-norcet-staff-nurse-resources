### Q1. A routine skin biopsy is intended for paraffin-embedded histological examination, with no special studies requested. Which fixative is generally appropriate according to the laboratory protocol?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Priority:** High | **Topic:** Diagnostic specimens | **Exam focus:** NORCET-style original

- **A.** Sterile distilled water
- **B.** EDTA whole-blood tube
- **C.** 10% neutral buffered formalin
- **D.** Dry gauze for prolonged storage

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: C. 10% neutral buffered formalin**

- **A.** Distilled water proper fixative nahi hai aur osmotic tissue changes ho sakte hain.
- **B.** EDTA blood hematology ke liye anticoagulant hai, routine tissue biopsy ka standard fixative nahi hai.
- **C.** Routine histology mein neutral buffered formalin tissue proteins ko stabilize karta hai aur morphology preserve karta hai. Special tests ke liye alag medium required ho sakta hai.
- **D.** Dry storage se tissue desiccate aur autolyze ho sakta hai, morphology preserve nahi hoti.

</details>

### Q2. A surgeon requests an intraoperative frozen section to guide a decision during surgery. How should the specimen be handled?

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Priority:** High | **Topic:** Diagnostic specimens | **Exam focus:** NORCET-style original

- **A.** Place immediately in routine formalin for overnight fixation
- **B.** Send fresh promptly to pathology using the agreed frozen-section protocol
- **C.** Leave uncovered until the operation ends
- **D.** Freeze in an unmonitored ward freezer without contacting pathology

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: B. Send fresh promptly to pathology using the agreed frozen-section protocol**

- **A.** Routine fixation permanent sections ke liye useful hai, par requested immediate frozen examination ko delay ya invalidate kar sakti hai.
- **B.** Frozen section ke liye tissue ko rapid freezing aur cutting ke liye promptly laboratory tak pahunchana hota hai. Formalin add karne se intended rapid procedure compromise ho sakta hai.
- **C.** Uncovered delay se tissue dry ho sakta hai aur intraoperative decision ka purpose fail hota hai.
- **D.** Frozen section controlled laboratory technique hai, ward freezer mein uncontrolled handling artifact aur delay create kar sakti hai.

</details>

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

- **B.** Formalin organisms ko inactivate kar sakta hai, isliye culture yield compromise hoti hai.
- **C.** Open nonsterile transport contamination aur specimen loss ka risk badhata hai.
- **D.** Fixed section morphology dikha sakta hai, viable growth ke liye suitable specimen nahi hota.

</details>
