## Cell injury

### Q1. Which cellular change is most consistent with irreversible injury?

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Topic:** Cell injury

- **A.** Loss of membrane integrity with cell death
- **B.** Transient swelling that resolves after oxygen restoration
- **C.** Temporary reduction in ATP without lasting injury
- **D.** Normal physiological adaptation to exercise

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: A. Loss of membrane integrity with cell death**

Cell ki membrane bahut damage ho jaaye toh cell apna internal balance maintain nahi kar paata. Irreversible injury mein cell death ho sakti hai.

**Baaki options kyun galat hain?**

- **B.** Transient swelling reversible ho sakti hai.
- **C.** Temporary ATP fall recover ho sakta hai.
- **D.** Exercise adaptation normal response ho sakti hai.

</details>

## Inflammation

### Q2. Which mechanism contributes to redness and warmth during acute inflammation?

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Topic:** Inflammation

- **A.** Complete absence of blood flow in every case
- **B.** Reduced local metabolism as the main cause
- **C.** Permanent loss of all inflammatory mediators
- **D.** Local vasodilation and increased blood flow

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: D. Local vasodilation and increased blood flow**

Injury ya infection par vessels widen ho sakti hain. Blood zyada aane se area red aur warm lagta hai. Fluid leak se swelling bhi ho sakti hai.

**Baaki options kyun galat hain?**

- **A.** Blood flow often badhta hai.
- **B.** Reduced metabolism main explanation nahi hai.
- **C.** Inflammatory mediators response ko guide karte hain.

</details>

## Biochemical tests

### Q3. A patient has low serum albumin due to chronic liver dysfunction. Which physiological effect can follow?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Topic:** Biochemical tests

- **A.** Guaranteed absence of ascites
- **B.** Immediate increase in all clotting factors
- **C.** Reduced plasma oncotic pressure and edema tendency
- **D.** Increased plasma protein binding capacity in every case

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: C. Reduced plasma oncotic pressure and edema tendency**

Albumin blood ke andar paani rokne mein help karta hai. Liver albumin kam banaye toh fluid tissues ya abdomen mein jama ho sakta hai.

**Baaki options kyun galat hain?**

- **A.** Ascites ka risk badh sakta hai.
- **B.** Liver disease clotting factors kam bhi kar sakti hai.
- **D.** Albumin kam hone se binding capacity kam ho sakti hai.

</details>

## Thrombosis

### Q4. A bedridden patient develops a venous thrombus. Which component of Virchow's triad is directly related to immobility?

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Topic:** Thrombosis

- **A.** Reduced blood viscosity in every case
- **B.** Venous stasis
- **C.** Increased arterial oxygen saturation
- **D.** Enhanced lymphatic drainage

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: B. Venous stasis**

Bed rest mein leg muscles kam move karti hain. Venous blood ka flow slow ho sakta hai, jise stasis kehte hain. Isse clot banne ka risk badhta hai.

**Baaki options kyun galat hain?**

- **A.** Reduced viscosity immobility ka defining component nahi hai.
- **C.** Oxygen saturation triad ka component nahi hai.
- **D.** Lymph drainage stasis ka direct explanation nahi hai.

</details>
