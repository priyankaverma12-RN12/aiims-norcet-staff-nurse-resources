## Part 1

### Q1. A red blood cell must produce ATP despite lacking mitochondria. Which pathway directly supplies this ATP?

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Priority:** High | **Topic:** Carbohydrate metabolism | **Exam focus:** NORCET-style original

- **A.** Citric acid cycle
- **B.** Glycolysis
- **C.** Beta-oxidation
- **D.** Oxidative phosphorylation

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: B. Glycolysis**

- **A.** Citric acid cycle mitochondrial matrix mein hoti hai.
- **B.** RBC ke paas mitochondria nahi hote, isliye cytoplasm mein glucose todkar ATP banata hai.
- **C.** Fatty acid oxidation ke liye mitochondria chahiye.
- **D.** Electron transport chain RBC mein nahi hoti.

</details>

### Q2. A glucose molecule yields four ATP during the payoff phase of glycolysis. After accounting for investment, what is the net direct ATP yield?

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Priority:** High | **Topic:** Carbohydrate metabolism | **Exam focus:** NORCET-style original

- **A.** 4 ATP
- **B.** 6 ATP
- **C.** 0 ATP
- **D.** 2 ATP

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: D. 2 ATP**

- **A.** Chaar gross yield hai, net nahi hai.
- **B.** Chhe ATP is calculation se nahi milte.
- **C.** ATP investment ke baad bhi positive yield rehti hai.
- **D.** Shuru mein do ATP kharch hote hain aur baad mein chaar milte hain, isliye net do bachte hain.

</details>

### Q3. During intense muscle activity, why does conversion of pyruvate to lactate support continued glycolysis?

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Priority:** High | **Topic:** Carbohydrate metabolism | **Exam focus:** NORCET-style original

- **A.** It regenerates NAD+
- **B.** It directly produces FADH2
- **C.** It imports oxygen into cells
- **D.** It synthesizes glycogen

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: A. It regenerates NAD+**

- **A.** Lactate dehydrogenase NADH ko NAD+ mein badalta hai, jo glycolysis ki oxidation reaction ke liye zaroori hai.
- **B.** Is reaction mein FADH2 nahi banta.
- **C.** Lactate formation oxygen transport ka mechanism nahi hai.
- **D.** Is waqt glucose toot raha hai, glycogen synthesis direct result nahi hai.

</details>

### Q4. Which enzyme catalyzes the committed regulatory step from fructose-6-phosphate to fructose-1,6-bisphosphate?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Priority:** High | **Topic:** Carbohydrate metabolism | **Exam focus:** NORCET-style original

- **A.** Glucose-6-phosphatase
- **B.** Glycogen phosphorylase
- **C.** Phosphofructokinase-1
- **D.** Lactate dehydrogenase

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: C. Phosphofructokinase-1**

- **A.** Glucose-6-phosphatase free glucose release mein help karta hai.
- **B.** Glycogen phosphorylase stored glycogen todta hai.
- **C.** PFK-1 fructose-6-phosphate par phosphate lagata hai aur glycolysis ka major control point hai.
- **D.** LDH pyruvate aur lactate ko interconvert karta hai.

</details>

### Q5. A patient has impaired hepatic glucose-6-phosphatase activity. Which function is directly compromised?

- **B.** Conversion of lactate into pyruvate

- **D.** Release of free glucose into blood from glucose-6-phosphate

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

- **A.** Intestinal absorption transporter se hoti hai.

- **C.** Insulin receptor binding alag signaling process hai.
- **D.** Liver ko glucose blood mein bhejne se pehle phosphate hatana padta hai, isliye enzyme ki kami fasting glucose regulation bigaadti hai.

</details>

- **B.** Muscle glycogen se glucose-6-phosphate bana sakta hai.
- **C.** Skeletal muscle mein glycogen store hota hai.

</details>

### Q8. Which product of glycogen phosphorylase can enter glucose metabolism without the initial hexokinase ATP cost after conversion to glucose-6-phosphate?

- **C.** Lactose
- **D.** Acetyl-CoA

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

- **A.** Fructose glycogen ka direct product nahi hai.

- **C.** Lactose milk sugar hai.
- **D.** Acetyl-CoA glycogen phosphorylase se nahi banta.

</details>

### Q20. Which reaction bypasses the irreversible pyruvate kinase step during gluconeogenesis?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Priority:** High | **Topic:** Carbohydrate metabolism | **Exam focus:** NORCET-style original

- **A.** Pyruvate to oxaloacetate to phosphoenolpyruvate
- **B.** Pyruvate directly to glucose-6-phosphate
- **C.** Lactate directly to glycogen
- **D.** Acetyl-CoA directly to pyruvate

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: A. Pyruvate to oxaloacetate to phosphoenolpyruvate**

- **A.** Pyruvate carboxylase aur PEP carboxykinase milkar reverse pathway ka bypass banate hain.
- **B.** Glucose-6-phosphate tak pahunchne ke liye multiple steps chahiye.
- **C.** Lactate pehle pyruvate ban sakta hai, direct glycogen nahi banta.
- **D.** Pyruvate dehydrogenase reaction physiologically irreversible hai.

</details>
