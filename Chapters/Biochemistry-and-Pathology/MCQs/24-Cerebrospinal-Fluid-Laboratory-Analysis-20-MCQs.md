### Q1. A CSF glucose result is 42 mg/dL and the relevant paired serum glucose is 120 mg/dL. What is the CSF-to-serum glucose ratio?

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Priority:** High | **Topic:** Cerebrospinal fluid laboratory analysis | **Exam focus:** NORCET-style original

- **A.** 0.70
- **B.** 0.35
- **C.** 2.86
- **D.** 0.42

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: B. 0.35**

- **A.** 0.70 ke liye CSF glucose 84 mg/dL hona chahiye. Yahan numerator 42 hai.
- **B.** CSF value ko serum value se divide karo. 42/120 = 0.35 hai. Is ratio ko timing aur clinical findings ke saath interpret karna chahiye.
- **C.** 2.86 reverse division se aata hai. Sawal CSF divided by serum ka hai.
- **D.** 0.42 CSF value ko 100 se divide karne jaisa hoga. Actual paired serum value 120 hai.

### Q8. In a blood-contaminated CSF sample, the blood WBC count is 8,000/microliter, blood RBC count is 4 million/microliter and CSF RBC count is 10,000/microliter. Using the supplied formula CSF RBC × blood WBC/blood RBC, what is the predicted contaminating WBC count?

- **D.** 800 cells/microliter

- **D.** 800 count given ratio se match nahi karta. Pehle blood WBC-to-RBC ratio calculate karo, phir CSF RBC se multiply karo.
