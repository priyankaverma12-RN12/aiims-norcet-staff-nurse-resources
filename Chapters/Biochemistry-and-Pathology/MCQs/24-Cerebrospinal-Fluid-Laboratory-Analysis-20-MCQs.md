### Q1. A CSF glucose result is 42 mg/dL and the relevant paired serum glucose is 120 mg/dL. What is the CSF-to-serum glucose ratio?

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Priority:** High | **Topic:** Cerebrospinal fluid laboratory analysis | **Exam focus:** NORCET-style original

- **A.** 0.70
- **B.** 2.86
- **C.** 0.42
- **D.** 0.35

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: D. 0.35**

- **A.** 0.70 ke liye CSF glucose 84 mg/dL hona chahiye. Yahan numerator 42 hai.
- **B.** 2.86 reverse division se aata hai. Sawal CSF divided by serum ka hai.
- **C.** 0.42 CSF value ko 100 se divide karne jaisa hoga. Actual paired serum value 120 hai.
- **D.** CSF value ko serum value se divide karo. 42/120 = 0.35 hai. Is ratio ko timing aur clinical findings ke saath interpret karna chahiye.

**Exam clue:** CSF glucose ko paired serum glucose se divide karo.
