### Q1. Immediately after a clean skin incision, which structure provides the provisional scaffold for later migration of repair cells?

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Priority:** High | **Topic:** Tissue healing and repair | **Exam focus:** NORCET-style original

- **A.** Mature type I collagen scar
- **B.** Keratinized epidermis
- **C.** Fibrin clot
- **D.** Calcified cartilage

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: C. Fibrin clot**

- **A.** Mature scar remodeling ke baad develop hota hai, fresh incision ke immediate scaffold ka role nahi karta.
- **B.** Epidermis barrier restore karti hai, par fresh wound gap mein fibrin matrix pehle banta hai.
- **C.** Fibrin mesh bleeding ko control karne ke saath migrating cells ke liye temporary matrix banata hai. Baad mein fibroblasts is scaffold ko replace karte hain.
- **D.** Cartilage skin incision ke normal initial repair scaffold ka component nahi hai.

</details>

- **C.** Erythrocyte

- **B.** Neutrophil early debris aur microbes ko target karta hai, collagen matrix ka principal producer nahi hai.
- **C.** RBC oxygen carry karta hai, mature RBC matrix proteins synthesize nahi karta.
- **D.** Melanocytes pigment produce karte hain, wound collagen synthesis ka main source nahi hain.

</details>

</details>

### Q4. Histology of an actively healing skin defect shows capillaries, fibroblasts and loose extracellular matrix. What is this tissue called?

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Priority:** High | **Topic:** Tissue healing and repair | **Exam focus:** NORCET-style original

- **A.** Mature hyaline cartilage
- **B.** Granuloma
- **C.** Coagulative necrosis
- **D.** Granulation tissue

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: D. Granulation tissue**

- **A.** Hyaline cartilage mein chondrocytes aur specialized matrix hota hai, capillary-rich wound bed nahi hota.
- **B.** Granuloma organized macrophage aggregate hai, capillary-rich fibroblast repair tissue se alag hai.
- **C.** Coagulative necrosis dead tissue ko describe karta hai, actively growing repair tissue ko nahi.
- **D.** Naye vessels aur fibroblasts ke saath loose matrix granulation tissue banata hai. Yeh wound bed fill karta hai aur surface closure support karta hai.

</details>

### Q5. Which collagen transition is typical as an early cutaneous scar matures?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Priority:** High | **Topic:** Tissue healing and repair | **Exam focus:** NORCET-style original

- **A.** Type I is completely replaced by type II
- **B.** Type III is progressively replaced by type I
- **C.** Type IV becomes the sole fibrillar scar collagen
- **D.** All collagen is removed without replacement

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: B. Type III is progressively replaced by type I**

- **A.** Type II cartilage mein prominent hai, normal skin scar mein type I ko replace nahi karta.
- **B.** Early matrix mein type III collagen relatively prominent hota hai. Remodeling mein stronger type I collagen aur organized fibers tensile strength badhate hain.
- **C.** Type IV basement membrane networks banata hai, mature dermal scar ka principal fibrillar collagen nahi hai.
- **D.** Collagen turnover hota hai par scar mein structural collagen remain karta hai, total removal normal outcome nahi hai.

</details>

### Q8. A clean surgical incision has edges approximated with sutures and minimal tissue loss. Which healing pattern is expected if uncomplicated?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Priority:** High | **Topic:** Tissue healing and repair | **Exam focus:** NORCET-style original

- **A.** Secondary intention
- **B.** Delayed primary closure
- **C.** Healing by ossification
- **D.** Primary intention

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: D. Primary intention**

- **A.** Secondary healing mein open defect ko granulation aur contraction se fill karna padta hai, closely approximated clean incision iska usual example nahi hai.
- **B.** Is approach mein wound pehle open rakha jata hai aur baad mein close hota hai, yahan immediate approximation hui hai.
- **C.** Ossification bone formation hai, routine skin incision closure ka classification nahi hai.
- **D.** Edges close hone se defect chhota hota hai, isliye kam granulation tissue aur contraction chahiye. Healing usually smaller scar ke saath hoti hai.

</details>

### Q10. A patient with severe vitamin C deficiency has fragile new wound tissue. Which biochemical step is most directly affected?

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

- **C.** Heme pathway mein glycine participate karta hai, lekin fragile collagen ka direct explanation hydroxylation defect hai.
- **D.** Lipase dietary fat digestion karta hai, collagen maturation ka affected step nahi hai.

### Q11. A wound is persistently hypoperfused despite adequate nutrition. Why may collagen maturation and antimicrobial defense remain impaired?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Priority:** High | **Topic:** Tissue healing and repair | **Exam focus:** NORCET-style original

- **A.** Insufficient local oxygen for oxygen-dependent repair and killing reactions
- **B.** Excess oxygen invariably stops fibroblast migration
- **C.** Low oxygen guarantees rapid epithelialization
- **D.** Perfusion affects only red cell color

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: A. Insufficient local oxygen for oxygen-dependent repair and killing reactions**

- **A.** Collagen hydroxylation aur phagocyte oxidative killing ko oxygen chahiye. Poor perfusion mein delivery kam hone se repair aur defense dono slow ho sakte hain.
- **B.** Yahan problem insufficient delivery hai. Excess oxygen ka universal inhibitory rule apply nahi hota.
- **C.** Hypoxia severe ya prolonged ho toh epithelial repair slow ho sakta hai, rapid closure guarantee nahi hoti.
- **D.** Perfusion oxygen aur nutrient delivery maintain karti hai, iska effect RBC color tak limited nahi hai.

</details>

### Q16. A postoperative incision has healed externally but the patient assumes its tensile strength equals uninjured skin. Which explanation is most accurate?

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Priority:** High | **Topic:** Tissue healing and repair | **Exam focus:** NORCET-style original

- **A.** Full strength is restored as soon as epithelium closes
- **B.** Scar strength improves gradually but generally does not regain full original strength
- **C.** Tensile strength decreases continuously in all normal scars
- **D.** Scar strength depends only on surface color

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: B. Scar strength improves gradually but generally does not regain full original strength**

- **A.** Surface barrier closure aur deep tensile strength alag processes hain, closure immediate full strength prove nahi karti.
- **B.** Collagen organization aur cross-linking se strength time ke saath badhti hai. Mature scar bhi usually uninjured tissue ke barabar strength regain nahi karta.
- **C.** Normal remodeling generally strength increase karta hai, continuous decrease expected nahi hai.
- **D.** Color appearance batata hai, collagen architecture aur depth ki strength directly measure nahi karta.

</details>

### Q20. A wound remains open with raised friable granulation tissue protruding above the skin margins. Why may surface closure be delayed?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Priority:** High | **Topic:** Tissue healing and repair | **Exam focus:** NORCET-style original

- **A.** Excess granulation can obstruct epithelial migration across the wound bed
- **B.** Granulation always accelerates closure regardless of height
- **C.** Epithelial cells can migrate only through blood vessels
- **D.** Raised tissue proves invasive cancer in every case

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: A. Excess granulation can obstruct epithelial migration across the wound bed**

- **A.** Agar granulation surface se upar grow kare toh keratinocyte migration difficult ho sakti hai. Clinical assessment underlying cause identify karne mein help karti hai.
- **B.** Healthy granulation useful hai par excessive tissue epithelial migration hinder kar sakta hai.
- **C.** Keratinocytes surface aur suitable matrix ke along migrate karte hain, blood vessels ke andar migration required nahi hai.
- **D.** Hypergranulation benign repair phenomenon ho sakta hai. Atypical lesions ko assess karna chahiye par appearance alone cancer prove nahi karti.

</details>
