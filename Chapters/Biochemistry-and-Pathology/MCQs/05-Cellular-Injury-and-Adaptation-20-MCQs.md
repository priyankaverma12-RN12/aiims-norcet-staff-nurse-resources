### Q1. In long-standing pressure overload from hypertension, which cellular mechanism most directly increases the size of adult ventricular cardiomyocytes?

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Priority:** High | **Topic:** Cellular injury and adaptation | **Exam focus:** NORCET-style original

- **A.** Rapid multiplication of mature cardiomyocytes as the dominant response
- **B.** Increased synthesis of contractile proteins within existing cardiomyocytes
- **C.** Replacement of cardiac muscle by mature squamous epithelium
- **D.** Progressive loss of sarcomeres within viable cardiomyocytes

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: B. Increased synthesis of contractile proteins within existing cardiomyocytes**

- **A.** Adult cardiomyocytes ki proliferative capacity limited hoti hai. Pressure overload mein wall thickening ka main response naye mature cells ka rapid multiplication nahi hota.
- **B.** Jab ventricle ko high pressure ke against pump karna padta hai, toh mechanical stress aur growth signals existing cells mein contractile proteins aur sarcomeres badha sakte hain. Isse cell ka size badhta hai aur hypertrophy develop hoti hai.
- **C.** Squamous metaplasia epithelial differentiation ka change hai. Cardiac muscle ki pressure-related growth ko yeh mechanism explain nahi karta.
- **D.** Sarcomeres lose hone se contractile mass aur cell size kam ho sakte hain. Yahan pressure workload badhne ke response mein hypertrophy develop ho rahi hai.

**Exam clue:** Pressure overload mein existing cardiomyocytes contractile mass badhate hain.

- **D.** Protein degradation kam ho aur synthesis same rahe, toh net protein loss explain nahi hota. Atrophy mein breakdown synthesis se zyada ho sakta hai.

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Priority:** High | **Topic:** Cellular injury and adaptation | **Exam focus:** NORCET-style original

- **A.** Metastatic calcification
- **B.** Dystrophic calcification exclusively
- **C.** Caseous necrosis
- **D.** Hydropic degeneration

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: A. Metastatic calcification**

- **A.** Systemic hypercalcemia ya phosphate imbalance se otherwise viable tissues mein mineral salts deposit ho sakte hain. Poorly controlled hypercalcemia aur renal tissue deposition metastatic calcification ko support karte hain.
- **B.** Dystrophic calcification damaged ya dead tissue mein local deposition hoti hai aur serum calcium normal bhi ho sakta hai. Sawal specifically systemic hypercalcemia aur viable tissue describe karta hai.
- **C.** Caseous necrosis granulomatous inflammation ke context mein characteristic dead tissue pattern ho sakti hai. Renal mineral deposition ke systemic calcium mechanism ka naam caseous necrosis nahi hai.
- **D.** Hydropic degeneration mein sodium aur water intracellular accumulate hone se cell swelling hoti hai. Yeh calcium salt deposition se different process hai.

**Exam clue:** Systemic mineral imbalance aur viable tissue metastatic calcification ko support karte hain.
