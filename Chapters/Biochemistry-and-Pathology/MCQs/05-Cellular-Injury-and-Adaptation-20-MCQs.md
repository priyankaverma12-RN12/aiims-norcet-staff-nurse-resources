## Part 1

### Q1. A patient with long-standing hypertension develops thickening of the left ventricular wall without a substantial increase in cardiomyocyte number. Which adaptation explains the change?

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Priority:** High | **Topic:** Cellular injury and adaptation | **Exam focus:** NORCET-style original

- **A.** Hyperplasia
- **B.** Hypertrophy
- **C.** Metaplasia
- **D.** Atrophy

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: B. Hypertrophy**

- **A.** Hyperplasia cell number ka increase hai.
- **B.** Pressure overload mein cardiomyocytes zyada contractile protein banakar size badhate hain. Adult cardiac muscle mein cell number ka increase main response nahi hai.
- **C.** Metaplasia mein mature cell type replace hota hai.
- **D.** Atrophy mein cell size kam hota hai.

</details>

### Q2. During normal pregnancy, the uterus enlarges through increased smooth muscle cell size and increased cell number. Which adaptations occur together?

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Priority:** High | **Topic:** Cellular injury and adaptation | **Exam focus:** NORCET-style original

- **A.** Atrophy and necrosis
- **B.** Metaplasia and dysplasia
- **C.** Hypertrophy and hyperplasia
- **D.** Apoptosis and calcification

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: C. Hypertrophy and hyperplasia**

- **A.** Atrophy tissue size kam karti hai aur necrosis cell death hai.
- **B.** Metaplasia cell type change hai aur dysplasia abnormal maturation hai.
- **C.** Pregnancy hormones smooth muscle cell growth aur proliferation dono promote kar sakte hain, isliye uterus size badhta hai.
- **D.** Apoptosis aur calcification normal uterine growth ka mechanism nahi hai.

</details>

### Q3. A patient has reduced thigh muscle bulk after six weeks of leg immobilization. Which adaptive process is most likely?

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Priority:** High | **Topic:** Cellular injury and adaptation | **Exam focus:** NORCET-style original

- **A.** Disuse atrophy
- **B.** Coagulative necrosis
- **C.** Squamous metaplasia
- **D.** Compensatory hyperplasia

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: A. Disuse atrophy**

- **A.** Muscle workload kam hone se protein synthesis kam aur degradation badh sakti hai. Isse fibers ka size aur strength kam hote hain.
- **B.** Necrosis irreversible cell death hai, simple disuse ka usual response nahi.
- **C.** Squamous metaplasia epithelial cell type change hai.
- **D.** Hyperplasia cell number badhati hai, disuse muscle wasting explain nahi karti.

</details>

### Q4. A chronic smoker's bronchial biopsy shows mature stratified squamous epithelium replacing ciliated respiratory epithelium without malignant invasion. What is this adaptation?

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Priority:** High | **Topic:** Cellular injury and adaptation | **Exam focus:** NORCET-style original

- **A.** Squamous cell carcinoma
- **B.** Physiological hyperplasia
- **C.** Coagulative necrosis
- **D.** Squamous metaplasia

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: D. Squamous metaplasia**

- **A.** Carcinoma diagnosis ke liye malignant features chahiye, metaplasia alone cancer nahi hai.
- **B.** Hyperplasia number badhati hai, mature cell type replacement nahi.
- **C.** Coagulative necrosis dead tissue ka pattern hai.
- **D.** Repeated irritation mein epithelial differentiation change ho sakti hai. Squamous cells protective ho sakte hain par cilia aur mucus clearance ka normal function lose ho sakta hai.

</details>

### Q5. Chronic gastroesophageal reflux leads to replacement of distal esophageal squamous epithelium by intestinal-type columnar epithelium. Which process is this?

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Priority:** High | **Topic:** Cellular injury and adaptation | **Exam focus:** NORCET-style original

- **A.** Anaplasia
- **B.** Hypertrophy
- **C.** Metaplasia
- **D.** Liquefactive necrosis

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: C. Metaplasia**

- **A.** Anaplasia malignant cells ki poor differentiation describe karti hai.
- **B.** Hypertrophy cell size increase hai.
- **C.** Barrett esophagus mein chronic reflux se columnar metaplasia develop ho sakti hai. Yeh cancer risk badha sakti hai par metaplasia khud invasive cancer nahi hai.
- **D.** Liquefactive necrosis tissue digestion aur death ka pattern hai.

</details>

### Q6. A biopsy shows disordered epithelial maturation, nuclear atypia and abnormal mitoses, but no stromal invasion. Which term best describes these changes?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Priority:** High | **Topic:** Cellular injury and adaptation | **Exam focus:** NORCET-style original

- **A.** Dysplasia
- **B.** Physiological hypertrophy
- **C.** Normal metaplasia only
- **D.** Simple disuse atrophy

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: A. Dysplasia**

- **A.** Dysplasia mein cells ki maturation aur architecture abnormal hoti hai. Invasion absent hone par invasive cancer automatically diagnose nahi karte.
- **B.** Hypertrophy size increase hai without the defining atypia.
- **C.** Metaplasia alone mein ek mature type doosre type se replace hota hai, atypia essential nahi hai.
- **D.** Atrophy tissue size kam karti hai, abnormal maturation explain nahi karti.

</details>

### Q8. Which change directly explains early hydropic swelling during reversible hypoxic cell injury?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Priority:** High | **Topic:** Cellular injury and adaptation | **Exam focus:** NORCET-style original

- **A.** Increased ATP drives excessive sodium extrusion
- **B.** ATP depletion impairs sodium-potassium pump activity
- **C.** Increased collagen synthesis replaces all intracellular water
- **D.** Lysosomal enzymes immediately digest every cell

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: B. ATP depletion impairs sodium-potassium pump activity**

- **A.** Sodium extrusion badhne se sodium retention explain nahi hoti.
- **B.** Oxygen kam milne par ATP production gir sakti hai. Na+/K+ pump weak ho toh sodium cell mein accumulate karta hai aur water follow karta hai.
- **C.** Collagen extracellular matrix ka component hai, early hydropic swelling ka direct mechanism nahi.
- **D.** Immediate complete digestion irreversible damage hoga, early reversible swelling nahi.

</details>

### Q10. A nurse is comparing hypoxia and ischemia. Which statement best distinguishes ischemia?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Priority:** High | **Topic:** Cellular injury and adaptation | **Exam focus:** NORCET-style original

- **A.** Ischemia reduces perfusion and therefore may impair nutrient delivery and waste removal as well as oxygen supply
- **B.** Ischemia always occurs despite completely normal tissue perfusion
- **C.** Hypoxia always means total absence of arterial blood flow
- **D.** Ischemia never causes ATP depletion

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: A. Ischemia reduces perfusion and therefore may impair nutrient delivery and waste removal as well as oxygen supply**

- **A.** Ischemia mein blood flow kam hota hai, isliye oxygen ke saath substrates bhi kam milte hain aur waste clearance disturb hoti hai.
- **B.** Reduced perfusion ischemia ki defining feature hai.
- **C.** Hypoxia adequate flow ke saath bhi ho sakti hai, jaise low blood oxygen content mein.
- **D.** Ischemia oxidative metabolism ko impair karke ATP depletion kar sakti hai.

</details>

### Q11. Following restoration of blood flow to ischemic tissue, which mechanism can contribute to additional reperfusion injury?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Priority:** High | **Topic:** Cellular injury and adaptation | **Exam focus:** NORCET-style original

- **A.** Permanent elimination of all free radicals
- **B.** Immediate reversal of all prior cell death
- **C.** Reactive oxygen species generation and inflammatory activation
- **D.** Complete inhibition of calcium entry in every cell

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: C. Reactive oxygen species generation and inflammatory activation**

- **A.** Reperfusion free radicals eliminate karne ki guarantee nahi hai.
- **B.** Already dead cells recover nahi hote.
- **C.** Reoxygenation se damaged mitochondria aur inflammatory cells reactive oxygen species produce kar sakte hain. Isse lipid, protein aur DNA injury worsen ho sakti hai.
- **D.** Calcium overload reperfusion injury mein contribute kar sakta hai.

</details>

### Q12. In an injured cell, excessive cytosolic calcium can activate phospholipases. Which structure is directly threatened by their activity?

- **A.** Nuclear DNA base pairing only
- **B.** Cellular membranes
- **C.** Extracellular hemoglobin exclusively
- **D.** The glycogen branch point only

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: B. Cellular membranes**

- **A.** DNA damage mein endonucleases aur oxidative mechanisms bhi involved ho sakte hain, par phospholipase ka direct substrate lipid hai.
- **B.** Phospholipases membrane phospholipids tod sakte hain. Plasma aur organelle membranes damage hone par cellular function aur homeostasis bigad sakte hain.
- **C.** Hemoglobin protein hai, phospholipase ka direct target nahi.
- **D.** Glycogen carbohydrate hai, membrane phospholipid nahi.

</details>

### Q13. A histology slide from an ischemic renal infarct shows preserved tissue outlines with loss of nuclei and cellular viability. Which pattern of necrosis is typical?

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Priority:** High | **Topic:** Cellular injury and adaptation | **Exam focus:** NORCET-style original

- **A.** Coagulative necrosis
- **B.** Liquefactive necrosis
- **C.** Caseous necrosis
- **D.** Fat necrosis

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: A. Coagulative necrosis**

- **A.** Ischemic solid-organ infarction mein protein denaturation tissue architecture ko temporarily preserve kar sakti hai, even though cells dead hote hain.
- **B.** Liquefactive necrosis mein tissue digestion se architecture lose hoti hai.
- **C.** Caseous necrosis classically tuberculosis ke granulomas mein milti hai.
- **D.** Fat necrosis adipose tissue injury ka pattern hai.

</details>

### Q14. A cerebral infarction evolves into a cavity containing digested tissue debris. Which necrosis pattern explains this?

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Priority:** High | **Topic:** Cellular injury and adaptation | **Exam focus:** NORCET-style original

- **A.** Coagulative necrosis
- **B.** Fibrinoid necrosis
- **C.** Dry gangrene
- **D.** Liquefactive necrosis

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: D. Liquefactive necrosis**

- **A.** Most solid organ infarcts coagulative hote hain, brain important exception hai.
- **B.** Fibrinoid necrosis vascular wall injury ka microscopic pattern hai.
- **C.** Dry gangrene usually ischemic extremity mein coagulative tissue death describe karta hai.
- **D.** Brain infarction mein hydrolytic digestion dead tissue ko liquefy kar sakti hai, jisse later cavity form hoti hai.

</details>

- **A.** Squamous metaplasia with keratin pearls
- **B.** Compensatory hyperplasia of adipocytes

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

- **A.** Keratin pearls squamous differentiation ka feature hain.
- **B.** Hyperplasia cell number badhati hai aur chalky calcium soaps nahi banati.

- **D.** Glycogen storage carbohydrate metabolism se related hai.

### Q17. Which microscopic finding most strongly favors apoptosis rather than typical acute necrotic cell death?

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Priority:** High | **Topic:** Cellular injury and adaptation | **Exam focus:** NORCET-style original

- **A.** Diffuse swelling with early membrane rupture
- **B.** Cell shrinkage with membrane-bound apoptotic bodies
- **C.** Large inflammatory exudate from spilled intracellular contents
- **D.** Complete tissue liquefaction

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: B. Cell shrinkage with membrane-bound apoptotic bodies**

- **A.** Swelling aur rupture typical necrotic injury mein mil sakte hain.
- **B.** Apoptosis mein cell shrink hota hai aur fragments membrane-bound bodies mein package ho sakte hain. Phagocytes unko clear karte hain, usually limited inflammation ke saath.
- **C.** Spilled contents inflammation trigger karte hain, typical apoptosis mein kam spillage hota hai.
- **D.** Tissue liquefaction necrosis ka pattern hai.

</details>

### Q20. A patient with poorly controlled hypercalcemia develops calcium deposition in otherwise viable renal tissues. Which type of calcification is most likely?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Priority:** High | **Topic:** Cellular injury and adaptation | **Exam focus:** NORCET-style original

- **A.** Metastatic calcification
- **B.** Dystrophic calcification exclusively
- **C.** Caseous necrosis
- **D.** Hydropic degeneration

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: A. Metastatic calcification**

- **A.** Systemic calcium ya phosphate imbalance se normal tissues mein mineral deposition ho sakta hai. Hypercalcemia is process ko support karti hai.
- **B.** Dystrophic calcification usually previously damaged tissue mein hoti hai, normal calcium par bhi.
- **C.** Caseous necrosis granulomatous tissue death ka pattern hai.
- **D.** Hydropic degeneration cellular water swelling hai, mineral deposition nahi.

</details>
