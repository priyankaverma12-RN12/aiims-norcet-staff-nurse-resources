## Part 1

### Q1. A coronary atherosclerotic plaque ruptures and a thrombus develops over the disrupted surface. Which local event initiates platelet-rich thrombosis?

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Priority:** High | **Topic:** Thrombosis and embolism | **Exam focus:** NORCET-style original

- **A.** Intact endothelial release of nitric oxide
- **B.** Increased circulating albumin alone
- **C.** Reduced fibrin formation alone
- **D.** Exposure of thrombogenic subendothelial material and tissue factor

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: D. Exposure of thrombogenic subendothelial material and tissue factor**

- **A.** Normal endothelium ka nitric oxide platelet activation oppose karta hai, plaque rupture ke thrombotic trigger ka best explanation nahi hai.
- **B.** Albumin oncotic pressure regulate karta hai, ruptured plaque par platelet adhesion ka direct trigger nahi hai.
- **C.** Fibrin formation reduce hone se clot stabilization kam hota hai, yeh thrombosis initiate karne ka typical mechanism nahi hai.
- **D.** Plaque rupture ke baad collagen aur tissue factor blood ke contact mein aate hain. Platelet adhesion aur coagulation activate hokar lumen block kar sakte hain.

</details>

### Q2. A histologic specimen from an arterial thrombus contains alternating platelet-fibrin pale layers and erythrocyte-rich darker layers. What are these laminations called?

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Priority:** High | **Topic:** Thrombosis and embolism | **Exam focus:** NORCET-style original

- **A.** Reed-Sternberg cells
- **B.** Lines of Zahn
- **C.** Councilman bodies
- **D.** Psammoma bodies

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: B. Lines of Zahn**

- **A.** Reed-Sternberg cells Hodgkin lymphoma se associated hain, clot ki lamination ka term nahi hai.
- **B.** Flowing blood mein platelet-fibrin aur RBC-rich layers deposit ho sakti hain. In laminations ko Lines of Zahn kehte hain aur yeh antemortem thrombus ko support karti hain.
- **C.** Councilman bodies apoptotic hepatocytes ko describe karte hain, arterial thrombus layers nahi hain.
- **D.** Psammoma bodies laminated calcifications hain, platelet aur RBC layers se alag hain.

</details>

### Q3. A pathologist compares a thrombus attached to an artery with a loose gelatinous postmortem clot. Which finding most strongly favors an antemortem thrombus?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Priority:** High | **Topic:** Thrombosis and embolism | **Exam focus:** NORCET-style original

- **A.** A freely removable gelatinous mass only
- **B.** Uniform yellow supernatant without attachment
- **C.** Firm attachment to the vascular wall with organized platelet-fibrin architecture
- **D.** Complete absence of any fibrin or blood cells

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: C. Firm attachment to the vascular wall with organized platelet-fibrin architecture**

- **A.** Loose gelatinous mass postmortem coagulation mein mil sakta hai. Attachment aur organization ki absence antemortem diagnosis ko support nahi karti.
- **B.** Postmortem clot mein sedimentation ke saath yellow component mil sakta hai, yeh viable thrombosis ka strong evidence nahi hai.
- **C.** Living circulation mein thrombus wall se attach hokar develop kar sakta hai aur platelet-fibrin structure dikhata hai. Yeh postmortem clot se distinction support karta hai.
- **D.** Thrombus blood components se banta hai, unki complete absence clot diagnosis support nahi karti.

</details>

### Q4. Why are venous thrombi often more erythrocyte-rich than arterial thrombi?

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Priority:** High | **Topic:** Thrombosis and embolism | **Exam focus:** NORCET-style original

- **A.** Slow venous flow favors fibrin-rich clot formation with trapped red cells
- **B.** Venous thrombi contain no fibrin
- **C.** Arterial thrombi contain no platelets
- **D.** Veins contain only leukocytes

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: A. Slow venous flow favors fibrin-rich clot formation with trapped red cells**

- **A.** Venous circulation mein low shear aur slow flow coagulation ko favor karte hain. Fibrin mesh RBC ko trap karta hai aur red appearance de sakta hai.
- **B.** Fibrin venous clot ka important structural component hai, absence red-cell trapping explain nahi karti.
- **C.** Arterial thrombi often platelet-rich hote hain, platelet absence comparison ko reverse kar deti hai.
- **D.** Venous blood mein RBC, WBC aur platelets sab hote hain, isolated leukocyte composition nahi hoti.

</details>

### Q5. An old venous thrombus is invaded by fibroblasts and capillaries and becomes incorporated into the vessel wall. Which fate is illustrated?

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Priority:** High | **Topic:** Thrombosis and embolism | **Exam focus:** NORCET-style original

- **A.** Organization
- **B.** Acute embolization
- **C.** Immediate complete enzymatic dissolution
- **D.** Fat necrosis

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: A. Organization**

- **A.** Thrombus mein repair cells aur new vessels grow karte hain. Clot gradually fibrous tissue ka part ban sakta hai, jise organization kehte hain.
- **B.** Embolization mein clot ka fragment detach hokar travel karta hai, local incorporation nahi hota.
- **C.** Dissolution mein fibrin breakdown se clot disappear ho sakta hai, fibrovascular ingrowth iska defining feature nahi hai.
- **D.** Fat necrosis adipose tissue injury ka pattern hai, organized venous thrombus ka fate nahi hai.

</details>

### Q6. A chronically occluded vessel develops small new endothelial-lined channels through an organized thrombus. What process is occurring?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Priority:** High | **Topic:** Thrombosis and embolism | **Exam focus:** NORCET-style original

- **A.** Extravascular hematoma formation
- **B.** Acute platelet aggregation without remodeling
- **C.** Diffuse metastatic calcification
- **D.** Recanalization

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: D. Recanalization**

- **A.** Hematoma vessel se bahar blood collection hai, thrombus ke through new channels nahi hain.
- **B.** Platelet aggregation early clot formation hai, endothelial-lined channels chronic remodeling ka sign hain.
- **C.** Metastatic calcification elevated mineral balance se related hai, vascular channel restoration ka term nahi hai.
- **D.** Organization ke dauran new channels clot ke andar form ho sakte hain. Isse kuch blood flow restore ho sakta hai, lekin normal lumen guaranteed nahi hai.

</details>

### Q7. A hospitalized patient's clot enlarges as additional platelets and fibrin accumulate at its advancing edge. Which thrombus fate does this represent?

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Priority:** High | **Topic:** Thrombosis and embolism | **Exam focus:** NORCET-style original

- **A.** Resolution
- **B.** Propagation
- **C.** Recanalization
- **D.** Embolization

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: B. Propagation**

- **A.** Resolution mein clot burden kam hota hai, enlargement nahi hota.
- **B.** Existing thrombus par aur clot material add hone se woh extend hota hai. Is process ko propagation kehte hain aur obstruction worsen ho sakti hai.
- **C.** Recanalization new channels banne ka late process hai, edge par fresh deposition nahi hai.
- **D.** Embolization mein material separate hokar distant site tak travel karta hai, local growth nahi hai.

</details>

### Q8. Which endogenous enzyme directly breaks down cross-linked fibrin during physiological fibrinolysis?

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Priority:** High | **Topic:** Thrombosis and embolism | **Exam focus:** NORCET-style original

- **A.** Thrombin
- **B.** Factor XIIIa
- **C.** Plasmin
- **D.** Platelet cyclooxygenase

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: C. Plasmin**

- **A.** Thrombin fibrinogen ko fibrin mein convert karke clot formation support karta hai, fibrinolysis ka direct protease nahi hai.
- **B.** Factor XIIIa fibrin strands cross-link karke clot stabilize karta hai, fibrin breakdown nahi karta.
- **C.** Plasmin fibrin strands cleave karta hai. Isse clot matrix degrade ho sakti hai aur breakdown products release hote hain.
- **D.** Platelet COX thromboxane pathway mein kaam karta hai, cross-linked fibrin cleavage ka enzyme nahi hai.

</details>

### Q9. A patient with a recent thrombus has an elevated D-dimer. What biochemical event is required to generate this marker?

- **A.** Degradation of albumin alone
- **B.** Degradation of cross-linked fibrin
- **C.** Synthesis of new hemoglobin
- **D.** Activation of platelets without any fibrin formation

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: B. Degradation of cross-linked fibrin**

- **A.** Albumin breakdown se amino acid products milte hain, fibrin-specific D-dimer nahi banta.
- **B.** D-dimer tab release hota hai jab cross-linked fibrin degrade hota hai. Elevated result clot turnover indicate kar sakta hai par specific thrombus location prove nahi karta.
- **C.** Hemoglobin synthesis erythroid cells mein hoti hai, D-dimer fibrin metabolism se related hai.
- **D.** Platelet activation alone cross-linked fibrin degradation product generate nahi kar sakti.

</details>

### Q10. A thrombus forms in the akinetic left ventricular apex after a large anterior myocardial infarction. What pathological category best describes its location?

- **B.** Extravascular hematoma
- **C.** Mural thrombus
- **D.** Isolated pericardial effusion

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: C. Mural thrombus**

- **A.** Venous valve thrombus peripheral vein mein form hota hai, left ventricular wall par nahi.
- **B.** Hematoma blood vessel ya heart lumen ke bahar collect hota hai, intracardiac wall clot nahi hai.
- **C.** Mural thrombus heart chamber ki wall par attached hota hai. Akinetic segment mein abnormal flow aur endocardial injury formation promote kar sakte hain.
- **D.** Pericardial effusion heart ke outer sac mein fluid hai, ventricular lumen thrombus se alag hai.

</details>

### Q11. A venous thrombus fragment reaches the systemic arterial circulation through a right-to-left intracardiac shunt. What is this process called?

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Priority:** High | **Topic:** Thrombosis and embolism | **Exam focus:** NORCET-style original

- **A.** Paradoxical embolism
- **B.** Ordinary pulmonary thromboembolism
- **C.** Local arterial thrombosis
- **D.** Lymphatic metastasis

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: A. Paradoxical embolism**

- **A.** Venous embolus right-to-left passage se pulmonary filter bypass kar sakta hai aur systemic artery block kar sakta hai. Is route ko paradoxical embolism kehte hain.
- **B.** Ordinary venous thrombus right heart se pulmonary arteries tak jata hai, systemic shunt crossing defining feature nahi hai.
- **C.** Local thrombosis same arterial site par form hoti hai, venous origin se travel nahi karti.
- **D.** Lymphatic metastasis malignant cells ka spread hai, venous clot crossing ka term nahi hai.

</details>

### Q13. A wedge-shaped infarct develops in the spleen following arterial obstruction. Why is the lesion typically pale rather than hemorrhagic?

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Priority:** High | **Topic:** Thrombosis and embolism | **Exam focus:** NORCET-style original

- **A.** Abundant dual pulmonary-type circulation
- **B.** Primary venous congestion with persistent inflow
- **C.** Relatively solid tissue with limited collateral blood entry
- **D.** Diffuse intravascular fat deposition only

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: C. Relatively solid tissue with limited collateral blood entry**

- **A.** Dual supply blood ko injured region mein enter karne de sakti hai, red infarct ko favor kar sakti hai.
- **B.** Venous outflow obstruction aur ongoing inflow hemorrhagic change ko favor karte hain, pale pattern ka best explanation nahi hai.
- **C.** Spleen jaise solid organ mein terminal arterial obstruction ke baad surrounding blood entry limited ho sakti hai. Necrotic area pale appearance le sakta hai.
- **D.** Fat deposition wedge-shaped pale ischemic lesion ka usual mechanism nahi hai.

</details>

- **B.** Agar blood entry bilkul na ho toh hemorrhagic accumulation ko explain karna difficult hai.
- **C.** High oncotic pressure fluid retention support kar sakta hai, venous congestion-associated hemorrhage ka main mechanism nahi hai.
- **D.** RBC fibroblast mein convert nahi hote, hemorrhagic infarct blood extravasation se related hai.

</details>

- **B.** Infected vegetation ka fragment travel karke vessel occlude kar sakta hai aur organisms seed kar sakta hai. Isse infarction ke saath abscess develop ho sakta hai.

### Q20. A specimen shows a thrombus occupying only part of a vessel lumen, with blood still passing alongside it. Which description is anatomically accurate?

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Priority:** High | **Topic:** Thrombosis and embolism | **Exam focus:** NORCET-style original

- **A.** Complete vascular occlusion
- **B.** Extravascular hemorrhage alone
- **C.** Normal vessel without thrombosis
- **D.** Nonocclusive thrombus

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: D. Nonocclusive thrombus**

- **A.** Complete occlusion mein patent flow channel absent hota hai, yahan blood passage remain hai.
- **B.** Extravascular hemorrhage blood outside vessel ko kehte hain, partial intraluminal clot nahi hai.
- **C.** Visible intraluminal thrombus abnormal finding hai, flow present hone se clot exclude nahi hota.
- **D.** Nonocclusive clot lumen ka part occupy karta hai aur kuch flow remain karta hai. Yeh benign guarantee nahi hai, clot extend ya embolize ho sakta hai.

</details>
