### Q1. A venous glucose sample is left unseparated at room temperature while blood cells remain in contact with plasma. Which process can falsely lower the result?

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Priority:** High | **Topic:** Glucose diagnostic biochemistry | **Exam focus:** NORCET-style original

- **A.** Renal glucose excretion inside the tube
- **B.** Continued cellular glycolysis
- **C.** Conversion of plasma albumin to glucose
- **D.** Insulin secretion by the collected erythrocytes

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: B. Continued cellular glycolysis**

- **A.** Kidney body mein glucose filter karti hai. Collection tube mein glomerular filtration nahi hoti.
- **B.** Sample ke cells collection ke baad bhi glucose consume kar sakte hain. Plasma promptly separate nahi hua toh measured glucose actual collection-time value se kam ho sakta hai.
- **C.** Albumin protein hai aur sample mein spontaneous gluconeogenesis nahi karta. Glucose consumption relevant mechanism hai.
- **D.** RBC pancreatic beta cells nahi hain. Unka glucose use insulin secretion ke bina bhi continue ho sakta hai.

</details>

### Q2. A glucose specimen is collected in a sodium fluoride tube and the laboratory warns that the first-hour glucose may still fall. Why?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Priority:** High | **Topic:** Glucose diagnostic biochemistry | **Exam focus:** NORCET-style original

- **A.** Fluoride immediately destroys all glucose molecules
- **B.** Fluoride stimulates insulin release from plasma
- **C.** Fluoride converts glucose directly into hemoglobin
- **D.** Fluoride inhibits enolase but does not immediately stop all upstream glycolysis

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: D. Fluoride inhibits enolase but does not immediately stop all upstream glycolysis**

- **A.** Fluoride preservative ka purpose glucose loss reduce karna hai, glucose destroy karna nahi.
- **B.** Separated blood mein pancreatic insulin secretion nahi hoti. Yeh preservative enzyme inhibition se related hai.
- **C.** Hemoglobin protein synthesis erythroid cells mein hoti hai. Tube fluoride aisa conversion nahi karta.
- **D.** Fluoride glycolysis ke late enzyme enolase ko inhibit karta hai. Initial delay mein glucose consumption continue ho sakti hai, isliye validated rapid processing important hai.

</details>

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>
