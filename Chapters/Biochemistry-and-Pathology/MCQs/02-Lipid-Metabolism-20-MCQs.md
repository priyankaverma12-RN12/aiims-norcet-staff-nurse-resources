- **D.** Even-chain saturated fatty acyl-CoA ke liye cycles ka formula carbon count divided by two minus one hai. Palmitoyl-CoA ke liye 16/2 minus 1, yani saat cycles hote hain aur aath acetyl-CoA bante hain.
