### Q5. What is the major biochemical role of the carnitine shuttle in mitochondrial lipid oxidation?

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Priority:** High | **Topic:** Lipid metabolism | **Exam focus:** NORCET-style original

- **A.** Transport of long-chain fatty acyl groups into the mitochondrial matrix
- **B.** Transport of glucose into the nucleus
- **C.** Export of ketone bodies from red cells
- **D.** Hydrolysis of dietary triglycerides in the intestine

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: A. Transport of long-chain fatty acyl groups into the mitochondrial matrix**

- **A.** Long-chain fatty acyl-CoA inner mitochondrial membrane directly cross nahi kar pata, carnitine shuttle group ko matrix tak pahunchata hai.
- **B.** Glucose transport carnitine ka function nahi hai.
- **C.** RBC mein mitochondria aur ketogenesis nahi hoti.
- **D.** Dietary fat hydrolysis mainly pancreatic lipase karta hai.

</details>

### Q8. How many beta-oxidation cycles are required to completely cleave palmitoyl-CoA, a 16-carbon saturated fatty acyl-CoA?

- **B.** 16 cycles
- **C.** 4 cycles
- **D.** 7 cycles

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

</details>

### Q20. A child with a fatty acid oxidation disorder develops hypoglycemia with unexpectedly low ketones during fasting. Which mechanism best explains this pattern?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Priority:** High | **Topic:** Lipid metabolism | **Exam focus:** NORCET-style original

- **A.** Impaired generation of acetyl-CoA from fatty acids limits ketogenesis
- **B.** Excessive ketogenesis consumes all circulating glucose
- **C.** Increased glycogen storage directly produces ketones
- **D.** Increased hepatic beta-oxidation suppresses all ketone production

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: A. Impaired generation of acetyl-CoA from fatty acids limits ketogenesis**

- **A.** Fatty acid oxidation fail ho toh liver ko ketogenesis ke liye acetyl-CoA aur fasting energy support kam milte hain, isliye hypoketotic hypoglycemia ho sakta hai.
- **B.** Ketones kam hain, excessive production is finding ko explain nahi karta.
- **C.** Glycogen storage ketones directly nahi banata.
- **D.** Beta-oxidation fasting ketogenesis ko substrate deta hai.

</details>
