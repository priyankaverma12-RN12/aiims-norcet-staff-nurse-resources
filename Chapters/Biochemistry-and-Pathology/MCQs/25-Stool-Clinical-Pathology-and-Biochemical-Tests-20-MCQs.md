### Q15. A stool electrolyte report gives sodium 40 mmol/L and potassium 30 mmol/L. Using the stated estimated osmotic gap formula 290 − 2(Na + K), what is the result?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Priority:** High | **Topic:** Stool clinical pathology and biochemical tests | **Exam focus:** NORCET-style original

- **A.** 150 mOsm/kg
- **B.** 220 mOsm/kg
- **C.** 290 mOsm/kg
- **D.** 70 mOsm/kg

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: A. 150 mOsm/kg**

- **A.** Na aur K add karo, 40 plus 30 = 70. Twice 70 = 140, aur 290 minus 140 = 150 mOsm/kg hai.
- **B.** 220 tab aayega jab sum ko double nahi karoge. Cations ke counterions ko estimate karne ke liye 2 factor diya hai.
- **C.** 290 assumed total osmolality hai. Gap ke liye measured electrolytes ka contribution subtract karte hain.
- **D.** 70 sirf sodium aur potassium ka sum hai. Formula mein doubling aur subtraction bhi hai.

**Exam clue:** 290 se twice sodium plus potassium subtract karo.

**Clinical reference:** [Laboratory method and interpretation](https://hpn.hms.harvard.edu/fluid-electrolyte-disorder)
