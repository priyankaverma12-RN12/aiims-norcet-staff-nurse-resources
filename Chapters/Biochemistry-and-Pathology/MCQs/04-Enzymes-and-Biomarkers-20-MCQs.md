### Q1. What is the main effect of an enzyme on the activation energy of a reaction?

**Difficulty:** Easy | **Priority:** High | **Topic:** Enzymes and biomarkers | **Exam focus:** NORCET-style original

- **A.** It lowers the activation energy
- **B.** It raises the energy difference between products and reactants
- **C.** It changes the equilibrium constant
- **D.** It is consumed stoichiometrically with the substrate

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: A. It lowers the activation energy**

- **A.** Reaction shuru hone ke liye molecules ko ek energy barrier cross karna padta hai. Enzyme transition state ko stabilize karke is barrier ko kam karta hai, isliye reaction zyada jaldi hoti hai.
- **B.** Enzyme reactants aur products ki total free energy ka difference nahi badalta. Woh sirf reaction tak pahunchne ka energy barrier kam karta hai.
- **C.** Equilibrium constant thermodynamics par depend karta hai. Enzyme forward aur reverse reactions ko speed up kar sakta hai, par final equilibrium position nahi badalta.
- **D.** Catalyst substrate ki tarah har reaction mein permanently consume nahi hota. Product release hone ke baad enzyme dobara catalytic cycle mein kaam kar sakta hai.

**Exam clue:** Enzyme activation energy kam karta hai, overall reaction free energy nahi badalta.

### Q2. In a simple Michaelis-Menten reaction, what does Km represent?

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Priority:** High | **Topic:** Enzymes and biomarkers | **Exam focus:** NORCET-style original

**Correct: C. Substrate concentration at half of Vmax**

- **D.** Equilibrium par product concentration thermodynamic properties se relate karti hai. Km initial-rate kinetics ka parameter hai, equilibrium concentration nahi.

**Exam clue:** Km substrate concentration hoti hai jab rate half Vmax ho.

### Q3. An inhibitor raises apparent Km while leaving Vmax unchanged. Which idealized inhibition pattern fits?

- **D.** Agar active enzyme molecules irreversibly destroy ho jaayein, toh catalytic capacity kam hoti hai. Vmax unchanged rehna is explanation ke against jaata hai.

**Exam clue:** Competitive inhibition mein apparent Km badhta hai aur Vmax unchanged rehta hai.

### Q20. Which laboratory marker is a structural cardiac protein rather than an enzyme?

**Difficulty:** Easy | **Priority:** High | **Topic:** Enzymes and biomarkers | **Exam focus:** NORCET-style original

- **A.** Creatine kinase
- **B.** Lactate dehydrogenase
- **C.** Cardiac troponin
- **D.** Alanine aminotransferase

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: C. Cardiac troponin**

- **A.** Creatine kinase phosphoryl group transfer catalyze karta hai. Yeh catalytic enzyme hai, contractile regulatory structural protein nahi hai.
- **B.** LDH lactate aur pyruvate ke beech oxidation-reduction reaction catalyze karta hai. Isliye LDH enzyme activity marker hai.
- **C.** Cardiac troponin muscle contraction regulate karne wale protein complex ka part hai. Injury ke baad circulating troponin concentration measure hoti hai, enzyme catalytic activity nahi.
- **D.** ALT amino group transfer reaction catalyze karta hai. Iska blood activity result enzyme measurement hai, cardiac structural protein measurement nahi.

**Exam clue:** Troponin regulatory protein hai, catalytic enzyme nahi.
