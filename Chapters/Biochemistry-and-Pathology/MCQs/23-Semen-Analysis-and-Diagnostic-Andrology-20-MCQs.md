### Q1. A specimen is collected for semen analysis after one day of ejaculatory abstinence, but the laboratory uses the WHO 2021 standard collection interval. Which preanalytical condition should be recorded?

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Priority:** High | **Topic:** Semen analysis and diagnostic andrology | **Exam focus:** NORCET-style original

- **A.** The interval is within the standard range
- **B.** The abstinence interval is shorter than the recommended 2–7 days
- **C.** The interval is longer than the recommended range
- **D.** Abstinence affects only semen pH

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: B. The abstinence interval is shorter than the recommended 2–7 days**

- **A.** Ek din standard minimum se kam hai, isliye sample ko routine interval ke andar assume nahi karna chahiye.

- **C.** Ek din upper limit se zyada nahi hai. Zyada interval ka concern tab hoga jab saat din se aage ho.
- **D.** Abstinence volume, total sperm number aur kuch other parameters ko affect kar sakta hai. Iska effect sirf pH tak limited nahi hai.
