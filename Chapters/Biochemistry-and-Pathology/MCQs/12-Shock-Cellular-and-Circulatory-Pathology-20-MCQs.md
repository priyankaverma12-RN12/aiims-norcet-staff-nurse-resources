### Q1. A patient has a cardiac output of 4 L/min and arterial oxygen content of 15 mL/dL. What is the approximate systemic oxygen delivery under this simplified model?

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Priority:** High | **Topic:** Shock pathology | **Exam focus:** NORCET-style original

- **A.** 60 mL/min
- **B.** 1500 mL/min
- **C.** 600 mL/min
- **D.** 6000 mL/min

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: C. 600 mL/min**

- **A.** Yeh answer L se dL conversion omit karta hai. Units match karke calculation karna zaroori hai.
- **B.** Yeh given cardiac output aur oxygen content ka product nahi hai. Correct conversion ke baad 600 milta hai.
- **C.** Cardiac output ko dL/min mein convert karne par 40 dL/min milta hai. Isko 15 mL/dL se multiply karne par oxygen delivery 600 mL/min hoti hai.
- **D.** Yahan volume conversion ten times zyada apply ho gaya hai. 4 L ka matlab 40 dL hai.

</details>

### Q2. Two patients have the same arterial oxygen saturation and cardiac output but one has profound anemia. Which oxygen transport variable is most directly reduced in the anemic patient?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Priority:** High | **Topic:** Shock pathology | **Exam focus:** NORCET-style original

- **A.** Arterial oxygen content
- **B.** Arterial oxygen saturation necessarily falls
- **C.** Inspired oxygen concentration
- **D.** Pulmonary ventilation necessarily stops

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: A. Arterial oxygen content**

- **A.** Most blood oxygen hemoglobin ke saath bound hoti hai. Hemoglobin kam hone par same saturation ke bawajood total oxygen content reduce ho sakta hai.
- **B.** Saturation hemoglobin binding sites ka proportion batati hai, total hemoglobin amount nahi. Normal saturation anemia ko exclude nahi karti.
- **C.** Anemia ambient ya administered oxygen fraction directly determine nahi karti.
- **D.** Anemia oxygen carrying capacity reduce karti hai, spontaneous ventilation necessarily stop nahi hoti.

</details>

### Q3. A patient in circulatory shock has preserved arterial oxygen content but markedly reduced cardiac output. Which component of systemic oxygen delivery is impaired?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Priority:** High | **Topic:** Shock pathology | **Exam focus:** NORCET-style original

- **A.** Oxygen binding to every hemoglobin molecule is absent
- **B.** Blood flow carrying oxygen to tissues per minute
- **C.** Atmospheric oxygen percentage is reduced
- **D.** Hepatic conjugation of bilirubin is the sole defect

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: B. Blood flow carrying oxygen to tissues per minute**

- **A.** Normal arterial content se absent binding assume nahi kar sakte. Yahan given defect flow ka hai.
- **B.** Oxygen content normal ho sakta hai par kam flow se tissues tak per minute oxygen delivery kam ho jati hai. Shock mein delivery aur utilization dono assess hote hain.
- **C.** Cardiac output atmospheric oxygen concentration change nahi karta.
- **D.** Bilirubin metabolism tissue oxygen transport ka immediate flow component nahi hai.

</details>

### Q4. A tissue bed initially maintains its oxygen consumption despite a moderate decrease in oxygen delivery. Which compensation helps explain this?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Priority:** High | **Topic:** Shock pathology | **Exam focus:** NORCET-style original

- **A.** Complete cessation of cellular respiration
- **B.** Immediate replacement of hemoglobin with albumin
- **C.** Conversion of arterial blood into lymph before tissue entry
- **D.** Increased fractional extraction of oxygen from delivered blood

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: D. Increased fractional extraction of oxygen from delivered blood**

- **A.** Respiration stop ho toh oxygen consumption maintain nahi hogi. Compensation increased extraction se possible hai.
- **B.** Albumin hemoglobin ki oxygen carrying role replace nahi karta. Extraction delivered hemoglobin-bound oxygen ko use karti hai.
- **C.** Arterial supply capillary bed se gas exchange karti hai. Lymph conversion compensatory oxygen mechanism nahi hai.
- **D.** Tissues available blood se proportionately more oxygen extract kar sakte hain. Extraction reserve limited hoti hai aur severe delivery fall par consumption compromise ho sakti hai.

</details>

### Q5. During severe shock, oxygen delivery falls below the level at which extraction can compensate. What happens to aerobic oxygen consumption in affected tissues?

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Priority:** High | **Topic:** Shock pathology | **Exam focus:** NORCET-style original

- **A.** It rises indefinitely regardless of oxygen supply
- **B.** It becomes limited by oxygen supply
- **C.** It becomes independent of mitochondrial function
- **D.** It is guaranteed to remain normal in every organ

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: B. It becomes limited by oxygen supply**

- **A.** Aerobic metabolism oxygen availability par depend karta hai. Unlimited compensation possible nahi hai.
- **B.** Extraction maximum reserve ke paas pahunchne ke baad further supply reduction aerobic metabolism limit kar sakti hai. Cellular injury ka risk badhta hai.
- **C.** Aerobic oxygen utilization mitochondria se linked hai. Shock is dependence ko remove nahi karta.
- **D.** Organ-specific perfusion aur reserve vary karte hain. Severe shock mein normal consumption guarantee nahi.

</details>

### Q6. A critically ill patient has high central venous oxygen saturation despite signs of tissue hypoperfusion. What mechanism can explain this apparently paradoxical finding in distributive shock?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Priority:** High | **Topic:** Shock pathology | **Exam focus:** NORCET-style original

- **A.** Every tissue is necessarily receiving sufficient usable oxygen
- **B.** The arterial oxygen saturation must be zero
- **C.** Maldistributed microvascular flow or impaired tissue oxygen extraction
- **D.** The patient has no circulating blood

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: C. Maldistributed microvascular flow or impaired tissue oxygen extraction**

- **A.** Global venous measurement local perfusion heterogeneity hide kar sakti hai. Clinical organ dysfunction ko ignore nahi karna chahiye.
- **B.** High venous saturation se zero arterial saturation infer nahi hoti. Dono measurements different compartments represent karte hain.
- **C.** Sepsis jaise states mein blood kuch regions se effectively exchange kiye bina pass ho sakta hai aur extraction impaired ho sakti hai. High venous saturation adequate cellular oxygen use prove nahi karti.
- **D.** Venous saturation blood sample ka measurement hai. It does not indicate absence of circulating volume.

</details>

### Q7. A patient receives epinephrine and develops a lactate rise while other perfusion indicators improve. Which biochemical interpretation should be considered?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Priority:** High | **Topic:** Shock pathology | **Exam focus:** NORCET-style original

- **A.** Adrenergic stimulation can increase glycolytic lactate production even without global anaerobic metabolism
- **B.** Lactate can be formed only if all tissue oxygen is absent
- **C.** Epinephrine directly converts lactate to bilirubin
- **D.** Improved perfusion indicators are always invalid when lactate rises

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: A. Adrenergic stimulation can increase glycolytic lactate production even without global anaerobic metabolism**

- **A.** Beta-adrenergic stimulation glycolytic flux badha sakti hai. Lactate trend ko perfusion, drug exposure aur clearance ke context mein interpret karna chahiye.
- **B.** Lactate aerobic conditions mein bhi produce ho sakta hai. Universal anaerobic assumption inaccurate hai.
- **C.** Lactate aur bilirubin distinct metabolic pathways hain. Epinephrine direct conversion enzyme nahi hai.
- **D.** Discordant findings ko integrate karna chahiye. Ek marker baaki clinical findings automatically invalidate nahi karta.

</details>

</details>

### Q9. A septic patient has some perfused capillaries adjacent to poorly perfused capillaries despite apparently adequate global cardiac output. What does this illustrate?

- **B.** Microvascular dysfunction endothelial loss universal hona require nahi karti. Functional flow changes bhi possible hain.
- **C.** Epithelial regeneration repair process hai. Capillary flow heterogeneity circulatory dysfunction se related hai.
- **D.** Different capillary beds mein flow uneven ho sakta hai. Global output adequate dikhne ke bawajood local oxygen exchange impaired reh sakta hai.

</details>

### Q10. Endothelial barrier dysfunction during systemic inflammation permits plasma proteins and water to enter interstitial tissues. What hemodynamic consequence may result?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Priority:** High | **Topic:** Shock pathology | **Exam focus:** NORCET-style original

- **A.** A guaranteed increase in circulating plasma volume
- **B.** Reduced effective intravascular volume despite increased interstitial fluid
- **C.** Elimination of all tissue edema
- **D.** A permanent increase in erythrocyte production within minutes

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

- **B.** Fluid vascular compartment se tissue mein shift ho sakta hai. Isliye swelling ke bawajood effective circulation compromise ho sakti hai.
- **C.** Protein aur water leakage edema promote kar sakti hai. Yeh edema elimination ka mechanism nahi.
- **D.** RBC production marrow mein time leti hai. Immediate leakage ka effect fluid distribution par hai.

</details>

### Q11. A patient has shock with a very low systemic vascular resistance but a preserved or elevated cardiac output. Which hemodynamic abnormality best accounts for low arterial pressure in this pattern?

- **A.** Loss of vascular tone despite maintained flow
- **B.** Complete absence of cardiac contraction
- **C.** Excessive systemic resistance as the only defect
- **D.** Increased plasma protein synthesis alone

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: A. Loss of vascular tone despite maintained flow**

- **A.** Arterial pressure flow aur vascular resistance ke relationship se influenced hoti hai. Resistance markedly reduce hone par maintained output ke bawajood pressure low ho sakta hai.
- **B.** Preserved output se complete contraction absence supported nahi hai. Yahan major abnormality vascular tone hai.
- **C.** Given resistance low hai. High resistance low-pressure pattern ko explain nahi karti.
- **D.** Protein synthesis pressure distribution influence kar sakti hai par described low resistance ka direct explanation nahi.

</details>

### Q12. A patient loses intravascular volume rapidly. Which change in the Frank-Starling relationship can initially reduce stroke volume if other factors remain comparable?

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: C. Reduced ventricular filling and end-diastolic fiber stretch**

- **D.** Frank-Starling mechanism filling aur stroke volume ke relation ko describe karta hai. Independence is concept ke opposite hai.

</details>
