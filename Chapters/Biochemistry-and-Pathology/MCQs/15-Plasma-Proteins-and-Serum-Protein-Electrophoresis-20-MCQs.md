## Part 1

### Q1. In conventional alkaline serum protein electrophoresis, which broad protein fraction generally migrates farthest toward the anode?

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Priority:** High | **Topic:** Plasma proteins and serum protein electrophoresis | **Exam focus:** NORCET-style original

- **A.** Gamma globulins
- **B.** Alpha-2 globulins
- **C.** Albumin
- **D.** Beta globulins

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: C. Albumin**

- **A.** Gamma fraction mein immunoglobulins ki mobility comparatively low aur heterogeneous hoti hai. Yeh farthest anodal major fraction nahi hai.
- **B.** Alpha-2 proteins albumin ke baad separate region mein migrate karte hain. Albumin generally faster move karta hai.
- **C.** Alkaline pH par albumin strong net negative charge aur high electrophoretic mobility rakhta hai. Isliye conventional pattern mein albumin band anodal side par hoti hai.
- **D.** Beta fraction ki mobility albumin se lower hoti hai. Conventional alkaline pattern mein beta region intermediate position par hota hai.

</details>

### Q2. Why is serum generally preferred over plasma for routine serum protein electrophoresis?

- **A.** Plasma fibrinogen may form an additional band that complicates interpretation
- **B.** Plasma contains no albumin
- **C.** Serum contains all intact circulating red cells
- **D.** Serum eliminates every possible analytical interference

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: A. Plasma fibrinogen may form an additional band that complicates interpretation**

- **A.** Serum clotting ke baad fibrinogen largely remove ho jata hai. Plasma mein fibrinogen beta-gamma region mein band create karke abnormal protein ki tarah appear ho sakta hai.
- **B.** Albumin serum aur plasma dono mein hota hai. Difference clotting proteins ka bhi hota hai.
- **C.** Serum liquid portion hai jo clot separation ke baad milta hai. Intact RBCs desired component nahi hain.
- **D.** Serum selection fibrinogen interference reduce karti hai, lekin other interference possibilities remain karti hain.

</details>

</details>

</details>

### Q18. A patient has a low serum albumin result during an acute systemic inflammatory illness. Why is this result alone inadequate to diagnose protein-energy malnutrition?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Priority:** High | **Topic:** Plasma proteins and serum protein electrophoresis | **Exam focus:** NORCET-style original

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

</details>

### Q19. A patient with impaired albumin synthesis receives a medication that is highly albumin-bound. Which pharmacokinetic consequence warrants clinician-directed interpretation?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Priority:** High | **Topic:** Plasma proteins and serum protein electrophoresis | **Exam focus:** NORCET-style original

- **A.** All drug molecules become permanently inactive
- **B.** The unbound fraction of the drug may increase, although total and free concentrations depend on clearance and other factors
- **C.** Drug concentration must always double exactly
- **D.** Serum albumin has no relationship to protein-bound drugs

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: B. The unbound fraction of the drug may increase, although total and free concentrations depend on clearance and other factors**

- **A.** Protein binding reversible ho sakti hai. Albumin reduction all drug molecules inactive nahi banati.
- **B.** Albumin binding sites kam hone par free fraction increase ho sakta hai. Clinical effect drug-specific clearance aur free exposure par depend karta hai, so dose independently change nahi karni chahiye.
- **C.** Magnitude drug binding, distribution aur clearance par depend karta hai. Fixed doubling rule valid nahi hai.
- **D.** Albumin many medications bind karta hai. Altered binding relevant pharmacokinetic issue ho sakti hai.

</details>

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>
