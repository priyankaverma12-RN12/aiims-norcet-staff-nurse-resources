## Part 1

### Q1. A standard lipid profile reports total cholesterol 212 mg/dL and HDL cholesterol 48 mg/dL. Which value represents non-HDL cholesterol?

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Priority:** High | **Topic:** Lipid profile and cholesterol derivatives | **Exam focus:** NORCET-style original

- **A.** 260 mg/dL
- **B.** 48 mg/dL
- **C.** 164 mg/dL
- **D.** 4.42 mg/dL

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: C. 164 mg/dL**

- **A.** Yeh dono values add karne se milta hai. HDL fraction ko remove karna hai, add nahi.
- **B.** Yeh HDL-C itself hai. Non-HDL-C mein LDL aur other atherogenic fractions ka cholesterol include hota hai.
- **C.** Non-HDL-C total cholesterol minus HDL-C hai. 212 se 48 subtract karne par 164 milta hai, jo atherogenic lipoproteins mein carried cholesterol estimate karta hai.
- **D.** Yeh total cholesterol to HDL ka approximate ratio hai, non-HDL concentration nahi.

</details>

### Q2. A laboratory exercise specifically requires the historical Friedewald equation. Total cholesterol is 200 mg/dL, HDL-C 50 mg/dL and triglycerides 150 mg/dL. What is the estimated LDL-C under its stated assumptions?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Priority:** High | **Topic:** Lipid profile and cholesterol derivatives | **Exam focus:** NORCET-style original

- **A.** 120 mg/dL
- **B.** 150 mg/dL
- **C.** 30 mg/dL
- **D.** 180 mg/dL

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: A. 120 mg/dL**

- **A.** Historical formula TC minus HDL-C minus TG/5 hai when units mg/dL hain. 200 minus 50 minus 30 equals 120. Current guideline newer estimation methods prefer karti hai.
- **B.** Yeh non-HDL-C hai. Friedewald estimate mein VLDL-C ka approximate 30 mg/dL bhi subtract karte hain.
- **C.** Yeh TG/5 estimate hai, LDL-C nahi.
- **D.** Yeh VLDL estimate add karne se milta hai. Historical equation mein subtract karte hain.

</details>

### Q4. Two patients have similar LDL-C concentrations but one has substantially higher apolipoprotein B. Which explanation accounts for this discordance?

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Priority:** High | **Topic:** Lipid profile and cholesterol derivatives | **Exam focus:** NORCET-style original

- **A.** ApoB measures exclusively the cholesterol carried by HDL
- **B.** LDL-C directly counts every LDL particle
- **C.** ApoB is simply another reporting unit for triglycerides
- **D.** The cholesterol content per atherogenic particle can differ between patients

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: D. The cholesterol content per atherogenic particle can differ between patients**

- **A.** HDL principally apoA-I-associated hai. ApoB LDL aur other atherogenic particles ke saath related hai.
- **B.** LDL-C cholesterol mass concentration measure karta hai, particle count nahi. Composition variation discordance create kar sakti hai.
- **C.** ApoB structural protein analyte hai. TG lipid concentration se directly unit conversion nahi hota.
- **D.** ApoB-containing particles mein generally one apoB molecule hota hai. Same cholesterol mass more cholesterol-poor particles mein distribute ho sakta hai, so apoB particle burden ka additional clue deta hai.

</details>
