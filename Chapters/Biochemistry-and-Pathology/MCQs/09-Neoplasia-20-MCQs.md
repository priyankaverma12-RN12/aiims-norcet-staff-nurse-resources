### Q1. A salivary gland tumor contains ductal and myoepithelial components in a chondromyxoid matrix. Which concept explains the term mixed tumor in this context?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Priority:** High | **Topic:** Neoplasia | **Exam focus:** NORCET-style original

- **A.** Two distant metastases are necessarily present
- **B.** Divergent differentiation within a single neoplastic process
- **C.** Mixed bacterial infection
- **D.** Mandatory contamination by another patient

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: B. Divergent differentiation within a single neoplastic process**

- **A.** Different components ek lesion mein milne se distant spread establish nahi hota. Metastasis ko separate evidence chahiye.
- **B.** Ek neoplasm multiple differentiation patterns dikha sakta hai. Pleomorphic adenoma mein epithelial aur myoepithelial elements characteristic matrix ke saath milte hain.
- **C.** Neoplastic differentiation microbial culture result nahi hai. Mixed appearance ka matlab bacterial mixture nahi hota.
- **D.** Mixed components recognized tumor pattern ho sakte hain. Contamination ke liye independent evidence chahiye.

</details>

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

- **C.** Osteoma benign bone-forming lesion hai, malignant osteoid-producing cells ke description se match nahi karta.
- **D.** Fibrosarcoma malignant fibroblastic differentiation dikhata hai, direct malignant osteoid production osteosarcoma ko favor karta hai.

</details>

### Q4. A pathology report describes a well-differentiated malignant epithelial tumor. What does well-differentiated mean in this context?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Priority:** High | **Topic:** Neoplasia | **Exam focus:** NORCET-style original

- **A.** Tumor is necessarily benign
- **B.** Tumor is necessarily confined to the primary organ
- **C.** Tumor cells retain substantial resemblance to their normal tissue of origin
- **D.** Tumor cells are completely identical to normal cells in all behavior

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: C. Tumor cells retain substantial resemblance to their normal tissue of origin**

- **A.** Malignant tumor bhi well differentiated ho sakta hai, differentiation alone benign status prove nahi karti.
- **B.** Local aur distant extent staging se assess hota hai, differentiation se stage automatically nahi pata chalta.
- **C.** Differentiation batati hai ki tumor ka appearance aur specialized features normal tissue se kitne milte hain. Well-differentiated malignancy phir bhi invade ya spread kar sakti hai.
- **D.** Morphological similarity uncontrolled growth aur malignant behavior ko exclude nahi karti.

</details>

</details>
