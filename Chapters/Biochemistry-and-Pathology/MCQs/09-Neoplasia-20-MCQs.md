### Q1. A colon tumor forms malignant gland-like structures on microscopy. Which broad histogenetic category best fits this morphology?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Priority:** High | **Topic:** Neoplasia | **Exam focus:** NORCET-style original

- **A.** Lipoma
- **B.** Adenocarcinoma
- **C.** Leiomyosarcoma
- **D.** Lymphoma

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: B. Adenocarcinoma**

- **A.** Lipoma mature fat cells ka benign tumor hai, malignant glandular architecture nahi banata.
- **B.** Adenocarcinoma epithelial malignancy hai jisme glandular differentiation ya mucin production mil sakti hai. Colon ke gland-forming malignant tumor mein yeh category fit karti hai.
- **C.** Leiomyosarcoma smooth muscle malignancy hai, epithelial glands iski defining feature nahi hain.
- **D.** Lymphoma lymphoid cells ka neoplasm hai, malignant gland formation iska usual pattern nahi hai.

</details>

</details>

### Q4. A pathology report describes a well-differentiated malignant epithelial tumor. What does well-differentiated mean in this context?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Priority:** High | **Topic:** Neoplasia | **Exam focus:** NORCET-style original

- **A.** Tumor is necessarily benign
- **B.** Tumor is necessarily confined to the primary organ
- **C.** Tumor cells retain substantial resemblance to their normal tissue of origin
- **D.** Tumor cells are completely identical to normal cells in all behavior

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: C. Tumor cells retain substantial resemblance to their normal tissue of origin**

- **A.** Malignant tumor bhi well differentiated ho sakta hai, differentiation alone benign status prove nahi karti.
- **B.** Local aur distant extent staging se assess hota hai, differentiation se stage automatically nahi pata chalta.
- **C.** Differentiation batati hai ki tumor ka appearance aur specialized features normal tissue se kitne milte hain. Well-differentiated malignancy phir bhi invade ya spread kar sakti hai.
- **D.** Morphological similarity uncontrolled growth aur malignant behavior ko exclude nahi karti.

</details>

</details>

- **C.** A marker result alone establishes anatomical TNM stage

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: D. The marker may support investigation but cannot establish malignancy by itself**

- **D.** Tumor marker benign conditions mein bhi increase ho sakta hai aur kuch cancers mein normal reh sakta hai. Isliye diagnosis clinical context aur appropriate investigations se establish hota hai.

</details>
