## Part 1

### Q1. Two samples have pH values of 7.4 and 7.1. Approximately how does the hydrogen ion concentration of the second compare with the first?

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Priority:** High | **Topic:** Acid-base diagnostic biochemistry | **Exam focus:** NORCET-style original

- **A.** It is 0.3 times as high
- **B.** It is twice as high
- **C.** It is ten times as high
- **D.** It is half as high

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: B. It is twice as high**

- **A.** pH difference ko directly concentration multiplier nahi bana sakte. Logarithmic relationship use karna hota hai.
- **B.** pH negative logarithmic scale hai. 0.3 unit fall ka concentration ratio 10 to the power 0.3, approximately 2 hota hai. Small pH change bhi significant hydrogen ion change represent kar sakta hai.
- **C.** Tenfold increase ke liye approximately one whole pH unit fall chahiye. Yahan difference 0.3 hai.
- **D.** Lower pH higher hydrogen ion concentration indicate karta hai. Half concentration se pH rise hota, fall nahi.

</details>

### Q2. Using pKa 6.1 in the simplified bicarbonate buffer equation, which bicarbonate-to-dissolved-CO2 ratio corresponds approximately to pH 7.4?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Priority:** High | **Topic:** Acid-base diagnostic biochemistry | **Exam focus:** NORCET-style original

- **A.** 1:1
- **B.** 2:1
- **C.** 40:1
- **D.** 20:1

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: D. 20:1**

- **A.** Equal concentrations mein logarithm zero hota hai. pH pKa 6.1 ke near hota, 7.4 nahi.
- **B.** Ratio 2 ka logarithm approximately 0.3 hai. Isse pH around 6.4 hoga.
- **C.** Ratio 40 ka logarithm approximately 1.6 hai. Simplified equation pH about 7.7 predict karegi.
- **D.** Henderson-Hasselbalch relation mein pH minus pKa 1.3 hai. Antilog 1.3 approximately 20 hai, so bicarbonate to dissolved CO2 ratio near 20:1 hota hai.

</details>

### Q3. At 37°C the teaching solubility coefficient for carbon dioxide in plasma is 0.03 mmol/L/mmHg. What is the approximate dissolved CO2 concentration at PaCO2 50 mmHg?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Priority:** High | **Topic:** Acid-base diagnostic biochemistry | **Exam focus:** NORCET-style original

- **A.** 1.5 mmol/L
- **B.** 15 mmol/L
- **C.** 50 mmol/L
- **D.** 0.6 mmol/L

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: A. 1.5 mmol/L**

- **A.** Dissolved CO2 partial pressure times solubility coefficient hai. 50 times 0.03 equals 1.5 mmol/L. Yeh total bicarbonate concentration nahi hai.
- **B.** Yeh coefficient ko ten times larger use karne se aata hai. Given coefficient 0.03 hai.
- **C.** 50 pressure in mmHg hai. Directly concentration assume karna units mix karta hai.
- **D.** Yeh stated pressure aur coefficient ka product nahi. 0.03 times 50 calculate karna chahiye.

</details>

### Q4. A chemistry panel measures total carbon dioxide, while a blood gas analyzer reports calculated bicarbonate. Which distinction explains a small difference between the two results?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Priority:** High | **Topic:** Acid-base diagnostic biochemistry | **Exam focus:** NORCET-style original

- **A.** Both tests directly measure precisely the same dissolved molecular species
- **B.** Blood gas bicarbonate is calculated from serum sodium alone
- **C.** Total CO2 includes dissolved CO2 as well as bicarbonate, whereas blood gas bicarbonate is calculated from pH and PCO2
- **D.** Chemistry total CO2 measures only hemoglobin-bound oxygen

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: C. Total CO2 includes dissolved CO2 as well as bicarbonate, whereas blood gas bicarbonate is calculated from pH and PCO2**

- **A.** Methods aur measured quantities exactly identical nahi hain. Isliye close values expected ho sakti hain but exact equality obligatory nahi.
- **B.** Bicarbonate calculation Henderson-Hasselbalch relation use karti hai. Sodium alone sufficient nahi.
- **C.** Chemistry total CO2 mein bicarbonate dominant hai plus dissolved CO2 contribution. Gas analyzer measured pH aur PCO2 se bicarbonate estimate karta hai, so small difference possible hai.
- **D.** Total CO2 carbon dioxide species assess karta hai. Hemoglobin oxygenation separate analyte hai.

</details>

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Priority:** High | **Topic:** Acid-base diagnostic biochemistry | **Exam focus:** NORCET-style original

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: C. It reduces the immediate effect of variable PCO2 when assessing the metabolic component**

- **A.** Standardized bicarbonate hypothetical reference conditions ka parameter hai. Actual ventilation directly measure nahi karta.
- **B.** Standardization respiratory effect reduce karti hai but hemoglobin aur protein buffers remain relevant hain.
- **C.** Standard conditions usually PCO2 40 mmHg aur temperature 37°C use karti hain. Respiratory variation standardize karne se metabolic contribution assess karne mein help milti hai.
- **D.** Actual bicarbonate current pH aur PCO2 par depend karti hai. Standardized value different ho sakti hai.

</details>

### Q7. A blood specimen is exposed to room air for a prolonged interval before blood gas analysis. Which acid-base artifact is particularly plausible from loss of carbon dioxide?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Priority:** High | **Topic:** Acid-base diagnostic biochemistry | **Exam focus:** NORCET-style original

- **B.** No change in pH is possible if oxygen saturation remains stable
- **C.** Immediate increase in true circulating bicarbonate caused by the laboratory delay
- **D.** Lower measured PCO2 with a falsely higher pH

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: D. Lower measured PCO2 with a falsely higher pH**

- **A.** Room air mein CO2 partial pressure blood se much lower hoti hai. Air exchange alone CO2 loss favor karti hai, opposite pattern nahi.
- **B.** Acid-base parameters oxygen saturation se independently gas contamination ke through change ho sakte hain.
- **C.** Sample artifact patient ke circulating physiology ko change nahi karta. Measured value aur true in vivo condition distinguish karni chahiye.
- **D.** CO2 gas atmosphere mein escape kar sakti hai. Carbonic acid component decrease hone par measured pH rise ho sakta hai, so sample handling important hai.

</details>

### Q11. A patient has metabolic alkalosis after remote gastric fluid loss, with no recent diuretic use and urine chloride 8 mmol/L. What does this urine chemistry support in the appropriate clinical context?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Priority:** High | **Topic:** Acid-base diagnostic biochemistry | **Exam focus:** NORCET-style original

- **A.** Definite mineralocorticoid excess regardless of blood pressure and history
- **B.** A chloride-depleted, potentially chloride-responsive pattern
- **C.** Complete failure of the kidney to reabsorb any chloride
- **D.** A measured arterial PCO2 of exactly 8 mmHg

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: B. A chloride-depleted, potentially chloride-responsive pattern**

- **A.** Mineralocorticoid-associated alkalosis often higher urine chloride pattern show kar sakti hai. Single low result definitive endocrine diagnosis nahi.
- **B.** Low urine chloride kidney chloride conserve kar rahi ho sakti hai. Volume and clinical assessment ke saath chloride-responsive alkalosis support hoti hai, fluid prescription individualized hoti hai.
- **C.** Low urine chloride chloride conservation ke compatible hai. Complete reabsorption failure usually opposite finding support karegi.
- **D.** Urine chloride concentration aur arterial carbon dioxide pressure distinct quantities hain. Ek se doosra directly infer nahi hota.

</details>

### Q20. A patient has a low plasma pH while the clinical team is determining whether one or more acidifying processes are present. Which terminology distinguishes the measured state from its mechanism?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Priority:** High | **Topic:** Acid-base diagnostic biochemistry | **Exam focus:** NORCET-style original

- **A.** Acidosis refers only to renal failure and acidemia only to lung failure
- **B.** Acidemia always means bicarbonate alone is decreased
- **C.** Acidemia describes the low pH state, whereas acidosis describes a process tending to lower pH
- **D.** Acidosis and alkalosis cannot coexist in one patient

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: C. Acidemia describes the low pH state, whereas acidosis describes a process tending to lower pH**

- **A.** Both terms organ-specific diagnoses nahi hain. State aur process distinction relevant hai.
- **B.** Low pH respiratory or metabolic mechanisms se ho sakta hai. Bicarbonate alone se state define nahi hoti.
- **C.** Acidemia observed blood pH state hai. Acidosis physiological process hai jo pH lower karne ki direction mein act karta hai, mixed processes simultaneously present ho sakte hain.
- **D.** Different primary acid-base processes simultaneously occur kar sakte hain. Net pH unke combined effects reflect karti hai.

</details>
