### PYQ1. Which management theorist formulated the classical fourteen principles of management?

**Difficulty:** Easy/Medium | **Topic:** Management theory

- **A.** Henri Fayol
- **B.** Frederick Herzberg
- **C.** Abraham Maslow
- **D.** Elton Mayo

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation</summary>

**Correct: A. Henri Fayol**

Henri Fayol ne management ke fourteen principles describe kiye, jaise unity of command aur division of work. Herzberg motivation theory se, Maslow hierarchy of needs se aur Mayo human relations approach se related hain.
