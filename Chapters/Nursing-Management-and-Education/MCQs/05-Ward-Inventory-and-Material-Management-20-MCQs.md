<details><summary>✅ Correct answer and detailed explanation</summary>

**B kyun galat hai:** Clinical urgency VED classification ka basis hai. High clinical importance aur high expenditure same baat nahi hain.

**C kyun galat hai:** Alphabetical arrangement item locate karne mein helpful ho sakta hai. ABC financial prioritization item name se decide nahi hoti.

**D kyun galat hai:** First entry date inventory history batati hai. Annual spending rank ke liye quantity aur cost data chahiye.

### Q2. Item X costs ₹250 per unit and has an annual consumption of 80 units. Item Y costs ₹40 per unit and has an annual consumption of 800 units. Which has the higher annual consumption expenditure?

### Q20. A hospital maintains a separately managed reserve of supplies for major disruptions rather than using the entire reserve for ordinary daily consumption. What is the key management purpose?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD | **Topic:** Ward inventory and material management | **Exam focus:** NORCET / Indian Nursing Officer original practice

- **A.** Avoid recording reserve stock in the inventory system
- **B.** Keep reserve items indefinitely even after expiry
- **C.** Replace routine demand forecasting entirely
- **D.** Preserve contingency stock for exceptional demand or supply interruption, with planned rotation and release criteria

<details><summary>✅ Correct answer and detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: D. Preserve contingency stock for exceptional demand or supply interruption, with planned rotation and release criteria**

**A kyun galat hai:** Reserve stock bhi organization ki inventory hai. Recording aur traceability ke bina emergency mein actual availability unknown ho sakti hai.

**B kyun galat hai:** Expired stock emergency readiness provide nahi karta. Reserve inventory ko bhi shelf life aur rotation ke according manage karna chahiye.

**C kyun galat hai:** Routine forecasting daily demand plan karta hai. Contingency stock forecasting ka substitute nahi, balki additional preparedness measure hai.

**D kyun sahi hai:** Contingency stock unusual emergency demand ya supply interruption handle karne ke liye reserve rakha jaata hai. Rotation, expiry review aur release criteria se stock usable aur available rehta hai.
