### Q1. Two wards each have 24 occupied beds, but one has eight patients requiring frequent neurological observations and assisted mobility. What should guide the allocation of additional nurses?

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD | **Topic:** Staffing and delegation | **Exam focus:** NORCET / Indian Nursing Officer original practice

- **B.** Patient acuity and required nursing workload
- **C.** The age of the ward building
- **D.** The order in which wards request staff

<details><summary>✅ Correct answer and detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: B. Patient acuity and required nursing workload**

**A kyun galat hai:** Equal bed count se care complexity ka pata nahi chalta. Isliye sirf census use karne se high-workload ward understaffed reh sakta hai.

**B kyun sahi hai:** Dono wards mein patient count same hai, lekin required care ka amount alag hai. Acuity aur workload measure karne se staff actual care needs ke according allocate hota hai.

**C kyun galat hai:** Building ki age nursing workload ka direct measure nahi hai. Patient dependency aur required observations zyada relevant hain.

**D kyun galat hai:** Pehle request aane se us ward ki need zyada prove nahi hoti. Allocation clinical demand par based honi chahiye.

**D kyun galat hai:** 144 total nursing care hours hai. Isko patient-days se divide kiye bina per-patient measure nahi milega.

### Q20. A nurse delegates several permitted tasks but remains so occupied that no appropriate follow-up can be provided. Which management action is most suitable before adding further delegated tasks?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD | **Topic:** Staffing and delegation | **Exam focus:** NORCET / Indian Nursing Officer original practice

- **A.** Delegate additional tasks because supervision is optional
- **B.** Ask workers to evaluate all clinical outcomes independently
- **C.** Document that delegation eliminates the nurse's responsibility
- **D.** Reorganize the workload or arrange authorized supervision before further delegation

<details><summary>✅ Correct answer and detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: D. Reorganize the workload or arrange authorized supervision before further delegation**

**A kyun galat hai:** Supervision optional maan lene se delegation ka safety gate remove ho jaata hai. Staff workload badhne par bhi oversight zaroori hai.

**B kyun galat hai:** Worker permitted observations report kar sakta hai, lekin independent clinical evaluation authorized professional ka kaam ho sakta hai. Scope ko respect karna chahiye.

**C kyun galat hai:** Delegation se nurse ki own decision aur supervision accountability disappear nahi hoti. Documentation mein actual arrangement reflect hona chahiye.

**D kyun sahi hai:** Safe delegation mein follow-up aur required supervision available honi chahiye. Workload reorganize karke nurse ensure kar sakti hai ki patient outcomes check hon.

**Reference:** [Open RN — Delegation and supervision](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/NBK610432/) · [OpenStax — Nursing standards of delegation](https://openstax.org/books/fundamentals-nursing/pages/15-4-nursing-standards-of-delegation)
