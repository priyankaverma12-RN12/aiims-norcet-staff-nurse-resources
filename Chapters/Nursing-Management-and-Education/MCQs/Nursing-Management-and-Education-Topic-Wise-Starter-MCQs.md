## Delegation

### Q1. A nurse is delegating care to a trained assistive worker. Which task is generally suitable if the patient is stable and local scope permits?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Topic:** Delegation

- **A.** Assist with routine hygiene and report observations
- **B.** Independently formulate the nursing diagnosis
- **C.** Independently change a high-alert medication order
- **D.** Perform the initial comprehensive clinical assessment

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: A. Assist with routine hygiene and report observations**

Stable patient ki routine hygiene trained worker ko delegate ho sakti hai. Registered nurse assessment, judgment aur supervision ki responsibility rakhti hai. Local scope follow karna zaroori hai.

**Baaki options kyun galat hain?**

- **B.** Nursing diagnosis professional judgment hai.
- **C.** Medication order independently change nahi kar sakte.
- **D.** Initial comprehensive assessment RN ki responsibility hoti hai.

</details>

## Quality improvement

### Q2. A ward has repeated medication near-misses during shift changes. Which approach best supports quality improvement?

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Topic:** Quality improvement

- **A.** Hide near-miss reports to protect statistics
- **B.** Blame one worker without reviewing the system
- **C.** Stop reporting events that did not cause harm
- **D.** Analyze the handover process and test system-level improvements

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: D. Analyze the handover process and test system-level improvements**

Near-miss mein harm nahi hua par risk tha. Process analyze karke handover aur verification improve ki ja sakti hai. Reporting se future errors prevent karne mein help milti hai.

**Baaki options kyun galat hain?**

- **A.** Reports hide karne se learning miss hoti hai.
- **B.** Sirf blame karna system problem solve nahi karta.
- **C.** Near-miss reporting safety ke liye useful hai.

</details>

## Patient education

### Q3. After teaching wound care, how should the nurse best check the patient's understanding?

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Topic:** Patient education

- **A.** Provide a complex booklet without discussion
- **B.** Assume education succeeded because it was documented
- **C.** Ask the patient to explain and demonstrate the steps
- **D.** Ask only whether the patient understood

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: C. Ask the patient to explain and demonstrate the steps**

Teach-back mein patient apne words mein baat samjhata hai. Return demonstration se nurse dekh sakti hai ki skill sahi aayi ya nahi.

**Baaki options kyun galat hain?**

- **A.** Complex booklet alone enough nahi hai.
- **B.** Documentation learning outcome confirm nahi karti.
- **D.** Yes bolna actual understanding prove nahi karta.

</details>

## Prioritization

### Q4. A charge nurse has one experienced nurse available. Which patient should receive the most immediate experienced assessment?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Topic:** Prioritization

- **A.** A patient due for routine health teaching with no acute symptoms
- **B.** A patient with new hypotension and altered consciousness
- **C.** A stable patient awaiting routine discharge paperwork
- **D.** A stable patient requesting fresh linens

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: B. A patient with new hypotension and altered consciousness**

New low BP aur confusion organs ko blood kam milne ka sign ho sakte hain. Experienced assessment urgent hai. Stable routine tasks ko safe delegation ya later care se manage kiya ja sakta hai.

**Baaki options kyun galat hain?**

- **A.** Stable teaching acute deterioration se lower priority hai.
- **C.** Discharge paperwork immediate physiological threat nahi hai.
- **D.** Fresh linen routine task hai.

</details>
