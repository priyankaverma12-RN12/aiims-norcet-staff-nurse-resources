**Correct answer: B. It creates a traceable, actionable record for continuity of supervision**

**A kyun galat hai:** Documentation follow-up ki need remove nahi karti. Unmet criterion ho toh planned reassessment still required hai.

**B kyun sahi hai:** Specific documentation se next supervisor ko pata chalta hai kya observe hua aur kya reassess karna hai. Traceable record fairness aur continuity support karta hai.

**C kyun galat hai:** Supervision record sensitive staff information contain kar sakta hai. Access aur storage policy follow karni chahiye.

**D kyun galat hai:** Record banane se independent authorization automatically nahi milti. Required competency aur institutional approval criteria complete hone chahiye.
