### Q2. During a rapidly evolving ward evacuation, the incident lead assigns immediate roles without waiting for a group vote. Which leadership approach is most apparent?

- **C.** Democratic leadership
- **D.** Servant leadership

### Q20. A hospital forms a council of bedside nurses who have defined authority to recommend and participate in decisions about clinical practice standards. Which organizational approach is best represented?

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD | **Topic:** Leadership and management | **Exam focus:** NORCET / Indian Nursing Officer original practice

- **A.** Exclusive top-down administration
- **B.** Shared governance
- **C.** Informal social networking
- **D.** Individual reward contracting

<details><summary>✅ Correct answer and detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: B. Shared governance**

**A kyun galat hai:** Exclusive top-down administration mein decisions sirf senior management leti hai. Council participation iske opposite feature ko show karta hai.

**B kyun sahi hai:** Shared governance mein frontline nurses professional practice decisions mein structured participation karti hain. Defined council authority staff ki voice ko formal decision process se connect karti hai.

**C kyun galat hai:** Informal networking colleagues ke personal communication se hoti hai. Yahan council aur defined authority formal structure ka part hain.

**D kyun galat hai:** Individual reward contracting personal performance aur reward ke exchange se related hai. Practice standards par council involvement collective governance hai.
