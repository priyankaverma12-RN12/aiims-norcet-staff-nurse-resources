## Initial study explanations

### Delegation

Stable patient ki routine hygiene trained worker ko delegate ho sakti hai. Registered nurse assessment, judgment aur supervision ki responsibility rakhti hai. Local scope follow karna zaroori hai.

### Quality improvement

Near-miss mein harm nahi hua par risk tha. Process analyze karke handover aur verification improve ki ja sakti hai. Reporting se future errors prevent karne mein help milti hai.

### Patient education

Teach-back mein patient apne words mein baat samjhata hai. Return demonstration se nurse dekh sakti hai ki skill sahi aayi ya nahi.

### Prioritization

New low BP aur confusion organs ko blood kam milne ka sign ho sakte hain. Experienced assessment urgent hai. Stable routine tasks ko safe delegation ya later care se manage kiya ja sakta hai.
