## Cubes and cuboids

**Priority:** 🔴 MOST IMPORTANT

3D shape mein length, width aur height hoti hain. Surface area shape ki surfaces ko cover karne wali jagah hai, jaise box par paper lagana. Volume shape ke andar occupy hone wali jagah hai, jaise container mein kitna liquid aa sakta hai. Surface area square units aur volume cubic units mein measure hota hai.

Original schematic, not to scale:

```text
          +---------+
         /         /|
        +---------+ |
        |         | | height h
        |         | /
        +---------+/
           length l
    depth = width w
```

| Shape | Volume | Total surface area | Lateral surface area |
|---|---|---|---|
| Cube, side a | a³ | 6a² | 4a² |
| Cuboid, l × w × h | lwh | 2(lw+wh+hl) | 2h(l+w) |

Closed cuboid mein six faces hoti hain. Open-top box mein lid nahi hota, toh base aur four walls ka area lw+2h(l+w) hai. Example: 20 cm by 15 cm base aur height 5 cm ho toh open surface area 300+2×5×35=650 cm² hai. Capacity 1500 cm³ yani 1.5 L hai.

Space diagonal √(l²+w²+h²) hai. Cube ko small cubes mein kaatne par volume conserve hota hai, lekin exposed surface area badh sakta hai. Example: 8 cm cube ko 2 cm cubes mein kaato. Number (8/2)³=64 hai. Total small-cube surface area 64×6×2²=1536 cm² hai, original 384 cm² tha.

## Cylinders and pipes

**Priority:** 🔴 MOST IMPORTANT

Cylinder mein do circular bases aur ek curved wall hoti hai. Curved wall ko flat karoge toh rectangle banta hai jiska length circumference 2πr aur height h hai.

```text
        .--------.
      /            \
      |            |
      |            | h
      |            |
      \            /
        '--------'
        radius r
```

| Quantity | Formula |
|---|---|
| Base area | πr² |
| Volume | πr²h |
| Curved surface area | 2πrh |
| Closed total surface area | 2πr(h+r) |
| Open-top cylinder interior area | 2πrh+πr² |
| Hollow pipe material volume | π(R²−r²)h |

Example: r=7 cm, h=10 cm, π=22/7. Base 154 cm², volume 1540 cm³, curved area 440 cm² aur closed total area 748 cm² hai. Label sirf curved surface cover karta hai. Open vessel mein ek base count hota hai.

Agar volume aur height known hain, r²=V/(πh) se radius nikalo. Phir requested surface area formula use karo. [🔵 RRB Nursing Superintendent 12 March 2026 shift 2 — reported cylinder question](../Previous-Year-Questions/16-Mensuration-3D-Reported-Evidence.md).

## Cones

**Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD

Right circular cone ka ek circular base aur ek pointed vertex hota hai. Perpendicular height h centre se vertex tak hai. Slant height l boundary se vertex tak hai. Right triangle se l=√(r²+h²) milta hai.

```text
             apex
              /|\
           l / | \
            /  |h \
           /___|___\
               < r >
```

Volume πr²h/3, curved surface πrl aur total surface πr(l+r) hai. Example: r=3 cm aur h=4 cm toh slant height 5 cm hai. Volume 12π cm³, curved area 15π cm² aur total area 24π cm² hai. Height aur slant height ko exchange nahi karna.

Liquid transfer mein volume same rehta hai agar leakage ya overflow nahi ho. Cone volume ko cylinder base area se divide karne par new depth milti hai.

## Spheres and combined solids

**Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD

Sphere ka har boundary point centre se same distance r par hai. Hemisphere sphere ka half hai. Hemisphere curved area sphere ka half hai, lekin flat base count karna ho toh circle area add hoga.

| Figure | Volume | Surface area |
|---|---|---|
| Sphere | (4/3)πr³ | 4πr² |
| Hemisphere | (2/3)πr³ | Curved 2πr², total 3πr² |

Example: r=3 cm sphere ka volume 36π cm³ aur surface area 36π cm² hai. Same numerical value ka matlab quantities same nahi hain. Hemisphere volume 18π cm³ aur total surface 27π cm² hai.

Recasting mein material volume conserve hota hai, surface area nahi. Number of small similar solids = original volume / each small volume. Combined solids mein non-overlapping volumes add karo. Exposed surface mein joined faces count nahi karne. Cylinder ke ek end par hemisphere joda ho toh exposed area cylinder curved area + hemisphere curved area + cylinder ka doosra base hai.

## Prisms, pyramids and applications

**Priority:** 🟡 IMPORTANT

Prism ka cross-section length ke along same shape rehta hai. Volume = base area × perpendicular length. Pyramid base se apex tak narrow hoti hai. Volume = base area × perpendicular height / 3. Cone circular-base pyramid jaisa relationship follow karta hai.

Frustum cone ka truncated part hai jiske circular ends ki radii R aur r hain. Volume πh(R²+Rr+r²)/3 hai. Ye competitive aptitude extension hai, specific current NORCET depth independently established nahi hai.

| Conversion | Value |
|---|---|
| 1 cm³ | 1 mL |
| 1000 cm³ | 1 L |
| 1 m³ | 1000 L |
| 1 m² | 10,000 cm² |
| 1 m³ | 1,000,000 cm³ |

Water displacement mein fully submerged solid jitna volume displace karta hai, utna liquid-level-change volume hota hai, agar overflow na ho aur horizontal cross-section constant ho. Rise = displaced volume / base area. Example: 2 L=0.002 m³, base area 0.1 m², rise 0.02 m=2 cm. Full tank ya insufficient space mein model directly apply nahi hota. Density mass per unit volume hai. Mass = density × volume. Ye arithmetic examples hain, clinical device capacity ya dosage advice nahi.

## Quick revision

- Volume cubic units, surface area square units.
- Internal dimensions se capacity nikalo.
- Open top, closed top, curved area aur total area alag rakho.
- Cone volume mein perpendicular height, curved area mein slant height.
- Combined solids mein hidden joining faces remove karo.
- Linear factor k, surface area factor k², volume factor k³.
