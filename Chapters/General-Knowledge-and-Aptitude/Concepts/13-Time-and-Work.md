## Work rate and efficiency

**Priority:** 🔴 MOST IMPORTANT

Socho ek machine ko 12 hours mein ek poora batch complete karna hai. Agar uska rate har hour same hai, toh ek hour mein woh batch ka 1/12 hissa complete karegi. Isi per-hour kaam ko work rate ya efficiency kehte hain. Jab rate zyada hota hai, toh same work karne ka time kam hota hai.

- Work = rate × time.
- Rate = work / time.
- Time = work / rate.

Agar poora kaam 1 maan lo, toh 12 days mein complete karne wale worker ka daily rate 1/12 hai. Agar worker 3 days kaam kare, toh 3/12 = 1/4 kaam complete hota hai.

### Original rate diagram

| Worker | Individual completion time | Work in one day | Work units per day if total = 24 |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| A | 8 days | 1/8 | 3 |
| B | 12 days | 1/12 | 2 |
| A + B | 24/5 days | 5/24 | 5 |

Dono workers alag portions par saath kaam karte hain. Har din 3+2 = 5 units complete hote hain. Total 24 units ko 5 se divide karo. Time 4.8 days hai. Individual times ko add karna galat hoga.

Efficiency 25% badhne ka matlab rate 1.25 times hai. Same work ke liye time original time ka 1/1.25 = 0.8 times ho jaata hai. Isliye time 20% kam hota hai, 25% nahi.

## Combined and changing work

**Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD

Jab do log saath kaam karte hain, toh unke work rates add karo. Agar ek worker chhod de ya naya worker join kare, toh kaam ko phases mein divide karo. Har phase ka completed work nikaalne ke baad remaining work par naya rate lagao.

Example: A alone 15 days leta hai aur B alone 10 days leta hai. A pehle 3 days kaam karta hai. A ne 3/15 = 1/5 work complete kiya. Bacha 4/5 hai. Dono ka rate 1/15 + 1/10 = 1/6 hai. Remaining time (4/5)×6 = 4.8 days hai. Total time 3 + 4.8 = 7.8 days hai. Exam mein total aur extra time ka difference dhyan se dekho.

Alternate-day questions mein actual turn order matter karta hai. A 6 days aur B 12 days mein kaam karta hai. Ek two-day cycle mein 1/6 + 1/12 = 1/4 kaam hota hai. Teen cycles ke baad 3/4 kaam complete hai. Day 7 par A 1/6 complete karta hai aur remaining 1/12 B day 8 par karta hai. Is example mein exactly 8 days lagte hain. Agar last day par kaam jaldi khatam ho jaata hai, toh fractional time use karo.

Pair rates mein A+B, B+C aur C+A ko add karne par har individual rate do baar count hota hai. Sum ka half all-three rate hai. Agar C ka rate chahiye, toh all-three se A+B subtract karo.

## Worker hours and wages

**Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD

Worker-hours ka matlab hai workers ki count × actual hours worked. Agar sab log same rate par kaam karte hain, toh 6 workers ke 5 hours = 30 worker-hours hote hain. Same work ke liye 10 workers ko 3 hours chahiye. Ye ideal mathematical assumption hai. Real nursing staffing patient acuity, skill mix aur safety par depend karti hai.

Agar total work 360 worker-hours hai, crew mein 10 workers hain aur har din 6 hours kaam hota hai, toh time 360/(10×6) = 6 days hai. Agar har din 5 hours kaam ho, toh time 7.2 days hoga. Agar full-day schedule required ho, toh 8th day par finish hoga.

Payment agar actual work ke proportion mein divide hona hai, toh sirf time ya sirf rate ka ratio nahi lena. A 4 days mein 3 units/day karta hai aur B 3 days mein 6 units/day. A ne 12 units aur B ne 18 units kiye. ₹3,000 payment ka ratio 12:18 = 2:3 hai. A ko ₹1,200 aur B ko ₹1,800 milenge.

Practice: Q14–20, Q30.

## Advanced output and schedules

**Priority:** 🟡 IMPORTANT

Effective rate ka matlab usable output per unit time hai. Agar machine 100 items per hour banati hai lekin 5% items reject ho jaate hain, toh usable rate 95 per hour hai. Target acceptable items ka hai, toh gross rate nahi use karna.

Downtime mein output zero hota hai. Example: har 10 minutes ke cycle mein machine 7 minutes chalti hai aur 3 minutes rukti hai. 25 minutes ke interval mein 2 complete cycles aur 5 active minutes hain. Total active time 19 minutes hai. Agar machine 20 items/minute banati hai, toh output 380 hai.

Sequential work mein stages ek doosre ke baad hote hain. Unke durations add karo. Parallel work mein independent portions simultaneously process hote hain, toh suitable rates add kar sakte ho. Bottleneck ya mandatory dependency diya ho toh question ki conditions follow karo.

Practice: Q21–27.

## Quick revision

- Pehle total work aur rate ki units match karo.
- Time aur efficiency ka relation inverse hai, jab total work constant ho.
- Joining/leaving questions mein remaining work par changed rate lagao.
- Alternating workers mein last turn check karo.
- Payment ko actual work ke ratio mein divide karo, agar question aisa kehta hai.
