## Formula and time

**Priority:** 🔴 MOST IMPORTANT

Principal ka matlab original paisa hai. Interest us paisa ke use ke badle milne wali extra rakam hai. Simple interest mein har saal interest original principal par calculate hota hai. Purane interest par interest nahi lagta.

- P = original principal, R = annual percentage rate, T = years.
- SI = P × R × T / 100.
- A = P + SI.

Agar rate decimal mein 0.06 hai, toh formula SI = P × 0.06 × T hoga. Dobara 100 se divide nahi karna. Months ko 12 se divide karke years mein badlo. Days ke liye question ka specified day-count convention use karo.

Example: ₹5,000 par 6% annual rate se 18 months mein interest 5000 × 6/100 × 1.5 = ₹450 hai. Amount ₹5,450 hai.

| Timeline at 10% | Original principal | Accrued interest | Total amount |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Start | ₹1,000 | ₹0 | ₹1,000 |
| Year 1 | ₹1,000 | ₹100 | ₹1,100 |
| Year 2 | ₹1,000 | ₹200 | ₹1,200 |
| Year 3 | ₹1,000 | ₹300 | ₹1,300 |

Har saal ₹100 interest add hota hai. Interest ka base ₹1,000 hi rehta hai. Original bank Q1–4 and Q17 isi calculation par practice karwate hain.

## Amount and reverse calculation

**Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD

Kabhi question total amount deta hai aur original principal poochta hai. Tab A = P(1 + RT/100) use karo. Agar ₹6,600 do saal ke baad 5% simple interest se milte hain, toh amount principal ka 110% hai. Principal 6600/1.10 = ₹6,000 hai. Final amount se 10% subtract karna galat hoga kyunki interest ka base final amount nahi hai.

Agar year 2 par ₹9,800 aur year 5 par ₹11,000 milte hain, toh annual interest (11000−9800)/3 = ₹400 hai. Principal 9800−800 = ₹9,000 hai. Annual rate 400/9000×100 = 4.44% approximately hai.

Double amount mein interest 100% of principal hai. Triple amount ke liye interest 200% hai. Time = required interest percentage / annual rate.

Practice: original Q5–9, Q20–21 and Q23.

## Split principal and changing terms

**Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD

Agar ₹10,000 ka kuch hissa 4% par aur baaki 7% par rakha hai aur ek saal ka total interest ₹580 hai, toh pehle assume karo ki sab paisa 4% par hai. Isse ₹400 interest milta. Extra ₹180 higher rate ke 3% difference se aaya hai. Isliye 180/0.03 = ₹6,000 higher rate wala hissa hai.

Agar rate beech mein change ho, lekin principal same rahe, toh har period ka P × R × T /100 alag nikaalo. Agar principal bhi change ho, jaise partial payment ke baad, toh new principal ke liye alag period use karo. Fee ko interest se alag rakho. Actual bank contracts mein repayment rules alag ho sakte hain. Ye sirf exam arithmetic hai.

Practice: original Q10–16 and Q18–19.

## Advanced application

**Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD

Equal-interest questions mein P1 × R1 × T1 = P2 × R2 × T2 hota hai. Same principal mein R × T equal hona chahiye. Agar principal 20% badhe aur rate 25% kam ho, toh interest factor 1.20×0.75 = 0.90 hai. Interest 10% kam hoga. Percent changes ko seedha add nahi karna.

Instalment question mein payment date samajhna zaroori hai. Agar due-date simple-interest equivalence explicitly diya hai aur do equal payments X hain, pehli payment ko due date tak ek saal aage value karo. 8% par total value 1.08X + X = 2.08X hogi. Ye assumed arithmetic method hai, actual amortized loan rule nahi.

Practice: original Q22–30.
