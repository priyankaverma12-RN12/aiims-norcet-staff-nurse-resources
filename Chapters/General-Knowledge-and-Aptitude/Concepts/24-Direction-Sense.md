## Compass and turns

**Priority:** 🔴 MOST IMPORTANT

Direction sense questions mein person ka facing direction, position aur movement track karna hota hai. Compass ko paper par fixed rakhna helpful hota hai. North upar, south neeche, east right aur west left mark karo. Person ki right aur left direction uske facing par depend karti hai, paper ki right aur left par nahi.

Original compass schematic:

```text
           N 0°
 NW 315°    |    NE 45°
       \    |    /
W 270° -----O----- E 90°
       /    |    \
 SW 225°    |    SE 135°
           S 180°
```

Bearing ko north se clockwise measure karte hain. Right turn mein angle add hota hai aur left turn mein subtract hota hai. Result ko 0° se 360° ke beech convert kar sakte ho.

| Facing | Left | Right | Behind |
|---|---|---|---|
| North | West | East | South |
| East | North | South | West |
| South | East | West | North |
| West | South | North | East |

Example: Person south-east face karta hai, bearing 135° hai. Left turn 90° ke baad 45° bachta hai, north-east. Phir right turn 45° se east ho jaata hai. Person move nahi karta toh uski location same rahegi.

Reverse-turn question mein final bearing diya ho toh net rotation ko subtract karo. Agar final bearing 180° hai aur net right turn 90° tha, initial bearing 90° east tha.

**Practice:** Q1–8.

## Routes and displacement

**Priority:** 🔴 MOST IMPORTANT

Distance travelled poore path ki length hai. Displacement start se final point tak net straight change hai, jisme direction bhi hoti hai. Dono ko confuse mat karo. East ko positive x aur north ko positive y maan sakte ho. West aur south negative honge.

Original worked route. O start hai, A pehla turn hai aur B endpoint hai.

```text
N ↑
  |
  |            B(4,3)
  |           /|
  |     5 m  / | 3 m
  |         /  |
  O--------A(4,0) --> E
      4 m
```

O se A tak 4 m east aur A se B tak 3 m north movement hai. Total path 7 m hai. Net displacement magnitude √(4²+3²)=5 m hai. B start ke north-east quadrant mein hai. Exact bearing zaroori nahi ki 45° ho. Wapas O jaana ho toh vector reverse hoga, south-west quadrant.

Agar route mein east 12 m aur west 5 m ho toh net east 7 m hai. North 9 m aur south 9 m cancel ho jaayenge. Final displacement 7 m east hoga. Har direction ko separately calculate karo.

Grid questions mein agar sirf perpendicular streets allowed hain, shortest permitted route net absolute x aur y components ka sum hai. Straight diagonal tab allowed nahi hai. Example: 3 blocks east aur 4 north ke liye grid route 7 blocks aur unrestricted diagonal 5 blocks hai.

**Practice:** Q9–19. [RRB 2019 and AIIMS Bhopal 2018 reported questions](../Previous-Year-Questions/24-Direction-Sense-Reported-Evidence.md).

## Coded and rotated directions

**Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD

Kabhi question directions ko symbols deta hai, kabhi map rotate hota hai. Pehle har symbol ki definition likho. Real world compass ko fixed rakho. Agar map mein east top par hai, top se clockwise 90° south hai, toh map ka right edge true south hai.

Movement code mein translation aur rotation alag hain. Translation position change karti hai. Rotation facing direction change karti hai, location tab tak same rehti hai jab tak move nahi kiya. Example: Person north face kar raha hai. Usko 2 m right side step karne ko bola gaya bina turn kiye. Ab location 2 m east hai, lekin facing north hi hai. Agar next instruction left turn without movement hai, location same rahegi aur facing west hogi.

## Shadow, clock and relative facing

**Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD

Shadow light source se opposite direction mein extend hoti hai. Competitive reasoning questions often assume sunrise exactly east aur sunset exactly west. Actual Sun ka azimuth season aur location par depend karta hai. Isliye real life mein morning ya evening alone se exact cardinal direction guaranteed nahi hoti. Question ka idealised assumption check karo.

```text
Idealised sunset

Sun (W) ---> person ---> shadow (E)

If person faces N, E is on their right.
If person faces S, E is on their left.
```

Do people ek doosre ko face karte hain toh ek ka right side doosre ke left side ki same geographical direction hota hai. Example: North-facing person ka right east hai. Uske saamne south-facing person ka left bhi east hai.

Clock ka 12 mark kisi fixed direction mein diya ho sakta hai. Clockwise marks ko 30° ke intervals par count karo. Hour hand har hour 30° aur har minute 0.5° move karti hai. Minute hand har minute 6° move karti hai. Agar 12 west point karta hai aur time 3:00 hai, hour hand west se 90° clockwise north point karegi. Agar 6:15 diya ho, hour hand exactly 6 par nahi hoti, 7.5° aage hoti hai.

**Practice:** Q25–30. [NORCET 2020 shadow report with corrected logical explanation](../Previous-Year-Questions/24-Direction-Sense-Reported-Evidence.md).

## Quick revision

Compass fixed rakho. Right turn clockwise aur left turn anticlockwise. Position aur facing alag track karo. Opposite movement cancel karo. Shortest distance ke liye Pythagoras tab use karo jab straight route allowed ho. Reference point reverse hone par vector reverse hoga. Sun assumption aur clock orientation dhyan se padho.
