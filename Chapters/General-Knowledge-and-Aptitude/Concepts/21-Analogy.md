## Word relationships

**Priority:** 🔴 MOST IMPORTANT

Analogy ka matlab hai do cheezon ke beech ka relation pehchanna aur wahi relation doosre pair mein apply karna. A : B :: C : D mein A aur B ka relation C aur D ke relation jaisa hona chahiye. Sirf related words dekhkar answer select mat karo. Relation ka type aur direction bhi same hona chahiye.

Socho "key : lock" diya hai. Key lock ko open karne ke liye use hoti hai. Agar "remote : television" diya hai toh dono mein tool aur uske controlled object ka relation milta hai. Lekin "lock : key" likhne par relation ki direction reverse ho jaati hai.

```text
First pair       Second pair
A ----rule----> B
C ----same----> ?
```

| Relationship | Example | Kaise pehchanein |
|---|---|---|
| Part to whole | Petal : flower | Pehli cheez doosri ka component hai. |
| Worker to tool | Tailor : scissors | Pehla person doosri cheez ka use karta hai. |
| Raw material to product | Wool : sweater | Pehli material se doosra product banta hai. |
| Class to member | Fruit : mango | Pehli broad category hai. |
| Instrument to quantity | Ruler : length | Instrument doosri quantity measure karta hai. |
| Process to result | Freezing : ice | Doosri cheez specified process ka result hai. |
| Antonym | Empty : full | Meanings opposite hain. |
| Work to creator | Poem : poet | Pehli work hai aur doosra creator hai. |

Example: "nest : bird :: hive : ?" mein nest aur bird ka relation animal ke usual dwelling ka hai. Isliye hive ke saath bee fit hoti hai. Honey bhi hive se related hai, lekin animal nahi hai. Yahi distractor trap hai.

## Number relationships

**Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD

Number analogy mein pehle given pair ka operation samjho. Jaise 6 : 35 :: 10 : ? diya ho aur rule square minus one specified ho, toh 6²−1=35 aur 10²−1=99 hai. Agar rule specify nahi kiya ho, toh available examples aur options se simplest consistent relation choose karo. Sirf ek example se infinitely many mathematical rules ban sakte hain, isliye exam mein arbitrary complicated formula mat invent karo.

| Rule family | Example |
|---|---|
| Product with successor | 4 → 4×5=20 |
| Square plus constant | 7 → 7²+2=51 |
| Digit operation | 23 → 2²+3²=13 |
| Fixed multiplier plus constant | 3 → 2×3+5=11 |
| Divisor count | 16 → 5 positive divisors |

Agar fixed multiplication aur addition wali analogy mein do examples 2→11 aur 5→23 hain, input 3 se badhta hai aur output 12 se badhta hai. Isliye multiplier 12/3=4 hai. 2×4+3=11 se addend 3 milta hai. 8 ka output 8×4+3=35 hoga.

Divisor count ke liye prime powers use kar sakte ho. 18=2¹×3² hai. Positive divisors ki count (1+1)(2+1)=6 hai. LCM aur reduced ratio bhi relationship questions mein aa sakte hain. Inki foundational methods already [HCF and LCM](02-HCF-and-LCM.md) aur [Ratios](07-Ratios-and-Proportion.md) mein hain. Un existing questions ko new originality count mein add nahi kiya gaya.

**Practice:** Q16–23.

## Structural and figure relationships

**Priority:** 🟡 IMPORTANT

Figure analogy mein shape ka size, orientation, shading, number of sides ya symmetry change ho sakti hai. Rotation mein shape centre ke around turn hoti hai. Reflection mein shape mirror ke across flip hoti hai. Dono alag transformations hain. Agar arrow right point kar raha hai aur usko 90° anticlockwise turn karte ho toh arrow up point karega.

```text
Rotation 90 degrees anticlockwise
       ↑
       |
       O ---->

Horizontal reflection
       ↑
  ------------ mirror
       ↓
```

Coordinate plane mein origin ke around 90° anticlockwise rotation (x,y) ko (−y,x) banaati hai. Agar (2,5) diya hai, transformed point (−5,2) hai. Shape ki length rotation se change nahi hoti. Similar shapes mein linear scale factor k se area k² times hota hai.

Clock relationships mein elapsed time aur displayed clock time alag rakho. 08:50 ke 25 minutes baad 09:15 hota hai. Inverse operation questions mein check karo ki doosri operation pehli ko undo karti hai.

## Common mistakes and quick revision

- Pehle exact relation ko sentence mein explain karo.
- Relation ki direction reverse mat karo.
- Number rule ko har provided example par test karo.
- Rotation aur reflection mein farq samjho.
- Similarity ka matlab proof of causation nahi hai.
