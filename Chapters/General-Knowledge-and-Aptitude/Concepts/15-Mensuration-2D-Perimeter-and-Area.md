## Rectangles and squares

**Priority:** 🔴 MOST IMPORTANT

Mensuration ka matlab shapes ki measurement hai. Perimeter kisi flat shape ki bahar ki poori boundary ki length hoti hai. Area us shape ke andar cover hone wali jagah hai. Socho ek rectangular room mein skirting lagani hai toh boundary measure karoge. Floor par tiles lagani hain toh area measure karoge. Dono ka answer ek hi unit mein nahi aata. Perimeter metre ya centimetre mein hota hai aur area square metre ya square centimetre mein hota hai.

Original diagram, scale par nahi:

```text
           length l
       +--------------+
       |              |
width w|     area     | w
       |              |
       +--------------+
             l
```

| Shape | Perimeter | Area | Diagonal |
|---|---|---|---|
| Rectangle | 2(l+w) | lw | √(l²+w²) |
| Square | 4a | a² | a√2 |

Agar 12 m by 8 m floor hai, toh boundary 2(12+8)=40 m hai aur floor area 12×8=96 m² hai. Agar 1 m ka path outer edges ke bahar banaya jaata hai, toh outer length 14 aur outer width 10 hogi. Dono opposite sides par width add hoti hai. Path area 14×10−96=44 m² hai. Agar path andar hota, toh central rectangle 10×6 hota aur path area 96−60=36 m² hota.

Perimeter se reverse calculation karne mein pehle half perimeter lo. Agar perimeter 50 m hai aur length width se 5 m zyada hai, toh l+w=25 aur l−w=5. Dono add karne par 2l=30, isliye length 15 m aur width 10 m hai.

## Triangles and quadrilaterals

**Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD

Triangle ke area ke liye base aur us base ke perpendicular height ka use karte hain. Agar triangle ko ek matching triangle ke saath jodo, toh parallelogram ban sakta hai. Isliye triangle ka area base×height ka half hota hai. Slanted side ko height tabhi maano jab woh perpendicular ho.

```text
             /\
            / |\
           /  | \     h = perpendicular height
          /   |  \
         +----+---+
             b
```

| Figure | Area | Important condition |
|---|---|---|
| Triangle | bh/2 | Height perpendicular ho |
| Equilateral triangle | √3 a²/4 | Teeno sides equal |
| Parallelogram | bh | Slanted side ko height mat samjho |
| Trapezium | (a+b)h/2 | a aur b parallel sides |
| Rhombus | d1×d2/2 | Diagonals perpendicular |

Agar triangle ki teeno sides p, q, r known hain, toh Heron's formula use karo. Semiperimeter s=(p+q+r)/2 aur area √[s(s−p)(s−q)(s−r)] hai. Example: sides 5, 12, 13 cm. s=15 hai, area √(15×10×3×2)=30 cm² hai. Ye right triangle bhi hai, isliye 5×12/2 se bhi 30 milta hai.

Rhombus ki diagonals ek doosre ko 90° par bisect karti hain. Agar diagonals 12 cm aur 16 cm hain toh half diagonals 6 aur 8 hain. Side √(6²+8²)=10 cm hai. Perimeter 40 cm aur area 12×16/2=96 cm² hai.

## Circles and sectors

**Priority:** 🔴 MOST IMPORTANT

Circle ke centre se boundary tak radius r hota hai. Centre se guzarti poori straight line diameter d hoti hai. Diameter 2r hai. Circle ki boundary circumference 2πr ya πd hai. Area πr² hai. π ki value question ke instruction ke hisaab se lo.

```text
         .-------.
       /           \
      |    O------->| r
       \           /
         '-------'
       <---- d ---->
```

| Figure | Area | Complete boundary |
|---|---|---|
| Circle | πr² | 2πr |
| Semicircle | πr²/2 | πr+2r |
| Sector angle θ degrees | θπr²/360 | θ(2πr)/360+2r |
| Annulus (ring) | π(R²−r²) | 2πR+2πr if both edges counted |

Semicircle ki boundary sirf curved arc nahi hoti. Straight diameter bhi include hota hai. Example: radius 7 cm aur π=22/7 ho toh curved arc 22 cm aur complete boundary 36 cm hai. Area 77 cm² hai.

Sector pizza slice ki tarah hai. 90° sector full circle ka one-quarter hai. Agar radius 8 cm hai toh sector area 16π cm² aur arc length 4π cm hai. Complete perimeter 4π+16 cm hoga.

Ring mein outer circle se inner hole subtract karna hai. Wheel ek full revolution mein circumference jitna distance cover karta hai, agar slipping nahi ho. Units check karna mat bhoolna.

## Composite area and scale

**Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD

Composite figure mein ek se zyada simple shapes judti hain. Pehle shape ko simple parts mein divide karo. Jo parts add karne hain unke areas add karo aur jo holes ya openings hain unke areas subtract karo. Overlap ko do baar count nahi karna.

Example: rectangular wall 6×4=24 m² hai. Door 2×1=2 m² aur window 1×1=1 m² hai. Paintable area 21 m² hai. Agar tiles ka area 0.25 m² ho, toh 21/0.25=84 tiles ke equivalent material chahiye. Actual fitting aur wastage is ideal calculation mein include nahi hain.

Linear scale k hone par area k² times hota hai. Agar drawing ka 1 cm actual 3 m hai, toh drawing mein 2 cm by 4 cm rectangle actual 6 m by 12 m yani 72 m² represent karta hai. Similar figures mein corresponding dimensions ka ratio square karne se area ratio milta hai.

Fixed perimeter wale rectangles mein maximum area square ka hota hai. Perimeter 24 cm hai toh l+w=12. 6×6=36 cm², jabki 7×5=35 cm². Dono ka perimeter same hai lekin area different hai.

Area percentage changes ki existing original questions [Percentages](../MCQs/06-Percentages-30-MCQs.md) mein preserved hain. Same method ko new mensuration authorship mein dobara count nahi kiya gaya.

## Quick revision

- Perimeter linear units, area square units.
- 1 m² = 10,000 cm². Length conversion ko square karo.
- Triangle height perpendicular honi chahiye.
- Circle mein d=2r. Semicircle boundary mein diameter include karo.
- Path width dono opposite sides par lagti hai.
- Composite areas mein overlap aur removed portions ka dhyan rakho.
