## Speed units and journey time

**Priority:** 🔴 MOST IMPORTANT

Speed ka matlab hai koi object ek unit time mein kitna distance cover karta hai. Agar vehicle 60 km/h ki constant speed par chalti hai, toh ek hour mein 60 km cover karti hai. Half hour mein 30 km cover karegi.

- Distance = speed × time.
- Speed = distance / time.
- Time = distance / speed.

Km/h ko m/s mein badalne ke liye 5/18 se multiply karo, kyunki ek kilometre 1000 metres hai aur ek hour 3600 seconds hai. M/s ko km/h mein badalne ke liye 18/5 ya 3.6 se multiply karo.

Example: 54 km/h = 54×5/18 = 15 m/s. Agar object 20 seconds tak chale, toh distance 15×20 = 300 m hai.

Same distance ke liye speed badhne par time kam hota hai. 40 km/h se 60 km/h par jaane par new time original ka 40/60 = 2/3 hota hai. Time mein 33⅓% ki reduction aati hai.

Agar trip mein stop hai toh moving speed ke liye elapsed time mein se stop subtract karo. Suppose 120 km ka trip 3 hours mein finish karna hai aur 30 minutes stop hai. Moving time 2.5 hours hai. Required speed 120/2.5 = 48 km/h hai.

Early/late questions mein dono arrival times ka difference use karo. Agar first speed par 10 minutes late aur second speed par 5 minutes early ho, toh travel time difference 15 minutes hai. Same route ke liye d/v1 − d/v2 = 15/60 hour likh sakte ho.

Average speed total distance divided by total elapsed time hoti hai. Isko simple arithmetic mean mat samjho. Equal-distance aur unequal-duration practice already [Averages Q26–27](../MCQs/08-Averages-30-MCQs.md) mein allocated hai. Unhe dobara original count mein include nahi kiya gaya.

## Relative speed

**Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD

Relative speed ka matlab ek moving object doosre ke comparison mein kitni jaldi paas aa raha hai ya door ja raha hai. Agar objects ek doosre ki taraf chal rahe hain, toh closing speed unki speeds ka sum hai. Agar same direction mein ek faster object doosre ko catch kar raha hai, toh closing speed unki speeds ka difference hai.

Original direction diagram:

```text
Opposite directions
A ----->                 <----- B
      closing rate = vA + vB

Same direction
A ------>              B ---->
      closing rate = vA - vB  (vA > vB)
```

Example: Do towns 180 km apart hain. Vehicles 50 aur 40 km/h ki speed se ek doosre ki taraf chalti hain. Closing speed 90 km/h hai. Meeting time 180/90 = 2 hours hai. First vehicle 100 km aur second vehicle 80 km cover karti hai. Dono ka distance add karke original gap milta hai.

Agar slower vehicle pehle nikalti hai toh head start calculate karo. Suppose first vehicle 30 km/h par half hour pehle start hoti hai. Gap 15 km hai. Faster vehicle 45 km/h par aati hai. Relative speed 15 km/h hai, toh catch-up mein faster vehicle ko ek hour lagega.

Practice: Q9–12.

## Trains and crossing

**Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD

Train ko complete cross karne ka matlab train ka rear end bhi reference point se aage nikal jaaye. Isliye pole ke liye train ki poori length count hoti hai. Platform ya tunnel ke liye train length aur platform length dono add hote hain.

Original crossing diagram:

```text
Start:  [==== train ====] |------ platform ------|
Finish:                   |------ platform ------| [==== train ====]

Front travel distance = train length + platform length
```

| Situation | Required relative distance | Relative speed |
|---|---|---|
| Stationary pole | Train length | Train speed |
| Platform or tunnel | Train length + structure length | Train speed |
| Moving pedestrian | Train length | Sum or difference according to direction |
| Two moving trains | Sum of both train lengths | Sum or difference according to direction |

Example: 120 m train at 54 km/h crosses a 180 m platform. Train speed 15 m/s hai. Required distance 120+180 = 300 m hai. Crossing time 300/15 = 20 seconds hai. Agar question sirf front ke platform se nikalne tak ka time poochta hai, toh train length add nahi karna.

Agar same train do different platforms ko alag times mein cross karti hai, toh extra platform length divided by extra time se train speed mil sakti hai. Fir speed × crossing time se total length aur platform length subtract karke train length recover karo.

Practice: Q13–20. [Reported train questions](../Previous-Year-Questions/14-Speed-Time-and-Distance-Reported-Evidence.md).

## Boats and streams

**Priority:** 🟡 IMPORTANT

Still-water speed boat ka paani ke relative speed hai. River current ki wajah se ground ke relative speed badalti hai. Downstream mein boat current ke saath chalti hai. Upstream mein current ke opposite chalti hai.

- Downstream speed D = boat speed b + current speed c.
- Upstream speed U = b − c, assuming b > c.
- Still-water speed b = (D+U)/2.
- Current speed c = (D−U)/2.

Example: Boat 15 km/h ki still-water speed aur 3 km/h current ke saath 36 km downstream travel karti hai. Downstream speed 18 hai, toh 2 hours lagte hain. Return upstream speed 12 hai, toh 3 hours lagte hain. Total moving time 5 hours hai. Agar beech mein wait diya hai, toh wait bhi elapsed time mein add karo.

Floating object sirf current ke saath move karta hai. Boat aur floating object ki same-direction separation rate boat ki still-water speed ke barabar hoti hai, assuming both experience the same constant current.

Practice: Q21–25.

## Circular tracks and deadlines

**Priority:** 🟡 IMPORTANT

Circular track mein first meeting ka matlab hai dono ke beech relative distance ek complete lap ke barabar ho. Same starting point aur opposite directions mein track length divided by sum of speeds use karo. Same direction mein faster runner ek lap gain kare tab track length divided by difference of speeds use karo.

Example: 360 m track par 4 m/s aur 8 m/s ke runners same direction mein start karte hain. Faster runner 360/(8−4)=90 seconds mein ek lap gain karega. Opposite directions mein 360/(8+4)=30 seconds mein pehli meeting hogi.

Race margin ke liye finish time par dono ki position calculate karo. Agar A 100 m race 20 seconds mein finish karta hai aur B 4 m/s par chal raha hai, toh B ne 80 m cover kiya. Gap 20 m hai.

Route reversal mein total distance aur starting point se separation different hain. Agar 10 km aage aur 3 km wapas jaate ho, toh total path 13 km hai lekin starting point se distance 7 km hai.

Deadline problems mein already elapsed time, remaining distance aur stops ka hisaab karo. Exam arithmetic ki idealized required speed ko real-world driving advice mat samjho.

Practice: Q26–30.
