- static GK and current affairs

## Initial study explanations

### Percentage

Accuracy nikaalne ke liye correct answers ko total attempted questions se divide karke 100 se multiply karte hain. 72/90 x 100 = 80%.

### Ratio

Patients ko nurses se divide karo. 36/6 = 6, isliye har nurse par 6 patients ka arithmetic ratio hai. Actual safe staffing clinical acuity par bhi depend karti hai.

### Reasoning

Har step mein previous number ko 2 se multiply kiya gaya hai. 48 x 2 = 96.

### General science

Pressure ka formula force divided by area hai. Same force chhoti area par lage toh pressure zyada hota hai. Isi wajah se sharp point easily penetrate kar sakta hai.
