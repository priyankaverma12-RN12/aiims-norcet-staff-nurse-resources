## Annual and periodic compounding

**Priority:** 🔴 MOST IMPORTANT

Compound interest ka matlab hai ki interest ko balance mein add kar diya jaata hai. Agle period mein naye balance par interest milta hai. Isliye pehle ka interest bhi interest kama sakta hai. Simple interest mein aisa nahi hota.

Suppose ₹1,000 par 10% annual rate hai aur saara interest account mein rehta hai.

| Time | Opening balance | Interest credited | Closing balance |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Year 1 | ₹1,000 | ₹100 | ₹1,100 |
| Year 2 | ₹1,100 | ₹110 | ₹1,210 |
| Year 3 | ₹1,210 | ₹121 | ₹1,331 |

Har saal ka interest badh raha hai, kyunki calculation base badal raha hai. Three-year compound interest ₹331 hai, lekin simple interest ₹300 hota.

- Annual compounding: A = P × (1 + R/100)^T.
- Compound interest CI = A − P.
- Agar nominal annual rate R hai aur saal mein n equal compounding periods hain, toh A = P × (1 + R/(100n))^(nT).
- Half-yearly mein n = 2, quarterly mein n = 4 aur monthly mein n = 12.

Example: ₹10,000 at 12% nominal annual rate compounded quarterly for 6 months. Period rate 3% hai aur do quarters hain. Amount 10000×1.03² = ₹10,609 hai. Interest ₹609 hai.

Reverse calculation mein final amount ko compound factor se divide karo. Agar two-year amount ₹12,100 at 10% annual rate hai, toh P = 12100/1.21 = ₹10,000.

Incomplete year mein contract kya kehta hai, woh zaroor dekho. Agar two full years ke baad ek quarter bacha hai aur question specifically simple proration kehta hai, toh current balance par one-quarter simple interest lagao. Ye har real contract ke liye universal rule nahi hai.

Practice: original Q1–7, Q23, Q28–29.

## Effective rate and SI comparison

**Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD

Nominal annual rate quoted rate hoti hai. Effective annual rate batati hai ki ek saal mein compounding ke baad actual percentage growth kitni hui. 12% nominal with monthly compounding ka monthly rate 1% hai. Effective annual rate (1.01^12 − 1)×100 = 12.68% approximately hai.

Same principal aur same positive annual rate ke saath one-year annual compounding mein CI aur SI equal hote hain. Two years mein CI − SI = P × (R/100)^2. Ye shortcut tabhi use karo jab same principal, rate aur annual compounding ho. Three years mein direct amounts calculate karna safer hai.

Example: ₹10,000 at 8% for two years. SI ₹1,600 hai. CI 10000×(1.08²−1) = ₹1,664 hai. Difference ₹64 hai. Shortcut 10000×0.08² bhi ₹64 deta hai.

Practice: original Q8–13 and Q26.

## Changing rates and cash flows

**Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD

Agar annual rate badalta hai toh har year ka factor multiply karo. 5% ke baad 8% increase ka combined factor 1.05×1.08 = 1.134 hai. Total growth 13.4% hai, 13% nahi.

Agar year-end interest consecutive years mein diya ho aur rate constant ho, toh agle year ka interest pichhle year ke interest ke 1+r guna hota hai. Amounts ke successive ratio se bhi rate recover kar sakte ho.

Additional deposit ya withdrawal ki exact timing dekho. Example: ₹5,000 par 10% year 1 interest ke baad balance ₹5,500 hai. Us samay ₹2,000 extra deposit karo. Year 2 opening balance ₹7,500 hai. Year 2 close ₹8,250 hoga.

Loan instalments mein same due date par payments ki values compare karo. Two equal year-end payments X with rate r ke liye year 2 par value X(1+r)+X hoti hai. Isko unpaid maturity debt ke equal rakho. Real loans ke charges, rounding aur contractual rules alag ho sakte hain.

Practice: original Q14–22, Q27 and Q30.

## Growth and depreciation

**Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD

Depreciation ka matlab hai value ka time ke saath kam hona. Agar current value par har saal 10% loss ho, toh multiplier 0.90 hai. ₹20,000 ki value two years ke baad 20000×0.9² = ₹16,200 hogi. Original cost se fixed ₹2,000 har saal subtract karna alag straight-line assumption hai.

Reverse depreciation mein current value ko remaining factor se divide karo. Successive increase aur decrease bhi multiplication se calculate hote hain. Percentage changes ko seedha add karna galat ho sakta hai.

Practice: original Q15–16 and Q24–25.
