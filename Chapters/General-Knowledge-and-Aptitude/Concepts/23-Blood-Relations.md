## Kinship and generations

**Priority:** 🔴 MOST IMPORTANT

Blood relation questions mein family ke members ke beech ka relation identify karna hota hai. Ye general reasoning questions hain, medical blood-group ya inheritance questions nahi. Family tree banaoge toh long sentence bhi chhote clear links mein divide ho jaata hai. Pehle jis person ke respect mein relation poocha hai usko mark karo.

Original family-tree schematic. Vertical line parent-to-child hai. Horizontal line full siblings ko connect karti hai.

```text
              Grandmother G
              /           \
        Father F -------- Uncle U
             |               |
           Child C        Cousin K
```

Is example mein G dono siblings ki mother hai. F, C ka father hai. U, F ka brother hai aur C ka paternal uncle hai. U ka child K, C ka first cousin hai. Agar C se G ka relation poocha jaaye toh G paternal grandmother hai. Agar G se C ka relation poocha jaaye toh C grandchild hai. C ka gender given nahi hai, isliye grandson ya granddaughter assume nahi karna.

| English relationship | Easy meaning |
|---|---|
| Maternal uncle | Mother ka brother |
| Paternal uncle | Father ka brother |
| Maternal aunt | Mother ki sister |
| Paternal aunt | Father ki sister |
| Niece | Sibling ki daughter |
| Nephew | Sibling ka son |
| First cousin | Parent ke sibling ka child |
| Grandparent | Parent ka parent |
| Mother-in-law | Spouse ki mother |
| Brother-in-law | Spouse ka brother ya sibling ka husband, depending on the statement |

In-law relation marriage se establish hota hai. Do log ek child ke parents hain, iska matlab automatically ye nahi ki woh currently married hain. Agar question specifically wife ya husband pooch raha hai toh marriage fact ya conventional exam assumption dhyan se check karo.

**Practice:** Q1–10. [HPSSSB and ESIC reports](../Previous-Year-Questions/23-Blood-Relations-Reported-Evidence.md).

## Statement and introduction problems

**Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD

Pointing ya introduction questions mein speaker ko "I" mark karo. Sentence mein "my father's only son" ya "her mother's brother" aaye toh possessive chain ko ek-ek link mein resolve karo. Only daughter ka matlab sirf ek daughter hai, zaroori nahi ki family mein koi son nahi hai.

Example: A woman says that the child's mother is her father's only daughter. Agar woman apne father's only daughter khud hai, toh statement ke according wahi child ki mother hai. Agar speaker ka gender given nahi hota, toh same conclusion ke liye additional information ki zaroorat ho sakti hai.

Example: Man kehta hai ki ek person's father uski mother's full brother hai. Mother ka brother maternal uncle hai. Uncle ka child first cousin hoga. Question reverse ho toh relation fir se requested viewpoint se read karo.

**Practice:** Q11–15.

## Coded relations

**Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD

Symbols ko ordinary mathematics ki tarah evaluate mat karo. Exam mein + ya # ka meaning question khud define karta hai. A # B agar A is brother of B ke liye diya hai, toh A male hai. B ka gender is symbol se automatically establish nahi hota.

Original worked chain:

```text
A # B @ C
# means brother of
@ means mother of

A (male) --- sibling --- B (female)
                           |
                           C

A is the maternal uncle of C.
```

Har symbol ko adjacent person par apply karo. Brackets ya equations assume mat karo. Agar A is parent of B aur B is sibling of C diya hai, toh A C ka parent tab certain hai jab common parent A explicitly establish ho, ya conventional full-sibling assumption stated ho.

**Practice:** Q16–21.

## Extended family and data sufficiency

**Priority:** 🟡 IMPORTANT

First cousins ke parents siblings hote hain. Second cousins ke parents first cousins hote hain. Once removed ka matlab generation difference one hai. Great-aunt grandparent ki sister hoti hai. Ye extra terminology reasoning scope ko strengthen karti hai, lekin official NORCET weightage ka claim nahi hai.

Half-siblings exactly one biological parent share karte hain. Full siblings dono biological parents share karte hain. Step-siblings ke relation mein marriage se family connection ho sakta hai, lekin shared biological parent necessary nahi.

Agar question mein N, R ka sibling hai aur R, S ka parent hai, toh N S ke parent ka sibling hai. N aur R ka gender nahi pata, isliye maternal uncle ya paternal aunt jaisi precise term choose nahi kar sakte. Insufficient information bhi genuine answer ho sakta hai.

**Practice:** Q22–30.

## Quick revision

Pehle viewpoint mark karo, generation count karo, phir maternal ya paternal side check karo. Symbols ko defined meaning se read karo. Gender, marriage aur shared parents ke baare mein extra assumptions mat lagao. Family tree ke arrows ko reverse karne par answer relation bhi reverse ho sakta hai.
