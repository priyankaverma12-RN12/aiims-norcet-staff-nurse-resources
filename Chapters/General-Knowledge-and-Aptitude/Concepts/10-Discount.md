## Marked price and sale price

**Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD | **Difficulty:** 🟡 Medium | **Evidence:** Core aptitude concept.

Imagine shop par ₹1000 ka tag laga hai. Ye marked price (MP) hai. Shop 15% discount deti hai toh reduction ₹150 aur selling price (SP) ₹850 hai. Discount MP par calculate hota hai, whereas profit percentage usually cost price (CP) par calculate hota hai. Isliye teen amounts ko separate rakhna important hai.

| Quantity | Calculation |
|---|---|
| Discount amount | MP − SP |
| Discount rate | (MP − SP)/MP × 100% |
| Selling price | MP × (1 − d/100) |
| Marked price | SP/(1 − d/100), if d is less than 100 |

For example, if sale price ₹640 after 20% discount hai, MP=640/0.8=800. Sale price par 20% add karne se original MP recover nahi hota.

Practice: Q1–6, [Q1](../MCQs/10-Discount-30-MCQs.md#q1-a-bag-has-a-marked-price-of-850-a-discount-of-14-is-offered-what-is-the-sale-price).

## Successive and fixed discounts

**Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD | **Difficulty:** 🔴 Difficult | **Evidence:** Related DSSSB reported successive-discount question.

Agar 20% discount ke baad another 15% discount hai, first discount original MP par apply hota hai aur second remaining price par. ₹1000 → ₹800 → ₹680. Total discount ₹320 or 32%, not 35%.

Two discounts a% and b% give equivalent rate a+b−ab/100 percent. For any number of discounts, remaining multiplier = product of all remaining fractions. If total reduction and initial discount given hain, find the second factor by dividing final remaining fraction by first remaining fraction.

A fixed rupee coupon aur percentage discount ka order matter kar sakta hai. Percentage ke baad coupon ₹50 deduct karna aur coupon ke baad percentage apply karna same nahi hai. Inverse problem mein last operation ko pehle reverse karo.

Practice: Q7–12, [Q7](../MCQs/10-Discount-30-MCQs.md#q7-two-successive-discounts-of-15-and-10-are-applied-what-is-the-equivalent-single-discount). Reported DSSSB evidence Q1.

## Discount and profit

**Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD | **Difficulty:** 🔴 Difficult | **Evidence:** Related DSSSB and AIIMS Bhopal reported questions.

```text
Cost price → apply markup → Marked price
Marked price → subtract discount → Selling price
Selling price − cost price → Gain or loss
```

If CP ₹400 and MP ₹600, markup is 50% on cost. A 20% discount on MP gives SP ₹480. Profit is ₹80 or 20% on cost. Markup minus discount is not the profit percentage because bases differ.

Break-even discount = (MP−CP)/MP ×100% when MP is at least CP. Required sale to earn p% profit is CP(1+p/100). Required discount = (MP−target SP)/MP ×100%. Include transport and other stated costs before calculating target profit. If desired sale exceeds MP, a positive discount cannot achieve the desired profit.

Practice: Q13–18, [Q13](../MCQs/10-Discount-30-MCQs.md#q13-an-item-costs-720-and-is-marked-at-960-what-is-the-greatest-discount-rate-on-the-marked-price-that-permits-break-even). Reported evidence Q2–3.

## Bundles and coupons

**Priority:** 🟡 IMPORTANT | **Difficulty:** 🟡 Medium | **Evidence:** Original applied aptitude.

Buy three and get one identical item free means four items receive total discount of one item's price. Effective discount 1/4=25% on the four-item list total, not 1/3. If items have unequal prices, use actual free item's value. Basket discounts with different percentages need monetary savings separately calculated, then divide total savings by total marked value.

Practice: Q19–23, [Q19](../MCQs/10-Discount-30-MCQs.md#q19-a-customer-buys-three-identical-items-marked-240-each-a-promotion-charges-for-two-at-full-marked-price-and-supplies-the-third-free-what-is-the-effective-discount-on-the-three-item-total).

## Tax and changed marked price

**Priority:** 🟡 IMPORTANT | **Difficulty:** 🔴 Difficult | **Evidence:** Original arithmetic practice.

Tax base depends on rules stated in question. If hypothetical 10% tax is charged on discounted price ₹500, bill ₹550 hai. A 20% price increase followed by 20% discount gives factor 1.2×0.8=0.96, overall 4% decrease. These exercises do not state actual Indian tax rates.

Practice: Q24–27.

## Offer comparison and caps

**Priority:** 🟡 IMPORTANT | **Difficulty:** 🔴 Difficult | **Evidence:** Original arithmetic practice.

Offer compare karte waqt final price calculate karo for same basket. A capped discount means actual saving is minimum of nominal saving and cap. Tiered discount mein each specified portion par respective rate apply karo. Example first ₹500 par 10% and next ₹300 par 5% gives ₹50+₹15=₹65 saving. Do not apply the larger rate to the whole basket unless stated.

Practice: Q28–30.
