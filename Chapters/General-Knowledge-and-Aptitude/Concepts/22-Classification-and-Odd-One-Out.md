## Word and category classification

**Priority:** 🔴 MOST IMPORTANT

Classification ka matlab hai cheezon ko unki common property ke basis par groups mein rakhna. Odd one out mein teen options ek meaningful property share karte hain aur chautha option us rule ko follow nahi karta. Sirf ek option unfamiliar lagne se woh odd nahi ho jaata. Question mein jo criterion diya hai, usi ko har option par test karo.

Socho pencil, pen, marker aur eraser diye hain. Agar criterion writing marks banana hai, toh pencil, pen aur marker ek group mein hain. Eraser marks ko remove karta hai. Lekin agar criterion stationery hona hai, toh chaaron ek hi category mein hain. Isliye question ki wording bahut important hai.

```text
             Check the stated property
                       |
        +--------------+--------------+
        |                             |
   Property present             Property absent
     A, B, C                         D
                                 odd item
```

| Group type | Example | Odd item ka reason |
|---|---|---|
| Animal class | Sparrow, crow, pigeon, lizard | Lizard bird nahi hai. |
| Direction of relation | Metal→tool, cloth→shirt, wood→table, bread→flour | Last pair product se material ki direction mein hai. |
| Grammar | Teach→teacher, paint→painter, sing→singer, decide→decision | Decision person nahi hai. |
| Function | Monitor, speaker, printer, keyboard | Keyboard input leta hai. |

Words, country-capital pairs aur objects ki classification mein factual relationship sahi hona chahiye. Sirf naam ya spelling ke basis par answer na choose karo jab question category pooch raha ho.

## Number and fraction classification

**Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD

Number classification mein har option ko ek common mathematical test par check karte hain. Agar question perfect square poochta hai, toh nearby integers ke squares check karo. Agar prime number poochta hai, toh dekho ki 1 aur number khud ke alawa koi positive divisor hai ya nahi.

| Property | Test |
|---|---|
| Divisible by 3 and 4 | Dono conditions independently check karo. |
| Prime | Sirf 1 aur khud positive divisors hon. |
| Perfect square | Kisi integer ka square ho. |
| Perfect cube | Kisi integer ka cube ho. |
| Coprime pair | HCF 1 ho. |
| Terminating rational decimal | Lowest terms mein denominator ke prime factors sirf 2 aur/or 5 hon. |

Example: 11/40, 9/16, 7/25 aur 5/18 mein se non-terminating recurring decimal kaunsa hai? Pehli teen fractions ke simplified denominators mein sirf 2 ya 5 factors hain. 5/18 ka denominator 2×3² hai. Factor 3 ki wajah se decimal repeat karega. Isliye 5/18 odd hai.

Divisor-based classification mein prime factorisation use karo. Agar n=p^a×q^b hai toh positive divisors ki count (a+1)(b+1) hai. Lekin agar question distinct prime factors poochta hai, toh bas alag prime bases count karo, exponent count nahi.

Ordered pairs ya triples mein stated equation har option par apply karo. Pehle do correct examples dekhkar rule conclude mat karo. Teeno matching options ko verify karna zaroori hai.

## Letter and word patterns

**Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD

Alphabet classification mein letters ki actual positions likhne se galti kam hoti hai. A=1, B=2 aur Z=26 hain. Agar group mein first se last tak increasing order chahiye, toh har next letter ki position previous letter se zyada honi chahiye. Equal gaps aur simple increasing order same condition nahi hain.

Example: B, F, J ki positions 2,6,10 hain. Gaps 4 aur 4 hain. C, G, L ki positions 3,7,12 hain. Gaps 4 aur 5 hain. Dono groups increasing hain, par second group equal-gap condition follow nahi karta.

Vowel classification mein A,E,I,O,U ko vowels maan lo jab question ye rule de. "PLANE" mein two vowels aur three consonants hain. Agar ek word mein two vowels aur four consonants hain, toh ratio alag hoga. Agar question pronunciation poochta hai, toh written-letter count sufficient nahi hai.

**Practice:** Q21–25. Existing [Series](19-Number-and-Alphabet-Series.md) aur [Coding-decoding](20-Coding-and-Decoding.md) questions separate ownership mein hain. In tasks mein grouping criterion apply karna hai, next term ya coded output predict nahi karna.

## Shape and measurement classification

**Priority:** 🟡 IMPORTANT

Figure classification mein number of sides, parallel lines, symmetry, size aur orientation jaise properties check karte hain. Rotation, translation aur reflection distances preserve karti hain. Enlargement scale factor 1 ke alawa size change karta hai. Isliye position change aur size change ko ek hi property mat samjho.

Coordinate points ke liye origin se distance √(x²+y²) hota hai. Example: (3,4) aur (−4,3) dono origin se 5 units door hain, chahe unki direction alag ho. Calendar questions mein actual valid date check karo. April, June, September aur November mein 30 days hote hain. February 28 days ya Gregorian leap year mein 29 days hota hai.

Decimal hours ko minutes mein badalne ke liye fractional hour ko 60 se multiply karo. 1.5 hours ka matlab 1 hour 30 minutes hai, 1 hour 50 minutes nahi.

**Practice:** Q26–30.

## Quick revision

- Question ki stated property ko identify karo.
- Teeno matching options par property prove karo.
- Similar meanings, opposite meanings aur reverse relationships ko alag rakho.
- Number classification mein units aur factorisation check karo.
- Har option ke liye same criterion use karo.
- Agar multiple plausible answers milte hain toh question ambiguous ho sakta hai.
