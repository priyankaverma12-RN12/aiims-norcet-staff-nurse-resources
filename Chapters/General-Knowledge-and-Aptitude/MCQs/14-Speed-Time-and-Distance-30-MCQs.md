### Q1. Which speed in metres per second is equivalent to 90 km/h?

**Difficulty:** 🟢 Easy · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD · Calculation · Original practice

- **A.** 32.4 m/s
- **B.** 324 m/s
- **C.** 25 m/s
- **D.** 15 m/s

<details><summary>Sahi answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct answer: C. 25 m/s**

**Kyun sahi hai:** Kilometres ko metres aur hours ko seconds mein badlo. 90×1000/3600=25 m/s hai.

**Baaki options kyun galat hain?**

- **A.** Yeh 90 ko 0.36 se multiply karta hai, correct factor 5/18 hai.
- **B.** Yeh 3.6 se multiply karta hai, jo reverse conversion hai.
- **D.** 15 m/s ka equivalent 54 km/h hota hai.

**Exam trick:** Pehle distance aur time ki units match karo. Stop ko moving time se alag rakho.
