### Q1. A deposit of ₹7,500 earns 6.4% simple interest per year for 15 months. Find the interest earned.

**Difficulty:** 🟡 Medium · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD · Calculation · Original practice

- **A.** ₹480
- **B.** ₹600
- **C.** ₹7200
- **D.** ₹8100

<details><summary>Sahi answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct answer: B. ₹600**

**Kyun sahi hai:** 7500 × 6.4/100 × 15/12 = 600. Annual rate ke saath months ko years mein badalna zaroori hai.

**Baaki options kyun galat hain?**

- **A.** 480 sirf ek saal ka interest hai.
- **C.** 7200 tab aata hai jab 15 months ko 15 years maan lete hain.
- **D.** 8100 principal aur interest ka total hai.

**Exam trick:** Pehle dekho ki question interest, principal, rate ya time mein se kya pooch raha hai. Annual rate ke saath time years mein rakho.
