### Q1. Find the HCF of 84 and 126.

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Priority:** High

- **A.** 252
- **B.** 42
- **C.** 21
- **D.** 63

<details><summary>Sahi answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct answer: B. 42**

**Kyun sahi hai:** 84=2²×3×7 and 126=2×3²×7. Common minimum powers 2×3×7=42.

**Baaki options kyun galat hain?**

- **A.** 252 is a common multiple, not factor.
- **C.** 21 common factor hai but highest nahi.
- **D.** 63 does not divide 84.

</details>
