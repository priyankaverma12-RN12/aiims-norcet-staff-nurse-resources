### Q1. An investment of ₹6,000 earns 7% annual compound interest for 2 years. What is the interest, to the nearest paise?

**Difficulty:** 🟡 Medium · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD · Calculation · Original practice

- **A.** ₹840
- **B.** ₹6,869.40
- **C.** ₹869.40
- **D.** ₹420

<details><summary>Sahi answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct answer: C. ₹869.40**

**Kyun sahi hai:** 6000×1.07×1.07 = 6869.40. Interest ke liye original 6000 subtract karne par 869.40 milta hai.

**Baaki options kyun galat hain?**

- **A.** Yeh simple interest ka result hai.
- **B.** Yeh total amount hai, sirf interest nahi.
- **D.** Yeh first year ka interest hai.

**Exam trick:** Rate per compounding period aur periods ki count pehle identify karo. Amount aur interest alag hote hain.
