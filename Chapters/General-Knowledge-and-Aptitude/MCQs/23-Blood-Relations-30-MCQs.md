### Q1. What is the conventional relationship of a mother's brother to her child?

**Difficulty:** 🟢 Easy · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD · Original practice

- **A.** Paternal uncle
- **B.** Maternal uncle
- **C.** Grandfather
- **D.** Nephew
