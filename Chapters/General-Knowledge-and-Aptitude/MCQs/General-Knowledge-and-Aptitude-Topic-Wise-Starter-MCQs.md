## Percentage

### Q1. A candidate answers 72 of 90 practice questions correctly. What is the accuracy percentage?

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Topic:** Percentage

- **A.** 80%
- **B.** 72%
- **C.** 90%
- **D.** 18%

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: A. 80%**

Accuracy nikaalne ke liye correct answers ko total attempted questions se divide karke 100 se multiply karte hain. 72/90 x 100 = 80%.

**Baaki options kyun galat hain?**

- **B.** 72 correct count hai.
- **C.** 90 total questions hain.
- **D.** 18 wrong count hai.

</details>

## Ratio

### Q2. A ward has 36 patients and 6 nurses. What is the patient-to-nurse ratio?

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Topic:** Ratio

- **A.** 1:6
- **B.** 36:6 expressed in lowest terms as 12:1
- **C.** 3:1
- **D.** 6:1

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: D. 6:1**

Patients ko nurses se divide karo. 36/6 = 6, isliye har nurse par 6 patients ka arithmetic ratio hai. Actual safe staffing clinical acuity par bhi depend karti hai.

**Baaki options kyun galat hain?**

- **A.** 1:6 reverse ratio hai.
- **B.** 36:6 simplify karne par 6:1 hota hai.
- **C.** 3:1 ke liye 12 nurses chahiye hote.

</details>

## Reasoning

### Q3. Find the next number in the sequence 3, 6, 12, 24, 48.

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Topic:** Reasoning

- **A.** 60
- **B.** 84
- **C.** 96
- **D.** 72

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: C. 96**

Har step mein previous number ko 2 se multiply kiya gaya hai. 48 x 2 = 96.

**Baaki options kyun galat hain?**

- **A.** 60 mein 12 add hua hai.
- **B.** 84 doubling pattern follow nahi karta.
- **D.** 72 mein 24 add kiya gaya hai, pattern follow nahi hota.

</details>

## General science

### Q4. Which physical principle explains why a sharp needle exerts greater pressure than a blunt surface at the same applied force?

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Topic:** General science

- **A.** Force becomes zero at a sharp point
- **B.** Pressure increases when the contact area decreases
- **C.** Pressure is independent of area
- **D.** Pressure always decreases with smaller area

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: B. Pressure increases when the contact area decreases**

Pressure ka formula force divided by area hai. Same force chhoti area par lage toh pressure zyada hota hai. Isi wajah se sharp point easily penetrate kar sakta hai.

**Baaki options kyun galat hain?**

- **A.** Sharp point par applied force zero nahi hota.
- **C.** Area pressure ko affect karti hai.
- **D.** Smaller area se pressure badhta hai.

</details>
