### Q1. In a row of 42 people, a participant stands 16th from the left. What is the participant's position from the right?

**Difficulty:** 🟢 Easy · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD · Original practice

- **A.** 26th
- **B.** 16th
- **C.** 27th
- **D.** 28th

<details><summary>✅ Sahi answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct answer: C. 27th**

**Kyun sahi hai:** Position conversion mein total se left rank subtract karke 1 add karte hain, kyunki person khud bhi right rank mein count hota hai.

**Calculation or order:** 42−16+1=27.

**Baaki options kyun galat hain?**

- **A.** Person ko right count mein include nahi kiya.
- **B.** Left rank copy kiya, jabki person centre mein nahi hai.
- **D.** Ek extra person count kar diya.

**Exam trick:** Ranks ko same end se compare karo. Strictly between mein dono endpoints exclude hote hain.
