### Q1. A clerk completes 3/8 of an indexing task in 6 hours at a constant rate. How long would the entire task take?

**Difficulty:** 🟡 Medium · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD · Calculation · Original practice

- **A.** 2.25 hours
- **B.** 16 hours
- **C.** 10 hours
- **D.** 8 hours

<details><summary>Sahi answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct answer: B. 16 hours**

**Kyun sahi hai:** 6 hours mein 3/8 complete hua hai, toh poora kaam 6÷(3/8)=16 hours mein hoga.

**Baaki options kyun galat hain?**

- **A.** Yeh 6 ko 3/8 se multiply karta hai, jabki poora task bada hai.
- **C.** 10 hours sirf abhi bache 5/8 ka time hai.
- **D.** Yeh fraction ke denominator ko time maan leta hai.

**Exam trick:** Time ko directly add karne ke bajay per-unit-time work nikaalo.
