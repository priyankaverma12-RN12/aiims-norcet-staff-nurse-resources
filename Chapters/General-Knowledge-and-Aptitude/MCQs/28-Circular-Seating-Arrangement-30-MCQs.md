# 📝 Circular Seating Arrangement — 30 original MCQs

**Study-ready, unattempted.** Original practice, not Previous Year Questions. Each shared puzzle has three different tasks. Seats are equally spaced, numbered clockwise from seat 1 at the top. For inward-facing people personal left is clockwise and personal right anticlockwise. For outward-facing people the reverse applies. An opposite seat exists only for an even number of equally spaced seats. Rotations are not counted twice because the first named person is fixed at seat 1. Reflections are different if they satisfy the clues.

## Puzzle 1 — inward facing

A, B, C, D, E, F occupy 6 circular seats. A occupies seat 1. A, B, C, D, E, F face the centre. B sits 1 seat(s) clockwise from A. C sits 3 seat(s) clockwise from A. D sits 1 seat(s) anticlockwise from C. E sits 1 seat(s) clockwise from C.

### Q1. Puzzle 1: Who sits 2 seat(s) to the personal left of A?

**Difficulty:** 🟡 Medium · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD · Original practice

- **A.** A
- **B.** B
- **C.** C
- **D.** D

<details><summary>✅ Sahi answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct answer: D. D**

**Kyun sahi hai:** A centre face karta hai. Personal left ke liye 2 step clockwise chalo. Seat 1 se seat 3 milti hai, jahan D hai.

Clockwise order starting at seat 1: `A → B → D → C → E → F → back to A`. Total valid arrangements: 1. Har clue ko sabhi rotation-fixed permutations par test kiya gaya hai.

**Baaki options kyun galat hain?**

- **A.** A example order mein seat 1 par hai. Requested relation ki seat par D milta hai.
- **B.** B example order mein seat 2 par hai. Requested relation ki seat par D milta hai.
- **C.** C example order mein seat 4 par hai. Requested relation ki seat par D milta hai.

**Exam trick:** Centre-facing personal left clockwise hai. Outside-facing personal left anticlockwise hai. Wrap-around par seat 1 ko dobara count mat karo.
