### Q1. Express 7/16 as a percentage.

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Priority:** High

- **A.** 56.25%
- **B.** 43.75%
- **C.** 4.375%
- **D.** 37.5%

<details><summary>Sahi answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct answer: B. 43.75%**

**Kyun sahi hai:** 7÷16=0.4375 hai. Percent per hundred hota hai, so ×100=43.75%.

**Baaki options kyun galat hain?**

- **A.** 56.25% remaining 9/16 hai.
- **C.** 4.375% decimal shift one place kam hai.
- **D.** 37.5% equals 6/16, not 7/16.

</details>
