### Q1. An item receives three successive reductions of 20%, 10% and 12.5% from its current price. Find the equivalent overall reduction from the original price.

**Difficulty:** 🔴 Difficult | **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD | **Tag:** Calculation | **Evidence:** Third-party reported exam adaptation

- **A.** 42.5%
- **B.** 37%
- **C.** 63%
- **D.** 32%

<details><summary>Sahi answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct answer: B. 37%**

**Kyun sahi hai:** Remaining fraction 0.8×0.9×0.875=0.63 hai. Total discount 1−0.63=0.37, so 37%.

**Baaki options kyun galat hain?**

- **A.** 42.5% rates ko directly add karta hai aur changing base ignore karta hai.
- **C.** 63% amount remaining hai, not amount saved.
- **D.** 32% would leave 68% of price, actual 63% nahi.

Reported DSSSB Nursing Officer, 27 August 2019 shift III. [Direct report inspected](https://testbook.com/question-answer/what-is-the-total-discount-applicable-if-3-success--61d3f073976c016c8b5c63ca). Related 28 August shift II variant retained as a lead without count increment. [Concept](../Concepts/10-Discount.md#successive-and-fixed-discounts).

### Q2. A merchant marks an item exactly one third above its cost. What is the largest percentage reduction on that marked price which avoids a loss?

**Difficulty:** 🔴 Difficult | **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD | **Tag:** Calculation | **Evidence:** Third-party reported adaptation, precision clarified

- **A.** 33 1/3%
- **B.** 20%
- **C.** 12.5%
- **D.** 25%

<details><summary>Sahi answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct answer: D. 25%**

**Kyun sahi hai:** CP ₹300 maan lo. Marked price ₹400 hoga. Break-even ke liye sale ₹300 chahiye, discount ₹100. Marked base par 100/400×100=25%.

**Baaki options kyun galat hain?**

- **A.** One third original cost base ka markup hai, MP base ka break-even discount nahi.
- **B.** 20% discount se sale ₹320 hota hai, still profit ₹20 hai.
- **C.** 12.5% discount se sale ₹350 hota hai, so greatest permissible discount nahi.

Reported DSSSB Nursing Officer, 28 August 2019 shift III. [Direct report inspected](https://testbook.com/question-answer/an-article-is-marked-33-33-above-the-price-what--61cd686b0e836bad3540107f). Original report's rounded 33.33% has been expressed as exactly one third, consistent with its solution. Its unrelated marks formula is not used. [Concept](../Concepts/10-Discount.md#discount-and-profit).

### Q3. A dealer pays for a product listed at ₹750 after consecutive discounts of 10% and 20%, then pays ₹100 for transport. What resale price gives 15% profit on the combined expenditure?

**Difficulty:** 🔴 Difficult | **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD | **Tag:** Calculation | **Evidence:** Third-party reported exam adaptation

- **A.** ₹720
- **B.** ₹737
- **C.** ₹736
- **D.** ₹712

<details><summary>Sahi answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct answer: C. ₹736**

**Kyun sahi hai:** Purchase price 750×0.9×0.8=540. Transport add karne par total cost 640 hai. Required gain 640×0.15=96, selling price 736.

**Baaki options kyun galat hain?**

- **A.** ₹720 gives gain ₹80, only 12.5% of total cost.
- **B.** ₹737 gives gain ₹97, not exact 15%.
- **D.** ₹712 gives gain ₹72, only 11.25% of total cost.

Reported AIIMS Bhopal Nursing Officer, 23 May 2018 shift II. [Direct report inspected](https://testbook.com/question-answer/a-dealer-buys-an-article-listed-at-rs-750-and-get--62909daca4e6503523bfae0e). Pre-NORCET recruitment report, not claimed as a NORCET cycle. Allocated to discount only, not counted again in profit and loss. [Concept](../Concepts/10-Discount.md#discount-and-profit).

</details>
