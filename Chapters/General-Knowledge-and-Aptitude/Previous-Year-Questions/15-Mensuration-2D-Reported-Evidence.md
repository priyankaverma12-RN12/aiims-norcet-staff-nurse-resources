### Q1. Fencing a rectangular plot costs ₹2,200 at ₹5 per metre. Its longer side exceeds the shorter side by 20 m. Determine the length.

**Difficulty:** 🟡 Medium · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD · **🔵 DSSSB Nursing Officer, 29 August 2019, shift 3 — retrieved third-party report**

- **A.** 100 m
- **B.** 110 m
- **C.** 120 m
- **D.** 220 m

<details><summary>✅ Sahi answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct answer: C. 120 m**

Fencing ki total length 2200/5=440 m hai. Rectangle mein l+w=440/2=220 m hai. Length width se 20 m zyada hai. Isliye l−w=20. Dono equations add karne par 2l=240, toh length 120 m hai.

- **A.** 100 m shorter side hai.
- **B.** 110 m dono sides ka average hai. Rectangle ki sides equal nahi hain.
- **D.** 220 m adjacent sides ka sum hai, sirf length nahi.

**Exam trick:** Fencing cost ko per-metre rate se divide karne par perimeter milta hai, area nahi.
