### Q1. Every object of class K belongs to class L, and every object of class L belongs to class M. Class K contains at least one object. Which conclusions necessarily follow? I. All K objects belong to M. II. Some M objects belong to K.

**Difficulty:** 🟡 Medium · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD · **🔵 NVS Staff Nurse, September 2019 — retrieved report with explicit existence assumption**

- **A.** Only conclusion I
- **B.** Only conclusion II
- **C.** Both conclusions I and II
- **D.** Neither conclusion

<details><summary>✅ Sahi answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct answer: C. Both conclusions I and II**

K poora L mein hai aur L poora M mein hai. Isliye har K object M mein hai. Conclusion I follow karta hai. K mein at least one object explicitly diya hai, toh woh object M mein bhi hai. Conclusion II bhi follow karta hai.

- **A.** Yeh existence condition miss karta hai, jisse II bhi true hai.
- **B.** Yeh subset chain ko ignore karta hai, jisse I bhi true hai.
- **D.** Dono conclusions statements se guaranteed hain.

[Direct retrieved NVS report](https://testbook.com/question-answer/below-are-given-two-statements-and-two-conclusions--61e15d6f0cdb0981aa12f436). Displayed key says both conclusions follow. Original wording does not explicitly establish the existence of the innermost category. Under modern set logic only I follows without that assumption. The adaptation explicitly states existence.
