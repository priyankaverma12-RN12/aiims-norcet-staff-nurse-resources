### Q1. A computer component sells for ₹6325 with a gain of 15%. What selling price would produce a gain of 25% on the original cost?

**Difficulty:** 🟡 Medium | **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD | **Tag:** Calculation | **Evidence:** Third-party reported exam adaptation

- **A.** ₹6675
- **B.** ₹6875
- **C.** ₹6975
- **D.** ₹6775

<details><summary>Sahi answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct answer: B. ₹6875**

**Kyun sahi hai:** Present selling price 115% of cost hai. CP=6325/1.15=5500. Target price 5500×1.25=6875.

**Baaki options kyun galat hain?**

- **A.** ₹6675 gives ₹1175 gain, less than required ₹1375.
- **C.** ₹6975 gives ₹1475 gain, target se ₹100 zyada.
- **D.** ₹6775 gives ₹1275 gain, target se ₹100 kam.

Reported RRB Staff Nurse, 20 July 2019 shift I. [Inspected report](https://testbook.com/question-answer/by-selling-a-cpu-for-rs-6325-a-man-makes-a-profit--619e18407a81b38e9a3f939c). [Concept](../Concepts/09-Profit-and-Loss.md#reverse-and-changing-cost).

### Q2. A book sells for ₹1140 at a loss of 5%. Find the new selling price needed for a gain of 5%.

**Difficulty:** 🟡 Medium | **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD | **Tag:** Calculation | **Evidence:** Third-party reported variant

- **A.** ₹1260
- **B.** ₹1150
- **C.** ₹1450
- **D.** ₹1560

<details><summary>Sahi answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct answer: A. ₹1260**

**Kyun sahi hai:** ₹1140 cost ka 95% hai, so CP=1140/0.95=1200. Required selling price 1200×1.05=1260.

**Baaki options kyun galat hain?**

- **B.** ₹1150 remains below cost, isliye gain nahi hota.
- **C.** ₹1450 gives gain ₹250, not ₹60.
- **D.** ₹1560 gives gain ₹360, equivalent to 30%.

Reported ESIC Staff Nurse, 26 February 2019 shift II. [Inspected report](https://testbook.com/question-answer/a-person-incurs-5-loss-by-selling-a-book-for-rs-1--6232adcecf6d8d8a4a56d6e9). Related reverse-price family, not a second counted family. [Concept](../Concepts/09-Profit-and-Loss.md#reverse-and-changing-cost).

### Q3. A shop buys 15 dozen toys at ₹300 per dozen, then sells each toy for ₹30. What is the percentage gain on cost?

**Difficulty:** 🟡 Medium | **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD | **Tag:** Calculation | **Evidence:** Third-party reported exam adaptation

- **A.** 25%
- **B.** 12.5%
- **C.** 20%
- **D.** 33 1/3%

<details><summary>Sahi answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct answer: C. 20%**

**Kyun sahi hai:** One dozen has 12 items. Per toy cost 300/12=25. Per toy gain 30−25=5. Gain rate 5/25×100=20%.

**Baaki options kyun galat hain?**

- **A.** 25% would give ₹6.25 gain per toy instead of ₹5.
- **B.** 12.5% would give ₹3.125 gain per toy.
- **D.** 33 1/3% would give ₹8 1/3 gain per toy.

Reported DSSSB Nursing Officer, 28 August 2019 shift III. [Inspected report](https://testbook.com/question-answer/x-bought-15-dozen-toys-at-the-rate-of-rs-300-per-d--61cd68d77807bfe52df6145c). [Concept](../Concepts/09-Profit-and-Loss.md#unit-cost-and-expenses).

</details>
