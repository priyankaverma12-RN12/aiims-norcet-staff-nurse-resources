### Q1. A loan of ₹1,400 incurs ₹1,210 simple interest. The number of years equals the numerical annual percentage rate. Find the rate to two decimal places.

**Difficulty:** 🔴 Difficult · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD · Source-based adaptation

- **A.** 9.29%
- **B.** 8.64%
- **C.** 9.30%
- **D.** 86.43%

<details><summary>Sahi answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct answer: C. 9.30%**

Let rate aur time dono x ho. 1210 = 1400×x²/100, toh x²=86.428571. Positive square root approximately 9.2967 hai, jo two decimals par 9.30% hai. Source 9.29% deta hai, isliye exact rounded answer mein discrepancy hai.

- **A.** Yeh source ka reported result hai, lekin standard nearest two decimal rounding mein 9.30% aata hai.
- **B.** Yeh square root step correctly apply nahi karta.
- **D.** Yeh x² hai, x nahi.

**Reported exam:** DSSSB Nursing Officer, 28 August 2019, shift 3. Third-party question and solution inspected. Question number not stated. Disputed rounding, excluded from counted families. </details>

### Q2. At an annual simple-interest rate of 20%, an investment earns ₹480 over 3 years. Determine the initial investment.

**Difficulty:** 🟡 Medium · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD · Source-based adaptation

- **A.** ₹800
- **B.** ₹480
- **C.** ₹1,600
- **D.** ₹2,400

<details><summary>Sahi answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct answer: A. ₹800**

3 saal ka interest 60% of principal hai. 480/0.60 = 800.

- **B.** Yeh earned interest hai, principal nahi.
- **C.** 1600 ka 60% 960 hai.
- **D.** Yeh sirf rate 20% use karta hai, 3 years bhool jaata hai.

### Q3. A deposit must grow to three times its initial value at 10% annual simple interest. How long is needed?

**Difficulty:** 🟡 Medium · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD · Source-based adaptation

- **A.** 10 years
- **B.** 30 years
- **C.** 40 years
- **D.** 20 years

<details><summary>Sahi answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct answer: D. 20 years**

Triple amount mein interest original principal ka 200% hai. 200/10 = 20 years.

- **A.** 10 saal mein amount double hoga.
- **B.** Yeh 300% ko interest maan leta hai.
- **C.** 40 saal mein interest 400% hoga.

### Q4. A lender provides ₹1,500 for six years at 9% annual simple interest. Calculate the total interest earned.

**Difficulty:** 🟡 Medium · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD · Source-based adaptation

- **A.** ₹135
- **B.** ₹810
- **C.** ₹2,310
- **D.** ₹900

<details><summary>Sahi answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct answer: B. ₹810**

1500×9/100×6 = 810. Har saal 135 milta hai aur 6 saal mein 810.

- **A.** Yeh sirf annual interest hai.
- **C.** Yeh total amount hai, interest nahi.
- **D.** Yeh 10% rate se calculate karta hai, 9% se nahi.
