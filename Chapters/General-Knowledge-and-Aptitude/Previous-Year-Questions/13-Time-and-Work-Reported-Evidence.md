### Q1. Worker A takes 42 days to complete a job. After A works alone for 21 days, B joins at three times A's work rate. How much additional time is required?

**Difficulty:** 🔴 Difficult · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD · Source-based adaptation

- **A.** 26.25 days
- **B.** 10.5 days
- **C.** 5.25 days
- **D.** 7 days

<details><summary>Sahi answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct answer: C. 5.25 days**

A ne 21/42=1/2 work complete kiya. B ka rate 3/42 hai, toh combined rate 4/42=2/21 hai. Remaining half ko divide karne par (1/2)÷(2/21)=21/4=5.25 days milta hai.

- **A.** Yeh total time from start hai, additional time nahi.
- **B.** Yeh dono ko poore work par saath lagaane ka time hai.
- **D.** Yeh remaining half ko B alone ke rate par calculate karta hai.

**Reported exam:** DSSSB Nursing Officer, 28 August 2019, shift 3. The source wording is ambiguous about total versus additional time. This adaptation explicitly asks for remaining time after joining. [Direct source](https://testbook.com/question-answer/a-can-do-a-piece-of-work-in-42-days-and-b-is-thric--61cd65ce9a0942b48d971312). ### Q2. Ten workers of group X finish a job in six days, and fifteen workers of group Y finish the same job in five days. Find the time taken by eight X workers and five Y workers together.

**Difficulty:** 🔴 Difficult · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD · Source-based adaptation

- **A.** 5 days
- **B.** 6 days
- **C.** 7 days
- **D.** 4 days

<details><summary>Sahi answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct answer: A. 5 days**

X worker ka rate 1/60 work/day hai aur Y ka 1/75. Combined rate 8/60+5/75=1/5 hai. Poora kaam 5 days mein hoga.

- **B.** Yeh original ten-X crew ka time hai.
- **C.** 7 days mein combined crew ek se zyada job ka kaam karegi.
- **D.** 4 days mein combined crew sirf 4/5 kaam karegi.

**Reported exam:** MPESB Nursing Officer, 25 November 2022, shift 2. Third-party question and solution directly retrieved. Worker categories are abstract arithmetic groups, not gender-based assumptions. [Direct source](https://testbook.com/question-answer/if-10-men-can-do-a-work-in-6-days-and-15-women-can--677e3a055e657b51f0875078). Official answer-key authentication pending.

### Q3. A worker completes one eighth of a job in four days and then leaves. A second worker finishes the remaining seven eighths in sixteen days. How long would both workers take if they worked together from the start?

**Difficulty:** 🔴 Difficult · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD · Source-based adaptation

- **A.** 16 days
- **B.** 20 days
- **C.** 32 days
- **D.** 11 7/11 days

<details><summary>Sahi answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct answer: D. 11 7/11 days**

First rate (1/8)/4=1/32 hai. Second rate (7/8)/16=7/128 hai. Dono ka rate 4/128+7/128=11/128 hai. Time 128/11=11 7/11 days hai.

- **A.** Yeh second worker ka remaining work ka time hai, together time nahi.
- **B.** Yeh actual sequential schedule ka total hai.
- **C.** Yeh first worker ka individual completion time hai.

**Reported exam:** RRB Nursing Superintendent, 29 April 2025, shift 1, Q24. Third-party hosted paper extracted text retrieved. Screenshot failed. Official key not independently authenticated.
