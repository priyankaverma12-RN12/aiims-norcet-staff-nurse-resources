## Further topic-specific reported practice, reserved for time and work

DSSSB Nursing Officer, 28 August 2019, shift 1, reported Q163, adapted from [NextNurse paper transcript](https://nextnurse.in/pyq/dsssb-nursing-officer/dsssb-nursing-officer-dsssb-28-august-2019-shift-1).

One worker can finish a job in 16 days, another in 24 days. After working together for four days, what fraction of the job remains?

- A. 5/6
- B. 7/12
- C. 5/12
- D. 3/4

<details><summary>Answer and explanation</summary>

**B. 7/12.** Per day total 1/16+1/24=5/48 hai. Four days mein 20/48=5/12 complete hota hai. Remaining 1−5/12=7/12. A actual remaining work se greater hai. C completed fraction hai. D sirf first worker ka contribution count karta hai. This adaptation is cross-topic and reserved for the time-and-work inventory. It is not counted again under fractions.

</details>
