### Q1. A traveller starts at O and follows a route of 10 km south, 5 km west, 6 km north and 5 km east. What straight distance separates the endpoint and O?

**Difficulty:** 🟡 Medium · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD · **🔵 RRB Staff Nurse, 20 July 2019 shift III — retrieved report**

- **A.** 4 km
- **B.** 6 km
- **C.** 16 km
- **D.** 26 km

<details><summary>✅ Sahi answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct answer: A. 4 km**

East-west displacement 5−5=0 hai. South 10 km mein se north 6 km cancel hone par 4 km south bachta hai. Straight distance 4 km hai.

- **B.** Yeh northward return leg hai, net displacement nahi.
- **C.** North aur south legs ko add karta hai, cancel nahi karta.
- **D.** Yeh total path length hai.

[Direct reported question and solution](https://testbook.com/question-answer/a-man-walks-10-km-in-the-south-direction-he-takes--61adf4550d952e26969ea952). Retrieved 27 November 2026. Source key 4 km independently recalculated. Original wording and options adapted.

### Q2. Starting at a reference point, a walker travels east for 3 km, then north for 4 km, and finally west for 6 km. What is the endpoint's straight-line separation and quadrant from the starting point?

**Difficulty:** 🟡 Medium · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD · **🔵 AIIMS Bhopal Nursing Officer, 23 May 2018 shift II — retrieved report**

- **A.** 5 km north-east
- **B.** 7 km north-west
- **C.** 5 km north-west
- **D.** 13 km north-west

<details><summary>✅ Sahi answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct answer: C. 5 km north-west**

Net horizontal movement 3−6=−3 km, yani west. Net vertical movement +4 km north hai. Distance √(3²+4²)=5 km aur quadrant NW hai.

- **A.** Magnitude sahi hai lekin horizontal direction reverse hai.
- **B.** Yeh axis components ka sum hai, shortest distance nahi.
- **D.** Yeh complete route ki length hai.

[Direct reported question and solution](https://testbook.com/question-answer/shaurya-walks-3-km-towards-the-east-and-them-turns--6294b09d8cc52148e743a413). Retrieved 27 November 2026. Source key agrees with calculation. Official key pending.

### Q3. Assume that the evening Sun is exactly due west. A and D face each other on a north-south line. A's shadow extends directly toward D's right-hand side. Which direction does D face?

**Difficulty:** 🟡 Medium · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD · **🔵 AIIMS NORCET, 1 September 2020 — memory-based report, explanation corrected**

- **A.** East
- **B.** South
- **C.** West
- **D.** North

<details><summary>✅ Sahi answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct answer: D. North**

Sun west mein hai, toh A ki shadow east extend hogi. D ke right par east hai. Right side east tab hoti hai jab D north face kare. Isliye D north aur A south face karta hai.

- **A.** East face karne par right south hoti hai.
- **B.** South face karne par right west hoti hai.
- **C.** West face karne par right north hoti hai.
