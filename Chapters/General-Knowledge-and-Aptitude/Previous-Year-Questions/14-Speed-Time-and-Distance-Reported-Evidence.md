### Q1. At a constant speed of 60 km/h, how many minutes does a railway vehicle take to cover 2 km?

**Difficulty:** 🟢 Easy · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD · Source-based adaptation

- **A.** 1 minute
- **B.** 2 minutes
- **C.** 3 minutes
- **D.** 4 minutes

<details><summary>Sahi answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct answer: B. 2 minutes**

60 km/h ka matlab har minute 1 km hai. Isliye 2 km ke liye 2 minutes chahiye.

- **A.** Is time mein sirf 1 km cover hoga.
- **C.** Is time mein 3 km cover hoga.
- **D.** Is time mein 4 km cover hoga.

### Q2. A train measuring 130 m travels at 45 km/h. Its front reaches a bridge and 30 seconds later its rear clears it. How long is the bridge?

**Difficulty:** 🟡 Medium · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD · Source-based adaptation

- **A.** 375 m
- **B.** 130 m
- **C.** 245 m
- **D.** 505 m

<details><summary>Sahi answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct answer: C. 245 m**

Speed 45×5/18=12.5 m/s hai. Front 30 seconds mein 375 m chalta hai. Ismein train ki 130 m length bhi included hai. Bridge 375−130=245 m hai.

- **A.** Yeh combined train aur bridge length hai.
- **B.** Yeh train ki length hai, bridge ki nahi.
- **D.** Yeh train length ko subtract karne ki jagah add karta hai.

**Reported exam:** AIIMS NORCET, 20 November 2021, memory-based report. Third-party memory-based question and solution retrieved. No authenticated exact paper or official key. [Direct source](https://testbook.com/question-answer/the-length-of-the-bridge-which-a-train-130-m-long--5fad224354f2b8acb79c1199).

</details>

### Q3. A train needs 20 seconds to clear a pole and 36 seconds to clear a 200 m bridge, maintaining the same speed. How long would it take to travel 108 km?

**Difficulty:** 🔴 Difficult · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD · Source-based adaptation

- **A.** 2.4 hours
- **B.** 3.2 hours
- **C.** 2 hours
- **D.** 3.6 hours

<details><summary>Sahi answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct answer: A. 2.4 hours**

Bridge ke extra 200 m ke liye 36−20=16 seconds lagte hain. Speed 200/16=12.5 m/s, ya 45 km/h hai. Time 108/45=2.4 hours hai.

- **B.** Yeh 33.75 km/h ka speed assume karta hai, given crossing times se nahi milta.
- **C.** Yeh speed 54 km/h assume karta hai.
- **D.** Yeh speed 30 km/h assume karta hai.

### Q4. Two 100 m trains cross completely in 60 seconds when travelling in the same direction and in 3 seconds when travelling in opposite directions. Calculate the faster train's speed in km/h.

**Difficulty:** 🔴 Difficult · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD · Source-based adaptation

- **A.** 114 km/h
- **B.** 240 km/h
- **C.** 12 km/h
- **D.** 126 km/h

<details><summary>Sahi answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct answer: D. 126 km/h**

Combined length 200 m hai. Sum of speeds 200/3 m/s hai. Difference 200/60=10/3 m/s hai. Faster speed (200/3+10/3)/2=35 m/s hai. 35×3.6=126 km/h hai.

- **A.** Yeh slower train ki speed hai, faster ki nahi.
- **B.** Yeh sum of both speeds hai.
- **C.** Yeh difference of both speeds hai.

**Reported exam:** MPESB Nursing Officer, 25 November 2022, shift 1. Third-party question and solution directly retrieved. Official key not independently authenticated. [Direct source](https://testbook.com/question-answer/two-trains-each-100-m-long-pass-each-other-on-pa--677e265f20c58e057d82e733).
