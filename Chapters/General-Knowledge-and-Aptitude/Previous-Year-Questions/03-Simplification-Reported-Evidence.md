## RRB Nursing Superintendent — 12 March 2026, shift 3 (reported)

Evaluate the following expression, with the product in the last denominator explicitly grouped:

( (17/21) ÷ (17/3) ) ÷ ( (2/6) × (12/6) + 2/7 ) + (3/7) ÷ ( (20/12) × (12/7) ).

- A. 3/10
- B. 4/9
- C. 2/15
- D. 10/7

<details><summary>Answer and explanation</summary>

**A.** First nested quotient 1/7 hai. Next bracket 2/3+2/7=20/21 hai, so first term 3/20 hai. Last denominator 20/7 hai, so last term bhi 3/20 hai. Total 6/20=3/10. B, C aur D in two terms ka correct sum nahi hain. Grouping original third-party solution ke interpretation ko explicit banati hai.

- A. 14

## DSSSB Nursing Officer — 29 August 2019, shift 2, reported Q92 (adapted to clarify domain)

For a nonzero number x, 80x² = (4x)³. Find x.

- A. 1
- B. 1.25
- C. 0.8
- D. 1.2

<details><summary>Answer and explanation</summary>

**B.** Right side 64x³ hai. Since x nonzero hai, x² divide kar sakte hain. 80=64x, so x=80/64=1.25. A gives 64 on the reduced right side. C gives 51.2. D gives 76.8. None equals 80. Without the nonzero condition, x=0 bhi solution hota. That condition was added transparently to repair ambiguity in the reported wording.

</details>
