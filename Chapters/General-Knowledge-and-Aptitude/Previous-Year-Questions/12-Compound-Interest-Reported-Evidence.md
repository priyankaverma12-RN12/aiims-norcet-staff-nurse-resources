### Q1. At 8% annual compounding, a deposit is worth ₹2,916 after two years. Determine its starting principal.

**Difficulty:** 🟡 Medium · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD · Source-based adaptation

- **A.** ₹2,700
- **B.** ₹2,500
- **C.** ₹2,916
- **D.** ₹2,400

<details><summary>Sahi answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct answer: B. ₹2,500**

Two years ka factor 1.08² = 1.1664 hai. Principal 2916/1.1664 = 2500.

- **A.** Yeh one-year reverse factor use karta hai.
- **C.** Yeh maturity balance hai.
- **D.** 2400×1.1664 = 2799.36 hai, given amount nahi.

### Q2. A loan of ₹2,000 accrues interest at 10% per year, compounded annually. Calculate interest alone after three years.

**Difficulty:** 🟡 Medium · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD · Source-based adaptation

- **A.** ₹600
- **B.** ₹200
- **C.** ₹2,662
- **D.** ₹662

<details><summary>Sahi answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct answer: D. ₹662**

2000×1.1³ = 2662 total amount hai. Principal subtract karne par 662 milta hai.

- **A.** Yeh three-year simple interest hai.
- **B.** Yeh first-year interest hai.
- **C.** Yeh total amount hai.

### Q3. A ₹52,500 investment receives 8% nominal annual interest with six-monthly compounding. What balance is obtained at one year?

**Difficulty:** 🟡 Medium · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD · Source-based adaptation

- **A.** ₹56,784
- **B.** ₹56,700
- **C.** ₹4,284
- **D.** ₹54,600

<details><summary>Sahi answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct answer: A. ₹56,784**

Har six-month period ka rate 4% hai. Do periods ke baad 52500×1.04² = 56784.

- **B.** Yeh annual simple interest ya one-period annual compounding use karta hai.
- **C.** Yeh interest alone hai, balance nahi.
- **D.** Yeh one half-year ka balance hai.
