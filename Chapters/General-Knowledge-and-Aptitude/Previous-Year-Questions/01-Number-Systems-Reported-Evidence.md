## RRB Staff Nurse — 20 July 2019, shift I (reported)

An integer N can be expressed as 385k + 54 for some integer k. Which remainder results on dividing N by 55?

- A. 1
- B. 54
- C. 7
- D. 0

<details><summary>Answer and explanation</summary>

**B.** 385 = 7 × 55. So N = 55(7k)+54. Remainder 54 hai because 54 < 55. A complementary difference hai, remainder nahi. C multiplier hai. D tab hota if remainder zero hota.

## RRB JPR Staff Nurse — 6 June 2018 (reported, related method variant)

A remainder of 47 is obtained when dividing N by 342. If 342 is divisible by 18, what is the remainder of N on division by 18?

- A. 47
- B. 18
- C. 11
- D. 2

<details><summary>Answer and explanation</summary>

**C.** 342 = 19 × 18, so original remainder ko 18 se divide karo. 47 = 2×18+11. A final remainder divisor se greater hai. B remainder divisor ke equal nahi ho sakta. D quotient hai.
