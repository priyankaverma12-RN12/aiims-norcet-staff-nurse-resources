### Q1. Five trainees K, L, M, N and P sit in a north-facing row. K occupies seat 1 and L occupies seat 4. M is immediately adjacent to both L and N. Who occupies seat 5?

**Difficulty:** 🟡 Medium · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD · **⚪ HPRCA Assistant Staff Nurse topic collection — source-based practice, actual exam appearance unverified**

- **A.** N
- **B.** P
- **C.** M
- **D.** K

<details><summary>✅ Sahi answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct answer: B. P**

L seat 4 par hai. M seat 3 ya 5 par ho sakta hai. Agar M seat 5 par ho, uska only neighbour L hoga, isliye N adjacent nahi ho sakta. Toh M seat 3 par hai aur N seat 2 par hai. K seat 1 par hai. P ke liye seat 5 bachi hai.

- **A.** N seat 2 par hona chahiye, taaki M ke dono neighbours N aur L ho sakein.
- **C.** M seat 5 par dono required neighbours nahi rakh sakta.
- **D.** K seat 1 par fixed hai.
