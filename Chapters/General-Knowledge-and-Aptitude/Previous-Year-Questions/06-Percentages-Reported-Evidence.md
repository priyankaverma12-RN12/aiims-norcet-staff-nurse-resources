### Q1. Two workers together complete 110 units of work. The first completes 20% more units than the second. How many units does the second complete?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Priority:** High

- **A.** 60
- **B.** 50
- **C.** 44
- **D.** 55

<details><summary>Sahi answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct answer: B. 50**

**Kyun sahi hai:** Second worker x units complete karta hai. First 1.2x complete karta hai. Total 2.2x=110, x=50.

**Baaki options kyun galat hain?**

- **A.** 60 first worker ka share hai.
- **C.** 44 par total 96.8 hoga, not 110.
- **D.** Equal shares assume karta hai, jabki first worker ka work greater hai.

</details>

AIIMS Delhi Nursing Officer, 15 September 2019, memory-based report. [Question source](https://testbook.com/question-answer/person-a-work-20-more-than-person-b-total-works--62c249f8e5943685a4e34863). Also indexed in [NORCET aptitude practice](https://testbook.com/questions/aiims-norcet-quantitative-aptitude-questions--64d6328f204757d2fac2f395/amp). Do not count as separate NORCET cycle PYQ or assign again under ratios.

### Q2. A cricketer makes 160 runs, including 24 fours and 8 sixes. What proportion of the total runs comes from those boundaries, as a percent?

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Priority:** High

- **A.** 90%
- **B.** 60%
- **C.** 30%
- **D.** 80%

<details><summary>Sahi answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct answer: A. 90%**

**Kyun sahi hai:** Fours contribute 96 runs and sixes contribute 48 runs. Combined 144. Required 144/160×100=90%.

**Baaki options kyun galat hain?**

- **B.** 96/160=60%, only fours include kiye.
- **C.** 48/160=30%, only sixes include kiye.
- **D.** 80% means 128 runs, actual 144 nahi.

</details>

DSSSB Nursing Officer, 27 August 2019 shift 3, reported. [Question source](https://testbook.com/question-answer/a-batsman-scored-160-runs-in-a-cricket-match-he-h--61d3f151153e280f5817e786).

### Q3. When 30% of a number is subtracted from that same number, 49 remains. Find the number.

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Priority:** High

- **A.** 63
- **B.** 70
- **C.** 49
- **D.** 163 1/3

<details><summary>Sahi answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct answer: B. 70**

**Kyun sahi hai:** Original x maan lo. x−0.30x=0.70x=49. Divide by 0.70, x=70.

**Baaki options kyun galat hain?**

- **A.** 70% of 63 is 44.1.
- **C.** 49 remaining quantity hai, original nahi.
- **D.** 49÷0.3 gives 163 1/3, but 49 is 70% of original.

</details>

OSSSC Nursing Officer, 19 March 2023, reported. [Question source](https://testbook.com/question-answer/a-number-exceeds-30-of-itself-by-49-the-number-i--6a3baa79078b79a3c418e514).

### Q4. Taking three fifths of 60% of an unknown number gives 36. What is the original number?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Priority:** High

- **A.** 60
- **B.** 90
- **C.** 100
- **D.** 36

<details><summary>Sahi answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct answer: C. 100**

**Kyun sahi hai:** 60%=3/5. Nested factors multiply: 3/5×3/5=9/25. So 9x/25=36, x=36×25/9=100.

**Baaki options kyun galat hain?**

- **A.** 60×9/25=21.6, not 36.
- **B.** 90×9/25=32.4, not 36.
- **D.** 36 final result hai, original number nahi.

</details>

OSSSC Nursing Officer, 7 February 2021, reported. [Question source](https://testbook.com/question-answer/if-three-fifthleft-frac35-right-nbsp--627a1c1703917b73127ce87e).

### Q5. If positive number A is 50% greater than positive number B, by what percentage is B below A?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Priority:** High

- **A.** 16 2/3%
- **B.** 50%
- **C.** 33 1/3%
- **D.** 20%

<details><summary>Sahi answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct answer: C. 33 1/3%**

**Kyun sahi hai:** B=100, A=150. B is 50 units smaller. A=150 is the requested base. 50÷150×100=100/3%=33 1/3%.

**Baaki options kyun galat hain?**

- **A.** 16 2/3% is an arithmetic error in the third-party source.
- **B.** 50% compares against B, not A.
- **D.** 20% of 150 is 30, not 50.

</details>

OSSSC Nursing Officer, 19 March 2023, [reported question with inconsistent key](https://testbook.com/question-answer/if-a-number-is-50-more-than-the-other-how-much-p--6a3baa752d1b3d4115767954). Retrieved report lists 16 2/3% as correct while its own formula produces 33 1/3%. Mathematical correction established independently. **Disputed source key, excluded from counted PYQs pending official scan/key.** Do not memorize the incorrect reported key.
