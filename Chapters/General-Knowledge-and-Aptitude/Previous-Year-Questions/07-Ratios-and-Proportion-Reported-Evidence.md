### Q1. A school has 4500 students. Girls and boys are in the ratio 49:51. If only girls are added, how many must join for the two groups to have equal counts?

**Difficulty:** 🟡 Medium | **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD | **Tag:** Calculation | **Evidence:** Third-party reported exam adaptation

- **A.** 86
- **B.** 92
- **C.** 90
- **D.** 88

<details><summary>Sahi answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct answer: C. 90**

**Kyun sahi hai:** Total ratio parts 49+51=100 hain. One part=4500/100=45. Girls 49×45=2205 aur boys 51×45=2295 hain. Boys unchanged hain, so 2295−2205=90 girls add karne se counts equal ho jayenge.

**Baaki options kyun galat hain?**

- **A.** 86 add karne se girls 2291 hongi, jo boys se four fewer hain.
- **B.** 92 add karne se girls 2297 hongi, jo boys se two more hain.
- **D.** 88 add karne se girls 2293 hongi, jo boys se two fewer hain.

Reported RRB Staff Nurse, 21 July 2019 shift I. [Direct question report inspected](https://testbook.com/question-answer/the-ratio-of-number-of-girls-to-boys-in-a-school-o--61a9ba8b9a5a4b760e13a44b). The report has a stray inconsistent 35x/37x line, but its actual calculation and answer 90 are consistent with the stem. [Concept](../Concepts/07-Ratios-and-Proportion.md#combined-ratios-and-changing-counts).

### Q2. A school initially has 800 students with boys-to-girls ratio 3:2. After 400 additional students enroll, the ratio becomes 13:7. How many of the new students are boys?

**Difficulty:** 🔴 Difficult | **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD | **Tag:** Calculation | **Evidence:** Provisional indexed transcription adaptation

- **A.** 320
- **B.** 280
- **C.** 350
- **D.** 300

<details><summary>Sahi answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct answer: D. 300**

**Kyun sahi hai:** Initially boys 800×3/5=480 hain. Final total 1200 aur boys fraction 13/20 hai, so final boys=780. New boys=780−480=300.

**Baaki options kyun galat hain?**

- **A.** 320 new boys se final boys 800 honge, required 780 nahi.
- **B.** 280 new boys se final boys 760 honge, required 780 nahi.
- **C.** 350 new boys se final boys 830 honge, given ratio se match nahi karta.

Reported DSSSB Nursing Officer, 29 August 2019 shift 1, indexed Q70. [Indexed paper transcript](https://nextnurse.in/pyq/dsssb-nursing-officer/dsssb-nursing-officer-dsssb-29-august-2019-shift-1). ### Q3. A bucket holds exactly 25 full mugs of water. A replacement mug has two fifths of the original mug capacity. How many new-mug equivalents fill the same bucket, allowing a partial mug?

**Difficulty:** 🔴 Difficult | **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD | **Tag:** Calculation | **Evidence:** Provisional indexed transcription adaptation

- **A.** 62.5
- **B.** 57.5
- **C.** 60
- **D.** 50

<details><summary>Sahi answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct answer: A. 62.5**

**Kyun sahi hai:** Old mug capacity v maan lo. Bucket capacity 25v hai. New mug capacity 2v/5 hai. Required count 25v ÷ (2v/5)=25×5/2=62.5. Meaning 62 full mugs plus half a mug. Stem explicitly allows partial mug.

**Baaki options kyun galat hain?**

- **B.** 57.5 mugs only 23 old mug equivalents bharte hain.
- **C.** 60 mugs only 24 old mug equivalents bharte hain.
- **D.** 50 mugs only 20 old mug equivalents bharte hain.

Reported DSSSB Nursing Officer, 29 August 2019 shift 1, indexed Q63. [Indexed paper transcript](https://nextnurse.in/pyq/dsssb-nursing-officer/dsssb-nursing-officer-dsssb-29-august-2019-shift-1). Direct retrieval failed, not counted as confirmed historical evidence. [Concept](../Concepts/07-Ratios-and-Proportion.md#direct-and-inverse-variation).

</details>
