## 1. UPPSC Staff Nurse — 10 April 2022 (reported)

Two numbers have an LCM three times their HCF. Their HCF and LCM add to 120. If the larger number is 90, find the smaller.

- A. 30
- B. 40
- C. 60
- D. 120

<details><summary>Answer and explanation</summary>

**A.** HCF=h and LCM=3h. 4h=120, so h=30 and LCM=90. Product=30×90, smaller=30. B and C do not give required product. D is not the smaller number.

## 2. DSSSB Nursing Officer — 28 August 2019, shift III (reported)

Find the largest divisor that gives identical remainders for 36, 64 and 92.

- A. 14
- B. 7
- C. 28
- D. 56

<details><summary>Answer and explanation</summary>

**C.** Differences 28, 28, 56. HCF 28 hai, all three remainders 8 hain. A and B common divisors but smaller. D does not divide difference 28.

## 3. RRB Staff Nurse — 20 July 2019, shift III (reported)

Two alarms that repeat at 63-second and 74-second intervals signal at the same instant. Find the next interval until they signal together.

- A. 137 seconds
- B. 2331 seconds
- C. 9324 seconds
- D. 4662 seconds

<details><summary>Answer and explanation</summary>

**D.** 63 and 74 coprime hain, so LCM=63×74=4662. A sum hai. B half of the LCM is not divisible by 74. C later coincidence hai, first nahi.

[Retrieved RRB report](https://testbook.com/question-answer/two-bells-ring-at-intervals-of-63-seconds-and-74-s--61adf18d82a378b9116cbfa7). ## 4. OSSSC Nursing Officer — 19 March 2023 (reported)

Which is the largest four-digit common multiple of 15, 20 and 25?

- A. 9000
- B. 9900
- C. 9990
- D. 9999

<details><summary>Answer and explanation</summary>

**B.** LCM=300. Floor(9999/300)=33 and 33×300=9900. A is a smaller common multiple. C and D are not divisible by 300.

[Retrieved OSSSC report](https://testbook.com/question-answer/the-greatest-4-digit-number-exactly-divisible-by-1--6a3baa77cc80799643944492)

</details>
