### Q1. Three workers have pairwise average monthly earnings as follows: P and Q ₹3200, Q and R ₹3600, and R and P ₹3500. What is their combined monthly income?

**Difficulty:** 🔴 Difficult | **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD | **Tag:** Calculation | **Evidence:** Third-party reported exam adaptation

- **A.** ₹9700
- **B.** ₹10300
- **C.** ₹10500
- **D.** ₹10700

<details><summary>Sahi answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct answer: B. ₹10300**

**Kyun sahi hai:** P+Q=6400, Q+R=7200 aur R+P=7000 hain. Inko add karne par each worker ki income twice count hoti hai. Total 20600/2=10300 hai. Cross-check ke liye individual amounts 3100, 3300 aur 3900 hain.

**Baaki options kyun galat hain?**

- **A.** 9700 ka double 19400 hai, pairwise sums ka total 20600 nahi.
- **C.** 10500 ka double 21000 hai, given pair sums satisfy nahi hote.
- **D.** 10700 ka double 21400 hai, actual sum se 800 zyada hai.

### Q2. Which statistic is calculated by dividing the sum of all numeric observations by their number?

**Difficulty:** 🟢 Easy | **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD | **Tag:** Concept | **Evidence:** Third-party reported exam adaptation, cross-topic allocation reserved

- **A.** Mode
- **B.** Standard deviation
- **C.** Arithmetic mean
- **D.** Median

<details><summary>Sahi answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct answer: C. Arithmetic mean**

**Kyun sahi hai:** Arithmetic mean mein all observations add karke count se divide karte hain. Example 2,4,9 ka total 15 aur mean 5 hai.

**Baaki options kyun galat hain?**

- **A.** Mode sabse frequently occurring value hota hai, sum/count nahi.
- **B.** Standard deviation values ka spread measure karta hai, central value ka arithmetic formula nahi.
- **D.** Median sorted dataset ki middle position se milta hai. Example 2,4,9 mein median 4 hai, mean 5 hai.

Reported RRB Staff Nurse, 20 July 2019 shift II. [Direct question report inspected](https://testbook.com/question-answer/the-arithmetic-average-which-is-obtained-by-suming--61a8bc77a468cbb793fc1034). ### Q3. Which measure of central tendency is obtained using every numeric value in the dataset, by summing and dividing by count?

**Difficulty:** 🟢 Easy | **Priority:** 🟡 IMPORTANT | **Tag:** Concept | **Evidence:** Clarified practice from ambiguous memory-based report, excluded from historical count

- **A.** Median
- **B.** Arithmetic mean
- **C.** Mode
- **D.** Standard deviation

<details><summary>Sahi answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct answer: B. Arithmetic mean**

**Kyun sahi hai:** Sum/count formula arithmetic mean define karta hai. Har numeric observation is calculation mein contribute karta hai.

**Baaki options kyun galat hain?**

- **A.** Median ordered middle value hai. Ye bhi central tendency hai, but given formula nahi.
- **C.** Mode most frequent value hai. Ye bhi central tendency hai, but sum/count nahi.
- **D.** Standard deviation dispersion measure hai.

[AIIMS Raipur Nursing Officer memory-based report, 31 August 2019 shift II](https://testbook.com/question-answer/average-that-is-used-to-derive-the-central-tendenc--62c40e48b3883eeb89d5f8d6) was directly inspected. Its loose central-tendency wording has multiple defensible options, since median and mode also measure center. Stem deliberately clarified here. Do not memorize the reported answer without the mathematical distinction. Similar concept to Q2, excluded from distinct historical count.
