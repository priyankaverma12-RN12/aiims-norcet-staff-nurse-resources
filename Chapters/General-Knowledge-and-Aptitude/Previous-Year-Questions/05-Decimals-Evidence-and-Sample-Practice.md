### Q1. Round 11.047 to one decimal place.

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Priority:** High

- **A.** 11.1
- **B.** 11.0
- **C.** 11.2
- **D.** 11.05

<details><summary>Sahi answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct answer: B. 11.0**

**Kyun sahi hai:** Tenths digit zero hai. Hundredths digit 4 hai, jo 5 se chhota hai. Tenths unchanged rehta hai, so 11.0.

**Baaki options kyun galat hain?**

- **A.** 11.1 tab aata agar hundredths 5 ya greater hota.
- **C.** 11.2 nearest tenth nahi hai.
- **D.** 11.05 nearest hundredth hai, not tenth.

</details>

### Q2. Express one minute as a decimal portion of a 24-hour day, rounded to five decimal places.

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Priority:** High

- **A.** 0.00694
- **B.** 0.00062
- **C.** 0.00069
- **D.** 0.00072

<details><summary>Sahi answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct answer: C. 0.00069**

**Kyun sahi hai:** A day has 24×60=1440 minutes. Required part 1/1440=0.000694444... . Five decimal places par 0.00069 aata hai.

**Baaki options kyun galat hain?**

- **A.** Decimal position tenfold greater hai.
- **B.** 0.00062 is not the rounded quotient of 1/1440.
- **D.** 0.00072 actual ratio se greater hai.

</details>

Reported DSSSB Nursing Officer, 29 August 2019 shift 2, Q150, [NextNurse transcript](https://nextnurse.in/pyq/dsssb-nursing-officer/dsssb-nursing-officer-dsssb-29-august-2019-shift-2). Precision explicitly specified in adaptation. Official key pending.

### Q3. Convert five thousandths to a decimal.

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Priority:** High

- **A.** 0.05
- **B.** 0.005
- **C.** 5
- **D.** 0.0005

<details><summary>Sahi answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct answer: B. 0.005**

**Kyun sahi hai:** Five thousandths means 5/1000. Decimal point ke baad third position par 5 hai.

**Baaki options kyun galat hain?**

- **A.** 0.05 five hundredths hai.
- **C.** 5 five whole units hai.
- **D.** 0.0005 five ten-thousandths hai.

</details>
