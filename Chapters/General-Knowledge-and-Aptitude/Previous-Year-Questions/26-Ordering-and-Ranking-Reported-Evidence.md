### Q1. A participant occupies the thirteenth position when a row is counted from either end. How many other people stand in that row?

**Difficulty:** 🟡 Medium · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD · **🔵 DSSSB Nursing Officer, 29 August 2019, Shift III — retrieved report, adapted question**

- **A.** 24
- **B.** 25
- **C.** 26
- **D.** 12

<details><summary>✅ Sahi answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct answer: A. 24**

Same person's opposite ranks 13 aur 13 hain. Total 13 + 13 − 1 = 25 hai. Adaptation specifically other people poochti hai, toh target participant ko exclude karo. 25 − 1 = 24.

- **B.** Yeh total people hain, other people nahi.
- **C.** Target ko twice count kar diya.
- **D.** Sirf ek side ke people count kiye.

[Direct DSSSB report](https://testbook.com/question-answer/if-you-are-13th-from-both-the-sides-in-a-row-then--61cc110864b39a2025133fda). Direct text retrieved 27 November 2026. Original report asks total, key 25. Adaptation asks other people, hence key 24. No claim of verbatim wording or official key authentication.

### Q2. A is sixth from the left, and B is ninth from the right. They exchange positions, after which A is nineteenth from the left. What is the original number of people standing strictly between A and B?

**Difficulty:** 🟡 Medium · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD · **🔵 NVS Staff Nurse, 12 January 2018 — retrieved report, adapted question**

- **A.** 13
- **B.** 27
- **C.** 12
- **D.** 14

<details><summary>✅ Sahi answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct answer: C. 12**

A swap ke baad B ki original position par hai. Isliye B ki original left position 19 thi. A originally 6th tha. Rank difference 19 − 6 = 13 hai. Strictly between mein endpoints exclude hote hain, toh 13 − 1 = 12.

- **A.** Rank difference hai, intervening people nahi.
- **B.** Row total 19 + 9 − 1 = 27 hai, requested count nahi.
- **D.** Inclusive count hai, dono endpoints include karta hai.

[Direct NVS report](https://testbook.com/question-answer/in-a-row-of-boys-rajesh-is-sixth-from-the-left-an--61fbccb295a998b7246a2634). Direct text retrieved 27 November 2026. Original report asks total and gives 27. Adaptation tests a different consequence of the same source information. Official answer key not authenticated.
