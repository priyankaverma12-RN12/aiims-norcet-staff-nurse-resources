### Q1. A company allocates 15% of its total project expenditure to Project A, 25% to Project B and 35% to Project D. Project B receives ₹60,000 more than Project A. What amount is allocated to Project D?

**Difficulty:** 🟡 Medium · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD · **🔵 DSSSB Nursing Officer, 27 August 2019, shift 3 — retrieved third-party report**

| Project | Share of total expenditure |
|---|---:|
| A | 15% |
| B | 25% |
| D | 35% |
| Other projects combined | 25% |

- **A.** ₹600,000
- **B.** ₹120,000
- **C.** ₹210,000
- **D.** ₹50,000

<details><summary>✅ Sahi answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct answer: C. ₹210,000**

Project B aur A ke percentage shares ka difference 25−15=10 percentage points hai. Ye total expenditure ka 10% represent karta hai. Given difference ₹60,000 hai. Isliye total expenditure 60000/0.10=₹600,000 hai. Project D ka share 35% hai, toh expenditure 600000×0.35=₹210,000 hai.

- **A.** ₹600,000 poori company ka project expenditure hai, sirf D ka nahi.
- **B.** ₹120,000 total ka 20% hai, jabki D ka share 35% hai.
- **D.** ₹50,000 neither total expenditure nor Project D ke 35% share ke barabar hai. Is value ko 10% difference se derive nahi kar sakte.

**Exam trick:** Known absolute difference ko corresponding fractional difference se divide karke total recover karo. Fir requested sector ka share multiply karo.
