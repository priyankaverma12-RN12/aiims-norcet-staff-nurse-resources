### Q1. A tank with no lid measures 31 m by 20 m internally and is 22 m deep. Find the internal area of its base and four walls.

**Difficulty:** 🟡 Medium · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD · **🔵 DSSSB Nursing Officer, 14 August 2024, shift 3 — reported arithmetic Q9**

- **A.** 2864 m²
- **B.** 3484 m²
- **C.** 2244 m²
- **D.** 620 m²

<details><summary>✅ Sahi answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct answer: A. 2864 m²**

Base area 31×20=620 m² hai. Four walls ka area 2×22×(31+20)=2244 m² hai. Top open hai, toh base plus walls 620+2244=2864 m² hain.

- **B.** Ismein lid ka 620 m² bhi include hai, jabki tank open hai.
- **C.** Yeh sirf four walls ka area hai.
- **D.** Yeh sirf bottom base ka area hai.

**Exam trick:** Open-top tank mein ek base count hota hai. Internal dimensions se internal area calculate karo.

### Q2. A closed solid cylinder occupies 80,850 cm³ and is 21 cm high. Taking π = 22/7, calculate its entire external surface area.

**Difficulty:** 🔴 Difficult · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD · **🔵 RRB Nursing Superintendent, 12 March 2026, shift 2 — reported General Ability Q1**

- **A.** 4620 cm²
- **B.** 7700 cm²
- **C.** 12320 cm²
- **D.** 8470 cm²

<details><summary>✅ Sahi answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct answer: C. 12320 cm²**

Volume πr²h=80850 hai. Given π=22/7 aur h=21 rakhne par 66r²=80850. Isliye r²=1225 aur r=35 cm hai. Curved area 2πrh=4620 cm² hai. Dono circular ends ka area 2πr²=7700 cm² hai. Total 12320 cm² hai.

- **A.** Sirf curved area hai.
- **B.** Sirf dono bases ka combined area hai.
- **D.** Curved area aur ek base ka sum hai. Closed solid ke liye dono bases count karne hain.

**Exam trick:** Volume se radius nikaal kar requested surface formula apply karo.
