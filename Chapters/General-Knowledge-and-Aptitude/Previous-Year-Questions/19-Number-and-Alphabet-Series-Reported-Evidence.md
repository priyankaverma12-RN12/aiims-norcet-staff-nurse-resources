### Q1. A geometric progression begins at 2 and each successive term is half the previous one. What is its fifth term?

**Difficulty:** 🟢 Easy · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD · **🔵 JIPMER Staff Nurse, 23 February 2020 — retrieved third-party report**

- **A.** 1/4
- **B.** 1/6
- **C.** 1/8
- **D.** 1/16

<details><summary>✅ Sahi answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct answer: C. 1/8**

First term 2 hai. Next 1, phir 1/2, phir 1/4 aur fifth term 1/8 hai. Formula a×r^(n−1) mein 2×(1/2)^4=1/8 milta hai.

- **A.** 1/4 fourth term hai, fifth nahi.
- **B.** 1/6 halving pattern follow nahi karta.
- **D.** 1/16 sixth term hai.

**Exam trick:** First term ko position 1 count karo, exponent n−1 hota hai.
