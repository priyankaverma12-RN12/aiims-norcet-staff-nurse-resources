### Q1. Two manufacturers had annual output of 150 and 175 million tonnes respectively in 2016, and 375 and 325 million tonnes in 2018. Calculate their respective percentage increases, rounding to two decimal places if needed.

**Difficulty:** 🟡 Medium · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD · **⚪ DSSSB Nursing Officer, 28 August 2019, shift 3 — indexed report, direct verification pending**

| Manufacturer | 2016 | 2018 |
|---|---:|---:|
| First | 150 | 375 |
| Second | 175 | 325 |

- **A.** 150% and 85.71%
- **B.** 60% and 46.15%
- **C.** 250% and 185.71%
- **D.** 225% and 150%

<details><summary>✅ Sahi answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct answer: A. 150% and 85.71%**

First manufacturer ka increase 375−150=225 million tonnes hai. Original 150 se divide karke 225/150×100=150% milta hai. Second ka increase 325−175=150 million tonnes hai. Original 175 se divide karne par 85.71% approximately hai.

- **B.** New output ko denominator banata hai, original ko nahi.
- **C.** New output as percentage of old output calculate karta hai. Increase ke liye 100% subtract karna chahiye.
- **D.** Absolute increases ko percentages samajhta hai.

**Exam trick:** Har series ki apni original value denominator hoti hai.
