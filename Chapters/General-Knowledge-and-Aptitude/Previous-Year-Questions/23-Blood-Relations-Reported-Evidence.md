### Q1. T is a girl's father and U is her mother. T and U are married to each other. How is U related to T?

**Difficulty:** 🟢 Easy · **Priority:** 🟡 IMPORTANT · **🔵 HPSSSB Staff Nurse, October 2020 — related source-based adaptation with clarified assumptions**

- **A.** Mother
- **B.** Wife
- **C.** Daughter
- **D.** Sister

<details><summary>✅ Sahi answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct answer: B. Wife**

U girl ki mother hai aur T father hai. Marriage explicitly given hai, toh U, T ki wife hai. Original reported question mein shared daughter aur sister-in-law ka link tha. Lekin shared child se marriage alone establish nahi hoti, isliye adaptation mein marriage clear kar di gayi hai.

- **A.** U girl ki mother hai, T ki nahi.
- **C.** U T ki daughter nahi hai.
- **D.** Sibling link nahi diya gaya.

### Q2. A woman explains that her father's sole male child is the biological father of a boy. The woman and that male child are full siblings. How is the woman related to the boy?

**Difficulty:** 🟡 Medium · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD · **🔵 ESIC Staff Nurse, 19 March 2016, shift I — retrieved report**

- **A.** Maternal aunt
- **B.** Sister
- **C.** Paternal aunt
- **D.** Grandmother

<details><summary>✅ Sahi answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct answer: C. Paternal aunt**

Father ka sole male child woman ka brother hai. Wahi boy ka biological father hai. Boy ke father ki sister paternal aunt hai.

- **A.** Maternal aunt mother ki sister hoti hai.
- **B.** Woman boy ke father ki sibling hai, boy ki nahi.
- **D.** Woman boy ke grandparent ki generation mein nahi hai.

[Direct reported question and solution](https://testbook.com/question-answer/anita-says-prabhus-father-is-the-only--6241fd0c34a8a21e0f024209). Direct text retrieved 27 November 2026. Source key aunt. Adaptation specifies the side and sex explicitly. Official key not independently authenticated.
