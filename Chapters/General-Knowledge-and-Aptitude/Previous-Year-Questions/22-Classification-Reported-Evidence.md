### Q1. Among the following countries, which is geographically outside Southeast Asia?

**Difficulty:** 🟢 Easy · **Priority:** 🟡 IMPORTANT · **🔵 AIIMS Nursing Officer Nagpur 2020 — retrieved memory-based report**

- **A.** Brunei
- **B.** Mauritius
- **C.** Cambodia
- **D.** Myanmar

<details><summary>✅ Sahi answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct answer: B. Mauritius**

Mauritius Indian Ocean mein Africa ke east mein ek island country hai. Geographical grouping mein Mauritius Southeast Asia ka country nahi hai. Source report mein Burma naam use hua hai, jise adaptation mein Myanmar likha gaya hai. Question ka intended regional classification same hai.

- **A.** Brunei Southeast Asia mein Borneo island par hai.
- **C.** Cambodia mainland Southeast Asia mein hai.
- **D.** Myanmar mainland Southeast Asia mein hai.

**Exam trick:** Region ka criterion geography hai. Sirf island country hone se koi country group ke bahar nahi ho jaati, kyunki Southeast Asia mein mainland aur island countries dono hain.
