### Q1. Six markers U, V, W, X, Y and Z are arranged at equal intervals around a circle, all pointing towards its centre. Starting clockwise from Z, X is two seats away. W is one seat anticlockwise from X. V is two seats anticlockwise from W, and Y is one seat anticlockwise from V. Which marker lies diametrically opposite Z?

**Difficulty:** 🟡 Medium · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD · **🔵 RRB Nursing Superintendent, 29 April 2025 Shift III — retrieved third-party report, adapted question**

- **A.** W
- **B.** Y
- **C.** U
- **D.** V

<details><summary>✅ Sahi answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct answer: C. U**

Z ko seat 1 par fix karo. X two clockwise steps se seat 3 par aata hai. W seat 2 par hai. W se two anticlockwise steps seat 6 tak jaate hain, toh V seat 6 par hai. Y seat 5 par hai. U ke liye seat 4 bachi hai. Six seats mein opposite three steps away hota hai. Isliye Z ke opposite U hai.

Clockwise order: Z → W → X → U → Y → V → Z.

- **A.** W seat 2 par hai, Z ka immediate clockwise neighbour hai.
- **B.** Y seat 5 par hai. Z se clockwise four steps door hai, three nahi.
- **D.** V seat 6 par hai, Z ka immediate anticlockwise neighbour hai.

### Q2. Seven people A, B, C, D, E, F and G are seated around a circle facing its centre. D and E are respectively one and two clockwise steps from A. F is three clockwise steps from B. C is adjacent to both G and B. Who is one clockwise step from C?

**Difficulty:** 🟡 Medium · **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD · **⚪ MPESB Nursing Officer aptitude collection — actual paper session unverified**

- **A.** G
- **B.** E
- **C.** F
- **D.** D

<details><summary>✅ Sahi answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct answer: A. G**

A ko seat 1 par fix karo. D seat 2 aur E seat 3 par hain. B agar seat 5, 6 ya 7 par ho toh F three clockwise steps ke baad already occupied seat par aayega. Isliye B seat 4 par hai aur F seat 7 par hai. C B ke next free seat 5 par hai, kyunki B ki doosri adjacent seat E se occupied hai. G seat 6 par hai. C se one clockwise step G hai.

- **B.** E seat 3 par hai, C se two anticlockwise steps door hai.
- **C.** F seat 7 par hai, C se two clockwise steps door hai.
- **D.** D seat 2 par hai, C se three anticlockwise steps door hai.
