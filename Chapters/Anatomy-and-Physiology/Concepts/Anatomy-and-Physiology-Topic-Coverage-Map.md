## Initial study explanations

### Cell physiology

Sodium channels khulne par positive sodium ions cell ke andar jaate hain. Andar ka electrical charge less negative ho jaata hai. Ise depolarization kehte hain.

### Renal physiology

Kidney filter se nikle fluid ka bada hissa proximal tubule mein blood mein wapas jaata hai. Isse body zaroori paani aur sodium bachati hai.

### Nervous system

Cerebellum movement ko smooth aur balanced rakhne mein help karta hai. Damage ho toh patient ko coordination aur walking mein dikkat ho sakti hai.

### Physiology of circulation

Heart mein zyada blood aata hai toh muscle fibers thode stretch hote hain. Normal limits mein contraction stronger ho sakta hai aur stroke volume badh sakta hai.
