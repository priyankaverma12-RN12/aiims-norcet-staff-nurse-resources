### Q1. A person develops low free T4 with high TSH. Which level of dysfunction is most consistent with this feedback pattern?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Priority:** High

- **A.** Primary thyroid gland dysfunction
- **B.** Pituitary inability to release TSH
- **C.** Complete absence of hypothalamic stimulation
- **D.** Normal suppression by excess T4

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: A. Primary thyroid gland dysfunction**

Thyroid adequate T4 nahi bana rahi toh feedback kam ho jaata hai. Pituitary zyada TSH bhejkar thyroid stimulate karne ki koshish karti hai.

**Baaki options kyun sahi nahi hain?** Pituitary failure mein TSH appropriately high nahi hota. Excess T4 TSH suppress karta hai.

</details>

### Q2. A patient has low cortisol and markedly elevated ACTH. Which endocrine site is most likely failing?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Priority:** High

- **A.** Anterior pituitary as the primary ACTH-deficient site
- **B.** Pineal gland
- **C.** Posterior pituitary
- **D.** Adrenal cortex

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: D. Adrenal cortex**

Cortisol kam hone par negative feedback reduce hota hai. ACTH high hone ka matlab pituitary signal de rahi hai lekin adrenal response inadequate hai.

**Baaki options kyun sahi nahi hain?** ACTH-deficient pituitary mein ACTH high expected nahi hai. Pineal aur posterior pituitary cortisol synthesis nahi karti.

</details>

### Q3. Why does prolonged external glucocorticoid exposure suppress endogenous cortisol production?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Priority:** High

- **A.** Cortisol is synthesized only in the thyroid
- **B.** Glucocorticoids directly convert ACTH into insulin
- **C.** Negative feedback reduces hypothalamic CRH and pituitary ACTH
- **D.** External steroid permanently increases ACTH without limit

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: C. Negative feedback reduces hypothalamic CRH and pituitary ACTH**

External steroid body ko cortisol-like signal deta hai. Brain CRH aur ACTH kam release kar sakta hai, jisse adrenal cortisol production reduce hoti hai.

**Baaki options kyun sahi nahi hain?** ACTH generally suppress hota hai. Adrenal cortex cortisol banati hai. ACTH insulin mein convert nahi hota.

</details>

### Q7. Why does severe dietary iodine deficiency impair thyroid hormone production?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Priority:** High

- **A.** Iodine is the principal amino acid in insulin
- **B.** Iodine replaces all thyroid receptors
- **C.** Iodine directly becomes pituitary TSH
- **D.** Iodine is required as a structural component of T3 and T4

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: D. Iodine is required as a structural component of T3 and T4**

T3 mein three aur T4 mein four iodine atoms hote hain. Adequate iodine na mile toh hormone synthesis affect ho sakti hai.

**Baaki options kyun sahi nahi hain?** Iodine amino acid nahi hai. Receptors aur TSH protein structures hain, iodine directly unmein convert nahi hota.

</details>

### Q9. A rise in thyroid hormone-binding protein increases total T4 but free T4 remains normal after adaptation. What does this illustrate?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Priority:** High

- **A.** Binding proteins are identical to hormone receptors
- **B.** The thyroid cannot respond to feedback
- **C.** Total hormone concentration may change without sustained change in free hormone
- **D.** Every rise in total hormone proves hyperthyroidism

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: C. Total hormone concentration may change without sustained change in free hormone**

Binding protein badhne par more hormone bound ho sakta hai. Feedback free hormone ko normal range mein maintain kar sakta hai. Total aur free results alag interpret karne chahiye.

**Baaki options kyun sahi nahi hain?** Total T4 alone hyperthyroidism prove nahi karta. Transport proteins receptors se different hain. Feedback functional hota hai.

</details>

### Q13. During prolonged fasting, why does the liver increase ketone production?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Priority:** High

- **A.** High insulin completely suppresses lipolysis while increasing ketones
- **B.** The liver converts all ketones into calcium
- **C.** Ketones are produced solely by red blood cells
- **D.** Low insulin and increased counterregulatory signaling promote fatty acid oxidation and ketogenesis

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

Insulin low hone par fat stores se fatty acids release ho sakti hain. Liver unhe oxidize karke ketones banata hai jo alternative fuel ho sakte hain.

**Baaki options kyun sahi nahi hain?** High insulin ketogenesis suppress karti hai. Ketones calcium nahi bante. RBC mitochondria absent hone se ketone production nahi karti.

</details>
