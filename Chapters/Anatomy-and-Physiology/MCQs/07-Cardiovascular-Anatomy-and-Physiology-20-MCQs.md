### Q1. Which chamber receives blood directly from the superior and inferior venae cavae?

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Priority:** Medium

- **A.** Right atrium
- **B.** Left atrium
- **C.** Right ventricle
- **D.** Left ventricle

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: A. Right atrium**

Body se oxygen kam wala blood venae cavae ke through right atrium mein aata hai. Phir tricuspid valve se right ventricle mein jaata hai.

**Baaki options kyun sahi nahi hain?** Left atrium pulmonary veins se blood leta hai. Ventricles blood ko aage pump karte hain.

</details>

### Q2. Which valve prevents backflow from the left ventricle into the left atrium during ventricular systole?

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Priority:** Medium

- **A.** Aortic valve
- **B.** Tricuspid valve
- **C.** Pulmonary valve
- **D.** Mitral valve

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: D. Mitral valve**

Left ventricle contract karta hai toh mitral valve band hota hai. Isse blood atrium mein wapas jaane ke bajay aorta ki taraf jaata hai.

**Baaki options kyun sahi nahi hain?** Aortic valve aorta ke outlet par hai. Tricuspid right atrium aur ventricle ke beech hai. Pulmonary valve right ventricular outlet par hai.

</details>

### Q3. Which vessels normally carry oxygenated blood from the lungs to the heart?

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Priority:** Medium

- **A.** Superior vena cava
- **B.** Coronary sinus
- **C.** Pulmonary veins
- **D.** Pulmonary arteries

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: C. Pulmonary veins**

Lungs mein blood oxygen leta hai aur pulmonary veins ke through left atrium mein pahunchta hai.

**Baaki options kyun sahi nahi hain?** Pulmonary arteries right ventricle se lungs tak blood le jaati hain. Vena cava aur coronary sinus venous blood right atrium mein laate hain.

</details>

### Q4. Why is the left ventricular myocardium normally thicker than the right ventricular myocardium?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Priority:** High

- **A.** It has no need to generate pressure
- **B.** It pumps against the higher resistance of systemic circulation
- **C.** It receives blood directly from both venae cavae
- **D.** It pumps only into the low-pressure pulmonary circulation

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: B. It pumps against the higher resistance of systemic circulation**

Left ventricle ko poori body tak blood pump karna padta hai. Systemic resistance pulmonary circulation se zyada hoti hai, isliye muscle thicker hoti hai.

**Baaki options kyun sahi nahi hain?** Venae cavae right atrium mein open hoti hain. Right ventricle pulmonary circulation mein pump karta hai. Left ventricle significant pressure banata hai.

</details>

### Q5. Which structure normally initiates the electrical impulse for each sinus heartbeat?

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Priority:** Medium

- **A.** Papillary muscles
- **B.** Sinoatrial node
- **C.** Atrioventricular node
- **D.** Bundle branches

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: B. Sinoatrial node**

SA node right atrium mein specialized cells ka group hai jo naturally electrical signals generate karta hai. Normal sinus rhythm yahin se start hoti hai.

**Baaki options kyun sahi nahi hain?** AV node signal delay karta hai. Bundle branches ventricles tak signal pahunchati hain. Papillary muscles valves support karti hain.

</details>

### Q6. Why does physiological delay at the AV node help ventricular function?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Priority:** High

- **A.** It allows atrial contraction to contribute to ventricular filling before ventricular systole
- **B.** It prevents all impulses from reaching the ventricles
- **C.** It makes ventricles contract before atria every time
- **D.** It permanently closes both AV valves

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: A. It allows atrial contraction to contribute to ventricular filling before ventricular systole**

AV node impulse ko thoda delay karta hai. Isse atria ko ventricles mein remaining blood push karne ka time milta hai.

**Baaki options kyun sahi nahi hain?** Normal AV node impulses conduct karta hai. Atrial contraction pehle hoti hai. AV valves filling ke waqt open hote hain.

</details>

### Q7. Which ECG component represents ventricular depolarization?

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Priority:** Medium

- **A.** P wave
- **B.** T wave
- **C.** PR segment alone
- **D.** QRS complex

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: D. QRS complex**

Ventricular muscle cells electrically activate hoti hain toh ECG mein QRS complex banta hai. Iske baad mechanical contraction hoti hai.

**Baaki options kyun sahi nahi hain?** P wave atrial depolarization hai. T wave ventricular repolarization hai. PR segment conduction delay se related hai.

</details>

### Q8. A patient has an end-diastolic volume of 125 mL and end-systolic volume of 50 mL. What is the stroke volume?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Priority:** High

- **A.** 50 mL
- **B.** 125 mL
- **C.** 75 mL
- **D.** 175 mL

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: C. 75 mL**

Stroke volume ek beat mein ventricle se nikla blood hai. Filled volume se remaining volume minus karo. 125 minus 50 = 75 mL.

**Baaki options kyun sahi nahi hain?** 175 dono values ka sum hai. 50 remaining volume hai. 125 initial filled volume hai.

</details>

### Q9. A patient's stroke volume is 72 mL and pulse is 75/min. What is the cardiac output?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Priority:** High

- **A.** 54 L/min
- **B.** 1.04 L/min
- **C.** 5.4 L/min
- **D.** 0.54 L/min

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: C. 5.4 L/min**

Cardiac output ek minute mein pumped blood hai. 72 x 75 = 5400 mL/min, yani 5.4 L/min.

**Baaki options kyun sahi nahi hain?** 0.54 aur 54 mein decimal conversion galat hai. 1.04 division se milta hai, multiplication se nahi.

</details>

### Q10. An echocardiogram reports EDV 120 mL and stroke volume 72 mL. What is the ejection fraction?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Priority:** High

- **A.** 120%
- **B.** 60%
- **C.** 40%
- **D.** 72%

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: B. 60%**

Ejection fraction filled blood ka percentage hai jo ek beat mein eject hota hai. 72/120 x 100 = 60%.

**Baaki options kyun sahi nahi hain?** 40% remaining fraction hai. 72 mL stroke volume hai. 120 mL EDV hai.

</details>

### Q11. During isovolumetric ventricular contraction, what is the valve position?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Priority:** High

- **A.** Both AV and semilunar valves are closed
- **B.** Both AV valves and semilunar valves are open
- **C.** AV valves are open and semilunar valves closed
- **D.** AV valves closed and semilunar valves open

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: A. Both AV and semilunar valves are closed**

Ventricular pressure badhna shuru hota hai aur AV valves close ho jaate hain. Semilunar valves abhi open nahi hue hote. Blood volume change nahi hota.

**Baaki options kyun sahi nahi hain?** Open AV valves filling phase mein milte hain. Open semilunar valves ejection phase mein milte hain. Dono groups ka open hona normal isovolumetric phase nahi hai.

</details>

### Q12. Which valve closure produces the principal components of the second heart sound?

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Priority:** Medium

- **A.** Mitral and tricuspid valve closure
- **B.** Opening of both AV valves
- **C.** Opening of coronary arteries
- **D.** Aortic and pulmonary valve closure

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: D. Aortic and pulmonary valve closure**

Ventricular ejection ke end par aortic aur pulmonary valves close hote hain. Inse S2 sound banti hai.

**Baaki options kyun sahi nahi hain?** Mitral aur tricuspid closure S1 banati hai. AV valve opening normally main S2 nahi banati. Coronary arteries S2 valve nahi hain.

</details>

### Q13. Why does left coronary blood flow occur predominantly during diastole?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Priority:** High

- **A.** Coronary arteries are entirely closed during diastole
- **B.** The aorta contains no blood during systole
- **C.** Myocardial oxygen demand disappears during diastole
- **D.** Systolic contraction compresses intramyocardial vessels, which are less compressed during relaxation

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: D. Systolic contraction compresses intramyocardial vessels, which are less compressed during relaxation**

Left ventricular contraction muscle ke andar vessels ko compress kar sakti hai. Diastole mein muscle relax hoti hai aur aortic pressure coronary flow drive karta hai.

**Baaki options kyun sahi nahi hain?** Coronary arteries diastole mein perfuse hoti hain. Aorta systole mein blood receive karti hai. Heart ko relaxation mein bhi oxygen chahiye.

</details>

### Q14. Which vessels are the principal adjustable resistance vessels in systemic circulation?

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Priority:** Medium

- **A.** Venules only
- **B.** Venae cavae
- **C.** Arterioles
- **D.** Large elastic arteries alone

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: C. Arterioles**

Arterioles ki smooth muscle diameter change kar sakti hai. Small radius change se resistance aur tissue blood flow par bada effect padta hai.

**Baaki options kyun sahi nahi hain?** Large arteries pressure pulses buffer karti hain. Veins aur venules capacitance aur return mein important hain.

</details>

### Q15. If systemic vascular resistance falls while cardiac output remains constant, what generally happens to mean arterial pressure?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Priority:** High

- **A.** It necessarily remains unchanged
- **B.** It decreases
- **C.** It necessarily doubles
- **D.** It becomes independent of resistance

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: B. It decreases**

MAP roughly cardiac output aur systemic resistance ke product se related hai. Output constant ho aur resistance kam ho toh pressure gir sakta hai.

**Baaki options kyun sahi nahi hain?** Double hona expected nahi hai. Resistance pressure ko affect karti hai. Unchanged hona stated conditions mein likely nahi hai.

</details>

### Q16. Which vessel type normally has valves that help prevent backflow, particularly in the limbs?

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Priority:** Medium

- **A.** Many medium-sized veins
- **B.** All capillaries
- **C.** All arterioles
- **D.** The aorta throughout its length

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

</details>

### Q17. During sudden standing, reduced carotid sinus stretch normally produces which reflex response?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Priority:** High

- **A.** Increased sympathetic activity and reduced parasympathetic activity
- **B.** Increased vagal activity with marked vasodilation
- **C.** Complete cessation of cardiac impulses
- **D.** Suppression of all vascular responses

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: A. Increased sympathetic activity and reduced parasympathetic activity**

Standing se blood temporarily legs mein pool ho sakta hai. Baroreceptor firing kam hoti hai aur brain heart rate aur vascular tone badhata hai.

**Baaki options kyun sahi nahi hain?** Vagal increase pressure aur gira sakta hai. Cardiac impulses normally continue karte hain. Vascular response important compensation hai.

</details>

### Q18. Which blood vessel supplies the cardiac muscle itself?

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Priority:** Medium

### Q19. Which capillary force favors fluid movement from blood into the surrounding interstitial space?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Priority:** High

- **A.** Intracellular ATP as the direct capillary force
- **B.** Lymphatic valve closure alone
- **C.** Capillary hydrostatic pressure
- **D.** Plasma albumin oncotic pressure

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: C. Capillary hydrostatic pressure**

Capillary ke andar blood pressure water ko outward push kar sakta hai. Protein-related oncotic pressure water ko vascular side retain karne mein help karta hai.

**Baaki options kyun sahi nahi hain?** Albumin outward filtration ko oppose karti hai. ATP direct Starling force nahi hai. Lymphatic drainage filtered fluid return karti hai.

</details>

### Q20. Which structure prevents the atrioventricular valve leaflets from prolapsing excessively during ventricular contraction?

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Priority:** Medium

- **A.** Interatrial septum alone
- **B.** Chordae tendineae attached to papillary muscles
- **C.** Semilunar valve cusps
- **D.** SA node

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: B. Chordae tendineae attached to papillary muscles**

Chordae tendineae strong cords hain jo papillary muscles aur AV leaflets ko connect karti hain. Systole mein ye leaflets ko atrium mein excessive flip hone se bachati hain.

**Baaki options kyun sahi nahi hain?** Semilunar valves chordae use nahi karte. SA node electrical signal banata hai. Septum chambers separate karta hai.

</details>
