### Q1. Which extraocular muscle elevates the upper eyelid in normal voluntary opening?

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Priority:** Medium

- **A.** Levator palpebrae superioris
- **B.** Orbicularis oculi
- **C.** Lateral rectus
- **D.** Superior oblique

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: A. Levator palpebrae superioris**

Levator palpebrae upper lid lift karti hai aur CN III motor supply leti hai. Sympathetic smooth muscle bhi lid elevation mein contribute karti hai.

**Baaki options kyun sahi nahi hain?** Orbicularis lid close karti hai. Lateral rectus aur superior oblique eye movement muscles hain.

</details>

### Q2. Which intrinsic iris muscle produces pupillary constriction under parasympathetic stimulation?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Priority:** High

- **A.** Dilator pupillae
- **B.** Ciliary muscle as the direct pupil sphincter
- **C.** Lateral rectus
- **D.** Sphincter pupillae

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: D. Sphincter pupillae**

Sphincter pupillae iris ka circular smooth muscle hai. Contract hone par pupil narrow hoti hai aur light entry kam hoti hai.

**Baaki options kyun sahi nahi hain?** Dilator pupil enlarge karti hai. Ciliary muscle lens accommodation mein hai. Lateral rectus eye abduct karti hai.

</details>

### Q3. Which iris muscle is primarily activated by sympathetic signaling during pupillary dilation?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Priority:** High

- **A.** Orbicularis oculi
- **B.** Levator palpebrae as the direct pupil dilator
- **C.** Dilator pupillae
- **D.** Sphincter pupillae

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: C. Dilator pupillae**

Dilator pupillae radial smooth muscle fibers se bani hai. Sympathetic alpha-1 activation pupil enlarge kar sakti hai.

**Baaki options kyun sahi nahi hain?** Sphincter pupil constrict karti hai. Orbicularis eyelid close aur levator upper lid lift karti hai.

</details>

### Q4. Which transparent gel fills most of the posterior segment of the eye?

- **B.** Vitreous humor
- **C.** Aqueous humor alone
- **D.** Tear film

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Baaki options kyun sahi nahi hain?** Aqueous anterior segment mein circulate karta hai. Tears surface protect karti hain. Endolymph inner ear fluid hai.

</details>

### Q5. A person with myopia focuses distant light in front of the retina without correction. Which lens type shifts the focus appropriately?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Priority:** High

- **A.** No optical lens can alter focus
- **B.** Concave diverging lens
- **C.** Convex converging lens
- **D.** Cylindrical lens as the universal correction

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: B. Concave diverging lens**

Myopia mein eye ki optical power ya length ki wajah se distant focus retina se pehle aata hai. Concave lens light diverge karke focal point ko retina ki taraf shift karti hai.

**Baaki options kyun sahi nahi hain?** Convex lens hyperopia mein often use hoti hai. Cylindrical astigmatism correct kar sakti hai. Optical correction focus alter karti hai.

</details>

### Q6. A person with uncomplicated hyperopia tends to focus near-object light behind the retina without accommodation. Which lens can assist?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Priority:** High

- **A.** Convex converging lens
- **B.** Concave diverging lens
- **C.** Opaque lens
- **D.** Prism as the standard sole correction

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: A. Convex converging lens**

Hyperopia mein converging convex lens light ko pehle focus karne mein help karti hai. Isse retinal focus improve ho sakta hai.

**Baaki options kyun sahi nahi hain?** Concave lens divergence badhati hai. Opaque lens light block karti hai. Prism primarily alignment correction mein use ho sakti hai.

</details>

### Q7. Why does presbyopia cause difficulty focusing on near objects with increasing age?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Priority:** High

- **A.** Permanent destruction of all rods in every adult
- **B.** Excessively flexible lens with unlimited accommodation
- **C.** Complete loss of aqueous humor
- **D.** Reduced lens flexibility and accommodative ability

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: D. Reduced lens flexibility and accommodative ability**

Age ke saath lens less flexible ho sakta hai. Near focus ke liye required shape change mushkil hoti hai, jise presbyopia kehte hain.

**Baaki options kyun sahi nahi hain?** Rods low-light vision se related hain. Accommodation unlimited nahi hoti. Aqueous humor normally present rehta hai.

</details>

### Q8. A lesion of the right optic tract produces which typical visual field loss?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Priority:** High

- **A.** Bitemporal hemianopia as the typical tract lesion
- **B.** Complete bilateral blindness in every case
- **C.** Left homonymous hemianopia
- **D.** Right monocular blindness in every case

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: C. Left homonymous hemianopia**

Right optic tract dono eyes ke left visual field ki information carry karta hai. Lesion se left homonymous field loss ho sakta hai.

**Baaki options kyun sahi nahi hain?** Monocular loss optic nerve se ho sakti hai. Bitemporal loss chiasm lesion mein typical hai. Tract lesion necessarily bilateral total blindness nahi karti.

</details>

### Q9. What is the normal role of the pupillary consensual response?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Priority:** High

- **A.** The nonilluminated pupil always dilates maximally
- **B.** Light reflex directly contracts the vocal cords
- **C.** Light entering one eye activates constriction pathways to both pupils
- **D.** Only the illuminated eye can ever constrict

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: C. Light entering one eye activates constriction pathways to both pupils**

Optic afferent pathway brainstem mein bilateral pretectal connections banati hai. Isliye ek eye par light dene se dono pupils constrict kar sakti hain.

**Baaki options kyun sahi nahi hain?** Direct response illuminated eye mein hai aur consensual other eye mein. Vocal cords alag motor pathway se control hoti hain.

</details>

### Q11. How does the normal middle ear improve transmission of sound from air to cochlear fluid?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Priority:** High

- **A.** Ossicular leverage and the tympanic membrane-to-oval window area difference increase effective pressure
- **B.** It converts sound into light before the cochlea
- **C.** It bypasses the tympanic membrane in all healthy ears
- **D.** It eliminates every vibration at the oval window

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: A. Ossicular leverage and the tympanic membrane-to-oval window area difference increase effective pressure**

Large tympanic membrane aur small oval window ka area difference, saath mein ossicle leverage, pressure transfer improve karta hai. Isse fluid-filled inner ear tak sound efficiently pahunch sakti hai.

**Baaki options kyun sahi nahi hain?** Sound light mein convert nahi hoti. Tympanic membrane normal pathway ka part hai. Oval window vibration receive karti hai.

</details>

### Q12. What is the role of the round window of the cochlea during stapes movement?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Priority:** High

- **A.** It transmits motor commands to the tongue
- **B.** It prevents all cochlear fluid motion
- **C.** It equalizes middle ear pressure with the pharynx
- **D.** It permits pressure displacement of cochlear fluid

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: D. It permits pressure displacement of cochlear fluid**

Stapes oval window ko move karta hai toh cochlear fluid displaced hota hai. Round window membrane opposite movement allow karke pressure changes accommodate karti hai.

**Baaki options kyun sahi nahi hain?** Tongue motor function CN XII se related hai. Fluid movement hearing ke liye needed hai. Eustachian tube middle ear pressure equalize karti hai.

</details>

### Q13. Which cochlear region is most responsive to high-frequency sound?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Priority:** High

- **A.** Apex as the usual high-frequency region
- **B.** Semicircular canals
- **C.** Tympanic cavity alone
- **D.** Base near the oval window

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: D. Base near the oval window**

Basilar membrane base par narrower aur stiffer hoti hai, isliye high frequencies yahan peak kar sakti hain. Apex lower frequencies ke liye suited hai.

**Baaki options kyun sahi nahi hain?** Apex low frequency detect karta hai. Semicircular canals balance mein hain. Tympanic cavity sound conduct karti hai.

</details>

### Q16. Why is flavor perception often impaired during nasal congestion despite preserved basic taste receptors?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Priority:** High

- **C.** Nasal congestion destroys all taste buds permanently

### Q20. Which sensory pathway conveys pain and temperature from most facial skin?

- **C.** Hypoglossal motor pathway
- **D.** Accessory motor pathway

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>
