### Q1. Which muscle tissue is striated, voluntary and usually multinucleated?

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Priority:** Medium

- **A.** Skeletal muscle
- **B.** Cardiac muscle
- **C.** Smooth muscle
- **D.** Myofibroblast tissue

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: A. Skeletal muscle**

Skeletal muscle fibers mein repeating contractile units ki wajah se stripes dikhte hain. Ye voluntary movement mein role rakhti hain aur ek fiber mein multiple nuclei hote hain.

**Baaki options kyun sahi nahi hain?** Cardiac muscle striated par involuntary hai. Smooth muscle nonstriated hai. Myofibroblasts repair mein role rakhte hain.

</details>

### Q2. Which structural unit extends from one Z disc to the next in skeletal muscle?

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Priority:** Medium

- **A.** Sarcoplasmic reticulum
- **B.** Motor neuron
- **C.** Fascicle
- **D.** Sarcomere

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: D. Sarcomere**

Sarcomere muscle contraction ki repeating basic unit hai. Ismein actin aur myosin filaments organized hote hain.

**Baaki options kyun sahi nahi hain?** Sarcoplasmic reticulum calcium store karta hai. Motor neuron signal bhejta hai. Fascicle muscle fibers ka bundle hai.

</details>

### Q3. During skeletal muscle contraction, which sarcomere band remains approximately the same length?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Priority:** High

- **A.** H zone
- **B.** Distance between Z discs
- **C.** A band
- **D.** I band

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: C. A band**

A band thick myosin filament ki length represent karta hai. Sliding filament mechanism mein filaments short nahi hote, isliye A band length same rehti hai.

**Baaki options kyun sahi nahi hain?** I band aur H zone narrow hote hain. Z discs paas aate hain.

</details>

### Q4. Which protein binds calcium to initiate skeletal muscle contraction?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Priority:** High

- **A.** Dystrophin
- **B.** Troponin C
- **C.** Tropomyosin directly as the calcium sensor
- **D.** Myoglobin

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: B. Troponin C**

Calcium troponin C se bind karta hai. Isse tropomyosin move hoti hai aur actin par myosin binding sites expose hoti hain.

**Baaki options kyun sahi nahi hain?** Tropomyosin sites cover karti hai. Myoglobin oxygen bind karta hai. Dystrophin structural support deta hai.

</details>

### Q5. Which intracellular structure releases calcium during skeletal muscle excitation-contraction coupling?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Priority:** High

- **A.** Mitochondrial outer membrane as the primary store
- **B.** Sarcoplasmic reticulum
- **C.** Golgi apparatus
- **D.** Nucleolus

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: B. Sarcoplasmic reticulum**

Action potential T tubules se muscle ke andar jaata hai aur sarcoplasmic reticulum se calcium release karwata hai. Calcium contraction start karne mein help karta hai.

**Baaki options kyun sahi nahi hain?** Golgi proteins process karta hai. Nucleolus ribosomal components banata hai. Mitochondria primary contractile calcium store nahi hain.

</details>

### Q6. Which event requires ATP during the skeletal muscle cross-bridge cycle?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Priority:** High

- **A.** Detachment of myosin from actin
- **B.** Permanent attachment of every myosin head
- **C.** Removal of all actin filaments
- **D.** Opening of the cell nucleus

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: A. Detachment of myosin from actin**

ATP myosin head se bind hokar usko actin se detach karta hai. ATP hydrolysis head ko next cycle ke liye ready karti hai.

**Baaki options kyun sahi nahi hain?** Permanent attachment normal cycling nahi hai. Actin remove nahi hota. Nucleus opening contraction ka step nahi hai.

</details>

### Q7. Why do muscles become stiff during rigor mortis?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Priority:** High

- **A.** Excess ATP keeps every cross-bridge detached
- **B.** New nerves grow immediately after death
- **C.** Calcium is permanently absent from muscle fibers
- **D.** ATP depletion prevents normal detachment of actin-myosin cross-bridges

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: D. ATP depletion prevents normal detachment of actin-myosin cross-bridges**

Death ke baad ATP production stop ho jaati hai. Myosin heads actin se detach nahi ho paate aur stiffness develop hoti hai.

**Baaki options kyun sahi nahi hain?** Excess ATP nahi hota. Nerves immediate grow nahi karti. Calcium release stiffness mein contribute kar sakti hai.

</details>

### Q8. Which neurotransmitter is normally released at the skeletal neuromuscular junction?

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Priority:** Medium

- **A.** Serotonin
- **B.** Norepinephrine as the normal motor end-plate transmitter
- **C.** Acetylcholine
- **D.** Dopamine

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: C. Acetylcholine**

Motor neuron acetylcholine release karta hai jo muscle ke nicotinic receptors activate karta hai. Isse muscle electrical response start hota hai.

**Baaki options kyun sahi nahi hain?** Dopamine movement control circuits mein important hai. Serotonin aur norepinephrine other neural functions mein role rakhte hain.

</details>

### Q9. A drug blocks acetylcholinesterase at the neuromuscular junction. What is its immediate biochemical effect?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Priority:** High

- **A.** Myosin filaments dissolve immediately
- **B.** Calcium can never enter any cell
- **C.** Acetylcholine remains available longer in the synaptic cleft
- **D.** Acetylcholine synthesis permanently stops

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: C. Acetylcholine remains available longer in the synaptic cleft**

Acetylcholinesterase acetylcholine ko break karti hai. Enzyme inhibit ho toh acetylcholine ka effect longer reh sakta hai. Excess effect harmful bhi ho sakta hai.

**Baaki options kyun sahi nahi hain?** Synthesis stop hona mechanism nahi hai. Myosin dissolve nahi hota. Calcium entry universally block nahi hoti.

</details>

### Q10. A motor neuron and all skeletal muscle fibers it innervates constitute what?

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Priority:** Medium

- **A.** Tendon fascicle
- **B.** Motor unit
- **C.** Sarcomere
- **D.** Myofibril

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: B. Motor unit**

Motor unit ek motor neuron aur usse connected muscle fibers ka group hai. Fine movement ke liye small motor units useful hote hain.

**Baaki options kyun sahi nahi hain?** Sarcomere contractile unit hai. Myofibril fiber ke andar contractile structure hai. Tendon fascicle connective tissue bundle hai.

</details>

### Q11. During a sustained isometric contraction, what happens to whole-muscle length?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Priority:** High

- **A.** It remains approximately constant while tension changes
- **B.** It necessarily shortens with every contraction
- **C.** It necessarily lengthens rapidly
- **D.** It becomes zero

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: A. It remains approximately constant while tension changes**

Isometric mein muscle force produce karti hai lekin joint movement aur muscle length mein major change nahi hota. Wall ko push karna example hai.

**Baaki options kyun sahi nahi hain?** Shortening concentric contraction mein hoti hai. Lengthening eccentric contraction mein ho sakti hai. Muscle length zero nahi hoti.

</details>

### Q12. A nurse lowers a heavy object slowly using the elbow flexor muscles. What type of contraction controls the lowering?

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Priority:** Medium

- **A.** Concentric contraction
- **B.** Complete muscle relaxation
- **C.** Pure isometric contraction throughout
- **D.** Eccentric contraction

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: D. Eccentric contraction**

Eccentric contraction mein active muscle force produce karte hue lengthen hoti hai. Isse object controlled manner mein neeche jaata hai.

**Baaki options kyun sahi nahi hain?** Concentric mein active muscle shorten hoti hai. Complete relaxation control nahi deti. Isometric mein length roughly constant hoti hai.

</details>

### Q14. Which muscle group primarily extends the knee?

### Q17. Which muscle-fiber type is best adapted to sustained aerobic endurance activity?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Priority:** High

- **A.** Type I slow oxidative fibers
- **B.** Type IIx fast glycolytic fibers
- **C.** Fibroblasts
- **D.** Motor neuron axons

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

</details>

### Q20. Which cellular junction helps cardiac muscle cells conduct electrical impulses to neighboring cells?

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Priority:** Medium

- **A.** Synovial joint
- **B.** Gap junction
- **C.** Tight junction as the main electrical pathway
- **D.** Neuromuscular junction between every cardiac cell

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: B. Gap junction**

Cardiac cells ke intercalated discs mein gap junctions ions ko cells ke beech move karne deti hain. Isse coordinated electrical activation hoti hai.

**Baaki options kyun sahi nahi hain?** Tight junction seal banati hai. Har cardiac cell ko separate motor end plate nahi chahiye. Synovial joint bone connection hai.

</details>
