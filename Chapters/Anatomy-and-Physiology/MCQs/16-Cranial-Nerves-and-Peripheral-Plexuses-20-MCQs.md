### Q1. A patient cannot identify a familiar odor with either nostril despite patent nasal passages. Which cranial nerve is assessed?

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Priority:** Medium

- **A.** CN I
- **B.** CN II
- **C.** CN V
- **D.** CN VII

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: A. CN I**

CN I smell ke signals nose se brain tak le jaati hai. Smell testing se is sensory pathway ka function assess hota hai.

**Baaki options kyun sahi nahi hain?** CN II vision carry karti hai. CN V facial sensation aur CN VII facial movement mein important hai.

</details>

### Q2. A patient cannot abduct the right eye. Which nerve normally innervates the affected lateral rectus muscle?

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Priority:** Medium

- **A.** CN III
- **B.** CN IV
- **C.** CN V
- **D.** CN VI

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: D. CN VI**

Abducens nerve ya CN VI lateral rectus ko signal deti hai. Ye muscle eye ko outward move karti hai.

**Baaki options kyun sahi nahi hain?** CN III most extraocular muscles supply karti hai. CN IV superior oblique supply karti hai. CN V eye movement ki main motor nerve nahi hai.

</details>

### Q3. A patient has vertical diplopia that becomes worse while walking downstairs. Which nerve supplies the superior oblique muscle relevant to this finding?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Priority:** High

- **A.** Optic nerve
- **B.** Facial nerve
- **C.** Trochlear nerve
- **D.** Abducens nerve

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: C. Trochlear nerve**

Trochlear nerve CN IV superior oblique ko supply karti hai. Ye muscle especially adducted eye ko depress karne mein help karti hai.

**Baaki options kyun sahi nahi hain?** Abducens lateral rectus supply karti hai. Optic nerve vision aur facial nerve facial muscles control karti hai.

</details>

### Q4. A patient has ptosis, a dilated pupil and an eye positioned down and out. Which cranial nerve lesion is most consistent?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Priority:** High

- **A.** Optic nerve alone
- **B.** Oculomotor nerve
- **C.** Trochlear nerve alone
- **D.** Abducens nerve alone

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: B. Oculomotor nerve**

CN III eyelid elevation, most eye movements aur pupil constriction mein role rakhti hai. Lesion se unopposed lateral rectus aur superior oblique eye ko down and out position de sakte hain.

**Baaki options kyun sahi nahi hain?** CN IV aur VI individual muscle deficits deti hain. CN II sensory vision nerve hai.

</details>

### Q5. Which cranial nerve supplies the muscles of mastication?

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Priority:** Medium

- **A.** Hypoglossal nerve
- **B.** Trigeminal nerve mandibular division
- **C.** Facial nerve
- **D.** Glossopharyngeal nerve

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: B. Trigeminal nerve mandibular division**

CN V ki mandibular division chewing muscles ko motor supply deti hai. Jaw clench assessment mein ye function check hota hai.

**Baaki options kyun sahi nahi hain?** Facial nerve expression muscles supply karti hai. Glossopharyngeal swallowing mein role rakhti hai. Hypoglossal tongue move karti hai.

</details>

### Q6. A nurse asks a patient to smile, raise the eyebrows and puff out the cheeks. Which cranial nerve is mainly being assessed?

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Priority:** Medium

- **A.** Facial nerve
- **B.** Trigeminal sensory division
- **C.** Accessory nerve
- **D.** Vagus nerve

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: A. Facial nerve**

Facial nerve CN VII facial expression muscles ko control karti hai. Smile aur eyebrow movement se motor function assess hota hai.

**Baaki options kyun sahi nahi hain?** Trigeminal mainly sensation aur chewing mein hai. Accessory neck muscles aur vagus pharyngeal-laryngeal function mein important hai.

</details>

### Q7. Which cranial nerve carries both auditory and vestibular sensory information?

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Priority:** Medium

- **A.** Facial nerve
- **B.** Glossopharyngeal nerve
- **C.** Oculomotor nerve
- **D.** Vestibulocochlear nerve

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: D. Vestibulocochlear nerve**

CN VIII ki cochlear branch hearing aur vestibular branch balance information brain tak le jaati hai.

**Baaki options kyun sahi nahi hain?** CN VII facial expression aur taste mein hai. CN IX taste aur swallowing mein hai. CN III eye movements control karti hai.

</details>

### Q8. When the posterior pharyngeal wall is stimulated, which nerve classically forms the sensory limb of the gag reflex?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Priority:** High

- **A.** Hypoglossal nerve
- **B.** Accessory nerve
- **C.** Glossopharyngeal nerve
- **D.** Vagus nerve as the principal sensory limb in the classic teaching model

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: C. Glossopharyngeal nerve**

Classic gag reflex mein CN IX pharyngeal stimulus brainstem tak carry karti hai. CN X motor response mein important hai. Reflex response alone swallowing safety prove nahi karta.

**Baaki options kyun sahi nahi hain?** Vagus mainly efferent limb ke roop mein padhayi jaati hai. Hypoglossal tongue aur accessory neck muscles supply karti hain.

</details>

### Q9. A patient has hoarseness after injury to a recurrent laryngeal nerve. From which cranial nerve does this branch arise?

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Priority:** Medium

- **A.** Facial nerve
- **B.** Glossopharyngeal nerve
- **C.** Vagus nerve
- **D.** Trigeminal nerve

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: C. Vagus nerve**

Recurrent laryngeal nerve vagus ki branch hai jo most intrinsic laryngeal muscles ko supply karti hai. Injury voice aur airway function affect kar sakti hai.

**Baaki options kyun sahi nahi hain?** CN V chewing aur sensation, CN VII expression aur CN IX pharyngeal sensation mein important hain.

</details>

### Q10. Which cranial nerve is assessed by asking a patient to shrug the shoulders against resistance?

- **A.** Abducens nerve
- **B.** Spinal accessory nerve
- **C.** Hypoglossal nerve
- **D.** Facial nerve

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

CN XI trapezius aur sternocleidomastoid muscles ko motor supply deti hai. Shoulder shrug se trapezius function assess hota hai.

**Baaki options kyun sahi nahi hain?** Hypoglossal tongue, facial face aur abducens eye abduction control karti hain.

</details>

### Q11. A patient protrudes the tongue and it deviates toward the weak side after a unilateral lower motor neuron lesion. Which nerve is affected?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Priority:** High

- **A.** Hypoglossal nerve
- **B.** Glossopharyngeal nerve
- **C.** Vagus nerve alone
- **D.** Trigeminal nerve ophthalmic division

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: A. Hypoglossal nerve**

CN XII tongue muscles ko control karti hai. Unilateral peripheral weakness mein opposite muscle ka unopposed pull tongue ko weak side ki taraf deviate kar sakta hai.

**Baaki options kyun sahi nahi hain?** CN IX taste aur swallowing mein hai. CN X palate aur larynx mein role rakhti hai. Ophthalmic branch sensory hai.

</details>

### Q12. Which spinal roots form the brachial plexus in the usual anatomical pattern?

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Priority:** Medium

- **A.** C1–C4
- **B.** T2–T6
- **C.** L1–L4
- **D.** C5–T1

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: D. C5–T1**

Brachial plexus cervical C5 se thoracic T1 ventral rami se banta hai. Ye upper limb ki major peripheral nerves form karta hai.

**Baaki options kyun sahi nahi hain?** C1–C4 cervical plexus ka main range hai. Thoracic roots intercostal nerves mein role rakhti hain. L1–L4 lumbar plexus ka range hai.

</details>

### Q13. After a shoulder dislocation, a patient has weak deltoid abduction and reduced sensation over the lateral shoulder. Which nerve may be injured?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Priority:** High

- **A.** Musculocutaneous nerve
- **B.** Median nerve
- **C.** Ulnar nerve
- **D.** Axillary nerve

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: D. Axillary nerve**

Axillary nerve surgical neck ke paas travel karti hai. Ye deltoid ko supply karti hai aur lateral shoulder skin sensation carry karti hai.

**Baaki options kyun sahi nahi hain?** Musculocutaneous elbow flexion mein important hai. Median aur ulnar hand functions supply karti hain.

</details>

### Q20. Which spinal roots are principally tested by the Achilles tendon reflex?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Priority:** High

- **A.** T1–T2
- **B.** S1–S2, mainly S1
- **C.** C5–C6
- **D.** L2–L4

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: B. S1–S2, mainly S1**

Achilles tendon stretch calf muscles ka reflex contraction trigger karta hai. Is reflex ka principal spinal segment S1 hai, with S2 contribution.

**Baaki options kyun sahi nahi hain?** C5–C6 biceps reflex se related hain. L2–L4 patellar reflex se related hain. T1–T2 Achilles reflex ka principal range nahi hai.

</details>
