### Q1. In an average healthy adult, which compartment contains the largest share of total body water?

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Priority:** Medium

- **A.** Intracellular fluid
- **B.** Plasma
- **C.** Interstitial fluid alone
- **D.** Transcellular fluid

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: A. Intracellular fluid**

Total body water ka around two-thirds cell ke andar hota hai. Remaining one-third extracellular compartments mein hota hai. Exact fraction age aur body composition se vary karti hai.

**Baaki options kyun sahi nahi hain?** Plasma aur interstitial fluid extracellular portions hain. Transcellular fluid comparatively small compartment hai.

</details>

### Q2. Which ion is the predominant extracellular cation?

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Priority:** Medium

- **A.** Potassium
- **B.** Magnesium
- **C.** Hydrogen
- **D.** Sodium

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: D. Sodium**

Extracellular fluid mein sodium main positively charged ion hai. Ye extracellular osmolality aur volume regulation mein major role rakhta hai.

**Baaki options kyun sahi nahi hain?** Potassium major intracellular cation hai. Magnesium important intracellular ion hai. Hydrogen concentration much lower hoti hai.

</details>

### Q4. A 60-kg adult is estimated to have total body water equal to 50% of body weight. What is the estimated volume?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Priority:** High

- **A.** 50 L
- **B.** 30 L
- **C.** 36 L
- **D.** 20 L

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: B. 30 L**

Water ka approximate volume 60 x 0.50 = 30 L hai. Ye estimate hai aur body fat, age aur illness se actual volume change ho sakta hai.

**Baaki options kyun sahi nahi hain?** 36 L 60% estimate hai. 20 L one-third weight hai. 50% ka matlab automatically 50 L nahi hota.

</details>

### Q5. A healthy person receives an isotonic crystalloid. Into which broad fluid compartment does it primarily distribute initially?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Priority:** High

- **A.** Only adipose tissue
- **B.** Extracellular fluid
- **C.** Intracellular fluid exclusively
- **D.** Only the cerebrospinal fluid

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: B. Extracellular fluid**

Isotonic saline jaise fluid extracellular sodium concentration ke similar hoti hain. Ye mainly plasma aur interstitial fluid expand karti hain, cells mein major free-water shift nahi karti.

**Baaki options kyun sahi nahi hain?** Intracellular-exclusive distribution isotonic saline ki property nahi hai. CSF aur fat only compartments nahi hain.

</details>

### Q6. When extracellular fluid becomes hypertonic because of sodium excess, which direction does water tend to move across cell membranes?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Priority:** High

- **A.** From intracellular to extracellular fluid
- **B.** From extracellular to intracellular fluid
- **C.** No water movement is possible
- **D.** Only into cell nuclei without cytoplasmic change

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: A. From intracellular to extracellular fluid**

Effective solutes bahar zyada ho toh water osmosis se cell ke bahar move kar sakta hai. Cells shrink ho sakti hain.

**Baaki options kyun sahi nahi hain?** Water usually higher effective solute side ki taraf jaata hai. Membranes water permit karti hain. Cytoplasm bhi affected hota hai.

</details>

### Q7. Which physiological response follows a rise in plasma osmolality in a healthy person?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Priority:** High

- **A.** Suppression of thirst and ADH in every case
- **B.** Immediate cessation of glomerular filtration
- **C.** Marked increase in insulin as the main response
- **D.** Increased thirst and ADH release

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: D. Increased thirst and ADH release**

Hypothalamic osmoreceptors concentrated plasma detect karte hain. Thirst water intake badhati hai aur ADH kidney mein water conserve karta hai.

**Baaki options kyun sahi nahi hain?** Suppression dilute plasma mein expected hai. Filtration necessarily stop nahi hoti. Insulin glucose regulation mein hai.

</details>

### Q10. What is the primary immediate buffering system of extracellular fluid?

### Q11. An ABG shows pH 7.28, PaCO2 55 mmHg and bicarbonate 25 mEq/L. What is the primary disturbance?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Priority:** High

- **A.** Respiratory acidosis
- **B.** Respiratory alkalosis
- **C.** Metabolic acidosis
- **D.** Metabolic alkalosis

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: A. Respiratory acidosis**

pH low hai aur CO2 high hai. CO2 retention blood mein acid badhata hai, isliye primary respiratory acidosis hai.

**Baaki options kyun sahi nahi hain?** Respiratory alkalosis mein CO2 low hoti hai. Metabolic acidosis mein bicarbonate primary low hota hai. Metabolic alkalosis mein bicarbonate high hota hai.

</details>

### Q12. An anxious patient hyperventilates and has pH 7.51 with PaCO2 28 mmHg. Which primary disturbance fits?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Priority:** High

- **A.** Metabolic acidosis
- **B.** Respiratory acidosis
- **C.** Metabolic alkalosis as the principal cause
- **D.** Respiratory alkalosis

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: D. Respiratory alkalosis**

Tez ventilation se CO2 zyada nikalti hai. Blood less acidic hota hai aur pH badhta hai. Isko respiratory alkalosis kehte hain.

**Baaki options kyun sahi nahi hain?** Metabolic acidosis mein pH low hota hai. Respiratory acidosis mein CO2 high hoti hai. Metabolic alkalosis mein primary bicarbonate high hota hai.

</details>

### Q13. A patient with severe diarrhea loses bicarbonate in stool. Which acid-base disturbance can result?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Priority:** High

- **A.** Metabolic alkalosis
- **B.** Respiratory alkalosis as the direct primary loss
- **C.** Respiratory acidosis as the direct primary loss
- **D.** Metabolic acidosis

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: D. Metabolic acidosis**

Diarrhea mein bicarbonate-rich fluid lose ho sakta hai. Base kam hone par blood pH gir sakta hai, jise metabolic acidosis kehte hain.

**Baaki options kyun sahi nahi hain?** Vomiting of gastric acid alkalosis cause kar sakti hai. Respiratory disorders primary CO2 change se hote hain.

</details>

### Q14. A patient has prolonged loss of gastric acid from vomiting. Which disturbance is physiologically expected?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Priority:** High

- **A.** Primary respiratory acidosis
- **B.** Primary respiratory alkalosis
- **C.** Metabolic alkalosis
- **D.** Metabolic acidosis from bicarbonate loss

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: C. Metabolic alkalosis**

Gastric HCl loss se body hydrogen ions lose karti hai. Bicarbonate relative excess mein reh sakta hai aur pH increase ho sakta hai.

**Baaki options kyun sahi nahi hain?** Bicarbonate loss diarrhea mein common mechanism hai. Respiratory disorders CO2 change se define hote hain.

</details>

### Q15. In metabolic acidosis, what immediate physiological compensation is usually expected?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Priority:** High

- **A.** Immediate increase in gastric acid secretion as the main compensation
- **B.** Increased ventilation to reduce PaCO2
- **C.** Reduced ventilation to retain more CO2
- **D.** Complete cessation of respiratory drive

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: B. Increased ventilation to reduce PaCO2**

Blood acidic hone par breathing increase ho sakti hai. CO2 bahar nikalne se carbonic acid kam hota hai aur pH support hota hai.

**Baaki options kyun sahi nahi hain?** CO2 retention acidosis worsen karti hai. Respiratory drive active rehti hai. Gastric secretion principal compensation nahi hai.

</details>

### Q16. During chronic respiratory acidosis, which renal adaptation helps compensate?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Priority:** High

- **A.** Increased net acid excretion and bicarbonate retention or generation
- **B.** Complete urinary loss of bicarbonate
- **C.** Reduced hydrogen ion excretion in every case
- **D.** Increased CO2 exhalation directly through renal tubules

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: A. Increased net acid excretion and bicarbonate retention or generation**

CO2 chronically high ho toh kidneys hydrogen ions excrete aur bicarbonate conserve ya generate kar sakti hain. Ye compensation develop hone mein time lagta hai.

**Baaki options kyun sahi nahi hain?** Bicarbonate loss pH aur gira sakti hai. Net acid excretion generally increase hoti hai. CO2 ventilation lungs karte hain.

</details>

### Q17. Serum sodium is 140, chloride 104 and bicarbonate 20 mEq/L. What is the anion gap without potassium?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Priority:** High

- **A.** 16 mEq/L
- **B.** 36 mEq/L
- **C.** 20 mEq/L
- **D.** 264 mEq/L

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: A. 16 mEq/L**

Anion gap = sodium minus chloride aur bicarbonate ka sum. 140 minus (104 + 20) = 16 mEq/L. Lab reference aur albumin context important hain.

**Baaki options kyun sahi nahi hain?** 36 bicarbonate omit karta hai. 20 bicarbonate value hai. 264 addition error hai.

</details>

### Q18. Why can marked alkalemia reduce ionized calcium despite unchanged total serum calcium?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Priority:** High

- **A.** Immediate destruction of all calcium atoms
- **B.** Complete absence of plasma proteins
- **C.** Direct conversion of calcium to sodium
- **D.** Increased calcium binding to albumin

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: D. Increased calcium binding to albumin**

High pH par albumin calcium zyada bind kar sakta hai. Free ya ionized calcium decrease ho sakta hai, jisse tingling ya muscle spasm ho sakta hai.

**Baaki options kyun sahi nahi hain?** Calcium atoms destroy ya sodium mein convert nahi hote. Albumin present hone ki wajah se binding change hoti hai.

</details>

### Q19. A patient receives insulin and serum potassium falls without immediate loss of total body potassium. What explains the change?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Priority:** High

- **A.** Insulin directly eliminates all potassium through the lungs
- **B.** Potassium cannot enter any cell
- **C.** Potassium shifts into cells
- **D.** Potassium is converted into glucose

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: C. Potassium shifts into cells**

Insulin Na-K pump activity promote kar sakti hai. Potassium blood se cells ke andar shift hota hai, isliye serum level fall kar sakta hai.

**Baaki options kyun sahi nahi hain?** Potassium glucose mein convert nahi hota. Lungs potassium excretion ka main route nahi hain. Cells potassium uptake karti hain.

</details>

### Q20. Which electrolyte is especially important for ATP-dependent enzymes and normal neuromuscular function?

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Priority:** Medium

- **A.** Albumin
- **B.** Magnesium
- **C.** Chloride as the only ATP-associated ion
- **D.** Bicarbonate as a metal cofactor

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: B. Magnesium**

Magnesium kai enzymes ke cofactor ke roop mein kaam karta hai aur ATP utilization support karta hai. Low magnesium muscle aur heart rhythm function affect kar sakta hai.

**Baaki options kyun sahi nahi hain?** Chloride fluid balance mein hai. Bicarbonate buffer hai. Albumin electrolyte metal nahi hai.

</details>
