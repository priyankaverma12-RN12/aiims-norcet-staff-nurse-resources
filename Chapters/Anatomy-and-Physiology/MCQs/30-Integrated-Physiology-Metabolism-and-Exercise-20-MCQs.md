### Q1. During moderate dynamic exercise, which change usually helps increase oxygen delivery to active skeletal muscles?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Priority:** High

- **A.** Increased cardiac output and local muscle vasodilation
- **B.** Marked reduction of all muscle perfusion
- **C.** Complete inhibition of oxygen extraction
- **D.** Permanent cessation of venous return

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: A. Increased cardiac output and local muscle vasodilation**

Exercise mein muscles oxygen demand badhati hain. Heart output increase karta hai aur local metabolites active muscle vessels dilate kar sakte hain.

**Baaki options kyun sahi nahi hain?** Muscle perfusion typically increase hoti hai. Oxygen extraction bhi increase ho sakti hai. Venous return pumping support karti hai.

</details>

### Q2. Why does contraction of leg muscles enhance venous return during walking?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Priority:** High

- **A.** Leg muscles create new arterial blood cells with each step
- **B.** Venous valves force blood away from the heart
- **C.** Muscle contractions eliminate every pressure gradient
- **D.** Muscle compression propels venous blood past one-way valves

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: D. Muscle compression propels venous blood past one-way valves**

Leg muscles veins ko squeeze karti hain aur valves backflow limit karti hain. Is muscle pump se blood heart ki taraf return karne mein help milti hai.

**Baaki options kyun sahi nahi hain?** Blood cells instantly create nahi hoti hain. Valves heartward flow support karti hain. Pressure gradients still required hain.

</details>

### Q3. Which mechanism helps increase venous return during normal spontaneous inspiration?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Priority:** High

- **A.** Complete closure of the venae cavae
- **B.** Permanent interruption of diaphragm activity
- **C.** Reduced intrathoracic pressure and changes in abdominal pressure
- **D.** Sustained positive thoracic pressure in every spontaneous breath

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: C. Reduced intrathoracic pressure and changes in abdominal pressure**

Inspiration par chest pressure more negative ho sakta hai aur abdominal pressure changes venous blood ko thorax ki taraf support karte hain. Ise respiratory pump kehte hain.

**Baaki options kyun sahi nahi hain?** Spontaneous inspiration usually positive pressure generate nahi karti. Venae cavae patent hoti hain. Diaphragm active hoti hai.

</details>

### Q4. A patient has cardiac output 5 L/min and arteriovenous oxygen content difference 5 mL/dL. What is oxygen consumption by the Fick principle?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Priority:** High

- **A.** 2500 mL/min
- **B.** 250 mL/min
- **C.** 25 mL/min
- **D.** 100 mL/min

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: B. 250 mL/min**

5 L/min ko 50 dL/min mein convert karo. Oxygen consumption = flow x arterial-venous oxygen difference = 50 x 5 = 250 mL/min.

**Baaki options kyun sahi nahi hain?** 25 conversion omit karta hai. 100 correct product nahi hai. 2500 tenfold error hai.

</details>

### Q5. A healthy adult exercises at a higher workload and produces more CO2. Which ventilatory response helps maintain arterial CO2 near normal?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Priority:** High

- **A.** Permanent closure of respiratory chemoreceptors
- **B.** Increased alveolar ventilation matched to metabolic CO2 production
- **C.** Complete cessation of breathing
- **D.** Reduced alveolar ventilation despite rising CO2 production

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: B. Increased alveolar ventilation matched to metabolic CO2 production**

Exercise mein CO2 production badhti hai. Brain aur sensory signals ventilation increase karte hain, jisse excess CO2 exhale ho sakti hai.

**Baaki options kyun sahi nahi hain?** Breathing stop ya decrease ho toh CO2 retain ho sakti hai. Chemoreceptors function continue karte hain.

</details>

### Q6. Which energy system provides rapidly available ATP for the first few seconds of intense muscle activity?

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Priority:** Medium

- **A.** Stored ATP and phosphocreatine system
- **B.** Only prolonged fatty acid oxidation
- **C.** Urea cycle as the immediate ATP source
- **D.** Hemoglobin synthesis

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: A. Stored ATP and phosphocreatine system**

Muscle mein stored ATP limited hota hai. Phosphocreatine phosphate transfer karke ATP quickly regenerate kar sakta hai, especially short intense effort mein.

**Baaki options kyun sahi nahi hain?** Fat oxidation slower sustained energy source hai. Urea cycle nitrogen disposal karti hai. Hb synthesis direct immediate ATP regeneration nahi hai.

</details>

### Q7. Why can lactate production increase during intense exercise even when oxygen is not completely absent?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Priority:** High

- **A.** Lactate is made only when the entire body has no oxygen
- **B.** Lactate is produced only by bones
- **C.** Glycolysis stops completely during exertion
- **D.** Rapid glycolytic flux can exceed mitochondrial oxidative handling of pyruvate

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: D. Rapid glycolytic flux can exceed mitochondrial oxidative handling of pyruvate**

Intense activity mein glycolysis fast ho sakti hai aur pyruvate ka kuch hissa lactate mein convert hota hai. Lactate later fuel ya glucose precursor ke roop mein use ho sakta hai.

**Baaki options kyun sahi nahi hain?** Complete oxygen absence required nahi hai. Many tissues lactate bana sakti hain. Glycolysis intense activity mein increase ho sakti hai.

</details>

### Q8. Which organ stores substantial glycogen for maintenance of systemic blood glucose between meals?

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Priority:** Medium

- **A.** Gallbladder
- **B.** Thymus
- **C.** Liver
- **D.** Skeletal muscle as a direct major source of free glucose released into blood

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

Liver glycogen ko break karke glucose blood mein release kar sakta hai. Isse fasting glucose maintain karne mein help milti hai.

**Baaki options kyun sahi nahi hain?** Skeletal muscle glycogen mainly local use ke liye hai. Gallbladder bile store aur thymus immune maturation karti hai.

</details>

### Q9. Why does skeletal muscle glycogen generally not supply free glucose directly to the circulation in the same way as liver glycogen?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Priority:** High

- **A.** Liver contains no glycogen
- **B.** All muscle glycogen is converted into calcium
- **C.** Skeletal muscle lacks significant glucose-6-phosphatase activity
- **D.** Muscle contains no glucose molecules

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: C. Skeletal muscle lacks significant glucose-6-phosphatase activity**

Muscle glucose-6-phosphate ko local energy ke liye use karti hai. Liver glucose-6-phosphatase ke through free glucose release kar sakta hai.

**Baaki options kyun sahi nahi hain?** Muscle glucose use karti hai. Liver glycogen store karta hai. Glycogen calcium mein convert nahi hota.

</details>

### Q10. Which process allows the liver to convert lactate into glucose that may be reused by muscle?

- **B.** Cori cycle
- **C.** Urea cycle alone
- **D.** Coagulation cascade

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

### Q11. Which process produces most ATP during aerobic cellular respiration?

- **D.** Lysosomal digestion alone

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

### Q13. A patient has hypothermia and begins involuntary repetitive skeletal muscle contractions. Which heat-production mechanism is occurring?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Priority:** High

- **A.** Evaporative cooling
- **B.** Cutaneous vasodilation as the sole response
- **C.** Heat loss by conduction
- **D.** Shivering thermogenesis

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: D. Shivering thermogenesis**

Shivering mein muscles repeatedly contract karke ATP use karti hain aur heat generate hoti hai. Cold defense mein ye useful mechanism hai.

**Baaki options kyun sahi nahi hain?** Evaporation cooling karti hai. Vasodilation heat loss badha sakti hai. Conduction heat transfer hai, production nahi.

</details>

### Q20. Which adaptation commonly occurs after repeated endurance training at a given submaximal workload?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Priority:** High

- **A.** Inability to increase oxygen consumption during exercise

- **C.** Permanent loss of all muscle mitochondria
- **D.** Reduced capillary supply in active muscle in every case

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: B. Lower heart rate with improved stroke volume and aerobic efficiency**

Endurance training heart pumping efficiency aur muscle oxidative capacity improve kar sakti hai. Same workload par lower pulse enough output maintain kar sakti hai.

**Baaki options kyun sahi nahi hain?** Mitochondrial aur capillary adaptations often improve hoti hain. Exercise oxygen consumption increase kar sakti hai.

</details>
