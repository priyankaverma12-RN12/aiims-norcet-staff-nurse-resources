### Q1. Which ion movement is the major cause of repolarization in a typical neuronal action potential?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Priority:** High

- **A.** Potassium efflux
- **B.** Persistent rapid sodium influx
- **C.** Calcium influx as the only cause
- **D.** Chloride efflux as the principal mechanism

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: A. Potassium efflux**

Depolarization ke baad potassium channels open hote hain aur positive potassium cell se bahar nikalta hai. Membrane potential negative direction mein return hota hai.

**Baaki options kyun sahi nahi hain?** Sodium influx depolarization karta hai. Calcium aur chloride currents cell-specific ho sakte hain par typical neuronal repolarization ka main mechanism potassium efflux hai.

</details>

### Q2. Why can a neuron not immediately generate another action potential during the absolute refractory period?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Priority:** High

- **A.** All extracellular sodium has been permanently depleted
- **B.** The axon has physically disappeared
- **C.** Potassium channels have no role in any phase
- **D.** Many voltage-gated sodium channels are inactivated

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: D. Many voltage-gated sodium channels are inactivated**

Sodium channels depolarization ke baad temporarily inactivated state mein chale jaate hain. Reset hone se pehle new action potential generate nahi ho sakta.

**Baaki options kyun sahi nahi hain?** Extracellular sodium permanently depleted nahi hota. Axon present rehta hai. Potassium currents repolarization mein role rakhte hain.

</details>

### Q3. What usually triggers neurotransmitter release at a chemical synapse when an action potential reaches the presynaptic terminal?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Priority:** High

- **A.** Closing every calcium channel with no ion entry
- **B.** Direct diffusion of all neurotransmitters through the postsynaptic nucleus
- **C.** Opening of voltage-gated calcium channels and calcium entry
- **D.** Complete removal of all synaptic vesicles before stimulation

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: C. Opening of voltage-gated calcium channels and calcium entry**

Action potential terminal par calcium channels open kar sakta hai. Calcium vesicles ko membrane se fuse karwata hai, jisse neurotransmitter release hota hai.

**Baaki options kyun sahi nahi hain?** Vesicles release ke liye needed hain. Calcium entry normally important hai. Postsynaptic nucleus direct release route nahi hai.

</details>

### Q4. Which neurotransmitter is the major inhibitory neurotransmitter in much of the adult brain?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Priority:** High

- **A.** Histamine as the sole inhibitor
- **B.** GABA
- **C.** Glutamate as the main inhibitory transmitter
- **D.** Acetylcholine at every inhibitory synapse

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: B. GABA**

GABA many CNS circuits mein neuronal excitation reduce karta hai. GABA receptor activation cell firing ko less likely bana sakti hai.

**Baaki options kyun sahi nahi hain?** Glutamate major excitatory transmitter hai. Acetylcholine aur histamine context-specific functions rakhte hain.

</details>

### Q5. Which neurotransmitter is the principal excitatory neurotransmitter in the CNS?

### Q6. A patient has impaired voluntary movement after injury to the primary motor cortex. Which gyrus contains this cortical region?

- **C.** Superior temporal gyrus as the primary motor cortex
- **D.** Cingulate gyrus alone

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

### Q7. Most corticospinal fibers cross to the opposite side at which location?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Priority:** High

- **A.** Optic chiasm
- **B.** Corpus callosum as the principal motor crossing
- **C.** Dorsal root ganglion
- **D.** Pyramidal decussation in the lower medulla

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: D. Pyramidal decussation in the lower medulla**

Corticospinal tract ke most fibers medulla ke lower region mein cross karte hain. Isliye cerebral motor lesion often opposite body side weakness cause karta hai.

**Baaki options kyun sahi nahi hain?** Optic chiasm visual fibers ka crossing hai. Corpus callosum hemispheres connect karta hai. Dorsal ganglion sensory cell bodies contain karta hai.

</details>

### Q12. Which autonomic receptors are responsible for sympathetic stimulation of eccrine sweat glands?

### Q19. Which nervous system process allows repeated weak postsynaptic signals arriving close together in time to reach threshold?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Priority:** High

- **A.** Saltatory conduction alone
- **B.** Permanent synaptic inhibition
- **C.** Temporal summation
- **D.** Absolute refractory period

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: C. Temporal summation**

Temporal summation mein same pathway se rapid successive signals add ho sakte hain. Combined depolarization threshold tak pahunch jaaye toh action potential start ho sakta hai.

**Baaki options kyun sahi nahi hain?** Refractory period firing temporarily restrict karta hai. Saltatory conduction axon par propagation hai. Inhibition threshold reach karna difficult banati hai.

</details>

### Q20. Why does compression of the spinal cord above the sacral segments affect bladder control even if local sacral reflexes survive?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Priority:** High

- **A.** Urine production stops permanently after every cord injury
- **B.** Descending supraspinal pathways coordinate reflex bladder activity and voluntary control
- **C.** The bladder is directly innervated by the optic nerve
- **D.** All detrusor muscle cells disappear immediately

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: B. Descending supraspinal pathways coordinate reflex bladder activity and voluntary control**

Brain aur spinal pathways bladder storage aur emptying coordinate karte hain. Cord lesion se descending control interrupt ho sakta hai aur sacral reflex activity abnormal pattern mein return kar sakti hai.

**Baaki options kyun sahi nahi hain?** Optic nerve vision carry karti hai. Detrusor remains present. Kidney urine formation necessarily stop nahi hoti.

</details>
