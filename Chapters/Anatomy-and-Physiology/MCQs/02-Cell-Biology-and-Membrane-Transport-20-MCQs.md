### Q1. A cell with high ATP demand contains abundant which organelle?

### Q5. A red blood cell is placed in a markedly hypotonic solution. What is the expected change?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Priority:** High

- **A.** The membrane becomes completely impermeable to water
- **B.** Water enters the cell and it may swell or lyse
- **C.** Water leaves the cell and it shrinks
- **D.** The cell immediately begins mitosis

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: B. Water enters the cell and it may swell or lyse**

Hypotonic fluid mein cell ke bahar effective solute concentration kam hoti hai. Water osmosis se cell ke andar jaata hai aur cell swell ya burst ho sakta hai.

**Baaki options kyun sahi nahi hain?** Shrinking hypertonic fluid mein hoti hai. Mature RBC mitosis nahi karti. Water permeability present rehti hai.

</details>

### Q9. Which ion movement occurs per ATP hydrolyzed by the sodium-potassium pump?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Priority:** High

- **C.** Three sodium ions out and two potassium ions in
- **D.** Two sodium ions out and three potassium ions in

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

Na-K pump ATP ki energy se 3 sodium bahar aur 2 potassium andar bhejta hai. Isse gradients aur cell volume maintain hote hain.

**Baaki options kyun sahi nahi hain?** Do sodium bahar wala ratio galat hai. Potassium mainly andar pump hota hai. Dono ions ek direction mein nahi jaate.

</details>

### Q10. Intestinal glucose absorption through a sodium-glucose cotransporter depends on which mechanism?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Priority:** High

- **C.** Simple diffusion of glucose through lipid only
- **D.** Primary ATP hydrolysis by the cotransporter itself

**Baaki options kyun sahi nahi hain?** Glucose lipid se freely diffuse nahi karta. Cotransporter direct ATP hydrolyze nahi karta. Osmosis water ka process hai.

### Q20. A cell membrane is described as selectively permeable. What does this mean?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Priority:** High

- **A.** Only large proteins can diffuse freely
- **B.** It permits some substances to cross more readily than others
- **C.** All substances cross at identical rates
- **D.** No water can cross the membrane

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: B. It permits some substances to cross more readily than others**

Membrane lipid layer aur transport proteins ke through substances ka movement regulate karti hai. Small nonpolar molecules aur ions ka transport alag hota hai.

**Baaki options kyun sahi nahi hain?** Sab substances equal rate se nahi jaate. Water channels se cross kar sakta hai. Large proteins freely diffuse nahi karte.

</details>
