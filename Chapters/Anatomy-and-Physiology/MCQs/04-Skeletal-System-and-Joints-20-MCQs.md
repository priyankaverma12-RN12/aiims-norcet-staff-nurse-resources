### Q1. Which bone cell is primarily responsible for laying down new bone matrix?

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Priority:** Medium

- **A.** Osteoblast
- **B.** Osteoclast
- **C.** Osteocyte as the main matrix-producing cell
- **D.** Chondroclast

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: A. Osteoblast**

Osteoblast naya bone matrix banati hai. Matrix mineralize hone ke baad kuch osteoblasts osteocytes ban jaati hain.

**Baaki options kyun sahi nahi hain?** Osteoclast bone resorb karti hai. Osteocyte mature bone maintain karti hai. Chondroclast cartilage breakdown se related hai.

</details>

### Q2. After prolonged immobilization, a patient loses bone mass. Which physiological change contributes?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Priority:** High

- **A.** All osteoclasts permanently stop working
- **B.** Calcium absorption becomes unlimited
- **C.** Bone matrix becomes entirely cartilage
- **D.** Bone resorption may exceed bone formation when mechanical loading decreases

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: D. Bone resorption may exceed bone formation when mechanical loading decreases**

Normal loading bone remodeling ko stimulate karta hai. Bed rest mein bone par load kam hota hai aur resorption formation se zyada ho sakti hai.

**Baaki options kyun sahi nahi hain?** Osteoclast activity stop hona expected nahi hai. Calcium absorption unlimited nahi hoti. Bone cartilage mein entirely convert nahi hoti.

</details>

### Q3. Which structure permits longitudinal growth of a long bone during childhood?

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Priority:** Medium

- **A.** Medullary cavity alone
- **B.** Periosteal outer fibrous layer alone
- **C.** Epiphyseal growth plate
- **D.** Articular capsule

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: C. Epiphyseal growth plate**

Growth plate cartilage ki layer hai jahan cells multiply aur mature hote hain. Is process se bone length badhti hai jab tak plate close nahi hoti.

**Baaki options kyun sahi nahi hain?** Capsule joint surround karti hai. Medullary cavity marrow contain karti hai. Periosteum mainly appositional growth aur repair mein role rakhta hai.

</details>

### Q4. A child develops injury to the distal femoral growth plate. Which long-term complication is anatomically possible?

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Priority:** Medium

- **A.** Increased production of bile
- **B.** Altered longitudinal limb growth
- **C.** Loss of all cranial nerve function
- **D.** Immediate permanent enlargement of every bone

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: B. Altered longitudinal limb growth**

Growth plate damage ho toh affected bone ki length ya alignment disturb ho sakti hai. Isliye follow-up growth assessment important hai.

**Baaki options kyun sahi nahi hain?** Cranial nerves femoral growth plate se control nahi hoti. Har bone enlarge nahi hoti. Bile liver banata hai.

</details>

### Q5. Which component of an adult long bone commonly contains fatty marrow?

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Priority:** Medium

- **A.** Synovial membrane
- **B.** Medullary cavity of the diaphysis
- **C.** Articular cartilage surface
- **D.** Epiphyseal cartilage in every adult

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: B. Medullary cavity of the diaphysis**

Long bone ke shaft mein medullary cavity hoti hai. Adult mein is cavity mein often yellow fatty marrow milta hai.

**Baaki options kyun sahi nahi hain?** Articular cartilage joint surface cover karti hai. Growth plate adult mein usually closed hai. Synovial membrane joint capsule ke andar lining hai.

</details>

### Q6. Which part of a synovial joint produces lubricating synovial fluid?

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Priority:** Medium

- **A.** Synovial membrane
- **B.** Articular cartilage alone
- **C.** Periosteum alone
- **D.** Tendon sheath in every joint

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: A. Synovial membrane**

Synovial membrane joint cavity ke andar fluid secrete karti hai. Fluid friction kam karta hai aur cartilage ko nutrients pahunchata hai.

**Baaki options kyun sahi nahi hain?** Cartilage smooth surface provide karti hai. Periosteum bone cover karta hai. Tendon sheath alag structure hai.

</details>

### Q7. Which joint allows movement in several planes, including rotation and circumduction?

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Priority:** Medium

- **A.** Hinge joint
- **B.** Fibrous suture
- **C.** Pivot joint alone
- **D.** Ball-and-socket joint

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: D. Ball-and-socket joint**

Shoulder aur hip ball-and-socket joints hain. Rounded bone head socket mein move karke multiple directions mein motion allow karta hai.

**Baaki options kyun sahi nahi hain?** Hinge mostly flexion-extension karta hai. Suture nearly immovable hai. Pivot mainly rotation allow karta hai.

</details>

### Q8. The articulation between the atlas and axis primarily permits which movement?

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Priority:** Medium

- **A.** Abduction of the hip
- **B.** Extension of the wrist
- **C.** Rotation of the head
- **D.** Flexion of the knee

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: C. Rotation of the head**

C1 atlas aur C2 axis ka joint head ko side-to-side turn karne mein help karta hai. Dens is movement ke pivot ka part hai.

**Baaki options kyun sahi nahi hain?** Knee, hip aur wrist movements alag joints par hote hain.

</details>

### Q9. A patient moves the arm away from the body midline. What is the movement called?

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Priority:** Medium

- **A.** Pronation
- **B.** Inversion
- **C.** Abduction
- **D.** Adduction

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: C. Abduction**

Abduction mein limb body ki midline se door move karti hai. Shoulder par arm ko side mein lift karna iska example hai.

**Baaki options kyun sahi nahi hain?** Adduction midline ki taraf hoti hai. Pronation forearm rotation hai. Inversion sole ko inward turn karti hai.

</details>

### Q10. A patient turns the palm downward with the forearm supported horizontally. Which movement occurs?

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Priority:** Medium

- **A.** Circumduction
- **B.** Pronation
- **C.** Supination
- **D.** Dorsiflexion

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: B. Pronation**

Pronation mein radius ulna ke across rotate karti hai aur palm downward turn hoti hai. Anatomical position mein supination palm forward rakhti hai.

**Baaki options kyun sahi nahi hain?** Supination opposite rotation hai. Dorsiflexion ankle movement hai. Circumduction circular movement hai.

</details>

### Q11. Which structure connects bone to bone at most joints?

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Priority:** Medium

- **A.** Ligament
- **B.** Tendon
- **C.** Fascia as the primary bone-to-bone connector
- **D.** Muscle spindle

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

Ligament strong connective tissue band hai jo bones ko connect karke joint stability maintain karta hai.

**Baaki options kyun sahi nahi hain?** Tendon muscle ko bone se connect karta hai. Fascia tissues surround aur separate karti hai. Muscle spindle stretch sense karta hai.

</details>

### Q12. Which bony landmark is the normal site of articulation with the femoral head?

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Priority:** Medium

- **A.** Glenoid cavity
- **B.** Obturator foramen
- **C.** Iliac crest
- **D.** Acetabulum

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: D. Acetabulum**

Acetabulum pelvis ka cup-shaped socket hai jisme femur ka head fit hota hai. Dono milkar hip joint banate hain.

**Baaki options kyun sahi nahi hain?** Glenoid shoulder socket hai. Obturator foramen pelvic opening hai. Iliac crest hip bone ka upper border hai.

</details>

### Q13. A fracture through the femoral neck threatens blood supply to the femoral head. Which consequence is anatomically important?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Priority:** High

- **A.** Primary loss of kidney filtration
- **B.** Immediate fusion of the shoulder
- **C.** Excessive production of synovial fluid in every case
- **D.** Avascular necrosis

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: D. Avascular necrosis**

Femoral head ki blood supply ka important part neck ke along aata hai. Fracture se vessels damage ho sakti hain aur bone tissue ko blood kam milne se necrosis ho sakti hai.

**Baaki options kyun sahi nahi hain?** Kidney aur shoulder directly is blood supply par depend nahi karte. Extra joint fluid avascular necrosis ka mechanism nahi hai.

</details>

### Q15. Which vertebral region normally consists of twelve vertebrae?

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Priority:** Medium

- **A.** Sacral before fusion
- **B.** Thoracic
- **C.** Cervical
- **D.** Lumbar

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

Thoracic spine mein T1 se T12 tak vertebrae hote hain jo ribs se articulate karte hain.

</details>

### Q16. Which vertebral feature allows the spinal cord to pass through successive vertebrae?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Priority:** High

- **A.** Vertebral foramen
- **B.** Intervertebral foramen alone
- **C.** Spinous process
- **D.** Transverse process

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: A. Vertebral foramen**

Har vertebra ka central vertebral foramen milkar vertebral canal banata hai. Spinal cord is canal mein protected hoti hai.

</details>

### Q19. Which hormone increases osteoclast-mediated bone resorption indirectly when sustained at high levels?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Priority:** High

- **A.** Insulin as the main regulator of osteoclast activation
- **B.** Melatonin
- **C.** Parathyroid hormone
- **D.** Calcitonin

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: C. Parathyroid hormone**

PTH osteoblast-lineage cells ko RANKL signal badhane ke liye stimulate kar sakta hai. Isse osteoclast formation aur resorption badhti hai. Intermittent PTH effects different ho sakte hain.

**Baaki options kyun sahi nahi hain?** Calcitonin osteoclast activity reduce kar sakti hai. Insulin aur melatonin is mechanism ke main physiological regulators nahi hain.

</details>

### Q20. Which type of cartilage covers the articular surfaces of most synovial joints?

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Priority:** Medium

- **A.** Calcified tendon
- **B.** Hyaline cartilage
- **C.** Elastic cartilage
- **D.** Fibrocartilage in every joint

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: B. Hyaline cartilage**

Hyaline cartilage smooth surface banati hai aur load distribute karne mein help karti hai. Isse bones ke beech friction kam hota hai.

**Baaki options kyun sahi nahi hain?** Elastic cartilage pinna jaise structures mein hai. Fibrocartilage menisci aur discs mein milti hai. Tendon articular surface nahi hai.

</details>
