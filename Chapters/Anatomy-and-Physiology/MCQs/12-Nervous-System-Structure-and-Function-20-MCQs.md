### Q1. Which part of a typical neuron receives many incoming synaptic signals?

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Priority:** Medium

- **A.** Dendrites
- **B.** Axon terminal only
- **C.** Myelin sheath
- **D.** Node of Ranvier alone

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: A. Dendrites**

Dendrites branching structures hain jo doosri cells se signals receive karne mein help karti hain. Cell body inputs integrate karti hai.

**Baaki options kyun sahi nahi hain?** Axon terminal signal release karta hai. Myelin insulation deti hai. Nodes action potential conduction mein help karte hain.

</details>

### Q2. Which cells form myelin around axons in the central nervous system?

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Priority:** Medium

- **A.** Schwann cells
- **B.** Microglia
- **C.** Ependymal cells
- **D.** Oligodendrocytes

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: D. Oligodendrocytes**

Oligodendrocytes brain aur spinal cord mein axons ko myelin cover deti hain. Ek cell multiple axon segments cover kar sakti hai.

**Baaki options kyun sahi nahi hain?** Schwann cells peripheral nerves mein myelin banati hain. Microglia immune cells hain. Ependymal cells ventricular lining hain.

</details>

### Q3. Why does myelination increase axonal conduction speed?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Priority:** High

- **A.** Myelin eliminates all ion channels from the axon
- **B.** Myelin prevents any electrical signal from reaching terminals
- **C.** Action potentials propagate between nodes of Ranvier with reduced current leakage
- **D.** Myelin makes every axon release more hormones

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: C. Action potentials propagate between nodes of Ranvier with reduced current leakage**

Myelin insulating layer hai jo current leakage kam karti hai. Action potential exposed nodes par regenerate hota hai, jise saltatory conduction kehte hain.

**Baaki options kyun sahi nahi hain?** Hormone release main mechanism nahi hai. Nodes par ion channels present hote hain. Myelin conduction improve karti hai.

</details>

### Q4. Which glial cells act as resident immune phagocytes in the CNS?

- **C.** Oligodendrocytes
- **D.** Schwann cells

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

</details>

### Q5. Which structures are the main physiological basis of the blood-brain barrier?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Priority:** High

- **A.** The dura mater alone
- **B.** Tight junctions between CNS capillary endothelial cells
- **C.** Open gaps between all cerebral endothelial cells
- **D.** The skull bone alone

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: B. Tight junctions between CNS capillary endothelial cells**

Brain capillary endothelial tight junctions uncontrolled movement restrict karti hain. Astrocytes aur pericytes barrier function support karte hain.

**Baaki options kyun sahi nahi hain?** Open gaps leakage badha sakte hain. Skull mechanical protection deta hai. Dura outer meninges layer hai.

</details>

### Q20. A local anesthetic blocks voltage-gated sodium channels in a peripheral nerve. Why does pain transmission decrease?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Priority:** High

- **A.** The drug directly converts sensory neurons into muscle cells
- **B.** Action potential initiation and propagation are impaired
- **C.** All myelin is permanently destroyed
- **D.** Nerve conduction becomes infinitely fast

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: B. Action potential initiation and propagation are impaired**

Sodium channels action potential generate karne mein important hain. Block hone par nerve pain signal effectively propagate nahi kar paati.

**Baaki options kyun sahi nahi hain?** Myelin destruction required nahi hai. Conduction slow ya blocked hoti hai. Cell type change nahi hota.

</details>
