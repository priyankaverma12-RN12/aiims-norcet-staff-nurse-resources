### Q1. In a ventricular contractile cell, which ion influx mainly produces the rapid phase 0 upstroke?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Priority:** High

- **A.** Sodium influx through fast voltage-gated channels
- **B.** Potassium influx through leak channels
- **C.** Chloride efflux as the principal upstroke
- **D.** Calcium efflux from the cell

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: A. Sodium influx through fast voltage-gated channels**

Ventricular cell ke fast sodium channels open hone par positive sodium rapidly enter karta hai. Membrane depolarize hoti hai.

**Baaki options kyun sahi nahi hain?** Potassium efflux repolarization mein role rakhta hai. Chloride aur calcium efflux main phase 0 mechanism nahi hain.

</details>

### Q2. Which inward current is especially important for the ventricular action potential plateau?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Priority:** High

- **A.** Fast sodium current alone throughout phase 2
- **B.** Chloride influx as the sole plateau current
- **C.** Potassium efflux alone
- **D.** L-type calcium current

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: D. L-type calcium current**

Phase 2 mein L-type calcium channels se calcium enter karta hai. Outward potassium current ke saath balance plateau maintain karta hai.

**Baaki options kyun sahi nahi hain?** Fast sodium channels phase 0 ke baad inactivate hote hain. Chloride sole mechanism nahi hai. Potassium efflux alone repolarize karega.

</details>

### Q3. Why is the cardiac muscle refractory period physiologically long?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Priority:** High

- **A.** It eliminates the need for calcium
- **B.** It prevents all normal heartbeats
- **C.** It prevents sustained tetanic contraction and permits relaxation and filling
- **D.** It allows every cardiac cell to contract continuously without relaxation

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: C. It prevents sustained tetanic contraction and permits relaxation and filling**

Long refractory period mein cell turant dobara excite nahi hoti. Isse heart tetanus se bachta hai aur diastolic filling possible hoti hai.

**Baaki options kyun sahi nahi hain?** Continuous contraction filling rok degi. Calcium contraction ke liye chahiye. Normal beats refractory period ke baad possible hain.

</details>

### Q4. Which characteristic allows SA nodal cells to initiate impulses without external motor neuron input?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Priority:** High

- **B.** Spontaneous phase 4 depolarization
- **C.** A permanently fixed resting potential without ion movement
- **D.** Absence of calcium channels

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

SA node cells ka membrane potential gradually threshold tak pahunchta hai. Pacemaker currents is automaticity ko support karte hain.

**Baaki options kyun sahi nahi hain?** Fixed potential automaticity nahi dega. Calcium channels nodal depolarization mein important hain. Ion transport essential hai.

</details>

### Q5. Which part of the cardiac conduction system normally has the fastest conduction velocity?

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Priority:** Medium

- **A.** Atrial myocardium as the fastest specialized tissue
- **B.** Purkinje fibers
- **C.** AV node
- **D.** SA node tissue alone

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

Purkinje fibers ventricles mein impulses rapidly distribute karti hain. Isse ventricular contraction coordinated hoti hai.

**Baaki options kyun sahi nahi hain?** AV node slow conduction karta hai. SA node pacemaker hai. Atrial myocardium Purkinje network se slower hai.

### Q6. On a normal ECG, why is atrial repolarization usually not seen as a separate wave?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Priority:** High

- **A.** It is obscured by the larger QRS complex
- **B.** Atrial cells never repolarize
- **C.** It occurs only after the next P wave
- **D.** It produces the entire T wave

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: A. It is obscured by the larger QRS complex**

Atria repolarize hoti hain lekin timing ventricular depolarization ke aas-paas hoti hai. Large QRS small atrial signal ko hide kar sakta hai.

**Baaki options kyun sahi nahi hain?** Atrial repolarization hoti hai. T wave mainly ventricular repolarization hai. Separate next-cycle wave expected nahi hai.

</details>

### Q8. Which event marks the start of ventricular ejection?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Priority:** High

- **A.** AV valves open while semilunar valves stay closed
- **B.** All chambers reach identical pressure
- **C.** Ventricular pressure exceeds the pressure in the aorta or pulmonary artery
- **D.** Atrial pressure exceeds ventricular pressure

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: C. Ventricular pressure exceeds the pressure in the aorta or pulmonary artery**

Ventricles contract karke outflow artery se higher pressure banati hain. Semilunar valves open hote hain aur blood eject hota hai.

**Baaki options kyun sahi nahi hain?** Atrial pressure higher ho toh AV valve filling favor hoti hai. Closed semilunar valves ejection allow nahi karte. Equal pressure flow drive nahi karta.

</details>

### Q9. During isovolumetric relaxation, which statement is correct?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Priority:** High

- **A.** All valves open simultaneously
- **B.** Atrial systole necessarily ejects blood into the aorta
- **C.** Ventricular pressure falls while all four valves are closed
- **D.** Ventricular blood volume rises rapidly

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: C. Ventricular pressure falls while all four valves are closed**

Ejection end par semilunar valves close hote hain. Ventricles relax hoti hain lekin AV valves abhi open nahi hue, isliye volume constant rehta hai.

**Baaki options kyun sahi nahi hain?** Filling AV valves open hone par hoti hai. All valves simultaneously open nahi hote. Atria aorta mein directly eject nahi karti hain.

</details>

### Q10. Which part of the cardiac cycle contributes the final portion of ventricular filling at normal sinus rhythm?

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Priority:** Medium

- **A.** Semilunar valve closure
- **B.** Atrial systole
- **C.** Isovolumetric contraction
- **D.** Ventricular ejection

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: B. Atrial systole**

Atrial contraction diastole ke end mein additional blood ventricles mein push karti hai. Isse atrial kick kehte hain.

**Baaki options kyun sahi nahi hain?** Isovolumetric contraction aur ejection ventricular systole mein hain. Semilunar closure ejection end par hoti hai.

</details>

### Q11. How does very rapid heart rate potentially reduce stroke volume?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Priority:** High

- **A.** It disproportionately shortens diastolic filling time
- **B.** It guarantees unlimited ventricular filling
- **C.** It permanently increases end-diastolic volume in every case
- **D.** It eliminates myocardial oxygen demand

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: A. It disproportionately shortens diastolic filling time**

Heart rate bahut fast ho toh diastole short hoti hai. Ventricles ko fill hone ka time kam mil sakta hai aur stroke volume gir sakta hai.

**Baaki options kyun sahi nahi hain?** Filling unlimited nahi hoti. EDV decrease ho sakta hai. Oxygen demand often increase hoti hai.

</details>

### Q12. A patient has a heart rate of 80/min. Approximately how long is one cardiac cycle?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Priority:** High

- **A.** 1.25 seconds
- **B.** 0.08 seconds
- **C.** 80 seconds
- **D.** 0.75 seconds

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

Ek minute mein 60 seconds hain. Cycle duration = 60/80 = 0.75 seconds.

**Baaki options kyun sahi nahi hain?** 1.25 second 48/min ke aas-paas rate hai. 0.08 aur 80 given rate se match nahi karte.

</details>

### Q16. What is the major direct determinant of the normal first heart sound timing?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Priority:** High

- **A.** Closure of the AV valves as ventricular pressure rises
- **B.** Opening of aortic valve before ventricular pressure rises
- **C.** Atrial repolarization alone
- **D.** Opening of the mitral valve during rapid filling

</details>

### Q19. A patient's arterial pressure is 90/60 mmHg. What is the usual resting approximation of MAP?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Priority:** High

- **A.** 30 mmHg
- **B.** 150 mmHg
- **C.** 70 mmHg
- **D.** 75 mmHg

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: C. 70 mmHg**

MAP = diastolic BP plus one-third pulse pressure. 60 + (90 minus 60)/3 = 70 mmHg.

**Baaki options kyun sahi nahi hain?** 75 simple mean hai. 30 pulse pressure hai. 150 dono values ka sum hai.

</details>

### Q20. Why does the Frank-Starling mechanism help balance the outputs of the two ventricles?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Priority:** High

- **A.** It removes the need for venous return
- **B.** Increased filling within physiological limits increases force and stroke volume
- **C.** Increased filling necessarily stops ventricular ejection
- **D.** It makes the two ventricles anatomically identical

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: B. Increased filling within physiological limits increases force and stroke volume**

Extra venous return se cardiac muscle fibers stretch hoti hain. Normal limits mein force aur output badh sakte hain, jisse received blood ke hisaab se pumping adjust hoti hai.

**Baaki options kyun sahi nahi hain?** Filling ejection stop nahi karti. Ventricular anatomy different rehti hai. Venous return mechanism ka input hai.

</details>
