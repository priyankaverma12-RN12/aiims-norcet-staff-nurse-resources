## Cell physiology

### Q1. During an action potential in a typical neuron, opening of voltage-gated sodium channels primarily causes which change?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Topic:** Cell physiology

- **A.** Membrane depolarization
- **B.** Immediate repolarization from sodium efflux
- **C.** Permanent loss of membrane potential
- **D.** Inhibition of all ionic movement

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: A. Membrane depolarization**

Sodium channels khulne par positive sodium ions cell ke andar jaate hain. Andar ka electrical charge less negative ho jaata hai. Ise depolarization kehte hain.

**Baaki options kyun galat hain?**

- **B.** Repolarization mein potassium efflux ka main role hota hai.
- **C.** Potential permanently lost nahi hota.
- **D.** Ions move karte hain.

</details>

## Renal physiology

### Q2. Which nephron segment normally reabsorbs the largest fraction of filtered sodium and water?

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Topic:** Renal physiology

- **A.** Distal convoluted tubule
- **B.** Collecting duct alone
- **C.** Bowman's space
- **D.** Proximal convoluted tubule

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: D. Proximal convoluted tubule**

Kidney filter se nikle fluid ka bada hissa proximal tubule mein blood mein wapas jaata hai. Isse body zaroori paani aur sodium bachati hai.

**Baaki options kyun galat hain?**

- **A.** Distal tubule smaller adjustable fraction reabsorb karti hai.
- **B.** Collecting duct final water balance adjust karti hai.
- **C.** Bowman's space filtrate collect karta hai.

</details>

## Nervous system

### Q3. Which structure coordinates balance and fine motor coordination?

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Topic:** Nervous system

- **A.** Medulla as the sole coordination center
- **B.** Thyroid gland
- **C.** Cerebellum
- **D.** Pituitary gland

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: C. Cerebellum**

Cerebellum movement ko smooth aur balanced rakhne mein help karta hai. Damage ho toh patient ko coordination aur walking mein dikkat ho sakti hai.

**Baaki options kyun galat hain?**

- **A.** Medulla vital autonomic functions mein important hai.
- **B.** Thyroid metabolism regulate karti hai.
- **D.** Pituitary hormones control karti hai.

</details>

## Physiology of circulation

### Q4. When venous return increases within physiological limits, which mechanism can increase stroke volume?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Topic:** Physiology of circulation

- **A.** Closure of the aortic valve throughout systole
- **B.** Frank-Starling mechanism
- **C.** Complete inhibition of ventricular filling
- **D.** Loss of all myocardial contractility

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: B. Frank-Starling mechanism**

Heart mein zyada blood aata hai toh muscle fibers thode stretch hote hain. Normal limits mein contraction stronger ho sakta hai aur stroke volume badh sakta hai.

**Baaki options kyun galat hain?**

- **A.** Systole mein aortic valve blood ejection ke liye khulta hai.
- **C.** Filling inhibit nahi hoti.
- **D.** Contractility completely lost nahi hoti.

</details>
