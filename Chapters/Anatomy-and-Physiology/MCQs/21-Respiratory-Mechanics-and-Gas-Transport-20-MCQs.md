### Q1. A patient's inspiratory reserve volume is 2.5 L, tidal volume 0.5 L and expiratory reserve volume 1.0 L. What is vital capacity?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Priority:** High

- **A.** 4.0 L
- **B.** 3.0 L
- **C.** 3.5 L
- **D.** 1.5 L

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: A. 4.0 L**

Vital capacity = IRV + TV + ERV. 2.5 + 0.5 + 1.0 = 4.0 L.

**Baaki options kyun sahi nahi hain?** 3.0 ERV omit karta hai. 3.5 tidal volume omit karta hai. 1.5 sirf TV aur ERV hai.

</details>

### Q2. A patient's expiratory reserve volume is 1.1 L and residual volume is 1.3 L. What is functional residual capacity?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Priority:** High

- **A.** 0.2 L
- **B.** 1.1 L
- **C.** 1.3 L
- **D.** 2.4 L

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: D. 2.4 L**

Normal quiet expiration ke baad remaining lung air FRC hoti hai. ERV + RV = 1.1 + 1.3 = 2.4 L.

**Baaki options kyun sahi nahi hain?** 0.2 subtraction hai. 1.1 ERV aur 1.3 RV alone hain.

</details>

### Q4. Why is alveolar ventilation more sensitive to shallow rapid breathing than total minute ventilation?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Priority:** High

- **A.** Minute ventilation is independent of tidal volume
- **B.** A larger fraction of each small breath remains in anatomical dead space
- **C.** Dead space vanishes with each shallow breath
- **D.** Each small breath necessarily reaches all alveoli

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: B. A larger fraction of each small breath remains in anatomical dead space**

Conducting airways ka dead space har breath mein fill hota hai. TV chhota ho toh less fresh air alveoli tak jaati hai, even minute ventilation maintained ho.

**Baaki options kyun sahi nahi hain?** Dead space disappear nahi hota. Small breath ka large fraction wasted ho sakta hai. Minute ventilation TV x rate hai.

</details>

### Q5. Which muscle groups assist forced expiration?

### Q7. What happens to airway resistance when airway radius decreases substantially, assuming other factors are unchanged?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Priority:** High

- **A.** Resistance falls in direct proportion
- **B.** Resistance becomes zero
- **C.** Resistance becomes independent of radius
- **D.** Resistance increases markedly

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: D. Resistance increases markedly**

Airway narrow ho toh airflow ke liye pressure gradient zyada chahiye. Laminar-flow model mein resistance radius ki fourth power ke inverse se related hai.

**Baaki options kyun sahi nahi hain?** Radius decrease resistance kam nahi karti. Resistance zero nahi hoti aur radius important determinant hai.

</details>

### Q8. Which lung property explains the increased work needed to inflate a fibrotic lung?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Priority:** High

- **A.** Absence of all elastic recoil
- **B.** Increased anatomical dead space as the only cause
- **C.** Reduced compliance
- **D.** Increased compliance as the main problem

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: C. Reduced compliance**

Fibrosis lung tissue ko stiff bana sakti hai. Same volume change ke liye zyada pressure chahiye, yani compliance low hai.

**Baaki options kyun sahi nahi hain?** High compliance inflation easier banati hai. Recoil necessarily absent nahi hota. Dead space stiffness ka definition nahi hai.

</details>

### Q9. In emphysema, which mechanical alteration can make expiration difficult despite relatively high lung compliance?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Priority:** High

- **A.** Increased surfactant eliminating all airway resistance
- **B.** Complete absence of residual air
- **C.** Reduced elastic recoil and dynamic airway collapse
- **D.** Excessively rigid lung tissue in every case

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: C. Reduced elastic recoil and dynamic airway collapse**

Alveolar wall damage se elastic recoil kam ho sakta hai. Expiration mein small airways collapse aur air trapping ho sakti hai.

**Baaki options kyun sahi nahi hain?** Fibrosis rigid lung ka example hai. Surfactant airway resistance eliminate nahi karti. Residual air often increases ho sakti hai.

</details>

### Q10. Which partial pressure gradient drives oxygen from alveoli into pulmonary capillary blood?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Priority:** High

- **A.** Equal oxygen pressures on both sides with no gradient
- **B.** Higher alveolar PO2 than incoming mixed venous blood PO2
- **C.** Higher venous PO2 than alveolar PO2
- **D.** Higher alveolar PCO2 than arterial PO2

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: B. Higher alveolar PO2 than incoming mixed venous blood PO2**

Oxygen higher partial pressure se lower partial pressure ki taraf diffuse karti hai. Incoming venous blood ka PO2 alveolar air se lower hota hai.

**Baaki options kyun sahi nahi hain?** Reverse gradient oxygen opposite direction move karega. CO2 pressure oxygen gradient ka substitute nahi hai. Equal pressure net diffusion drive nahi karta.

</details>

### Q11. Which factor increases diffusion across a respiratory membrane according to Fick's law?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Priority:** High

- **A.** Greater surface area with other factors unchanged
- **B.** Greater membrane thickness with all else unchanged
- **C.** Reduced partial pressure gradient
- **D.** Complete removal of alveolar capillaries

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: A. Greater surface area with other factors unchanged**

Larger surface area gas molecules ko cross karne ke liye more exchange sites deti hai. Thin membrane aur greater gradient diffusion support karte hain.

**Baaki options kyun sahi nahi hain?** Thickness increase diffusion reduce karti hai. Smaller gradient diffusion slow karta hai. Capillaries exchange ke liye chahiye.

</details>

### Q12. A patient has Hb 10 g/dL and arterial saturation 100%. Ignoring dissolved oxygen, estimate oxygen content using 1.34 mL O2/g Hb.

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Priority:** High

- **A.** 1.34 mL O2/dL
- **B.** 20.1 mL O2/dL
- **C.** 134 mL O2/dL
- **D.** 13.4 mL O2/dL

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: D. 13.4 mL O2/dL**

Oxygen content = Hb x saturation fraction x binding capacity. 10 x 1 x 1.34 = 13.4 mL/dL.

**Baaki options kyun sahi nahi hain?** 1.34 one gram capacity hai. 20.1 Hb 15 par milta hai. 134 decimal error hai.

</details>

### Q13. Which factor explains why fetal hemoglobin has higher oxygen affinity than adult hemoglobin?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Priority:** High

- **A.** Complete absence of heme iron
- **B.** Inability to bind oxygen
- **C.** Higher affinity for carbon dioxide as the only reason
- **D.** Reduced interaction with 2,3-BPG

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: D. Reduced interaction with 2,3-BPG**

Fetal hemoglobin 2,3-BPG se less strongly interact karti hai. Isse oxygen affinity higher hoti hai, jo placental oxygen transfer support karti hai.

**Baaki options kyun sahi nahi hain?** Heme iron present hota hai. HbF oxygen bind karti hai. CO2 affinity is difference ka main explanation nahi hai.

</details>

### Q14. Which effect describes reduced hemoglobin oxygen affinity in tissues with increased CO2 and hydrogen ion concentration?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Priority:** High

- **A.** Frank-Starling effect
- **B.** Donnan equilibrium alone
- **C.** Bohr effect
- **D.** Haldane effect as the same process

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: C. Bohr effect**

Active tissues CO2 aur acid produce karti hain. Hemoglobin affinity kam hone se oxygen unload hoti hai. Ise Bohr effect kehte hain.

**Baaki options kyun sahi nahi hain?** Haldane effect oxygenation ke CO2 carriage par effect se related hai. Frank-Starling cardiac filling mechanism hai. Donnan equilibrium ion distribution se related hai.

</details>

### Q15. What does the Haldane effect help explain?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Priority:** High

- **A.** Renal sodium reabsorption directly produces surfactant
- **B.** Oxygenation of hemoglobin in the lungs facilitates CO2 unloading
- **C.** Low pH increases hemoglobin oxygen affinity in active tissues
- **D.** Atrial stretch increases stroke volume

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: B. Oxygenation of hemoglobin in the lungs facilitates CO2 unloading**

Deoxygenated hemoglobin CO2 aur hydrogen ions ko better carry kar sakti hai. Lungs mein oxygen bind hone par CO2 release facilitate hoti hai.

**Baaki options kyun sahi nahi hain?** Low pH oxygen affinity kam karta hai, jo Bohr effect hai. Atrial stretch aur renal sodium handling separate mechanisms hain.

</details>

### Q19. What is the normal pulmonary vascular response to low oxygen tension in a localized alveolar region?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Priority:** High

- **A.** Complete cessation of systemic circulation
- **B.** Increased alveolar volume by direct vascular action
- **C.** Local hypoxic vasoconstriction that can redirect blood toward better ventilated regions
- **D.** Marked local vasodilation as the usual response

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: C. Local hypoxic vasoconstriction that can redirect blood toward better ventilated regions**

Pulmonary vessels low alveolar oxygen par constrict ho sakti hain. Local response blood ko better ventilated areas ki taraf redirect karne mein help karta hai. Widespread hypoxia pulmonary pressure badha sakti hai.

**Baaki options kyun sahi nahi hain?** Systemic hypoxia often vasodilation karti hai, par pulmonary response different hai. Systemic circulation stop nahi hoti. Vascular response directly alveolar inflation nahi karta.

</details>

### Q20. Why can carbon monoxide exposure produce misleadingly normal conventional pulse oximetry readings?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Priority:** High

- **A.** Carboxyhemoglobin increases available oxygen binding sites
- **B.** Standard two-wavelength oximeters cannot reliably distinguish carboxyhemoglobin from oxyhemoglobin
- **C.** Carbon monoxide improves oxygen unloading in every tissue
- **D.** Pulse oximetry directly measures carbon monoxide concentration

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: B. Standard two-wavelength oximeters cannot reliably distinguish carboxyhemoglobin from oxyhemoglobin**

Carbon monoxide hemoglobin se strongly bind karta hai aur oxygen transport disturb karta hai. Conventional SpO2 misleading ho sakti hai, isliye co-oximetry clinical evaluation mein useful hai.

**Baaki options kyun sahi nahi hain?** CO oxygen delivery impair karta hai. Ordinary oximeter CO level directly nahi measure karta. Binding sites occupy ho jaati hain.

</details>
