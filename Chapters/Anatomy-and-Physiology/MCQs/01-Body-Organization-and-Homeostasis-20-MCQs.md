### Q1. A wound lies closer to the elbow than another wound on the same forearm. Which term describes the first wound relative to the second?

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Priority:** Medium

- **A.** Proximal
- **B.** Distal
- **C.** Superficial
- **D.** Contralateral

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: A. Proximal**

Proximal ka matlab limb ke attachment point ke paas hona hai. Elbow ke paas wala wound wrist ke paas wale wound se proximal hai.

**Baaki options kyun sahi nahi hain?** Distal attachment se door hota hai. Superficial skin ke paas hota hai. Contralateral opposite side ko kehte hain.

</details>

### Q2. An imaging report divides the body into anterior and posterior portions. Which plane was used?

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Priority:** Medium

- **A.** Sagittal plane
- **B.** Transverse plane
- **C.** Oblique plane
- **D.** Coronal plane

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: D. Coronal plane**

Coronal plane body ko front aur back portions mein divide karta hai. Is plane mein chest ki front aur back structures ka relation samajh sakte hain.

**Baaki options kyun sahi nahi hain?** Sagittal left-right divide karta hai. Transverse upper-lower divide karta hai. Oblique diagonal direction mein hota hai.

</details>

### Q3. A CT image shows a horizontal cross-section of the abdomen. Which plane does it represent?

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Priority:** Medium

- **A.** Frontal plane
- **B.** Parasagittal plane
- **C.** Transverse plane
- **D.** Midsagittal plane

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: C. Transverse plane**

Transverse plane body ko upper aur lower parts mein divide karta hai. CT ke axial slices commonly isi orientation mein dikhte hain.

**Baaki options kyun sahi nahi hain?** Midsagittal equal left-right halves banata hai. Frontal front-back divide karta hai. Parasagittal unequal left-right parts banata hai.

</details>

### Q4. In the standard anatomical position, where is the radius relative to the ulna?

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Priority:** Medium

- **A.** Deep to the ulna
- **B.** Lateral
- **C.** Medial
- **D.** Posterior in every position

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: B. Lateral**

Anatomical position mein palms forward hoti hain. Radius thumb ki taraf hoti hai, jo body midline se door hai. Isliye radius lateral hai.

**Baaki options kyun sahi nahi hain?** Ulna little finger ki taraf medial hai. Posterior aur deep is side-to-side relationship ko describe nahi karte.

</details>

### Q5. A nurse documents a lesion on the same side as a patient's previous injury. Which term is correct?

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Priority:** Medium

- **B.** Ipsilateral
- **C.** Contralateral
- **D.** Bilateral

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: B. Ipsilateral**

Ipsilateral ka matlab body ki same side hota hai. Agar dono findings right side par hain toh woh ipsilateral hain.

**Baaki options kyun sahi nahi hain?** Contralateral opposite side hai. Bilateral dono sides hai. Intermediate do structures ke beech ka relation hai.

</details>

### Q6. A patient lies on the back with the face directed upward. Which position is described?

- **D.** Sims position

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

### Q7. During assessment, a nurse notes that the sternum lies closer to the midline than the nipples. Which relationship is correct?

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Priority:** Medium

- **A.** The sternum is distal to the nipples
- **B.** The sternum is lateral to the nipples
- **C.** The sternum is inferior to both nipples by definition
- **D.** The sternum is medial to the nipples

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: D. The sternum is medial to the nipples**

Medial ka matlab body ki center line ke paas hona hai. Sternum chest ke center mein hota hai.

**Baaki options kyun sahi nahi hain?** Distal limb attachment se distance batata hai. Lateral midline se door hota hai. Inferior neeche ki direction hai, jo yahan defining relation nahi hai.

</details>

### Q8. A penetrating injury reaches the heart but not the lungs. Which serous cavity immediately surrounds the heart?

- **D.** Pleural cavity

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

### Q9. A patient has pain in the right lower abdominal quadrant. Which structure is commonly located there?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Priority:** High

- **A.** Gastric fundus
- **B.** Left adrenal gland
- **C.** Appendix
- **D.** Spleen

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: C. Appendix**

Appendix aam taur par cecum se judi hoti hai jo right lower abdomen mein hai. Position vary kar sakti hai, isliye pain location alone diagnosis nahi karti.

**Baaki options kyun sahi nahi hain?** Spleen left upper abdomen mein hai. Gastric fundus upper left side hai. Left adrenal gland left kidney ke upar hai.

</details>

### Q10. Which anatomical structure separates the thoracic and abdominal cavities?

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Priority:** Medium

- **A.** Pelvic floor
- **B.** Diaphragm
- **C.** Mediastinum
- **D.** Peritoneum

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: B. Diaphragm**

Diaphragm ek dome-shaped muscle hai jo chest aur abdomen ke beech partition banati hai. Ye breathing mein bhi kaam karti hai.

**Baaki options kyun sahi nahi hain?** Mediastinum chest ka central region hai. Peritoneum abdominal serous membrane hai. Pelvic floor pelvis ke neeche support deta hai.

</details>

### Q11. A patient's body temperature rises and sweating increases until temperature moves toward normal. Which regulatory mechanism is operating?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Priority:** High

- **A.** Negative feedback
- **B.** Positive feedback
- **C.** Feedforward alone
- **D.** Loss of homeostasis without compensation

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: A. Negative feedback**

Negative feedback mein body change ke opposite direction mein response deti hai. Sweating heat loss badhati hai aur temperature normal taraf laati hai.

**Baaki options kyun sahi nahi hain?** Positive feedback initial change ko amplify karta hai. Feedforward anticipated change se pehle response hai. Yahan active compensation ho rahi hai.

</details>

### Q12. During labor, cervical stretching stimulates oxytocin release, which increases uterine contractions and further stretching. Which mechanism is illustrated?

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Priority:** Medium

- **A.** Negative feedback
- **B.** Passive diffusion
- **C.** Osmotic equilibrium
- **D.** Positive feedback

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: D. Positive feedback**

Positive feedback mein response initial stimulus ko aur badhata hai. Labor mein contractions aur cervical stretching ek doosre ko reinforce karte hain jab tak delivery se cycle end hoti hai.

**Baaki options kyun sahi nahi hain?** Negative feedback change ko oppose karta hai. Diffusion particles ka movement hai. Osmotic equilibrium water balance ka concept hai.

</details>

### Q13. A patient's blood pressure falls after standing and the heart rate rises within seconds. Which component detects the initial change?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Priority:** High

- **A.** Skeletal muscle motor end plates
- **B.** Pancreatic beta cells
- **C.** Pulmonary surfactant cells
- **D.** Arterial baroreceptors

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: D. Arterial baroreceptors**

Baroreceptors arterial walls ka stretch sense karte hain. BP girne par stretch kam hota hai aur brain autonomic response adjust karta hai.

**Baaki options kyun sahi nahi hain?** Motor end plates muscles activate karti hain. Beta cells glucose sense karti hain. Surfactant cells alveoli ki stability maintain karti hain.

</details>

### Q15. A physiological variable returns toward its normal range after a disturbance. What is this maintenance of internal stability called?

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Priority:** Medium

- **A.** Hemolysis
- **B.** Homeostasis
- **C.** Metastasis
- **D.** Hemostasis

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: B. Homeostasis**

Homeostasis ka matlab body apne internal conditions ko suitable range mein maintain karti hai. Glucose, temperature aur pH iske examples hain.

**Baaki options kyun sahi nahi hain?** Metastasis cancer spread hai. Hemostasis bleeding rokne ka process hai. Hemolysis RBC tootna hai.

</details>

### Q16. A patient has extensive burns with loss of body water. Which homeostatic response normally helps conserve water?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Priority:** High

- **A.** Increased ADH release
- **B.** Decreased thirst in every case
- **C.** Reduced renal water reabsorption
- **D.** Complete suppression of aldosterone during volume loss

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: A. Increased ADH release**

Volume ya water loss par ADH kidney ko water retain karne ka signal deta hai. Isse urine mein water loss kam ho sakta hai.

**Baaki options kyun sahi nahi hain?** Thirst usually increase ho sakti hai. Renal water reabsorption badh sakti hai. Volume loss RAAS activate kar sakta hai.

</details>

### Q19. Why can inflammation of the diaphragm produce pain felt near the shoulder?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Priority:** High

- **A.** All abdominal organs receive only radial nerve supply
- **B.** Shoulder skin directly secretes pleural fluid
- **C.** Shared cervical spinal segments receive diaphragmatic and shoulder-region sensory input
- **D.** The diaphragm is located inside the shoulder joint

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: C. Shared cervical spinal segments receive diaphragmatic and shoulder-region sensory input**

Diaphragm ki sensory supply ka central hissa phrenic nerve C3–C5 se related hai. Shoulder region ke signals bhi overlapping spinal segments tak jaate hain. Brain pain ko shoulder mein feel kar sakta hai.

**Baaki options kyun sahi nahi hain?** Diaphragm shoulder joint mein nahi hai. Radial nerve abdominal organs ki sole supply nahi hai. Skin pleural fluid nahi banati.

</details>

### Q20. A nurse describes the skin relative to the underlying skeletal muscle. Which term is appropriate?

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Priority:** Medium

- **A.** Inferior
- **B.** Superficial
- **C.** Proximal
- **D.** Distal

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: B. Superficial**

Superficial ka matlab body surface ke paas hota hai. Skin muscle se zyada surface par hai.

**Baaki options kyun sahi nahi hain?** Proximal aur distal attachment se distance batate hain. Inferior neeche ki direction batata hai.

</details>
