### Q1. What is a major physiological function of lymphatic vessels?

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Priority:** Medium

- **A.** Return excess interstitial fluid and proteins to the circulation
- **B.** Pump oxygenated blood directly into all arteries
- **C.** Produce all red cells in the skin
- **D.** Filter urine into the bladder

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: A. Return excess interstitial fluid and proteins to the circulation**

Capillaries se filtered fluid ka hissa lymphatic vessels collect karti hain. Lymph veins mein return hoti hai aur tissue fluid balance maintain karne mein help karti hai.

**Baaki options kyun sahi nahi hain?** Arterial pumping heart karta hai. RBC marrow mein banti hain. Urine kidney aur bladder se related hai.

</details>

### Q2. Which duct drains lymph from both lower limbs, abdomen and most of the left upper body?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Priority:** High

- **A.** Right lymphatic duct alone
- **B.** Common bile duct
- **C.** Ureter
- **D.** Thoracic duct

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: D. Thoracic duct**

Thoracic duct body ke majority regions ki lymph left venous angle tak laati hai. Right upper quadrant ka drainage generally right lymphatic duct karti hai.

**Baaki options kyun sahi nahi hain?** Right lymphatic duct limited right upper body drain karti hai. Bile duct bile aur ureter urine transport karta hai.

</details>

### Q8. Which immune response is usually rapid and does not require previous exposure to a specific antigen?

- **D.** Secondary adaptive immunity only

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

### Q9. Which feature distinguishes adaptive immunity from most innate responses?

- **D.** Complete absence of lymphocytes

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

Adaptive cells specific antigens recognize karti hain. Exposure ke baad memory cells ban sakti hain, jisse next response faster aur stronger hota hai.

**Baaki options kyun sahi nahi hain?** Lymphocytes adaptive immunity ke major cells hain. Skin barrier innate defense hai. Memory adaptive immunity ka key feature hai.

</details>

### Q10. Which antibody class can cross the placenta in significant amounts?

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Priority:** Medium

- **A.** IgE
- **B.** IgG
- **C.** IgM
- **D.** IgA as the main placental antibody

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: B. IgG**

IgG placenta ke through fetus tak transfer ho sakti hai. Isse newborn ko temporary maternal antibody protection milti hai.

**Baaki options kyun sahi nahi hain?** IgM large pentamer hai aur placenta readily cross nahi karti. IgA mainly mucosal secretions mein hai. IgE allergy se related hai.

</details>

### Q18. A person develops immediate wheezing and urticaria after antigen re-exposure. Which hypersensitivity mechanism is classically involved?

### Q20. After surgical removal of axillary lymph nodes, a patient develops persistent ipsilateral arm swelling. Which mechanism contributes?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Priority:** High

- **A.** Increased pulmonary ventilation
- **B.** Impaired lymphatic drainage and accumulation of protein-rich interstitial fluid
- **C.** Increased lymphatic return from the arm in every case
- **D.** Complete absence of capillary filtration

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: B. Impaired lymphatic drainage and accumulation of protein-rich interstitial fluid**

Lymph pathways damage ya remove ho jaayein toh tissue fluid aur proteins ka return kam ho sakta hai. Isse lymphedema develop ho sakta hai.

**Baaki options kyun sahi nahi hain?** Increased return swelling ko reduce kar sakta hai. Capillary filtration still hoti hai. Ventilation arm lymph drainage ka primary mechanism nahi hai.

</details>
