### Q1. Which cells of the testes produce testosterone?

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Priority:** Medium

- **A.** Leydig cells
- **B.** Sertoli cells as the principal testosterone source
- **C.** Spermatids
- **D.** Prostatic epithelial cells

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: A. Leydig cells**

Leydig cells seminiferous tubules ke beech hoti hain. LH inhe testosterone banane ka signal deta hai.

**Baaki options kyun sahi nahi hain?** Sertoli cells developing sperm support karti hain. Spermatids immature sperm cells hain. Prostate seminal fluid mein contribute karti hai.

</details>

### Q2. Where does spermatogenesis occur?

- **A.** Epididymal duct alone

- **C.** Seminal vesicle
- **D.** Seminiferous tubules

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

Seminiferous tubules testes mein sperm cells develop karti hain. Sertoli cells maturation environment support karti hain.

**Baaki options kyun sahi nahi hain?** Epididymis sperm maturation aur storage mein role rakhta hai. Vas deferens transport karta hai. Seminal vesicle fluid release karti hai.

</details>

### Q6. Which hormone surge is the principal immediate trigger for ovulation?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Priority:** High

- **A.** LH surge
- **B.** Prolactin surge
- **C.** ADH surge
- **D.** Sustained low FSH alone

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: A. LH surge**

Dominant follicle se estrogen increase hota hai aur midcycle positive feedback LH surge trigger karta hai. LH surge ovulation initiate karne mein help karta hai.

**Baaki options kyun sahi nahi hain?** Prolactin lactation mein hai. ADH water balance mein hai. Low FSH alone ovulation ka immediate trigger nahi hai.

</details>

### Q7. Which structure produces most progesterone during the luteal phase?

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Baaki options kyun sahi nahi hain?** Oocyte egg cell hai. Cervical glands mucus banati hain. Pituitary gonadotropins release karti hai.

</details>

### Q8. Which endometrial phase is predominantly supported by progesterone after ovulation?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Priority:** High

- **A.** Menstrual shedding in every case
- **B.** Permanent absence of endometrial glands
- **C.** Secretory phase
- **D.** Proliferative phase driven mainly by estrogen

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: C. Secretory phase**

Progesterone endometrial glands ko secretory changes ke liye stimulate karta hai. Isse potential embryo implantation ke liye suitable environment ban sakta hai.

**Baaki options kyun sahi nahi hain?** Proliferative phase estrogen-dominant hai. Menstruation hormone withdrawal se hoti hai. Glands present hoti hain.

</details>

</details>

### Q11. What is the main role of trophoblast-derived hCG in early pregnancy?

- **C.** Trigger fetal lung inflation before birth
- **D.** Replace all maternal thyroid hormones

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

</details>

### Q13. Which placental function allows exchange while normally keeping maternal and fetal circulations mostly separate?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Priority:** High

- **A.** Direct permanent mixing of all maternal and fetal blood
- **B.** Production of all fetal red cells throughout life
- **C.** Complete exclusion of gases from fetal blood
- **D.** Exchange across placental villous barriers

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: D. Exchange across placental villous barriers**

Chorionic villi mein fetal capillaries hoti hain jo maternal blood spaces ke paas exchange karti hain. Oxygen aur nutrients barrier cross kar sakte hain bina normal large-scale blood mixing ke.

**Baaki options kyun sahi nahi hain?** Direct complete mixing normal nahi hai. RBC production sites development ke saath change hote hain. Gas exchange essential hai.

</details>

### Q18. Why does prolactin rise during breastfeeding after birth?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Priority:** High

- **A.** Suckling permanently destroys all pituitary cells
- **B.** Oxytocin blocks every prolactin receptor
- **C.** The placenta continues producing all milk after delivery
- **D.** Suckling reduces hypothalamic dopaminergic inhibition of prolactin release

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: D. Suckling reduces hypothalamic dopaminergic inhibition of prolactin release**

Baby suckle karta hai toh sensory signals hypothalamus tak jaate hain. Dopamine ka inhibiting effect kam ho sakta hai aur prolactin release badhta hai, jisse milk production support hoti hai.

**Baaki options kyun sahi nahi hain?** Pituitary destruction normal response nahi hai. Oxytocin milk ejection promote karta hai. Placenta delivery ke baad milk produce nahi karti.

</details>

### Q20. Which physiological change is most directly responsible for normal menopause-related elevation of FSH?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Priority:** High

- **A.** Complete suppression of hypothalamic signaling
- **B.** Reduced ovarian inhibin and estrogen feedback
- **C.** Increased ovarian follicle reserve
- **D.** Permanent increase in placental hCG

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: B. Reduced ovarian inhibin and estrogen feedback**

Ovarian follicles kam hone par estrogen aur inhibin feedback reduce hota hai. Pituitary FSH zyada release kar sakti hai. Menopause diagnosis clinical context mein hota hai.

**Baaki options kyun sahi nahi hain?** Follicle reserve decrease hota hai. Placental hCG menopause ka normal mechanism nahi hai. Hypothalamic-pituitary stimulation completely suppress nahi hoti.

</details>
