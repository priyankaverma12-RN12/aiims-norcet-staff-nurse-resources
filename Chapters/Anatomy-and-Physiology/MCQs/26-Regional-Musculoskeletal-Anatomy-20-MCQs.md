### Q1. Which bone forms the upper arm between shoulder and elbow?

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Priority:** Medium

- **A.** Humerus
- **B.** Radius
- **C.** Ulna
- **D.** Scapula

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: A. Humerus**

Humerus upper arm ki long bone hai. Proximally shoulder aur distally elbow joint mein participate karti hai.

**Baaki options kyun sahi nahi hain?** Radius aur ulna forearm mein hain. Scapula shoulder girdle bone hai.

</details>

### Q2. Which bony structure forms the point of the elbow?

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Priority:** Medium

- **A.** Radial styloid
- **B.** Coracoid process
- **C.** Acromion
- **D.** Olecranon of the ulna

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: D. Olecranon of the ulna**

Olecranon ulna ka proximal projection hai. Elbow extend hone par ye humerus ke olecranon fossa mein fit hota hai.

**Baaki options kyun sahi nahi hain?** Radial styloid wrist ke paas hai. Coracoid aur acromion scapula ke projections hain.

</details>

### Q3. Which carpal bone is classically vulnerable after a fall onto an outstretched hand with anatomical snuffbox tenderness?

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Priority:** Medium

- **A.** Hamate as the classic snuffbox bone
- **B.** Capitate
- **C.** Scaphoid
- **D.** Pisiform

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: C. Scaphoid**

Scaphoid thumb side ki carpal bone hai. Outstretched hand par fall ke baad snuffbox pain is fracture ka clue ho sakta hai.

**Baaki options kyun sahi nahi hain?** Pisiform ulnar side par hai. Hamate aur capitate other carpal bones hain, classic snuffbox location nahi.

</details>

### Q6. Which bone articulates superiorly with the tibia and fibula to form the ankle joint?

- **D.** First metatarsal

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

### Q8. Which vertebral structure lies between adjacent vertebral bodies and distributes compressive loads?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Priority:** High

- **A.** Spinous process
- **B.** Vertebral artery
- **C.** Intervertebral disc
- **D.** Ligamentum flavum alone

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: C. Intervertebral disc**

Intervertebral disc mein outer annulus aur inner nucleus pulposus hota hai. Ye load distribute aur movement allow karta hai.

**Baaki options kyun sahi nahi hain?** Ligamentum flavum vertebral arches connect karta hai. Spinous process attachment point hai. Vertebral artery blood vessel hai.

</details>

### Q9. A posterolateral lumbar disc herniation commonly compresses which structure?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Priority:** High

- **A.** The superior vena cava
- **B.** The recurrent laryngeal nerve
- **C.** An adjacent traversing spinal nerve root
- **D.** The optic nerve

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: C. An adjacent traversing spinal nerve root**

Disc ka inner material annulus tear ke through protrude ho sakta hai. Posterolateral protrusion nearby traversing nerve root ko compress kar sakti hai, level par depend karta hai.

**Baaki options kyun sahi nahi hain?** Optic, vena cava aur laryngeal structures lumbar canal mein nahi hote.

</details>

### Q14. Which soft tissue structure limits anterior translation of the tibia relative to the femur?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Priority:** High

- **A.** Patellar ligament alone
- **B.** Medial meniscus alone
- **C.** Anterior cruciate ligament
- **D.** Posterior cruciate ligament

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: C. Anterior cruciate ligament**

ACL tibia ko femur ke relative excessive anterior movement se rokne mein help karta hai. Ye knee rotational stability bhi support karta hai.

**Baaki options kyun sahi nahi hain?** PCL posterior translation limit karta hai. Patellar ligament extensor mechanism aur meniscus cushioning mein role rakhta hai.

</details>

### Q15. What is a major function of the medial and lateral menisci in the knee?

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Priority:** Medium

- **A.** Produce red blood cells
- **B.** Distribute load and improve joint congruence
- **C.** Generate all quadriceps force
- **D.** Replace the cruciate ligaments completely

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: B. Distribute load and improve joint congruence**

Menisci fibrocartilage wedges hain jo femur aur tibia ke beech contact area badhati hain. Isse load distribute aur stability support hoti hai.

**Baaki options kyun sahi nahi hain?** Quadriceps force muscle generate karti hai. Cruciate ligaments separate stabilizers hain. RBC marrow banata hai.

</details>

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

</details>

### Q20. During single-leg stance, which muscle group on the weight-bearing side helps keep the opposite pelvis from dropping?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Priority:** High

- **A.** Hip adductors as the sole stabilizers
- **B.** Hip abductors, especially gluteus medius and minimus
- **C.** Ankle plantarflexors alone
- **D.** Elbow flexors

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: B. Hip abductors, especially gluteus medius and minimus**

Weight-bearing hip abductors pelvis ko level maintain karne mein help karti hain. Weakness se opposite pelvis drop ho sakti hai, jise Trendelenburg sign kehte hain.

**Baaki options kyun sahi nahi hain?** Ankle aur elbow muscles pelvic stabilization ki principal muscles nahi hain. Adductors sole stabilizers nahi hain.

</details>
