### Q1. Which salivary gland is located anterior and inferior to the external ear and drains through Stensen duct?

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Priority:** Medium

- **A.** Parotid gland
- **B.** Submandibular gland
- **C.** Sublingual gland
- **D.** Thyroid gland

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: A. Parotid gland**

Parotid major salivary gland hai jo ear ke paas hoti hai. Stensen duct cheek ke andar upper molar ke paas open hoti hai.

**Baaki options kyun sahi nahi hain?** Submandibular Wharton duct se drain karti hai. Sublingual tongue ke neeche hai. Thyroid hormone banati hai.

</details>

### Q2. Which structure folds toward the laryngeal inlet during swallowing and contributes to airway protection?

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Priority:** Medium

- **A.** Uvula alone
- **B.** Pylorus
- **C.** Ileocecal valve
- **D.** Epiglottis

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: D. Epiglottis**

Swallowing ke dauran larynx elevate hoti hai aur epiglottis airway inlet cover karne mein help karti hai. Vocal fold closure bhi important protection hai.

**Baaki options kyun sahi nahi hain?** Uvula nasopharynx closure mein role rakhti hai. Pylorus stomach outlet hai. Ileocecal valve bowel junction hai.

</details>

### Q3. Which part of swallowing is normally initiated voluntarily?

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Priority:** Medium

- **A.** Esophageal peristalsis throughout
- **B.** Gastric emptying
- **C.** Oral phase
- **D.** Pharyngeal phase in its entirety

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: C. Oral phase**

Oral phase mein tongue food bolus ko pharynx ki taraf push karti hai. Iske baad reflex swallowing phases activate hoti hain.

**Baaki options kyun sahi nahi hain?** Pharyngeal aur esophageal phases largely involuntary hain. Gastric emptying swallowing phase nahi hai.

</details>

### Q4. Which anatomical opening in the diaphragm transmits the esophagus?

- **A.** Obturator canal
- **B.** Esophageal hiatus
- **C.** Aortic hiatus
- **D.** Caval opening

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: B. Esophageal hiatus**

Esophagus diaphragm ke esophageal hiatus se abdomen mein enter karta hai. Hiatus ke paas stomach-esophagus junction hota hai.

**Baaki options kyun sahi nahi hain?** Aortic hiatus aorta aur caval opening IVC transmit karti hain. Obturator canal pelvis mein hai.

</details>

### Q6. Which stomach region controls the passage of chyme into the duodenum through a sphincter?

- **D.** Gastric body alone

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

</details>

### Q7. Which intestinal segment forms the first portion of the small intestine immediately beyond the pylorus?

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Priority:** Medium

- **A.** Jejunum
- **B.** Ileum
- **C.** Cecum
- **D.** Duodenum

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

Duodenum stomach se chyme receive karti hai aur bile-pancreatic secretions mix karti hai.

</details>

### Q8. Where do the common bile duct and main pancreatic duct commonly empty through the major duodenal papilla?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Priority:** High

- **A.** Terminal ileum
- **B.** Ascending colon
- **C.** Descending second part of the duodenum
- **D.** Gastric fundus

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: C. Descending second part of the duodenum**

Bile aur pancreatic juice usually hepatopancreatic ampulla ke through descending duodenum mein enter karte hain. Variations possible hain.

**Baaki options kyun sahi nahi hain?** Fundus stomach mein hai. Terminal ileum distal small bowel hai. Ascending colon large bowel hai.

</details>

### Q9. Which structure separates the terminal ileum from the cecum?

- **D.** Pyloric sphincter

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

</details>

### Q11. Which peritoneal fold suspends much of the small intestine and carries vessels and nerves to it?

- **B.** Falx cerebri
- **C.** Pleural membrane
- **D.** Pericardial sac

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

</details>

### Q12. Which peritoneal structure hangs apron-like from the greater curvature of the stomach?

### Q13. Which structure is mainly retroperitoneal in an adult?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Priority:** High

- **A.** Entire stomach
- **B.** Most jejunal loops
- **C.** Transverse colon as a fully retroperitoneal structure
- **D.** Most of the pancreas except its tail

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

Pancreas ka head aur body posterior abdominal wall ke paas largely retroperitoneal hain. Tail splenic region mein different relation rakhti hai.

**Baaki options kyun sahi nahi hain?** Stomach, jejunum aur transverse colon generally intraperitoneal hain.

</details>

### Q14. Which nervous plexus of the gastrointestinal tract primarily regulates smooth muscle motility?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Priority:** High

- **A.** Brachial plexus
- **B.** Choroid plexus
- **C.** Myenteric plexus
- **D.** Submucosal plexus as the primary motility plexus

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: C. Myenteric plexus**

Myenteric ya Auerbach plexus muscle layers ke beech hota hai. Ye peristalsis aur gut muscle activity regulate karta hai.

**Baaki options kyun sahi nahi hain?** Submucosal plexus secretion aur local blood flow mein prominent hai. Brachial upper limb aur choroid CSF se related hain.

</details>

### Q15. Which enteric plexus primarily regulates mucosal secretion and local blood flow?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Priority:** High

- **A.** Cardiac Purkinje network
- **B.** Submucosal plexus
- **C.** Myenteric plexus alone
- **D.** Sacral somatic plexus

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: B. Submucosal plexus**

Submucosal ya Meissner plexus intestinal lining ke neeche hota hai. Ye secretion aur local circulation regulate karta hai.

**Baaki options kyun sahi nahi hain?** Myenteric main motility plexus hai. Sacral plexus peripheral nerves aur Purkinje heart conduction se related hain.

</details>

### Q16. What is the primary role of segmentation contractions in the small intestine?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Priority:** High

- **A.** Mix chyme with digestive secretions and expose it to the mucosa
- **B.** Propel every bolus rapidly in one direction without mixing
- **C.** Close the pylorus permanently
- **D.** Stop contact with absorptive cells

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: A. Mix chyme with digestive secretions and expose it to the mucosa**

Segmentation mein intestinal muscle local contractions karti hai. Isse chyme mix hota hai aur absorption surface ke contact mein aata hai.

**Baaki options kyun sahi nahi hain?** Rapid forward propulsion peristalsis se related hai. Pylorus permanently close nahi hota. Mucosal contact increase hota hai.

</details>

### Q18. Why does fatty chyme in the duodenum tend to slow gastric emptying?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Priority:** High

- **A.** Fat directly paralyzes every intestinal muscle permanently
- **B.** The pylorus is anatomically absent after a fatty meal
- **C.** Fat increases the rate of emptying without limit
- **D.** Enterogastric feedback and hormones such as CCK reduce gastric delivery

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: D. Enterogastric feedback and hormones such as CCK reduce gastric delivery**

Duodenum fatty meal ko process karne ke liye time chahti hai. CCK aur neural feedback stomach emptying slow karne mein help karte hain.

**Baaki options kyun sahi nahi hain?** Permanent paralysis nahi hoti. Pylorus present rehta hai. Emptying regulated process hai.

</details>

### Q19. Which normal mechanism permits voluntary postponement of defecation?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Priority:** High

- **A.** Contraction of the gallbladder
- **B.** Increased pyloric relaxation
- **C.** Contraction of the external anal sphincter and pelvic floor
- **D.** Permanent involuntary closure of the internal sphincter

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: C. Contraction of the external anal sphincter and pelvic floor**

External anal sphincter skeletal muscle hai aur voluntary control mein hai. Pelvic floor ke saath temporarily stool passage postpone kar sakta hai.

**Baaki options kyun sahi nahi hain?** Internal sphincter smooth muscle hai. Gallbladder aur pylorus defecation ke voluntary control structures nahi hain.

</details>

### Q20. Which liver cells are resident macrophages that help clear particles from sinusoidal blood?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Priority:** High

- **A.** Gastric chief cells
- **B.** Kupffer cells
- **C.** Hepatocytes as the sole phagocytes
- **D.** Pancreatic beta cells

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: B. Kupffer cells**

Kupffer cells liver sinusoids mein resident macrophages hain. Ye particles aur microbes remove karne mein help karti hain.

**Baaki options kyun sahi nahi hain?** Hepatocytes metabolic functions karti hain. Beta cells insulin aur chief cells pepsinogen banati hain.

</details>
