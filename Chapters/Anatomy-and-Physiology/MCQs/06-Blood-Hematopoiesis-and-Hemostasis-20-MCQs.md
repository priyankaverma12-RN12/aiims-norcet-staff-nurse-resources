### Q1. Which organ produces most erythropoietin in a healthy adult?

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Priority:** Medium

- **A.** Kidney
- **B.** Spleen
- **C.** Pancreas
- **D.** Thymus

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: A. Kidney**

Kidney oxygen supply kam hone par erythropoietin release kar sakti hai. Ye bone marrow ko RBC production badhane ka signal deta hai.

**Baaki options kyun sahi nahi hain?** Spleen old RBC remove karne mein help karti hai. Pancreas digestive enzymes aur hormones banata hai. Thymus T-cell maturation mein role rakhta hai.

</details>

### Q2. Which is the approximate normal lifespan of a mature human erythrocyte?

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Priority:** Medium

- **A.** 12 days
- **B.** 365 days
- **C.** 5 days
- **D.** 120 days

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: D. 120 days**

Mature RBC blood mein roughly 120 din circulate karti hai. Purani cells spleen aur other macrophages remove karte hain.

**Baaki options kyun sahi nahi hain?** 12 aur 5 din usual RBC lifespan se bahut kam hain. 365 din typical lifespan se zyada hai.

</details>

### Q3. A patient with severe anemia has normal arterial oxygen saturation. Which physiological variable can still be reduced?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Priority:** High

- **A.** Arterial carbon dioxide tension necessarily
- **B.** Atmospheric oxygen partial pressure
- **C.** Total arterial oxygen content
- **D.** Percentage of hemoglobin saturated by oxygen in every case

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: C. Total arterial oxygen content**

Saturation batati hai available hemoglobin ka kitna percent oxygen bind kar raha hai. Hemoglobin amount kam ho toh total oxygen content kam ho sakta hai.

**Baaki options kyun sahi nahi hain?** Saturation normal reh sakti hai. PaCO2 ventilation par depend karta hai. Atmospheric oxygen anemia se change nahi hoti.

</details>

### Q4. Which portion of hemoglobin directly binds oxygen reversibly?

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Priority:** Medium

- **A.** Membrane cholesterol
- **B.** Iron in the heme group
- **C.** Globin chain amino nitrogen alone
- **D.** Plasma albumin attached to the RBC

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: B. Iron in the heme group**

Heme group ka ferrous iron oxygen bind karta hai. Hemoglobin mein four heme groups hote hain, jo oxygen transport allow karte hain.

**Baaki options kyun sahi nahi hain?** Globin heme ko support karta hai. Albumin mainly plasma protein hai. Cholesterol membrane structure mein role rakhta hai.

</details>

### Q5. A patient's bone marrow responds appropriately to acute blood loss. Which circulating cell count may increase after a delay?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Priority:** High

- **A.** Eosinophil count as the primary RBC marker
- **B.** Reticulocyte count
- **C.** Mature platelet count as the only indicator of erythropoiesis
- **D.** Basophil count as the primary RBC marker

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: B. Reticulocyte count**

Reticulocytes immature RBCs hain jo marrow se blood mein aati hain. Blood loss ke response mein erythropoietin se production badhe toh reticulocytes increase ho sakti hain.

**Baaki options kyun sahi nahi hain?** Platelets clotting mein role rakhti hain. Basophils aur eosinophils white cells hain, RBC production ke direct markers nahi hain.

</details>

### Q9. Immediately after a small blood vessel is injured, which process forms the initial platelet plug?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Priority:** High

- **A.** Erythrocyte production
- **B.** Antibody secretion by plasma cells
- **C.** Platelet adhesion, activation and aggregation
- **D.** Fibrinolysis as the first hemostatic event

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: C. Platelet adhesion, activation and aggregation**

Injury par collagen aur von Willebrand factor platelet adhesion mein help karte hain. Platelets activate hokar ek doosre se judti hain aur initial plug banati hain.

**Baaki options kyun sahi nahi hain?** Fibrinolysis clot breakdown karti hai. RBC production delayed process hai. Antibodies primary hemostasis ka mechanism nahi hain.

</details>

### Q10. Which enzyme converts fibrinogen into fibrin during coagulation?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Priority:** High

- **A.** Amylase
- **B.** Thrombin
- **C.** Plasmin
- **D.** Pepsin

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: B. Thrombin**

Thrombin soluble fibrinogen ko fibrin strands mein convert karta hai. Fibrin platelet plug ko stable banane mein help karta hai.

**Baaki options kyun sahi nahi hain?** Plasmin fibrin break karta hai. Pepsin protein digestion aur amylase starch digestion karti hai.

</details>

### Q16. An adult with blood group A has which expected naturally occurring ABO antibody in plasma?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Priority:** High

- **D.** Neither anti-A nor anti-B in every case

### Q19. Why can an Rh-negative mother become sensitized after exposure to Rh-positive fetal red cells?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Priority:** High

- **A.** Fetal hemoglobin converts maternal cells into group O
- **B.** The D antigen is a plasma electrolyte
- **C.** Her immune system may form anti-D antibodies against the D antigen
- **D.** Rh-negative mothers naturally have all anti-D antibodies from birth

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: C. Her immune system may form anti-D antibodies against the D antigen**

Rh-negative mother ke blood mein fetal Rh-positive RBCs enter karein toh D antigen immune response trigger kar sakta hai. Anti-D prophylaxis sensitization risk kam karne mein help karti hai.

**Baaki options kyun sahi nahi hain?** Anti-D typically exposure ke baad banti hai. Fetal hemoglobin ABO group change nahi karta. D antigen RBC surface protein hai.

</details>

### Q20. Which enzyme breaks down fibrin during physiological clot dissolution?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Priority:** High

- **A.** Trypsin
- **B.** Plasmin
- **C.** Thrombin
- **D.** Renin

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: B. Plasmin**

Plasmin fibrin strands ko break karta hai. Is process ko fibrinolysis kehte hain aur ye clot remodeling mein important hai.

**Baaki options kyun sahi nahi hain?** Thrombin fibrin formation karta hai. Renin RAAS start karta hai. Trypsin digestive enzyme hai.

</details>
