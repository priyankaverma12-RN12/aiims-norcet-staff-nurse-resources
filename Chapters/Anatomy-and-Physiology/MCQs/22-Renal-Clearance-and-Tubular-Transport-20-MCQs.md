### Q1. A freely filtered marker has urine concentration 100 mg/dL, plasma concentration 1 mg/dL and urine flow 1.2 mL/min. What is its clearance?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Priority:** High

- **A.** 120 mL/min
- **B.** 83.3 mL/min
- **C.** 1.2 mL/min
- **D.** 12 mL/min

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: A. 120 mL/min**

Clearance = urine concentration x urine flow / plasma concentration. Yahan 100 x 1.2 / 1 = 120 mL/min.

**Baaki options kyun sahi nahi hain?** 83.3 inverse calculation hai. 1.2 sirf urine flow hai. 12 decimal error hai.

</details>

### Q2. A patient has GFR 120 mL/min and renal plasma flow 600 mL/min. What is the filtration fraction?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Priority:** High

- **A.** 5%
- **B.** 50%
- **C.** 80%
- **D.** 20%

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: D. 20%**

Filtration fraction renal plasma flow ka portion hai jo filter hota hai. 120/600 x 100 = 20%.

**Baaki options kyun sahi nahi hain?** 5% given ratio nahi hai. 50% half hota hai. 80% unfiltered fraction hai.

</details>

### Q3. If renal blood flow is 1000 mL/min and hematocrit is 40%, what is renal plasma flow?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Priority:** High

- **A.** 1400 mL/min
- **B.** 2500 mL/min
- **C.** 600 mL/min
- **D.** 400 mL/min

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: C. 600 mL/min**

Blood mein RBC volume 40% hai toh plasma fraction 60% hai. 1000 x 0.60 = 600 mL/min.

**Baaki options kyun sahi nahi hain?** 400 RBC volume flow hai. 1400 addition error hai. 2500 wrong division hai.

</details>

### Q5. Why can glucose appear in urine when the filtered glucose load exceeds tubular transport capacity?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Priority:** High

- **A.** ADH converts glucose into urea
- **B.** Glucose reabsorption becomes saturated
- **C.** Glomerular filtration always stops
- **D.** All glucose is actively secreted by the collecting duct

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: B. Glucose reabsorption becomes saturated**

Proximal tubule glucose transporters ki maximum capacity hoti hai. Load capacity se zyada ho toh kuch glucose urine mein reh jaata hai.

**Baaki options kyun sahi nahi hain?** Filtration stop nahi hoti. Glucose collecting duct se routinely secrete nahi hota. ADH water balance control karta hai.

</details>

### Q6. What is the expected effect of glucose remaining in tubular fluid in large amounts?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Priority:** High

- **A.** Osmotic water retention within the tubule and increased urine output
- **B.** Complete water reabsorption regardless of solute
- **C.** Reduced urinary flow in every case
- **D.** Immediate disappearance of all sodium from plasma

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: A. Osmotic water retention within the tubule and increased urine output**

Tubular glucose water ko lumen mein retain kar sakta hai. Isse urine output badhta hai aur dehydration ho sakti hai.

**Baaki options kyun sahi nahi hain?** Complete water reabsorption nahi hoti. Urine flow often badhta hai. Plasma sodium response volume aur water balance par depend karta hai.

</details>

### Q7. Why can creatinine clearance slightly overestimate measured GFR?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Priority:** High

- **A.** Creatinine is normally completely reabsorbed
- **B.** Creatinine is never filtered
- **C.** Creatinine is produced only in the bladder
- **D.** Creatinine undergoes some tubular secretion in addition to filtration

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: D. Creatinine undergoes some tubular secretion in addition to filtration**

Creatinine glomerulus se filter hota hai aur kuch amount tubules se secrete bhi hota hai. Isliye excretion pure filtered load se thodi zyada ho sakti hai.

**Baaki options kyun sahi nahi hain?** Complete reabsorption expected nahi hai. Filtration hoti hai. Creatinine muscle metabolism se banta hai.

</details>

### Q8. Which effect does moderate efferent arteriolar constriction initially tend to have on glomerular capillary pressure?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Priority:** High

- **A.** It eliminates Bowman space pressure
- **B.** It stops all tubular reabsorption
- **C.** It increases glomerular hydrostatic pressure
- **D.** It always reduces glomerular pressure to zero

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: C. It increases glomerular hydrostatic pressure**

Efferent vessel outflow resistance badhaye toh glomerular pressure initially increase ho sakta hai. Severe constriction renal flow aur GFR ko reduce bhi kar sakti hai.

**Baaki options kyun sahi nahi hain?** Pressure zero hona expected nahi hai. Bowman space still present hai. Reabsorption necessarily stop nahi hoti.

</details>

### Q9. What is the principal effect of constricting renal arterioles in response to a moderate rise in renal perfusion pressure through the myogenic mechanism?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Priority:** High

- **A.** Permanently stops renal blood flow
- **B.** Creates urinary glucose without filtration
- **C.** Helps stabilize renal blood flow and GFR
- **D.** Makes GFR rise without limit

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: C. Helps stabilize renal blood flow and GFR**

Renal afferent arteriole pressure rise par constrict kar sakti hai. Is autoregulation se filtration relatively stable rehti hai.

**Baaki options kyun sahi nahi hain?** Unlimited rise autoregulation ka opposite hai. Flow normally continue hota hai. Glucose filtration independent nahi hoti.

</details>

### Q10. Which renal region has the highest interstitial osmolality during effective urine concentration?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Priority:** High

- **A.** Urinary bladder wall
- **B.** Deep renal medulla
- **C.** Renal cortex near the capsule
- **D.** Renal artery lumen

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: B. Deep renal medulla**

Countercurrent multiplication aur urea recycling deep medulla ko concentrated banate hain. ADH present ho toh water collecting duct se yahan move kar sakta hai.

**Baaki options kyun sahi nahi hain?** Cortex relatively isotonic hoti hai. Artery systemic blood carry karti hai. Bladder urine store karta hai.

</details>

### Q11. Which specialized capillary system helps preserve the medullary osmotic gradient through countercurrent exchange?

### Q17. What does a urine specific gravity close to 1.001 generally suggest about the sample?

### Q18. A patient produces 300 mL urine in 10 hours and weighs 60 kg. What is the average weight-adjusted urine output?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Priority:** High

- **A.** 5 mL/kg/hour

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

</details>

### Q19. Why is urinary glucose testing alone unreliable for estimating current plasma glucose?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Priority:** High

- **A.** Glucose never enters the glomerular filtrate

- **C.** The renal glucose threshold and tubular handling vary
- **D.** Urine glucose always equals plasma glucose numerically

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

Urine glucose filtered load, transport threshold aur collection time par depend karta hai. Blood glucose test current level better assess karta hai.

**Baaki options kyun sahi nahi hain?** Urine aur plasma values equal nahi hoti. Glucose freely filter hota hai. ADH water regulate karta hai.

</details>

### Q20. Which mechanism normally moves urine through the ureter even against gravity?

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Priority:** Medium

- **C.** Skeletal muscle voluntary contraction throughout the ureter
- **D.** Glomerular filtration pressure alone

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

Ureter ki smooth muscle rhythmic waves generate karti hai jo urine bladder tak push karti hain.

**Baaki options kyun sahi nahi hain?** Ureter voluntary skeletal muscle tube nahi hai. Filtration pressure upstream hai. Cilia urine transport ka principal mechanism nahi hain.

</details>
