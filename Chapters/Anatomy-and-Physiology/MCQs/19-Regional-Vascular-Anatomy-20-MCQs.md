### Q1. Which artery is normally palpated in the anatomical snuffbox or at the lateral wrist?

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Priority:** Medium

- **A.** Radial artery
- **B.** Ulnar artery
- **C.** Brachial artery
- **D.** Axillary artery

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: A. Radial artery**

Radial artery forearm ki thumb side par travel karti hai. Wrist par ye superficial hoti hai, isliye pulse feel karna easy hota hai.

**Baaki options kyun sahi nahi hain?** Ulnar artery little-finger side hai. Brachial upper arm aur axillary armpit mein hai.

</details>

### Q2. Which artery is compressed during routine manual blood pressure measurement at the upper arm?

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Priority:** Medium

- **A.** Radial artery
- **B.** Subclavian artery
- **C.** Ulnar artery
- **D.** Brachial artery

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: D. Brachial artery**

Cuff upper arm ki brachial artery ko compress karta hai. Stethoscope se antecubital area mein Korotkoff sounds sunte hain.

**Baaki options kyun sahi nahi hain?** Radial aur ulnar forearm arteries hain. Subclavian clavicle ke neeche hai.

</details>

### Q3. Which pulse is palpated on the dorsum of the foot lateral to the extensor hallucis longus tendon?

- **C.** Dorsalis pedis
- **D.** Posterior tibial

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

Dorsalis pedis anterior tibial artery ki continuation hai. Foot ke dorsum par is pulse se distal arterial flow assess kar sakte hain.

**Baaki options kyun sahi nahi hain?** Posterior tibial medial malleolus ke peeche hai. Popliteal knee ke peeche aur femoral groin mein hai.

</details>

### Q6. Which two arteries are joined through the superficial and deep palmar arches to provide collateral hand circulation?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Priority:** High

- **A.** Radial and ulnar
- **B.** Brachial and axillary
- **C.** Femoral and popliteal
- **D.** Carotid and vertebral

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: A. Radial and ulnar**

Radial aur ulnar arteries hand mein arches banati hain. In connections se hand ko alternate arterial supply mil sakti hai, though anatomy vary karti hai.

**Baaki options kyun sahi nahi hain?** Brachial aur axillary proximal upper-limb vessels hain. Femoral-popliteal leg mein aur carotid-vertebral brain supply mein hain.

</details>

### Q7. Which superficial vein is often accessed in the antecubital fossa for venipuncture?

- **D.** Median cubital vein

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

Median cubital vein elbow ke front mein superficial veins ko connect karti hai. Accessible hone ki wajah se blood collection mein commonly use hoti hai.

### Q8. Which superficial vein passes along the medial lower limb and joins the femoral vein?

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

</details>

### Q9. Which vessel drains venous blood from the brain toward the brachiocephalic vein?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Priority:** High

- **A.** Vertebral artery
- **B.** Hepatic portal vein
- **C.** Internal jugular vein
- **D.** External carotid artery

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: C. Internal jugular vein**

Brain ke dural venous sinuses ka blood internal jugular vein se drain hota hai. Ye subclavian vein ke saath join karke brachiocephalic vein banati hai.

**Baaki options kyun sahi nahi hain?** Carotid aur vertebral arteries blood brain ki taraf laati hain. Portal vein abdominal blood liver tak laati hai.

</details>

### Q10. Which arteries normally contribute to the anterior cerebral circulation through the carotid system?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Priority:** High

- **A.** Renal arteries
- **B.** Internal carotid arteries
- **C.** External carotid arteries as the main intracranial source
- **D.** Pulmonary arteries

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: B. Internal carotid arteries**

Internal carotid arteries skull ke andar enter karke anterior aur middle cerebral arterial supply mein contribute karti hain.

**Baaki options kyun sahi nahi hain?** External carotid mainly face aur scalp supply karti hai. Pulmonary aur renal arteries lungs aur kidneys se related hain.

</details>

- **D.** Superior mesenteric artery

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

### Q13. Which coronary vessel typically runs in the anterior interventricular groove?

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Priority:** Medium

- **B.** Circumflex artery
- **C.** Coronary sinus
- **D.** Left anterior descending artery

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

LAD anterior interventricular groove mein descend karti hai. Ye anterior myocardium aur septum ke large portion ko supply karti hai.

**Baaki options kyun sahi nahi hain?** Right marginal right heart border ke paas hai. Circumflex AV groove mein hai. Coronary sinus venous drainage karta hai.

</details>

### Q17. Which two veins normally join to form the inferior vena cava?

- **B.** Right and left renal veins
- **C.** Portal and hepatic veins
- **D.** Right and left subclavian veins

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

</details>

### Q19. What is the physiological purpose of a collateral arterial connection?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Priority:** High

- **A.** Convert venous blood into lymph
- **B.** Replace capillary gas exchange entirely
- **C.** Provide an alternative route for blood flow if a primary route is narrowed
- **D.** Prevent all arterial blood flow during exercise

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: C. Provide an alternative route for blood flow if a primary route is narrowed**

Collateral channels connected arterial routes hain. Primary artery narrow hone par alternate route tissue perfusion support kar sakta hai, lekin protection guaranteed nahi hoti.

**Baaki options kyun sahi nahi hain?** Exercise mein blood flow demand badh sakti hai. Collaterals lymph nahi banati aur capillary exchange replace nahi karti.

</details>

### Q20. Why does arterial pressure normally become less pulsatile as blood approaches capillaries?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Priority:** High

- **A.** Venous valves directly create arterial pulses
- **B.** Elastic arteries and arteriolar resistance dampen pulsatile flow
- **C.** Capillaries generate a second heartbeat
- **D.** All blood becomes stationary before capillaries

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: B. Elastic arteries and arteriolar resistance dampen pulsatile flow**

Large elastic arteries systolic energy store aur diastole mein release karti hain. Arteriolar resistance further pulse fluctuations dampen karti hai.

**Baaki options kyun sahi nahi hain?** Capillaries heartbeat generate nahi karti. Blood flow continue hota hai. Venous valves arterial pulse ka source nahi hain.

</details>
