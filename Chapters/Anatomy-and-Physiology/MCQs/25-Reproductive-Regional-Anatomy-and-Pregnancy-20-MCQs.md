### Q1. Which portion of the uterus lies above the openings of the uterine tubes?

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Priority:** Medium

- **A.** Fundus
- **B.** Cervix
- **C.** Isthmus
- **D.** Vaginal fornix

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: A. Fundus**

Fundus uterus ka rounded upper portion hai. Pregnancy mein fundal height uterus ki growth assess karne mein help karti hai.

**Baaki options kyun sahi nahi hain?** Cervix lower neck hai. Isthmus body aur cervix ke beech hai. Fornix vagina ka recess hai.

</details>

### Q2. Which uterine layer produces the force of labor contractions?

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Priority:** Medium

- **A.** Endometrium
- **B.** Perimetrium
- **C.** Cervical epithelium
- **D.** Myometrium

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: D. Myometrium**

Myometrium uterus ki thick smooth muscle layer hai. Labor mein coordinated contractions baby ko birth canal ki taraf move karti hain.

**Baaki options kyun sahi nahi hain?** Endometrium inner lining hai. Perimetrium outer covering hai. Cervical epithelium contraction ki main layer nahi hai.

</details>

### Q3. Which cervical opening communicates directly with the uterine cavity?

- **D.** External os

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

### Q4. Which pelvic diameter is conventionally assessed clinically through the diagonal conjugate to estimate the obstetric conjugate?

</details>

### Q7. What causes the normal midcycle thinning and increased stretchability of cervical mucus?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Priority:** High

- **A.** Sustained high progesterone as the principal cause
- **B.** Complete loss of ovarian hormones
- **C.** Increased aldosterone
- **D.** Rising estrogen before ovulation

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: D. Rising estrogen before ovulation**

Estrogen ovulation ke aas-paas cervical mucus ko thin aur stretchy bana sakta hai. Isse sperm passage facilitate hoti hai.

**Baaki options kyun sahi nahi hain?** Progesterone mucus ko thicker bana sakta hai. Ovarian hormone loss is response ko support nahi karta. Aldosterone salts regulate karta hai.

</details>

### Q8. Why is basal body temperature often slightly higher during the luteal phase?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Priority:** High

- **A.** Estrogen permanently stops heat loss
- **B.** Ovulation eliminates hypothalamic control
- **C.** Progesterone has a thermogenic effect
- **D.** FSH directly produces fever

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: C. Progesterone has a thermogenic effect**

Corpus luteum progesterone release karta hai. Progesterone temperature set point ko slightly increase kar sakta hai, jisse basal temperature rise hota hai.

**Baaki options kyun sahi nahi hain?** FSH fever ka normal cause nahi hai. Heat loss continue hoti hai. Hypothalamic regulation present rehti hai.

</details>

### Q13. Which circulatory change commonly occurs during uncomplicated pregnancy?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Priority:** High

- **C.** Constant cardiac output with no physiological adaptation
- **D.** Increased plasma volume and cardiac output

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

### Q14. Why can physiological dilutional anemia occur during pregnancy?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Priority:** High

- **A.** All maternal iron is lost through the lungs
- **B.** Hemoglobin is converted into glucose
- **C.** Plasma volume increases proportionally more than red cell mass
- **D.** Red cell mass is always reduced to zero

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: C. Plasma volume increases proportionally more than red cell mass**

Pregnancy mein plasma aur RBC mass dono badh sakte hain, lekin plasma ka rise zyada hota hai. Isse measured Hb dilute ho sakta hai. True deficiency bhi assess karni hoti hai.

**Baaki options kyun sahi nahi hain?** RBC mass zero nahi hota. Iron lungs se lose nahi hota. Hemoglobin glucose mein convert nahi hota.

</details>

### Q18. Which pressure change normally helps close the foramen ovale functionally after birth?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Priority:** High

- **A.** Right atrial pressure remains markedly greater indefinitely
- **B.** Both atria permanently lose all blood
- **C.** Left ventricular pressure falls to zero for days
- **D.** Left atrial pressure becomes greater than right atrial pressure

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: D. Left atrial pressure becomes greater than right atrial pressure**

Birth par lungs expand hote hain aur pulmonary blood flow increase hota hai. Left atrial return badhta hai aur placental venous return stop hoti hai, jisse flap closure favor hoti hai.

**Baaki options kyun sahi nahi hain?** Persistent right-to-left gradient closure oppose kar sakta hai. Atria blood receive karti hain. Ventricular pressure zero nahi hota.

</details>

### Q20. Why does milk production increase substantially after placental delivery despite high prolactin during pregnancy?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Priority:** High

- **A.** Oxytocin directly replaces every placental steroid
- **B.** The fall in placental progesterone permits secretory activation
- **C.** Placenta continues secreting milk into the breast
- **D.** Prolactin permanently falls to zero immediately after birth

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: B. The fall in placental progesterone permits secretory activation**

Pregnancy mein progesterone high rehkar full milk secretion ko inhibit kar sakta hai. Placenta deliver hone ke baad progesterone girta hai aur prolactin milk production support karta hai.

**Baaki options kyun sahi nahi hain?** Placenta milk nahi banati. Prolactin zero nahi hota. Oxytocin milk ejection mein kaam karta hai.

</details>
