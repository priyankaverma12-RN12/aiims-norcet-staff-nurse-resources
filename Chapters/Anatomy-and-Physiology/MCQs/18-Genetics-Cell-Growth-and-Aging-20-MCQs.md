### Q1. How many chromosomes are normally present in a diploid human somatic cell?

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Priority:** Medium

- **A.** 46
- **B.** 23
- **C.** 44 total
- **D.** 92 pairs

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: A. 46**

Somatic cell mein 23 chromosome pairs hote hain, total 46. Gametes mein haploid 23 chromosomes hote hain.

**Baaki options kyun sahi nahi hain?** 23 gamete count hai. 44 autosomes hain, sex chromosomes add karne hote hain. 92 pairs normal diploid count nahi hai.

</details>

### Q2. What is the usual chromosome complement of a human ovum?

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Priority:** Medium

- **A.** 22 autosomes and one Y chromosome
- **B.** 44 autosomes and two X chromosomes
- **C.** 23 autosomes and one X chromosome
- **D.** 22 autosomes and one X chromosome

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: D. 22 autosomes and one X chromosome**

Ovum meiosis ke baad 23 chromosomes carry karta hai. Ismein 22 autosomes aur one X chromosome hota hai.

**Baaki options kyun sahi nahi hain?** Ovum normally Y carry nahi karta. 44 autosomes diploid count hai. 23 autosomes plus X se total 24 ho jaata hai.

</details>

### Q4. Which base-pairing relationship is correct for double-stranded DNA?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Priority:** High

- **A.** Guanine with thymine exclusively
- **B.** Adenine with thymine and guanine with cytosine
- **C.** Adenine with guanine and thymine with cytosine
- **D.** Adenine with uracil in standard DNA

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: B. Adenine with thymine and guanine with cytosine**

DNA mein complementary bases specific pairing karti hain. A-T aur G-C pairing replication aur stable double helix ko support karti hai.

**Baaki options kyun sahi nahi hain?** A-G aur T-C standard complementary pairs nahi hain. Uracil RNA mein thymine ki jagah use hota hai.

</details>

### Q6. Which chromosome abnormality is classically associated with Down syndrome?

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Priority:** Medium

- **A.** Trisomy 21
- **B.** Trisomy 18
- **C.** Monosomy X
- **D.** 47,XXY

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

### Q9. Two parents are carriers of the same autosomal recessive condition. What is the probability that each pregnancy produces an affected child?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Priority:** High

- **A.** 75%
- **B.** 100%
- **C.** 25%
- **D.** 50%

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: C. 25%**

Dono parents ek normal aur ek altered allele carry karte hain. Punnett square mein four equally likely combinations mein ek child dono altered alleles inherit karta hai. Risk har pregnancy mein 25% hota hai.

**Baaki options kyun sahi nahi hain?** 50% carrier probability hai. 75% at least one normal allele milne ka chance hai. 100% affected risk nahi hai.

</details>

### Q10. One parent is heterozygous for a fully penetrant autosomal dominant condition and the other has no altered allele. What is the probability of transmission to each child?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Priority:** High

- **A.** 100%
- **B.** 50%
- **C.** 25%
- **D.** 75%

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: B. 50%**

Affected heterozygous parent apne do alleles mein se ek pass karta hai. Altered allele pass hone ka chance 1 out of 2, yani 50% hai.

**Baaki options kyun sahi nahi hain?** 25% typical two-carrier recessive risk hai. 75% aur 100% given cross se nahi milte.

</details>

### Q11. A carrier mother for an X-linked recessive disorder has children with an unaffected father. What proportion of sons is expected to inherit the altered X chromosome?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Priority:** High

- **A.** 50%
- **B.** 25%
- **C.** 0%
- **D.** 100%

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: A. 50%**

Son father se Y aur mother se X leta hai. Carrier mother ke do X mein se altered X milne ka chance half hai. Risk sons ke subgroup ka 50% hai.

**Baaki options kyun sahi nahi hain?** 25% total births ka approximate affected-son chance ho sakta hai. 0% aur 100% carrier mother ke cross mein sahi nahi hain.

</details>

### Q12. Which inheritance pattern is characteristic of mitochondrial DNA variants transmitted to children?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Priority:** High

- **A.** Paternal Y-linked inheritance
- **B.** Autosomal dominant inheritance in every case
- **C.** Equal transmission through sperm mitochondria in every case
- **D.** Predominantly maternal inheritance

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: D. Predominantly maternal inheritance**

Embryo ko mitochondria predominantly ovum se milti hain. Isliye mitochondrial DNA variants mother se transmit ho sakte hain. Clinical expression heteroplasmy se vary kar sakti hai.

**Baaki options kyun sahi nahi hain?** Y-linked paternal pattern different hai. Mitochondrial inheritance autosomal rule follow nahi karti. Sperm mitochondria usual inherited source nahi hain.

</details>

### Q14. What is the genetic significance of crossing over during meiosis I?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Priority:** High

- **A.** It doubles the chromosome number in every gamete
- **B.** It separates every sister chromatid before homologous pairing
- **C.** It exchanges DNA between homologous chromosomes and increases genetic variation
- **D.** It prevents all genetic variation

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: C. It exchanges DNA between homologous chromosomes and increases genetic variation**

Prophase I mein homologous chromosomes ke nonsister chromatids segments exchange kar sakte hain. Isse new allele combinations bante hain.

**Baaki options kyun sahi nahi hain?** Variation increase hoti hai. Gamete chromosome number half hota hai. Sister chromatids normally meiosis II mein separate hoti hain.

</details>

### Q20. Which normal age-associated physiological change can increase susceptibility to dehydration in older adults?

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Priority:** Medium

- **A.** Increased maximum renal concentrating ability in every person
- **B.** Reduced thirst sensation and reduced renal concentrating reserve
- **C.** Guaranteed increase in total body water
- **D.** Complete absence of fluid loss through skin

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: B. Reduced thirst sensation and reduced renal concentrating reserve**

Older adults mein thirst signal weak ho sakta hai aur kidneys concentrated urine banane ki reserve lose kar sakti hain. Isliye intake aur fluid loss assess karna important hai.

**Baaki options kyun sahi nahi hain?** Total body water often decrease hota hai. Skin fluid loss continue hota hai. Renal concentrating reserve generally improve nahi hoti.

</details>
