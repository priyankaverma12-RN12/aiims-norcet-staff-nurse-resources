### Q1. Which epithelial tissue is best suited for rapid diffusion across alveolar walls?

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Priority:** Medium

- **A.** Simple squamous epithelium
- **B.** Stratified squamous keratinized epithelium
- **C.** Transitional epithelium
- **D.** Simple columnar epithelium

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: A. Simple squamous epithelium**

Simple squamous tissue ek patli layer hoti hai. Alveoli mein thin barrier oxygen aur carbon dioxide exchange ko easy banata hai.

**Baaki options kyun sahi nahi hain?** Keratinized layers protection deti hain. Transitional tissue stretch karta hai. Columnar epithelium absorption aur secretion mein common hai.

</details>

### Q2. Which epithelium allows the urinary bladder to expand as it fills?

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Priority:** Medium

- **A.** Simple squamous epithelium
- **B.** Pseudostratified ciliated epithelium
- **C.** Keratinized stratified squamous epithelium
- **D.** Transitional epithelium

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: D. Transitional epithelium**

Transitional epithelium ya urothelium stretch hone par shape change kar sakta hai. Isse bladder urine store karte hue expand hota hai.

**Baaki options kyun sahi nahi hain?** Simple squamous diffusion ke liye suited hai. Ciliated tissue airway mein milta hai. Keratinized epithelium skin protection karta hai.

</details>

### Q3. Which epithelial tissue helps move trapped mucus toward the pharynx in the trachea?

### Q6. A patient has articular cartilage damage that heals slowly. Which normal feature contributes to limited repair?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Priority:** High

- **A.** Cartilage is largely avascular
- **B.** Cartilage contains abundant direct arterial branches
- **C.** Cartilage is composed entirely of neurons
- **D.** Cartilage normally has no extracellular matrix

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: A. Cartilage is largely avascular**

Cartilage ke andar direct blood vessels usually nahi hoti hain. Nutrients diffusion se pahunchte hain, isliye repair slow ho sakti hai.

**Baaki options kyun sahi nahi hain?** Abundant arteries normal cartilage mein nahi hoti hain. Chondrocytes cartilage cells hain. Extracellular matrix cartilage ka major part hai.

</details>

### Q10. Which skin layer contains blood vessels, sensory nerve endings and most sweat glands?

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Priority:** Medium

- **A.** Epidermal surface alone
- **B.** Dermis
- **C.** Stratum corneum
- **D.** Stratum lucidum

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: B. Dermis**

Dermis epidermis ke neeche connective tissue layer hai. Ismein vessels, nerves aur sweat glands milti hain. Epidermis ko nutrients diffusion se milte hain.

**Baaki options kyun sahi nahi hain?** Stratum corneum dead keratinized cells hai. Lucidum thick skin ki thin layer hai. Epidermis avascular hoti hai.

</details>

### Q13. A patient with extensive loss of epidermal barrier is at increased risk of which physiological problem?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Priority:** High

- **A.** Complete prevention of bacterial entry
- **B.** Increased skin insulation in every case
- **C.** Permanent absence of heat loss
- **D.** Increased transepidermal water loss and infection

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

Epidermis protective barrier banati hai jo water loss aur germs entry kam karti hai. Barrier damage ho toh dehydration, infection aur heat loss ka risk badhta hai.

**Baaki options kyun sahi nahi hain?** Bacterial entry risk badhta hai. Insulation damage ho sakti hai. Heat loss ho sakta hai.

</details>

### Q15. A patient has normal sweat production but is in a highly humid environment. Why may cooling be less effective?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Priority:** High

</details>

### Q16. Which skin response helps conserve heat during cold exposure?

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Priority:** Medium

- **D.** Permanent loss of shivering

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

### Q20. Why does prolonged pressure over a bony prominence injure tissue even when the skin initially appears intact?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Priority:** High

- **A.** Pressure stimulates unlimited collagen synthesis without damage
- **B.** Compression can impair local microvascular blood flow and tissue oxygenation
- **C.** Pressure increases oxygen delivery indefinitely
- **D.** Intact skin excludes deeper tissue injury

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: B. Compression can impair local microvascular blood flow and tissue oxygenation**

Sustained pressure se small blood vessels compress ho sakti hain. Tissue ko oxygen kam mile toh deeper damage skin ke visible breakdown se pehle bhi shuru ho sakta hai.

**Baaki options kyun sahi nahi hain?** Blood flow improve hona guaranteed nahi hai. Intact skin deeper injury exclude nahi karti. Collagen synthesis pressure damage ko prevent nahi karti.

</details>
