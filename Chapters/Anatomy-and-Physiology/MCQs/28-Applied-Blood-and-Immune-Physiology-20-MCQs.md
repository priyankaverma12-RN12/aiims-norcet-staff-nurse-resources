### Q1. A blood sample contains 45 mL of packed red cells per 100 mL of whole blood. What is the hematocrit?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Priority:** High

- **A.** 45%
- **B.** 55%
- **C.** 4.5%
- **D.** 100%

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: A. 45%**

Hematocrit whole blood volume ka RBC fraction hai. 45/100 x 100 = 45%. Plasma fraction roughly remaining 55% hota hai.

**Baaki options kyun sahi nahi hain?** 55% plasma-side fraction hai. 4.5 decimal error hai. 100% ka matlab entire sample RBC hota.

</details>

### Q2. A patient has hemoglobin 9 g/dL and hematocrit 27%. What is the approximate MCHC using Hb/Hct x 100?

- **C.** 243 g/dL
- **D.** 33.3 g/dL

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

MCHC red cells ke packed volume mein hemoglobin concentration estimate karti hai. 9/27 x 100 = 33.3 g/dL.

**Baaki options kyun sahi nahi hain?** 3 reverse division hai. 27 hematocrit value hai. 243 multiplication se aata hai.

### Q5. Why does the biconcave shape of a normal red cell support gas exchange?

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Priority:** Medium

- **A.** It prevents RBC passage through small capillaries
- **B.** It provides a high surface-area-to-volume ratio and flexibility
- **C.** It prevents hemoglobin from binding oxygen
- **D.** It gives each mature RBC a large nucleus

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: B. It provides a high surface-area-to-volume ratio and flexibility**

RBC disc center mein thin hoti hai, jisse gas exchange surface aur flexibility improve hoti hai. Ye narrow capillaries se pass hone mein help karti hai.

**Baaki options kyun sahi nahi hain?** Hemoglobin oxygen bind karti hai. Mature RBC nucleus lose karti hai. Flexible shape capillary passage support karti hai.

</details>

### Q6. Why do mature human erythrocytes rely mainly on anaerobic glycolysis for ATP?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Priority:** High

- **A.** They lack mitochondria
- **B.** They contain more mitochondria than skeletal muscle fibers
- **C.** They have no need for ATP
- **D.** They generate ATP only by nuclear transcription

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: A. They lack mitochondria**

Mature RBC mitochondria lose kar deti hai. Isliye glucose glycolysis se ATP banta hai, jo membrane pumps aur cell stability ke liye needed hai.

**Baaki options kyun sahi nahi hain?** Mitochondria absent hoti hain. ATP still needed hai. Mature RBC nucleus bhi absent hota hai.

</details>

### Q12. Which clotting factors are classically vitamin K dependent?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Priority:** High

- **C.** All clotting factors without exception
- **D.** II, VII, IX and X

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

### Q19. During fever, why may a patient initially shiver even when measured body temperature is already elevated?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Priority:** High

- **A.** The body has permanently lost temperature regulation
- **B.** Fever always lowers the hypothalamic set point
- **C.** Pyrogen-induced elevation of the hypothalamic temperature set point makes the current temperature relatively low
- **D.** Shivering is caused only by absent muscle activity

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: C. Pyrogen-induced elevation of the hypothalamic temperature set point makes the current temperature relatively low**

Fever mediators hypothalamic set point ko upar kar sakte hain. Body current temperature ko target se low samajhkar shivering aur vasoconstriction se heat generate aur retain karti hai.

**Baaki options kyun sahi nahi hain?** Shivering active muscle contraction hai. Regulation modified hoti hai, absent nahi. Set point fever onset mein rise kar sakta hai.

</details>

### Q20. Why does a secondary antibody response generally develop faster after repeat exposure to the same antigen?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Priority:** High

- **A.** Red blood cells become antibody-secreting cells

- **C.** All antigen-specific cells were destroyed after the first exposure
- **D.** The second antigen bypasses all immune receptors

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: B. Previously formed memory lymphocytes respond rapidly**

First exposure ke baad memory cells survive kar sakti hain. Repeat antigen par faster activation aur antibody production possible hoti hai.

**Baaki options kyun sahi nahi hain?** Memory cells survive kar sakti hain. Specific receptors important hain. RBC antibodies secrete nahi karti.

</details>
