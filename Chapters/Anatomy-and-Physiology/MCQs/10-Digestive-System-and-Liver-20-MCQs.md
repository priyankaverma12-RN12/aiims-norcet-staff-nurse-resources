### Q1. Which cells in the stomach secrete hydrochloric acid?

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Priority:** Medium

- **A.** Parietal cells
- **B.** Chief cells
- **C.** Goblet cells
- **D.** G cells

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: A. Parietal cells**

Parietal cells stomach mein acid release karti hain. Acid protein digestion ke environment aur kuch germs ko control karne mein help karta hai.

**Baaki options kyun sahi nahi hain?** Chief cells pepsinogen banati hain. Goblet cells mucus banati hain. G cells gastrin hormone release karti hain.

</details>

### Q2. Which substance secreted by gastric parietal cells is necessary for normal vitamin B12 absorption?

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Priority:** Medium

- **A.** Pepsinogen
- **B.** Secretin
- **C.** Bile salts
- **D.** Intrinsic factor

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: D. Intrinsic factor**

Intrinsic factor vitamin B12 ke saath bind karta hai. Is complex ko terminal ileum absorb karta hai. Parietal cells damage ho toh B12 absorption kam ho sakti hai.

**Baaki options kyun sahi nahi hain?** Pepsinogen protein digestion enzyme ka precursor hai. Secretin pancreatic bicarbonate stimulate karta hai. Bile salts fat digestion mein help karte hain.

</details>

### Q4. Why does pancreatic bicarbonate secretion increase when acidic chyme enters the duodenum?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Priority:** High

- **A.** ADH converts acid into bile
- **B.** Secretin stimulates bicarbonate-rich pancreatic fluid
- **C.** Gastrin directly stops all pancreatic secretion
- **D.** Insulin causes gastric acid to enter the bloodstream

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: B. Secretin stimulates bicarbonate-rich pancreatic fluid**

Duodenum mein acidic food mixture aata hai toh S cells secretin release karti hain. Ye pancreas ko bicarbonate-rich fluid banane ka signal deta hai, jisse acid neutralize hota hai.

**Baaki options kyun sahi nahi hain?** Gastrin gastric acid se related hai. Insulin glucose control karta hai. ADH water balance maintain karta hai.

</details>

### Q5. A fatty meal enters the duodenum. Which hormone stimulates gallbladder contraction and pancreatic enzyme secretion?

- **C.** Aldosterone
- **D.** Erythropoietin

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Baaki options kyun sahi nahi hain?** Aldosterone sodium balance, erythropoietin RBC formation aur calcitonin calcium regulation se related hain.

</details>

### Q6. Where does most nutrient absorption normally occur?

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Priority:** Medium

- **A.** Small intestine
- **B.** Esophagus
- **C.** Stomach alone
- **D.** Rectum

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: A. Small intestine**

Small intestine mein folds, villi aur microvilli absorption ke liye bahut surface area provide karte hain. Digested nutrients yahin se blood ya lymph mein jaate hain.

**Baaki options kyun sahi nahi hain?** Esophagus food conduct karta hai. Stomach mixing aur digestion start karta hai. Rectum stool store karta hai.

</details>

### Q9. Why do dietary long-chain fatty acids enter lymphatic vessels after absorption into intestinal cells?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Priority:** High

- **A.** They enter the urinary bladder first
- **B.** They are transported only as free oxygen molecules
- **C.** They are packaged into chylomicrons too large for ordinary blood-capillary uptake
- **D.** They are converted entirely into glucose before leaving the cell

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: C. They are packaged into chylomicrons too large for ordinary blood-capillary uptake**

Intestinal cells long-chain fat ko triglycerides mein assemble karke chylomicrons banati hain. Ye lacteals mein enter hote hain aur lymph ke through blood tak pahunchte hain.

**Baaki options kyun sahi nahi hain?** Fat entirely glucose nahi banta. Bladder digestion pathway nahi hai. Oxygen molecules fat transport particles nahi hain.

</details>

### Q12. What is the main digestive function of bile salts?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Priority:** High

- **A.** Hydrolyze proteins into amino acids directly
- **B.** Convert starch into maltose enzymatically
- **C.** Absorb vitamin B12 by binding intrinsic factor
- **D.** Emulsify fats and facilitate micelle formation

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: D. Emulsify fats and facilitate micelle formation**

Bile salts large fat droplets ko smaller droplets mein disperse karne aur micelles banane mein help karte hain. Isse pancreatic lipase aur fat absorption efficient ho sakti hai.

**Baaki options kyun sahi nahi hain?** Protein proteases digest karte hain. Starch amylase digest karti hai. B12 absorption intrinsic factor par depend karti hai.

</details>

### Q18. Which normal liver pathway converts toxic ammonia from amino acid metabolism into a less toxic nitrogenous product?

### Q20. A patient lacks lactase in the small intestine. Which physiological consequence follows ingestion of lactose?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Priority:** High

- **A.** Bile production immediately ceases
- **B.** Undigested lactose can retain water osmotically and undergo bacterial fermentation
- **C.** Lactose is absorbed unchanged into blood without limitation
- **D.** All protein digestion stops

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: B. Undigested lactose can retain water osmotically and undergo bacterial fermentation**

Lactase lactose ko simpler sugars mein todti hai. Enzyme kam ho toh lactose intestine mein reh sakta hai, water kheench sakta hai aur bacteria gas bana sakte hain.

**Baaki options kyun sahi nahi hain?** Lactose normally hydrolysis ke baad absorb hota hai. Protein digestion separate enzymes se hoti hai. Bile production direct lactase par depend nahi karti.

</details>
