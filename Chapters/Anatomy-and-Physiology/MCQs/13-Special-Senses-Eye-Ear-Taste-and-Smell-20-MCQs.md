### Q1. Which transparent structure provides most of the refractive power of the eye?

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Priority:** Medium

- **A.** Cornea
- **B.** Iris
- **C.** Retina
- **D.** Optic disc

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: A. Cornea**

Cornea aankh ki transparent front surface hai. Air se cornea mein light enter karte hue bend hoti hai. Lens focus ko adjust karta hai.

**Baaki options kyun sahi nahi hain?** Iris pupil size regulate karti hai. Retina light detect karti hai. Optic disc optic nerve exit point hai.

</details>

### Q2. During accommodation for a near object, what happens to the lens?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Priority:** High

- **A.** It becomes flatter as zonular tension decreases
- **B.** It moves entirely into the anterior chamber
- **C.** It becomes opaque in normal accommodation
- **D.** It becomes more convex as ciliary muscle contracts and zonular tension decreases

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: D. It becomes more convex as ciliary muscle contracts and zonular tension decreases**

Near object focus karne ke liye ciliary muscle contract karti hai. Zonular fibers loose hote hain aur lens round ho jaata hai, jisse refractive power badhti hai.

**Baaki options kyun sahi nahi hain?** Flattening distance vision mein hoti hai. Lens chamber change nahi karta. Normal accommodation opacity cause nahi karti.

</details>

### Q3. Which retinal photoreceptors are most sensitive in dim light?

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Priority:** Medium

- **A.** Bipolar cells as the main light detectors
- **B.** Ganglion cells as the main night-vision receptors
- **C.** Rods
- **D.** Cones

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: C. Rods**

Rods low light mein sensitive hoti hain aur night vision mein help karti hain. Color discrimination mein cones important hain.

**Baaki options kyun sahi nahi hain?** Cones bright light aur color detect karti hain. Bipolar aur ganglion cells retinal signals transmit karti hain.

</details>

### Q4. Which region of the retina provides the greatest visual acuity?

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Priority:** Medium

- **A.** Ciliary body
- **B.** Fovea centralis
- **C.** Optic disc
- **D.** Ora serrata

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: B. Fovea centralis**

Fovea centralis mein cones densely packed hoti hain. Is region se detailed central vision milti hai, jaise reading ke waqt.

**Baaki options kyun sahi nahi hain?** Optic disc mein photoreceptors absent hain. Ora serrata retinal margin hai. Ciliary body accommodation aur aqueous humor se related hai.

</details>

### Q5. Why is the optic disc called the physiological blind spot?

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Priority:** Medium

- **A.** It blocks sound transmission
- **B.** It contains no rods or cones where the optic nerve exits
- **C.** It contains the greatest concentration of cones
- **D.** It produces all aqueous humor

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: B. It contains no rods or cones where the optic nerve exits**

Optic disc par nerve fibers eye se bahar jaati hain. Yahan photoreceptors nahi hain, isliye light falling here directly detect nahi hoti.

**Baaki options kyun sahi nahi hain?** Cones fovea mein dense hoti hain. Aqueous humor ciliary processes banate hain. Sound hearing se related hai.

</details>

### Q6. Which visual fibers cross at the optic chiasm?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Priority:** High

- **A.** Fibers from the nasal halves of both retinas
- **B.** All fibers from both temporal retinas
- **C.** Only motor fibers to the eyelids
- **D.** All optic nerve fibers without exception

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: A. Fibers from the nasal halves of both retinas**

Nasal retinal fibers chiasm par opposite side cross karti hain. Temporal fibers same side continue karti hain. Is arrangement se visual fields brain mein organize hote hain.

**Baaki options kyun sahi nahi hain?** Temporal fibers normally cross nahi karti. Eyelid motor fibers optic pathway ka part nahi hain. Sab fibers cross nahi karti.

</details>

### Q7. Which pathway forms the efferent limb of the pupillary light reflex?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Priority:** High

- **A.** Optic nerve sensory fibers alone
- **B.** Facial nerve to the orbicularis oris
- **C.** Abducens nerve to lateral rectus
- **D.** Parasympathetic fibers traveling with the oculomotor nerve

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: D. Parasympathetic fibers traveling with the oculomotor nerve**

Light ka sensory signal optic nerve se brainstem tak jaata hai. Efferent signal CN III ke parasympathetic fibers se iris sphincter ko contract karwata hai.

**Baaki options kyun sahi nahi hain?** Optic nerve afferent limb hai. Facial aur abducens nerves pupil constriction ke principal efferent nerves nahi hain.

</details>

### Q8. Which structure produces aqueous humor?

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Priority:** Medium

- **A.** Optic nerve head
- **B.** Lacrimal gland
- **C.** Ciliary processes
- **D.** Retinal rods

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: C. Ciliary processes**

Ciliary processes aqueous humor banate hain jo anterior segment ke tissues ko nourish karta hai. Fluid drainage intraocular pressure balance karne mein help karti hai.

**Baaki options kyun sahi nahi hain?** Rods light detect karti hain. Optic nerve signal carry karti hai. Lacrimal gland tears banati hai.

</details>

### Q9. Which channel receives aqueous humor after passage through the trabecular meshwork?

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Priority:** Medium

- **A.** Cochlear duct
- **B.** Central retinal artery
- **C.** Canal of Schlemm
- **D.** Eustachian tube

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: C. Canal of Schlemm**

Aqueous humor trabecular meshwork se canal of Schlemm mein drain ho sakta hai. Drainage impaired ho toh pressure badh sakta hai.

**Baaki options kyun sahi nahi hain?** Eustachian tube middle ear se related hai. Cochlear duct hearing structure hai. Retinal artery blood supply deti hai.

</details>

### Q10. Which structure separates the external ear canal from the middle ear?

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Priority:** Medium

- **A.** Cochlear basilar membrane
- **B.** Tympanic membrane
- **C.** Oval window
- **D.** Round window

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: B. Tympanic membrane**

Tympanic membrane sound waves se vibrate karti hai. Ye vibration middle ear ossicles tak transfer hoti hai.

**Baaki options kyun sahi nahi hain?** Oval aur round windows middle-inner ear junction se related hain. Basilar membrane cochlea mein hoti hai.

</details>

### Q11. Which middle ear ossicle transmits vibration directly to the oval window?

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Priority:** Medium

- **A.** Stapes
- **B.** Malleus
- **C.** Incus directly in every normal ear
- **D.** Hyoid

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: A. Stapes**

Stapes ka footplate oval window par fit hota hai. Ossicles ki movement inner-ear fluid mein vibration generate karti hai.

**Baaki options kyun sahi nahi hain?** Malleus tympanic membrane se attached hai. Incus malleus aur stapes ko connect karta hai. Hyoid neck bone hai.

</details>

### Q12. Which structure equalizes air pressure between the middle ear and nasopharynx?

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Priority:** Medium

- **A.** External auditory canal alone
- **B.** Cochlear duct
- **C.** Semicircular canal
- **D.** Auditory or Eustachian tube

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: D. Auditory or Eustachian tube**

Eustachian tube middle ear ko nasopharynx se connect karti hai. Swallowing ke waqt opening pressure equalize karne mein help karti hai.

**Baaki options kyun sahi nahi hain?** External canal eardrum tak sound laata hai. Cochlear duct hearing aur semicircular canals balance mein role rakhti hain.

</details>

### Q13. Where are the sensory hair cells for hearing located?

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Priority:** Medium

- **A.** Macula of utricle
- **B.** Crista ampullaris
- **C.** Tympanic membrane
- **D.** Organ of Corti

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: D. Organ of Corti**

Organ of Corti cochlea ki basilar membrane par hota hai. Vibration hair cells ko bend karke neural signal generate karwati hai.

**Baaki options kyun sahi nahi hain?** Utricle linear acceleration sense karti hai. Crista rotational acceleration detect karti hai. Tympanic membrane vibration transmit karti hai.

</details>

### Q14. Which inner-ear structures primarily detect angular acceleration of the head?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Priority:** High

- **A.** Utricle and saccule as the main angular sensors
- **B.** Middle ear ossicles
- **C.** Semicircular canals
- **D.** Cochlea

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: C. Semicircular canals**

Head rotate hone par semicircular canals ki endolymph move karti hai. Cupula bend hone se sensory hair cells rotation detect karti hain.

**Baaki options kyun sahi nahi hain?** Cochlea hearing ke liye hai. Utricle aur saccule mainly linear acceleration aur gravity sense karte hain. Ossicles sound transmit karte hain.

</details>

### Q15. A patient has a Rinne test in which bone conduction exceeds air conduction in one ear. What does this suggest when interpreted with the full hearing assessment?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Priority:** High

- **A.** Loss of smell
- **B.** Conductive hearing loss in that ear
- **C.** Normal Rinne response in every adult
- **D.** Pure sensorineural loss proven by Rinne alone

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: B. Conductive hearing loss in that ear**

Normal ear mein air conduction generally bone conduction se better hoti hai. Conductive problem mein air transmission reduce ho sakti hai aur BC exceed AC kar sakti hai.

**Baaki options kyun sahi nahi hain?** Normal Rinne AC greater than BC hota hai. Sensorineural loss mein Rinne positive reh sakta hai. Smell ka test alag hai.

</details>

### Q16. In unilateral conductive hearing loss, toward which ear does the Weber test classically lateralize?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Priority:** High

- **A.** Affected ear
- **B.** Unaffected ear
- **C.** Neither ear in every case
- **D.** Both ears alternately by definition

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: A. Affected ear**

Conductive loss mein bone-conducted sound affected ear mein louder perceive ho sakta hai. Weber ko Rinne aur audiometry ke saath interpret karna chahiye.

**Baaki options kyun sahi nahi hain?** Sensorineural loss mein Weber better ear ki taraf ja sakta hai. Midline normal equal hearing mein mil sakta hai. Alternating sound defining pattern nahi hai.

</details>

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Priority:** Medium

### Q18. Which cranial nerve carries taste from most of the anterior two-thirds of the tongue?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Priority:** High

- **C.** Accessory nerve

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

Anterior tongue ki taste information chorda tympani branch of facial nerve se jaati hai. General touch sensation lingual nerve se jaati hai.

</details>

### Q19. Which nerve supplies taste from most of the posterior one-third of the tongue?

- **D.** Optic nerve

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

</details>

### Q20. Which type of receptor converts light into neural signals?

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Priority:** Medium

- **A.** Osmoreceptor
- **B.** Photoreceptor
- **C.** Mechanoreceptor only
- **D.** Thermoreceptor

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: B. Photoreceptor**

Retina ke rods aur cones light energy ko electrical changes mein convert karte hain. Is process se visual pathway activate hoti hai.

**Baaki options kyun sahi nahi hain?** Mechanoreceptors pressure ya movement sense karte hain. Thermoreceptors temperature aur osmoreceptors fluid concentration sense karte hain.

</details>
