# Renal and urinary anatomy and physiology — 20 NORCET-style MCQs

### Q1. Which structure is the basic functional unit of the kidney?

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Priority:** Medium

- **A.** Nephron
- **B.** Renal pelvis
- **C.** Ureter
- **D.** Urinary bladder

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: A. Nephron**

Nephron blood filtration, useful substances ki reabsorption aur waste secretion karta hai. Har kidney mein bahut nephrons hote hain.

**Baaki options kyun sahi nahi hain?** Renal pelvis urine collect karti hai. Ureter urine bladder tak le jaata hai. Bladder urine store karta hai.

</details>

### Q2. Which vessel delivers blood to the glomerular capillary tuft?

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Priority:** Medium

- **A.** Efferent arteriole
- **B.** Renal vein
- **C.** Collecting duct
- **D.** Afferent arteriole

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: D. Afferent arteriole**

Afferent arteriole blood glomerulus mein laati hai. Efferent arteriole blood ko glomerulus se bahar le jaati hai.

**Baaki options kyun sahi nahi hain?** Renal vein kidney se venous blood drain karti hai. Collecting duct urine transport karta hai.

</details>

### Q3. Which normal glomerular filtration barrier property helps retain most large plasma proteins?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Priority:** High

- **A.** Active pumping of all proteins into Bowman's space
- **B.** Complete absence of capillary pores
- **C.** Size and charge selectivity of the filtration barrier
- **D.** Unrestricted passage of all albumin

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: C. Size and charge selectivity of the filtration barrier**

Glomerular endothelium, basement membrane aur podocyte slit diaphragm milkar selective barrier banate hain. Large proteins jaise albumin normally largely blood mein rehte hain.

**Baaki options kyun sahi nahi hain?** Albumin ka unrestricted passage normal nahi hai. Proteins actively filtrate mein pump nahi hote. Fenestrated capillary pores present hote hain.

</details>

### Q4. Afferent arteriolar constriction markedly reduces blood entering a nephron. What is the likely effect on glomerular filtration pressure?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Priority:** High

- **A.** It rises because all renal arteries dilate
- **B.** It decreases
- **C.** It necessarily doubles
- **D.** It becomes independent of blood flow

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: B. It decreases**

Afferent arteriole narrow ho toh glomerular capillaries mein blood flow aur pressure kam ho sakte hain. Isse GFR decrease ho sakti hai.

**Baaki options kyun sahi nahi hain?** Filtration pressure necessarily double nahi hota. Renal flow se related hota hai. Afferent constriction dilation nahi hai.

</details>

### Q5. Which portion of the loop of Henle is highly permeable to water under normal conditions?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Priority:** High

- **A.** Macula densa alone
- **B.** Thin descending limb
- **C.** Thick ascending limb
- **D.** Distal convoluted tubule in all states

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: B. Thin descending limb**

Descending limb water ko medullary interstitium ki taraf pass hone deti hai. Isse tubular fluid concentrate ho sakta hai.

**Baaki options kyun sahi nahi hain?** Thick ascending limb water ke liye relatively impermeable hai. Distal water permeability segment aur hormones par depend karti hai. Macula densa salt sensing karti hai.

</details>

### Q6. Which property of the thick ascending limb is essential for the medullary concentration gradient?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Priority:** High

- **A.** Active salt reabsorption with low water permeability
- **B.** Free water reabsorption without salt transport
- **C.** Secretion of all filtered glucose
- **D.** Direct synthesis of erythrocytes

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: A. Active salt reabsorption with low water permeability**

Thick ascending limb Na-K-2Cl transport se salt interstitium mein move karti hai. Water follow nahi kar pata, jisse medullary gradient develop hota hai.

**Baaki options kyun sahi nahi hain?** Water freely follow nahi karti. Glucose mainly proximal tubule mein reabsorb hota hai. RBC marrow banata hai.

</details>

### Q7. Which hormone increases water permeability of collecting-duct principal cells?

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Priority:** Medium

- **A.** Insulin
- **B.** Calcitonin
- **C.** Gastrin
- **D.** Antidiuretic hormone

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: D. Antidiuretic hormone**

ADH collecting duct mein aquaporin-2 channels insert karne ka signal deta hai. Isse water blood ki taraf reabsorb ho sakta hai aur urine concentrate hota hai.

**Baaki options kyun sahi nahi hain?** Insulin glucose, calcitonin calcium aur gastrin gastric function regulate karte hain.

</details>

### Q8. A patient has absent ADH action despite an intact renal medullary gradient. Which urine pattern is expected?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Priority:** High

- **A.** No urine production in every case
- **B.** Urine containing all circulating albumin
- **C.** Large volume of dilute urine
- **D.** Small volume of maximally concentrated urine

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: C. Large volume of dilute urine**

ADH ka effect nahi ho toh collecting duct water ko efficiently reabsorb nahi karti. Isliye zyada dilute urine nikal sakta hai.

**Baaki options kyun sahi nahi hain?** Concentrated urine ADH action se favored hai. Filtration still ho sakti hai. Albumin leakage separate barrier problem hai.

</details>

### Q9. Which hormone increases sodium reabsorption and promotes potassium secretion in the distal nephron?

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Priority:** Medium

- **A.** Melatonin
- **B.** Thyroxine
- **C.** Aldosterone
- **D.** Prolactin

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: C. Aldosterone**

Aldosterone distal nephron mein sodium channels aur pumps ki activity badhata hai. Sodium retain hota hai aur potassium secretion badh sakti hai.

**Baaki options kyun sahi nahi hain?** Prolactin milk production, melatonin sleep timing aur thyroxine metabolism se related hain.

</details>

### Q10. Which renal cells release renin in response to reduced renal perfusion pressure?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Priority:** High

- **A.** Urothelial cells
- **B.** Juxtaglomerular granular cells
- **C.** Podocytes as the main renin source
- **D.** Collecting duct principal cells

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: B. Juxtaglomerular granular cells**

Juxtaglomerular cells afferent arteriole ki wall ke specialized cells hain. Reduced perfusion aur other signals par renin release kar sakti hain.

**Baaki options kyun sahi nahi hain?** Podocytes filtration barrier banati hain. Principal cells water aur salts regulate karti hain. Urothelial cells urinary lining hain.

</details>

### Q11. Which structure senses tubular sodium chloride delivery near the vascular pole of the glomerulus?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Priority:** High

- **A.** Macula densa
- **B.** Renal papilla
- **C.** Ureteric orifice
- **D.** Bowman's parietal epithelium

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: A. Macula densa**

Macula densa distal nephron ke specialized cells hain jo tubular NaCl sense karti hain. Inka signal renal blood flow aur renin regulate karne mein help karta hai.

**Baaki options kyun sahi nahi hain?** Renal papilla collecting ducts drain karti hai. Ureteric orifice bladder entry hai. Bowman's parietal cells capsule lining hain.

</details>

### Q12. Which enzyme converts angiotensin I into angiotensin II?

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Priority:** Medium

- **A.** Renin
- **B.** Carbonic anhydrase
- **C.** Pepsin
- **D.** Angiotensin-converting enzyme

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: D. Angiotensin-converting enzyme**

ACE angiotensin I ko angiotensin II mein convert karta hai. Angiotensin II vascular tone aur aldosterone release ko affect karta hai.

**Baaki options kyun sahi nahi hain?** Renin angiotensinogen ko angiotensin I banata hai. Carbonic anhydrase acid-base reactions mein hai. Pepsin gastric protein digestion karta hai.

</details>

### Q13. Which renal mechanism is particularly important for excretion of daily nonvolatile acid loads?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Priority:** High

- **A.** Exhalation of carbon dioxide through the kidney
- **B.** Complete loss of filtered bicarbonate in every healthy person
- **C.** Suppression of all tubular transport
- **D.** Hydrogen ion secretion with ammonium and titratable acid excretion

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: D. Hydrogen ion secretion with ammonium and titratable acid excretion**

Kidney hydrogen ions secrete karti hai aur ammonia ke saath ammonium banakar acid urine mein nikaal sakti hai. Isse new bicarbonate generation blood pH support karti hai.

**Baaki options kyun sahi nahi hain?** CO2 lungs exhale karte hain. Kidney bicarbonate conserve karti hai. Tubular transport acid handling ke liye needed hai.

</details>

### Q14. Which renal hormone-related function helps explain anemia in advanced chronic kidney disease?

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Priority:** Medium

- **A.** Increased melatonin secretion from renal pelvis
- **B.** Reduced bile storage
- **C.** Reduced erythropoietin production
- **D.** Excess salivary amylase production

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: C. Reduced erythropoietin production**

Kidneys erythropoietin banati hain jo bone marrow ko RBC formation ka signal deta hai. Severe chronic kidney disease mein ye signal kam ho sakta hai.

**Baaki options kyun sahi nahi hain?** Amylase saliva aur pancreas se related hai. Melatonin mainly pineal gland banati hai. Bile gallbladder store karti hai.

</details>

### Q15. Which active vitamin D form is produced by a hydroxylation step in the kidney?

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Priority:** Medium

- **A.** Phylloquinone
- **B.** Calcitriol
- **C.** Cholecalciferol as the fully activated renal product
- **D.** Retinol

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: B. Calcitriol**

Kidney 25-hydroxyvitamin D ko 1,25-dihydroxyvitamin D ya calcitriol mein convert karti hai. Ye calcium absorption aur bone balance mein important hai.

**Baaki options kyun sahi nahi hain?** Cholecalciferol vitamin D3 precursor hai. Retinol vitamin A aur phylloquinone vitamin K1 hai.

</details>

### Q16. Which smooth muscle layer contracts to empty the urinary bladder?

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Priority:** Medium

- **A.** Detrusor muscle
- **B.** External urethral sphincter as the primary expelling muscle
- **C.** Rectus abdominis alone
- **D.** Cremaster muscle

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: A. Detrusor muscle**

Detrusor bladder wall ki smooth muscle hai. Micturition ke time ye contract karke bladder pressure badhati hai aur urine expel hota hai.

**Baaki options kyun sahi nahi hain?** External sphincter voluntary urine control mein role rakhta hai. Abdominal muscles assist kar sakti hain. Cremaster testes elevate karta hai.

</details>

### Q17. Which autonomic division mainly stimulates detrusor contraction during normal bladder emptying?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Priority:** High

- **A.** Parasympathetic sacral outflow
- **B.** Sympathetic outflow as the principal emptying signal
- **C.** Somatic motor supply to the diaphragm
- **D.** Optic nerve

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

S2–S4 parasympathetic signals detrusor contraction promote karte hain. Bladder outlet relaxation ke coordination se urination possible hoti hai.

</details>

### Q18. Where is urine temporarily collected before entering the ureter?

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Priority:** Medium

- **A.** Glomerular capsule as the final collecting reservoir
- **B.** Renal artery
- **C.** Adrenal cortex
- **D.** Renal pelvis

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: D. Renal pelvis**

Collecting ducts urine ko calyces mein drain karte hain. Calyces renal pelvis mein join hote hain, jahan se urine ureter mein jaata hai.

**Baaki options kyun sahi nahi hain?** Bowman's capsule initial filtrate receive karti hai. Renal artery blood laati hai. Adrenal cortex hormones banati hai.

</details>

### Q19. A substance is freely filtered and is neither reabsorbed nor secreted by renal tubules. What does its renal clearance approximate?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Priority:** High

- **A.** Cardiac output
- **B.** Urinary bladder capacity
- **C.** Glomerular filtration rate
- **D.** Renal plasma flow in every case

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: C. Glomerular filtration rate**

Aise marker ki filtered amount urine mein excreted amount ke equal hoti hai. Isliye clearance GFR approximate karti hai. Inulin classic example hai.

**Baaki options kyun sahi nahi hain?** Renal plasma flow ke liye different marker use hota hai. Cardiac output aur bladder capacity filtration clearance nahi hain.

</details>

### Q20. A urine collection contains 1200 mL over 24 hours. What is the mean hourly urine output?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Priority:** High

- **A.** 500 mL/hour
- **B.** 50 mL/hour
- **C.** 20 mL/hour
- **D.** 120 mL/hour

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: B. 50 mL/hour**

Total urine 1200 mL ko 24 hours se divide karna hai. Average 50 mL/hour aata hai. Clinical adequacy weight aur condition se assess hoti hai.

**Baaki options kyun sahi nahi hain?** 20 aur 120 division result nahi hain. 500 se total daily volume bahut zyada hota.

</details>
