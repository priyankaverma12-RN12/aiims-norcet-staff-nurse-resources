### PYQ1. Which condition is least likely to cause polyuria through increased water or solute excretion?

**Reported exam:** PGIMER Nursing Officer, 2020

[Question source](https://nprep.in/question-answer/all-of-the-following-are-causes-of-polyuria-except-89n1495eAvfby1MjJ7NGV)

- **A.** Severe oliguric acute kidney injury
- **B.** Diabetes insipidus
- **C.** Uncontrolled diabetes mellitus with glycosuria
- **D.** Excessive water intake

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: A. Severe oliguric acute kidney injury**

Oliguric AKI mein urine output kam hota hai. DI water loss, glycosuria osmotic diuresis aur excess water intake dilute urine increase kar sakte hain. Original reported EXCEPT concept ko clearly word kiya gaya hai.

</details>

### PYQ2. Which cerebral lobe is particularly important in spatial attention and orientation?

**Reported exam:** PGIMER Nursing Officer, 2020

[Question source](https://nprep.in/question-answer/which-part-of-the-brain-is-affected-in-spatial-disorientation-AT-Us40C-D0QU-5NlnsDE)

- **A.** Occipital lobe as the sole spatial attention center
- **B.** Medulla
- **C.** Pituitary gland
- **D.** Parietal lobe

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: D. Parietal lobe**

Parietal networks body aur surrounding space ki sensory information integrate karti hain. Occipital lobe visual processing mein role rakhta hai, medulla autonomic reflexes mein aur pituitary hormones mein.

</details>

### PYQ3. Why may an excessively rapid heart rate reduce cardiac output despite more beats each minute?

**Reported exam:** PGIMER Sangrur Nursing Officer, 2023

[Question source](https://nprep.in/question-answer/excessively-high-heart-rate-170-can-KzFue6yChLfLPpwEouVIg)

- **A.** Systemic venous return stops permanently in every case
- **B.** Myocardial oxygen demand always becomes zero
- **C.** Diastolic filling time and stroke volume may fall
- **D.** Ventricular filling becomes unlimited

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: C. Diastolic filling time and stroke volume may fall**

Bahut fast rate par diastole short ho sakti hai. Ventricles less fill hoti hain aur stroke volume gir sakta hai. Filling unlimited nahi hoti, venous return necessarily stop nahi hoti aur myocardial oxygen demand often badhti hai.

</details>

### PYQ4. Which anatomical movement brings the thumb pad toward the pad of the little finger?

**Reported exam:** PGIMER Sangrur Nursing Officer, 2023

[Question source](https://nprep.in/question-answer/movement-of-thumb-little-finger-towards-each-other-called-F5VQBy55WdT916dvSU6Fl)

- **A.** Eversion
- **B.** Opposition
- **C.** Pronation
- **D.** Dorsiflexion

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: B. Opposition**

Opposition thumb ki combined movement hai jo finger pad ke against pinch possible banati hai. Pronation forearm rotation hai. Dorsiflexion ankle movement aur eversion sole outward turning hai.

</details>

### PYQ5. Which specialized tissue normally determines the initial rhythm of the healthy heart?

**Reported exam:** SGPGI Nursing Officer, 2022

[Question source](https://nprep.in/question-answer/the-pacemaker-of-the-heart-is-0LRcn-YtJ5J9o9uL7DADN)

- **A.** Papillary muscles
- **B.** Sinoatrial node
- **C.** AV bundle
- **D.** Purkinje fibers

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: B. Sinoatrial node**

SA node spontaneous impulses generate karti hai. AV bundle aur Purkinje network ventricular conduction mein help karte hain. Papillary muscles valves support karti hain, pacemaker nahi hain.

</details>

### PYQ6. Which approximate GFR is conventionally taught for a healthy young adult with standard body surface area?

**Reported exam:** DSSSB Public Health Nurse, 2015

[Question source](https://nprep.in/question-answer/what-is-the-average-gfr-of-an-adult-X1giAEh9feXRfuu5BM2MV)

- **A.** 125 mL/min/1.73 m²
- **B.** 12.5 mL/min/1.73 m²
- **C.** 500 mL/min/1.73 m²
- **D.** 1.25 mL/min/1.73 m²

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: A. 125 mL/min/1.73 m²**

Healthy young adult ka textbook GFR around 120–125 mL/min/1.73 m² ho sakta hai. Age, body size aur clinical condition se actual value vary karti hai. 12.5 aur 1.25 markedly low hain, 500 typical GFR se bahut high hai.

</details>

### PYQ7. Which brainstem region coordinates normal bladder emptying with urethral sphincter relaxation?

**Reported exam:** DSSSB Public Health Nurse, 2015

[Question source](https://nprep.in/question-answer/the-micturition-reflex-center-is-located-in-the-_1wD4VEW0tRVGqLIzCRLD)

- **A.** Occipital cortex
- **B.** Cerebellar vermis
- **C.** Pituitary stalk
- **D.** Pons

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: D. Pons**

Pontine micturition center bladder aur outlet ko coordinate karta hai. Basic spinal reflex S2–S4 mein integrate hota hai, isliye original broad wording ko clarify kiya hai. Occipital cortex vision, vermis balance aur pituitary stalk hormones se related hain.

</details>
