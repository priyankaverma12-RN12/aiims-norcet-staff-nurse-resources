### PYQ1. Which brain structure integrates signals to regulate core body temperature?

**Difficulty:** Easy/Medium | **Topic:** Temperature regulation

- **A.** Hypothalamus
- **B.** Cerebellum
- **C.** Basal ganglia
- **D.** Occipital cortex

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation</summary>

**Correct: A. Hypothalamus**

Hypothalamus body temperature ka control center hai. Jab temperature badhta hai, toh sweating aur skin blood flow jaise responses activate ho sakte hain. Cerebellum movement coordinate karta hai, basal ganglia motor control mein help karti hain aur occipital cortex vision se related hai.

</details>
