### PYQ1. Hoarseness after thyroid surgery may result from damage to which branch of the vagus nerve?

**Reported exam:** NORCET 2, 2021 Shift 2

[Question source](https://nprep.in/question-answer/after-thyroidectomy-the-patient-complains-for-hoarseness-of-voice-that-occurs-due-sJ5ccO9vMpM-rjGu5NgKT)

- **A.** Recurrent laryngeal nerve
- **B.** Phrenic nerve
- **C.** Hypoglossal nerve
- **D.** Greater auricular nerve

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: A. Recurrent laryngeal nerve**

Recurrent laryngeal nerve most intrinsic laryngeal muscles ko control karti hai. Injury se voice hoarse ho sakti hai. Phrenic diaphragm, hypoglossal tongue aur greater auricular skin sensation se related hain. Airway symptoms urgently assess karo.

</details>

### PYQ2. Which cranial nerve supplies the superior oblique muscle that depresses the adducted eye?

**Reported exam:** NORCET 5 Prelims, September 2023 Shift 1

[Question source](https://nprep.in/question-answer/a-patient-is-unable-to-see-downward-and-lateral-movement-of-the-QfMMPe2FnnYnGAToOAzjA)

- **A.** Abducens nerve
- **B.** Optic nerve
- **C.** Facial nerve
- **D.** Trochlear nerve

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: D. Trochlear nerve**

CN IV superior oblique ko supply karti hai. Source ka movement description ambiguous ho sakta hai, isliye question ko specific muscle ke basis par clear kiya hai. CN VI lateral rectus, CN II vision aur CN VII facial expression se related hain.

</details>

### PYQ4. After head trauma, marked dilute polyuria raises concern for impaired secretion of which hormone?

**Reported exam:** NORCET 5 Prelims, September 2023 Shift 1

[Question source](https://nprep.in/question-answer/a-polytrauma-patient-following-a-head-injury-passed-1000-ml-urine-output-eKhdyRX6XpYxhqyghwByo)

- **A.** Calcitonin
- **B.** ADH
- **C.** TSH
- **D.** Prolactin

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: B. ADH**

Head trauma hypothalamic-pituitary ADH pathway affect kar sakta hai. ADH kam ho toh kidney water conserve nahi karti. TSH thyroid, prolactin breast milk production aur calcitonin calcium regulation mein role rakhte hain. Urine concentration aur sodium assess karke cause establish hota hai.

</details>

### PYQ5. Which urine pattern is typical when ADH action is markedly deficient?

**Reported exam:** NORCET 5 Prelims, September 2023 Shift 2

[Question source](https://nprep.in/question-answer/which-is-a-classical-clinical-feature-present-in-diabetes-insipidus-PxAuRCuv9V9ZnMo6Pve1g)

- **A.** Urine containing all plasma albumin
- **B.** High-volume dilute urine
- **C.** Low-volume concentrated urine
- **D.** Complete absence of urine in every case

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: B. High-volume dilute urine**

ADH collecting ducts mein water reabsorption support karta hai. ADH action kam ho toh water urine mein reh jaata hai. Concentrated urine ADH action se favored hai. Anuria aur heavy albumin loss alag renal problems suggest kar sakte hain.

</details>

### PYQ6. Which endocrine glands are the main source of the hormone that raises blood calcium when calcium falls?

**Reported exam:** NORCET 7 Prelims, 2024

[Question source](https://nprep.in/question-answer/the-nurse-is-educating-a-patient-with-hypercalcemia-which-gland-is-primarily-Q9U_mgnu65Mb4zekbeYs5)

- **A.** Parathyroid glands
- **B.** Pineal gland
- **C.** Adrenal medulla
- **D.** Pancreatic islets

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: A. Parathyroid glands**

Parathyroid PTH release karti hain. PTH kidney calcium conserve karne aur vitamin D activation support karne mein help karta hai. Pineal melatonin, adrenal medulla catecholamines aur pancreas insulin-glucagon banata hai.

</details>

### PYQ7. Which term describes bow-legged alignment with the knees separated when the ankles are approximated?

**Reported exam:** NORCET 10 Prelims, 2026

[Question source](https://nprep.in/question-answer/lateral-bowing-of-the-legs-is-known-as-ScS3TnEMyDhuEmHUDgsJ4)

- **A.** Genu valgum
- **B.** Kyphosis
- **C.** Lordosis
- **D.** Genu varum

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: D. Genu varum**

Genu varum mein legs bow outward dikh sakti hain. Genu valgum knock-knee pattern hai. Kyphosis aur lordosis spinal curvature terms hain. Age aur clinical context assessment mein important hote hain.

</details>
