### PYQ1. Which microscopic structures in compact bone contain blood vessels within osteons?

**Reported exam:** HPSSC Staff Nurse 2020, Series A, Q1

[Question source](https://hpgeneralstudies.com/solved-paper-hp-staff-nurse-exam-2020-hpssc-hamirpur-part-1/)

- **A.** Haversian canals
- **B.** Renal collecting ducts
- **C.** Cerebral ventricles
- **D.** Bronchioles

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: A. Haversian canals**

Haversian canals osteons ke center mein blood vessels aur nerves carry karte hain. Collecting ducts kidney, ventricles brain aur bronchioles lungs mein hote hain.

</details>

### PYQ9. In a reflex arc, which direction does an afferent nerve impulse travel?

<details><summary>Answer aur explanation dekho</summary>

### PYQ14. Which type of extracellular solution draws water out of cells by osmosis when its effective solute concentration is higher than intracellular fluid?
