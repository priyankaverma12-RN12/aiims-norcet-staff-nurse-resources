## Neutropenia

### Q1. A chemotherapy patient with a very low neutrophil count develops a temperature of 38.5°C. What is the priority?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Topic:** Neutropenia

- **A.** Urgent assessment for febrile neutropenia and prompt treatment pathway
- **B.** Wait for a high fever lasting three days
- **C.** Give a live vaccine immediately
- **D.** Treat only with a cooling sponge and discharge

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: A. Urgent assessment for febrile neutropenia and prompt treatment pathway**

Neutrophils infection se ladne wale white cells hain. Chemotherapy se ye bahut kam ho sakte hain. Fever serious infection ka eklauta early sign bhi ho sakta hai, isliye urgent protocol chahiye.

**Baaki options kyun galat hain?**

- **B.** Wait karne se sepsis worsen ho sakti hai.
- **C.** Live vaccine immunosuppression mein unsafe ho sakti hai.
- **D.** Cooling infection ka cause treat nahi karti.

</details>

## Extravasation

### Q2. During vesicant chemotherapy infusion, a patient reports burning and swelling at the IV site. Which immediate action is appropriate?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Topic:** Extravasation

- **A.** Increase the infusion pressure
- **B.** Flush the line forcefully
- **C.** Remove the cannula immediately before checking the drug-specific protocol
- **D.** Stop infusion and follow the extravasation protocol without flushing

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: D. Stop infusion and follow the extravasation protocol without flushing**

Vesicant medicine vein se bahar leak ho toh tissue damage ho sakta hai. Nurse infusion stop karti hai aur drug-specific protocol follow karti hai. Cannula aspiration ke liye temporarily needed ho sakti hai.

**Baaki options kyun galat hain?**

- **A.** Pressure leakage badha sakta hai.
- **B.** Flush se drug tissues mein aur spread ho sakti hai.
- **C.** Cannula removal ka timing protocol se decide hota hai.

</details>

## Tumor lysis syndrome

### Q3. A patient after chemotherapy has rapidly rising potassium, phosphate and uric acid with declining renal function. Which condition fits?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Topic:** Tumor lysis syndrome

- **A.** Isolated dehydration with no metabolic concern
- **B.** Stable allergic rhinitis
- **C.** Tumor lysis syndrome
- **D.** Uncomplicated iron deficiency

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: C. Tumor lysis syndrome**

Cancer cells bahut jaldi tootne par unke andar ke substances blood mein aa jaate hain. Kidney aur heart ko harm ho sakta hai. Nurse labs aur ECG ke saath urgent treatment review leti hai.

**Baaki options kyun galat hain?**

- **A.** Dehydration possible ho sakti hai par tumor lysis clues strong hain.
- **B.** Rhinitis unrelated hai.
- **D.** Iron deficiency ye electrolyte pattern nahi deti.

</details>

## Palliative care

### Q4. A terminally ill patient reports pain despite scheduled analgesia. Which response is appropriate?

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Topic:** Palliative care

- **A.** Avoid asking the patient about preferences
- **B.** Assess pain, review the prescribed breakthrough plan and communicate unmet needs
- **C.** Assume pain is inevitable and cannot be treated
- **D.** Stop analgesia because the disease is incurable

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: B. Assess pain, review the prescribed breakthrough plan and communicate unmet needs**

Palliative care ka aim symptoms kam karke comfort aur quality of life improve karna hai. Nurse pain ki intensity aur patient ki preference samajhkar treatment team se plan review karati hai.

**Baaki options kyun galat hain?**

- **A.** Patient ki choice important hai.
- **C.** Pain relief possible ho sakti hai.
- **D.** Disease incurable hone se pain treatment ki need khatam nahi hoti.

</details>
