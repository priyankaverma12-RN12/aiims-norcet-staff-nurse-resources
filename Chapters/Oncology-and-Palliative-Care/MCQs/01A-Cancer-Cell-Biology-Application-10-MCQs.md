### Q1. A cancer laboratory reports increased activity of an enzyme that adds DNA to chromosome ends. Which growth advantage can this change confer?

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD | **Exam focus:** NORCET / all-India Staff Nurse | **Type:** Original practice

- **A.** Reduced ability to repair chromosome ends during division
- **B.** Permanent arrest of every dividing cell
- **C.** Maintenance of telomeres during repeated cell division
- **D.** Elimination of all acquired DNA mutations

<details><summary>Answer aur detailed explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: C. Maintenance of telomeres during repeated cell division**

- **A (Incorrect).** Chromosome ends par telomeres protective structures hote hain. Telomerase activity badhne se unki maintenance support ho sakti hai, isliye reduced maintenance opposite effect hai.
- **B (Incorrect).** Telomerase activity badhne se cells ko repeated division support mil sakta hai. Isse har dividing cell permanently arrest ho jaaye, aisa nahi hota.
- **C (Correct).** Telomerase telomeres mein DNA add karne wala enzyme hai. Kai cancer cells mein iski activity badh sakti hai, jis wajah se repeated divisions ke dauran chromosome ends maintain ho sakte hain.
- **D (Incorrect).** Telomerase ka specific role chromosome ends ki maintenance hai. Yeh cell ke saare acquired mutations remove karne wala universal DNA repair mechanism nahi hai.

**Exam clue:** Telomerase chromosome ends ko maintain karne mein help karta hai.

### Q2. A patient with cancer and recent involuntary weight loss plans to eliminate all carbohydrates to starve the tumor. Which response is most appropriate?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Priority:** 🔴 MOST IMPORTANT | **Exam focus:** NORCET / all-India Staff Nurse | **Type:** Original practice

- **A.** Arrange nutrition assessment and explain that carbohydrate elimination is not a proven cancer treatment
- **B.** Recommend complete carbohydrate restriction until the tumor disappears
- **C.** Advise replacing prescribed cancer therapy with a sugar-free diet
- **D.** Recommend a low-calorie diet solely because cancer cells use glucose

<details><summary>Answer aur detailed explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: A. Arrange nutrition assessment and explain that carbohydrate elimination is not a proven cancer treatment**

- **A (Correct).** Cancer cells glucose use kar sakti hain, lekin healthy cells bhi energy ke liye glucose use karti hain. Carbohydrate elimination se cancer cure hota hai, iska proof nahi hai. Weight loss ke kaaran nurse ko dietary intake assess karke oncology dietitian se individualized plan discuss karna chahiye.
- **B (Incorrect).** Complete restriction se patient ki energy intake aur weight aur kam ho sakte hain. Tumor disappear hone tak aisi diet dena evidence-based oncology treatment nahi hai.
- **C (Incorrect).** Sugar-free diet prescribed cancer therapy ka replacement nahi hai. Nurse ko patient ki concerns samajhkar evidence-based treatment aur nutrition support continue karne mein madad karni chahiye.
- **D (Incorrect).** Weight loss wale patient mein calories aur kam karna nutritional risk badha sakta hai. Cancer cells glucose use karti hain, lekin is fact se har patient ko low-calorie diet dene ka conclusion nahi nikalta.

**Exam clue:** Tumor metabolism aur nutrition advice ko direct cure claim mein convert mat karo.

**Concept:** [Application concepts](../Concepts/01A-Cancer-Cell-Biology-Application.md#growth-survival-and-metabolism)

### Q3. A rapidly enlarging tumor contains a central area of dead tissue where blood supply is inadequate. Which term describes this tissue finding?

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD | **Exam focus:** NORCET / all-India Staff Nurse | **Type:** Original practice

- **A.** Metaplasia
- **B.** Hyperplasia
- **C.** Tumor differentiation
- **D.** Necrosis

<details><summary>Answer aur detailed explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: D. Necrosis**

- **A (Incorrect).** Metaplasia mein viable mature cell type ki jagah doosra mature cell type develop ho sakta hai. Dead central tissue ka finding mature cell replacement nahi hai.
- **B (Incorrect).** Hyperplasia mein cells ki number badhti hai. Central area mein tissue death hona hyperplasia ke definition se match nahi karta.
- **C (Incorrect).** Differentiation batati hai ki viable cells original mature tissue se kitni milti hain. Is term se blood supply kam hone ke baad tissue death describe nahi hoti.
- **D (Correct).** Necrosis ka matlab living tissue ka mar jaana hai. Agar tumor ke kuch areas ko sufficient blood supply na mile, toh wahan tissue death ho sakti hai. Baaki tumor viable ho sakta hai, isliye central necrosis ko complete cure mat samjho.

**Exam clue:** Necrosis tissue death ko describe karta hai, poore tumor ki eradication ko nahi.

**Concept:** [Application concepts](../Concepts/01A-Cancer-Cell-Biology-Application.md#growth-survival-and-metabolism)

**Clinical reference:** [NCI / SEER](https://www.cancer.gov/publications/dictionaries/cancer-terms/def/necrosis)

### Q4. A breast surgery team uses a tracer to identify the first lymph node or nodes likely to receive drainage from a primary tumor. Which structure is being identified?

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD | **Exam focus:** NORCET / all-India Staff Nurse | **Type:** Original practice

- **A.** A distant metastatic deposit
- **B.** A sentinel lymph node
- **C.** A tumor-free lymphatic vessel
- **D.** A primary bone marrow site

<details><summary>Answer aur detailed explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: B. A sentinel lymph node**

- **A (Incorrect).** Distant metastatic deposit primary site se door cancer cells ke spread ko describe karta hai. Tracer se drainage node identify karna distant spread ka proof nahi hai.
- **B (Correct).** Sentinel lymph node primary tumor se drainage receive karne wale likely first node ya nodes mein se hota hai. Uski pathological examination regional spread assess karne mein madad kar sakti hai. Tracer identification alone malignancy confirm nahi karti.
- **C (Incorrect).** Lymphatic vessel lymph fluid ko carry karta hai, jabki question mein identified structure node hai. Tracer ka uptake vessel ya node mein cancer absent hone ka proof nahi deta.
- **D (Incorrect).** Bone marrow blood cells banane ka important site hai. Breast tumor ke first draining lymph node ki mapping ko marrow assessment nahi kehte.

**Exam clue:** Sentinel node likely first drainage node hai, automatically positive node nahi.

### Q5. During surgery for ovarian carcinoma, small tumor deposits are found on the omentum after malignant cells have shed into the peritoneal cavity. Which route best explains the described spread?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Priority:** 🔴 MOST IMPORTANT | **Exam focus:** NORCET / all-India Staff Nurse | **Type:** Original practice

- **A.** Transcoelomic implantation
- **B.** Hematogenous spread exclusively
- **C.** Direct extension through a continuous tissue bridge
- **D.** Lymphatic spread exclusively

<details><summary>Answer aur detailed explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: A. Transcoelomic implantation**

- **A (Correct).** Peritoneal cavity abdomen ki body cavity hai. Jab malignant cells cavity mein shed hokar omentum ya peritoneal surfaces par implant karti hain, toh ise transcoelomic spread kehte hain. Is route ko automatically distant organ parenchymal spread ke barabar mat samjho.
- **B (Incorrect).** Hematogenous spread blood vessels ke through hota hai. Ovarian cancer blood ke through bhi spread kar sakta hai, lekin stem mein cavity shedding aur surface deposits ka specific route describe hua hai.
- **C (Incorrect).** Direct extension mein tumor adjacent tissue mein continuous growth ke through ghusta hai. Yahan separated implants cells ke cavity mein shed hone ke baad bane hain.
- **D (Incorrect).** Lymphatic route lymph vessels ke through spread ko describe karta hai. Question mein cells ke peritoneal cavity mein shed hone ka evidence diya gaya hai, isliye lymphatic route alone best explanation nahi hai.

**Exam clue:** Body cavity mein shedding aur surface implantation ko transcoelomic spread kehte hain.

**Concept:** [Application concepts](../Concepts/01A-Cancer-Cell-Biology-Application.md#routes-of-spread)

### Q6. A pathology report documents malignant cells entering a vein from a renal tumor, followed by confirmed tumor deposits in the lungs. Which route is most directly supported by the documented vascular entry?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Priority:** 🔴 MOST IMPORTANT | **Exam focus:** NORCET / all-India Staff Nurse | **Type:** Original practice

- **A.** Transcoelomic implantation
- **B.** Local direct extension alone
- **C.** Hematogenous dissemination
- **D.** Perineural spread alone

<details><summary>Answer aur detailed explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: C. Hematogenous dissemination**

- **A (Incorrect).** Transcoelomic implantation mein cells body cavity mein shed hokar surfaces par attach ho sakti hain. Question mein vein ke andar entry documented hai, isliye blood route zyada directly support hota hai.
- **B (Incorrect).** Local direct extension nearby tissue mein continuous growth ko describe karta hai. Renal tumor se lung deposits tak blood vessel entry ke saath spread ko sirf local extension nahi kehte.
- **C (Correct).** Hematogenous dissemination mein tumor cells bloodstream mein enter karke door ke tissues tak pahunch sakti hain. Renal tumor ki venous entry aur confirmed lung deposits is mechanism ko support karte hain. Sirf imaging se har case mein exact route prove nahi hota.
- **D (Incorrect).** Perineural spread mein malignant cells nerve ke aas-paas ya along nerve grow kar sakti hain. Stem mein venous entry ka evidence hai, nerve-associated spread ka nahi.

**Exam clue:** Vein ke through door ke organ tak spread hematogenous route ko support karta hai.

- **A.** Lipoma
- **B.** Adenoma
- **C.** Osteoma
- **D.** Melanoma

<details><summary>Answer aur detailed explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: D. Melanoma**

- **A (Incorrect).** Lipoma generally fatty tissue ka benign tumor hai. Is example se suffix rule ka malignant exception demonstrate nahi hota.
- **B (Incorrect).** Adenoma benign epithelial tumor ko describe karta hai. Kuch adenomas ka clinical follow-up important ho sakta hai, lekin word itself malignant exception nahi hai.

- **D (Correct).** Melanoma melanocytes ki malignant neoplasm hai, bhale hi naam '-oma' par end hota hai. Lymphoma jaise doosre malignant exceptions bhi hain. Nurse ko exact pathological diagnosis dekhna chahiye, sirf suffix se prognosis assume nahi karna chahiye.

### Q8. A patient with a tumor develops a systemic endocrine disturbance caused by a substance released by the tumor, without direct invasion of the affected endocrine organ. Which mechanism best explains this presentation?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Priority:** 🔴 MOST IMPORTANT | **Exam focus:** NORCET / all-India Staff Nurse | **Type:** Original practice

- **A.** Direct compression of the affected organ
- **B.** A paraneoplastic effect
- **C.** A new primary endocrine malignancy established by symptoms alone
- **D.** Normal physiological hormone regulation

<details><summary>Answer aur detailed explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: B. A paraneoplastic effect**

- **A (Incorrect).** Direct compression mein mass nearby tissue par pressure daalta hai. Stem mein endocrine disturbance tumor-released substance ke kaaran hai, compression ke kaaran nahi.
- **B (Correct).** Paraneoplastic effects mein tumor se release hone wale substances ya tumor-related immune responses body ke doosre functions disturb kar sakte hain. Nurse symptoms aur relevant laboratory changes assess karke medical team ko report karegi.
- **C (Incorrect).** Endocrine disturbance hone se naya primary endocrine cancer confirm nahi hota. Tumor-related secretion aur separate primary malignancy ko distinguish karne ke liye clinical evaluation chahiye.
- **D (Incorrect).** Normal hormone regulation body ki needs ke hisaab se adjust hoti hai. Tumor-derived substance se systemic disturbance hona physiological regulation alone nahi hai.

**Exam clue:** Paraneoplastic symptoms local mass effect ke bina bhi ho sakte hain.

### Q9. A patient's relative refuses to hold the patient's hand because they fear acquiring the patient's cancer. Which nursing explanation is best?

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD | **Exam focus:** NORCET / all-India Staff Nurse | **Type:** Original practice

- **A.** Ordinary social contact does not transmit cancer between people
- **B.** All cancers are transmissible through direct skin contact
- **C.** A tumor diagnosis requires airborne isolation in every case
- **D.** Contact is unsafe until all tumor cells have been eliminated

<details><summary>Answer aur detailed explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: A. Ordinary social contact does not transmit cancer between people**

- **A (Correct).** Cancer ordinary social contact, jaise haath pakadne ya saath baithne, se spread nahi hota. Kuch infections cancer risk badha sakti hain, lekin infection transmission aur cancer transmission alag baatein hain. Nurse normal supportive contact encourage kar sakti hai.
- **B (Incorrect).** Direct skin contact se har cancer transmit nahi hota. Aisa belief unnecessary isolation aur stigma badha sakta hai.
- **C (Incorrect).** Airborne isolation specific infectious indications ke liye hoti hai. Cancer diagnosis alone airborne precautions ka indication nahi hai, lekin patient ki infection aur immunity ke hisaab se precautions decide hote hain.
- **D (Incorrect).** Patient se normal social contact karne ke liye tumor elimination ka wait zaruri nahi hai. Standard precautions aur any indicated infection-specific advice follow karni chahiye.

**Exam clue:** Cancer aur cancer risk badhane wali transmissible infection alag concepts hain.

### Q10. A tumor study identifies low oxygen concentration within a solid mass even though the patient's measured peripheral oxygen saturation is normal. Which interpretation is most accurate?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Priority:** 🔴 MOST IMPORTANT | **Exam focus:** NORCET / all-India Staff Nurse | **Type:** Original practice

- **A.** Normal peripheral saturation excludes oxygen deficiency in every tissue
- **B.** Low tumor oxygen necessarily proves systemic respiratory failure
- **C.** Local tumor hypoxia can occur despite normal systemic oxygen saturation
- **D.** Low tumor oxygen establishes complete tumor necrosis

<details><summary>Answer aur detailed explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: C. Local tumor hypoxia can occur despite normal systemic oxygen saturation**

- **A (Incorrect).** Peripheral pulse oximetry arterial hemoglobin ki oxygen saturation estimate karti hai. Kisi local tissue ko blood supply insufficient ho sakti hai, isliye normal SpO2 har tissue ki adequate oxygen delivery prove nahi karti.
- **B (Incorrect).** Local tumor hypoxia se systemic respiratory failure automatically diagnose nahi hota. Respiratory status ko clinical signs aur appropriate investigations ke saath assess karte hain.
- **C (Correct).** Hypoxia ka matlab tissue ko oxygen supply kam milna hai. Tumor ke abnormal vascular supply ki wajah se kuch regions hypoxic ho sakte hain, jabki peripheral oxygen saturation normal rahe. Local tumor biology aur systemic respiratory assessment alag hain.
- **D (Incorrect).** Hypoxic region mein viable cells abhi bhi present ho sakti hain. Low oxygen concentration se complete tumor tissue death establish nahi hoti.

**Exam clue:** SpO2 systemic arterial oxygenation ko reflect karti hai, har tumor region ki oxygen supply ko nahi.

**Concept:** [Application concepts](../Concepts/01A-Cancer-Cell-Biology-Application.md#growth-survival-and-metabolism)

**Clinical reference:** [NCI / SEER](https://www.cancer.gov/publications/dictionaries/cancer-terms/def/hypoxia)
