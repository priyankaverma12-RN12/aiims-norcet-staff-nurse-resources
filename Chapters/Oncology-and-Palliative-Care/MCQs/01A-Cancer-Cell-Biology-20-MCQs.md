### Q1. A healing wound stops producing extra epithelial cells once its surface is restored. Which normal feature does this demonstrate?

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD | **Exam focus:** NORCET / all-India Staff Nurse | **Type:** Original practice

- **A.** Growth regulation in response to tissue needs
- **B.** Permanent activation of growth signals
- **C.** Loss of programmed cell death
- **D.** Invasion of adjacent tissue

<details><summary>Answer aur detailed explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: A. Growth regulation in response to tissue needs**

- **A (Correct).** Normal cells zarurat ke hisaab se divide karti hain. Jab wound cover ho jaata hai, toh growth rokne wale signals extra cell division ko control karte hain.
- **B (Incorrect).** Permanent growth signal se cells repair complete hone ke baad bhi divide kar sakti hain. Yeh normal control ka example nahi hai.
- **C (Incorrect).** Programmed cell death ka matlab unwanted cells ka orderly removal hai. Wound ka cover hone ke baad growth rukna mainly growth regulation ko dikhata hai.
- **D (Incorrect).** Invasion mein abnormal cells paas ke tissues mein ghus jaati hain. Normal wound repair mein tissue ki zarurat ke hisaab se growth hoti hai.

- **D.** Extracellular matrix formation

- **A (Incorrect).** Angiogenesis mein naye blood vessels bante hain. Yeh damaged cell ko orderly tareeke se remove karne ka mechanism nahi hai.
