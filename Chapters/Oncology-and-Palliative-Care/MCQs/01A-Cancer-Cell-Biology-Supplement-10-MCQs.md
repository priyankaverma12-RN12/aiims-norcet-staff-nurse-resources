### Q1. A smoker's bronchial biopsy shows replacement of ciliated columnar epithelium by mature squamous epithelium without reported atypia. Which adaptive change is described?

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD | **Exam focus:** NORCET / all-India Staff Nurse | **Type:** Original practice

- **A.** Hyperplasia
- **B.** Metaplasia
- **C.** Dysplasia
- **D.** Carcinoma in situ

<details><summary>Answer aur detailed explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: B. Metaplasia**

- **A (Incorrect).** Hyperplasia mein cells ki number badhti hai. Is report mein mature lining cell ka type change hua hai, isliye sirf number badhne wala process fit nahi hota.
- **B (Correct).** Metaplasia mein ek mature cell type ki jagah doosra mature cell type aa sakta hai. Chronic irritation mein airway ki ciliated lining squamous lining mein badal sakti hai. Is finding se invasive cancer establish nahi hota, lekin ongoing exposure ko address karna zaruri hai.
- **C (Incorrect).** Dysplasia mein cellular atypia aur disordered maturation important findings hain. Is report mein atypia mention nahi hui hai, isliye described mature cell replacement metaplasia ko support karta hai.
- **D (Incorrect).** Carcinoma in situ mein neoplastic epithelial cells boundary ke andar confined hoti hain. Mature cell type change alone se aisi neoplastic lesion diagnose nahi ki ja sakti.

**Exam clue:** Mature cell type badalne aur cell number badhne mein difference yaad rakho.

**Concept:** [Extended theory](../Concepts/01A-Cancer-Cell-Biology-Extended-Theory.md#metaplasia)

### Q2. A tumor specimen has 24% of cells actively duplicating their DNA. Which cell-cycle phase is being measured?

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD | **Exam focus:** NORCET / all-India Staff Nurse | **Type:** Original practice

- **A.** G1
- **B.** G2
- **C.** M
- **D.** S

<details><summary>Answer aur detailed explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: D. S**

- **A (Incorrect).** G1 mein cell grow karti hai aur DNA copying ke liye proteins aur doosri zaruri cheezein prepare karti hai. Question mein active DNA duplication measure hui hai, sirf preparation nahi.
- **B (Incorrect).** G2 mein DNA copy ho chuka hota hai aur cell division se pehle preparation karti hai. Isliye G2 percentage ko active DNA synthesis percentage nahi bolenge.
- **C (Incorrect).** M phase mein chromosomes separate hote hain aur cell division hoti hai. DNA ki main copying is phase se pehle ho chuki hoti hai.
- **D (Correct).** S phase mein DNA duplicate hota hai taaki daughter cells ke liye genetic material ready rahe. Is report ka 24% S-phase fraction hai. Is value se akela treatment outcome guarantee nahi kiya ja sakta.

**Exam clue:** S ko synthesis of DNA se connect karo.

### Q3. A drug-development report describes a population of viable tumor cells that have exited active cycling but may later re-enter it. Which phase best corresponds to this resting state?

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD | **Exam focus:** NORCET / all-India Staff Nurse | **Type:** Original practice

- **A.** G0
- **B.** G1
- **C.** S
- **D.** G2

<details><summary>Answer aur detailed explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: A. G0**

- **A (Correct).** G0 mein cell active division cycle se bahar hoti hai. Kuch cells viable reh sakti hain aur conditions badalne par cycle mein wapas aa sakti hain, isliye resting state ko cell elimination samajhna sahi nahi hai.
- **B (Incorrect).** G1 active cell cycle ka growth aur preparation phase hai. G0 se wapas aayi cell G1 mein enter kar sakti hai, lekin question specifically cycle se bahar resting cells ki baat kar raha hai.
- **C (Incorrect).** S phase mein DNA actively duplicate hota hai. Resting cells jo active cycle se bahar hain unhe S phase mein count nahi karte.
- **D (Incorrect).** G2 mein cell DNA replication complete karne ke baad mitosis ke liye ready hoti hai. Ismein cell active cycle ke andar hai, bahar nahi.

**Exam clue:** G0 active division cycle ke bahar ki resting state hai, cell death nahi.

**Concept:** [Extended theory](../Concepts/01A-Cancer-Cell-Biology-Extended-Theory.md#cell-cycle-and-treatment-effects)

### Q4. In a tumor cell, duplicated chromosomes are being pulled toward opposite poles. Which mitotic event is occurring?

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD | **Exam focus:** NORCET / all-India Staff Nurse | **Type:** Original practice

- **A.** Prophase
- **B.** Metaphase
- **C.** Anaphase
- **D.** Telophase

<details><summary>Answer aur detailed explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: C. Anaphase**

- **A (Incorrect).** Prophase mein chromatin condense hota hai aur division ki machinery develop hoti hai. Chromatids ka opposite poles ki taraf actual separation anaphase mein hota hai.
- **B (Incorrect).** Metaphase mein chromosomes cell ke middle plane par line up karte hain. Agar chromosomes poles ki taraf move kar rahe hain, toh alignment wali stage se aage badh chuke hain.
- **C (Correct).** Anaphase mein sister chromatids separate hokar opposite poles ki taraf jaati hain. Isse dono future daughter cells ko chromosome copies milne mein madad milti hai.
- **D (Incorrect).** Telophase mein chromosomes poles tak pahunchne ke baad naye nuclei form hone lagte hain. Question mein chromatids abhi pull ho rahi hain, isliye anaphase best hai.

**Exam clue:** Metaphase mein alignment aur anaphase mein separation hoti hai.

### Q5. A patient receiving cytotoxic chemotherapy asks why the lining of the mouth can be injured even though the treatment is directed at cancer. Which explanation best relates cell biology to this effect?

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD | **Exam focus:** NORCET / all-India Staff Nurse | **Type:** Original practice

- **A.** Oral epithelial cells also renew rapidly
- **B.** The oral mucosa consists mainly of permanently nondividing cells
- **C.** Mouth ulcers always demonstrate new oral metastases
- **D.** All cytotoxic agents selectively destroy only malignant cells

<details><summary>Answer aur detailed explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: A. Oral epithelial cells also renew rapidly**

- **A (Correct).** Mouth ki lining ki cells regularly replace hoti hain. Kai cytotoxic medicines fast-dividing cells ko harm kar sakti hain, isliye healthy oral lining ki repair slow ho sakti hai aur sores develop ho sakte hain. Nurse ko pain, intake, infection signs aur oral care assess karna chahiye.
- **B (Incorrect).** Oral epithelium ki renewal active hoti hai. Permanent nondividing tissue samajhne se chemotherapy ke mucosal effects ka mechanism galat samjha jaayega.
- **C (Incorrect).** Mouth sores treatment injury, infection ya doosre causes se bhi ho sakte hain. Har ulcer ko metastasis kehna unsafe hai aur appropriate assessment ko delay kar sakta hai.
- **D (Incorrect).** Cytotoxic treatment cancer cells ke saath kuch healthy fast-dividing cells ko bhi affect kar sakta hai. Har medicine ka effect same nahi hota aur dose ya management oncology team decide karti hai.

**Exam clue:** Rapidly renewing healthy tissues bhi cytotoxic treatment se affect ho sakte hain.

### Q6. A molecular report identifies altered DNA packaging and reduced expression of a growth-regulating gene without a change in its DNA base sequence. Which mechanism best fits the description?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Priority:** 🔴 MOST IMPORTANT | **Exam focus:** NORCET / all-India Staff Nurse | **Type:** Original practice

- **A.** Chromosomal translocation
- **B.** Point mutation
- **C.** Gene deletion
- **D.** Epigenetic alteration

<details><summary>Answer aur detailed explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: D. Epigenetic alteration**

- **A (Incorrect).** Translocation mein chromosome ka segment rearrange ho sakta hai. Report mein DNA packaging aur expression change diya hai, chromosomal segment movement nahi.
- **B (Incorrect).** Point mutation mein DNA sequence ke specific base mein change hota hai. Yahan base sequence unchanged hai, isliye point mutation description fit nahi karti.
- **C (Incorrect).** Deletion mein genetic material ka kuch hissa lost hota hai. Is report mein gene ki expression kam hui hai lekin sequence deletion demonstrate nahi hui hai.
- **D (Correct).** Epigenetic alteration DNA ke letters ko badle bina gene ki activity par effect daal sakti hai. DNA packaging ya chemical marking change hone se gene expression change ho sakti hai. Yeh genetic sequence mutation se alag concept hai.

**Exam clue:** DNA letters unchanged hon lekin gene expression badle, toh epigenetic regulation consider karo.

### Q7. A patient with a confirmed inherited cancer-predisposing variant asks whether developing cancer is now unavoidable. Which response is most appropriate?

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Priority:** 🟠 HIGH YIELD | **Exam focus:** NORCET / all-India Staff Nurse | **Type:** Original practice

- **A.** Cancer is inevitable because the variant was inherited
- **B.** The variant may increase risk without making cancer inevitable
- **C.** The variant becomes irrelevant once adulthood is reached
- **D.** Cancer risk can be inferred only from the current tumor size

<details><summary>Answer aur detailed explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: B. The variant may increase risk without making cancer inevitable**

- **A (Incorrect).** Inherited variant se risk badh sakta hai, lekin cancer develop hona guaranteed nahi hota. Inevitable bolne se patient ko unnecessary fear ho sakta hai.
- **B (Correct).** Kuch inherited variants cells mein protective pathways ko affect karke cancer ka risk badha sakti hain. Individual risk gene, family history aur doosre factors se vary karta hai. Genetic counseling aur advised surveillance plan useful ho sakte hain.
- **C (Incorrect).** Adulthood ke baad inherited variant disappear nahi hoti hai. Age ke saath risk aur surveillance needs change ho sakti hain, isliye professional follow-up important hai.
- **D (Incorrect).** Tumor size existing disease ke assessment ka ek part ho sakta hai. Inherited susceptibility assess karne ke liye current tumor size alone sufficient nahi hai.

**Exam clue:** Inherited susceptibility aur confirmed cancer diagnosis alag baatein hain.

### Q8. Two patients have the same histological cancer type, but only one has a confirmed actionable tumor biomarker. What is the most appropriate interpretation?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Priority:** 🔴 MOST IMPORTANT | **Exam focus:** NORCET / all-India Staff Nurse | **Type:** Original practice

- **A.** Both must have identical responses to every targeted drug
- **B.** The biomarker establishes the anatomical stage by itself
- **C.** Treatment selection may differ according to the molecular findings and clinical context
- **D.** The biomarker makes all subsequent monitoring unnecessary

<details><summary>Answer aur detailed explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: C. Treatment selection may differ according to the molecular findings and clinical context**

- **A (Incorrect).** Same histological diagnosis ke bawajood tumors ke molecular features different ho sakte hain. Isliye har targeted drug ka response dono patients mein identical assume nahi karna chahiye.
- **B (Incorrect).** Biomarker treatment selection ya prognostic assessment mein help kar sakta hai. Anatomical extent establish karne ke liye cancer-specific staging assessment bhi chahiye.
- **C (Correct).** Actionable biomarker aisi molecular finding hai jo specific treatment choice mein help kar sakti hai. Oncology team biomarker, cancer type, available therapy aur patient ki clinical condition ko saath mein consider karti hai. Response guaranteed nahi hota.
- **D (Incorrect).** Treatment ke baad clinical assessment, adverse effects aur response monitoring ki zarurat rehti hai. Biomarker result monitoring ka replacement nahi hai.

**Exam clue:** Same histology ka matlab same actionable molecular target nahi hota.

### Q9. A treated patient asks whether a scan showing no detectable tumor proves that no malignant cell remains anywhere in the body. Which reply is best?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Priority:** 🔴 MOST IMPORTANT | **Exam focus:** NORCET / all-India Staff Nurse | **Type:** Original practice

- **A.** A negative scan establishes microscopic absence of cancer in every tissue
- **B.** No detectable disease is reassuring, but microscopic residual cells may still be present
- **C.** A negative scan is by definition evidence of disease progression
- **D.** Further follow-up is useful only when a visible tumor is present

<details><summary>Answer aur detailed explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: B. No detectable disease is reassuring, but microscopic residual cells may still be present**

- **A (Incorrect).** Scan ki detection limits hoti hain. Bohot chhoti cell populations visible nahi ho sakti hain, isliye negative scan se har tissue mein microscopic absence prove nahi hoti.
- **B (Correct).** Scan mein disease detect na hona reassuring finding hai. Lekin microscopic residual cells kabhi-kabhi remain kar sakti hain. Nurse patient ko realistic explanation degi aur oncology team ka follow-up plan continue karne mein help karegi.
- **C (Incorrect).** Progression ka matlab disease ka badhna ya spread hona hai. Negative scan alone progression ka evidence nahi hota.
- **D (Incorrect).** Follow-up sirf visible tumor ke liye nahi hota. Recurrence ke signs aur treatment ke late effects monitor karna bhi important ho sakta hai.

**Exam clue:** No detectable disease aur guaranteed cure ko interchangeable mat samjho.

### Q10. A patient with leukemia has a very small abnormal cell population detected by a validated sensitive assay after treatment despite routine morphological remission. Which term best describes this finding?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Priority:** 🔴 MOST IMPORTANT | **Exam focus:** NORCET / all-India Staff Nurse | **Type:** Original practice

- **A.** Measurable residual disease
- **B.** A new solid primary tumor
- **C.** Physiological bone-marrow regeneration alone
- **D.** Anatomical T-stage progression

<details><summary>Answer aur detailed explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: A. Measurable residual disease**

- **A (Correct).** Measurable residual disease ka matlab treatment ke baad ya treatment ke dauran bahut kam cancer cells ka sensitive test se detect hona hai. Leukemia mein yeh information treatment planning aur prognosis mein help kar sakti hai. Result ko disease-specific assay aur specialist interpretation ke saath samajhna chahiye.
- **B (Incorrect).** Leukemia mein remaining abnormal cell population se naya solid primary tumor establish nahi hota. Is finding ka relation existing blood malignancy se hai.
- **C (Incorrect).** Marrow recovery mein normal developing cells ho sakti hain. Lekin validated assay ne specifically abnormal malignant population detect ki hai, isliye normal regeneration alone explanation nahi hai.
- **D (Incorrect).** T-stage solid tumors mein primary tumor extent describe karne ke liye use hota hai. Leukemia ke sensitive residual cell assay ko anatomical T-stage progression nahi kehte.

**Exam clue:** Sensitive assay se detected remaining malignant cells ko MRD kaha ja sakta hai.

**Concept:** [Extended theory](../Concepts/01A-Cancer-Cell-Biology-Extended-Theory.md#residual-disease-and-remission)
