## Initial study explanations

### Neutropenia

Neutrophils infection se ladne wale white cells hain. Chemotherapy se ye bahut kam ho sakte hain. Fever serious infection ka eklauta early sign bhi ho sakta hai, isliye urgent protocol chahiye.

### Extravasation

Vesicant medicine vein se bahar leak ho toh tissue damage ho sakta hai. Nurse infusion stop karti hai aur drug-specific protocol follow karti hai. Cannula aspiration ke liye temporarily needed ho sakti hai.

### Tumor lysis syndrome

Cancer cells bahut jaldi tootne par unke andar ke substances blood mein aa jaate hain. Kidney aur heart ko harm ho sakta hai. Nurse labs aur ECG ke saath urgent treatment review leti hai.

### Palliative care

Palliative care ka aim symptoms kam karke comfort aur quality of life improve karna hai. Nurse pain ki intensity aur patient ki preference samajhkar treatment team se plan review karati hai.
