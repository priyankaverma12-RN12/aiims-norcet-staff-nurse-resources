## Anaphylaxis

### Q1. Minutes after an antibiotic infusion starts, a patient develops wheeze, generalized urticaria and hypotension. Which medication is first-line for anaphylaxis according to emergency protocol?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Topic:** Anaphylaxis

- **A.** Intramuscular epinephrine
- **B.** Oral antihistamine alone
- **C.** Oral calcium alone
- **D.** Intravenous iron

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: A. Intramuscular epinephrine**

Anaphylaxis mein allergic reaction airway ko narrow aur BP ko low kar sakti hai. IM epinephrine airway aur circulation ko support karti hai. Emergency team ko activate karna bhi zaroori hai.

**Baaki options kyun galat hain?**

- **B.** Antihistamine skin symptoms help kar sakti hai par shock ka first-line treatment nahi hai.
- **C.** Calcium anaphylaxis ka first-line drug nahi hai.
- **D.** Iron allergic reaction treat nahi karta.

</details>

## SLE

### Q2. A patient with systemic lupus erythematosus develops new proteinuria and hypertension. Which complication warrants evaluation?

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Topic:** SLE

- **A.** Uncomplicated conjunctivitis
- **B.** Stable allergic rhinitis
- **C.** Isolated hearing loss
- **D.** Lupus nephritis

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: D. Lupus nephritis**

Lupus mein defense system kidney ko damage kar sakti hai. Urine mein protein aur BP badhna kidney involvement ke clues hain. Nurse renal assessment arrange karti hai.

**Baaki options kyun galat hain?**

- **A.** Conjunctivitis kidney findings explain nahi karti.
- **B.** Rhinitis proteinuria ka typical cause nahi hai.
- **C.** Hearing loss hypertension aur proteinuria explain nahi karta.

</details>

## Immunosuppression

### Q3. A transplant recipient taking immunosuppressants develops fever and chills. Which response is safest?

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Topic:** Immunosuppression

- **A.** Assume fever is always a normal drug effect
- **B.** Wait until symptoms persist for a month
- **C.** Prompt clinical evaluation for infection
- **D.** Stop all immunosuppressants permanently without advice

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: C. Prompt clinical evaluation for infection**

Immunosuppressants rejection rokne ke liye defense system ka response kam karti hain. Infection jaldi serious ho sakti hai. Nurse fever ko promptly report karti hai.

**Baaki options kyun galat hain?**

- **A.** Fever ko normal assume karna unsafe hai.
- **B.** Delay infection ko worsen kar sakta hai.
- **D.** Medicines abruptly stop karne se rejection ka risk ho sakta hai.

</details>

## Immunity

### Q4. Which immunological process explains protection after administration of an effective vaccine?

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Topic:** Immunity

- **A.** Complete elimination of all future infection risk
- **B.** Development of antigen-specific immune memory
- **C.** Permanent destruction of all white blood cells
- **D.** Immediate replacement of all antibodies by antibiotics

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: B. Development of antigen-specific immune memory**

Vaccine immune system ko antigen pehchanne ka chance deti hai. Memory cells ban sakti hain, jisse future exposure par faster response mil sakta hai.

**Baaki options kyun galat hain?**

- **A.** Vaccine risk kam karti hai par har infection ko guarantee se nahi rokti.
- **C.** White cells immunity ke liye chahiye.
- **D.** Antibiotics antibodies nahi banati hain.

</details>
