### Q01. Which term describes an immune defect resulting from an underlying inherited disorder?

**Difficulty:** Easy | **Priority:** Important | **Exam focus:** NORCET / HPRCA Staff Nurse | **Topic:** Immunodeficiency assessment

- **A.** Primary immunodeficiency
- **B.** Secondary immunodeficiency from chemotherapy
- **C.** Irritant contact dermatitis
- **D.** Physiological intolerance

<details><summary>Correct answer and detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: A. Primary immunodeficiency**

**A kyun sahi hai:** Primary immunodeficiency mein immune system ka basic defect aksar genetic hota hai. Ye kisi acquired medicine effect se alag hota hai.

**B kyun galat hai:** Chemotherapy ke baad immune function kam hona acquired ya secondary deficiency ka example hai.

**C kyun galat hai:** Irritant dermatitis mein skin barrier damage hota hai, inherited immune defect zaroori nahi hai.

**D kyun galat hai:** Intolerance kisi substance ko tolerate karne mein problem batati hai, genetic immune deficiency ka naam nahi hai.

**Exam clue:** Primary aur secondary deficiency ko cause se distinguish karo.

**Clinical reference:** [Source A](https://www.merckmanuals.com/professional/immunology-allergic-disorders/immunodeficiency-disorders/overview-of-immunodeficiency-disorders)

</details>

### Q02. Which class of medicines may cause secondary immunodeficiency by suppressing immune responses?

- **A.** Calcium carbonate used as a supplement
- **B.** Systemic immunosuppressants
- **C.** Simple oral rehydration salts
- **D.** Topical emollients

<details><summary>Correct answer and detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: B. Systemic immunosuppressants**

**A kyun galat hai:** Calcium supplement ka usual role mineral replacement hai, immunosuppression nahi.

**B kyun sahi hai:** Systemic immunosuppressants immune response ko intentionally kam karte hain. Is wajah se infection susceptibility badh sakti hai.

**C kyun galat hai:** Oral rehydration salts water aur electrolytes replace karte hain, immune cells ko suppress nahi karte.

**D kyun galat hai:** Emollients skin barrier ko support karte hain, systemic immunity ko generally suppress nahi karte.

**Exam clue:** Medication history secondary immune deficiency mein important hai.

**Clinical reference:** [Source A](https://www.merckmanuals.com/professional/immunology-allergic-disorders/immunodeficiency-disorders/overview-of-immunodeficiency-disorders)

### Q05. Which immune component is primarily deficient in X-linked agammaglobulinemia?

**Difficulty:** Easy | **Priority:** Important | **Exam focus:** NORCET / HPRCA Staff Nurse | **Topic:** Immunodeficiency assessment

- **A.** Mature B cells and antibody production
- **B.** Erythrocyte hemoglobin synthesis
- **C.** Platelet aggregation alone
- **D.** Renal tubular filtration alone

<details><summary>Correct answer and detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: A. Mature B cells and antibody production**

**A kyun sahi hai:** Is disorder mein B-cell maturation impaired hoti hai, jisse antibody production bahut kam ho sakti hai. Isliye bacterial infection ka risk badhta hai.

**B kyun galat hai:** Hemoglobin synthesis red cell disorders se related hai, X-linked agammaglobulinemia ka main defect nahi.

**C kyun galat hai:** Platelet aggregation clot formation ka hissa hai, antibody production ka substitute nahi.

**D kyun galat hai:** Renal filtration kidney function hai, B-cell maturation ka process nahi.

**Exam clue:** Agammaglobulinemia mein antibody production par focus karo.

**Clinical reference:** [Source D](https://www.merckmanuals.com/professional/immunology-allergic-disorders/immunodeficiency-disorders/x-linked-agammaglobulinemia)

### Q07. What is the purpose of immunoglobulin replacement in selected antibody deficiency disorders?

- **A.** Replace red blood cells
- **B.** Stimulate the thyroid to release more hormone
- **C.** Provide antibodies that the patient cannot produce adequately
- **D.** Permanently eliminate all infectious exposures

<details><summary>Correct answer and detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: C. Provide antibodies that the patient cannot produce adequately**

**A kyun galat hai:** Red cell replacement anemia ya blood loss ke liye use ho sakta hai, antibody replacement alag therapy hai.

**B kyun galat hai:** Thyroid hormone production ko improve karna immunoglobulin replacement ka goal nahi hai.

**C kyun sahi hai:** Replacement therapy patient ko functional antibodies provide karti hai. Isse selected antibody deficiency mein infection risk kam karne mein madad milti hai.

**D kyun galat hai:** Antibodies infection risk kam kar sakti hain, par har exposure ko eliminate nahi karti hain.

**Exam clue:** Antibody replacement immune support hai, permanent infection guarantee nahi.

**Clinical reference:** [Source A](https://www.merckmanuals.com/professional/immunology-allergic-disorders/immunodeficiency-disorders/overview-of-immunodeficiency-disorders)

### Q08. A child has recurrent respiratory infections despite a normal total white blood cell count. What is the best interpretation?

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Priority:** High Yield | **Exam focus:** NORCET / HPRCA Staff Nurse | **Topic:** Immunodeficiency assessment

- **A.** Normal white cell count rules out all immunodeficiency
- **B.** The child must have a platelet disorder
- **C.** No clinical history is needed
- **D.** Normal total white cell count does not exclude an immune function defect

<details><summary>Correct answer and detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: D. Normal total white cell count does not exclude an immune function defect**

**A kyun galat hai:** Total count sirf broad number batata hai, har cell function nahi.

**B kyun galat hai:** Platelet disorder ko confirm karne ke liye separate clinical aur laboratory evidence chahiye.

**C kyun galat hai:** History se severity, organisms, treatment response aur family pattern pata chalta hai.

**D kyun sahi hai:** Total count normal hone par bhi individual cell subsets ya antibody function abnormal ho sakta hai. Recurrent infections ki pattern-based evaluation zaroori hai.

**Exam clue:** Normal CBC ke baad bhi clinically indicated immune assessment useful hai.

**Clinical reference:** [Source H](https://www.merckmanuals.com/professional/immunology-allergic-disorders/immunodeficiency-disorders/approach-to-the-patient-with-suspected-immunodeficiency)
