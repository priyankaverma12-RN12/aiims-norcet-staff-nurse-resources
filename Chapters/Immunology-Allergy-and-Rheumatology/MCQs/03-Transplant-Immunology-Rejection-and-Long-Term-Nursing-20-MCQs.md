### Q01. What is an autograft?

**Difficulty:** Easy | **Priority:** Important | **Exam focus:** NORCET / HPRCA Staff Nurse | **Topic:** Transplant immunology and nursing

- **A.** Tissue transferred from one site to another in the same individual
- **B.** An organ donated by an unrelated person
- **C.** Tissue transferred between different species
- **D.** A graft from a genetically nonidentical sibling

<details><summary>Correct answer and detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: A. Tissue transferred from one site to another in the same individual**

**A kyun sahi hai:** Autograft mein donor aur recipient ek hi person hote hain. Example mein patient ki apni skin ek site se doosri site par transfer ki ja sakti hai.

**B kyun galat hai:** Unrelated human donor ka graft allograft category mein aa sakta hai.

**C kyun galat hai:** Different species ke beech tissue transfer xenograft kehlata hai.

**D kyun galat hai:** Genetically nonidentical sibling ka graft allograft hota hai, apna tissue nahi.

**Exam clue:** Auto ka matlab same individual yaad rakho.

**Clinical reference:** [Source A](https://www.merckmanuals.com/professional/immunology-allergic-disorders/transplantation/overview-of-transplantation)

</details>

### Q06. In graft-versus-host disease, whose immune cells attack the other person's tissues?

**Difficulty:** Easy | **Priority:** Important | **Exam focus:** NORCET / HPRCA Staff Nurse | **Topic:** Transplant immunology and nursing

- **A.** Donor red cells attack their own graft only
- **B.** Donor immune cells attack recipient tissues
- **C.** Recipient erythrocytes attack donor platelets
- **D.** Recipient platelets attack donor albumin

<details><summary>Correct answer and detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: B. Donor immune cells attack recipient tissues**

**A kyun galat hai:** Donor red cells ke apne graft par attack se GVHD define nahi hota.

**B kyun sahi hai:** GVHD mein graft ke saath aaye donor immune cells recipient ke tissues ko foreign samajhkar attack karte hain. Ye ordinary host-versus-graft rejection se different direction hai.

**C kyun galat hai:** Recipient red cells immune tissue attack ka main effector nahi hote.

**D kyun galat hai:** Platelets clotting mein kaam karte hain, GVHD ka main immune mechanism nahi.

**Exam clue:** GVHD mein attack ki direction donor se host ki taraf hoti hai.

**Clinical reference:** [Source A](https://www.merckmanuals.com/professional/immunology-allergic-disorders/transplantation/overview-of-transplantation)

</details>

### Q14. A kidney graft becomes abruptly nonfunctional within minutes of reperfusion in a sensitized recipient. Which rejection mechanism is most consistent?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Priority:** High Yield | **Exam focus:** NORCET / HPRCA Staff Nurse | **Topic:** Transplant immunology and nursing

- **D.** Isolated delayed T-cell response developing over several months

<details><summary>Correct answer and detailed explanation</summary>

**B kyun sahi hai:** Pre-existing donor-reactive antibodies reperfusion ke baad rapid graft vascular injury cause kar sakti hain. Ye hyperacute rejection ka classical mechanism hai.

**C kyun galat hai:** Chronic process usually gradual hota hai, minute-level onset explain nahi karta.

**D kyun galat hai:** New T-cell response ko develop hone mein time chahiye, immediate antibody injury se different hai.

**Exam clue:** Minutes after reperfusion aur pre-existing antibodies hyperacute pattern suggest karte hain.
