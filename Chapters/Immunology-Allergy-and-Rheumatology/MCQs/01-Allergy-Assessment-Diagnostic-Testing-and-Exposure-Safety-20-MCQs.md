### Q01. Which information should a nurse record when a patient reports a suspected drug allergy?

**Difficulty:** Easy | **Priority:** Important | **Exam focus:** NORCET / HPRCA Staff Nurse | **Topic:** Allergy assessment, testing and exposure safety

- **A.** Drug name and the actual reaction
- **B.** Drug price and manufacturer alone
- **C.** Bed number and admission time alone
- **D.** Only the preferred pharmacy

<details><summary>Correct answer and detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: A. Drug name and the actual reaction**

**A kyun sahi hai:** Drug ka naam aur patient ko kya reaction hua tha, dono likhne se agle clinician ko risk samajhne mein madad milti hai. Sirf allergy likhna kaafi nahi hota.

**B kyun galat hai:** Price se allergy ka clinical pattern pata nahi chalta, bhale hi manufacturer ki information kabhi useful ho.

**C kyun galat hai:** Bed number patient ko locate karne mein madad karta hai, par reaction ko identify nahi karta.

**D kyun galat hai:** Pharmacy ka naam medicine supply ke liye useful hai, allergy assessment ke liye reaction history chahiye.

**Exam clue:** Allergy history mein substance aur reaction dono yaad rakho.

**Clinical reference:** [Source C](https://www.nice.org.uk/guidance/cg183/chapter/Recommendations)

</details>

- **C.** 6 hours

**C kyun galat hai:** Chhe ghante ka wait routine initial prick reading ke liye zaroori nahi hota.

**D kyun galat hai:** Do din baad ka reading delayed patch-test process mein useful ho sakta hai, prick test mein nahi.

**Exam clue:** Immediate skin testing aur delayed patch testing ka time alag hota hai.

**Clinical reference:** [Source A](https://www.aaaai.org/tools-for-the-public/conditions-library/allergies/all-about-allergy-testing)

- **A.** Choose powdered latex gloves
- **B.** Wash latex gloves before reuse
- **C.** Use suitable non-latex gloves
- **D.** Wear two pairs of latex gloves

<details><summary>Correct answer and detailed explanation</summary>

**A kyun galat hai:** Powdered latex products exposure aur badha sakte hain.

**B kyun galat hai:** Disposable gloves ko dhokar reuse karna safe glove practice nahi hai aur latex allergy ko solve nahi karta.

**C kyun sahi hai:** Non-latex material chunne se natural rubber protein ka contact kam hota hai. Gloves phir bhi procedure ke hisaab se appropriate hone chahiye.

**D kyun galat hai:** Do latex layers bhi allergen exposure ko remove nahi karti hain.

**Exam clue:** Confirmed latex allergy mein suitable alternative material choose karo.

### Q04. Which term describes difficulty digesting lactose without an allergic immune response?

- **A.** Contact sensitization
- **B.** Anaphylaxis
- **C.** Autoimmune disease
- **D.** Food intolerance

<details><summary>Correct answer and detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: D. Food intolerance**

**A kyun galat hai:** Contact sensitization kisi contact allergen ke against immune response se judi hoti hai.

**B kyun galat hai:** Anaphylaxis ek severe systemic allergic reaction hai, simple lactose digestion problem nahi.

**C kyun galat hai:** Autoimmune disease mein immune system apne tissues ko target karta hai.

**D kyun sahi hai:** Lactose intolerance mein milk sugar ko digest karne mein dikkat hoti hai. Is mechanism mein food protein ke against allergic immune response hona zaroori nahi hai.

**Exam clue:** Food intolerance aur food allergy ko ek hi diagnosis mat samjho.

### Q16. A patient with suspected anaphylaxis after an infusion is hypotensive, awake and has no respiratory distress. While emergency treatment is being given, which position is appropriate?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Priority:** High Yield | **Exam focus:** NORCET / HPRCA Staff Nurse | **Topic:** Allergy assessment, testing and exposure safety

- **A.** Standing to improve venous return
- **B.** Walking to assess exercise tolerance
- **C.** Sitting upright with legs dangling as a routine shock measure
- **D.** Supine, avoiding sudden standing

<details><summary>Correct answer and detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: D. Supine, avoiding sudden standing**

**A kyun galat hai:** Standing se blood lower body mein pool ho sakta hai aur shock worsen ho sakta hai.

**B kyun galat hai:** Walking unstable circulation mein unsafe hai aur treatment delay karegi.

**C kyun galat hai:** Legs dangling karne se venous return kam ho sakta hai, isliye uncomplicated hypotension mein ye routine position nahi hai.

**D kyun sahi hai:** Hypotension mein supine position circulation support karti hai. Patient ko achanak khada karna blood return aur kam kar sakta hai.

**Exam clue:** Shock mein posture ko breathing aur circulation ki current condition se match karo.

**Clinical reference:** [Source G](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7607509/)

</details>
