### Q01. Which skin change is characteristic of systemic sclerosis?

**Difficulty:** Easy | **Priority:** Important | **Exam focus:** NORCET / HPRCA Staff Nurse | **Topic:** Systemic sclerosis and Sjögren disease

- **A.** Thickening and tightening of skin
- **B.** Generalized loss of all skin pigment

- **C.** Painless honey-coloured crusts
- **D.** Dermatomal vesicles

<details><summary>Correct answer and detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: A. Thickening and tightening of skin**

**A kyun sahi hai:** Systemic sclerosis mein collagen jaise connective tissue proteins zaroorat se zyada jama ho sakte hain. Is process ko fibrosis kehte hain. Isse skin thick aur tight ho sakti hai aur kuch internal organs bhi affect ho sakte hain.

**B kyun galat hai:** Pigment loss vitiligo ka feature ho sakta hai, characteristic fibrosis nahi.

**C kyun galat hai:** Honey-coloured crust impetigo ka clue hai.

**D kyun galat hai:** Dermatomal vesicles herpes zoster suggest karte hain.

**Exam clue:** Sclerosis ka meaning tissue hardening ya thickening se connect karo.

**Clinical reference:** [Clinical guidance](https://academic.oup.com/rheumatology/article/63/11/2956/7750184)

</details>

### Q02. Which phenomenon causes episodic colour changes of the fingers after cold exposure in systemic sclerosis?

**Difficulty:** Easy | **Priority:** Important | **Exam focus:** NORCET / HPRCA Staff Nurse | **Topic:** Systemic sclerosis and Sjögren disease

- **A.** Trousseau sign
- **B.** Raynaud phenomenon

- **C.** Babinski response
- **D.** Kussmaul breathing

<details><summary>Correct answer and detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: B. Raynaud phenomenon**

**A kyun galat hai:** Trousseau sign hypocalcaemia ka clue hai.

**B kyun sahi hai:** Raynaud mein fingers ki chhoti blood vessels temporarily narrow ho jaati hain. Blood supply kam hone par fingers white ya blue ho sakti hain aur blood flow wapas aane par red ho sakti hain.

**C kyun galat hai:** Babinski plantar reflex assessment hai.

**D kyun galat hai:** Kussmaul breathing metabolic acidosis se related hai.

**Exam clue:** Cold-triggered digital colour change vascular spasm ka clue hai.

**Clinical reference:** [Clinical guidance](https://academic.oup.com/rheumatology/article/63/11/2956/7750184)

</details>

### Q13. A patient with diffuse systemic sclerosis develops severe headache, abrupt hypertension and rising serum creatinine. Which complication needs urgent assessment?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Priority:** Important | **Exam focus:** NORCET / HPRCA Staff Nurse | **Topic:** Systemic sclerosis and Sjögren disease

- **A.** Scleroderma renal crisis
- **B.** Stable uncomplicated Raynaud phenomenon

- **C.** Isolated dry mouth
- **D.** Chronic uncomplicated reflux

<details><summary>Correct answer and detailed explanation</summary>

**Correct answer: A. Scleroderma renal crisis**

**A kyun sahi hai:** Systemic sclerosis mein kidney ki chhoti blood vessels damage hone se BP achanak badh sakta hai aur acute kidney injury ho sakti hai. Headache, naya severe BP rise aur creatinine badhna renal crisis ke warning signs hain. Urgent medical assessment chahiye. Confirmed renal crisis mein specialist ACE inhibitor treatment shuru ya adjust karta hai.

**B kyun galat hai:** Raynaud digital circulation affect karta hai aur ye renal pattern explain nahi karta.

**C kyun galat hai:** Dry mouth hypertension aur AKI explain nahi karta.

**D kyun galat hai:** Reflux acute renal dysfunction ka expected cause nahi.

**Exam clue:** New severe hypertension plus AKI in systemic sclerosis is an emergency.

**Clinical reference:** [Clinical guidance](https://academic.oup.com/rheumatology/article/63/11/2956/7750184)

</details>
