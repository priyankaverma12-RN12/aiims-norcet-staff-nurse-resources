## Initial study explanations

### Anaphylaxis

Anaphylaxis mein allergic reaction airway ko narrow aur BP ko low kar sakti hai. IM epinephrine airway aur circulation ko support karti hai. Emergency team ko activate karna bhi zaroori hai.

### SLE

Lupus mein defense system kidney ko damage kar sakti hai. Urine mein protein aur BP badhna kidney involvement ke clues hain. Nurse renal assessment arrange karti hai.

### Immunosuppression

Immunosuppressants rejection rokne ke liye defense system ka response kam karti hain. Infection jaldi serious ho sakti hai. Nurse fever ko promptly report karti hai.

### Immunity

Vaccine immune system ko antigen pehchanne ka chance deti hai. Memory cells ban sakti hain, jisse future exposure par faster response mil sakta hai.
