### Q1. Which parasite causes invasive human amoebiasis?

**Difficulty:** Easy | **Priority:** Medium | **Topic:** Amoebiasis — Clinical Nursing | **Type:** Original practice

- **A.** Plasmodium falciparum
- **B.** Enterobius vermicularis
- **C.** Entamoeba histolytica
- **D.** Giardia duodenalis

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: C. Entamoeba histolytica**

- **A.** Plasmodium malaria ka cause hai.
- **B.** Enterobius pinworm infection karta hai.
- **C.** E histolytica intestinal aur extraintestinal invasive disease cause kar sakti hai.
- **D.** Giardia usually small-intestinal infection karta hai.

**Exam clue:** Histo means tissue; histolytica can invade tissue.

</details>

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: D. Species-specific testing may be needed to identify pathogenic E histolytica**

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: B. Urgent reassessment for alternative diagnosis, complications and whether drainage or other management is indicated**
