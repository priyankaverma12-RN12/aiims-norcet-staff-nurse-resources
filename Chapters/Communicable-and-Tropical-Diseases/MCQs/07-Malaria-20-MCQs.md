### Q1. Which human blood cells are invaded by the erythrocytic stage of malaria parasites?

**Difficulty:** Easy | **Priority:** Medium | **Topic:** Malaria — Clinical Nursing | **Type:** Original practice

- **A.** Megakaryocytes
- **B.** Red blood cells
- **C.** Platelets
- **D.** Eosinophils

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: B. Red blood cells**

- **A.** Megakaryocytes platelets produce karte hain.
- **B.** Blood-stage parasite RBC ke andar multiply karta hai.
- **C.** Platelets clotting mein help karte hain, principal host nahi hain.
- **D.** Eosinophils immune cells hain, erythrocytic target nahi.

**Exam clue:** Erythrocytic means red-cell stage.
