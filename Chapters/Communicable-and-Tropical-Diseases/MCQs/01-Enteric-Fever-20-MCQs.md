### Q1. Which organism causes classical typhoid fever?

**Difficulty:** Easy | **Priority:** Medium | **Topic:** Enteric Fever — Clinical Nursing | **Type:** Original practice

- **A.** Shigella dysenteriae
- **B.** Vibrio cholerae
- **C.** Salmonella enterica serovar Typhi
- **D.** Salmonella Enteritidis

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: C. Salmonella enterica serovar Typhi**

- **A.** Shigella dysentery ka cause hai.
- **B.** Vibrio cholerae secretory diarrhoea karta hai.
- **C.** Typhi human-adapted Salmonella hai jo systemic enteric fever cause karta hai.
- **D.** Enteritidis usually nontyphoidal gastroenteritis karta hai.

**Exam clue:** Typhi means typhoid.
