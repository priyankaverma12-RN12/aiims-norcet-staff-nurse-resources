### Q1. What type of organism causes shigellosis?

**Difficulty:** Easy | **Priority:** Medium | **Topic:** Shigellosis — Clinical Nursing | **Type:** Original practice

- **A.** Bacterium
- **B.** Helminth
- **C.** Virus
- **D.** Fungus

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: A. Bacterium**

- **A.** Shigella gram-negative bacterial pathogen hai.
- **B.** Helminth multicellular parasitic worm hota hai.
- **C.** Virus shigellosis ka cause nahi hai.
- **D.** Fungal infection se shigellosis nahi hoti.

**Exam clue:** Shigella is bacterial.
