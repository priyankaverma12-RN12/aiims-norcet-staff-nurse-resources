### Q1. Which virus genus includes chikungunya virus?

**Difficulty:** Easy | **Priority:** Medium | **Topic:** Chikungunya — Clinical Nursing | **Type:** Original practice

- **A.** Flavivirus
- **B.** Enterovirus
- **C.** Morbillivirus
- **D.** Alphavirus

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: D. Alphavirus**

- **A.** Dengue flavivirus hai, chikungunya nahi.
- **B.** Enterovirus different virus group hai.
- **C.** Morbillivirus measles se related hai.
- **D.** Chikungunya Togaviridae family ka alphavirus hai.

**Exam clue:** Chikungunya is an alphavirus.
