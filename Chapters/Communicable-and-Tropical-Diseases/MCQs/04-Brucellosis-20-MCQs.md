### Q1. Which genus causes human brucellosis?

**Difficulty:** Easy | **Priority:** Medium | **Topic:** Brucellosis — Clinical Nursing | **Type:** Original practice

- **A.** Brucella
- **B.** Borrelia
- **C.** Bordetella
- **D.** Bacillus

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: A. Brucella**

- **A.** Brucella species zoonotic bacterial infection cause karti hain.
- **B.** Borrelia relapsing fever ya Lyme disease se related hai.
- **C.** Bordetella pertussis cause karta hai.
- **D.** Bacillus genus alag organisms contain karta hai.

**Exam clue:** Brucellosis is bacterial zoonosis.
