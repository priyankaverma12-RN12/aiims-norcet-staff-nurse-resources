### Q1. Which symptom commonly accompanies the febrile phase of dengue?

**Difficulty:** Easy | **Priority:** Medium | **Topic:** Dengue — Clinical Nursing | **Type:** Original practice

- **A.** Isolated chronic heartburn
- **B.** Painless urinary retention
- **C.** Pain behind the eyes
- **D.** Painless progressive deafness

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: C. Pain behind the eyes**

- **A.** Heartburn alone dengue ka characteristic sign nahi hai.
- **B.** Urinary retention usual presenting symptom nahi hai.

- **D.** Deafness typical febrile dengue feature nahi hai.

**Exam clue:** Retro-orbital pain is a classical clue.
