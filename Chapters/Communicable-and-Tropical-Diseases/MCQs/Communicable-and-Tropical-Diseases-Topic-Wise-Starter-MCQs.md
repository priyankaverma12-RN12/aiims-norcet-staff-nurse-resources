## Dengue

### Q1. A patient with dengue develops severe abdominal pain, persistent vomiting and rising hematocrit as fever subsides. What is the concern?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Topic:** Dengue

- **A.** Plasma leakage and progression to severe dengue
- **B.** Definite recovery because fever decreased
- **C.** Uncomplicated iron deficiency
- **D.** A reason to give aspirin independently

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: A. Plasma leakage and progression to severe dengue**

Dengue mein fever girne ke aas-paas fluid blood vessels se leak ho sakta hai. Hematocrit badh sakta hai aur shock ka risk hota hai. Warning signs par urgent review chahiye.

**Baaki options kyun galat hain?**

- **B.** Fever kam hona recovery guarantee nahi karta.
- **C.** Iron deficiency rising hematocrit pattern nahi deti.
- **D.** Aspirin bleeding risk badha sakti hai.

</details>

## Malaria

### Q2. A febrile patient from a malaria-endemic area develops altered consciousness. What is the appropriate priority?

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Topic:** Malaria

- **A.** Treat as simple fever without assessment
- **B.** Delay testing until fever follows a perfect periodic pattern
- **C.** Use antibiotics alone without clinical review
- **D.** Urgent evaluation for severe malaria and other causes of encephalopathy

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: D. Urgent evaluation for severe malaria and other causes of encephalopathy**

Malaria mein parasites RBC ko infect karte hain. Severe disease brain ko affect kar sakti hai. Altered consciousness ke saath urgent glucose, airway aur diagnostic assessment chahiye.

**Baaki options kyun galat hain?**

- **A.** Simple fever assume karna dangerous hai.
- **B.** Fever periodic hona zaroori nahi hai.
- **C.** Malaria ke liye specific treatment chahiye.

</details>

## Rabies exposure

### Q3. A person presents after a potentially rabid dog bite. Which immediate local measure is recommended?

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Topic:** Rabies exposure

- **A.** Wait for neurological symptoms before seeking care
- **B.** Cover the wound without cleaning it
- **C.** Wash the wound thoroughly with soap and running water and seek post-exposure assessment
- **D.** Seal the wound with an irritant paste

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: C. Wash the wound thoroughly with soap and running water and seek post-exposure assessment**

Rabies virus bite wound se body mein enter kar sakta hai. Achhi tarah wash karne se virus ki quantity kam ho sakti hai. Vaccine aur immunoglobulin ki need exposure category se assess hoti hai.

**Baaki options kyun galat hain?**

- **A.** Symptoms ka wait bahut dangerous hai.
- **B.** Cleaning important first aid hai.
- **D.** Irritant paste tissue damage kar sakti hai.

</details>

## HIV

### Q4. A patient with HIV asks whether routine social contact spreads the virus. Which explanation is correct?

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Topic:** HIV

- **A.** HIV spreads through sharing clean utensils
- **B.** HIV is not transmitted through ordinary touching or sharing a room
- **C.** HIV routinely spreads through intact skin contact
- **D.** All people with HIV require airborne isolation

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: B. HIV is not transmitted through ordinary touching or sharing a room**

HIV specific body fluids ke exposure se spread hota hai. Haath milane aur saath baithne se spread nahi hota. Nurse patient ko respectful care aur standard precautions deti hai.

**Baaki options kyun galat hain?**

- **A.** Clean utensils share karna HIV transmission route nahi hai.
- **C.** Intact skin casual contact se HIV nahi spread hota.
- **D.** HIV ke liye routine airborne isolation nahi chahiye.

</details>
