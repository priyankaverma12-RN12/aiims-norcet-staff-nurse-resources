### Q1. Which organisms are responsible for epidemic cholera?

**Difficulty:** Easy | **Priority:** Medium | **Topic:** Cholera — Clinical Nursing | **Type:** Original practice

- **A.** Shigella sonnei
- **B.** Toxigenic Vibrio cholerae O1 or O139
- **C.** Vibrio parahaemolyticus alone
- **D.** Salmonella Typhi

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: B. Toxigenic Vibrio cholerae O1 or O139**

- **A.** Shigella inflammatory diarrhoea karta hai.

- **C.** Parahaemolyticus alag gastroenteritis cause hai.

### Q3. What is the principal mechanism of watery diarrhoea in cholera?

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Priority:** High | **Topic:** Cholera — Clinical Nursing | **Type:** Original practice

- **A.** Toxin-mediated chloride and water secretion
- **B.** Invasion with extensive colonic ulceration
- **C.** Complete intestinal obstruction
- **D.** Loss of gastric acid alone

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: A. Toxin-mediated chloride and water secretion**

- **A.** Cholera toxin cAMP signalling badhakar chloride aur water secretion karta hai.
- **B.** Cholera primarily secretory diarrhoea hai, destructive colitis nahi.

- **D.** Gastric acid loss secretory mechanism explain nahi karta.

**Exam clue:** Secretory diarrhoea can be massive without blood.
