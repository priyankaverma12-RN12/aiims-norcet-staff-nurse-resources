### Q1. Which virus family includes Japanese encephalitis virus?

**Difficulty:** Easy | **Priority:** Medium | **Topic:** Japanese Encephalitis — Clinical Nursing | **Type:** Original practice

- **A.** Orthomyxoviridae
- **B.** Retroviridae
- **C.** Flaviviridae
- **D.** Herpesviridae

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: C. Flaviviridae**

- **A.** Orthomyxoviridae influenza viruses se associated hai.
- **B.** Retroviridae mein HIV jaise viruses hote hain.
- **C.** JEV flavivirus hai, dengue aur West Nile viruses ka related organism hai.
- **D.** Herpesviridae HSV jaise viruses ki family hai.

**Exam clue:** JE is a mosquito-borne flavivirus.
