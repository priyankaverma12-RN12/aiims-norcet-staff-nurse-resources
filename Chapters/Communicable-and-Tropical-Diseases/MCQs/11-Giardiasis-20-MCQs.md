### Q1. Which parasite causes giardiasis?

**Difficulty:** Easy | **Priority:** Medium | **Topic:** Giardiasis — Clinical Nursing | **Type:** Original practice

- **A.** Trichuris trichiura
- **B.** Cryptosporidium parvum
- **C.** Giardia duodenalis
- **D.** Entamoeba histolytica

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: C. Giardia duodenalis**

- **A.** Trichuris whipworm hai.
- **B.** Cryptosporidium alag protozoal diarrhoea cause karta hai.
- **C.** Giardia small intestine mein colonize karke giardiasis cause karta hai.
- **D.** Entamoeba invasive amoebiasis cause kar sakta hai.

**Exam clue:** Giardia is also called G. lamblia or intestinalis.
