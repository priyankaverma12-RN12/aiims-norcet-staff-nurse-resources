# Surgical energy, fire and smoke safety

Monopolar electrosurgery mein active tip par current concentrated hota hai aur return electrode se generator tak circuit complete hota hai. Bipolar forceps mein local tissue ke through dono tips ke beech current pass hota hai. Nurse compatible accessories, intact insulation, correct contact aur safe storage verify karti hai.

Fire triangle mein fuel, ignition aur oxidizer hote hain. Open oxygen aur energy-device activation ko team coordinate karti hai. Laser aur electrosurgery se plume generate ho sakta hai. Local smoke evacuation source ke paas capture karne mein help karti hai. Device filter aur maintenance instructions follow karna important hai.
