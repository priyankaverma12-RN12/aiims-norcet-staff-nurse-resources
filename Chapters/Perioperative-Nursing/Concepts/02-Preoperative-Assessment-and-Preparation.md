# Preoperative Assessment and Preparation

Jab patient elective surgery ke liye aata hai, toh preoperative nurse sirf operation ka form complete nahi karti. Woh patient ki current condition aur existing health problems ko samajh kar risk identify karti hai. Preadmission assessment aur day-of-surgery assessment ka comparison important hota hai, kyunki patient ki condition beech mein change ho sakti hai.

## Consent aur communication

Patient ko surgery ke benefits, risks aur relevant alternatives samajhne ka mauka milna chahiye. Responsible surgeon procedure-specific informed consent explain karta hai. Nurse patient ki understanding mein gap notice kare toh concern communicate karti hai. Decision-capable patient apni consent withdraw bhi kar sakta hai. Language barrier ho toh qualified interpreter arrange karna useful hai, taaki complex medical information patient ko accurately samajh aaye.

## Medicines, fasting aur clinical readiness

Patient se current medicines, last dose, allergy aur previous anesthesia experience ki details lo. Diabetes medicines aur anticoagulants ka perioperative plan individualized hota hai. Nurse bina verified order ke unko blanket continue ya stop nahi karti. Fasting instructions breach ho jayein toh actual food ya drink ki type aur time document karo. Anesthesia team risk aur next step decide karti hai. New abnormal lab result ya acute chest symptoms milne par patient ki current stability assess karke timely escalation zaroori hai.

## Baseline assessment aur physical preparation

Pehle se weakness, implanted device, mobility issue ya removable appliance ho toh record mein clear information rakho. Operation ke baad comparison ke liye baseline useful hota hai. Tight jewelry swelling ke saath tissue injury cause kar sakti hai, isliye facility policy ke hisaab se safe removal plan banao. Routine razor shaving avoid karo. Hair removal necessary ho toh approved clipping method follow karo. Agar bowel preparation ke baad dizziness ya dehydration ka clue mile, toh additional preparation se pehle clinical review chahiye.

## Teaching aur handover

Patient ko expected postoperative care ke baare mein simple language mein batao. Jab nurse breathing exercise teach karti hai, toh return demonstration se patient ki understanding check ho sakti hai. Pain management aur supported coughing ka purpose samjhana patient ko recovery mein participate karne mein madad karta hai. Handover ke waqt current abnormal findings, relevant medicines aur specific patient concerns receiving team ko actively communicate karo.
