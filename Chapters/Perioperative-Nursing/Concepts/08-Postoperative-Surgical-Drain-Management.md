# Noncardiac surgical drains

Drain ka purpose selected operative spaces se fluid remove karna hota hai. Passive Penrose aur active vacuum devices ka mechanism alag hai. Drain type aur order identify karo, kyunki suction restore karne ya removal ka method device-specific hota hai. Multiple drains ke outputs individually record karo.

Moving patient mein reservoir support aur tubing alignment check karo. Dislodged drain ko blind reinsert nahi karna chahiye. Site protect karke team ko notify karo. Locking pigtail aur unexpected resistance mein trained operator ko device-specific instructions follow karni chahiye. Removal ke baad intactness aur site assessment document karo. Home care mein patient ya caregiver ki return demonstration aur warning signs clarify karo.
