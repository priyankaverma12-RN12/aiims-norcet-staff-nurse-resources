# Suture materials, needles and closure safety

Suture ko origin, absorption, filament configuration aur diameter ke according classify karte hain. Polyglactin braided absorbable hai; poliglecaprone aur polydioxanone absorbable monofilaments hain; polypropylene aur conventional nylon nonabsorbable monofilaments hain. Zero-series mein higher number smaller diameter indicate karta hai. Material memory handling affect karti hai. Tissue healing duration aur tension ke hisaab se support choose hoti hai.

Swage needle aur thread ka junction hai. Reverse-cutting needle outer arc edge rakhti hai; taper-point needle soft tissues spread kar sakti hai. Needle holder grasp aur suture integrity verify karo. Running aur interrupted closures ki mechanical failure patterns different hain. Superficial closure deep dead space ko automatically close nahi karti. Pale wound edges aur damaged closure material report karo. Actual choice surgeon aur device guidance se confirm hoti hai.
