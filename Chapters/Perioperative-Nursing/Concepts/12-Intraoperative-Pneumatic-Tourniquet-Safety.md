# Intraoperative pneumatic tourniquet safety

Selected limb surgery mein tourniquet blood flow temporarily reduce karta hai. Clinical justification aur baseline limb assessment pehle karo. Cuff size, contour aur even padding appropriate honi chahiye. Skin preparation solution ko cuff ke neeche collect hone se prevent karo. Pressure individualize karo aur actual inflation duration monitor karo. BOAST specific time thresholds aur reminders define karta hai, lekin clinical decision individual risk par depend karta hai.

Inflation ke dauran pain aur circulatory signs communicate karo. Release anesthesia aur surgeon ke saath coordinate karo. Skin, limb aur vital signs reassess karo. Small digital tourniquets ki removal tracking important hai. Suspected burn ya nerve injury ko timely report karo aur approved documentation preserve karo.
