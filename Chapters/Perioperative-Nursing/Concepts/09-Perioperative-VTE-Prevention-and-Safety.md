# Perioperative VTE prevention and safety

Surgery ke baad immobility, tissue injury aur patient-specific risk se venous clot ka risk badh sakta hai. Mechanical compression venous return support karti hai. Stockings ka correct fit, arterial suitability aur regular skin assessment important hain. IPC sleeve present hone se actual functioning prove nahi hoti. Patient ki mobility aur bleeding risk ke hisaab se prevention plan review karo.

ASH conditional recommendations ko universal rules nahi samjho. Neuraxial catheter ke saath anticoagulant timing drug-specific hoti hai. New back pain aur progressive weakness urgent evaluation require karte hain. VTE prophylaxis risk kam karti hai, eliminate nahi karti. New symptoms aur unusual bleeding promptly report karo. Discharge par prescribed duration aur safety instructions teach-back se confirm karo.
