# Postoperative fascial complications and wound collections

Deep fascia ki closure skin se alag layer hai. Skin closed hone par bhi new bulge, popping sensation ya abnormal drainage deeper problem indicate kar sakti hai. Intact staples ko unauthorized remove mat karo. Stable suspected defect mein surgeon imaging choose kar sakta hai. Peritoneal signs, obstruction ya deterioration urgent review require karte hain.

Seroma dead space mein serous fluid collection hai, hematoma blood collection hai. Size, symptoms, wound impact aur infection signs management guide karte hain. Small asymptomatic seroma observation ke liye suitable ho sakti hai. Implant ke paas aspiration special risks rakhti hai. New wound plan, constipation prevention aur warning-sign education recovery mein important hain.
