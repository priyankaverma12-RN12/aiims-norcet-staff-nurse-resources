# Perioperative thermal management

Anesthesia ke baad vasodilation core heat ko peripheral tissues mein redistribute kar sakti hai. Skin exposure aur unwarmed fluids bhi heat loss contribute karte hain. Validated core temperature method use karo aur peripheral discrepancy assess karo. NICE CG65 adult pathway mein risk assessment, prewarming aur phase-specific monitoring important hain. Clinical urgency ko elective threshold se unnecessarily delay nahi karna chahiye.

Active warming heat transfer karti hai aur passive insulation heat loss reduce karti hai. IV fluid aur irrigation warming targets alag hain. Approved equipment use karo aur patient response ke hisaab se warming adjust karo. PACU transfer aur ward warming mein temperature criteria aur reassessment continue rakho.
