# Surgical item accounting and retention prevention

Count ka purpose items ki accurate inventory maintain karna hai. Physical separation aur audible two-person verification packaging errors detect karne mein help karte hain. New items ko running record mein add karo. Interrupted count restart karo. Countable items jo sterile field se remove ho jayein unko safe containment aur accounting mein maintain karo. Component parts aur device integrity inspect karo.

Accountable throat pack aur intentionally placed packing ka specific status communicate karo. Internal cavity closure, reopening aur staff relief policy-based verification points hain. Matching count retained item ko absolutely exclude nahi karta. Surgeon wound exploration aur indicated imaging additional defenses hain. Adjunct technology ko approved scope ke according use karo. Emergency exceptions aur subsequent actions accurately document karo.
