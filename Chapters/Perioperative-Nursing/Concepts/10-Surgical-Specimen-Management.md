# Surgical specimen management

Specimen handling collection se diagnostic report tak continuous process hai. Routine histology ke liye approved fixative aur suitable container use karo. Frozen section, culture, flow cytometry aur reimplantable tissue ka pathway alag hai. Special request ko fixative add karne se pehle clarify karo. Tissue ko crush ya distort mat karo. Margin orientation aur fixation time accurately record karo.

Transfer se pehle secure labeling, hazard communication aur clean secondary containment check karo. Temporarily retained specimens ko track karo. Time-sensitive dispatch ka actual laboratory receipt confirm karo. Barcode unavailable ho toh approved manual traceability log use karo. Missing material ya canceled order ko lab aur clinical team ke saath reconcile karo.
