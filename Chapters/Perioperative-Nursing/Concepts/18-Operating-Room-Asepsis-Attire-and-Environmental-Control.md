# OR asepsis, attire and environmental control

Surgical attire, hair containment, appropriate masks aur clean personal items theatre environment protect karne mein help karte hain. Personal phones aur ID items bhi contamination transfer kar sakte hain. Door openings aur unnecessary movement limit karo. Field ko use ke near prepare karo. Delay mein approved covering, actual timestamps aur facility policy follow karo. Universal safe duration assume mat karo.

HVAC control engineering aur infection prevention ke saath coordinate hota hai. Baseline aur occupied-room assessment complementary information dete hain. Closed cart mein unused packs ka actual handling history aur barrier integrity verify karo. Major contamination event ke baad correction aur wound classification reassessment document karo.
