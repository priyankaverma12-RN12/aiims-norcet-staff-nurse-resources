# Postoperative pulmonary risk and recovery

ARISCAT pulmonary complication risk estimate karta hai, diagnosis nahi. Recent respiratory infection, anemia aur incision site planning mein relevant hain. Anesthesia aur posture lung volume affect kar sakte hain. Collapse se perfused but unventilated lung region shunt create kar sakta hai. Airway obstruction, external compression aur gas absorption different mechanisms hain.

I COUGH care mein oral hygiene aur patient understanding bhi important hain. High-risk patient ka plan ward tak handover karo. New wheeze ya persistent focal signs ko assess karo. Preoperative physiotherapy evidence selected abdominal surgery patients mein relevant hai. Routine prophylactic CPAP aur indicated respiratory support ko confuse mat karo.
