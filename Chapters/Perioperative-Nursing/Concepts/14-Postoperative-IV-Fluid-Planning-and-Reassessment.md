# Postoperative IV fluid planning and reassessment

NICE five Rs: resuscitation, routine maintenance, replacement, redistribution aur reassessment. Routine maintenance aur abnormal losses separately assess karo. Oral ya enteral route sufficient ho toh IV indication review karo. Ongoing gastric losses ka amount aur nature document karo. Edema alone circulating volume establish nahi karti. Complex redistribution mein expert help lo.

Maintenance estimates uncomplicated adults ke liye starting point hain. Frailty, obesity, cardiac aur renal impairment ke saath adjustment required ho sakta hai. Maintenance glucose complete nutrition nahi hai. Chloride-rich infusion ke saath chloride aur acid-base changes review karo. Oliguria alone automatic bolus order ka reason nahi. Each intervention ke baad response aur adverse effects assess karo. Handover mein indication, delivered volumes, losses, monitoring aur next review include karo.
