# Spinal, epidural anesthesia and neuraxial safety

Spinal injection subarachnoid CSF mein hoti hai aur epidural medicine dura ke bahar deliver hoti hai. Adult cord commonly L1–L2 ke around terminate karti hai. Local anesthetics sodium channel block karte hain. Baricity aur posture intrathecal spread influence kar sakte hain. Combined spinal-epidural rapid onset aur continuing catheter analgesia combine karta hai.

Differential block mein pressure perception remain kar sakti hai. Ascending weakness, respiratory compromise aur marked circulatory change urgent anesthesia assessment require karte hain. Neuraxial opioid respiratory monitoring motor recovery se alag hai. Postural headache CSF leak suggest kar sakta hai, lekin changing pattern ya neurological signs urgent differential assessment require karte hain. Delayed fever, insertion-site pain aur sphincter dysfunction urgent warning signs hain. Trained staff, dedicated connectors aur line verification safety support karte hain.
