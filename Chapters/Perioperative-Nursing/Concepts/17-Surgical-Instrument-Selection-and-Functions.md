# Surgical instrument selection and functions

Cutting instruments mein scalpel aur scissors aate hain. No. 3 aur No. 7 handles conventional small blade group ke liye hain, jabki No. 4 large group ke liye hai. Straight Mayo suture cutting aur curved Mayo heavy tissue cutting ke liye commonly used hai. Metzenbaum delicate dissection ke liye preserve karo.

Babcock delicate tubular tissue, DeBakey vascular tissue aur Kocher tough tissue ke liye different gripping designs provide karte hain. Pean vessel occlusion aur Mixter angled access ke liye use ho sakta hai. Army-Navy superficial exposure, Deaver deep exposure aur malleable retractor adaptable contour provide karta hai. Mayo-Hegar needle control karta hai. Scrub nurse tissue type, requested task aur compatibility verify karti hai.
