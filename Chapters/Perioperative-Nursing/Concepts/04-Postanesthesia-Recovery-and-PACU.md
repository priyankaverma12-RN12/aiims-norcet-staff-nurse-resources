# Postanesthesia recovery and PACU

**Candidate learning guide — independent review pending.**

## Recovery assessment

PACU mein airway, breathing, circulation, consciousness, temperature, pain, nausea aur ongoing support assess karte hain. Phase I immediate higher-acuity recovery hai aur phase II lower-acuity recovery/discharge preparation hai. Care destination patient ki support requirement par depend karta hai.

## Monitoring distinctions

SpO2 oxygenation batata hai. Supplemental oxygen ke saath low ventilation initially mask ho sakti hai. Capnography aur respiratory assessment additional information dete hain. Quantitative neuromuscular monitoring mein adductor pollicis par TOF ratio 0.9 ya usse zyada confirm karna extubation assessment ka important component hai. Yeh baaki extubation criteria replace nahi karta.

## Comfort and safety

Pain self-report ko respect karo. Nonverbal patients mein appropriate validated observational tool use karo. Analgesia ke baad benefit aur adverse effects reassess karo. Repeated vomiting mein hydration aur losses assess karo. Measured hypothermia mein approved warming aur reassessment use karo. Acute inattention ko hypoactive delirium ke liye assess karo.

## Transfer and discharge

Residual regional block mein protective sensation aur motor function assess karo. Assisted mobilization aur fall prevention maintain karo. Transfer scores clinical judgment ka replacement nahi hain. New abnormality ko reassess karo aur authorized clinician ke plan ke according transfer karo. Oral fluids ka requirement patient-specific hai.
