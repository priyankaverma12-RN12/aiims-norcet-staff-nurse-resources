# Anesthesia sedation depth and intraoperative monitoring

Minimal sedation mein normal verbal response rehta hai. Moderate sedation mein light stimulation se purposeful response expected hai. Deep sedation mein repeated ya painful stimulation needed ho sakti hai aur airway assistance required ho sakti hai. General anesthesia mein painful stimulation se bhi arousal nahi hota. Reflex withdrawal purposeful response nahi hai. MAC service type hai, sedation depth nahi. Intended level se deeper response mein qualified rescue readiness chahiye.

Inspired gas oxygen analyzer aur patient SpO2 monitor different information dete hain. ECG electrical rhythm show karta hai, effective perfusion separately assess karo. Circuit disconnection alarm mechanical ventilation safety layer hai. Clinical respiratory assessment aur indicated expired CO2 monitoring maintain karo. Airway-device placement confirm karo aur conflicting monitor findings patient examination ke saath reconcile karo. Actual monitoring limitations aur justified exceptions document karo.
