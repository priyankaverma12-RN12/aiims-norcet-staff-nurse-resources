# Postoperative bowel recovery

Surgery ke baad bowel propulsion temporarily slow ho sakti hai. Ileus functional motility problem hai, mechanical obstruction mein physical blockage hoti hai. Dono mein distension aur vomiting overlap kar sakte hain. New severe pain, peritoneal signs ya deterioration mein urgent evaluation chahiye.

Stable selected colorectal patients mein enhanced recovery pathway early feeding as tolerated, safe mobilization aur individualized opioid-sparing pain management support karta hai. Routine postoperative NG tube har patient ke liye required nahi hai. Ordered therapeutic decompression symptom relief de sakti hai, lekin underlying motility recovery separately assess hoti hai.

Anastomotic leak mein tachycardia, fever aur increasing pain concerning ho sakte hain. CRP trend clinical assessment ke saath interpret hota hai. Diverting stoma leak ko exclude nahi karta. Nurse actual symptoms, intake, output aur changes communicate karti hai.
