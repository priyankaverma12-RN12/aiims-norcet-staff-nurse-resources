# Ambulatory surgery home readiness and safety

Same-day discharge ka matlab complete cognitive recovery nahi. Residual anesthesia effects ke dauran driving, hazardous household tasks, alcohol aur important decisions avoid karne ki written advice chahiye. Caregiver aur safe transport plan verify karo. PADSS home-readiness assessment support karta hai, lekin individual procedure restrictions replace nahi karta. Peripheral block aur neuraxial motor recovery criteria alag hain. Prescribed analgesia block offset se pehle plan karo.

Nurse-led discharge trained and authorized pathway se hoti hai. Unmet criteria ke liye unplanned admission ka plan hona chahiye. Discharge summary, urgent contact aur planned follow-up verify karo. Community service availability aur emergency care access bhi day-case selection mein relevant hain.
