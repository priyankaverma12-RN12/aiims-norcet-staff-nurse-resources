## Initial study explanations

### Preoperative safety

Wrong-site surgery se serious harm ho sakta hai. Jab patient aur record mein difference ho, toh nurse process rok kar surgical team se identity, procedure aur side confirm karwati hai.

### PACU

Opioid brain ke breathing control ko slow kar sakta hai. Patient ki saans aur alertness gir rahi hai, isliye airway aur ventilation support pehle chahiye.

### Postoperative wound

Wound khulkar organs bahar aa jaayein toh evisceration kehte hain. Nurse tissues ko sterile moist dressing se protect karti hai aur emergency help leti hai. Organs ko andar force nahi karna chahiye.

### Anesthesia

Kuch susceptible patients mein anesthesia drugs se muscles ka metabolism bahut tez ho sakta hai. CO2 badhna aur rigidity early warning hain. Team emergency malignant hyperthermia protocol follow karti hai.
