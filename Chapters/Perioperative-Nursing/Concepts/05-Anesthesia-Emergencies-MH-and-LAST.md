# Anesthesia emergencies: MH and LAST

Malignant hyperthermia mein susceptible patient ki skeletal muscle calcium regulation trigger exposure ke baad disturb ho sakti hai. Muscle activity aur metabolism bahut badh jate hain, isliye CO2, rigidity, acidosis aur temperature changes ko team urgent assess karti hai. MHAUS crisis pathway trigger withdrawal, emergency assistance, oxygenation, dantrolene aur complication monitoring ko coordinate karta hai. Formulation-specific preparation verify karna important hai. Initial response ke baad recurrence aur renal risk ke liye monitoring chahiye.

LAST mein local anesthetic bloodstream mein excessive concentration tak pahunch sakta hai. CNS symptoms jaise tinnitus aur perioral numbness ya cardiac instability develop ho sakti hai. Classic symptoms hamesha sequential nahi hote. ASRA-specific rescue pathway aur lipid emulsion readiness important hai. Nurse cumulative exposure, timely escalation aur ongoing monitoring mein team ko support karti hai.
