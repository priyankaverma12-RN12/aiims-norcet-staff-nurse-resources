# Surgical item accounting evidence

20 new original MCQs and 80 individual rationales. WHO NCBI objective on retained items (lines 44–81), ACS 2016 statement, AORN February 2026 public FAQ and AHRQ PSNet 2025 primer directly retrieved. Foundations bank 25 Q15–17 addresses contamination, count purpose and generic incorrect-count response; those learning tasks were not deliberately repeated. The bank focuses on pack errors, added items, count interruptions, nested closure, accountability, fragment integrity, reopening, handover, emergency exception, imaging context and adjuncts. Some related concepts require full cross-bank semantic assessment. No proprietary full AORN guideline, historical question or exact video credit. Independent review and all release gates pending.
