### Q1. Which three elements form the surgical fire triangle?

**Difficulty:** Easy | **Priority:** IMPORTANT | **Exam focus:** NORCET / HPRCA HP Staff Nurse original practice

- **A.** Pressure, humidity and light
- **B.** Water, smoke and carbon dioxide
- **C.** Oxidizer, ignition source and fuel
- **D.** Electricity, sound and temperature

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: C. Oxidizer, ignition source and fuel**

- **A.** Ye environmental conditions hain, fire triangle ke necessary components nahi hain.

- **B.** Smoke combustion ka product ho sakta hai, water aur CO2 fire control mein useful ho sakte hain.

- **C.** Oxygen jaise oxidizer combustion support karte hain, ignition heat deti hai aur fuel jal sakta hai. Prevention mein inke interaction ko control karte hain.

- **D.** Electricity ignition contribute kar sakti hai, lekin sound fuel ya oxidizer nahi hai.

**Exam clue:** Fire risk assess karte waqt triangle ke teen components identify karo.
