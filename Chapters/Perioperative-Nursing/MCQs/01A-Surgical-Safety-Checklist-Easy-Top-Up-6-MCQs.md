### Q1. At which point is the WHO checklist Sign In performed?

**Difficulty:** Easy | **Priority:** IMPORTANT | **Exam focus:** NORCET / HPRCA HP Staff Nurse original practice

- **A.** Before induction of anesthesia
- **B.** After the first incision
- **C.** After the patient returns to the ward
- **D.** During postoperative clinic follow-up

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: A. Before induction of anesthesia**

- **A.** Sign In anesthesia start hone se pehle safety requirements verify karta hai.

- **B.** Incision se pehle Time Out hota hai, Sign In earlier stage hai.

- **C.** Ward arrival checklist ka operative Sign In stage nahi hai.

- **D.** Clinic follow-up surgical safety pause nahi hai.

**Exam clue:** Sign In precedes induction.

**Clinical reference:** [Clinical source I](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/NBK143244/)

### Q2. Which WHO checklist phase is the full-team pause immediately before incision?

**Difficulty:** Easy | **Priority:** IMPORTANT | **Exam focus:** NORCET / HPRCA HP Staff Nurse original practice

- **A.** Sign Out
- **B.** Ward discharge
- **C.** Time Out
- **D.** Preadmission registration

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: C. Time Out**

- **A.** Sign Out operation complete hone ke around hota hai.

- **B.** Discharge care transition hai, pre-incision safety phase nahi.

- **C.** Time Out incision se just pehle team ke shared verification ke liye hota hai.

- **D.** Registration administrative step hai, full-team operative pause nahi.

**Exam clue:** Time Out precedes incision.

### Q3. By which point should WHO Sign Out be completed?

**Difficulty:** Easy | **Priority:** IMPORTANT | **Exam focus:** NORCET / HPRCA HP Staff Nurse original practice

- **A.** After hospital discharge
- **B.** Before the patient leaves the operating room
- **C.** At the next day's operating list
- **D.** Only at the first clinic visit

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: B. Before the patient leaves the operating room**

- **A.** Itna late review receiving care team ko timely information nahi dega.

- **B.** Sign Out actual operation aur recovery concerns verify karta hai before transfer; surgeon room leave kare usse pehle complete karo.

- **C.** Next list current patient ki safety pause replace nahi karti.

- **D.** Clinic review immediate operative handover se different hai.

**Exam clue:** Sign Out precedes OR exit.

**Clinical reference:** [Clinical source O](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/NBK143248/)

### Q5. In the WHO 2009 checklist, what adult anticipated blood-loss threshold is explicitly queried at Sign In?

**Difficulty:** Easy | **Priority:** IMPORTANT | **Exam focus:** NORCET / HPRCA HP Staff Nurse original practice

- **A.** More than 500 mL
- **B.** More than 50 mL
- **C.** More than 5 L
- **D.** Exactly 10 mL in every adult

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: A. More than 500 mL**

- **A.** Adult threshold 500 mL se greater hai. Patient-specific risk aur lower tolerable loss separately assess karte hain.

- **B.** Yeh specified adult checklist threshold nahi hai.

- **C.** Itna high threshold clinically significant earlier loss risk miss karega.

- **D.** Yeh WHO checklist criterion nahi hai.

**Exam clue:** Adult named checklist threshold differs from pediatric weight-based criterion.

**Clinical reference:** [Clinical source I](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/NBK143244/)
