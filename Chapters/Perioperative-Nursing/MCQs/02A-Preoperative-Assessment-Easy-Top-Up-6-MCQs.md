### Q1. Which pair of organs is especially important to assess for the metabolism and clearance of many anesthetic medicines?

**Difficulty:** Easy | **Priority:** IMPORTANT | **Exam focus:** NORCET / HPRCA HP Staff Nurse original practice

- **A.** Skin and hair follicles
- **B.** Liver and kidneys
- **C.** Spleen and appendix
- **D.** Cornea and lens

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: B. Liver and kidneys**

- **A.** Skin aur hair many systemic anesthetic drugs ki principal clearance pair nahi hain.

- **B.** Liver many drugs metabolize karta hai aur kidneys excretion mein important hain. Dono ki impairment drug handling alter kar sakti hai.

- **C.** Spleen immune function aur appendix lymphoid tissue se associated hain, primary drug-clearance pair nahi.

- **D.** Eye structures vision se related hain, systemic clearance nahi.

**Exam clue:** Hepatic and renal function guide anesthesia planning.

### Q2. Which endocrine disorder places a surgical patient at particular risk of adrenal crisis during physiological stress?

**Difficulty:** Easy | **Priority:** IMPORTANT | **Exam focus:** NORCET / HPRCA HP Staff Nurse original practice

- **A.** Uncomplicated myopia
- **B.** Benign positional vertigo
- **C.** Isolated dental caries
- **D.** Addison disease

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: D. Addison disease**

- **A.** Myopia refractive vision issue hai, cortisol deficiency nahi.

- **B.** Vertigo vestibular disorder hai, adrenal crisis mechanism nahi.

- **C.** Dental decay primary adrenal insufficiency nahi hai.

- **D.** Addison disease mein cortisol reserve inadequate ho sakta hai. Surgical stress ke liye individualized steroid plan assess karna chahiye.

**Exam clue:** Surgical stress increases cortisol requirements.

### Q3. Which endocrine emergency is associated with uncontrolled hyperthyroidism during perioperative stress?

**Difficulty:** Easy | **Priority:** IMPORTANT | **Exam focus:** NORCET / HPRCA HP Staff Nurse original practice

- **A.** Thyroid storm
- **B.** Myxedema coma
- **C.** Diabetes insipidus
- **D.** Hypoparathyroidism

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: A. Thyroid storm**

- **A.** Uncontrolled thyroid hormone excess severe systemic decompensation cause kar sakta hai. Elective readiness team assess karti hai.

- **B.** Myxedema coma severe hypothyroidism se associated hai, hyperthyroidism se nahi.

- **C.** DI water balance disorder hai, thyrotoxic crisis nahi.

- **D.** Parathyroid hormone deficiency calcium regulation affect karti hai.

**Exam clue:** Thyrotoxicosis and surgical stress require attention.

### Q4. Which preoperative history item helps identify a patient at risk of withdrawal during a hospital stay?

**Difficulty:** Easy | **Priority:** IMPORTANT | **Exam focus:** NORCET / HPRCA HP Staff Nurse original practice

- **A.** Preferred hospital meal tray color
- **B.** The brand of the patient's spectacles
- **C.** Usual alcohol intake and recent cessation
- **D.** Shoe size alone

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: C. Usual alcohol intake and recent cessation**

- **A.** Tray preference withdrawal risk identify nahi karti.

- **B.** Spectacle brand alcohol dependence ka clue nahi hai.

- **C.** Regular heavy alcohol exposure ke baad abrupt cessation withdrawal risk create kar sakti hai. Nonjudgmental history aur team planning useful hain.

- **D.** Shoe size withdrawal physiology establish nahi karti.

**Exam clue:** Ask about substance use and recent changes.

### Q6. Which medication history is especially relevant to possible suppression of the normal adrenal stress response?

**Difficulty:** Easy | **Priority:** IMPORTANT | **Exam focus:** NORCET / HPRCA HP Staff Nurse original practice

- **A.** Occasional artificial tears
- **B.** Routine oral rehydration solution
- **C.** Prolonged systemic corticosteroid use
- **D.** Topical plain petroleum jelly

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: C. Prolonged systemic corticosteroid use**

- **A.** Lubricating eye drops systemic adrenal suppression ka usual cause nahi hain.

- **B.** ORS water aur electrolytes provide karta hai, adrenal axis suppress nahi karta.

- **C.** Long-term glucocorticoid exposure hypothalamic-pituitary-adrenal axis suppress kar sakti hai. Anesthesia team history aur perioperative plan assess karti hai.

- **D.** Plain emollient glucocorticoid nahi hai.

**Exam clue:** Steroid history matters beyond the current medication list.

**Clinical reference:** [Clinical source P](https://openstax.org/books/medical-surgical-nursing/pages/25-2-preoperative-nursing-priorities)
