## Preoperative safety

### Q1. Before transfer to theatre, the patient states that the planned operation is on the opposite side from the consent form. What should the nurse do?

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Topic:** Preoperative safety

- **A.** Stop transfer progression and resolve the discrepancy with the surgical team
- **B.** Choose the side written on the form without review
- **C.** Ask the patient to sign a blank consent form
- **D.** Proceed and correct the record afterward

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: A. Stop transfer progression and resolve the discrepancy with the surgical team**

Wrong-site surgery se serious harm ho sakta hai. Jab patient aur record mein difference ho, toh nurse process rok kar surgical team se identity, procedure aur side confirm karwati hai.

**Baaki options kyun galat hain?**

- **B.** Form alone mismatch resolve nahi karta.
- **C.** Blank consent valid informed consent nahi hai.
- **D.** Baad mein record change karna harm nahi rokega.

</details>

## PACU

### Q2. A patient in the recovery room is difficult to awaken, has shallow breathing and falling oxygen saturation after opioid analgesia. What is the priority?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Topic:** PACU

- **A.** Give another opioid dose for comfort
- **B.** Offer oral water immediately
- **C.** Wait for the next scheduled observation
- **D.** Support airway and ventilation and obtain urgent assistance

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: D. Support airway and ventilation and obtain urgent assistance**

Opioid brain ke breathing control ko slow kar sakta hai. Patient ki saans aur alertness gir rahi hai, isliye airway aur ventilation support pehle chahiye.

**Baaki options kyun galat hain?**

- **A.** Extra opioid breathing aur slow kar sakta hai.
- **B.** Drowsy patient safely swallow nahi kar sakta.
- **C.** Scheduled check ka wait unsafe hai.

</details>

## Postoperative wound

### Q3. A patient coughs after abdominal surgery and bowel loops protrude through the incision. Which action is appropriate?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Topic:** Postoperative wound

- **A.** Apply a dry tight compression bandage
- **B.** Ask the patient to walk to reduce pressure
- **C.** Cover with sterile saline-moistened dressings and obtain emergency surgical help
- **D.** Push the bowel back into the abdomen

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: C. Cover with sterile saline-moistened dressings and obtain emergency surgical help**

Wound khulkar organs bahar aa jaayein toh evisceration kehte hain. Nurse tissues ko sterile moist dressing se protect karti hai aur emergency help leti hai. Organs ko andar force nahi karna chahiye.

**Baaki options kyun galat hain?**

- **A.** Dry tight dressing tissue ko harm kar sakti hai.
- **B.** Walking wound tension badha sakti hai.
- **D.** Forceful reduction tissue damage kar sakta hai.

</details>

## Anesthesia

### Q4. During general anesthesia, a patient develops rapidly increasing end-tidal CO2, muscle rigidity and rising temperature. Which complication should be suspected?

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Topic:** Anesthesia

- **A.** Stable hypothermia
- **B.** Malignant hyperthermia
- **C.** Uncomplicated postoperative shivering
- **D.** Routine local anesthetic effect

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: B. Malignant hyperthermia**

Kuch susceptible patients mein anesthesia drugs se muscles ka metabolism bahut tez ho sakta hai. CO2 badhna aur rigidity early warning hain. Team emergency malignant hyperthermia protocol follow karti hai.

**Baaki options kyun galat hain?**

- **A.** Temperature badh raha hai, kam nahi.
- **C.** Normal shivering is severe intraoperative pattern ko explain nahi karti.
- **D.** Local anesthesia effect generally ye syndrome nahi deta.

</details>
