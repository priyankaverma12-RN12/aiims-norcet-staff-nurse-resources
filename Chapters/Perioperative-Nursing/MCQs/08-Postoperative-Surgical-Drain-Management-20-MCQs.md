### Q1. Which surgical drain is classically an open passive drainage system?

**Difficulty:** Easy | **Priority:** IMPORTANT | **Exam focus:** NORCET / HPRCA HP Staff Nurse original practice

- **A.** Jackson-Pratt bulb drain
- **B.** Penrose drain
- **C.** Redivac drain
- **D.** Hemovac drain

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: B. Penrose drain**

- **A.** JP bulb compressed reservoir se negative pressure create karta hai, isliye yeh closed suction system hai.

- **B.** Penrose tube pressure gradient aur capillary action se fluid ko dressing ya collection system tak drain hone deti hai. Yeh sealed suction reservoir nahi hoti.

- **C.** Redivac vacuum container se active drainage provide karta hai. Yeh passive open strip nahi hai.

- **D.** Hemovac compressible reservoir se suction provide kar sakta hai. Iska mechanism Penrose se alag hai.

**Exam clue:** Open passive aur closed active drain mechanisms distinguish karo.
