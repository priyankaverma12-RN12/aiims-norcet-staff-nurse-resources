### Q1. Which layer has failed in abdominal fascial dehiscence?

**Difficulty:** Easy | **Priority:** IMPORTANT | **Exam focus:** NORCET / HPRCA HP Staff Nurse original practice

- **A.** Only the epidermal layer
- **B.** The sutured deep fascial layer of the abdominal wall
- **C.** Only the subcutaneous fat
- **D.** Only the visceral peritoneum of an intact bowel

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: B. The sutured deep fascial layer of the abdominal wall**

- **A.** Epidermis ki separation superficial problem ho sakti hai, deep fascial failure nahi.

- **B.** Fascia abdominal wall ki structural support layer hai. Iski closure fail hone se deep defect develop hota hai.

- **C.** Fat layer skin aur fascia ke beech hoti hai, fascial defect alag hai.

- **D.** Visceral peritoneum bowel cover karti hai, abdominal wall closure ki fascia nahi hai.

**Exam clue:** Skin intact hone par bhi deep fascial failure possible hai.
