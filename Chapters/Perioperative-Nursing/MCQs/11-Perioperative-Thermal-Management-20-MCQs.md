### Q1. During the first 30 minutes after induction of general anesthesia, core temperature falls while peripheral skin warms. Which mechanism best explains this early change?

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Priority:** IMPORTANT | **Exam focus:** NORCET / HPRCA HP Staff Nurse original practice

- **A.** Sudden cessation of all heat production by skeletal muscle
- **B.** Redistribution of heat from core to peripheral tissues due to vasodilation
- **C.** Increased central vasoconstriction retaining heat in the core
- **D.** Rapid evaporation of fluid inside a closed IV container

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: B. Redistribution of heat from core to peripheral tissues due to vasodilation**

- **A.** Metabolism reduce ho sakta hai, lekin warm periphery ke saath rapid early core fall redistribution ko support karta hai.

- **B.** Anesthetic-induced vasodilation se core heat peripheral tissues mein redistribute hoti hai. Total body heat loss ke bina bhi early core temperature fall ho sakta hai.

- **C.** Vasoconstriction core heat conserve karne mein help karti hai. Described peripheral warming vasodilation se better explain hoti hai.

- **D.** Closed container evaporation patient ki core-to-peripheral temperature change ka main explanation nahi hai.

**Exam clue:** Early anesthetic cooling mein redistribution important hai.
