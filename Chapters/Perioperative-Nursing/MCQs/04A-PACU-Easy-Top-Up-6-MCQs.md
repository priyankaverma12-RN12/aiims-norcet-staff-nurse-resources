### Q1. What is emergence from general anesthesia?

**Difficulty:** Easy | **Priority:** IMPORTANT | **Exam focus:** NORCET / HPRCA HP Staff Nurse original practice

- **A.** The first administration of an induction drug
- **B.** The preparation of the surgical incision site
- **C.** The final surgical pathology report
- **D.** The process of regaining wakefulness as general anesthesia ends

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: D. The process of regaining wakefulness as general anesthesia ends**

- **A.** Induction anesthesia start karne ka phase hai, emergence end/recovery phase hai.

- **B.** Skin preparation operative safety task hai, consciousness recovery nahi.

- **C.** Pathology report specimen diagnosis se related hai.

- **D.** Emergence drug effect decrease aur neurological recovery ka transition hai. Airway aur circulation monitoring continue hoti hai.

**Exam clue:** Emergence is recovery from the anesthetized state.

### Q2. What is laryngospasm in the perioperative period?

**Difficulty:** Easy | **Priority:** IMPORTANT | **Exam focus:** NORCET / HPRCA HP Staff Nurse original practice

- **A.** Permanent widening of the bronchi
- **B.** Reflex closure of the glottis
- **C.** Fluid accumulation in the pleural cavity
- **D.** Blood clot in a pulmonary artery

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: B. Reflex closure of the glottis**

- **A.** Bronchiectasis persistent bronchial dilation hai, glottic closure nahi.

- **B.** Laryngeal reflex closure airflow obstruct kar sakti hai. Suspected event urgent anesthesia assistance require karta hai.

- **C.** Pleural effusion fluid collection hai, vocal cord reflex closure nahi.

- **D.** Pulmonary embolism vascular obstruction hai, laryngospasm airway reflex hai.

**Exam clue:** Laryngospasm affects the glottic airway.

### Q3. Which abnormal breath sound commonly suggests upper-airway narrowing after extubation?

**Difficulty:** Easy | **Priority:** IMPORTANT | **Exam focus:** NORCET / HPRCA HP Staff Nurse original practice

- **A.** Inspiratory stridor
- **B.** Normal vesicular breath sounds
- **C.** Normal bowel sounds
- **D.** Physiological heart sounds

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: A. Inspiratory stridor**

- **A.** High-pitched inspiratory sound upper airway obstruction ka clue ho sakta hai. Urgent respiratory assessment chahiye.

- **B.** Normal lung sounds airway narrowing ka typical warning sign nahi hain.

- **C.** Bowel sounds abdominal motility assess karte hain.

- **D.** Heart sounds upper-airway airflow evaluate nahi karte.

**Exam clue:** Stridor is an airway warning sign.

### Q4. Which common minor airway complaint can occur after uncomplicated tracheal intubation?

**Difficulty:** Easy | **Priority:** IMPORTANT | **Exam focus:** NORCET / HPRCA HP Staff Nurse original practice

- **A.** Acute renal colic as an expected intubation effect
- **B.** A new fractured femur
- **C.** Sore throat
- **D.** A newly formed abdominal incisional hernia

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: C. Sore throat**

- **A.** Renal colic urinary tract pathology se associated hai, usual intubation irritation nahi.

- **B.** Femoral fracture airway instrumentation ka common minor effect nahi hai.

- **C.** Airway instrumentation mucosa irritate kar sakti hai. Mild throat discomfort possible hai; severe ya progressive symptoms evaluate karne chahiye.

- **D.** Incisional hernia fascia defect se related hai, tracheal tube irritation se nahi.

**Exam clue:** Expected mild irritation is distinct from respiratory deterioration.

### Q5. What is the basic mechanism of negative-pressure pulmonary edema after an obstructed airway?

**Difficulty:** Easy | **Priority:** IMPORTANT | **Exam focus:** NORCET / HPRCA HP Staff Nurse original practice

- **A.** Excessive production of digestive bile
- **B.** Forceful inspiratory efforts against obstruction create very negative intrathoracic pressure
- **C.** Loss of all pulmonary blood flow from normal inspiration
- **D.** Increased skin evaporation alone

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: B. Forceful inspiratory efforts against obstruction create very negative intrathoracic pressure**

- **A.** Bile production airway-related pulmonary edema ka mechanism nahi hai.

- **B.** Obstructed inspiration se pressure gradient lung fluid accumulation contribute kar sakta hai. Acute hypoxemia urgent team management require karti hai.

- **C.** Normal inspiration pulmonary blood flow eliminate nahi karti.

- **D.** Skin evaporation thermal and water balance affect karti hai, described edema mechanism nahi.

**Exam clue:** Post-obstructive edema is linked to forceful inspiration.

**Clinical reference:** [Clinical source A](https://www.openanesthesia.org/keywords/complications-of-airway-management/)

### Q6. Which behavior is compatible with hyperactive emergence delirium rather than quiet hypoactive recovery?

**Difficulty:** Easy | **Priority:** IMPORTANT | **Exam focus:** NORCET / HPRCA HP Staff Nurse original practice

- **A.** Normal calm orientation to person and place
- **B.** Stable sleep with prompt appropriate responses when awakened
- **C.** Normal purposeful cooperation throughout assessment
- **D.** Acute agitation with disorientation

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: D. Acute agitation with disorientation**

- **A.** Normal orientation uncomplicated recovery ke saath compatible hai.

- **B.** Appropriate responsiveness normal recovery suggest kar sakti hai.

- **C.** Purposeful cooperation acute agitated delirium ka defining pattern nahi hai.

- **D.** Emergence mein patient restless aur disoriented ho sakta hai. Safety protect karke hypoxia, pain aur other causes assess karo.

**Exam clue:** Agitation needs assessment, not a reflex assumption of pain.

**Clinical reference:** [Clinical source E](https://www.openanesthesia.org/keywords/emergence-from-general-anesthesia/)
