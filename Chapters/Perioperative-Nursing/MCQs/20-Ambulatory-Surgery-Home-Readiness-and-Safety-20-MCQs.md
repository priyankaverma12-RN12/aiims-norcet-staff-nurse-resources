### Q1. After uncomplicated general anesthesia for adult day surgery, what is the usual minimum driving restriction in the cited RCoA patient guidance?

**Difficulty:** Easy | **Priority:** IMPORTANT | **Exam focus:** NORCET / HPRCA HP Staff Nurse original practice

- **A.** Until the patient first drinks water
- **B.** Until the patient leaves the hospital building
- **C.** 24 hours, with longer restrictions when clinically indicated
- **D.** Only while the patient is asleep

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: C. 24 hours, with longer restrictions when clinically indicated**

- **A.** Water tolerate karna driving ke liye cognition aur coordination recovery prove nahi karta.

- **B.** Hospital se discharge hona driving clearance ke barabar nahi hai.

- **C.** Anesthesia ke baad judgement aur reaction time affect ho sakte hain. Kam se kam 24 hours drive nahi karna, aur procedure-specific longer restriction follow karna chahiye.

- **D.** Awake patient mein bhi residual drug effect ho sakta hai.

**Exam clue:** Discharge readiness is not driving readiness.
