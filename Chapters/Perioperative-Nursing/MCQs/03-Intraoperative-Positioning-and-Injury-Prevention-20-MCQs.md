### Q1. A patient under general anesthesia cannot report discomfort while lying on a rigid operating table. Which mechanism explains the particular risk of positioning injury?

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Priority:** IMPORTANT | **Exam focus:** NORCET / HP Staff Nurse original practice

- **A.** Increased voluntary repositioning during unconsciousness
- **B.** Complete protection of peripheral nerves by muscle relaxation
- **C.** Increased ability to detect local ischemic pain
- **D.** Loss of protective movement and feedback during sustained pressure or stretch

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: D. Loss of protective movement and feedback during sustained pressure or stretch**

- **A.** Unconscious patient voluntarily reposition nahi karta. Team ki monitoring aur support is limitation ko compensate karti hai.

- **B.** Muscle relaxation excessive stretch ko prevent nahi karti. Joint aur nerve safe range mein support karne ki responsibility team par rehti hai.

- **C.** Anesthesia pain feedback ko suppress kar sakti hai. Local ischemia ki early complaint absent reh sakti hai.

- **D.** Anesthesia ke dauran patient pressure ya stretch feel karke position change nahi kar pata. Sustained loading se tissue perfusion aur nerves affect ho sakte hain, isliye team ko position protect karni hoti hai.

**Exam clue:** Anesthesia mein patient apne pressure points khud protect nahi kar pata.

**Difficulty:** Medium | **Priority:** IMPORTANT | **Exam focus:** NORCET / HP Staff Nurse original practice

- **A.** Verify patient security, alignment, pressure points and lines after the tilt in coordination with the team
- **B.** Check only whether the surgeon can see the incision
- **C.** Review the patient's laboratory results without checking positioning
- **D.** Confirm only that the original strap was present before the tilt

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: A. Verify patient security, alignment, pressure points and lines after the tilt in coordination with the team**

- **A.** Table angle change se body loading aur equipment position change ho sakti hai. Nurse team ke saath current security, alignment, vulnerable areas aur lines check karti hai.

- **B.** Exposure important hai, lekin patient slide, line displacement aur compression ka risk bhi evaluate karna chahiye. Sirf operative view sufficient nahi hai.

- **C.** Lab results overall clinical care mein useful hain, par table tilt se mechanical shift detect nahi karte. Actual position reassess karna chahiye.

- **D.** Original strap ki presence current alignment prove nahi karti. Tilt ke baad security aur supports confirm karne chahiye.

**Exam clue:** Har relevant position change ke baad actual safety reassess karo.
