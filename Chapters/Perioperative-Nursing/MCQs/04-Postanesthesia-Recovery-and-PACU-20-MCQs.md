### Q1. A patient receiving supplemental oxygen and IV opioid analgesia in PACU has SpO2 98%. Which additional monitoring approach is especially useful for detecting hypoventilation before oxygen desaturation?

**Difficulty:** Difficult | **Priority:** IMPORTANT | **Exam focus:** NORCET / HP Staff Nurse original practice

- **A.** Intermittent temperature measurement
- **B.** Capnography with respiratory assessment
- **C.** Repeat hemoglobin measurement
- **D.** Pulse oximetry alone

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: B. Capnography with respiratory assessment**

- **A.** Temperature infection ya hypothermia assessment mein useful hai, lekin alveolar ventilation ka direct trend nahi batata.

- **B.** Capnography exhaled CO2 aur respiratory cycling ke changes dikha sakti hai. Oxygen supplementation ke saath clinical respiratory assessment bhi zaroori hai.

- **C.** Hemoglobin oxygen carrying capacity se related hai. Yeh acute hypoventilation ko detect nahi karta.

- **D.** Supplemental oxygen saturation ko temporarily normal rakh sakti hai, jabki ventilation kam ho rahi ho.

**Exam clue:** Oxygenation aur ventilation ko alag concepts samjho.
