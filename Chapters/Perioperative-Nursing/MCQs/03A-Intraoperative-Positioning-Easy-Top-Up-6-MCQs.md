### Q1. Which operative position commonly provides access to the posterior spine?

**Difficulty:** Easy | **Priority:** IMPORTANT | **Exam focus:** NORCET / HPRCA HP Staff Nurse original practice

- **A.** Lithotomy position
- **B.** Beach-chair position
- **C.** Prone position
- **D.** Supine position

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: C. Prone position**

- **A.** Lithotomy pelvis aur perineal access facilitate karti hai.

- **B.** Beach-chair selected shoulder surgery ke liye useful hai, routine posterior spine exposure nahi.

- **C.** Prone mein patient face-down hota hai, posterior spinal region expose ho sakta hai. Safe supports aur airway coordination zaroori hain.

- **D.** Supine mein posterior surface table ki taraf hoti hai.

**Exam clue:** Posterior spine access commonly uses prone positioning.

### Q2. Where should an axillary support roll be placed for appropriate lateral surgical positioning?

**Difficulty:** Easy | **Priority:** IMPORTANT | **Exam focus:** NORCET / HPRCA HP Staff Nurse original practice

- **A.** Below the dependent axilla
- **B.** Directly inside the axillary hollow
- **C.** Across the patient's throat
- **D.** Over the dependent eye

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: A. Below the dependent axilla**

- **A.** Support below axilla thorax lift karne mein help karta hai without direct pressure on axillary structures. Actual placement team verify karti hai.

- **B.** Direct axillary compression neurovascular injury risk create kar sakti hai.

- **C.** Neck pressure airway aur circulation compromise kar sakti hai.

- **D.** Eye compression injury cause kar sakti hai.

**Exam clue:** Axillary roll belongs below, not in, the axilla.

**Clinical reference:** [Clinical source B](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/NBK513320/)

### Q3. Which nerve is particularly vulnerable to compression near the medial elbow during supine surgical positioning?

**Difficulty:** Easy | **Priority:** IMPORTANT | **Exam focus:** NORCET / HPRCA HP Staff Nurse original practice

- **A.** Optic nerve
- **B.** Phrenic nerve
- **C.** Olfactory nerve
- **D.** Ulnar nerve

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: D. Ulnar nerve**

- **A.** Optic nerve vision transmit karta hai, medial elbow par nahi hota.

- **B.** Phrenic diaphragm function control karta hai, elbow compression ka typical nerve nahi.

- **C.** Olfactory nerve smell se related hai, upper limb anatomy nahi.

- **D.** Ulnar nerve medial elbow ke near superficial hota hai. Appropriate elbow padding aur arm alignment compression reduce karte hain.

**Exam clue:** Protect the ulnar nerve at the elbow.

### Q4. How should the two legs ordinarily be moved when placing an anesthetized patient into lithotomy?

**Difficulty:** Easy | **Priority:** IMPORTANT | **Exam focus:** NORCET / HPRCA HP Staff Nurse original practice

- **A.** One leg rapidly without supporting the other
- **B.** Together in a coordinated movement
- **C.** By pulling both ankles without supporting limb weight
- **D.** Only after the patient has been left unobserved

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: B. Together in a coordinated movement**

- **A.** Asymmetric uncontrolled movement joint aur tissue strain create kar sakti hai.

- **B.** Simultaneous controlled movement hip, spine aur soft tissue strain reduce karne mein help karta hai. Enough trained personnel use karo.

- **C.** Traction aur unsupported weight tissue stress badha sakte hain.

- **D.** Anesthetized patient needs continuous team protection.

**Exam clue:** Move both legs in concert.

**Clinical reference:** [Clinical source B](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/NBK513320/)

### Q5. Which lower-limb complication is associated with prolonged lithotomy and inadequate limb perfusion even without a leg injury?

- **C.** Gallstone colic
- **D.** Well-leg compartment syndrome

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: D. Well-leg compartment syndrome**

- **A.** Middle ear infection limb pressure complication nahi hai.

- **B.** Retinal pathology lower-leg compartment pressure ka definition nahi.

- **C.** Gallstone-related pain biliary obstruction se related hai.

- **D.** Prolonged elevated support perfusion affect kar sakta hai. Ischemia, edema aur compartment pressure serious complication create kar sakte hain.

**Exam clue:** Prolonged lithotomy can threaten lower-limb perfusion.

### Q6. Which surgical table modification can improve exposure of the upper abdomen by tilting the head end upward?

**Difficulty:** Easy | **Priority:** IMPORTANT | **Exam focus:** NORCET / HPRCA HP Staff Nurse original practice

- **A.** Reverse Trendelenburg
- **B.** Head-down Trendelenburg
- **C.** Flat lithotomy without tilt
- **D.** Prone jackknife

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: A. Reverse Trendelenburg**

- **A.** Head-up tilt abdominal contents ko caudal direction mein shift karne mein help kar sakti hai. Sliding aur perfusion precautions necessary hain.

- **B.** Head-down tilt direction opposite hai, often pelvic exposure ke liye used hota hai.

- **C.** Leg elevation perineal access facilitate karti hai, described head-up tilt nahi.

- **D.** Jackknife posterior anorectal exposure ke liye selected ho sakta hai.

**Exam clue:** Match the tilt to operative exposure.

**Clinical reference:** [Clinical source B](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/NBK513320/)

</details>
