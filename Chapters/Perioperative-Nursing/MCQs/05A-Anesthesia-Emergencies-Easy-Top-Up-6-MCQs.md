### Q1. Which specialized muscle test may be used to assess susceptibility to malignant hyperthermia in North America?

**Difficulty:** Easy | **Priority:** IMPORTANT | **Exam focus:** NORCET / HPRCA HP Staff Nurse original practice

- **A.** Caffeine-halothane contracture test
- **B.** Routine urine pregnancy test
- **C.** Mantoux tuberculin test
- **D.** Serum amylase alone

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: A. Caffeine-halothane contracture test**

- **A.** CHCT specialized center mein biopsied muscle response evaluate karta hai. Yeh routine bedside blood test nahi hai.

- **B.** Pregnancy test hCG detect karta hai, MH susceptibility assess nahi karta.

- **C.** Mantoux TB-related immune response assess karta hai.

- **D.** Amylase pancreatic or salivary enzyme marker hai, MH susceptibility confirm nahi karta.

**Exam clue:** CHCT is a specialist susceptibility test.

### Q2. Which gene is a major target of genetic evaluation after a confirmed fulminant malignant hyperthermia event?

**Difficulty:** Easy | **Priority:** IMPORTANT | **Exam focus:** NORCET / HPRCA HP Staff Nurse original practice

- **A.** HBB
- **B.** CFTR
- **C.** RYR1
- **D.** F8

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: C. RYR1**

- **A.** HBB beta-globin gene hai, primarily hemoglobin disorders se related hai.

- **B.** CFTR epithelial ion transport aur cystic fibrosis se related hai.

- **C.** RYR1 skeletal muscle calcium release channel se related hai. Genetic testing expert counseling ke saath interpret hoti hai; negative result susceptibility exclude nahi karta.

- **D.** F8 coagulation factor VIII encode karta hai, MH ka major susceptibility gene nahi.

**Exam clue:** RYR1 is linked with skeletal muscle calcium regulation.

### Q4. What is the purpose of approved activated-charcoal filters when preparing an anesthesia machine for a susceptible patient?

**Difficulty:** Easy | **Priority:** IMPORTANT | **Exam focus:** NORCET / HPRCA HP Staff Nurse original practice

- **A.** Increase the dose of inhaled triggering anesthetic
- **B.** Measure the patient's serum potassium
- **C.** Replace the anesthesia machine oxygen supply
- **D.** Reduce residual volatile anesthetic contamination in the breathing system

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: D. Reduce residual volatile anesthetic contamination in the breathing system**

- **A.** Higher trigger exposure prevention goal ke opposite hai.

- **B.** Filter breathing gas contamination control karta hai, blood electrolytes measure nahi karta.

- **C.** Filter oxygen source replace nahi karta.

- **D.** Approved filter setup residual triggering vapors reduce karne mein help karta hai. Machine-specific preparation trained team perform karti hai.

**Exam clue:** Machine preparation reduces residual trigger exposure.

### Q5. Which laboratory system should be followed after an MH crisis to help recognize disseminated intravascular coagulation?

- **A.** Visual acuity testing
- **B.** Routine hearing screening alone
- **C.** Coagulation profile
- **D.** Blood group identification alone

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: C. Coagulation profile**

- **A.** Vision testing coagulation function assess nahi karti.

- **B.** Hearing screening clotting abnormalities detect nahi karti.

- **C.** MH crisis mein DIC complication ho sakti hai. Platelets aur relevant clotting investigations team ke plan ke according monitor hote hain.

- **D.** Blood group transfusion matching mein useful hai, active coagulation assessment nahi.

**Exam clue:** MH complications include coagulation abnormalities.

### Q6. A known MH-susceptible patient has an uneventful nontriggering day-case anesthetic. What discharge principle does MHAUS describe?

**Difficulty:** Easy | **Priority:** IMPORTANT | **Exam focus:** NORCET / HPRCA HP Staff Nurse original practice

- **A.** Standard discharge criteria can apply without mandatory prolonged observation solely for susceptibility
- **B.** Every susceptible patient requires ICU admission after any operation
- **C.** Discharge before any routine recovery assessment
- **D.** Administer prophylactic dantrolene at discharge to every susceptible patient

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: A. Standard discharge criteria can apply without mandatory prolonged observation solely for susceptibility**

- **A.** Susceptibility aur actual MH crisis different situations hain. Uneventful nontriggering case mein individualized routine criteria apply ho sakte hain.

- **B.** Actual event ya clinical need absent ho toh blanket ICU requirement cited guidance nahi hai.

- **C.** Standard recovery assessment still required hai.

- **D.** Routine discharge dantrolene universal requirement nahi hai.

**Exam clue:** Susceptibility alone is not the same as a crisis.

**Clinical reference:** [Clinical source P](https://www.mhaus.org/healthcare-professionals/be-prepared/post-operative-procedure/)
