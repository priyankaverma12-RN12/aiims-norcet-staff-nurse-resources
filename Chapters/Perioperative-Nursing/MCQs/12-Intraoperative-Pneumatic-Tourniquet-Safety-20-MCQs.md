- **C.** Improve arterial circulation through continuous occlusion
- **D.** Temporarily reduce blood flow to improve operative visibility

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: D. Temporarily reduce blood flow to improve operative visibility**

- **A.** IPC prophylaxis intermittent compression se venous return support karti hai, surgical tourniquet arterial occlusion ka different device hai.

- **B.** Mechanical pressure anesthesia ka reliable substitute nahi hai. Anesthetic plan separately required hai.

- **C.** Arterial occlusion inflow reduce karti hai, improve nahi.

- **D.** Controlled arterial occlusion se bloodless field create karne mein help milti hai. Ischemia ka duration aur pressure minimize karna important hai.

**Exam clue:** Surgical tourniquet aur IPC sleeve ko confuse mat karo.
