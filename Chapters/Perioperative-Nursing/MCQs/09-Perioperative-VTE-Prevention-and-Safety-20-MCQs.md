### Q1. What is the intended physiologic effect of intermittent pneumatic compression during perioperative VTE prevention?

- **A.** Intermittent external compression promotes venous return and reduces venous stasis
- **B.** The device directly dissolves every existing thrombus
- **C.** The device intentionally stops arterial blood supply
- **D.** The device acts as an injected anticoagulant

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: A. Intermittent external compression promotes venous return and reduces venous stasis**

- **A.** Sleeve inflate aur deflate hokar limb veins se blood movement support karti hai. Yeh stasis reduce karne ka mechanical approach hai.

- **B.** Mechanical prophylaxis prevention ke liye hoti hai. Existing thrombus ke treatment ka substitute nahi hai.

- **C.** Arterial perfusion stop karna desired effect nahi hai. Limb safety aur device suitability assess karna zaroori hai.

- **D.** IPC external mechanical device hai, systemic drug nahi hai.

**Exam clue:** Mechanical prophylaxis ko thrombolytic treatment se alag samjho.
