### Q1. What does ERAS stand for in a perioperative recovery pathway?

**Difficulty:** Easy | **Priority:** IMPORTANT | **Exam focus:** NORCET / HPRCA HP Staff Nurse original practice

- **A.** Emergency Resuscitation After Sedation
- **B.** Endoscopic Repair of Abdominal Structures
- **C.** Enhanced Recovery After Surgery
- **D.** Elective Radiology Assessment Service

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: C. Enhanced Recovery After Surgery**

- **A.** Yeh ERAS ka recognized expansion nahi hai; resuscitation separate emergency process hai.

- **B.** Endoscopic repair procedure-specific technique ho sakti hai, ERAS pathway ka full form nahi.

- **C.** ERAS coordinated evidence-based interventions ko integrate karta hai to support recovery and reduce avoidable complications.

- **D.** Radiology assessment diagnostic service hai, ERAS recovery framework nahi.

**Exam clue:** ERAS is a multimodal recovery pathway.

**Clinical reference:** [Clinical source E](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9746347/)

### Q2. Which part of the gastrointestinal tract generally regains motility last after abdominal surgery?

**Difficulty:** Easy | **Priority:** IMPORTANT | **Exam focus:** NORCET / HPRCA HP Staff Nurse original practice

- **A.** Colon
- **B.** Esophagus
- **C.** Duodenum
- **D.** Oral cavity

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: A. Colon**

- **A.** Colonic motility commonly small-bowel recovery se later return karti hai. Actual symptoms aur recovery trajectory assess karo.

- **B.** Esophagus swallowing ke baad food transport karta hai; cited postoperative recovery pattern mein last segment colon hai.

- **C.** Proximal small bowel commonly earlier recover karta hai compared with colon.

- **D.** Oral cavity chewing aur swallowing mein involved hai, intestinal motility sequence ka last segment nahi.

**Exam clue:** Colonic recovery can be slower.

**Clinical reference:** [Clinical source I](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/NBK560780/)

### Q3. Which autonomic response tends to inhibit bowel motility during surgical stress?

**Difficulty:** Easy | **Priority:** IMPORTANT | **Exam focus:** NORCET / HPRCA HP Staff Nurse original practice

- **A.** Increased parasympathetic stimulation alone
- **B.** A somatic reflex confined to the pupil
- **C.** An isolated auditory reflex
- **D.** Increased sympathetic activity

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: D. Increased sympathetic activity**

- **A.** Parasympathetic activity generally motility support karti hai.

- **B.** Pupillary response directly intestinal propulsion explain nahi karta.

- **C.** Auditory reflex bowel autonomic regulation ka principal mechanism nahi.

- **D.** Sympathetic activation gut propulsion reduce kar sakti hai. Postoperative ileus multifactorial hota hai, including inflammatory and drug effects.

**Exam clue:** Sympathetic stress can slow gastrointestinal propulsion.

### Q4. Which intraoperative factor is associated with a greater risk of postoperative ileus?

**Difficulty:** Easy | **Priority:** IMPORTANT | **Exam focus:** NORCET / HPRCA HP Staff Nurse original practice

- **A.** A brief superficial incision that does not involve the bowel
- **B.** Extensive manipulation of the intestine
- **C.** Routine application of an arm identification band
- **D.** A preoperative visual acuity assessment

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: B. Extensive manipulation of the intestine**

- **A.** Is described procedure mein direct bowel manipulation minimal hai.

- **B.** Bowel manipulation neurogenic aur inflammatory responses trigger kar sakti hai, jisse recovery delay ho sakti hai.

- **C.** Identity band gut handling ya motility mechanism se related nahi hai.

- **D.** Vision assessment ileus ka recognized direct operative risk factor nahi.

**Exam clue:** Operative bowel handling contributes to motility disturbance.

**Clinical reference:** [Clinical source I](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/NBK560780/)

### Q5. Which characteristic of the abdominal percussion note may occur when postoperative bowel distension is predominantly caused by gas?

**Difficulty:** Easy | **Priority:** IMPORTANT | **Exam focus:** NORCET / HPRCA HP Staff Nurse original practice

- **A.** Stony dullness as the defining sound of all intestinal gas
- **B.** A heart murmur over the abdomen
- **C.** Tympany
- **D.** A normal vesicular breath sound

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: C. Tympany**

- **A.** Stony dullness gas-related tympany ke opposite pattern hai.

- **B.** Heart murmur cardiac flow sound hai, abdominal percussion note nahi.

- **C.** Gas-filled bowel percussion par drum-like tympanic note de sakta hai. Is finding alone se ileus aur obstruction distinguish nahi hote.

- **D.** Vesicular sound lung auscultation finding hai, percussion description nahi.

**Exam clue:** Gas distension can produce tympany, but it is nonspecific.

**Clinical reference:** [Clinical source I](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/NBK560780/)
