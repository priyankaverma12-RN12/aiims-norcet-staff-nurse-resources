### Q1. Besides oxygen, which anesthetic gas can support combustion as an oxidizer in the surgical fire triangle?

**Difficulty:** Easy | **Priority:** IMPORTANT | **Exam focus:** NORCET / HPRCA HP Staff Nurse original practice

- **A.** Carbon dioxide
- **B.** Nitrous oxide
- **C.** Helium
- **D.** Nitrogen

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: B. Nitrous oxide**

- **A.** CO2 usual operating-room fire triangle oxidizer nahi hai.

- **B.** Nitrous oxide combustion support kar sakta hai despite being nonflammable itself. Fire risk assessment mein oxidizer exposure consider karo.

- **C.** Helium inert gas hai aur combustion support nahi karta.

- **D.** Nitrogen ordinary anesthetic environment mein oxidizer ka role nahi rakhta.

**Exam clue:** Oxidizers are not necessarily themselves fuels.

### Q3. When a surgical laser or fiberoptic light source is temporarily not in use, which mode helps prevent unintended activation?

**Difficulty:** Easy | **Priority:** IMPORTANT | **Exam focus:** NORCET / HPRCA HP Staff Nurse original practice

- **A.** Standby mode according to device instructions
- **B.** Maximum continuous output
- **C.** Alarm-disabled active mode
- **D.** Uncontrolled automatic activation

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: A. Standby mode according to device instructions**

- **A.** Standby unintentional active energy exposure reduce karne mein help karta hai. Manufacturer instructions follow karo.

- **B.** Unneeded maximum output thermal injury aur fire risk increase kar sakta hai.

- **C.** Alarm disable karna safe inactive state nahi banata.

- **D.** Unexpected activation injury risk create karti hai.

**Exam clue:** Inactive energy devices should be made safe.

**Clinical reference:** [Clinical source F](https://www.aorn.org/article/3-risks-for-fire-in-the-or-interventions-you-need)

### Q4. What type of approved lubricant is preferred near the surgical field during laser procedures to reduce flammable-material exposure?

**Difficulty:** Easy | **Priority:** IMPORTANT | **Exam focus:** NORCET / HPRCA HP Staff Nurse original practice

- **A.** Petroleum-based lubricant as a routine default
- **B.** Alcohol-based fragrance oil
- **C.** Water-soluble lubricant
- **D.** Unspecified household grease

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: C. Water-soluble lubricant**

- **A.** Petroleum-containing material flammable fuel contribute kar sakta hai.

- **B.** Fragrance oil lubricant ka suitable sterile substitute nahi aur flammability concern ho sakti hai.

- **C.** Water-soluble product laser fire precautions ke saath compatible ho sakta hai. Procedure-specific approved formulation use karo.

- **D.** Household product clinical compatibility aur sterility establish nahi karta.

**Exam clue:** Laser vicinity uses appropriate water-soluble products.

### Q5. What is the intended warning function of active-electrode monitoring during laparoscopic electrosurgery?

**Difficulty:** Easy | **Priority:** IMPORTANT | **Exam focus:** NORCET / HPRCA HP Staff Nurse original practice

- **A.** Determine the patient's blood glucose
- **B.** Detect insulation failure and unintended stray electrical energy
- **C.** Measure the amount of urine in the catheter
- **D.** Identify the patient's blood group

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: B. Detect insulation failure and unintended stray electrical energy**

- **A.** Electrode monitoring glucose test nahi hai.

- **B.** Monitoring system insulation defect aur unintended current pathway identify karne mein help karta hai. Actual device checks still required hain.

- **C.** Urine measurement separate fluid-balance assessment hai.

- **D.** Blood group laboratory testing se establish hota hai, electrical monitoring se nahi.

**Exam clue:** Active-electrode monitoring addresses unintended energy transfer.
