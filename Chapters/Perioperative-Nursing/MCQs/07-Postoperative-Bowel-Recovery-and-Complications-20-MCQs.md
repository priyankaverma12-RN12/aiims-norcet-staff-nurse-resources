### Q1. Which process defines postoperative paralytic ileus?

**Difficulty:** Easy | **Priority:** IMPORTANT | **Exam focus:** NORCET / HPRCA HP Staff Nurse original practice

- **A.** Mechanical narrowing by an adhesive band
- **B.** Excess gastric acid secretion
- **C.** Isolated difficulty swallowing
- **D.** Reduced intestinal propulsion without a mechanical blockage

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: D. Reduced intestinal propulsion without a mechanical blockage**

- **A.** Adhesion se physical obstruction ho sakti hai. Yeh paralytic ileus se different mechanism hai.

- **B.** Acid secretion dyspepsia mein role rakh sakti hai, lekin intestinal propulsion failure define nahi karti.

- **C.** Dysphagia swallowing process ka problem hai, distal intestinal motility ka nahi.

- **D.** Surgery ke baad gut ki coordinated motility temporarily slow ho sakti hai. Lumen mein physical obstruction hona ileus ki definition ke liye necessary nahi hai.

**Exam clue:** Functional aur mechanical obstruction ka mechanism alag hai.
