### Q1. Which instrument category is primarily used to expose a surgical site by moving tissue aside?

**Difficulty:** Easy | **Priority:** IMPORTANT | **Exam focus:** NORCET / HPRCA HP Staff Nurse original practice

- **A.** Needle holders
- **B.** Hemostatic clamps
- **C.** Scalpels
- **D.** Retractors

<details><summary>Answer aur easy explanation dekho</summary>

**Correct: D. Retractors**

- **A.** Needle holder suturing ke dauran needle control karta hai.

- **B.** Hemostatic clamps vessels ya tissue ko occlude karte hain.

- **C.** Scalpel incision banane ke liye cutting instrument hai.

- **D.** Retractors tissue ko side mein hold karke operative site visible banate hain.
