## Air route
**Nose → pharynx → larynx → trachea → bronchi → bronchioles → respiratory bronchioles → alveolar ducts/sacs → alveoli.**

- **Nose:** warms, filters and humidifies inspired air (hawa ko garam, saaf aur moist karta hai).
- **Pharynx:** common passage for air and food.
- **Larynx:** voice box; **epiglottis** helps protect laryngeal inlet during swallowing (airway ki safety).
- **Trachea:** windpipe supported by C-shaped cartilage rings.
- **Bronchi:** carry air into lungs. **Bronchioles:** progressively smaller airways.
- **Conducting zone:** passage of air; ends at **terminal bronchioles**.
- **Respiratory zone:** starts at **respiratory bronchioles** and includes alveolar ducts/sacs/alveoli, where exchange can occur.

**NORCET memory:** Terminal = last *conducting* part; respiratory bronchiole = first *respiratory* part.
