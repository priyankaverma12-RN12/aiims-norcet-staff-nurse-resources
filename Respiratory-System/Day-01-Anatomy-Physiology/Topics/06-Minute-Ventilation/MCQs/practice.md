### Q1. RR 17/min and TV 400 mL. MV is:

- **A.** 6.8 L/min
- **B.** 7.2 L/min
- **C.** 5.8 L/min
- **D.** 8.0 L/min

<details><summary>✅ Answer & explanation</summary>

**A.** 17 × 400 = 6800 mL/min = 6.8 L/min.

</details>

### Q2. MV is 8.1 L/min and TV is 450 mL. RR is:

- **A.** 15/min
- **B.** 16/min
- **C.** 18/min
- **D.** 20/min

<details><summary>✅ Answer & explanation</summary>

**C.** 8100 ÷ 450 = 18 breaths/min.

</details>

### Q3. MV is 7.2 L/min and RR is 16/min. TV is:

- **A.** 350 mL
- **B.** 400 mL
- **C.** 450 mL
- **D.** 500 mL

<details><summary>✅ Answer & explanation</summary>

**C.** 7200 ÷ 16 = 450 mL.

</details>

### Q4. Before: RR 10, TV 600; after: RR 15, TV 400. Change in MV?

- **A.** Increase by 2 L/min
- **B.** Decrease by 1 L/min
- **C.** Increase by 1 L/min
- **D.** No change

<details><summary>✅ Answer & explanation</summary>

**D.** Both are 6000 mL/min = 6 L/min.

</details>

### Q5. MV rises from 6 L/min to 7.5 L/min. Percent increase?

- **A.** 15%
- **B.** 20%
- **C.** 25%
- **D.** 30%

<details><summary>✅ Answer & explanation</summary>

**C.** (7.5−6)/6 × 100 = 25%.

</details>
