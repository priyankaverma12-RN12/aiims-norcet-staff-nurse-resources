### Q1. What remains after maximal forced expiration?

- **A.** TV
- **B.** IRV
- **C.** RV
- **D.** ERV

<details><summary>✅ Answer & explanation</summary>

**C.** Residual volume cannot be voluntarily expelled.

</details>

### Q2. What remains after a normal quiet expiration?

- **A.** VC
- **B.** FRC
- **C.** IRV
- **D.** IC

<details><summary>✅ Answer & explanation</summary>

**B.** Functional residual capacity equals ERV + RV.

</details>

### Q3. If IC = 3450 mL and TV = 500 mL, IRV equals:

- **A.** 3950
- **B.** 2950
- **C.** 2450
- **D.** 3450

<details><summary>✅ Answer & explanation</summary>

**B.** IRV = IC − TV = 3450 − 500 = 2950 mL.

</details>

### Q4. If VC = 4400 mL, ERV = 900 mL and TV = 500 mL, IRV equals:

- **A.** 3000
- **B.** 3400
- **C.** 3900
- **D.** 2500

<details><summary>✅ Answer & explanation</summary>

**A.** IRV = VC − ERV − TV = 4400 − 900 − 500 = 3000 mL.

</details>

### Q5. If TLC = 5900 mL and IC = 3600 mL, FRC equals:

- **A.** 1300
- **B.** 3600
- **C.** 2300
- **D.** 9500

<details><summary>✅ Answer & explanation</summary>

**C.** FRC = TLC − IC = 2300 mL.

</details>
