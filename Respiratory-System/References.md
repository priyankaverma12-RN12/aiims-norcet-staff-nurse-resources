## Limits and cautions

- Normal adult resting respiratory rate is commonly taught as **12–20/min**. Exam ranges for blood gases include **PaCO₂ 35–45 mmHg**, **pH 7.35–7.45**, **HCO₃⁻ roughly 22–26 mEq/L**. Local laboratories may use slightly different reference intervals.
- **SpO₂ ~95–100%** and **PaO₂ ~80–100 mmHg** are common healthy adult **sea-level teaching ranges**, not unconditional targets or diagnostic rules. Altitude, age and condition matter.
- ABG interpretation uses pH, PaCO₂ and HCO₃⁻ together, not one number in isolation.
- Spirometry is effort-dependent; obstructive and restrictive patterns need proper interpretation. A low FVC alone does **not** confirm restriction (TLC is needed).
- This collection is an educational study aid. For patient care, use official institutional protocols and trained clinical judgment.
