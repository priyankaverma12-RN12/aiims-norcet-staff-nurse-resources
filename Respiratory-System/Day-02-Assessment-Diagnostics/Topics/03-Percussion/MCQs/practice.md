### Q1. Expected percussion sound over normal aerated lung:

- **A.** Dullness
- **B.** Resonance
- **C.** Flatness
- **D.** Tympany

<details><summary>✅ Answer & explanation</summary>

**B.** Healthy air-filled lung usually sounds resonant.

</details>

### Q2. Percussion over pleural fluid usually gives:

- **A.** Hyperresonance
- **B.** Tympany
- **C.** Dullness
- **D.** Normal resonance

<details><summary>✅ Answer & explanation</summary>

**C.** Pleural fluid produces a relatively dull note.

</details>

### Q3. A hyperresonant percussion note suggests:

- **A.** Extra air
- **B.** Lung consolidation
- **C.** Pleural effusion
- **D.** Bone

<details><summary>✅ Answer & explanation</summary>

**A.** More air can yield hyperresonance.

</details>

### Q4. Which method compares percussion notes side to side?

- **A.** Only one apex tested
- **B.** Listen only at sternum
- **C.** Test over clothing
- **D.** Compare corresponding areas

<details><summary>✅ Answer & explanation</summary>

**D.** Bilateral comparison helps detect asymmetry.

</details>
