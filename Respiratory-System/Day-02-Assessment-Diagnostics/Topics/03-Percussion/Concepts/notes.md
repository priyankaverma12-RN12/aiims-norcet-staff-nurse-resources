## Tapping to judge underlying tissue
Percussion is tapping the chest wall and **listening to the resulting note**. Compare corresponding sites on both sides.

| Note | Usual significance |
|---|---|
| **Resonant** | Expected over healthy air-filled lung |
| **Dull** | Denser tissue or fluid, e.g., consolidation/effusion |
| **Hyperresonant** | Extra air, e.g., pneumothorax or hyperinflation |
| **Flat** | Very dense structure such as muscle or bone |

**Exam memory:** Normal air = resonant; fluid/solid = dull; extra air = hyperresonant.

Percussion findings support but cannot by themselves confirm a diagnosis.
