## First look (dekho)
Inspection means **observe without touching**. Introduce yourself; check patient comfort and position.

Look at:
- **Respiratory rate** (count breaths/min), **rhythm** and **depth**.
- Work of breathing: accessory muscles, nasal flaring, retractions.
- Chest movement: whether both sides rise **symmetrically**.
- Posture and ability to speak in full sentences; visible distress.
- Skin/lip colour and oxygen saturation as part of broader assessment.

**Usual resting adult RR: 12–20/min** (teaching reference). Abnormally increased rate = tachypnea; abnormally decreased rate = bradypnea.

**Important:** A visual observation alone does not establish a disease. Sudden respiratory distress requires urgent clinical assessment according to protocol.
