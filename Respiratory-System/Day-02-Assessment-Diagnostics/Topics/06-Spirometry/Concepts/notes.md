## Spirometry basics
Spirometry measures **air movement** in and out during forced breathing manoeuvres. Correct coaching and a good seal matter.

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **FVC** | Forced vital capacity: total forcibly exhaled after maximal inspiration |
| **FEV₁** | Air forcibly exhaled in first **1 second** |
| **FEV₁/FVC** | Proportion exhaled in first second |
| **PEF** | Peak expiratory flow (maximal instantaneous flow) |

**Obstruction:** FEV₁/FVC is reduced, suggesting airflow limitation. **Restriction suspicion:** FVC may be reduced with normal/high FEV₁/FVC, but **restriction requires confirmation with TLC**, not spirometry alone.

**Do not confuse:** **RV** cannot be measured using ordinary simple spirometry because it cannot be exhaled.

### Example
FEV₁ = 2.1 L and FVC = 3.0 L → FEV₁/FVC = 2.1/3.0 × 100 = **70%**.

### Technique
Explain procedure, upright posture when possible, maximal inspiration, explosive forced expiration sustained to completion. Interpretation uses quality criteria and appropriate reference equations.

**NORCET memory:** FEV₁ = **first 1 second**; FVC = **all forced exhaled air**.
