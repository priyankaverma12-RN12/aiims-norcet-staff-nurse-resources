### Q1. A nurse places hands on the posterior chest to compare movement during inhalation. What is assessed?

- **A.** Chest expansion
- **B.** Percussion note
- **C.** Bronchial breathing
- **D.** PaCO₂

<details><summary>✅ Answer & explanation</summary>

**A.** Palpation can compare right and left chest expansion.

</details>

### Q2. A vibration felt over chest while patient speaks is called:

- **A.** Resonance
- **B.** Fremitus
- **C.** Wheeze
- **D.** Stridor

<details><summary>✅ Answer & explanation</summary>

**B.** Tactile fremitus is palpable vocal vibration.

</details>

### Q3. Tactile fremitus may increase with:

- **A.** Pleural effusion
- **B.** Pneumothorax
- **C.** Lung consolidation with open bronchus
- **D.** Large airway obstruction

<details><summary>✅ Answer & explanation</summary>

**C.** Consolidated tissue can transmit sound vibration better.

</details>

### Q4. A normally positioned trachea is typically:

- **A.** Deviated right
- **B.** Deviated left
- **C.** Not palpable
- **D.** Midline

<details><summary>✅ Answer & explanation</summary>

**D.** Trachea is normally near the midline.

</details>
