### Q1. Which non-invasive device estimates oxygen saturation?

- **A.** Pulse oximeter
- **B.** Spirometer
- **C.** Percussion hammer
- **D.** Stethoscope

<details><summary>✅ Answer & explanation</summary>

**A.** Pulse oximetry estimates peripheral oxygen saturation (SpO₂).

</details>

### Q2. Which test provides arterial pH and PaCO₂?

- **A.** Chest X-ray
- **B.** ABG
- **C.** Spirometry
- **D.** Routine auscultation

<details><summary>✅ Answer & explanation</summary>

**B.** Arterial blood gas directly measures these values.

</details>

### Q3. Which basic test is designed to measure forced expiratory airflow?

- **A.** Sputum culture
- **B.** Pulse oximetry
- **C.** Spirometry
- **D.** Chest X-ray

<details><summary>✅ Answer & explanation</summary>

**C.** Spirometry measures forced lung volumes and airflow.

</details>
