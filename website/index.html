How progress works and reset options

Your progress increases when you check an answer or mark a topic done. Opening a topic does not count. In timed tests, answers count when you submit. Progress is different from your test score. If questions change, you may need to mark the topic done again. Resetting removes manual completion credit but keeps your answered-question history, bookmarks and saved test results. Progress is saved on this browser.

How are these counts calculated?

Counts show available questions. Theory counts represent published teaching documents, not every subheading or syllabus topic. Coverage maps and research indexes are excluded. Unfinished mock sections are question batches still being prepared and are not counted as complete mock tests. Some questions also appear in mock tests, so the categories are shown separately. New questions appear here when the website is updated.
