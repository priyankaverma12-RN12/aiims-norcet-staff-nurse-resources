## How to use this library

1. **Concepts:** Read the notes and diagrams to understand the topic.
2. **MCQs:** Attempt the English questions before opening the Hinglish answer explanations.
3. **Mock tests:** Use the chapter's published tests to practice under timed conditions.
4. **Previous year questions:** Check the exam name, source and evidence label before treating a question as an official PYQ.
5. **Revision:** Revisit the high-yield notes and incorrect answers.

## Study and evidence standards

- New original practice and source-based exam adaptations are tracked separately. A reported recall or adaptation is not an authenticated official question.
- Published study-ready material is not automatically marked as studied or complete.
- The current custom mock target is ten chapter tests of 120 new mock-exclusive questions each. Legacy compiled revision mocks remain available and are not counted as new originals.
- Check the individual chapter and question indexes for current availability and evidence gaps. Historical counts are not live totals.
